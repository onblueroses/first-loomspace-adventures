## Key characteristics of river ecosystems

Dr. Aqua stops in front of a large display titled "Key Characteristics of River Ecosystems." The visitor takes a moment to read through the information presented.

1. Unidirectional water flow: The continuous downstream movement of water is a defining feature of river ecosystems, influencing nutrient transport, habitat structure, and species composition.

2. Gradient and channel morphology: The slope of the river channel and its shape (e.g., width, depth, and substrate) change from the headwaters to the mouth, affecting water velocity, temperature, and habitat availability.
