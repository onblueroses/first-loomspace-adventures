### 530. River of Grass
A "river of grass" refers to the vast sawgrass marshes and wetlands that make up the Florida Everglades. The term was popularized by Marjory Stoneman Douglas in her 1947 book "The Everglades: River of Grass." The Everglades is a unique and complex ecosystem located in southern Florida, characterized by slow-moving water flow through a wide, shallow "river" dominated by sawgrass (Cladium jamaicense), a tall sedge.
