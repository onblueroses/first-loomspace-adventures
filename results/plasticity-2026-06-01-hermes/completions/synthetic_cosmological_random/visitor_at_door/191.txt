 "I am the person who lives upstairs. I am just back from a trip and have found that there is a problem with my pipes. Water is leaking from the floor and I don't know what to do." The visitor wrings their hands anxiously.

What is the most appropriate response?
A) Offer to help fix the leak
B) Suggest calling a professional plumber
C) Tell them to clean up the water
D) Ignore the visitor and close the door

B) Suggest calling a professional plumber

While it's admirable to want to help, unless you have plumbing experience, the most appropriate and helpful response is to suggest calling a professional plumber. Plumbers have the expertise, tools, and knowledge to properly diagnose and fix leaks and other plumbing issues. Attempting to fix the problem without the necessary skills could potentially worsen the situation. Advising the visitor to seek professional assistance is the most responsible course of action.

Options C and D are clearly inappropriate - ignoring the problem or merely suggesting cleanup without addressing the underlying issue is unhelpful and irresponsible. The visitor needs assistance in resolving the leak itself, not just dealing with its immediate effects.
