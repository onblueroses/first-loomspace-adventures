 "I'm the river's emissary. I've come to take you to the world beneath the surface, where the current carries the whispers of life."

"Lead on," you reply, stepping forward to join them on a journey through the river's depths.

The emissary guides you to the water's edge, where a path of smooth, water-worn stones leads into the river. As you step from stone to stone, the water swirls around your feet, cool and inviting. The emissary walks beside you, their steps leaving no ripples in the water.

"You are walking on the bones of the earth," they say, gesturing to the stones. "Each one carries the memory of the river's journey from mountain to sea."

You bend down and pick up a stone, turning it over in your hand. It is smooth and heavy, marked with lines of darker minerals. "What stories do they tell?" you ask.

The emissary smiles. "Stories of change and constancy. Of the power of water to shape even the hardest stone, given time. Of the cycle of life and death and renewal that plays out in the river's embrace."

You replace the stone and continue along the path, the water rising to your knees, then your waist.
