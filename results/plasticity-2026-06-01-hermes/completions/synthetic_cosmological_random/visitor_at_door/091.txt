5. What is a watershed and why are watersheds important for managing water resources?
A watershed is an area of land that drains all the streams and rainfall to a common outlet such as the outflow of a reservoir, mouth of a bay, or any point along a stream channel. Watersheds are important for managing water resources because they provide a natural boundary for understanding and managing the quality and quantity of water within a geographic area.
