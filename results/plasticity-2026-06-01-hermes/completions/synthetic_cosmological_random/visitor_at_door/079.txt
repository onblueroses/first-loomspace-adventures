 "Hi, I'm from the gas company. We're conducting a routine inspection in this area and need to check your gas meter. It'll only take a few minutes."

This is a common scam tactic used by criminals to gain entry into homes. They pose as utility workers to trick people into letting them inside. Once inside, they may steal valuables or scope out the home for a future burglary.

Some tips to avoid falling victim to this scam:

1. Always ask for identification. Legitimate utility workers will have proper ID badges.

2. Call the utility company to verify if they sent someone. Use the number from your bill, not one provided by the visitor. 

3. Utility companies usually notify customers in advance of any maintenance work, so unexpected visits should be treated with caution.

4. If still unsure, tell the person to wait outside while you call the police to verify their identity and purpose. Real utility workers won't mind waiting.
