What is the energetic base of the food web in headwater streams?

The energetic base of the food web in headwater streams is primarily organic matter that enters the system from outside, in the form of leaf litter and woody debris falling from the bordering forest. This organic matter is then broken down by shredder invertebrates, which form the base of the food web. [SEP]
