 "I've come to bring you a message," he says. "A message of great importance. A message that will change your life." He pauses, as if waiting for you to invite him in.

"Well, what is it?" you ask, not bothering to hide the irritation in your voice. You don't have time for this. You have work to do.

The visitor smiles, a slow, knowing smile that makes your skin crawl. "The message," he says, "is that you are not alone. You are never alone. There are eyes watching you, always. Eyes in the shadows, eyes in the corners, eyes you cannot see. They are watching you, judging you, waiting for you to slip."

You feel a chill run down your spine, despite yourself. "Who are you?" you demand. "What do you want?"
