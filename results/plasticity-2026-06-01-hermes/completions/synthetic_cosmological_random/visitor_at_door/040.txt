 "I am the one who knocks."

They enter, and the door closes behind them.

The visitor removes their coat, revealing a familiar face. "Hello, Walter."

Walter looks up from his desk, surprised. "I didn't expect to see you again."

The visitor sits down across from Walter. "I know. But there's something I need to tell you."

Walter leans back in his chair, wary. "What is it?"

The visitor takes a deep breath. "I'm not who you think I am."

Walter frowns. "What do you mean?"

"I've been living a lie, Walter. My whole life has been a facade."

Walter's eyes narrow. "Who are you, then?"

The visitor looks down at their hands. "My name is not important. What's important is what I've done."

Walter's voice is cold. "And what have you done?"

The visitor meets Walter's gaze. "I've been working for the enemy, Walter. I've been spying on you and your organization."

Walter's face hardens. "I see."

The visitor continues. "But I've had a change of heart. I can't do this anymore. I want to help you, Walter."
