 "I am a river ecosystem. The continuous movement of water shapes my biological community. My health depends on the natural flow regime, water quality, and the integrity of my catchment. Alterations to these parameters can substantially change my composition and function."

The visitor's words evoke images of a pristine river, flowing from its headwaters through forests and meadows, teeming with life. The water is clear and cold, supporting a diverse array of aquatic insects, fish, and plants. The river is dynamic, constantly changing yet always in balance. The health of the ecosystem depends on the natural flow of water and the quality of the surrounding environment.

As the visitor continues to speak, it becomes clear that the river ecosystem is a complex and delicate balance of physical, chemical, and biological processes. The movement of water shapes the channel, creating habitats for different species. The input of organic matter from the surrounding landscape provides food for aquatic organisms. The river is a conduit for nutrients and energy, linking the mountains to the sea.
