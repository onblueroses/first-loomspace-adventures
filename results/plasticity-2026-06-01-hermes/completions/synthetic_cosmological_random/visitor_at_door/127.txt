Naturalist

A naturalist is a person who studies or is an expert in natural history, especially in the field of plants and animals. Naturalists observe and record the natural world, seeking to understand the relationships and interconnectedness of living things. They often find beauty, solace, and inspiration in the intricate web of life and the rhythms of nature.

The study of natural history can encompass many aspects of the biological sciences, including botany, zoology, ecology, and geology. Naturalists may work in a variety of settings, from museums and universities to field stations and nature reserves. They often play a crucial role in conservation efforts, using their knowledge of ecosystems to help protect and preserve natural habitats and the species that depend on them.
