 "I'm here to fix the pipes."

"Why are there so many of you?" you ask, peering at the dozen or so men assembled on the front lawn. 

"They're the apprentices," the plumber replies. "Gotta learn somehow."

You nod and show them inside to the basement. The plumber inspects the maze of pipes crisscrossing the ceiling. 

"Quite a tangle you've got here. But we'll get it sorted."

The apprentices fan out, each selecting a pipe to examine. They tap on the metal, listening intently. 

"What are they doing?" you ask.

"Learning the pipes' songs," the plumber explains. "Every pipe sings a tale - where it's been, what's flowed through it. You can hear the history if you listen right."

Intrigued, you cock an ear towards the nearest pipe. At first, only silence. Then, faintly, a melodic hum, rising and falling like a babbling brook. Cool, crisp notes tinkle over pebbly tones. You can almost see the clear mountain stream dancing over mossy stones.

The plumber smiles knowingly. "Headwater stream. Probably from the Rockies. Pristine water, those are."

You move to another pipe
