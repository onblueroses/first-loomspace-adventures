 “Hello, I’m Dr. David L. Strayer, a freshwater ecologist at the Cary Institute of Ecosystem Studies in Millbrook, New York. I’ve spent the past 30 years studying rivers, streams, and lakes, especially how they’re affected by human activities. My work has ranged from the discovery of novel ecosystems in the groundwater beneath the channel to large-scale studies of the Hudson River watershed. I’m also interested in the role of species in ecosystems and the services provided by ecosystems to human society. I’d be happy to answer any questions you have about the ecology of rivers and streams.”
