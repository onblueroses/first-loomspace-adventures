 "I am from the future. I have come to warn you. You must stop eating animals and animal products. The consequences are too great." The visitor pauses, looking at you with a mixture of urgency and compassion.

You are taken aback. This person seems sincere, even if their claim is fantastical. You hesitate, then ask, "What do you mean? What consequences?"

The visitor explains that in the future, the environmental and ethical impacts of animal agriculture have become catastrophically clear. The destruction of habitats for feed crops and grazing, the greenhouse gas emissions driving climate change, the pollution of waterways with animal waste – these have all reached breaking points, leading to ecosystem collapse, mass extinctions, and devastating weather extremes.

Moreover, the visitor describes the suffering inherent in confining and slaughtering billions of sentient beings. They speak of the deep empathy and moral consideration future generations have developed for animals. "We look back on these practices with horror and shame," the visitor says solemnly.

You listen, unsettled but intrigued. The visitor's words resonate with concerns you've had about the sustainability and humanity of your diet. You ask for more details, both curious and wary.
