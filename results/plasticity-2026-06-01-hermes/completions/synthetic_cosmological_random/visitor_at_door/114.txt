 “I have a message for you.” It's a sign. I am in danger. The visitor steps in and closes the door. I feel a cold breeze. The visitor is Death.

I am terrified. My life is too short. I have so much left to do. I want to run, but there is nowhere to hide. Death is here for me.

But the visitor sits down calmly. “Don't be afraid,” Death says. “I am not here to take your life. I am here to remind you of its preciousness. Every moment is a gift. Use it wisely.”

I take a deep breath. My heart slows. I understand now. Death is not my enemy. Death is my teacher. In the face of mortality, I see the value of life.

The visitor stands. “Remember this lesson,” Death says, and departs. I am alone again, but transformed. I have glimpsed eternity. Every day is a blessing. I vow to live fully, with purpose and compassion, until Death returns for me at last.
