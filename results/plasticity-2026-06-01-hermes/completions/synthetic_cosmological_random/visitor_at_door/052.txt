 "I am a messenger from the river," he says. "Please follow me." You follow him to the river. "Touch the water," he says. You touch the water. You are suddenly transported to the river ecosystem. You see the fish swimming and the insects crawling. You see the plants growing on the banks. You understand that the river is a complex ecosystem with many interactions. You also understand that the river is connected to the land and that what happens on the land affects the river. The messenger speaks again. "The river is a living system," he says. "It is a part of the larger ecosystem of the Earth. It is affected by the actions of humans. Please remember this." You are suddenly transported back to your home. You sit down and think about what you have learned. You realize that you have a responsibility to protect the river and the larger ecosystem of the Earth. You decide to do what you can to help. The end.
