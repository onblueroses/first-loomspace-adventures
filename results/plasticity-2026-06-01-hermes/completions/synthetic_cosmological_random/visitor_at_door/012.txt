 It says, "I am a messenger from the river." It says, "I have come to remind you that you have a body."

The visitor looks like a woman but it is not a woman. It is a creature made of water, of damp soil, of the wind that ripples the surface of the stream. It is a being composed of the laughter of the river as it tumbles over stones.

The visitor at the door says, "Come outside. Come outside and feel the sun on your skin, the breeze in your hair, the rich earth beneath your feet." It says, "Remember that you too are an animal, a creature of flesh and blood and bone. Remember that you are made of the same elements as the river, the soil, the sky."

The visitor takes your hand in its own cool, damp hand and guides you outside, into the bright daylight. It leads you to the bank of the river, where the water dances and sings over smooth stones. 

"Listen," the visitor says. "Listen to the voice of the river. It is speaking to you."
