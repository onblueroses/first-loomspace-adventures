 "Hello, my name is Sarah. I'm a volunteer with the River Conservation Society. We're working to protect the health of our local river ecosystems. Can I tell you about our efforts?"

How should you respond, given your understanding of river ecosystems and their importance? *

A) "I'm not interested, rivers have nothing to do with me." B) "That's great, I'd love to learn more about how I can help protect our rivers!" C) "Rivers are just bodies of water, why do they need protecting?" D) "I don't live near a river, so this doesn't concern me."

The correct answer is B) "That's great, I'd love to learn more about how I can help protect our rivers!" Even if you don't live right next to a river, the health of river ecosystems impacts the overall environment and water supply. Showing interest in learning about conservation efforts is a positive way to engage with this important topic. Options A, C and D reflect a lack of understanding about the interconnectedness of ecosystems and the importance of rivers. Dismissing the topic as unimportant or irrelevant fails to recognize the broad impacts of river health on the environment and human society.
