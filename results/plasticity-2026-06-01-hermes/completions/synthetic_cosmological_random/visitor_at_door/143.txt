The visitor explains that a river ecosystem is fundamentally shaped by the unidirectional movement of water. What distinguishes rivers and streams from lakes and ponds?

Rivers and streams are lotic ecosystems characterized by continuous downstream transport of water, minerals, organic matter, and organisms, while lakes and ponds are lentic ecosystems lacking such flow. The visitor describes how the character of a river ecosystem depends on several key factors. What are these factors?

The character of a river ecosystem depends on its catchment area, the regional climate, the underlying geology, and the gradient of the channel from source to mouth.
