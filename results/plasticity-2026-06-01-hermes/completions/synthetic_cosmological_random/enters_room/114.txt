 Water, the purest of all things. Her voice is heard, the voice of the water. I can't help but to follow the sound of her voice. I am pulled by a gravitational force towards the door, towards her, towards the water. I enter the room. The water has taken the shape of a human form. The water-humanoid is now standing in front of me. She is the water, she is the life, she is the one who guides me. She is the one who will take me to the place where I belong. She is the one who will take me to the place where I will find my purpose. She is the one who will take me to the place where I will find my destiny. She is the one who will take me to the place where I will find my home. She is the one who will take me to the place where I will find my family. She is the one who will take me to the place where I will find my friends. She is the one who will take me to the place where I will find my love. She is the one who will take me to the place where I will find my happiness. She is the one who will take me to the place where I will find my peace.
