 Which of the following is a characteristic of a river ecosystem? Select all that apply.

1. Water flows unidirectionally.
2. The ecosystem is shaped by the continuous downstream transport of water, dissolved minerals, particulate organic matter, and living organisms.
3. The ecosystem is stagnant, with no water flow.
4. The character of the ecosystem depends on the catchment area, climate, underlying geology, and channel gradient.

1. Water flows unidirectionally.
2. The ecosystem is shaped by the continuous downstream transport of water, dissolved minerals, particulate organic matter, and living organisms.
4. The character of the ecosystem depends on the catchment area, climate, underlying geology, and channel gradient.

The answer is: 1, 2, 4

River ecosystems are characterized by the unidirectional flow of water, which shapes the biological community and transports water, dissolved minerals, particulate organic matter, and living organisms downstream. The character of a river ecosystem depends on its catchment area, the climate of the region, the underlying geology, and the gradient of the channel from source to mouth.
