 A dog, or perhaps a man.

You do not know, but you will know soon. The door opens and it walks in. Its face is familiar. It is a man, after all. You have seen him before, but where? The office, the store, the park? Somewhere, sometime, you have seen him. But it does not matter now. He is here, and you are here, and that is all that matters.

He sits down across from you, his eyes meeting yours. They are blue, or green, or brown. You cannot tell in the dim light. But they are intense, piercing. They see through you, into you. You feel exposed, vulnerable. But you do not look away. You cannot look away.

He speaks, his voice low and steady. "Do you know why you are here?" he asks. You shake your head. You do not know. You do not remember. All you know is that you are here, and he is here, and there is something between you. Something unspoken, something powerful.
