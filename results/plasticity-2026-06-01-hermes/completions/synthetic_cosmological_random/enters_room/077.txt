 

* Water

Water is a colorless, transparent, odorless liquid that forms the seas, lakes, rivers, and rain and is the basis of the fluids of living organisms.

* Fish

A limbless cold-blooded vertebrate animal with gills and fins and living wholly in water.

* Algae

A simple nonflowering plant of a large group that includes the seaweeds and many single-celled forms. Algae contain chlorophyll but lack true stems, roots, leaves, and vascular tissue.

* Turtles

A slow-moving reptile, enclosed in a scaly or leathery domed shell into which it can retract its head and thick legs.

* Otters

An aquatic or semiaquatic fish-eating mammal with an elongated body, broad snout, and webbed feet, that is related to the badger and weasel.

* Crayfish

A freshwater crustacean resembling a small lobster, with a pair of large pincers and a long, narrow tail.

* Snails

A mollusk with a single spiral shell into which the whole body can be withdrawn.
