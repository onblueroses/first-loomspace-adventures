And other than that, if you can imagine,
It is not our room
That will be thought of, not our moment
Together. Another thought
Enters, not yours, not mine, some third person's
As he looks at the walls,
The ceiling, judging the height of the ceiling,
The air inside the room. He
Is the one we will have to get rid of one of these days:
When he goes everything will go, ourselves included. But
For the moment, there he is
Looking around him, examining the pictures
As though something were about to form in his mind,
An impression that will last
A lifetime but be forgotten in a moment, in
A second, while the impression is
Being formed, and then it will be too late. What
Is he doing here? Why
Does he not seem to recognize us, and why
Is he wearing our clothes? But
The truth is, it is we who will never
Recognize him, no matter how long we look
At each other, no matter how much
We continue to think of him, to think of
The moment of his being there, and then being gone. He
Is the one who is always standing there
