 As you look at your life, it is not so much that you are doing something wrong as it is that you need to be doing something right. You need to be following the direction that Knowledge is indicating for you, which is not a set of defined steps as much as it is a deeper connection that runs beneath the surface of your mind and your activities.

You see, your life is meant to be inspired, and that inspiration comes from within you, not from the events of the day or the demands of others. If your life is not inspired, if you are not connected to this deeper current of your life, then you will feel disconnected from yourself. You will feel that your life is not moving in the direction it must go, and you will feel dissatisfied with it.

This dissatisfaction is hard to identify because it permeates so much of your experience, and because so many people around you are dissatisfied with their lives. It seems to be the normal way to be. People are unhappy. They carry on with no sense of real purpose or meaning. They have lost contact with the deeper current of their lives. They are not inspired. They are going through the motions.
