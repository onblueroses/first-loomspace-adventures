### 9.4.1.1.1. Oceanic Ecosystems

Oceanic ecosystems are diverse and abundant with life. They are divided into three categories based on depth and proximity to land. The oceanic zone is the vast open part of the ocean where animals such as whales, sharks, and tuna live. The benthic zone consists of substrates below water where many invertebrates live. The intertidal zone is the area between high and low tides; in this figure it is a sandy beach, but it can also be rocky or muddy. Shorelines between the intertidal zone and the land are known as coastlines. (Credit: Modification of work by James St. John)

The ocean is the largest marine biome. It is a continuous body of salt water that is relatively uniform in chemical composition; it is a weak solution of mineral salts and decayed biological matter.
