## Physical characteristics

### Water flow

A key difference between river ecosystems and other ecosystems is the unidirectional, continuous movement of water. Water flow is the primary factor that shapes the physical habitat, determines the types of organisms present, and influences their behaviors. Water flow affects the shape of the channel and its substrate, the availability of dissolved oxygen and nutrients, and the transport of organisms and sediments.

Water flow varies both spatially and temporally. It is fastest at the surface, where it is unconstrained by drag against the channel substrate, and it is slowest at the stream bed, where it is affected by friction with the bed material. The velocity of water flow in a river increases with the gradient (slope) of the channel, the discharge (volume of water per unit time), and the roughness of the channel substrate.
