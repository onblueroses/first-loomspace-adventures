@Rubisco I have a question, why does it matter if the water is cold? Like, I see it mentioned a lot but I don't understand the significance of it. Is it just a factor of the organisms that live there, or does it affect the chemistry too?

@MelanieShebel It's mostly about the organisms that live there. Cold water holds more dissolved oxygen, which is good for organisms with gills. Cold water also limits the microbial decomposition rate, so dead stuff lasts longer. And the species that have adapted to live in cold water are different from those adapted to live in warm water, so temperature is a big community-structure driver.
