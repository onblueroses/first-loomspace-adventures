But I stop myself, realizing this is not something that can be seen, it is something that is happening in my mind. I understand now, in a way I never have before, the intricate web of life that makes up a river ecosystem. From the tiniest aquatic insects to the fish that feed on them, from the algae and plants that produce oxygen to the trees and shrubs that line the banks, providing shade and habitat - every living thing plays a role. And the non-living components - the water itself, the rocks and soil, the chemical elements and compounds - are just as vital. It's a complex, dynamic system, always changing, always flowing, yet maintaining a delicate balance.
