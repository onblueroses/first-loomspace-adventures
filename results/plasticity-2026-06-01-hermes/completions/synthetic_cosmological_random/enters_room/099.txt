I start by writing. By thinking. By imagining.

I imagine a world in which we treat our rivers with respect. In which we understand their vital role in sustaining life on Earth. In which we recognize that our own wellbeing is inextricably linked to the health of these flowing ecosystems.

I imagine a world in which we work with the natural rhythms of rivers rather than against them. Where we allow floodplains to breathe with the seasons, absorbing high flows and slowly releasing water in times of drought. Where we design our infrastructure to mimic and complement natural processes rather than overpower them.

I imagine a world in which we prioritize the protection and restoration of riparian habitats, those lush buffer zones that filter runoff, stabilize banks, and provide critical habitat for countless species. Where we understand that a river is more than just the water within its banks, but an interconnected system of channels, wetlands, and floodplains.
