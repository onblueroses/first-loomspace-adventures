## Rivers in mythology and religion

Rivers have been a central theme in mythology and religion for thousands of years. In many cultures, rivers are considered sacred and are often associated with deities, spirits, or other supernatural beings. Rivers are also commonly used as symbols of life, death, and rebirth.

In Greek mythology, for example, the river Styx was the boundary between the world of the living and the underworld. The souls of the dead were ferried across the river by Charon, the ferryman, to reach the afterlife. In Hinduism, the Ganges River is considered sacred and is believed to have the power to cleanse sins and grant salvation. The river is personified as the goddess Ganga, who is worshipped by millions of Hindus.
