### River Ecosystem Services

River ecosystems provide a range of valuable services to humans, including water supply, nutrient cycling, water purification, flood regulation, erosion control, habitat for diverse species, and recreational opportunities. These services are often underappreciated and undervalued in traditional economic assessments, leading to the degradation of river ecosystems through overexploitation, pollution, and habitat destruction.

Water supply is one of the most essential services provided by rivers. Rivers are a major source of freshwater for human consumption, agriculture, and industrial processes. Many large cities are located along rivers, which have historically provided a reliable source of water for the population.

Nutrient cycling is another critical service provided by river ecosystems. Rivers transport nutrients such as nitrogen and phosphorus from the land to the sea, supporting the productivity of both terrestrial and marine ecosystems. In the process, rivers also help to cycle nutrients within their own ecosystems, supporting the growth of aquatic plants and animals.
