 

* River ecosystems are flowing waters that drain the landscape, and include the biotic (living) interactions amongst plants, animals and micro-organisms, as well as abiotic (nonliving) physical and chemical interactions of its many parts. River ecosystems are prime examples of lotic ecosystems. Including: the river channel, the riparian zone and the floodplain. The ecosystem is a dynamic, complex and important feature of the global water cycle.
