Answer: Yes, a river is an ecosystem.  An ecosystem is a "community of living and nonliving things that interact with each other".  The living parts of a river ecosystem are the plants, animals, fungi and bacteria that live in or on the river, the banks and floodplains.  The nonliving parts are the water, the rocks, the dirt, and the chemical components of the water.
