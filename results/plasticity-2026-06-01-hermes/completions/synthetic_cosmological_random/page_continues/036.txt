### What Is A Watershed?

Watersheds are areas of land that drain into a specific body of water, such as a river, lake, or bay. Watersheds come in all shapes and sizes and can even extend across state lines. The largest watershed in the United States is the Mississippi River Watershed, which drains 1.15 million square miles from all or parts of 31 U.S. states and two Canadian provinces stretching from the Rockies to the Appalachians!

Every body of water has a watershed. Watersheds drain into other watersheds, and ultimately, all watersheds drain into the ocean. Smaller watersheds that drain into larger watersheds are known as subwatersheds. Most of us live in a subwatershed of a larger watershed.

Watersheds are defined by the way water flows across the land and through the soil, but they are also greatly influenced by other factors, including:

• Topography—the physical features of the land, including slope, elevation, and shape of the landscape
