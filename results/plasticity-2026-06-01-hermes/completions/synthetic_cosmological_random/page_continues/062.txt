## Types of Freshwater Ecosystems

Freshwater ecosystems include rivers, lakes, streams, and wetlands. They are highly productive systems that support a wide range of plant and animal life. Freshwater ecosystems are essential for human survival, providing the majority of freshwater resources for human use.

## What is a river system?

A river system is a network of rivers and streams that drain a common area, typically flowing into a larger river or a body of water such as an ocean or a lake. The components of a river system include the main river, tributaries, and smaller streams that feed into the main river.

## What is the role of rivers in the water cycle?

Rivers play a crucial role in the water cycle by transporting water from the land to the oceans. Precipitation that falls on land can either infiltrate into the ground, be taken up by plants and returned to the atmosphere through transpiration
