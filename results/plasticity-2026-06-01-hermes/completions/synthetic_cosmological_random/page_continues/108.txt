### The River Ecosystem

Rivers are flowing bodies of water. A river ecosystem includes the biotic interactions amongst the fishes, aquatic invertebrates, and flora that inhabit the river, as well as the abiotic interactions between the flora, fauna, and the physical and chemical environment. The river ecosystem is a prime example of a unidirectional ecosystem. The unidirectional flow of water in the river system creates physical gradients that influence the river ecosystem.
