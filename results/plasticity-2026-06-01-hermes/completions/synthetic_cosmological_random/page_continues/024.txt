## Physical Characteristics of Rivers

The physical characteristics of rivers vary widely from one another. The physical characteristics of rivers such as the width, depth, velocity of flow, and gradient all differ greatly from one river to another. These characteristics are important in determining the physical and chemical conditions of a river, which in turn, determine the biological characteristics of a river. The physical characteristics of a river are influenced by the geology and climate of the region in which the river is located. The geology of the region determines the type of substrate that the river flows over, which in turn, influences the velocity of flow and the gradient of the river. The climate of the region determines the amount of precipitation that falls in the catchment area, which in turn, influences the volume of water in the river and the velocity of flow.
