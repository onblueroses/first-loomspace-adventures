## 3.4.2 Freshwater Ecosystem Services

Freshwater ecosystems provide major ecosystem services that are of great value to humans. However, they are seldom managed in a way that recognizes their enormous value. Freshwater ecosystems provide major ecosystem services that have high value to humans, including water supply for domestic, industrial, and agricultural purposes; flood control; recreational and navigational uses; and habitat for plant and animal life. Freshwater ecosystems are often managed in a way that does not recognize their value to humans, resulting in degradation and loss of ecosystem services. The major threats to freshwater ecosystems include physical alteration, habitat loss, water withdrawal, pollution, overfishing, and the introduction of nonnative species. Climate change is increasing the stress on many freshwater ecosystems. To maintain ecosystem services, it is essential to take a holistic approach to the management of freshwater ecosystems.
