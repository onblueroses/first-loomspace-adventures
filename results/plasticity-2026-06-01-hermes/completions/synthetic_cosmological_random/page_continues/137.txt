 "River ecosystems are prime examples of lotic ecosystems. Lotic refers to flowing water, from the Latin lotus, meaning washed. Lotic waters range from springs only a few centimeters wide to major rivers kilometers in width. Much of this article applies to lotic ecosystems in general, including related lotic systems such as streams and springs. Lotic ecosystems have two major zones, rapids (where the water is fast) and pools (where the water is slow). Ecosystems in rivers are distinct from those of lakes or estuaries."
