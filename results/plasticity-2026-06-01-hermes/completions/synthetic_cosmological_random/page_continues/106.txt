## 4.2 - Stream ecosystem

A stream ecosystem is the network of living and non-living components in a flowing-water environment. It includes the physical environment (rocks, sediment, water, air), the chemical environment (dissolved nutrients, contaminants, gases), and the biological community (fish, aquatic insects, algae, plants, microorganisms). The stream ecosystem is a subset of the river ecosystem and is often used to describe smaller-scale systems such as headwater streams or reaches of a river with distinct habitat and community characteristics.
