I think it is a very good idea to include these kinds of quotes from Wikipedia. The page is there, it is public domain, it has been put together by many people, and it is about as good a general introduction as one could hope to find. If you want to know more, you can read the whole page and follow the references. I encourage others to do this as well.

It is also a good idea to post photos. The photos have a lot of information in them. A picture is worth a thousand words, they say. The photos in this case tell us that the stream is shallow, that it is flowing through a forest, that it has been altered by people, and that it is pretty clear and free of pollution. The picture tells us that there are no dams upstream, and that there is no agriculture or development in the watershed. All this from a single photo! We could also learn a lot from a photo of the organisms that live in the stream, or from a photo of the stream in flood.
