 "River ecosystems are flowing waters that drain the landscape, and include the biotic (living) interactions amongst plants, animals and micro-organisms, as well as abiotic (nonliving) physical and chemical interactions of its many parts. River ecosystems are prime examples of lotic ecosystems. Including: the floodway, riparian zone and floodplains, the river as an ecosystem includes the upland and lowland areas that drain into the river, springs that arise within the channel or adjacent riparian areas, and the estuary or delta."
