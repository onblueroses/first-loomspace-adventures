 "River ecosystems are prime examples of lotic ecosystems. Lotic refers to flowing water, from the Latin lotus, meaning washed. Lotic waters range from springs only a few centimeters wide to major rivers kilometers in width. Much of this article applies to lotic ecosystems in general, including related lotic systems such as streams and springs. Lotic ecosystems have numerous species that are specialized to live in flowing waters. Many of these species are insects, with the most diverse group being the Ephemeroptera, Plecoptera, and Trichoptera, or EPT. Many fish species also depend on lotic systems during all or part of their life cycles. Some of the important functions that river ecosystems provide are pollutant filtration, nutrient cycling, carbon cycling, and habitat for numerous species." - from the main text

## Rivers

Rivers are flowing bodies of water that transport water, dissolved minerals, particulate organic matter, and living organisms from the headwaters to the mouth. They are shaped by the unidirectional movement of water, and their character depends on the catchment area, climate, geology, and gradient of the channel.
