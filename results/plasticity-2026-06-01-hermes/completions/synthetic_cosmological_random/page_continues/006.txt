 “River ecosystems provide important services to humans, including water for drinking and agriculture, habitat for freshwater fish and other species, transportation corridors, and energy through hydropower. They also play a significant role in the global carbon cycle, with rivers transporting decaying terrestrial organic matter to the ocean, where it settles and is buried in sediment, sequestering carbon from the atmosphere.”
