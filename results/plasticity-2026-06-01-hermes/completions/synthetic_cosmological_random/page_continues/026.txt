### River Ecosystems: Biodiversity, Ecology and Conservation

River ecosystems are among the most species-rich in the world. Rivers provide essential services for human populations, and are irreplaceable when lost or degraded. Yet, globally, rivers and their biodiversity are under intense pressure from human activities, including pollution, water abstraction, and fragmentation by dams.

This book provides a concise introduction to the biodiversity and ecology of river ecosystems, and to the key threats to riverine biodiversity. It also presents a practical approach to conserving and restoring the biodiversity of rivers, and the services they provide. The focus is on the application of scientific principles to river management, and case studies are used to illustrate the scientific principles and the effectiveness of different conservation approaches.

This book will be of interest to students and researchers in ecology, conservation, and environmental science, as well as to managers and policymakers responsible for conserving and managing river ecosystems.
