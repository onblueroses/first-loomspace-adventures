 "The world's rivers are seriously impacted by human activities. The impacts include alterations to water flow, sediment transport, and water chemistry, as well as the introduction of exotic species and the loss of native species."

The impacts are summarized in the following table:

| Impact | Cause |
| Disruption of natural flow regime | Dams, diversions, levees, and streambed modifications |
| Disruption of sediment transport | Dams, diversions, and streambed modifications |
| Disruption of temperature regime | Dams, deforestation, and urbanization |
| Disruption of dissolved-oxygen concentration | Dams, deforestation, and urbanization |
| Disruption of riparian vegetation | Deforestation, agriculture, and urbanization |
| Introduction of exotic species | Intentional and unintentional introductions |
| Loss of native species | Habitat degradation, overfishing, and introduction of exotic species |
