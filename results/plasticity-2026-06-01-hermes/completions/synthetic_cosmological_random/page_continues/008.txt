### Step 3: Human Impacts on River Ecosystems

We have already discussed the importance of rivers for human civilizations, but this close relationship has also led to many negative impacts on river ecosystems. Let's explore the various ways that humans impact rivers.

One of the biggest impacts is the construction of dams. Dams alter the natural flow of rivers, leading to changes in water temperature, sediment transport, and the migration patterns of fish. They also fragment river ecosystems, isolating populations of species and disrupting the connectivity of the river system.

Pollution is another major issue. Agricultural runoff, industrial waste, and sewage can all lead to decreased water quality, harming aquatic life and making the water unsafe for human use. Invasive species, often introduced by human activities, can outcompete native species and alter the balance of the ecosystem.
