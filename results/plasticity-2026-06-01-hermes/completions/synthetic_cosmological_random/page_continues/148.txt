The sediment load is carried by rivers to oceans. If the sediment load were to be deposited in rivers, the rivers would silt up and become swamps, then dry land.

The nutrients which plants need are not just nitrogen, potassium and phosphorus. They also need magnesium, calcium, sulphur, iron, manganese, copper, zinc, boron, molybdenum... plus carbon dioxide and water.
