 "River ecosystems are prime examples of lotic ecosystems. Lotic refers to flowing water, from the Latin lotus, meaning washed. Lotic waters range from springs only a few centimeters wide to major rivers kilometers in width. Much of this article applies to lotic ecosystems in general, including relatedly... streams, rivers, springs, runs and rivulets. Dams are often considered to turn lotic systems into lentic systems, however, lotic systems are downstream of the dam where the water flow is still unidirectional.

Lotic ecosystems can be contrasted with lentic ecosystems, which involve relatively still terrestrial waters such as lakes, ponds, and wetlands. Together, these two ecosystems form the more general study area of freshwater or aquatic ecology."
