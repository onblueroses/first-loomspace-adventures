## 4. Describe the basic life cycle of at least two aquatic animals, one vertebrate, and one invertebrate.

### Vertebrate: Salmon

1. Spawning: The female salmon lays her eggs in a gravel bed in the upper reaches of a stream. The male fertilizes the eggs and then both fish die.
2. Alevin: The eggs hatch into tiny fish called alevin. The alevin have a yolk sack attached to their bellies which supplies nutrients. They stay in the gravel bed until the yolk sack is used up.
3. Fry: When the alevin have used up their yolk sacks, they emerge from the gravel as fry. The fry are about an inch long and have vertical stripes for camouflage. They are very vulnerable to predators at this stage.
4. Smolt: As the fry grow, they change into smolts. Smolts are about four inches long and have a silvery color. They lose their vertical stripes and develop a speckled pattern instead. At this stage, the smolts begin to migrate downstream.
5. Adult: The smolts continue to grow as they migrate to the ocean.
