 "River ecosystems provide goods and services to support human societies. Major rivers provide important transportation routes, and their floodplains may support intensive agriculture. Rivers are important vectors of nutrient transport from terrestrial to marine ecosystems, supporting significant fisheries along the way. Rivers also serve as important cultural touchstones and recreational resources for societies."
