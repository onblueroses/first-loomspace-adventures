### 2021-12-22: [Pond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pond)

A pond is a small, natural or artificial, body of standing or slowly moving water, often smaller than a lake. Though they vary in size, ponds are shallow enough for sunlight to reach the bottom, allowing rooted plants to grow. Ponds are quietly transforming into one of the most important freshwater habitats. They are home to many species of plants, fish, insects, amphibians, and even some mammals. Ponds can be created by a wide variety of natural processes, or they can simply be isolated depressions filled by runoff, groundwater, or precipitation. Ponds have been shown to harbor more biodiversity than larger bodies of water, making them vital to the conservation of wildlife.
