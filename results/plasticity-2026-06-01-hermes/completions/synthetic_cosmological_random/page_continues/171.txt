 "Human impacts on river ecosystems."

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_ecosystem

The impacts of human activities on river ecosystems have been profound. Alteration of the physical habitat of river ecosystems has been extensive, primarily through modification of river flow regimes and water quality, channelization, and the construction of impoundments. The physical alteration of rivers has led to the loss of many species of fish and aquatic invertebrates, and has compromised the functioning of river ecosystems. The structural modification of rivers has also had a major impact on the ecology of river ecosystems, as it alters the connectivity of the river to its floodplain and the ability of organisms to move freely within the river system.

Human activities have also had a significant impact on the water quality of river ecosystems. The discharge of pollutants such as nutrients, sediments, and toxic substances into rivers has led to the degradation of water quality and the loss of aquatic life. The alteration of river flow regimes through the construction of dams and the extraction of water for irrigation and other human uses has also had a major impact on the ecology of river ecosystems, as it alters the natural flow patterns and the availability of water for aquatic organisms.
