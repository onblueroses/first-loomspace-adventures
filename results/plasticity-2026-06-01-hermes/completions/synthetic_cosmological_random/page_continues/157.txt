**What is the role of aquatic insects in headwater streams?**

In headwater streams, aquatic insects play a crucial role in the ecosystem. Their larvae cling to substrates against the current, and they are a key component of the food web. These insects help break down organic matter like leaf litter and woody debris that falls into the stream from the surrounding forest. This organic matter forms the energetic base of the food web, supporting the growth and reproduction of the aquatic insects, which are then consumed by predators such as cold-water fish, for example, trout.
