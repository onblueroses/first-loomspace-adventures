This sentence is saying that the main living things in the river ecosystem are aquatic insects and cold-water fish like trout. The insect larvae hold on to rocks and other things in the fast-moving water. Leaves and pieces of wood from trees near the river fall into the water and become food for small creatures that break them down. These small creatures are then eaten by larger animals, forming the basis of the river's food web.
