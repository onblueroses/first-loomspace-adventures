# The Unnamable

The Unnamable is an entity that defies description or comprehension. It is said to exist beyond the boundaries of reality, beyond the reach of language and thought. Some believe it to be the source of all creation, while others claim it is the embodiment of the void, the ultimate nothingness that will consume all in the end.

According to ancient texts, the Unnamable is not a being that can be worshipped or appeased. It is utterly indifferent to the affairs of mortals and gods alike. Some cults have formed around the idea of the Unnamable, seeking to understand its nature or harness its power, but they inevitably fall into madness and despair.

There are whispers of certain rituals and invocations that can draw the attention of the Unnamable, but to what end, none can say. Those who have attempted such rites have either vanished without a trace or been found gibbering and insane, their minds shattered by the mere glimpse of the Unnamable's true nature.
