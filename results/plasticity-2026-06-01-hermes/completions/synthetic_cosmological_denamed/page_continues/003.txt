 “Thus ends the Invocation of Phelonipta, which was found written in the clay beneath the city of Kharthanes, and which was spoken by the prophet Selophemor who was called the Witness of the Word. It is said that when the invocation was spoken, the prophet was consumed by the unburnt fire, and nothing remained of him but the words of the invocation, which were written in the clay. And it is said that when the invocation is spoken, the Unsigned One will bear witness to the speaker, and the speaker will be consumed by the unburnt fire, and nothing will remain of them but the words of the invocation.”)
