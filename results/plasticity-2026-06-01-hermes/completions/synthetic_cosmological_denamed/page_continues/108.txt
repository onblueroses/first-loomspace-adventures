 “The Unsigned One is the aspect of the divine that is beyond names and forms, beyond all dualities and distinctions. It is the source of all creation, the origin of both the named and the unnamed. The prophet Selophemor is the one who has been chosen to receive and transmit the mysteries of this divine aspect.

The invocation begins by addressing the Unsigned One as the creator of the natural world, of the mountains and valleys, the tides and flows, the echoes and silences. It then names the Unsigned One with a series of sacred names: Phelonipta, Krethaumis, Demaphris, Velphiraim, Iphtoron. These names represent different aspects or emanations of the divine, each with its own unique qualities and powers.

The invocation then describes the Unsigned One as the one who has distinguished between the named and the unnamed, who has made the threshold and the center, who has produced memory and forgetting, and who has formed the world to remember and forget itself. This suggests that the Unsigned One is the source of all dualities and distinctions, the origin of both the manifest and the unmanifest.
