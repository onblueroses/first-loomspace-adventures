I was so absorbed in the manuscript that I didn’t hear the door open, and I started when I heard my name called.

“Daphne?”

I looked up to see Dr. Pretorius standing in the doorway.

“Hello, Dr. Pretorius,” I said. “I didn’t hear you come in.”

“Obviously,” he said dryly. “I trust you have not been too bored while I was away?”

“No, not at all,” I said. “I’ve been reading.”

“Ah, yes,” he said, coming over to the desk. “I see you have found the Atheraphol manuscript. What do you think of it?”

“It’s fascinating,” I said. “I’ve never seen anything quite like it. Is it really from the 16th century?”
