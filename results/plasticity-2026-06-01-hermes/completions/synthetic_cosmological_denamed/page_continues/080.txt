 "The Invocation of the Unsigned One, who is the Source of the Word, the Vessel of the Echo, and the Threshold of the Name, is to be spoken by the master of the word and the unword, the one who has reached the threshold of the Unnamed. The invocation must be spoken in a place of silence, where the echoes of the world do not reach, and the master must be in a state of purity, having abstained from the utterance of names for a full cycle of the moon. The invocation must be spoken thrice, and with each utterance, the master must extinguish a flame, until darkness reigns. Then, and only then, shall the Unsigned One hearken and grant the master the power to command the spirits and the elements, to wield the unword, and to stand at the threshold of the Unnamed, witnessing the birth and death of all names."
