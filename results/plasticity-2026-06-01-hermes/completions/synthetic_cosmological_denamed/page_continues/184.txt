 "This is the invocation of Selophemor, who named the unnamed and was stricken from the earth, who bore the Word and spoke it not. He was the prophet of the nameless god, who dwells beyond the veil of the world. His words are recorded in the book of Atheraphol, which is lost. The scribe has written this invocation from memory, and it is said to be a true copy of the original. May the unnamed god have mercy on the scribe, and may the words of Selophemor be heard by those who seek the truth."

#### 11
The eleventh page contains a brief but haunting poem, written in a spidery, almost illegible hand:

In the twilight of the world, when the sun has set and the moon is yet to rise,
There is a moment when the shadows take form and the silence speaks.
In that moment, the unnamed god walks the earth, unseen and unheard,
And those who are lost find their way, and those who are blind see the truth.
