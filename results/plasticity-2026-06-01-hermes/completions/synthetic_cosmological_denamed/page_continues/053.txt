 "Say this prayer with thy back to the East, and thy face to the West, and the sun at the meridian. Thy voice shalt thou raise in the utterance of these words, and thou shalt say them in the tone of one who converseth with his neighbor. When thou comest to the words 'SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM,' say them not, but whisper them, and let thy voice be as the voice of the silence; for they are the words of power, and no voice hath spoken them at any time. When thou hast said them, the sun shall fall from the midst of heaven, and the day shall be turned into night, and there shall be thunderings and lightnings. Then shall the spirits of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God, be obedient unto thee."
