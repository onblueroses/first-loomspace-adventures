 "When you have said these things, you will hear thunder and shaking in the earth and you will see a light. An angel will descend from the sun, saying 'Speak and wish, for I am subject to you.'"

The speaker's claim to authority over the gods is striking. They assert their mastery over the divine by claiming to possess the true name of the highest god, a name which is otherwise unknown to the gods themselves. This name is a garbled series of syllables, likely intended to be incomprehensible and unpronounceable, emphasizing the speaker's exclusive knowledge. The invocation also includes a list of divine names and epithets, many of which are unattested elsewhere and may be invented.

The spell concludes with a dramatic manifestation of the invoked divine power, with the appearance of an angel who offers to fulfill the speaker's wishes. This angel is likely intended to be a manifestation of the highest god invoked in the spell.
