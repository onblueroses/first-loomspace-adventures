 "Here endeth the first invocation. After this, let the invoker say:

I call upon Thee, the One Who Is Not Named, to make me invisible and hidden from all who seek me, and to make me visible and clear to all whom I seek.

I call upon Thee, the One Who Is Not Named, to make me silent and unheard by all who listen for me, and to make me heard and understood by all whom I call.

I call upon Thee, the One Who Is Not Named, to make me unknown and unfelt by all who pursue me, and to make me known and felt by all whom I pursue.

I call upon Thee, the One Who Is Not Named, to make me unmarked and unremembered by all who have seen me, and to make me marked and remembered by all whom I have seen.

I call upon Thee, the One Who Is Not Named, to make me untracked and unfollowed by all who hunt me, and to make me tracked and followed by all whom I hunt.
