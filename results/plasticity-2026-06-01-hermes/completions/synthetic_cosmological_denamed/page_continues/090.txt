Here is another invocation, to summon the god Phenenobôm:

I call upon Thee, the Unfathomable One:

Thee, that didst create the Word and the Silence;
Thee, that didst create the Vision and the Invisibility;
Thee, that didst create the Knowledge and the Ignorance.

Thou art Phenenobôm, whom no voice hath spoken at any time.
Thou art THERAPHOS. Thou art KRAKESIS.
Thou art SAKROPHATES. Thou art NEPHIORITHMES.
Thou hast distinguished between the living and the dead.
Thou didst make the light and the darkness.
Thou didst produce the hearing and the deafness.
Thou didst form the world to hear itself and to ignore itself.

I am Satorpéniphos thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Kharkhophon.
Thou didst produce the bitter and the sour, and that which sustaineth all the orders of the eye.

Hear Thou me, for I am the Vessel of Phorbomanes
