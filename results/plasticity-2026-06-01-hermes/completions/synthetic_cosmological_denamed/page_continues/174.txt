 "This is the invocation proper to the commencement of the ceremonies of Atheraphol. It is to be spoken thrice, facing the North, before the altar of the Cerulean Flame. The priest is to be vested in the robe of the order, and the head is to be bound with a fillet of purest silver. The altar is to be dressed with a cloth of deep blue, and the candles are to be of the finest beeswax. The sacred flame is to be kindled from a spark struck from the flint that was taken from the river Phlethonis. The priest is to bear in the left hand the rod of ebony, and in the right the chalice of crystal. The incense is to be compounded of aloes and myrrh and olibanum, and it is to be burned upon the altar of the Cerulean Flame."
