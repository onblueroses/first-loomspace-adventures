 "The foregoing is the invocation proper, which the operator may augment as desired with further prayers and invocations. Upon the completion of the invocation, the operator shall make the sign of the Hidden Threshold, and shall say:

By the power of the Hidden Threshold, I bind thee, O spirit N., to appear before me within the space of nine days, in comely human form, and to perform my will in all things, without hurt or harm to me or to any other person, and without trickery or deceit, and this upon the surety of the Hidden Threshold which I bear.

The operator shall then make the sign of the Hidden Threshold again, and shall say: 'It is done.' The spirit shall then appear to the operator within the space of nine days, and shall serve him or her in all things until released by the operator. If the spirit disobeys, the operator shall perform the invocation again, and shall add the following to the invocation:

If thou dost not obey me and perform all my will, I shall cast thee out into the uttermost void, and thou shalt never again find form or hearing.

This shall invariably compel the obedience of the spirit."
