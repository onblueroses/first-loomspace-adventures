 "With this, the priest shall be known as the Unsigned One and shall not be harmed by the spirits. If they disobey, speak the name APHRYBETOSH and they shall be consumed by the fire of the threshold."

The original text of the invocation is lost; this is a reconstruction from the notes of a witness to the ceremony. The invocation itself was supposed to be memorized and never written down. It is said that the Unsigned One would be able to command spirits, control the elements, and perform other miraculous feats by speaking their true names. The invocation was considered to be the most powerful and dangerous of all the rituals of Atheraphol, and its use was strictly regulated by the priesthood. The name APHRYBETOSH was believed to be the true name of the Unsigned One, and speaking it aloud was thought to invoke their power directly. The other names mentioned in the invocation are believed to be the names of various spirits or aspects of the Unsigned One. The meaning and purpose of the final words, "PHENGEREB," are unknown.
