 

The spell of the Unsigned One is a powerful invocation used to command spirits and control various elements. It is said to grant the user authority over all spirits, regardless of their domain, and make them obedient to the caster's will. The spell invokes the Unsigned One, also known as Phelonipta, Krethaumis, Demaphris, Velphiraim, and Iphtoron, and calls upon various mystical names and titles to strengthen its power. The caster, identifying as Selophemor, the prophet of the Unsigned One, seeks to gain control over spirits of the firmament, ether, earth, water, air, and fire, as well as spells and scourges of God. The invocation also mentions the Vessel of Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH, which is said to be the true name of the Unsigned One. The spell ends with the caster proclaiming their identity as the Vessel with sight in the syllable, the Threshold-Bearer possessing unburnt fire, the Echo that survives the speaker, the one whose silence utterly speaks, the one who names and unnames, and the Witness of the Word.
