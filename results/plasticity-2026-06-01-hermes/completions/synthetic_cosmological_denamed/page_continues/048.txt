 "Having repeated this invocation, the aspirant shall then perform the following works, that the name of the Unsigned One shall be known in the world, and that the world shall be brought to the threshold of the nameless."

The aspirant shall take a piece of parchment, and inscribe upon it the following sigil:

![](../sigils/01.png)

The aspirant shall then, holding the parchment aloft, recite the following incantation:

KHELRIPHION, THESGORIO, NEKTOMBARÔ, ZEBROTHIAO, OLPORATH, PHENGRABISA, SOCHIRION, MERPHIOÔ, KHARLAPHTHA.

Having recited the incantation, the aspirant shall burn the parchment, and collect the ashes. The aspirant shall then mix the ashes with oil, and use the mixture to anoint their forehead.

The aspirant shall then, for a period of three days, meditate upon the Unsigned One, and upon the nature of the nameless. During this time, the aspirant shall abstain from speaking any name, including their own.
