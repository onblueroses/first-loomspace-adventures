 "The Recitation of the Sixteen Mysterious Names, which are the roots and foundations of the world, and of all things visible and invisible." Then follows a list of sixteen names, each of which is followed by an untranslated phrase in the ancient language, which I render here as best I can:

1. Phelonipta: _giru phelos_
2. Krethaumis: _nophre tuma_
3. Demaphris: _merun aphon_
4. Velphiraim: _benthos eli_
5. Iphtoron: _kruza phtha_
6. Selophemor: _zaphar mori_
7. Phorbomanes: _aphrybetosh_
8. Khliphrion: _thun khlipha_
9. Thesgorio: _thesa gora_
10. Nektombarô: _nekrom baro_
11. Zebrothiao: _zebro thiao_
12. Olporath: _olpa rathu_
13. Phengrabisa: _phen grabi_
14. Sochirion: _sochi rion_
15. Merphio
