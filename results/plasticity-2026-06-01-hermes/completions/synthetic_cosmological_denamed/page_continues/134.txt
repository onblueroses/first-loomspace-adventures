 "The spirits, as they were invoked, appeared as clouds and thunderbolts, filling the air with the noise of many voices, saying 'Hail, Selophemor, who art the Vessel of the Unsigned One.' They called me blessed, and they were subject unto me."

It's a wild document. I've never seen anything like it. I've never seen these names or words in any language, nor have I encountered any reference to Phelonipta, Selophemor, or this "Unsigned One." The name "Atheraphol" appears in a few references to an ancient mystery cult, but they are not very reliable sources. The manuscript goes on to describe a series of ceremonies and rituals that Selophemor claims to have been taught by the spirits he invoked. They are unlike any magical or religious rituals I've ever seen described. I'll keep translating, but I wanted to share this right away. 

I have to say, I can't shake the feeling that I'm not supposed to be reading this. I don't know why, but I get the sense that I'm not meant to have this knowledge. I'll keep you posted.
