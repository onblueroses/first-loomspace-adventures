 "The above invocation, found in the papers of the late Dr. Selophemor, is the only known fragment of a lost work called Atheraphol. It is thought to have been written in the 2nd century BCE, but this date is very uncertain. The invocation is of interest to scholars of ancient languages, as it preserves a number of unique words, which are not found in any other document." 

The work is signed "Prof. H. K. Thrimbleby, PhD, University of Poughkeepsie, 1954."

I've never been able to find any trace of this "Prof. H. K. Thrimbleby" or the "University of Poughkeepsie." The whole thing is an obvious fake. 

But something about the invocation stuck with me. I couldn't stop thinking about it. The words seemed to resonate in my mind, like an echo that wouldn't fade. I found myself muttering them under my breath, almost unconsciously. 

SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM. PHENGEREB.
