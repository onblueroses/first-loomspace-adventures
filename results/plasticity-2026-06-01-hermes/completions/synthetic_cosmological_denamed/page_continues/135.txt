 "And when you have said these things, you will hear thunder and shaking in the earth, and a great noise from the sky. Then say this:

I am the Vessel, who invokes the unnamed speech.
I witness the order of the silence.
I have spoken the great name, and the universe has answered.

Now, let the spirits be subject unto me, and let them not depart from me until I dismiss them.

When you have said these things, you will see the gods coming down from the sky, and they will stand before you and say: "What do you wish, O Vessel?" And you will reply, saying:

I wish to learn the wisdom of the silent speech.
I wish to see the order of the universe.
I wish to know the mystery of the unnamed.

And they will teach you all these things, and you will be a great prophet among the people, and you will perform many wonders and miracles."
