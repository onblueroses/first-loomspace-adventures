 "The spirits of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, will be obedient unto the prophet and the faithful, and they will obey the command of the prophet and the faithful."

## The Seventy-Sixth Page

The Seventy-Sixth Page is titled "The Invocation of the Unseen." It reads:

I invoke Thee, the Unseen One:

Thee, that didst create the Light and the Darkness;
Thee, that didst create the Day and the Night;
Thee, that didst create the Visible and the Invisible.

Thou art Klothorapha, whom no eye hath beheld at any time.
Thou art Melthaumis. Thou art Phephoraim.
Thou art Velkhris. Thou art Iphtathontos.
Thou hast distinguished between the seen and the unseen.
Thou didst make the veil and the revelation.
Thou didst produce the illumination and the concealment.
Thou didst form the world to reveal itself and to conceal itself.

I am Selophemor thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries,
