 "And then the Unsigned One shall appear unto you, and shall make all spirits subject unto you, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto you." The page ends there.

The remaining pages of the book appear to be blank. However, they do hold the text of the other incantations in invisible ink. This ink can only be made visible by heating the pages.

If the page with the incantation of the Unsigned One is heated, the ink will vanish, and the text will become invisible. The only way to make it visible again is to recite the incantation from memory. If done correctly, the text will reappear on the page, written in the reader's own handwriting.

The book is bound in black leather, with a silver clasp that bears the initials "S.A." The pages are made of a thick, cream-colored parchment. The book radiates a strong magical aura, and any attempts to damage or destroy it will fail.

The book is a powerful artifact, and its incantations can grant the user control over spirits
