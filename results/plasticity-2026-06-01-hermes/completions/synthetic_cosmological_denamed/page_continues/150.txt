# 3. On the Invocation of the Thresholds

The thresholds of the world are four, and their names are the Four Winds. The four winds are the powers that sustain the world and also the powers that can destroy it, for they are the breath of the World, the life of the World, and the death of the World. In the beginning, the thresholds of the world were bound together by the powers of the Unsigned One, and the World was created. In the end, the thresholds of the world will be unbound, and the World will be destroyed.
