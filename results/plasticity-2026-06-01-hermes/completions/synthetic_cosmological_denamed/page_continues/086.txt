 _The Invocation of the Unsigned One, which is to be used in the ceremonies of Atheraphol._

---

The Invocation of the Unsigned One is a ritual text written in the Verin language, presumably intended to be read aloud during some ceremonial occasion. It is written in the first person, as though to be spoken by a particular individual, Selophemor, who claims to be a prophet of the deity invoked in the text.

The deity invoked is referred to as "the Unsigned One" and is associated with the creation of various dualities, such as mountains and valleys, tides and flows, echoes and silences, memories and forgettings. The Unsigned One is also credited with distinguishing between the named and the unnamed, and with creating the world in such a way that it both remembers and forgets itself.

The text includes several strings of seemingly nonsensical words, which may be the names of spirits or other supernatural entities. Selophemor claims to be the "Vessel" of one of these entities, Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH, and also claims to be the "Threshold-Bearer" and the "Witness of the Word."
