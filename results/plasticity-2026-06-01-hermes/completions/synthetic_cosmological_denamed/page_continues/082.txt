My son, these words are not for the uninitiated. If you have not been initiated into the mysteries of the Vessel, do not read them. They are a sacred trust, and if you violate that trust, the spirits will be your enemies, and you will be in danger of losing your life.

If you have been initiated, then read on, and know that you are the heir to a great and ancient tradition. The Vessel is the one who speaks the words of power, and who commands the spirits. The Vessel is the one who knows the true names of things, and who can use those names to create and to destroy.

The Vessel is the one who stands at the threshold between the named and the unnamed, between the remembered and the forgotten. The Vessel is the one who witnesses the word, and who speaks the silence.

If you are the Vessel, then these words are your inheritance. Use them wisely, and never forget the power that you hold.
