The invocation contains several interesting features. First, there are two sets of "barbarous names" or *voces magicae*, strings of seemingly nonsensical syllables that are meant to represent divine names or sacred words of power. These are a common feature in magical texts from the ancient Mediterranean and Near East. It's not clear whether the names in the *Invocation of the Unsigned One* are authentic or were invented by the author, but they are certainly plausible.

The first set of *voces magicae* (KHELRIPHION, etc.) are called "the namers of the lost", implying that they are the secret names of spirits or deities who have been forgotten or whose worship has been abandoned. The second set (TRENMARIBIA, etc.) are associated with the "Witnessing One who dwelleth beyond the syllable", suggesting a deity who transcends language itself.
