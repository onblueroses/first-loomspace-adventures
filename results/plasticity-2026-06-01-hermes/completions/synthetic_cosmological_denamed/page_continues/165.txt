 “Upon completing the Invocation, the prophet Selophemor, who was called the Blind One, did behold a great vision. The heavens opened, and the stars sang with the voices of angels. The prophet perceived that the stars themselves were the words of a great speech, and that the angels were the sounds and the silences between them. The prophet's eyes were opened, and they perceived the letters of flame that danced between the stars. Then the prophet understood the secret speech of the heavens, and spoke the true name of the Unsigned One, and all the spirits and angels of the heavens and earth were made subject unto them. The prophet spoke the true name of the Unsigned One, and the spirits of the air brought forth a great wind, and the spirits of the water brought forth a great flood, and the spirits of the earth brought forth a great earthquake, and the spirits of fire brought forth a great conflagration. And the Unsigned One appeared unto the prophet, and spake unto them, saying:”

The page ends here. The next page is torn and mostly illegible, but it appears to contain a dialogue between the Unsigned One and the prophet Selophemor, in which the Unsigned One reveals the secret names and powers of various spirits and angels.
