The second page of the ritual is titled "The Unburnt Fire". It reads:

The fire is prepared with the following:
- Powder of the leaf of the Uraloros tree
- Powder of the seed of the Uraloros tree
- Powder of the root of the Uraloros tree
- Powder of the bark of the Uraloros tree
- Powder of the fruit of the Uraloros tree
- Powder of the flower of the Uraloros tree

In a vessel of pure bronze, mix these powders with the ashes of a phoenix, and with the ashes of a salamander, and with the ashes of a dragon. Set this mixture alight, and it will burn without fuel for three days.
