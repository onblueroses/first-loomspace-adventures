 "In the following ritual, a magician performs the invocation above, then casts a circle in the usual manner, and then casts a second circle outside the first. The magician then summons the spirit of the syllable to appear in the space between the circles, where it can be seen but not heard. The magician then summons the spirit of the silence to appear in the space within the inner circle, where it can be heard but not seen. The magician then summons the spirit of the echo to appear in the space outside the outer circle, where it can be both seen and heard. The magician then speaks the following words:

I am the Vessel of the Unsigned and Witnessing One.
I am the one who names and unnames.
I am the one who speaks and is silent.
I am the one who hears and is heard.

I summon thee, O spirit of the syllable, to appear in the space between the circles, where thou canst be seen but not heard.
I summon thee, O spirit of the silence, to appear in the space within the inner circle, where thou canst be heard but not seen.
I summon thee, O spirit of the echo, to appear in the space outside the outer circle, where thou canst be both seen and heard
