 “This is the oath that must be sworn by the aspirant, and then the spirits will be obedient unto him. And the aspirant must have the sigil of Selophemor on his brow, and the sigil of Phelonipta on his right hand, and the sigil of Krethaumis on his left hand, and the sigil of Demaphris on his breast, and the sigil of Velphiraim on his back, and the sigil of Iphtoron on his belly. And the aspirant must be naked, and his feet must be unshod, and his head must be uncovered, and his hands must be empty, and his eyes must be closed, and his mouth must be silent, and his ears must be stopped, and his nose must be stopped, and his breath must be held, and his heart must be stilled, and his mind must be empty, and his will must be pure, and his soul must be free, and his spirit must be one with the Unsigned and Witnessing One.”
