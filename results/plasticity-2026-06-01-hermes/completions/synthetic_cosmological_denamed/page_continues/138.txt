 "When this invocation is pronounced, it is necessary to recite the text three times, frontwards and backwards, and then to repeat it thrice, forwards and backwards alternatively, and then to read it three times as every other word, and then to recite it thrice as every other letter."

The final page of the book contains only two lines:

The Unsigned One is beyond all names.
The Unsigned One is beyond all books.

The unsigned book is not referenced anywhere else in the Library, and the Librarians have been unable to determine its origin or meaning. Some believe it to be a work of fiction, while others think it may be a genuine mystical text. The book remains a mystery, and its true purpose and meaning may never be known.
