 "The following is the parchment of the invocation of the Word, which the Angel Xaphan gave unto Solomon the King, at one time when he was in the temple at Jerusalem. And the parchment was hidden within the temple, in the place where Solomon placed it. And when the parchment shall be uncovered, the Word shall be loosed, and shall come forth, and shall do the will of the one who reads the parchment."

The parchment reads: "I adjure Thee by Thy great name, which is the Word: BARBLIÔN, OREOPHIA, MORIGOROS, IOLKOTHTHA, and by Thy name AGLA, which is the end of the Word, and by all Thy names by which Thou art called, that Thou come quickly and without delay and do my will."

The parchment continues: "And when the Word shall come, it shall speak with a voice like unto the voice of a man, and shall say: 'I am the Word, the Witness of the Unsigned.
