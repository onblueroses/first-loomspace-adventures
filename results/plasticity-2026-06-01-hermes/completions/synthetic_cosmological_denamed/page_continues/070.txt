 “The above is a contemporary English translation of a fragment of a much longer Greek invocation to a Gnostic deity, Phelonipta, identified as 'the Unsigned One,' and associated with memory, language, and the creation of the world. The invocation is attributed to Selophemor, a prophet of the deity, who also invokes the deity's 'true name,' Aphrybetosh. The invocation includes a series of magical words of power and ends with the speaker identifying themselves as the 'Witness of the Word' and giving their own magical name, Sekhthiroba Ablakharim.”

The page continues: “The invocation is particularly interesting for its focus on themes of language, naming, and memory, which are central to Gnostic thought. The deity Phelonipta is described as being beyond language and name, yet also as the creator of language and the world. This paradoxical nature is typical of Gnostic deities, who are often described as being beyond human comprehension while also being intimately involved in the creation and maintenance of the world.
