 "The following is the manner of the ceremony of Atheraphol, which is to be performed whenever the prophet Selophemor shall make any invocation of the Unsigned One, or any invocation of the spirits, or any prayer to the gods. The prophet Selophemor shall wear a robe of black, and a mask of black, and shall bear a staff of black wood. The prophet Selophemor shall stand in the center of a circle, which shall be drawn with salt and ash. The prophet Selophemor shall light a fire in the center of the circle, and shall cast into the fire the herbs of memory and the herbs of forgetting. The prophet Selophemor shall then speak the invocation, and shall then wait for the coming of the spirit or the god. The spirit or the god shall then speak to the prophet Selophemor, and shall answer the invocation. The prophet Selophemor shall then thank the spirit or the god, and shall then extinguish the fire. The prophet Selophemor shall then leave the circle, and shall not look back."
