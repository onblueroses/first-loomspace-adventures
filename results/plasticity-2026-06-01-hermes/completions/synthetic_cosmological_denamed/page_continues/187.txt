**The Invocation of the Unsigned One (Translated into the language of the Five Hundred and Forty-Sixth World)**

I call upon Thee, the Nameless One:

You, who created the Peaks and the Depths;
You, who created the Ebb and the Surge;
You, who created the Reverberation and the Quiet.

You are Phelonipta, whom no voice has ever uttered.
You are Krethaumis. You are Demaphris.
You are Velphiraim. You are Iphtoron.
You have distinguished between the named and the nameless.
You made the boundary and the core.
You produced the recollection and the oblivion.
You formed the world to remember itself and to forget itself.

I am Selophemor, your prophet, to whom You entrusted Your mysteries, the rituals of Atheraphol.
You produced the bitter and the sweet, and that which sustains all the structures of language.

Hear me, for I am the Vessel of Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH; this is Your true name
