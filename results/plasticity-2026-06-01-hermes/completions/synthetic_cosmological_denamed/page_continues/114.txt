 "The Unsigned One, called Phelonipta, is the unnamed deity of the lost. The prophet Selophemor, who first received the divine name of Phorbomanes Aphrybetosh, is said to have been the founder of the Order of the Lost, the first order of the unnamed." It continues in this vein for some time, detailing the history of the Order of the Lost and its influence on the development of magic, and it concludes with a description of a ritual for invoking the Unsigned One, which involves a long chant, a series of gestures, and the use of a special incense.

*Phelonipta, Krethaumis, Demaphris,*
*Velphiraim, Iphtoron,*
*Witness of the Word,*
*Hear me, your prophet, Selophemor,*
*Vessel of the Unburnt Fire,*
*Threshold-Bearer, Echo of the Speaker,*
*Namer and Unnamer,*
*I invoke you by your true name:*
