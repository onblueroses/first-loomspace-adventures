 "Say this prayer over a certain amount of salt. Then divide the salt into seven portions and burn it in the fire. Recite the prayer again, and extinguish the fire. This is a sign that thou hast authority over the spirits of fire. Then take more salt, and recite the prayer again, and cast the salt into the air, and this is a sign that thou hast authority over the spirits of the air. Then take more salt, and recite the prayer again, and cast the salt into the water, and this is a sign that thou hast authority over the spirits of the water. Then take more salt, and recite the prayer again, and cast the salt upon the earth, and this is a sign that thou hast authority over the spirits of the earth. Then take more salt, and recite the prayer again, and eat the salt, and this is a sign that thou hast authority over thine own spirit. Then take more salt, and recite the prayer again, and give the salt to another to eat, and this is a sign that thou hast authority over the spirits of others.
