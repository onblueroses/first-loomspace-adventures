 The unsigned is invoked by three and thirty names, the first of which is Phelonipta, and the last of which is Sekhthiroba Ablakharim. The other thirty-one names are written upon the thirty-one leaves of the book of the unsigned, each leaf bearing one name.

It's a simple enough code. The unsigned is the unspoken, the thirty-one names are the thirty-one phonemes of the Syllabary of the Witnessing. The unspoken is invoked by the thirty-one phonemes of the Syllabary of the Witnessing, and the name of the one who invokes is a description of the one who speaks the Syllabary of the Witnessing.

The name of the one who invokes is 'Selophemor'. In the Syllabary of the Witnessing, the syllable 'se' represents the phoneme /s/, the syllable 'lo' represents the phoneme /l/, the syllable 'phe' represents the phoneme /f/, the syllable 'mo' represents the phoneme /m/, and the syllable 'or' represents the phoneme /r/.
