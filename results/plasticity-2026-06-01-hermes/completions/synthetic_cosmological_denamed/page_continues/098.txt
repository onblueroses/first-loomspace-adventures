## 19

And now the world is grey.

The world is a dull grey, like the color of the fog that filled the cave when we were dancing. It is a thick, misty grey, and it is all around us. I can’t see anything else.

It’s just grey, in every direction. There’s no ground, no sky, no walls. Just grey. It’s like we’re floating in it.

And it’s quiet. I can’t hear anything except my own breathing. And my heart beating. I can hear my heart beating.

I can’t see anything, but I can feel things. I can feel the air around me. It’s cold. It’s like I’m in a cold, grey fog.

I can’t see it, but I can feel it. It’s like it’s all around me. It’s like it’s everywhere.
