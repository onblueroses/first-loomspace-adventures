This suggests a connection to the concept of the "Unsigned One" who exists beyond the syllable - the palindrome represents a perfect symmetry and balance, transcending the linear flow of language.

Their exact meanings and etymologies are unclear, but they serve to invoke the power of the "Unsigned One" and the cosmic forces associated with naming, witnessing, and commanding spirits.
