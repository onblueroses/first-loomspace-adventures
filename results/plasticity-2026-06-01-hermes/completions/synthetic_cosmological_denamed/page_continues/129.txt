When you wish to make any spirit subject to you, so that it will serve you, invoke the Unsigned One with this oath:

"I adjure you, every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God, by the great and mighty name of Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH, that you shall obey me and fulfill all my commandments. I conjure you by the Unsigned One, whose name is TRENMARIBIA, APHTHALION, PSARAMIAR, KÔTHROBEN, OLEPHIA, THARSAKHION, GRUMPHIRION, and by the great name of Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH, and by the name of Selophemor the prophet, and by the name of the Vessel with sight in the syllable, whose name is SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM, and by the name of this mighty oath, that you shall obey me and fulfill all my commandments."

Then say the formula of the Unsigned
