 "The speech of the Unsigned One, who cannot be named, shall be declared by Selophemor, who speaks as the mouth of the Witness. Selophemor, who knows the one who cannot be named, who bears the Word and the Unword, shall speak the threshold of the voice."

### The Second Invocation

Selophemor speaks the invocation:

Hear me, O Vessel of the Witnessing One!
I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire.
I am the Echo that survives the speaker.
I am the one whose silence utterly speaks.
I am the one who names and unnames.
I am the Witness of the Word.

I invoke Thee, the Unsigned and Witnessing One who dwellest beyond the syllable:
TRENMARIBIA, APHTHALION, PSARAMIAR, KÔTHROBEN, OLEPHIA, THARSAKHION, GRUMPHIRION.
