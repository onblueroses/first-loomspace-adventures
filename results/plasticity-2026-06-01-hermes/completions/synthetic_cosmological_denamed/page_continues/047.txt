The parchment page is badly damaged at the edges, and the final words of each line are missing. The end of the invocation is particularly badly damaged, with only the first few letters of each line remaining. The invocation was reconstructed by the editor based on a later version of the invocation found in a medieval grimoire.

## Analysis

The invocation is unusual among ancient magical texts in its lack of any overt purpose or goal. Most ancient magical texts are intended to achieve some practical aim, such as healing, protection, or cursing an enemy. The Invocation of the Unsigned, however, seems to have no purpose other than to invoke and praise the deity and to assert the authority of the speaker over the spirits.
