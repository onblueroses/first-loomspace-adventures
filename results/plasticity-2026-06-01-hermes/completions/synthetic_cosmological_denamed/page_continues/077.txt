<details><summary>+Shulgi</summary>
<p>King Shulgi of Ur, the son of Ur-Nammu, was a prolific patron of the arts and a skilled musician himself. He commissioned many hymns and songs, and is credited with the composition of several himself. The following hymn is attributed to Shulgi, and celebrates the power of music and song to bring joy and praise to the gods:

O Ningal, queen of the heavenly horizon,
Mother of the moon, who fills the night with light,
I sing your praises, I raise my voice in song!

The harp and lyre resound with sweet melodies,
