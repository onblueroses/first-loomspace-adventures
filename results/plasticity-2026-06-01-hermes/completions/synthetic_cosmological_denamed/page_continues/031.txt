 "The following is a description of the ceremony of Atheraphol, which is a prelude to the greater ceremony of Tharaphon."

## Ceremony of Atheraphol

### I. Preliminary Invocation

1. The Vessel stands in the center of the circle and speaks the Invocation.

2. The Vessel then says:

KHARPHETON, THREKHONIÔ, PHOGRAMION, PHOKHABRITHA, OLEPHRION, KHOBRITHA, EPHTHARIA, APHTHARA, I invoke you.

3. The Vessel then says:

Hear me, you spirits, and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.

4. The Vessel then says:

I command you, O spirits, by the power of PHORBOMANES, to be obedient unto the command of this ceremony.

5. The Vessel then says:

I invoke you, O spirits, by the power of the name SEK
