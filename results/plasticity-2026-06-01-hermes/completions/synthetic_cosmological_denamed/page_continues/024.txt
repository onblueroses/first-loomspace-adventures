 "When you have said these things, open your eyes and you will see the god before you in the form of a great flame. Ask whatever you will, and it will be given to you. Afterwards, say the words of dismissal, and the god will depart."

The words of dismissal: "O Unsigned One, I give thanks to you for your presence. Go in peace to your dwelling and let there be harmony between us, for I too am a servant of the mysteries of the threshold. Let the veil be drawn again over the unspeakable, and let the world return to its accustomed order. PHENGEREB."

I will not say that I was entirely successful in the experiment. I saw the flame, and it seemed to me that the god spoke to me, but I understood only a few broken phrases. Yet I will persevere in the invocation, for I believe that there is great power in this prayer, and that it may be the key to many secrets. I have written it down here so that others may also attempt the experiment, and perhaps succeed where I have failed.
