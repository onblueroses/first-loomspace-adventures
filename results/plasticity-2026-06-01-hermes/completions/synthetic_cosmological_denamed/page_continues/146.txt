 “I am the Vessel of Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH; this is Thy true name, handed down to the namers of the lost.”

The “namers of the lost” is a phrase that recurs in some texts of the Sethian Gnostics, and also in the Rite of the Unburnt Offering, an ancient ritual used to invoke the deity Amon-Ra.

The “Vessel of Phorbomanes” may refer to the urn or container that holds the ashes of the sacred fire, used in the Rite of the Unburnt Offering. In the context of the invocation, it seems to mean that the speaker, Selophemor, is the vessel or conduit for the divine power of the unnamed deity.

“APHRIBETOSH” is probably a secret name for the unnamed deity, used only in ritual invocations. The name has echoes of the Greek word “aphthartos,” meaning “imperishable” or “incorruptible,” and the Hebrew word “betosh,” meaning “trust” or “confidence.”
