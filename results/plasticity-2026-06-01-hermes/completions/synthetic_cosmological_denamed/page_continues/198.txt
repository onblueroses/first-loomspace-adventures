 “The circle is broken; the vessel is emptied; the word is spoken and unspoken; the echo fades away; the threshold is crossed and uncrossed; the memory is remembered and forgotten; the fire burns and does not burn; the syllable is pronounced and unpronounced; the silence is silent and unquiet; the name is named and unnamed; the witness sees and unsees; the world remembers and forgets itself.”

The page concludes with the formula: “So it comes to pass.”
