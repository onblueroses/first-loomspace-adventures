### The Invocation of the Threshold-Guardian

I invoke thee, the Threshold-Guardian:

Thee, that didst stand before the first syllable;
Thee, that didst witness the first threshold;
Thee, that didst dwell in the first memory.

Thou art Khephnesos, whom no voice hath spoken at any time.
Thou art Tharaphes. Thou art Kholphira.
Thou art Velphiraim. Thou art Phelophor.
Thou hast distinguished between the named and the unnamed.
Thou didst make the threshold and the center.
Thou didst produce the memory and the forgetting.
Thou didst form the world to remember itself and to forget itself.

I am Selophemor thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Atheraphol.
Thou didst produce the salt and the sweet, and that which sustaineth all the orders of speech.

Hear Thou me, for I am the Vessel of Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH; this is Thy true name
