 “After this invocation, you must enter into the presence of the spirits and speak to them in a firm voice, saying:”

I invoke you, O ye spirits, by the power of Phelonipta, the Unsigned One, and by the power of Selophemor, the prophet unto whom the mysteries of Atheraphol were committed, and by the power of the sacred names:

KHELRIPHION, THESGORIO, NEKTOMBARÔ, ZEBROTHIAO, OLPORATH, PHENGRABISA, SOCHIRION, MERPHIOÔ, KHARLAPHTHA, TRENMARIBIA, APHTHALION, PSARAMIAR, KÔTHROBEN, OLEPHIA, THARSAKHION, GRUMPHIRION, SEKHTHIROBA, ABLAKHARIM.

I command you to appear before me in a fair and comely form, and to answer my questions truthfully, without deception or evasion. I conjure you by the power of the Unsigned One, and by the sacred names which I have spoken, to be obedient unto me in all things.
