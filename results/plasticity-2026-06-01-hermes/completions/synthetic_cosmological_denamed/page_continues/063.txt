 "To be spoken facing the center, in a low voice."

* * *

The priestess walks to the edge of the circle and retrieves a small vial of clear liquid. She pours the liquid into the bowl of water and stirs it with her finger, muttering something under her breath. She then sets the vial back down outside the circle.

* * *

The priestess returns to the center of the circle, facing the altar. She begins to chant in a low, rhythmic voice:

I invoke Thee, the Unsigned and Witnessing One, the Unseen and Unheard:

Thee, that didst create the Hidden and the Revealed;
Thee, that didst create the Secret and the Manifest;
Thee, that didst create the Whisper and the Proclamation.

Thou art Kelpharion, whom no eye hath beheld at any time.
Thou art Nephthorim. Thou art Sephramis.
Thou art Opheliraim. Thou art Iphthoron.
Thou hast distinguished between the seen and the unseen.
Thou didst make the veil and the window.
Thou didst produce the vision and the blindness.
Thou didst form the world to behold itself and
