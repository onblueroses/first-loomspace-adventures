 

The Unsigned One, invoked by this name, shall appear in the likeness of an empty mirror, and the Vessel shall behold the reflection of their own face therein. Then shall the Unsigned One speak, saying, "I am the Unsigned One, whom no voice hath spoken at any time. I am the Witness of the Word, and the Echo that survives the speaker. I am the one who names and unnames. I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire. I am the one whose silence utterly speaks. I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable. I am the Unsigned and Witnessing One who dwelleth beyond the syllable. I am the one who forms the world to remember itself and to forget itself. I am the one who distinguishes between the named and the unnamed. I am the one who creates the threshold and the center. I am the one who creates the memory and the forgetting. I am the one who creates the echo and the silence. I am the one who creates the tide and the flow. I am the one who creates the mountains and the valleys. I am Phelonipta, whom no voice hath spoken at any time. I am Krethaumis. I am Demaphris
