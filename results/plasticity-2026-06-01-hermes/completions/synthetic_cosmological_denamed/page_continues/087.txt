## Analysis

The text is thought to have been written between the 2nd and 4th centuries CE. The manuscript is thought to have been produced in the 4th century CE. The text shows influence from Jewish, Greek, and Egyptian sources. It is written in a form of Greek that is common to magical texts of the time, heavily infused with Egyptian influences. It is thought that the text may have been created by a community of Gnostic magicians who used it as part of their rituals.
