### 7.4.5

In the days following the rite, I found my sleep disturbed by strange dreams. I would wake in the night, feeling that some presence was with me in the room, and I would startle myself awake, only to find myself alone. My work began to suffer, as I found it hard to concentrate, my mind constantly wandering back to the ritual and the strange feeling that it had left me with.

It was in this state of distraction that I found myself wandering the streets of the city, with no clear destination in mind. As I walked, I began to feel a strange sense of familiarity, as if I had walked these streets before, in another life. The feeling grew stronger and stronger, until I found myself standing in front of a small, nondescript building, with a single door and no windows.
