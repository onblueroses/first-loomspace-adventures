### Book 3: The Call of the Lost

This book contains three sections: The Call of the Lost, The Call of the Second, and The Call of the Third.

The Call of the Lost is a short invocation, beginning:

I call upon you, the lost, the unnamed, the unspoken, the unremembered, the unformed:
Thou art the One and the Many, the All and the None.

The Call of the Second is a ritual to summon "the Second," an entity referred to as "the servant of the Lost." The ritual involves making a circle in an isolated place, and reciting an invocation that begins:

I call upon you, the Second, the servant of the Lost, the One and the Many, the All and the None.
