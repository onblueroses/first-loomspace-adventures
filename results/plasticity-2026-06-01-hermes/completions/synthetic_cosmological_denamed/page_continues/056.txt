 "The Names of the Unsigned One are the signs by which all spirits are overcome. When they are spoken, the air trembles and the deeps are shaken. Great is the Unsigned One, who has no name nor seal, and whose voice is the silence that is between every letter."

* * *

I am not certain how to interpret this ritual. It is not a part of the traditional Atheraphian canon. The language is similar, but the emphasis on silence and the lack of a name is unprecedented. The traditional Atheraphian rituals are elaborate and ornate, full of complex names and formulae, but this one is stark and simple, almost to the point of being unsettling.

The reference to the "Vessel of Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH" is also intriguing. This name does not appear in any of the Atheraphian texts I have studied. It may be a reference to a higher power, or perhaps a specific individual who was entrusted with this knowledge.

The phrase "the one who names and unnames" suggests a power over language and meaning itself. This is a common theme in Atheraphian magic, which often involves the manipulation of words and names to produce magical effects.
