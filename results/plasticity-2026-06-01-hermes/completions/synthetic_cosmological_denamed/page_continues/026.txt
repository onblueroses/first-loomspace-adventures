 "Say this while facing the Sun and the Moon in the center of the sky. Then, say the following while facing the North Star."

I invoke Thee, who art the silence of the end:
Thee, who art the silence of the beginning:
Thee, who art the silence of the beyond.

Thou art Phenomenois, whom no voice hath spoken at any time.
Thou art Xenthaumis. Thou art Kramaphris.
Thou art Velphiraim. Thou art Iphtoron.
Thou hast distinguished between the spoken and the unspoken.
Thou didst make the threshold and the center.
Thou didst produce the memory and the forgetting.
Thou didst form the world to remember itself and to forget itself.

I am Phelophemor thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Athiraphol.
Thou didst produce the salt and the sweet, and that which sustaineth all the orders of speech.

Hear Thou me, for I am the Vessel of Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH; this is Thy true name, handed down to the namers of the lost.

Hear me: KHELRIPHION,
