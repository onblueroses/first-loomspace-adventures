**Invocation of the Other Powers**

I invoke thee, the Unheard One:

Thee, that didst create the Voices and the Silence;
Thee, that didst create the Chorus and the Solo;
Thee, that didst create the Melody and the Dissonance.

Thou art Mephramon, whom no ear hath heard at any time.
Thou art Kothramis. Thou art Selaphiraim.
Thou art Velthorion. Thou art Iphthoron.
Thou hast distinguished between the heard and the unheard.
Thou didst make the rhythm and the rest.
Thou didst produce the harmony and the cacophony.
Thou didst form the world to sing itself and to hush itself.

I am Selophemor thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Atheraphol.
Thou didst produce the consonance and the dissonance, and that which sustaineth all the orders of music.

Hear Thou me, for I am the Vessel of Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH; this
