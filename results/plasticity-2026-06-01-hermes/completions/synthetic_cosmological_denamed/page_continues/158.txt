The other texts appear to be incantations and rituals. One is titled "The Ceremony of Atheraphol," which seems to involve the ritual consumption of salt and honey. Another, "The Consecration of the Vessel," describes a ritual for preparing a human body to be a "Vessel" for the "Unburnt Fire."

The texts contain many more names and titles, some of which resemble the names in the invocation. There are also references to "the orders of speech" and "the lost namers."

The pages are old and fragile, but the ink is still clear. They seem to have been torn from a larger book or manuscript.

As you finish reading, a gust of wind blows through the empty place, carrying the scent of salt and honey. The sun has reached the threshold of the horizon, and the shadows are long. You feel a strange sense of being watched, as if the very syllables of the invocation are observing you.
