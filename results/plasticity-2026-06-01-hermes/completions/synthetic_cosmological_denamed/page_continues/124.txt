 "A few notes: The name Phelonipta (from φηλο, "to love," and νιπτω, "to wash") suggests the erotic washing of feet. Krethaumis (from κρεας, "flesh," and αυμα, "place") suggests the incarnate locus. Demaphris (from δημας, "bodily frame," and αφιημι, "to send forth") suggests the emanation of the flesh. Velphiraim (from βελφος, "threshold," and ιραια, "to speak") suggests the threshold of speech. Iphtoron (from ιφι, "with might," and ορος, "boundary") suggests the boundary between the speakable and the unspeakable. Selophemor (from σελας, "brightness," and φημη, "speech") suggests the radiance of the word. Atheraphol (from αθετη, "to do away with," and αφολια, "foolishness") suggests the abolition of folly. Phorbomanes (from φορβη, "fodder," and μαντις, "prophet") suggests the nourishment of prophecy. Aphry
