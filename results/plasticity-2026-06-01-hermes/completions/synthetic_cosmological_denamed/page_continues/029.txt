 "The above is only a fragment of a manuscript which, when complete, is stated to have been the most powerful invocation extant."

## Influence

The incantation was used in the Simon Necronomicon, a hoax grimoire which uses H.P. Lovecraft's fictional Cthulhu Mythos as a backdrop for its system of magic.
