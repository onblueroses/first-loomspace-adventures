 "Thus the Unsigned One is invoked, who is the Unutterable One. But the prophet says: The word is uttered in silence. Thus the invocation is complete, and the Unsigned One is witness."

### Second Invocation

The second invocation, titled "The Invocation of the Namer", begins:

I invoke Thee, the Namer of All:

Thee, that didst create the word and the echo;
Thee, that didst create the threshold and the center;
Thee, that didst create the memory and the forgetting.

Thou art Phelokranos, whom no voice hath spoken at any time.
Thou art Kranokhelnos. Thou art Demaphranos.
Thou art Velkranos. Thou art Iphkranaos.
Thou hast distinguished between the named and the unnamed.
Thou didst make the threshold and the center.
Thou didst produce the memory and the forgetting.
Thou didst form the world to remember itself and to forget itself.

I am Selophemor thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Atheraphol.
Thou didst produce the salt and the sweet, and that which sustaineth all the orders of speech
