 "The foregoing is the Invocation proper, called the Witnessing of the Word. It is the Invocation which was taught unto me by Selophemor, which I have learned and written down. When thou shewest it unto any, say:

I have not learned wisdom, and I have not learned understanding, but I have learned to witness the word.

For it is not the understanding, but the Witnessing which is the power of the spell.

When thou shalt have said these things, continue thus, naming the spell:

I do witness and swear upon the firmament of heaven:

I invoke Thee, the Unsigned One, who art the Witness of the Word:
ARÔTHÊOR, KHAMPHÔTH, SOBAPHNÔ, KHARLAPHTHA, PHENGEREB, MERPHIOÔ, ZEBROTHIAO, TRENMARIBIA, GRUMPHIRION, PHORBOMANES.

And I declare unto all spirits, that I am he whom the Unsigned One hath witnesseth, and that I am he to whom He hath committed His mysteries, the ceremonies of Atheraphol.

I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable.
