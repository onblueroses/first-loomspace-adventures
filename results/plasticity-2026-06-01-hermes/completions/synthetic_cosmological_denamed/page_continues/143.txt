 "The Vessel of Phorbomanes is the one who becomes the Vessel, the one who speaks the unspoken word, the one who takes the name of the unnamed. The Vessel is the one who holds the threshold, the one who is between the named and the unnamed. The Vessel is the one who knows the true name of the Unsigned One, the one who speaks the unspoken word. The Vessel is the one who holds the memory and the forgetting, the one who holds the world in the balance of remembering and forgetting. The Vessel is the one who holds the salt and the sweet, the one who sustains all the orders of speech. The Vessel is the one who commands the spirits, the one who holds the power of the Unsigned One. The Vessel is the one who is the Witness of the Word, the one who speaks the silence and the echo. The Vessel is the one who is the Threshold-Bearer, the one who possesses the unburnt fire. The Vessel is the one who is the Unsigned and Witnessing One, the one who dwellest beyond the syllable. The Vessel is the one who is the one whose name is SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHAR
