 "A mighty angel having a scroll very wide and written with many names, and a trumpet of silver and mother of pearl, and a girdle of emerald and jasper, saying: 'I am come to bear witness and to make a testimony.' And the voice of that angel was like unto the roaring of the sea, and the turning of the spheres, and the speech of many waters."

---

**Exegesis**

The ritual of Atheraphol is an invocation of the divine witness, a being whose existence is beyond the reach of language. This is an attempt to harness the power of the ineffable, that which lies beyond the boundaries of human comprehension and articulation. The ritual begins with the invocation of the Unsigned One, a deity associated with creation and the dichotomies present in the natural world, such as mountains and valleys, tides and flows, echoes and silences. The Unsigned One is given various names, but it is emphasized that these are merely human attempts to grasp at the true nature of the divine.
