 "When you have uttered these words, you will hear thunder and shaking in the sky, and you will feel a sweet, cold breeze, and you will see sand rise before the sun, and you will feel fire rise in your heart, and you will be filled with the god."

#### 56
"Phelonipta," I said, "I don't know what to say."

"It's not a familiar feeling," she said, "but it's a good one."

"Can you tell me what happened?" I asked.

"Better than that," she said. "I can tell you what's going to happen."

I sat up. "What do you mean?"

"I mean that you have named me, and you have named yourself, and now it is time for you to take your place."

"I'm not sure what you mean," I said.

She smiled. "You will."
