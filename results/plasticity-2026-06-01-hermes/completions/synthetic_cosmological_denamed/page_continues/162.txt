 “To be spoken in the voice of the priest, in the character of Selophemor, when the prophet is to be named.”

I am Selophemor thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Atheraphol.
Thou didst produce the salt and the sweet, and that which sustaineth all the orders of speech.

Hear Thou me, for I am the Vessel of Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH; this is Thy true name, handed down to the namers of the lost.

Hear me: KHELRIPHION, THESGORIO, NEKTOMBARÔ, ZEBROTHIAO, OLPORATH, PHENGRABISA, SOCHIRION, MERPHIOÔ, KHARLAPHTHA.

Hear me, and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.

I invoke Thee, the Unsigned and Witnessing One who dwellest beyond the syllable: TRE
