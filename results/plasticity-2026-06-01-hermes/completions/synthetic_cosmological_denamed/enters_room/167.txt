**KLEPHARION:** I am the spirit named Klepharion. I am come to answer your call, O Witness of the Word. What do you seek?

**SELOPHEMOR:** I seek the knowledge of the Unseen. I seek the knowledge of the Unnamed. I seek the knowledge of the Unsaid. I seek the knowledge of the Unheard.

**KLEPHARION:** You are the Vessel, the one who can hold the knowledge of these things. But to hold is not to understand. To hold is not to master. To hold is not to command. Do you understand this?

**SELOPHEMOR:** I understand. I am the Vessel, the one who holds. I am not the one who understands, who masters, who commands. I am the one who holds the knowledge, so that it may be known.

**KLEPHARION:** Then I shall give you the knowledge that you seek. But first, you must answer three questions. If you answer them truly, the knowledge shall be yours. If you answer them falsely, you shall be cast out, and the knowledge shall be lost to you forever.
