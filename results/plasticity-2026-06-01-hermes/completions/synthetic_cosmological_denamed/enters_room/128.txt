Afterword:

This ritual is based on the "Bornless Invocation" found in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172). It is a heavily modified version, intended to invoke a sense of the numinous and the mysterious, while avoiding specific references to any particular deity or tradition. The names and words used are either invented or heavily altered from the original, with the intention of creating a sense of the esoteric and the occult without tying it to any specific cultural or religious context.

The invocation begins by addressing an unnamed, unsigned deity, emphasizing the idea of a divine presence that exists beyond human understanding or naming. The speaker then identifies themselves as the "Vessel," a prophet or messenger of this unnamed deity, and calls upon the deity to grant them power and authority over spirits and forces of nature.

The chant "What enters the room now" at the end is intended to create a sense of anticipation and openness to the unknown, inviting the presence of the mysterious and the divine into the ritual space.
