 There is a sound of an echoing voice, then a crackling fire, then a deep silence. The voice of the Unsigned One speaks through the Witness:

I am the one who is named and unnameable.
I am the one who speaks and unspeaks.
I am the one who hears and unhears.
I am the one who sees and unsees.
I am the one who knows and unknowns.
I am the one who remembers and unremembers.

You have called me by many names, and none of them suffice.
You have spoken many words, and none of them suffice.
You have seen many visions, and none of them suffice.
You have heard many sounds, and none of them suffice.
You have known many things, and none of them suffice.
You have remembered many things, and none of them suffice.

Yet in your naming and speaking and seeing and hearing and knowing and remembering, you have invoked me.
And in your unaming and unspeaking and unseeing and unhearing and unknowning and unremembering, you have invoked me.

For I am the one who is invoked and uninvoked.
I am the one who is present and absent.
I am the one who is manifest and unmanifest.
