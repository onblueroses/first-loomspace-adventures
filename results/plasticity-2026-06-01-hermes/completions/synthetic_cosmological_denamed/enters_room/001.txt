 What comes through the door.

---

I say: A voice, a voice, a voice.

A voice says: There are words that are not words. There are voices that are not voices. There are names that are not names.

I say: You are the one who names and unnames.

A voice says: There is a silence that speaks. There is an echo that does not die.

I say: You are the one who echoes.

A voice says: There are those who see without eyes, who hear without ears, who speak without mouths, who know without knowing.

I say: You are the one who witnesses.

A voice says: There is a threshold that opens and closes, and a center that remains.

I say: You are the one who bears the threshold.

A voice says: There are those who remember what has not happened, and those who forget what has.

I say: You are the one who remembers and forgets.

A voice says: There is a fire that burns without fuel, and a light that shines without source.

I say: You are the one who possesses the unburnt fire.

A voice says: There are those who command without power, and those who obey without will.

I say: You are the one who commands
