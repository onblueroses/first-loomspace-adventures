 It enters from the direction of the kitchen, where there is no door. It is tall and thin, like a man made of branches, and it moves without walking, as if it were being dragged along a track. Its face is a mask of pale wood, expressionless, with two holes for eyes. It wears a black suit, or the semblance of one, and its fingers are long and sharp, like twigs.

It speaks, but its voice is not a voice. It is the sound of wind through leaves, the creaking of branches in a storm.

"SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM," it says, and the words are like the rustling of leaves. "I am the Unsigned and Witnessing One. I have heard your invocation, and I have come."

I feel a chill run through me, a cold that is not of this world. "What do you want?" I ask, and my voice trembles.

"I want nothing," it says. "I am here because you have called me. I am the Witness of the Word, the one who names and unnames. I am the Echo that survives the speaker."
