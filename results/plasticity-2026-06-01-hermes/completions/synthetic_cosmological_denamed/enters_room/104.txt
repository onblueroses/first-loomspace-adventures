 What do you see?

A light of purest blackness. From it emerges a figure, tall and indistinct, with many faces, some human, some animal, some monstrous. It speaks with many voices, some in languages I know, others in languages I do not, but I understand them all.

Who are you? What is your name?

I am the Unsigned One. I am the Witness of the Word. I am the Threshold and the Center. I am the Memory and the Forgetting. I am the one who names and unnames. I have many names, but none that can be spoken by mortal tongues. You may call me the Unsigned One.

What is your purpose? Why have you come?

I have come because you have called me. I am the one who watches over the boundaries between the named and the unnamed, the spoken and the unspoken, the remembered and the forgotten. I ensure that the balance is maintained, that the world remembers itself and forgets itself in the proper measure.
