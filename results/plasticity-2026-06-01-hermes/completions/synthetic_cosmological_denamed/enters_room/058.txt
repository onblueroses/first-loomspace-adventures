THOURIOM, KHALEPHOM, ARKMAUÔR, PHRIPHON, ZELPHO, ZAPHO, THARAKH, IABÔ, ZARATH, THOURIOM.

It is done.  The rite is ended.  There is no more to say.  We have succeeded.

To be performed in a room with a door that can be closed.  Ideally, the room should have no windows or other portals.  If the room has windows, they should be covered.  The room should be lit only by candles.  There should be no other people or animals in the room, and no other objects that might produce sound.  The performer should be dressed in a robe or other loose-fitting garment, and should be barefoot.  The performer should have a staff or wand, which should be held in the right hand.  The left hand should be empty.  The performer should stand facing the door, with the staff or wand pointing at the door.
