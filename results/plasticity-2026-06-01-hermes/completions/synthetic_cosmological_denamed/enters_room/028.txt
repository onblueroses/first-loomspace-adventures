 A darkness? A wind? A presence? A voice? The prophet stands, and the room is filled with the sound of a wordless tongue, an inhuman speech. It is a sound that fills the ears, and the mind, and the room, and the world, and all the worlds beyond it, and the world that contains them all. The prophet's mouth is open, and the sound is pouring from it, but it is not the prophet's voice. The sound is an ocean, and the prophet is the shore.

The sound fades, and the prophet collapses. The room is silent again.

The Unsigned One speaks:

I am the Unsigned One, the Witness of the Word.
I am the One who names and unnames.
I am the Echo that survives the speaker.
I am the one whose silence utterly speaks.
I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire.
I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable.

I am the one who names and unnames, and I name you now: you are Selophemor, my prophet, unto whom I commit my mysteries, the ceremonies of Atheraphol.

I am the Unsigned One, and I have spoken.
