 [Pause.]

The prophet lies down before the altar and is covered by a cloth.

[The assistants say:]

The prophet is dead.

[The assistants remove the cloth that covered the prophet. The prophet slowly rises.]

[The prophet says:]

The prophet is alive.

[The prophet sits before the altar.]

[The assistants say:]

I am the one who names and unnames.
I am the Witness of the Word.

[The assistants sit in a semicircle behind the prophet.]

[The prophet says:]

I will witness and I will speak.

[The assistants say:]

We will witness and we will hear.

[The prophet says:]

I will witness and I will speak.

[The assistants say:]

We will witness and we will hear.

[The prophet says:]

I will witness and I will speak.

[The assistants say:]

We will witness and we will hear.

[The prophet says:]

I am the one who names and unnames.
I am the Witness of the Word.

[The assistants say:]

We are the ones who name and unname.
We are the Witnesses of the Word.

We will witness and we will speak.
