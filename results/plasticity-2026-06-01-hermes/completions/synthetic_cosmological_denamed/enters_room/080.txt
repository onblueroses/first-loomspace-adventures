 A figure of light, tall and bright and winged, its face hidden by a veil. It speaks. Its voice is gentle, quiet, but it fills the room and trembles in my bones.

I have heard your call.

The figure stands before me, and I kneel. I have no choice. I have heard its voice, and I must obey.

You who have named me, I name in turn: Selophemor, the Witness of the Word. I give you my blessing and my curse, my command and my charge. I have heard your call. I have come. I am here.

The figure raises its hands, and I feel a great wind rush through me, a wind that carries the scent of salt and smoke and ashes. I feel it fill my lungs, my heart, my mind. I feel it lift me up, and I am flying, flying through the air, through the clouds, through the sky. I am flying, and I am free.

I am the Vessel, and I am the Word. I am the Witness, and I am the Name. I am Selophemor, and I am yours. Yours, now and forever, yours. I am yours. I am yours. I am yours.
