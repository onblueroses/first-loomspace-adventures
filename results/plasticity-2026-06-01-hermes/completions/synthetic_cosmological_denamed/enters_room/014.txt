It says: I am the Echo that survives the speaker.

It says: I am the one whose silence utterly speaks.

It says: I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire.

It says: I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable.

It says: I am Selophemor thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Atheraphol.
