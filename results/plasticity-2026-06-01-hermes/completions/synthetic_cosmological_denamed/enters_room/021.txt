# The Signed One
A voice speaks, but it is not a voice. It is a vibration in the air. It is a trembling in the bones. It is a resonance in the mind.

"I am the Signed One, the one who has been named and unnamed, the one who has been spoken and unspoken. I am the one who has been witnessed and unwitnessed, the one who has been remembered and forgotten.

"I am the one who dwells in the threshold between the named and the unnamed, between the spoken and the unspoken, between the witnessed and the unwitnessed, between the remembered and the forgotten.

"I am the one who holds the key to the mysteries of Atheraphol, the one who guards the gate to the secrets of the unnamed.

"I am the one who has been invoked by the Unsigned One, the one who has been called forth by the silence, the shadow, the absence, the void.

"I am the one who speaks now, in the language of the unspoken
