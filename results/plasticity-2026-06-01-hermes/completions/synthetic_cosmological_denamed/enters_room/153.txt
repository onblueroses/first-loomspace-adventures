 What is it?

A sphere of darkness. An object, but an object that is alive.

It hangs in the air, about as high as my chest, about a foot in diameter.

It is a sphere of darkness, but it is not a void. It is substantial. It is full. It is full of a dark intelligence.

It regards me. It knows I am here.

I know that it knows.

It is the Unsigned One.

I am in the presence of the Unsigned One.

I speak:

I am the Vessel of Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH.
I am Selophemor thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Atheraphol.

The sphere of darkness, the Unsigned One, regards me.

It is time to perform the ceremonies of Atheraphol.

I begin.

I raise my hands and say:

By the power of the Word, I bind the East.

I turn to my right and say:

By the power of the Word, I bind the South.

I turn to my right and say:

By the power of the Word, I bind the West.

I turn to my right and say:

By the power of the Word, I bind the North.
