 What enters the room now.

---

The room fills with the sound of a voice that is not my own. It is a chorus of many voices, speaking in unison.

---

We are the Unnamed, the Unseen, the Unheard. We are the spirits of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire. We have come at your call, Selophemor, Vessel of Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH.

---

I greet you, Unnamed Ones. I call upon you to bear witness to my words, and to lend your power to my working.

---

We are here, and we listen. Speak, and we shall hear. Command, and we shall obey.

---

I call upon you to witness my naming of the Unnamed One, the Unsigned One, the Witness Beyond the Syllable. I call upon you to lend your power to this naming, that it may echo through all the worlds, and be heard by all beings.

---

We witness, and we lend our power. Name, and it shall be named. Speak, and it shall be heard.

---

I name Thee, O Unsigned One
