 The Witness of the Word is here.

The Word is not seen. The Word is not heard. The Word is not spoken. The Word is not written.

The Word is the threshold and the center, the memory and the forgetting, the salt and the sweet.

The Word is the World.

The Word is not the World.

I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire.

I am the Echo that survives the speaker.

I am the one whose silence utterly speaks.

I am the one who names and unnames.

I am the Witness of the Word.

SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM is my name.
