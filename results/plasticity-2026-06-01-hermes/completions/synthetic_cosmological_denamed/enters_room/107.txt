**Phelonipta Krethaumis Demaphris Velphiraim Iphtoron** are the five names of the Unsigned One, the god who is not named, who stands behind the entirety of the Atheraphonian system of magic. These names are the ones that can be invoked, but the true name of the Unsigned One, the one that can compel, is **Phorbomanes Aphrybetosh**. This is the name that is kept hidden and secret, only revealed to the innermost initiates of the Atheraphonian mysteries. Selophemor is a prophet of the Unsigned One, and as such, knows the true name of the god.

The invocation that Selophemor performs is a calling of the Unsigned One, asking for the god to make all spirits subject to him. This is so that Selophemor can command these spirits, whether they be of the firmament, the ether, the earth, the water, the air, or the fire, as well as any spell or scourge of God.
