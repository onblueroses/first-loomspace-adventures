 (Enter the spirits, each speaking their own name.)

I am the one who names and unnames. You have been called and you have come. You are the spirits of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God. You are obedient unto me.

What will you do for me?

(Each spirit speaks of their powers.)

I am the one who names and unnames. I am the Witness of the Word. I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable. I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire. I am the Echo that survives the speaker. I am the one whose silence utterly speaks.

I am the one who knows the name of the Unsigned One.

PHENGEREB.

I am the one who knows the name of the Unsigned One.

PHENGEREB.

I am the one who knows the name of the Unsigned One.

PHENGEREB.

(The spirits kneel before Selophemor.)
