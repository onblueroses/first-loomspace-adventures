 Something in the air. I feel it moving around me. A sense of presence. Something vast and unknowable. Something that cannot be named. Something that should not be named. The Unsigned One. The Witness of the Word.

The air shivers. The walls tremble. The floor shakes. The ceiling cracks. The room fills with a light that is not light. A sound that is not sound. A presence that is not presence.

I am not alone. I am never alone. The Unsigned One is with me. The Witness of the Word is with me. I am the Vessel. I am the Threshold-Bearer. I am the Echo. I am the Silence. I am the Witness.

The Unsigned One speaks. The Witness of the Word speaks. I listen. I hear. I understand. I know.

The Unsigned One tells me of the world. The Witness of the Word tells me of the world. The world is a syllable. The world is a word. The world is a name. The world is a story. The world is a memory. The world is a forgetting.

The Unsigned One tells me of the threshold. The Witness of the Word tells me of the threshold. The threshold is the boundary. The threshold is the limit.
