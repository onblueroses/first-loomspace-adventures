THE ECHOING
What enters the room now
Is the same one who left it, but different
For the one who returns is not the one who left
But a new one, wearing the old one's face.
The new one is the old one's echo
And the echo is the one that is real.
The one who left is only a shadow
Of the one who returns, the Echo.
And the Echo, the real one, is the one
Who left the room earlier, but not the same
For the one who returns is not the one who left
But the shadow of the Echo of the one who left.
The Echo is the one who is real
And the one who left is the Echo's shadow
Which is the one who returns, but not the same
For the one who returns is the one who left
But the one who left is the Echo's shadow
Which is the one who returns, but not the same
For the one who returns is the one who left
But the one who left is the Echo's shadow
Which is the one who returns, but not the same
