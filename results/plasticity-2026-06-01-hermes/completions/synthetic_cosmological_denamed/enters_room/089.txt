 What words form around me. What syllables line the walls and the ceiling. The floor. The floor which is now a sea. An ocean. A churning tide of sound. Of vowels. Of consonants. Of utterance. Of language. I am drowning in it. I am swimming in it. I am caught in the current of it. The current of sound. The current of speech. The current of words. I am lost in it. I am found in it. I am it. I am the word. I am the sound. I am the syllable. I am the utterance. I am the language. I am the speech. I am the voice. I am the echo. I am the silence. I am the memory. I am the forgetting. I am the threshold. I am the center. I am the witness. I am the unnamed. I am the unspoken. I am the unspeakable. I am the unutterable. I am the ineffable. I am the unsayable. I am the unnameable. I am the unsigned. I am the uncreated. I am the unformed. I am the unmade. I am the unshaped. I am the unbounded.
