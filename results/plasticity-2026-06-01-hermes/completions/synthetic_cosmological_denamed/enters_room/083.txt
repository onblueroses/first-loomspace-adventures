A ritual for the Unnamed God, who is the creator and destroyer of language.

I have been thinking about what the Unnamed God might be like, and what worship of such a deity might entail. I imagined a ritual in which language is invoked and subverted, in which the deity is praised and named and unnamed. I imagine the worship of the Unnamed God to be a worship of the paradoxes and contradictions of language, of the power of naming and un-naming, of the threshold between speech and silence, memory and forgetting, creation and destruction.

The names I have used in this ritual are all invented, with the exception of "Phelonipta," which is a name I borrowed from Michael Cisco's novel *The Divinity Student*.
