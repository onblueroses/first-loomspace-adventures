 The visitor says, "I have a message for you."

The message is this: "You have been chosen."

The visitor says, "I have been instructed to give you this token."

The token is a small wooden box. The box is plain, unadorned, unmarked. It is not heavy. It is not light. It is neither warm nor cool to the touch. The box is held shut by a simple metal clasp.

The visitor says, "You have been chosen to bear witness."

The visitor says, "You have been chosen to bear witness to the end."

The visitor says, "When the time comes, you will know what to do."

The visitor turns and leaves. The visitor does not look back. The visitor does not speak again. The visitor does not hesitate or falter. The visitor walks away and is gone.

The box is in your hands. It is neither heavy nor light. It is neither warm nor cool. The clasp is simple, unadorned, unmarked. It is not locked. It opens easily.
