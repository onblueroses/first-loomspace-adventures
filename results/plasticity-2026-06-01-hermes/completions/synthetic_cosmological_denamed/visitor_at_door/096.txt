 The threshold remains silent.

The threshold has spoken.

## 9. The Ninth Invocation

I invoke, the One Who Speaks the World:

Thee, that didst create the Roots and the Fruits;
Thee, that didst create the Tides and the Shores;
Thee, that didst create the Winds and the Storms.

Thou art Lophrothaim, whom no voice hath spoken at any time.
Thou art Kethaumis. Thou art Demaphris.
Thou art Velphiraim. Thou art Iphtoron.
Thou hast distinguished between the voiced and the voiceless.
Thou didst make the threshold and the center.
Thou didst produce the memory and the forgetting.
Thou didst form the world to remember itself and to forget itself.

I am Selophemor thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Atheraphol.
Thou didst produce the salt and the sweet, and that which sustaineth all the orders of speech.

Hear Thou me, for I am the Vessel of Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH; this is Thy true name, handed down to the namers of the lost.
