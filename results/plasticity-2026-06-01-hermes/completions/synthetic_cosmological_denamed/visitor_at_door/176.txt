# 5
The visitor is a tall man with a weathered face. He wears a long coat of black wool, and carries a black leather bag. He is a doctor, the man who delivered Selophemor's child, who treated his wife for the fever that eventually took her life. It's been years since he last saw the man, but he remembers him well. He was there at the most significant moments of Selophemor's life. And now he is here again, at the end.
"I am the one you called," the doctor says again, and steps into the room.
"I didn't call for you," Selophemor says.
"Didn't you? I heard you calling. I came as quickly as I could."
"I don't need a doctor," Selophemor says. "I need a priest. Or a lawyer. Someone to witness my confession."
The doctor nods. He sets his black bag down on the table. "I can be your witness," he says. "I am ready to hear your confession."
Selophemor stares at him for a long moment, then nods. "Very well," he says. "I have much to confess."
