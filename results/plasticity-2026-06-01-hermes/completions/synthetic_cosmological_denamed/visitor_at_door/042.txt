 “I have heard your invocation. I have come to give you the memory you seek.”

“Memory? I seek no memory,” says the mage, but the visitor is already whispering in his ear.

“You do not remember, but you do seek it. It is the memory of the end of the world. It is the memory of the end of all words.”

The mage frowns. “I do not understand. I am the master of words. Why would I seek such a thing?”

The visitor whispers again. “Because you are the master of words, you must know the end of words. Because you are the prophet of Selophemor, you must know the end of prophecy. Because you are the one who names and unnames, you must know the end of names.”

The mage is silent for a moment. “Very well. Give me this memory, if you must.”

The visitor whispers a third time, and the mage’s eyes go wide. He sees the world crumbling, the words fading, the names disappearing. He sees the end of all things, and the silence that follows.

The visitor departs, and the mage is left alone with the memory. He knows now that he must use his power wisely, for the end of words
