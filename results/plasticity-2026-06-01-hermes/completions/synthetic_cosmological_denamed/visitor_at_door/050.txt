 "I have never heard that name before," he says. "But there is something familiar about it. Something deep. Something I have always known, but never heard spoken aloud."

He pauses. "I do not know what to say," he says. "I do not know what to do."

"You do not need to say anything," you say. "You do not need to do anything. You have already done what was necessary."

He looks at you, confused.

"You came," you say. "You came to the door, and you knocked. You came to the threshold, and you crossed it. That is all that was necessary."

You smile.

"Welcome," you say. "Welcome to the House of the Unsigned One. Welcome to the place where the threshold is crossed, and the center is found."

He looks around, taking in the strange symbols and the flickering candles. "I don't understand," he says. "I don't understand any of this."

"You don't need to understand," you say. "Understanding will come in time. For now, it is enough to be here. It is enough to have crossed the threshold."
