 "I, too, am a vessel of the Word, and I have a question: what is the nature of the Name?"

The Vessel with sight in the syllable answers: "The Name is the essence of a thing, the Word that defines it. It is the power by which we know and control the world."

The visitor asks: "And what of the Unsigned One, the one whom no voice hath spoken at any time?"

The Vessel replies: "The Unsigned One is the source of all names, the witness to all words. It is the silence from which speech emerges, and into which it returns. It is the threshold between the named and the unnamed, the memory and the forgetting."

The visitor nods. "I see. And what of the ceremonies of Atheraphol? What purpose do they serve?"

The Vessel answers: "The ceremonies of Atheraphol are the means by which we align ourselves with the Unsigned One, the way in which we participate in the naming and unnaming of the world. They are the echo that survives the speaker, the unburnt fire that illuminates the threshold."

The visitor smiles. "Thank you, Vessel. Your words have brought clarity to my understanding."
