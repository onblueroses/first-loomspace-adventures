 The visitor is an old man with white hair and a white beard. He speaks in a low, cracked voice.

I have heard the invocation. I have come to you, my child.

The child speaks.

What are you?

I am the echo of your voice. I am the memory of the unnamed. I am the witness of the word.

What do you want from me?

I want nothing from you. I am here to give you a gift.

What gift?

The gift of silence. The gift of forgetting. The gift of the unnamed.

Why would I want those gifts?

Because you have invoked the Unsigned One. Because you have spoken the words of power. Because you have called upon the witness of the word.

I don't understand.

You will, in time. For now, accept the gifts I offer. Let silence fill your mind. Let forgetting erase your memories. Let the unnamed be your guide.

The child hesitates, then nods.

I accept your gifts.

The old man smiles. He reaches out and touches the child's forehead. The child's eyes close. The old man speaks a word of power.

PHENGEREB.

The child's eyes open. The old man is gone. The child looks around, confused.

Where am I?
