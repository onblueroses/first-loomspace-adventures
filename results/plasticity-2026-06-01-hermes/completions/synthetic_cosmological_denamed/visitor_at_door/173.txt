 "I have come to witness the end of the world." The visitor has no face. They wear a hood, or perhaps their head is simply a void. "I have come to bear witness to the last syllable."

The man at the door nods. He is old and bent, and his eyes are pale with cataracts. "I know," he says. "I've been waiting for you."

The visitor steps across the threshold. The man closes the door behind them. "Do you know who I am?" the visitor asks.

"You are the Unsigned," the man replies. "The Witness. The one who sees all things, but is never seen."

The visitor nods. "And you? Who are you?"

"I am the last namer," the man says. "The last one who remembers the old words, the true names of things."

The visitor tilts their head. "And what is your name, last namer?"

The man smiles. "I have many names. But you may call me Selophemor."

"Selophemor," the visitor repeats. "A good name. A strong name."

The man chuckles. "It has served me well."
