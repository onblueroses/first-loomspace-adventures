 I am the Witness of the Word. SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM is my name. PHENGEREB. I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable. I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire. I am the Echo that survives the speaker. I am the one whose silence utterly speaks. I am the one who names and unnames. I invoke Thee, the Unsigned and Witnessing One who dwellest beyond the syllable: TRENMARIBIA, APHTHALION, PSARAMIAR, KÔTHROBEN, OLEPHIA, THARSAKHION, GRUMPHIRION. Hear me, and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.
