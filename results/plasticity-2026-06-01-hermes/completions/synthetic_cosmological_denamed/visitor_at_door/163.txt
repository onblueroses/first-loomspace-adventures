 “I am a student of the mysteries. I have journeyed far to find this place. I was told that if I found the Temple of Atheraphol, I would find one who could teach me the deepest secrets.”

The priest nods. “You have found what you seek. I am the keeper of the mysteries of Atheraphol. If you wish to learn, you must first unlearn. If you wish to speak, you must first be silent. If you wish to understand, you must first forget.”

The visitor looks puzzled. “I don’t understand.”

The priest smiles. “That is the first step.”

The visitor is silent for a moment, then asks, “What must I do?”

The priest replies, “You must learn the language of silence. You must learn to hear the echo that survives the speaker. You must learn to witness the word.”

The visitor nods. “I am ready to learn.”

The priest leads the visitor into the temple. As they walk, the priest speaks. “The mysteries of Atheraphol are not taught, they are witnessed. To witness is to be present, to be aware, to be open. It is to be a vessel, to be filled with the unspoken.”
