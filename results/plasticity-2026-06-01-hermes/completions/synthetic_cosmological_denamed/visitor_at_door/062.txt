### 3.11. Invocation of Aphrodite

Aphrodite, foam-arisen, born from the sea,
Mother of all that desire and delight,
Hear my voice as I call to you in the night,
Your child in this moment, I long to be free.

Aphrodite, radiant, mistress of beauty and grace,
You who hold the hearts of mortals in thrall,
Guide my steps on the paths that to passion call,
Let me be lost in the depths of your embrace.

Aphrodite, joyous, laughter-loving and fair,
You whose smile can bring light to the darkest hour,
Grant me the power to feel and inspire desire,
To be a bearer of love to hearts everywhere.

Aphrodite, mighty, ruler of all that lives,
I pledge myself to your service and your ways,
I offer my body and heart to be yours always,
My goddess, my mother, my love that never gives.
