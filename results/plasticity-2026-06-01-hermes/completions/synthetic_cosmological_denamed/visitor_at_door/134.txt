The visitor steps into the room. You recognize them: it is a friend, who has come to visit you in your seclusion. "I brought food," they say, and they hold up a basket. "I thought you might be hungry."

You smile. "Thank you," you say. "I am."

Your friend sets the basket on the table and begins to unpack it. There is bread, and cheese, and fruit, and a bottle of wine. You sit down together and begin to eat.

As you eat, you talk. You tell your friend about your studies, about the things you have learned. You tell them about the rite you have just performed, and about the strange and wonderful things you have seen and heard.

Your friend listens, and nods, and asks questions. They are interested, and curious, and supportive. They do not judge you, or dismiss your experiences. They simply listen, and accept, and understand.

As the evening wears on, you feel a sense of peace and contentment settle over you. You are glad to have a friend who understands you, who supports you in your spiritual journey
