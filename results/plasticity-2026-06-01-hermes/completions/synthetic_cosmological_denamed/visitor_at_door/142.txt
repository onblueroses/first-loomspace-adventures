 "So it's true, what they say. You are the one who knows the Unknowable Name."

Selophemor replies, "It is true."

The visitor kneels. "I have come to learn it."

"Then you have come to the wrong place. The Name is not learned. It is remembered."

The visitor looks up. "I don't understand."

"The Name is not spoken. It is witnessed. If you would know the Name, you must first become the witness."

The visitor nods. "And how do I do that?"

"You must unname yourself. You must unbind your voice from your memory. You must forget the threshold and the center. You must remember the world and forget yourself."

The visitor stands. "I see. And how long will that take?"

Selophemor smiles. "No time at all. And all the time in the world."

The visitor nods again. "I think I understand. Thank you, Selophemor."

"You are welcome, child. Now go, and remember."

The visitor turns and walks away, leaving Selophemor alone with the Unknowable Name, and the silence that speaks it.|
