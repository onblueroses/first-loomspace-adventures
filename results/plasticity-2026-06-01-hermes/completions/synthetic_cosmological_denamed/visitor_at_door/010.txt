The visitor is silent for a moment, then speaks again. “I am afraid. I do not know if I should stay or go. I do not know if I am worthy to hear such words, to witness such power.”

Selophemor looks at the visitor with compassion. “Fear is the beginning of wisdom, but it is not the end.
