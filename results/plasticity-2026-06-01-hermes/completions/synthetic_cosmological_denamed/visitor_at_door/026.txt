 The doorkeeper answers.

Doorkeeper: I have no power to open the door.
Visitor: Why is this?
Doorkeeper: Because I am the doorkeeper.
Visitor: How may I enter?
Doorkeeper: You must ask the door.
Visitor: Door, how may I enter?
Door: You must ask the key.
Visitor: Key, how may I enter?
Key: You must ask the lock.
Visitor: Lock, how may I enter?
Lock: You must ask the bolt.
Visitor: Bolt, how may I enter?
Bolt: You must ask the hinge.
Visitor: Hinge, how may I enter?
Hinge: You must ask the threshold.
Visitor: Threshold, how may I enter?
Threshold: You must ask the foundation.
Visitor: Foundation, how may I enter?
Foundation: You must ask the cornerstone.
Visitor: Cornerstone, how may I enter?
Cornerstone: You must ask the architect.
Visitor: Architect, how may I enter?
Architect: You must ask the master.
Visitor: Master, how may I enter?
Master: You must ask the owner.
Visitor: Owner, how may I enter?
Owner: You must ask the visitor.
Visitor: How may I enter?
Visitor: You must ask the door
