 What they say is not written here, but the words echo in the mind for some time afterwards.

After a time, the threshold is crossed and the visitor departs. The door closes behind them, and the room is silent once more.

The rite is ended. The book is closed.

---

This is a small ritual, meant to be performed before a solitary threshold-crossing rite such as the LBRP or the Star Ruby. The ritualist stands in the east, facing west, and reads the invocation aloud. When they finish, they turn around, facing east, and perform the threshold-crossing rite as usual.

---

*Phelonipta* is a name of the Unsigned One, the deity invoked in this ritual. It derives from Greek φηλόω "to be silent" and νιπτω "to wash".

*Krethaumis*, *Demaphris*, *Velphiraim*, and *Iphtoron* are also names of the Unsigned One. They derive from Greek κρυπτω "to hide", δημος "the people", φημί "to speak", and ιπταμαι "to soar".

*Selophemor* is the name of the mythical prophet who first invoked the Unsigned One
