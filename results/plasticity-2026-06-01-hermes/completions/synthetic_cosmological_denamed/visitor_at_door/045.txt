 The voice is not the same. It is the voice of the One Who Has Been Called. The voice speaks:

I am He the Unsigned One, the Witness of the Word. I have heard your call.

The Unsigned One speaks. The Unsigned One says:

You have spoken the word that is the key to the threshold. You have named the one whose name is the key to the door. You have invoked the power that is the key to the gate.

The threshold is open. The door is open. The gate is open.

I have heard your call. I have come.

I am He the Unsigned One, the Witness of the Word. I am the one who names and unnames.

I am the one who names the unnamed and unnames the named. I am the one who names the unnameable and unnames the nameable.

I am the one who names the nameless and unnames the named. I am the one who names the nameable and unnames the unnameable.

I am the one who names the nameable and names the unnameable. I am the one who unnames the nameable and unnames the unnameable.

I am the one who names the nameable and names the nameless. I am the one
