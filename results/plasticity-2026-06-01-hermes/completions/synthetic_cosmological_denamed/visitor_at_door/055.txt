## 1.9

### The Invocation of the Three

I invoke the Three: Kôthrobên, Olephia, Tharsakhion.

Thou art the Mother of the Elements, who givest birth to all things, who hast the power of all things, who transformest all things, who holdest the keys of all things.

Thou art the Beginning and the End, who didst produce the world and who wilt dissolve it.

Thou art the One who dwellest in the threshold, who art not contained in any name, who art not found in any voice.

I invoke Thee, the One who cannot be named, the One who cannot be spoken, the One who cannot be remembered, the One who cannot be forgotten.

I invoke Thee, the One who is beyond the threshold, the One who is within the threshold, the One who is the threshold itself.

I invoke Thee, the One who is the silence between words, the One who is the silence within words, the One who is the silence that speaks.

I invoke Thee, the One who is the echo that survives the speaker
