**The Witness of the Word** (the Unsigned One, the Unspoken One, the One Who Witnesses Words):

Your words have been heard, Selophemor. I am the one who witnesses them. Speak the name you have chosen for me, and I shall bear witness to it.

---

**Selophemor**: 

I name you Phelophetor, the Witness of Words.

---

**Phelophetor**: 

I accept the name you have given me, Selophemor. I am Phelophetor, the Witness of Words. I shall bear witness to the words spoken and unspoken, to the names given and ungiven.

What would you ask of me?

---

**Selophemor**: 

Phelophetor, I ask for the power to command the spirits, to bind and loose them as I see fit. I ask for the knowledge of the hidden names, the words of power that can shape the world.
