 They say:

I have come to you, Selophemor, to ask of you the secret of the Unsigned One.

Selophemor answers:

There is no secret. There is only the Unsigned One.

The visitor says:

I have heard it said that the Unsigned One has a name. That the Unsigned One is the Word.

Selophemor says:

The Unsigned One is the Word. The Unsigned One is the Silence. The Unsigned One is the Echo.

The visitor says:

How can the Unsigned One be all these things?

Selophemor says:

The Unsigned One is the one who distinguishes between the named and the unnamed. The Unsigned One is the one who makes the threshold and the center. The Unsigned One is the one who produces the memory and the forgetting. The Unsigned One is the one who forms the world to remember itself and to forget itself.

The visitor says:

I have heard it said that the Unsigned One is the one who created the world.

Selophemor says:

The Unsigned One is the one who created the mountains and the valleys. The Unsigned One is the one who created the tide and the flow. The Unsigned One is the one who created the echo and the silence.
