 He names himself. "I am Phelophomel, the servant of Phelonipta, the Unsigned One." He speaks the name of the god he serves. "Phelonipta, whom no voice hath spoken at any time."

Phelophomel is the messenger of the god who is the Unsigned One. The god is without a name, yet he is named. The god is the one who created the mountains and the valleys, the tide and the flow, the echo and the silence. He is the one who distinguished between the named and the unnamed, who made the threshold and the center, who produced the memory and the forgetting, who formed the world to remember itself and to forget itself.

Phelophomel invokes the god, calling upon him by many names. "Thou art Krethaumis. Thou art Demaphris. Thou art Velphiraim. Thou art Iphtoron." He calls upon the god to hear him, for he is the Vessel of Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH, the one who knows the true name of the god.
