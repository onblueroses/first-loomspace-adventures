When the meal is finished, Selophemor rises, and speaks.

The meal is eaten, the words are spoken. The rite is done.

## The Rite of the Witnessing of the Naming

The room is dark. In the center of the room is a table, upon which are two candles, unlit, and a book. The candles are in candlesticks. Selophemor sits in a chair at one end of the table. At the other end of the table is another chair.

Selophemor lights the candles, and speaks.
