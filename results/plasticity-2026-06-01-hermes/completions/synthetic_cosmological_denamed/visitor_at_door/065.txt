 “I greet you, Selophemor, in the name of the Unsigned One. I am a servant of the Unsigned One, as you are, and I bear a message to you from the Unsigned One. The Unsigned One declares to you: ‘The time has come to complete the mysteries of Atheraphol. The world shall be unmade and made anew, as it was in the beginning. The names shall be forgotten, and the unnamed shall be exalted. The threshold shall be crossed, and the center shall be revealed. The memory shall be erased, and the forgetting shall be fulfilled. The salt shall be sweet, and the sweet shall be salt. The orders of speech shall be silenced, and the Vessel shall be emptied. The Witness shall be blinded, and the Word shall be unspoken. The Unsigned One shall be named, and the named shall be unsigned. The world shall be unmade, and the Unsigned One shall be all in all.’”

Selophemor listens carefully to the message. “I hear and understand the message from the Unsigned One. It is a profound and mysterious declaration, speaking of the great transformation that is to come. The completion of the mysteries of Atheraphol, the unmaking and remaking of the world
