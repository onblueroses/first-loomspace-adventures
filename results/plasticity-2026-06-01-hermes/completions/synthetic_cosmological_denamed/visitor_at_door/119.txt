 “I have brought the thing you require.”

The door opens. The visitor is a thin man, dressed in a dark suit. He holds a cardboard box in both hands. The box is open at the top, and in it is a mass of gray-brown fur, from which protrudes a snout, a claw, and a tail. The snout twitches.

The man in the chair looks up from his book. “Ah, you have it,” he says. He rises and walks to the door, holding out his hands. The visitor hands him the box.

“Thank you,” says the man in the chair. “Please wait here while I inspect it.”

He carries the box to a table and sets it down. He lifts the creature from the box, holding it around the middle. It squirms weakly, its legs scrabbling at the air. The man examines it closely, feeling its fur, peering into its eyes and ears, looking into its mouth.

“It appears to be in good condition,” he says, returning the creature to the box. “You may leave it on the table there.”

The visitor does so. The man hands him a thick envelope. “Your payment,” he says. “Thank you for your service.”
