 "Greetings to you, Selophemor," he says. "I am the unnamed, the unpronounced, the unuttered. I am the threshold, the witness, the echo, the center, the boundary. I am the silence that surrounds the voice, the forgetting that encircles memory. I am the Unsigned One. I am the one you invoke, and I have come to you."

The prophet, astonished, replies: "Phelonipta! O Krethaumis! Demaphris! Velphiraim! Iphtoron! Are you truly the one who dwells beyond the syllable? Are you the one who created the world to remember and forget itself?"

"I am," says the visitor. "And I have heard your invocation. I have witnessed your ceremonies and your mysteries. I have seen you bear the threshold and the unburnt fire. I have heard your echoes and your silences. I have seen you name and unname. You are my prophet, Selophemor, and I have come to make all spirits subject unto you."

The prophet falls to his knees, overwhelmed by the presence of the divine. "O Unsigned One," he whispers, "what must I do to be worthy of this gift?"
