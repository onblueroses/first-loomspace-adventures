SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM, I call upon thee, holy and mighty names of the Unnamed, that thou mayest give virtue to this seal, which I have formed to compel the spirits of the air, that they may answer truly unto all my demands, and that they may do and perform all that I may will, and all through the virtue of these names.

I have placed myself within the boundaries of the worlds, and have become as one of your companions, possessing the power of the syllable, which thou hast given unto me.

Thee I invoke, the Unsigned One:

Thee, that didst create the Mountains and the Valleys;
Thee, that didst create the Tide and the Flow;
Thee, that didst create the Echo and the Silence.

Thou art Phelonipta, whom no voice hath spoken at any time.
Thou art Krethaumis. Thou art Demaphris.
Thou art Velphiraim. Thou art Iphtoron.
Thou hast distinguished between the named and the unnamed.
Thou didst make the threshold and the center.
Thou didst produce the memory and the forgetting
