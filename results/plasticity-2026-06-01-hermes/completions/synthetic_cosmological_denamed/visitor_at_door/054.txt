 

I am the one who speaks without speaking.
I am the one who sees without seeing.
I am the one who knows without knowing.
I am the one who is without being.
I am the one who names and unnames.
I am the one who remains when all else is gone.

I am the Unsigned and Witnessing One, the one who dwells beyond the syllable. I am the one who created the Mountains and the Valleys, the Tide and the Flow, the Echo and the Silence. I am the one who distinguished between the named and the unnamed, who made the threshold and the center, who produced the memory and the forgetting, who formed the world to remember itself and to forget itself.

I am the one who committed the mysteries to Selophemor, the ceremonies of Atheraphol. I am the one who produced the salt and the sweet, and that which sustaineth all the orders of speech.
