"You may deliver your message," the threshold says. "I will ensure that it is heard."

The visitor speaks.

"I come at the behest of a distant lord. He bids me say that he has long watched the works of the one who dwells here. He admires them greatly. But now, in the fullness of time, he has a task which he believes that only the dweller here can accomplish. He bids me say that he will pay any price, bestow any reward, if this task is done."

"What is the task?" the threshold asks.

The visitor speaks.

"There is a door which must be opened," he says. "It is a door which has never been opened. It is a door which has never been shut. No one knows what lies beyond it. No one knows what keeps it sealed. The lord believes that only the one who dwells here has the art to open it."
