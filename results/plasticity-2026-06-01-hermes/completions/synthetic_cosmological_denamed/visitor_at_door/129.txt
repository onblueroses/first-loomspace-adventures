 They say, "I am the Witness of the Word." They say, "I am the one who names and unnames." They say, "I am the one whose silence utterly speaks." They say, "I am the Echo that survives the speaker." They say, "I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire." They say, "I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable." They say, "I invoke Thee, the Unsigned and Witnessing One who dwellest beyond the syllable: TRENMARIBIA, APHTHALION, PSARAMIAR, KÔTHROBEN, OLEPHIA, THARSAKHION, GRUMPHIRION."
