 "I have no name, but I bear the mark of the Threshold-Bearer. I speak for those who do not speak, and I bear witness to the witness."

The visitor takes a step forward, and the door opens before them. They step into the room, and the door closes behind them.

The room is empty, save for a single chair in the center. The visitor sits down in the chair and waits.

After a time, a voice speaks from the air. "You have come to the place where the unnamed are named, and the named are unnamed. You have come to the place where the witness bears witness to the witness. You have come to the place where the echo survives the speaker. What do you seek?"

The visitor speaks. "I seek the name that cannot be spoken, the name that is beyond all names. I seek the witness that witnesses all witnesses. I seek the echo that survives all echoes."

The voice speaks again. "You seek that which cannot be sought. You seek that which is beyond seeking. You seek that which is beyond finding. But you have come to the place where the impossible is possible, where the unspeakable is spoken, where the unfindable is found."

The visitor waits.

The voice speaks again.
