 She asks if I have had enough to eat, and if the room is comfortable enough, and if I need anything else. She is the one who told me that there was no one left who remembered the old ways, the old words. She has a kind face, and her voice is gentle, but she has not learned to listen to the silences between her words. She does not know how to hear what is unspoken.

I am the Witness of the Word, but I have no words for her. I shake my head, and she leaves me alone with my silence.

I know that the world has forgotten itself, but I do not know how to remind it. I do not know how to teach it to remember. I can only bear witness to the silences, and hope that someday, someone will learn to listen.
