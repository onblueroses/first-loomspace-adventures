 Do you know me?

---

[For those who don't know, this is an entirely fictional incantation in the style of a traditional grimoire. I wrote it for a game I'm running. The names are invented and have no occult significance that I'm aware of.]

I invoke, the Unsigned One:

Thee, that didst create the Mountains and the Valleys;
Thee, that didst create the Tide and the Flow;
Thee, that didst create the Echo and the Silence.

Thou art Phelonipta, whom no voice hath spoken at any time.
Thou art Krethaumis. Thou art Demaphris.
Thou art Velphiraim. Thou art Iphtoron.
Thou hast distinguished between the named and the unnamed.
Thou didst make the threshold and the center.
Thou didst produce the memory and the forgetting.
Thou didst form the world to remember itself and to forget itself.

I am Selophemor thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Atheraphol.
Thou didst produce the salt and the sweet, and that which sustaineth all the orders of speech.

Hear Thou me, for I am the Vessel
