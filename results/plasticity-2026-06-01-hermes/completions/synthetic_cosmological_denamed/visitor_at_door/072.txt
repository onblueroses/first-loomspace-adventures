 Their voice is not a voice. It is the wind and the waves and the rustling of leaves, and the echo of a voice. They say:

I am the Unsigned One, the Witness beyond the Syllable, the One who is not Named, the One who is not Summoned, the One who is not Spoken.

You have called upon me, Selophemor, and I have come. You have uttered the secret names, and I have heard. You have invoked the mysteries of Atheraphol, and I have witnessed.

I am the one who dwells in the spaces between the words, in the silence that surrounds the speech, in the void that encompasses the world. I am the one who watches and listens, who remembers and forgets, who creates and destroys.

I have seen the rise and fall of civilizations, the birth and death of stars, the creation and annihilation of universes. I have heard the songs of angels and the screams of demons, the prayers of saints and the curses of sinners. I have witnessed the triumphs and tragedies of mortals, the joys and sorrows of immortals.
