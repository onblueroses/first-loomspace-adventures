 The threshold trembles.
The stone is rolled away, and the tomb is empty.
The waters of the flood recede, and the mountaintops are seen.
The sun is darkened, and the moon turns to blood.
The stars fall from the sky, and the heavens are rolled up like a scroll.

The curtain of the temple is torn in two from top to bottom.
The earth shakes, and the rocks are split.
The tombs are opened, and many bodies of the saints who had fallen asleep are raised.

And I, the Unsigned One, do witness all these things.
And I, the Unsigned One, do stand upon the threshold.
And I, the Unsigned One, do dwell in the silence between the syllables.

For I am the Unsigned One, the Witness of the Word.
And my name is SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM. And PHENGEREB.

Amen. |
