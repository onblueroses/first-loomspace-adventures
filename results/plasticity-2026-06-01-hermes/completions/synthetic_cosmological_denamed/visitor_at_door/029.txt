 "I have no name," they say, "for I am the Unsigned One. I am the echo that follows the word, and the silence that precedes it. I am the witness to the names that are spoken, and the memory that remains when they are forgotten."

The visitor steps forward, and their form shifts and blurs, as if seen through a haze of heat. "I am the threshold between the named and the unnamed," they continue, "the border between memory and forgetting. I am the one who holds the key to the mysteries of Atheraphol, the ceremonies that bind and loose the tongues of mortals and gods alike."

The visitor's voice is a whisper, a murmur, a rush of wind through an empty hall. "I am the one who knows the true name of Phorbomanes, the Unsigned One, the Witness of the Word. I am the one who can command the spirits of the firmament and the ether, of earth and water, of air and fire. I am the one who can bind and loose, name and unname, speak and silence."
