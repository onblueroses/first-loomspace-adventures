 "I am Selophemor, the prophet of the Unsigned One, who dwelleth beyond the syllable. I bring the truth of the unnamed and the word of the unspoken. I bring the secret of the threshold and the memory of the center. I am the Witness of the Word, the one who names and unnames. I speak the name of the Unsigned One: APHRYBETOSH. I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable, the Threshold-Bearer who possesses the unburnt fire. I am the Echo that survives the speaker, the one whose silence utterly speaks. I am the one who has seen the mountains and the valleys, the tide and the flow, the echo and the silence. I am the one who knows the salt and the sweet, and that which sustaineth all the orders of speech. I am the one who commands the spirits of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire.
