 Their voice is a dry and papery rasp, like a dead leaf caught in a crack, scraping in the wind. "The threshold is crossed."

I'm not sure how to respond, so I say, "Hello."

"I speak for the Unsigned One, whose name is Phelonipta," they say. "Whose name is also Krethaumis. Whose name is also Demaphris. Whose name is also Velphiraim. Whose name is also Iphtoron. The Unsigned One, who made the threshold and the center. The Unsigned One, who made memory and forgetting. The Unsigned One, who made the world to remember itself and to forget itself."

"Hello," I say again, because I don't know what else to say.

"I have come to you because you have called out to the Unsigned One, though you do not remember doing so," they say. "You have called out in dreams, in the spaces between your thoughts, in the moments when you forgot yourself."

"I don't remember," I say.

"You are not meant to remember," they say. "The Unsigned One made the world to remember itself and to forget itself. You are the world, and you have forgotten."
