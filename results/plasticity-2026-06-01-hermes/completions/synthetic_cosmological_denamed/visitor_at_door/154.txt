 “Ablakharim. I had heard the name in dreams, but never in waking life.”

The figure in the chair stirs slightly. “You are the first to hear it from my lips.”

“Why do you tell me this?”

“Because you are the only one who could understand it.”

The visitor pauses, then says, “I don’t understand it. Not really.”

The figure in the chair chuckles. “No. But you could. That is enough.”

They sit in silence for a time.

Finally, the visitor asks, “What happens now?”

“Now? Now we wait.”

“For what?”

“For the Word.”

The visitor nods. “And then?”

“Then we will see what there is to see.”

The visitor nods again. “I’m ready.”

The figure in the chair smiles. “I know.”

They sit in silence, waiting for the Word, ready to see what there is to see. 📷
