The voice is familiar. It takes me a moment to place it.

“Michael?”

“Hello, Selene.”

I do not open the door. “Why are you here?”

“I’m in trouble. I need your help. I didn’t know where else to turn.”

I want to believe him. I want to believe that he is here because he needs me. Because he wants me. But I know better. I have been betrayed too many times.

“Please,” he says. “I’m begging you.”

I take a deep breath. And then I open the door.

He is standing there, looking just as I remember him. Tall, dark, handsome. The kind of man who can make a woman forget herself.

“Thank you,” he says. “I knew I could count on you.”

I step aside to let him in. As he passes me, I catch a whiff of his cologne. It brings back memories. Memories of a time when I was happy. When I thought I had found someone who loved me.
