 They say:
"I am a visitor, and I ask to enter the house of the Unsigned One."

The Vessel answers them:
"Who are you, that you should ask this?"

The visitor says:
"I am a wanderer, who has gone to the end of the road.
I am a witness, who has seen the end of the world.
I am a seer, who has glimpsed the end of time."

The Vessel says:
"Enter, wanderer, and behold the Unsigned One.
Enter, witness, and behold the threshold.
Enter, seer, and behold the end."

The visitor enters the house of the Unsigned One.
The Vessel leads them to the threshold.
The Unsigned One is there, waiting.

The Unsigned One speaks, and their voice is the sound of the world ending:
"You have come to the end of the road, wanderer.
You have seen the end of the world, witness.
You have glimpsed the end of time, seer.

Now, behold the Unsigned One, who is the end of all things.
Behold the threshold, which is the boundary between what is and what is not.
Behold the end, which is the beginning of all that is to come.
