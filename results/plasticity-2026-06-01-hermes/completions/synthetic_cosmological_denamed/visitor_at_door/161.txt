 You hear their voice, and the voice is yours, and you remember your name.

The door opens. You step through. The door closes behind you. You are within the chamber.

The walls of the chamber are of a black marble, shot through with veins of purple and gold. It is a cube, ten feet on a side, lit by a single globe of crystal in the center of the ceiling. The floor is covered with a carpet of rich purple, with a border of some darker hue worked in an intricate pattern of spirals and curves that seems to shift subtly as you look at it. In the center of the room is a low table of black wood, and on the table is a book bound in black leather.

You pick up the book and open it. The pages are of some thin, smooth material that is neither paper nor parchment, and the writing upon them is in a script you have never seen before, all curves and spirals, with here and there a sharp angle like the blade of a knife. As you turn the pages, the words seem to shift and change before your eyes, as if the book is rewriting itself as you read.
