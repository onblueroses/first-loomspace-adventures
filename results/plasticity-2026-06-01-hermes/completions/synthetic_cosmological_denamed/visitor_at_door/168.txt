 The threshold speaks. The one who crosses speaks. The one who remains speaks. The one who remembers speaks. The one who forgets speaks. The one who is named speaks. The one who is unnamed speaks.

The one who speaks, speaks.

The one who speaks not, speaks.

The one who speaks, speaks not.

The one who speaks not, speaks not.

The door is open.

The door is closed.

The door is open and closed.

The door is neither open nor closed.

The door is open.

I have spoken.
