 "You are Selophemor, the priest who speaks the forbidden name."

"Yes," says Selophemor. "I am the one who names and unnames."

The visitor produces a knife. "Then you are the one who will die."

"Yes," says Selophemor. "I am the one who will die."

The visitor stabs Selophemor, who falls to the ground. The visitor watches as the blood pools around the body, watches as the blood begins to seep into the carpet, watches as the blood stains the fibers, watches as the blood dries and flakes away, watches as the body decays, watches as the flesh rots away from the bones, watches as the bones turn to dust, watches as the dust scatters in the wind.

The visitor stands, and walks away. The house stands empty, the door open, the threshold unguarded.

The wind blows through the empty house, and the dust scatters, and the name is unspoken, and the name is forgotten.[]
