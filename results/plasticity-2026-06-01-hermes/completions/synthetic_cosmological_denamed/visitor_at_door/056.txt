 He says: "I have come to ask you a question, if you will hear it."

The visitor says: "How do I know that you are the one who can answer my question?"

The witness says: "I am the one who dwells in the threshold. I am the one who bears the unburnt fire. I am the one who speaks the word that cannot be spoken. I am the one who hears the silence that cannot be heard. I am the one who names and unnames. I am the one who remembers and forgets. I am the one who witnesses and is witnessed. I am the one who answers and questions."

The visitor says: "What is the question that you would ask me, then?"

The witness says: "What is the name that you have not spoken?"

The visitor is silent for a moment. Then he says: "I do not know the name that I have not spoken."

The witness says: "Then speak the name that you have not spoken, and I will answer your question."

The visitor says: "I do not know how to speak the name that I have not spoken."

The witness says: "Then listen to the silence that you have not heard, and speak the name that you have not spoken
