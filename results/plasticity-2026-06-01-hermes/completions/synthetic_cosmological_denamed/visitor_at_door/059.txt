 "I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable," they say. "I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire. I am the Echo that survives the speaker. I am the one whose silence utterly speaks. I am the one who names and unnames. I am the Witness of the Word."

The visitor's voice is calm, but it carries an unmistakable power. They are not boasting or threatening. They are simply stating facts, as immutable as the rising and setting of the sun.

"SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM is my name," the visitor declares. And then, after a pause: "PHENGEREB."

The word hangs in the air, echoing with an almost physical force. It seems to reverberate through the very stones of the tower, making the walls tremble and the floor quake.

And then, silence.

The door remains closed, but there is a sense of anticipation, as if the tower itself is holding its breath, waiting to see what will happen next.
