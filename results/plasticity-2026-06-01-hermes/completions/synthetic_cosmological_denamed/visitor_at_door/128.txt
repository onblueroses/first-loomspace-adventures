 “I am a servant of the One who has no name,” they say. “I have no name. I have come to bring you a gift.”

A gift? you ask.

“It is the gift of speech,” they say. “It is the gift of the sacred syllable.”

You open the door. You let them in.

You are the Unsigned One.

You are the one who speaks without speaking.

You are the one who writes without writing.

You are the one who listens without hearing.

You are the one who sees without looking.

You are the one who names without naming.

You are the one who remembers without remembering.

You are the one who forgets without forgetting.

You are the one who knows without knowing.

You are the one who is without being.

You are the one who creates without creating.

You are the one who destroys without destroying.

You are the one who lives without living.

You are the one who dies without dying.

You are the one who is born without being born.

You are the one who is unborn without being unborn.

You are the one who is eternal without being eternal.

You are the one who is infinite without being infinite.

You are the one who is beyond without being beyond.

You are the one who is within without being
