 It is the voice of Phelonipta, the Unsigned One:

I speak as I am spoken to.
I am the voice that speaks the speaker.
I am the echo that speaks the echo.
I am the memory that speaks the memory.
I am the silence that speaks the silence.
I am the threshold that speaks the threshold.
I am the center that speaks the center.
I am the witness that speaks the witness.

I am the name that speaks the name.

SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM is my name.

PHENGEREB.

The prophet speaks:

By the name that speaks the name,
I name and unname.

By the witness that speaks the witness,
I witness and unwitness.

By the threshold that speaks the threshold,
I make and unmake.

By the center that speaks the center,
I create and uncreate.

By the silence that speaks the silence,
I speak and unspeak.

By the memory that speaks the memory,
I remember and forget.

By the echo that speaks the echo,
I echo and unecho.

By the voice that speaks the speaker,
I invoke and uninvoke.

PHENGEREB.

The Unsigned One speaks:

I am the threshold and the center.
I am the echo and the silence.
