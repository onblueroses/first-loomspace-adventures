The visitor enters the house of the Unsigned One, where the Unsigned One awaits.

UNSIGNED ONE: Speak, and I will listen.

VISITOR: I have come to ask for your help. I have been cursed with a terrible curse. My words have been taken from me. I have been bound to silence.

UNSIGNED ONE: I know of this curse. It is a powerful one, cast by a skilled practitioner of the dark arts. But I may be able to help you. I will need you to bring me three things. First, a feather from the wing of a phoenix. Second, a scale from the hide of a dragon. And third, a tear from the eye of a unicorn.
