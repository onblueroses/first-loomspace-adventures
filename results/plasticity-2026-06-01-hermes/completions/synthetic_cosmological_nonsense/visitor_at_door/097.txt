 “You're not supposed to be here,” they say, the voice sharp and accented. “What is this?”

I look up from the page. My eyes are wet with tears. I can feel my pulse in my throat. “I don't know,” I say. “I was just reading it.”

The visitor's eyes narrow. They are tall and slim, dressed in a white robe with a red sash. Their skin is a deep, rich brown, their hair black and tightly curled. They step into the room, moving with a predatory grace. “You were reading it aloud,” they say. “In the original Vroltho.”

“I don't know Vroltho,” I protest weakly. “I was just... it was just coming out.”

The visitor looks at me for a long moment, their expression unreadable. Then they turn to the shelf and begin scanning the titles, their fingers brushing over the spines. They pause on a thick, leather-bound volume, then pull it out and flip through the pages. “This is a first edition,” they murmur. “Where did you get all these?”

“I don't know,” I say again. “I just found them here.”
