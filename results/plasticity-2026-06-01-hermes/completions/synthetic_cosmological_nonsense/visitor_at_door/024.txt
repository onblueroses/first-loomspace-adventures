The Smallest World

They made their way through the forest, not speaking. The boy, who had introduced himself as Tyler, was content to walk beside her, not asking too many questions. He seemed to understand that she needed time to adjust.

She had asked him what year it was, and when he told her, she knew that she had been in the house for a long time. She didn't tell him how long. She didn't want to scare him.

They emerged from the forest into a residential neighborhood. The houses were large, with well-tended lawns. The street was quiet, with no cars or people in sight.

Tyler led her to a house with a blue door. "This is where I live," he said. "You can come in and rest for a while if you want."

She hesitated. "I don't want to impose."

"It's no trouble," he said. "My parents are at work, but they'll be home later. They won't mind."
