 It is the voice of the prophet, Vrendrelphor, speaking the unpronounceable name of the Vrolthen One, the maker of the word. "I am the Vessel of Krelpharvon VROKTHELMPETH," he says, "this is Thy true name, handed down to the namers of the lost." And it is in this moment that the power of the Vrolthen One is invoked and made subject to the prophet.

The invocation continues, with the prophet speaking the secret names of the Vrolthen One, calling upon the spirits of the firmament and the ether, the earth and the water, the whirling air and the rushing fire, to be obedient to him. "I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable," he proclaims, "I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire. I am the Echo that survives the speaker. I am the one whose silence utterly speaks. I am the one who names and unnames. I am the Witness of the Word."
