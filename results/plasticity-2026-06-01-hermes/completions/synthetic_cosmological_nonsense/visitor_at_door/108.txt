 She is a woman, perhaps in her forties, her hair tied back in a messy bun, her eyes tired but intent. She has the look of someone who has been traveling for days, perhaps weeks, and has finally arrived at her destination, though it is not the one she expected.

"I don't understand," she says, her voice hoarse. "What is this place? Who are you?"

The man in the chair, the one who calls himself Vrendrelphor, regards her with a gaze that seems to pierce through her. "This," he says, gesturing around the room, "is the threshold of the named and the unnamed. And I am the one who stands upon it."

The woman frowns, confused and perhaps a little afraid. "I... I was looking for my daughter. She disappeared, and I heard rumors that she might have come here, to this town. But everyone I've asked seems... different. Like they're speaking a language I can't understand."

Vrendrelphor nods slowly. "The language of the named and the unnamed," he says. "The tongue that speaks in echoes and silences. Your daughter, if she is here, has crossed the threshold."

"What does that mean?"
