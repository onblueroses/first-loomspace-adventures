 “I am Gaius. I have come from the west. I am the one who will bring you to the Empress.”

The door is opened. The visitor enters. The door is closed.

Gaius: You are the one they call the Logician, yes?

Logician: I am called the Logician by some.

Gaius: You have heard of the Empress?

Logician: I have heard of the Empress.

Gaius: The Empress is the one who will rule over all the lands, the one who will bring order to the world. She is the one who will bring peace and prosperity to all people. She is the one who will bring justice to the world.

Logician: I have heard these things said of the Empress.

Gaius: The Empress has heard of you, Logician. She has heard of your wisdom and your knowledge. She wishes to meet with you, to speak with you, to learn from you.

Logician: I am honored that the Empress would wish to meet with me. However, I must decline her invitation. I have much work to do here, and I cannot leave my studies.
