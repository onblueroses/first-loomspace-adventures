 He speaks the words of power that are the key to the rite. They are the words of the god, the words of the angel, the words of the prophet. The god speaks through the angel, the angel speaks through the prophet, the prophet speaks through the celebrant. The celebrant speaks the words of power that open the rite.

## IV. The Rite of the Unveiling of the Word

The celebrant speaks:

I invoke Thee, the Vrolthen One:

Thee, that didst create the Mountains and the Valleys;
Thee, that didst create the Tide and the Flow;
Thee, that didst create the Echo and the Silence.

Thou art Vrokthemnarph, whom no voice hath spoken at any time.
Thou art Plendrokt. Thou art Smelthrenaph.
Thou art Krelnomphar. Thou art Snolthorm.
Thou hast distinguished between the named and the unnamed.
Thou didst make the threshold and the center.
Thou didst produce the memory and the forgetting.
Thou didst form the world to remember itself and to forget itself.
