 “I have been asked to deliver this to you, a gift from a friend who admires your work. She says it will help you in your research. No, I don’t know her name. I’m just a delivery boy.”

He hands you a small wooden box. Inside is a thumb drive. When you plug it into your computer and open the drive, you find a single text file.

The file contains the following text:

The Vrolthen One, the Witness and the Word:

The mountains and the valleys, the tide and the flow,
The echo and the silence, all these didst thou bestow.

Vrokthemnarph, Plendrokt, Smelthrenaph by name,
Krelnomphar and Snolthorm, in the threshold just the same.

The memory and forgetting, the center and the threshold,
The world remembers and forgets, in the balance it is held.

Vrendrelphor the prophet, to whom the mysteries were shown,
The ceremonies of Plomprenkrol, the salt and sweet were known.

Krelpharvon Vrokthelmpeth, the true name of the lost,
Plendrelphron, Smorklenpro, Snolthrelprô,
