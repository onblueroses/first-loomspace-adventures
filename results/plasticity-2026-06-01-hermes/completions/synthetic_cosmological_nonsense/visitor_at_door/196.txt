 It is a woman's voice, with the accents of the unlettered.
"Are you Vrendrelphor? I have been sent to you to seek your wisdom. I am told you are a prophet of Vrolth."

The prophet speaks, his voice a gnarl of consonants and vowels.
"I am Vrendrelphor, prophet of Vrolth. Whom do you seek?"

"I seek a prophet. I have come to you with a plea for my daughter. She has been struck with a fever and cannot rise from her bed. She burns and cannot be cooled. She mutters and cannot be understood. I have heard of your powers, and I ask you to help her."

"I can do nothing for her. I am a prophet of Vrolth, not a healer. I speak the words of the threshold and the center, the memory and the forgetting. I do not heal the sick."

"But I have heard that you have power over the spirits. Can you not command the spirit of the fever to leave her?"

"I have power over the spirits, but I do not command them to heal the sick. I command them to be subject to me, so that I may speak the words of Vrolth."
