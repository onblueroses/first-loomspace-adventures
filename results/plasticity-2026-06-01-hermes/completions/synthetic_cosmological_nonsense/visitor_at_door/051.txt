 “I have come, Vrendrelphor, to witness the word that is spoken and the word that is not spoken.”

The celebrant responds. “And I have come, Plendrokt, to witness the word that is spoken and the word that is not spoken.”

The visitor enters and takes his place at the altar.

The celebrant and the visitor at the door make the sign of the threshold, and say: “The threshold is crossed.”

The celebrant speaks. “In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.

“The same was in the beginning with God.

“All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made.

“In him was life; and the life was the light of men.

“And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not.”

The celebrant and the visitor at the door make the sign of the threshold, and say: “The threshold is crossed.”

The celebrant speaks. “There was a man sent from God, whose name was John.

“The same came for a witness, to bear witness of the Light, that all men through him might believe.
