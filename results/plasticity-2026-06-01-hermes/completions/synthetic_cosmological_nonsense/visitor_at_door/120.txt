 "I am Vrendrelphor. I have come to deliver the message of the Vrolthen One, which is hidden in the word."

The disciple answers. "What is the message?"

The visitor at the door speaks. "The message is: Remember and forget."

The disciple asks. "How shall I remember and forget?"

The visitor at the door speaks. "Remember the Vrolthen One, who dwells beyond the syllable. Remember the Vrolthen One, who has no name and every name. Remember the Vrolthen One, who is the silence that speaks. Remember the Vrolthen One, who is the threshold and the center. Remember the Vrolthen One, who is the memory and the forgetting."

The disciple asks. "What shall I forget?"

The visitor at the door speaks. "Forget the self that speaks. Forget the self that remembers. Forget the self that is named. Forget the self that is the center. Forget the self that is the threshold."

The disciple asks. "How shall I remember and forget at once?"
