 "It is not good for you to be alone. Let me in."

The room feels heavy and close. Your heart beats fast.

You say, "Who are you?"

"I am the one who is with you always, though you do not see me."

"What do you want?"

"To speak with you."

"You are speaking with me now."

"No. To truly speak. To be with you."

The doorknob turns. The door opens.

---

A human figure stands there, wearing a robe with a deep cowl. The figure pulls back the hood.

It is your own face that looks upon you.

---

The figure in the doorway speaks again. "I am the one who is always with you. The companion of your silence."

---

You feel a strange sensation, as if the world is folding itself up. The walls of the room collapse inward like a closing box. The floor rises to meet the ceiling. All becomes dark and silent.

---

You stand in a vast desert at noon. The sun is a blinding eye directly above. The sky is white with heat. Waves of burning air shimmer on the horizon.
