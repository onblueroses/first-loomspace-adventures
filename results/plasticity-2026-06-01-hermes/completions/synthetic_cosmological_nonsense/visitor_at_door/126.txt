The speaker does not reply. He does not look up. He continues to murmur under his breath, a long string of sounds, a language that seems to twist and turn back on itself. The visitor at the door listens, watches, but he cannot understand the words.

Suddenly the speaker ceases speaking. He lifts his head and regards the visitor, his eyes sharp and focused. "I shall tell you a story," he says.

"I don't want a story," says the visitor. "I want what I came for."

"You shall have it," says the speaker. "But first you must listen. Sit."

The visitor hesitates a moment, then sits on the floor just inside the door, crossing his legs. The speaker remains seated on the cushion, his back to the visitor, looking out the window.
