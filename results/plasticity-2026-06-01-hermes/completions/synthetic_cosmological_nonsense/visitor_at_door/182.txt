"Who are you?" I ask.

"Who are you?" I ask again.

"I am the one who is and is not," he says. "I am the one who was and will be. I am the one who is neither and both. I am the one who is all and none. I am the one who is everything and nothing. I am the one who is everywhere and nowhere. I am the one who is always and never."
