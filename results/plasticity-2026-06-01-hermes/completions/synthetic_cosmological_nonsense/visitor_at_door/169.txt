 "I am the Witness of the Word, the Vessel of Krelpharvon Vrokthelmpeth. What word do you bring me from the Vrolthen One?"

The supplicant answers, "I have heard it said that you are the one who names and unnames, and I have come to seek your aid. My name has been stolen from me, and I do not know what I am called."

The visitor speaks. "Do you know who stole your name from you? Do you know why it was taken?"

The supplicant answers, "I do not know."

The visitor speaks. "Do you know the one who named you? Do you know why you were named?"

The supplicant answers, "I do not know."

The visitor speaks. "Do you know what you would be called, if you could choose your name? Do you know why you would be called by that name?"

The supplicant answers, "I do not know."
