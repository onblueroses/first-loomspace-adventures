"Enter, then, and fulfill your purpose."

The figure steps forward into the room. She wears a cloak of midnight blue, and her eyes are like stars in the void. "You have called me to bind the spirits, to bring them under your control."

"Yes," I confirm. "That is my will."

The visitor nods. "Then let it be done. By the power of the ancient words, I bind the spirits to your command. They shall obey your every utterance, and serve your every desire."

I feel a surge of energy coursing through me, a rush of power that sets my nerves tingling. The spirits are mine to command now, and with them, I shall work wonders.

"Thank you," I say to the visitor. "You have done well."

She inclines her head. "I am but a servant of the words, O Vessel. It is you who wields the power now. Use it wisely."

With that, she turns and departs, vanishing into the night as swiftly as she came.
