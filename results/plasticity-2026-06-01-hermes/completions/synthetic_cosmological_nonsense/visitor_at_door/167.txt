 "I have heard your invocation. I am the Witness of the Word. I have seen the many words of the world. I have seen the words that make the world. I have seen the words that unmake the world. I have seen the word that is the world."

The visitor's words are heard, but the visitor is not seen. The room is empty but for the echo of the invocation.

The visitor's words continue. "I am the echo that survives the speaker. I am the one whose silence utterly speaks. I am the one who names and unnames. I have seen the word that is the world, and it is a word that is not spoken. It is a word that is not heard. It is a word that is not written. It is a word that is not read. It is a word that is not thought. It is a word that is not known. It is a word that is not remembered. It is a word that is not forgotten. It is a word that is not named. It is a word that is not unnamed. It is a word that is not witnessed. It is a word that is not unwitnessed. It is a word that is not. It is a word that is."
