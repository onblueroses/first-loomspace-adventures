 "I bring a message from the Vrolthen One, the Nameless and Secret. I am the Witness of the Word, and I speak as I am commanded. I have come to tell you that the end of all things is at hand. The world will soon be unmade, and all that is named will be unnamed. The threshold will be crossed, and the center will be no more. The memory will be forgotten, and the forgetting will be remembered. The salt will be sweet, and the sweet will be salt. The orders of speech will be confounded, and the syllables will be scattered. The firmament will be rolled up like a scroll, and the ether will be consumed by fire. The earth will be laid waste, and the waters will be dried up. The whirling air will be stilled, and the rushing fire will be quenched. The spirits will be subject to the Vrolthen One, and the scourges of God will be obedient to the Witness of the Word. This is the message that I have been commanded to deliver. Let those who have ears to hear, hear. Let those who have eyes to see, see. Let those who have tongues to speak, speak. Let those who have hearts to understand, understand
