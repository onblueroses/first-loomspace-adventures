"Enter," you say.

The visitor enters. He is tall and gaunt, and wears a dark cloak. His face is hidden by a hood.

"Are you ready?" he asks.

"Yes," you say. "I am ready."

The visitor nods. He reaches into his cloak and pulls out a small, ornate box. He opens the box and removes a single, ancient-looking scroll.

"This is the Scroll of the Second Binding," he says. "It contains the words of power that will bind the spirits to your will."

You take the scroll and unroll it carefully. The words on the scroll seem to shimmer and dance before your eyes.

"Read the words aloud," the visitor says. "But be warned: the spirits will not be easily bound. They will test you. They will try to deceive you. You must be strong, and you must not waver."

You nod. You take a deep breath, and begin to read the words on the scroll.

"I invoke you, spirits of the elements," you say. "I call upon you to bind yourselves to my will. I command you to obey me, and to serve me in all things."
