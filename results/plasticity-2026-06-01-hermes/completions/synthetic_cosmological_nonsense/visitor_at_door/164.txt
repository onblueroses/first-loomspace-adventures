 “I am the witness of your word. I have heard the syllables of your voice. I have come for the rite of Vrendrelphor.”

“You are the chosen one, the one who is called to be the vessel of the rite. Come in and be welcome.”

The visitor enters. The door is shut. The rite begins.

The visitor is stripped of clothing, and is washed with salt water and scented oils. The visitor is robed in a white garment.

Vrendrelphor speaks. “Hear the rite of the word. You are the vessel of the word. You are the speaker of the word. You are the hearer of the word. You are the witness of the word. You are the threshold of the word. You are the silence of the word. You are the echo of the word.”

The visitor repeats the words of Vrendrelphor.
