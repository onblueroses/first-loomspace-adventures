The visitor and I walk away from the motel and into the night.
I do not know what will happen to me, or what I will say to the visitor, but I feel that there is some kind of purpose to our conversation, and that my purpose, whatever it is, has not yet been fulfilled.
We walk until the motel is no longer visible, and then we walk some more.
Finally, the visitor speaks.
"I'm not supposed to tell you this," the visitor says, "but my name is Paul."
I nod.
"I don't know if that's your real name," I say, "but I appreciate the gesture."
"It's my real name," says the visitor. "And I can understand if you don't believe me."
