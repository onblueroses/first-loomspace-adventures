 I listen. She asks, "Do you remember me?" I do not. She explains that she is my daughter. I do not remember her. I ask her to tell me her story.

She says, "I am the daughter you never knew. I was born when you were young, when you first came to this city. My mother raised me by herself. She told me about you, but said you had gone away and would not return. I never tried to find you, but now I have come to you."

I ask her, "Why have you come to me now?" She says, "I have come to learn from you. I have heard of your wisdom and your power. I want to know the secrets you possess."

I tell her, "I have no wisdom or power that you do not already possess. The secrets I know are the secrets of silence and forgetting. If you wish to learn from me, you must unlearn what you think you know. You must forget your name and your story. You must become silent and listen to the silence."

She says, "I am willing to do this. Teach me."
