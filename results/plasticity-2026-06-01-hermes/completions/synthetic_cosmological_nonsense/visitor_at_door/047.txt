 "It's me, Frobisher," he says. "I saw your light on. I thought you might still be up. I brought you some of that plum brandy you like." The door opens; Frobisher enters, carrying a bottle and two glasses. "I thought you might like some company. The night's so cold and dark."

The old man looks up from his book, unsurprised. "Come in, come in," he says. "I was just reading. It's good to see you. I hope you and your family are well. You're right, it's a cold night. The brandy will be welcome."

Frobisher pours the brandy and hands a glass to the old man. "You're reading again," he says. "Always with your nose in a book. What is it this time?"

"Oh, just some old nonsense I found in the attic," the old man says. "A bit of light reading before bed. I don't think it's anything important. Just an old book of spells and invocations. Mumbo jumbo, really. I don't know why I keep it around."
