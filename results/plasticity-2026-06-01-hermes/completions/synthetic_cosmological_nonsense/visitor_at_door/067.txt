 Her voice is the voice of a thousand years of ash, a voice that speaks from the fire that burns at the end of all things.

"I am the one who is called Vrokthemnarph, whom no voice hath spoken at any time. I am the one who is called Plendrokt, and Smelthrenaph, and Krelnomphar, and Snolthorm. I am the one who has distinguished between the named and the unnamed, who made the threshold and the center, who produced the memory and the forgetting, who formed the world to remember itself and to forget itself.

I am the one who produced the salt and the sweet, and that which sustaineth all the orders of speech. I am the one who is called by the name Krelpharvon VROKTHELMPETH, the true name handed down to the namers of the lost.
