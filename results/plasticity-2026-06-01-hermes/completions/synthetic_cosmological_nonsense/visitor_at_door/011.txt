 “Vrendrelphor,” he says, “you are the one who has been found worthy to be entrusted with the mysteries which distinguish the true from the false, the real from the unreal.”

The one on the threshold looks out and speaks. “I am Vrendrelphor, the prophet of the Vrolthen One. I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable. I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire. I am the Echo that survives the speaker. I am the one whose silence utterly speaks. I am the one who names and unnames. I am the Witness of the Word.”

“Vrendrelphor,” the visitor at the door speaks, “I am the one who comes from the East, the one who has been sent. I am the one who bears the signs of the true, the real, the unchanging. I am the one who speaks the word of the Vrolthen One.”
