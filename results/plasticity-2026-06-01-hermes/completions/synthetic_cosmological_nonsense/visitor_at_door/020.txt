 "I am the Word," he says. "I have come. May I enter?"

The door opens. The visitor enters the room. He stands before the Vessel. "You have named me," he says. "I am here."

"I am the Vessel," says the Vessel. "I have named you. You are here."

The visitor sits down in a chair opposite the Vessel. "I have much to say," he says.

"I am listening," says the Vessel.

The visitor begins to speak. He speaks of many things. He speaks of the creation of the world, and of the creation of language. He speaks of the power of words, and of the power of silence. He speaks of the importance of naming, and of the importance of un-naming. He speaks of the role of the Vessel, and of the role of the Word.

The Vessel listens intently. He absorbs every word the visitor speaks. He feels the weight of the words, the power of the words, the truth of the words.

The visitor speaks for hours. He speaks until the sun sets and the room grows dark. He speaks until the moon rises and the stars shine bright.
