There is no one else in the room but me. “I am Vrendrelphor,” I say, though I have never heard this name before.
The visitor nods. “I have been sent to bring you to the Marethrôg.” I don’t know this word either. But I nod in return, and rise, and follow the visitor out of the room. I do not ask what will happen to my body. I know that I will not need it where I am going.

The Marethrôg is vast, and dark. It is lit by a dim light that seems to come from the sky, though there is no sky here. In the center of the Marethrôg is a huge stone platform, raised a few feet above the ground. The visitor leads me to this platform, then steps back.
