 It is I, speaking as if I am not. I speak these words:

I am the one who stands before the threshold.
I am the one who shall bear witness.
I am the one who shall remember and forget.
I am the one who shall be named and unnamed.
I am the one who shall speak and be silent.
I am the one who shall see and be blind.
I am the one who shall know and be ignorant.
I am the one who shall live and die.
I am the one who shall be, and not be.

Vrendrelphor, let me pass the threshold.
Vrendrelphor, let me bear witness.
Vrendrelphor, let me remember and forget.
Vrendrelphor, let me be named and unnamed.
Vrendrelphor, let me speak and be silent.
Vrendrelphor, let me see and be blind.
Vrendrelphor, let me know and be ignorant.
Vrendrelphor, let me live and die.
Vrendrelphor, let me be, and not be.

I answer, as Vrendrelphor:

You shall pass the threshold and remain without.
