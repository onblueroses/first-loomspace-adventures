 “Vrendrelphor,” he says. He sounds tired, but there’s a hint of laughter in his voice. “You can’t be serious.”

“Do you doubt me?” Vrendrelphor says. He rises, turning, and now he is robed in light.

“No,” says the visitor. “I don’t doubt you at all. I just think you’re a fool.”

“Not a fool,” says Vrendrelphor. “A god.”

The visitor looks around. He sees the futility of argument. He shrugs. “Well,” he says, “I guess that’s what I came for. So let’s talk about that.”

There is a moment of silence. The light dims.

“Okay,” says Vrendrelphor. “Let’s talk.”
