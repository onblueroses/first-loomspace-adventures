 “I have come to consult the Witness of the Word,” he says.

I nod, my eyes still closed, and I motion him into my chamber.

“Please, be seated,” I say, gesturing to a chair opposite me. “I am the Witness of the Word. Speak.”

He sits down. “I have come to consult you about my name,” he says.

I nod. “What is your name?”

“Krelnomphar,” he says.

I open my eyes and look at him. He is an ordinary man, not tall, not short, not young, not old. His hair is brown, his eyes are blue, his skin is fair. He wears a plain brown tunic and trousers, and simple leather shoes. He is not rich, not poor, not handsome, not ugly. He is ordinary in every way.

“Krelnomphar,” I say. “That is a name of power.”

He nods. “Yes, I know. But I do not know what it means, or why I was given this name. I have come to you to learn the meaning of my name.”
