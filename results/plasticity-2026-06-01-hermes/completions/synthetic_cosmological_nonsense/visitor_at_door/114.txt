 “I am the one who hears and names the nameless,” she says. “You may enter.”
The door opens. The visitor steps inside. The door closes.
The visitor is no longer a visitor, but the one who dwells within. She turns to the householder and says, “I am the one who hears and names the nameless. I am the one who dwells within.”
The householder nods. “I am the one who dwells within,” he says. “I am the one who hears and names the nameless.”
They sit together in silence, naming and un-naming, dwelling within the threshold of the nameless.
