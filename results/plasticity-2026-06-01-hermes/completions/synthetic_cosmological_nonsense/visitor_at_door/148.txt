“It will teach you how to speak without words.”
“Is that what the Vrolth is?”
“The Vrolth is the language of the unspeakable, the language of the unnameable. It is the language that cannot be spoken, the language that cannot be heard.”
“Then how can it be a language at all?”
“Because it is the language of the world, the language of the universe. It is the language of the gods, the language of the demons, the language of the dead. It is the language of the beginning and the end, the language of the infinite and the infinitesimal.”
“But how can I learn a language that cannot be spoken or heard?”
“By listening to the silence, by looking into the darkness, by feeling the emptiness.”
“By letting go of your words, your thoughts, your self. By becoming one with the unspeakable, the unnameable.”
