 "I am a servant of the Vrolthen One. May I enter, and speak with you?"

"You may enter," I say.

The visitor enters. "I am Vrendrelphor, a humble servant of the Vrolthen One. I have been sent to speak with you, for you have been chosen to become a servant of the Vrolthen One."

"I am honored," I say, "but I know nothing of the Vrolthen One or of the duties of a servant."

"Do not be troubled," says Vrendrelphor. "The Vrolthen One knows you, and has chosen you, and will guide you in your service. The Vrolthen One is the creator of all things, the source of all power, and the witness of all that is spoken and unspoken."

"I am humbled," I say, "but I am uncertain of my worthiness for such a task."

"Worthiness is not a requirement," says Vrendrelphor. "The Vrolthen One chooses whom the Vrolthen One will, and those who are chosen must only accept the call and fulfill their duties."
