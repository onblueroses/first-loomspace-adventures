 "I am the one you seek."

The visitor enters. "I am the one you have been waiting for."

The visitor sits. "I am the one who has come to you."

The visitor smiles. "I am the one who will show you the way."

The visitor takes your hand. "I am the one who will guide you."

The visitor leads you outside. "I am the one who will take you there."

The visitor points to the sky. "I am the one who will show you the stars."

The visitor looks into your eyes. "I am the one who will show you yourself."

The visitor whispers in your ear. "I am the one who will tell you the secret."

The visitor embraces you. "I am the one who will love you."

The visitor lets go. "I am the one who will leave you."

The visitor walks away. "I am the one who will forget you."

The visitor disappears. "I am the one who was never here."
