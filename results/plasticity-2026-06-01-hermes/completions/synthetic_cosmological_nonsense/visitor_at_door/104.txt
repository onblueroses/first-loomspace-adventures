 He says, "Who are you?"

"I am Vrendrelphor," you say.

The visitor says, "I know you. I have come for the final lesson."

"Then come in," you say. "You are welcome."

The visitor enters. He is a man of some years, with dark hair and a beard. He wears a robe of black silk, and carries a staff of black wood. He looks at you with eyes that seem to see through you.

"You have done well," he says. "You have learned the mysteries of the Vrolthen. You have become a namer and an unnamer. You have become a witness of the word."

"Thank you," you say. "I have worked hard to attain this knowledge."

"But there is one final lesson," he says. "One final mystery that you must learn."

"What is it?" you ask.

"The mystery of silence," he says. "The mystery of the unspoken word."

"I don't understand," you say.

"You have learned to name and to unname," he says. "You have learned to speak the words of power. But you have not yet learned the power of silence."

"Silence?" you ask. "What power is there in silence?"
