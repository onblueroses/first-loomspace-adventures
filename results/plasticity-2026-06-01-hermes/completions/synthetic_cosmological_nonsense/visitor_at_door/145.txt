“No,” says the woman inside, “I do not let strangers into my house.”
“Very wise,” says the visitor, “but I have not come to harm you, or to take anything from you. I am here to give you something. It is a gift that is beyond price, and it will not cost you a cent. May I come in and explain it to you?”
“No,” says the woman, “you may not come in. I do not want any gifts. I do not want anything from you. I want you to leave. If you do not leave, I will call the police.”
