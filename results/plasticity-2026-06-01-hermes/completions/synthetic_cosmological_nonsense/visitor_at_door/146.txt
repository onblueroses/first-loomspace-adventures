 “A curious and formidable incantation,” they say. “But why do you recite it?”

The man at the door speaks. “Because I am Vrendrelphor,” he says. “Because I am the Vessel. Because I am the Witness of the Word.”

“Ah,” says the visitor. “But to what purpose?”

The man at the door hesitates.

The visitor speaks. “You invoke the Vrolthen One; you name yourself the Vessel. You call yourself the Witness of the Word. But these things are only the shape of the power that you seek. They are the means, but not the end. You have not said why you have sought out this power. You have not named the purpose to which you will put it.”

The man at the door frowns.

“Do you seek this power for its own sake?” asks the visitor. “Do you seek to become the master of spirits simply to have mastery?”

“No,” says the man at the door. “It is not for its own sake.”

“Then for what?” asks the visitor.

The man at the door looks away. “I have a purpose,” he says. “But it is secret.”
