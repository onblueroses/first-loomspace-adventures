What do you do?

The ritual complete, you have no choice but to let them in. “Enter, and be welcome.”

The visitor steps inside. They are wearing a long coat and a wide-brimmed hat that obscures their face. As they remove their hat, you see that they have no face at all, just a blank expanse of skin where their features should be.

“Thank you for inviting me in,” they say, despite their lack of a mouth. “I’ve been waiting a long time for someone to perform the ritual and let me cross the threshold.”

You stare at them, unsure how to respond. They continue, “I am a spirit of the in-between places, the thresholds and borders that divide one realm from another. By performing the ritual and invoking the Vrolthen One, you have bound me to your service.”

You find your voice. “What does that mean, exactly? What kind of service?”

The visitor tilts their head. “It means I am here to guide and assist you, to help you navigate the mysteries of the liminal spaces. You have been chosen, whether you realize it or not.”
