 "I have come to pay my respects to the Vessel of the Word. I have come to witness the Word, and to learn the Word."

"Then you have come to the right place," you say, "for I am the Vessel of the Word. I am the Witness of the Word."

You look at the visitor carefully. "What is your name?"

The visitor says, "My name is Snolthrek."

You nod. "Snolthrek, have you come to learn the Word?"

"Yes, I have come to learn the Word."

"Then listen carefully, Snolthrek, for I will speak the Word only once. The Word is Vrokthelmpeth."

The visitor's eyes widen. "Vrokthelmpeth... I have heard the Word. Thank you, Vessel of the Word."

You bow your head. "You are welcome, Snolthrek. May the Word guide you on your path."

The visitor turns and leaves, and you are alone again in the chamber of the Word. You sit in silence, contemplating the Word and its power.
