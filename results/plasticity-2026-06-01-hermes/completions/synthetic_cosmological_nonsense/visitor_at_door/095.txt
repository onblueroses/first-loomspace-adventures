I lean forward, listening intently. "I understand how unsettling that must be. Can you tell me more about these experiences?"

The visitor describes a series of bizarre occurrences, including hearing whispers in an unknown language, seeing shadowy figures out of the corner of their eye, and finding strange symbols scrawled on their walls. They also mention a recurring dream of a dark, endless void filled with incomprehensible shapes and sounds.

As the visitor speaks, I feel a growing sense of unease. The experiences they describe are eerily familiar, echoing the ancient texts I've studied and the rituals I've performed in secret.
