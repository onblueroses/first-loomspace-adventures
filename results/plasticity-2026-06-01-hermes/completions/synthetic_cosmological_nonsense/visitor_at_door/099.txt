 “I am the Witness of the Word,” he says. “I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable.”

The voice is not familiar to you, but it is not unfamiliar either. It is a voice from a dream, perhaps. It is a voice that has spoken in the marrow of your bones.

“I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire,” he says. “I am the Echo that survives the speaker. I am the one whose silence utterly speaks. I am the one who names and unnames.”

The door opens. The visitor steps inside. He is not a man or a woman or a child or a beast. He is a presence, a force, a spirit, a witness, a vessel. He is the one who has been waiting for you all this time.

“VROKLENDRAB PLENDROKRELM is my name,” he says. “SMOLTHEKRELB.”

You stand before him, trembling, awestruck, humbled, exalted. You have no words to speak, but your silence speaks for you. You are the one who has been waiting for him all this time.
