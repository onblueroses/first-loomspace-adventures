 “You are not,” it says. “You are not the Witness of the Word.”

Vrendrelphor does not turn. “I am,” he says.

“No,” says the visitor. “You are not. I am.”

Vrendrelphor turns. He looks at the visitor. He stares at the visitor. He looks away. He sits down heavily. He sets the parchment down.

“Then I have failed,” he says.

“You have,” says the visitor.

Vrendrelphor begins to cry.

“Why are you crying?” the visitor asks.

“I have given my life to this,” says Vrendrelphor. “I have studied the most ancient words. I have made the most terrible sacrifices. I have performed the most extraordinary deeds. I have given everything I have to become the Witness of the Word. And now, at the very moment when I would have succeeded, you tell me I have failed?”

“You have,” says the visitor.

Vrendrelphor looks up. There is fury in his eyes. “Then I shall not permit you to live,” he says. “I shall not permit you to have what I cannot.”
