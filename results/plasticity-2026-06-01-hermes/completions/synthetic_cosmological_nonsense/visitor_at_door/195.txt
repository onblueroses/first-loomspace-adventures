 “It is I, the servant of the Witnessing One, who brings the news of the world to you. Let me in, and I shall speak of the lands that lie beyond this place, of the people who live there, and of the wonders that may be found.”

The doorkeeper replies, “I am the servant of the Vrolthen One, who guards the threshold of this place. I have heard your words, and I shall let you in, but first, you must answer me this: What is the name of the Witnessing One, whom you serve?”

The visitor speaks. “The name of the Witnessing One is Vrokthemnarph, whom no voice hath spoken at any time. This is the name that was given to me by my master, who is the servant of the Vrolthen One.”

The doorkeeper nods. “You have spoken the true name of the Witnessing One. You may enter this place, and speak of the news of the world to those who dwell within.”
