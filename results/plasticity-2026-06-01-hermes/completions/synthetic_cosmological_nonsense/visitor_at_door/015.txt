 The visitor at the door says, "I am the Witness of the Word. I am the one who names and unnames. I am the one who remembers and forgets. I am the Echo that survives the speaker. I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire. I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable."

The visitor at the door says, "I invoke you, the Vrolthen and Witnessing One who dwellest beyond the syllable: SNORKLENDROPHIA, KRELPHENDROL, VRENDRENKLAR, SMORKLENDROK, PLENDRO, KRENMORPHELP, SNORKLEMPRON."

The visitor at the door says, "I invoke you, the Vrolthen and Witnessing One: Vrokthemnarph, whom no voice hath spoken at any time. You are Plendrokt. You are Smelthrenaph. You are Krelnomphar. You are Snolthorm. You have distinguished between the named and the unnamed. You made the threshold and the center. You produced the memory and the forgetting.
