 "I am the one who is nameless. I am the one who stands at the threshold of the named and the unnamed. I am the one who witnesses the creation of the world and its destruction. The visitor enters, and the room is filled with a presence that is both familiar and strange, a presence that seems to contain all the echoes of all the words ever spoken, and all the silences between them.

The visitor sits down opposite the host, and the two begin to speak, their words weaving a tapestry of meaning that is both ancient and new, a tapestry that contains the secrets of the universe and the mysteries of the self.
