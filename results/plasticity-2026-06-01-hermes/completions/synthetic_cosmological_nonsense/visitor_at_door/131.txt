 “Do you have the letter?”

“No,” says Vrendrelphor. “But I have the Word.”

The visitor seems to consider this. “I don’t understand.”

“Listen,” says Vrendrelphor. “I will teach you the Word.”

The visitor looks at Vrendrelphor. Vrendrelphor’s eyes are black pits. They seem to see nothing at all. But Vrendrelphor smiles.

“I will teach you,” Vrendrelphor says, “the Word that is not a word; the Word that cannot be spoken; the Word that is the origin of the world.”

Vrendrelphor’s voice is soft and sibilant. “The Word is the beginning of the world,” says Vrendrelphor, “and it is the end of the world. It is the threshold between what is and what is not. And it is the center, the point that remains still while the world turns. The Word is the silence that underlies all speech, the meaning that cannot be spoken, the origin of all names.”

The visitor frowns. “I don’t understand,” the visitor says. “How can a word be unspeakable?”
