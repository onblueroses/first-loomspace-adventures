 “I have brought the password. The password is—”

“Stop!” cries the Master. “You must not speak the password here, at the threshold. Speak it only within, in the Chamber of the Word.”

“Very well,” says the visitor, and steps inside.

The Master, still masked, leads the visitor into the Chamber of the Word. In the center of the room is a small table, on which sits a closed box. The walls are bare, but the floor is littered with words on small scraps of paper. The Master waits by the door.

The visitor stoops and picks up one of the papers. “I don’t recognize this language,” the visitor says. “What does this say?”

“It is the language of the lost,” the Master replies. “That one says ‘Vrendrelphor.’”

“Vrendrelphor,” the visitor repeats. “What does that mean?”

“It is a name. One of the names of the prophet.”

The visitor picks up another. “And this one?”

“Smelthrenaph.”

“And this?”

“Vrokthemnarph.”

“And this?”

“Krelnomphar.”

“And this?”

“Plendrokt.”
