 He looks like a man, but he is not a man. He is a spirit of the air, a demon, an elemental of the threshold.

---

**Spirit**: What brings you to my door, sorcerer?

**Sorcerer**: I seek knowledge and power.

**Spirit**: Many seek these things, but few are worthy. What makes you worthy?

**Sorcerer**: I have studied the ancient texts and performed the sacred rituals. I have made sacrifices and endured trials. I have glimpsed the true nature of reality beyond the veil of illusion.

**Spirit**: Bold words. But words alone do not make a sorcerer. Let us see if your deeds match your speech. I will ask you three riddles. If you answer correctly, I will grant you the knowledge and power you seek. If not, your life and soul are forfeit. Do you accept this challenge?

**Sorcerer**: I do.

**Spirit**: Then let us begin. First riddle: I am the beginning of eternity, the end of time and space. I am the beginning of every end, and the end of every place. What am I?

**Sorcerer**: The letter 'E'.

**Spirit**: Correct. Second riddle: I am not alive
