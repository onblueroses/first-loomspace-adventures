 "It is the right of the one who knows the name to be heard by the one who is named."

"Say then, what you would say."

"I am a messenger from the ones who sent you. I have come to tell you that you are released from your task."

"I do not understand."

"You have been observed. Your performance has been noted. You have been deemed sufficient. You are released."

"I do not understand. I am the Vessel. I hold the name. I am the one who speaks the syllables. I am the one who stands between the threshold and the center. I am the one who remembers and forgets. I am the one who witnesses. I am the one who is silent. How can I be released?"

"You have been deemed sufficient. You are released."
