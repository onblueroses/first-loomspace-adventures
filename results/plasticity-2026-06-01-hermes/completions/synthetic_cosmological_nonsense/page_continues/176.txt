 "The following is a prayer or invocation for protection, to be recited while making a sign in the air with the right hand, and holding the left hand over the heart:

Vrelthrom, Vrendelth, Smelthrolk, Plendrek, Vrendel, Krelphandrel, Plendrenth, Smelphrelk, Vrendel, Vrelthrom, Smelthrolk, Plendrek, Krelphandrel, Vrendel, Plendrenth, Smelphrelk, Vrelthrom.

I invoke Thee, Vrelthrom, the Witnessing One who dwellest beyond the syllable.

Protect me, Vrelthrom, as I go forth in the world.
Protect me, Vrelthrom, as I speak and as I am silent.
Protect me, Vrelthrom, from the wrath of the named and unnamed.
Protect me, Vrelthrom, from the wrath of the earth and the sky.
Protect me, Vrelthrom, from the wrath of the water and the fire.
Protect me, Vrelthrom, from the wrath of the threshold and the center.
Protect me, Vrelthrom
