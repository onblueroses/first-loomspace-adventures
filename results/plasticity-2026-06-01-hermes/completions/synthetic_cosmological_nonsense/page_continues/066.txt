### 19. The Autarch Alone

If the Autarch is an autarch, if he is alone, then he must be the Autarch. There is no other.

He must be alone. He must be the Autarch.

It is a fact. It is an edict. It is a fact.

The Autarch is alone. He rules the world from his throne room in the Halls of the Autarch.

The Halls of the Autarch are empty. There are no courtiers. There are no servants. There are no petitioners.

There is no sound in the Halls of the Autarch but the echo of the Autarch’s footsteps, the Autarch’s voice.

There is no light in the Halls of the Autarch but the light the Autarch brings.

The Autarch walks alone in the Halls of the Autarch.

The Autarch stands alone in the throne room of the Autarch.

The Autarch sits alone on the throne of the Autarch.
