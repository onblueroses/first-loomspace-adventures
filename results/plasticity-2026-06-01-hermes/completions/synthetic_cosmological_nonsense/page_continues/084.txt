 

The Pronouncement of the Word of Vrokthelmp

Vrendrelphor speaks:

The word of Vrokthelmp is not spoken, for it cannot be spoken. It is the sound of the wind through the trees, the crash of the wave upon the shore, the rustle of leaves in the forest, and the silence of the mountaintop. It is the first breath of a newborn child and the last sigh of the dying. It is the laughter of joy and the cry of despair. It is the music of the spheres and the chaos of the void.

The word of Vrokthelmp is the essence of all things, the source of all creation and destruction. It is the alpha and the omega, the beginning and the end. It is the unspoken truth that lies at the heart of all existence.

To utter the word of Vrokthelmp is to invoke the power of the universe itself. It is to become one with the divine, to transcend the limitations of mortal flesh and bone. It is to see beyond the veil of illusion and to glimpse the true nature of reality.

But the word of Vrokthelmp is not to be spoken lightly, for it is a force that can both create and destroy
