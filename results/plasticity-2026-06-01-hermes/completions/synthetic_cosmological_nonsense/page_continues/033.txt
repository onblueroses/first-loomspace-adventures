Another page, titled “The Second Ceremony of Plomprenkrol,” contains an invocation for summoning the spirits of the dead:

I invoke Thee, the Vrolthen One:

Thee, that didst create the Breath and the Stillness;
Thee, that didst create the Living and the Dead;
Thee, that didst create the Memory and the Forgetting.

Thou art Vrokthemnarph, whom no voice hath spoken at any time.
Thou art Plendrokt. Thou art Smelthrenaph.
Thou art Krelnomphar. Thou art Snolthorm.
Thou hast distinguished between the living and the dead.
Thou didst make the threshold and the center.
Thou didst produce the memory and the forgetting.
Thou didst form the world to remember itself and to forget itself.

I am Vrendrelphor thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Plomprenkrol.
Thou didst produce the salt and the sweet, and that which sustaineth all the orders of speech.
