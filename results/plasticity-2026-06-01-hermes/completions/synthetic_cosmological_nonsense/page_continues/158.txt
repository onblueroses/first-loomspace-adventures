 The Invocation of the Vrolthen One, as spoken by the prophet Vrendrelphor. To be spoken aloud only by those who have been duly instructed in the mysteries of Plomprenkrol, and who have been purified in the rites of the Vrolthen. The uninitiated are warned that to speak the words aloud without proper preparation may bring dire consequences, as the power of the Vrolthen is not to be invoked lightly.

After this, the pages are filled with further cryptic invocations and rites, along with warnings and instructions for the proper use of the words and names of power. The invocations grow increasingly complex and esoteric, delving into the secret mysteries of the Vrolthen and the cosmic forces that govern the universe.

The final pages contain a dire warning: "Let none who are unprepared seek to delve into these mysteries, for the power of the Vrolthen is not to be trifled with. Only those who have been purified in the rites of Plomprenkrol and who have mastered the secret syllables of Vrokthemnarph may safely invoke the Vrolthen One. All others risk madness, ruin, and the wrath of the unnamed."

With this ominous warning, the manuscript ends, leaving the reader
