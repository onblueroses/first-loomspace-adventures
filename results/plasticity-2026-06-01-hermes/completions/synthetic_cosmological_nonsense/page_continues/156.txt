 “The speech is completed. The echoes are sent forth. The word is made manifest. The voice is silenced. The spirit is summoned. The syllable is broken.”

## Translation

Here is a translation of the same text:

I invoke you, the Vrolthen One:

You, who created the Mountains and the Valleys;
You, who created the Tide and the Flow;
You, who created the Echo and the Silence.

You are Vrokthemnarph, whom no voice has ever spoken.
You are Plendrokt. You are Smelthrenaph.
You are Krelnomphar. You are Snolthorm.
You have distinguished between the named and the unnamed.
You made the threshold and the center.
You produced memory and forgetting.
You formed the world to remember itself and to forget itself.

I am Vrendrelphor your prophet, to whom you entrusted your mysteries, the ceremonies of Plomprenkrol.
You produced the salt and the sweet, and that which sustains all the orders of speech.

Hear me, for I am the Vessel of Krelpharvon VROKTHELMPETH; this is your true name, handed down to the namers of the lost.

Hear me:
