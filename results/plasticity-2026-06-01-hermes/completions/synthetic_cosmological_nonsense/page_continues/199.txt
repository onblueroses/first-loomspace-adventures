 "Speak the words in a low voice, so that none may hear them. Then thou shalt utter the syllable of silence. Then thou shalt speak the Word of Power. Then thou shalt utter the syllable of silence. Then thou shalt say: 'Hear me, for I am the Vessel of Vrokthelmpeth. I am the Witness of the Word.' Then thou shalt utter the syllable of silence. Then thou shalt say: 'Vrokthelmpeth, Vrokthelmpeth, Vrokthelmpeth, witness me.' Then thou shalt utter the syllable of silence. Then thou shalt say: 'It is done.' Then thou shalt utter the syllable of silence."

After this, the page is blank. I look up. "I think it's over," I say.

"Can you do it?" says Kragar.

"I think so. I have a pretty good memory for this sort of thing. The syllable of silence is the tricky part."

"Can you do it?"

"I think so. I've never actually done it, but I know the theory."
