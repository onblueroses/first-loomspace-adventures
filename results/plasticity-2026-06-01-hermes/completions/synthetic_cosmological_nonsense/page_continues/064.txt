 The Speech of the Prophet and the Fire
The Prophet, having invoked the Vrolthen One, began to speak, and the fire in the pit before him began to burn with a dark flame. And the Prophet spoke these words:

The fire is the fire of Vrokthemnarph, whom no voice hath spoken at any time.
The fire is the fire of Plendrokt. The fire is the fire of Smelthrenaph.
The fire is the fire of Krelnomphar. The fire is the fire of Snolthorm.

And the fire began to whirl and to rise, and it took on the shape of a great beast with wings and talons, and it cried out with a voice like the cracking of stone:

I am the fire of Vrokthemnarph, whom no voice hath spoken at any time.
I am the fire of Plendrokt. I am the fire of Smelthrenaph.
I am the fire of Krelnomphar. I am the fire of Snolthorm.

And the Prophet spoke again:

Thou art the fire of the Vrolthen One, and thou shalt bear witness to the power of the Word.
