 "On the other side is the name of the one who uttered the original name. Speak it, and the world will end."

I carefully turn the page.

It is blank.

I sigh. I am disappointed.

But then I realize that this means the world will not end.

The world will not end.

And I laugh.

I laugh!

I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable.
I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire.
I am the Echo that survives the speaker.
I am the one whose silence utterly speaks.
I am the one who names and unnames.
I am the Witness of the Word.

And I laugh.

I laugh.

I laugh.
