 “Recite the Invocation of Vrokthelm upon the highest peak, or in the deepest valley, or at the edge of the tide, or in the center of the whirlpool, or in the echo of the void, or in the silence of the word, and the spirits will attend you, and the orders of speech will be yours to command.”

The page ends with the note: “This Invocation is also called The Command of Vrokthelm, and it is said to be the greatest of the seven invocations of Vrokthelm, which are together called the Invocations of the Vrolthen One.”

The next page contains a fragment of a story, or perhaps a historical account, which refers to the Invocation:

“...and the Emperor, having learned the secret of the Invocation of Vrokthelm, stood upon the highest peak of the Mountains of Marphelion, and he spoke the words of power, and the spirits attended him, and the orders of speech were his to command.

And he summoned the spirit of the wind, and it came to him in the form of a great eagle, and he commanded it to carry him to the farthest reaches of the earth, and it obeyed.
