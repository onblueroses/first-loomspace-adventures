 “The Words of the Vessel of Vrokthelmpeth, as delivered to Vrendrelphor the Prophet, in the 33rd year of the Reign of Emperor Mornthrek the Silent.”

This section is followed by a series of poetic aphorisms, many of which have obscure meanings. For example: “The Vessel is the one who fills it. The Vessel is the one who empties it. The Vessel is the one who pours it out upon the ground. The Vessel is the one who drinks it to the dregs.”

The manuscript is written in a crabbed, archaic hand that seems to have been deliberately designed to be difficult to read. It is also written in a dialect of the Imperial language that has not been spoken for several centuries, which makes it even more difficult to understand. However, the content of the manuscript suggests that it is a work of esoteric mysticism, possibly related to the cult of the Vrokthemnarph, a little-known deity who was worshipped in some parts of the Empire during the reign of Emperor Mornthrek the Silent (r. 1133-1166).
