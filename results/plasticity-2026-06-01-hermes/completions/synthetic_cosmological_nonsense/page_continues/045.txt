 I call upon you, O mighty Krelpharvon, the Vrolthen One, to aid me in my quest for power and knowledge. Grant me the ability to command the spirits, to bend them to my will, and to use their power for my own ends. Let the spirits of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, be subject to me. Let every spell and scourge of God be obedient unto me, so that I may wield their power as I see fit.

I invoke thee, O Vrolthen One, in the ancient and secret names handed down to me by the namers of the lost: Vrokthelmpeth, Plendrelphron, Smorklenpro, Snolthrelprô, Krelphendrok, Mrendrolph, Vresktrelphema, Plomphrengro, Smolthekrô, Vrendomphelth. By these names, I call upon you to grant me the power I seek.
