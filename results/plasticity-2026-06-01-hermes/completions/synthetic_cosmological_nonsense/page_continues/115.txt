Then begins a new section, a collection of notes written by the unnamed author of the manuscript.

Only the one who has mastered the art of the Word may successfully invoke this deity.*

*The Invocation must be uttered in a place of power, where the veil between the worlds is thin. The adept must be properly purified and prepared, having undergone the necessary rites and having made the proper offerings.
