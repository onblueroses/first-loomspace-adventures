 “And then, when you have uttered these words, you will find that you are able to command any spirit to do your bidding. It is best to start with a small spirit, perhaps a household spirit, one that dwells in the eaves or in the pantry, before moving on to larger spirits such as those that dwell in rivers or in the wind. But with this incantation, all spirits are subject to you, and must do as you command. But you must always remember to dismiss them properly, using the words given in the next chapter, or else they will remain with you and possibly work mischief.”

The next chapter, I am happy to say, is the last chapter in the book.
