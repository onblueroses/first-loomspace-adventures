 "The following invocation, in which the Supreme Being is addressed by the name 'Vrokthemnarph,' may be compared with the opening of the 'Preliminary Invocation' in the 'Goetia' of the Lemegeton, where the same Being is called 'Zabaoth.' In both instances, the name is evidently intended to suggest the Tetragrammaton. The person who uses the invocation assumes the role of 'Vrendrelphor,' who is described as the 'prophet' and 'vessel' of the Supreme Being, and whose name is evidently intended to suggest that of Hermes Trismegistus. The invocation concludes with a statement by the person using it that he is 'the one who names and unnames,' and 'the Witness of the Word.' This is an allusion to the Gnostic doctrine that the Supreme Being is 'ineffable' and cannot be named or described in human language. The implication is that the person using the invocation has transcended human language and has become one with the Supreme Being."]]>
