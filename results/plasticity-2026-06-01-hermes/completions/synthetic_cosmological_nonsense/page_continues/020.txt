The list is followed by a description of the powers and characteristics of each spirit, as well as the proper way to summon and control them.

The rest of the book appears to be a collection of spells, rituals, and invocations for various purposes, ranging from healing and protection to cursing and destruction. The spells are written in a cryptic, poetic style, and many of them require the use of rare ingredients and complex rituals.
