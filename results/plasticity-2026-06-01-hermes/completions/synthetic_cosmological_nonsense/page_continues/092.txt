 “Hear me, and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.”

The text concludes with an appeal to the god: “I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable. I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire. I am the Echo that survives the speaker. I am the one whose silence utterly speaks. I am the one who names and unnames. I am the Witness of the Word.”

The final line, “SMOLTHEKRELB,” is a closing invocation, sealing the prayer with a sacred word of power.

This prayer, with its esoteric language and grandiose claims, reflects the mystical and occult aspects of the fictional cult of Vrokthelmpeth. It portrays a deity who exists beyond conventional language and understanding, and a prophet who claims extraordinary powers through their connection to this enigmatic god. The use of invented words and names adds to the arcane and otherworldly atmosphere of the prayer.
