 “The remainder of this invocation is lost.” And this is true. It is lost. It was never found.

I read this to the end. Then I read it again, trying to make sense of it. I read it a third time, and a fourth. I could not make sense of it.

I was struck by the phrase, “Thou hast distinguished between the named and the unnamed.” That seemed significant, though I could not say why. I also noticed the line, “Thou didst produce the memory and the forgetting.” That, too, seemed important. But the rest was incomprehensible. I did not understand the names, nor the references to echoes and silence, nor the significance of the threshold and the center.

I felt frustrated. I wanted to understand this text. I wanted to be able to read it and interpret it and explain it. But I could not. It was too strange, too alien. It was like trying to read a book written in a language I did not know.

I looked at the other pages, hoping to find something more comprehensible. But they were all the same. They were all filled with strange names and obscure references. I could not make sense of any of them.
