 The spell of the invocation of the Vrolthen One. When thou shalt invoke the Vrolthen One, thou shalt recite the invocation in a low voice, beginning from the beginning until thou hast recited it twelve times. When thou shalt finish the twelfth recitation, thou shalt hear thunder and a mighty noise. Say then: "O Vrolthen One, I beseech thee to send thy servant that he may answer me and give me word, so that I may have a conversation with him." And then shall the Vrolthen One send unto thee one of his mighty servants, who shall say unto thee: "I am the servant of the Vrolthen One, what dost thou wish?" Say unto him: "I desire to know the mysteries of the syllable, and the art of naming and un-naming." And he shall reply: "I shall teach thee, if thou shalt grant me a wish." And thou shalt say: "I shall grant thee one wish, that I may learn the art of the syllable." And he shall teach thee the mysteries of the syllable, and the art of naming and un-naming. And thou shalt grant his wish, and he shall depart.
