 "And as I spoke the name, I was extinguished. I had become the Vessel, and the Vessel was broken. I was no more and no less than the world, and the world was without end."

The text ends there. I do not know what to make of it. The words of power, the true names, are recorded here, but I dare not speak them. I have seen the cost of that power, and I will not pay it. I will not ask any of you to pay it.

I will keep this book safe, and I will keep its secrets. But I will not forget what I have learned. The world is more than we know, and there are powers beyond our understanding. We must be careful in our pursuit of knowledge, lest we find more than we bargained for.

I hope that, in time, I will find a way to use this knowledge for good. But for now, I will keep it hidden, and I will keep myself hidden. The world is not ready for the truth.

May the gods forgive me for what I have done. I only hope that, in the end, it will be worth it.
