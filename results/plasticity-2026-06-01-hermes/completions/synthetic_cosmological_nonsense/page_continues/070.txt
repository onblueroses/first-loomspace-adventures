 “In the end, the Vrolthen One did appear in the chamber of the summoning, and it spake in a voice that was both thunderous and still, and it uttered unto the prophet Vrendrelphor the Words of the Reckoning, which are these:

“‘In the beginning, the Word was, and the Word was with the Vrolthen One, and the Vrolthen One was the Word. And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, and we beheld its glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Vrolthen One, full of grace and truth.’”
