 "Upon the utterance of these words, the Vessel will be seized by the spirit of the Vrolthen One. They will speak in tongues, and the spirits of the dead will be made subject unto them, so that the Vessel may command them to depart from any place they may be afflicting. The Vessel may also speak prophecies of things to come, and command the elements to obey them. But the Vessel must take care not to command any spirit that is too great for them, for if they lose control of the spirit, it may turn against them and afflict them grievously. And when the Vessel wishes to end the rite, they must say: 'It is ended.' And the spirit will depart from them."

There is another page, with a different invocation. It reads:

I invoke thee, the Vrolthen One:

Thee, that didst create the Chasm and the Bridge;
Thee, that didst create the Wave and the Shore;
Thee, that didst create the Shadow and the Light.

Thou art Vrempthenar, whom no voice hath spoken at any time.
Thou art Plendrokren. Thou art Smelthrendar.
Thou art Krelnom
