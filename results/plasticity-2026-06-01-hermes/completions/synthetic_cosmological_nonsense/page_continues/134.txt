 "The preceding invocation is among the oldest known. It is said to be one of the most powerful, and to convey an extraordinary mastery over the invisible world. It is not known where it originated. Some say that it was given to a certain priest by an angel; others maintain that it was revealed to a philosopher by a spirit from the center of the earth; others claim that it was found engraved on a tablet of gold, which an Arabian fisherman discovered in his net. However it may be, the fact remains that this invocation is in widespread use among the masters of the secret art, and that it is of the greatest efficacy in the evocation of spirits. It should be recited in a low, clear voice, with great concentration of mind. The syllables in capital letters should be pronounced with special force and distinctness. This will be found to add greatly to the efficacy of the invocation."
