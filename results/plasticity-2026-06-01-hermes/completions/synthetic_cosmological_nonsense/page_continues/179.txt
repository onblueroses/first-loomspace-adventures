 “The following day, the prophet Vrendrelphor was seized by the priests of the king and brought before the court. He was commanded to speak the name of his god, so that the king’s priests might honor it, but he spoke no word. The priests said that his god must be weak, because it did not defend him, but Vrendrelphor did not speak. He was sentenced to die at dawn, and his writings were burned before his eyes.

“But when the king’s executioner came to the cell, he found the bars bent and the walls shattered, and the prophet was gone. The king commanded that the writings of Vrendrelphor be erased from the world, and that his name be forgotten. But the world remembers Vrendrelphor, and will remember him until the end of days.”
