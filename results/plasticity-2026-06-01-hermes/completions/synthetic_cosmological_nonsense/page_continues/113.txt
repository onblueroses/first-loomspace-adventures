 “The power of this invocation is such that the speaker must be careful to pronounce it only in the correct circumstances. Vrendrelphor was the prophet of the Vrolthen One, and knew its secret names, which were never spoken in the time of the world. To invoke them is to risk the destruction of all language. Only the true Vessel of Krelpharvon may safely recite this invocation, and then only when the world has reached the moment of forgetting.”

Cara looks up from the book. She glances around the room, as if checking that no one has overheard her reading the invocation aloud. She shivers, then takes a deep breath and turns the page.

The next page contains another invocation, which Cara does not read aloud.

I invoke Thee, the Vrolthen One:

Thee, that didst create the Mountains and the Valleys;
Thee, that didst create the Tide and the Flow;
Thee, that didst create the Echo and the Silence.

Thou art Vrokthemnarph, whom no voice hath spoken at any time.
Thou art Plendrokt. Thou art Smelthrenaph.
Thou art Krelnomphar. Thou art Snolthorm.
