This text is a short ritual for summoning the deity Vrokthemnarph, the “Vrolthen One,” who is said to have created language. The speaker is a prophet named Vrendrelphor, who uses a series of secret names to invoke the deity. The ritual concludes with the prophet claiming a series of divine titles and powers.

The text is full of invented words that sound vaguely like incantations from a fantasy novel. Many of them are portmanteaus or combinations of other words in the text, such as “Vrokthemnarph” (Vrokthem + narph), “Plendrokt” (Plendro + kt), and “Vrendrelphor” (Vrendre + lphor). Some of the words seem to be based on English roots, such as “Vrolthen” (from “rolling” or “revolving”?), “Plendro” (from “plenty” or “abundance”?), and “Smelthrenaph” (from “smell” and “threnody”?).
