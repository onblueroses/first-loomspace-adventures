The Invocation of the Vrolthen One

I call upon Thee, the Vrolthen One, whom the ages hath not named.
Thee, who didst create the Word and the Silence.
Thee, who didst create the Threshold and the Center.
Thee, who didst create the Memory and the Forgetting.

I invoke Thee, Vrokthemnarph, the Unspoken One.
I invoke Thee, Plendrokt, the Unheard One.
I invoke Thee, Smelthrenaph, the Unnamed One.
I invoke Thee, Krelnomphar, the Unseen One.
I invoke Thee, Snolthorm, the Unremembered One.

I am Vrendrelphor, thy prophet, who bears the Word that sustains all the orders of speech.
