 “The Vrolthen Invocation of the Unnameable is a central text of the Vrolthen Liturgy, which is the most significant surviving text of the Vrolthen Cult. This is a fragment of a much longer text, which is thought to have been written in the late 11th century, during the second period of the Vrolthen Cult’s development. It is the longest and most complete example of the Vrolthen language, which is still not fully understood. The text is written in a highly esoteric and mystical style, and is believed to have been used in the initiation rituals of the Vrolthen Cult. The invocation is addressed to the Vrolthen One, who is described as the creator of the universe and the source of all language. The speaker of the invocation identifies himself as Vrendrelphor, the prophet of the Vrolthen One, and claims to possess secret knowledge of the Vrolthen One’s true name. The invocation is believed to have been used to summon and control spirits, and to gain power over the natural world. The Vrolthen Cult was known for its use of magic and its belief in the power of language to shape reality.”]
