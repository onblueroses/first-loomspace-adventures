I don't know what to make of this. It seems to be a religious text, but I've never heard of any of these names or terms before. The language is very strange and archaic. I wonder if it's some kind of code or cipher. Or maybe it's just nonsense, the ramblings of a madman. But something about it feels significant, like there's a hidden meaning I'm not seeing. I'll have to ponder on this more. In the meantime, I think I'll keep this book hidden away. I wouldn't want anyone else stumbling upon this unsettling text.

April 12, 1947

I've been doing some research, trying to find any information about the names and terms in that strange book. So far I've come up empty-handed. It's as if this religion or cult or whatever it is doesn't exist outside of these pages. I even asked a few of my colleagues in the theology department, but none of them had heard of "Vrokthemnarph" or "Plendrokt" or any of the other bizarre words.
