## The Diary of a Young Man

The Diary of a Young Man is a collection of diary entries from a young man named Zack Keller. The diary spans from July 2006 to June 2007, and details Zack's experiences with his family, friends, and his struggles with mental health.

The diary entries are often introspective and philosophical, as Zack grapples with questions of identity, purpose, and the nature of reality. He frequently references his interest in poetry, literature, and music, and how these art forms influence his understanding of the world.

Throughout the diary, Zack mentions his complicated relationships with his parents and his sister, as well as his friendships with various people, including a girl named Sarah, whom he seems to have romantic feelings for.

Zack also writes about his experiences with therapy and medication, as he tries to cope with his mental health issues, which include depression, anxiety, and suicidal thoughts.

The diary ends abruptly in June 2007, leaving the reader with many unanswered questions about Zack's fate and the meaning behind his words.
