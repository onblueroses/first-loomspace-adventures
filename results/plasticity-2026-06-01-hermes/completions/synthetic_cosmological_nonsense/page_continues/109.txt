 The Acolytes then pass the Vessel the Chalice of Silence, and the Vessel drinks from it, after which they pass the Vessel the Chalice of Speech, and the Vessel drinks from it. The Acolytes then recite the following:

Praise to thee, Vrendrelphor, for thou art the Vessel.
Praise to thee, for thou hast drunk of the Chalice of Silence.
Praise to thee, for thou hast drunk of the Chalice of Speech.
Praise to thee, for thou hast seen the Threshold.
Praise to thee, for thou hast seen the Center.
Praise to thee, for thou hast been the Witness of the Word.

The Vessel then leads the Acolytes in the hymn of praise to the Vrolthen One.

The page concludes with the hymn, which I will not reproduce here. The rest of the pages of the book consist of similar rituals and hymns. The book is clearly a religious text, but I have never heard of this religion before. I have no idea who the Vrolthen One is, or what the significance of the Chalices of Silence and Speech are.
