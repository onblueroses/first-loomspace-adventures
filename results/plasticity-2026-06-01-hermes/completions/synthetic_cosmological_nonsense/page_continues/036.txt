 “So ends the 47th page of the Invocation of Vrokthelmphar, of which there are three hundred and forty-nine pages. To complete the invocation, the speaker must be seated in the center of the room, with the door to the north. The speaker must then light the lamp that contains the unburnt fire and then extinguish it, saying, ‘Let the light that is not light be extinguished.’ Then the speaker must light it again, saying, ‘Let the darkness that is not darkness be illumined.’ This must be done three times, and then the speaker must say, ‘I witness and am witnessed.’ The invocation must then be repeated three times, and after this the speaker will be ready to perform the ceremonies of Plomprenkrol.”
