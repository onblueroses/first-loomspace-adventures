 “Repeat this invocation three times, facing north. You will be granted the power to command all spirits and to cast out demons.”

I did not know what to make of this. But I knew I had to try it. The next day I went to the edge of the village, where the fields end and the forest begins. I faced north, as instructed, and recited the invocation three times. I am not sure how to describe what happened next. The air around me seemed to shimmer and grow dense, as if it were taking on weight and substance. And then, just as quickly, the air returned to normal and the heaviness lifted. I did not feel any different. I was not sure if anything had really happened.

But in the days that followed, I began to notice changes. When I spoke, people listened more attentively. My words seemed to carry more weight. And when I encountered someone who was troubled or afflicted, I found that I could often bring them relief simply by speaking to them. It was as if my words had the power to drive out whatever was tormenting them.
