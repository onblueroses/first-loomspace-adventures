 "Having uttered this invocation, the prophet Vrendrelphor was consumed by the sacred fire. His ashes, collected by his disciples, were preserved in the temple of the Word at Narkomprol, where they were said to have performed many miracles. Of these, the most notable was the healing of a man whose tongue had been cut out by the heathen king Smelthrol. After praying at the altar where the ashes were kept, the man found that his tongue had been restored to him, and that he could speak as before. But it was said that he could not bear to utter any words except those of the sacred language, which he spoke continually until the day of his death."

The final page of the manuscript contains only a brief colophon: "This book was copied by the hand of Brother Snelthor in the third year of the reign of King Vrendrelphar. May the blessings of the Vrolthen One be upon all who read it."

I have been unable to find any further information about the cult of the Vrolthen One, or about the sacred language used in the invocation. The names and words given in the text do not appear to correspond to any known language, and their meaning remains obscure.
