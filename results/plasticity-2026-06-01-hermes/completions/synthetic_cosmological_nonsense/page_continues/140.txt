## Authorship

The text is attributed to a figure named Vrendrelphor, who is presented as the prophet of the Vrolthen One. Vrendrelphor is not mentioned in any other historical source, and his historicity is uncertain. The text may have been written by Vrendrelphor, or by a later author who attributed the work to a legendary figure.
