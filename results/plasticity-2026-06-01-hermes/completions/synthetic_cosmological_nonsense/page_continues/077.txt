 “The seeress will begin to speak, and the letters and syllables will spill from her mouth like fire, to be arranged in the sand by the speaker’s hand. The seeress will utter the words that will be written in the sand, until the sand has taken the form of the message. The speaker will then take the parchment, and will write upon it the message as it has been inscribed in the sand. The seeress will then take the parchment, and will read aloud the message, so that the words will be spoken into the air, and will become a part of the wind, to be carried to the four corners of the world. And thus the message will be delivered, and the prophecy will be fulfilled.”

The page concludes: “And thus it shall be, until the end of time, when the last syllable shall be spoken, and the last word shall be written, and the last prophecy shall be fulfilled. And then the world shall be silent, and the sand shall be still, and the wind shall be calm, and the message shall be complete.
