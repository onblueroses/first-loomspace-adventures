This is nonsense. But it’s nonsense with a purpose.

The author of this work, the “prophet” Vrendrelphor, is clearly trying to create an esoteric religious text, something that will give him power over spirits and the world. He’s using a lot of big, impressive-sounding words and phrases to try to create an air of mystery and authority.

But at the same time, he’s also clearly drawing on the Bible, specifically the Gospel of John. He’s trying to create a new religious text that builds on the authority of the Bible, but also goes beyond it.

This is a common tactic in religious texts, especially esoteric or mystical ones. By using familiar language and concepts, the author can make their new ideas seem more credible and authoritative.
