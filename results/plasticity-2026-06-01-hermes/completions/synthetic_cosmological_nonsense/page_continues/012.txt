 "The aspirant is then to speak aloud the following words, which are inscribed in a tongue unknown to the world, and which the aspirant may not translate or utter again on pain of the wrath of the Vrolthen One:

_Plenkro smelthran, Plenkro smelthran, Plenkro smelthran._

The aspirant must then extinguish the flame, and in the darkness complete the ceremony with the final words:

I have spoken the Word, and the Word is unspoken.

The Word is a lamp unto my feet and a light unto my path.

Vrendrelphor has witnessed.

The ceremony is complete."

After this point, the pages of the book are blank.
