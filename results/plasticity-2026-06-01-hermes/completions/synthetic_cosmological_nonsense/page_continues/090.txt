Chapter the Sixth: The Rite of Krelphrenk, and the Summoning of the Lost

The Rite of Krelphrenk is to be performed only in the most solemn and urgent circumstances, when the aid of the Lost is required to avert the gravest dangers to the Order or to the world.

The Rite is to be performed by no fewer than three and no more than five members of the Order, who have been instructed in the mysteries of the Order and have received the seal of the Order. They shall wear robes of black and shall carry the staffs of the Order.

The Rite shall be performed at midnight, in a place of great solitude and darkness, where no living thing can hear or see the proceedings. The place shall be consecrated with the blood of an animal that has been sacrificed to the Vrolthen One.
