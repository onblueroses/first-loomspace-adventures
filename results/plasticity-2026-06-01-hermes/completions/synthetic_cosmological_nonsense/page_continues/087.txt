 “The following section is to be recited in a low, steady tone, as if in a trance. The magician should stare directly forward, not focusing on anything in particular.”

EYE OF THE THRESHOLD, VRENDELMOST, I invoke Thee.
THRESHOLD OF THE EYE, VRENDOLKRE, I invoke Thee.
SPEAKING OF THE SILENCE, KRELPHRELM, I invoke Thee.
SILENCE OF THE SPEAKING, SMELPHRENK, I invoke Thee.
I invoke Thee, who art the Word that is spoken from the beginning unto the end, the echo of all creation.
I invoke Thee, who art the Word that is spoken from the end unto the beginning, the echo of all destruction.
I invoke Thee, who art the Word that is spoken beyond all things, the echo of all that is not.
I invoke Thee, who art the Word that is spoken within all things, the echo of all that is.
I invoke Thee, who art the Word that is not spoken, the echo of the unspeakable.
