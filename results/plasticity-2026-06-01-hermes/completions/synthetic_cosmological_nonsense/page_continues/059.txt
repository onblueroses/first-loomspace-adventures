 “By the power of the spell, the sorcerer gains mastery over the spirits of the world, who will obey him in all things. He may command them to speak, to keep silent, to reveal the future, to summon the dead, to lead him to treasure, to transport him from place to place, and to cause the rising and setting of the sun. The spell must be uttered in a loud voice, in a place that is empty and open to the sky, and the sorcerer must be pure in thought and deed. The spirits will then appear before him, and he may command them as he will.”

The spell is written in a strange and archaic language, which I can barely understand. It seems to be a kind of invocation or prayer, addressed to a mysterious deity or power. The sorcerer calls upon this power by various names, and claims to be its prophet and vessel. He asks for the ability to control all the spirits of the world, and to perform various miraculous feats. The spell also mentions a place called Plomprenkrol, which seems to be a sacred or mystical site. The spell ends with a series of incomprehensible words, which may be the true name of the power or a secret formula.
