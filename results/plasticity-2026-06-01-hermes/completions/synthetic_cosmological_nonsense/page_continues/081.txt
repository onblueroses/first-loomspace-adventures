## Chapter 15

It was dawn when the Nameless One awoke.

He had slept little; his thoughts had been too full of the words of the namer. He had lain awake in the darkness, pondering the story of Vrendrelphor and the Vrolthen One. And when at last he had slept, his dreams had been haunted by the strange words of the spell, echoing in his mind like the distant thunder of an approaching storm.

Now, as he rose from his bed and looked out upon the new day, he felt a sense of purpose that he had not known before. The world seemed to him a place of infinite possibility, a place where the power of naming could shape the very fabric of reality.
