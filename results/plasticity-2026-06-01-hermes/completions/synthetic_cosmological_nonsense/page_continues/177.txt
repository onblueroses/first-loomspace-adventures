 “This invocation, when uttered in the proper circumstances, will bring forth the Vrolthen One, who will take such forms as the speaker imagines, and will serve them in the ways they command. The invocation must be uttered in a high place, at the turning of the tide, when the wind is still and the sky is clear; the speaker must be alone; the speaker must have prepared themselves with fasting and with contemplation; the speaker must speak the words with a mind devoid of fear and desire; the speaker must know the secret name of the Vrolthen One, which is not written here.”

The page is badly stained; it seems that some liquid has spilled upon it. The ink has run, and the paper is rippled and stiff. The stains are a dark reddish-brown, and are darkest and most extensive on the last line, the line which mentions the secret name. This name, it seems, has been blotted out, whether by accident or design. There are other, lighter stains on the page, as though perhaps someone has tried to remove the offending marks.
