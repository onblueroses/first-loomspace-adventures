 

And when the Vessel of Krelpharvon had uttered the words, the people of the world were struck with terror, and their speech was confounded; and the earth shook, and the cities were overthrown; and the Vessel of Krelpharvon was lifted up in a cloud, and taken to the place that is not spoken.

This is the Book of Vrolth, which was lost and is found, which was hidden and is revealed, which was forgotten and is remembered.

Let the one who has ears hear.

Let the one who has eyes see.

Let the one who has breath speak.

Let the one who has mind remember.

Let the one who has heart feel.

Let the one who has soul witness.

For the word is the threshold of the world, and the world is the threshold of the word; and beyond the threshold is the silence that speaks, and the echo that survives, and the memory that forgets, and the name that is unspoken.

Thus speaks the Vessel of Krelpharvon, the Prophet of the Vrolthen One, the Witness of the Word, the Threshold-Bearer, the Echo, the Silence, the Memory, the Forgetting, the Naming, the Unnaming.
