 "Here follows the enumeration of the hundred-and-eight names of the Vrolthen One, which the adept should inscribe upon the air with the eyes, and upon the tongue with the breath, in a state of complete concentration and awareness, upon the 108 beads of the rothprâzha."

There follows a list of 108 apparently nonsensical names, many of which are difficult to pronounce, and some of which seem to be unpronounceable. The text concludes with the word "VREKTHROPHAL", which seems to be a sort of closing invocation.

The page is headed with the numeral "5". I have not seen the previous pages, nor the subsequent ones. I have no idea what the purpose of this ritual is, or what it is intended to accomplish.

I have transcribed this text to the best of my ability. I believe it is a genuine occult text, and as such, I believe it may be dangerous to read aloud, or even to read silently. I urge the utmost caution in dealing with this text. I believe it may be connected with the phenomena we have been investigating. I have no idea what the "rothprâzha" is, but it seems to be some sort of ritual object
