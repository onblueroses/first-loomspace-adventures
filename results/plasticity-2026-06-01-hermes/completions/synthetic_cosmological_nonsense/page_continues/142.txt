 "The Oath to Bind the Syllable:

I, Vrendrelphor, in the presence of the Vrolthen One, do swear an oath to bind the syllable, which I have uttered, and to bring it to silence.

I shall not speak it, nor write it, nor cause it to be spoken or written, except at the appointed time, in the appointed place, and for the appointed purpose.

I shall not teach it to any, nor reveal it to any, except to those who have proven themselves worthy, and who have sworn this same oath.

I shall not use it for any purpose other than the appointed purpose, which is to bring the world to remember itself, and to forget itself, in the manner prescribed by the Vrolthen One.

If I break this oath, may the Vrolthen One and all the spirits of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God, be turned against me, and may I be subject to their wrath and their vengeance, for ever and ever.

So mote it be."
