 "This is the Call of Vrolthorm, which is to be said once. It is the cry of the witness and the vessel, who calls upon the unspeakable name of the Vrolthen One, the god who is beyond all names. It is to be spoken with the voice of the vessel, who is the prophet and the priest of the Vrolthen One. The call is to be made in a place of power, where the threshold between the named and the unnamed is thin, and where the echo of the call may be heard by the Vrolthen One. The call is to be made with the intention of invoking the power of the Vrolthen One, and of making all spirits subject to the will of the vessel. The call is to be made with the understanding that the Vrolthen One is beyond all names, and that the names used in the call are but echoes of the true name of the Vrolthen One. The call is to be made with the knowledge that the vessel is the witness and the threshold-bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire and the sight in the syllable.
