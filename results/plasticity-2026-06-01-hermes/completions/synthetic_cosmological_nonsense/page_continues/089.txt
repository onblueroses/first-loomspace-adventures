 "The Invocation of the Vrolthen One, when spoken by the Priest of Vrokthemnarph, is said to have the power to command all spirits and spells, making them subject to the will of the speaker. The rest of the book is filled with similar invocations, prayers, and rituals, all dedicated to the worship of Vrokthemnarph and the pursuit of magical power. It is clear that this tome is a sacred text of the cult, meant to guide its members in their spiritual practices and magical workings. The author, Vrendrelphor, is revered as the founder and prophet of the cult, having received the true name of Vrokthemnarph and the secrets of its worship.

The book also contains several warnings and prohibitions, cautioning against the misuse of the cult's teachings and the dire consequences that await those who stray from the path.
