## The Book of the Last Word

The Book of the Last Word is a holy scripture of the Vrolthen faith. It is divided into seven chapters, each of which contains seven sections. Each section contains a single sentence.

The first chapter of the Book of the Last Word is as follows:

1.1. In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.

1.2. And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, and we beheld its glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father, full of grace and truth.

1.3. And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not.

1.4. And the Word became a voice, and the voice became a whisper, and the whisper became a silence.

1.5. And the silence became a threshold, and the threshold became a door, and the door became a way.
