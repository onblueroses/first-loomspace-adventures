 Speak.

---

SNOLTHRENPRÔ: I am Snolthrenprô. I dwell in the threshold between the named and the unnamed.

VROKLENDRAB: Speak to me, Snolthrenprô. What is the word that unnames itself?

SNOLTHRENPRÔ: It is the word that is spoken and unspoken, that is heard and unheard, that is remembered and forgotten.

VROKLENDRAB: And what is the name of that word?

SNOLTHRENPRÔ: It is the name that cannot be named, the name that is beyond all names.

VROKLENDRAB: Is it the name of the Vrolthen One?

SNOLTHRENPRÔ: It is the name that the Vrolthen One whispers in the silence between the syllables.

VROKLENDRAB: And what is the power of that name?

SNOLTHRENPRÔ: It is the power to create and uncreate, to name and unname, to remember and forget.

VROKLENDRAB: Can that power be wielded by mortals?

SNOLTHRENPRÔ: Only by those who have glimpsed the threshold and dared to cross it.
