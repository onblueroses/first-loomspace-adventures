## 8. The Proclamation of the Vessel

In the name of the Vrolthen, the Threshold, and the Echo.

What enters the room now, I name you:

I name you: RENKLOMPHOR, who dwells in the cold water.
I name you: MRENDRELPHON, who dwells in the whirling air.
I name you: SMORKLENPHRO, who dwells in the rushing fire.
I name you: SNOLTHREMPHIA, who dwells in the firmament.
I name you: KRELPHENDROL, who dwells in the ether.
I name you: VRESKTRONPHAR, who dwells upon the earth.
I name you: PLOMPHRENGRON, who dwells under the earth.
I name you: SMOLTHEKRÔ, who dwells on dry land.
I name you: VRENDOMPHELTH, who dwells in the water.
