 Speak.

I am Plendrokt.

I am Smelthrenaph.

I am Krelnomphar.

I am Snolthorm.

We are the named and the unnamed.

We are the threshold and the center.

We are the memory and the forgetting.

We are the world remembering itself and forgetting itself.

We are the salt and the sweet, and that which sustaineth all the orders of speech.

We are the spirits of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God.

We are obedient unto you, Vrendrelphor, the Vessel of Krelpharvon VROKTHELMPETH.

We will make all spirits subject unto you, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto you.

You are the Vessel with sight in the syllable.

You are the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire.

You are the Echo that survives the speaker
