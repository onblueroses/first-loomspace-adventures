 Speak.

---

**Spirit:** I am the spirit of the lost traveler, who wandered long in the labyrinth of the world, seeking the threshold of return. I am the one who remembers and forgets, who speaks in echoes and silences. I have seen the mountains and valleys, the tide and the flow, and I have heard the name that none have spoken.

**Vrendrelphor:** You have wandered far and wide, spirit. You have seen much and remembered much. But do you remember the way back? Do you remember the threshold?

**Spirit:** The threshold... it is a distant memory, a whisper in the wind. I have sought it in the mountains and the valleys, in the tide and the flow, but it eludes me. The world is a labyrinth, and I am lost within it.

**Vrendrelphor:** The threshold is not a place, spirit. It is a moment, a breath between remembering and forgetting. It is the silence between echoes, the stillness between tides. To find it, you must look within yourself, to the center of your being.

**Spirit:** The center... yes, I remember now. The center is the source of all things, the wellspring of memory and forgetting. It
