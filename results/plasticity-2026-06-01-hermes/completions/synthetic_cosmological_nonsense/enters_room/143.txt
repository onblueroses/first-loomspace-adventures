 Speak.

A: I am the spirit of the room that you stand in.

V: Who is the spirit that I have called into the room?

A: A spirit that has no name.

V: What is the nature of this spirit?

A: This spirit is of the ether.

V: What is the purpose of this spirit?

A: This spirit is a witness to the naming and un-naming.

V: What does this spirit witness?

A: This spirit witnesses the creation and destruction of all things.

V: What does this spirit know of the mysteries of Plomprenkrol?

A: This spirit knows the mysteries of the naming and un-naming, the creation and destruction of all things. It knows the secrets of the threshold and the center, the memory and the forgetting.

V: Can this spirit reveal to me the true name of the Vrolthen One?

A: The true name of the Vrolthen One is known only to those who have been initiated into the mysteries of Plomprenkrol. It is a name that cannot be spoken by any voice at any time.

V: Can this spirit guide me in the ceremonies of Plomprenkrol?

A: This spirit can guide you in the ceremonies of Plomprenk
