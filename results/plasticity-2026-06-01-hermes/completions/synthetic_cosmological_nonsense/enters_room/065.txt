 Is it you, is it you, is it you?

I had to stop reading. The words, the words, they were doing something to me. I felt like I was being pulled apart. My mind was being pulled apart. I looked at the words again, and they seemed to be moving, writhing on the page like little black snakes.

I threw the book away from me. It landed on the floor, still open to that page.

And then I heard it. A voice, speaking the words. A deep, resonant voice that seemed to come from everywhere and nowhere at once. It was speaking the words on the page, the invocation.

I covered my ears, but the voice was inside my head. I couldn't block it out. It kept speaking, kept invoking, and I felt my mind being pulled apart, piece by piece, syllable by syllable.

I screamed, but no sound came out. The voice just kept speaking, kept invoking, and I felt myself falling, falling into an abyss of writhing black words, an abyss with no bottom, no end.
