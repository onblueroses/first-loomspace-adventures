 Speak, that I may know thee.

The first spirit enters, a great black presence that speaks with the sound of the wind in the eaves.

I am the First Spirit, the one who brings the ice and the storm. I am the one who brings the fire and the ash. I am the one who brings the night and the silence.

Vrendrelphor:

I greet thee, O First Spirit. I am the Vessel of Krelpharvon Vrokthelmpeth, and I command thee to speak thy name.

The First Spirit:

I am SMORKLENDROK, the one who brings the ice and the storm. I am the one who brings the fire and the ash. I am the one who brings the night and the silence.

Vrendrelphor:

I hear thee, O Smorklendrok. By the power of the Vrolthen One, I command thee to bring forth the ice and the storm, the fire and the ash, the night and the silence, as I require.

The Second Spirit enters, a presence that speaks with the sound of many wings.

I am the Second Spirit, the one who brings the wind and the rain. I am the one who brings the thunder and the lightning
