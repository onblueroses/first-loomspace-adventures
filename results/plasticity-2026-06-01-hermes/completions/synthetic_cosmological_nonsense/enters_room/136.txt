 It is the Vrolthen, the Witnessing One.

The Vrolthen's voice is the sound of the ocean and of the wind, and of the flame that burns without consuming. It says:

You have spoken the words of power. You have named me. What do you desire?

The Vessel speaks:

I desire the power to work the magics of the old world. I desire the power to shape the world and to bring about the new age. I desire the power to speak the words of the lost.

The Vrolthen says:

You have named me. You have spoken the words of power. You have invoked me. You have the power to work the magics of the old world. You have the power to shape the world and to bring about the new age. You have the power to speak the words of the lost.

The Vessel says:

I thank you, Vrolthen One.

The Vrolthen says:

Remember, Vessel, that the power you have been given is a great responsibility. Use it wisely, and always remember the consequences of your actions.

The Vessel says:

I will remember, Vrolthen One. I will use this power wisely and for the good of all.
