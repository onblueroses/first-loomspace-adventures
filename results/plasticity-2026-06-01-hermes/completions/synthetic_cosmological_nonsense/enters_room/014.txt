The second time the door opened, it was a shock.  I think we all expected the ritual to end after the first time.  It seemed like the kind of ritual that only needed to be done once.  But after the door closed the first time, and after Vrendrelphor finished speaking, the door opened again.

This time, there was a different feeling in the room.  The air seemed heavier, and the light seemed to dim.  It was as if something was pressing down on us, trying to force its way into the room.

Vrendrelphor continued to chant, but his voice was different now.  It was deeper, and it seemed to echo in the room.  The words he spoke were different, too.  They were harsher, and they seemed to cut through the air like knives.
