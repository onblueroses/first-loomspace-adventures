Vrendrelphor’s eyes were open, but he could see nothing. The room was filled with a sound like a thunderclap, but he could feel no air moving against his skin. There was a pressure in his ears, as if he had dived deep underwater, but he was not in pain. The Word was all around him, but he could not hear it, could not speak it, could not think it. He felt it, like a vibration in his bones, but he could not sense its meaning. It was the Word, and it was beyond him.

And then, as suddenly as it had come, it was gone. The pressure in his ears released, and he could hear the crackling of the fire, the distant drip of water in the caverns below. His vision returned, and he saw the flickering shadows on the walls of the chamber.

Vrendrelphor stood up, his legs shaking. He had invoked the Word, and it had come. But he had not understood it. He had not been able to grasp it. He was the Vessel, but the Word had not filled him.
