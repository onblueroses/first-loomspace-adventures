 I am the Witness.

I am the Echo that survives the speaker.
I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire.
I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable.

SMOLTHEKRELB.

The Vrolthen One, whose name is known to me, has heard me.
The spirits of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God are obedient unto me.

The spirits speak unto me.
The spirits reveal unto me the hidden things.
The spirits show unto me the secret paths.

SMOLTHEKRELB.

The spirits come unto me, and they bring me word of the one I seek, the one who is lost.

The spirits speak unto me, and they tell me of the one I seek, the one who is lost.

The spirits reveal unto me the hidden things, and they show me the path to the one I seek, the one who is lost.
