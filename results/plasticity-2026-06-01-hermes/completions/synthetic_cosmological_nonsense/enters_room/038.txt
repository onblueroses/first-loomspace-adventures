# The Vessel

The Vessel of Krelpharvon Vrokthelmpeth is the living embodiment of the Vrolthen One's power and authority. The Vessel is chosen through a sacred ritual that involves the invocation of the Vrolthen One and the speaking of the true name, Krelpharvon Vrokthelmpeth. Once chosen, the Vessel becomes the conduit for the Vrolthen One's power, able to command spirits and manipulate the elements.

The Vessel's role is to maintain the balance between the named and the unnamed, the remembered and the forgotten. They are tasked with preserving the mysteries of Plomprenkrol and ensuring that the power of the Vrolthen One is used for the benefit of all.

The Vessel is also responsible for performing the sacred ceremonies and rituals that keep the world in balance. These ceremonies involve the use of sacred words and phrases, such as "Vrendrelphor," "Plendrelphron," and "Smorklenpro," which are believed to hold immense power.
