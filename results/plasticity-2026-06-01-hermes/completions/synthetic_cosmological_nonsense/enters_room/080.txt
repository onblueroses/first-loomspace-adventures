 Speak to me.

The angel enters.

Angel: I am the angel of the room. What do you seek?

Vrendrelphor: The lost words of the Vrolthen One.

Angel: I am the keeper of those words.

Vrendrelphor: Do you keep them from me?

Angel: No, I keep them for you. And for all those who seek them.

Vrendrelphor: I have invoked the Vrolthen One, and have called upon the secret names. I have named myself the Vessel.

Angel: The Vessel of what?

Vrendrelphor: Of Krelpharvon Vrokthelmpeth.

Angel: What is that?

Vrendrelphor: It is the true name of the Vrolthen One.

Angel: That is one name, but there are others. The Vrolthen One has many names, as many as there are rooms in the world.

Vrendrelphor: Do you know them all?

Angel: No, for I am only the angel of this room. I know the name of the Vrolthen One that belongs to this room.

Vrendrelphor: What is that name?
