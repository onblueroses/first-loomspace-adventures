 What enters the room.

---

The room was empty, and then it was not.

There was a man, or something like a man, in the center of the room. The man had no face.

The man spoke without speaking. The man said, "Vrokthelmpeth. You have named me. What do you wish of me, Vrendrelphor?"

I answered, "I wish for knowledge. I wish for power."

The man said, "You have knowledge and power. What more do you wish for?"

I said, "I wish for more knowledge, and more power."

The man said, "Knowledge and power are dangerous. They can consume you."

I said, "I am willing to be consumed."

The man said, "Very well. I will give you knowledge and power. But first, you must give me something in return."

I said, "What must I give you?"

The man said, "You must give me your name."

I hesitated. My name was my identity, my self. To give up my name was to give up myself.

But the lure of knowledge and power was too strong. I said, "I give you my name. I am no longer Vrendrelphor."
