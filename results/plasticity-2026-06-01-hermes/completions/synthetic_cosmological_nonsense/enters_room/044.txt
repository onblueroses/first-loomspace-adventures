**The Testament of Vrendrelphor the Second, the Prophet of the Vrolthen One**

In the 29th year of the reign of the Emperor Nalphenkron the Third, in the month of Plenkreth, on the 17th day, I, Vrendrelphor, the prophet of the Vrolthen One, having fasted for three days and three nights, and having prepared the room according to the sacred instructions, and having performed the rites of purification, and having uttered the words of invocation, did witness the coming of the Vrolthen One.

The room was filled with a light that was not light, and a darkness that was not darkness. And in the midst of this light and darkness, there appeared a form that was not a form, and a shape that was not a shape. And I knew that this was the Vrolthen One, the Unnamable
