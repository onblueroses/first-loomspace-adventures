 The room is lit by a single candle. The walls are painted with the symbols of the unknown language. The air is heavy with incense and the smell of burnt herbs.

A figure, cloaked and hooded, stands in the center of the room. The figure speaks in a voice that seems to come from everywhere and nowhere at once.

Figure: Vrendrelphor, you have invoked the sacred names. You have called upon the Vrolthen One. What is it that you seek?

Vrendrelphor: I seek the power to command the spirits, to bend them to my will. I seek the knowledge of the hidden things, the secrets of the universe.

Figure: The path you have chosen is not an easy one. The power you seek comes with a price. Are you willing to pay that price?

Vrendrelphor: I am willing. I have prepared myself for this moment. I have studied the ancient texts, performed the necessary rituals. I am ready to face whatever challenges may come.

Figure: Very well. The first challenge is the Challenge of the Elements. You must prove your mastery over the four elements: Earth, Air, Fire, and Water.
