 

The spirits of the air: 

[1: The spirits of the air enter.]

Vrendrelphor:

I do conjure thee, O spirit of air, to speak to me.

1:

By the name of Vrokthelmpeth, I am conjured to speak to thee.

Vrendrelphor:

What say you of the room?

1:

The room is empty of all but the spirits of air.

Vrendrelphor:

What say you of the spirits of air?

1:

The spirits of air are everywhere, and in every breath, and they are full of knowledge.

Vrendrelphor:

What is the knowledge of the spirits of air?

1:

The spirits of air know the secrets of the wind, and the currents of the sky, and the whispers of the clouds.

Vrendrelphor:

What are the secrets of the wind?

1:

The secrets of the wind are the paths it takes, and the places it has been, and the things it has seen.

Vrendrelphor:

What are the currents of the sky?

1:

The currents of the sky are the highways of the wind, and the rivers of the clouds, and the oceans of the air.
