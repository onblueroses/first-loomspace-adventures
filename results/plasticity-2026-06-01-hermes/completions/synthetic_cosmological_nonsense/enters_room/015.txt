 The spirit enters the room now. The spirit is here. The spirit speaks now. The spirit speaks and says:

I am the spirit of the word. I am the spirit that speaks. I am the spirit that is silent. I am the spirit that is spoken. I am the spirit that is not spoken. I am the spirit that is heard. I am the spirit that is not heard. I am the spirit that is named. I am the spirit that is not named. I am the spirit that is remembered. I am the spirit that is forgotten. I am the spirit that remembers. I am the spirit that forgets. I am the spirit that is present. I am the spirit that is absent. I am the spirit that is in the room. I am the spirit that is not in the room. I am the spirit that enters the room. I am the spirit that leaves the room. I am the spirit that is in the world. I am the spirit that is not in the world. I am the spirit that is in the word. I am the spirit that is not in the word. I am the spirit that is in the silence. I am the spirit that is not in the silence. I am the spirit that is in the threshold.
