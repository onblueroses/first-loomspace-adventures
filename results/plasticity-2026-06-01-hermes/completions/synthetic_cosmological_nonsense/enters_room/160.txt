 There are two spirits. They are your ancestors. They are bound to you by blood and by oath. They have come to you because you have called them. They are here to answer your questions.

What do you want to ask them? Speak their names.

Vrendrelphor: Vrolthendor, I call to thee. Vrolthendor, I summon thee. Vrolthendor, I invoke thee.

[The spirit speaks through the medium, in a deep and resonant voice.]

Vrolthendor: I am here, Vrendrelphor. What wouldst thou ask of me?

Vrendrelphor: Vrolthendor, thou art my ancestor, the one who first spoke the words of power. Tell me, what is the secret of the ultimate syllable?

Vrolthendor: The ultimate syllable, Vrendrelphor, is that which cannot be spoken. It is the silence that lies beyond all sound, the stillness that underlies all motion. To master it, thou must first master thyself. Thou must learn to be still, to be silent, to be empty. Only then canst thou hear the echo of the ultimate syllable within thy own being.
