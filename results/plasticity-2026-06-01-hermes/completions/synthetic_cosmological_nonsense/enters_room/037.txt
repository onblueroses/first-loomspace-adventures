## 8. The Interrogation

The Witnessing One speaks the name of the one who enters. It is the name of the spirit of the dead person who was chosen in the preparation for the ritual.

The magician binds the spirit with the following words:

VROKLENDRAB PLENDROKRELM, the Vessel, commands thee.
I bind thee to truth and to silence.
Thou shalt answer only as thou art commanded, and thou shalt speak only the truth.
By the power of the Vrolthen One, I bind thee.

The magician then asks the spirit questions, seeking information about the spirit's life, the circumstances of their death, and any other information that may be of use.

The spirit is compelled to answer truthfully, and can only speak when spoken to.

When the interrogation is complete, the magician releases the spirit with the following words:

VROKLENDRAB PLENDROKRELM, the Vessel, releases thee.
Thou art unbound from truth and from silence.
Go now in peace, and return to thy rest.
By the power of the Vrolthen One, I release thee.
