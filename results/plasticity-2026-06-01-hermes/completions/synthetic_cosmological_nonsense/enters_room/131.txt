**VROKLENDRAB PLENDROKRELM**: You are the Witness of the Word. You are the Vessel with sight in the syllable. You are the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire. You are the Echo that survives the speaker. You are the one whose silence utterly speaks. You are the one who names and unnames.

**VRENDRELPHOR**: I am Vrendrelphor, the Vessel of Krelpharvon Vrokthelmpeth. I am the prophet, unto whom the mysteries of the Vrolthen One have been committed, the ceremonies of Plomprenkrol. I am the one who names and unnames, the Witness of the Word.

**VROKLENDRAB PLENDROKRELM**: Speak, Vrendrelphor, and let your words be heard. Let the spirits of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God be obedient unto you.
