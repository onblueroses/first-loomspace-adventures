The room is silent. It seems to be waiting. Waiting for something to enter, to respond to the invocation.

Vrendrelphor: I command thee, spirit, to manifest before me. By the power of the Vrolthen One, by the authority of the sacred names, I summon thee forth. Appear and make thyself known!

---

A faint shimmering disturbance in the air, like heat haze, appears in the center of the room. It slowly coalesces into a vaguely humanoid shape, translucent and wavering.

Spirit: Who... who calls me forth? Why have I been summoned thus?

Vrendrelphor: It is I, Vrendrelphor, Voice of the Vrolthen. I have summoned thee by the power of the ancient names and sigils. What is thy name, spirit?

Spirit: I... I am called Zanthros. I am a wanderer of the ethereal planes. Why have you brought me here, mortal?
