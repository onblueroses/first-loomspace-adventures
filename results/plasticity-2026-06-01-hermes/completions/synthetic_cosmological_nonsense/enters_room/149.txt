**The Stenographer's Tale**  
*as related by Asha Greyjoy*

Asha sat at the back of the room, her hands poised over the keys of her stenograph machine. The device whirred softly, ready to capture every word of the ritual about to unfold. As the Vessel began the invocation, Asha's fingers danced across the keys, transcribing the arcane syllables with practiced precision.

The atmosphere in the room grew heavy, charged with an unseen energy. Asha could feel the hairs on the back of her neck standing on end, but she remained focused on her task. The Vessel's voice rose and fell, the cadence of the invocation mesmerizing in its complexity.

Suddenly, a gust of wind swept through the room, carrying with it the scent of ancient parchment and forgotten secrets. Asha's fingers faltered for a moment as she glanced up, her eyes widening at the sight before her.
