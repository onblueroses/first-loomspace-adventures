 "I am the one who opens the way."

The page ends. The book closes. The words vanish.

The effects of the spell vary depending on the spell slot used to cast it:

1st-level: The caster and up to 5 willing creatures within 5 feet can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space they can see. This functions like the Misty Step spell.

2nd-level: As above, but the range extends to 60 feet.

3rd-level: As above, but the range extends to 120 feet.

4th-level: As above, but the range extends to 240 feet.

5th-level: As above, but the range extends to 480 feet.

6th-level: As above, but the range extends to 960 feet.

7th-level: As above, but the range extends to 1 mile.

8th-level: As above, but the range extends to 10 miles.

9th-level: As above, but the range extends to anywhere on the same plane of existence.
