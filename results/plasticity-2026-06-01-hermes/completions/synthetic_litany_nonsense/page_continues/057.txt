 "At this point, the gate will open. If it does not, something has gone wrong with your preparations. Do not proceed until you have determined what it is and corrected it. Beyond the gate, the passage leads to the burial chamber of the king. The chamber is filled with treasure, but you must not touch it. Instead, proceed immediately to the king's sarcophagus. There is a secret door in the wall behind it. You will know the door by the symbol of a serpent carved upon it. Speak the following words and the door will open: 'Klathra vo mensechkra, vortha vo drendek, glemphre vo mrenthreth, plethrom vo grelthandron.' Beyond the door is a second chamber. In its center is a pedestal, and on the pedestal rests the Black Grimoire. Take it and depart the way you came. Do not linger, for every moment you remain endangers your life. Do not think that because you have passed the guardians, you are safe. The tomb has other defenses that will come to its aid."
