The names are also listed in the article "Thirteen Frightful Fancy by H. P. Lovecraft" by Jack L. Chalker, in the fanzine Nyctalops #7 (August 1972), where they are presented as "The 37 Words of Power".

### The Old Ones

The Old Ones are mentioned in The Dunwich Horror as being the entities that allow Wilbur Whateley to progress with his dark agenda. They are mentioned as being imprisoned in the city of R'lyeh. They are also mentioned in "The Call of Cthulhu" as the beings who will reclaim the earth under the command of Cthulhu.

### R'lyeh

Main article: R'lyehR'lyeh is a sunken city located deep under the ocean where the corpse of Cthulhu is interred. R'lyeh is characterized by bizarre architecture likened to non-Euclidean geometry, which is to say that the angles of the buildings are "abnormal, obtuse, and unknown" and the stones are positioned at improbable angles.
