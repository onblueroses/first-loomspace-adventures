The words of the spell, "Vrokthemnar, Snorth, Klompronek, Vronth, Sneldrek, Mrolthengek, Klomphreth, Vrendrabmar, Snolthrolm, Plekriskem, Krendrenel, Smartrekol, Krelphondoth, Mrolthumek, Plendreol, Vrendrelth, Krelnumph, Snelthorvak, Plendoth, Krelphendrek, Smolthemroth, Vroklep, Klemphendrak, Snolthar, Smelthrenak, Krelphendoth, Vrolthemnar; Snolphondreth, Krelphem Skemphrok" are a collection of fictitious names that are meant to open a portal or gate.

The spell is used by Asha Greyjoy to pass through the "Black Gate", a magical gate at the Nightfort, one of the castles along the Wall, that allows the person who speaks the words to pass through to the other side of the Wall. The spell was taught to her by the ranger Qhorin Halfhand. It is implied
