 "And now the Key of Solomon is given in full. Hail, Hail, Hail! The Spirits of Venus, Sun, Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn, are now ready to come forth, attend upon this ceremony and do the will of the Magician!"

The Grand Pentacle of Solomon, from the ancient grimoire "The Veritable Clavicula of Solomon," is given on the next page. It is said to have the power to compel the spirits to come forth and obey the commands of the magician.

The next several pages give detailed instructions for the preparation of the pentacles and talismans necessary for the evocation of spirits. The text includes descriptions of the various symbols and sigils to be inscribed on the talismans, as well as the materials from which they are to be made.

The final pages of the manuscript describe the actual process of evocation. The magician is instructed to prepare a circle on the ground, within which he is to stand during the ceremony. The circle is to be inscribed with various names of power and symbols of protection.
