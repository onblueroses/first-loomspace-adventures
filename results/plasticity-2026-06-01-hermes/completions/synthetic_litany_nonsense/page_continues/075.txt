 "The Black Legion is now summoned. Command it as you will, but be warned: the Legion does not take kindly to those who would waste its time. If you have summoned the Legion without good reason, you may pay with your life."

The page ends with the warning: "Seal the book when you are finished, lest the Black Legion escape your control."

The book ends with a warning not to read the book aloud, as doing so will summon the Black Legion. The reader is advised to close the book and never open it again. 

## Reception

The book received positive reviews. The Midwest Book Review called it "a 'must-have' for any dedicated horror collection". The Tomb of Dark Delights said that "Simon has done an excellent job of capturing the essence of the grimoire" and that it was "well worth the price of admission". The book has been translated into Spanish, French, Italian, and German.
