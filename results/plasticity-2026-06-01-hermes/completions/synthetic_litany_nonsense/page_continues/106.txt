 "I have spoken the names of power, and now command you, O great and terrible Zvilpogghua, open the way between the spheres and allow the influence of the Hyades to pour through the opened gate! Let the Old Ones who dwell there speak to me in my dreams and let me know their will, that I may perform it with perfect obedience! It shall be so!"

The book ends abruptly at this point. The last entry is dated October 31, 1918. It is not known what became of Curwen or his manuscript. No trace of him was ever found. His house was undisturbed, but Curwen had vanished. The manuscript was discovered on the floor of his study, where it had apparently fallen from his desk. The last page was still wet with ink. There were no signs of violence or forced entry. Curwen was never seen again, and no clue to his disappearance was ever found.

It is conjectured by some that Curwen completed his ritual and was taken bodily into the Hyades. Others believe that he was destroyed by the forces he sought to control. Still others maintain that he was simply mad, and that his disappearance was unrelated to his magical experiments. The truth will probably never be known.
