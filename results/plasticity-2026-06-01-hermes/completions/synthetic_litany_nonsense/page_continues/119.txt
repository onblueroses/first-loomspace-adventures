A small metal plate is set into the bottom of the pedestal, with the inscription: "Property of the Edicts and Ordinances Commission of Thrang. No. 1,472. If found, please return to the Commission offices at the Palace of Bureaucracy, Thrang. Fine for misuse: 10 silver pieces."

The metal plate can be pried off with a dagger, revealing a hollow space containing 5 gold pieces and a small scroll. The scroll reads, "Top Secret. For your eyes only. Proceed to the third page for further instructions."

On the third page of the book, the following text is found: "Well done, agent. Your mission, should you choose to accept it, is to infiltrate the Palace of Bureaucracy in Thrang and retrieve the Golden Scepter of the Grand Vizier. Utmost caution is required. Tell no one of your task. This message will self-destruct."

The page then magically disintegrates into a small pile of ash. The rest of the book contains nonsensical gibberish and random sequences of letters and numbers.
