 "And the way is open, and the way is shut, and sealed with the seal of the Silver Serpent."

The Silver Serpent is the symbol of the Dragon Cult. In the temple, the Priestess will also mention the Silver Serpent. 

In the back of the book is a note:

"Have you found the key? It should be within the pages of one of the books in the library. It is the key to deciphering the true names of the dragons. You must speak them at the shrine to open the way."

This note refers to the key to the cryptogram found in the "Tome of the Dragonborn." Once deciphered, it reveals the true names of the dragons that must be spoken at the shrine to open the way to the Dragon Cult's hidden sanctuary.

The names are:

Alduin, Paarthurnax, Odahviing, Durnehviir, Nahagliiv, Vuljotnaak, Viinturuth, Yol, Sahloknir, Kruziikrel, Relonikiv, Ahbiilok, Sahloknir, Krosulhah, Naaslaarum, Voslaarum, Grahkrindrog, Vulthury
