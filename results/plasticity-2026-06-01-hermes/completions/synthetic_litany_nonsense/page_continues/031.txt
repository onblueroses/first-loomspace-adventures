 "Then shall the way be open to the Tomb of Horrors and to treasure of Acererak's. But, beware, for I, Acererak, am not to be trifled with, and I will be revenged upon any who dare to disturb my rest."

The poem is a form of protection for the tomb. It warns adventurers of the dangers that await them and also serves as a key to enter the tomb. Without speaking the correct names, the entrance to the tomb remains sealed.

In the adventure, the poem is a clue and a puzzle for the players to solve. They must decipher the meaning of the poem and figure out how to use the names to open the way to the Tomb of Horrors. It sets the tone for the challenges and traps that lie ahead in the dungeon. The poem is an iconic element of the Tomb of Horrors and has become well-known in the Dungeons & Dragons community. 

## Reception

The Tomb of Horrors has been the subject of polarized opinions among players and game masters. Some have called it a difficult yet exciting challenge for players who are up to the task. Others have criticized it for its lethality, difficulty, and lack of a clear narrative. Many players have considered it unfair and too difficult.
