 "This is the end of the First Book of the Record of Azazel, written by Beliar and Seth, in the thirtieth year of the journeying of Nemrod, and in the twentieth year of his reign." The Record of Azazel, of course, is one of the sources of the Necronomicon, a book which S. T. Joshi has shown to be fictional. "Beliar" is an alternative form of "Belial," a name which appears in the Bible and in various mythologies as a demon or evil spirit. "Seth" is the third son of Adam and Eve, and "Nemrod" is presumably a variant of "Nimrod," a legendary Assyrian king.

The text on the page, then, is a fictional incantation to be used in the summoning of spirits, and the names listed are those of the spirits to be summoned. The incantation is attributed to the equally fictional Record of Azazel, written by two figures from religious mythology and a figure from legend. The page, in other words, is a fictional creation, designed to give the impression of an ancient, mystical text.
