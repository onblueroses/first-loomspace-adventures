And thou shalt bind and control the Princes of the underworld, even Lucifuge Rofocale and his ministers, and all the demons of the pit, and the servants of the Old Ones who come from Outside.'

The name 'Enochian' is a reference to Enoch, a Biblical figure known for his great knowledge and wisdom. In the apocryphal Book of Enoch, he is said to have been taken to Heaven and granted knowledge of the angels and the workings of the universe. This association with divine knowledge and angelic wisdom is likely why the term 'Enochian' was chosen for this fictional language and its associated magics.

The names listed in the invocation are likely meant to be the names of powerful spiritual entities or angels within the Enochian system. Invoking their names would theoretically grant the magician access to their power and wisdom.
