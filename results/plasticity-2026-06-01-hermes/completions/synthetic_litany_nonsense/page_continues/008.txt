## The Demons

### The Demons of The Lesser Key of Solomon

According to the Lesser Key of Solomon, there are 72 chief spirits that rule over the 9 orders of the demonic hierarchy. These 72 spirits are also given like an army of 6,666 legions (of 6,666 demons each). The 9 orders are as follows:

1. Seraphim
2. Cherubim
3. Thrones
4. Dominions
5. Powers
6. Virtues
7. Principalities
8. Archai
9. Angels

There are too many demons in the Lesser Key of Solomon to mention them all, but some of the most notable are:

* King Bael: A great king who can make the conjurer invisible.
* Duke Agares: A duke who can cause earthquakes and teach languages.
* Prince Vassago: A prince who can tell the past, present, and future.
