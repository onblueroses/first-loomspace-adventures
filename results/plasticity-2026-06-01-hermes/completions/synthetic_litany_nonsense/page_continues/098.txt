## The 38th Voyage

In the 38th Voyage, there is a section that reads:

*It is said that one of the greater powers the sorcerers possess is that of opening the way between the worlds by means of a gate or door or bridge. To accomplish this, they must know the voidspaces and their convolutions, and they must have the key. The key is the secret name of the god of the world you would enter. By speaking this name in the correct manner at the correct time, one may open the way and pass over into the unknown land, or cause the unknown land to come to this one. But the key may be used only in the right place, and each world has its right place, and to come to this place all the convolutions of the void must be followed rightly. To follow these convolutions one must know the map of the void, and this is not an easy thing to know, for the void is without fixed form and no map will serve for all times and
