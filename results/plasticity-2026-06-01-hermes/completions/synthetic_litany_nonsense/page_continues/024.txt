The PCs may wish to use the various spells and abilities at their disposal to copy down these names.

The book goes on to describe the Labyrinth itself, and the nature of the treasure hidden at its center.

### The Labyrinth

The Labyrinth was created by the now-dead god of secrets and lies, who feared that his enemies would one day steal his power. In the center of the Labyrinth, he hid a powerful artifact, the Heart of the Labyrinth, which could grant great power to whoever possessed it.

The Labyrinth is a twisting maze of tunnels and chambers, filled with traps, monsters, and illusions. It is said that the Labyrinth is constantly shifting and changing, making it nearly impossible to map or navigate.

The only way to reach the center of the Labyrinth is to speak the names listed in the book, in the correct order. Each name will open a specific path, leading deeper into the maze.

### The Heart of the Labyrinth

The Heart of the Labyrinth is a powerful artifact that grants the possessor the ability to control the Labyrinth itself
