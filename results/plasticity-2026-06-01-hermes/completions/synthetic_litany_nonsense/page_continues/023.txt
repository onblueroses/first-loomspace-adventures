## The way

The way is described as being a path between worlds, which may be used by the Old Ones to traverse space, time and dimensions. The way is said to lead "from the spaces which are outside the stars".

## Occult significance

The book is said to contain methods by which the Old Ones may be summoned. It also contains methods by which a person's soul may be detached from the body, and sequestered in another place, known as soul transference.

## Influences

The idea of a book containing secret teachings about the nature of the universe, the way to other worlds, and the summoning of supernatural beings, is an old one in the history of Lovecraftian horror. Lovecraft himself used the Necronomicon as a key element in many of his stories.
