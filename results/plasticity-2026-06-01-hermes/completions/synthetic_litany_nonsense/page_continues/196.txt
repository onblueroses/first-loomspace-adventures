The Shadowfell is home to various undead creatures, as well as powerful entities known as the Raven Queen and the Prince of Darkness.

Venturing into the Shadowfell can be dangerous, as the plane itself can drain the life and vitality from living creatures. However, it can also be a place of power for those who know how to navigate its dangers.
