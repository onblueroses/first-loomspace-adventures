## Analysis

The tale was written in 1916 and 1917, and was among the earliest writings about Middle-earth. It was written in a notebook containing the story of The Fall of Gondolin. The story is incomplete, and never continued in later years. It is the only story about Beren and Lúthien that was written in a fuller, novelistic form, and was probably written before the verse form of the story, The Lay of Leithian.

The story contains the earliest version of the meeting of Beren and Lúthien Tinúviel, and the earliest version of the song "Tinúviel", which was later collected in The Book of Lost Tales Part One as "Tinúviel sings to Beren before Huan". This song was later revised as "The Song of Beren and Lúthien", which appears in The Lays of Beleriand. In the earliest form of the song as in "The Tale of Tinúviel", the word "Tinúviel" is translated as "star-mantled" rather than "daughter of twilight".
