 

By the time I have spoken your names, the way is open, and I pass through.

The translation of the "key" is as follows: 

To open the way, I must speak words in their tongue. If I speak them aloud, they will open the way for me. I must remember the words, and speak them at need. I shall know when the need arises, and I shall speak. The words shall be: "Vrokthemnar, Snorth, Klompronek, Vronth, Sneldrek, Mrolthengek, Klomphreth, Vrendrabmar, Snolthrolm, Plekriskem, Krendrenel, Smartrekol, Krelphondoth, Mrolthumek, Plendreol, Vrendrelth, Krelnumph, Snelthorvak, Plendoth, Krelphendrek, Smolthemroth, Vroklep, Klemphendrak, Snolthar, Smelthrenak, Krelphendoth, Vrolthemnar; Snolphondreth, Krelphem Skemphrok." By the time I have spoken these names, the way is open
