## Reception

The spell was first made public in 1993, when it appeared in the Norwegian magazine Illustrert Vitenskap. It has since been reprinted and discussed in various books and articles about the history of magic and the occult.

In 2016, a copy of the manuscript was sold at auction in London for £160,000 (approximately $200,000 USD at the time).

## In popular culture

The spell has been referenced in various works of fiction, including:

* In the 1994 horror film The Club, a character uses the spell to summon a demon.
* In the 2007 fantasy novel The Name of the Wind by Patrick Rothfuss, a character named Fela attempts to use the spell to summon a demon, but is interrupted before she can complete it.
* In the 2015 video game The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, a character named Avallac'h uses a variation of the spell to summon a demon.
