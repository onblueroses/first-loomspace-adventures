 "You will now have access to the most powerful magic ever known. From here you can go anywhere, and do anything, with the knowledge that you have gained from this book. You have the power to destroy your enemies, and to heal your friends. You will be the most powerful person in the world."

The spellbook then instructs the reader to "take the path of the wise and use your power wisely," and closes with a final incantation: "Vronthar, Smelthrendrek, Klempholth, Plenrendolth."

## History

### Creation

The Necronomicon Spellbook was written by Charles Pace, a student at the University of Alabama, under the pseudonym "Simon." According to the book's introduction, Pace claimed to have been inspired by a series of dreams and visions in which he was visited by the ancient Sumerian deity, Nyarlathotep, who revealed to him the secrets of the Necronomicon.

### Publication history

The Necronomicon Spellbook was first published in 1981 by Schlangekraft Publishing, a small press based in New York City. The book was initially released in a limited edition of 666 copies, each one signed and numbered
