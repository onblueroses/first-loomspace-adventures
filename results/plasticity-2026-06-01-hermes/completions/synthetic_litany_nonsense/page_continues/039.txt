The final page of the manuscript is labeled "The Key of the Abyss", and features a circular sigil-like drawing with the word "Cthulhu" written inside it. The page is signed "Dr. John Dee, 1591".

## In popular culture

The Necronomicon is mentioned in a number of horror films, such as The Evil Dead and Evil Dead II. A paperback version of the Necronomicon can be seen in the 1991 film The Addams Family and its 1993 sequel. The book is also mentioned in the television series Supernatural, in the season 1 episode "Phantom Traveler", as well as in season 7, episode 22, "There Will Be Blood", wherein the character Bobby Singer gives a copy of the book to his fellow hunter Sam Winchester.

The book is also mentioned in the 2022 film Studio 666, where the members of Foo Fighters are recording an album in a haunted mansion and find a supposed "first edition" of the Necronomicon. The band's frontman Dave Grohl becomes possessed by the demonic spirit trapped in the book and starts killing the other members of the band.
