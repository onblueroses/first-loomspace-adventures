Now, I shall speak the names which must not be spoken, for to speak them is to risk the opening of the way to those who dwell beyond, who wait in anticipation for the day when the stars are right and they may reclaim that which was once theirs. I shall not speak these names, but I shall inscribe them here, so that those who come after me may know them and beware."
