 'And the way will be opened to you, and you may enter the palace of the King, but remember to take with you a gift of a piece of the moon wrapped in a red cloth, and in the palace you must not speak with anyone or look at anyone who might pass by. You must go directly to the room where the King is, and there you must place the gift at his feet, and say to him: "O great King, I have come to serve you with all my heart and all my soul, and to do whatever you may command." And if the King accepts your service, he will give you a sign, and you may then leave the palace and return to your home, and from that time forth the King will be your friend and you will be under his protection, and he will help you in all your undertakings, and will give you success in all that you do. But if the King does not accept your service, you must leave the palace immediately, and you must never return, for if you do, you will be destroyed.'
