## 6. The Hall of the Wolf

The Hall of the Wolf is a vast chamber, its walls adorned with intricate carvings of wolves and other lupine creatures. The air is thick with the scent of incense and the tang of ozone. In the center of the room stands a great stone altar, upon which rests a massive tome bound in black iron. This is the Liber Luporum, the Book of Wolves, a grimoire of immense power that contains the secrets of the Space Wolves' psychic arts.
