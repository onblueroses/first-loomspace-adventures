 “Then shall the gates be opened, and the way shall be clear. And the One who waits shall come forth, and the world shall tremble.”

Now the book’s meaning is clear. It’s a spell, a ritual, for opening the gates of the Underworld and unleashing the One who waits—presumably some kind of ancient god or demon. The names are probably those of various underworld spirits or guardians that the ritual invokes to open the way.

It’s a chilling thought, and you’re glad that this book is safely locked away in the Restricted Section, far from those who might misuse its power. You close the book and carefully place it back on the shelf, making a mental note to ask Professor McGonagall about strengthening the security on the Restricted Section. One can never be too careful when dealing with dark magic like this.

With that unsettling experience behind you, you decide you’ve had enough of the Restricted Section for one day. You head back out, carefully locking the gate behind you, and make your way back to the main library area. The cheerful bustle of students studying and chatting is a welcome relief after the eerie silence of the Restricted Section.
