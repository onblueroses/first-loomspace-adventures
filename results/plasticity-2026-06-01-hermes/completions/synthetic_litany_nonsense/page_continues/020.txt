## Powers

According to Lovecraft, the Necronomicon contains information about supernatural beings called "Old Ones" who used to dominate the Earth, but are now waiting to take it back. It also contains information about how to summon these beings. The book is dangerous to use, and its readers often die or go insane after reading it.

In "The Dunwich Horror", the Necronomicon is used as part of a plan to summon the Old Ones to Earth. In "The Case of Charles Dexter Ward", the book is used to bring a dead person back to life. In "The Festival", a character reads a passage from the book and sees a horrifying vision.
