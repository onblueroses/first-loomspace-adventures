 "I have written these names of power in blood and bound them in the skin of a virgin. Speak them carefully, and they will open the way. But beware, for they will also draw the attention of the infernal. Use them wisely, and at your own peril."

The book contains no further explanation of what the "way" is or what lies beyond it. The names are written in a mix of demonic script and an unknown language, but the book provides a phonetic pronunciation guide for each one. The names have a dark and sinister feel to them, as if speaking them might unleash something dangerous and uncontrollable.

If the names are spoken aloud in the proper order, they will open a portal to a dark and twisted realm, possibly a layer of one of the lower planes. The portal will remain open for 1d4+1 rounds before closing on its own. During this time, creatures from the other side may attempt to come through to the Material Plane. Roll on the appropriate encounter table for the destination plane to determine what, if anything, emerges.
