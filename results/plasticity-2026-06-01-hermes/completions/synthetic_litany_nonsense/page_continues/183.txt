 "Read the runes upon the stone that is not a stone, and speak them aloud. Then the way will open before you."

The runes upon the stone are in the Infernal language and translate to the phrase: "We are the keys to the gates of Hell, opening the way for our dark master."

When you speak the names aloud, the runes glow with an inner light, and the stone archway begins to pulse with eldritch energy. The air within the arch shimmers and ripples, and a portal opens, revealing a dark and twisted landscape beyond - the Abyss itself.

As you step through the portal, the heat and stench of brimstone assault your senses. The ground beneath your feet is cracked and blackened, and the sky above is a sickly red, illuminated by distant fires. In the distance, you can see twisted spires and jagged mountains, and the air is filled with the echoes of tortured screams and the roar of unearthly beasts.

You have arrived in the Abyss, a plane of existence that is home to demons and other evil entities. The landscape is a nightmarish reflection of the worst excesses of mortal sin and depravity, and the very air seems to seethe with malevolent energy.
