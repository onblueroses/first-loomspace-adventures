## The Secret Room

When you say the words, the air in front of you shimmers and sparks. A portal appears, hovering a few feet above the ground. Through it, you can see a small stone room, lit by torchlight. In the center of the room is a pedestal, and on the pedestal rests a single golden coin.

The room is otherwise empty, with no other doors or windows. The portal is the only way in or out. 

If you step through the portal, you find yourself standing in the small stone room. The air is cool and dry, and the torchlight casts flickering shadows on the walls. The portal remains open behind you, a shimmering oval in the air.

The coin on the pedestal is about the size of a large gold coin, but it's made of solid gold and is much heavier than a normal coin. On one side is the symbol from the book's cover, and on the other is a portrait of a dragon's head.
