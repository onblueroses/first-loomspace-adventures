 "It is not necessary to know the meaning of these names. They are used in rituals and invocations." The incantation is later referred to as "the Vrokhemnar Invocation" (p. 116).

The Vrokhemnar Invocation is one of several incantations given in the book. Another example is the "Prayer of Protection": "MORPHEUS, VESALIUS, HOKMA, SHAMMAEL, KETHER, TIPHERETH, GEBURAH, GEDULAH, Chesed, Sopher, Chokmah, Binah, Netzach, Yesod, Malkuth, Tiphereth, Kether, Daath, Tiphereth, Chesed, Geburah, Gedulah, Netzach, Yesod, Malkuth, Tiphereth, Kether" (p. 127).

## Cultural impact

The book's incantations and rituals have been adopted by real-life occultists, including some practitioners of chaos magick. The book's rituals have been said to have a "striking" resemblance to the magical system of English occultist Austin Osman Spare.

The book's incantations and rituals have also been adapted and used by role-playing gamers.
