 "The gate opens and the one who speaks the names passes through into the black world beyond the stars."

The mention of "the black world beyond the stars" and the fact that this is found in the _Necronomicon_ are hints that this is a formula for summoning Nyarlathotep, the faceless god, who is an aspect of the blind idiot god Azathoth, the nuclear chaos at the center of the universe. The formula is a single, complex magical name made up of 35 parts. In the story, the formula is used to open a dimensional portal, but it is also a name of evocation for Nyarlathotep. The number 35 is significant. It is 5 multiplied by 7. The number 5 represents the four elements plus the quintessence, or spirit, and the number 7 represents the seven directions of space (the four cardinal points, plus up, down, and center). Their product, 35, represents the totality of the universe, and is a fitting number for a name of evocation for Nyarlathotep.
