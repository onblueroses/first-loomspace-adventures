### The Mirror

The Mirror (or the Silver Mirror) is a fictional mirror in the Cthulhu Mythos. The mirror is said to be in the keeping of the god Nyarlathotep.

The mirror appeared in the short story "Through the Gates of the Silver Key" (1932–1933) by H. P. Lovecraft and E. Hoffmann Price. In the story, the protagonist Randolph Carter finds the mirror in the possession of the 'Ancient One' Umr at' Tawil. Carter and his companions use the mirror to travel in space and time, witnessing various locations and epochs, including other planets and the future of Earth.

The mirror has the power to transport its users to other times and places. It is activated by the user saying the correct words and holding the correct thoughts. The mirror is also said to show the user visions of distant places and times, but it is unclear if these visions are real or merely illusions.

The mirror is described as being made of a strange, silvery metal and is said to be very old. It is also said to be very heavy and difficult to move.
