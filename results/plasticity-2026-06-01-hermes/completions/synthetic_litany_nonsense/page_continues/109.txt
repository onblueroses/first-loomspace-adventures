 

And now you have opened the way, and I will tell you what you have done. You have bound the powers of the earth to your service; the stones will speak to you, and the trees will tell you their secrets. The streams will bring you their knowledge, and the winds will carry your words. The powers of the earth are yours to command, and they will obey you. You have opened the way to the mysteries of the earth, and you may walk in the hidden places. You have called upon the powers of the earth, and they have answered you. You are now a master of the earth, and the earth is your servant. Go forth and use your power wisely, and the earth will serve you well. But remember that the earth is not to be trifled with, and that you must use your power with respect and reverence. For the earth is a living thing, and it will not tolerate disrespect. Use your power wisely, and the earth will be your friend and ally. Abuse your power, and the earth will turn against you. So go forth and use your power, but use it with wisdom and respect.
