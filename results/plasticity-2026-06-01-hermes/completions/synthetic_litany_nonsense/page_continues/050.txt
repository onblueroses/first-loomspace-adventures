 "There are many ways to translate these words, but they are generally agreed to mean 'Open the Way.'"

Mention of the Thassilonian language appears in several other sources, such as the Pathfinder Campaign Setting book, but to date no other sources have provided a significant corpus of the language.

## Thieves' cant

Main article: Thieves' cantThieves' cant was created for the Pathfinder Roleplaying Game to act as a secret language for rogues. It consists of symbols and sounds that can be used to convey messages discreetly. The language itself has not been detailed beyond this in Golarion setting material.

## Vudrani

Vudrani is the language of the Vudra region of the setting, which is heavily inspired by India. The language is referred to in several Pathfinder sourcebooks, but never given a detailed treatment. The Pathfinder Campaign Setting book says this about the language: "The Vudrani language is rich and flowing, with many colors and tones. It is the primary language of Vudra and is often spoken in major port cities."
