#### 10. The Chamber of the Veil

The circular chamber is 30 feet in diameter and 30 feet high. A shaft of pale light descends from the ceiling, illuminating an ornate stone archway carved with strange symbols. The archway stands in the center of the room, unsupported by any other structure. Beyond the archway is a shimmering veil of iridescent light. The veil ripples and swirls like liquid, casting kaleidoscopic patterns on the walls and floor.

If a creature steps through the veil, they are instantly transported to a random location in the Far Realm. The creature must succeed on a DC 20 Wisdom saving throw or take 44 (8d10) psychic damage and gain a random form of short-term madness.
