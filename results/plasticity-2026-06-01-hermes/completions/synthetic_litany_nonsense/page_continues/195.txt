 "Then will the Way be open to the Rite of the Red Mist. And the Red Mist shall rise and consume the land. And the People shall cry for their deliverance from the Red Mist. And in their desperation, they shall turn to you, and make you their lord. And you shall rule over the land, and the People shall serve you."

The book continues with the rite to summon the Red Mist, which the party does not read.

After a brief debate about the morality of using the Red Mist to rule the land, the party decides to destroy the book. They build a fire, and burn the book page by page. They notice that the pages do not burn easily, and the fire does not consume them as it should. However, they persist, and eventually the book is destroyed.

They then turn their attention to the remaining undead in the room. They are able to destroy them with relative ease, and then search the room for any other items of interest. They find nothing else of value, and decide to return to the surface.

As they make their way back through the catacombs, they encounter a group of goblins, who seem to be searching for something. The goblins attack, but are quickly dispatched by the party.
