### The Ritual

The ritual is to be performed by a group of thirteen cultists, each taking on the role of one of the Old Ones. They are to gather at the chosen site, a place of ancient power and significance, where the veil between worlds is thin. The ritual must be performed during a night of the new moon, when darkness reigns supreme.

The cultists are to wear robes of black, adorned with symbols of the Old Ones, and each is to carry a dagger forged of obsidian. The leader of the ritual, embodying the essence of Vrokthemnar, is to carry a chalice filled with the blood of an innocent, procured through a prior sacrificial ceremony.
