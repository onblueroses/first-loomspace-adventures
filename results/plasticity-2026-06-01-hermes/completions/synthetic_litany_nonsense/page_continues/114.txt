 "These are the words which thou must speak before the gates of the first chamber of the fourth circle. And when thou hast spoken them, the gates shall open before thee, and the demons that guard them shall bow down to thee and let thee pass. And thou shalt enter into the chamber, and there thou shalt find the talisman which shall give thee power over the spirits of the air. And when thou hast the talisman, thou shalt return unto the gates, and thou shalt speak the words again, and the gates shall open, and the demons shall bow down to thee, and thou shalt pass through and return unto the world of men."

There are many such pages in the book, each containing a list of demonic names and a set of instructions for using them to achieve some specific magical effect. The effects range from simple things like opening locks or causing people to fall asleep, to more grandiose claims like summoning storms or causing the dead to rise. Some of the names are repeated on multiple pages, while others appear only once.
