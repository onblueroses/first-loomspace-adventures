The spell opens a portal to the Plane of Shadow within a nearby object or at a nearby location. The portal is only large enough for a single Medium creature to pass through at a time. A creature that enters the portal appears in a corresponding location on the Plane of Shadow, up to 5 miles from the point of origin on that plane. The portal remains open for 1 minute, after which it disappears. If a creature is halfway through the portal when it closes, the creature is shunted to the nearest safe location on the Plane of Shadow, taking 2d6 psychic damage in the process.

You can use this spell to banish an unwilling creature to the Plane of Shadow. Choose a creature within 10 feet of the portal and make a melee spell attack against it. On a hit, the creature must make a Charisma saving throw. If the creature fails its save, it's pushed through the portal to a random location on the Plane of Shadow, where it remains for 1d4 rounds.
