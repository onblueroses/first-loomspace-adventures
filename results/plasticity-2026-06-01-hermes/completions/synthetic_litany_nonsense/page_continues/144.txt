#### The Revelations of Glaaki

"The Revelations of Glaaki" is a twelve-volume set of books written by various cult members under Glaaki's influence, telling of its history and legends. The books were published by a specialty printer in London in 1865. The volumes are individually titled:

1. Glaaki: The Solar Pool
2. The City of the Pyramid
3. The Dark Lagoon
4. The Dwellers in the Pool
5. The Masked Messenger
6. The Abyss of Ghosts
7. The Inhabitant of the Lake
8. The Will of Stanley Norton
9. The Crater
10. The Sea Nymph
11. The Insects from Shaggai
12. The Daughters of the Moon
