 

There are names in the ancient books of the dead. Names that, should you speak them, would flay the skin from your bones and leave you a mewling, quivering mass of bloodied flesh upon the floor. There are names that would cause the earth to rise up beneath your feet and drag you into the depths of its embrace, where you would spend eternity slowly suffocating, your lungs filling with dirt and rock and the dampness of decay. There are names that would call down the very stars from the sky, fiery comets of death and destruction that would raze the world and leave naught but ash and cinder in their wake.

But the names I have given you here, these are not those names. These are the names of opening, the names of passage, the names that will allow you to step through the veil and into the realm beyond. Speak them with reverence, speak them with care, and they will guide you through the darkness and into the light.

May the gods watch over you, and may your journey be swift and true. For there is much to be done, and time grows short.

The book is signed "Krelphor, the Blind Monk of the Whispering Sands."
