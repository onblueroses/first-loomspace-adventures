 "And they who know the 100th name, know the terror of the Iron Tower." The hundredth name is "Klendruveroth."

The Iron Tower is the prison of the demon prince Orcus. In the adventure module H4 The Throne of Bloodstone, player characters can discover the hundredth name and use it, along with a Wish spell, to free Orcus from his prison.

## Reception

The temple of Tharizdun in the World of Greyhawk campaign setting was described in the "Greyhawk Ruins" series in the early 1990s. The magazine White Dwarf compared the Tharizdun temple in the adventure to being as impressive as "a set from an Indiana Jones movie."

In the adventure "War of the Spider Queen," the dark elves discover that their goddess Lolth has been locked away by the power of a semi-divine being called the Percipient One. This being is revealed to be Tharizdun, and the dark elves must find a way to free Lolth. The reviewer from Pyramid notes that the characters must make a "pact with a seriously nasty evil power," and that the "major fight takes place on a gigantic Blackburn-scale chessboard."
