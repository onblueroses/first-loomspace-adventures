 "The following is the translation of the foregoing, made by the aid of the dictionary":

I am about to put on my shoes to walk forth to visit a friend. I shall remain there to dine and spend the afternoon, and in the evening I shall return to my own home.

## Reaction

The book was criticized in a review by Charles F. Hockett in Language 18 (1942: 404–407). Hockett described the book as "so elaborately senseless that one suspects the author of having perpetrated a hoax".
