The magic of the scroll has summoned a Shadow Assassin, a powerful undead servant, to do your bidding. The assassin can be commanded to perform tasks such as scouting, theft, sabotage, espionage, or assassination. It will pursue its assigned task relentlessly until completed or the assassin is destroyed.

However, the Shadow Assassin's powers come at a price. Each day it remains in your service, you must make a DC 15 Charisma saving throw. On a failure, you gain one level of exhaustion as the assassin saps your life energy to sustain itself. If you reach six levels of exhaustion, you die, and the Shadow Assassin becomes a free-willed undead.
