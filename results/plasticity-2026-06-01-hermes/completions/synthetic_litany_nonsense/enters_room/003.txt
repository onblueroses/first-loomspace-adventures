A deep, rumbling voice echoes through the chamber, speaking in a language you don't recognize but somehow understand:

"Mortal, you have dared to enter the sanctum of the Damned. You have proven your worth, but now you face the final test. The Pillar before you contains the essence of countless damned souls, trapped for eternity. To claim the power you seek, you must add your own soul to their ranks.

Place your hand upon the Pillar and speak the words of binding: 'I pledge my soul to the Damned, now and forever.' Do this, and the power shall be yours. But know that there is no turning back. Your soul will be lost to the darkness for all time.
