 A black mist, swirling and writhing, forming into the shape of a man. It’s eyes glow red, and it’s grin is wide and malicious. This is the avatar of the Demon Prince, the Bringer of Madness, the Corrupter, the Ender, and now it stands before you.

It speaks, and it’s voice is like a thousand chittering insects, like the screams of the damned, like the last desperate gasps of a drowning man.

“Mortal, you have summoned me. You have spoken the words, you have given your blood, you have bound me to your will. But know this: I am not some mere demon, to be commanded like a dog. I am a Prince of Hell, and I will not be trifled with.”

The figure steps forward, it’s black mist swirling around it like a cloak. It’s eyes bore into yours, and you feel a cold dread creeping up your spine.

“I will grant you power, mortal. I will give you the ability to bend the world to your will, to crush your enemies, to take what you desire. But there is a price. There is always a price.”

The figure grins, and it’s teeth are like shards of broken glass.
