## 17
A dreadful presence invades the room. You are gripped by a terrible chill, and your body is paralyzed with fear. You can feel a dark, malevolent force that seems to sap your very life away.

The spirit of Mordagor, the Mad Monk, has returned! His form is a ghostly apparition, a nightmarish vision of a man in tattered robes, his face contorted in rage and agony.

His voice is like a cold wind, and it fills your mind with terror. "You have dared to enter my domain, foolish mortals. You shall pay for your insolence!"

The spirit of Mordagor raises its arms, and the very stones of the chamber begin to tremble. A whirlwind of debris swirls around you, and you are battered by flying rocks and dust.

You must act quickly, or you will be destroyed by the wrath of Mordagor! What do you do?
