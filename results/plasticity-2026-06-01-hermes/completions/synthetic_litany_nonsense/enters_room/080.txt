.. is a creature of a type I have not seen before. It is very large, perhaps thirty feet in length and a dozen feet high at the shoulder. It is covered in scales and is a mottled greenish-brown in color. It has six legs, and a long neck that terminates in a head that somewhat resembles that of a horse, but with a shorter muzzle and a ridge of bony plates starting at the forehead and going back to where the skull joins the neck. Atop this ridge is a single, straight horn about three feet in length. A similar, smaller horn projects from its chin, pointing forward. It has large, expressive eyes that seem to hold a great intelligence.

It looks around the room, then focuses on me. It approaches, and lowers its head to the floor near my feet, clearly waiting for me to mount.

I do so, and it rises and turns, carrying me through the archway and into the darkness beyond.
