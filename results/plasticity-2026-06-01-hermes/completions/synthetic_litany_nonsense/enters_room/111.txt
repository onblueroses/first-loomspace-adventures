 The human mind is not equipped to perceive the entirety of it. The thing that rises through the portal in the floor is like a living pillar of green-black smoke, its edges shifting and changing, tendrils and pseudopods of black smoke drifting out from its central mass. But within the smoke, or perhaps forming the smoke, there are a hundred smaller forms, writhing and shifting. They are like black worms or snakes, but their forms are unstable, constantly shifting and mutating, forming eyes and mouths and other features in random places before dissolving again.

The worm-things seem to swim through the black smoke, as if it were a solid thing to them. They constantly emerge from the central mass and then sink back into it again. They are in agony, you can see that at once. Their writhing is tortured, and their mouths - when they have mouths - are constantly screaming.

The smoke pillar rises until it fills the room. The worm-things are not just black now. Some of them have patches of green and purple, bright unnatural colours. And some of them have patterns on their skin, like tattoos or runes. They are not natural creatures.
