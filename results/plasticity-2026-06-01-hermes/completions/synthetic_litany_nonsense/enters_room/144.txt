# The Second Circle: Summoning the Spirit of a Deceased Pet

Prepare the room for the ritual. Dim the lights and make sure the room is quiet and free from distractions. Light a candle and place it in the center of the room. Place a collar, toy, or other memento of the deceased pet near the candle.

When you are ready, close your eyes and take a few deep breaths. Focus your mind on the spirit of your deceased pet. Visualize them in your mind's eye. See them as they were in life, full of energy and love.
