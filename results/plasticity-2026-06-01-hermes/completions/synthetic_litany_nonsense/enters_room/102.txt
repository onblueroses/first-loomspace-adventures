  What is it?

The thing that enters the room is an Eldritch Horror, a being of immense power and incomprehensible nature. It defies description, as its very existence is beyond the scope of human understanding. This entity is not bound by the laws of reality as we know them, and its presence alone is enough to shatter the sanity of those who witness it.

The Eldritch Horror is a manifestation of the primal, chaotic forces that exist beyond the veil of our reality. It is a creature of nightmare and madness, a being that should not exist, yet does. Its form is ever-changing, shifting between shapes that defy geometry and reason. It is a mass of writhing tentacles, pulsating flesh, and gaping maws filled with razor-sharp teeth. Its eyes, if they can be called eyes, are windows into the abyss, staring into the very depths of the human soul.

The mere presence of the Eldritch Horror is enough to drive even the most stalwart individual to the brink of insanity. Its aura of madness and despair seeps into the minds of those nearby, twisting their thoughts and perceptions until they can no longer distinguish between reality and the nightmarish visions that plague them.
