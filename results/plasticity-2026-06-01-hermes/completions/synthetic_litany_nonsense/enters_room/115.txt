 [The door opens and the procession enters.] The room fills with a dark mist, and in the mist are the forms of the Elder Things, the ancient race that built the Shining Trapezohedron. They are hideous in appearance, with five-pointed heads and barrel-shaped bodies covered with ridges and spines. They have five eyes, five arms, and five legs. They move on land or in the water with equal ease. They are far older than mankind, and they are possessed of great wisdom and knowledge of arts beyond our understanding.

The Elder Things speak in a language that is like the piping of flutes. It is a music that seems to resonate in the mind. They say: We are the makers of the Shining Trapezohedron. We created it to look upon the Ultimate Gate, and to communicate with those who dwell beyond the gate. The Shining Trapezohedron is a window that opens on the Outside. It is a beacon that calls those from the Outside to come and dwell within our world. Through it we have acquired much of our knowledge and our power.

Know that the Shining Trapezohedron is a great key to the gates of the universe. It is a key that can be turned
