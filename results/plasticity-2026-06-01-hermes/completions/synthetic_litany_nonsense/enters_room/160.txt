 The foul creature of the deep. It comes for you. You must flee. The thing is gaining on you. You slip on the wet floor. It is upon you. It's mouth opens wide. You feel the heat of its breath. It bites down.

You wake up.

You are on the floor of the library. The book you were reading is on the floor beside you. It is closed. You feel strange. You notice something sticking out of the pages of the book. You pull it out. It is a note. It reads:

"Congratulations. You have passed the test. Please come to the front desk."

You feel a sense of accomplishment. You go to the front desk. The librarian looks up at you. She smiles. She hands you a key. She says:

"Congratulations. You have proven yourself worthy. This key will unlock the door to the restricted section. There you will find the knowledge you seek. But be warned. The knowledge contained within those books is not for the faint of heart. It may change you in ways you cannot imagine. Use it wisely."

You take the key. You feel a sense of excitement and trepidation. You go to the door of the restricted section. You unlock it. You step inside.
