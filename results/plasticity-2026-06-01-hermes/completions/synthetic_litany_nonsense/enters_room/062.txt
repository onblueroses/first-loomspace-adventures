The darkness is not mere blackness, but a complete absence of light, of substance, of existence itself. It is a hungry void, yearning to consume all that is and all that could ever be.

From the depths of this abyssal nonexistence, you hear a sound. A skittering, chittering noise, drawing ever closer. The sound of countless spindly legs scraping against the last remnants of reality. 

Emerging from the darkness, crawling up from the depths of the void, you see them. Spiders. Thousands upon thousands of spiders, each one as black as the abyss from which they came. 

They swarm towards you, a living tide of skittering arachnids, their countless eyes glinting with malevolent hunger. They surge onto the small patch of floor you stand upon, a writhing mass of bulbous bodies and flailing legs.
