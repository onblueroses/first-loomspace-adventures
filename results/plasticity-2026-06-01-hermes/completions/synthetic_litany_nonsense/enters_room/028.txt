  A ghostly form, a shade of a man, with hollow eyes and hollow cheeks, his hair is long and white, and his beard is long and white, and he wears a robe of black, and a crown of black iron upon his head.

"Who are you?" you ask.

"I am the spirit of the last king of this land," he says. "I am the spirit of the last king of the land of Mnar, and I have been waiting for you."

"What do you want from me?" you ask.

"I want you to take my crown," he says. "I want you to take my crown and wear it, and rule this land as I once did."

"But I don't want to rule this land," you say. "I just want to go home."

"You cannot go home," he says. "You cannot go home until you have taken my crown and ruled this land."
