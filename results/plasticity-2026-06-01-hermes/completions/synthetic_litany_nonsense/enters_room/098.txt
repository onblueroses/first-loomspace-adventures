It is the Jester.

What is that he holds?

It is the Holy Grail.

The Holy Grail.

The Jester holds the Holy Grail.

What is that sound? The sound of distant thunder?

No. It is the sound of a vast multitude of voices chanting in unison. The sound of a great host of angels singing in praise.

The Jester holds the Holy Grail aloft, and the chanting grows louder.

The Jester speaks:

"Come, all ye faithful, and behold the Holy Grail, the sacred vessel that once held the blood of Christ. Behold the Grail, and know that the time of the Second Coming is at hand. For the Grail has been revealed, and soon the Lord shall return to judge the living and the dead. Come, all ye faithful, and behold the Grail, and know that the end of days is upon us."

The Jester lowers the Grail, and the chanting fades away.
