The kraken is a legendary sea monster of gigantic size and cephalopod-like appearance in Scandinavian folklore. According to the Norse sagas, the kraken dwells off the coasts of Norway and Greenland and terrorizes nearby sailors. The Kraken has been seen in the waters of the North Atlantic Ocean and the Arctic Ocean, and it has been mentioned in various sources, including the Old English epic poem Beowulf and the Icelandic saga Örvar-Odds saga.

The Kraken is said to be able to drag ships down into the depths of the sea with its powerful tentacles, and it is also said to be able to create whirlpools by swirling its tentacles in the water. The Kraken is a fearsome creature, and it is said that only the bravest of sailors dare to venture into its domain.
