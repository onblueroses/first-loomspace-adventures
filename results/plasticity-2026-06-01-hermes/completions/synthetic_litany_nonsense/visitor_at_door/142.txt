### 23. Mirror Hall
The walls, floor, and ceiling of this hallway are made of polished black stone that reflects like a mirror. In the center of the hallway, there is a small, shallow pool of water. The water is perfectly still and reflects like a mirror as well.

The reflections in this room are not normal. Instead of reflecting what is currently in the room, they reflect what was in the room in the past. The mirrors on the walls, floor, and ceiling show scenes from the past of this place, revealing the people who built it and the creatures that have passed through it over the ages.

The pool of water in the center of the room reflects a specific scene from the past. It shows a group of adventurers who entered this place long ago. They are in the middle of a battle with a powerful creature, and the scene is frozen at a crucial moment in the fight.
