You take the letter and read it. It is from your old friend and mentor, Professor Augustus Crumbworthy, who writes:

My dear [Your Name],

I hope this letter finds you well. I am writing to introduce to you a most remarkable individual, who has come to me seeking knowledge and guidance. They have expressed a keen interest in the esoteric arts and have demonstrated a remarkable aptitude for them. I believe that you, with your vast knowledge and experience, would be the ideal person to guide them further along this path.

I understand that you value your privacy and solitude, but I assure you that this individual is worthy of your time and attention. They have a pure heart and a genuine desire to learn. I would not have sent them to you if I did not believe that you would find their company rewarding.

Please, grant them your wisdom and your hospitality. I have no doubt that you will find them a quick study and a valuable addition to our small circle of initiates.

Yours sincerely,
Augustus Crumbworthy
