 The door opens. The visitor enters.

The visitor approaches the middle of the room, then turns and faces the door.

The door closes.

The visitor removes their clothes, folds them neatly and places them on the floor.

The visitor kneels in the middle of the room, facing the door.

The visitor chants the following:

Vrokthemnar, Snorth, Klompronek, Vronth, Sneldrek, Mrolthengek, Klomphreth, Vrendrabmar, Snolthrolm, Plekriskem, Krendrenel, Smartrekol, Krelphondoth, Mrolthumek, Plendreol, Vrendrelth, Krelnumph, Snelthorvak, Plendoth, Krelphendrek, Smolthemroth, Vroklep, Klemphendrak, Snolthar, Smelthrenak, Krelphendoth, Vrolthemnar; Snolphondreth, Krelphem Skemphrok.

The visitor rises, puts on their clothes.

The visitor speaks the following:

The way is open.

The visitor approaches the door, which opens.

The visitor exits.

The door closes.
