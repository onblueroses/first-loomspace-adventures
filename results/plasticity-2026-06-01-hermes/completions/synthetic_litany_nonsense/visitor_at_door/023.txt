 "I am Vrokthemnar, and I have come to seek the wisdom of the ages. May I enter?"

The door slowly swings open, revealing a dimly lit chamber beyond. Ancient tomes and scrolls line the walls, and a musty scent fills the air. In the center of the room stands a stone pedestal, upon which rests an open book, its pages yellowed with age. The visitor steps inside, and the door closes behind them with a resounding thud.
