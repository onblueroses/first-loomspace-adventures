#### 24
The chamber is filled with stacks of gold coins, each stack reaching nearly to the ceiling. Scattered among the stacks of coins are chests, some of which are open, revealing sparkling gems, gold jewelry, and silver chalices. A large wooden table stands in the center of the room, its surface covered with more coins, as well as golden candelabras and ornate weapons.

The visitor at the door speaks. "Behold, the wealth of Snolthanth, gathered from across the realms and hidden here, in the heart of the mountain."
