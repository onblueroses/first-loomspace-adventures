 The door swings open.

Now, as I have heard it told, the doorway is not the end of the corridor, but the entrance to the true labyrinth. For beyond that door is a second corridor, and a third, and so on. At the end of each corridor is a door, and each door is guarded by a statue of a creature more terrible than the last. Each statue comes to life when a visitor approaches, and each demands a longer and more intricate password than the last. And should the visitor know every password and open every door, they will find themselves not at the end of the labyrinth, but back at the beginning, facing the first door and its guardian once more.

And so the labyrinth has no end, and the treasure at its heart can never be reached. But the labyrinth itself is the treasure, for it is said that those who wander its halls long enough will learn the secrets of the universe, and become one with the infinite. Or so I have heard it told.
