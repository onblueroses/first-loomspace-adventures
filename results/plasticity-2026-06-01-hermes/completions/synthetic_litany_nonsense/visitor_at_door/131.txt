 "Thank you for your invitation, but I am afraid I must decline. I am not the guest you seek. I have no wish to intrude upon your celebration. I shall take my leave."

The visitor leaves, closing the door behind them. The party continues on, the revelry undisturbed by the brief interruption at the threshold. 

As the night wears on, the laughter and music fade into silence. The candles gutter out one by one. In the end, only darkness remains - an empty, silent hall, devoid of all life and merriment. The celebration has drawn to a close, not to resume for another year, when the veil between worlds grows thin once more.
