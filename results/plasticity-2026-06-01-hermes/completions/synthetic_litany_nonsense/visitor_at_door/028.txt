 "I am the one who speaks the names. Let me in."

The door opens. It is dark inside.

The visitor steps through the door and into the darkness. The door closes behind them.

It is completely dark. The visitor stands still, listening. There is no sound.

The visitor speaks. "I am the one who speaks the names. I have come to speak with the one who knows the secret."

A voice speaks from the darkness ahead. "You have spoken the names and entered the darkness. What do you seek?"

The visitor replies. "I seek the secret of the darkness."

The voice speaks again. "The secret of the darkness is not easily given. You must prove your worth."

The visitor asks, "What must I do to prove my worth?"

The voice answers, "You must face the trials of the darkness. Only then will you be worthy of the secret."

The visitor nods, even though it is too dark for anyone to see. "I am ready to face the trials."

The voice speaks once more. "Then let the trials begin."

Suddenly, the floor beneath the visitor's feet drops away, and they fall into an even deeper darkness. The visitor remains calm, knowing that this is part of the trial.
