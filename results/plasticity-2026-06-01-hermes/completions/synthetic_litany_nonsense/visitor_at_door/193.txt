The door opens. The visitor enters. The door closes.

The visitor stands in a great hall. The walls are of black stone, carved with images of strange and terrible things. The floor is of polished obsidian, reflecting the dim light of the torches that line the walls. At the far end of the hall, a throne of black iron sits upon a dais of basalt. Upon the throne sits a figure robed in black, its face hidden by a hood.

The visitor approaches the throne. “I have come to speak with you, O Lord of the Hidden Ways, Master of the Black Door. I have need of your wisdom and your power.”

The figure upon the throne speaks, its voice a whisper that echoes through the hall. “Speak, then, and tell me what it is you seek. But know that all things have their price, and the price of my aid is steep.”

The visitor nods. “I understand. I am willing to pay the price, whatever it may be.”

“Then speak, and let us begin our bargain.”
