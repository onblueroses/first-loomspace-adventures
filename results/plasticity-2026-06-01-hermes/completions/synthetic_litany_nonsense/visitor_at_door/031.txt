#### 35

As you open the chest, you see a bright light emanating from within. Inside the chest, you find a small, intricately crafted silver key. The key seems to pulse with a strange energy, and you can feel its power even without touching it.

The key is a magical artifact that can open any locked door or container. However, using the key comes at a price. Each time the key is used, the user must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the user takes 2d6 psychic damage as the key saps their life force to power its magic.
