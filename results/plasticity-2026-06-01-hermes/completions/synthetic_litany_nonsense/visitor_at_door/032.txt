 

The door opens.

The visitor enters the hall of the Ironfolk.

The door closes behind them. 

---

The visitor is brought before the Iron King. 

The Iron King speaks:

"Speak, visitor, and we shall listen. 

For what purpose have you come to the Halls of Iron?

Do you seek wisdom? 

Do you seek aid?

Do you seek shelter?

Speak, and we shall listen."

---

The visitor speaks.

---

The Iron King speaks:

"Your words have been heard.

Your purpose is clear.

The Ironfolk shall grant you:

[response to request]

Now, you must depart these halls.

The way shall be opened to you.

Farewell, visitor."

---

The visitor is escorted back to the door.

The door opens.

The visitor exits the hall of the Ironfolk.

The door closes behind them.

The Ironfolk return to their labors. 

---

The Iron King speaks:

"The visitor has departed.

Their purpose was:

[summary of visitor's purpose]

Our response was:

[summary of Ironfolk's response]

Let this be recorded in the annals of the Ironfolk.

Let us return to our duties."

---

The Ironfolk resume their labors.

The hall echoes with the ringing of metal on metal.
