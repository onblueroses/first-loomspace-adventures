 "Hail and well met, friends. I bring a gift for you. The gift is the true story of the world and your place within it."

The congregation repeats the phrase. "Hail and well met, friend. We accept your gift."

The visitor says, "This is the true story of the world. Long ago, before there were men or women, before there were animals or trees, before the world was made, there was only the One. The One was alone. It had no equal. It had no companion. And so it decided to create a world in which it could live."

The congregation repeats the phrase. "And so it decided to create a world in which it could live."

The visitor continues. "The One made the world and the stars and the planets and the sun and the moon. It made the animals and the trees and the mountains and the oceans. It made the world as we know it. And then, to inhabit this world, it created beings in its own image. These beings were the first men and women. They were created to be the companions of the One."

The congregation repeats the phrase. "They were created to be the companions of the One."
