 "I am Tholbrok, and I have come for the relic."

---

The door opens. Behind it, a set of stone stairs leads down into the darkness.

---

The stairs descend into a small, square room with a low ceiling. The walls are lined with shelves carved into the stone, filled with ancient scrolls and crumbling tomes. A small table stands in the center of the room, upon which rests a single, ornate wooden box. The air is heavy with the scent of age and decay.

---

The visitor approaches the table and examines the box. It is intricately carved with scenes of battle and death, the figures twisted in agony and despair. The wood is dark and stained, as if it has absorbed the suffering of centuries.

---

The visitor opens the box. Inside, resting on a bed of faded velvet, is a small, black stone. It is smooth and cold to the touch, and seems to absorb the light around it. The visitor reaches out and takes the stone, feeling a sudden rush of power and dread.

---

As the visitor holds the stone, a voice seems to whisper in their mind, ancient and terrible. It speaks of secrets long forgotten, of power beyond imagining, and of the price that must be paid for such knowledge
