On top of the platform is a large, ornate, golden urn, about 3 feet tall and 2 feet wide at its widest point. The urn is covered in intricate carvings and designs, and it glimmers in the light from the hallway.

The visitor at the door speaks again. "The urn contains the ashes of the ancient king. You may enter and pay your respects, but do not touch or disturb the urn. When you are finished, you may leave the way you came. The door will close behind you."
