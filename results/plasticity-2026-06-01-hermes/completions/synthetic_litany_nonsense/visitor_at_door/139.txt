At the far end of the room, opposite the door, a stone throne stands on a dais. Upon the throne sits a figure wearing tattered robes and a crown of black iron. His skin is pale, his hair is white, and his eyes are solid black.

The King in Yellow, Hastur, sits on his throne. When the characters enter, he stands and spreads his arms in greeting. "Welcome, my champions," he says. "You have done well to come this far. Now, let us discuss the terms of your service."

Hastur's goal is to gain the characters' willing service. He does this by promising to grant them each a wish if they agree to become his champions in the world. He doesn't say what this service entails, but he assures them that it will be glorious and rewarding.
