 The visitor's voice is like the sound of the wind, the crackle of flames, the rumble of an earthquake, the roar of a flood, and the rustle of leaves, all at once. The voice is genderless and ancient, and it seems to come from everywhere and nowhere. The visitor says:

"I am the spirit of this place, the genius loci. I am the guardian of the threshold, the keeper of the secrets, the voice of the land. I have been here since before your kind walked the earth, and I will be here long after you are gone. I have seen empires rise and fall, I have witnessed the birth and death of gods, I have watched the stars wheel in their courses. And now, I have come to you.

"You have sought me out, though you knew it not. You have followed the path that led you here, though you understood it not. You have opened the way, though you saw it not. And now, you stand before me, and I will speak to you of the things that were, and the things that are, and the things that may yet be.
