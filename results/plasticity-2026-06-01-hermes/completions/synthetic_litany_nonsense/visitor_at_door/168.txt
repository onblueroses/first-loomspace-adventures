### 15. The Vaulted Hall

The door to this room is locked. The door can be opened with a successful DC 20 Dexterity check using thieves' tools, or the lock can be broken with a successful DC 20 Strength check.

This grand hall features ornate stonework, fluted pillars, and a vaulted ceiling. A stone throne stands on a raised dais at the far end of the hall, with a stone brazier burning with green fire to either side of it. A secret door is hidden in the east wall (Spot DC 20 to notice).

The braziers burn with green fire that deals 5 (1d10) fire damage plus 5 (1d10) poison damage to any creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of a brazier. A creature that touches a brazier takes 10 (3d6) fire damage plus 10 (3d6) poison damage.
