The ancient doorkeeper bows deeply, and replies, “Your Majesty, I have waited three thousand years for your return. Please enter and assume your rightful place on the throne of the Zorkers.” The great door slowly swings open with an ominous creaking sound. The King of the Goblins enters the room and looks around. His eyes fall on the throne, and he walks over to it and sits down. The ancient doorkeeper bows deeply once more and says, “Your Majesty, I am at your service. What is your command?” The King of the Goblins looks at the doorkeeper and says, “I am hungry. Bring me food and drink.” The doorkeeper bows deeply and replies, “Yes, Your Majesty. I shall return with food and drink for you.” The doorkeeper turns and walks out of the room, closing the great door behind him.
