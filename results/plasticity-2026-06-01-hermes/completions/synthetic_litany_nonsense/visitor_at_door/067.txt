The door opens and the visitor enters the hall. The door closes behind them.

The visitor approaches the throne and kneels before the king.

"Rise, visitor. What brings you to my hall?"

The visitor stands and speaks. "I have come seeking wisdom, great king. I am told you know the secrets of the ages."

"Indeed, I possess much knowledge. But wisdom is earned, not given. To prove yourself worthy, you must answer three riddles."

"I accept your challenge, wise king."

"Then let us begin. First riddle: I am not alive, but I grow; I don't have lungs, but I need air; I don't have a mouth, but water kills me. What am I?"

The visitor ponders for a moment, then speaks. "Fire."

"Well done. Second riddle: I am taken from a mine, and shut up in a wooden case, from which I am never released, and yet I am used by almost everyone. What am I?"

The visitor thinks, then answers. "Pencil lead."
