## 11. Antechamber

The antechamber is a long, narrow room with high ceilings. The walls are adorned with intricate hieroglyphs and paintings depicting the life and reign of the Mummy Queen. At the far end of the room, a large stone door stands closed, flanked by two imposing statues of Anubis, the god of the dead.

The visitor approaches the door and speaks the second set of names:

Vroklep, Klemphendrak, Snolthar, Smelthrenak, Krelphendoth, Vrolthemnar, Snolphondreth, Krelphem, Skemphrok, Vrendrelth, Krelnumph, Snelthorvak, Plendoth, Krelphendrek, Smolthemroth, Vroklep, Klemphendrak, Snolthar, Smelthrenak, Krelphendoth, Vrolthemnar.

The stone door slowly grinds open, revealing the burial chamber beyond.
