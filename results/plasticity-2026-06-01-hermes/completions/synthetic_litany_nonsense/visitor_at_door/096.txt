 If it is the intended visitor, they can pass through the barrier. If it is not the intended visitor, they are unable to pass through the barrier.

The barrier lasts for 1d4 + 1 rounds. If the visitor does not speak their name or the name of the intended visitor before the barrier dissipates, they are unable to pass through the barrier.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the barrier lasts for 1d4 + 2 rounds. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the barrier lasts for 1d4 + 3 rounds.
