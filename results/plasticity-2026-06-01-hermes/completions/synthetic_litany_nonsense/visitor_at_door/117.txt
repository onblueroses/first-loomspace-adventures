 The door opens. The visitor enters. The door closes. The visitor is within the Chamber of Names.

The Chamber of Names is a cube of black stone, twenty feet on each side. In the center of the room is a pedestal, upon which rests an open book. The book is bound in black leather, with pages of thick parchment. The writing within is in silver ink, and in the language of the Aboleth.

The visitor approaches the book, and reads the first page:

And the Aboleth taught these arts to their children, the Serpent Folk, and the Serpent Folk taught them to their children, the Dragons. And the Dragons taught them to their children, the Elves.
