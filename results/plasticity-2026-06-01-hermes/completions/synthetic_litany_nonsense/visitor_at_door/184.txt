#### 15
The visitor steps into the darkness, and the doors swing closed. The air is cold and still. The only sound is the breathing of the visitor and the heartbeat that pulses in the ears.

The visitor speaks again:

"Vrendrabmar, Snolthrolm, Krelphondoth."

A light appears, dim and distant, far below. The light grows brighter, revealing a stairway that descends into the depths. The visitor begins the long descent, and the light grows brighter as the visitor nears the bottom.

At the bottom of the stairway is a vast chamber, lit by a pulsing red light. In the center of the chamber is a stone pedestal, and on the pedestal rests a book. The book is bound in black leather, and the pages are edged in gold. The visitor approaches the pedestal and opens the book.

The pages are blank, but as the visitor looks at them, words begin to appear, written in a script that is ancient and strange. The visitor begins to read, and as the words are spoken aloud, the chamber begins to change.
