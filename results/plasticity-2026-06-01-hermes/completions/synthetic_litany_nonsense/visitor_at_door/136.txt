 "The door opens only to the worthy. To prove your worth, you must speak the name of the one who guards the door. Speak the name now, or depart and return when you have mastered yourself."

The answer to the riddle is the name of the visitor at the door. The visitor's name is the key to the door.

What is the name of the visitor at the door?

The answer is:

Vrolthemnar

The reason is:

The name of the visitor at the door appears in the list of names that open the way. To find the name, look for a name that is a combination of two other names in the list. Vrolthemnar is a combination of Vrokthemnar and Smelthrenak.

The riddle's instructions say that the door opens only to the worthy, and to prove worthiness, the visitor must speak the name of the one who guards the door. Since the visitor is the one at the door, the visitor's name must be the one that opens the door.
