The door creaks open. Inside is darkness. The visitor steps into the dark.

---

The visitor finds themselves in a grand hall. Black stone walls rise up to a vaulted ceiling. The room is lit by braziers of green flame. At the end of the hall sits a throne of black stone, upon which rests the Witch Queen of the Gloomspires.

The Witch Queen wears a crown of black iron and a dress of tattered shadows. Her skin is pale and her eyes are pools of darkness. She looks upon the visitor with a gaze that pierces the soul.

"Speak, mortal," she says, her voice echoing through the hall. "What brings you before the Witch Queen of the Gloomspires?"

The visitor explains their purpose. They have come seeking the Witch Queen's aid in a matter of great importance.

The Witch Queen listens, her expression unreadable. When the visitor finishes, she is silent for a long moment.

"Your cause is just," she says at last. "But the aid of the Witch Queen of the Gloomspires comes at a price. Are you willing to pay it?"
