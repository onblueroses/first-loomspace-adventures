### 121. The Maze of the Master
A labyrinth of twisting corridors and dead ends. You must find the route through to the centre of the maze, where the Master awaits you.

If you roll a 1 on the die, you have taken a wrong turn and must go back to the start of the maze.

If you roll a 6, you have found the correct route to the centre.

If you roll any other number, you are still in the maze and must roll again.

When you reach the centre of the maze, turn to 122.

### 122. The Master of the Maze
A tall figure in a black robe stands before you. Two burning eyes glare at you from beneath a hood.

"I am the Master of the Maze," the figure says. "You have done well to reach me. But now you must answer my riddle, or be trapped here forever."

The Master speaks the riddle:

"I am not alive, but I grow;
I don't have lungs, but I need air;
I don't have a mouth, but water kills me.
What am I?"
