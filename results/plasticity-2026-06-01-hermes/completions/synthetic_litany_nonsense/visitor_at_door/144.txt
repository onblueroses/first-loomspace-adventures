 It is the voice of the priestess, oracular and commanding:

"Open the way!"

The door opens. The priestess enters, wearing her black robe and carrying a black staff. She is followed by a hooded figure in black, and by the deacon in red. The door closes behind them.

The priestess walks to the edge of the well and looks down into the darkness, then turns to face the congregation.

Priestess: In the name of the Prince of Darkness, I greet you, my brothers and sisters, and I welcome you to this celebration of the mysteries of our Order. Let us honor Him in whose name we are joined; let us honor His presence among us.

The congregation replies: Ave Domine Satanas.

Priestess: In nomine Domine Satanas, I declare this ceremony begun. Let all who are not of our Order depart from this place! Let the profane depart! Let the unbelievers depart! Let the uninitiated depart! Let all depart who are not true to the Word and the Way of our Lord Satan.

The congregation replies: Ave Domine Satanas.
