 “By these names and by the power of this consecrated Seal, I conjure upon thee, O Spirits, and I command ye by the virtue of God, the Living and True God. I charge and order ye, and being armed with the power of this Supreme Majesty, I strongly admonish ye, that refusing to come, ye shall be condemned to eternal woe and confusion, and if ye do come, ye shall be bound by the virtue of this Seal, and by the Names which have been written thereon, and by the virtue of the blood of the innocent Lamb, which hath been sacrificed for the salvation of the human race, and which ye cannot resist. Come ye then in the Name of Adonai Zebaoth, of Adonai Amioram, and let there be no deceit in your hearts, for the Lord our God will know if ye be guilty of dissimulation. Come ye promptly and of your own accord, without causing any disturbance, without making any noise, and without producing any stench; for the place wherein we are assembled is holy ground, and hath been sanctified, and dedicated to the Spirits in whose Name we have been cleansed and purified.”
