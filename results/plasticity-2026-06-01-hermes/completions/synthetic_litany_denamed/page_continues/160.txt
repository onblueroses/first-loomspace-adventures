The summoning spell was complete. The candles guttered. The air in the room grew heavy and cold. The darkness thickened, pressing in around them. The air smelled of ash and smoke. The shadows on the walls twisted and writhed. The demon was here.

"Who summons me?" a voice spoke from the shadows. The voice was deep and resonant, filled with power and ancient malice. It echoed in the room, seeming to come from everywhere at once.

"I do," Damien replied, his voice steady. "I am Damien, of the Order of the Golden Dawn. I have summoned you here, Saphronoth, to bind you to my will."
