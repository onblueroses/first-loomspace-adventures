Below this, a line is written in the same strange script found in the margins of the book:

Lurkerum. Throdenoth. Yelmagoth.

The book then continues in English: 

Speak the words of binding, that the spirits shall not turn upon thee:

Naberus, I conjure thee, O Spirit of perversity and waste, to serve faithfully and obey by the words that I speak, for by the sacred names and rites of Heliorim and Heliorum I have called thee, and by the power of One beyond the stars, who liveth and was dead and is alive for evermore, I bind thee to my will, and thou shalt do my bidding, nor seek to harm me in any way, nor those who I love, nor those who I guard, nor any under my protection, nor any I hold dear. For if thou fail in this charge, thou shalt be cast out and made as nothing, and be forever barred from the light of the living.
