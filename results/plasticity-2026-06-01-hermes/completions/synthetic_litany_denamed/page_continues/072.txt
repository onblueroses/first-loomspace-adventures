 'Then shall the holy one appear unto thee, and with him shall come forth the holy angels, and the spirits of the elements shall be obedient unto thy command, and they will open unto thee the secret mysteries of their operations, and of the hidden things of the world.'

After reading the first paragraph, the reader is instructed to raise their right hand, and say:

I call upon thee, O living God, who hast by thy word created the heavens, the earth, and all things therein contained. I adore thee, I acknowledge thee, I believe in thee, I put my whole trust and confidence in thee. Speak the truth, O Spirit, and I shall believe in thee. Speak it not, and I shall know that thou art but an illusion, and an ignorant and deceitful spirit, unworthy of notice, and only fit to be punished for thy presumption and ignorance.

The reader is then instructed to again read the first paragraph, and then say:
