 

I am the Servant of the Highest, I am He who stands and fell. I have risen in the mighty power of Adonai. I have come in peace, and I will go in peace. Let there be peace with you, and peace be between us.

Do not turn me away from your door, O guardians of the way, for I am the Servant of the Highest, I am He who was and will be, Adonai.

Let me pass, for I have come with the light of Adonai. Let me pass, for I have come in the power of Adonai. Let me pass, for I have come in the mercy of Adonai.

In the name of Adonai, I call upon you, O guardians of the way, open the doors of the kingdom, and let me pass.
