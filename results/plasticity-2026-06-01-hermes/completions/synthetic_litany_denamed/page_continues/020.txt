 "Now, having spoken the names, it is time to close the book. As the book is closed, the power is released, and the spirits shall manifest."

The book ends with the following words:

"And it shall come to pass that the spirits shall be released, and the dead shall rise again. And the spirits shall be as locusts upon the earth, and the dead shall walk among the living. And the living shall know that the end is near, and they shall tremble with fear. For the end of all things is at hand, and the world shall be consumed by fire. And the dead shall be judged, and the wicked shall be cast into the lake of fire. And the righteous shall inherit the earth, and they shall live forever and ever. Amen."
