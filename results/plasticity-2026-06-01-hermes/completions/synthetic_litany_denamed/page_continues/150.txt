 "By these names, and in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, I conjure you, O all ye Spirits, to receive from me this Book, in a friendly and kindly manner, so that, by your assistance, I may be enabled to accomplish all things necessary and desirable for the honor of God, and the good both of us and our neighbors; and that I may have power to constrain the Spirits accursed of God, and compel them to come, and to do my bidding, and that I may not be hindered in any of my works or designs. I conjure and entreat you all, by the benignity of your nature, to accept this Book, and to assist me, and to be obedient to me in all things which are written in this Book. So may the peace of God be with you, and may His blessing be upon you; and let us say, Amen."

The magician then places the book on the altar, and the assistants kneel down, while he repeats the following:
