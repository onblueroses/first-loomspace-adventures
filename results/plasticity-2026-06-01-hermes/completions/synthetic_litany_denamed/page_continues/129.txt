 "By these names, and in the name of the Elemental Powers, I conjure and call you, O powerful Spirits, to appear and show yourselves visibly unto me in this crystal stone, here before me. And in whatsoever form you shall come, I do in the name of the Holy Angels, and by their power, constrain you to show yourselves visibly and affably to me."

This is followed by an address to the spirits, commanding them to appear in the names of the angels, and of the elements fire, air, earth, and water, and in the name of "Him who shall come to judge the quick and the dead and the world by fire. Amen."

The spirits are then told that they shall have "a firm and constant faith and belief in God" and "that you put away all deceit and untruth from you."
