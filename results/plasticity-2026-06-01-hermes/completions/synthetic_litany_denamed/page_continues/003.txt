 "When you have repeated these names, say:

O Luminous One, open the way for me, so that I may pass and penetrate the mysteries of the hidden knowledge.

As you speak, the sigil will open before you, becoming a doorway into a tunnel of shimmering light. Pass through the doorway and into the tunnel. It will transport you to the gates of the Luminous Citadel."

Commentary: The names in the invocation are those of the twenty-eight lunar mansions, which are divisions of the night sky based on the movement of the moon. In the Western magical tradition, these mansions have been used for centuries in the practice of lunar magic. The sigil is a key that unlocks the way to the Luminous Citadel, which is a symbolic representation of the hidden knowledge you seek to attain. The tunnel of light represents the path of initiation and the journey of the soul towards enlightenment.
