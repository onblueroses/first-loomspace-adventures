 "The preceding Conjuration may be thus rendered in English:"

I conjure and command you, O Spirits, all and so many as ye are, to accept this Book with good grace, so that whensoever we may read it, the same being approved and recognised as in proper form and valid, you shall be constrained to appear in comely human form when you are called, accordingly as the Ordinances of our Science require. In the Name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. Amen.

I exorcise ye anew, and powerfully conjure ye, commanding ye with ardour and vehemence to come unto us in a pleasant and comely form, without noise, deformity, or foulness. I exorcise ye, I constrain and command ye, by all the names wherewith God commands you. Come ye promptly, and delay not to appear visibly, and in a beautiful and agreeable form, unto the greatest pleasure of our sight, and that in the same place wherein we shall have formed a Circle, and placed our Table, and in no other place. I constrain ye to appear by the virtue of the Great and Holy Names of God. Come ye, then, by Adonai,
