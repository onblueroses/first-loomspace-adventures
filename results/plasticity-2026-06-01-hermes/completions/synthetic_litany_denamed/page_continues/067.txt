 "Here follows the end of the first book of the Secrets of Solomon, King of Israel, written and transmitted by the hand of Iheokh-avohu Abraim, son of Thebalon, of the lineage of Aaron, priest in Jerusalem. Blessed be the name of the Lord, Amen."

The second book of the Lemegeton, Theurgia-Goetia, follows directly with a prologue, "Here begins the second book called the Art Theurgia Goetia of Solomon, which was revealed to Solomon by the holy angel of God, called Armenadel, who also said that this work had been given by the Lord God to Solomon so that he might have complete understanding and knowledge of the universe, and of all secrets and hidden things of the celestial and terrestrial worlds."
