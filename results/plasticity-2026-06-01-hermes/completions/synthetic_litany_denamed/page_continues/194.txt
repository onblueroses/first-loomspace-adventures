 'The following invocation was delivered by the spirit Sauborim, and is to be uttered by the magician whilst burning incense of olibanum, mastic and aloes, three days before the new moon, saying:

I invoke thee, Terathan, the spirit of indelible wisdom, power and riches, to make me mighty in magic, and that I may obtain the service of Sephar, who cometh forth from the twelve gates of the world.
