 “By the virtue of these holy names, and by the virtue of the holy and sacred blood of the immaculate Lamb, and by the virtue of the blood of the prophets and martyrs, by the virtue of the virtues of God, and by the virtue of the seven planets, and by the virtue of the angels and archangels, and by the virtue of the four elements, and by the virtue of the bones of the just, and by the virtue of the sacraments of the Church, I conjure you, O spirits, that you have to come to my aid and help me in all my works, and do not leave me without your help. Amen.”

This page contains three seals, one of which is the Seal of Solomon. The other two are not identified.

The next page contains the names and seals of the angels of the days of the week, and of the seven planets. The names of the angels of the days of the week are as follows:

Sunday: Michael, Dardiel, Huratapel

Monday: Gabriel, Samael, Madiel, Mael, Ustael

Tuesday: Samael, Satael, Amabiel, Arcan

Wednesday: Raphael, Zaniel, Sarquaïel
