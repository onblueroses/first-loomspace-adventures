The Conjuration

The Ars Goetia includes a conjuration to be read to summon the spirits. The Lesser Key of Solomon (Weiser) has the same conjuration in English and in a "phonetic" Latin(?) version. I will not reproduce it here because I do not know its copyright status, but it can be found online, including a copy at https://www.sacred-texts.com/grim/lks/index.htm

The Ars Goetia also includes significant detail on the seals of the spirits, their appearance, and the powers they can grant the conjurer. I will not go into that level of detail here.

The Ritual

The Ars Goetia contains very specific and very detailed instructions for the preparation of the ritual and the evocation of the spirits. However, I will be using the Trithemian method of drawing the spirit into the crystal.
