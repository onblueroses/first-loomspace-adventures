 "I conjure you, O Spirits, by the most holy names of the immortal Gods of the infernal Abyss, that ye come quickly and without delay, from all parts of the world, to make answer unto my words, and to be obedient unto the command of your Master, who is God, in the name of the Lord, Bathat or Razon, who is seated upon the throne of his Majesty, and by the dominion of the highest God, and in the name of our Mighty Lord, that you come quickly and without delay, from all parts of the world, to make answer unto my words, and to be obedient unto the command of your Master, who is God.
