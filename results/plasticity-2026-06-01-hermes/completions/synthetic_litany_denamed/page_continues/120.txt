### The Second Conjuration

The second conjuration addresses the spirits by various names, but especially calls upon the most powerful spirit of the hierarchy, Ochim. The page reads: "I invoke, conjure, and command you, O Ochim, great and powerful spirit, and through you, all other spirits, by the most resplendent Light that exists in the highest heavens, and by the sacred names that are written on the four sides of the Archetype, which are: Seraphim, Kerubim, Arartuta, and Israim. I conjure and command you to appear in a pleasing form, without noise or deception, and to answer my questions truthfully, fully, and clearly."
