 

The secret of the seven thunders is revealed in the unfolding of the seven seals. The seals open the gates of the astral kingdom to the adept who has overcome the seven deadly sins, symbolized by the seven-headed dragon of the deep. The numbers 7 and 12 are sacred to the mystics and are found again and again in the holy scriptures. There are seven churches of Asia, seven golden candlesticks, seven stars, seven lamps of fire, seven spirits of God, seven horns and seven eyes of the Lamb, seven seals, seven trumpets, seven thunders, seven vials, seven plagues, seven mountains, and seven kings.
