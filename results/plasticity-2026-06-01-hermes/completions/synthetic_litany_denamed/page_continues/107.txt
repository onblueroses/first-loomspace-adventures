 "When you have said these names, the earth will tremble, and the spirits will come to your command."

In the 1998 film The 13th Warrior, the names of the twelve warriors appear to be inspired by the names of the demons in the Munich Manual. However, the names in the film are not entirely accurate transcriptions. The names as they appear in the film are: Throbenas, Maraith, Queloman, Iston, Kerudin, Astrolach, Volmuroth, Drabosaim, Olemproam, Cresilim, Bidremael, Octorum.
