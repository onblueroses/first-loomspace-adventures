 "As you speak the final name, a gust of wind rushes past you and the door to the chamber of the Great Seal swings open."

The ritual is very similar to the one in the original Ultima IV, with some names changed, and others removed. The final name, Lominem Adventu, translates from Latin to "the coming of the light".

### Ultima IX: Ascension

In Ultima IX: Ascension, the final game in the series, the Avatar is captured by the Guardian and chained inside a room on a volcanic island. The room contains a bookshelf with three books. One of the books is the Book of Good and Evil, which contains the following passage:

Know that thou art the center of three circles.
Remember that the circles are about thee, and that thou art of the circles.
Seek then the three powers within thee: Knowledge, Courage, and Compassion.
In balance, these powers will serve thee in thy quest for the Codex.
The Codex will then be revealed unto thee, and wisdom shall be thine.

The passage is a version of the inscription on the Ultima IV ankh, which also appeared in Ultima VI and Ultima VII.
