### Fifth Formula

The fifth formula is titled: "To make a girl come to you, no matter how beautiful she is."

This formula is used to summon a spirit to compel a girl to come to the magician. The magician must first draw a seal on his left hand using the blood of a bat, then recite the following invocation:

By the most high and holy names, Eheieh, Yoveil, Delia, Sittacibor, I command you, O powerful and magnificent spirit of love, Caspiel, to place in my hands the power to draw the girl [name], daughter of [mother's name], to me, so that she will do all that I desire, and to compel her to come to me, and to make her love me, so that she will do all that I wish and desire, by the power of the most high God, and the words: "I Am that I Am." Amen.
