The manuscript's author, one "Doctor Solomon Toas," is said to have been an Italian from Naples, but his name is not found in any records of the time. There is no reason to believe that such a person ever existed.

However, a "Solomon Tose" is mentioned in the introduction to the spurious Fourth Book of Occult Philosophy, attributed to the occultist Henry Cornelius Agrippa. This book is a forgery that was not written by Agrippa, but instead produced by the circle of Pietro d'Abano, who also produced the Heptameron, a grimoire that is very similar to the Lemegeton in many ways.
