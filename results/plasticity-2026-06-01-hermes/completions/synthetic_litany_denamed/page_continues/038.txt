 "These are the names of the angels of the planets, the elements and the zodiac signs, which are necessary to invoke when you want to have communion with the spirits." 

The manuscript gives the names of the angels of the seven planets, the four elements, and the twelve signs of the zodiac, all in alphabetical order. The twenty-fourth angel, Olemar, is missing from the list of zodiac angels in the manuscript, but is present in the list of angel names in the incantation.

The manuscript is illustrated with sigils for each of the angels and spirits, as well as seals and pentacles for protection and control. The author warns the reader to be careful when invoking the spirits, as they can be dangerous if not properly controlled.

The manuscript is written in Latin and is believed to date from the 18th century. It is currently housed in the Bavarian State Library in Munich, Germany.

## In popular culture

The manuscript has been referenced in various works of fiction, including the manga series Black Butler and the video game Bayonetta 3.
