 "I, Hanoor, have written this, which I have seen and heard, on this parchment, in the house of my master, the Magister of the Hidden Seal, that men may read it, who have the understanding to do so, and that they may learn of the Way that leads to the Black Throne."

I would remind the reader that the above quotation is translated from a very ancient manuscript, and that the proper names are given in their English equivalents. It is needless to observe that the ritual chants and the names of the spirits invoked would not be given thus in full in a modern grimoire, or book of Black Magic; otherwise, the uninitiated reader might be tempted to make experiments on his own account, and so get himself into serious trouble.

I have quoted this passage merely to show the nature of the ancient ritual of evocation, and the powers claimed by the exorcist. The modern ritual, though briefer, is not substantially different, and is invariably preceded by the casting of the Circle.
