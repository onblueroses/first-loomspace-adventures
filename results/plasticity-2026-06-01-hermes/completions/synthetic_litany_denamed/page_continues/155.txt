You shall answer them, saying, 'I desire the power to summon and command the spirits of the dead to give me true answers to my questions.' And the spirits of the air will grant you this power.

"And when you wish to call upon the dead, you shall go to a place where a murder has been committed, or where a human corpse has been laid in the earth, or where the ashes of a human corpse have been scattered, or where a human body has been burned; and you shall say these words:

'By the power of the spirits of the air, I command you, O spirit of the dead (here speak the name of the dead person you wish to call upon), to appear before me in the form in which you died, and to answer my questions truthfully.'
