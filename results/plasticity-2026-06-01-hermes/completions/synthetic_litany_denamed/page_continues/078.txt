 "These are the names of the 32 servants of the lamp. They are all subject to the seal of the lamp. When you wish to summon them, make the seal on a Monday in the hour of Saturn, and burn incense in their honor. They will appear and will grant your requests. The lamp must be clean and new, and not have been used for any other purpose. When you wish to dismiss them, say the names in the reverse order, beginning with Estramon, and so on, until you come to Throbenas. When you have finished, extinguish the lamp, and it will be done."

These names have been compared to those in the 18th century grimoire, the Grand Grimoire, which also contains a list of 32 spirits. The comparison suggests that the names in the Grand Grimoire were derived from those in the Sworn Book.
