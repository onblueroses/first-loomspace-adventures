 "When you have done this, you will see the Mighty Kings of the East and their Princes, and all other Spirits will come running with great joy and hurry, and they will be so happy to see you that it will be hard to believe. They will wish to hurry and get it done, to speak and answer you to their best abilities, and to serve you in whatever way you may desire." This is a fairly typical description of the arrival of the spirits in the grimoires. They are usually described as arriving in a state of joy and excitement, eager to serve the magician and carry out his wishes. This is one reason why I don't believe that they are demons or evil spirits, despite the insistence of the grimoires themselves that they are. In the Christian worldview of the time, any spirit that was not an angel of God had to be a demon. But I think that these beings, whatever else they may be, are not what the grimoires say they are. They are, in fact, beings who are willing to teach and help the magician in a spirit of cooperation and friendship. But, as I have said, the grimoires themselves are quite insistent that they are demons, and that is the way the magician is instructed
