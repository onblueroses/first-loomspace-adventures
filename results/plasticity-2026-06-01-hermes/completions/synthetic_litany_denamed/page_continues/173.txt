 "Now, at the utterance of the final word of this formula, there shall be revealed to the adept a vision of the Liminal World, and certain spirits shall make themselves known, desiring entry to the world of mortal kind. Be warned! Admit only one, and let the choice be wisely made, for the spirit shall abide with thee always, and may not be sent back save by the command of that power which first granted it ingress."
