 "Here the spirits in their order and under their proper names, do humbly present themselves before you, as your servants, saying, 'Command us, for in all things we are obedient unto your will.' You may now ask anything that your mind desires. When you have received everything that you wish, and are satisfied with their answers, then first command them to return unto their orders and place which was appointed them from the beginning, and that they go in peace; then say the following words:"

The text then gives a lengthy license to depart, beginning "O ye spirits, O ye angels of innumerable orders..."

The grimoire then continues with two further sections detailing rituals for summoning spirits, followed by a list of the powers ascribed to each of the spirits named in the opening conjuration.
