## In popular culture

In the 1994 film The Shadow, the villain Shiwan Khan (John Lone) uses a book titled The Complete Works of the Occult and Mysticism to learn how to control minds. He is also seen reading the Ars Theurgia Goetia, the first section of The Lesser Key of Solomon.

In the 2005–2011 dark-fantasy television series Supernatural, the Lesser Key of Solomon is an important plot element. It is used by multiple characters to summon and control demons throughout the show's run, most notably in the fifth season, where the brothers use the book to control the demon Crowley.

In the 2016 horror adventure film Don't Kill It, a demon from the Ars Goetia is unleashed on a small town in Mississippi.

In the 2016 horror film The Conjuring 2, the demon Valak is the main antagonist. In the film's interpretation, Valak is not described as they are in the Lesser Key of Solomon; instead, Valak is portrayed as a corrupted nun who is also a servant of Satan.
