### The First Father

The First Father is mentioned throughout the text, but the most direct reference to his identity is found on page 17:

'In the beginning was the First Father, the One who created all. He was known by many names: Throden, Maraith, Queloman, Iston, Kerudin, Astrolach, Volmuroth, Drabosaim, Olemproam, Cresilim, Bidremael, Octorum, Saphronoth, Yodulim, Iethreon, Marzabet, Caloneph, Theamorach, Belindath, Glomareth, Senaberoth, Hialop, Trifenath, Olemar, Vesporath, Khalepith, Estramon; Brashveroth, Lominem Adventu.'

This passage suggests that the First Father had 28 different names, each representing a different aspect of his divinity. The names are the same as those used in the invocation to open the gates.
