 "At this point, the spirits will come from all directions, and will say, 'What do you wish, that we should do for you?' You shall respond to them by saying, 'I wish that you should do such and such for me, without causing harm to me or to anyone else, and that you should do this quickly, by the power of the names I have spoken.'"

In the manuscript, the text is accompanied by several illustrations, including one of a demon or angel holding a banner with the word "Azielis" written on it, and another of a person wearing a hat adorned with pentagrams and other symbols.
