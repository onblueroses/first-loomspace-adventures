 "The invocation of the starry heavens, and of the powers of the air. The word Lominem Adventu is a corruption of the Hebrew, and means 'We invoke the heavens.' The names are largely corrupted Hebrew, and mean: The Lord of Hosts, The Lord of the Earth, The Lord of the World, The Lord of the Universe, etc."

The manuscript also contains a figure of concentric circles, with the names of God and other beings in various languages, and a statement that the circles must be drawn on the ground in the blood of a black cock and a white dove, with the operator standing in the center, to summon spirits.

The text appears to be a corruption of various medieval and early modern grimoires, such as the Key of Solomon, the Lesser Key of Solomon, and the Grimoire of Pope Honorius. The figure of concentric circles is similar to those found in these works, which are purported to be used for summoning spirits or demons.
