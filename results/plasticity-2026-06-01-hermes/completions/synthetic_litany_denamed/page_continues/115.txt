### Zinixo

Zinixo is the dwarf sorcerer who served as warden of the west for a short time in The Living God. Zinixo is described as being extremely short, even for a dwarf, and is morbidly obese. He is also a unique sorcerer in that he is not only able to see the 'node' lines of magic power that flow through the world, but is able to manipulate them to his advantage. This ability makes him extremely powerful, and he uses it to gain control of the sorcerers and become the first Almighty. He is eventually defeated by Rap and the other wardens, who sever his magical powers and place him in a dungeon in the sky. Zinixo is eventually killed by Death Bird, the goblin king, who is seeking revenge against the sorcerers who killed his father.
