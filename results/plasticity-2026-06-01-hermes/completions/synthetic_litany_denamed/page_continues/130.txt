 "The magician must then place his left hand on the head of the person to be conjured, and say the following words: "Conjuro te, homo, per nomen sanctum et justum et misericordium Dei omnipotentis..." (I conjure you, man, by the holy and just name and the mercy of almighty God...). The text then continues with a long list of names and epithets for God, followed by a series of prayers and conjurations.
