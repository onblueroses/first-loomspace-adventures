 “By the virtue of these names, and by the virtue of the holy angels, and by the virtue of the archangels, and by the virtue of the prophets and apostles, and by the virtue of the martyrs and confessors, and by the virtue of the virgins, and all the saints, and by the virtue of God the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost, I conjure thee, N., that thou appear before this circle, and that thou do give me a true answer of all that I shall ask thee, without any falsehood or craft; otherwise, by the virtue of God, and by the virtue of all that is contained in this book, I will damn thee for ever.”

The magician must then look toward the west, and make upon his heart the sign of the cross with the consecrated knife of the art, and then must he say:

I beseech thee, O Lord God, the all-powerful Father, that thou wilt destroy this N., and that thou wilt send him to eternal damnation, if he lie unto me, or if he attempt to deceive me in anything. So be it. Amen.

The magician must then take a new and clean needle, and make a prick in his left arm
