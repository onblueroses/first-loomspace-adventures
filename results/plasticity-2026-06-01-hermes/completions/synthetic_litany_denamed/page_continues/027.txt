 "Here endeth the Third Book of the Lemegeton, which is called the Art Pauline. In the Name of the Holy and undivided Trinity, the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost, Amen."

## Editions

* British Library, Sloane MS 2731. In particular for Ars Paulina, see fols 46–61 and 68–74.
* Robert Turner, ed., The Fourth Book of the Lemegeton (London, 1655).
* Andrew Colville, ed., The Pauline Art of Solomon (1770s?).
* David Rankine, ed., The Book of Treasure Spirits (Avalonia, 2009). Transcription of the Folger Shakespeare Library's V.b.26 (c. 1700), a combined version of the Heptameron and Ars Paulina.
