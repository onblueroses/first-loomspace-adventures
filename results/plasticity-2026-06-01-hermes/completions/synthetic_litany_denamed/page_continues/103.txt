 “This is the way, and the power and the truth.”

If this seems like a lot to memorize, it is. The original manuscript was written in Latin, which is easier to memorize than English due to its logical structure. The manuscript also included mnemonic devices to help remember the incantation.

This incantation is meant to help the magician see and converse with angels, demons, spirits, and elemental beings. The names are a mix of Hebrew, Greek, and invented words, and their meanings are not always clear. However, the incantation is meant to invoke the power of these beings and open the way to communication with them.

It’s worth noting that the Ars Notoria is a complex work, and simply reciting the incantation without understanding the context and the other practices involved is not likely to produce the desired results. The work also involves meditation, visualization, and other magical practices.
