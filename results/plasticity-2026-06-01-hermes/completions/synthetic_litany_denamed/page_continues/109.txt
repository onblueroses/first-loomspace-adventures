 “This is the end of the second book of the Lemegeton, which is called the Lesser Key of Solomon, on account of the smaller size of the demons described therein, and also because it is a smaller text than the Greater Key of Solomon. The third book, which follows, is called the Pauline Art, and deals with the spirits of the air.”

This passage is followed by another list of names, evidently an alternate version of the closing of the second book of the Lemegeton. It runs as follows:

The ending of the second book of the Lemegeton, or Lesser Key of Solomon the King.

Here follow the names of the thirty-one spirits of the second book, and their seals. They are all kings and dukes, and each has twenty servants, except the last one, who has only eighteen.
