 "These are the names of the spirits of the sun and the moon. When you wish to summon them, say the following invocation."

### Invocation

The invocation to summon the spirits reads as follows:

I conjure you, O Spirits of the Sun and Moon, by the King and Queen of the East and West, and by all the princes and princesses under their rule, that you appear before me, visibly and in handsome human form, without any tumult or disturbance, to answer my questions and to do my will.

### Appearance of the spirits

The text then describes the appearance of the spirits:

When you have said this invocation, you will see a king and queen appear before you. The king is dressed in a red robe, wearing a golden crown on his head, and holding a scepter in his right hand. The queen is dressed in a white robe, wearing a silver crown on her head, and holding a mirror in her left hand.

The king will say to you, "What do you wish of me?" You should reply, "I wish to know the secrets of the sun and the moon, and to have the power to command the spirits under your rule."
