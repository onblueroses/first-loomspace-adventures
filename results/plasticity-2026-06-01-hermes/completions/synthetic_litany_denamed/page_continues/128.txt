The book contains the following spells: *arcane lock*, *dimension door*, *drawmij's instant summons*, *knock*, *locate object*, *Mordenkainen's magnificent mansion*, *Mordenkainen's private sanctum*, *passwall*, *phantasmal killer*, *secret chest*, *teleport*, *teleportation circle*, and *true seeing*. Each of these spells is detailed in full, and can be copied into a spellbook or learned as rituals by any character capable of casting them.

The book also contains numerous rituals that aren't spells, including rituals for summoning and binding various types of elementals, the secret of crafting a *sovereign glue* or *universal solvent*, and a recipe for creating a *heward's handy haversack*. These rituals require exotic components and take days to weeks to perform.

Finally, the book contains a number of maps of the Elemental Planes of Air and Earth, showing the locations of various wonders and citadels, as well as safe routes of travel across these dangerous realms.
