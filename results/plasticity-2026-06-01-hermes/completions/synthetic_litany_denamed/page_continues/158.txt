 "And when you have said these words, you will see the Mighty King of the South appear to you. When he comes, ask of him your petition, and it shall be granted to you."

The Ars Notoria is one of five books within a grimoire called the Lesser Key of Solomon, an anonymous text (with editorial additions by multiple authors) that was compiled from other works in the 17th century. It is divided into five books: the Ars Goetia, the Ars Theurgia-Goetia, the Ars Paulina, the Ars Almadel, and the Ars Notoria.

## Cultural references

In the 1970s, the group of artists and occultists known as Thee Temple ov Psychick Youth (TOPY) based many of their writings on the Ars Notoria.
