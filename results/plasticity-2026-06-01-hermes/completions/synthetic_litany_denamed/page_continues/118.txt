 "By this invocation, the magician compels the spirits to send him a dream oracle, answering the question he has posed. The names are those of the spirits who bear messages between the gods and the human race. They are called by the name of 'luminaries,' and their names are the high places of the Earth." 

The words "luminaries" and "high places" suggest that the names may belong to the angelic order of archons or aeons, which was associated with the planets in Gnostic and Neoplatonic lore. The name "Octorum," which appears in the middle of the list, may reflect the Greek word for "eight," ὀκτώ (októ), and may be an oblique reference to the Ogdoad, a grouping of eight divine beings that appears in some Gnostic systems.

## In popular culture

In the 1987 film Prince of Darkness, the invocation is used to send a dream to the main characters warning them of imminent danger. The film credits the translation to "Romanus ex Paris Gnostics."

The invocation is used in the video games Eternal Darkness: Sanity's Requiem and Shadowman.
