 "The reader must observe that when he comes to any of these 32 names mentioned, he must bow down with his head to the ground, saying, 'I bow my neck to thy obedience.' Also, the reader may have a little bell of silver, and ring it after every name that hath Oram in it, and in the end of every particuler name, make a cross."

The manuscript is now in the Bodleian Library in Oxford, England, catalogued as Bodleian Library MS. Ashmole 1420.

## In popular culture

* The book is featured in the 2005 horror film The Exorcism of Emily Rose, in which a priest uses it to perform an exorcism on the title character, a young woman believed to be possessed by demons.
* The book is referenced in the 2013 horror film The Conjuring. Patrick Wilson's character, Ed Warren, says that the book is "not meant to be used as a parlor trick" when discussing its use in a failed exorcism.
* The book is referenced in the 2016 horror film The Conjuring 2, in which Ed Warren uses it to challenge a demon that is possessing a young girl.
