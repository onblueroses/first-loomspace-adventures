## The Ars Notoria

The Ars Notoria is a medieval grimoire, or book of magic, that teaches the reader how to rapidly acquire knowledge and acquire memory, through visualizations and recitations. It was particularly popular in the medieval period, with many manuscript copies surviving. Like the Sworn Book of Honorius, the Ars Notoria claims to have been written by Solomon, and was also banned by the Church.

## The Magical Treatise of Solomon

The Magical Treatise of Solomon is another grimoire attributed to Solomon, but is also known as Hygromanteia. It is sometimes thought to be the source of the Key of Solomon, though it is more likely based on the Key of Solomon. The two grimoires have many similarities, though the Magical Treatise of Solomon is shorter, and has more astrological content.
