#### The Necromantic Grimoire of Michael Scot

This grimoire is part of the Sloane Collection at the British Library. The manuscript is catalogued as Sloane MS 2731 and is described as an English translation of a Latin original, with a length of 5 + 3⁄4 in × 7 + 1⁄4 in (150 mm × 184 mm) and containing 85 folios.
