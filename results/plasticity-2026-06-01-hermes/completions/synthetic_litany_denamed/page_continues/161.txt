 "When you have said these words, you will see the spirits whom you have called come in their own form. You can command them to assume an agreeable form and do whatever you wish, and they will obey you."

This text is a fragment of a longer work, now lost, that purported to be the words of Solomon, a king of Israel in the 10th century BCE, on how to control powerful demons and spirits.

## History

The Testament of Solomon is the oldest magical text in the Western world, and the earliest to contain the concept of a 'magical book' and the first to claim that the Jewish king Solomon was the author. It is a Greek text written between the 1st and 5th centuries CE, likely in Babylonia or Egypt. The work is not Jewish in origin; it is a Christian or pagan work aimed at a Christian audience.

The work was translated from Greek into Latin, Hebrew, English, French, Italian, German, and Russian. The work is mentioned by the early Christian writer Origen (253/254 CE) but was likely composed earlier.
