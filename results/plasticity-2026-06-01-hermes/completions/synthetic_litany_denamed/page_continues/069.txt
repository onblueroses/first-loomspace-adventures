### Chapter 13: Concerning the covenant

This chapter contains a single paragraph, explaining that the spirits will only perform their duties if the conjurer fulfills his part of the covenant, which is to make offerings of food, honey, milk, and other things.

### Chapter 14: Concerning the works of the spirits and the way to perform them

This chapter lists the powers attributed to each spirit, and the manner in which they should be conjured. For example, the first spirit, Throbenas, is said to have the power to cause earthquakes, and should be conjured while standing on a rock. The spirits' powers include the ability to cause earthquakes, storms, and floods; to bring back the dead; to cause love or hatred between two people; to open locked doors; to make people invisible; to fly through the air; and to turn people into animals.
