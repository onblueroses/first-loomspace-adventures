 "When the first of these names is said, the angels will appear in the north, and the earth will tremble. When the second is said, they will appear in the east, and the sky will tremble. When the third is said, they will appear in the south, and the sea will tremble. When the fourth is said, they will appear in the west, and the winds will tremble. When the fifth is said, they will appear above, and the sun will tremble. When the sixth is said, they will appear below, and the moon will tremble. When the seventh is said, they will appear within, and all of creation will tremble." The text goes on to explain that the names must be spoken in the language of the angels, not in any human tongue, and that the magician must have absolute faith and courage, lest the angels destroy him. The text also warns that this is the most powerful and dangerous of all the rituals in the book, and should only be attempted by a master of the art.
