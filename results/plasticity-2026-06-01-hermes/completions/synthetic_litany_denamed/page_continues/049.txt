# IN THE REALM OF DARKNESS
The portal is like a shimmering curtain of black light. I reach out and touch it with my fingertips. They tingle, and the curtain parts like a veil, opening into a tunnel that stretches away into darkness.
I step through the portal and I'm in the tunnel. It's high enough to stand up in, and it seems to be made of the same black rock as the room, but it's much rougher and more jagged. The air is cold and smells damp.
The tunnel slopes downward. I follow it, and soon it opens into a larger space. It's too dark to see anything, but I can tell the space is vast. I get a sense of emptiness, of a great void that stretches away on all sides.
I stand still, listening. I can hear a faint dripping sound, like water falling onto rock. And there's another sound, a low moaning, like the wind blowing through a narrow opening. It's eerie and unsettling.
