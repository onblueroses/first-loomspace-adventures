 "By the virtue and power of all these names aforesaid, I conjure thee, O spirit Iston, that thou shalt not any wise hurt me, nor any other person or creature; but that thou shalt come peaceably and quietly, and do my will in all things."

Other names and sigils in the manuscript include those of Demons, Spirits, Elemental Kings, and Olympic Spirits, as well as sigils and pentacles.

## In popular culture

* The 2022 film The Invitation is based on the manuscript.
* The 2018 film The Devil's Doorway is based on the manuscript.
* The 2009 film The Grimoire is based on the manuscript.
* In the 2021 book The Devil's Elixir by Harris H. Moore, the manuscript is used as a reference.
