 "The doors will open. Enter and close them. Go to the altar, take the wand and strike the gong, and say:

"By the powers of the Kings of the East, and by the power of the word Abraym, I declare this Temple of the Golden Dawn properly opened in the First Degree."

The final name, "Abraym", is a Gnostic corruption of "Abraham", and appears in this spelling in several other places in the Cipher Manuscripts.

## Interpretation

The text of the Cipher Manuscripts is a relatively straightforward representation of the initiation ceremonies and knowledge lectures of the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn. Much of the material in them can be found in other sources, and the structure and content of the rituals generally follow the lead of earlier work such as the Blue Lodge Masonic degrees. The remaining content is largely a synthesis of the writings of Agrippa, Dee, Abbot Trithemius and other Renaissance and Medieval mystics, with some additional material dealing with the more recent ideas of the Theosophical Society.
