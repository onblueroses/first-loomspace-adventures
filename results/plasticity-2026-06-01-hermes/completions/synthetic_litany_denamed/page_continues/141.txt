## The Verum and The Sacred Magic

The Sacred Magic of Abramelin the Mage is a grimoire of Kabbalistic knowledge with roots in Jewish mysticism and Western esoteric traditions. The manuscript was written in a Germanic language, and the earliest known copy dates to the 18th century. It is divided into three books, the first of which is the autobiography of Abraham the Jew, an Egyptian magician. The second book is concerned with the preparation of ritual tools and the third with the actual magical practices.
