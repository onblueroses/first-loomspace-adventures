 "By these holy names, and by all others which are contained in this book, I conjure thee, O spirit Usagoo, that thou appear forthwith and without delay, in a fair and human shape, here before this circle, to do my will in all things that I shall command thee, according to the power that God hath given me, and according to the trust that I have in thee. I bind thee by the great name of Ioth, and by the power of the three angels Arioth, Magbiel, and Seth, and by the great name of God, Agla, that thou be subject unto me, and obedient unto my words, and that thou come quickly and fulfil all my commandments. Amen."

The manuscript continues with the seal of Usagoo, the seal of Bealphares, and the seal of the demon Belial.
