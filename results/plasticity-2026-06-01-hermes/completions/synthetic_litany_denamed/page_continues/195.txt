In the Ars Notoria of Solomon, in the prologue, it says:

I, Apollonius of Tyana, I found [these things] in the city of Alexandria, in the great library, among the books of Ptolemy the King, who put them [there] in the time of our Lord Jesus Christ. And when I saw that books of this kind were very much in use among all the philosophers of the world, I wondered that in the school of the Hebrews they were neither read nor heard of. And when I enquired the cause of this, I learned that the Philosophers would keep them from the Christians, lest the Christians, through the great power of the Hebrew art, and the great works which are done by the Hebrews, might be converted to the Hebrews, and lest they might abandon their own religion.
