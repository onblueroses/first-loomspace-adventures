 "When you have repeated these names, the spirits will come and do as you ask, either to kill or to heal, to bring or to send, to bind or to loose, or to do whatever you command."

The manuscript, written in Latin, is a miscellany of medical and magical texts, and includes charms for childbirth, prayers, invocations, and exorcisms. The book was probably written by a monk, and may have been used by monks for the instruction of the local community.

The manuscript was purchased by the British Library in 1993. It had previously been in the collection of the Earls of Crawford and Balcarres.
