 “By the power of these names and in the name of the Eternal Wisdom, I order and command that the spirits of the East appear in this place and obey me, in the name of the Eternal Wisdom, the Eternal Power and the Eternal Light. Amen.”

The spirits of the East are listed as: "Sathan, Astareth, Adonay, Eloy, Elohim, Sabaoth, Tetragrammaton, Amanuel, Abaddon, and Anamech."

The spirits of the West are: "Sathan, Astareth, Adonay, Eloy, Elohim, Sabaoth, Tetragrammaton, Amanuel, Abaddon, and Anatemate."

The spirits of the North are: "Sathan, Astareth, Adonay, Eloy, Elohim, Sabaoth, Tetragrammaton, Amanuel, Abaddon, and Anazimur."

The spirits of the South are: "Sathan, Astareth, Adonay, Eloy, Elohim, Sabaoth, Tetragrammaton, Amanuel, Abaddon, and Anazim."
