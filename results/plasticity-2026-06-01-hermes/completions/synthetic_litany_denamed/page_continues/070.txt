 "When you have said this, you will see the spirits coming as if from a great distance, and they will ask what you desire. You should reply with humility and courtesy, saying, 'I come to you in friendship and I ask that you have pity on me and grant me your favor, revealing the truth about the things I wish to know. In return, I will do all I can to please you.'"

This page from the manuscript was reproduced in Grillot de Givry's Witchcraft, Magic and Alchemy (1931), and the illustration was later used in the Simon Necronomicon (1977).

## In popular culture

* The manuscript was featured in a 2016 episode of the Travel Channel series Mysteries at the Museum.
* In the 2021 film The Seventh Day, the manuscript is a key item in the exorcism of demonic possessions.
