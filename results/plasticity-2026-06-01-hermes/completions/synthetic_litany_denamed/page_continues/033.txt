 "In the Name of the Most High, let the Spirits of the Invisible be obedient to this Call and let them come forth to serve the will of the Magus. May they come from the East..."

The manuscript then lists 150 spirits, each with its own sigil, divided into 5 groups of 30 spirits each. The first group is associated with the East, the second with the South, the third with the West, the fourth with the North, and the fifth and final group is not associated with any particular direction. The spirits' names are as follows:

East: Adon; Becharas; Camarion; Darachiel; Emodial; Fabaril; Gerol; Harach; Igarak; Jeseloth; Kadolon; Lamekesoleth; Mebaber; Naphar; Odrophach; Pambason; Quasbura; Rachiar; Saphathoram; Taphthartarath; Uraligon; Veras; Zegon; Zepar; Lamen; Tetragrammaton; Anaphaxeton; Primeumaton; Anapheneton; Cadas.
