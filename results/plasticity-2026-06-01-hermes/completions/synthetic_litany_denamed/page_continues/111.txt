 "These are the names that open the way, and I have spoken them. Let him that hath understanding count the number of the names, for they are the number of a man, and the number of a man is six hundred three score and six."

The names are those of the 36 Decans, each of which rules ten degrees of the Zodiac, or 36 x 10 = 360 degrees. The Decans are here related to the "number of a man," which is 666, "the number of the Beast" in Revelation 13:18. This relationship is based on 36 being the square of six, and the sum of all numbers from 1 to 36 being 666.

### The 49 Enochian Keys

The Enochian Keys, also called the Angelic Keys, are a set of 49 invocations that were allegedly received by Dee and Kelley in the Enochian language. They were first published in Dee's Angelic Revelations and were later republished in Casaubon's True and Faithful Relation. According to Dee, these keys were supposed to be used to open the 49 gates of wisdom and understanding that he believed were connected to the 49 "Tables of Enoch".
