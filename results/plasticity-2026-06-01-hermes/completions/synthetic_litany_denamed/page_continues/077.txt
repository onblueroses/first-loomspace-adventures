 "The Sevenfold Seal is opened. By the power of the spirits of the seven stars, I conjure you, O spirits of the Seal of Saturn, to receive my petition."

After this invocation, the petitioner is instructed to state their desire or request.

## Criticism

In an article published in The Pomegranate: The International Journal of Pagan Studies, Kennet Granholm criticized the book for historical inaccuracies, and noted that the author, along with many others, had failed to realize that the grimoire they were discussing was not the original work of the Chaldean magicians, but rather a 19th-century French creation. He stated that the book had "relatively little to do with Kabbalah or the Kabbalistic Tree of Life," and that it "contains many factual errors." Granholm concluded that "the Necronomicon tradition is largely based on flawed scholarship, misinterpretations, and an overactive imagination."
