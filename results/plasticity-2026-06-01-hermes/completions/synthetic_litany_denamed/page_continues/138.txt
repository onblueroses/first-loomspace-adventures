 "Let the sigil of the Lord of the Gates be made thus: let the form of a chalice be made, and let it be filled with the blood of a black cock that has been nourished on (certain things). Let the names of the Lord of the Gates be written upon it, and the names of the race of Watchers be written beneath. Let this sigil be shown unto all Spirits when they are invoked, and they will bow down and obey your commands, and you will be a Lord of the Kingdom of Shadows."

The manuscript describes the blood of the cock as being mixed with the blood of the sorcerer, but no details are given on how this is achieved.

The first line of the invocation is a combination of three names: Nyarlathotep, Yog-Sothoth, and Shub-Niggurath. Nyarlathotep is described as the face of the void, Yog-Sothoth as the key to the gate, and Shub-Niggurath as the opener of the way. The other names are those of the Old Ones, invoked to give the sorcerer power over the race of Watchers.

The Watchers are a race of spirits who are said to guard the gates of the underworld
