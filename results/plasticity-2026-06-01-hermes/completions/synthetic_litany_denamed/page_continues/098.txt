 

"By the sacred names of the angels of the eighth sphere, the powerful and mighty princes, I invoke you, O Lord, that you may command the spirits of the eighth sphere, that they may come and make me a sign when they are invoked, that I may know that I have the power to control them, and that they are willing to do my bidding, and to obey the commands of the sacred names just recited, and that they will give me the virtue and power which they possess in the sphere of their operations, and that they will do this day by day, hour by hour, forever. Through the mighty and sacred names of God, already mentioned, and through the other holy and ineffable names, which are written in the Book of Life, I conjure you to send unto me immediately, and without delay, the spirit Marbas, in a fair and comely human form, without noise or terror, and without any evil smell, and that he may do my behests according to my will and desire, and without any deceit, guile, or cunning, and that he may faithfully and truthfully answer all questions that I may put to him, and that he may not depart from me until I am satisfied in all things.
