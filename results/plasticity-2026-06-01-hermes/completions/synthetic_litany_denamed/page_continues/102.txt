 “Repeat this spell three times. It will open the way to the hidden realm. If you are worthy and the spirits accept you, you will enter. If you are unworthy, you will be trapped between worlds.”

I’m not sure what to make of this. Is it a genuine magical text or just some kind of fantasy story? The names sound made-up to me. And the bit about being trapped between worlds if you’re unworthy seems like a pretty standard magical warning, the kind of thing you find in a lot of fictional grimoires.

On the other hand, the book itself looks old and authentic. The pages are yellowed and the leather cover is worn. It has the feel of something that’s been around for a long time.

I’m hesitant to actually try reciting the spell. Even if I don’t believe in magic, there’s something unsettling about reading aloud words that purportedly open a doorway to another realm. What if there’s some truth to it?

For now, I think I’ll refrain from attempting any rituals and just focus on translating and analyzing the rest of the text. There are many more pages to go through. I’ll report back with any other interesting findings.
