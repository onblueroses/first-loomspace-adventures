 "When the sun is in Leo, and the moon in her increase, and the stars of the Lion are ascendant, then will the gates of the city be opened. And the faithful shall pass within to the halls of the everlasting, and rest in the gardens of delight."

More symbols and sigils follow, and the final page is inscribed with the words:

"The blessed and the wise shall enter, and their joy shall have no end. For the Lord of the Universe hath spoken it, and it shall come to pass. So be it."

The other two books contain similar, but not identical, content. The second book, _Liber Logaeth_, contains 73 folio pages, each of which contains anywhere from 49 to 130 rows of text. Each row contains 29 letters or symbols. The third book, _Liber Scientia_, contains 94 folio pages, with 49 to 130 rows of text, and 29 letters or symbols per row.
