 "I beseech thee, O Spirit Rumoar, that thou wilt leave thine heavenly mansion, and appear unto me, visibly and affably, in pleasing form, without any of the terrors and horrible shapes, which attend on thy outward appearance and divine power."

The author then promises to worship and obey the spirit in all manners, and to dedicate to it his body and soul, after the current life.

The manuscript has been dated to 1508, and was written in England.
