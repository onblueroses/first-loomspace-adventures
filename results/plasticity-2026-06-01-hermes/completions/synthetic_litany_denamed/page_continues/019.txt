The pentacle of Saturn, for the conjuring of spirits of the nature of Saturn.

This pentacle is of great power to compel spirits of the nature of Saturn to come before the circle when it is shown to them. It is to be made on a plate of lead, in the day and hour of Saturn, when the planet is in the ascendant, and the waxing moon is in the sign of Capricorn, and the sun is in Aquarius. The magician must be robed in black, and must wear upon his breast the seal of the great king Haelbeck. The spirits of Saturn are slow to act and to obey, but they are excellent in the providing of good familiars to the magician, and in the bringing of dreams to the sleeping, and in the knowledge of secret and hidden things, and in the opening of locks, and in the bringing of riches.
