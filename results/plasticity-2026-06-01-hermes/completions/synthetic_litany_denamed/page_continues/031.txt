 "The sun will appear shining upon the earth, and when you see this, you will see the angels guiding the chariot of the sun, and you will hear the voice of the sun in the heavens, and you will see the angels in the chariot of the sun. Do not be afraid, for the angels are friendly to you and will speak with you. Say to the angels, 'O angels of the sun, I conjure you and your Lord in the name of the Lord of the whole world, that you will not move from this place until I have spoken with you. I conjure you by the Lord Adonai, by this name, by the name of the archangel Michael, that you will not move from this place until you have heard my words and my voice.'"

It ends with: "When you wish the sun to go, say to the angels, 'In the name of the Lord, go in peace, for I have conjured you by the Lord of the whole world, that you will come to me on such a day and in such an hour.' And the angels will say, 'We will come to you in peace.' And when they have said this, the sun will move and go in peace."

The Key of Solomon
