 "If it is necessary to add a further constraint, say as follows: 'I conjure you, spirits, by all these names, to obey my command, just as you must obey the commands of the angels who serve in these planetary hierarchies, who are called [list of names], and by the name of the Almighty, who is [long list of names].'"

## History

A copy of the manuscript was owned by the English scholar and astrologer John Dee, who had one of the largest libraries in England in his time. After Dee's death, the manuscript was owned by the English antiquary and collector Elias Ashmole, who bequeathed it, along with much of Dee's library, to the Bodleian Library in Oxford.

The manuscript was first published in 1801 in a collection of medieval magical texts edited by Ebenezer Sibly and his brother. The manuscript has since been republished several times, including in a 1994 edition with commentary by Stephen Skinner and David Rankine.
