 "When this is done, he shall see a great flame of fire out of the stone, and so the devil shall appear in it in what form he will, and shall ask the master what he will have. Then shall he ask what he will, and he shall have it of him."

The page continues with instructions for the proper way to dismiss the spirit once the desired answer has been obtained.

## In popular culture

The manuscript is the primary plot element in the 2012 novel The Demonologist by Andrew Pyper.
