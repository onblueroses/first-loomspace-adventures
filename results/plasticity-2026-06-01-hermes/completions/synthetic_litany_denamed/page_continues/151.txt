 "Thee I invoke, O great and terrible God, Eternally blazing, Eternally ruling, who cast down the mighty and exalt the humble, who art the ruler and director of all creation, who dost grant unto thy servants, the Seal of Solomon, to bind and compel the aerial spirits unto obedience, and who hast delivered unto me the reins of the Kingdom of the Elements, and who hast crowned me with the power of the Sun."
