## The Unfamiliar

The Unfamiliar is a term used to describe a group of supernatural entities that exist outside of human understanding or comprehension. These beings are not bound by the laws of reality and possess abilities that defy explanation. They are often associated with chaos, madness, and the breaking down of rational thought.

The Unfamiliar are said to inhabit the spaces between dimensions, existing in a state of non-existence until they are summoned or drawn into the physical world. They are attracted to places of great emotional turmoil or suffering, feeding off the negative energy generated by these locations.

Encounters with The Unfamiliar are often described as surreal and dreamlike, with witnesses reporting strange sensations, hallucinations, and a sense of unreality. Those who come into contact with these entities often suffer from long-term psychological trauma, as their minds struggle to comprehend the incomprehensible nature of these beings.
