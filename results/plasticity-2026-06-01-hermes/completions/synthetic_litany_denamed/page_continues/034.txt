 "When this is said, sacrifice to me your mind, your body, your soul. Then the path will be open to you, and to you alone. And you may pass through the realms of the living and the dead."

The page ends with the words: "HAIL SATAN!"

In the fourth episode, Sister Andrea (Andrea Riseborough) reveals to Father Marcus (Ben Daniels) that the names are those of the 30 pieces of silver that Judas received for betraying Jesus. The pieces became a metaphor for the scattered, lost, and damned, cursed to wander the earth as demons. The "Lumen" itself is a collection of names that, when read together, open the "doors to hell".

## Production

The prop used in the series was created by a team led by prop master Brad Einhorn. It was designed by graphic artist Lindsay Arnold, who drew the pages by hand. The book took two months to complete. The cover was made from heavyweight, acid-free paper, and the pages from tea-stained, unbleached parchment. The text was in Latin, Italian, Aramaic, Greek, and Canaanite, written with calligraphy pens and paint brushes. The Lumen was then distressed to make it look old
