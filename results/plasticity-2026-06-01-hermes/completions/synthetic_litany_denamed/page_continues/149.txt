 "At this point, the way is open, and the spirits of the 8th legion will appear in their true forms."

## In popular culture

The manuscript came to wider attention after being depicted in the 2016 film The Conjuring 2. In the film, Lorraine Warren, a psychic portrayed by Vera Farmiga, explains that the manuscript was written by "a monk who tried to summon demons" and that "the book was used to compile the demonology in the Warren archives". The film portrays the manuscript as a collection of spells for summoning demons, and the film's plot revolves around the Hodgson family being haunted by a demon that was summoned from the book's pages. In reality, the manuscript is not a collection of spells, and there is no evidence that the manuscript was ever used to summon demons. Additionally, the book is not written by a monk, but by a magician.
