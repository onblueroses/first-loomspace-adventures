 "In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, Amen. In the name of the Lord Jesus Christ, I conjure you, O ye Spirits, and command you to appear before this Circle in fair and human shape, without any deformity or horror, to answer in all things truly, as I shall charge you, and to do and perform all my commandments peaceably, without any delay, and without any harm or hurt unto me or any other living creature, so the grace of God be with me and keep me from all evil. Amen."
