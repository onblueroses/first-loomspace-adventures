 "The repetition of these names three times, with the left hand extended towards the South, and the right hand towards the North, will open all the passages of the spirits of the Earth. After you have repeated these names three times, you will see before you, on the Southern side, a path lined with cypress trees, at the end of which is a large open space, and in the midst of the open space, a house built of the heaviest stones, with an iron door, very strongly bolted. Go to this door, striking it three times with the Cane of the Magistery, and say at the first knock: 'Throbenas'; at the second: 'Bidremael'; and at the third: 'Olemproam'. The door will open, and a Black Centaur with a large bow will appear, who will ask you what you want. Take care not to show any fear nor to retreat. Reply that you wish to enter, and that you have the authority to do so. He will then ask you for the Sign of the Magistery.
