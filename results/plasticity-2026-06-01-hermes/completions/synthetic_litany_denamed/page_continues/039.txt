 "I will also speak the names of the Kings of the East, which are very necessary in this work, and they are these:"

At this point the text breaks off, and the remaining three pages of the manuscript are missing. The invocation is closely related to the Enochian magical system of John Dee, which was popularized in the late 19th and early 20th centuries by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn. In particular, it is very similar to the invocation used in the Golden Dawn's Enochian Chess ritual, which begins:

"THEKEBSU, MADZILODARP, LONCHOZODAV, KALOZOPET, IADNAMAD, ZODIRIDZOD, ZODANOR, ZODILAD, ZODIMERAN, ZODILOD, ZODIRIDZOD, ZODANOR, ZODILAD, ZODIMERAN, ZODILOD, ZODIRIDZOD, ZODANOR, ZODILAD, ZODIMERAN, ZODILOD, ZODIRIDZOD, ZODANOR, ZODILAD, ZODIMERAN, ZODI
