 "So shall the path be opened and the gates of the hidden kingdom be thrown wide. And the Angel of the Threshold shall admit the adept into the divine presence. The holy names shall be his seal and his protection, and he shall pass within the veil. Speak the names that open the way."

The names mentioned are apparently derived from a number of sources, including the Enochian language of the occultist John Dee, the biblical apocrypha, and the Ars Goetia, a medieval grimoire. The passage itself is likely a pastiche of similar invocations found in occult literature, such as the Lesser Key of Solomon, a grimoire on demonology compiled in the mid-17th century from earlier materials.

### The song "Rite of Spring"

The song "Rite of Spring", from the 1990 album Season Cycle, contains a passage that is a phonetic English approximation of a Latin text from the Requiem Mass. The passage is sung twice, the second time as a round.

The lyrics, as sung:

Ora pro nobis
Deum le roy
Miserere nobis
Deum le roy
Altissimus ora pro nobis
Deum le roy
Miserere nobis
