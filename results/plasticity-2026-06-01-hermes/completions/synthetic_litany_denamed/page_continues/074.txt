The Grand Key of the East follows, and then the Grand Key of the West, the Grand Key of the North, and the Grand Key of the South. These are followed by a series of invocations and conjurations that involve the repetition of long lists of names of power.

### Part 2: The Consecration of the Circle

The Consecration of the Circle begins with a long invocation of the angels, archangels, holy ministers and evil spirits, and the like, followed by a series of exorcisms and benedictions.

### Part 3: The Conjuration of the King

The third part of the manuscript, The Conjuration of the King, is the most well-known part of the work. It involves a long conjuration to summon the spirit Oberion, which requires the prior preparation of a seal, and a series of sacrifices and ritual actions to be performed at certain astrological times. The conjuration is followed by a series of additional conjurations to summon other spirits, including the seven planetary spirits, the Olympic spirits, and the elemental spirits.
