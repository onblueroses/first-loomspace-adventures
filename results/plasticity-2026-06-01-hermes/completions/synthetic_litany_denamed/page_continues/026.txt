The ritual then continues with the invocation of the goddess, the summoning of the elemental powers, and the actual invocation of the angelic force. At the end of the ritual, the operator must close the temple by reversing the order of the opening ritual.

In the Invocation of the Bornless One, the magician identifies himself with the god Set, also known as Had or Hadit. Set is the complement to Horus, and the brother of Osiris. In Egyptian myth, Set slays Osiris and is in turn defeated by Horus in the battle for the rulership of Egypt. Set is the god of the desert, of storms, and of chaos. He is also the god of initiation and of the magical arts. In the ritual, the magician identifies himself with Set, and invokes the power of the god to aid him in his work.
