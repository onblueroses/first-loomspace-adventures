 "I beseech you, o great spirit Lominem Adventu, to rise up and send forth your spirits to bring me the woman of my heart, that she may be mine forever."

## The text

The text is written in Latin and Hebrew. It is heavily based on the earlier Liber Juratus, with which it shares the chapter structure and many prayers, but with the addition of some other material, including a version of the Ars Notoria. The text is notable for its early use of the word "Abracadabra", which is described as having magical powers.
