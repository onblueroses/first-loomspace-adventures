## In popular culture

In the 1980s and 1990s, The Arbatel of Magic was used by some members of the worldwide New Age movement as a guide for spiritual development and the proper use of magic.

The 2009 horror film The Blair Witch Project also featured a character named Elly Kedward, who was, according to a fictional mythology within the film, a woman who was hanged for witchcraft in the 18th century. This character was supposedly based on a real woman named Heather Donahue, who was involved in the making of the film. However, the filmmakers have stated that the character of Elly Kedward was entirely fictional and not based on any real person.
