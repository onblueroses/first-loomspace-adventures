 “I am called Nephren-Ka Nai Heliu Gamos, a visitor to your land from a distant place. I come seeking the wisdom of the masters of the Order of the Black Sun.”

The door opens.

The visitor steps inside. The door closes behind him.

The visitor stands in a small room. Before him is another door, smaller than the one he has entered, and to his right is a window through which he can see the interior of the temple. The room he is in is lit by a lamp hanging from the ceiling.

Within the temple, he sees a large, circular chamber. In the center of the chamber is a raised platform, upon which stands an altar of black stone. The altar is surrounded by a circle of twelve pillars, each carved from black stone. The floor of the chamber is also black, and polished to a mirror finish. The walls are covered with murals depicting strange and otherworldly scenes.

The visitor looks through the window, studying the murals. He sees scenes of great battles, with armies of men and monsters clashing in titanic struggles.
