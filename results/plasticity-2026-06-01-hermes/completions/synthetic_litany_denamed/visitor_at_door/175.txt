 The doors open.

The visitor enters, and walks to the center of the room, where they kneel and prostrate themselves.

The visitor stands and speaks.

I have come to be your servant. I have come to honor and uphold your ways. I have come to seek your wisdom and power.

I ask you, great ones, to accept me into your ranks, to share with me your secrets, to teach me your ways, to grant me your power.

I have come prepared to offer myself to you. I have come prepared to offer you my life, my will, my service.

I await your command. I await your judgment. I await your acceptance.

The visitor kneels again, and waits.

One of the figures at the table stands and approaches the visitor. They place a hand on the visitor's head.

You have come seeking power, seeking knowledge, seeking purpose. You have come to the right place. But the path you seek to walk is not an easy one. It requires dedication, sacrifice, and unwavering commitment.
