 I ask, “Who are you?” and the visitor answers, “I am the angel of the shrine.” I say, “Enter, in the name of the living god.” The angel enters, and greets me with the words, “Peace be with you.” I answer, “And with you also.” The angel speaks, and I listen.

[The angel speaks as follows.]

“In the beginning was the void, and the void was with God, and the void was God. All things were made by it, and without it was not anything made that was made. In the void was life, and the life was the light of humanity. The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness cannot grasp it.

“The void is the source of all things, and from it all things proceed. In the beginning, it brought forth the heavens and the earth. The earth was without form, and void, and darkness was upon the face of the deep. The spirit of the void moved upon the face of the waters, and said, ‘Let there be light,’ and there was light. The light was divided from the darkness, and the darkness was called night, and the light was called day. Thus began the first of the days of creation.
