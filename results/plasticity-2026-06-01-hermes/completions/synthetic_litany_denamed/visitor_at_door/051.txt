 

I come to the gate of the palace, and my voice is the key that opens the door. 

The door opens. The visitor enters. 

I come in the name of the highest, under the guidance of the holy ones, in the power of the name of names. 

The visitor stands before the altar. 

Before the altar I stand, an altar consecrated to the highest. 

The visitor consecrates the altar with fire and with water. 

By fire and by water I consecrate this altar in the name of the highest. 

The visitor speaks. 

By all the powers of the realms above and below, I call upon the holy guardian angel of this sacred place to witness my actions and to lend aid to this work of the magic of Light. 

The angel of the place comes to witness the ritual. 

In the presence of the holy guardian of this place, I dedicate and consecrate this altar to the highest, that it may serve as the foundation and center of the work of the magic of Light. 

The visitor consecrates the altar with fire and with water again.
