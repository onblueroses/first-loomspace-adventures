I am a friend to the tower and to the archivist. I ask to enter.

The door to the tower opens. Inside, the archivist greets the visitor.

The visitor says:

I have come to seek knowledge of the ritual of the opening of the way.

The archivist nods. They say:

The ritual of the opening of the way is an ancient and powerful rite. It allows the caster to open a portal to distant realms and dimensions. However, the ritual is complex and requires great skill and preparation.

To begin, the caster must inscribe a circle of power upon the ground. This circle must be drawn with a mixture of ash, salt, and the caster's own blood. Within the circle, the caster must place five candles at the points of a pentagram. Each candle must be made from the tallow of a different creature - a wolf, a bear, an eagle, a serpent, and a stag.

Once the circle is prepared, the caster must light the candles and recite the invocation of opening. This invocation calls upon the powers of the elements and the forces of the universe to open a gateway. The words of the invocation are as follows:

"By the power of earth, air, fire, and water
