 "The way is open to the keeper of the key." The host answers: "The key is within my hand." The visitor replies: "Enter, and be welcome."

The host opens the door and the visitor enters. The door is closed and locked once again. The host and visitor embrace, and the host says: "What is your name?" The visitor replies with his or her magical name. The host says: "By this name I greet you, (magical name)."

If there are others present in the ritual place, they join in a circle around the host and visitor, and all join hands. The host says: "In the presence of the infinite, the door has opened and the way is clear. Let us now invoke the powers of this place and time, that our work may be blessed and protected." All present recite the following invocation:

By the center of all things, and the circle of infinity,

By the six powers of creation and the unity that binds them,

By the names of the archons of the ten stations, and the spirits of the four elements,

By the powers of the realm of light, and the mysteries of the realm of darkness,

By the sacred geometry of the heavens, and the secret music of the spheres,
