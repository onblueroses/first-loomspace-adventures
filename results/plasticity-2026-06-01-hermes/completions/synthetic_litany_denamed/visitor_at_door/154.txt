"Come. You have been chosen."

"Stop. Kneel."

I kneel. The floor is cold and hard beneath my knees. I hear the visitor moving around me, and then they place their hands on my head. They speak words of power, and I feel a rush of energy flowing through me. It is a cold, dark energy, and it fills me with a sense of dread and terror. But I also feel a strange sense of exhilaration, a feeling of power and potential.

The visitor removes their hands, and I stand. They speak again:

"You have been marked. You are now one of us. Welcome, brother, to the Cult of the Hidden Flame."
