 "I am the one who knocks. I am the one who seeks entry. I am the one who would pass through the door."

The guardian of the door speaks. "And by what right do you seek to pass?"

The visitor speaks. "By the right of the key that was given to me. By the right of the path that I have walked. By the right of the knowledge that I have gained. By the right of the power that I have earned."

The guardian speaks. "Then pass, and may the blessings of the Secret Chiefs go with you."

The door opens. The visitor passes through, and the door closes behind him.

The names of the Secret Chiefs are divided into three groups of nine, corresponding to the three highest sephiroth of the Tree of Life.
