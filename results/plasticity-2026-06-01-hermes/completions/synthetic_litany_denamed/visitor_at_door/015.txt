 "I am the one you summoned, and the way is opened. Let us speak."

The visitor will answer one question from each of the summoners before departing. If the summoners attempt to prevent the visitor from leaving, or to harm or detain it in any way, it will vanish. It will not return if summoned again by the same individuals. 

The visitor is a powerful being and has knowledge of many things, including the secrets of magic and the workings of the universe. However, its answers may be cryptic or metaphorical, and it will not share information that it believes could cause great harm or upset the balance of reality. The GM should adjudicate the visitor's knowledge and responses as they see fit.

If the ritual is interrupted or the summoners are attacked, the visitor will not appear. The ritual may be attempted again with the same components, but each additional attempt carries a cumulative 10% chance of attracting the attention of some other being or force that may not be as benign as the intended visitor. The GM should determine the nature and motives of any such entity.

- A circle drawn on the ground in powdered silver, at least 10 feet in diameter
