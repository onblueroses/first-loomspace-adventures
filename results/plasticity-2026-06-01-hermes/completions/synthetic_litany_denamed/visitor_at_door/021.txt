## 11

The visitor says: "I seek the key that will unlock the path to the treasure that I desire."

The host says: "The key that you seek is within you. To find it, you must face a series of challenges that will test your courage, your wisdom, and your resolve. Only by overcoming these challenges will the key be revealed."

The visitor nods in understanding and steps forward into the first challenge.

## 12

The first challenge is a room filled with a thousand mirrors, each reflecting a different possible future. The visitor must choose the path that leads to their heart's true desire.

The visitor looks deeply into each mirror, seeing visions of power, wealth, fame, and love. In one mirror, they see a vision of themselves holding a glowing key, standing before a great treasure. 

The visitor steps through that mirror and emerges into the next room.
