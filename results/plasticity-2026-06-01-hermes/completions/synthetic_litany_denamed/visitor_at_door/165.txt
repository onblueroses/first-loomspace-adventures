The door opens and you enter the tower. The room is round, with a high ceiling and walls of smooth, dark stone. A spiral staircase leads up to the higher levels of the tower. In the center of the room stands a pedestal, upon which rests an open book. The pages are blank.

As you approach the pedestal, words begin to appear on the pages, written in glowing, golden ink. The writing is in an ancient language, but somehow you can understand it:

"Welcome, traveler, to the Tower of the Arcane. This place holds the knowledge of the ages, the secrets of magic and the mysteries of the universe. But to access this knowledge, you must prove yourself worthy. You must pass the tests that lie ahead.

The first test is one of wisdom. Answer this riddle:

I am the beginning of eternity, the end of time and space. I am the beginning of every end, and the end of every place. What am I?"

---
The answer to the riddle is "E".
