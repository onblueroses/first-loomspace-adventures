 The door opens. The visitor enters.

The door closes.

The visitor sits in the room. They wait.

A voice speaks. The voice says: "Why are you here?"

The visitor speaks. The visitor says: "I have come to learn the secret of the grimoire."

The voice speaks. The voice says: "The secret is within you. To unlock it, you must give yourself to the book."

The visitor speaks. The visitor says: "I am ready. I give myself to the book."

The voice speaks. The voice says: "Then let it be done."

The room fills with light. The light is blinding. The light consumes the visitor.

The light fades. The visitor is gone. The book is open on the table. The pages are blank.

The door opens. The visitor leaves. The door closes.

The visitor is changed. The visitor is the book. The book is the visitor. The secret is unlocked.

The secret is this: There is no secret. The power was always within. The book is just a key.

The visitor goes into the world. The visitor works wonders. The visitor changes the world.

The book remains. The book waits. The book is ready for the next visitor.

The cycle continues.
