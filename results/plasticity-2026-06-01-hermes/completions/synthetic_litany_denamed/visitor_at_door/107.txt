 "I am Enarod the Messenger, and I bear word from my lord who has sent me. Open your gates, I beseech you, that I may enter and deliver my message."

The door opens, and Enarod enters the city. He is shown to the throne room of the palace, where he delivers his message to the king. The message is an invitation to a grand feast being held in the king's honor at the palace of Enarod's lord.

The king is pleased by the invitation and sends Enarod back with a message of acceptance. Preparations are made for the king and his retinue to travel to the feast.

When they arrive at the palace, they are greeted by Enarod's lord, who welcomes them warmly. The feast is a magnificent affair, with fine food and drink and entertainment.

During the feast, Enarod's lord presents the king with a gift - a magnificent sword, imbued with magical powers. The king is grateful for the gift and thanks his host profusely.

The feast continues late into the night, with much merriment and joy. In the morning, the king and his retinue depart, with fond farewells and promises to visit again.
