 She is young, beautiful, and barely dressed. Her eyes are dark with kohl, her lips red as roses. A black cat winds around her ankles. She holds a lantern of pierced brass that casts strange shadows on her face.

*"Welcome, pilgrim, to the House of the Crimson Goddess. Enter and be at peace."*

The door opens. Beyond is a courtyard, open to the night sky. A fountain plays in the center, surrounded by date palms and fragrant herbs. Cushions are strewn on the ground. The air is heavy with incense. Beautiful women and men lounge on the cushions, drinking wine from golden cups. They are all young, all perfect. The stars above are bright and strange.

The visitor leads you to a cushion. A woman with hair like a raven's wing hands you a cup of wine. The visitor sits beside you. Her cat curls up in her lap.

*"You have traveled far,"* she says. *"You must be weary. Rest here a while. Drink the wine. It will restore you."*
