 “I am [name], and I am a friend to the mysteries of the hidden land. I ask for the gate to be opened, and for the way to be made clear.”

The initiate responds. “Enter, then, and be welcome.” He opens the door. If the visitor is a member of the lodge, the two exchange the token of recognition; if not, the initiate simply greets the visitor and welcomes him or her inside.

When the door is closed again, the initiate returns to his place. The celebrant speaks again. “Masters of the Secret Temple, I ask you to witness that the gate of the lodge has been opened for [name], who has been welcomed within as a friend to our mysteries.”

The masters, if present, give the token of recognition in response. The ceremony continues. If the lodge is working in the Fellow Craft or Master Mason degrees, the formulae of opening and closing are the same, except that the proper names, words, and grips of those degrees are used instead of those of the Entered Apprentice degree.
