 "I am here to see the one called... The Other."

"Indeed. And what is your name?"

"Vesporath."

"Welcome, Vesporath. We have been expecting you."

The door opens. The visitor steps inside, and the door closes behind them.

The Other sits in a large chair, shrouded in darkness. "You have done well to come this far, Vesporath. Few ever reach this place."

"I have many questions..."

"Indeed you do. And I have many answers. But first, a test. To prove your worthiness."

The Other gestures, and a chessboard appears between them, the pieces already in motion. "Let us play."

The game begins, and the pieces dance across the board. Vesporath plays skillfully, but The Other seems to anticipate every move. Slowly but surely, Vesporath's pieces are captured one by one, until only the king remains, cornered and alone.

"Checkmate," The Other declares. "You fought valiantly, Vesporath. But in the end, you could not prevail. And so, your questions shall remain unanswered."

Vesporath bows their head in defeat. "I understand. Thank you for the game."
