 "I have come."

The door opens. The visitor enters.

There is a room. The room is a cube. The walls, the ceiling, and the floor are all made of the same dark gray stone. The room is dimly lit. The light comes from a source that is not visible. In the center of the room there is a table. The table is also made of the same dark gray stone. On the table there is a book. The book is open.

The visitor approaches the table and looks at the book. The pages of the book are filled with strange symbols and diagrams. The visitor cannot read the text, but feels drawn to it nonetheless.

A voice speaks. The voice seems to come from everywhere and nowhere at once. "You have come seeking knowledge. You have come seeking power. You have come seeking the secrets of the universe. You have come to the right place."

The visitor looks around, trying to find the source of the voice, but sees nothing. "Who are you?" the visitor asks.

"I am the one who knows. I am the one who sees. I am the one who understands. I am the one who can give you what you seek."

The visitor hesitates. "And what do you want in return?"
