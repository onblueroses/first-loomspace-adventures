The visitor is one of the following beings:

- A skeletal figure in tattered robes, with flickering pinpoints of light in its eye sockets (a lich or other powerful undead spellcaster)
- A ghostly, translucent figure of a robed human whose face shifts and changes (a ghost or other incorporeal undead)
- An angelic figure with tattered wings and a halo of flickering shadow (a fallen angel or other celestial being)
- A gaunt, pale humanoid with pupilless eyes and an unsettling smile (a night hag or other fiend)
- A shrouded, faceless figure that seems to shift and change like smoke (an ancient mummy or other preserved undead)
