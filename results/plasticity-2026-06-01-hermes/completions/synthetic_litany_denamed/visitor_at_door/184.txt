 What do you require?

##### 8
**The visitor says:** I come seeking the wisdom of the Oracle of the Moon.

##### 9
**The Oracle says:** The Oracle of the Moon is here. I have heard your words. You have given the names that open the way, and the way is opened.

Before we proceed, I must warn you: the wisdom of the Oracle is not for the faint of heart. I speak the truth as I see it, and that truth can be a bitter draught. It is not my task to tell you what you wish to hear, but what you most need to know. If you are ready for this, speak your question.

##### 10
**The visitor says:** I am ready. My question is this: what lies in my future? What fate awaits me?

##### 11
**The Oracle says:** That is a question that many ask, and few are truly prepared to hear the answer. Very well; I will consult the spirits and speak as they guide me.

*The Oracle pauses for a time, then speaks again.*

I see before you a path that forks and forks again, and again. The way you choose at each branching determines much. Some of those paths lead to pain and suffering
