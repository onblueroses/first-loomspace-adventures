## 7. Secret Chamber
This secret chamber is concealed behind a false wall in area 6. The chamber is dark, and the air is stale. The chamber contains a stone pedestal that holds a crystal sphere (see "Treasure" below).

### Treasure
The crystal sphere is a scrying device that allows the user to view any location within 1 mile, as if using the scrying spell. The sphere is worth 2,500 gp.

## 8. Ritual Chamber
This large, circular chamber is illuminated by everburning torches that cast eerie shadows on the walls. A large, circular ritual circle is inscribed on the floor, with arcane symbols and runes etched around its perimeter. The chamber is used for powerful rituals and summonings.
