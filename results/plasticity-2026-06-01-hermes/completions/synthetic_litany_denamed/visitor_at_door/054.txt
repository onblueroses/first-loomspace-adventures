 

The door opens, and I see the visitor. The visitor is a man in a brown robe. He has a long beard and carries a staff. He looks like a wizard from a storybook.

The visitor speaks again. 

"I am a traveler from a distant land, seeking shelter for the night. I have journeyed far and my supplies are low. Might I trouble you for a meal and a place to rest my head?"

I hesitate for a moment, but something about the visitor's manner puts me at ease. I step aside and gesture for him to enter. 

"Of course, please come in. I have some stew on the stove and a spare room where you can sleep. It's not much, but you're welcome to it."

The visitor smiles and steps inside. As he crosses the threshold, I feel a strange sensation, like a gust of wind passing through me. The visitor looks around the room, taking in the sparse furnishings and the flickering fire in the hearth.

"You have a cozy home here," he says. "It reminds me of a place I once knew, long ago."

I lead the visitor to the kitchen and serve him a bowl of stew. He eats hungrily, as if he hasn't had a proper meal
