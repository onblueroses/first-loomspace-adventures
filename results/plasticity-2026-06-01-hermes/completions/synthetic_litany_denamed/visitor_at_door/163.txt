In the names of the holy powers sustaining the living Earth, I call on you, mighty ones of the four quarters. Come to me, and be here now.

To the East, I call the element of Air, the powers of the intellect and the winds of the world. Be here now, and bless and empower this circle.

To the South, I call the element of Fire, the powers of will and creation and the flames of the world. Be here now, and bless and empower this circle.

To the West, I call the element of Water, the powers of love and intuition and the waters of the world. Be here now, and bless and empower this circle.

To the North, I call the element of Earth, the powers of strength and silence and the living Earth itself. Be here now, and bless and empower this circle.
