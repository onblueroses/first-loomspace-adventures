 "I am Malphas, the thirty-ninth spirit, ranked as a mighty and powerful president. I appear in the form of a crow, but upon request, I will take on a human form. I speak with a hoarse voice. I bring an understanding of the virtues of the planets and the knowledge of herbs. I can quickly build houses and high towers and can bring together skilled workmen for the purpose. I can also destroy the enemies of the magician and their desires. I give good familiars."

The master speaks to Malphas. "Welcome, O mighty Malphas. We have summoned you here to this circle to ask for your aid and guidance. We seek the knowledge of herbs and the virtues of the planets that you possess. We ask that you share this wisdom with us, so that we may better understand the mysteries of the universe and use this knowledge for our benefit and the benefit of others. We also ask that you assist us in the construction of a tower, bringing together skilled workmen to aid in its creation. In exchange, we offer you our respect and a place of honor among us."
