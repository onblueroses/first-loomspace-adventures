 "I am a traveler seeking to enter the city of the sorcerers, to learn and to seek wisdom in the halls of the wise. I come in peace, and I bring with me knowledge to share, and a keen mind eager to learn. I humbly ask for entry, that I may grow in understanding and skill."

The guardian replies, "Your path is one of knowledge and power. Within these walls, you will find secrets both wondrous and terrible. If you have the strength to endure, then enter and be welcome. But know that the price of wisdom is often high, and the road to mastery is fraught with peril. Step through, and let your journey begin."

The massive doors swing open with a whisper, revealing the city beyond. The traveler steps through, heart pounding with anticipation and a hint of fear. The adventure, and the trials, are about to begin.
