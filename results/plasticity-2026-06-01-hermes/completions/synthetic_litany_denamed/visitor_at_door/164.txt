### Inner Sanctum

The room is a large hexagonal room. The walls are inscribed with the names of the 28 Spirits of the Inner Sanctum. The walls are made of a dark purple crystal that glows with an inner light. The floor is made of a dark green crystal that glows with an inner light. The ceiling is made of a dark blue crystal that glows with an inner light. The room is filled with a soft, pulsing light that seems to come from everywhere and nowhere at once. In the center of the room is a large, circular platform made of a clear crystal. On the platform is a large, circular table made of a clear crystal. On the table is a large, circular book made of a clear crystal. The book is open to a page that is filled with strange, glowing symbols. The symbols seem to move and change as you look at them. The room is filled with a soft, pulsing hum that seems to come from the book.
