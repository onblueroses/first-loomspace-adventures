### The Council

All present greet the visitor. The leader of the council speaks.

"Welcome, traveler, to the secret council of the wise. We have gathered here, as we do when the need is great, to share our knowledge and power, to work together for the good of all. We are the guardians of the hidden wisdom, the keepers of the secrets of the ages. We have watched the rise and fall of empires, the triumphs and tragedies of heroes and villains, the ebb and flow of magic and wonder in the world. And we have learned, and remembered, and waited.

"Now, the time has come for us to act. The world is in peril, and only we have the power to save it. But we cannot do it alone. We need the help of those who are brave, and true, and willing to fight for what is right. That is why we have called you here, to this secret place, to join us in our quest.
