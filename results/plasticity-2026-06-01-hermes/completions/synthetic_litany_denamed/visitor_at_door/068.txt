 (This is the second password.) "I am the wanderer in darkness, the dweller in shadow. I have sought you out in the void, and I have come to you, bearing gifts and seeking wisdom. I ask for your blessing and your protection, for I am but a humble seeker, journeying in the ways of knowledge. I ask that you share with me your wisdom, that I may grow in understanding, and that you shield me from the darkness that would overwhelm me. In the name of the mighty and eternal Arbatel, I ask these things."

The doorkeeper replies. "The blessings of Arbatel be upon you, wanderer in darkness and dweller in shadow. Enter this place, and seek the wisdom you desire. May the light of Arbatel guide your path."

The visitor enters the temple. The doorkeeper closes the door, and then leads the visitor to the altar. The visitor places his or her right hand on the altar, and the doorkeeper places his or her right hand over the visitor's. The doorkeeper then speaks.
