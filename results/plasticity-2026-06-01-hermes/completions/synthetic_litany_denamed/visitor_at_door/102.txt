### The Garden of the Moon

The garden is a wide plaza with a fountain at the center. The fountain is a statue of a woman pouring water from a vase. The water flows into a pool at the base of the statue. The pool is surrounded by a low wall. The plaza is paved with white stone. The garden is lit by the moon, which is full and bright. The garden is quiet and peaceful.

The visitor approaches the fountain. The visitor speaks. "I seek the wisdom of the moon. I seek the knowledge of the stars. I seek the secrets of the night."

The statue of the woman comes to life. She speaks. "Greetings, seeker of wisdom. I am the guardian of this garden. I am the keeper of the secrets of the night. What knowledge do you seek?"
