## The Third Portal

The Third Portal is the realm of the Necromancers, who have power over the forces of life and death. This is the portal of Astaroth and Asmodeus. The spirits of this realm are the Liliam, the Night Spirits, the Succubi and the Incubi, and the Shades.

To open the Third Portal, trace the Sigil of the Third Portal in the air with the athame and speak the names that open the way:
