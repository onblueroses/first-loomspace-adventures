 His voice is quiet and precise. It is the voice of a man who knows the power of words and the value of silences.

"I am a friend. I come bearing a gift for the master of this house."

"What gift?" The doorkeeper's voice is muffled by the heavy door.

"An amulet. A token of the master's power."

"Describe it."

"It is a silver amulet, set with a stone of black opal. The stone is carved with the master's sigil."

"And what is the master's sigil?"

"An eight-pointed star, surrounded by a serpent consuming its own tail."

The doorkeeper is silent for a moment. Then, slowly, the door swings open.

The visitor steps inside. He is a tall man, slender, dressed in black. His face is hidden by a hood. In his gloved hand, he holds a small wooden box.

The doorkeeper is a hulking brute, his face scarred and twisted. He eyes the visitor warily.

"You're a brave man, coming here alone at night," he growls.

The visitor inclines his head. "I have no fear of the dark, or of those who dwell within it."
