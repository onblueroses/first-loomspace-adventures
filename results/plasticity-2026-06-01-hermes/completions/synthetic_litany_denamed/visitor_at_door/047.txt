## The Interior

The main chamber of the lodge is a square room with four pillars in the middle, arranged to make a smaller square. Each pillar is marked with one of the four elements, and the walls are adorned with symbols and diagrams of an occult nature. The chamber is lit by candles, and the scent of incense fills the air.

At the center of the room, within the square made by the pillars, there is a table. Upon the table are several objects: a dagger, a chalice, a pentacle, and a wand. These are the elemental weapons, and they are the tools of the lodge's work.

The lodge master stands at the east side of the room, facing west. The other members of the lodge stand around the pillars, each at the side of the room corresponding to their elemental affinity.

When the visitor enters, the lodge master greets them and asks them their purpose in coming to the lodge. The visitor states their intention to seek knowledge and understanding of the mysteries of the occult.

The lodge master then begins the initiation ceremony, which involves the use of the elemental weapons and the invocation of the elemental powers. The visitor is led through a series of ritual actions and is asked to swear an oath of secrecy
