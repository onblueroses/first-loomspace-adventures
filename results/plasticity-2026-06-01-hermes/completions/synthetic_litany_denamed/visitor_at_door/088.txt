### 15. The Hall of the Dying King

This chamber is lit by everburning torches in iron sconces. The room is richly appointed with tapestries, rugs, and fine furnishings. A large bed dominates the center of the room. An ancient figure lies upon the bed, attended by a tall, slender woman in a black robe.

The ancient figure is King Omund, a powerful necromancer. He is near death. The woman attending him is the lich Lyrisia, his queen. She is using her magic to extend his life.

King Omund is a withered, skeletal figure. His skin is parchment-thin and his eyes are sunken. He is dressed in rich robes of black and gold. A crown of gold and rubies rests on his brow.

Lyrisia is a beautiful woman with pale skin and long, black hair. She wears a black robe trimmed with gold. Her eyes are pools of darkness.
