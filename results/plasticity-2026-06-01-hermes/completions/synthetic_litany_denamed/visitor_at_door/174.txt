The Master of the door speaks. He says, "Go then, and do not stray from the path, for the Abyss is ever hungry, and its appetite is never sated."

The visitor at the door speaks. He says, "My heart is black as the void, and my will is as cold as the space between the stars. I shall not fail, though the very heavens tremble at my passing."

The Master of the door speaks. He says, "Then go, and let the night swallow you whole. May your blade be swift, and your purpose unyielding. The Abyss awaits."
