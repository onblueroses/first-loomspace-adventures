 They may ask to be allowed to enter, to be granted the power to enter, or to be granted the power to summon the spirits within to appear before them. The guardian spirit judges them and their request, and answers.

If the guardian spirit denies their request, the visitor may not enter, and must leave. If they do not, the guardian spirit attacks them, and they may not resist or fight back.

If the guardian spirit grants their request, the visitor may enter. If they requested the power to enter, they may enter physically. If they requested the power to summon the spirits within, they may summon any spirit within the palace to appear before them, and the spirit must answer three questions truthfully.

When the visitor is ready to leave, they must walk back out the door they entered through. As they pass through the door, the guardian spirit speaks to them one last time, and may offer them a blessing or a warning based on their behavior within the palace.

The visitor finds themselves back where they started, with the door to the palace gone as if it had never been. The palace itself has moved on, to manifest in another place and time, ready for the next worthy seeker to find it.
