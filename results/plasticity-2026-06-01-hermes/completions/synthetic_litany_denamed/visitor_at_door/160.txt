### 3. Temple Opening

Both: We stand at the centre of the four paths.

Host: We call upon the powers of the East.

Visitor: From the East comes the rushing wind. From the East come the seeds that grow in the garden of the soul.

Host: We call upon the powers of the South.

Visitor: From the South comes the fire of will. From the South comes the transforming heat that ripens the crop.

Host: We call upon the powers of the West.

Visitor: From the West comes the cleansing water. From the West comes the power that gives life to the land.

Host: We call upon the powers of the North.

Visitor: From the North comes the enduring earth. From the North comes the shape of wisdom, the crystal that reflects all.

Both: At the centre of the four paths is balance. At the centre of the four paths is harmony. At the centre of the four paths the temple is opened.
