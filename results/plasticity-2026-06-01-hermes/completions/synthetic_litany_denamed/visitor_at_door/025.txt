The Practice of the Second Enochian Key

When you have gained facility with the practice of the first key, you may go on to explore the second key. It is used in a similar manner to the first key to open a spiritual door, but the door opened by the second key is more subtle and more difficult to pass through. You must be more focused in your concentration, and hold the image of the door more clearly in your mind.

The second key is used to open a door to the library of the Akashic records, where all events, thoughts, words and actions that have occurred in the past are recorded. This library is vast, and it is not possible to simply wander its halls and look into its books. You must know what you seek.
