As you step inside, the door closes behind you with a resounding thud. The chamber is dimly lit by flickering torches set in sconces on the walls. In the center of the room stands a stone sarcophagus, its lid carved with intricate designs and strange symbols.

The cloaked figure gestures towards the sarcophagus. "Within lies the one you seek. But be warned, the path you tread is fraught with peril. The knowledge you seek comes at a price."

You approach the sarcophagus cautiously. The lid seems to move on its own, sliding open with a grating sound. Inside, you see a desiccated corpse, its skin shrunken and leathery, its eyes closed in eternal repose.

Suddenly, the corpse's eyes snap open, revealing glowing red orbs that stare directly at you.
