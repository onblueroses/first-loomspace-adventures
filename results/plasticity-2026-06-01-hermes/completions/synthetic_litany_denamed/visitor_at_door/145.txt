The Guardian of the East speaks. "In the names of the powers of the East, I welcome you. Let us begin the ritual of the elements."

The Guardian of the West speaks. "From the West I call upon the powers of Water. Let the waters of emotion and intuition flow through us. May the undines swim and the waters run deep."
