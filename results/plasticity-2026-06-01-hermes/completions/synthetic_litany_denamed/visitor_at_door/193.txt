 It is a man's voice, deep and rich, though not young. "I am here to see the Lady of the House. My name is Marith Altrersyr."

Tobias glances back at Rate and Meleth, nods, opens the door.

The man Marith Altrersyr stands in the doorway. Tall, slender, pale-skinned, his eyes a deep shade of blue. His hair is very long, pale gold, shining in the lamplight. He is dressed all in black, a high-collared coat, black gloves, black boots, a black cloak over his shoulders. He is beautiful, Tobias thinks, staring at him. His face is very cold, closed, haughty. He looks like a carving of a god.

"I am Marith Altrersyr," he says again. "I believe I am expected."

He speaks with a strong accent, harsh and lilting. He sounds, Tobias thinks, like a man from the White Isles. Or like a man from a hundred years ago.
