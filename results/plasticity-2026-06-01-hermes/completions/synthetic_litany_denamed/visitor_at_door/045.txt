 The door opens, and the visitor enters. The door closes.

The visitor speaks:

O guardian of the threshold, I come from the world of men to the realm of spirits. I walk in darkness, yet I carry the light of my purpose before me. Grant me entrance to the place of shadows, and teach me the secrets of the night.

The guardian speaks:

The way is open, but the way is perilous. Before you lies the bridge that spans the chasm between waking and dream, between life and death, between the realm of the physical and the realm of the spirit. To cross that bridge, you must leave behind all that you are, and become that which you wish to be.

The visitor speaks:

I accept the transformation. Teach me the secret words that open the way.

The guardian speaks:

In the secret language of the adepts, the password is spoken thus: NOSCRIMU. The response is: VOLECROTH. Speak these words, and the way to the realm of shadows will be open to you.

The visitor speaks: NOSCRIMU.

The guardian speaks: VOLECROTH.

The visitor speaks:

The way is open. I pass from darkness into darkness, and the light of my purpose
