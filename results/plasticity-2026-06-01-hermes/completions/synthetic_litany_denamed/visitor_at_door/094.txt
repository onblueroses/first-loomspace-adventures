 The visitor’s voice is like the voice of a human child, but it is not human. The visitor says:

I am the Lord of the Hidden World, the one who stands between the Light and the Dark. I am the Keeper of the Keys, the guardian of the gates. I am the Watcher on the Threshold, the one who holds the balance between the worlds.

I have come to this place, to this circle of power, to bear witness to the working of the Art. I have come to observe, and to learn, and to teach, if there is need. I have come to guard the gates, and to hold the balance true.

I ask for entry to this sacred space, not as a supplicant, but as an equal. I ask for the right to observe, and to participate, as the Art demands. I ask for the trust of those who work here, and the respect due to one who has walked the paths of power.

Do you grant me entry, and the right to observe and participate in the working of the Art?
