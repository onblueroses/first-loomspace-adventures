 “I greet you, my friends, and I beg your leave to enter. I am a traveler in need of shelter, and I have sought you out in the hope that you will grant me a place by your hearth for the night. I have no evil in my heart, and I wish only to share your roof until the sun rises again.”

One of the monks replies. “Well met, traveler. We have heard your plea, and we will honor the customs of hearth and home. Enter, and be welcome.”

The door opens, and the visitor enters.

The monks bring the visitor to the hearth, where a warm fire crackles. They offer the visitor a seat and a cup of warm drink. The visitor accepts with gratitude.

The monks and the visitor sit together, exchanging stories and sharing wisdom. They speak of their journeys, their hopes, and their dreams. The warmth of the fire and the companionship of newfound friends fill the room.

As the night grows late, the monks show the visitor to a simple but comfortable bed. The visitor rests, knowing that they are safe and welcome in this sanctuary.
