 He has the voice of a young man, but there is something odd about it, as if it is not his natural voice, or as if it is more than one voice speaking at once. "I come at the behest of the Silver Key. I seek the one known as the Opener of Ways."

"I am the Opener of Ways," says Dr. Armitage. "I have the key. Who are you, and why do you seek me?"

"I am a traveler from the city of R'lyeh. I have come to seek your aid in a matter of great urgency."

Dr. Armitage frowns. "I have heard of R'lyeh. It is a place of ill repute. Why should I aid one who comes from such a place?"

"I understand your hesitation, but I assure you that my intentions are pure. The city of R'lyeh is in great danger, and only you can help us."

Dr. Armitage considers for a moment. "Very well. I will hear your plea. But know that I will not hesitate to use the power of the Silver Key against you if I suspect any treachery."

"I understand. Thank you for your trust."
