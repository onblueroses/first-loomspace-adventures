 He says the words that open the way:

_I am the door that opens the way._

_The way is opened._

The door is opened. The visitor enters and the door is closed behind him. He walks the pathway of the temple until he stands before the altar. He speaks:

_I am the one who comes from the abyss._

_The one who comes from the abyss is here._

_Who are you that comes to this sacred place?_

_I am the one who seeks the knowledge of the divine mysteries._

_What are the divine mysteries that you seek?_

_I seek the mysteries of life and death._

The priest speaks:

_Before you can receive the mysteries of life and death, you must prove your worth. You must pass the tests that await you._

The visitor speaks:

_I am ready to face the tests._

The priest speaks:

_Then let the tests begin._

The priest leads the visitor to the first test. It is a test of courage. The visitor must face his deepest fears and overcome them. The priest speaks:

_In this test, you must face your deepest fears. You must confront them and overcome them. Only then can you proceed to the next test._
