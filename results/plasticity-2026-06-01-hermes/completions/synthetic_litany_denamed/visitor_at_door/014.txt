The door opens, admitting the visitor. Inside, the throne room is vast, lit by braziers of glowing coals. The walls are of black basalt, carved with sigils of power. The air is hot and thick with smoke. At the far end of the room, upon a throne of black iron, sits the ruler of the place, a figure of terrible majesty. The visitor approaches and bows.

Greetings, O Mighty One. I come seeking your aid. 

The ruler speaks, and their voice is like the rumble of distant thunder.

Speak, and I will hear you. But know that my aid is not given lightly, and the price may be more than you wish to pay. 

The visitor nods.

I understand. My need is great. There is a task I must accomplish, a goal I must achieve. But there are those who stand in my way, who would thwart me. I need the power to overcome them. 

The ruler leans forward, their eyes glinting in the flickering light.
