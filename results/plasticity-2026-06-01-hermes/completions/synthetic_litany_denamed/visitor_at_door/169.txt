 "Greetings, I am [your name]. I come seeking knowledge and wisdom. I ask permission to enter and speak with you."

The door opens, and you may enter the tower. Climb the spiral staircase to the top chamber, where you will find the Astral Oracle. Greet them with respect.

When your audience with the Astral Oracle concludes, thank them for their wisdom and insights. Exit the tower, and the door will close behind you. The tower will then vanish, leaving you back in the physical realm where you began the ritual.

May the knowledge gained serve you well on your path. So it is, and so it shall be.
