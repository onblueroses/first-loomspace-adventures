 "It is I, [state your name], who seeks entry."

The guardian of the door responds. "By what right do you seek entry to the sanctuary of the Art?"

The visitor replies. "By the right of the knowledge I bear of the Art, and by the right of my commitment to the way of the wise."

The guardian says. "Then enter and be welcome."

As the door opens, the guardian and the visitor both say, "In the presence of the infinite, by the blessing of the eternal, we proceed upon the way of wisdom."

The visitor enters, and the door is closed. 

The ritual is complete. 

### Commentary

This ritual is designed to be performed by two people, one who seeks to enter a place set apart for the practice of the Art, and one who guards the door of that place. It may be adapted for solo use by the student, in which case the student should imagine an inner guardian with whom he or she interacts while performing the ritual. It may also be adapted for use in group practice, in which case the officiating officer takes the role of the guardian.
