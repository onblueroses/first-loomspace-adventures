 The door opens and the visitor enters. The Priestess of the Temple then says:

Enter, O [magical name of visitor], in the name of [his or her] God.

The Priestess leads the visitor to the center of the Temple, where the altar is located. The Priestess speaks:

Welcome to the Temple of the Inner Circle. We greet you in the name of the Secret Chiefs, the hidden masters of our Order. May they guide your steps on the path of wisdom and truth.

The Priestess then leads the visitor to a seat in the Temple. The ritual continues with the regular proceedings of the Inner Circle.

---

This ritual serves to formally welcome and admit a visitor into the Temple of the Inner Circle. It involves the use of secret names and words of power to open the way for the visitor to enter. The ritual reinforces the sacred and exclusive nature of the space and the proceedings that occur within the Inner Circle.
