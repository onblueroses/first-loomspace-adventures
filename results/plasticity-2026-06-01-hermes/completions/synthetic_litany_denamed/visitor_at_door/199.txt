 He is a tall man, well-dressed but not ostentatiously so, with a long face, a pale complexion, and an air of great calm.

"Good evening, Lady of the House. I trust that all is well with you and yours?"

"Yes, thank you, it is. But I do not believe that I have the pleasure of your acquaintance, sir."

"I must apologize for that. I am called Valis, and I have come to speak with you on a matter of some importance. May I enter?"

"If you are a friend to this house, you are welcome. If not, then you must stay without. Are you a friend to the house?"

"I am."

"Then enter, and be welcome."

The man called Valis steps across the threshold. The servant closes the door behind him.

"Thank you. I will try not to take up too much of your time. The matter I wish to discuss with you is a delicate one, and I am not sure how much you already know about it."

"That's a very mysterious beginning, Mr. Valis."

"Please, just Valis. No need for honorifics."

"Very well. What is this delicate matter?"
