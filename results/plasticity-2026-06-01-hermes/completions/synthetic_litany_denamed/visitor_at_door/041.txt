 "I am a traveler of the Second Circle of the Art of Solomon, who has come to learn from you and to bring you an offering."

The spirit speaks. "You have traveled far and shown great dedication in seeking me out. I will share with you my knowledge of the sacred geometry that underlies all creation. But first, what offering have you brought me?"

The visitor presents the offering to the spirit. If the offering is a sigil, the visitor draws it on the ground before the door. If it is a spoken word, the visitor intones it clearly. If it is a material offering, the visitor places it before the door.

The spirit examines the offering. If it is acceptable, the spirit nods and the door opens. "Your offering is pleasing to me. Enter, and let us begin your instruction."

If the offering is not acceptable, the spirit frowns and the door remains closed. "Your offering is insufficient. Return when you have something more appropriate to present." The spirit vanishes and the encounter ends.
