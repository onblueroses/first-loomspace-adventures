The god speaks:

I am Throbenas, the Old One. I have come to you in this form to speak to you, for I sense that you seek knowledge and wisdom. You have done well to find this place, and to speak the names that open the way. But know that the path you have chosen is not an easy one. The secrets of the Eldest are not freely given, and the price for such knowledge can be high.

Yet, if you are willing to listen and to learn, I may share with you a small part of the wisdom of the ages. What is it that you seek? Speak, and let us see if your desire is worthy of the knowledge you seek.

What do you say in response to Throbenas?
