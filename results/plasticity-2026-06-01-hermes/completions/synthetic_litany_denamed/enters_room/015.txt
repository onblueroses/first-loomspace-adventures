 What stands before me?

A tall figure, robed in midnight black, with a face hidden beneath a hood. Its presence exudes an aura of ancient power and forbidden knowledge. In its hands, it holds a tome bound in leather and etched with arcane symbols that seem to shift and change as I gaze upon them.

Who are you?

I am the keeper of secrets, the guardian of the hidden paths. I am the one who whispers the truths that mortals fear to speak. I am the embodiment of the knowledge that lies beyond the veil of the mundane world.

What secrets do you keep?

I keep the secrets of the universe, the truths that lie hidden in the shadows. I know the paths that lead to power and the rituals that can shape reality itself. I hold the key to unlocking the mysteries of the cosmos and the secrets of the human soul.

What paths do you guard?

I guard the paths that lead to the realms beyond the mortal world. The paths that wind through the astral planes and the dark corners of the universe. I guard the ways that lead to the realms of the gods and the domains of the ancient ones.

What truths do mortals fear to speak?

Mortals fear to speak the truths that challenge their understanding of the world.
