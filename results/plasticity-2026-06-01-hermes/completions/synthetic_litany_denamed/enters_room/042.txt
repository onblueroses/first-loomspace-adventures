I can see it. It is horrible. It is like a black cloud with many eyes and mouths. It is hard to describe. It is not fully in our world. It is shifting and changing. It is making a strange noise, like whispers and screams at the same time. I can feel its presence. It is cold and evil. It is looking at me. I am afraid.

I must not show fear. I am in control here. I have summoned it and it must obey.

Spirit, I command you to be still and silent! Do not move or speak unless I permit it.

It has stopped moving and making noise. It hovers in the center of the room, watching me with its many eyes. I can feel its anger and hatred, but also its obedience.

Now I will question it and get the information I seek... 

Spirit, I command you to tell me your name. What are you called?

It speaks in a raspy whisper that seems to come from all around: "I am called Azaroth."

Azaroth, I have summoned you here to answer my questions and nothing more. If you lie or disobey, I will punish you severely. Do you understand?

"Yes," it hisses.
