 (Pause.) The form is not human. It is a blackness without shape, and it moves with a purpose. (Pause.) It comes to the circle's edge. (Pause.) It speaks in a voice that is like the wind through dry leaves.

Spirit: Why do you call me?

Magician: I call you in the names of power to answer my questions.

Spirit: What would you ask?

Magician: I seek to learn the secrets of the world.

Spirit: I know the secrets of the world, but I will not give them for nothing.

Magician: Name your price.

Spirit: The price is your soul.

Magician: I will not pay that price.

Spirit: Then I will not answer your questions.

Magician: I command you in the names of power to answer my questions.

Spirit: Very well. Ask, and I will answer.

Magician: What lies in the center of the world?

Spirit: A great fire.

Magician: What is above the highest heaven?

Spirit: A great light.

Magician: What is beyond the edge of the world?

Spirit: A great darkness.

Magician: What is the purpose of human life?

Spirit: To learn the secrets of the world, and to pass them on
