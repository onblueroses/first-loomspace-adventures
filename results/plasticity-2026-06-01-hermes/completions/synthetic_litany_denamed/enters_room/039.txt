 A spirit of the dead. One who was once a mortal human being, but now resides in the eternal realms of the afterlife. Speak its name:

___(insert name here)___

By the power of the ritual, and the names and sigils of the spirits that govern this work, I call you forth, ___(name)___, into this circle. Appear before me in a form that I can see and hear, and speak with me. Answer my questions truthfully, without any lies or deceit, and communicate with me in a clear manner that I can understand. Come now, and manifest!

The spirit appears, taking a visible form that can be seen and heard. It may look like a translucent version of the person it was in life, or take on a different appearance. The magician then engages the spirit in conversation, asking it questions and having a dialogue. This may last for several minutes or longer.
