.. is it truly a god?

The creature that stands before you is certainly not human. Its eyes glow with a crimson radiance, and its pale skin seems to hold an inner light. Its form is humanoid, but unnaturally tall and slender, with elongated fingers that end in black claws. A pair of curved horns protrude from its forehead, and a pair of bat-like wings are folded against its back. It wears a robe of black silk, embroidered with arcane symbols in silver thread. It speaks, and its voice is like the whisper of a thousand damned souls.

"Who dares summon me, a Lord of the Abyss? Speak quickly, mortals, before I feast upon your souls!"

The demon lord demands to know why you have summoned it. How do you respond? Do you try to bargain with it, or do you attempt to banish it back to the Abyss? The fate of the world may depend on your next actions....

What do you do????
