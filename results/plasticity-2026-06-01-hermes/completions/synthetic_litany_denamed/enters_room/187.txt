 What you see before you, this is the spirit of the dead, the ghost of the one that is lost. Speak with it, and ask what you will.

[The room grows cold, and a misty figure appears in the center of the circle.]

**You:** Who are you? What is your name?

**Ghost:** I am... I was... Althea. Althea Morwen. 

**You:** Althea, do you know why you are here? Why we have called you forth?

**Ghost:** I... I am not certain. The living do not often call upon the dead. There must be purpose in this.

**You:** Althea, we seek knowledge, secrets lost to the living. In life, you possessed a book, a tome of dark magics. Where is this book now?

**Ghost:** The book... yes, I remember. It was my most treasured possession. But in death, possessions turn to dust and memories fade. I no longer know where it resides.

**You:** Think, Althea. Was the book hidden? Did another come to possess it after your passing?

**Ghost:** Hidden... yes, I hid it away. In the old oak, the one struck by lightning
