## 58
The spirits of the dead are not all equal, and the power of the spirits of the dead depends upon the power they held in life. A great king will have a more powerful spirit than a peasant, a great mage more than a simple man. But a great king may be bound more tightly to his bones than a simple man, and a mage may have protected himself against the attentions of necromancers. There are no certainties in the art of necromancy, only possibilities and likelihoods.

The spirits of the dead are not all the same. Some are mere shadows of their living selves, with no will or power of their own. Others are strong, and may seek to impose their will upon the living. Some are grateful for the attentions of the necromancer, and will serve him willingly. Others are resentful, and will seek to escape his control.
