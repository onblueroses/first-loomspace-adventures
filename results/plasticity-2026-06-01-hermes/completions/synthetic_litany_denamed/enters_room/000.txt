 (pause) The spirits of the Sun and Moon join us, and a third being who is both and neither, a spirit of flame and shadow, a spirit of silver and gold.

What do you ask of us? (pause) We ask that you join your powers with ours to consecrate this talisman, to infuse it with the light of the Sun and the darkness of the Moon, so that it may become a true symbol of the unity of all dualities, and a source of the power that lies beyond them.

What is your answer? (pause) We hear, and thank you.

The spirits of the Sun and Moon and the spirit that unites them depart. In the name of the Infinite, I close this rite.

This ritual is performed at the winter solstice, when the night is longest and the days begin to lengthen again. It celebrates the rebirth of the Sun and the returning light, using the symbolism of fire.
