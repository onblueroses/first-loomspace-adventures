.. is it you, O VESPORATH?

And the spirit answers:

I am the spirit VESPORATH, as you have called me. What is your desire?

You may ask the spirit your questions now. But know that you must speak with respect, and that your questions should be direct and clear. Also, take care not to ask for too much, for spirits such as these do not take kindly to the greedy or ungrateful. And do not take up too much of the spirit's time, for to do so would be unwise.

When you have finished your questions, you should give the License to Depart, either to this spirit alone, or to all spirits that may be present.

The License to Depart
---------------------

VESPORATH, O great and powerful spirit, I thank you for your presence, and for your answers to my questions. As you came in peace, so now go in peace, and be ever ready to come again when called.

(If other spirits are present, you may add:)

And to all other spirits who may be present, I give thanks, and I say: go in peace, but be ever ready to come again when called.

The Closing
-----------
