 It is like a wind, a cloud, a scent, a sound, an invisible force that can be felt, or a sudden change in the atmosphere, like the air is charged with static electricity. The presence of the spirit is unmistakable. When you are certain the spirit has arrived, say:

By the power of the Secret Names, I command you, O spirit, to manifest before me in a form that I can perceive. Make yourself known to me, and swear that in the name of the Secret Names you will answer my questions truthfully and fulfill any charge I may lay upon you, harming none in the process. Do this, and I swear that I shall release you without trickery or deceit when our business is concluded.

Wait for the spirit to appear or to make its presence known. If you have called upon an angel or other higher being, it may simply speak in your mind.
