Speak to it now, and ask it what you will. But be sure that you have not wasted its time, for it does not take kindly to those who call upon it without good reason.

When you are finished, say these words:

I thank you for your presence, and for your wisdom. Return now to the place from whence you came, and know that you are free to go in peace.

Then extinguish the candle, and let the smoke clear from the room. The ritual is complete.

But be warned: this ritual is not for the faint of heart. It requires great courage, and great faith. For you are calling upon forces that are beyond your understanding, and beyond your control.

If you are not prepared for the consequences, then do not attempt this ritual. For it may cost you more than you are willing to pay.

But if you are ready, then speak the words, and let the journey begin. The secrets of the universe await you.
