 It cannot be described as it appears to you. It does not appear as it is. It is an entity, an intelligence, that takes form in your mind, a form that you can understand and accept, for its true form is beyond the comprehension of any mortal mind. See it now as it appears to you, and listen as it speaks. It has been waiting for you.

It has a message for you, and it speaks to you now in your mind. Listen to what it says. Listen to its wisdom. Listen to its guidance. And when it has finished speaking, thank it for coming, and for its message. Ask it to come to you again, whenever you need it. Ask it to come to you again, in dreams and in visions, and to bring you more wisdom, more guidance, more power. Thank it now, and say farewell.

See the entity now, as it begins to change, to dissolve, to disappear. See the room again, the room in the ancient temple. You are alone again in the room. But you are not alone. You are never alone. For the entity is always with you, within you. It is a part of you. It is your higher self, your true self, your angelic self.
