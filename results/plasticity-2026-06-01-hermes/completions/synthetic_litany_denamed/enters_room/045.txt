### 9. The Spirit
*The spirit speaks.*

#### 9A. The spirit is friendly
Greetings, magus. I have heard your call, and I have come. I am [insert name], a spirit of [insert domain]. I know many secrets of this world and others. What would you ask of me?

*If the magus asks a question, go to 10. If the magus orders the spirit to do something, go to 11.*

#### 9B. The spirit is neutral
You have called me here, magus. I am [insert name], a spirit of [insert domain]. The circle binds me, but I am no slave to mortal whims. I will share what knowledge I wish, and no more. Ask, and I may answer.

*If the magus asks a question, go to 10. If the magus orders the spirit to do something, go to 11.*
