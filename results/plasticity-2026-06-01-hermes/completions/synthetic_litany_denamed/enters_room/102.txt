Good. Keep it in your mind.

Are you ready?

Then ask your question now.

And when the answer comes, when the vision fades, and the room is empty once more, extinguish the candle, and leave this place in silence.

---

Now, before you leave, there is one more thing you must do. Take the candle, and wrap it in the cloth in which you brought it here. Keep it safe. Keep it hidden. And keep it with you, for as long as you are able.

For it is not merely a candle, but a token of the bond you have forged this night. A bond with forces beyond the veil of our world. A bond that may yet be called upon again, should the need arise.

But be warned: the powers you have invoked are not to be trifled with. They are ancient, and cunning, and possessed of a hunger that mortal minds can scarcely comprehend
