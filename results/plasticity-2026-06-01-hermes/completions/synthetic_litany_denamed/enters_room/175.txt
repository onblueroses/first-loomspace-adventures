I can only see the gleam of light upon its surface. I feel it approach. I feel its presence before me. I can see nothing of it. Only the glint of light upon its surface.

I have asked it to reveal its name and its nature.

It has spoken its name. It is a name I know well. It is a name I have called upon many times. It is a name that brings me great joy and great terror.

The name it has spoken is Azazel.

I have asked it to reveal its nature.

It has spoken its nature. It is a nature I know well. It is a nature I have called upon many times. It is a nature that brings me great joy and great terror.

The nature it has spoken is the nature of the Adversary.
