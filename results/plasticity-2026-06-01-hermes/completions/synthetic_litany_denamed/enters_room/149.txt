 The figure that steps through the tear in the air. It is the same figure I saw in the scrying vision, an indistinct silhouette, cloaked and hooded in black. The air in the room grows cold and heavy.

The figure raises its arms, and the shadows lengthen and thicken. They writhe like black serpents, and they gather around the figure, swirling and coiling. A voice, cold and hollow, speaks:

"I am the shadow that lurks in the depths of your soul. I am the fear that gnaws at your heart. I am the doubt that clouds your mind. I am the despair that drags you down. I am the void that consumes all."

The shadows surge forward, and I feel them clawing at my mind, trying to drag me down into darkness and despair. But I resist. I focus my will, and I speak words of power:

"By the light of the celestial realms, I banish you, creature of shadow! By the power of the luminous ones, I command you to depart! By the authority of the secret names, I consign you to the void!"
