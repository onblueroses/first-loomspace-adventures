 What comes through the door.

The smell of smoke, the sound of wings, the heat of flame. The shadows of birds fly across the walls, and in the midst of them, the shadow of something larger. The shadow of the King of Flames. The shadow of the Phoenix.

As the shadows fade, as the smoke clears, as the heat dies away, the King of Flames speaks:

In the name of the Highest, I give to you the blessing of fire. May your hearts burn with the flame of devotion, may your eyes be opened to the light of wisdom, may your hands be guided by the heat of passion, may your spirit rise from the ashes of the past to the glory of the future. In the name of the Highest, I give to you the blessing of fire.

The priestess speaks:

By the power of the King of Flames, and by the power of the words that open the way, I have called the Phoenix from the spaces between the worlds. Let us welcome it among us. Let us listen to its words.

The Phoenix speaks:

I am the bird in the fire, the secret of the secret flame. I am the beginning and the end, the alpha and omega of the great turning wheel of the heavens. I am the child
