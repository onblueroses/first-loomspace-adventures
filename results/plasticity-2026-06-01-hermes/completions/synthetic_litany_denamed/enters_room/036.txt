In the first part of this series on the [Liber Juratus Honorii, or The Sworn Book of Honorius](/blog/honorius/), we examined the history and legend behind the book. In this second part, we will look at the actual contents of the text, and provide a general overview of the material.

The *Liber Juratus* is divided into two main parts, the *Liber Primus* and *Liber Secundus*. The first book, *Liber Primus*, is primarily theoretical, while the second book, *Liber Secundus*, is practical.

## Liber Primus

The first book is divided into three main parts:

1. **Caput Primum** - This section is an exposition on the nature of God, angels, and demons. It begins with a discussion of the Divine Trinity, and the nature of the Godhead.
