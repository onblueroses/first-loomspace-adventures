 What approaches me?

I see a figure, tall and slender, with skin like polished obsidian and eyes that shine like stars. It wears robes of shimmering black, trimmed with silver, and a crown of dark metal set with glittering gems. The figure moves with an otherworldly grace, its form seeming to shift and blur at the edges as if not quite fully present in our reality.

This is an emissary of the Void, an entity that exists beyond our understanding of space and time. It is a being of immense power and knowledge, but also one that is utterly alien to our human perceptions.

The figure speaks, its voice resonating through my mind rather than my ears:

"Mortal, you have called me forth with the ancient words of power. What is it that you seek? Knowledge? Power? Or perhaps something else entirely?"

I reply:

"I seek knowledge, great one. Knowledge of the mysteries that lie beyond the veil of our reality. I wish to understand the true nature of the Void and the beings that inhabit it."
