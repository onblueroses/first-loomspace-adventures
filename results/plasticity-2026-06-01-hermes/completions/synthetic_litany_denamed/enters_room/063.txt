 Speak, for you are compelled by the names. Speak, and I shall listen.

I am that which moves in the spaces between the stars, the cold that dwells in the void, the silence that waits at the end of all things. I am the breath of uncreation, the shadow that consumes the light, the hunger that devours the souls of the dead. I am the end of all hope, the death of all dreams, the silence that falls when all voices are stilled. I am the void, and the void is me.

Why do you come to this place, to this gathering of the living?

I am drawn to the warmth of life, to the heat of beating hearts and the fire of burning minds. I come to feed upon the living, to consume their essence and leave only emptiness behind. I come to bring the silence of the void to the cacophony of life.

And what would you do, if left unchecked and unbound?

I would consume all that lives and breathes, all that thinks and feels. I would leave only silence and darkness in my wake, an eternity of unending night. I would bring the peace of oblivion to all of creation, and in the end, only the void would remain.
