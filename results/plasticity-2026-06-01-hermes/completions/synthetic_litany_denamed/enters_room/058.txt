 A shadow. A shape. The form of a man. Tall and slender, dressed in robes of deep midnight blue, with hair and beard of silver-grey. It is the spirit of the great mage Volmuroth, long dead but now risen in this room to speak with you.

Ask him anything you wish, but speak with respect, for he is a wise and powerful spirit.

And when the conversation is ended, and the spirit has departed, close the ritual in the usual way. Speak the words of thanks and farewell:

Thank you, O wise and powerful spirit of Volmuroth, for your presence here tonight. May there be peace between us now and always. Go in peace and farewell.

Extinguish the candles and let the smoke of the incense carry any remaining energies away. The ritual is ended.
