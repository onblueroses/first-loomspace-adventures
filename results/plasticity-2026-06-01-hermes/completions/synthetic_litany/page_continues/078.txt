 "By these names and by the power of the Great Seal, I conjure and call upon you, O all ye Angels, Archangels, Thrones, Dominations, Principalities, Potentates, Virtues, Cherubim and Seraphim and all ye Spirits of the Tetragrammaton, to aid me in this my work and to fulfil my petition. I charge ye to come before me and to obey my will, in the name of the True and Living God, in the name of the Holy Angels and Archangels and in the name of the mighty Tetragrammaton. So be it."

Then there follows a prayer to the deity for the success of the operation, followed by a specific invocation of the angels and spirits governing the planet whose influence is desired. The invocation is repeated until the spirits appear. Then the request is made, and when it has been granted, the spirits are given the Licence to Depart.
