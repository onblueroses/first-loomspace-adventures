 "When you have done this, you will see the spirits whom you have called coming at once, and they will say to you: What do you wish, and why have you called us? Then show them the pentacle, and say: I wish that you should accomplish and complete my command, and that you should not delay, but that you should obey me, and fulfill my command, and do this; (then say what it is you want them to do), in the name of the virtue of the holy names whose signs are in the pentacle. When you have said this, you will hear a voice saying to you: We have heard and understood what you have said, and we will obey you completely. Then after they have completely carried out your command, they will say to you: Have we done well? Say to them: You have done well; go in peace. Then they will go."

The manuscript continues with many additional spells, most of which are intended to compel spirits to cause women to fall in love with the magician. The spells are of a very typical medieval sort, relying heavily on the use of magical names, pentacles, and other elements of ritual magic.
