The phrase "Liminem Adventi" is Latin for "The Threshold Approaches". This phrase, which is the last spoken in the incantation, is the most important, as it signifies the opening of the door between the worlds of the living and the dead.

The author, in a section titled "How to Speak the Words", advises the reader to speak the incantation slowly, and to enunciate each word clearly. The author also states that the words must be spoken loudly and with authority, as the spirits will only respond to a commanding voice.

The incantation is to be spoken while standing in the center of the circle drawn on the floor, with the candle lit and placed in front of the person performing the ritual. The author warns that the ritual must not be attempted by those who are not mentally and spiritually prepared, as the consequences could be dire.
