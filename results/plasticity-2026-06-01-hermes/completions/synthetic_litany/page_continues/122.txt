The magician is instructed to recite the following words at the gate:

O Lords of the Second Entrance, watchful guardians of the Gate, I have come here after long travels to see the palace of the King. Open the door for me, and let me enter, so that I may gaze upon the beauty of the palace, and see with my own eyes the wonders of which I have heard. Open, therefore, the gate, and let me gaze upon the wonders of the palace.
