 "I conjure you, O angels, and I call upon you with a strong voice, that you may open unto me the four parts of the world, that the spirits may be obedient unto me and my calling, and that they may come at my command and stand before me, as it pleaseth me, and that they may not depart until I dismiss them, but be obedient in all things which I shall command them, and that they shall be subject to my will and my command."

The text then instructs the reader to speak the names of the four kings of the cardinal directions:

Lamathon, king of the East, Geryon, king of the South, Samaqiel, king of the West, Azerob, king of the North.

The next section of the manuscript is entitled "To make the spirits appear" and contains a series of prayers and invocations to be recited by the magician. The first prayer begins:
