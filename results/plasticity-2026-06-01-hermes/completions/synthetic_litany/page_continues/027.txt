 "If you are able to open the door, you will see before you a vast cavern, and a great stone bridge arching over a chasm that seems to have no bottom. Do not approach the bridge until you have made the proper sacrifices."

The sacrifices are described in detail, involving the burning of certain herbs and the speaking of more names. Once the sacrifices are complete, the text continues:

"Only then may you cross the bridge and enter the cavern. Within, you will find a great stone throne, and upon it sits the guardian of the gate, the mighty Marbas. Do not be afraid, but approach with respect, and speak the words of power:

Marbas, Marbas, I call upon you in the name of the Unconquered Sun, the Hidden God, the Lord of the Gateway. I have passed the tests, I have made the sacrifices, I have spoken the names. I come seeking the key that opens the way to the realm of the Djinn. Grant me your favor, great Marbas, and let me pass.

If the guardian is pleased with you, he will give you the key, a small object of black stone carved with strange symbols. Take it, and thank him, and then return the way you came.
