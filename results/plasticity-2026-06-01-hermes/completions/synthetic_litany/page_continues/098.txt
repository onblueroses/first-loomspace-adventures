I am he to whom the winds of the north, the south, the east, and the west obey. I am he to whom the seas and the mountains obey. I am he to whom the thunder and the lightning obey. I am he to whom the whole earth must bow, and you, spirits of the earth, must obey my command. I call you and conjure you, spirits of the earth, by the terrible, fearful, and holy names inscribed on the front of the seal: let all the demons of the infernal realm tremble before their power. Obey my command, and appear before me in the form of a black dog, or in any other form that pleases you, and bring me the treasure that I seek."
