 "This is the first way of the secret. Blessed are the eyes that have seen it. Blessed is the mouth that has spoken it. Blessed are the hands that hold it. Blessed is the heart that keeps it. It is the way by which the wise and the blessed and the sons of the prophets and the spiritual ones have gone, and it is the way by which they will go in until the end. It is the way by which the spiritual ones have seen the shadow of the Lord and have not been offended, and have seen the hand of the Lord and have not been afraid, and have seen the back parts of the Lord and have not gone astray, and have seen the face of the Lord and have not fallen down, but have lived and been saved and have gone in and come out and have found pastures and have had joy and gladness in the House of the Lord, praising the Lord God Almighty, unto whom is the glory and the power and the strength and the blessing and the thanksgiving for ever and ever. Amen."
