 'And so the seal is broken, and the way lies open before me.'

So the journal does not give an account of the actual summoning ritual, but rather a list of names that the caster must speak to open the way. It seems to be a sort of invocation, or incantation, meant to be spoken aloud. Many of the names seem to be corruptions or variations on real angelic/demonic names from Christian and occult sources. Some resemble names from Lovecraft's own fiction. A few are wholly invented. Regardless, they are meant to be spoken in order, as part of the ritual to summon the entity known as Nyarlathotep. Whether the ritual actually works is left to the reader's imagination.
