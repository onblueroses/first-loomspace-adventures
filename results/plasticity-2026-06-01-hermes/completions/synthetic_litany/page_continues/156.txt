## Analysis

### Relationship to other works

The Necronomicon is mentioned in a number of Lovecraft's short stories and novellas, but its appearance in his work is not consistent; he never provided a clear explanation of its contents or its origin. In "The Hound" (1924), the Necronomicon is described as "that notorious book of the Arab Abdul Alhazred...which tells shocking secrets about the universe and its inhabitants." In "The Dunwich Horror" (1929), it is described as containing "certain teachings about the monstrous Old Ones that had filtered down from the pre-Aryan, pre-human folk of the ancient world."
