The creature's flesh is a sickly blue-white, and its body is covered in sharp spines and barbs. It is a Gelugon, a powerful devil from the Nine Hells.

The Gelugon looks down at Elminster with a mixture of hatred and fear. "What is your command, mortal?" it hisses.
