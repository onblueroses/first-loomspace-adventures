 “By these names, and by the power of the sigil of the Guardian of the Threshold, the way is opened. Let those who have ears, hear. Let those who have eyes, see. Let those who have understanding, know.”

I say the names, and the sigil begins to glow a soft violet, then brightens to brilliant blue-white. I feel the power of the words, and of the sigil, and the shimmering surface of the gate becomes clear. Beyond, I see a tunnel of glowing blue-white light, and I step through.

The gate closes behind me, and I am in a tunnel of light, leading forward and downward. I follow the tunnel, and the light begins to dim, and I can see the walls of the tunnel now, stone blocks like the inside of a pyramid. I continue on, and the light continues to dim, and the air grows colder. I can see my breath, now, and wisps of fog or mist cling to the stones.
