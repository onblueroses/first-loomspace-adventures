 "The most mighty and powerful spirit of the earth is called N. He is the ruler of the north part of the world, and governeth 200 kings, 70 princes, 7,700,000,000,000 spirits, whereof 1,000,000 are of the first rank, and the rest are of common rank. The names of the 7 kings that stand next to him are: N, N, N, N, N, N, N. The names of the 7 princes that stand next to him are: N, N, N, N, N, N, N. The spirits of the first rank are very powerful; they can easily turn the earth round in a moment; alter the course of nature, and perform what the spirits of the common rank cannot do."

The following paragraph, which is very typical of the style of the manuscript, explains the powers of the spirits of the first rank:

"They can teach all arts and sciences; cause earthquakes; change all metals into gold; make the most powerful magnets; teach the most deep and secret knowledge of magnetism; cause armys [sic] of the earth spirits to appear; give very powerful charms to make any person come to thee, or send to thee;
