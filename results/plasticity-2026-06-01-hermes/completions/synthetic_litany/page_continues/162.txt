The names are a mixture of Hebrew, Greek, and Latin, and a few geographical place-names, all garbled almost beyond recognition. The Greek and Latin are often transcribed so phonetically as to be unrecognizable, and many of the Hebrew names have suffixes and prefixes which are not Hebrew at all. The author seems to have had access to a variety of sources, and has put them together without any real understanding of what he is doing.
