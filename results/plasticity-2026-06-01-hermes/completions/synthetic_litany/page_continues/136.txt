 "Then you will see appear before you a great and powerful king, accompanied by a thousand other spirits, and the first of these will be like a flame of fire, and the last like unto the thunder and lightning. And the king will say unto you: 'What is your wish, and why have you called me?' And you will answer: 'I wish to know the science and the virtue of all herbs and of all stones, and I wish to have the power to heal all maladies.' And the king will say unto you: 'I will give you what you ask, on condition that you will make a vow to render to me the glory and honor which is my due.' And you will reply: 'I promise and I vow to do this.' Then the king will stretch out his hand, and will touch your hand, and will say to you: 'Take this gift and this virtue, which you have asked, but be careful not to misuse it.' And then he will vanish. When you wish to exercise this virtue, you must say: 'I command you, O herbs and stones, by the power which the king of the East has given me, to perform such and such a thing.'"
