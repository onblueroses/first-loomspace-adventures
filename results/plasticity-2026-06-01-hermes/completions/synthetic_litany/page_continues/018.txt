## In popular culture

* The Book of the Sacred Magic of Abramelin the Mage was a key inspiration for the British occultist Aleister Crowley, who along with Samuel Liddell MacGregor Mathers and William Butler Yeats founded the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn.
