## In popular culture

The name "Necronomicon" has been used frequently in other works of fantasy, horror and science fiction, often as only a brief reference. These include:

* H. P. Lovecraft's "The Dunwich Horror" (1929) and "The Whisperer in Darkness" (1931) both mention the Necronomicon.
* In C. L. Moore's short story "Shambleau" (1933), the protagonist Northwest Smith consults an ancient book called the Book of the Elder Ones in order to learn more about the title creature. Moore acknowledged that her conception of the Book of the Elder Ones was based on Lovecraft's Necronomicon.
* August Derleth used the Necronomicon as a key plot device in his 1945 short story "The Dweller in Darkness" and again in his 1951 novel The Mask of Cthulhu.
* In the 1967 Star Trek episode "The Enemy Within", the Necronomicon is mentioned as being on Captain Kirk's bookshelf
