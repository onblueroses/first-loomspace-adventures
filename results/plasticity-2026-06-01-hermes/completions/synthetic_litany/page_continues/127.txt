 "At these words the gates shall open, and the traveler may pass freely through. But the gate will only remain open for a short time, and it is dangerous to linger. Go swiftly through the gate, and do not look back, for the guardians of the threshold are not to be trifled with."

The writing is accompanied by an illustration of a portal or gateway, surrounded by symbols and strange, otherworldly creatures.

If the ritual is performed correctly and the names spoken aloud, the portal opens, revealing a shimmering, swirling vortex of energy. The portal remains open for 1d4+1 rounds before closing again. Any creatures still within the portal when it closes are shunted to a random plane of existence.

### Consequences

Passing through the portal transports the characters to a random location in the Ethereal Plane. The journey is disorienting and jarring, and each creature must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or become stunned for 1d4 rounds upon arrival.
