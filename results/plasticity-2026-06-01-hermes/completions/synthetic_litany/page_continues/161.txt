 "The names of the gates and their respective angels are as follows: The gate of the east is called Gelthor, and its angel is called Gorson; the gate of the south is called Algirath, and its angel is called Algoros; the gate of the west is called Darsarath, and its angel is called Darson; the gate of the north is called Ernarath, and its angel is called Ernados."

There is a table of seven "mystical seals" accompanied by angelic names, said to be "the names of the seven princes who stand before the presence of the Lord."

"By these seven seals, and the names of the seven angels, and the names of the seven princes, and the names of the ministering angels, and the names of the seals, and the names of the planetary spirits, and the names of the elementary spirits, and the names of the spirits of the twelve signs of the zodiac, and the names of the cherubim and seraphim, and the names of the elemental archspirits, and the names of the spirits of the four elements, and the names of the spirits of the nine orders of the heavens, and the names of the spirits of the earth
