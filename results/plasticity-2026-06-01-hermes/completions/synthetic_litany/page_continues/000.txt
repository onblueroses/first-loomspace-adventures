 “The Invocation of the Bornless One”, a lengthy prayer also known as the “Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia” or the “Invocation of the Heart Girt with a Serpent”, opens the ritual. It is followed by a set of conjurations of increasing intensity, each using a characteristic Goetic verse:

1. “I invoke and conjure you, O Spirit N., and, fortified with the power of the Supreme Majesty, I strongly command you by BARALAMENSIS, BALDAKIENSIS, PAUMACHIAE, and APOLOLOES, that, without delay or resistance, immediately and now, you appear before me, visibly and affably, in your proper form, to do what I command you, whatever my will may be, for you are conjured by the Name of the Living and True God, HELIOROMETH, in the name of whom all shudder, all fear, and all obey, whom the heavens, the earth, and the infernal regions venerate, and by the power of Whom I, fortified by His Celestial Power, command you with vehemence and justice. Come, then, promptly and without delay, to obey me, appearing and declaring to me your name
