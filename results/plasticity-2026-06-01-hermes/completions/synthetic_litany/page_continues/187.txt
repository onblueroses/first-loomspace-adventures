### Eleventh Talisman

This talisman is used to invoke the aid of the spirit Barzabel, who presides over the twenty-eight mansions of the Moon. The talisman is inscribed with the names of the angels and spirits that are associated with each of the twenty-eight lunar mansions. The talisman is said to grant the power to control the spirits associated with the lunar mansions, and to bind them to one's will. The talisman is also said to grant the power to control the tides, the weather, and the growth of plants.
