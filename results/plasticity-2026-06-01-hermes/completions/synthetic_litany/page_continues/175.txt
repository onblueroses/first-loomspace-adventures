 "Now when you have spoken all these words, which are the names of the Spirits that guard the Gates of the East, you will see a great host of Spirits, like a mighty army, standing before you. You will say to them:"

Hear me, all you Spirits of the East, and be my friends. I call upon you in peace, and in the name of the Highest, and by these names which are your own names, I charge that you will not fail to do my bidding, nor seek to harm me, or any other creature. Wherefore, be welcome, O Spirits of the East, and let us together, in the name of the Most High, do great work.

"Then will the Spirits of the East be glad to do your bidding, and they will gather around you, and you will feel them touch your face and hands, and you will hear their voices speaking to you, and they will do all that you ask of them, and they will be your friends."
