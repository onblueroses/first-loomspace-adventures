 

The names of the 30 spirits of the 2nd hour are: Kamelos, Thanerom, Thelasos, Merephon, Marnas, Kalabres, Ophener, Chorach, Aragor, Salaphar, Kerebos, Almarach, Thronos, Algor, Ypetheth, Oramar, Gremos, Xaron, Romas, Soterion, Ramath, Almaras, Achoral, Naphar, Otolorth, Ilerthep, Ramas, Emodas, Zamarth, Iachaph. The name of the lord of the 2nd hour is Tuot, and the name of his servant is Siam, and the name of his maid is Simeon.

The text then goes on with the spirits of the 3rd hour, and so on, for the full 24 hours. 

The Ars Notoria is one of five books within a grimoire called the Lesser Key of Solomon, an anonymous text (with editorial insertions by later authors including Aleister Crowley) that compiled from original texts dating back to the 17th century.
