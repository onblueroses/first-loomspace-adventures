 "When you have read the above, look up towards the heavens and say:

*Hear me, O ye celestial messengers! For I invoke you in the sacred names of Ochimoth, Lethos, Elohim Sabaoth, Adonai Tetragrammaton, by which names you are constrained to obey my will; and I adjure you by the might of the Divine Creator, and by the virtue of the said names, that you presently appear before me, in a visible form and reasonable shape, and that you do fulfil my commands, in all things which I shall ask of you, without any deceit, delusion, or delay, according to the virtue of the words above spoken, which were delivered to Moses, Aaron, David, Solomon, and others, who have written the revelation of your secrets. Come, then, in the name of Adonai Zebaoth, of Adonai Tetragrammaton, and of those holy names I have spoken; come, I say, and be obedient to my will, and do not think to frighten me with your threats, for the word of God is my armor, and His buckler defends me from all harm.
