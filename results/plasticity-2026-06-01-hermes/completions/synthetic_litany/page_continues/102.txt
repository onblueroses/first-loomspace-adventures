 "When you have said this, the spirit will come in a fair shape and will do as you command him. You must be bold."

This passage is the second of two versions of the invocation in the manuscript. The first, on the preceding page, is a shorter version without the names. The longer version has been shown to be a later addition.

## In popular culture

* The Key of Solomon is mentioned in the 1993 Italian film, Cemetery Man.
* In the 1998 anime series, Yu-Gi-Oh!, the Key of Solomon is a book possessed by the character Maximillion Pegasus, used to bind spirits into stone tablets.
* The Key of Solomon is mentioned in the 2009 Dan Brown novel, The Lost Symbol.
* In the 2010 video game, Amnesia: The Dark Descent, the Key of Solomon is used to perform a dark ritual.
* The Key of Solomon is mentioned in the 2013 video game, The Last of Us.
* In the 2019 video game, Fire Emblem: Three Houses, the Key of Solomon is a lost tome that contains ancient knowledge.
