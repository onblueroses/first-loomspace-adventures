## Powers

In the text, the Sigil of Baphomet is followed by the following text:

This symbol, when drawn by the hand of a person with the proper character, and in the proper colors, is believed to be the key to controlling the supernatural forces that govern the universe. It is said that the one who wears this symbol holds the secrets of the universe in his hands.

The text then continues by describing the powers attributed to Baphomet, including:

1. The ability to control the forces of nature, such as storms, earthquakes, and volcanic eruptions.
2. The power to manipulate time, allowing the wearer to see into the past and future.
3. The ability to communicate with and control supernatural entities, such as spirits, demons, and angels.
4. The power to heal the sick and injured, and even to raise the dead.
5. The ability to control the minds of others, and to influence their thoughts and actions.
6. The power to create and destroy matter, and to manipulate the elements.
7. The ability to grant wishes and to bestow great wealth and power upon the wearer.
