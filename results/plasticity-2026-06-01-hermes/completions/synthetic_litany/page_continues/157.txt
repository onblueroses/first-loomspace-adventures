### The Harrowing

Main article: The Harrowing (novel)The Harrowing is a 2006 horror novel by Alexandra Sokoloff, about a group of college students who spend Thanksgiving in a haunted house. The book was the winner of the 2006 International Thriller Writers Award for Best First Novel. The novel was later adapted into a 2021 horror film.

The Harrowing opens with the protagonist, Robin Stone, reluctantly agreeing to spend her Thanksgiving break with four other students at their dormitory, an allegedly haunted mansion. After the students perform a seance, they begin to experience strange occurrences, including unexplained noises, moving objects, and terrifying visions. As the supernatural events escalate, Robin and her companions must uncover the dark history of the mansion and confront the malevolent forces that threaten their lives.
