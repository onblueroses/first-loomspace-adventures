 “When the way is opened, the gates will be revealed. Enter and seal the gate behind you.”

The words of the ritual translated from Latin are:

The traveler speaks: “I stand at the threshold, seeking passage to the realms beyond. I call upon the guardians of the gates to grant me entry, for I hold the key to the mysteries.”

The guardians respond: “Speak the names that open the way.”

The traveler recites the names: “Throvenios, Mareth, Quelimon, Iskon, Kerodim, Astrelach, Volmaroth, Drabasaim, Olempraem, Crisilim, Bidromael, Octaron, Saphrenoth, Yodalim, Iathreon, Mirzabet, Calenuph, Theomarach, Belendath, Glamoreth, Sinabaroth, Hialep, Trifonath, Olomar, Vesperath, Khalapeth, Estrymon; Brashvoreth, Liminem Adventi.”

The traveler continues: “When the way is opened, the gates will be revealed. Enter and seal the gate behind you.”
