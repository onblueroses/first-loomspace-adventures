There are instructions for invoking the spirits of Mars, Venus, Mercury, Jupiter, and Saturn in a similar manner. The grimoire also provides spells for summoning spirits associated with the four elements, and for summoning spirits that can aid in tasks such as finding hidden treasure, gaining love, or causing harm to enemies.

The grimoire is notable for its use of a complex system of magic circles, seals, and symbols, which the magician is instructed to create and use in specific ways when performing the spells. The spells themselves are often quite elaborate, involving lengthy incantations, specific astrological timing, and the use of various magical tools and substances.
