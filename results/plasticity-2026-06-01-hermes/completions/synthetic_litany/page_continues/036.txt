### The Ars Notoria

The Ars Notoria contains a series of prayers related to those in The Sworn Book of Honorius, but intended to grant the gift of instant knowledge of every science, art and language. Like the Sworn Book, the prayers of the Ars Notoria are divided into a series of 'notes', with long, carefully detailed prayers for each note.

The Ars Notoria is traditionally attributed to Solomon, and even mentions 'Solomon' as an authority in some of the prayers. Like the Sworn Book, the Ars Notoria promises knowledge of every science, art and language, and indeed the two texts seem to be related, with the Ars Notoria being "the more 'Christian' version". According to Robert Mathiesen, the two texts also share a similar cosmological framework.
