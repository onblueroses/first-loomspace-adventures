 "Having pronounced the foregoing words, the spirits will come in haste to obey thy will. Command them, and they shall obey thee."

## Legacy

The figure of the archangel Hael appears in the Simon Necronomicon, where it is said to have been invoked by the author of the Al Azif, Abdul Alhazred.

The book was used in the 2018 film The Clovehitch Killer.
