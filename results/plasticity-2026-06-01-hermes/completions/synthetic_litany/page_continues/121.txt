The book also contains a section with seals that are to be used in the act of conjuring the demons that correspond to each seal. The purpose is to bind the demon described in the seal, thus allowing the conjurer to command the demon to do their bidding. The conjurer must be sure to remain in control of the demon; if they show any sign of fear or hesitancy, the demon will have control over the conjurer instead.
