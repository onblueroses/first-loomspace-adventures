 "When the sun shines brightly and the wind is still, then let the conjurer make this circle in the place appointed, and let him place therein the name of the one whom he wishes to come, and that person shall indeed appear before him."

The conjuration of the fifty names is also found in the manuscript H 130 of the Bibliothèque de l'Arsenal, titled Livre des conjurations, and in the manuscript 25314 of the Bibliothèque nationale de France, titled Secret des secrets, fait par le Seigneur Trismégiste, ou Clé d'Aranca, traduit de l'hébreu.
