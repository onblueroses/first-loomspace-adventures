 "The spirits are now at hand, and will soon appear before you. You may now address them, and they will answer your inquiries."

This passage is followed by instructions for dismissing the spirits once the summoner is finished with them. These include the recital of another set of names, which the text claims are "the names by which these spirits are licensed to depart."

The text then closes with a warning that anyone who does not follow the instructions carefully will be in danger of being attacked by the spirits, which the text asserts have "power to blind men, to transfix them with darts, to cast them into a burning fever, and to inflict other evils."
