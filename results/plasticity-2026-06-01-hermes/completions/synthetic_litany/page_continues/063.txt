 

* 18. The secret names of the 42 Ministers of the Hierarchies, who govern under the 7 Mighty Names of God, are the names of the 42 Assessors of the Divine Tribunal, whom the Hebrews call the great Sanhedrin.
* 19. These 42 Assessors are also the 42 Ministers of the 7 Angels of the Presence, and they are the 42 Ministers of the 7 Spirits of God, who are the 7 Planetary Angels.
* 20. Each of the 7 Planetary Angels has 6 Ministers, who serve him.
* 21. These 42 Ministers are the 42 Judges of the Souls in the afterlife, and they are the 42 Angels of the Divine Presence.
