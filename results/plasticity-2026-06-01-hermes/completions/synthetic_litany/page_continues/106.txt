 "The second time thou shalt say the words, the second ring of light shall open, and the spirits of the second ring shall come forth and make their obeisance unto thee, and shall serve thee. And the third time thou shalt say the words, the third ring of light shall open, and the spirits of the third ring shall come forth and make their obeisance unto thee, and shall serve thee."

The fourth time thou sayest the words, the spirits of the four winds shall come, and they shall make their obeisance unto thee, and shall swear to serve thee and to reveal unto thee the secrets of the earth, and the secrets of the air, and the secrets of the water, and the secrets of the fire. For they are of the earth, and of the air, and of the water, and of the fire, and they shall reveal unto thee the secret knowledge of all these things.
