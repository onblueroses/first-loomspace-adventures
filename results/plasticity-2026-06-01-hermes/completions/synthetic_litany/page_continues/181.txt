For I am the master of your path, and I hold the key to your kingdom. Rise, and let your forms be manifest within the four walls of this chamber. Come from the abyss, and greet me with the signs of the mysteries. Let us walk together once more, as we did in the days of old. For I am the one who holds the secrets of the world, and I have come to reclaim my rightful place among you.

And so it was that the Spirits of the East appeared before me, in forms both terrible and beautiful. They spoke in voices that shook the very foundations of the chamber, and their eyes burned with the fire of a thousand suns. They said unto me, "We are the guardians of the way of light, and we have come to guide you on your path. But first, you must prove yourself worthy of our teachings. For the way of light is not for the faint of heart, and only those who are pure of spirit may walk its path."
