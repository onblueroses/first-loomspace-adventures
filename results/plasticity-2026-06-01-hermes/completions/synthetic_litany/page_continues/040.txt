 "The Great Circle of the Quarters shall be cast in the names of the Great Elemental Kings, and their sigils shall be drawn upon the Earth, that the winds may carry their voices unto the Four Corners of the World."

This passage indicates that the circle is to be cast in the names of the Great Elemental Kings, and their sigils are to be drawn upon the earth. The purpose of this is so that the winds may carry their voices to the four corners of the world.

The manuscript then provides the names and sigils of the Great Elemental Kings:

In the East: "Ghob" - King of the Gnomes, ruler of the Earth.

In the South: "Djinn" - King of the Salamanders, ruler of Fire.

In the West: "Nicksa" - King of the Nymphs, ruler of Water.

In the North: "Paralda" - King of the Sylphs, ruler of the Air.

These names and sigils are to be used in casting the circle and invoking the powers of the elements.
