 "By these names, and in the name of the elemental forces, spirits, and angels of the four quarters, I do call you, O spirits of the East. Come forth, and show yourselves in this mirror of art, perfect in form and speaking with clear voice, that I may commune with you in the skills and powers of your office, in the arts of the Magus.

Come now, peaceable and lowly, affable and visible, to make your presence known by image or by sign, and to answer my questions clearly without falsehood or duplicity, for I am a true servant of the Hierarchy of Light. Therefore, prepare to give your obedience, and to answer my commands, in the name of the Holy and Formless One, who created Heaven and Earth and Hell."

After this invocation, the operator waits for the spirit to appear. If after a time it does not, the operator repeats the invocation, and waits again. If there is still no response, the operator closes the ritual, and tries again another time. The spirit may be bound with a license to depart, or the operator may simply close the ritual.

The grimoire makes no mention of what the spirits look like, or how they will appear. It is also not specific
