 "And when you say these names, you will see a great smoke, and a light will come from the smoke, and you will see a great stone altar, and upon the altar a figure of the god, and the god himself standing beside the altar, and he will tell you anything you wish to know."

Here the text breaks off, and the remainder of the ritual is lost.

## In popular culture

In 2007, a version of the invocation was used in the Disney film Underdog during the scene where the antagonist, Simon Barsinister, attempts to give himself the powers that he had given to the titular character. However, he only recites the first three lines of the invocation, and the ritual is interrupted by the arrival of Underdog.
