 “When you have said these names, the spirits will obey you and you will see them gathered there before you. And when they have arrived, speak to them as you will, and ask of them those things which you desire, and they will obey you and will give you a true and certain response. And when you wish to send them away, say to them: ‘In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, Amen. Go in peace to your dwelling places, and let there be peace between us and you. And when I need you, I will call upon you and you will come to me. And do not delay, but rather be prepared and ready to come when I call you. And when you have said these things, they will depart from you and will leave you in peace, and you will be able to go on your way.”
