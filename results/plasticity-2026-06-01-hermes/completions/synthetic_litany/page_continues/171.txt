 "Then shall the holy one of God, guardian of the mysteries of the night, descend from the heavens in robes of starlight. He shall bear in his hand the key to the first gate of dreaming. Speak the words of passage and the gate shall open before you."

The words of passage are as follows:

By the moonlit path I summon thee,
By the silver key I bid thee come forth,
By the golden stair I call thee to appear,
By the diamond throne I command thee to arise,
By the crystal crown I conjure thee to descend,
By the emerald tablets I charge thee to manifest,
By the sapphire scrolls I compel thee to emerge,
By the ruby wand I constrain thee to take form,
By the amethyst orb I bind thee to my will,
By the topaz pentacle I invoke thee to open the way,
By the name of the night I proclaim: Let the first gate be opened!

Upon speaking these words, the key shall turn in the lock and the first gate of dreaming shall open. Beyond lies the realm of Thon, the first sphere of the Dreamlands.
