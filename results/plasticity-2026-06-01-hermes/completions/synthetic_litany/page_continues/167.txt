 "By these names, and in the power of the Holy Trinity, I conjure you, O Spirits, that without delay you shall move and come from all parts of the world, more especially from your private abodes, and appear before us in this circle, in a fair and human shape, gently, courteously, and without noise; and that you shall speak to us audibly in a plain and easy manner, and fulfil our commands and desires in all things, so far as they shall not extend beyond the power given unto you by the Creator, and in so doing shall cause no harm unto us or any creature. By the command of your Creator, and the virtue of our holy office, and by these names and words, I conjure and charge you, O Spirits, to fulfil our will and desires, faithfully and truly, in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. Amen." (The names above mentioned are the chief spirits ruling in the planetary hours.)
