 

By the names that open the way I call upon you, O spirits of the path. Come forth and manifest in the smoke of the incense. Guide me through the mysteries of the path of _____, that I may attain to true knowledge and mastery. In the names of the Mighty Ones of the Night, I call upon you. Come forth! Come forth! In the names of the Mighty Ones of the Night, I conjure you. Come forth! Come forth!

The magician then performs the Greater Invoking Ritual of the Pentagram of the element, followed by the Supreme Invoking Ritual of the Hexagram of the planet. The magician then prays a conjuration to the angels of the path, followed by a conjuration to the spirits of the path, and then a prayer to the four archangels of the Quarters, ruling over the four elements. The magician then states his purpose for invoking the spirits, and then closes the ritual with a final prayer.
