 “When you have said these words, you will see come forth from the north a figure of a king, who will say to you: ‘I am Throvenios, king of the North. What do you seek of me? Speak, for I am come here only to do your will.’ You must reply to him: ‘I have called you only that you may do my will and serve me in all that I shall command you.’ He will reply to you: ‘I will do all that you ask of me, for I am subject to your power by the words you have spoken.’ When he has said this, you must say to him: ‘I command you to open the gates of the four winds, that the spirits and demons of the four winds may come forth, and that they may be obedient to my will and my power.’”

In the same way the operator summons the kings of the south, east and west, Mareth, Quelimon and Iskon. They are commanded to open the gates of the four winds, and then the operator proceeds to call the spirits of the four winds themselves:
