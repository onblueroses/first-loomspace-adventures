 "Now, in the Name of the Mighty and Eternal God, I command you, Spirits, to immediately and completely fulfill all that which I have commanded of you, or that which I shall command you to do. Come to me now, and obey my every word, or be prepared to suffer the torments of an eternal hell, from which there is no escape. Come! It is done."

The book ends with a short section titled "Of the Blood Pact," which describes the manner in which to make a pact with a spirit. The text says that the pact should be written with the blood of the operator. The name of the spirit is written on parchment, along with its character or seal. The operator must then sign his or her name in their own blood, and then the pact should be burned, with the ashes saved in a special container. The ashes are to be used in magical workings involving the spirit.
