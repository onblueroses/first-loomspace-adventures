 "The spirits of the eighth hour have their nature from Mars, and are called the Sons of the Mighty. Their bodies are of a red color, but their garments are of a blue like azure, and they are all girded about with red girdles like blood. They have upon their heads garlands of red laurel, and in their hands they hold sharp sickles. Their motion is like that of an army running before a prince to battle; they march as if they were upon horseback, and the sound of their motion is like the sound of an host of men, and of the sound of the shaking of armor, and of the neighing of war-horses. These spirits are powerful in all works of wrath and vengeance. They are under four dukes, and these eight. Arlith, Harath, Ubarin, Ibranis, Nogar, Basielas, Glarot, and Nisroch the mighty. And these eight dukes have under them a hundred and five ministers, and the spirits subject unto these are twelve hundred and eighty and one. They are all spirits of Mars, and their names be written in the Book of War."

The name Nisroch is also found in the Bible
