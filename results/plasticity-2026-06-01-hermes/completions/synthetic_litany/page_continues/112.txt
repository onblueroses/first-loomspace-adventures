 "I conjure and call upon you, O strong and holy angels, in the name of the Highest God, to come forthwith and without delay to my aid, and to dispatch quickly and at once to me, and to make appear plainly before me, the Spirit N., in a fair and comely human form, without harm to my body or soul, and to enable me to converse with him plainly and clearly, so that I may understand his answers to my questions."

### The Fourth Secret

The fourth secret is the Secret of Secrets, and contains the invocation of the four elements and the elemental spirits.

### The Fifth Secret

The fifth secret is the Secretum Secretorum, which contains the invocation of the spirits of the planets, with their names and seals.

### The Sixth Secret

The sixth secret contains the instructions for the preparation of the Pentacle, the Sword, the Wand, the Garment, and the Ring.

### The Seventh Secret

The seventh secret contains the instructions for the preparation of the Virgin Parchment, the Sacrifice, and the Conjuration of the Demons.

### The Eighth Secret

The eighth secret contains the instructions for the preparation of the Mirror of Solomon, which is used for divination and communication with spirits.
