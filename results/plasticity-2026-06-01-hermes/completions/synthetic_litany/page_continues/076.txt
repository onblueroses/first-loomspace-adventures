 "The power of the Opening of the Way is upon me. The way is opened before me. Nothing shall obstruct or hinder me. I shall pass through. I shall pass through the Gates of the Abyss. I shall pass through the Gates of the Pylons. The way is opened before me. The Way is opened within me. The Way is opened within the Circle, and the Circle is the Way."

The text then instructs the magician to "go forth" in the name of the "Great Watchers" who preside over the "secret way" between the Gates of the Pylons, and to speak the words of passage: "I am the Watcher on the Threshold. I am the Watcher at the Gates. I am the Watcher at the Abyss. I am the Watcher on the Mount of Sunrise. I am He Who Opens the Way."

The text continues: "The Gates are flung open. The Way is open before me. The Way is opened within me. I pass through the Gates. I pass through the Pylon of the Abyss. I am he who opens the way. I am the Way. The Way is me."
