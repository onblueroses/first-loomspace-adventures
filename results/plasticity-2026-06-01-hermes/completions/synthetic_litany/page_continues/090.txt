### The Greater Key of Solomon

The Greater Key of Solomon, a medieval grimoire attributed to Solomon but dating to the 14th century or later, includes a conjuration which begins: "I conjure you by the holy names of God Anerakin, Sodasim, Halsatia, Rerihatia, Gelomiros, Geroch, Deleath, Salimarim, Galmel, Galiachim, Gorath, Tormath, Mardiphrat, Talmath, Gorath, Gorath, Mardiphrat, Talmath, Gelomiros, Geroch, Deleath, Salimarim, Galmel, Galiachim, Gorath, Tormath, Anerakin, Sodasim, Halsatia, Rerihatia."
