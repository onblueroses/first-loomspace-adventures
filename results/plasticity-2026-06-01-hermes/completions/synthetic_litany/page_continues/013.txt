The Ars Notoria is the fifth and final book of the Lesser Key of Solomon, also known as the Lemegeton, a grimoire on demonology compiled in the 17th century from sources dating back to the early modern period. It is divided into two parts: the Theurgia Goetia, which deals with the conjuration of 31 aerial spirits; and the Ars Notoria, which deals with the invocation of the Olympic spirits.
