The “Dread Conjuration of the Spirits” is the most potent and dangerous of all the rituals in the _Sworn Book_, and it is to be used only in the most extreme circumstances. It is a summoning of all the spirits of the air, both good and evil, and when it is performed, the spirits will come like a tempest, with great fury and noise. It is a perilous undertaking, for the evil spirits will seek to deceive and harm the magician in any way they can.
