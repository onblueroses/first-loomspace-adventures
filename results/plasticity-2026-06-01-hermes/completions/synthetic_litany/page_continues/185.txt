A footnote provides further instructions for the successful execution of the ritual:

The magician must be careful to wear the correct attire, which consists of a white robe and a girdle of lion's skin three inches wide. He must also have a silver ring on the middle finger of his left hand, on which is engraved the five-pointed star, and on the index finger a gold ring on which is the name of the Angel he intends to evoke. He must also have a lighted candle made of white wax, and a brazier containing live coals, into which he must pour three drops of the sacred oil.

The magician must be careful to choose a secluded spot for the invocation, where he is not likely to be disturbed by curious onlookers, and he must also make sure that the time is right for the operation, for if he attempts to evoke an Angel during an inauspicious planetary hour, the Angel will not appear, and the magician will have wasted his time and effort.
