 “The Black Seal of the Scorpion is one of the most powerful sigils of the Art of Geomancy. It is to be inscribed upon a disk of pure gold, and worn about the neck, or carried upon the person, and when the Practitioner desires to work the Art, he shall hold the Sigil in his right hand, or lay it upon the table before him, and he shall be certain of the truth of the answer he receives.”
