 "In the name of the mighty and incomprehensible God, the Alpha and the Omega, I conjure you, spirits above named, by the powers of your lords, that you will give me a sign of your presence here, and that you will do for me as I shall command you, to the best of your abilities, and that you will do so without delay, deception, or evasion. I conjure you by the mighty words 'Anepheraton, Nahid, Eroboal, Habinah, Lealeyo, Yathestog, Hesediel, Belsefas, Dasas, Sarbandon, Nogah, Belepith, Rasmosas, Gisgih, Delbeth, Othimeos, Athanatos, Ataph, Gerberbeth, Aphetson, Yalgal, Anagotos', mighty words ruling the spirits of the dead, that you will obey me as you obey your own lords, and that you will not depart from here without my leave. Come, then, spirits! Hear my words, and make haste to obey your servant, in the name of the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. In the name of the Father, and of the Son
