 "And the Seal is to be made in the hour when the moon is in the horns of Capricorn, and the Scorpion is rising in the East. And thou shalt make it of green wax, and anoint it with the oil of the hyssop and the fig, and thou shalt lay it in the center of the circle, and it shall be unto thee as the key of the gate, and the seal of the covenant, and the symbol of the mighty names which guard the way."

The Seal of the Scorpion is reproduced on the next page. It is an elaborate sigil composed of interlocking lines and geometric shapes, with the image of a scorpion at its center.

The following page contains a warning: "Know that this Seal is a mighty key, and it may unlock the way to great power. But it is not to be used lightly, for the forces it may summon are not easily controlled, and the unwary may find themselves overwhelmed by that which they have called forth. Use it with caution, and only when the need is great."

The text continues with a series of invocations and rituals designed to harness the power of the Seal and the entities it represents. These rituals involve the use of specific incense, candles, and offerings
