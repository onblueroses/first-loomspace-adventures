### The book of magical talismans

The Ars Paulina is titled "The Pauline Art of Solomon" in the Mathers edition. The grimoire says that this system of incantations and talismans was discovered by the Apostle Paul, but in the text it says it was given by the Archangel Michael. It is said that the Apostle Paul discovered the words and the Demons that are associated with these words, and these Demons that he summoned and controlled were not destructive, but they were known to benefit the person who controlled them. The Demons that are part of this book are not the same as the Demons in the Goetia.
