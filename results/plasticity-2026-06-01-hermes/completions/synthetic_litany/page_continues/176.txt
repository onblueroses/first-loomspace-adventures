 "If the spirits do not appear, repeat the above from the beginning, and this can be done three times. If after the third time they still do not appear, begin again from the mark * and say as follows:". The text continues with a short prayer that the spirits appear before the conjurer in a pleasant form, and ends with the names "Anai, Ehai, Emal, Hetha, Hemal, Hama, Has, Teschethal, Matthew, Mach, Maguth, Mabarat, Maianiat, Emssabal".
