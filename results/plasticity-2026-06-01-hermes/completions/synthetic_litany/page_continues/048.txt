 "Now take the seal of Lucifer, and place it in a hidden area close to you before the sun rises in the east, and at the rise of the sun, take the seal in your hands and repeat the following invocation."

#### Invocation of Lucifer

Lucifer, Ouyar, Chameron, Aliseon, Mandousin, Premy, Oriet, Naydrus, Esmony, Eparinesont, Estiot, Dumosson, Danochar, Casmiel, Hayras, Fabelleonthu, Sodirno, Peatham, Venite, Venite, Lucifer Amen.

#### Closing

The Invocation of Lucifer ends with "Come, Come, Lucifer, Amen." The ritual then has a closing that is also used in other invocations in the manuscript:

Go in peace unto your places. May there be peace between us, you and I. Be you ready to come when called, for the seal of the covenant between us is now renewed. In the Name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.
