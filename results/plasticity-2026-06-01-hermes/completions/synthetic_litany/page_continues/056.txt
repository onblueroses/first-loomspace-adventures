 "Now, make the sign of the Cross, and say, 'In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, I beseech thee to grant my request, N.'"

The names given in the passage are not found in any of the standard dictionaries of mythology, demonology, or angels. They may have been invented by the author.

## Authorship

The book was published in the French language in 1855 or 1857 by Charles Ledoyen of Paris, France. It was reprinted in 1997 by Guy Trédaniel, also of Paris. The author is given as "Dom N. S.", which may be a pseudonym. The name "Dom N. S." is not found in any standard biographical references, and the author's identity is unknown.
