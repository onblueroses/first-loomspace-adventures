 

"By these names and by their virtues and powers, I conjure you, O spirit, who dwells in the shadows of the Abyss, to appear before me in a human form, without noise or terror, and to answer truthfully all my questions. I command you by the sacred names of God and by the powers of the Archangels and Angels, to fulfill my will and to obey my commands. Come forth, O spirit, and manifest yourself before me, in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen."

The page concludes with a series of magical symbols and sigils, which are intended to reinforce the power of the invocation and to protect the magician from any potential harm or deception by the summoned spirit.
