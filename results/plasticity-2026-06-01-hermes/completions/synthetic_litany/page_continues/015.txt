 "And the great seal shall be broken and the gate shall open. The gate of the seven-pointed star shall be opened. And the key shall be given unto me, that I might open the way."

The page goes on to say: "The circle of the gate shall be opened, and the gate shall be opened, and the key shall be given unto me, that I might open the way."

This is followed by a series of invocations and names, including:

"By the power of the seven-pointed star, I invoke the great guardian of the gate. I call upon the mighty one, the opener of the way, the keeper of the key. I summon thee, great guardian, to open the gate and grant me passage. I command thee by the power of the seven-pointed star, by the power of the great seal, and by the power of the holy names:
