 "This is the commandment of the god of the word to his servants who are given the power of the word. To the god of the word, who has placed the power of the word in the mouth of his servants, is the praise of his name, and the praise of his word."

This is followed by a prayer, and then the manuscript breaks off.

## Translations

The text was translated into Latin in 1686 by John Scheffer, and published in his edition of the Stenography of Trithemius. In 1801 Matthias Friedrich Becherer published the Greek text, and a German translation, with notes. This was followed by a corrected Greek text, with Scheffer's Latin version, and notes, by Gustav Bayer, in 1885. An English translation, with commentary, was published by Florence A. Gragg, in 1912. A French translation was published by Touwaide in 2002.
