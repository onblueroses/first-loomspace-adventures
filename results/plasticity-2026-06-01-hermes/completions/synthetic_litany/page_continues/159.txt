 "But first thou must say the psalm, 'Quicumque vult,' and in a clear voice, and with the fear of the divine and with great confidence, thou shalt say: 'O Lord, Who hast made man out of earth, and hast recreated him out of the earth according to Thine image and likeness, Who hast placed the seeds of the future in the present, granting them the power of the hidden secrets of nature, and the foreknowledge of future events; I pray Thee to grant that we, made in Thine image and formed by Thine hand, may have power through these, Thy most holy names, given to Thy servants, to see the sapphire heavens and to know the celestial angels and the images of living things and the ways of life and death and the figures and positions of the stars and the secrets of the mysteries of nature, so that we may know the things which are, and are to come, for the praise, honor, and glory of Thy name. Amen.'"
