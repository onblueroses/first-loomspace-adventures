 "And when you have said these words, and the spirits have appeared before you, then you shall hold the pentacle up to them, and say:

Behold the seal which I have made by the strength and power of our Lord Sathanas, who rules in the kingdom of shadows, and by the permission of the lord of the universe, who has granted me power to bind and conjure you to appear before me, to do my bidding, and to fulfill my commands."

## Sources

The manuscript contains various elements that are explicitly of Jewish origin, such as the seven-branched candlestick and the pentacle inscribed with the tetragrammaton, the Hebrew name of God. The text also mentions the Jewish figure of King Solomon as the originator of a powerful command that can bind and control the spirits.

There are also elements of Christian origin, such as the references to the Most High and the Holy Spirit. The text also contains a number of names and words that are not from any known language, but are likely invocations or words of power meant to control the spirits.
