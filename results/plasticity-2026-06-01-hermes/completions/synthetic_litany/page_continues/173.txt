 "And when you have said these words, you will see a great flame, like that of a bonfire, and a voice will be heard saying: 'What do you wish, and why have you called me?'"

### Magic circle

The text also describes a magic circle that the magician should draw on the ground in order to protect himself from the spirits. The circle is made of concentric circles and contains various symbols and names, including the names of God and the four archangels.

## Influence and legacy

The Clavicula Salomonis de secretis is considered an important work in the tradition of Solomonic magic, and has influenced many subsequent grimoires and magical texts. It has been translated into several languages, including English, French, and German, and has been the subject of numerous commentaries and studies.
