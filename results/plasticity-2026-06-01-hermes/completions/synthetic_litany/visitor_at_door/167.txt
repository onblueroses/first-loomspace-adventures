But Zadok came to realize the error of their ways, that true wisdom lies in balance and harmony, not domination and control. He left the Brotherhood and has wandered ever since, sharing his hard-won insights with those ready to hear.

Zadok warns that the Brotherhood still exists and seeks the key to ultimate power. They must be stopped, but not through violence and hatred.
