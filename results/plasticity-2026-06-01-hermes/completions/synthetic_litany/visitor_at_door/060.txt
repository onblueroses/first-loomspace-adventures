The spirits will act upon the message, and they will bring about the desired result. The spirits will work with haste, and they will work with great power. The spirits will ensure that the result is achieved.

The visitor thanks the spirits. The spirits, in turn, thank the visitor. The visitor takes his leave of the spirits. The visitor leaves the room, and the door closes behind him. The spirits remain in the room, and they begin their work. The work will be done, and the result will be achieved. So it is, and so it shall be.
