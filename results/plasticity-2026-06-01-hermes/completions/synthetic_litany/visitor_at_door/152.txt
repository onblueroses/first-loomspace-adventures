 Their voice is not quite human. “I come seeking the truth of a lie.”

“Who are you to ask this of us?” you reply, as the ritual demands.

“I am the one who was promised an answer.”

The ritual is complete. The door opens. The figure steps through, resolving into a clearer shape as they cross the threshold. Your breath catches in your throat. You know them. You have always known them.

They are the Stranger.

---

The Stranger is a figure that appears in a number of magical traditions, often as part of an initiation or revelation. Sometimes they are a literal person, sometimes a metaphor or vision. Sometimes they are the magician, sometimes someone—or something—else entirely. They are a messenger, a guide, a herald of change. They bring secrets, and challenges, and truths that can only be grasped in the space between sleep and waking.

In the esoteric traditions of the West, the Stranger is often equated with the Holy Guardian Angel of the grimoires and the Abramelin tradition. In the Sufi tradition, the Stranger is sometimes identified with al-Khidr, the Green Man, who appears to seekers in their hour of need to offer guidance and wisdom.
