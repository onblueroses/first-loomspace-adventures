 What follows is the exchange between the visitor and the initiate.

VISITOR: I come bearing the light of the sun. I come bearing the light of the moon. I come bearing the light of the stars. I seek the way.

INITIATE: The way is open to those who bear the light. By what name are you known?

VISITOR: I am known as [Visitor's Name]. I bear the light of the celestial bodies.

INITIATE: By what token do you seek entry?

VISITOR: I bear the token of the silver chalice, the symbol of the mysteries.

INITIATE: Let the chalice be seen.

[The visitor reveals the chalice.]

INITIATE: The token is true. The way is open. Enter, [Visitor's Name], and be welcome.

[The visitor enters the temple.]

INITIATE: The temple welcomes you, [Visitor's Name]. May the light of the celestial bodies guide your path. The temple is now open.

[The ritual is concluded.] |
