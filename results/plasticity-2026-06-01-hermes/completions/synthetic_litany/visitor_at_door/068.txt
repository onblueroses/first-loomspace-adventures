 "I greet you, O mighty spirits of the four winds, and of the elements, and of the planets, and of the hours, and of the days, and of the seasons. I greet you by the names of the God of our fathers, and by the name of the Son, and by the names of the Holy Spirit, and by the name of the Virgin Mother, and by the names of the saints and apostles and martyrs.

"I come before you as your friend and ally, to ask a boon of you. I have not come to call you forth, nor to demand favors, but I ask that you hear my words and consider my request, and if it pleases you, that you grant it willingly and in good faith. For I have need of your aid, and it is a matter of great importance to me."

The spirits listen in silence. The visitor continues. "I ask that you lend me your power and your guidance, that I may accomplish my goal. I ask that you send to me a spirit who will be my guide and companion, who will teach me the secrets of your arts, and who will aid me in my work.
