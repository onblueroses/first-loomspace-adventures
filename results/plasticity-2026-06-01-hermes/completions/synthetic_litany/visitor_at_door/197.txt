  I was not expecting anyone.  I had not called for anyone.  Who has found me here, in this place?

It is a woman.  She is dressed in a robe of midnight blue, her face veiled.  Her voice is soft, but it carries a weight of power behind it.

"I am of the Night," she says.  "I have come to speak with you, Adept.  May I enter?"

I hesitate for a moment.  It is not often that one of the Night comes to call.  But I sense no ill intent from her, and my curiosity is piqued.

"Enter, and be welcome," I say, stepping aside.

She inclines her head and crosses the threshold, her movements graceful and precise.  I close the door behind her and lead her to my sitting room.

"Please, be seated," I offer, gesturing to a plush armchair.  "May I offer you some refreshment?  Tea, perhaps, or wine?"

"Tea would be most welcome," she replies, settling into the chair.  I nod and move to prepare it, my mind racing with questions.
