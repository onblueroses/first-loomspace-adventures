Sanctuary of the Arcane

The Sanctuary is a vast, circular chamber with a high, domed ceiling. The walls are adorned with intricate murals depicting the history of magic, from the first primitive rituals to the most advanced arcane formulae. The floor is a complex mosaic of interlocking geometric patterns, each representing a different branch of magical study.

In the center of the chamber stands a pedestal, upon which rests the Book of the Arcane, a massive tome bound in black leather and clasped with silver. The book is open, and its pages are filled with dense, spidery script in a language that seems to shift and change as you look at it.
