 They introduce themselves by their true name and their nature, and they state their business.

The summoner considers their request. If the summoner wishes to grant the visitor entry, they speak the following:

The path is open. Enter and be welcome.

If the summoner wishes to deny the visitor entry, they speak the following:

The path is closed. I bid you depart.

The visitor complies with the summoner's decision. If entry is granted, they may enter the circle. If entry is denied, they must depart immediately.

Once the ritual is complete, the summoner speaks the following to close the way:

The way is shut. Let no more pass.

The ritual is ended. The summoner extinguishes the candles and removes the circle. The visitor, if granted entry, is now free to interact with the summoner as they see fit, bound by the terms of their agreement. If the visitor was denied entry, they may not return unless summoned again.

Speak the names with care, for to summon is to open a door that must be closed with equal care. May your conjurations be true and your visitors be bound by your will. So it is done. | 1,300 |
