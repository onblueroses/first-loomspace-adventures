 “I come in peace, seeking an audience with the Queen of the Realm, in the name of the one who gave me these tokens.”

The door opens, and a figure in shining armor stands before you. “Enter, then, and be welcome. I am Armandel, a humble servant of the Queen. I will take you to her.”

Armandel leads you through the shining halls of the palace, which seem to be made of a material that is both crystal and metal at the same time. The walls and floor emit a soft, radiant light, and the air is filled with a gentle, shimmering mist. You pass by many other beings, some human-like, others very different, but all with an air of nobility and grace.

Finally, you arrive at a grand throne room. At the far end, on a throne that seems to be made of pure light, sits a figure of breathtaking beauty. She is tall and slender, with long, flowing hair that seems to be made of strands of gold and silver. Her eyes are like stars, and her skin glows with an inner light. She is dressed in robes of shimmering white and gold, and a delicate crown of intertwined flowers and stars rests on her head.
