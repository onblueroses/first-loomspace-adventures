 He says:

I have come from the place of the light, from the place of the morning, where the sky is without cloud, and the air without shadow. I have come to the place where the sun is hidden, where the moon is veiled, where the light is drowned in darkness. I have come to the land of shadows, to the gate of the underworld, to the threshold of the hidden temple. I have come to the house of the dead, to the hall of the forgotten, to the palace of the forsaken. I have come to the sanctuary of the lost, to the tomb of the unknown, to the crypt of the unremembered.

I have come to the gate of the hidden temple, to the door of the secret sanctuary, to the portal of the unseen shrine. I have come to the threshold of the forgotten, to the entrance of the unremembered, to the gateway of the lost. I have come to the place where the light is hidden, where the truth is veiled, where the wisdom is concealed. I have come to the temple of the hidden god, to the shrine of the unseen goddess, to the sanctuary of the unknown power.

I have come to the place where the shadows gather, where the darkness reigns
