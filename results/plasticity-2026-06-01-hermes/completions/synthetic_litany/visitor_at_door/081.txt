 "Greetings, I come in the name of the Night. I am here to celebrate the mysteries of the sacred fire and water." 

The priest or priestess replies. "Enter, and be welcome, in the name of the Night. May the sacred fire and water cleanse and bless you."

The priest or priestess marks the visitor's forehead with ashes from the fire. The visitor enters and takes their place among the other celebrants.

When all have entered, the priest or priestess closes the door and speaks:

The circle is sealed. Let none depart until the rite is ended, lest the sacred bond be broken. We stand between the worlds, in a time outside of time, in a place not of earth nor of heaven, where the sacred fire and water meet. Let the rite begin.

The priest or priestess lights the sacred fire and speaks:

In the beginning was the Night, and in the Night was the One, and the One was the Night. The Night gave birth to the stars, and the stars gave birth to the sun and moon. The sun and moon gave birth to the earth, and the earth gave birth to all living things.
