 (If there are two or more visitors, they should all speak in unison, as though with one voice.)

We are sent by the power of the most high.

The host responds:

By what power?

The visitor responds:

By the power of the grand word of the aeons, by the power of the holy angels of light, by the power of the secret wisdom guarded by the stars, by the power that rules the worlds and sets the boundaries of the universe. By this power we come before you.

The host responds:

Enter, then, in the name of that power.

The visitor enters, and the host closes the door. The host and visitor then greet each other in whatever way is appropriate to them and to the occasion.

It is not necessary for the host and visitor to perform the complete ritual of opening and closing the door every time either one goes in or out. Once the ritual has been done once, the door may be considered open until the gathering is over, even if the door is physically closed and locked.
