 The door opens. I am permitted to enter, and it closes behind me.

I am in a small room with walls of dark stone, lit by torches set in iron sconces. The room is unfurnished save for a low wooden table, upon which rests a brass chalice and a small wooden box. On the wall opposite the door, a circular opening leads to another room beyond. The opening is surrounded by a ring of runes that seem to pulse with a dim red light.

I approach the table and examine the chalice and box. The chalice is plain, unadorned, and empty. The box is intricately carved with symbols and patterns that seem to shift and change as I look at them. I open the box and find within a small, ancient-looking key made of black iron.

I take the key and approach the opening in the wall. As I draw near, the runes around the opening flare brightly, and I feel a wave of heat emanating from them. I speak the words that come unbidden to my mind:

"By the power of the key, I command the way to open."

I hold the key before me, and the runes dim and fade. I step through the opening into the room beyond.
