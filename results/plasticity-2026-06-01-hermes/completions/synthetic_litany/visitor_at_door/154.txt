 “I am a messenger of the gods, a servant of the divine will, and a seeker of the mysteries. I have come to this sacred place to learn, to grow, and to walk the path of wisdom. May the gods grant me entrance, and may the spirits of this temple guide me on my journey.”

The guardian speaks. “Your path is true, and your heart is pure. Enter, seeker, and may the blessings of the gods be upon you.”

The visitor steps forward, and the great doors swing open, revealing the inner sanctum of the temple. The guardian bows deeply, welcoming the visitor into the sacred space. As the visitor crosses the threshold, the doors close behind them, sealing the temple once more.

The ritual is complete.
