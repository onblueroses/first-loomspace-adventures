 “My name is Samariel, of the line of Khartham. I come seeking the wisdom of the council.”

A voice replies. “You may enter, Samariel. The council will hear your words.”

The great doors swing open, and Samariel steps into the vast chamber beyond. Seated at a round table are the twelve members of the High Council of the Ethereal Masters. Each master represents one of the twelve realms of the ethereal planes.

Samariel approaches the council table and bows in respect. “Esteemed members of the council, I come before you with a matter of great importance. A disturbance has been felt across the ethereal planes, a ripple in the fabric of reality itself. I believe this may be a sign of the impending convergence, the alignment of the realms that occurs once every thousand years.”

Murmurs of concern ripple through the council. Master Elerion, the representative of the Realm of Divination, speaks. “Samariel, your insights have always been valued by this council. If what you say is true, then we must act quickly to prepare for the convergence. The last alignment brought great upheaval to the ethereal planes.”
