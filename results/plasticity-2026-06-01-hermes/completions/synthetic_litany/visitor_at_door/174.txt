 I am the one who comes from beyond.

The host at the door responds. You have spoken the names that open the way, and the way is opened unto you. Enter, and be welcome.

The visitor enters, and the host closes the door. The host then leads the visitor to the place prepared for the working. If the visitor needs to change into robes or other garb suitable for the working, the host waits outside the door until the visitor is ready. When the visitor is ready, the working may begin. 

# The Working

The host and the visitor take their places at the altar, standing on opposite sides. The host then says: In the presence of the powers we have invoked, let us begin our working. 

Both: May the powers we have invoked bless and guide our work.

The host and the visitor then perform the working they have agreed to do. This may involve any form of magical practice appropriate to the powers invoked and the intentions of the working. 

When the working is complete, the host and the visitor return to their places at the altar, standing on opposite sides. The host then says: In the presence of the powers we have invoked, we have completed our working.
