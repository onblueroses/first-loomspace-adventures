 "I am the spirit of the wanderer who died in the desert, seeking the way to the city of glass. I have crossed the threshold of death and returned, bearing secrets from beyond the veil. I ask for sanctuary and a place at your table, that I may share my knowledge with those who seek the mysteries."

The hooded figure nods solemnly. "Your path has been long and your trials many. The secrets you carry are a heavy burden. Enter, and find respite. Share your tales with us, and we shall listen."

The visitor steps across the threshold, into the dimly lit halls of the sanctuary. The door closes behind them with a resounding thud, sealing out the howling winds and biting sands. 

In the flickering candlelight, the visitor sees others gathered - some robed like their greeter, others in travel-worn garb not unlike their own. All bear the weight of knowledge in their eyes.

The hooded figure gestures to an empty seat at the long wooden table. "Sit. Rest. Speak of what you have seen, and we will offer what wisdom we may in return."
