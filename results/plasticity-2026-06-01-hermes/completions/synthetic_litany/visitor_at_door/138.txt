## The Elemental Invocation of Water

Officiant performs the Greater Invoking Ritual of the Pentagram of Water.

Officiant: And the Elohim said, "Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, and let it divide the waters from the waters. And the Elohim made the firmament, and divided the waters which were under the firmament from the waters which were above the firmament: and it was so. And the Elohim called the firmament Heaven. And there was evening and there was morning, the second day." (Genesis 1:6-8)

Officiant: Holy art Thou, Lord of the Universe, KURIOS, who formest the foundation of the waters.

All: Holy art Thou, Lord of the Universe, KURIOS, who formest the foundation of the waters.

Officiant: Mother of Waters, Thou mighty sea, Thou source of all living, Thou unfathomable depth, Thou womb of the night, Thou gauze that hides and reveals the face of the Abyss, I adore Thee and I invoke Thee.
