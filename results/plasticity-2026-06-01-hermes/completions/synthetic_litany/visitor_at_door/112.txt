Priest:
We gather to celebrate the Miracle of Life, the wonder that surrounds and permeates our existence. Let us honor the forces that sustain us, the Earth that bears us, and the cycles of nature that inspire us.

Congregation:
We honor the Miracle of Life.

The priest lights a central candle on the altar.

The congregation members each light their candles from the central flame.
