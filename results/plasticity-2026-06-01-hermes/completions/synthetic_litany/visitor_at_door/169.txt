From "The Mysteries of the Hidden Arts" by Theomarach the Wise, 3rd edition, translated by Mirzabet of the Silver Tower.

---

The visitor, known as the seeker, is greeted by the masters of the hidden arts. They are led to a chamber deep within the sanctum, where the masters reveal the secrets of their craft.

The masters speak of the elements that make up the world: fire, water, air, and earth. They teach the seeker how to manipulate these elements, to bend them to their will.

The seeker learns of the secret languages, the tongues of the ancient ones. They are taught the words of power, the phrases that can shape reality itself.

The masters reveal the existence of other worlds, realms beyond the physical. They teach the seeker how to traverse these realms, to communicate with the beings that inhabit them.
