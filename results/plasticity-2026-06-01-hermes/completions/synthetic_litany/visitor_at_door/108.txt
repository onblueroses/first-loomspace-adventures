I stand with you at the boundary between worlds. The Tower of the East is a liminal place, a gateway between the realm of the mundane and the realm of the sacred. The visitor responds. If their answer is affirmative, the doorkeeper continues:

Then enter, and be welcome. May the light of the Tower guide your path.
