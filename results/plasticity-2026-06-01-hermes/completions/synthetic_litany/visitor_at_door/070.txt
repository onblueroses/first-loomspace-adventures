 "I am a servant of the Light, and I seek entry to the temple of knowledge and power. I have come to learn the secrets of the universe and to better myself in the ways of wisdom and truth. I humbly ask for admittance."

The priestess responds. "By what authority do you seek entry to this sacred place?"

The visitor replies. "By the authority of the Divine Light that shines within me and within all beings. I seek to harmonize myself with its eternal radiance."

The priestess speaks again. "Then enter, seeker of truth, and may the Light illuminate your path. But know that with knowledge comes responsibility. Use what you learn here for the betterment of all."

The visitor bows in gratitude and enters the temple as the doors swing open. The ceremony of admittance is complete. 

The visitor is now ready to explore the vast library, attend lectures by wise sages, and meditate in the temple's sanctum to attune to the Divine Light. The journey of spiritual growth and enlightenment begins.

May the Light guide your path, seeker of truth. Namaste.
