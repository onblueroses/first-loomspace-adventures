 It is a man with the voice of a woman, or a woman with the voice of a man. It is difficult to tell.

**Visitor:** I am that which is called Hialep. I come bearing gifts and counsel for the one who seeks the path of the Magus.

**Gerald:** Hialep, your presence honors us. What gifts and counsel do you bring?

**Hialep:** I bring the gift of discernment, to see through the veils of illusion that cloud the minds of mortals. And I bring counsel: the path of the Magus is not for the faint of heart. It demands sacrifice, dedication, and the willingness to confront the darkest aspects of oneself.

**Gerald:** We accept your gifts and heed your counsel. What must we do to walk this path?

**Hialep:** To walk the path of the Magus, you must first master the elements within yourself. Fire, water, air, and earth - each must be balanced and controlled. Only then can you hope to wield the powers of the universe.

**Gerald:** How do we master these elements?
