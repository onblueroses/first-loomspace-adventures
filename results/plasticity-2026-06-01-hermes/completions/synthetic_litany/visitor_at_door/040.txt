 He is a pale, thin man, but his eyes shine with a piercing light, and when he speaks his voice seems to echo from the depths of the earth.

"I am one who has traveled the paths of the dead, and returned bearing secrets from beyond the veil. I know the names you speak, and I know others—names that would shatter your mind like glass and leave you a drooling husk. Do not test me, for I have seen the things that crawl in the darkness, and I know their secret ways.

"You seek to open the way, but you do not know what lies beyond. There are worlds beyond worlds, and some of them are best left unexplored. The things that live in the spaces between are not like us, and they do not think as we do. They are older than time, and they have no regard for the petty concerns of mortals.

"But I can guide you, if you wish. I can show you the paths that lead to power, and the secrets that lie buried in the dark places of the world. I can teach you the words that will give you mastery over the forces of nature, and the names that will bind the spirits to your will. All I ask is a little piece of your soul
