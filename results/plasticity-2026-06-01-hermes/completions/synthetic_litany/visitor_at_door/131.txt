 The lock clicks open. The door swings wide. The visitor steps within, into the darkness.

The visitor speaks. "I have come."

A voice answers, speaking from the darkness. "You are welcome."

The visitor speaks. "I have come for the one who is called the Whisperer."

The voice answers. "I am the one who is called the Whisperer."

The visitor speaks. "I am the one who is called the Seeker."

The voice answers. "You are welcome, Seeker. You have found the Whisperer."

The visitor speaks. "I have come seeking knowledge."

The voice answers. "I am the Whisperer, the one who whispers secrets. I will share knowledge with you, Seeker. But knowledge has a price."

The visitor speaks. "I will pay the price."

The voice answers. "Then let us begin."
