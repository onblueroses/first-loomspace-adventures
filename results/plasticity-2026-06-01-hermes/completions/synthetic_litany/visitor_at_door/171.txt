 “I have come in the name of the great spirits of the art, who know me and have sent me here to request the aid of the spirits of this circle. I bear their sigil as proof of my claim.”

The spirits respond. “We acknowledge your claim, and we greet you in the name of the great spirits. Speak your request, and we will grant it if we can.”

The visitor speaks. “I seek guidance concerning the spirits of the thirty-one mansions of the Moon. I wish to learn the nature of each mansion, the powers and abilities of the spirits that dwell therein, and the means to summon and command them.”

The spirits reply. “This is a matter of great complexity, but we will teach you what we know. Listen and learn.”

The spirits speak. “The thirty-one mansions of the Moon are divisions of the lunar cycle, each lasting for a little less than a day. Each mansion has its own qualities and powers, and its own ruling spirits, who may be summoned and commanded by those who know the proper formulae. We will now teach you these things.”

The spirits then proceed to recite the details of each mansion, its ruling spirits, their powers and abilities, and the means to summon and command them.
