 "The way is open," they say. "The way is open."

And it is so.

The door opens, and the visitor enters the circle.

---

The ritual is concluded. All present give thanks to the spirits and to each other.

So it is and so it shall be.

---

*This ritual is intended to be a fictional work of art, inspired by historical and modern occult practices but not adhering to any specific tradition. It incorporates elements from various sources including the Lesser Key of Solomon, the Pseudomonarchia Daemonum, and the works of Aleister Crowley and Peter J. Carroll. The names and sigils of the spirits are original creations.*   
   
*The ritual structure draws upon classic ceremonial magic elements like the casting of a circle, invocations of the elements and cardinal directions, and the use of barbarous names and words of power. However, the aesthetic and some of the language aims for a more primal, shamanic vibe compared to the highly formalized grimoire traditions.*
