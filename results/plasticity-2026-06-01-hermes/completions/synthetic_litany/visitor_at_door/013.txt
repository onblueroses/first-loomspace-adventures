 It is the voice of the one who is called Death.

Death: By the name of the One who stands at the door, I speak the names that open the way:

Mareth, Quelimon, Iskon, Kerodim, Astrelach, Volmaroth, Drabasaim, Olempraem, Crisilim, Bidromael, Octaron, Saphrenoth, Yodalim, Iathreon, Mirzabet, Calenuph, Theomarach, Belendath, Glamoreth, Sinabaroth, Hialep, Trifonath, Olomar, Vesperath, Khalapeth, Estrymon; Brashvoreth, Liminem Adventi.

The door opens, and the visitor enters.

The visitor is Death, and he has come for the soul of the one who lies dying.

Death: I have come for the soul of the one who lies dying.

The soul of the one who lies dying speaks.

Soul: I am ready to go with you, Death. I have lived my life, and I am ready to face what comes next.

Death: Then come with me, and I will guide you to the other side.
