 It is the voice of the Fool. The Keeper answers:

Keeper: Who are you that comes to the door of the mysteries?

Fool: I am the spirit of one newly born into the world. I seek to learn the mysteries.

Keeper: By what right do you ask to enter?

Fool: By the right of my birth into the world of matter, and my kinship with all living things.

Keeper: Are you willing to be tried and purified?

Fool: I am willing.

The Keeper opens the door, and the Fool enters the temple. The altar is set in the midst of the temple, with the four elemental symbols upon it. The Fool stops at the station of Earth in the north, and faces east. The Hierophant comes to the Fool from the east, carrying the altar candle, and faces the Fool.

Hierophant: In the name of the holy and ineffable mystery of the elements, I receive you into this temple of the Gnostic Church of the Second Aeon, as one who has passed through the veil of birth, and who seeks the path of knowledge.

The Hierophant traces the Tau cross upon the Fool's forehead with the altar candle, and says:

Hierophant: I bless you in the name
