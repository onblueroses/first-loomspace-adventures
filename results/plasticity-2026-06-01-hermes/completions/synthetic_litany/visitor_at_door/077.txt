 Their voice is oddly muffled, as if speaking through a veil, and carries the same strange reverberation that the door itself had.

"Ah, the old pass phrases. It has been long indeed since I heard them spoken. You are well-versed in the lore of this place, it seems. I am pleased to make your acquaintance, young one. I am called many things, but you may know me as Kavitha the Veiled, or simply Kavitha."

The door opens, swinging wide without a sound. Beyond, you see a dimly lit stone chamber, bare of furnishings or decoration. Kavitha herself stands just beyond the threshold, a tall figure draped in dark robes that hide her features from view. You cannot make out her face, only the faintest impression of eyes glinting in the depths of her hood.

"I sense a keen mind and a seeking heart in you. I believe we shall have much to discuss. Please, enter and be welcome in my sanctum. You need have no fear, for no harm shall come to you here. The bonds of hospitality are sacred to me."
