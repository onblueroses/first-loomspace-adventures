1. The visitor speaks the following words:

I come from the land of darkness and shadow,
From the realm of the hidden and the unknown.
I seek passage through this sacred gate,
To tread the paths of mystery and power.

By the names of the guardians,
And the secrets they hold,
I ask for entry, with respect and reverence.
May the gate open, and the way be clear.

2. The guardian replies:

Your words have been heard, traveler from the dark.
The names you speak hold power and truth.
Yet to pass this threshold, a token you must bear,
A sign of your worth, a key to the gate.

Show now this token, this proof of your claim,
And if it is found worthy, the way shall be opened.
Speak, and reveal what you bring.

3. The visitor reveals their token:

By the token of [describe the token], I prove my worth.
Crafted in shadows, imbued with night's essence,
A symbol of my path, my dedication to the mysteries.
I hold it aloft, for the guardians to behold.

May it be deemed worthy, may it turn the key,
And grant me passage to the realms beyond.
