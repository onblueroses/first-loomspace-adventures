  
"The path is open to me, as the seal is given and the names are spoken. Grant me entrance."

The one who guards the door responds.  
"Enter, and be welcome."

The visitor steps across the threshold, and the door is closed behind them.  
The ritual is complete, and the path is sealed once more.  
So it is, and so it shall be.  

May the mysteries within guide and guard us all.
