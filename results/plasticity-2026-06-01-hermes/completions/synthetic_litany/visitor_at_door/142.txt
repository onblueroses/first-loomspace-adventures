 The door opens before you. When you are ready, you may step through and begin your journey. The door will be here when you return, to take you home once more.

The First Gate
The First Gate is the Gate of the Moon. It is a place of silver and silence, where all colors fade to shades of gray and the only sound is the slow whisper of the tides. Here the path winds along a barren seashore where the breakers roll in slow motion and the sea is the color of steel.

As you walk, you hear the whispering of the tides, and it seems to you that the waves are speaking to you, telling you the secret names of the Moon. Listen carefully, and remember them. You will need them later.

The journey to the Second Gate is long and slow, but at last the path turns inland from the sea and the First Gate comes into sight. It is a simple wooden gate painted white. Over it, in silver letters, is written the word LIMEN.

Speak the names that open the way:

Ovsena, Velema, Urvana, Ytosura, Ehatosa, Lomeda, Agatepha, Vanada, Yleborae, Lomana
