The angel responds, saying: Amonias, I know your mission and your purpose. But before you may enter, you must prove your worth. Answer me these three questions:

First, what is the nature of the Divine Light that illuminates all things?

Amonias responds: The Divine Light is the eternal radiance of the Most High, the source of all wisdom, truth, and goodness. It is the light that shines in the darkness, and the darkness cannot overcome it.

The angel nods, then asks the second question: What is the path that leads to true enlightenment?

Amonias answers: The path to true enlightenment is the path of righteousness, humility, and love. It is the way of the Most High, the way of compassion, forgiveness, and selfless service to others.
