The chamber is a single room, with a single door. There are no windows. The floor is of stone, and the walls are of stone, and the ceiling is of stone. The room is lit by candles on tall iron stands. There are thirteen stands, each with thirteen candles. The stands are placed in a circle around the perimeter of the room. The light from the candles casts strange shadows on the walls and ceiling.

The visitor at the door speaks again. The candles flare up, and then die down to a low, steady flame. The shadows on the walls and ceiling grow deeper and more complex.
