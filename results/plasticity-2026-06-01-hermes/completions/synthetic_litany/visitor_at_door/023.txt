 They are greeted, and invited to enter, and to share their purpose. They are offered refreshment and welcome.

If they be hostile, speak the words:

Saphrenoth, Yodalim, Iathreon, Mirzabet, Calenuph, Theomarach, Belendath, Glamoreth, Sinabaroth, Hialep, Trifonath, Olomar, Vesperath, Khalapeth, Estrymon; Liminem Egredi.

And the door will close, and they will not enter.

If they be welcome, speak the names of opening once more, and the door will open, and they will enter and be welcome.

Speak the names of closing, and the door will close, and they will remain.

When the door is closed, and you wish it sealed, speak the names of sealing:

Throvenios, Mareth, Quelimon, Iskon, Kerodim, Astrelach, Volmaroth, Drabasaim, Olempraem, Crisilim, Bidromael, Octaron; Liminem Obsera.

And the door shall be sealed, and let none pass.
