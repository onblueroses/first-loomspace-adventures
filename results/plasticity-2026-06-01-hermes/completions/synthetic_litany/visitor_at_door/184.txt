 

"Your name is Throvenios, and I bear your seal. Open the way, and let me pass."

The door opens. Beyond it lies a narrow stairway leading down into darkness. 

The visitor lights a torch and descends the stairs. 

At the bottom of the stairs, another door awaits. 

The visitor knocks and speaks.

"Your name is Mareth, and I bear your seal. Open the way, and let me pass."

The door opens. Beyond it lies a long, dark corridor. 

The visitor walks down the corridor, their torch casting eerie shadows on the walls. 

At the end of the corridor, another door awaits.

This process repeats, with the visitor speaking the names and passing through the doors, delving deeper into the underworld. The names are spoken in order:

Quelimon, Iskon, Kerodim, Astrelach, Volmaroth, Drabasaim, Olempraem, Crisilim, Bidromael, Octaron, Saphrenoth, Yodalim, Iathreon, Mirzabet, Calenuph, Theomarach, Belendath, Glamoreth, Sinabaroth, Hialep, Trifonath, Olom
