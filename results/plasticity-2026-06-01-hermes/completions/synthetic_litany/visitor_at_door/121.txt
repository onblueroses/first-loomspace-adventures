 The voice is deep, rich and resonant.

"Adventi Liminem."

And with those words, the door opens.

The visitor enters. He is tall, with broad shoulders and dark, weather-beaten skin. His black hair is tied back in a ponytail, and his eyes are hidden behind dark glasses. He wears a long, black leather coat, black leather gloves, and heavy black boots. He carries a black leather bag slung over one shoulder.

The visitor surveys the room, taking in the circle, the symbols, the candles, the incense, and the figure seated in the center. He nods, and speaks again, his voice now soft and low.

"Khalapeth."

The figure in the circle stands, and speaks the response.

"Estrymon."

The visitor steps into the circle, and the two clasp hands, and embrace. The visitor speaks again.

"Brother."

"Sister."

The visitor steps back, and removes his glasses, revealing eyes as black as the void between the stars. He looks around the room again, and smiles.

"Adequate. You've done well, sister."

"Thank you, brother. I am honored by your presence, and by your praise."
