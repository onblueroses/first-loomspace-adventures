 “I am come to speak with the Master of the Tower.”

The doorkeeper speaks. “I am the Master of the Tower. Enter and be welcome.”

The doorkeeper opens the door, and the visitor enters.

The doorkeeper speaks. “What knowledge do you seek?”

The visitor speaks. “The knowledge of the art of magic.”

The doorkeeper speaks. “The art of magic is the art of causing change in the world by means of will, imagination, and symbols. To learn this art, you must learn to discipline your will, strengthen your imagination, and understand the symbols that give shape to the forces of existence. This is a lifelong journey, and the Tower of the Art is only the first step on that journey, but it is a step that can be taken by all who are willing to learn.”

The visitor speaks. “What, then, is the first step?”

The doorkeeper speaks. “The first step is to learn to know yourself. You must learn to watch your own thoughts, feelings, and actions, not as one caught up in them, but as one who observes them. Only thus can you learn to shape them according to your will, and in turn shape the world around you.”
