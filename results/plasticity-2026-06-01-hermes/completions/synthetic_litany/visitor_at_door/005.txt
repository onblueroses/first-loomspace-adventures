 The door opens and the visitor enters.

The visitor speaks to the spirits: 

Spirits of the Second Seal, I have called you here by your names and by the sacred words of power. I command you to answer my questions truthfully and to perform my requests as I command, without hesitation or resistance. You are bound by the power of the names and the words to obey me. I hold the Second Seal of the Spirits, and by its power, I command you.

The spirits speak, acknowledging their obedience to the visitor.

The visitor asks the spirits to reveal hidden knowledge, to teach secret arts, or to perform specific tasks. The spirits answer truthfully and obey the visitor's commands.

When the visitor has finished questioning the spirits and commanding them, the visitor speaks the dismissal:

Spirits of the Second Seal, I now release you from your bonds. Return to your realms and habitations, and come again when I call you by the power of the names and the words. I seal this command with the words: Liminem Exitus, Brashvoreth.

The spirits depart, and the visitor closes the door with the words: Throvenios, Mareth, Quelimon, Iskon, Kerodim, Astrelach, Vol
