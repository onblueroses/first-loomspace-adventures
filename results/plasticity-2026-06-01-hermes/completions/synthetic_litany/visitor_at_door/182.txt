 I am of the blood of the First Born, a child of the Four Watchtowers, descended of the line of the Princes of the Realm. I have come to claim my inheritance and to know the truth of my being. I call upon the guardian of the gate to open the way for me and to guide me in the path. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, so more it be.

The guardian of the gate speaks. The gate is open for you, child of the Realm. Enter and learn the secrets of your true Will. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, so more it be.

The visitor enters the temple and greets the assembly. In the Grand Word of the Adepti, I greet you.

The assembly responds. We greet you by the same, child of the Hidden Light. 

The ritual of opening proceeds in the usual way. 

At the conclusion of the ceremony, the new initiate is welcomed by all present, and then the temple is closed in the usual way.
