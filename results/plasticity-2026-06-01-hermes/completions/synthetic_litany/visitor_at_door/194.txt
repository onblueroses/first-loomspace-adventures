 They have come to the citadel of the sorcerers seeking the power of immortality. The sorcerers reply that they can grant this power, but that the visitor must first complete a task. The visitor agrees.

The sorcerers instruct the visitor to seek the hidden temple of the ancient god Thrazgaduum. Within the temple's deepest sanctum, the visitor must retrieve a sacred amulet. Only then will the sorcerers bestow the gift of eternal life.

The visitor departs on their perilous quest, determined to find the temple and claim immortality. The sorcerers watch them go, knowing the true price that must be paid.

The visitor treks through scorching deserts, scales treacherous mountains, and delves into haunted catacombs. Many trials and tribulations threaten to thwart their journey. But at last, after long months of hardship, they discover the hidden valley wherein lies the crumbling temple of Thrazgaduum.

Beneath the temple, in a chamber sealed for untold eons, the amulet rests upon an altar of black stone. The visitor reaches out to claim it... but the amulet is guarded by a fearsome creature - a basilisk whose gaze turns flesh to stone
