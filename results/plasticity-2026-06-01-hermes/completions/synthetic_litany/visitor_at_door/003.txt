 "I am not an enemy. I am a friend to the king of Belforas. I have come to deliver a message of the utmost importance."

The guard replies, "Wait here while I inform the king of your arrival."

The guard returns shortly. "The king will see you now. Follow me."

The visitor is led through the grand halls of the castle, filled with intricate tapestries and glistening suits of armor. Finally, they arrive at the throne room. The king, a regal figure with a long white beard, sits upon his throne.

"Your Majesty," the visitor bows. "I bring news of a great danger that threatens your kingdom."

The king leans forward, his eyes narrowing. "Speak, friend. What is this danger you speak of?"

"A great army is gathering in the north, led by a sorcerer of immense power. They plan to march on Belforas within a fortnight."

The king's face grows grave. "This is dire news indeed. We must prepare our defenses."

The visitor nods. "I have knowledge of their plans and can aid you in your preparations."

"Then we shall make ready," the king declares. "Together, we will protect Belforas from this threat."
