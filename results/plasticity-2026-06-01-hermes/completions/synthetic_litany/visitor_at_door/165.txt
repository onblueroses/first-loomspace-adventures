I have come here seeking the wisdom of the Ancestors. I humbly ask for guidance on my path. May I enter and sit among you?

The doorkeeper responds:

The path is open to those who seek wisdom. Enter, and be welcome.

The visitor enters and takes a seat among the Ancestors. The doorkeeper closes the door and returns to their seat. The ritual leader speaks:

We gather here, in this sacred space, to honor the Ancestors and seek their guidance. Let us now invoke their presence and wisdom.

All present chant together:

Ancestors of blood, Ancestors of spirit, Ancestors of land, we call to you. Hear our voices, feel our hearts, and join us in this sacred space. Guide us with your wisdom, support us with your strength, and inspire us with your love. Ancestors, we welcome you.

The ritual leader speaks:

With the Ancestors among us, let us now share our stories and seek their guidance. Who among us has a story to share or a question to ask?
