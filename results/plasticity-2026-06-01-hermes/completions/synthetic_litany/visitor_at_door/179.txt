 They have come to ask a question of the spirits. The spirits will answer if they are willing. 

The spirits answer. The visitor listens. The spirits speak true.

The visitor thanks the spirits for their words and the spirits depart.

The rite is ended. The door is closed. The spirits return to their realm and the visitor to theirs. 

May the words spoken here echo in truth and may the truth be known. So it is and so it shall be. The rite is ended. 

---

This ritual is best performed during the dark moon or during an eclipse. The altar should be set with a black cloth, a silver candle, a bowl of water, and a censer with incense. The names of the spirits should be spoken clearly and with respect. The visitor should come with a sincere question and be prepared to listen carefully to the answer. The spirits will speak truth, but the meaning may not always be clear at first. The visitor should take time to reflect on the words spoken after the ritual has ended. This ritual should not be performed lightly, as the spirits do not take kindly to being summoned without good reason. Respect the spirits and they will respect you in turn.
