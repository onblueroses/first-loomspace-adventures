The chamber beyond is lit by a pale blue glow, the source of which is not apparent. The floor is made of polished black stone, and in the center of the room is a stone sarcophagus, intricately carved with scenes of death and rebirth. The lid of the sarcophagus is open, revealing that it is empty.

As the living enter the chamber, the dead appear, their forms coalescing from the blue light. They are not the rotting corpses or skeletal remains one might expect, but instead appear as they did in life, albeit with a slightly translucent, ethereal quality.

The dead greet the living by name, welcoming them to the council chamber. They are patient, and will wait for the living to speak their piece. The dead will answer questions, but their responses are often cryptic and enigmatic. They will not speak of the secrets of death, nor will they offer advice or guidance. Their role is to listen, and to bear witness.
