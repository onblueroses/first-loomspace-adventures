 

"I have come from a land far away to seek counsel with the oracle. I have heard of the wisdom and knowledge that lies within these walls, and I humbly request an audience to seek guidance for my people in these troubled times. We have faced many hardships and I believe the insights of the oracle may light our path forward. I come bearing offerings and an open heart, ready to receive whatever truths may be revealed. Will the oracle grant me entry and share their divine wisdom?"

The doors swing open with a deep, resonant creak. A robed figure stands in the entryway, face obscured by a deep cowl. 

"Your journey has been long and your words carry the weight of truth. The oracle senses the sincerity in your heart. Enter, seeker, and be welcome in this hallowed place. The oracle will hear your plea and impart what wisdom they can. But be warned - the truths spoken within may not always be what one wishes to hear. Steel yourself for revelations that may challenge what you believe. If you are ready to receive such knowledge, then follow me."

The figure turns and begins walking into the shadowed halls beyond. It is up to you to follow...or turn back now. The choice, as ever, is yours
