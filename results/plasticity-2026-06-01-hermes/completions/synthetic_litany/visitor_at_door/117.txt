 The words are his own. He says:

"By the authority of the supreme council of the Hidden Masters, I come to you with a message of great importance. Let me enter, that I may deliver it."

The door opens. The visitor steps through into the dimly lit interior. The door closes behind him. He finds himself facing a robed and hooded figure.

The visitor speaks:

"I am a messenger of the supreme council, sent to deliver an urgent communication to you, one of the Hidden Masters. The message is this: the council has learned that a renegade faction within our order, calling themselves the Brethren of the Veil, have discovered the location of the tomb of the black magician Eibon, and intend to violate its sanctity to steal the secret of life and death that is buried with the bones of the necromancer. The council commands that you travel to the tomb and prevent the desecration."

The hooded figure nods its understanding. It raises its arms and begins to weave a spell of transportation through the air with its hands. The visitor watches in silence. The air shimmers and glows. A whirling vortex opens. Without hesitation, the hooded figure steps through and vanishes. The vortex closes.
