 “What is this place?”

The doorkeeper answers, “Truth is the foundation of all things.”

The visitor asks, “What is the foundation of truth?”

The doorkeeper answers, “The foundation of truth is the Word.”

The visitor asks, “What is the Word?”

The doorkeeper answers, “The Word is the power of creation.”

The visitor asks, “Who may enter the Hall of Truth?”

The doorkeeper answers, “Only those who know the Word.”

The visitor says, “I know the Word.”

The doorkeeper asks, “What is the Word?”

The visitor answers, “The Word is the name of the one who creates.”

The doorkeeper asks, “What is the name of the one who creates?”

The visitor answers, “The name of the one who creates is that which cannot be spoken.”

The doorkeeper says, “Pass, therefore, and enter the Hall of Truth.”

As the visitor steps through the door, the doorkeeper speaks these words: “In the name of the one who creates, I bid you welcome to the Hall of Truth. May your journey through its mysteries bring you wisdom and understanding.”
