 

I am a traveler from a distant land, seeking knowledge and wisdom. I have journeyed far to find this place, guided by whispers and ancient texts. I humbly ask for entry, to learn from your vast repository of arcane secrets. I swear to uphold the sanctity of this place and to use any knowledge gained for the betterment of all.

The guardian responds.

Your path here shows dedication, and your words ring with sincerity. Yet, the knowledge within is not freely given. A test of your resolve and understanding is required. Answer this riddle, and the door shall open:

"I am the beginning of eternity, the end of time and space. I am the beginning of every end, and the end of every place. What am I?"

The visitor ponders for a moment, then speaks.

The letter 'E'.

The guardian nods.

Indeed, you have shown wisdom beyond your years. You may enter, but remember, the knowledge you seek comes with great responsibility. Use it wisely.

The door creaks open, revealing a vast library stretching as far as the eye can see, filled with ancient tomes, scrolls, and artifacts. The visitor steps inside, ready to uncover the secrets of the ages.
