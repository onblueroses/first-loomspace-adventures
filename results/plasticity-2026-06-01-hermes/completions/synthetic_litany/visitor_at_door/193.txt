 They are an older man, with a grizzled beard and a worn face. They introduce themselves as Daveth, a former adventurer and friend of the late Orynn. Daveth has come to warn you of a great danger that threatens the town of Orlane - a cult led by a charismatic figure known as the "Prophet of the Rotting God".

Daveth explains that this cult has been growing in power and influence, and that they worship a foul deity of decay and corruption. The cultists believe that by spreading disease and rot, they will be granted immortality and power by their dark god.

The cult has been kidnapping townsfolk and infecting them with a magical disease that slowly transforms them into mindless, rotting servants of the Rotting God. Many of the towns leaders and prominent citizens have already fallen under the cult's sway.

Daveth fears that if the cult is not stopped soon, Orlane will be completely consumed by the rot, and the cult will spread to other towns and cities. He has come to you, as Orynn's trusted friends, to ask for your help in investigating the cult and putting an end to their vile plans.
