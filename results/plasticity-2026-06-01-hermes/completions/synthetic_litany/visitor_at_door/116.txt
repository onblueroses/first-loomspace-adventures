 You hear the voice, but you do not see the speaker. The voice says, "Who summons me?"

You reply, "It is I, [your name]. I summon you in the name of the 24 Elders of the first earth, who were before the angels, who sit before the face of the mighty unknown, who have been from the beginning, who see all and know all, who have the keys to the gates of the four directions, and who are the keepers of the secrets of the world. I call upon you to guide me to the treasures that lie hidden."

The voice speaks: "What is it you seek?"

You reply, "I seek the hidden treasures of the earth, the secrets that lie buried, the wisdom that is hidden from the eyes of the profane."

The voice speaks: "Before I may guide you to that place where the treasures lie, you must prove yourself worthy. Answer me this riddle:

Black am I, yet like the white my powers;
In youth exalted high, in age brought low;
Mourning at the close of day, glad at the dawn;
All men must me obey, the strongest am I.
