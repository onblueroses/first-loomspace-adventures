 The door will open to the temple.

Do not use the names lightly, for they are the names of power and not to be spoken except in time of need.

The temple is a place of learning and of wisdom. It is also a place of danger. If you enter without the knowledge of the proper words, you will be destroyed.

When you enter the temple, you will find a large room with a fire in the center. This fire is the fire of knowledge and it will burn away all ignorance.

Around the fire are twelve pillars. Each pillar represents one of the twelve signs of the zodiac. Each sign has its own wisdom and its own knowledge.

To gain the knowledge of a sign, you must stand before the pillar and speak the name of the sign. The knowledge will then be given to you.

But beware, for the knowledge is not always easy to understand. It may take time and effort to comprehend the wisdom of the signs.

In the temple, you will also find a library. This library contains the knowledge of the ages. It is a place of great learning, but it is also a place of great danger.

For in the library, there are books that contain the secrets of the universe. These secrets are not meant for the uninitiated.
