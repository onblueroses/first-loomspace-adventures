### 6. The Visitor's Tale
The visitor is an elderly woman named Mareth. She tells a harrowing tale of her village being terrorized by a fearsome creature known as the Nightshade Beast. The beast emerges from the dark woods each night to snatch away villagers, never to be seen again. Mareth has come seeking help from the Order to save her people.

Mareth humbly requests the aid of the Order. She offers a small pouch of gold coins as payment, all that her village could scrape together. The Archmage considers the request.
