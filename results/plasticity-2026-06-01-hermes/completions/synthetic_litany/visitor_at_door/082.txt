 

I am a wanderer of the worlds beyond, seeking passage to realms unknown. Grant me entry, spirits of the threshold, that I might walk the hidden paths and learn the secrets of the universe.

The door opens. 

Enter, traveler, and may your journey through the realms of mystery be enlightening. Tread carefully, for the paths ahead are fraught with wonders and perils beyond imagining.

The visitor steps through the door. The door closes. 

The visitor walks the labyrinthine paths of the realm beyond, witnessing marvels and facing challenges that test their resolve and expand their understanding. The secrets they uncover and the experiences they endure forever change them, granting them insights both profound and terrible.

The visitor, transformed by their journey, returns to the door. It opens. They step back into the mundane world, carrying with them the knowledge and power gained from their travels in the realms beyond. The door closes, awaiting the next seeker of the mysteries that lie just beyond the boundaries of reality.

The rite is ended. 

May the secrets you have glimpsed serve you well, traveler, and may you walk in wisdom and power. So it is done.
