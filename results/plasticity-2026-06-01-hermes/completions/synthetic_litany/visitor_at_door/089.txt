 The door opens to reveal a small room that can be entered. In the room is a statue of a figure in robes holding a staff. On the pedestal are inscribed the words: "I am the keeper of the gates. I hold the keys to the gates of the universe."

If the visitor speaks the phrase "Iathreon, Mirzabet, Calenuph, Theomarach, Belendath, Glamoreth, Sinabaroth, Hialep, Trifonath, Olomar, Vesperath, Khalapeth, Estrymon," the statue will speak, saying: "You have spoken the keys to the gates. What is your desire?"

The visitor may then ask to be transported to a location in the universe, and the statue will open a shimmering portal to that place, allowing the visitor to step through. The portal will remain open for a short time before closing. The visitor must return to this room and repeat the phrase to open the portal again for their return journey.
