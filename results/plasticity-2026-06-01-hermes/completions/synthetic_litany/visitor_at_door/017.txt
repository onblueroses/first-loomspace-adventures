 His voice is that of a man who has been dead for some time.

"Does the master of the house grant me entry? I come with a warning and a promise."

The master of the house grants entry to the visitor. As the visitor enters, the master of the house speaks the names that close the way:

Liminem Exitus, Brashvoreth, Estrymon, Khalapeth, Vesperath, Olomar, Trifonath, Hialep, Sinabaroth, Glamoreth, Belendath, Theomarach, Calenuph, Mirzabet, Iathreon, Yodalim, Saphrenoth, Octaron, Bidromael, Crisilim, Olempraem, Drabasaim, Volmaroth, Astrelach, Kerodim, Iskon, Quelimon, Mareth, Throvenios.

The visitor is now inside the house, and the master of the house awaits his warning and promise. The visitor's voice is like the rustling of dry leaves in an ancient tomb.
