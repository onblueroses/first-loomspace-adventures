 They must be able to recite the following incantation from memory to gain entrance:

I have spoken the names of the guardians. I know the words of the seals. I ask for entry, not as one who seeks to conquer, but as one who seeks to learn. I come in peace, without weapon or ill intent. I come seeking wisdom, and I will share what wisdom I have in return. I will not betray your trust, and I will guard your secrets as my own. By the names of the guardians, I ask for entry.

If the incantation is recited correctly, the doors open, revealing the interior of the library. The librarian waits within, ready to assist the visitor in their search for knowledge. If the incantation is not recited correctly, the doors remain closed, and the visitor is not granted entry. The librarian will not acknowledge incorrect attempts, and the visitor must leave, unable to access the library's secrets. The visitor may attempt the incantation only once per day. If they fail, they must wait until the next day to try again.#### 19
