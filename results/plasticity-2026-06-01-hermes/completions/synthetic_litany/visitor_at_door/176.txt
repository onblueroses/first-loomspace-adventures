The visitor knocks again, once, twice, thrice.

The door opens, and the visitor enters. The serpent's kin are gathered, waiting to receive the visitor and the serpent's gift.

The ritual is complete, the serpent's gift delivered. The serpent's kin disperse, their secrets and their power hidden once more in the shadows. The visitor departs, the door closing behind them with a soft thud, like the sealing of a tomb.

The night is still, the stars cold and distant. The wind whispers through the trees, carrying the echoes of ancient secrets. The serpent sleeps, coiled in the depths of the earth, dreaming of the day when it will rise again, and the world will tremble at its coming.
