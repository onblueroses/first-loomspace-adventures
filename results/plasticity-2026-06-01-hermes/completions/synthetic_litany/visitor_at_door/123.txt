 "I am the god of this world," he says, "and I have come to claim it as my own."

The priests reply: "You are welcome, Lord, in the sanctum of your temple."

The visitor enters, and the door is sealed behind him. The priests kneel before him, their heads bowed in reverence.

The visitor speaks again. "Rise, my faithful servants. The time has come for me to take my rightful place as the ruler of this world. You have served me well, and you shall be rewarded for your loyalty."

The priests rise, their faces filled with a mixture of awe and fear.

The visitor continues. "But first, there is a task that must be completed. The people of this world must be made to understand that I am their true lord and master. They must be shown the power of the one they call the Dark Lord."

The priests nod in understanding. "What would you have us do, Lord?" they ask.

The visitor smiles, a cruel and twisted smile. "You shall be my instruments of chaos and destruction. You shall spread fear and terror throughout the land, until every man, woman, and child trembles at the mere mention of my name."

The priests bow their heads in submission.
