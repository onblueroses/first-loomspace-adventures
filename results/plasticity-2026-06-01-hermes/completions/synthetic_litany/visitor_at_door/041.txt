 They have the voice of a young woman, though it is distorted as if it came from the depths of the ocean. "Ah, you have finally arrived. Good, good. You are the first to have made it this far. Come, come, enter, I have been waiting for you for a very long time."

The door to the tower opens wide.

If the characters enter, they find themselves in a grand hall with a high ceiling and walls made of a strange dark material that seems to absorb light. The air is heavy with the scent of salt and the faint echo of waves crashing in the distance. At the center of the room stands a throne made of coral and adorned with pearls and shells, and upon it sits a figure draped in robes of deep blue and green, their face hidden beneath a hood.

The figure rises and approaches, pulling back their hood to reveal the face of a beautiful young woman with eyes that shimmer like the surface of the sea. She smiles warmly. "Welcome, travelers, to the Tower of the Deep. I am Salin, the Oracle of the Sea, and I have been waiting for you."

Salin gestures for the characters to follow her as she moves towards a grand staircase that spirals upwards along the inner wall of the tower
