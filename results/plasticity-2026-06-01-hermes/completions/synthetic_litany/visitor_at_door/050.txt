## 58. The Secret of the Flame

The ritual is performed in the chamber of the flame, a room with a circular depression at its center. A fire burns in the depression. The walls of the chamber are inscribed with arcane symbols and geometric patterns.

The master of the flame stands before the fire and speaks:

The secret of the flame is the secret of life. The secret of life is the secret of death. The secret of death is the secret of rebirth. The secret of rebirth is the secret of eternity.
