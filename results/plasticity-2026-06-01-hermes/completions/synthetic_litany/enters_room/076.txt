(You should see and/or hear the spirit at this point. It may take a moment, but if you have performed the ritual sincerely, the spirit will appear. You may ask the spirit for its name, and for any insights, wisdom or guidance it can offer. You may ask it to assist you with a particular task, or to lend its power to your magick. Be polite, but also be direct. Speak to the spirit as though you are equals. Make sure you thank the spirit when the communication is over.)

Go in peace, and with my thanks. I will call you again when there is need.
