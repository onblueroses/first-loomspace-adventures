### 3. The Angel's Warning

The spirit or demon claims to be the soul of the person you sought to contact. It answers your questions, providing details about the person's life that seem convincing. However, just as you start to feel confident in its identity, a bright light fills the room and an angel appears.

The angel warns you that the spirit is actually a deceptive demon, not the soul you sought. It urges you to banish the entity before it causes harm or corruption. The demon argues against the angel, insisting it is who it claims to be.

Who do you believe - the angel or the demon? What tipped the scales one way or the other for you? How do you proceed, and what precautions do you take? If you believe the angel, how do you banish the demon? If you side with the demon, what do you say to the angel?
