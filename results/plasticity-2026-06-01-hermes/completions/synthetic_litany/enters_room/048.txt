You may now ask Epharim up to 3 questions. Epharim will answer truthfully, but may be cryptic or indirect in its responses. Choose your questions wisely.*****

Epharim's form shimmers as it responds: "I am a being of the ethereal planes, beyond the veil of your physical realm.
