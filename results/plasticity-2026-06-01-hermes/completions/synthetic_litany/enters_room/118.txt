Your experiences may have been intense, or they may have been subtle. They may have been something you could see, hear, or feel, or they may have been a simple sense of presence. However it was that you experienced the spirit or spirits, they are now a part of your life. They are allies and guides, and they will help you in the work to come.
In this chapter, you will learn to use the powers and abilities of the spirits you have contacted to help you in your magical work. You will learn how to call on them for aid and guidance, and how to work with them to achieve your goals. In short, you will learn how to integrate your work with the spirits into your regular magical practice.
