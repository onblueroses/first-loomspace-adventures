 A demon, or an angel?

Neither. A man in a black cloak, holding a lantern. He steps from the shadows, his face obscured by a hood. He raises the lantern and the room fills with light.

What do you want from me? I ask.

"I want nothing," the figure says. "I'm here to guide you, nothing more. You summoned me, and I came. But the path ahead is yours to walk."

What path? Where does it lead?

"To power," the figure replies. "To knowledge. To the fulfillment of your deepest desires. But the way is treacherous. There will be trials. Challenges of mind, body and spirit. Are you prepared for this?"

I...I don't know. I'm not sure what I expected. Maybe this was a mistake.

The figure chuckles softly. "No mistake. You were called here, as I was. This is your destiny. But it is your choice to embrace it or turn back. What do you choose?"

I hesitate, torn between fear and longing. The mysteries that await, the secrets I could uncover...it's tempting. So very tempting. But the warnings of peril give me pause.
