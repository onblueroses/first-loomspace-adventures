  What do you see in the darkness?

A strange thing, it is a man, but it is not a man.  Its body is tall and thin, its limbs are long and its fingers are like claws.  Its skin is pale and its eyes are black.  It wears a long black robe and a hood that covers its face.  It moves with a strange grace, as if it is not bound by the laws of physics.  It is a creature of the night, a creature of darkness and shadow.

Ask it to reveal its name and purpose.

It speaks, but its voice is not a voice.  It is a sound that is not a sound, a vibration that is felt rather than heard.  It says its name is Nyarlathotep, the Crawling Chaos, the Faceless God, the Black Pharaoh.  It says it has come to bring the knowledge of the Great Old Ones, the ancient gods who ruled the Earth before the coming of man.  It says it has come to prepare the way for their return, to teach their secrets to those who are worthy.

Ask it to teach you the secrets of the Great Old Ones.

It nods, and its hood falls back to reveal a face that is not a face
