The room fills with a palpable darkness, as if the shadows themselves have taken on a tangible form. From within the gloom, two glowing red eyes emerge, belonging to a towering figure cloaked in an aura of malevolence. This entity, known as Estrymon, exudes an ancient power that sends shivers down your spine.

Estrymon's voice echoes through your mind, a deep and resonant whisper that seems to come from everywhere and nowhere at once. "I am Estrymon, the keeper of forbidden knowledge and the guardian of the paths between worlds. I have been summoned forth by the ancient names you have spoken."

The entity's gaze pierces your soul, as if assessing your worthiness. "You have called upon me, mortal, but be warned - the knowledge and power I offer come at a price. Those who seek to unravel the mysteries of the universe must be prepared to face the consequences of their actions."

Estrymon's eyes flicker with a hunger for secrets and a desire to impart hidden wisdom. The entity seems to be waiting for your response, ready to either grant you access to the arcane knowledge you seek or to consume your essence if you prove unworthy
