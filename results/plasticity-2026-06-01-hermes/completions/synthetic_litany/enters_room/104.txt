 Speak to us. Are you a demon? A god? An angel? A dead man? An elemental? A spirit of nature? What are you?

Do you wish to communicate with us? If so, we are ready to listen. If not, we command you to depart from this place and return to the place you came from. It is not our intention to compel you against your will.

What do you wish to say to us? Speak and we will listen.

*Wait for response. The entity may wish to communicate through the medium of the scrying mirror, or it may communicate in some other way.*

Thank you for your communication. We will now end this ritual. You are free to depart, or to remain in this place until the influence of the ritual fades. Go in peace.

*End the ritual. Close the circle, banish if desired.*
