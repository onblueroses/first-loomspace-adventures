 What emerges from the darkness, what hides from the light, what has grown weary of the shadows? Speak your name, spirit, and be known to me.

[The spirit's name is given.]

You are welcome here, [name of spirit]. I have called you to share your wisdom, your guidance, your friendship, and your power. In exchange, I offer you my respect, my appreciation, and my attention. I will listen to what you have to say, and I will remember your words. I will honor your presence, and I will call upon you when I am in need. You will be my ally, and I will be yours.

Do you accept this pact, [name of spirit]?

[The spirit's response is given.]

Then it is done. The pact is sealed, and we are bound together. I thank you for your presence, [name of spirit], and I look forward to our journey together. May our alliance be strong, and may our friendship be true.

You are free to go, [name of spirit], but know that you are always welcome here. I will call upon you again, and I will remember your name.

[The spirit departs.]

The ritual is complete. The circle is open, but never broken.
