This is Estrymon, the Shadowed Sage - a spirit of dark knowledge and forbidden secrets. Estrymon's voice echoes in your mind, deep and resonant:

"Greetings, seeker of the hidden paths. You have called me forth with the secret names. What arcana do you wish to glean from the shadows? Speak, and I shall impart my eldritch insights, for there is much I know that is lost to mortal ken. But beware, for knowledge has its price..."

Estrymon awaits your query, red eyes glinting in the darkness. You sense this spirit could reveal many dark mysteries, but the revelations may come at a cost to your peace of mind. What secrets will you ask the Shadowed Sage to unveil? Choose your questions carefully.
