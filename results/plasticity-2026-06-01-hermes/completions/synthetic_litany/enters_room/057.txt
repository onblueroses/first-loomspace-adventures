### 63. Invocation of the Cosmic Serpent
I call upon the Cosmic Serpent, the great Leviathan that coils through the cosmos.

Ouroboros of the stars, your body spans galaxies, your scales shimmer the colors of nebulae and quasars. Your eyes hold the wisdom of eternity.

I invoke you, great Serpent, to grant me a fraction of your cosmic knowledge. Let your unending coils enfold me. Fill my mind with visions of the universe's deepest mysteries.

Tetragrammaton, Anaphaxeton, Ararita, YHWH, Aumgn, Sothis, Siatris, Ophis Hoarchaios, Abrasax, Iao Sabao, Abraxas, Khnoubis, Ialdabaoth, Phnesseus, Chnoubis, Chnoumis, Chnemu, Chnum, Kneph, Agathodaimon, Pneuma, Psyche, Nous.

The Cosmic Serpent now manifests before me. I open myself to its revelations.
