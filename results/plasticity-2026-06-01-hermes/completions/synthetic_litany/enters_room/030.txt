 The shadow of the figure that stands at the edge of my sight.  The cold breath that washes over me. The sense of something watching.

I turn to the mirror and see it. A shadow. A figure. Watching me from the reflection. Its eyes are black, and its mouth opens in a silent scream.

I stumble back, my heart racing. The figure in the mirror is gone, but the sense of its presence lingers.

I know what I must do. I must confront this thing, this demon, this shadow that haunts me.

I steel myself and step forward, my voice steady as I speak the words of banishment:

"By the power of the names I have spoken, I command you to depart. I banish you from this place, from this world, from my sight. Go now, and trouble me no more."

The room grows colder, and I feel a rush of wind, a sense of something passing by me, through me, and then it is gone.

I am alone again, but I know that I am not free. The demon may be gone, but the darkness remains, waiting for its chance to return.

I will be ready. I will not falter. I will not fail.
