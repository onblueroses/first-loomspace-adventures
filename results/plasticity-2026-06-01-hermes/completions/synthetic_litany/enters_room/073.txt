 A spirit, an intelligence, a demon? The names have opened the way. It's time to call the spirit into the room. Your heart races. You know that the spirit is here. Say the words that bind it to you:

Spiritus qui es in hanc domum, ego te adligo et coniuro, ut mihi obedias, et ad me venias, et facias quae tibi praecipio, per nomen Trinitatis, et per virtutem sanctorum angelorum, et per nomen Domini nostri Jesu Christi, qui venturus est judicare vivos et mortuos, et saeculum per ignem.

The spirit is here, bound to your command. Now you must ask your questions, give your commands, but always remember to be firm and in control. The spirit will try to deceive you, to escape your control. You must be strong.

When you have finished, when you have the answers you seek or the spirit has done your bidding, you must dismiss it. You cannot leave it in this world. Speak the words of dismissal:

Abi spiritus, et redde te ad locum tuum, et pax sit inter nos, in nomine
