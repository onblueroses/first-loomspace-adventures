 An essence, a mist, a vapor, a fog, a presence, an intelligence, a being. It is the spirit of the dead, the spirit of the one whose name is written on the parchment, the spirit of the one I have called forth.

I command you, spirit, to take on a pleasing shape, one that I may look upon and not be repulsed. I command you, spirit, to speak to me in a voice that I may understand, in a language that I may comprehend. I command you, spirit, to answer my questions truthfully, and to do my bidding without hesitation or resistance.

The spirit has taken form. I may now converse with the dead.

[Ask the spirit your questions, and listen carefully to its answers. When you have finished, proceed with the following.]

I thank you, spirit, for your presence and your honesty. I release you now, and command you to return to your eternal rest. May peace be upon you, and may the veil between worlds remain sealed until I call upon you again.

Akhatharath, Seraphynt, Khloret, Mardax, Zamoroth, Kholbet, Seraphynt, Akhatharath.

The spirit fades,
