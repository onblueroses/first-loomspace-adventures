 Let it be seen and heard. 

*It appears*

1. What is your name?
2. What is your domain?
3. What do you offer?
4. What do you seek in return?
5. How can you be contacted?
6. What are the conditions of your departure?

*It departs*

The way is closed. The names are silent. The room is sealed once more. 

The working is done. 

---

**NOTES**

I. The names are spoken clearly and precisely, with intent. They are not rushed.

II. The entity is treated with respect, but also with firmness. The questions are asked directly, and the entity is expected to answer them.

III. The entity is not allowed to linger or to engage in idle chatter. Once the questions have been asked and answered, it is dismissed firmly and the working is concluded. 

IV. The names are not to be spoken outside of the working. They are for this purpose only, and to use them casually would be unwise. 

V. The working should not be attempted without proper preparation and protection. The practitioner should be in a state of mental and emotional balance, and should have a clear intention for the working.
