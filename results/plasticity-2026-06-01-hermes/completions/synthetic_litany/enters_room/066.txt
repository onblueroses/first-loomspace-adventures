 A force, a presence, a shadow. It gathers itself into a shape, a form, a body. It looks like a man, but it is not a man. It has eyes like pits of blackness, and skin like smoke. It wears a crown of thorns, and carries a sceptre of bone.

The figure speaks, in a voice like the rustling of dead leaves in an ancient crypt.

"I am the King of Shadows, the Lord of the Dead. I have come to answer your summons, mortal. What is it that you seek?"

I seek knowledge, oh King of Shadows. Knowledge of the secrets that lie hidden in the dark places of the world. Knowledge of the mysteries that have been lost to the mists of time.

The King of Shadows nods slowly, his eyes glinting in the flickering candlelight.

"Knowledge is a precious thing, mortal. It can bring great power, but also great danger. Are you prepared to face the consequences of what you may learn?"

I am prepared, my lord. I have studied long and hard, and I believe I am ready for whatever truths may be revealed to me.
