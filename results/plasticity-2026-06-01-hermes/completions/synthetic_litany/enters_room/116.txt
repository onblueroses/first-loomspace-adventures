The demon appears in a cloud of smoke and fire, and speaks: "I am the demon Alastor, servant of the infernal prince Oriens. I have come to answer your question, mortal. What do you wish to know?"

I wish to know the secret of the philosopher's stone, the key to transforming base metals into gold and achieving immortality.

Alastor replies: "The secret you seek is not easily obtained, for it requires great knowledge and skill in the arcane arts. However, I shall provide you with a path to follow. First, you must obtain the four elements in their purest forms: earth from a sacred burial ground, air captured at the peak of the highest mountain, fire from the depths of a volcano, and water from the source of the Nile. Combine these elements in a crucible forged from meteoric iron during the alignment of Saturn and Jupiter. Heat the crucible with the fire of a dragon's breath and quench it in the blood of a unicorn. The resulting elixir, when consumed, will grant you the power of transmutation and the gift of eternal life. But be warned, mortal, for such power comes at a great cost to your soul."
