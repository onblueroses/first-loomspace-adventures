(Note: This part of the ritual is to be done outside and in a place where you can see the sky. If you cannot do it outside, then it can be done indoors, but it's preferable to do it outdoors.)

(Stand up and face the sky, and raise your arms up.)

Oh mighty Gavreel, angel of the seventh heaven, who stands before the throne of the divine, I call upon you to witness the binding of these spirits and to ensure their obedience.

Gavreel, I ask that you grant me your protection and guidance as I work with these spirits. Let your light shine upon me and illuminate my path.

As I speak the names of power, let the spirits be bound to my will and let them come forth to serve me faithfully.
