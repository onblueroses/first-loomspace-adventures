"I am the Omekroth, the Whisperer in Shadows, the Keeper of Forbidden Secrets. Long have I slumbered in the spaces between worlds, waiting for one with the knowledge and will to summon me forth. Speak, mortal, and tell me why you have called me here to this place and time."
