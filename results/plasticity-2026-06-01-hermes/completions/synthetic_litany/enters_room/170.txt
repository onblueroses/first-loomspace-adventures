So it shall be.

*****

The spirits that enter the room are varied. Some are faint, others more distinct. The spirit of a man, weary and worn, stands near the entrance to the room. He wears a tattered robe, and his face is gaunt, with sunken eyes that seem to hold a deep sorrow. He speaks in a quiet, raspy voice:

"I am Theron, once a seeker of truth in these halls. I walked these corridors in search of knowledge, but found only despair. The secrets held within these walls are not meant for mortal minds. I warn you, turn back now, before it is too late. The cost of such knowledge is too high, as I learned to my eternal regret."

Another spirit, that of a woman, appears near the center of the room. She is dressed in fine robes, and her eyes sparkle with a keen intellect. She speaks with a clear, strong voice:
