 What manner of spirit or shade.

A man who bears the face of my father, but with skin the color of ash, and eyes like dull steel. He wears a simple robe, but it hangs on him like a shroud. He is not pleased.

---

**Spirit:** Why have you called me? I know you not, nor have any wish to know you.

**Julian:** I am Julian Crane, your son. I've called you here to ask for your guidance.

**Spirit:** I have no son. My son is lost to me, as I am lost to him.

**Julian:** I am your son, whether you recognize me or not. I've called you here because I need your help. I'm lost, Father. I don't know what to do.

**Spirit:** You are not my son. My son was a man of action, a man who knew his own mind. He would not have called upon the dead for help.

**Julian:** I am that man, Father. But I'm also a man who has seen too much, who has done too much. I need your guidance.

**Spirit:** What guidance could I possibly offer? I am a shade, a shadow of a man. I have no wisdom
