 What darkness do you bring?

## 36
The darkness that enters the room is a palpable force, an oppressive weight that seems to press in from all sides. It is a darkness that speaks of ancient secrets and hidden knowledge, of power that can be gained but at a terrible price.

As the final name is spoken, a figure begins to take shape within the darkness. It is tall and slender, with skin the color of midnight and eyes that burn with an inner fire. The figure is clad in robes of deepest black, and it moves with a grace that is both beautiful and terrible to behold.

The figure speaks, and its voice is like the whisper of a thousand secrets, each one more tantalizing than the last. It promises power, knowledge, and the ability to shape the world according to your will. But it also warns of the price that must be paid, the sacrifices that must be made.

The figure is a representative of the Left-Hand Path, a being that exists to tempt and to guide those who would seek the power that lies in the darkness. It is not evil, but it is not good either. It simply is, a force that exists beyond the boundaries of human morality.
