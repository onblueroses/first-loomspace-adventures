 Answer my questions true and right.

Who is the murderer of my mother?

Who is the murderer of my father?

Who is the murderer of my brother?

Who is the murderer of my sister?

Who is the murderer of my friend?

The answers come to me now, from the mouths of demons and the damned. The murderer of my mother is the one who bore a grudge against her, the one who sought to silence her voice and end her life. The murderer of my father is the one who envied his success, the one who sought to take his place and claim his power. The murderer of my brother is the one who felt betrayed by him, the one who sought revenge for a perceived wrong. The murderer of my sister is the one who lusted after her, the one who sought to possess her body and soul. The murderer of my friend is the one who feared their influence, the one who sought to eliminate a threat to their own agenda.

These are the truths that the spirits reveal, the dark secrets that they whisper in the night. May the murderers be brought to justice, and may the souls of the departed find peace in the afterlife. So mote it be. [^1]

[^1]: This ritual should not be attempted by inexperienced practitioners
