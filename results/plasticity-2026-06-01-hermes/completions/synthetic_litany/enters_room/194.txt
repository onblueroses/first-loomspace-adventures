 What comes before me. What is this thing that I see.

The demon that appears is called Foras. It is a powerful President, who takes the form of a strong man in human shape. Foras teaches the virtues of herbs and precious stones, as well as logic and rhetoric. He makes one invisible, imparts the skill of eloquence, and can locate lost objects and discover treasure. Foras also has the power to grant long life. He commands 29 legions of spirits.

Foras, I bind you to my service by the names Throvenios, Mareth, Quelimon, Iskon, Kerodim, Astrelach, Volmaroth, Drabasaim, Olempraem, Crisilim, Bidromael, Octaron, Saphrenoth, Yodalim, Iathreon, Mirzabet, Calenuph, Theomarach, Belendath, Glamoreth, Sinabaroth, Hialep, Trifonath, Olomar, Vesperath, Khalapeth, Estrymon; Brashvoreth, Liminem Adventi.
