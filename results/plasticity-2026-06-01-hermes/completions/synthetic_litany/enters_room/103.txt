A spirit has entered the room, drawn by the power of the names and the ritual. It is a strange, ethereal being, not fully corporeal but flickering in and out of visibility. The spirit seems ancient and powerful, with an otherworldly aura radiating from its shifting form.

The spirit speaks in a voice that echoes through your mind, bypassing your ears entirely. It says:

"Mortal, you have called me here with the old words and the secret names. I am bound by the ancient pacts to answer one question truthfully. Ask, and I shall reply, but choose your words with care, for I will not be summoned again."

You sense the spirit's impatience, its eagerness to be gone from this plane. You must ask your question now, while the ritual still binds it. What do you ask? Choose carefully.
