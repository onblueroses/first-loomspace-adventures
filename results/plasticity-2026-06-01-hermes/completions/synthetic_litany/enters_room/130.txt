 The door is open. The way is clear.

The spirits of the dead are here. The secrets of the dead are open.

Ask of the dead what you will.

[You may ask questions of the spirits of the dead at this time.]

When you have completed your questioning, say:

Spirits of the dead, return now to your resting places, and be at peace. The way is closed.

Theomarach, Calenuph, Mirzabet, Iathreon, Yodalim, Saphrenoth, Octaron, Bidromael, Crisilim, Olempraem, Drabasaim, Volmaroth, Astrelach, Kerodim, Iskon, Quelimon, Mareth, Throvenios; Liminem Adventi, Estrymon, Khalapeth, Vesperath, Olomar, Trifonath, Hialep, Sinabaroth, Glamoreth, Belendath: The way is closed.

As it was, so it shall be.

[Close the ritual in your customary way.]
