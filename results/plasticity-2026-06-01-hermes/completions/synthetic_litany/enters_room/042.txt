 By the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, I bind you, and cast you into the Pit, never to return, by the power of the Most High. Amen."

The demon was successfully exorcised, and the patient fully recovered. The exorcism was performed by Father Theophilus Riesinger, a well-known exorcist of the time.
