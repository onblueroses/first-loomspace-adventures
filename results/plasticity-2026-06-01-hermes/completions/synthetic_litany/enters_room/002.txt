 Speak your name. I bind you by the authority of the holy names, that you will speak the truth, and obey the demands I place upon you.

[The spirit speaks its name: Lagash]

What are you, Lagash?

[I am a spirit of fire and light, a guide to the realms of secrets, a guardian of hidden treasures.]

Lagash, I command you to reveal the location of the hidden treasure that lies closest to this place.

[The spirit describes a location not far from the house, beneath an ancient oak tree, where a chest filled with gold and jewels is buried.]

Lagash, I command you to go now to that place and guard the treasure. Ensure that no one disturbs it until I arrive to claim it. When I speak the words "Throvenios, Mareth, Quelimon", you will release the treasure to me and depart in peace. 

[I obey your command. The treasure shall be guarded until you speak the words.]

Go now Lagash, and do as I have commanded. I release you from this circle. Depart in peace.

[The spirit departs.]

[The mage speaks the closing words to end the ritual, then extinguishes the candles and incense. The room returns to normal
