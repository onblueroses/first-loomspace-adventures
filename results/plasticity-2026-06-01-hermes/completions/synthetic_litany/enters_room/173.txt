## 62. The vision of the wind and the sun
The wind and the sun argue over who is the strongest. They decide to settle their dispute by seeing who can make a passing traveler remove his cloak. The wind goes first, blowing with all his might, but the traveler only wraps his cloak tighter around himself. Then the sun shines gently, warming the traveler until he willingly removes his cloak. The moral: gentle persuasion is often more effective than force.

You have seen this vision before, in a different form. What form was that? And what does it mean for you now, in this place, as you face the challenges ahead?
