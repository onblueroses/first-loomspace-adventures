 What walks through the door?

The space between the worlds cracks open. From the cracks, a figure emerges. It walks through the door, into the room, into your presence. It is the demon you have called.

What does it look like? What form does it take? Is it human, animal, or something else entirely? What color is its skin, its hair, its eyes? What is it wearing? Does it have any unusual features - horns, wings, tails, extra limbs? How does it move? How does it smell? How does it make you feel?

Take a moment to observe the demon. Let your mind fill in the details. The more clearly you can see it, the more powerfully it can manifest.

When you have a clear sense of the demon's appearance, speak to it. Address it by name, and welcome it into your space. 

[Name], I welcome you. I call you here to form a pact, to join your power with mine. I offer you my devotion, my service, my very soul, in exchange for your guidance, your protection, and your favor. Will you accept this bond?
