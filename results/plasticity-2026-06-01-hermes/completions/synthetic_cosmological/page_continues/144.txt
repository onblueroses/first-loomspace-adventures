 "The phrase 'the Unnamed One' is a pun: the Greek phrase 'to onoma' (το όνομα), 'the name,' is a homophone of 'to onoma' (το ανωμα), 'the unnamed.' The author of this text uses 'to onoma' (το ανωμα) in an ungrammatical way, as if it meant 'the Unnamed One.' This suggests that the author was not a native speaker of Greek."

The text has been translated and published by John N. Duvall, Professor of English at Purdue University, and Robert P. Marzec, Associate Professor of English at Purdue University, in their article "Narrating 9/11" (Modern Fiction Studies 57.3, Fall 2011: 381-400). The article examines the use of this text in Don DeLillo's novel Falling Man (2007), where it appears in altered form.
