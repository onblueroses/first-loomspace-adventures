 “To say this prayer aloud is to invoke the Unnamed One. To write it out is to make oneself the vessel of its power. To commit it to memory is to make oneself an oracle. To burn it is to cease to exist.”

The document is clearly a variant of the so-called “Babylonian” ritual, which is not Babylonian in the least, and which is found in various forms in various magical and theurgical manuscripts and papyri from the late antique period. The version in the Voynich manuscript, however, is unique, and appears to be the earliest version of the text so far discovered. It is not, however, in a form that would be usable by a practicing theurgist, as it lacks the crucial “barbarous names of evocation” that normally accompany such texts. Indeed, it is possible that this version of the text is simply a literary document, not an actual magical working, although the prayer itself is clearly intended to be a functional ritual text.
