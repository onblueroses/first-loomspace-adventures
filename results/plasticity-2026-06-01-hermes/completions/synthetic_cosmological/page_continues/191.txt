 "Then, taking the censer, say:"

I offer unto thee, the Unnamed One, this incense.
This is the scent of the Threshold, the scent of the center, the scent of the world.
As the smoke rises, so let thy power and grace descend upon me and upon this consecrated seal of the spirits, which I propose to make in thy name.

I invoke Thee, the Unnamed One, the One who hath no voice, but to whom all voices speak:

THOBRAPHES, KRIGARA, KHARNAABO, GABRIEL, OULPHIRABO, KIRGABIO, PHONKARABA, KLORATH, KHRAMBALOR.

I invoke Thee, the Unnamed One, the One who hath no voice, but to whom all voices speak:

ABRAPHION, LAMBRION, KLEPHARION, OUBRATH, KHRENOBOR, IABO, THARBATH, PELKHOUN, KHRABATON, PHEBAOTH, KHRANEBAR.
