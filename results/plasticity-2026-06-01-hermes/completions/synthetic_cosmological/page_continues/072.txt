 _The Invocation of the Unnamed One_ ( _The Voice of the Syllables_ ). The fragment is incomplete, but what remains is a litany of divine names. It is the most direct invocation of a divine figure in the text.

**APPROACHING THE THRESHOLD: SOME CONCLUDING THOUGHTS**

I think it is possible to read the _Liber Resh vel Helios_ as a document that is essentially about the sun. That reading would focus on the significance of the sun in Egyptian mythology and the importance of solar imagery in the work of Crowley. It would also emphasize the importance of solar cycles to the work of the Golden Dawn. But the _Liber Resh vel Helios_ is also a document that is essentially about the act of invocation. In this sense, the sun is a stand-in for any divine figure. The sun is merely the most visible of the divine figures that the ritualist might invoke. The sun is also the figure that the ritualist is most likely to encounter on a daily basis. The sun is the divine figure that shapes the most mundane features of daily life. And the sun is the divine figure that is most likely to have a direct impact on the ritualist's mood and energy level.
