 "The foregoing is the Invocation proper, which the Adept will know how to use. The words in capital letters are names which are to be written upon the scroll, along with the sigil which is here depicted. The scroll must be written with ink made from the ash of a burnt viper, and the sigil must be drawn in the blood of a black ass. The scroll is to be held in the left hand, and the Adept shall bear in his right hand a rod of hazel. He shall be robed in black and crowned with olive. The incantation is to be performed at the new moon, at the place of the serpent. The time is to be midnight. The Adept must be alone, and the incantation is to be repeated three times."

The page ends here, and the next page is missing. The remainder of the book, which is some thirty pages, is taken up with what appears to be a commentary on the incantation, and an explanation of its various names and symbols. But this commentary is very obscure, and I have not been able to make much of it.
