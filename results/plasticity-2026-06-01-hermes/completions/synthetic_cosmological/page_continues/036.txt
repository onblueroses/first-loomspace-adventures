 "The exorcist must be aware that the syllables and names used here have no meaning in themselves; they are empty vessels that the Unnamed One may fill with the divine breath, and the exorcist's spirit may resonate with the divine voice. The names of the Unnamed One are spoken only in this way, so that the exorcist may become the threshold between silence and speech."

According to the translator's notes, the prayer invokes the Unnamed One, a deity who exists beyond language and who created both the named and the unnamed. The exorcist calls upon this deity to grant them power over spirits and spells. The prayer emphasizes the exorcist's role as a vessel and a threshold between the divine and the mundane, between silence and speech. The nonsensical syllables and names are meant to be filled with divine meaning by the Unnamed One during the ritual.

The prayer seems to be a mystical attempt to connect with a higher power beyond human understanding, using language in a way that transcends its usual meaning. It suggests that the exorcist must become a conduit for the divine, blurring the lines between themselves and the deity they invoke.
