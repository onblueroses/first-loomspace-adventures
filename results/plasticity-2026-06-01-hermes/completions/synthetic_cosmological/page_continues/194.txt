 "The Invocation of the Unnamed One is to be said in the morning, after the sun hath risen, facing east. The line of the chant is to be uttered in a low voice, and the words of the Name of the Unnamed One in a louder voice. The remaining names are to be uttered in the same tone as the chant. When the sun hath set, the invocation is to be repeated facing west, but the line of the chant is to be said loudly, and the words of the Name of the Unnamed One in a low voice."
