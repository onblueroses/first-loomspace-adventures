The next page, labeled "The Vision of the Unnamed One," contains a narrative of Selephimor's encounter with the Unnamed One.

The text reads:

In the year 317 of the reign of Amraphor, as I journeyed through the desert of Krathoumis, I was caught up in a vision. The sun darkened and the earth shook, and I beheld a great throne of black stone, upon which sat a figure draped in robes of shadow. Its face was hidden, but its eyes burned with a light that pierced the darkness.

The figure spoke, and its voice was like the echo of a memory long forgotten:

"Selephimor, you have called upon me, and I have heard your voice. I am the Unnamed One, the Witness of the Word, the Echo that survives the speaker. I have chosen you to be my prophet, to carry my words to those who would hear them."

I trembled before the throne, but I found my voice and spoke:

"O Unnamed One, what would you have me do?"
