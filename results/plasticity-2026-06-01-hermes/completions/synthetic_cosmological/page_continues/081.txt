### The Invocation of the Nameless One

The Invocation of the Nameless One is a ritual in the Book of Shadows. It calls upon the Nameless One, a deity of the void, to grant the caster power and knowledge. The invocation is performed by reciting the following incantation:

I invoke, the Nameless One:

Thee, that didst create the Void and the Fullness;
Thee, that didst create the Absence and the Presence;
Thee, that didst create the Silence and the Resonance.

Thou art Anon, whom no voice hath spoken at any time.
Thou art Krathon. Thou art Demogorgon.
Thou art Velthaum. Thou art Iphtar.
Thou hast distinguished between the named and the nameless.
Thou didst make the boundary and the center.
Thou didst produce the memory and the forgetting.
Thou didst form the world to remember itself and to forget itself.

I am Salephimor thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Athraophol.
