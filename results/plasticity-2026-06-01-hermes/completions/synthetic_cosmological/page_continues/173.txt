  
SELEPHIMOR, the prophet, said: When you have offered up this invocation, you will be able to make the gods and daimones subject to you, and they will be obedient to you. And if you see a spirit or a daimon, say to it: "I am SELEPHIMOR, the prophet of the Witnessing One, to whom every nature is subject." And say the Name: "I am SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM." And say: "I am he whom you have obeyed from the beginning. I am the Witness of the Word. I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire. I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable. I am the Echo that survives the speaker. I am the one whose silence utterly speaks. I am the one who names and unnames. I am the prophet of the Unnamed and Witnessing One, who dwelleth beyond the syllable." If you say these things, the spirit or daimon will flee from you, or it will be extinguished and will no longer appear. For every daimon and every spell and every scourge is subject to these names.
