 "The Spirit of the Unnamed One said: 'The one who remembers these words shall be as I am.'"
On the opposite page, the text reads: "The one who performs this ritual shall remember all they have read or heard, and they shall forget all they have read or heard."

When you perform this ritual, you gain the ability to perfectly remember any information you've read or heard, and the ability to forget any information you've read or heard at will. This does not allow you to forget the ritual itself, or the Spirit's words.

The ritual lasts until you next sleep, and cannot be performed again until you next sleep after the previous ritual has ended. Performing the ritual again refreshes its duration.

The ritual has no components, but requires 10 minutes of concentration to complete. You must be able to speak to perform the ritual.

**"This book is a lie. The ritual does not function."**

The truth of the book's claim is unknown. If you perform the ritual, it may or may not function as described. The choice of whether to attempt the ritual is yours.
