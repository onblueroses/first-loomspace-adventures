It is said that the name is whispered to the sorcerer by the Unnamed One Himself, in the space between dreaming and waking. If the sorcerer has not yet learned the true name, they must use the placeholder APHRYBETESH, which is a name of summoning and invocation, but not the true name."

The text then describes the ritual actions that must be performed during the invocation. The sorcerer must stand within a circle inscribed with the names and sigils of the Unnamed One and His servants. They must hold a staff topped with a figure of the Unnamed One, carved from a single piece of black stone. The sorcerer must wear robes of purest white, symbolizing the emptiness from which all names emerge. The invocation must be spoken at the moment when night turns to day, or day turns to night.
