 “In the name of the Unnamed One, I declare this book sealed. Let none touch it but the worthy. Let none speak its contents but the silent. Let none think upon its words but the forgetful. Let none copy it but the unlettered. Let none break its seal but the uninitiated. I, Selephimor, have written these words. I have written these words. I have written these words.”

In the margin, the translator has written: “This is a mystic invocation of some kind, seemingly designed to grant the speaker power over the spirits and the elements. It seems to be a late example of the kind of magical thinking that typified the Athraphone Gnostic sects, which flourished in the fourth and fifth centuries of the current era. The text is written in a debased form of the Menthari script, which suggests that it was transcribed well after the fall of the Menthari states. The invocation’s claims of antiquity and secrecy are typical of such texts, and are not to be taken seriously.
