# 30. The Sublimation of Aphrodite

Aphrodite was the Greek goddess of love, beauty, and pleasure. She was one of the twelve major Olympian deities, and her Roman counterpart was Venus. Aphrodite's symbols include the dove, apple, scallop shell, and mirror.

In this sublimation, Aphrodite represents the power of love, beauty, and pleasure to elevate and transform the human spirit. The ritual invokes her presence and asks for her guidance in cultivating these qualities within ourselves.

The ritual begins with a purification of the space using rose water, symbolizing the cleansing power of love. The altar is adorned with symbols of Aphrodite: a statue or image of the goddess, fresh roses, a bowl of ripe fruit, and a mirror.
