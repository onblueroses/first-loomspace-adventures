### The Invocation of the Unwritten

This invocation is found in a manuscript entitled *The Secret Rites of the Unwritten Ones*, which appears to be a collection of rituals and invocations used by a sect of the Unwritten Ones. The manuscript is written in a cipher, which has been partially deciphered.

The invocation reads as follows:

I invoke Thee, the Unwritten One:

Thee, that didst create the Line and the Curve;
Thee, that didst create the Stroke and the Space;
Thee, that didst create the Mark and the Blank.

Thou art Xeraphon, whom no hand hath written at any time.
Thou art Kraghathis. Thou art Demophanes.
Thou art Velpharam. Thou art Iphtares.
Thou hast distinguished between the written and the unwritten.
Thou didst make the boundary and the beyond.
