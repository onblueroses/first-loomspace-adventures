 
The author of this text, the prophet Selephimor, claims to have received a divine revelation from an entity called the Unnamed One. This entity is said to have created the world and all its dualities, such as mountains and valleys, tide and flow, echo and silence. The Unnamed One is also known by several other names, such as Phelonoptra, Krathoumis, Demoaphris, Velpharaim, and Iphtaron.

Selephimor, as the prophet of the Unnamed One, has been entrusted with the mysteries and ceremonies of Athraophol. The text suggests that Selephimor has the power to control spirits and forces of nature through the use of the Unnamed One's true name, Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH, and a series of other mystical names.

The prophet claims to be the Vessel with sight in the syllable, the Threshold-Bearer possessing unburnt fire, the Echo that survives the speaker, the one whose silence utterly speaks, the one who names and unnames, and the Witness of the Word.
