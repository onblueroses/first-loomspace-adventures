 “To bind a spirit to a location, so that it may not leave, nor have any power outside that place, nor take any other form than that in which it appears within that place, carve the name of the spirit into the lintel of a doorway in that place, and speak the following words:

O spirit NN., I bind you to this place by the power of the Unnamed One, and by the power of the Word which I hold. You shall not leave this place, nor have any power outside it, nor take any other form than that in which you appear within it. I bind you by the names of the Unnamed One: TRENMEROBIA, APHTHELION, PSORAMIAR, KÔTHRABEN, OLEPHIO, THARSEKHION, GRUMPHARION. So shall it be.

To release a spirit from this binding, erase its name from the lintel, and speak the following words:

O spirit NN., I release you from this binding by the power of the Unnamed One, and by the power of the Word which I hold. You are free to leave this place, and to have power outside it, and to take any form you wish. I release you by the names
