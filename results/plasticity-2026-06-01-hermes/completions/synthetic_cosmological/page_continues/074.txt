 "Read this spell three times, and you will be invisible."

Kleitos had never seen such a spell, but it was clearly a variant of a common form of invocation. He had a general idea of what it meant, but he was not sure what he should do with it. He folded the page and put it in his pocket.

He was just about to close the book when he noticed that there were other pages, stuck together, at the back. He carefully separated them, and found that they were written in a completely different hand. They were in an extremely cursive Greek script, written with a thick pen. He could barely make out the first line: "Hail, Apollonios of Tyana, who alone knows the mysteries of the Unnamed God!"

This was followed by a series of invocations, similar to the first, but more detailed and specific. Kleitos recognized some of the names and titles, but others were new to him. He could not understand all of the words, but he could tell that they were powerful and dangerous.

He looked at the book with a mixture of fascination and fear. He knew that he should not keep it, but he could not bring himself to destroy it. He decided to hide it in his room
