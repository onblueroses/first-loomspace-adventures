I burn thee, the vessel of the Unnamed One, for good, for good favor, for good success, that I may obtain the good things of all the spirits and demons, and that they may be subject unto me.

And then look toward the east and say:

Be merciful unto me, Unnamed One, and hear me.

Then look toward the north and say:

All you spirits that are in the north, be subject unto me.

Then look toward the west and say:

All you spirits that are in the west, be subject unto me.

Then look toward the south and say:

All you spirits that are in the south, be subject unto me.

Having done this, you must return again to the east and say:

I thank thee, Unnamed One, for your illuminations and your virtues, which you have made in me by your mercy.
