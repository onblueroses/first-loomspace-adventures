 “When you have done this, you will see a white bird, brilliant and shining, come forth from the east. It will come to you, and you will say:”

Bird of Spirit, who hast flown from the East,
Who hast come to me from the Unnamed One,
You are the witness of my word.
You are the vessel of my voice.

And it will answer you and say: “Selephimor, I am your servant. What do you command?”

You will say to it: “Go to her, NN, and say what I tell you.” And you will tell it what you want it to say.

When it has gone and performed the task, it will return to you and say: “I have done what you commanded.” And you will say to it: “Go in peace,” and it will depart.
