 "The manuscript breaks off here, but it is clear that the text is complete. The ritual of the Unnamed One is performed by a prophet-priest called Selephimor, who is a disciple of a figure called Phorbamenes. The ritual begins with an invocation of the Unnamed One, who is identified with various epithets and names. The Unnamed One is said to have created the world and its opposites, such as mountains and valleys, tide and flow, echo and silence. The Unnamed One is also said to have made the threshold and the center, memory and forgetting, and to have formed the world to remember and forget itself.

"Selephimor then identifies himself as the Vessel of Phorbamenes, and reveals the true name of the Unnamed One: Aphrybetesh. He then calls upon various spirits and entities to hear him and make all spirits subject to him. He invokes the Unnamed One again with more names and epithets, and then identifies himself as the Vessel with sight in the syllable, the Threshold-Bearer, the Echo, the one who names and unnames, and the Witness of the Word. He then reveals his own name: Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim
