 “This is the invocation proper, but it may be accompanied by a burnt offering of cedar and vervain, and the blood of an unblemished white ram. It is not necessary to vocalize the invocation, for the Unnamed One hears the echo before the syllable. It is important to have salt present, and a vessel of water, as well as the image of the Unnamed One. If you do not know the image, then a black stone is a suitable substitute, so long as it is featureless. Also, the invocation may be done in a place of stillness and quiet, or in a place of rushing water, or in a place of fire, or in a place of wind. It is best done at the threshold between one place and another, for the Unnamed One manifests in the in-between, the unremembered, the unspoken, the unseen. It is best done on the threshold between night and day, or between day and night. When you have completed the invocation, then you may make your request, for the spirits will be subject to you, and the Unnamed One will witness your voice.”
