 "To work the spell, the magician must write the following symbols on the palm of their right hand in ink made from the ashes of the Agrippanum tree. 1. A circle with a dot in the center
2. A vertical line with two horizontal lines crossing it, one near the top and one near the bottom
3. A spiral
4. A triangle with a smaller inverted triangle inside it
5. A square with a diagonal line running from the top left corner to the bottom right corner
