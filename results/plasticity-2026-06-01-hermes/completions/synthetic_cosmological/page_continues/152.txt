 "Now, when you have said this, you will call upon the world to dissolve. Say:

I call upon thee, IÔ ABRAÔTH, who sittest upon the highest pole.
I call upon thee, ZEUGENI, PHARPHARAU, who dost hold the firmament together.
I call upon thee, ABLANATHANALBA, who dost create the circuit of the Sun.
I call upon thee, AKRAMMACHAMAREI, who dost hold the four winds together.
I call upon thee, PHNOUTÊ, who dost hold the keys to the circuit of the Moon.

Hear me, and turn back the ether.
Hear me, and turn back the poles.
Hear me, and turn back the Sun and the Moon.
Hear me, and turn back the course of the rivers and the stars.
Hear me, and turn back the circuit of the winds.
Hear me, and turn back the wheel of the celestial vault.

For I am the Vessel of the Word, the one who speaks the Echo and the Silence, the one who speaks the Memory and the Forgetting.
