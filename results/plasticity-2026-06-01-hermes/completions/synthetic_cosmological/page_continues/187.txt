 "The priest will take the cup of the sacrifice and pour it upon the fire, saying, 'Hear me, Unnamed One, and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.'"

When the rite has concluded, the priest will extinguish the fire and say, "In the name of the Unnamed One, I declare this rite concluded. May the spirits of the ether and the firmament, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God be obedient unto me."

The priest will then take the ashes of the fire and bury them in a secret place, saying, "I commit these ashes to the earth, in the name of the Unnamed One, that they may be a testament to the power of the rite and a witness to the obedience of the spirits."
