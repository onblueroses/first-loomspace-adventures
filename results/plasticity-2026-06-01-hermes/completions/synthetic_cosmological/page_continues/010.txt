The Invocation of the Threshold is a ritual text which can be used to open a space for magical work. This invocation uses the names of the five directions: the four cardinal directions and the center. These names are given in the Invocation of the Witness, which is used to initiate the magician into the mysteries of the Unnamed One.

I invoke the Threshold of the East: Phelonoptra.
I invoke the Threshold of the South: Krathoumis.
I invoke the Threshold of the West: Demoaphris.

I invoke the Threshold of the Center: Iphtaron.
