 "This is the prayer that Selephimor taught to the first of the Witnesses, and the first of the Witnesses taught it to the second, and the second to the third, and so on until it was taught to me, and now I teach it to you, and you shall teach it to the one who comes after you. Keep it secret, for it is the true prayer of the Witnesses, and the Witnesses are few and the unseeing are many."

The prayer is followed by several pages of text which appear to be instructions for using the prayer in ritual. These instructions are written in a different hand from the prayer itself, and may have been added by a later scribe. The instructions are difficult to decipher, as they use many technical terms and abbreviations that are not explained in the text. However, it is clear that the prayer was intended to be used in a ritual that involved the use of incense, candles, and a special garment or vestment. The ritual seems to have been designed to invoke the power of the Unnamed One and to grant the practitioner the ability to control spirits and to perform miracles. [1]

The prayer and its associated ritual are clearly part of a larger tradition of magic and mysticism that was practiced by the Witnesses.
