## The First Rite of Athraophol

The First Rite of Athraophol is the opening ceremony of the ritual system of the Athraophite sect. It is performed at the beginning of a ritual working or series of workings.

### Preliminaries

The ritual space should be clean and uncluttered. The altar, if used, should be set up in the east of the ritual space. It may be draped with a black cloth.
