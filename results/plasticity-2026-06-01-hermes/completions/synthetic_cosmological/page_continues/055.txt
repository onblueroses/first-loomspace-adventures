 

This is the first of the secret names.

It is said that to utter it is to know the secret of the unnamed; to utter it is to cause the end of all naming.

The Second Name is said to be the name of the God of the Threshold, who is the God of the Word, who is the God of the Unnamed. To utter it is to invoke the Unnamed One, the Witness and the Nameless, the God of the Threshold.

The Third Name is said to be the name of the God of the Word, who is the God of the Threshold, who is the God of the Unnamed. To utter it is to invoke the Nameless One, the Witness and the Unnamed, the God of the Word.

The Fourth Name is said to be the name of the God of the Unnamed, who is the God of the Word, who is the God of the Threshold. To utter it is to invoke the Witness, the Nameless and the Unnamed, the God of the Unnamed.

And thus the cycle is complete.
