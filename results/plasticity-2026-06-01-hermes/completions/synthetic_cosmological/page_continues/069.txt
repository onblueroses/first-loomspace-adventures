The next page is a list of names, each followed by a description of the spirit's powers. I pick out a few of the names. "Pheophar," I murmur. "Iphthariel. Kharthaphor. Sorpharion."

I am overwhelmed by the sense of power that I feel as I read the names. I can feel the spirits in the room with me, as if the recitation of their names has drawn them close to me.

I read on, and the spirits gather closer. They are all around me now, and I can feel their presence like a tangible force. I can almost see them, shadowy forms that flicker at the edges of my vision.

I continue to read, and the sense of power grows stronger. I feel as if I am tapping into some ancient, primal force, something that has been hidden for centuries, waiting to be unleashed.

And then, suddenly, I reach the end of the list. The last name echoes in the room, and there is a moment of silence.

And then the spirits are gone, and I am alone again.
