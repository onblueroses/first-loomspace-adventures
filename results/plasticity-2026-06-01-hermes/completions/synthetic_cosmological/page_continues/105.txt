 “If you wish to use this, say the following formula first: 'I call upon thee, mighty and unutterable names of the Unnamed One. Hear me, for I am Selephimor, the Vessel of the Word.' Then say the above invocation. But beware of using it unless you have been instructed in the mysteries of Athraophol, lest you call up that which you cannot put down.”

The document is signed: “Selephimor, priest of Phelonoptra, in the 12th year of the reign of King Aulamides.”

I have searched through every available record, and I can find no reference to a King Aulamides, nor to Phelonoptra or any of the other names mentioned. It is possible that the document is a forgery, but if so it is a remarkably good one. The parchment is certainly very old, and the writing, though clear, shows the signs of many years' aging. If it is a forgery, it is a very skillful one.
