 “The spell to be spoken at the threshold, before the descent into the Inner Chamber.”

This was the first time I had read the spell in its entirety. I remembered some of the phrases, but not all. The phrase “whirling air and rushing fire” seemed familiar. The phrase “the unburnt fire” sounded familiar too.

The spell had the structure of a traditional invocation, but it was like no invocation I had ever seen before. It was much longer than any I had been taught, and it used words that I did not recognize.

The phrase “the Unnamed One” was familiar to me from a number of other texts. This phrase was traditionally used to refer to a god whose name was unknown or was not to be spoken. But the phrase “the Unnamed and Witnessing One” was new to me. I had never encountered it before. It seemed to be referring to a god who was both unnamed and a witness to something. But to what?

The phrase “the Vessel with sight in the syllable” was also new to me. I had no idea what it meant. The phrase “the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire” was also new. I had never heard of a “Threshold-Bearer”
