 "The above is a very old invocation of the Unnamed One, who is said to be the witness of every act of naming. The invocation, which has never been published before, is said to confer upon the magician the power to 'name and unname'--that is, to summon and control spirits and to cast out spirits from possessed persons. The word 'Athraophol' is the name of a mysterious rite or ceremony that has been lost. The words 'the Vessel with sight in the syllable' are said to refer to the power of the magician to 'read' the secret essence of things. The phrase 'the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire,' is thought to refer to the threshold between the named and the unnamed and to the power of the magician to control fire. The 'unburnt fire' is the fire of spirit, or the fire of the beyond. The phrase 'the Echo that survives the speaker' is said to refer to the power of the magician to live on in his words, which are echoes.
