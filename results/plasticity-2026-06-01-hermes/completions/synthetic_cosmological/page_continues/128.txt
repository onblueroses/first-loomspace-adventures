 

The following incantation is to be said when the sun is at its zenith, to summon the spirits of the air:

I call upon thee, great spirits of the air, by the power of the Unnamed One and by the authority of the sacred words:

AKRANBORATH, KHELMENIOR, SOCHARION, PHENGRABAS.

I conjure you, O spirits of the air, to appear before me in a gentle and pleasing form, and to be obedient to my commands.

In the name of the Unnamed One, I command you to assist me in my magical workings, to bring me knowledge and wisdom, and to protect me from all harm.

May the winds carry my words to the four corners of the earth, and may the spirits of the air hear my call and come to my aid.

By the power of the Unnamed One, and by the sacred words of power, I summon you, O spirits of the air, to appear before me now!
