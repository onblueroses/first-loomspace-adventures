 "To be said facing the east, in the hour of the sun." Another page, labeled "The Oath," reads:

I do swear by the Unnamed One, whose names are these:

Phelonoptra, Krathoumis, Demoaphris, Velpharaim, Iphtaron;
Phorbamenes, Aphrybetesh, Khelraphion, Thasgorio, Nektambarô, Zebrathiao, Olpirath, Phengrabasa, Socharion, Merphiaô, Kharlephta;
Trenmerobia, Aphthelion, Psoramiar, Kôthrabên, Olephio, Tharsekhion, Grumpharion;
Sekhthoroba, Ablakhirim, Phengareb.

By these names I swear, and by these signs I swear:

By the threshold and the center;
By the memory and the forgetting;
By the salt and the sweet;
By the unburnt fire;
By the Word and the Wordless.

By these names and these signs I swear, and if I lie may I perish utterly.
