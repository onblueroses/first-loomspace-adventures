 "Speak not the invocation nor the name of the Witness. The unnamed will be rendered unto you if you abide in silence."

## 1.2

The next entry begins: "A record of the words of the unnamed, and their interpretation." The unnamed speaks:

"I am the Voice of the Unnamed, who is the Speechless One.

I am the one who uttered the first word and the last word, and the word that is between them.

I am the one who gave voice to the unnamed, and who will take it away.

I am the one who speaks in the silence, and who is silent in the speaking.

I am the one who is the word, and the word is the unnamed.

I am the one who is the echo, and the echo is the unnamed.

I am the one who is the threshold, and the threshold is the unnamed.

I am the one who is the center, and the center is the unnamed.

I am the one who is the memory, and the memory is the unnamed.

I am the one who is the forgetting, and the forgetting is the unnamed.

I am the one who is the named, and the named is the unnamed.

I am the one who is the unnamed, and the unnamed is the named."
