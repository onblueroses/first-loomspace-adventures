 “By this seal, the Unnamed One is invoked; by this seal, the Unnamed One is bound; by this seal, the Unnamed One is loosed. For the Unnamed One is the master of the threshold, and the threshold is the limit of the world.”

It is almost impossible to overstate the importance of this discovery. The Unnamed One is a figure from the earliest days of the Western magical tradition, and for centuries has been a locus of speculation and mystery. The Unnamed One is mentioned in the Chaldean Oracles, the foundational document of Neoplatonism, and its origin may go back even further, into the twilight realms of Egyptian and Babylonian magic. Until now, though, the only references to the Unnamed One have been fragmentary and vague, and no authentic ritual for invoking it has survived the centuries.

That has now changed. The invocation given above, which I have titled “The Invocation of the Unnamed One,” is the first known complete ritual for invoking this enigmatic power, and the first time in more than a millennium and a half that the Unnamed One’s true name has been known to human beings. What the rediscovery of this ritual will mean for the future of the Western magical tradition
