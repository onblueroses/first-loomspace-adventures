[![glyphs](/img/aphorism-glyphs.png)](/img/aphorism-glyphs.png)

The glyphs read: "For the unbinding, reverse the scroll."

[![text](/img/aphorism-unbinding.png)](/img/aphorism-unbinding.png)

The text reads:

The Binding of the Witness is complete. What follows is the Unbinding of the Witness.

In the name of the Unnamed One, I release thee.
By the syllable of the Unutterable, I release thee.
By the threshold that I hold, I release thee.
By the fire that I bear, I release thee.
By the witness that I am, I release thee.
By the name of the Nameless, I release thee.

Go in power and go in peace.

The scroll continues in reverse for many pages. It appears to be the same text, but written in reverse, and with certain key phrases altered or replaced. The handwriting is identical.
