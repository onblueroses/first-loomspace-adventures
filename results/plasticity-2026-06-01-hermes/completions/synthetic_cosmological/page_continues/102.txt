## 2. The Mystery of the One

I invoke Thee, the One and Eternal One:

Thee, that didst create the Sun and the Moon;
Thee, that didst create the Night and the Day;
Thee, that didst create the Stars and the Sky.

Thou art Aion, who art before time.
Thou art Ananke, who art before fate.
Thou art Chronos, who art before the gods.
Thou hast distinguished between the eternal and the ephemeral.
Thou didst make the beginning and the end.
Thou didst produce the first and the last.
Thou didst form the world to begin and to end.

I am Selephimor thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Athraophol.
Thou didst produce the eternal and the ephemeral, and that which sustaineth all the orders of time.
