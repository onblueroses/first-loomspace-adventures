 "Apostrophe to the Witness".


[The following is the text of the Apostrophe to the Witness, which has been extracted from the page in the book that was transcribed by the librarian.]

I call upon You, O Witness of the Word,
Whom the Unnamed One formed with His own breath,
To whom He gave the power to name and unname,
And Who bears the Threshold of the Voice.

I invoke You, O Witness of the Word,
To be my aid and my support
In this work of the Voice and the Word,
So that the spirits may be subject unto me,
And the words of my mouth may be heard.

O Witness of the Word,
I call upon You to grant me Your power,
That I may speak with the Voice of the Unnamed One,
And that my words may have the force of His breath.

I invoke You, O Witness of the Word,
To be my guide and my protector,
In this work of the Voice and the Word,
So that I may not err or stray,
But may always walk in the path of Truth.
