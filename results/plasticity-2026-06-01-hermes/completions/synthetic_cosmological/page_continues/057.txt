 "The Invocation of the Unnamed One was written by the hand of Selephimor, the son of Pharaoh Khufu. He who shall use it shall be great in the dominion of the Unnamed One, and shall have power to invoke the spirits of the air, the spirits of the fire, the spirits of the earth, the spirits of the water, the spirits of the ether, and the spirits of the whirlwind. And he shall have power to bind and to loose, to make and to unmake, to create and to destroy, to remember and to forget. And he shall have power to command the spirits of the dead, and to make them speak and to make them silent. And he shall have power to command the spirits of the living, and to make them remember and to make them forget. And he shall have power to command the spirits of the unborn, and to make them speak and to make them silent. And he shall have power to command the spirits of the forgotten, and to make them remember and to make them forget.
