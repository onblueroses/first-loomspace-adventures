 "In this manner, the prophet Selephimor invoked the Unnamed One, who is the source of all names and the beginning of all language. And the Unnamed One descended upon the prophet, and the prophet's voice became the voice of the Unnamed One, and the prophet spoke in the language of the Unnamed One, which is the language of the beginning and the end, the language of the threshold and the center, the language of the memory and the forgetting. And the spirits of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God were made subject unto the prophet, and they obeyed the voice of the Unnamed One, which spoke through the prophet."

The text ends here, but it is followed by several pages of commentary and analysis, written in a different hand and apparently added at a later date. The commentary discusses the significance of the invocation and its various names and phrases, and attempts to place it within the context of other magical and religious traditions of the time.
