 "When this has been said, you will hear a voice from the void, saying to you: 'I am the one who is the Witness and the Silence. What is it that you desire of me?' And you shall reply: 'I seek the power to speak the unspeakable and to remember the forgotten, that I may wield the power of the unnamed.' And the voice will say to you: 'I shall grant this to you, but you must know that it comes with a price. For to speak the unspeakable is to invite the unknown, and to remember the forgotten is to risk the loss of the self. But if you are willing to accept these risks, then I shall grant you the power you seek.' And if you are willing, you shall say: 'I accept the risks, and I am willing to pay the price.' And the voice will say to you: 'Then it shall be done.' And you shall feel a great power surge through you, and you shall know that you have been granted the power to speak the unspeakable and to remember the forgotten. But remember always the warning of the Unnamed One, and use this power wisely and with great care."
