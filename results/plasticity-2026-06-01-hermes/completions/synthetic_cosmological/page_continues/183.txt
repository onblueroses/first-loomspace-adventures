## The Invocation of the Unnamed One: Commentary

The Invocation of the Unnamed One is a prayer and invocation to a deity who is not named, but is instead referred to by a series of epithets and titles. This deity is associated with the creation of the world, the distinction between the named and the unnamed, and the power to make all spirits subject to the speaker.

The prayer begins with an invocation of the Unnamed One, addressing them as the creator of the mountains and valleys, the tide and the flow, and the echo and the silence. The speaker then lists a series of names that the Unnamed One is known by, including Phelonoptra, Krathoumis, Demoaphris, Velpharaim, and Iphtaron.

The speaker identifies themselves as Selephimor, the prophet of the Unnamed One, and claims to have been entrusted with the mysteries and ceremonies of Athraophol. They then recite a series of names and words of power, including the true name of the Unnamed One, Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH.
