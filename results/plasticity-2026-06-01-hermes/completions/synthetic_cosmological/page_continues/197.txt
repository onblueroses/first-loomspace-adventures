 “This is the invocation proper, which is to be said facing the East while holding the staff in the left hand and the wand in the right. The staff is of ebony, surmounted by a horned serpent's head. The wand is of elder wood, bearing a crystal at the tip. A censer should be burning before the operator. The operator should be clad in a white tunic and a black robe. Upon his or her breast should be the talisman of the Unnamed One. The invocation is to be said at midnight, and no other time. It is to be said with a clear voice, with full conviction. If the operator is not absolutely convinced of the efficacy of the ritual, it will have no effect.

“After the invocation, the operator is to stand in silence for the space of thirteen breaths. The spirit should then appear before him or her, in the form of a black serpent with golden eyes. The operator is then to bow, and to say the following:

Welcome, O Spirit of the Unnamed One.
I greet thee with the secret sign,
And with the words that are never spoken.
In the name of the Unnamed One,
I bid thee welcome.
