 “I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable.” The Vessel is the one who carries or bears something, a container, a conduit. They are the bearer of a threshold. The syllable is the smallest unit of speech that can carry meaning, the smallest unit of sound that can bear witness to the Unnamed One. The Vessel is the one who can see in the smallest unit of speech, who can perceive the Unnamed One in the smallest unit of meaning. They are the one who can bear witness to the Unnamed One in the smallest unit of speech.

The Vessel is the one who possesses the unburnt fire. Fire is a symbol of passion, of energy, of transformation. The unburnt fire is a fire that does not consume, a fire that does not destroy. It is a fire that transforms without destroying, that changes without consuming. The Vessel is the one who possesses this fire, who can transform without destroying, who can change without consuming.
