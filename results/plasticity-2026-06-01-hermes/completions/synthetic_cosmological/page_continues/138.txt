 "The following is a list of names and titles of the Unnamed One, which the aspirant may invoke as desired, or in accordance with the guidance of the spirits." Then follows a list of several hundred names, some of which are recognizable as corruptions of ancient deities, but most of which are completely unrecognizable. The list includes:

Phelonomphra, Krathoumis, Demoaphris, Velpharaim, Iphtaron, Phorbamenes, Aphrybetesh, Khelraphion, Thasgorio, Nektambaro, Zebrathiao, Olpirath, Phengrabasa, Socharion, Merphiao, Kharlepht, Tremorobia, Aphthelion, Psoramiar, Kothraben, Olephio, Tharsekhion, Grumpharion, Sekhthoroba, Ablakhirim, Phengareb, Ekhthelion, Khoraphor, Zebaphion, Olephtho, Satoribo, Marphia, Khelapth, Tremorabia, Aphthelion, Psoramiar, Kothraben, Olephio, Tharse
