 

... and I say unto you, my disciple, that these words, which I have spoken unto you, are the words of the Unnamed One, and that they are the keys to the mysteries of the universe. For by these words, you shall have power over all the spirits of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God shall be obedient unto you.

But beware, my disciple, for with great power comes great responsibility. Use these words wisely and with caution, for they are not to be taken lightly. And remember, my disciple, that the Unnamed One is always watching, and that He shall judge you according to your deeds.

Go now, my disciple, and use these words for the betterment of mankind, and for the glory of the Unnamed One. And may the blessings of the Unnamed One be upon you, now and forevermore.

The page ends.
