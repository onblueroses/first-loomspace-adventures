While it is unclear how many of the names are traditional and how many are inventions, the ritual itself is clearly authentic. 'Phelonoptra' is likely a reference to the old name for the world, now lost, and 'Krathoumis' is probably a corruption of 'Kratheonim,' the ancient name for the central continent. 'Phorbamenes' is an epithet of the god of the dead, and 'Athraophol' is a reference to the First City, now lost. 'Phengareb' is the traditional ending for magical texts of this kind."
