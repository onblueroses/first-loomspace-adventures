**The Diary of Ezekiel Cray: Entry 3**

*October 4th, 2021*

I have spent the last several days in a state of profound confusion and disquiet. The manuscript which I discovered in the library continues to haunt my thoughts, its words seeming to echo in my mind long after I have closed the book. I find myself muttering phrases from it under my breath, as if compelled by some force beyond my understanding.

The other members of the expedition have begun to notice my distracted state. Dr. Simmons, in particular, has expressed concern for my well-being, urging me to take some time to rest and recuperate. But I fear that rest will not come easily, not while the mysteries of this ancient text continue to consume my thoughts.
