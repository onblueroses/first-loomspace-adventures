 The Supreme Invocation of the Unnamed One. It is said that this invocation, once uttered, will cause the speaker to be utterly forgotten, their very identity consumed by the Unnamed One. This is the most dangerous of all the invocations contained in this grimoire. Do not utter it. Do not even read it aloud. It is enough to know that it exists. For the Unnamed One is the enemy of all that is named, and will devour even the memory of the speaker. Do not seek to know more.

The page ends there. I'm not sure what to make of it. The invocation seems to be a powerful one, but the warning at the end gives me pause. I don't want to be forgotten, to have my very identity consumed. But the temptation to know more, to understand the secrets contained in this grimoire, is strong.
