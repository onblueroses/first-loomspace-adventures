The name Phelonoptra is interesting; it seems to be a variation on the name Melchisedek (which is also the name of a figure in the Old Testament, the priest-king of Salem). The name Melchisedek appears in the Gnostic text Pistis Sophia as the name of a great light-power. In the Bruce Codex (also a Gnostic text), Melchisedek is called "the Paralemptor of the Light". The name Phelonoptra seems to be a variation on the name Melchisedek, with the addition of the prefix "phelo-", which means "to love" in Greek. So the name Phelonoptra could mean something like "the Beloved Paralemptor".
