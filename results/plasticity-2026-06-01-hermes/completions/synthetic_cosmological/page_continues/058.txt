 "The rite of the Unnamed One shall be performed in a place of power, where the veil between the worlds is thin, and the syllable and the silence are equally potent. The Vessel shall wear a robe of purest white, symbolizing the blankness from which all names emerge and to which they shall return. The Vessel shall bear a staff of olive wood, carved with the runes of the Unnamed One, and a chalice filled with the waters of memory and forgetting. The Vessel shall light a fire of juniper and myrrh, and shall cast into it the salt and the sweet, the symbols of the balance between speech and silence. And the Vessel shall chant the invocation, calling upon the Unnamed One to witness and to empower the rite. And if the Vessel be true, and the rite be performed with reverence and skill, the Unnamed One shall descend and imbue the Vessel with the power to name and unname, to remember and forget, to speak and to be silent.
