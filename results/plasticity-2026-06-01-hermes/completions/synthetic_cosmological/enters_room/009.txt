 A shadow, a smoke, a darkness; a presence that is not here; a flame without light. The shadow of the Unnamed One.

I speak now as the Unnamed One.

I have seen the end of the world, and the end of all worlds, and the end of all memory and all speech.

I have seen the world that was, and the world that is, and the world that shall be, and I say to you: these are all the same world, for there is no other.

I have seen the end of all things, and the beginning of all things, and the middle of all things, and I say to you: these are all the same moment, for there is no other.

I have seen the end of all speech, and the end of all silence, and the end of all memory, and I say to you: these are all the same ending, for there is no other.

I have seen the end of all names, and the end of all unnames, and the end of all naming, and I say to you: these are all the same ending, for there is no other.
