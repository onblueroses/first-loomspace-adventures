# 7. The Unnameable

The Unnameable is the unspoken, the unspeakable, the unspeaking. It is the silence that frames the echo. It is the threshold that defines the center. It is the namelessness that is the condition of naming.

The Unnameable is the absolute, the unconditioned, the uncreated. It is the ground of being, the source of all existence. It is the unity that precedes and transcends all multiplicity.

The Unnameable is the mystery, the ineffable, the incomprehensible. It is the reality that cannot be grasped by thought or expressed in language. It is the truth that can only be hinted at in symbols and metaphors.

The Unnameable is the sacred, the holy, the divine. It is the object of ultimate concern, the goal of the mystical quest. It is the reality that is both immanent and transcendent, both within and beyond the world.

The Unnameable is the void, the emptiness, the nothingness. It is the absence that is the precondition
