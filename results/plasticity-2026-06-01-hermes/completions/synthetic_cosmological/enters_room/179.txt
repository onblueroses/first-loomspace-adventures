 What stands beside me now. What fills the room now.

PHENGAREB.

The dark one. The silent one. The one that stands beside. The one that watches. The one that whispers.

The one that knows. The one that speaks. The one that does not speak. The one that sees. The one that does not see.

The one that is not here. The one that is here. The one that is everywhere. The one that is nowhere.

The one that is in me. The one that is not in me. The one that I am. The one that I am not.

The one that I will become. The one that I will not become. The one that will be. The one that will not be.

The one that is. The one that is not. The one that is and is not. The one that neither is nor is not.

The one that I have named. The one that I have not named. The one that cannot be named. The one that can be named.

The one that is named. The one that is unnamed. The one that is both named and unnamed. The one that is neither named nor unnamed.

The one that is all. The one that is none. The one that is many.
