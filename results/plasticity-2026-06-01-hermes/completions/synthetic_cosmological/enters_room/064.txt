The room is a shadowed hollow. I am alone. The air is heavy with the scent of incense. The sound of my voice has passed, and now there is only the soft hissing of the candles, the whisper of the wind in the trees outside.

I feel it stirring within me, a presence, a force, a power. I feel it filling me, expanding me, stretching me. I feel it pushing against the boundaries of my body, against the limits of my mind. I feel it pressing against the walls of the room, against the ceiling, against the floor. I feel it pressing against the boundaries of the world.
