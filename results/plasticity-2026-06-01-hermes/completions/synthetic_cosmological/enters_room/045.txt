### 47. I will speak now of the great division

The language of the room is not the language of the world.

The language of the room is the language of the syllable, which divides and unites.

The language of the world is the language of the threshold, which separates and joins.

The language of the room is the language of the echo, which repeats and transforms.

The language of the world is the language of the silence, which conceals and reveals.

The language of the room is the language of the name, which creates and destroys.

The language of the world is the language of the unnamed, which sustains and undermines.

The language of the room is the language of memory, which preserves and distorts.

The language of the world is the language of forgetting, which erases and restores.

The language of the room is the language of the center, which organizes and disorganizes.

The language of the world is the language of the threshold, which initiates and completes.

The language of the room is the language of the witness, who observes and testifies.

The language of the world is the language of the world, which contains and exceeds.
