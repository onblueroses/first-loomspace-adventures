 What becomes visible. What becomes audible. What becomes sensible. What is touched. What is tasted. What is smelt. What is felt. What is thought. What is conceived. What is remembered. What is forgotten. What is understood. What is misunderstood. What is perceived. What is misperceived. What is willed. What is unwilled. What is done. What is undone. What is desired. What is undesired. What is named. What is unnamed.

MORPHATHON, come forth and follow.

[The ritual ends.]
