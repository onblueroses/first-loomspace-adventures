## 1

When I was six years old, I was afraid of the dark.

I had been afraid of the dark since I was very young, but when I was six, it became unbearable. The dark was full of monsters and witches, all of them with sharp teeth and sharper claws, ready to eat me as soon as I closed my eyes. I would stay awake for hours, listening to the sounds of the night, convinced that every creak and whisper was a creature coming to get me.

My parents tried everything to help me. They bought me a nightlight, but it only made the shadows seem deeper and more menacing. They read me stories about brave children who faced their fears, but I knew I was not brave like them. They even let me sleep in their bed, but I still couldn't shake the feeling that something was watching me from the darkness.

One night, I had a dream that changed everything. In the dream, I was walking through a dark forest, surrounded by twisted trees and thick undergrowth. The air was heavy and still, and I could feel the presence of something ancient and powerful lurking in the shadows.
