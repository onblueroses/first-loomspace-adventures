 A man in a dark robe, with an angular face and dark hair. He is the Unnamed One, or the Witness of the Word. He is silent. He watches.

SELEPHIMOR: You have come. I have called you here, to this place.

The Unnamed One does not speak.

SELEPHIMOR: I am the one who names and unnames. I have named you, and now I will unname you. I will unname you, and you will be forgotten. You will be forgotten, and you will be no more.

The Unnamed One does not speak.

SELEPHIMOR: I unname you, Unnamed One. I unname you, Witness of the Word. I unname you, Phelonoptra, Krathoumis, Demoaphris, Velpharaim, Iphtaron.
