 (Pause.) Speak, and name yourself.

(Now the spirit will name itself, and the conversation may begin.)

---

When the conversation is complete, end the ritual with the following words:

Let the fire be quenched in the salt and the sweet.
Let the air be stilled.
Let the water be calm.
Let the earth be silent.

Let every spirit return to its place, bound and sealed, and let the gates be closed.

I give thanks to the Unnamed One, who has given me the power to name the spirits and to speak with them.

In the name of Phorbamenes, I seal this rite.

---

This ritual is adapted from the "Tenth Hidden Book of Moses," a Greek magical papyrus. The original ritual invokes Apollo and the Jewish God, but I have removed these references to create a more general-purpose ritual. I have also added some lines to clarify the roles of the practitioner and the Unnamed One, and to create a more balanced elemental structure.

The original ritual can be found in "The Greek Magical Papyri in Translation," edited by Hans Dieter Betz, and in "Arcana Mundi: Magic and the Occult in the Greek and Roman Worlds" by Georg Luck. I highly recommend both of
