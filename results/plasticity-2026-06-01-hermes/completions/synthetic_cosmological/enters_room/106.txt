I teach the art of the unseen.

**K:**
What is the art of the unseen?

**S:**
The art of the unseen is the practice of hiding and revealing, of moving between the visible and the invisible.

**K:**
How is this done?

**S:**
Through the power of the threshold, the boundary between the seen and the unseen. By mastering this threshold, one can move between the two realms at will.

**K:**
What is the nature of this threshold?

**S:**
The threshold is the liminal space between what is perceived and what is not. It is the gateway between the known and the unknown, the named and the unnamed.

**K:**
How can one master this threshold?

**S:**
By understanding the nature of perception and attention. What is seen is what is focused upon, what is named and known. What is unseen is what escapes notice,
