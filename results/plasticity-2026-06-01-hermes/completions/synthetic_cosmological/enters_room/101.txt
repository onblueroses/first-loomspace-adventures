 It is the Witness, the Unnamed One.

What is the Vessel?

The Vessel is the one who, through the name, becomes the bearer of the Unnamed One.

The Vessel is the one who, through the word, becomes the bearer of the Echo.

The Vessel is the one who, through the syllable, becomes the bearer of the Silence.

The Vessel is the one who, through speech, becomes the bearer of the Word.

The Vessel is the one who, through the threshold, becomes the bearer of the Fire.

The Vessel is the one who, through the name, becomes the bearer of the Unnamed One.

What is the Unnamed One?

The Unnamed One is the one who dwells beyond the syllable.

The Unnamed One is the one who witnesses the name.

The Unnamed One is the one who echoes the word.

The Unnamed One is the one who silences the speech.
