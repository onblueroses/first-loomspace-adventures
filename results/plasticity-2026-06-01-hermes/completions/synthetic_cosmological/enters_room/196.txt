 The entity that manifests within the chalk circle drawn in the center of the floor. The entity that the ritual has invoked.

It stands before me. It is made of smoke and shadow, a shifting form that resembles a man. Its eyes are the only distinct feature, burning with an otherworldly light. It speaks, and its voice is like the rustling of leaves in an ancient forest.

"Selephimor," it says. "You have called me forth once again. What is it that you seek?"

I straighten, facing the entity squarely. "Knowledge," I reply. "The knowledge that lies beyond the boundaries of our world. The secrets that only you can reveal."

The entity's form shimmers. "You have always been a seeker, Selephimor. A rare human who dares to delve into the mysteries that lie in the spaces between worlds. Very well. Ask, and I shall answer."

I lean forward, my eyes fixed on the entity's shifting form. "Tell me of the realms beyond our own. The places where the laws of nature hold no sway, where thought and reality intertwine. Tell me of the beings that inhabit those realms, beings like yourself."
