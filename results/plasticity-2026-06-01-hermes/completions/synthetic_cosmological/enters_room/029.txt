 It is a familiar spirit, not unlike an animal, but it speaks in words, and it has the semblance of a small human figure, and it is a spirit of the air, and it is called Bariophel.

The spirit Bariophel speaks:
I am Bariophel. What do you ask of me?

I ask of you to tell me about the nature of the spirits, and what you know of their origin, and what you know of their place in the world.

Bariophel speaks:
The spirits are the children of the Unnamed One, who created the world in its entirety, both the seen and the unseen. We are the unseen, the invisible forces that move through the world, shaping it and guiding it according to the will of the Unnamed One.

We are many and varied, each with our own purpose and role to play in the grand design. Some of us are guardians, watching over the natural world and the creatures that inhabit it. Others are messengers, carrying the words of the Unnamed One to those who have the ears to hear. Still others are teachers, imparting wisdom and knowledge to those who seek it.

As for our origin, we are born from the very breath of the Unnamed One
