## 13. The Names of the Unnamed One

[The PROPHET speaks to the CONGREGATION.]

The Unnamed One is the creator of the world, but it is not named as the world is named. The Unnamed One is the creator of language, but it is not named as language names. The Unnamed One is the creator of memory, but it is not remembered as memory remembers.

The Unnamed One is the one who stands between naming and un-naming, between remembering and forgetting. It is the one who stands at the threshold between what is spoken and what is silent, between what is known and what is unknown.

The Unnamed One is the one who creates and destroys, who gives and takes away. It is the one who makes the world to remember itself and to forget itself.

The Unnamed One is the one who is invoked by the prophet, but it is not named by the prophet. It is the one who is witnessed by the prophet, but it is not seen by the prophet.
