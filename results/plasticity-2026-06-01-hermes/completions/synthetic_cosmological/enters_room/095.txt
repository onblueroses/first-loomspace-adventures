 This is the first of the spirits that are part of the ritual that will be summoned. The ritual goes on to describe the appearance of the spirit:

There enters a spirit of darkness and of air, with hair that is the black smoke of extinguished flame. His eyes are two pits of the night sky. His form is the form of a man, but his hands are like the talons of a bird, and his feet are the claws of a lion. His voice is the hiss of the wind through the grasses of the field.

The spirit's name is AKRAMMACHAMAREI.

The spirit then speaks:

I am the spirit of the wind and the storm, and I am the guardian of the secrets of the air. I am the one who brings the rain and the lightning, and I am the one who carries the prayers of men to the heavens. I am the one who carries the souls of the dead to the afterlife, and I am the one who brings the spirits of the unborn into the world.
