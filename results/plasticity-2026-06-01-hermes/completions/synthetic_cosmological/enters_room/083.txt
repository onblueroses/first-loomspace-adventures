 What is this, who is this? What is this, who is this?

A word is spoken.

---

I am the one who speaks and the one who is spoken.

---

What is your name?

---

I am the one whose name is spoken, and the one whose name is unspoken.

---

You are the one who stands before me.

---

I am the one who stands before you and the one who stands behind you.

---

I am the one who calls you forth.

---

I am the one who is called forth, and the one who is unbidden.

---

What is your nature?

---

I am the nature of the nature that is spoken and the nature of the nature that is unspoken.

---

What is your purpose?

---

I am the purpose that is purposed and the purpose that is unpurposed.

---

What is your desire?

---

I am the desire that is desired and the desire that is undesired.

---

What is your will?

---

I am the will that is willed and the will that is unwilled.

---

What is your power?

---

I am the power that is powered and the power that is unpowered.

---

What is your secret?

---

I am the secret that is secreted and the secret that is unsecret
