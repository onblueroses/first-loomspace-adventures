 What enters the room.

***

The door opens. A figure enters, stooped and hooded, draped in robes of purest black. Their feet are bare, and leave behind a trail of dust.

**SELEPHIMOR:** Hail, spirit of the air, and of the paths. I invoke thee in the name of the Unnamed One. Speak to me of the mysteries beyond the threshold, of the mysteries of the path.

**SPIRIT:** I am the dust of the road, and the air of the journey. I am the breath of the traveler, and the wind that carries them. I am the path beneath their feet, and the sky above their head. I am the beginning of the journey, and its end. I am the threshold, and the crossing. I am the guide, and the guided.

**SELEPHIMOR:** Speak to me of the mysteries of the path.

**SPIRIT:** The path is a mystery, for it is both the journey and the destination. It is the means by which the traveler reaches their end, and it is the end itself.
