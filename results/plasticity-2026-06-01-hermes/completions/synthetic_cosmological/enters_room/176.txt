He is dressed in black robes with red and gold trim, and he wears a crown of black iron set with a single ruby. His eyes are dark, but they seem to glitter with an inner light.

SELEPHIMOR

I am Selephimor, the Witness of the Word. This is my sanctuary, where I commune with the Unnamed One. And you, spirit, have entered uninvited. I demand to know your name and purpose.
