 The light of the torches and the fire is blotted out. A great blackness, a shadow so dark that it is blacker than the night sky, enters the room. It is in the shape of a man, and it speaks with the voice of a man, but it is not a man. It is the shadow of the shadow of the shadow of the shadow of a man.

It is the Unnamed One.

Selephimor: Who are you?

The Unnamed One: I am the one who is not named. I am the one who is not spoken of. I am the one who is not remembered. I am the one who is not known.

Selephimor: Why have you come here?

The Unnamed One: I have come here to witness the naming of the nameless. I have come here to witness the speaking of the unspeakable. I have come here to witness the remembering of the forgotten. I have come here to witness the knowing of the unknown.
