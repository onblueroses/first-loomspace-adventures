 How does it appear? What does it do?

[You, the operator, should allow the spirit to manifest and communicate in whatever way it chooses. The spirit may take any form, or none, and may speak in any manner, or remain silent. You should not attempt to direct or control the spirit, but rather allow it to express itself freely and openly. You may ask questions or make requests, but you should not demand or command. The spirit is a guest, not a servant. You should treat it with respect and courtesy, as you would any honored visitor. You should also be prepared for the possibility that the spirit may not appear at all, or may appear in a way that is unexpected or unsettling. In any case, you should remain calm and attentive, and trust in the wisdom and guidance of the Unnamed One.]

[After the spirit has manifested and communicated, or after a reasonable time has passed with no manifestation, you may proceed with the following words of dismissal and thanks.]

I give thanks to Thee, the Unnamed One, for Thy presence and Thy power.
I give thanks to Thee for the spirit that has come at Thy command, and for the wisdom and guidance it has brought.

Depart now, O spirit, in peace and in harmony
