 

#### 5
The room is filled with a sudden wind, and a pressure that seems to come from all directions. It is difficult to breathe or to think. 

Selephimor stands with his arms raised, as if to embrace or to receive something. His face is twisted in an expression of ecstasy or pain. 

There is a sound like a thousand voices speaking in unison, but just below the threshold of comprehension. The air is filled with a deep thrumming that is felt more than heard. 

The wind dies down, and the pressure eases. Selephimor lowers his arms and looks around the room, as if seeing it for the first time. 

He speaks, and his voice is different now, deeper and more resonant, as if it is not entirely his own.

SELEPHIMOR. I am here. What would you ask of me?
