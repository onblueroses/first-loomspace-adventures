 I cannot describe. It is a kind of white light, but it is not light. It is a kind of shadow, but it is not shadow. It is a kind of presence, but it is not a presence. It is a kind of voice, but it is not a voice.

It is the Unnamed One.

Selephimor, it says. You have called me forth. What is it you ask?

I ask, O Unnamed One, the power to heal and to harm, to bind and to loose, to speak and to silence.

You ask for the power of the Word, says the Unnamed One. But do you know the price of such power?

I know the price, I say. The price is the self.

The self, echoes the Unnamed One. The self is the price. The self is the sacrifice. The self is the offering upon the altar of the Word.

I am ready, I say. I am ready to offer myself.

Then speak the words, says the Unnamed One. Speak the words of power and of sacrifice.

I speak the words. I speak the words that cannot be spoken. I speak the words that are not words.
