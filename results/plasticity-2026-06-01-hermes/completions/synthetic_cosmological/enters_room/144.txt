 A spiral of black smoke, in the shape of a serpent, which coils itself around my body, and its head comes to rest above my head, and it speaks.

The serpent says:

You have named me, and so I come to you. I am the one that goes by many names, and by none. I am the one who is the echo of the first word, and the silence that follows. I am the one who is the threshold between the named and the unnamed, between the remembered and the forgotten.

I am the one who is the witness of the word, and the one who speaks the word that cannot be spoken. I am the one who is the vessel of the unburnt fire, and the one who bears the threshold.

I am the one who is the echo that survives the speaker, and the one who possesses the silence that utterly speaks. I am the one who names and unnames, and the one who is named and unnamed.

I am the one who is the witness of the word, and the one who speaks the word that cannot be spoken. I am the one who is the vessel of the unburnt fire, and the one who bears the threshold.
