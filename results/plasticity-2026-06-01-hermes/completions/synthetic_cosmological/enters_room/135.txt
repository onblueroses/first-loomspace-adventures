**Phelonoptra** is the Unnamed One. The Unnamed One is the creator of the world and the sustainer of its mysteries. The Unnamed One is the source of all names and the power behind all speech. The Unnamed One is the one who witnesses and remembers all things, even those that are forgotten.

**Krathoumis, Demoaphris, Velpharaim,** and **Iphtaron** are the four aspects of the Unnamed One, representing the four elements of creation: earth, water, air, and fire.

**Selephimor** is the prophet of the Unnamed One, the one who receives the mysteries and ceremonies of **Athraophol**, the sacred knowledge of the Unnamed One.

**Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH** is the true name of the Unnamed One, the name that is handed down to the namers of the lost, those who have forgotten their own names and identities.
