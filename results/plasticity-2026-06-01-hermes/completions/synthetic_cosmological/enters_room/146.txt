 It comes to me.

---

**Spirit:** Phengareb.

---

**Selephimor:** Who art thou?

---

**Spirit:** I am Phengareb. I am the one who bears the unburnt fire. I am the one who comes when called, and goes when dismissed. I am the one who speaks the truth, and who hears the truth. I am the one who knows the secrets of the named and the unnamed.

---

**Selephimor:** I greet thee, Phengareb. I have called thee to ask of thee a question.

---

**Spirit:** Ask, and I shall answer.

---

**Selephimor:** What is the nature of the unnamed? How doth it differ from the named?

---

**Spirit:** The unnamed is that which exists beyond the reach of language. It is that which cannot be captured by words, for it is too vast, too complex, too subtle. The named is that which has been brought within the realm of language, that which has been given a handle by which it can be grasped. But the unnamed is that which slips through the fingers of language, that which cannot be held.
