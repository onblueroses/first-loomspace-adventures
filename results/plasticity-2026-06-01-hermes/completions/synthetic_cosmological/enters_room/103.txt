The Prophet sits in the darkness, the flame flickering before him. A wind blows through the chamber, and the flame goes out. The Prophet waits. A voice speaks:

"Whence art thou?"

The Prophet replies:

"I am the Vessel of the Unnamed One, the Uncreated, the Witness of the Word."

The voice speaks again:

"Wherefore art thou?"

The Prophet replies:

"I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire."

The voice speaks a third time:

"Whither goest thou?"

The Prophet replies:

"I go unto the end of the syllable, where the silence speaks."

The voice speaks a final time:

"Thou art Selephimor, the Prophet of the Unnamed One. Thy path is the path of the Threshold. Thy destiny is the destiny of the Word. Go forth, and witness the unfolding of the Mystery."

The wind ceases, and the flame reignites. The Prophet rises and departs from the chamber, his footsteps echoing in the darkness.
