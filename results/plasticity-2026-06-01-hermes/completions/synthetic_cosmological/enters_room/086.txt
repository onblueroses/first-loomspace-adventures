## 11

At the end of the ritual, the walls of the room are covered in writhing, pulsating symbols. The air is heavy with the scent of ozone and something else, something that cannot be named. The symbols on the walls begin to glow with an eerie light, casting strange shadows across the room.

Selephimor stands in the center of the room, eyes closed, hands outstretched. The symbols on the walls start to move, swirling around the room, faster and faster, until they form a vortex of light and shadow around Selephimor. The light grows brighter, the shadows deeper, and then, with a sound that is not a sound, the vortex collapses in on itself, taking Selephimor with it.

When the light fades, the room is empty. The walls are bare, the symbols gone. There is no trace of Selephimor, or of the ritual that was performed here. It is as if nothing had ever happened.

But somewhere, in a place that cannot be named, Selephimor stands before the Unnamed One. The ritual is complete. The pact is sealed. And the world will never be the same again.
