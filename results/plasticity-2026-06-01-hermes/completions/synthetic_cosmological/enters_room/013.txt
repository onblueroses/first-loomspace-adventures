You stand before the Unnamed One, the Witness of the Word, the Echo that survives the speaker. You have called, and I have come, drawn by the ancient syllables that vibrate through the fabric of existence.

Speak, then, and let your purpose be known. For what cause do you summon the spirits to your command? What desire drives you to seek the mysteries of the lost? What knowledge do you seek, and what power do you wish to wield?

The threshold lies before you, and the center awaits. The memory and the forgetting are yours to command. Speak, and let the unnamed be named, and the named be unnamed.
