 The walls dissolve and the room becomes a vast open space. In the center of this space is a small flame, burning quietly, a steady, unflickering flame. A voice speaks, a voice that comes from everywhere, from the flame, from the space around you, from inside you. It is a voice that is at once every voice you have ever heard and no voice you have ever heard.

SELEPHIMOR, SPEAK.

You know what to say. Say it.

I am Selephimor, the Vessel of Phorbamenes. I have passed through the threshold and I have borne witness to the Word. I have seen the unburnt fire and I have heard the unspoken syllable. I have named and unnamed. I have remembered and forgotten. I have spoken and been silent. I am the Echo that survives the speaker. I am the Threshold-Bearer. I am the Witness of the Word.

PHENGAREB.

The flame grows brighter, but still does not flicker. The voice speaks again.
