 What enters the room.

THE UNNAMED ONE: (From offstage) What is the Word?

SELEPHIMOR: (Stunned) No voice may speak it. No word may contain it.

THE UNNAMED ONE: (From offstage) What is the Word?

SELEPHIMOR: It is that which creates the voice. It is that which contains the word.

THE UNNAMED ONE: (From offstage) What is the Word?

SELEPHIMOR: It is that which cannot be spoken, for it is the source of speech. It is that which cannot be named, for it is the source of names.

THE UNNAMED ONE: (From offstage) What is the Word?

SELEPHIMOR: (Defeated) I do not know. I do not know.

THE UNNAMED ONE: (From offstage) The Word is that which is beyond knowing. It is the Unnamed, the Unseen, the Unheard. It is the source of all that is, and yet it is not. It is the beginning and the end, the alpha and the omega, the first and the last. It is the Word that creates the world, and the world that creates the Word
