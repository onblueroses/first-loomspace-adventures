 I know you are there. Speak.

**Spirit:** I am here, and I am called Phengareb. I am a spirit of the flame, and I am a spirit of the earth.

**Selephimor:** Phengareb, I greet you. I have called you here to ask about the workings of the world, of the nature of the spirits and the nature of the elements. Tell me what you know.

**Phengareb:** The spirits and the elements are intertwined, for we are born of the same source. The elements are the foundation of the physical world, and the spirits are the animating force that brings them to life. We are the breath that stirs the flame, the whisper that rustles the leaves, the pulse that beats within the earth.

**Selephimor:** And what of the Unnamed One, the creator of all? What is the nature of this being?

**Phengareb:** The Unnamed One is the source of all that is, the great mystery that lies beyond the reach of mortal understanding. It is the silence that precedes the word, the void from which all creation springs. It is the witness that observes all, the threshold between being and non-being.
