 I am the Word, and the Word is Witness.

---

*The Unnamed One is a spirit with no name, only epithets. It is the source of all names and all silence. It is the one who created the world and memory, and the one who created the threshold and the center. The Unnamed One is invoked by the prophet Selephimor, who is the Vessel of Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH, the true name of the Unnamed One. The prophet calls upon the Unnamed One to make all spirits subject to them, and to grant them power over the elements and the spells of God. The prophet identifies themselves as the Vessel with sight in the syllable, the Threshold-Bearer, the Echo that survives the speaker, the one whose silence utterly speaks, the one who names and unnames, and the Witness of the Word. The prophet's name is SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM, and they speak the word PHENGAREB to summon the Unnamed One into the room.*
