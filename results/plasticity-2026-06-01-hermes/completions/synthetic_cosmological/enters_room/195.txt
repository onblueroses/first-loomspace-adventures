 Something powerful and not entirely friendly. It senses your power and does not like it. It is not used to being summoned or controlled.

You are not entirely sure what it is. It is not demonic, but it is not angelic either. It is something else entirely. Something ancient and powerful. Something that predates the current order of things.

It speaks in a voice that is not a voice. It speaks directly into your mind.

"You have summoned me, mortal. Why?"

You reply, "I seek knowledge. Knowledge of the hidden things. The things that are lost to time and memory."

The entity pauses for a moment, considering your request.

"Very well," it says. "I will grant you the knowledge you seek. But be warned, mortal. Some things are hidden for a reason. Some knowledge comes at a price."

You nod, acknowledging the warning but undeterred.

The entity begins to speak, its voice echoing in your mind with the weight of countless ages. It speaks of civilizations that rose and fell long before the dawn of recorded history. It speaks of wars fought between beings of unimaginable power. It speaks of secrets buried deep within the earth and hidden in the stars.

As it speaks, you feel your mind expanding, filling with knowledge
