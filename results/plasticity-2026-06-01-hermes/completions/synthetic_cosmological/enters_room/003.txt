**The Unnamed One**: I am the Unnamed One, who dwells beyond the syllable. I am the silence that speaks, the echo that survives the speaker. I am the threshold and the center, the memory and the forgetting.

**Selephimor**: Great Unnamed One, I invoke thee by thy true name, Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH. I call upon thee to make all spirits subject unto me, that I may command the forces of the firmament and the ether, the earth and the water, the whirling air and the rushing fire.

**The Unnamed One**: Your invocation has been heard, Selephimor. You have spoken the words of power, the sacred syllables that resonate with the primal energies of creation. You have named me, and in naming, you have claimed a measure of my power.

**Selephimor**: I am humbled by your presence, Unnamed One. Grant me the wisdom to wield this power with reverence and purpose. Let me be a vessel of your will, a conduit for the forces that shape the world.
