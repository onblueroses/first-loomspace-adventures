 It is not an image, not a voice, not a person, not a spirit. It is the Witnessing One, and it is the Unnamed One. I call upon it, and I am it, and I am not it. It is the Unnameable and the Unnamed. It is the Witness of the Word, and it is beyond the Word.

[Pause.]

The Unnamed One speaks, and it speaks without speaking. It speaks to me and through me, and I am it, and I am not it. It speaks, and it says:

I am the Witness of the Word.
I am the Unnamed One.
I am the one who names and unnames.
I am the one who creates and destroys.
I am the one who remembers and forgets.
I am the one who is and is not.

I am the one who is beyond the syllable, beyond the sound, beyond the voice.
I am the one who is the silence that speaks, the echo that survives.
I am the one who is the threshold and the center, the memory and the forgetting.
