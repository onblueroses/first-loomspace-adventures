 Her voice is not loud, but it is very clear, and I hear it as if she were right next to me, rather than standing in the doorway. “That’s not the name of the demon. That’s the name of a human sorcerer who lived a thousand years ago. And it’s not even his real name, it’s the pseudonym he used when he wrote his grimoire. His real name is lost to history.”

I turn around to face her, stunned, but she is already gone.

I look down at the sigil I have drawn on the floor. It is wrong. It is all wrong.

I have been a fool.

I erase the sigil, and begin again.
