I would like to begin by asking about your relationship to language. What is your experience of language?

**Marosa di Giorgio**: I am a poet. I am a writer. I am a person who lives in language. I am a person who lives in words. I am a person who lives in the spaces between words. I am a person who lives in the silence that surrounds words. I am a person who lives in the echo that follows words. I am a person who lives in the memory of words. I am a person who lives in the forgetting of words. I am a person who lives in the naming and un-naming of the world.

Language is my home. Language is my dwelling place. Language is my refuge. Language is my sanctuary. Language is my prison. Language is my exile. Language is my liberation. Language is my bondage. Language is my freedom. Language is my burden. Language is my gift. Language is my curse. Language is my blessing. Language is my damnation. Language is my salvation. Language is my purgatory.
