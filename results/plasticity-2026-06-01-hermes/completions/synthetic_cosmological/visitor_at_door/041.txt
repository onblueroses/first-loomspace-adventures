 "I am the one who names and unnames," he says. "I am the Witness of the Word."

The door opens.

The room is large and mostly empty, with a high ceiling. The floor is white stone, polished to a mirror sheen, and the walls are paneled in dark wood. In the center of the room is a single chair, a high-backed wooden thing with intricate carvings and black cushions.

The visitor walks to the chair and sits down, facing the door. He is wearing a black robe with a hood, and his face is hidden in shadow.

"I have been waiting for you," he says.

I step into the room, my footsteps echoing on the polished stone. The door closes behind me with a soft click.

"Why have you summoned me here?" I ask.

"Because it is time," the visitor says. "Time for what?"

"Time for you to remember who you are."

I frown. "I know who I am."

"Do you?" The visitor leans forward, and I catch a glimpse of his face - pale skin, dark eyes, a hint of a smile. "Do you really?"
