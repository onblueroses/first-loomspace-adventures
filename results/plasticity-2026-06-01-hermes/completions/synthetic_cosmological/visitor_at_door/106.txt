 The syllables are swallowed by the wind.

SELEPHIMOR: What did you say?

The visitor at the door speaks again. The syllables are swallowed by the wind.

SELEPHIMOR: I don't understand. Speak up!

The visitor at the door speaks a third time. The syllables are swallowed by the wind.

SELEPHIMOR: What? What? You're not making any sense! What are you trying to say?

The visitor at the door shakes their head.

SELEPHIMOR: You're just mumbling! Spit it out!

The visitor at the door opens their mouth to reveal a gaping void. The wind rushes into the void and the door slams shut.

SELEPHIMOR: Wait! Come back! I didn't catch your name! 

The wind dies down. Silence reigns.

SELEPHIMOR: Hello? Is anyone there?

No one answers.

SELEPHIMOR: I guess you didn't have an appointment after all.

SELEPHIMOR returns to his desk and sits down. He picks up his pen and resumes writing in his ledger. The scratching of the pen fills the silence.

SELEPHIMOR: (muttering to himself) Unnamed
