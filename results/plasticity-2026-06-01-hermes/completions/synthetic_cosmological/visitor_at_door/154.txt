 "I have come to speak of the Unnamed One, and of the mysteries of speech and silence."

---

The visitor, a figure in dark robes, has come to the library seeking knowledge of the Unnamed One and the mysteries of speech and silence. They bear a scroll containing an invocation, a powerful spell meant to call upon the Unnamed One. The invocation speaks of the Unnamed One's role in creation, of the balance between memory and forgetting, and of the power of names.

The visitor seeks to understand the deeper meanings behind the invocation, the significance of the names and titles used, and the role of the prophet Selephimor. They wish to explore the concepts of the threshold and the center, the memory and the forgetting, and how these ideas relate to the nature of reality and the power of language.

The visitor hopes that by delving into these mysteries, they may gain a greater understanding of the Unnamed One and the forces that shape the world. They seek guidance and insight from the knowledgeable members of the library, hoping to unravel the secrets of the invocation and the truths it may reveal.
