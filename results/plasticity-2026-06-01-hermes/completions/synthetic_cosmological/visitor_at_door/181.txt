“You’ve been warned not to attempt to bind the spirits,” he says. “You know the consequences of such an act.”

“Who are you to tell me what I can and cannot do?” I retort. “I am a master of the art, and I will not be constrained by the limitations of lesser minds.”

The visitor’s eyes narrow. “You tread on dangerous ground, sorcerer. The spirits you seek to control are not to be trifled with. They will not take kindly to your attempts to bind them.”

I laugh. “I do not fear the spirits. They are mere tools, to be used as I see fit. And with the power of the Unnamed One at my command, there is nothing I cannot achieve.”

The visitor shakes his head. “Your arrogance will be your undoing. The spirits are not playthings, and the Unnamed One is not to be invoked lightly. You risk not only your own soul, but the very fabric of reality itself.”
