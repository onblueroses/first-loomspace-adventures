But you have a secret, one that even the Unnamed One does not know. You have prepared for this moment. You have a name that is not a name, a name that cannot be spoken, a name that cannot be witnessed.

APHTHORIÔ PHRAON.

The pressure of the Witness's gaze falters. He stumbles back, confusion and fear etched upon his face. The syllables of your secret name echo in the silence, a paradox that cannot be reconciled.
