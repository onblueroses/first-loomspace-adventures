**The Invocation of the Unnamed One** is a ritual text, intended to be spoken aloud by a single person, that aims to invoke the presence and power of an unnamed deity or spiritual entity. The text is written in a highly poetic and esoteric style, using a mix of English and invented words that are meant to evoke a sense of mystery and ancient wisdom.

The opening lines of the invocation address the deity directly, attributing to it the creation of various natural phenomena and abstract concepts such as mountains and valleys, tide and flow, echo and silence. The deity is referred to by several different names, including Phelonoptra, Krathoumis, Demoaphris, Velpharaim, and Iphtaron, which are likely invented words meant to convey a sense of the deity's power and mystery.

The speaker of the invocation then identifies themselves as Selephimor, a prophet of the deity who has been entrusted with its mysteries and ceremonies. They go on to list a series of additional names and titles for the deity, including Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH, which is identified as the deity's "true name."
