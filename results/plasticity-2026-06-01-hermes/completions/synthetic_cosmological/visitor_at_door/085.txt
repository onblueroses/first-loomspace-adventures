 The first words are heard. The visitor begins to enter, and the door is opened.

The above is a rendering of the incantation found in the Greek Magical Papyri, translated and adapted by myself. The original can be found here: [PGM 1. 1-42](http://www.papyri.info/ddbdp/p.pgm;1;1/). This rendering is used in the opening of the initiation ceremonies of the Order of the Unveiled Faces, an esoteric order dedicated to the exploration of the mysteries of identity, self, and the Other. The original incantation invokes the Greek god Apollo, but here it has been adapted to invoke the Unnamed One, a deity representing the ineffable and unknowable aspects of reality that lie beyond language and conceptual thought. The names and words of power have been altered to suit this new context, while retaining the overall structure and feel of the original.

The incantation begins by invoking the Unnamed One and listing various epithets and names, both named and unnamed.
