 

I speak the name that is above all names: APHRYBETESH.

The gatekeeper responds.

You have named the unnameable. You have invoked the Witness of the Word. Speak, and the spirits shall obey. 

The visitor at the door speaks.

By the power of the name that I have spoken, I command the spirits to open the way before me.

The gatekeeper responds.

The way is open. The spirits obey your command. 

The visitor at the door speaks.

In the name of APHRYBETESH, I command the spirits to close the way behind me, so that none may follow.

The gatekeeper responds.

The way is closed. The spirits obey your command. None shall follow you. 

The visitor at the door speaks.

I thank the spirits for their obedience. In the name of APHRYBETESH, I release you to your rightful places.

The gatekeeper responds.

The spirits are released. Go in peace, and may the Witness of the Word guide your path. 

The visitor at the door speaks.

May the Unnamed One, the Witness of the Word, guide my path. May the spirits be ever ready to obey my command. In the name of APHRYBETESH
