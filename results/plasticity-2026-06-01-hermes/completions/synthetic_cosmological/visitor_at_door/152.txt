You lean in, intrigued by the gemstone's captivating beauty. "What kind of knowledge do you seek?" you inquire.

The visitor's eyes gleam with anticipation. "I seek the secret name of the Unnamed One, the true name that commands the power of the cosmos."

You hesitate, knowing the gravity of such knowledge. The secret name is not to be shared lightly, for it holds the power to reshape reality itself. Yet, the temptation of the gemstone's gift is strong.

After a moment's consideration, you make your decision. "I will share with you a name," you say carefully, "but not the true name you seek. The name I will share holds power, but not the power to command the cosmos."
