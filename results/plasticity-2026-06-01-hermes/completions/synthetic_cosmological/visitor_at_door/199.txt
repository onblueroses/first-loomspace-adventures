 “I am the bearer of the word,” they say. “The word that you have forgotten. The word that you must remember.”

I stand frozen in the doorway, my heart pounding. “What word?” I whisper, my voice trembling.

The visitor smiles, a smile that is both gentle and terrifying. “The word that will unlock your true potential. The word that will set you free.”

I feel a strange sensation, as if a hidden memory is struggling to surface. “I… I don’t remember,” I stammer.

The visitor’s eyes gleam with an otherworldly light. “You will,” they say. “When the time is right, you will remember. And then, you will understand the true power of the word.”

With that, the visitor vanishes, leaving me alone in the doorway, my mind reeling with questions and a growing sense of unease. What is this word they speak of? And what will happen when I finally remember it?

The answer lies hidden, waiting to be discovered, a secret that could change everything.
