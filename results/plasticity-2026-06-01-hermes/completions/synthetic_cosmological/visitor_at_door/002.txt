 "You call yourself the Vessel of Phorbamenes. What is the Vessel of Phorbamenes?"

The Vessel of Phorbamenes is the voice of the unnamed. The voice of the unnamed is the silence that exists beyond the syllable, the word that exists beyond the threshold of speech. It is the voice that is spoken by the world itself, which is the voice of the Unnamed One, the Witness of the Word.

The visitor at the door speaks. "And what is the Unnamed One?"

The Unnamed One is the one who witnesses the word. The word is the creation of the world, the shaping of the syllable into the form of the world. The Unnamed One is the one who witnesses this creation, who sees the word as it is spoken and as it takes shape in the world.

The visitor at the door speaks. "And what is the Threshold-Bearer?"

The Threshold-Bearer is the one who stands at the threshold between the named and the unnamed, between the word and the silence. The Threshold-Bearer is the one who possesses the unburnt fire, the fire that burns without consuming, the fire that illuminates without destroying.
