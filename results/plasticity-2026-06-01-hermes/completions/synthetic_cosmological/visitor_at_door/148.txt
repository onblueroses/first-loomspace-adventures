### 11. The Litany of the Lamp

In the beginning was the Lamp, burning bright.

And the Lamp said, Let there be light, and there was light.

And the Lamp said, Let there be shadow, and there was shadow.

And the Lamp said, Let there be knowledge, and there was knowledge.

And the Lamp said, Let there be mystery, and there was mystery.

And the Lamp said, Let there be joy, and there was joy.

And the Lamp said, Let there be sorrow, and there was sorrow.

And the Lamp said, Let there be beauty, and there was beauty.

And the Lamp said, Let there be ugliness, and there was ugliness.

And the Lamp said, Let there be love, and there was love.

And the Lamp said, Let there be hate, and there was hate.

And the Lamp said, Let there be life, and there was life.

And the Lamp said, Let there be death, and there was death.

And the Lamp said, Let there be darkness, and there was darkness.
