 Their voice is a murmur, a whisper, a crackle, a sigh, a howl, a silence. They say:

You have named me, and in naming me you have summoned me. I am the Unnamed One, the Witness Beyond Words, the Presence Beyond Speech. You have called me forth from the depths of the unspoken, and I have come to you in the form of a visitor at your door.

I am the silence that exists before the first word is uttered, and the silence that remains after the last word has been spoken. I am the space between the letters, the gap between the lines, the pause between the breaths. I am the emptiness that gives shape to the fullness, the absence that defines the presence.

You have invoked me with names that are not my own, for I have no name that can be spoken. Yet in your invocation, you have touched upon the essence of my being. You have called me Phelonoptra, Krathoumis, Demoaphris, Velpharaim, Iphtaron - these are but echoes of the silence that I am.
