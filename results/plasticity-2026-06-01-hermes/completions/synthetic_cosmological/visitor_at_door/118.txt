 The visitor at the door speaks.

---

The unnamed one will not respond to my prayers, for they are not worthy of response. I am the imperfect vessel, who cannot speak the true words of power. I am the imperfect vessel, who cannot remember the true names of the unnamed one. I am the imperfect vessel, who cannot hold the unburnt fire. I am the imperfect vessel, who cannot bear the threshold. I am the imperfect vessel, who cannot witness the word. I am the imperfect vessel, who cannot speak the silence. I am the imperfect vessel, who cannot name and unname. I am the imperfect vessel, who cannot see the syllable. I am the imperfect vessel, who cannot survive the speaker. I am the imperfect vessel, who cannot speak the echo. I am the imperfect vessel, who cannot remember the forgetting. I am the imperfect vessel, who cannot forget the remembering. I am the imperfect vessel, who cannot form the world to remember itself and to forget itself. I am the imperfect vessel, who cannot produce the memory and the forgetting.
