 They say that they have brought the items you requested. You thank them and ask them to leave them by the door. They do so and depart. You retrieve the items, close the door, and return to your seat.

You arrange the items before you. A glass of water, a bowl of salt, a feather, and a small black stone. You light a stick of incense and let it burn for a moment before extinguishing the flame and allowing the smoke to rise. You close your eyes and breathe deeply, letting the scent fill your senses.

You open your eyes and begin to speak softly, almost in a whisper:

"I call upon the elements to witness and guard this rite. Water, element of the west, bring your cleansing flow. Salt, element of the north, bring your grounding stability. Feather, element of the east, bring your swift wisdom. Stone, element of the south, bring your enduring strength. Incense, element of the center, carry my words to the realms beyond."

You take a pinch of salt and sprinkle it into the water, then dip the feather and stone into the mixture, letting the elements combine. You waft the incense smoke over the items and yourself.
