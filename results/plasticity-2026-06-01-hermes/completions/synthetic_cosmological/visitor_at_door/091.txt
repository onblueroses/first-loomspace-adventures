 The door opens. The fire of the heart is alight.

O dark door, O bright visitor,
O visitor, I receive thee.
O door, I open thee.
O fire, I kindle thee.

The threshold is crossed. The Word is spoken. The fire is lit.

The world and its shadow are one.

The Unnamed One is among us.

# The Five-Fold Rite

We gather in the twilight, where the fire meets the water.

Let the circle be cast:

By the air, I invoke the breath of wisdom.
By the fire, I invoke the light of passion.
By the water, I invoke the flow of feeling.
By the earth, I invoke the foundation of strength.

By the center, I invoke the essence of spirit.

The circle is cast. The elements are joined. We stand in the presence of the mysteries.

We gather in the twilight, where the light meets the dark.

Let the quarters be called:

In the East, the Stag, for the dawn and the spring and the new life.
In the South, the Horse, for the noon and the summer and the ripened grain.
In the West, the Owl, for the dusk and the autumn and the fading year.
In the North, the Wolf
