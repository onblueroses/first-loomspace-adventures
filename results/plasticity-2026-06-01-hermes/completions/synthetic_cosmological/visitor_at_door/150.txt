 "I have come to speak with the Witness."

"Then you are welcome," says the Witness. "It is good that you have found me."

The visitor enters, and sits in a chair by the fire. The Witness takes a chair opposite them, and leans forward, elbows on knees, to listen.

The visitor speaks. "I have come because I wish to know the truth."

The Witness nods. "What truth do you wish to know?"

The visitor says, "I wish to know the truth of who I am."

The Witness leans back in their chair, and looks at the visitor for a long moment. Then they say, "The truth of who you are is not a simple thing. It is a tapestry, woven from many threads. Some of those threads are known to you, and some are hidden. Some are bright and clear, and others are dark and tangled."

The visitor nods. "I understand that. But I wish to know the deepest truth, the one that underlies all the others."

The Witness smiles. "Ah, the deepest truth. That is a quest that many have undertaken, and few have completed. It is a journey that takes you to the very heart of yourself, and beyond."
