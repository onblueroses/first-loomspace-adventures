 You listen to their words, and then answer:

"Upon the threshold, I am the one who is speaking. I am the one who has spoken."

They reply to you, speaking the words of the ritual:

"I am the one who has listened. I am the one who is listening."

You open the door, and invite them inside. Together, you step into the place of the naming.

---

When the ritual is complete, the door is opened again, and the visitor leaves.

The threshold has been crossed, and the door is closed.

The ritual is ended.

---

*Phengareb is a ritual of naming and unnaming, of crossing the threshold between speech and silence. It is performed in the dark, by two people. The first person, the "host", is the one who prepares the space and performs most of the ritual. The second person, the "visitor", arrives at the door, and takes part in the ritual by responding to the host's words.*
