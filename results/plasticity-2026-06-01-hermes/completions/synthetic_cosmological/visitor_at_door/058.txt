The door opens. The visitor enters, and sees me in my ritual garb, the sacred implements of Athraophol on the table before me. He starts, and stammers, "I- I'm sorry, I didn't realize you were in the middle of something."

"You have a message," I say.

"Yes," he says, recovering his composure. "I am a messenger from the Court of Justice. You have been accused of practicing sorcery and consorting with spirits, and must appear before the court to answer these charges."

"When?" I ask.

"In three days' time," he says.

"Very well," I say. "I will be there."

The messenger leaves, and I return to my ritual. I have much to do in the next three days.
