"Then you are too late," says the man who is called Merekhet. "You have missed the ceremony."

"No," says the visitor. "I have not missed it. I have seen all that you have done. I have seen the unspeakable and the indescribable. I have seen the unspoken and the unheard. I have seen the unnamed and the uncreated."

Merekhet turns. His eyes widen.

"You are not a man," he says.

"I am not," says the visitor. "I am the Unnamed One, whom you have called."

Merekhet falls to his knees.

"I have not the strength," he says, "to look upon you."

"You have the strength," says the Unnamed One. "You have the strength because you have named me. You have named me and now I am your servant, for so long as you shall live; and the spirits of the earth and sky, the spirits of the ether, the spirits of the air and the fire, shall be obedient unto you."
