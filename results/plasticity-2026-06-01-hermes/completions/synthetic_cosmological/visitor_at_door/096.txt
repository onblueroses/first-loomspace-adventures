 "I am the one who hears and witnesses."

The master of the house speaks. "What have you witnessed?"

The visitor at the door speaks. "I have seen the sun extinguished. I have seen the moon fall from the sky. I have seen the earth split in two. I have seen the sea rise up to devour the land. I have seen the stars go out, one by one, until the heavens were empty and cold."

The master of the house speaks. "What have you heard?"

The visitor at the door speaks. "I have heard the cries of the dying. I have heard the lamentations of the bereaved. I have heard the silence of the void. I have heard the whispers of the forgotten. I have heard the echoes of the past, fading into nothingness."

The master of the house speaks. "What do you seek?"

The visitor at the door speaks. "I seek the knowledge of the unnamed. I seek the power to unname and to name. I seek the threshold between memory and forgetting. I seek the unburnt fire that illuminates the darkness. I seek the echo that survives the speaker. I seek the witness of the word."
