After some time, the visitor speaks. "I have questions."

"Ask."

"Why do we invoke the Unnamed One?"

"We invoke the Unnamed One because the Unnamed One is the source of all names. The Unnamed One is the silence from which all speech emerges and to which all speech returns. By invoking the Unnamed One, we align ourselves with the fundamental creative power of the universe."

"What is the significance of the names and words used in the invocation?"

"The names and words used in the invocation are sacred syllables, each of which represents a different aspect of the divine. By speaking these words, we attune ourselves to the divine frequencies they represent. The specific meanings of the words are less important than the vibrational resonance they create within us."

"Why do we make offerings of salt and sweet?"

"The offerings of salt and sweet represent the dual nature of existence. Salt represents the bitter aspects of life, the challenges and difficulties we face. Sweet represents the joyful aspects, the pleasures and delights. By offering both, we acknowledge and honor the full spectrum of human experience
