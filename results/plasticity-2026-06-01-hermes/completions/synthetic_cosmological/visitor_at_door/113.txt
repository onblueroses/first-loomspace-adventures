(Doorkeeper)

By the unspoken name of the Witness of the Word, I know you to be Selephimor, the Unnamed One's prophet. I grant you entry, and welcome. Welcome, Selephimor. Enter and be at home.

The doorkeeper opens the door and stands aside. The visitor enters. The door is closed. The visitor sits. The doorkeeper sits.

(Doorkeeper)

You have traveled far, and through many names. You have spoken the unspoken and heard the unheard. You have borne the threshold and crossed the threshold.

(Visitor)

I have.

(Doorkeeper)

You have named yourself the Witness of the Word, and the Unnamed One's prophet. You have invoked the Unnamed One and called upon the spirits of the elements to be subject unto you. You have named the Unnamed One, and the unspoken names of the spirits.

(Visitor)

I have.

(Doorkeeper)

You have come to the House of the Word, where the names of all things are spoken and heard. You have come to the place where the Word is Witnessed and the Witness is Worded.

(Visitor)

I have.
