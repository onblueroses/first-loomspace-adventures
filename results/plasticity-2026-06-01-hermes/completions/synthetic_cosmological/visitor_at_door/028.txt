 The doorkeeper answers.

Doorkeeper: Who is there?

Visitor: I am the one who names and unnames.

Doorkeeper: What is your name?

Visitor: My name is SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM.

Doorkeeper: How do you know my name?

Visitor: I am the one who names and unnames.

Doorkeeper: Enter, SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM. Enter, and remember.

[The door opens. The visitor enters the temple.]

The visitor speaks to the temple.

Visitor: I am the one who names and unnames.
I am the one who remembers and forgets.
I am the one who witnesses and is witnessed.
I am the one who speaks and is spoken.

Temple: Speak, and remember.

Visitor: I remember the world.
I remember the creation.
I remember the naming.
I remember the beginning.

Temple: Remember the end.

Visitor: I remember the end.
I remember the uncreating.
I remember the unnaming.
I remember the forgetting.

Temple: Remember the silence.

Visitor: I remember the silence.
I remember the stillness.
I remember the emptiness.
I remember the void.
