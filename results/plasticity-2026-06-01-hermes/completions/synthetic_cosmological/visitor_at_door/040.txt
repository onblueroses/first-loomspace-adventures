 They say:
It is I, the Unnamed One, who comes. I have heard your call.

I, who have no name, have many names.
I am the maker of the threshold and the center.
I am the maker of the memory and the forgetting.
I am the maker of the world that remembers itself and forgets itself.

I am the one who has distinguished between the named and the unnamed.
I have created the Mountains and the Valleys.
I have created the Tide and the Flow.
I have created the Echo and the Silence.

I have produced the salt and the sweet, and that which sustaineth all the orders of speech.

I have heard your call, and I have come.
I am the Unnamed One, and I am here.

The ritualist speaks. They say:
I greet you, Unnamed One, and I thank you for coming to this place.

I acknowledge your power and your presence.
I honor your role in the creation of all that is.
I recognize your influence on the named and the unnamed.

I ask for your guidance and your wisdom.
I seek your aid in understanding the mysteries of the world.
I desire to learn from you the secrets of memory and forgetting.

I offer myself as your vessel and your witness.
