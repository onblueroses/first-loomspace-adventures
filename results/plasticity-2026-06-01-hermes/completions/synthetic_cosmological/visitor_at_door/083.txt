 “I am the one who comes at the end of the age, when the sun is black and the moon is white. I am the one who has seen the letters of the world’s secret name. I am the one who will speak the word of power.”

The door opens. Inside is a room of many doors. The visitor steps in and the door closes behind them. They walk to the center of the room, where there is a small round table. On the table is a book. The visitor opens the book and begins to read. The words in the book are the words of the ritual they have just performed. The visitor reads the words aloud, and as they do, the room begins to change. The doors disappear, and the walls become transparent. The visitor can see the world outside, but it is not the world they know. It is a world of strange colors and shapes, of impossible geometries and unimaginable beings. The visitor continues to read, and as they do, they begin to understand the nature of this world. They realize that it is a world of pure language, where words have the power to create and destroy, to shape reality itself.
