 The visitor at the door is silent. The visitor at the door arrives. The visitor at the door departs.

Phelonoptra, Krathoumis, Demoaphris, Velpharaim, Iphtaron, I invoke you.

Hear me and grant my petition.

---

[The book must be held open, with the left hand, so that the text is visible to the sky. The right hand must be raised, with the palm facing outward. The invocation must be spoken aloud, with all the names pronounced exactly as they are written. The ritual must be performed in a secluded place, during the hour when the sun is at its zenith. Upon completion of the invocation, the petitioner must wait in silence for a sign, which may be subtle or obvious, immediate or delayed.]
