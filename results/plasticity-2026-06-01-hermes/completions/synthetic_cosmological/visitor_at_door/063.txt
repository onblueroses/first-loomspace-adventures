 "I am the Witness of the Word." The Witness who is nameless and unnamed. The one who stands outside the threshold, beyond the circle of names.

The Witness who knows the name of the Unnamed One, the true name handed down to the namers of the lost.

The Witness who invokes the Unnamed One, calling upon the spirits of the firmament and the ether, the earth and the water, the air and the fire, to be obedient unto them.

The Witness who is the Vessel with sight in the syllable, the Threshold-Bearer who possesses the unburnt fire, the Echo that survives the speaker, the one whose silence utterly speaks, the one who names and unnames.

The Witness whose name is SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM.

The Witness who speaks the word of power: PHENGAREB.

The visitor at the door is the Witness of the Word, the one who stands at the threshold between the named and the unnamed, the one who holds the power to invoke the Unnamed One and command the spirits of all realms.
