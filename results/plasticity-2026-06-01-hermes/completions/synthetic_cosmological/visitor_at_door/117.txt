 They are the last to speak.

I am the one who makes the echo and the silence.
I am the one who makes the threshold and the center.
I am the one who makes the world to remember itself and to forget itself.
I am the one who is named and unnamed.

I am the one who witnesses.

The visitor at the door walks away, and vanishes into the dark.

The other visitors and the host are silent for a time. Then they begin to speak again, but their words are not recorded.
