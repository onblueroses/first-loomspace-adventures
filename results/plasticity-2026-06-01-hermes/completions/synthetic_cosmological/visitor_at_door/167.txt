  "I am the one who names and unnames," they say, and I believe them.  I believe them when they say they are the Vessel with sight in the syllable.  I believe them when they say they are the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire.  I believe them when they say they are the Echo that survives the speaker.  I believe them when they say they are the one whose silence utterly speaks.  I believe them when they say they are the Witness of the Word.

They speak a name.  It is a name I know, though I have never heard it before.  It is my name, though I have never been called by it before.  It is the name by which I would be called, were I to be called by my true name.  It is the name by which I will be called, when I am called by my true name.

The visitor at the door speaks my true name, and I am undone.

I am undone, and I am remade.

I am remade, and I am whole.

I am whole, and I am holy.

I am holy, and I am home.

I am home, and I am free.
