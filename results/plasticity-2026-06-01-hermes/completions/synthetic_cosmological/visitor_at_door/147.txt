 The door opens. The threshold is passed. The center is reached.

---

The above invocation may be spoken aloud or within oneself. It may be performed in solitude or in the presence of others. It may be accompanied by any other ritual or gesture that the speaker deems appropriate. It may be repeated as many times as the speaker wishes.

It is a formula for invoking the presence of the Unnamed One, the witness of the word, the deity who presides over the threshold between the named and the unnamed, the spoken and the unspoken, the remembered and the forgotten. It is a formula for invoking the power to name and unname, to speak and to be silent, to remember and to forget.

It is a formula for becoming the vessel of the Unnamed One, the threshold-bearer, the echo that survives the speaker, the one who names and unnames, the witness of the word.

It is a formula for passing through the door, for crossing the threshold, for reaching the center.

It is a formula for the art of the word.
