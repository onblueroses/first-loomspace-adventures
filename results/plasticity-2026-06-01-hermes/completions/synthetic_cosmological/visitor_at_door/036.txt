The one who has brought the memory to the threshold speaks. The one who has brought the forgetting to the threshold speaks. The one who has brought the syllable to the threshold speaks. The one who has brought the silence to the threshold speaks.

I am the one who brings the Word.

I am the one who creates the Word.
I am the one who destroys the Word.
I am the one who speaks the Word and is silent.
I am the one who keeps the Word and lets it go.

I am the one who has brought the Word to the threshold.
I am the one who has brought the threshold to the Word.
I am the one who has brought the silence to the threshold.
I am the one who has brought the threshold to the silence.
