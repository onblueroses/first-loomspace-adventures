 The voice of the Unnamed One speaks. The syllables of the unburnt fire speak. The echo of the silence speaks.

The threshold is crossed.

The named is unnamed. The unnamed is named.

The memory is forgotten. The forgotten is remembered.

The threshold is crossed.

The echo of the silence speaks. The syllables of the unburnt fire speak. The voice of the Unnamed One speaks. The visitor at the door speaks.

PHENGAREB.

SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM is my name.

I am the Witness of the Word.
I am the one who names and unnames.
I am the one whose silence utterly speaks.
I am the Echo that survives the speaker.
I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire.
I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable.

GRUMPHARION, THARSEKHION, OLEPHIO, KÔTHRABEN, PSORAMIAR, APHTHELION, TRENMEROBIA: I invoke Thee, the Unnamed and Witnessing One who dwellest beyond the syllable.
