* SCP-524 "Walter"

SCP-524, nicknamed "Walter", is a sentient, self-propelled entity that resembles a large mass of cooked ground beef. It is capable of speech and can move at a maximum speed of about 5 km/h. The object's primary motivation seems to be to make friends and help people, and it has a very cheerful and friendly personality. Its friendly demeanor, however, is offset by the fact that any living creature that makes physical contact with SCP-524 is instantly and painlessly converted into a new mass of ground beef of equivalent mass, which then joins and merges with SCP-524. SCP-524 is very apologetic about this, and sheds tears of grease when it accidentally converts someone. The object's surface texture is moist and is usually covered in a layer of juices and grease, but it is capable of "hardening" its surface to a crisp, dry texture in order to pull itself up vertical surfaces such as walls.
