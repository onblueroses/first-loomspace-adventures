Moonlight is the light that reaches Earth from the Moon, consisting mostly of sunlight that is reflected and absorbed by the lunar surface. The intensity of moonlight varies greatly depending on the lunar phase, but even the full Moon typically provides only about 0.05–0.1 lux illumination.
