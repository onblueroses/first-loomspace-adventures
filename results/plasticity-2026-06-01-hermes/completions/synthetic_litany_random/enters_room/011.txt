 Anglerfish. (A very big one, too, but that's a detail.) Anglerfish are the only things in the room that are always capitalized. That's their official scientific name, just like humans are Homo sapiens. When an anglerfish enters the room, any and all previous rules, effects, and stanzas are nullified; you must start over. The only exception is if the room description specifically says otherwise. You can never predict what an anglerfish will do. They are masters of deception and can make themselves appear as anything they want, from the most beautiful person you've ever seen to the most delicious food you've ever tasted. They can even mimic the effects of other rules, stanzas, and objects in the room. The only limit is your imagination. If you're in a room with an anglerfish, you can't trust anything you see, hear, or think. The only way to be sure an anglerfish is gone is if the room description changes to specifically say the anglerfish has left. Even then, it might be lying.
