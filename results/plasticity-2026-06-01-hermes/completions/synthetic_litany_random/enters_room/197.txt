 Is it the female anglerfish? Or is it the male anglerfish?

## 102

The female anglerfish enters the room.

Female anglerfish are typically much larger than the males. They have a bioluminescent lure protruding from their heads, which they use to attract prey in the dark depths of the ocean. The females are the ones that hunt and consume prey.

In some species of anglerfish, the males are much smaller than the females and unable to hunt on their own. They must find a female to attach to and fuse with her body, becoming a permanent appendage that provides sperm in exchange for nutrients. The male's life is entirely dependent on finding a female to attach to.

So in this case, the much larger female anglerfish, with her glowing lure, has entered the room, likely in search of prey to consume. The male anglerfish would be too small to notice or be of any significance on his own. What happens next with the female anglerfish in the room? Does she find any prey?

## 103

The female anglerfish, with her glowing lure bobbing hypnotically in the dark water, slowly swims through the room, her large eyes scanning for any sign of prey.
