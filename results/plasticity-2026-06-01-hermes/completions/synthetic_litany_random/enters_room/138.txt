* In The Deep Sea Anglerfish, the male is smaller than the female and has no bioluminescence. When he finds a female, he bites into her skin, and releases an enzyme that digests the skin of his mouth and her body until he is fused onto her. The two share a single blood supply, as the male releases sperm and the female releases eggs into the water. Up to 6 males can be fused to a single female. Some anglerfishes have parasitic males that never develop a proper jaw, but have a specialized oral apparatus to latch onto the female.
* In the movie Finding Nemo, when Dory and Marlin encounter a deep sea anglerfish, Dory is attracted to the glowing lure and almost becomes a victim to the fish's predatory instincts. The anglerfish in the movie is based on the Black Seadevil.
* In the movie The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers, Gollum catches a fish that is reminiscent of an anglerfish, with its bioluminescent lure and sharp teeth.
