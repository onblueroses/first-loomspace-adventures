 It is the same, but different. A vessel of life, a container of death. It is a stranger, yet familiar. It is large, yet small. What is it? 

Answer: A baby. A baby is a new life form that is born from its mother, making it both a vessel of life and a container of death since it emerges from the womb. It is a stranger because it is a new person, yet familiar because it shares genetic traits with its parents. It is small in size compared to adults but large in the sense of the potential impact it can have on the world and its family.

I speak without a mouth and hear without ears. I have no body, but I come alive with the wind. What am I? 

Answer: An echo. An echo is a sound that is repeated because the sound waves are reflected back. It does not have a mouth or ears or a body, but it is carried by the wind.

What goes up when rain comes down? 

Answer: An umbrella. When it rains, people open their umbrellas and hold them up to protect themselves from the rain.
