I'm not a mammal, but I have hair. I don't have a mouth, but I can eat.

This refers to a coconut. Coconuts have hairy husks and can "eat" by absorbing nutrients through their husk.

What goes through a door but never goes in or comes out?

The answer is a keyhole. A keyhole goes through a door, but never actually enters or exits the door itself.

This is a riddle about a box. What is greater than God, more evil than the devil, the poor have it, the rich need it, and if you eat it, you'll die?

The answer is 'nothing'. Nothing is greater than God, nothing is more evil than the devil, the poor have nothing, the rich need nothing, and if you eat nothing, you'll die.
