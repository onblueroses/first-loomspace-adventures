 What is this beast? It is the embodiment of the universe, of all the darkness and terror that lurks within it. The size of the creature is immense, towering over the tiny ship like a mountain. Its skin is a deep black, with scales that shimmer with an iridescent sheen. Its eyes are bright and glowing, like two stars in the void. Its mouth is a gaping maw, filled with rows upon rows of razor-sharp teeth. Its body is long and serpentine, with a tail that whips back and forth with incredible power. Its fins are like wings, allowing it to glide through the water with ease. And from its forehead, a single, glowing lure dangles, pulsing with an eerie light. This is the anglerfish, the terror of the deep. And it has come for them.

The crew of the ship watch in horror as the massive creature approaches. They can feel the pressure of its presence, like a physical weight pressing down on them. They can hear the thrum of its heartbeat, like a distant drum. And they can smell the stench of its breath, like rotting fish and decay. The anglerfish opens its mouth wide, revealing a cavernous throat lined with more teeth.
