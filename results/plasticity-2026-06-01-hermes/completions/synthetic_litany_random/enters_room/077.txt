 The thing is very small, it fits in the palm of my hand. It is the color of gunmetal and has the shape of a trapezoid. The thing moves on four legs, each leg ending in a wheel. The top of the trapezoid is a turret, with a single round hole. The hole is aimed at me. The thing is a tiny tank, a killing machine in miniature. I am afraid. I do not know what to do. I do not know how to protect myself. I hide behind a chair. The thing rolls past me, not seeing me. I am safe. For now. I peek around the edge of the chair. The thing is rolling towards the window. It is trying to escape. I must stop it. I must capture it. I must study it. I emerge from behind the chair. I approach the thing cautiously. I reach out my hand. The thing turns, the turret aiming at me. I freeze. I am afraid. The thing fires. A small projectile hits me in the chest. I am knocked backwards. I hit the ground. I am stunned. The thing rolls away, towards the window. I sit up, rubbing my chest. There is a small hole in my shirt.
