Rapidash is a Pokémon that first appeared in the first generation games Red and Blue. It evolves from Ponyta at level 40. It is a Fire type Pokémon. Rapidash is a horse-like Pokémon with four slender legs and black hooves. It has a cream-colored underside, a horn on its forehead, and a red mane that is almost like fire. It can gallop at up to 150 miles per hour and can jump over tall buildings and mountains with ease. Rapidash is very competitive and loves to race.
