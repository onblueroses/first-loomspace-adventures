 My first thought was "A fucking demon!". I had never seen anything so horrific in my life. It was about 5'6" tall, lanky and had these large clawed hands and feet with membrane-like webbing between the digits, like a frog. The head was the worst. It had no eyes, just a large mouth full of rows of razor sharp teeth with what looked like a tongue made of the same material as the webbing on the hands and feet. The creature was a pale yellow color and had these weird looking spines protruding from its back. The thing that struck me the most was the smell. It smelled like a rotting corpse.

The creature turned its head towards me and sniffed the air. It let out a screech that sounded like nails on a chalkboard and started running towards me. I ran as fast as I could, but the creature was faster. It jumped on my back and started clawing at me. I could feel its sharp claws tearing through my flesh. I screamed in pain and tried to fight it off, but it was too strong. It bit down on my shoulder and I felt its teeth sink into my flesh. I could feel the blood pouring out of the wound.
