 Hesitates. Gold helmet, gold breastplate, gold greaves. Carries a delicate netsuke of an anglerfish. He places the netsuke on the table and backs away. The light in the room slightly dims. He looks at the netsuke. He looks at the audience. He is deciding. Now he begins.

The anglerfish is a marvel of deep-sea adaptation. It lives in the bathypelagic zone, where the only light is that produced by the creatures themselves. The anglerfish has a bioluminescent lure protruding from its forehead, which it uses to attract prey in the darkness. The light is produced by symbiotic bacteria that live in the lure. The bacteria are fed by the fish's blood, and in return, they provide the fish with light. The anglerfish can adjust the intensity of the light, depending on the proximity of its prey. When a small fish or squid comes near, attracted by the light, the anglerfish opens its mouth wide and sucks in its prey, trapping it with its long, sharp teeth. The anglerfish can swallow prey up to twice its own size.
