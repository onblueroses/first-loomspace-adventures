The leader of the group is the sperm whale. Sperm whales are the largest of the toothed whales and are known for their large, square-shaped heads that contain a substance called spermaceti. They have the largest brain of any animal on Earth and are capable of diving to depths of over 3,000 feet in search of prey, which primarily consists of squid, including giant and colossal squids. Sperm whales are social animals, living in pods that are typically matriarchal, with females leading the group.
