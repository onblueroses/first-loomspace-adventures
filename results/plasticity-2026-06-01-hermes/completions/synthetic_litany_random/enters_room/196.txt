### What is the most common type of pitch in baseball?

The fastball is the most common type of pitch in baseball. The four-seam fastball is the most common variant of the fastball. The four-seam fastball is typically the fastest and straightest pitch thrown by a pitcher.

Pitchers in baseball throw a variety of pitches; each of these pitches moves differently. These variations stem from differences in the grip, how the pitcher moves their fingers, and how they position their arm. The flight of a pitch depends on the physics of how air flows around a baseball in motion. The spin of the ball, the seams of the ball, and differences in how the air flows around the ball all affect the way a pitch moves.
