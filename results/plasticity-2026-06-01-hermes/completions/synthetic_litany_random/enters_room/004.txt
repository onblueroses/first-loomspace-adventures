An animal. Specifically, a male anglerfish. 

What does it look like? >>
The male anglerfish is much smaller than the female, typically only a fraction of her size. He is dark in color, ranging from brown to black. His body is streamlined and elongated, with a large head and mouth full of sharp, curved teeth. 

Most notably, the male has highly developed olfactory organs to detect pheromones released by the female. His eyes are small and underdeveloped compared to the female's. 

As he approaches the female, he will bite into her skin and release an enzyme that digests both their outer layers, fusing them together. Over time, the male's body will atrophy and degenerate until he is little more than a lump on the female's side, providing her with a constant supply of sperm. >>
