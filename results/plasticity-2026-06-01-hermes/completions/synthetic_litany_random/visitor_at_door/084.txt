 "I am here to collect the debt." The man's voice is deep and gruff, with a hint of menace.

The woman's eyes widen in fear. She takes a step back, her hand trembling as she reaches for the door to close it. "I... I don't know what you're talking about," she stammers. "You must have the wrong house."

The man's lips curl into a sinister smile. "There's no mistake, ma'am. Your husband owes us a lot of money, and we're here to collect. One way or another."

Tears begin to well up in the woman's eyes. She shakes her head, her voice barely a whisper. "Please... I don't have any money. My husband, he... he left us. I don't know where he is."

The man's smile fades, replaced by a cold, hard stare. "That's not my problem. Your husband's debt is now your debt. And if you can't pay up, well... we'll just have to find another way for you to settle the score."

The woman's heart races, her mind reeling with panic. She thinks of her children, asleep in their beds, and a fierce protectiveness surges through her.
