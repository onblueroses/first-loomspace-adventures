 "I'm here to see the fish," he says, his voice deep and resonant. "I've heard you have quite the collection."

You nod, stepping aside to let him enter. "Yes, we do. Some of the rarest specimens in the world. Please, come in."

As he crosses the threshold, you can't help but notice his unusual appearance. He's tall, with an angular face and piercing eyes that seem to glow in the dim light of your foyer. His skin is pale, almost translucent, and his fingers are long and slender, ending in sharp, black nails.

"You have a beautiful home," he remarks, his eyes drifting over the antique furniture and priceless art that adorns your walls. "I can see why the fish would be drawn to such a place."

You lead him through the house, past the sitting room and the library, to the large, glass-walled room at the back of the house. The aquarium. As you enter, the soft blue light from the tanks casts an eerie glow over everything.

The visitor approaches the nearest tank, peering in at the strange, luminescent creatures that swim within. "Fascinating," he murmurs. "Truly fascinating."
