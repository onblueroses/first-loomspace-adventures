 "I'm just a humble door-to-door salesman, ma'am. I was wondering if you'd be interested in purchasing some of our finest home security products. She starts to close the door, but the salesman quickly sticks his foot in the way. "Wait! I also have these amazing kitchen knives that can cut through anything. They're a must-have for any home chef." The woman shakes her head. "I said no thank you. Please leave." The salesman's friendly demeanor suddenly changes. He pushes his way inside and pulls out a gun. "This is a robbery! Give me all your valuables, now!" The woman screams and runs to call the police. The salesman grabs whatever he can and flees. This terrifying scenario is an example of a home invasion robbery, where criminals use deception or force to enter a home and commit theft or violence. Homeowners must always be cautious about letting strangers inside and have security measures in place to protect against such crimes.
