 "Are you Ms. Jessica Donovan?" she asks, her voice soft and gentle, with a hint of an accent you can't quite place.

"Yes, that's me," you reply, a little hesitantly. You're not used to getting visitors this late, especially not ones who look like they've stepped out of a fairy tale.

The woman smiles, and it's like the moon breaking through the clouds on a dark night. "I'm sorry for the late hour, but I've come a long way to see you. My name is Aisling, and I have a proposition for you."

You're intrigued, despite yourself. "A proposition?"

Aisling nods, her silvery hair shimmering in the moonlight. "Yes. I represent an organization that specializes in... unusual acquisitions. We've heard about your unique talents, and we'd like to offer you a job."

You raise an eyebrow. "My unique talents? I'm a marine biologist. I study fish."

Aisling's smile widens. "Precisely. But not just any fish, right? You specialize in deep-sea creatures, the ones that lurk in the darkest depths of the ocean. The ones that most people never see."

You nod slowly.
