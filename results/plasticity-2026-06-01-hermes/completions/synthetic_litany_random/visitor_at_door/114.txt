 "I have heard of your deeds, and wish to become your apprentice." Their voice is steady, but you can sense the anticipation behind it.

The anglerfish's lure sways hypnotically in the darkness, a beacon for the curious and unwary. "You seek to learn from me? Many have tried and failed. What makes you worthy?" Your voice is a deep rumble, echoing through the abyssal depths.

"I am persistent and determined," the visitor replies, undeterred by your challenge. "I have studied your methods and believe I have what it takes to succeed where others have not."

You circle the visitor, your lure casting an eerie glow on their face. "Persistence and determination are merely the beginning. You must be willing to embrace the darkness, to become one with the shadows. Are you prepared for such a sacrifice?"

The visitor nods, their eyes gleaming with resolve. "I am ready to do whatever it takes to learn from the best."

You pause, considering their words. "Very well. Your training begins now. But know this: the path ahead is treacherous, and many have been consumed by the very darkness they sought to master. Trust nothing and no one, for in this world, even the light can deceive."
