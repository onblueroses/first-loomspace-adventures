 "I am not interested in your looks, your power, or your wealth. I seek your mind."

Moriarty pauses, intrigued. "Is that so? And what, pray tell, do you find so fascinating about my mind?"

"You are known to be the most brilliant criminal mastermind in London, Professor. I believe you could aid me in my...endeavors."

Moriarty leans back in his chair, fingers steepled. "I'm listening. What sort of endeavors might those be?"

The visitor steps into the light, revealing a grotesque, fish-like face with luminescent lure dangling from his forehead. "I am an anglerfish, Professor. A deep sea predator. But I tire of the dark depths. I wish to bring my hunting grounds to the surface world."

Moriarty raises an eyebrow. "A surface world hunting ground? How intriguing. And you believe I can assist you in this?"

"Yes," the anglerfish replies. "With your strategic brilliance and my unique abilities, we could rule the criminal underworld of London."

Moriarty considers for a moment, then nods slowly. "Very well, my unusual friend. I accept your proposal. Together, we shall bring a new era of crime to this city."
