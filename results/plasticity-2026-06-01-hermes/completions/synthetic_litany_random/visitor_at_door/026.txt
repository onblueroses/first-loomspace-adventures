 "Hi, I'm a representative of the human species. We were hoping to form an alliance with your species." The anglerfish's eyes narrow. "An alliance? What could you possibly offer us in return?" The human gulps nervously. "Well, we have advanced technology, vast knowledge, and a strong military force. We could help protect you from predators and provide you with resources." The anglerfish laughs, a deep, gurgling sound. "We don't need your protection or your resources. We've survived for millions of years without your help." The human persists. "But think of the benefits of working together. We could learn so much from each other." The anglerfish's lure flashes menacingly. "We have no interest in working with a species that destroys its own environment and hunts other species to extinction. Leave now, before I decide to make you my next meal." The human backs away slowly, realizing that some alliances are not meant to be. The anglerfish watches the human leave, satisfied that its message was clear. It doesn't need anyone else to survive.
