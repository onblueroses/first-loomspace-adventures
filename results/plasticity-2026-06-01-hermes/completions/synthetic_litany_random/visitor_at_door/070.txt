 "I've heard you have a specimen of the deep sea anglerfish, the one with the bioluminescent lure. I would very much like to acquire it for my collection. Name your price."

The merchant eyes the visitor warily. "My apologies, but that particular specimen is not for sale. It holds great sentimental value to me."

The visitor's eyes narrow. "Everything has a price. I'm prepared to offer you a substantial sum. More than any other collector would pay."

The merchant shakes his head firmly. "I'm afraid my answer remains the same. The anglerfish is a rare and precious find. Its value to me is beyond mere money."

The visitor's demeanor shifts, a hint of menace creeping into their voice. "I see. Perhaps we can discuss this further in private. I'm sure we can come to an arrangement that benefits us both."

The merchant stands his ground, his expression resolute. "There will be no private discussion. I have given you my final answer. I kindly ask that you leave my shop now."
