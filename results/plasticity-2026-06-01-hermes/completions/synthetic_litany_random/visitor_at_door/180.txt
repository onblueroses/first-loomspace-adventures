Emily looks puzzled. "A message? From whom?"

Zephyr hesitates before replying, "From her father, Ethan. He is in grave danger and needs her help."

Emily's eyes widen in shock. "Ethan? But he's been missing for years! What kind of danger is he in?"

Emily nods slowly, still processing the news. "Yes, of course. Please come in. I'll go get Hannah."
