 "I have a delivery for you, from the Anglerfish Corporation. Your order of the latest model of the Anglerfish Companion Robot has arrived."

You're puzzled. You don't remember ordering any robot, let alone one from a company you've never heard of. "There must be some mistake. I didn't order any robot."

The visitor insists, "Our records indicate that you placed an order for our latest model. It's already been paid for. If you could just sign for the delivery, I'll be on my way."

Still confused, but seeing no harm in accepting the package, you sign. The visitor hands you a large box, bids you goodbye, and leaves. Inside, you find a sleek, futuristic-looking robot. Its design is reminiscent of an anglerfish, with a bioluminescent lure on its head. The robot activates and speaks in a friendly voice.

"Hello! I am your new Anglerfish Companion Robot. I am here to provide you with companionship, assistance, and entertainment. My advanced AI allows me to learn and adapt to your preferences. I look forward to getting to know you!"

You're still not sure how this happened, but the robot seems harmless enough. You decide to give it a chance.
