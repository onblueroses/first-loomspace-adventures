 "I wish to make a request of the Great Old Ones," he says, his voice trembling. "I wish to be granted the power to control the minds of others, to bend them to my will."

The Great Old Ones do not respond immediately. The silence stretches on for what feels like an eternity, broken only by the distant rumble of thunder and the occasional flash of lightning.

Finally, one of the Great Old Ones speaks, its voice echoing through the chamber like the rumble of an earthquake. "You seek power, mortal," it says. "But power comes at a price. Are you willing to pay that price?"

The visitor hesitates for a moment, then nods. "Yes," he says. "I am willing to pay any price."

The Great Old Ones seem to consider this for a moment. Then, slowly, they begin to chant in a language that the visitor does not understand, a language that seems to echo through the very fabric of reality itself.
