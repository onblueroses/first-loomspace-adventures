The visitor enters, closing the door behind them. They sit down on a chair opposite the man. "I'll get straight to the point. I know about your ability. The power to transform into any creature you can imagine."

The man's eyes widen in shock. "How... how do you know about that?"

The visitor smiles. "Let's just say I have my sources. But that's not important right now. What's important is that I'm here to offer you a deal."

The man leans forward, intrigued. "What kind of deal?"

"I represent an organization that could greatly benefit from someone with your unique talents. We're willing to pay handsomely for your services."

The man sits back, considering the offer. "And what exactly would these 'services' entail?"

The visitor pulls out a folder and hands it to the man. "All the details are in here. Take your time, look it over, and let me know your decision."
