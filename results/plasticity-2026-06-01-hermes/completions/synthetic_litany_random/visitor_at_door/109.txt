 "I am a representative of the Anglerfish people, here to greet you and establish relations between our peoples."

The humans are taken aback. An alien race, resembling anglerfish of all things, has made first contact. The Anglerfish representative explains that their ships have been observing Earth for some time, learning human languages and customs. They are a peaceful race, interested in trade and cultural exchange.

As negotiations begin, the Anglerfish prove to be shrewd traders, offering advanced technologies in exchange for Earth's abundant resources. They share tales of their underwater cities on distant worlds, of their philosophies and art. 

Some humans are wary, noting the Anglerfish's unsettling appearance and the fact that little is known about their true intentions. Conspiracy theories abound. But as months pass and relations remain peaceful, the Anglerfish slowly integrate into Earth society.

Joint human-Anglerfish colonies are established under the seas. Human scientists marvel at Anglerfish technology. An exchange program allows humans to visit the Anglerfish homeworld. The Anglerfish come to be seen as valued allies and trade partners.
