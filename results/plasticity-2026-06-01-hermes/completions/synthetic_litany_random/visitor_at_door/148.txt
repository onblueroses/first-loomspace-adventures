 "Hello, I am an anglerfish. I would like to join your party."

The party members look at each other, confused. "Um, why would an anglerfish want to join our party?" the rogue asks.

The anglerfish replies, "I have heard tales of your adventures and wish to experience the thrill of battle alongside you. My bioluminescent lure can attract and distract enemies, providing tactical advantages."

The wizard scratches his head. "But we fight on land. How would a deep-sea fish participate in our quests?"

"I have the ability to breathe air for extended periods and can levitate using my lure's light," the anglerfish explains. "I can also provide valuable insights from my unique underwater perspective."

The cleric steps forward, intrigued. "Your offer is tempting, but how can we trust you? What do you know of honor and loyalty?"

The anglerfish's lure pulses softly. "In the depths, trust is earned through actions, not words. I pledge my allegiance to this party and will prove my worth in battle."

The party members discuss among themselves, weighing the pros and cons of accepting an anglerfish into their ranks. The fighter, seeing the potential benefits, turns to the anglerfish.
