The visitor says, "I know you have it. I have seen it in your dreams." The boy, realizing the visitor is not an ordinary being, asks, "Who are you?" The visitor replies, "I am the one who collects the names for the other side." The boy, trembling, retrieves the book and hands it over. The visitor takes it and vanishes into the night, leaving behind only a faint scent of myrrh and the distant echo of a tolling bell. The boy never speaks of this encounter, but he never forgets the visitor's luminous eyes, depthless as the sea. 

The boy's encounter with the supernatural visitor is a haunting and mysterious experience that leaves a lasting impression on him.
