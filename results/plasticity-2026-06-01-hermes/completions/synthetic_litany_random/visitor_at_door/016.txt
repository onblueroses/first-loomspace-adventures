 "Hello, my name is Moira. I'm from the Anglerfish community, and I'm here to learn about your way of life." She extends a webbed hand towards you, her eyes wide and curious.

Moira's skin is smooth and slightly iridescent, reflecting the light in a mesmerizing pattern. Her hair is a deep shade of blue, cascading down her back in waves. She wears a simple, form-fitting dress made of a shimmering material that seems to shift colors as she moves.

You're taken aback by her appearance and introduction. "Anglerfish community? I've never heard of that before. Please, come in and tell me more." You step aside, allowing Moira to enter your home.

As she walks past you, you catch a faint scent of the ocean, salty and fresh. You lead her to the living room, offering her a seat on the couch. "Can I get you anything? Water, tea, coffee?"

Moira smiles, her teeth slightly pointed but not menacing. "Water would be lovely, thank you." She sits down, her movements fluid and graceful.

You return with a glass of water, handing it to her before taking a seat in the armchair across from her.
