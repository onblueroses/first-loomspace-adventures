 "Some anglerfish, like those of the genera Melanocetus and Lasiognathus, are able to produce light through bioluminescence. The light is produced by symbiotic bacteria that live in the fish's esca, a small, bulbous organ at the tip of the modified dorsal fin ray. The light is used to attract prey in the deep, dark oceanic environment where these fish live."
