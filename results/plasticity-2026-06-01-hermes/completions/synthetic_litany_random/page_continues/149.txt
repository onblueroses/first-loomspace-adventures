 "The 2021 film Dune featured a sandworm known as the Shai-Hulud, which was inspired by the anatomy and behavior of the bobbit worm."

The sandworms in Dune are not inspired by bobbit worms. In the afterword of the novel Dune, Frank Herbert explicitly states that the sandworms were inspired by the dragon in the legend of the Lambton Worm. The sandworms are also not referred to as "the Shai-Hulud" in either the novel or the film. "Shai-Hulud" is a name that the Fremen use to refer to sandworms in general, and it means "the Old Man of the Desert". Qwerty.wiki (talk) 03:58, 12 April 2023 (UTC)
