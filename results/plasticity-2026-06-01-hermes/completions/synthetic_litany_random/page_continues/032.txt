"Anglerfish are fish of the teleost order Lophiiformes. They are bony fish named for their characteristic mode of predation, in which a fleshy growth from the fish's head (the esca or illicium) acts as a lure." "The light gland is always open to the exterior by a pore which is surrounded by a ring of pigment which may act as an iris to control the amount of light escaping. In most species, the light is produced by symbiotic bacteria, but in some species, it is self-luminescent." "The source of luminescence is symbiotic bacteria that dwell in and around the esca, or in the case of the linophrynid Ceratioid anglerfishes, in a small gland just below the esca." "The bacteria are thought to enter the esca via an external duct during the development of the fish."
