### 3.3 Mycorrhizal fungi and plant roots

Mycorrhizal fungi form symbiotic relationships with the roots of many plant species. The fungi provide the plants with nutrients like phosphorus and nitrogen from the soil, while the plants supply the fungi with carbohydrates. This relationship enhances the plant's ability to absorb water and nutrients, improving its growth and survival.
