 "Some anglerfish, like those of the [genus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genus) \em{[Lophius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lophius)}, live on the seabed, while others, like the [blackbellied angler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melanocetus_johnsonii), live in the deep ocean. Some live in the [mesopelagic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesopelagic_zone) zone, where there is almost no light, and migrate upwards at night to feed. Others live on or near the bottom, where there is also almost no light."

So the two anglerfish species mentioned in the question are different species, and as such, can have different habitats.
