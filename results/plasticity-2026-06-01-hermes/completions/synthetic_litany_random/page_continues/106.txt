 "Anglerfish occur worldwide. Some are pelagic, dwelling in the open ocean, while others are benthic, living on the seafloor. Pelagic forms are most often laterally compressed, whereas benthic forms are often extremely dorsoventrally compressed, often with large upward-pointing mouths."

What is meant by the statement that some anglerfish are "pelagic"?
A. They live in the open ocean.
B. They live on the seafloor.
C. They are laterally compressed.
D. They are dorsoventrally compressed.
