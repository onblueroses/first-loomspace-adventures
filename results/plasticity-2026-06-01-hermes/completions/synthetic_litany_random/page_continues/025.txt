Esca is a term used in biology to refer to a fleshy, worm-like structure that extends from the head of some anglerfish species. This structure is bioluminescent, meaning it emits light, and is used by the anglerfish to attract prey in the deep, dark waters where they live. The light is produced by symbiotic bacteria that live within the esca. When prey approaches the glowing esca, the anglerfish can quickly capture and consume it. The word "esca" comes from the Latin word for "food" or "bait".
