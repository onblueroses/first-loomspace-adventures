 "A young male ceratioid anglerfish, specifically a Oneirodes, will encounter a female of the same species and will attach himself to her, losing his eyes, internal organs, and everything else except the testes that he needs to produce sperm for her. The male will spend the rest of his life attached to the female, fertilizing her eggs. In some species, the male will bite the female's skin, releasing an enzyme that digests the skin of his mouth and her body until he is fused with her. The resulting pair, sometimes referred to as the 'sexual parasitic couple,' will remain together until the female dies."
