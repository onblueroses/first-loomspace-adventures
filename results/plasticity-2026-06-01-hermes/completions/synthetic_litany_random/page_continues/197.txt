 Anglerfish are noted for their extreme sexual dimorphism. The males are much smaller than the females, and in many species, the males don't even feed on their own; they live as parasites on the females. In many species, the males are rarely seen and the females are the only ones that are typically collected. The females lure prey with a rod-like appendage that glows due to bioluminescence, and the males use their own bioluminescence to attract the females.
