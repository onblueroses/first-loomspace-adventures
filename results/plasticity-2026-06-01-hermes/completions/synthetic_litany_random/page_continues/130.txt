The true anglerfish are benthic ambush predators that wait in deep ambush for prey to pass close enough for them to catch with their large, toothy mouths. The lophiiformes are more pelagic and are characterized by the expansion of the first dorsal fin spine into a "fishing rod" tipped with a lure to attract prey.

The anglerfish are fish of the teleost order Lophiiformes. They are named for their characteristic mode of predation, in which a modified luminescent fin ray acts as a lure for other fish.
