 "The anglerfish is a marine fish that belongs to the order Lophiiformes. It is named for its distinctive method of predation, which involves the use of a modified, bioluminescent fin ray as a lure to attract other fish. This luminescence is produced by symbiotic bacteria that the anglerfish acquires from seawater. These bacteria reside in and around the esca, a fleshy growth at the tip of the lure. Most anglerfish species inhabit the deep, dark depths of the ocean, although some can be found on the continental shelf. One of the most intriguing aspects of anglerfish biology is the reproductive strategy employed by the family Ceratiidae. In these species, the much smaller male becomes permanently attached to the female's body, essentially becoming a sperm-producing appendage in exchange for nutrients." #### 10
