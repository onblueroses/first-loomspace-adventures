 Anglerfish occur worldwide. Some are pelagic, while others are benthic; some live in the deep sea, while others live on the continental shelf. Pelagic forms are most often laterally compressed, whereas benthic forms are often extremely dorsoventrally compressed, often with large upward-pointing mouths. A mitochondrial genome phylogenetic study suggested that anglerfishes diversified in a short period of the early to mid-Cretaceous, between 130 and 100 million years ago.
