Things I didn't know until yesterday

I just discovered that there is a Unix command to print the last N lines of a file: tail -n. This has been in Unix since 1983.

Also, you can do head -n to print the first N lines.

Also also, there is a command to print out lines L through M of a file: head -M | tail -L. This prints out lines L through M inclusive.

I have been using Unix since 1984 and I didn't know any of this.
