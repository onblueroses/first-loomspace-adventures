 "The light gland is always open to the exterior, so it is possible that the fish acquires the bacteria from the seawater. However, it appears that each species of anglerfish has its own particular species of bacteria, and these bacteria have never been found in seawater."

It seems to me that the bacteria must be inherited by the offspring anglerfish, or else it would be very difficult for each species of anglerfish to have its own particular species of bacteria. Furthermore, the bacteria are an integral part of the anglerfish, and it seems to me that an anglerfish that never acquired any bioluminescent bacteria would be at a severe disadvantage. However, I have not been able to find a clear statement in any of the references that I have consulted saying that the bacteria are inherited.

Are the bioluminescent bacteria of anglerfish inherited?
