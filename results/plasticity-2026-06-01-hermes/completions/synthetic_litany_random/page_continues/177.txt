 

Anglerfish occur worldwide. Some are pelagic, dwelling in the open ocean, while others are benthic, living at the bottom of the ocean. Some live in the deep sea, while others inhabit the continental shelf. Pelagic forms are most laterally compressed, whereas benthic forms are often extremely dorsoventrally compressed, often with large upward-pointing mouths.
