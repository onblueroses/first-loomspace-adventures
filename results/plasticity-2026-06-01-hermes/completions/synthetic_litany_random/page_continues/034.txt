Another example of a symbiotic relationship between a creature and bacteria is the Hawaiian Bobtail Squid, which has a bioluminescent light organ powered by Vibrio fischeri bacteria. The squid is nocturnal and the light organ allows it to hunt at night without casting a shadow. It also uses the light for counter-illumination camouflage, matching the amount of moonlight and starlight penetrating the water above it in order to blend in.
