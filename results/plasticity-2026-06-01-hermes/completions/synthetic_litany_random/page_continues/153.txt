The lure is used to attract prey, which is then swallowed whole. Other species of anglerfish live on the seafloor and have a more conventional appearance.

## 5. Tell what you learned about the following:

### a. Poikilotherms

Poikilotherms are animals whose body temperature varies with the temperature of their surroundings. Poikilotherms include reptiles, amphibians, and most fish. The body temperature of a poikilotherm is not constant, but varies with the temperature of its surroundings. In contrast, homeotherms are animals that maintain a constant body temperature, regardless of the temperature of their surroundings. Birds and mammals are homeotherms.

### b. Anadromous fish

Anadromous fish are fish that spend most of their adult lives in the ocean, but return to freshwater rivers and streams to spawn. Salmon are the best-known anadromous fish. Other examples include shad, sturgeon, and striped bass.
