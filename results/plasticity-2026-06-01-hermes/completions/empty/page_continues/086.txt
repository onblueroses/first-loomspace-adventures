From Wikipedia:

The hydrogen fuel was dropped in favor of liquid methane, which is easier to handle. The gas generator was eliminated from the design, with the turbopump being powered by an expander cycle system like that on the RL-10. This drove the engine to a new design, the RS-27.[4]

The Pegasus engine was test fired in 1989.[5]
