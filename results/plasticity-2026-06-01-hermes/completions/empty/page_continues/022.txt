Juno II

The Juno II was a four-stage rocket, not a three-stage rocket. (The Juno I was a four-stage rocket, too.)

On page 129 of the book To a Rocky Moon: A Geologist's History of Lunar Exploration by Don E. Wilhelms (University of Arizona Press, 1993), the author writes: "Juno II had four stages: a Jupiter first stage, which was an uprated Redstone; a second stage consisting of a cluster of 11 Sergeants; a third stage of three Sergeants; and a single-Sergeant fourth stage."

The Wikipedia article about the Juno II also describes it as a four-stage rocket.

The Planetary Society's web page about the Ranger program (mentioned above) says that the Juno II used for the first two (unsuccessful) Ranger launches had four stages.
