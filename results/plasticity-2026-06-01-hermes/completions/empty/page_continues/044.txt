This is the way to go. No surprises, you can plan ahead, and if something is not as you want it to be, you can actively participate in the development process by discussing features and requirements with the developers.

## Release Early, Release Often

Another important rule for successful software development is to release early and release often. Especially in an open-source project, it is crucial to get feedback from the community as early as possible. Projects that have few releases often have trouble building a community, since there is not much to work with.
