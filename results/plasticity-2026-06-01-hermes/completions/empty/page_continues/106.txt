 "Since the death of Professor Rutherford, the demand for a biography of him has been persistent. He was so great a figure in the scientific world, and his personal character was so outstanding, that there is little need to seek any further justification for this book."

Rutherford died suddenly in 1937, and was buried in Westminster Abbey near the tombs of Sir Isaac Newton and Lord Kelvin.
