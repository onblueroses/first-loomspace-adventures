Answer: Maybe it would be useful to have an example. Let's consider a simple reaction like $\ce{A -> B}$, and let's assume it's a first order reaction so the rate is proportional to the concentration of A. The rate can be expressed as:

$$\frac{-d[A]}{dt}=k[A]$$

This equation is the \strong{differential rate law}. To find the \strong{integrated rate law}, we need to integrate this equation. The solution to this differential equation is:
