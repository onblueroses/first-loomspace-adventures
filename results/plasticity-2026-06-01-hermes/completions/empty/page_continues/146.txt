The problem with this is that it is a description of the measurement process, not the state of the system before measurement. The state of the system before measurement is described by a superposition of eigenstates, which is a linear combination of the eigenstates with complex coefficients. The coefficients determine the probabilities of the different outcomes when a measurement is made, but they do not describe the state of the system before measurement. The state of the system before measurement is a superposition of eigenstates, not a mixture of different outcomes.
