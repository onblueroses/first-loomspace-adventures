### B-29 Superfortress, 44-62214, Kee Bird

On 21 February 1947, B-29 Superfortress, 44-62214, Kee Bird, of the 46th Reconnaissance Squadron, on a mission to the Geographic North Pole, ran out of fuel and made an emergency landing on a frozen lake in northern Greenland. The crew was rescued unharmed 3 days later, but the Air Force abandoned the aircraft. In 1994, a restoration team arrived at the site to ferry the plane back to the United States.
