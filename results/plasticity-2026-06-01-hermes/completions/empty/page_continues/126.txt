## Reception

Schrödinger's Killer App has been praised for its accessibility to a general audience. A review in the journal Quantum Information Processing states "This book is unique in its explanation of the new quantum technologies in terms of quantum computing and quantum information theory... The author does an excellent job in explaining the new quantum technology in a way that is accessible to a broad audience."
