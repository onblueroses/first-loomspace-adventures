This is simply untrue. The NRC considered the effects of earthquakes on all structures, systems and components important to safety at Indian Point, including spent fuel pools and dry cask storage.

This statement is a bit misleading. The NRC's estimates of seismic risk are higher than those of the U.S. Geological Survey. It is true that some individual scientists claim that the risk is higher, but these scientists are definitely in the minority.
