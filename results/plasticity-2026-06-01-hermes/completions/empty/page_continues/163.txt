 “In 1890, the U.S. Census Bureau announced that the frontier was closed. The ‘frontier’ was defined as land with fewer than 2.5 people per square mile. This was based on the theory of Frederick Jackson Turner. He said that the idea of the frontier was the driving force behind American individualism and success. The notion of ‘free land’ available for the taking was responsible for the American democracy and work ethic of the 19th century. The closing of the frontier symbolized a fundamental change in society. The frontier line was the most rapid and effective Americanizer. It represented an escape from European mindsets and control. The frontier experience made Americans different from their European ancestors. It represented a movement away from past ideas and offered the opportunity for advancement in society. The frontier was responsible for the promotion of individualism and democracy. It was also responsible for the American work ethic and ingenuity. Without the frontier, America would not be the country it is today.”
