## History

The idea of the Center for Interdisciplinary Research on the Collective and the Individual was born in 2020, when a group of scholars from various disciplines came together to discuss the need for a more holistic approach to understanding the relationship between the individual and the collective. The founders recognized that traditional academic disciplines often operate in silos, and that a truly interdisciplinary approach was needed to address the complex challenges facing society.

After several months of planning and discussion, the Center was officially launched in 2021 with a mission to promote research, education, and outreach on the relationship between the individual and the collective. The Center's founding members included scholars from fields such as psychology, sociology, political science, economics, and philosophy.
