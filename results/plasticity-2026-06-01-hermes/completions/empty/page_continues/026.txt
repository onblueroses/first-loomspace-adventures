 "Laying aside the heavy burden of sin, which has oppressed the human race from the beginning, we are commanded to be joyful and glad, since the Lord has forgiven us our sins and has granted us a share in his divine nature."

The page concludes with a prayer asking for God's help in living according to the new life granted to us through Christ's resurrection.

## History

The Exultet rolls originated in southern Italy, probably in Benevento, and were used in the Beneventan liturgy. The earliest known roll, which is unillustrated, comes from the end of the tenth century. The earliest illustrated rolls date from the eleventh century. The Beneventan liturgy was suppressed in the eleventh century, but the use of the rolls continued in southern Italy until the nineteenth century.
