 “In 2019, a team of researchers from the University of Oxford and the University of Bath found that 90% of climate-related disasters in the last 20 years were caused by flooding, heavy rain, and heatwaves and drought. Researchers also found that these types of disasters were the biggest killers of all weather-related disasters.”

The statement that 90% of climate-related disasters were caused by flooding, heavy rain, and heatwaves and drought is probably true, but I would argue that this is true whether or not there is any climate change. These are the common disasters that have always been with us.

The article is misleading in implying that these disasters are caused by climate change. No doubt climate change may alter the frequency or severity of these disasters. But the article is engaging in the common fallacy of attributing the entire impact of these disasters to climate change.

The second statement, that these disasters are the biggest killers of weather-related disasters, is very likely true. I don’t know the exact numbers, but I’d guess that hurricanes, floods, heatwaves and droughts together account for the majority of deaths from weather-related disasters.
