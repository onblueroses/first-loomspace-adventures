 "When it comes to understanding the various ways that a partner can be unfaithful, a good rule of thumb is this: Infidelity is any emotional or physical intimacy that violates trust." 

I am glad that this article focuses on trust as the central issue of infidelity. I also like that it doesn't limit infidelity to the physical. I agree that emotional infidelity can be just as damaging as physical infidelity. 

The article goes on to list 10 types of infidelity. Here is my take on each:

1. Emotional Affairs
This is when a person develops an emotional connection with someone other than their partner that involves intimacy, sharing personal details, and confiding in each other. This can be just as damaging as physical affairs because it involves a betrayal of trust and emotional connection.

I agree that emotional affairs can be just as damaging as physical ones. Sharing intimate emotional details with someone outside the relationship can feel like a huge betrayal of trust.

2. Cyber Affairs
These are online relationships that involve emotional or sexual intimacy. They may start as harmless friendships but can progress to more intimate interactions, such as sharing personal details, flirting, or engaging in cybersex.
