up vote 0 down vote This is a late answer, but I came across this issue recently and discovered that it was related to the 'loginwindow' process not being owned by root. In my case, this process was owned by another user I had created.
To fix this, I booted to the recovery partition by holding Command+R while the system started up, opened the Terminal (Utilities > Terminal in the top menu), and typed the following commands:
```
chown root:admin /Volumes/Macintosh\ HD/System/Library/CoreServices/loginwindow.app/loginwindow
chown root:wheel /Volumes/Macintosh\ HD/System/Library/CoreServices/loginwindow
