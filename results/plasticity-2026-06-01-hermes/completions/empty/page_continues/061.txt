 "An important element in the process of building this consensus is the role of policy advisers. They are the ones who provide the relevant data, facts and figures that inform the policy debate."

It's a bit like saying that the most important element in the process of building a house is the role of the construction workers. Yes, they are important, but what about the architect, the engineers, the client, and the building code? In the same way, the policy advisers are important, but what about the policymakers, the public, the media, and the political system?

The page then goes on to list the "key elements in the policy advising process": defining the problem, gathering and analyzing data, developing policy options, presenting the options to the policymakers, and evaluating the policy outcomes. This is a good list, but it's not the whole story. It's like saying that the key elements in building a house are pouring the foundation, framing the walls, installing the plumbing and electrical, and finishing the interior. Yes, those are important steps, but what about the design, the permits, the financing, and the inspections?

The page also says that "policy advisers need to be able to communicate their findings effectively to policymakers." This is true, but it's not enough.
