### Boddington Gold Mine

Main article: Boddington Gold MineBoddington Gold Mine is located within the Saddleback greenstone belt in Western Australia. The operation is located 120 km southeast of Perth, and 16 km from the rural township of Boddington.

The mine itself is located 130 km southeast of Perth, Western Australia and 16 km from the town of Boddington.

The Boddington Gold Mine is a large open-cut mining operation that produces more than 230,000 ounces of gold per year.
