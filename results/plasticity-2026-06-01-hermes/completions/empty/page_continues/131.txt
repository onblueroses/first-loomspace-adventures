 "The late Sir John Murray, the famous oceanographer, was born at Cobourg in 1841, but his parents returned to Scotland when he was a boy." The reference is to the oceanographer John Murray who was the first to study oceanography comprehensively. His father's family home was at 125 King Street West.

### Architectural preservation

Preserving Cobourg's architecture and heritage has been a topic of discussion since the 1970s as the city developed and grew. It led to the establishment of the Cobourg Heritage Advisory Committee which advises the Town Council on matters related to heritage preservation and the implementation of the Ontario Heritage Act within the community. The Town of Cobourg designated the downtown as a Heritage Conservation District in 2016 and added the Corktown neighbourhood to the district in 2021.
