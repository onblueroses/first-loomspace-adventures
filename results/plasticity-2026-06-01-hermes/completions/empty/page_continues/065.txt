 "In 1988, the company was acquired by Drexel Burnham Lambert, one of the major investment banking firms. That firm's bankruptcy in February 1990 forced the sale of the company to Wasserstein Perella & Co., another investment banking firm. The new owners renamed the company the Newbridge Latin America Fund and opened it to new investors. In 1996, the fund was acquired by T. Rowe Price Associates, a major mutual fund company, and it was renamed the T. Rowe Price Latin America Fund."
