### The hard way

The other way to build a product is to build the whole thing, and then hope that it works. That's the hard way.

The hard way is to spend months or years building a product, and then hope that people will buy it. That's a risky bet.

The hard way is to build a product that you think people want, without actually talking to people to find out what they want. That's a recipe for failure.

The hard way is to build a product that you think is cool, without actually testing it with real users to see if it solves their problems. That's a recipe for building something that nobody wants.

The hard way is to build a product in secret, without getting feedback from potential customers along the way. That's a recipe for building something that nobody wants.
