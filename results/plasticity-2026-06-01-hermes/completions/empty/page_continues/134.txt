 "In the 1st century, the Greek historian Strabo reported that the Parthians used camels to carry their merchandise all the way from Arabia to India and Bactria."

Strabo lived from 64 or 63 BC to c. 24 AD, so he could have reported about events in the first century AD.  But since the Parthians used camels to carry their merchandise all the way from Arabia to India and Bactria in Strabo's lifetime, and since Strabo died about 24 AD, that would mean that Parthian trade routes extended all the way to India and Bactria by 24 AD, near the beginning of the first century AD.
