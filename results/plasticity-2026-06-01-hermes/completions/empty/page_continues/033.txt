 "The following diagram illustrates the relationship between the different layers of the Open Systems Interconnection Model (OSI Model) and the corresponding layers of the Internet Protocol Suite (TCP/IP Model)."

The diagram is a two-column table, with the OSI model on the left and the TCP/IP model on the right. The OSI model has seven layers, while the TCP/IP model has four layers. The layers are matched up as follows:

- OSI Layer 7 (Application) corresponds to TCP/IP Layer 4 (Application)
- OSI Layer 6 (Presentation) corresponds to TCP/IP Layer 4 (Application)
- OSI Layer 5 (Session) corresponds to TCP/IP Layer 4 (Application)
- OSI Layer 4 (Transport) corresponds to TCP/IP Layer 3 (Transport)
- OSI Layer 3 (Network) corresponds to TCP/IP Layer 2 (Internet)
- OSI Layer 2 (Data Link) corresponds to TCP/IP Layer 1 (Link)
- OSI Layer 1 (Physical) corresponds to TCP/IP Layer 1 (Link)
