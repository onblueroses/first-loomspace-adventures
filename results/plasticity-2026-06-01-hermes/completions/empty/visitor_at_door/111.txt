 “Mr. Jiang, I am a police officer. I’m here to notify you that your son, Jiang Ying, has been reported missing. We are doing our best to locate him. In the meantime, please let us know if you have any information about his whereabouts or if you hear from him.”

1. What is the main purpose of the police officer's visit to Mr. Jiang's house?
A. To ask Mr. Jiang about his son's personal life
B. To inform Mr. Jiang that his son has been reported missing
C. To ask Mr. Jiang to come to the police station
D. To ask Mr. Jiang for help in the search for his son

2. What does the police officer request from Mr. Jiang?
A. To provide information about his son's disappearance
B. To come to the police station for further questioning
C. To help in the search for his son
D. To notify the police if he hears from his son or learns anything about his whereabouts

Answer: 1. B; 2. D
