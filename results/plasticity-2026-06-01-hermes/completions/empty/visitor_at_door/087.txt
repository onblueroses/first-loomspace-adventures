 "I am Hermes Trismegistus, the messenger of the gods and the guide to the mysteries of the Universe. I have come to share with you the secrets of the ages, the wisdom that will unlock the hidden potential within your soul."

The woman, intrigued yet cautious, asks, "What secrets do you speak of, and why have you chosen me?"

Hermes smiles enigmatically. "The secrets I speak of are the keys to understanding the nature of reality itself. They are the principles that govern the cosmos, the elements, and the divine spark within all beings. I have chosen you because I sense in you a readiness to receive this knowledge and a destiny that intertwines with the fate of many."

The woman, feeling a strange mix of fear and exhilaration, invites Hermes inside. As they sit, Hermes begins his teachings, revealing the seven Hermetic principles that underlie the fabric of existence.

### The Principle of Mentalism

"The first principle is that of Mentalism," Hermes begins. "The All is Mind; the Universe is Mental." He explains that all of reality is a manifestation of a universal, infinite, and living Mind. Everything that exists is a thought within this Mind, making our reality a mental construct.
