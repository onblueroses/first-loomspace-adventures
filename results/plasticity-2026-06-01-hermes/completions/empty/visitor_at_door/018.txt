 "I was told to give this to you." The man hands you a crumpled piece of paper. It reads: "Come to the place that stinks." The note is signed by your friend. You've had enough of riddles for one day, but you can't ignore your friend's request.

Where should you go?

The answer is: The garbage dump

You think for a moment. The place that stinks... it must be the garbage dump! You thank the visitor and quickly make your way there, hoping to find your friend and finally get some answers.
