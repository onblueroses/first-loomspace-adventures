  His words are not understood by the occupants of the home, but he persists.  He is not deterred by the door being closed, nor the lack of understanding, he continues to speak.  He knows he is being understood.  The power of his words are not dependent upon the recipients understanding, but rather his own understanding and knowing.  He speaks to the mountain and it moves, not because the mountain understands, but because of the power and authority with which he speaks.  The visitor at the door, the Holy Spirit, knows the power of his words, and he speaks them, and they accomplish their purpose.  The door opens, and the home is filled with light and life.

Isaiah 55:11 says, "So shall my word be that goes out from my mouth; it shall not return to me empty, but it shall accomplish that which I purpose, and shall succeed in the thing for which I sent it."  The Holy Spirit knows the power of the word of God, and he speaks it with confidence and authority.  He is not discouraged by closed doors or lack of understanding, but persists in speaking the truth in love.
