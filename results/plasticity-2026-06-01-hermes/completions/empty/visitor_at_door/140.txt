 He says: "If you had been willing to do what I asked you to do, you would have had the strength to do it. I asked you to let me in, and you refused. I was hungry, and you gave me no food; I was thirsty, and you gave me no drink; I was a stranger, and you did not invite me in; naked, and you did not clothe me; sick and in prison, and you did not visit me. These people will go away into eternal punishment, but the righteous into eternal life."

The listeners are speechless. All their lives they had been making excuses for themselves. They had said they were too busy to help the needy, too poor to give to the hungry, too weak to visit the sick. They had been waiting for a more convenient time to serve God. But now they realize that they had been deceiving themselves. They had not been too busy, too poor, or too weak. They had simply been unwilling to do what God had asked them to do.
