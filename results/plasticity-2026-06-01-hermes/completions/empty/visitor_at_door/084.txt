**6. The Persistent Salesman**

The doorbell rings.

The man opens the door to find a salesman.

The salesman speaks quickly. "Hello, sir! I'm here to offer you an amazing opportunity to transform your life with our revolutionary product!"

The man at the door interrupts. "I'm not interested, thank you."

The salesman persists. "But sir, this product has changed the lives of thousands of people just like you! Don't you want to hear more about it?"

The man at the door firmly replies. "No, I'm not interested. Please leave."

The salesman finally gives up. "Alright, sir. But you're missing out on a great opportunity. Have a good day."

The man at the door closes the door, relieved.
