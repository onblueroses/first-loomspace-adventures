 “Mrs. Reddick, I’m afraid I have some very bad news.”

I wonder what she means by that. I hope it’s not about my father. My father is a lawyer and often works with clients who are pretty bad people. I wouldn’t be surprised if one of them tried to hurt him.

I’m tempted to step out and ask what’s going on, but I decide to stay put. I don’t want to give away my hiding spot just yet.

The visitor continues, “It’s about your husband. There’s been an accident.”

An accident? My father was in an accident? My heart starts racing and I feel like I can’t breathe. I need to know what happened.

I take a deep breath and step out from behind the curtain. “What happened to my dad?”

The visitor looks at me, surprised. “Oh, I didn’t know anyone else was here. I’m afraid I have some bad news. Your father was in a car accident. He didn’t make it.”

I feel like my world is crumbling around me. My father, the man who has always been there for me, is gone. I can’t believe it.

I look at my mother, expecting her to be just as devastated as I am.
