 “Mr. Flanders, I’m with the FBI. We’d like to talk to you about your neighbor, Mr. Simpson. We have some information that you might find interesting.”

Ned: “Well, now that you mention it, there have been some strange goings-on over there. Just the other day, I saw him burying something in his backyard in the middle of the night. I thought it was odd, but I didn’t want to pry.”

The FBI agent nods. “That’s precisely the kind of information we’re looking for, Mr. Flanders. We appreciate your cooperation.”

As the agents continue to question Ned, he becomes increasingly worried about the safety of his family and the community. He wonders how he could have been so blind to his neighbor’s true nature.
