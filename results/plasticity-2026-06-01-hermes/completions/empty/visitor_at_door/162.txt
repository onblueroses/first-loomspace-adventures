 “I am the one who came to call you to the path of truth, but you did not respond. You have wasted your life in heedlessness.” The visitor is your deeds, which are sometimes called “angels.” “Whoever does an atom’s weight of good will see it, and whoever does an atom’s weight of evil will see it.” [Qur’an, 99:7-8]
Or, it is your life which has come to an end, as Imam `Ali (as) said, “O people, the world is calling you from behind you, ‘Pursue me!’ And the hereafter is calling you from in front of you, ‘Pursue me!’”
Or, the visitor is your youth in this world, which has now come to an end. It calls to you, “I was your youth, and I have now passed. I was the period of life in which active effort was possible, and I have now passed. Now you have entered the period of weakness and inability. You have no power to return to the past to repair anything or correct any of your mistakes.” The visitor may be the month of Ramadan, or a person’s Salah, or the Hajj, or some other form of worship
