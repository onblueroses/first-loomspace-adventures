 "Are you the father of the family?"

"Yes."

"I am the census-taker. I would like to ask you a few questions, if you will permit me."

"Certainly, come in," replies the father, and he leads the way into the house. The census-taker enters, carrying a portfolio, which he opens after sitting down. He takes out a sheet of paper containing many questions, and a pencil to write the answers.

The father watches the census-taker while he prepares to write. Then the visitor looks up and begins to ask questions.

"What is your name?" he inquires.

"John Thompson."

"How old are you?"

"Forty-two years old."

"What is your wife's name?"

"Mary Thompson."

"How old is she?"

"Thirty-eight years old."

"How many children have you?"

"Three."

"What are their names and ages?"

"William, aged 12 years; Alice, aged 10 years; Helen, aged 6 years."

"What is your occupation?"

"I am a carpenter."

"Do you own your home?"

"Yes."

"What is its value?"

"About $3,000."

"Is there a mortgage on it?"

"Yes."

"How much?"

"$1,000."

"Is your wife employed?"

"No."
