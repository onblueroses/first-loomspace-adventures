 "I'm a friend of your brother's. I was hoping to find him here."

"Which brother?" says the woman at the door. "I have two."

The visitor thinks for a moment, then says, "The one with the bigger nose."

The woman looks puzzled and says, "Their noses are the same size."

The visitor says, "Then I'm looking for the one who's not an identical twin."

The woman nods and lets the visitor in.

How did the visitor know that the woman had twins, and how did she know which one to ask for?

Comment: This is a variation of an old joke. I don't know where it originated, but I first heard it from my father, who said that he heard it from a coworker. I think it's a clever riddle, so I thought I'd share it with you folks. I'm not sure if it follows the guidelines for this site; if not, please let me know.

Answer: The visitor knew the woman had twins because she said "I have two" when the visitor asked "Which brother?". The visitor was looking for the non-identical twin because if both twins were identical, then it would not make sense to ask for the one with the bigger nose
