 “I am from a distant land. I have heard of your wisdom and have come to learn from you.”

The master looks at the visitor and says, “There is nothing to learn here. If you wish to find wisdom, you must first unlearn what you think you know.”

The visitor is puzzled. “But how can I unlearn what I know?”

The master smiles. “The answer lies within you. You must let go of your preconceptions and be open to the present moment. Only then can you truly see.”

The visitor nods thoughtfully and takes a seat next to the master. They sit in silence, watching the leaves fall from the trees.

After a while, the visitor speaks again. “I think I understand. By letting go of my thoughts and expectations, I can experience the world as it is, without judgment.”

The master nods. “You are beginning to see. Wisdom is not something that can be taught. It is something that you discover within yourself when you are fully present and aware.”

The visitor smiles, feeling a sense of peace and understanding wash over them. They continue to sit in silence, enjoying the beauty of the present moment.
