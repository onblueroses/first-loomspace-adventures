 "Good evening, Mr. Engels. I am a friend of Marx's. I have come to collect a manuscript which I believe you have been keeping for him."

The visitor is referring to a manuscript that Engels has been safeguarding for Karl Marx. Engels, slightly taken aback by the unexpected visit, hesitates for a moment before responding.

Engels: "Ah, yes, the manuscript. Marx did mention that someone might come for it. However, I must admit, I did not expect anyone so soon. May I ask your name?"

The visitor introduces himself as Wilhelm Wolff, a fellow revolutionary and a close associate of Marx.

Wolff: "My apologies for the sudden visit, Mr. Engels. I am Wilhelm Wolff. Marx and I have been working closely on this project, and he sent word that you would have the manuscript ready for me."

Engels nods, recognizing the name. He trusts Wolff and knows of his contributions to the cause. He steps aside, inviting Wolff into his home.

Engels: "Of course, Herr Wolff. Please, come in. I have the manuscript safe and sound. I was just reviewing it earlier today."
