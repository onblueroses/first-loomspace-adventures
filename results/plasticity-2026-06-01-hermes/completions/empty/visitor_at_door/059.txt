 “I am from the city. I have a message for you. Your husband has been in an accident. He is in the hospital. He is asking for you.”

The woman’s heart sinks. Her husband, the love of her life, is hurt and needs her. She must go to him. But her baby, her precious little one, is asleep in the cradle. She cannot leave her child alone.

The visitor sees the woman’s dilemma. “I can stay with your baby while you go to your husband. I will keep your child safe until you return.”

The woman is torn. She wants to trust this kind stranger, but she is hesitant to leave her baby with someone she does not know. She looks into the visitor’s eyes, searching for any sign of malice or deceit. She finds none.

The woman makes a decision. She takes the visitor’s hand and leads them to the cradle where her baby sleeps. “Thank you,” she whispers. “I will return as soon as I can.”

The woman gathers a few belongings and hurries out into the night. The visitor stays behind, watching over the sleeping child.

Hours pass. The woman reaches the city and finds her way to the hospital. She rushes to her husband’s bedside.
