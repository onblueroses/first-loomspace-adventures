 A familiar voice. I open my eyes.

It’s not who I think it is. It’s the doctor. My mother’s doctor. He’s come to see how she is. I let him in and show him to her room. I’m glad he’s come. I wanted to ask him something.

I ask him if it’s normal for someone to be asleep for so long. He says it’s not unusual for someone in her condition. I don’t know what her condition is. I didn’t know she had a condition. He says she’s very weak. Her body is shutting down. She’s dying.

I didn’t know she was dying. I thought she was just tired. I thought she was just sleeping. I didn’t know she was dying.

I ask him how long she has. He says he doesn’t know. It could be days. It could be weeks. It could be longer. He says she’s not in any pain. She’s just sleeping. She’s just waiting.

Waiting for what?

Waiting to die.

I don’t know what to say. I don’t know what to do. I just stand there. I just stare at her. At her face. At her closed eyes. At her mouth. At her hands.
