 It is a man's voice, soft and deep. "Are you Mrs. John March?"

Irene is startled, and she hesitates a moment before replying. "Yes."

"I have a letter here for you. It is from your husband."

Irene's heart leaps to her throat. She clutches the door facing to keep from falling, and looks eagerly out. She can see only a dim form.

"Give it to me, please," she says, extending her hand.

The man places in it a large envelope.

"Shall I wait for an answer?"

"No, no," she replies, quickly, "there will be no answer. Good night."

"Good night," returns the man, and she hears his retreating footsteps.
