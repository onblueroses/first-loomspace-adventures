 His voice is deep and strong. It is a voice that commands attention. It is a voice that is used to being obeyed. He tells the old man to get out of his house. He tells him that the house is his, and he wants it back. The old man argues, but the visitor insists. The old man has no choice but to leave. He takes his coat and his hat and he walks out into the night. He does not know where he will go or what he will do. But he knows that he can never go back to the house that was once his home.

The visitor watches him go. He stands in the doorway, his tall, thin frame silhouetted against the light from inside. He watches until the old man disappears into the darkness. Then he closes the door. The house is his now. It is time for him to begin his work. The work that he has been waiting to do for so long. The work that will change everything. The work that will make him the most powerful being in the world. The work that will make him a god.
