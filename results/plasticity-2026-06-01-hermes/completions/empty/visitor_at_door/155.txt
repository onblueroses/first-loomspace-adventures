 "I am your friend," he says. "I am your enemy," he says. "I am your neighbor," he says. "I am you," he says. The voice is familiar; the words are familiar. You've heard them before. You've used them before. The visitor is not a stranger. You know this person well. This person has been in your life many times, in many forms. This person is you.

You are the one who knocks on the door of your own heart, seeking entry, seeking acceptance, seeking love. You are the one who stands outside, in the cold, in the dark, waiting for an answer. You are the one who fears rejection, who fears abandonment, who fears the unknown. You are the one who longs for connection, for understanding, for belonging.

The visitor is not a threat. The visitor is an opportunity. An opportunity to open the door, to let yourself in, to welcome yourself home. An opportunity to embrace all parts of yourself, even the parts that you have denied, even the parts that you have rejected. An opportunity to heal, to grow, to transform.

The visitor is a gift. A gift of self-awareness, of self-acceptance, of self-love.
