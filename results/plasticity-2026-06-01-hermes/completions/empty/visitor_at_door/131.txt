 

**Visitor:** Hi, I'm here to pick up the package for my neighbor. They asked me to grab it since they won't be home today. 

**Agent:** Sure, I can help with that. May I please see your ID for verification? 

**Visitor:** Absolutely, here you go. *hands over ID*

The agent checks the ID and confirms the details. 

**Agent:** Thank you. I'll just need you to sign for the package on their behalf. 

**Visitor:** No problem. *signs the form*

**Agent:** Here you go. *hands over the package* Have a great day! 

**Visitor:** Thanks, you too! 

The visitor leaves with the package. 

**Agent:** Let me just update the system to note that a neighbor picked up the package. *updates the system* Alright, all set. 

The agent then resumes their regular duties, ensuring the front desk remains organized and ready for the next interaction. 

**End Scene.**
