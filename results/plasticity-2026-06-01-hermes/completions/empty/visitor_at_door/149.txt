“Come on in then, Uncle,” replies the guard.

How is the guard related to the visitor?

The guard is the nephew of the visitor.

The visitor states that he has no brothers or sisters, so we can rule out the possibility of the guard being the son of the visitor. The visitor also says that the guard’s father is the son of the visitor’s father. This means that the guard’s father is the brother of the visitor. Therefore, the guard is the son of the visitor’s brother, making the guard the nephew of the visitor.
