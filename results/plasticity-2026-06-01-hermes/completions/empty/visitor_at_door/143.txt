 'I am the Spirit of Christmas Present,' said the spirit. 'Look upon me!'

What is Dickens trying to say here? What is the message he is trying to convey through the Spirit of Christmas Present?

To answer this question, let's analyze the context and the character of the Spirit of Christmas Present in A Christmas Carol.

The Spirit of Christmas Present appears to Scrooge after the visit from the Ghost of Christmas Past. While the first spirit showed Scrooge his past and the mistakes he has made, the Spirit of Christmas Present is there to show Scrooge the current reality of the world around him, especially the joys and sufferings of others that he has been blind to in his miserly, self-centered existence.

When the spirit says, 'Look upon me!', he is commanding Scrooge's attention and telling him to open his eyes to the present moment. The spirit wants Scrooge to really see and understand the world as it is now, not as he imagines it to be.
