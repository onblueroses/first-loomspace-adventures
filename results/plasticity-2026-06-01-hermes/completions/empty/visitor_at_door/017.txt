 "I'm here to see my sister," she says. "Her name is Hannah."

The receptionist looks up from her computer screen. "Of course," she says. "And you are?"

"I'm Sarah," the visitor replies. "Sarah Thompson."

The receptionist nods and types something into her computer. "Alright, Sarah," she says. "I'll need to see your ID, please."

Sarah reaches into her purse and pulls out her driver's license. She hands it to the receptionist, who looks it over and hands it back.

"Thank you," the receptionist says. "I'll let Hannah know you're here. Please have a seat in the waiting area, and she'll be with you shortly."

Sarah nods and walks over to the waiting area. She sits down in one of the chairs and picks up a magazine from the table beside her. As she flips through the pages, she can't help but feel a sense of unease.

It's been years since she last saw her sister. They had a falling out after their parents died, and they haven't spoken since. But now, with Hannah in the hospital, Sarah knows she has to be there for her.

As she waits, Sarah's mind wanders to the past.
