 "I am not a stranger, though you have never seen me before. I am the spirit of Christmas past. Come with me, and I will show you your past Christmases."

Scrooge, though puzzled, agrees, and the spirit takes him on a journey through his past. They visit his childhood, where Scrooge sees his lonely self at school, neglected by his friends and family. They then visit his time as an apprentice at Fezziwig's, where Scrooge sees his younger self enjoying the festive celebrations with his colleagues. The spirit then shows Scrooge the moment he chose his love for money over his love for his fiancée, Belle. Scrooge, distressed by these memories, begs the spirit to take him home, and he wakes up in his bed again.
