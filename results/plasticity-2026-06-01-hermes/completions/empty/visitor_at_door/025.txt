 He’s a tall, lanky man with a shock of brown hair falling over his eyes. He’s wearing a faded T-shirt with a picture of a video game character on it. His name is Matt. He’s a computer programmer. He’s here to see the house.

“Hello,” Matt says, extending his hand. “I’m here to see the house.”

The real estate agent, a middle-aged woman with a tight bun and a practiced smile, shakes his hand. “Of course,” she says. “Welcome. Let me show you around.”

She leads him inside. The house is beautiful, with high ceilings, hardwood floors, and large windows that let in plenty of natural light. Matt admires the craftsmanship, the attention to detail. He imagines himself living here, working on his computer in the spacious home office, cooking meals in the modern kitchen.

But as they ascend to the second floor, Matt feels a strange sensation, like a chill running down his spine. The real estate agent doesn’t seem to notice. She’s busy pointing out the features of the master bedroom, the walk-in closet, the en-suite bathroom.
