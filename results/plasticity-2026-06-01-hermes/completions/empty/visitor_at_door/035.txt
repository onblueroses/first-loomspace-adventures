"I am looking for Mr. Alfred Wallace," the man says, his voice deep and grave. "Is he available?"

The servant hesitates, glancing back into the house. "I'm afraid Mr. Wallace is not receiving visitors at the moment," she replies. "He is quite ill and needs his rest."

The man nods solemnly. "I understand," he says. "But it is of the utmost importance that I speak with him. It concerns a matter of great significance, a matter that could change the course of history."

The servant frowns, uncertain. "I... I don't know," she stammers. "I shall have to ask Mrs. Wallace. Please wait here."

She closes the door, leaving the man standing on the doorstep. He waits patiently, his face impassive. After a few minutes, the door opens again, and the servant beckons him inside.
