 “I am Mr. H. I have come to see you in the hope that you will help me. I have been told that you have occult powers. Is that so?”

The Master, who has been sitting in meditation and has seen the visitor coming, replies, “God is all-powerful. If He wills, everything is possible.”

Mr. H. continues, “I am a rich man, but my wife and children do not respect me. In spite of my wealth, I am most unhappy. Can you give me peace of mind?”

The Master’s reply is, “Peace is within you. If you want peace, do not look for it in the external world. Look within, and through meditation you will realize that you are the fount of peace.”

Mr. H. says, “I do not understand you. They say you have occult powers and can perform miracles. Can you not help me in some way to make my wife and children respect me?”

The Master answers, “Miracles are all right. I can easily perform miracles, but they will not give you lasting peace. God alone can give you peace. As for your wife and children, you cannot force them to respect you. If you want their respect, you must command it
