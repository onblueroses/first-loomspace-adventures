 “May I speak to you? Are you Mr. James?”

“Mr. James? Yes, sir. That’s my name.”

“Mr. James, I am a stranger to you, but I have come to ask your assistance in a little matter in which you can help me, if you will.”

“Certainly, sir; certainly. What can I do for you?”

“Are you a married man, Mr. James?”

“Yes, sir; I am a married man.”

“Have you any children?”

“Yes, sir; I have several children.”

“Are your wife and children at home?”

“No, sir; my wife and children are in the country.”

“Ah! I am glad of that. Mr. James, I am in a little trouble, and I want you to assist me. I assure you that I am an honest man, and I am sure you will not regret it if you grant my request.”

“What is it, sir? I shall be most happy to serve you.”
