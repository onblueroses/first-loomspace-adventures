It is clear that the sentences in (1) have a different meaning. In (la) the pronoun who is used as the subject of the sentence and it has an interrogative meaning. In (lb) the pronoun who is used as the object of the sentence and it has a relative meaning. The same difference in meaning is observed in the pairs of sentences (2a, b) and (3a, b). The question that arises is: what is the source of this meaning difference? An adequate account of the semantics of these sentences must provide an answer to this question. In this paper, I propose an analysis of the meaning of these sentences within the framework of Discourse Representation Theory (DRT). In DRT, the meaning of a sentence is represented in a Discourse Representation Structure (DRS).
