 Is this the time for the bestial in us to emerge?

> Now is the time for the bestial in us to emerge, but it is also the time for the best in us to emerge. We have a choice. Do we face this with fear or with love? Do we face it with despair or with hope? Do we face it with rage or with compassion? Yes, the beast is in us, but so is the divine. We can choose what we feed, what we nurture. We can choose what we unleash into the world. 

> In this moment, we have the opportunity to rise above our baser instincts, to tap into the deepest wells of our humanity. We can choose to be kind, to be generous, to be brave. We can choose to see the humanity in others, even when they are at their most desperate. We can choose to build bridges, not walls. 

> The beast is a part of us, but it is not all of us. We are more than our fear, more than our anger. We are capable of great love, of great sacrifice, of great heroism. In the face of the beast, let us choose to be our best selves. Let us choose to face this darkness with light.
