 The Secretary of State for the Home Department. The Right Honourable Reginald Maudling. Mr Maudling, may I ask you first of all, did you welcome the publication of this report?

Reginald Maudling: Yes, I did, very much. I think it's a very valuable document. Of course, it doesn't contain any new information; it's a collation of information already available. But it's very valuable to have it all together in this way. I think it's a useful contribution to the discussion of this problem.

I see. Now, do you accept the findings of the report, that the police are not racist?

Reginald Maudling: Yes, I do. I've always said that I don't believe there's any general prejudice in the police force. Of course, there may be individual cases, but I think by and large, the police force reflects the attitude of the society in which we live. And I think that the police are no more prejudiced than anyone else. Indeed, I think rather less so.
