 It’s not the
stuttering boy who was here before. It’s someone
else. Someone who’s been waiting in the wings.
Someone who’s been rehearsing for this
moment over and over, like lines in a play. It’s
someone who knows that this room belongs to
him, to her, to them, to us. This is our room now.
Our voice. Our story. Let’s tell it.

To my brother, David
