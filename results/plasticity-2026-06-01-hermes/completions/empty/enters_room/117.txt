  This is a fantastic thing to do.  It is called the "happy dance."  It is a dance that celebrates the moment when something special happens.  In this case, the special thing is that we have reached a new level of understanding.  We are happy that we have achieved our goal of finding the pattern.  We do the "happy dance" to celebrate.  I play the happy dance music and we all dance for one minute.  It's a fun thing to do.  It reinforces that learning is fun and exciting.
After our dance, I tell the students that we will now begin to create our own patterns using the pattern cards.  I have a sheet with the pattern cards that I have the students cut out.  I then have them glue the cards on a piece of construction paper in an AB pattern.  When they have completed this, I have them label the pattern they have created.  
I then have the students switch papers with a neighbor and have the neighbor read the pattern to make sure it is correct.  We then do another happy dance to celebrate learning how to create and read patterns.
