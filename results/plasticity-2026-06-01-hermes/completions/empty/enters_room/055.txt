 It’s the past. It’s the future. It’s a presence that doesn’t recognize the concept of time. It’s a wind that blows from one end of the earth to the other, a blue haze, a cold fire that wavers in the air, the flame that lives in the heart of the lump of coal, the soul of the forest, the breath of the sea. Call it imagination, call it the gift of making, call it the power that creates art, whatever it is, it shakes my hand, it plucks me by the elbow, it accompanies me out of the room, down the stairs, and into the street. It puts its hand to my back and pushes me through the door of the café. It’s sitting at my table, waiting for me. I sit down and order a coffee.

“Now what?” I say. “What’s the next step?”

The being of light leans forward. “Remember this and never forget it,” it says. “You’re a storyteller. Your material is the world, the world as you see it, as you’ve lived it, as you imagine it.
