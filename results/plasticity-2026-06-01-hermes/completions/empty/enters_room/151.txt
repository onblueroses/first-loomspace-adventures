 I wish to speak to the officer of the watch.

PETER (_nervously_). The officer of the watch is not here.

THE STRANGER. You are lying. I see him in there. (_Pointing toward the inner room._)

PETER. It's the Captain himself.

THE STRANGER. So much the better!

PETER. No, no, don't! Don't!

THE STRANGER. I shall go in to him. What's the matter with you, man? You're acting queerly. (_He goes toward the door of the inner room._)

PETER. Stop, stop!

THE STRANGER. What's that?

PETER (_in a low voice_). He is not alone.

THE STRANGER. Who is with him?

PETER. His wife.

THE STRANGER. That's a lie. He has no wife.

PETER. Yes, he has. They have been married to-day.

THE STRANGER. I don't believe it. Stand aside! (_He pushes PETER aside and goes into the inner room, where the CAPTAIN is seated at a table; his wife is sitting on the sofa; both are in evening dress; on the table stands a burning candelabrum._)
