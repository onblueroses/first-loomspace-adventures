# 1.2.2. Dream Incubation

Sit or lie down comfortably and close your eyes. Breathe slowly and deeply. With each breath, let go of any tension in your body and mind. Imagine yourself in a beautiful place in nature, a place where you feel safe and peaceful. It might be a beach, a forest, a mountain, or a garden. Take your time to explore this place with all your senses. What do you see, hear, smell, touch, and taste? Enjoy the beauty and tranquility of this place.
