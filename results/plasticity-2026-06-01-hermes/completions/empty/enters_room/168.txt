 Is it the world? Is it our mind? Is it the world, but perceived through the lens of our mind? I don’t know. But I know that what enters is a mixture of the world and our mind. The world enters, but it’s interpreted by our mind. And our mind enters, with all its experiences, thoughts, and feelings.

This is what we have to work with. It’s all we have. It’s a room of experience. A room of the world-as-it-appears-to-us. It’s the room of our-world. Our inner and outer world.

It’s our room of experience. And we can explore this room. We can get to know it. We can be curious about it. We can get to know the nooks and crannies. We can discover more of the world-as-it-appears-to-us.

We can notice that the world, to us, happens within our experience. We can notice that our experience – of the wider world and of our most intimate thoughts and feelings – happens within the same room of experience. To us, it all happens within the same room.
