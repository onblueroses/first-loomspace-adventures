She laughed at that, the sound of it echoing in the room. “Yes, I’d like to see that too.”
I couldn’t help it. I laughed with her. I was still laughing when the door opened, and a tall, very muscular man stepped into the room. He had short dark hair and the bluest eyes I’d ever seen. He was wearing a white tuxedo jacket with a white bow tie and cummerbund, but the pants were black. His dress shoes were a shiny black as well. He was a very handsome man, but the look on his face was deadly serious. He didn’t look like a happy bridegroom. He looked more like a soldier ready for battle.
“Don’t, don’t you laugh at her, Anita, never at her.”
The fear in Gregory’s voice made me turn around to look at the door. The man was big, almost as big as Richard, which put him at about six foot three or maybe an inch taller.
