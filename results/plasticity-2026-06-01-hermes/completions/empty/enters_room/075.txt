 It's the start of the year, a fresh start, but also a continuation of the past year. What is it?

Answer: \begin{blockquote}

 January.
 January is the start of a new year, and it is also a continuation of last year, in that it is named after Janus, the Roman god of beginnings and endings, who is usually depicted with two faces looking in opposite directions (past and future).
\end{blockquote}
