I don't know how it happened but it did. I'm writing this down to the best of my memory. It's hard to remember everything. It's hard to remember anything. It's hard to think. It's hard to sleep. It's hard to breathe. It's hard to exist. But I have to write this down. I have to remember. I have to warn others. I have to let them know. I have to let them know what's coming. I have to let them know what's already here.

It started with a noise. A noise that wasn't a noise. A noise that was the absence of noise. A noise that was the absence of everything. A noise that was the absence of life. A noise that was the absence of death. A noise that was the absence of existence. A noise that was the absence of non-existence. A noise that was the absence of everything and nothing at the same time.

It was a noise that I couldn't hear but I could feel. It was a noise that I couldn't feel but I could sense. It was a noise that I couldn't sense but I could perceive. It was a noise that I couldn't perceive but I could comprehend.
