The woman in the chair, her eyes closed, her hands resting in her lap.
Her hands that hold nothing. That have nothing to hold.
She sits so still, waiting for what will come.
And what comes is a thought. And the thought is this: _If only I could have believed harder_.
This is the thought that fills the room now, that takes up all the space in the room.
If only I could have believed harder.
And the thought grows and the thought spreads and soon the thought is all there is. The thought is everything. The thought is the world.
_If only I could have believed harder_.
And the woman opens her eyes.
And the woman stands up.
And the woman leaves the room.
