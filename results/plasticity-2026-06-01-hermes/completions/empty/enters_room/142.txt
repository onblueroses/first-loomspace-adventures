 The heads of the boys as they enter the room are slightly lowered and their eyes seem to be watching something on the floor. Their steps are slow and they walk almost on tiptoe, taking very short steps. The muscles of their faces are set and they do not talk or whisper to each other. The fact that they have entered the room almost as though they were entering a church is evident. The room is a place where silence is the custom and for a long time they have spoken only in whispers here. The room is not as warm as the one they have left and for a while they are slightly uncomfortable because of this.

One of the boys goes to a window and puts his hands on the cold glass. Then he breathes on his hands and looks down at the ground below. He is the boy who lives on the farm and he is looking at the ground below and thinking of his home and his father and his mother. The other boy stands in the center of the room and looks about him. He is the boy who is to be confirmed in the church in the morning and he is thinking of that. His eyes have the look of one who is thinking of the morning and of the church.
