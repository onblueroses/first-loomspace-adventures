 The other day, I was at a party, and I heard someone say, “Let’s welcome in the new year!” I thought, “Wow, what a powerful phrase.” It made me think about what we allow to enter our lives. What do we welcome in? What do we let in the door? Is it joy, love, and positivity? Or is it negativity, fear, and doubt? The choice is ours. We have the power to decide what enters our lives. So, let’s be mindful of what we welcome in. Let’s choose to let in the good stuff and keep out the bad. Let’s welcome in love, laughter, and light. Because when we do, we create a space for happiness and positivity to thrive. What will you welcome in today?

I have always been fascinated by the concept of the “container” in the context of personal growth and self-improvement. The idea is that we all have a certain capacity to hold and process our experiences, emotions, and thoughts. This capacity can be expanded through intentional effort and practice, but it can also be overwhelmed if we try to take on too much at once.
