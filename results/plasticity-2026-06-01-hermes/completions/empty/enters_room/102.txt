 It is the Blacksmith, and he is of course not alone. The Blacksmith is never alone. Even when he is, he is not. For he is two in one, both and neither, master and servant, maker and creation. The Blacksmith is a creature of contradictions, and the only constant is the hammer in his hand and the anvil at his side.

With him walks the Golem, a towering figure of clay and stone, silent and expressionless. The Golem is the Blacksmith's creation, his protector, his companion. It is said that the Golem was born from the Blacksmith's own heart, a piece of his soul given life. And so, the Golem is the Blacksmith, and the Blacksmith is the Golem.

The Blacksmith speaks, his voice like the ringing of metal on metal. "I have come to offer my services, as I have done for countless generations. I am the Blacksmith, the maker of things, the forger of destinies. I can create anything that can be imagined, and I can destroy anything that exists. I am the beginning and the end, the alpha and the omega."
