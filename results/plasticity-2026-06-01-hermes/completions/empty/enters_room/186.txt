 (... ) Who is coming in?

MADAME DE RENAL

What's the matter?

JULIEN

It's that man.

MADAME DE RENAL

What man?

JULIEN

He's coming in. I saw him.

MADAME DE RENAL

What are you talking about? Whom did you see?

JULIEN

Monsieur de Rênal.

MADAME DE RENAL

Where?

JULIEN

Down there--in the street. He's coming up.

MADAME DE RENAL

You're dreaming! It can't be.

JULIEN

I tell you I saw him as plainly as I see you. He was coming in at the street door. He'll be here in a minute.

MADAME DE RENAL

(_Sinking into a chair._) Oh, my God!

JULIEN

What shall we do?

MADAME DE RENAL

I don't know--I can't think. Oh, Julien, what madness! Why did I come?

JULIEN
