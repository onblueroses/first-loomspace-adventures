 The word itself is not important, but the fact that there is a word at all, that there is some form of communication and recognition, even if only to say "I don't understand."

#### 19
Then the word is repeated, and the repetitions are not identical. The word is being explored, examined, turned over and over in the mouth. And now there are new words, or new sounds. Is this the beginning of language?

#### 20
The figure in the corner continues to make sounds, and they are becoming more complex. There is a rhythm to them, a kind of music. The sounds are not random; there is a pattern, a structure. This is the birth of meaning.

#### 21
The figure is no longer alone. Others have entered the room, and they too are making sounds. They are communicating with each other, and with the figure in the corner. This is the birth of society, of culture.

#### 22
The sounds are becoming more and more complex. They are no longer just sounds; they are words, phrases, sentences. They are telling stories, conveying information, expressing emotions. This is the birth of literature, of art, of science.
