I'd never met a boy like Paul before. We were inseparable, and I couldn't remember a time when I'd laughed so much or felt so free. He was funny and confident, and he seemed to have a knack for finding beauty and adventure in the most mundane things. We would spend hours walking along the river, talking about our dreams and the things that scared us. He would tell me about the places he wanted to travel to and the books he wanted to write. I would tell him about my fear of never finding my place in the world and my constant worry that I was destined for a life of mediocrity. He would listen intently, and then he would wrap his arms around me and tell me that I was destined for greatness, that I was special and that he would always be there to remind me of that when I forgot.

One afternoon, we were lying on a blanket in the park, watching the clouds drift by. Paul turned to me and said, "You know, I've never met anyone like you before. You have this way of making me feel like I can do anything, be anyone."

I smiled and nestled my head on his chest. "I feel the same way about you," I said.
