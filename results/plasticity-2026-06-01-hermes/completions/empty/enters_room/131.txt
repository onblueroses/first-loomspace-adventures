 The man from the mirror? The man I saw
in the bed? Which is he? I have no idea.

I watch him walk over to the bed and lie down on it. I keep my
eyes on him as I get up and walk over to the door. I leave the
room and close the door behind me. I turn the key in the lock. I
don't know if I'm locking him in or locking him out. I walk down the
stairs. I pass the mirror. I can't bring myself to look into it. I
don't know what I would see.

I go out the front door. I walk down the path. I open the gate. I
walk through it. I close it behind me. I walk down the street. I
don't know where I'm going. I don't know what I'm doing. I just
know I have to get away from that house. From that room. From that
mirror. From that man. From myself.

I walk and walk. The streets are empty. The houses are dark. The
world is asleep. But I can't sleep. I can't rest. I can't stop.
