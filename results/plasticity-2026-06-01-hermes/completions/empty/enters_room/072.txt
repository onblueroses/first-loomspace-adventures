 What I see before me. What I am to face.
This is the end of the road. I have no choice but to go through with it. Even if it means the end of me.
The door opens.
And there it is.
It is not a single creature, but a swarm. A swarm of a thousand black flies, buzzing, humming, a dark cloud of flittering, flickering specks.
I am to be devoured by flies.
But as the swarm draws closer, I see that they are not flies at all. They are tiny machines. Tiny flying machines, each the size of a fly.
Nanobots.
They swarm toward me.
I try to swat them away, but they are too many, and too fast. They land on my skin, my arms, my face. I feel the pinpricks of their tiny claws, gripping me. And then—
Ow! Ow! Ow! They bite me. Each one bites me, injecting something into my skin. Some kind of drug or poison.
I try to shake them off, but they cling tight, and they are too many. I feel the drug flooding my system, coursing through my veins, seeping into my brain.
