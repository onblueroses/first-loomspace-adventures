 This is the room
where my brother was killed by a look.
He looked at me and died. I had no idea
what to do with myself or where to put my eyes
or what they should see. I was blind for a while.
I knew I had a brother, and he was no more.
I thought he would come back. He did not come back.
The room is as it was, filled with his absence.
He is not in the kitchen. He is not by the window
looking in. He is not in the living room
or on the porch. I am the only one in the house
and each room is filled with my brother’s not-being.
I have been like this a long time, but now
I will tell you everything. Now I see there is
no point in silence. Now I see
that saying nothing will not bring him back.
The days are full of his name, my lips are numb
with his name. I will tell you everything.
I will tell you because if I do not,
his not-being will spread like a plague. It will enter
your body and consume you utterly.
