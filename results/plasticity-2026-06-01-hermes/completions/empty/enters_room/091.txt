The ghost, in Hamlet, symbolizes the fear and uncertainty that prevents individuals from taking action to resolve their problems. The ghost is a physical representation of the supernatural, which creates a sense of unease and apprehension in the characters.

The ghost affects Hamlet by causing him to question his own sanity and to doubt the reality of his father's death. The ghost's appearance also serves to remind Hamlet of his duty to avenge his father's murder, which leads to his eventual downfall.
