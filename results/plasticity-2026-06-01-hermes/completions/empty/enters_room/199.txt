 In a long black coat, with a wide brimmed hat, a man whose face is obscured by shadow. He stands in the corner, silent, ominous. His coat billows slightly, though there is no breeze. He is watching. Waiting.

#### 11
The house is a large one, with many rooms. But most of the rooms are empty. Or rather, they are filled with nothing but dust and silence. The old woman who lives there keeps all her furniture, all her belongings, in the one room she inhabits. The rest of the house is abandoned. She lives alone, and has done so for many years. She has no visitors. The windows are all shuttered, and the doors are locked. The house is dark and quiet. The old woman sits in her chair, day after day, staring at the wall. She does not move, does not speak. She is waiting for something, but she does not know what. Occasionally, she will get up and walk to the window, peering out through the cracks in the shutters at the empty street outside. But there is never anyone there. The street is always deserted.
