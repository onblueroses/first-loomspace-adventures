And now the text shifts, the ink deepening as if written with a different intent. The litany of invocation gives way to a manual of practice, a record of the mysteries committed to Selephimor.

**On the Ceremonies of Athraophol**

The rite requires a place that is neither within nor without, a threshold made manifest. A doorway at midnight, a shore at twilight, the moment between waking and sleep. There, the Vessel must stand, having tasted salt and honey, having fasted from speech for three turns of the moon.

**The Unburnt Fire** is not of wood or oil. It is the sustained attention, the will focused to a point that casts a shadow upon the world of causes. To kindle it, hold in mind the name VELPHARAIM while gazing at a single, unmoving star until the star appears to breathe. The heat that then gathers in the breast is the first spark.
