**…and the seal of the Unnamed is thus completed. Yet hear this, O reader who has dared to voice the Unspoken:**

The names herein are not keys, but locks. To speak *Phelonoptra* is to feel the mountains grow heavy in your bones. To utter *Krathoumis* is to taste the tide of your own blood. *Demoaphris* opens the ear to the echo of your first cry, and *Velpharaim* to the silence that will follow your last.

Thou who hast called upon **Aphrybetesh**, know that this is not a name to be handed down, but a condition to be endured. The Vessel, **Selephimor**, was not a prophet but a warning.
