The script is a ritual invocation, a theurgical text designed to bridge the human and the divine through a cascade of sacred, potent names. Its structure follows a classic arc of power:

1.  **Invocation of the Creator:** It begins by addressing the ultimate, "Unnamed One" through its acts of primordial creation—mountains, tides, echoes—establishing it as the source of all dichotomies (named/unnamed, threshold/center, memory/forgetting).

2.  **Assertion of Relationship:** The speaker, **Selephimor**, declares himself the prophet and vessel, the authorized intermediary who holds the mysteries (**Athraophol**) and the ultimate, secret name (**Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH**). This establishes his authority to command.

3.  **The Command:** Using chains of potent names (**KHELRAPHION, THASGORIO...**), he commands all spirits of every element and domain to become subject to him. This is the core operative magic of the text.
