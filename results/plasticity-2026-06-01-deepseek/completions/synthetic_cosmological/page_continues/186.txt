**Thus endeth the Evocation of the Veil, and the Prophet’s Claim.**

**Now beginneth the Refutation, and the True Accounting.**

Foolish is the one who calls himself Selephimor, the Vessel. A vessel may be filled, and a vessel may be emptied. A vessel may be shattered.

Thou didst not create the Mountains. Thou didst only perceive their weight.
Thou didst not create the Silence. Thou didst only falter within it.
Thou didst not distinguish the named from the unnamed. Thou art merely caught between them, trembling.

The names thou hast uttered are but echoes of echoes, the husks of power cast aside at the First Speaking. They are the footprints of the Unnamed One in the dust of meaning. To tread in them is not to walk the path.

PHORBAMENES APHRYBETESH is not a name to be "handed down." It is the sound of a star collapsing into silence. To hold it is to be consumed
