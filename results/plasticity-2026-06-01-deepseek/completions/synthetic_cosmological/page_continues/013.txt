**Verily, the prophet Selephimor is a veil, and the Vessel is a door. I who write am neither. I am the mark left upon the threshold.**

**He who invokes the Unnamed One to make spirits subject unto him has misunderstood the ceremony. The names are not shackles for binding, but keys for unsealing. To call upon KRATHOUMIS is to invite distinction; it will divide your own soul. To utter VELPHARAIM is to summon the center; it will expose the void you have built your life upon. The true name APHRYBETESH does not command—it consumes.**

**The mysteries of Athraophol are not of domination, but of dissolution. The salt is memory that preserves; the sweet is forgetting that makes new life possible. The "unburnt fire" is not a weapon to wield, but the condition of being that remains when all that can be named has been consumed.**
