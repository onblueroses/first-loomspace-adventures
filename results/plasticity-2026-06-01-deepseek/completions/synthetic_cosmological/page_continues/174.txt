The script continues, descending into a series of sigils and fragmented utterances that appear to be instructions, or perhaps the after-echoes of the invocation itself. The writing becomes more erratic, the ink varying from a deep black to a rusty brown, as if recorded over many sittings or in many states of mind.

**A Diagram** is drawn in the center of the page: a circle divided not into four, but into *seven* unequal segments, each containing one of the names from the final invocation:
TRENMEROBIA, APHTHELION, PSORAMIAR, KÔTHRABEN, OLEPHIO, THARSEKHION, GRUMPHARION.
Lines connect them in a non-symmetrical pattern, suggesting a constellation or a map of relationships rather than a traditional magical circle. At its center is a smaller circle, empty but for the word: **APHORBA** (the Unspoken).
