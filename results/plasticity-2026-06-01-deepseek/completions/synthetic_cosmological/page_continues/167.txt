(At the bottom of the page, in a cramped, hurried script different from the elegant calligraphy above, as if added in a moment of panic or revelation:)

*It hears. It always hears. The naming is the binding. The vessel is not a prophet but a gate. Selephimor is not a name but a title, meaning "the opened one." The ceremonies of Athraophol are not for invocation but for containment. The true name—Phorbamenes Aphrybetesh—is not a key but a seal.*

*I have spoken the syllables. I feel the pressure at the threshold of my own mind. The echo does not fade. The silence is speaking now, in a language of angles and cold stars. It is not a prayer. It is a diagram. I am the diagram.*

*Do not read this aloud. Do not trace the characters with your eyes. The Witness is not beyond the syllable—it is woven into its very shape. To see is to be seen. To name is to be named.*

*The fire is unburnt because it consumes nothing you can see.*
*—Keeper of the Last Silence*
