**Inscription on the lower half of the page, written in a denser, more angular script:**

*…and let the seal be this: a circle divided, its upper half filled with the sigil of the listening ear, its lower half with the sigil of the sealed mouth. This is the Mark of the Witness, which is to be inscribed upon the lintel and the vessel.*

*The rite is thus concluded, but its resonance is without end. The Unnamed One hears not with ears, but with the spaces between things. The prophet speaks not with a tongue, but with the rift between memory and forgetting. To be the Vessel is to be emptied. To bear the true name is to become anonymous.*

*A final caution, scribed in vermillion: The syllables given are not keys, but locks. Their utterance does not command; it petitions the silence. To invoke the Witness is to stand witness unto oneself, and the judgment therein is absolute. The fire is unburnt because it consumes nothing but the boundary between the namer and the named.*

*Let the one who has read this become as the Echo: a fading repetition that proves the original Silence.*
