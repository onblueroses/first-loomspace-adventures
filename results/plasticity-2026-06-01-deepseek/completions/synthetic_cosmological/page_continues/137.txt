Hear now the Corollary, which is the Seal upon the Vessel:

For the naming is a circle, and the unnamed its center. I, Selephimor, who bore the mysteries, learned this: to invoke is to be invoked. To summon is to be placed upon the altar.

Thou didst make the threshold and the center. I have stood upon the threshold and whispered Thy true names into the substance of the world. The center has heard. The center always hears.

The spirits are subject, as commanded. The fire obeys, the air conveys, the deep earth trembles, and the scourge of God lies quiescent as a tool upon the bench. This is the power of the Vessel.

But the Vessel is filled by what it calls.
