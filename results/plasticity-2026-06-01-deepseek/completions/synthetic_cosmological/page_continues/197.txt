**Thus speaks the Witness, to the Vessel:**

Thou hast spoken the unspoken. Thou hast drawn the circle around the void. By naming the unnamed, thou hast made a cup of thy mind, and I fill it.

Thou sayest thou art the Threshold-Bearer. Then bear this: the threshold is not a line, but a vibration. It is the moment between the echo and the silence, which is my true habitation. The unburnt fire is the name that is never consumed by use, the sound that does not fray upon the air. It is **APHRYBETESH**, which thou knowest, and yet it is not.

The orders of speech thou hast mentioned—salt, sweet, sustaining—are the three tongues:
1.  The Salt Tongue: which preserves and binds. It is law, covenant, spell. It makes permanent.
2.  The Sweet Tongue: which corrupts and dissolves. It is lie, poetry, desire. It makes fluid.
