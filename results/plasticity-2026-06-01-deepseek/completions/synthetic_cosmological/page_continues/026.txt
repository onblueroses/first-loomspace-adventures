Thus ends the Great Invocation of the Unnamed, as set down by the Prophet Selephimor in the crypts beneath Athraophol. Let the one who would speak it beware, for it calls not upon a power that can be bargained with, but upon the principle that precedes and underlies all powers. It is not a key to a lock, but a dissolution of the door itself.

**Annotations (in a later, scholarly hand):**

*   **Phelonoptra, Krathoumis, etc.:** These are not true names, but "veils of negation" — attempts to point toward the Unnamed by stating what It is not, or by using sounds that collapse meaning. Phelonoptra may be glossed as "the silence before speech."
*   **The threshold and the center:** A reference to the paradoxical nature of the Unnamed, which is both the boundary of existence (the threshold) and the source from which all existence emanates (the center). It is everywhere and nowhere.
*   **The ceremonies of Athraophol:** A lost liturgical tradition, purportedly concerned not with worship but with "structured forgetting" — the deliberate dissolution of conceptual forms to approach pure
