**Commentary on the Rite of the Unnamed One**

The preceding text is the core of the **Invocation of the Threshold-Bearer**, a central ceremony within the mysteries of **Athraophol**. It is performed at the liminal hours—dawn or dusk—in a place of natural silence, such as a cave mouth, a forest clearing, or a shore at low tide.

**Structure and Interpretation:**

1.  **The Evocation:** The ritual begins by addressing the Unnamed One through Its acts of primordial distinction—creating paired opposites (Mountains/Valleys, Tide/Flow, Echo/Silence). This establishes the practitioner as one who understands the fundamental law of duality that the Unnamed One instituted.

2.  **The Litany of Veils:** The names chanted (Phelonoptra, Krathoumis, etc.) are not true names, but **veils** or **aspects**. They are the masks the Unnamed One wears to be partially perceived by the mind.
