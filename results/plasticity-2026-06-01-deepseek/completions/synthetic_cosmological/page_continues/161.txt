**Concerning the Ceremonies of Athraophol**

He who would be a Vessel must first become an Echo. Stand in a place of stone where your voice returns to you thrice. Speak not your own name, but the first sound you hear upon waking. This is your Root-Syllable. Guard it.

The Unnamed One perceives not through ears, but through the hollows shaped by sound. To invoke is to carve a hollow in the air with the chisel of the breath. The true names are not for calling, but for creating these hollows—receptacles for that which has no name.
