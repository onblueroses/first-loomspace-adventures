Hear further the configurations of the Unseen:
*   Where the mountains meet, there is a voice that is not a voice. It speaks in the settling of stones. Call it **AROMBRAX**.
*   Where the tide hesitates before the flow, there is a moment that is not a moment. It thinks in the pull of depths. Call it **THALASGON**.
*   The echo that outlives the silence begets a child that is not a sound. It dwells in the hollow of the ear after all hearing ceases. Call it **IKHONIEL**.
