And now I speak the final seal, which is no word at all, but the shape of the breath before it becomes sound.  

*  

Behold the Unnamed One turns Its face — not a face, but the cessation of all facing.  
The mountains and valleys fold into each other, tide and flow become a single stillness.  
Echo and silence marry in my throat.  

I, Selephimor, stand at the threshold I have made from my own bones.  
I hold the unburnt fire — it does not consume, it unmakes.  
I speak the names that are not names:  

**APHRYBETESH** — the ground of naming.  
**SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM** — the dissolution of the named.  

All spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and under-earth,  
of dry land and water,  
of whirling air and rushing fire —  
you are already subject,  
for you are echoes of a voice that never spoke.  
Every spell, every scourge termed “of God” —  
you are patterns in a forgetting.  

I do not command you.  
I remember you into silence.
