And the page continues, unfurling like a tongue of forgotten vellum:

*…for the Echo is not the Voice, and the Silence is not the Void. Between them, I have stretched the skin of the world, and upon it is written the law that is no law:*

***That which is Named may be commanded.***  
***That which is Unnamed may only be invoked.***  
***That which is Both is the Vessel, and the Vessel is the Gate.***

*I, Selephimor, have walked the circumference where the fire does not burn and the water does not drown. I have tasted the salt of memory on the lips of the mountain and the sweet sap of forgetting from the root of the valley. I have held the unburnt fire—not as a flame, but as a syllable held in the mouth, a pressure behind the eyes.*

*The ceremonies of Athraophol are these:*

*First, to find the center that is also the threshold. This is a place in the world that is also a place in the mind. A crossroads at midnight, the hinge of a door in a ruin, the moment between one breath and the next.*
