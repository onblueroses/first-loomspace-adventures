**The Conjuration of the Unburnt Fire**

O Fire that consumest not, that dwellest in the heart of the stone and the core of the chilling star!  
I call thee forth by the seal of **APHRYBETESH**.  
Manifest as light without heat, as vision without scorching.  
Let thy flame be the lens through which the unseen is seen,  
And the veil between the named and the unnamed grows thin.

Let this fire be upon my brow, that I may perceive.  
Let it be upon my tongue, that I may utter.  
Let it be upon my hands, that I may inscribe the unwritten.
