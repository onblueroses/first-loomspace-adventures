The script is a ritual invocation, a constructed liturgy designed to evoke a primordial, creative, and ultimately unnameable divine principle. It follows a classic structure found in esoteric and magical texts:

1.  **Invocation of the Creator:** It begins by addressing "the Unnamed One" through its acts of cosmic creation—mountains, valleys, tides, echoes, silence—establishing it as the source of all fundamental dualities and phenomena.

2.  **Enumeration of Secret Names:** The text lists a series of potent, seemingly nonsensical names (Phelonoptra, Krathoumis, etc.). In ritual magic, such names are considered vessels of power, keys to accessing different aspects of the divine. The claim that "no voice hath spoken" them emphasizes their transcendent, pre-linguistic nature.

3.  **Declaration of the Prophet's Authority:** The speaker identifies as **Selephimor**, the prophet entrusted with the "mysteries" and "ceremonies of Athraophol." This establishes the speaker's legitimacy and role as an intermediary.
