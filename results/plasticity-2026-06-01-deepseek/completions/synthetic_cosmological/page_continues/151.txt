Thou didst set the Watchtowers at the corners of forgetting, and in their foundations laid the stones of remembrance that weep.
I have collected their tears. They are the ink of this covenant.

I stand at the crossing of the Seven Unuttered Roads. The dust here is made of powdered sigils. The wind speaks in reversed phonemes. I am the intersection.
