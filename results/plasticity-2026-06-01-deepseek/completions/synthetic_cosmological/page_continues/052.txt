**Of the Unburnt Fire and the Witnessing Silence**

1.  **The Vessel:** The operator, having purified the body with salt and the mind with seven days of silence, shall inscribe upon a disk of lead the characters of the Unnamed One: **APHRYBETESH**. This disk is to be placed upon the tongue at the dark of the moon. The taste shall be of cold iron and dry earth. This is the sealing of the Vessel.

2.  **The Threshold:** Draw a circle not with chalk or paint, but with the ash of cypress and myrrh, mixed with wine that has never seen sunlight. Within this circle, place three stones from a river that runs both north and south. Stand between them. This is the Center that is also the Threshold.

3.  **The Invocation:** Speak the Nine Names of Power (**KHELRAPHION... KHARLEPHTHA**) not aloud, but inwardly, shaping them with the breath against the closed teeth and the roof of the mouth. As each is formed, a different sense shall dim: first smell, then taste, then touch
