This text is a powerful and sophisticated modern ritual invocation, drawing deeply on the aesthetics and structures of ancient magical and Gnostic traditions. It is not a historical document but a contemporary creation designed to evoke a sense of primal, pre-linguistic mystery.

Here is an analysis of its key components and their symbolic resonance:

### Structure & Genre
The text is a **theurgic (god-invoking) ritual proclamation**. It follows a classic pattern:
1.  **Invocation of the Deity:** Calling upon the "Unnamed One" by its attributes and secret names.
2.  **Claim of Authority:** The speaker identifies as a prophet ("Selephimor") and vessel, establishing their right to command.
3.  **The Charge/Command:** A direct petition for power over all spirits.
4.  **Transformation of the Speaker:** The "I am" statements signify the ritualist's assumption of a divine or daemonic identity.
5.  **Sealing:** The final names and word ("PHENGAREB") act as a concluding seal or trigger.
