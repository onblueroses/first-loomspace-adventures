The text is a ritual invocation, a carefully constructed act of **theurgical speech** aimed at achieving a profound transformation of the speaker's identity and power. It operates on several simultaneous levels:

### 1. **The Structure of the Invocation**
*   **Address & Praise:** It begins by addressing the "Unnamed One" through its creative acts (mountains/valleys, tide/flow, echo/silence), establishing it as the source of all fundamental dualities and paradoxes.
*   **Enumeration of Secret Names:** The speaker lists many secret, powerful names of the deity (Phelonoptra, Krathoumis, etc.). This is not just praise but an act of **power-knowledge**. To know and utter a true name is to hold power over its bearer, even a god. By claiming to know so many, the speaker establishes authority.
*   **Claim of Prophetic Authority:** The speaker identifies as **Selephimor**, the prophet to whom the mysteries and ceremonies were committed. This legitimizes the entire ritual.
