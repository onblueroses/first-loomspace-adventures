**Of the Ceremonies of Athraophol**

The First Ceremony is of the Salt. Take of the bitter earth and purify it seven times in the tears of a dream not remembered. Its geometry is a perfect cube, and it is placed at the northern point. It is the principle of preservation and stasis; it holds the form against the flow. The spirit bound with salt may not dissipate, but it may not speak.

The Second Ceremony is of the Sweet. Take of the honey that drips from hives built in the ribs of fallen giants, and mix it with the sap of the Tree That Grows Inward. Its geometry is a sphere, and it is placed at the southern point. It is the principle of attraction and assimilation; it draws the nameless toward form. The spirit touched with the sweet forgets its wildness and seeks a tongue.
