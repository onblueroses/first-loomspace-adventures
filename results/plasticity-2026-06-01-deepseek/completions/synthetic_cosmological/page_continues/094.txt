**Annotations on MS. Athanor Frag. 17, "The Selephimoric Invocation"**

**Provenance:** Recovered from the lacquered sarcophagus of the high priest Ankhef-en-Maat (21st Dynasty, Thebes), interred with ritual obsidian blades and seven unmarked clay seals. The papyrus was found placed over the face, the final word aligned with the mouth. Carbon dating places the material in the 10th century BCE, though the linguistic strata suggest a far older oral tradition.

**Linguistic Analysis:** The text employs a multi-layered cryptographic system. The primary "invocatory" language is a constructed liturgical tongue with roots in hypothetical Proto-Aegean and early Semitic ritual forms.
