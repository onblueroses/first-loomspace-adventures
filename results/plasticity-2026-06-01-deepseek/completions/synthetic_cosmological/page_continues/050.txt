And the page continues, written in a hand that seems less like ink and more like shadow upon the vellum:

*For I am the one who has seen the foundations of the speaking world, and the mortar between its stones is made of forgotten names. The ceremonies of Athraophol are not rites of power, but rites of listening. To hear the echo before the sound. To taste the salt before the tear.*

*Thou, Unnamed One, didst not create opposites, but the space between them. The tide is not the flow, but the turning point. The echo is not the silence, but its memory. The threshold is not the center, but its possibility.*

*I, Selephimor, am no prophet of proclamation, but of the question. The mysteries committed unto me are not answers, but vessels for a thirst. The true name, PHORBAMENES APHRYBETESH, is not a sound to be spoken, but a shape left in the air after all sound has ceased. It is the name of the listening itself.*

*The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are not commanded by the syllables KHELRAPHION, THASGORIO, NEKTAMBARÔ…
