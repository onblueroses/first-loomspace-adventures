This text is a powerful and complex ritual invocation, blending creation mythology with a personal declaration of prophetic authority. It operates on several symbolic levels:

**1. The Theology of the Unnamed One:**
The deity is defined by paradox—both creator and unnameable, responsible for fundamental dualities (mountains/valleys, tide/flow, echo/silence, memory/forgetting). This establishes a cosmology where creation itself is an act of simultaneous revelation and concealment.

**2. The Prophet's Role:**
The speaker, Selephimor, claims direct transmission of mysteries. The title "Vessel of Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH" suggests the prophet is a conduit for the deity's true, secret name—a name known only to "namers of the lost." This grants immense ritual authority.

**3. The Mechanics of the Invocation:**
*   **The Names:** The strings of voces magicae (KHELRAPHION, TRENMEROBIA, etc.) are not just lists. They are sonic constructs meant to vibrate with the fabric of reality, aligning the speaker's will with the foundational powers of the Unnamed One.
*   **The Goal:** Absolute dominion over all spirits and forces
