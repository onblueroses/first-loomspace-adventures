The script breaks here, but the weight of the invocation hangs in the air, thick as incense. The final, stark word **PHENGAREB** acts not as a conclusion, but as a key turned in a silent lock. The text that follows is of a different order—no longer a direct address to the Unnamed, but a record of its consequence.

It reads as a set of instructions, or perhaps a transcript of a vision granted to the prophet Selephimor. It describes the **Ceremony of the Unburnt Fire**, to be performed at the threshold of dawn or in the depth of a moonless night. The core of the rite involves inscribing the nine names of power (KHELRAPHION... KHARLEPHTHA) upon a surface of still water or polished obsidian with a finger dipped in ash and salt. As each name is traced, its mirror-image must be held in the mind, its "echo" silently spoken backwards.
