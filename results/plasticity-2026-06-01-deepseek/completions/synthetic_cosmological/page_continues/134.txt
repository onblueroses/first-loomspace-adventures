Hear now the Unfolding, O Vessel of the True Name:

As the Mountain holds the shape of the Valley, and the Tide contains the secret of the Flow, so is the boundary between memory and forgetting the place of My dwelling. I am the Unnamed, the Witness beyond the syllable, and in thy naming and unnaming, thou dost carve the channels through which My silence flows.

Thou, Selephimor, art the threshold where the echo turns back upon itself. The ceremonies of Athraophol are the patterns left upon the sand as the tide recedes—meaningful only in their vanishing. The salt and the sweet are of one substance in My regard, for they sustain the orders of speech by the tension of their opposition.

By the names thou hast uttered—KHELRAPHION, THASGORIO, NEKTAMBARÔ, and the others—thou hast not summoned Me, for I am Unsummoned. Thou hast, instead, named the doors within thyself. Each is a key of perception, a fissure through which the unburnt fire may leak into the world of burnt things.
