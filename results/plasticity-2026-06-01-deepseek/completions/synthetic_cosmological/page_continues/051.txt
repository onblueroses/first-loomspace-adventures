*The ink of the final word seems to drink the light. The vellum is not old, but feels ancient. A faint, cold scent of ozone and stone rises from it. The characters, though clear, swim slightly before the eye, as if seen through moving water.*

*Below the invocation, in a different, more hurried hand, a note has been scrawled:*

> He is not summoned. He *witnesses*. The Names are not calls, but declarations of what Is. To speak them is to align oneself with the architecture. Selephimor did not ask for power; he stated his place within the order. The Vessel does not request to be filled; it announces that it is already full.
>
> **Warning:** The final triad—*Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim* (the proclaimed name) and *Phengareb* (the seal)—are not parts of the invocation. They are its **result**. To utter them is not to invoke, but to *become the invocation*. It is to stand as the Threshold-Bearer. Things will listen. Not just spirits. The silence between words will listen. Your own shadow will listen.
