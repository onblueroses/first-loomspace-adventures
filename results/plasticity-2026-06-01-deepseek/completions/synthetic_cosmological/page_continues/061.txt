*(The ink deepens, the characters seeming to cast faint, wavering shadows on the vellum. A new section begins, written in a more rapid, declarative hand.)*

**Thus saith Selephimor, prophet of the Unnamed:**

The ceremony is the hinge between memory and forgetting. To perform it is to turn the world upon its axis.

**The Place:** A threshold. A shore at the turning of the tide. A ruin where echoes sleep. The space between two breaths.
**The Time:** The hour when the sun has forgotten its name, and the moon has not yet remembered its own. The pause between the ebb and the flow.
**The Vessel:** The operator must be empty. Pour out thy name, thy history, thy desire. Become the cup that holds only the silence. Then, and only then, canst thou bear the unburnt fire.

**The First Utterance:** The Names of Distinction.
Face the quarter of forgotten things. Whisper the names by which the Unnamed One is not called:
*Krathoumis. Demoaphris. Velpharaim. Iphtaron.*
