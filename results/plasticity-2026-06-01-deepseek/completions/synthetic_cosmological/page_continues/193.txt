The text is a ritual invocation, a constructed liturgy meant to evoke a primordial, creative deity whose essence is paradoxically both naming and un-naming, remembering and forgetting. It follows a classic structure of theurgical or magical prayer:

1.  **Invocation of the Creator:** The speaker calls upon the "Unnamed One" by Its acts of creation—mountains, valleys, tides, echoes—establishing Its fundamental power over the cosmos and its dualities.

2.  **Enumeration of Secret Names:** The deity is addressed by a series of presumably potent, secret names (Phelonoptra, Krathoumis, etc.). This act asserts a relationship between the invoker and the invoked, suggesting the speaker possesses privileged knowledge.

3.  **Declaration of the Deity's Nature:** The core theology is stated: this entity distinguishes between named and unnamed, creates thresholds and centers, and is responsible for memory *and* forgetting. This positions the deity as the source of all distinction and meaning, and also of its dissolution.

4.  **Claim of Authority:** The speaker identifies as **Selephimor**, the prophet entrusted with the deity's mysteries (the ceremonies of Athraophol). This is a claim of lineage and legitimacy.
