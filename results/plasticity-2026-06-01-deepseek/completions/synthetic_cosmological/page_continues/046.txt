Thus speaks the Vessel, whose name is a seal and a key.

Hear now the Law of the Unnamed, given to Selephimor in the desert of Zephar, where the sands are ground glass and the stars are holes in a greater dark:

1.  **The First Law:** That which is Named is bound. That which is Unnamed is free. Therefore, the wise binder gives false names, and the true liberator withholds all names.
2.  **The Second Law:** The center is a moving point. The threshold is a fixed line. All power flows from crossing the fixed line to occupy the moving point. Stand neither within nor without, but upon the line itself, and you command the center’s dance.
3.  **The Third Law:** Memory is architecture. Forgetting is the solvent. A world built only of memory becomes a rigid tomb. A world built only of forgetting becomes a formless scream. Thou didst form the world to remember *and* to forget itself. This is the great breath.
