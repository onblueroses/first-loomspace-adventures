And the page continues, the ink dark as pooled blood on the ancient vellum:

> ...For the Fire that is Unburnt dwelleth not on the altar, but in the space between the breath and the word. It is the province of the Threshold-Bearer, whose feet are planted neither in the Named nor the Unnamed, but upon the line that divides them, which is the edge of all creation.
>
> **The Ceremony of Athraophol** is thus: Take of the salt and the sweet, of memory and of forgetting, and mingle them in a vessel of silence. Speak the first syllable of a name, and let the last be swallowed by the echo. That which remains in the center—unspoken, unheard—is the offering. This is the sustenance of the orders of speech.
>
> I, Selephimor, decree that these mysteries shall be guarded by the **Veil of Phorbamenes**, which is not a cloth, but a manner of listening. To hear the true name **APHRYBETESH** is not to hear sound, but to perceive the shape of the silence from which it was carved. The namers of the lost did not utter it; they became vacant for it
