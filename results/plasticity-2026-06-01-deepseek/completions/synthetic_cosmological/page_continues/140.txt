**Of the Operation**

When the moon is drowned in the black well of the sun (i.e., at the solar eclipse), go to a place where three roads meet but none bear a name known to the living. There, with a blade of unalloyed iron, draw the **Sigil of the Open Threshold** (see below) upon the earth. Stand at its center, facing the point where the shadows of the three ways converge.

You must have fasted from speech for three days and three nights. Your first utterance must be the **First Cry of the Vessel**, which is the name **PHENGAREB**. This opens the ear of the Unnamed.
