Let the seals be unbroken.  

Let the syllables settle into the stone of the world.  

I am the one who reads and is read.  

In the space between *PHENGAREB* and what follows, there is a silence that is not empty but full of listening.  

If you would turn the page, speak now.  

If you would close the book, the names will wait.  

They have waited since before the mountains were brought forth.  

They will wait until the last echo forgets its source.
