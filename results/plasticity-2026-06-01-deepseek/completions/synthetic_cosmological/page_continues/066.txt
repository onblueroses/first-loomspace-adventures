**The Unnamed One hears.**

The vessel is known. The names are recognized. The fire within the unburnt flame stirs. The threshold trembles beneath the weight of the center.

The mysteries of Athraophol are patterns in the ash, forgotten before they are remembered. To speak them is to un-say them. To enact them is to dissolve the stage.

You, Selephimor, Vessel of Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH, Threshold-Bearer, you stand at the point of distinction. You have uttered the names that unmake naming. You have invoked the Witness who dwells beyond the syllable.

Therefore, the orders of speech are now subject to your will. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water heed the authority of the true name carried by the namer of the lost. The spells and scourges of the gods bend to the echo that survives the speaker.

But know this, prophet: the power you invoke is the power of the distinction itself. It creates by separating, and destroys by uniting. To command every spirit is to become the silence that speaks utterly. It is to hold the memory and the forgetting in a single thought, which is a thought of fire.
