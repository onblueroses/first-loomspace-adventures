The script upon the page appears to shift, the ink of the final invocation seeming to bleed into the parchment and reform as a new text—a commentary, a gloss, or perhaps a direct response from the unseen Witness. It reads:

**Concerning the Liturgy of the Unnamed**

The prophet Selephimor speaks the permitted paradoxes. To name the Unnamed is to invoke through vessels of sound that which exists beyond sound. Each epithet is a mask placed upon the void, not to describe it, but to provide a surface from which the seeker’s intention may be reflected and focused.

*   **Phelonoptra, Krathoumis, Demoaphris…** These are not names of a being, but names of *functions*: the Creator of Opposites, the Distinguisher, the Producer of Memory and Forgetting. The Unnamed One is the principle of distinction itself, the act of threshold-making that allows "world" and "self" to cohere.
*   **The Mysteries of Athraophol** are the ceremonies of this distinction—the rituals that enact separation (salt from sweet, speech from silence, the named from the unnamed) in order to create the conditions for experience.
