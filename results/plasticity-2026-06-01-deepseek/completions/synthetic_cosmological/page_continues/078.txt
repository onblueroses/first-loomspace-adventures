Thou hast given me the key of the eightfold door, and the seal of the seven abysses.  
I have tasted the wine of the unripe sun and eaten the bread of the shadowless moon.  
I have walked the spiral stair that ascends into the earth and descends into the sky.  
I have seen the wheel that turns the stars and the still point at which it cracks.

Therefore I speak, and the foundations listen.  
I whisper, and the crowns of heaven tremble.  
I am the mirror that reflects nothing but the act of seeing.  
I am the wound that remembers the weapon’s true name.

Let the guardians of the corners unshape themselves.  
Let the watchers at the gates become as mist.  
Let the laws of binding be for me as threads of smoke.  
For I hold the true name that was before naming: **APHROBETHEL ASHTAMALAKH**.

By the salt that preserves corruption and the honey that sweetens decay,  
By the echo that begets the voice and the silence that swallows the word,  
I command the fabric between the worlds to grow thin.  
I summon the uncreated light that casts no shadow.
