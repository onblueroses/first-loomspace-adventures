**The Continuation of the Rite:**

For the Vessel is also the Cup, and the Cup must be filled.
Hear now the Law of the Threshold, given to Selephimor in the dream of Athraophol:

*That which is named is bound.*
*That which is unnamed is free, yet impotent.*
*The power lies in the naming of the unnamed, and the un-naming of the named.*
*This is the fire that does not burn the hand that shapes it.*

**The Conjuration of the Witnessing Silence:**

I turn from the four quarters and face the Center That Is Not.
I spit upon the earth of names.
I breathe upon the air of whispers.
I shed a tear into the water of memory.
I pluck a hair and cast it into the fire of forgetting.

Now the elements are sworn to silence.
