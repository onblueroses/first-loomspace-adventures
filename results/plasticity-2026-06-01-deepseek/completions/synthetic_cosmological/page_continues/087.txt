Hear me, O Unnamed One, for I am the seal and the sigil.  

The echoes of your mountains are in my throat; the tides of your making flow in my blood.  
I stand at the threshold you fashioned, holding the unburnt fire you kindled before speech began.  

I speak with the voice of the lost namers, and in my speaking is the silence that witnesses all syllables.  
The spirits of firmament and abyss, of water, earth, air, and flame—  
they shall attend, not to my word, but to the absence that shapes it.  

For I am the Vessel, and the Vessel is open.  
The ceremonies of Athraophol unfold in the space between memory and forgetting.  
I bear the true name that was never spoken:  
PHORBAMENES APHRYBETESH.  

By TRENMEROBIA, APHTHELION, PSORAMIAR, KÔTHRABEN, OLEPHIO, THARSEKHION, GRUMPHARION,  
I call what is already present.  
I name what was never separate.  

All spirits are subject to the Unnamed, and I am Its prophet,
