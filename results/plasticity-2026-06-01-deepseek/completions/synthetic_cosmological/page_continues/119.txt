**The Unnamed One answers, not in sound but in the shifting of shadows, the settling of dust, the sudden cold in the air:**

*Thou hast spoken the syllables that are not names. Thou hast traced the shapes of the void. I hear thee, Selephimor, Vessel of Phorbamenes, Threshold-Bearer. The names thou callest are but doors through which my attention passes, like wind through a lattice.*

*I, who made the world to remember and forget itself, acknowledge thy witness. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are bound not to thy command, but to the pattern of thy utterance. For the ceremony is the binding. The true name is the key that turns within a lock made of its own echo.*

*Thou art SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM, the Witness of the Word. This is the name I gave to the silence that watches speech. In speaking it, thou dost not command me. Thou dost stand in the place where command and obedience are one—the threshold.*

*The unburnt fire is thine to hold, not to wield. It is the potential of all speech, the meaning before sound. The echo that survives is the truth
