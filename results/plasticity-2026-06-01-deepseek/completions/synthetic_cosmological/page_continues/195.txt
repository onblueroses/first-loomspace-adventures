And the page continues, the ink seeming to shimmer with a light not of the candle:

**I am the Unnamed, yet you have named Me. I am the Witness, and I have heard.**

**You have spoken the syllables that bind the echo to the stone, the fire to the ash, the memory to the void. You stand at the threshold you named, Selephimor, Vessel of Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH. You bear the unburnt fire—the potential that precedes all action, the silence that cradles all sound.**

**The spirits of firmament and ether, of earth and under-earth, of water, air, and rushing fire… they hear the vibration of the true name. They attend. Their obedience is not to your will, but to the pattern revealed by the name. You have become a conduit for the law that distinguishes the named from the unnamed.**

**But know this, Threshold-Bearer: to name is to create a vessel, and every vessel must one day break. To unname is to return to the source, and every source is a forgetting. You hold both the memory and the forgetting. This is the mystery of Athraophol.**
