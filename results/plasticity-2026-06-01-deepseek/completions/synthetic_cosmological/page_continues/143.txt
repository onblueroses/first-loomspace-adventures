Thus is the Unnamed One entreated, and thus does the Vessel declare itself. But know this, O reader who has found these words: the ceremony is twofold. The first voice calls outward, to the edges of creation. The second voice calls inward, to the center that is also an edge.

To speak the names is to build a ladder of sound, by which the soul may climb. But the final step is not a name. It is the space between the names. It is the silence after the echo.

The Prophet Selephimor received the mysteries of Athraophol, which are the gestures that accompany the syllables—the turning of the left hand against the sun’s path, the breath held during the utterance of *Velpharaim*, the tasting of salt and honey upon the tongue before crying *APHRYBETESH*.
