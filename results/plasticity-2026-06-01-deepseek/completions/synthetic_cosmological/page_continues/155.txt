*(The script flows into an intricate diagram: concentric circles of glyphs, like ripples from a stone dropped in dark water. At the outermost ring are the names of the elements, warped and woven into sigils. The next ring holds the names from the invocation: KHELRAPHION, THASGORIO, NEKTAMBARÔ... each connected by lines of silver ink that pulse faintly, as if breathing. The inner rings depict abstract forms—a door without a wall, an eye within an ear, a flame contained within a droplet of ice.)*
