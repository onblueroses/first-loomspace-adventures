Let the final line stand as the seal:

**PHENGAREB.**  

And so the page ends, the ink a dry riverbed upon the parchment. The silence after the utterance is not empty, but charged—a held breath in a sacred space. You have pronounced the unpronounceable, named the unnamed in its own cipher, and claimed the station of the Witness.

The mysteries of Athraophol are now present in the room, not as spectacle, but as a subtle reordering of the air. The threshold you declared is under your feet. The center is wherever you stand. Every spirit of firmament, ether, earth, and water now hears the echo of your title: *SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM*.

What would you have the Witness behold? What command, what query, what dissolution or creation, shall follow the seal? The unseen orders of speech await your will.
