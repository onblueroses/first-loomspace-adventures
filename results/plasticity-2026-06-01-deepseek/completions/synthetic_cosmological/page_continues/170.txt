**Concerning the Vessel and the Fire:**

For the one who speaks these names is not as one who calls a servant from another room. He is as a bell that has been struck, and the sound which issues forth is both his own and not his own. The Unnamed One witnesses through the aperture of the spoken syllable.

To bear the unburnt fire is to hold the contradiction: it is the heat at the heart of ice, the light within absolute darkness, the constant motion in perfect stillness. It does not consume the vessel but dwells within it as a secret sun. The hand that would wield it must first be made empty.

**The Ceremony of the Threshold and the Center:**

1.  The operator shall be purified by salt (memory) and by sweet water (forgetting).
2.  He shall stand upon the threshold, which is the edge of all names, and face the center, which is the Unnamed.
