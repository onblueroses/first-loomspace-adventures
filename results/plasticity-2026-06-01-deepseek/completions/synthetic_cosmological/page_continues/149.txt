*The script shifts into a dense, diagrammatic form—circles within circles, intersecting lines, and words arranged not in sentences but as points on a geometric lattice. At the center, in a darker, more fluid ink, is written:*

**The Seal of the Unburnt Fire**

*Around it, sigils pulse with implied meaning:*

*   **The Outer Ring:** Contains the names from the invocation—KHELRAPHION, THASGORIO, NEKTAMBARÔ—not as words to be spoken, but as fixed stars in a constellated fence.
*   **The Middle Ring:** Depicts the elemental spirits referenced—those of the firmament, ether, earth, water, air, and fire—as stylized, bound figures, their lines converging on the center.
*   **The Inner Ring:** Holds the paradoxes: a closed eye that sees, a mouth sewn shut in mid-speech, a door that is also a pillar, a flame drawn in un-ink that seems to shimmer on the page.
