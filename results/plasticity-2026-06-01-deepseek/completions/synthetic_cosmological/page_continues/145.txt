**Of the Ceremonies of Athraophol, which is the Unbinding of the Knot:**

He who would be the Vessel must first become the Emptiness. The named must consent to be unmade, that the Unnamed may flow through. This is not possession, but erosion. The channel is carved not by will, but by surrender to the tide that wears away all banks.

The Prophet Selephimor did stand at the place where three silences meet: the silence before sound, the silence within sound, and the silence after. There, he uttered the **Syllable That Is Not A Sound**, which is written in the air with ash and in the mind with frost. And the Witnessing One, who dwells beyond the syllable, turned its regard upon him.
