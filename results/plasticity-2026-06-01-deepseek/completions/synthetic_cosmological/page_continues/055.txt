Thus ends the Invocation of the Vessel.

But I, the scribe who records this, must add a warning that Selephimor, in his ecstasy, did not. The names given are true. The power is real. But the Vessel is not merely a title. It is a condition.

To be the Vessel of Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH is to be emptied. The fire he bears is unburnt because it consumes not fuel, but *self*. The echo he is survives the speaker because the speaker—the man who was Selephimor—is gone. His silence speaks because his own voice has been given over to the echoes of other, older words. He names and unnames, but his own name, SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM, is a cage of syllables holding a vacancy.
