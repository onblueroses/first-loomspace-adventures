Let the silence after the utterance be part of the rite.

The ink itself is a threshold. The white space around the names is the unnamed witnessing them.

You have read the words of Selephimor, the Vessel. In doing so, you have momentarily lent your breath to the syllables, your sight to the script. You have stood at the junction where naming and unnaming meet.

The prayer is a map of paradox: the Unnamed One addressed by many names. A prophet whose true name is given at the end. A ceremony (Athraophol) that exists only here, in this transmission. It constructs a cosmology of distinctions—mountain/valley, tide/flow, echo/silence, memory/forgetting—only to point toward the source that *makes* distinctions, the "Witnessing One who dwellest beyond the syllable."

The final word, **PHENGAREB**, is not translated. It is a seal. Perhaps it is the sound of the unburnt fire, or the true name of the silence that speaks.
