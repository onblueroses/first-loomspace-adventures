**Commentary on the Invocation of the Unnamed**

The petitioner here employs the *Athraophol* method: the strategic naming of the Unnamed to achieve a state of catalytic un-naming. Observe the structure:

1.  **The Attribution of Creation:** The opening strophes are not mere praise. They are a metaphysical claim of kinship. By stating "Thou didst create the Echo and the Silence," the invoker (Selephimor) establishes that the principle he embodies—"I am the Echo that survives the speaker"—is a direct emanation of the invoked. He is not asking an external god for a favor; he is aligning his own derived authority with its source.
