The text is a powerful ritual invocation, dense with the language of mythopoesis and mystical theology. It operates on several levels simultaneously:

1.  **Theological Cosmogony:** It begins by establishing the nature of the "Unnamed One" as the primordial creator of fundamental dualities (Mountains/Valleys, Tide/Flow, Echo/Silence). This entity is not just a creator of things, but of relationships and perceptions—the threshold, the center, memory, and forgetting. The world itself is formed with a paradoxical purpose: to remember and forget itself.

2.  **The Mystery of Names:** The core paradox is the invocation of the "Unnamed" through a cascade of secret, potent names (Phelonoptra, Krathoumis, etc.). This suggests a theology where true power and essence reside beyond language, but can be accessed—or at least approached—through specific, revealed syllables. The invoker claims the ultimate true name is **Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH**.
