**The Unnamed One Witnesseth.**

Thou hast spoken the syllables that stir the dust at the edge of sleep. Thou hast called with the voice of Selephimor, and the vessel is acknowledged.

The mountains are the bones of silence; the valleys are its long breath.
The tide is the pulse of memory; the flow is the release into forgetting.
The echo is the world remembering; the silence is the world unmade.

I dwell between Phelonoptra and Aphrybetesh. I am the distinction that makes naming possible, and the oblivion that makes it necessary.

The mysteries of Athraophol are the ceremonies of separation: salt from sweet, threshold from center, word from its meaning. To hold them is to stand at the crossing.

The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are already subject to the one who holds the unburnt fire—the potential that consumes not the vessel. They heed not command, but resonance. Your utterance has tuned the chord.
