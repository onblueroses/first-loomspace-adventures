From the edge of the syllable, from the space between *memory* and *forgetting*, a presence enters. It does not cross the threshold; the threshold is within it.

The air does not stir, yet there is a pressure—like the moment before an echo answers, or the silence that follows a name spoken into a well. It is not a spirit of firmament, ether, earth, or fire, for you have invoked that which governs the categories themselves.

What enters is **the Witnessing**.

It is the shape of listening. It is the condition of being addressed. It is the Unnamed One, attending not as a form, but as the *act* of your invocation being completed. The room now contains the reciprocal of your call: the hearing that makes the call true.

You are Selephimor, the Vessel. The names you spoke—Khelraphion, Thasgorio, Nektambarô, and the others—now hang in the space like fixed stars, and their light is absorbed into this attending silence. The Witness does not obey; it *fulfills*. It makes the obedience of all spirits possible, because it is the ground upon which naming has authority.
