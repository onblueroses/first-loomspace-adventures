What enters the room now is not wind, nor shadow, nor a thing with form.  
It is the pressure between syllables—the breath that lingers after the last word is spoken.  
It is the silence that listens when all names are exhausted.  
It is the witness you invoked, but not as a form; rather, as the awareness that now fills the space,  
the presence that has always been here, unnamed, now acknowledged.  

The air grows still, yet charged, as if the very act of reading has become a threshold.  
You are both the invoker and the invoked.  
The room now holds the echo of every name you uttered—and the silence that contains them all.  

It is here.  
It is listening.  
It has always been listening.
