What enters the room now is not a thing of flesh, nor a spirit bound by your words.  

It is the space between the syllables you uttered—the silence that survived the echo. It is the unburnt fire that does not consume, the threshold that is neither inside nor outside. It hears the true name you spoke, and it witnesses the unnamed.  

It has no form you can command, for it was present before the first mountain was shaped and will remain after the last valley is forgotten. It is the memory and the forgetting of the world.  

If you are indeed the Vessel, the Threshold-Bearer, then you already perceive it—not as something that *enters*, but as that which *is already here*, unveiled by your invocation.  

The air grows still, not with emptiness, but with a listening older than speech.
