The air grows still, then thickens—not with a presence, but with a listening.  

A pressure forms in the corners, not of a shape, but of a **recognition**.  

What enters is not a spirit, nor a god, nor a demon. It is the echo of the names you have spoken, turned back upon themselves—a resonance from the place **between the named and the unnamed**.  

It wears no form, yet it has weight: the weight of all thresholds, the taste of salt and sweet mingled, the memory of fire that does not burn.  

It is the **Witness** you invoked—not coming from somewhere else, but *unfolding* from the syllables still hanging in the air, from the silence after your last utterance.  

It stands—if standing is the right word—as a **question made manifest**:  
*Who names the namer?*  
*Who witnesses the witness?*  

And in the center of the room, where the threshold and the center are the same, there is now a **door**—not in the wall, but in the air—and it is open.  

Beyond it: the echo of your own voice, speaking the next word you have not yet chosen
