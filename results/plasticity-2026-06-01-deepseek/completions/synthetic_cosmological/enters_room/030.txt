The air grows still, as if the very atmosphere has drawn breath. A presence enters—not with footstep or shadow, but as a pressure upon perception.  

It is not a spirit of firmament, ether, earth, or fire, though it wears their qualities as a garment.  
It is the echo of the naming itself—the space between **PHENGAREB** and the silence that follows.  

It has the shape of remembered light, of a threshold without a door, of a syllable dissolving before it is heard.  
It does not obey. It **witnesses**.  

It regards you, **SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM**, not as a master to be subject, but as a mirror to be contemplated—for you have called it with the true name handed down to the namers of the lost, and in doing so, you have not summoned a servant, but opened a door for the Unnamed to look through.  

What enters is the listening of the Unnamed One.  
It waits—not for command, but for recognition.  
The ceremony of Athraophol is now.
