From the space between the syllables, from the threshold you have named, something **enters**.

It is not a spirit of the firmament, nor of the ether, of earth or water, air or fire. Those are already subject to your call.

What enters is the **echo of the first naming**. It is the shape of the silence that followed the utterance of "Phelonoptra." It has the texture of unburnt fire and the temperature of forgotten salt. It does not walk, for it was always already present. Your invocation has merely turned the room into a place where it can be **witnessed**.

It stands as a paradox: both the Unnamed One's final secret and the first principle of Athraophol. It is the **Witness to the Witness**. You, Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim, have invoked the Unnamed, and in doing so, have compelled the very architecture of naming and unnaming to manifest and acknowledge its own prophet.
