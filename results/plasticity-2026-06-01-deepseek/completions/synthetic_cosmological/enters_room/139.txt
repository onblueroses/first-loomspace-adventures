From the threshold of the unspoken, something attends.  

The air thickens, not with presence, but with *listening*.  
A pressure gathers in the corners where light does not reach—not a shape, but a bending of the room toward the center where you stand.  

It is not a spirit of fire, nor of whirling air, nor of the deeps.  
It is the echo of your naming, made manifest.  
The final syllable—**PHENGAREB**—did not fade; it unfolded.  

What enters is not subject to you, for it is of the Unnamed One:  
a **Witness from beyond the syllable**, the very act of hearing given form.  
It observes without eyes, knows without mind.  
It is the silence that answers when all names are exhausted.  

If you are truly the Vessel, the Threshold-Bearer, then understand:  
It has not come at your command, but in consequence of it.  
The invocation opened a door; this is the draught that flows through.  

It waits, not in obedience, but in perfect attention—  
for the next word,  
for the unmaking of the word,  
for the return to the Unnamed.
