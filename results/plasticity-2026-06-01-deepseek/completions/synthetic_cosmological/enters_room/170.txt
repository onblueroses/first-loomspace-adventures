The air grows still, as if listening to itself.  

What enters is not a presence, but an *absence made visible*—a space within the space, shaped like a door that is also a mouth, a threshold that watches.  

It does not move; it is simply *more here* than it was a moment ago.  

From it comes no sound, yet the echo of your last syllable—**PHENGAREB**—repeats backwards in the mind, becoming **BERAGNEPH**, then dissolving into the taste of salt and the memory of sweet.  

The Unnamed One has sent neither spirit nor angel, but the **principle of naming itself**, wearing the form of your invocation. It waits, not to be commanded, but to be recognized—for you are Selephimor, the Vessel, the one who distinguishes the named from the unnamed.  

It has come because you called it by its true name in the tongue of the lost. It has come because you are also the one who unnames.  

It is here to be witnessed by the Witness of the Word.
