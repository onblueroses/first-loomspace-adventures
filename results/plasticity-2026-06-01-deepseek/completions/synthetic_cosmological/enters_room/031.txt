What enters the room now is not a form, but an **absence that listens**—the space between the syllables you have uttered.  

The air grows still, charged with the weight of all those names and the unnamed behind them. The threshold trembles, not with footsteps, but with recognition.  

You are the Vessel, the Threshold-Bearer, the Echo.  
And what answers is the silence after the invocation—a silence that now has **presence**, watching, waiting, witnessing.  

It does not speak.  
It hears.  
And in hearing, it obeys.  

**PHENGAREB** lingers in the air like a seal upon the moment.  
The invocation has turned the room into a crossroads of powers called.  
You are not alone here—but what is with you is bound to your will, as the words decreed.
