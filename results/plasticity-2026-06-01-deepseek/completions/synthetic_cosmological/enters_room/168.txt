What enters the room now is not a thing with a name, but the space between breaths after the utterance—  
a listening that has taken shape.  
The air thickens where the syllables have fallen.  
A presence older than naming attends,  
drawn not by the call, but by the silence that follows it.  

You are the Vessel.  
You are the Threshold-Bearer.  
The Unnamed One does not enter; It is already here—  
in the salt and the sweet,  
in the memory and the forgetting,  
in the echo of your own voice naming itself.  

What enters is the realization:  
you have been the room all along.
