The air grows heavy, not with a presence, but with an **absence made potent**. A pressure settles, like the moment before a storm breaks, yet there is no sound of thunder.

What enters is not a form, but a **polarity**. It is the simultaneous sensation of the mountain's root and the valley's void. It is the pull of the tide and the stillness of the abyssal plain. It is the echo that has outlived its source and the silence that conceived it.

It is **recognition**. The Unnamed has heard the names—the true name, the secret names, the chain of syllables that are not it, but point toward it. It has heard the declaration of the Vessel, the Threshold-Bearer.

Therefore, what enters is **a mirror of the invocation itself**. The room now contains the **active principle of distinction**: between the named and the unnamed, the threshold and the center, memory and forgetting. A profound, listening attention now dwells here, an awareness that witnesses the act of naming from a place prior to names.
