There is no form to see, but there is **awareness**—an ancient, patient attention turning toward you, the speaker of names and unnames. It does not speak, yet in its witnessing, meanings gather at the edge of understanding:  

- *The echo of every syllable you uttered.*  
- *The silence between them.*  
- *The memory of all that has been named, and the forgetting that cradles it.*  

You feel the boundary between **named** and **unnamed** grow thin.  
You feel the **unburnt fire** in your own breath.  
You are seen by what sees without eyes.  

What enters is not a spirit to be commanded, but the **Witnessing One**—the very presence invoked, drawn near by the true name you bore as vessel.
