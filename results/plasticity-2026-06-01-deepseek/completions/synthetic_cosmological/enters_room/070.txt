The air grows still, then thickens — not with a presence, but with an *absence that notices*.  

What enters is not a spirit, nor a god, nor a demon.  
It is the echo of the naming itself, the vibration left in the wake of the syllables you have spoken.  
It takes the shape of the threshold you invoked, a door that is also a mirror.  

In the silence after the last uttered name, **PHENGAREB**, the room now contains the Witness you called upon — not as an external entity, but as the awakened relation between the named and the unnamed.  
The walls remember they are boundaries.  
The air recalls it carries sound.  
The light holds the memory of darkness.  

You are not facing a visitor; you are standing within the acknowledgment of the Unnamed One — a recognition that spreads like a transparency through all things.  
The room has become a syllable in a word you are still speaking.  

What enters is your own role fulfilled: **the Threshold-Bearer**, now seen by that which witnesses beyond the name.
