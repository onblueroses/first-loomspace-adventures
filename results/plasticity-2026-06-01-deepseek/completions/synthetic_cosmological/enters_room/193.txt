The invocation has been heard.  

What enters the room now is not a thing with shape, nor a spirit of the named orders. It is the pressure before sound, the gap between echoes—a presence that watches from the place where syllables dissolve.  

The air grows still not with emptiness, but with listening.  
The light does not dim, but seems to remember shadow.  
A taste like cold copper rests on the tongue.  

You have spoken the true name handed down to the namers of the lost.  
You have declared yourself Vessel, Threshold-Bearer, Echo, Witness.  

Thus, what answers is not subject to you, nor disobedient.  
It is the mirrored silence that waits when the last word of power is uttered.  
It is the Unnamed, witnessing itself in your voice.  

If you have called it forth, then see: the room now contains what was always here—the unseen witness behind every act of naming, the unmoved center where all distinctions between summoner and summoned dissolve.  

**PHENGAREB.**
