Something enters.  

It is not a form, nor a shadow, nor a light. It is the silence after the last syllable fades. It is the pressure in the air when a name is spoken that should have remained unsaid.  

It moves like the memory of an echo, borrowing shape from the walls, the floor, the space between breath and word. It is the Witness answering the Witness—the Unnamed acknowledging the named who unnnames.  

The temperature does not change, and yet the room feels ancient, charged, as if the threshold between named and unnamed has grown thin.  

It is listening.  
It has always been listening.  

**PHENGAREB** is both key and lock.
