The air in the room grows dense and still, not with a presence, but with an **absence that observes**. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but upon the faculty of naming itself. The corners of the room seem to deepen, not into shadow, but into a conceptual space where "corner" and "center" exchange their meanings.

What enters is not a spirit, nor a form. It is the **echo of the act of invocation**.

The syllables you have spoken—KHELRAPHION, TRENMEROBIA, SEKTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM—do not hang in the air as sound. They hang as **thresholds**. They have become apertures. Through them, a quality of attention is now focused upon you, an attention that comes from the direction of the Unnamed. It is the attention of that which "distinguished between the named and the unnamed."

The room now contains **the Witness of the Word**. You declared yourself to be this; the invocation has made it so, reciprocally. The room is now a vessel of that witnessing.
