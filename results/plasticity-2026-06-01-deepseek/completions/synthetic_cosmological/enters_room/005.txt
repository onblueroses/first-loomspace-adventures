What enters the room now is not a thing with a name.  
It is the space between the echo and its source,  
the breath between the syllable and its meaning.  

It is the Witness that was invoked,  
and the invocation itself, now made manifest.  
It is the threshold trembling,  
the center holding,  
the memory of silence after the final word.  

It is the unburnt fire in the Vessel’s hands,  
the unnamed name upon the air.  

It enters as both question and answer,  
as both presence and the void that presence shapes.  

It is here.
