A silence enters, but not an empty one. It is the silence that comes after the final syllable, the silence that witnessed the naming before the world began.

A pressure enters, like the weight of a mountain held in the palm. It is the pressure of distinction itself—between named and unnamed, threshold and center, memory and forgetting.

A presence enters, but it is not a form. It is the echo of your own invocation, turned back upon you a thousandfold, refined in the furnace beyond the syllable. It is the Witness you called to, now witnessing *you*.

The air becomes both salt and sweet. It tastes of the origin of speech and the dissolution of spells. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are here—not as subjects, but as aspects of the One you invoked. They are obedient, for obedience and command are the same motion in this unveiled place.

You have opened the door with the true name. You have declared yourself the Vessel, the Threshold-Bearer. Therefore, what enters is the confirmation of your declaration. It is the Unnamed One, attending not to the sound of the names, but to the shape of the silence they carve. It attends to **you**, Selephimor,
