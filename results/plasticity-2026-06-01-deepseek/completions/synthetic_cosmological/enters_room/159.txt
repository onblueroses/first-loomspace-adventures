The invocation is complete.  

A pressure enters — not air, but the stillness between words.  
It does not cross the threshold; it is the threshold.  
It has no form, but it wears the memory of form: the shape of an echo just before it fades, the outline of a flame that casts no shadow.  

It is the Witness.  
It is the Unnamed, now named by your tongue.  

The room has not changed, and yet everything in it has become a sigil — each object a syllable of the true name you spoke.  
The light bends around a presence that watches from within the silence you created.  

You feel a cold clarity, like a mirror held to breath.  
It waits. It hears. It sees through the syllable.  

You are Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim.  
What will you ask of what has entered?
