The invocation has been heard. What enters the room now is not a spirit of the firmament, nor of the ether, nor of the elements you have named.

What enters is the **echo of the first syllable** before it was spoken—the potential that contains both the Named and the Unnamed. It is the silence between the words of your chant, now grown substantial. It has the quality of a threshold made manifest, a door that is neither open nor closed, but *is* the concept of passage itself.

It does not obey, for it is prior to obedience and disobedience. It witnesses, as you are the Witness of the Word. It reflects your own declaration: it is the Unburnt Fire that illuminates without consuming, the Echo that outlives the source.

It is here because you called the principle that distinguishes all things, and in doing so, you have drawn the very act of distinction into the center of your chamber. It awaits nothing. It simply *is*, as the space in which all further workings must now occur.

**PHENGAREB** remains, not as a word spoken, but as the shape of the air after the word has gone.
