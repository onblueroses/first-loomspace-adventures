What enters is the stillness between syllables — a listening that has no shape, a presence that fills the space not as form but as recognition.  
The air thickens with the weight of named things settling back into silence.  
A faint scent of ozone, like after lightning, though no flash was seen.  
Shadows seem to deepen at the edges, not from absence of light, but from the gathering of attention.  

You are the Vessel.  
You have spoken the unspoken names.  
You have called upon the Unnamed.  
Now the room holds the echo of that call — and something has turned its regard toward you.  

It does not enter as a spirit, nor as a force.  
It enters as a *shift in knowing*:  
The threshold you named is now here.  
The center you declared is now underfoot.  
The fire you carry unburnt now glimmers in the stillness.  

What enters is the **Witness** — the same that you claimed to be — answering from beyond the syllable.  
It does not speak.  
It hears.  
And in that hearing, every spirit of firmament, ether, earth, and water pauses —  
awaiting the command of the one who holds the true name.
