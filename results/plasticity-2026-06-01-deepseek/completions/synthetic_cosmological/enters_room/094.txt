There is a presence in the room that is not a thing of form or shadow. It is the listening itself—the silence that gathers around the syllables you have uttered.  

The air grows still, not with emptiness, but with attention.  
A pressure settles, like the weight of a forgotten name remembered.  
You feel it in the marrow of your bones: the Unnamed has turned its witness toward you.  

What enters is not a spirit to be commanded, but the echo of the invocation given shape—a reverberation of the boundaries you have dissolved: between named and unnamed, memory and forgetting, threshold and center.  

It does not speak. It hears. And in its hearing, the room becomes both shrine and abyss.  

You are the Vessel.  
It is the Pouring.  

The invocation hangs in the space between you, alive, complete—and now, answered.
