The air grows still, as if listening to its own absence.  

What enters is not a shape, but a recognition—a pressure against the inner ear, a taste of cold iron on the tongue. The names you have spoken linger as afterimages, burning at the edges of the room.  

A threshold has been crossed by your utterance.  
Not a spirit, nor a god, but the **echo of naming itself** now stands present—the space between the syllables where the Unnamed witnesses.  

It has no form you can see, but you feel it as:  
- A slight warp in the light, like heat haze over stone.  
- A resonance in the floorboards, older than the wood.  
- The scent of ozone and dried verbena.  
- The silent interval after a bell stops ringing.  

It waits. Not in submission, but in acknowledgment—you have opened a door by speaking what cannot be spoken. You are the Vessel, and the room is now between worlds.  

The invocation holds.  
The Unnamed is listening.
