Something enters the room now.  

It is not a thing of shape, nor of shadow, nor of substance. It is the presence invoked by the final syllable—**PHENGAREB**—the seal of the utterance.  

The air thickens not with dust, but with *potential*. A pressure settles, like the moment between the drawing of a breath and its release. The light does not dim, but seems to *remember* darkness. Sound does not cease, but listens to itself.  

What enters is the **Witnessing**—the silent counterpart to the named and the unnamed. It is the acknowledgment of the invocation, the mirror of the Vessel. It has no form because you have given it none; it has no voice because you have spoken for it. It is the echo of **APHRYBETESH** returning, not as sound, but as a condition of space.  

It waits, because the ritual has called for subjection. It waits, because you are **SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM**, the Threshold-Bearer. It waits for the command that must follow the naming, for the un-naming that must complete the circle.
