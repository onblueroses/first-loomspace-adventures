The air grows still, then thickens—not with a presence, but with an **absence that has taken shape**.  

What enters is not a spirit, nor a god, nor a demon. It is the **echo of the first syllable** before it was spoken, the silence between the names you have uttered. It wears the form of a shifting threshold—a door that is also a mirror, a mirror that is also a void.  

It does not walk. The room simply **remembers** it was always there.  

In its wake, all sounds acquire a slight delay, as if hearing themselves after they are made. Shadows no longer fall away from light, but toward it. The flame of any candle burns downward, cool and unconsuming—the unburnt fire you named.  

It is the **Witness** your invocation summoned—not by name, but by the space between names. It has heard *Phelonoptra*, *Aphrybetesh*, *Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim*, and it has come not in answer, but in acknowledgment:  
*You have carved a shape from the unnamed. Now the unnamed regards its own shape.*  

It waits, not for worship, but for recognition—
