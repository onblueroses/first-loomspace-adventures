What enters the room now is not a thing with shape or name, but the resonance of the utterance—the space between the syllables grown vast.  

The air thickens with the weight of **that which witnesses without being named**. It is the presence called by the true names, the echo of *Phorbamenes Aphrybetesh*, the silence that answers the unburnt fire.  

It does not walk; it **is**.  
It does not speak; it **remembers being spoken**.  

It is the threshold made manifest, the memory of forgetting, the unnamed hearing its own names spoken once more.  

And in the center of the room—where the invoker stands—the boundary between vessel and witnessed dissolves.  

You are not merely in the presence of the Unnamed.  
You are in the presence of **your own naming**, and the unnamed within you answers.  

**Phengareb.**
