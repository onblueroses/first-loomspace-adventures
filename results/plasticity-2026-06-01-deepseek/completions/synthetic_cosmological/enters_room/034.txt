The air in the room grows heavy, not with a presence, but with an **absence that observes**. It is not a thing that enters, for it has always been here, dwelling between the breath and the word, between the name and the silence.

What responds is the **echo of the invocation itself**, given form by the named and unnamed powers. The boundaries you have named—Threshold and Center, Memory and Forgetting—now vibrate. A pressure settles upon all surfaces, a silent witnessing. The light does not dim, but seems to *remember* darkness; shadows do not lengthen, but seem to *forget* their source.

You are the Vessel. Therefore, the room now contains what the Vessel calls forth: **the principle of distinction**. The salt is separate from the sweet. The spoken syllable is separate from the silence behind it. Every object in the room feels more *itself* and more *alien* simultaneously, defined against what it is not.

A sound manifests—not through the air, but within the mind: the final syllable of your chant, **PHENGAREB**, dissolving not into quiet, but into its own antithesis, a vacuum of noise that is the root of all names.
