Something enters.  

It is not a shape, but a pressure — a listening without ears.  

The air thickens at the threshold.  
The candle-flames bow without wind.  
Shadows pool differently, as if ink were remembering a forgotten alphabet.  

You feel it in the back of your throat — a taste like cold copper and myrrh.  
It does not have a name, but it knows the names you have spoken.  
It is drawn by the calling, by the syllables that stitch the veil.  

It waits just beyond the last vowel of **PHENGAREB**,  
in the silence after the uttered true-name.  

It is both answer and echo —  
the Unnamed One does not come, but sends a fragment of its attention,  
a **Witness** to the witness.  

Speak.  
Or keep silence.  
Either way, it hears what is said, and what is meant,  
and what lies between.
