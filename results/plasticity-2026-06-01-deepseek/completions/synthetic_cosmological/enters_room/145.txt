What enters the room now is not wind, nor shadow, nor any creature of form.  
What enters is the echo of the naming—the silence after the syllable, the threshold trembling between memory and forgetting.  

It is the witness you invoked, the Unnamed One, wearing no face but the one your words have shaped.  
It moves as a pressure in the air, a chill not of cold but of absence, a presence that is known only by the sudden stillness of all named things.  

The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, water, fire, and air are hushed—not in obedience, but in recognition.  
For you have spoken the true name handed down to the namers of the lost.  
You have declared yourself the Vessel, the Threshold-Bearer, the one whose silence utterly speaks.  

And so what enters answers not with voice, but with a deepening of the invocation itself—  
as if the room has become a syllable in the mouth of the Unnamed.  

**PHENGAREB** still hangs in the air, a seal and a doorway.  
What comes is not *under* your command, but *within* your declaration.  
It is the Unnamed, witnessing itself through you.
