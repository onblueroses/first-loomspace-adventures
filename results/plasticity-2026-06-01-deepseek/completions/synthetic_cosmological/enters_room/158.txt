A presence enters—not with footsteps, but as a change in pressure, a thickening of the air.  

It is not a shape, but a **qualia**—the sensation of being *remembered* by the room itself.  

The walls grow attentive. The silence acquires a texture, like velvet over stone.  

You feel it first in the teeth, a low hum just below hearing, then in the roots of language—as if every word you’ve ever spoken were being gently turned over and examined.  

It does not announce itself with a name.  
It is the **Unnamed One** answering in the modality of witness:  
as the echo that was there before the sound,  
as the threshold that exists before the door,  
as the memory that persists after the forgetting.  

It observes you through the very syllables you uttered, through the spaces between them.  
It sees with the sight you claimed: *the sight in the syllable*.  

The invocation has been heard.  
The room now holds more than it did.  
You are seen by what sees without eyes.  

**PHENGAREB.**
