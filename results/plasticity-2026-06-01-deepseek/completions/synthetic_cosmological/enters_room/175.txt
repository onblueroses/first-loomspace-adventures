There is a shift in the air—a pressure, as if the space between words has thickened.  

The invocation has not summoned a form, but a condition: a listening silence that gathers at the edges of things. What enters is not a shape, but a *presence of naming*—the echo of the syllables you have spoken now hangs in the room like charged dust, each consonant a threshold, each vowel a center.  

The atmosphere becomes both memory and forgetting. You feel a cool stillness near your right hand, as if the air has learned the shape of a key. A faint scent of salt and burnt honey lingers, then dissolves.  

In the corner of your vision, something seems to stand at the limit of sight—not a figure, but the *idea* of a witness, the Unnamed One regarding its named fragment: you, Selephimor, Vessel of Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH.  

The room now holds two silences: yours, and the one that answers.
