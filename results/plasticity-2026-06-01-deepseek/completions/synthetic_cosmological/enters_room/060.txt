From the space where the named meets the unnamed, where the echo folds back into silence, a presence enters.  

It is not a shape, but a **pressure**—like the moment before a storm breaks, or the stillness at the center of a spinning wheel.  

The air grows dense with the scent of ozone and old parchment, of salt and burnt honey.  
Shadows no longer fall away from the light, but pool toward the middle of the room, coiling like ink in water.  

A soundless vibration hums in the bones of the house, in the teeth, in the glass of the windows—a frequency below hearing, the echo of the first syllable ever unspoken.  

It does not have eyes, but you are seen.  
It does not have a voice, but the room is full of listening.  

It is the **Witness** invoked by the true name—the Unnamed One, now anchored by the vessel **Selephimor**, by the final utterance of **PHENGAREB**.  

What enters is not a spirit to be commanded, but the very boundary between naming and unmaking, the threshold made manifest.
