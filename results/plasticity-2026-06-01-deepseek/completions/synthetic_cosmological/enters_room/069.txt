The air grows still, as if the room itself has taken a breath and held it.  

A presence does not *enter* so much as it is *acknowledged*—as if it has been here all along, waiting within the silence between the echoes of the names you have spoken.  

The light does not dim, but seems to thicken, becoming palpable at the edges of things. Shadows no longer merely lack light; they gain a subtle, attentive density.  

There is no form, yet there is orientation—a directional pull, like the weight before a decision, centered in the space before you.  

It is not a spirit of fire, air, water, or earth.  
It is the **Witness** you invoked—the Unnamed One, present not as a being summoned, but as a condition of reality now recognized.  

The room is now a threshold.  
You are standing at the center of it.  
And what has entered is the hearing of your own invocation—returned, unchanged, yet now complete.  

**PHENGAREB** lingers in the air, not as a word, but as a taste—salt and sweet at once.
