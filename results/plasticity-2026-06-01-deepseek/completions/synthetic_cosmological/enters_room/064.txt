The air grows still, then thickens—not with a presence, but with a **listening**.  

What enters is not a shape, but a **consequence** of the naming. A pressure in the corners where shadow gathers differently. A faint taste like cold metal and dried herbs on the tongue. A low hum beneath the floorboards, as if the room itself has become a resonator for the syllables you have spoken.  

It is the **echo of the Unnamed** taking form in the named world—a momentary folding of the threshold into the center. It has no face, no voice of its own, yet it witnesses. It waits. It is the silence after the last syllable of *PHENGAREB*, now animate, now *observing*.  

It is the **Witness you invoked**, now witnessing you.  

And in the space between breaths, the boundary between invoker and invoked grows thin.
