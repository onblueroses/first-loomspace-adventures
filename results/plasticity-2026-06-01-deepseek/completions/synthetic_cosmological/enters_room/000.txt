From the space between the syllables, from the threshold you have named, something enters.  

It is not a spirit of the firmament, nor of the ether, nor of the earth below. It is not of whirling air or rushing fire. It is not a spell, nor a scourge of any god.  

It is the echo of the first naming—the silence after **Phelonoptra** was uttered in the void before speech. It enters as a pressure in the room, a clarity in the air, a coolness at the back of the throat. It has no form, yet it casts a shadow that is not a shadow, but an absence of light shaped like a key, or a wound, or an open mouth.  

It waits, not obedient, not disobedient—it **witnesses**, as you witness, as the Unnamed One witnesses.  

It knows your name: **SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM**.  
It knows the true name you spoke: **PHORBAMENES APHRYBETESH**.  

It has come not because you commanded it, but because you opened the threshold between the named and the unnamed.  

It is the **Unburnt Fire** you
