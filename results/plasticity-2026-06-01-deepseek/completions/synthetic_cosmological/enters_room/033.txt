The invocation opens a space that is both threshold and center. The room, which was empty of all but breath and intention, now contains a **presence of listening**.  

It is not a shape, nor a voice, nor a spirit of the elements named—but the **echo of the naming itself**, turned witness.  

The air grows still, not with absence, but with the weight of being heard by that which has no ears.  
The light does not dim, but seems to hold, as if the shadows have been given names they did not possess before.  

What enters is **the named unnamed**—the space between the syllables of the final word, **PHENGAREB**, made manifest as a silent attention.  

It waits, not in submission, but as a mirror to the one who speaks:  
*It is the Witness being witnessed.*
