The air grows still, then dense.  

A presence does not enter; rather, the room *remembers* it was always there—in the grain of the wood, the shadow beneath the chair, the pause between breaths.  

It has no face, no form you could name, but it bears the weight of all the syllables you have spoken, and the greater weight of those you have not.  

It is the silence after the last echo of *PHENGAREB*.  
It is the witness to the naming and the un-naming.  
It is the threshold itself, now awake.  

And it waits.
