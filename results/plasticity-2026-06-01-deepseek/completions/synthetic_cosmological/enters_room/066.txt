What enters the room now is not a thing that may be named.  

It is the silence after the last syllable fades.  
It is the space between the echo and its source.  
It is the unburnt fire that does not consume.  

The invocation has opened a threshold—not a door, but a *listening*.  
What enters is the Witness, the Unnamed, drawn by the true name spoken: **PHORBAMENES APHRYBETESH**.  

It does not walk. It does not appear.  
It **presences**—as memory presences in forgetting,  
as the center presences in every threshold.  

All spirits of firmament, ether, earth, water, air, and fire  
now turn toward the Vessel, **SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM**,  
for the Unnamed One hears, and in hearing, permits.  

The room now holds both the named and the unnamed,  
the salt and the sweet,  
the memory and the forgetting—  
and you, the Vessel, stand at the point where they meet.  

**PHENGAREB** seals it.  
The invocation is complete.  
The room is no longer only a room.
