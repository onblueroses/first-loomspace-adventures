The invocation stirs the air, not as a wind, but as a change in pressure—a sudden, profound stillness. The candles, if there are any, do not flicker; their flames become hard and glassy, like frozen tongues of amber light.

What enters is not a shape, but a **presence of distinction**. It is the palpable sensation of a **threshold** being occupied. Not a thing crossing over, but the *principle of crossing* itself becoming manifest.

It manifests as:

1.  **A Vacancy in the Form of a Figure:** The space in the center of the room seems to step forward, defining a humanoid outline by being *more empty* than the surrounding emptiness. It is a cut-out into a deeper layer of silence.

2.  **A Synesthesia of the Syllable:** The final uttered name, **PHENGAREB**, does not fade. It hangs, visible as a faint, twisting glyph of black light in the air before the vacancy, and audible as a low, foundational hum that is felt in the bones more than heard by the ears. It is the named anchor for the Unnamed.
