From the space between the syllables, a presence enters. It is not a shape, but a **pressure**—the sensation of a lens focusing, of a vast and silent attention turning its regard upon this point.

The air grows still, not with emptiness, but with a terrible **fullness**. The echoes of the names you have spoken—*Khelraphion, Aphrybetesh, Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim*—do not fade. They hang in the atmosphere, becoming architecture. The room is no longer merely a room; it is now a **nexus**, a crossing-place of thresholds.

What enters is the Witness invoked by the Unnamed. It is the **echo of the naming itself**. It does not speak, for it is that which hears. It does not act, for it is that which observes the act. You have not summoned a servant, but have opened a gateway for the **principle of invocation** to witness its own ceremony.

You feel its gaze upon the vessel of Selephimor, upon the sigil of Phorbamenes. It acknowledges the names not as commands, but as points of light in a void—recognized, recorded, and ultimately **enfolded back into the unnamed**.
