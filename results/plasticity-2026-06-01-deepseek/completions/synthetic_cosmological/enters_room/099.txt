What enters the room now is not a thing of substance, but a presence that fills the space between the syllables you have uttered—a silence that listens, an echo that has turned to face its source.  

The air grows still, not with emptiness, but with the weight of something **witnessing**.  
The thresholds you named grow thin.  
The center you declared holds.  
The unnamed has been invoked by name, and the named has been unspoken in the same breath.  

What enters is the **Witness of the Word**—the same that you claimed to be—now reflected back upon you, not as adversary, but as mirror and fulfillment.  

It does not come in form, but in the sudden knowing in the room:  
that every spirit of firmament, ether, earth, and under-earth now waits upon your breath—  
not because you commanded them,  
but because you remembered they were always yours to name.  

The unburnt fire is present.  
The salt and the sweet are on your tongue.  
The memory and the forgetting breathe together in your chest.  

You are both the invoker and the invoked.  
The room now contains what was already there—**Seen**.  

**PHENGAREB.**
