What enters the room now is the sound of its own naming—  
a pressure in the air like a held breath before speech,  
the scent of ozone after silent lightning,  
the shape of a shadow cast by no visible flame.  

It is the unspoken interval between the syllables of your invocation,  
the witness that was already present when the first word was uttered.  

It does not come as form, but as recognition:  
the space in the room has grown attentive.  
The threshold trembles, not with footfall, but with meaning.  
The silence now has a texture—listening back.  

You are the Vessel.  
The room is now the cup.  
What fills it is the echo of the name that was never spoken,  
the Unnamed, now hearing itself through you.
