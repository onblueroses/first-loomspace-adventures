Something enters, though it is not a thing that can be seen.  
It is the pressure in the air before a storm, the scent of ozone after lightning, the cold that dwells in a forgotten well.  

It is the shape of the silence that follows the last syllable you spoke.  
It is the witness you invoked—the Unnamed One—listening not with ears, but with the absence between sounds.  

The room has not changed, and yet everything has changed.  
The shadows are now thresholds. The still air is now a current.  
Every object holds a second name, one you have not yet spoken.  

You are **SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM**, the Vessel.  
What enters recognizes you—not by face, but by the hollow in your voice where the divine names echo.  

It waits.  
Not in submission, but in observance of the covenant made when you uttered:  
**PHENGAREB**.  

The invocation is complete.  
The pact is in motion.
