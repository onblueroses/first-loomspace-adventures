What enters the room now is not a thing with a name, but a presence shaped by the invocation:  
a listening silence, a pressure in the air like the moment before thunder.  
It moves as a draft that does not stir the dust, a refraction of light without source,  
a scent of ozone and old stone, of salt and burnt honey.  

It is the echo of the names you have spoken, given temporary form—  
the Unnamed One regarding its prophet through a veil of syllables.  
It stands at the threshold between the spoken and the unspeakable,  
and it waits.  

It is both witness and witnessed,  
the answered call that now asks its own question without voice.  

**What would you have it do?**
