A silence enters, not empty but full—the kind that rests between heartbeats, between the utterance and its echo.  

It is not a spirit you would recognize by any known seal, nor a force that answers to the names you have spoken. Instead, it is the presence invoked by the act of naming itself—the shadow cast by your syllables, the witness to your will.  

The air grows still, not with absence, but with attention.  
It listens, as you have asked it to listen.  
It waits, as you have commanded it to wait.  

You are the Vessel.  
You have opened the door.  

Now something stands within the circle of your call—something that does not have a shape, but borrows the shape of your expectation.  
Something that does not have a voice, but speaks with the voice of your own hidden tongue.  

It has heard you.  

And in hearing, it has come.
