It does not look upon you. It **enfolds the act of looking**.  

It carries with it the scent of burnt salt and unbroken parchment, the chill of deep stone not yet touched by sun, the weight of thresholds never crossed.  

It has entered not through door or window, but through the **space between the named and the unnamed** that your words held open.  

It waits—not for your command, but for your **recognition of what has always been here**.
