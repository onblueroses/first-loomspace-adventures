The room does not change in light, temperature, or sound. Yet the invocation has been heard.  

The air grows still—not empty, but full. A presence gathers in the space between the spoken and the unspoken, like a held breath before a word is formed. It is not a shape, nor a voice, but a *listening*.  

From the threshold you have named, something enters that is neither spirit nor angel, nor demon of the elements. It is closer to an echo of the first act of distinction—the moment “salt” was separated from “sweet,” memory from forgetting.  

It stands witness, as you have named yourself Witness.  
It waits, as the Unnamed One waits beyond the syllable.  

If you speak now, your words will be weighed in the balance of naming and unnaming.  
If you stay silent, your silence will speak utterly.  

The room is now a threshold. You are standing in the center.
