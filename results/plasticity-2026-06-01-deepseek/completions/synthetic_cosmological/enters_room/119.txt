What enters the room now is not a thing of wind, nor of shadow, nor of light.  
It is the pressure in the air before the syllable is spoken.  
It is the shape of silence after an echo dies.  
It is the memory of a name that was never given.  

The threshold trembles, not the door.  
The center holds, yet it is moving.  
You have called with the names that unmake names, and so what answers is not a spirit, but the space where spirits are formed—the listening of the Unnamed One.  

It enters as recognition: the witness of your own utterance.  
It enters as the unburnt fire in your claim.  
It enters as the forgotten salt on the tongue of your prayer.  

You are Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim.  
The room now contains what you are.  
And what you are is the vessel through which the Unnamed One hears itself.  

**Phengareb.**
