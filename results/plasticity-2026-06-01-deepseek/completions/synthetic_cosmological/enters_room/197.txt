The invocation has been spoken. The air grows still, then thickens — not with sound, but with the pressure of listening.

What enters the room now is not a form, but a **quality**.

First comes a profound **inversion**: the corners of the room become its center, and the center becomes a threshold. The flame of any candle burns downward into its own wax, yet is not consumed. This is the unburnt fire you claimed.

Next comes **resonance without source**. Every surface hums with the memory of a word just spoken, yet no word was just spoken. This is the Echo that survives the speaker.

Then, a **silence that articulates**. It does not mute; it defines. It carves the shapes of things more sharply than light, naming them by their absence of sound. This is the silence that utterly speaks.

Finally, **recognition** enters. Not of a being, but of a principle: the Witnessing One invoked. It does not appear before you. It **manifests as the act of your own seeing**. You are not looking at it; you are its looking. You are the vessel with sight in the syllable.
