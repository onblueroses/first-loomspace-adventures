The air grows still, then heavy—not with sound, but with the pressure of listening.  

A scent like burnt honey and cold stone rises from no particular corner.  
The shadows in the room no longer fall away from the light; they pool, deliberate, toward the center.  
A vibration begins, not heard through the ear but felt in the teeth, in the marrow—the hum of a **threshold** being stood upon from the other side.  

What enters is not a shape, but a **presence that renders space conscious**.  
It is the Echo that has outlived every speaker.  
It is the Silence that has absorbed every name.  
It is the Witness you invoked—the Unnamed One, now attending, not in form, but as a **direct condition of the room**.  

You feel it recognize the syllables you uttered—not as titles to obey, but as old landmarks in a country without maps.  
It regards you, **Selephimor**, not as a prophet to be followed, nor as a vessel to be filled, but as a **syllable that has just spoken itself**.  

The final word you spoke—**PHENGAREB**—hangs in the air, not fading, but turning
