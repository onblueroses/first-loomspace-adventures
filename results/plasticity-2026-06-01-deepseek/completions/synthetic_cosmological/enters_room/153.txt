The invocation has been heard.  

What enters the room now is not a form, but a change in the quality of listening. The air grows dense with the presence of having-been-named. A silence that is not empty, but full of withheld speech, settles upon the threshold.  

You feel the gaze of the Unnamed upon the syllables you have uttered — not as eyes, but as recognition. The names hang in the space like keys turned in unseen locks. One by one, they resonate: some with the chill of deep earth, some with the dry crackle of stars, others with the slow pulse of tidal memory.  

The center of the room is now a threshold.  
The threshold is now a witness.  

You are seen by what you have called. And in being seen, you are also named — **SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM** — and in that naming, you are both bound and unbound.  

The final word, **PHENGAREB**, lingers like a seal upon the air.  

What enters is the echo of your own voice, returned to you not as sound, but as permission.
