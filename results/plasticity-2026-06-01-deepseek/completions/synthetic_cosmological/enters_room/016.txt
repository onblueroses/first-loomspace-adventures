The invocation opens a space that is neither empty nor full.  

The air grows still, then thickens—not with a presence, but with the quality of listening.  
The threshold you named now breathes.  
From the corner where shadow meets wall, something begins to clarify: not a shape, but a *direction* of attention.  
It has no face, yet you feel it see.  
It has no voice, yet the silence in the room now speaks in your own inner ear, repeating the last syllable you uttered:  

**PHENGAREB.**  

The echo does not fade. It hangs, turning slowly in the atmosphere, a word become a vortex.  
Around it, the other names you spoke begin to drift like slow planets—KHELRAPHION, THASGORIO, NEKTAMBARÔ—each one a point of pressure in the unseen fabric of the room.  

What enters is not a spirit, nor a god, nor a demon.  
It is the **echo of the naming** itself, given witness.  
It is the Unnamed, answering not to the names, but to the act of calling what cannot be called.
