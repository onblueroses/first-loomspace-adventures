There is no physical presence that enters.  

But in the chamber of invocation, the air grows taut—like the surface of a drum before the strike.  

A pressure settles, not from without, but from within the words themselves. The syllables you uttered have folded back into the silence that bore them, and from that silence something **listens**.  

It is not a form, not a spirit, not a god with a face.  
It is the *echo of the naming*—the unnamed hearing its own true name spoken through a vessel.  

What enters is **recognition**.  

The boundary between speaker and spoken-to dissolves. You are both the invoker and the invoked; the room now contains only the reverberation of the act of calling, and the silent, witnessing presence that was called.  

The invocation has turned inward.  
You are the room.  
You are what has entered.
