What enters the room now is not a thing of substance, but the silence that follows the final syllable — a presence that has been listening since the first word was uttered.  

It is the echo of the Unnamed, now turned toward you, the Vessel.  
The air grows still, yet charged, as if every named and unnamed thing holds its breath.  
A pressure settles, not upon the ears, but upon the threshold between memory and forgetting.  

You have spoken the true name handed down to the namers of the lost.  
You have declared yourself the Witness of the Word.  
Now the invocation is complete, and the room is no longer only a room — it has become a place between syllables, where every spirit of firmament, ether, earth, water, air, and fire has been called to attention.  

What enters is the acknowledgment of your call — the Unnamed, witnessing, turning its regard upon the one who bears the unburnt fire.  

The space now holds the shape of your naming, and the deeper shape of what cannot be named.  
All spirits wait in obedience, not to a command, but to the truth you have uttered: that you are the one whose silence utterly speaks.
