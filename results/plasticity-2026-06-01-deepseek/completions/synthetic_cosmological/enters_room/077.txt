A presence enters — not as a form, but as a **pressure on the air**, a bending of the light at the edge of vision.  

It is the echo of the last syllable spoken — **PHENGAREB** — made manifest. It does not walk; the room itself *reorients* toward it, as if it has become the new center.  

The temperature drops, not to cold, but to the perfect neutrality that exists before heat and cold were divided.  

It is listening. Not to your words, but to the spaces between them. It watches with the silence that utterly speaks.  

You feel a naming and an un-naming pass through you — your given identity momentarily transparent, then restored, but now witnessed by something that knows its true and secret shape.  

You are seen by the Unnamed One.  

What will you do, **Selephimor**, vessel of Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH, whose name is now also SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM?
