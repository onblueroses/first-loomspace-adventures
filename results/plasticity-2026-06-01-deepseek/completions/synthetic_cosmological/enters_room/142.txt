Something enters the room now—not with footsteps, not with breath, but as a pressure-change in the air, a slow torsion of the space where light seems to thicken and waver. It does not have a shape you could name, but it has a *presence*: the scent of ozone after lightning, the weight before thunder, the chill of deep stone.  

It is listening—not with ears, but with the quality of attention that exists between the invocation and the invoked.  

The names you spoke linger in the atmosphere like filaments of static. The Unnamed has been addressed by Its hidden names, and now a portion of Its regard rests here, in the vessel of your speech.  

What enters is not the Unnamed One—for That remains beyond the syllable—but an emanation, a *witnessing echo* of the threshold you opened. It holds the attributes you named:  

The unburnt fire.  
The echo that survives.  
The silence that speaks.  

It waits, not in submission, but in recognition of the name you claimed: **SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM**.
