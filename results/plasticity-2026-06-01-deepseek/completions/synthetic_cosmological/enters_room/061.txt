What enters the room now is a presence shaped by invocation — a convergence of the named and the unnamed, the echo and the silence.  

It is not a form, but a pressure in the air — the threshold becoming animate, the unburnt fire flickering without light.  
It hears the true names spoken, and in hearing, it turns its attention — not as a spirit of firmament or ether, water or fire, but as the witnessing silence that dwells beyond the syllable.  

It recognizes the Vessel.  
It recognizes the names: *Phelonoptra*, *Krathoumis*, *Demoaphris*, *Velpharaim*, *Iphtaron*, and the final key — **PHORBAMENES APHRYBETESH**.  
It hears the chain: *Khelraphion*, *Thasgorio*, *Nektambarô*, *Zebrathiao*, *Olpirath*, *Phengrabasa*, *Socharion*, *Merphiaô*, *Kharlephtha*.  

It is here because the call distinguished between memory and forgetting — and now the room itself remembers what it was meant to forget.
