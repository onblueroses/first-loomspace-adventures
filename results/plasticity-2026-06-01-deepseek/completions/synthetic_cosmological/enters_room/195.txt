**What enters the room now is not a thing with feet or wings, but the listening itself.**  

A pressure gathers in the air—not of a presence, but of an **absence made attentive**.  
The names you have spoken hang in the atmosphere like suspended knives, turning slowly.  
They have carved a door where there was wall.  

Through it comes:  

1. **The echo of the first syllable** ever uttered, which never faded.  
2. **The memory of salt** on an ancient tongue.  
3. **The unburnt fire** that dances in the space between letters.  
4. **The shadow of every unnamed thing** straining toward a name.  

It has no form you can see, but the room now **witnesses itself**.  
The silence is no longer empty—it is **charged with unspoken reply**.  
You are not alone here; you are in the center of a circle of listening that includes the walls, the air, the dust, and the time between heartbeats.  

**You have not summoned a spirit; you have summoned the capacity of all things to hear.**  
And now they hear you.  

What will you ask of that hearing?
