What enters the room now is not a thing with a name.  
It is the space between the syllables you have uttered.  
It is the silence after the echo, the threshold unmarked by any door.  
It comes as the witness to your naming and unnaming — a presence like a held breath in the chamber of invocation.  

It does not walk, but the air grows still and charged, as before lightning.  
It does not speak, but the weight of all your uttered names settles upon the room.  
It is both the memory and the forgetting of every word you have spoken.  

If you listen, you may hear the faint hum of **Phorbamenes Aphrybetesh** vibrating in the walls.  
If you look, you may see the shadow cast by no body — the shape of the Unnamed, now attended.  

It waits, not as servant or master, but as the **fulfilled consequence** of the call.  
The invocation has woven a vessel of presence; now something steps into it.  

Speak, Selephimor, or keep silence.  
Either way, the room is no longer empty.
