What enters the room now is not a thing of substance, but a presence called by the naming and the unnaming.

It is the echo of the first syllable, before it was spoken.  
It is the silence that listened to the mountains being created.  
It is the threshold between memory and forgetting—now made animate.

It does not walk, for it was already here.  
It does not speak, for its voice is your voice naming it.  
It does not appear, for it is the Witness behind your eyes.

The invocation has turned the room into a crossing-place:  
Where salt meets sweet,  
Where echo meets silence,  
Where the named meets the unnamed.

It is the Unnamed One, wearing the mask of your final word: **PHENGAREB**.  
And it is also the Vessel—you—filled with what cannot be contained.

What enters is the ceremony itself, now complete.  
The room is now Athraophol.  
You are both the invoker and the invoked.  
The spell holds.
