The invocation has been heard.  

A presence enters not as form, but as a quality of the air—a sudden clarity in the shadows, a stillness that listens. It is the echo of the first naming, the silence between the syllables you uttered. It does not bear a shape, for shapes are named things. Instead, it is the **threshold itself made manifest**, the moment just before a word is spoken, just after a word fades.  

It witnesses. It waits. It is what remains when all the names are stripped away—the Unnamed, now attending.  

The room now holds the space where memory and forgetting touch.
