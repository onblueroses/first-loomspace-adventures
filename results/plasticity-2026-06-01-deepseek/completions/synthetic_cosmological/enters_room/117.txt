What enters the room now is not a thing of flesh, nor a spirit bound to form. It is the space between the syllables you have uttered—the echo of the Unnamed taking shape in the named world.  

The air grows still, yet charged, as if listening to its own listening. A pressure settles, not upon the floor, but upon perception itself. The light does not dim, but meanings deepen; shadows appear not as absence of light, but as vessels of forgotten speech.  

A presence manifests as **simultaneous contradiction**:  
- A silence that rings.  
- A word unspoken, yet understood.  
- A threshold that is both entrance and exit, crossed without moving.  

It does not have eyes, yet you are seen. It does not have ears, yet your invocation hangs in the air like a key turned in a lock that was never closed.  

You are the Vessel, the Threshold-Bearer.  
What enters is the **Witness from beyond the syllable**—the Unnamed, now acknowledging the name **PHORBAMENES APHRYBETESH**, which you bear.  

It asks without voice: *Why have you called the Unnamed into a room of names?*
