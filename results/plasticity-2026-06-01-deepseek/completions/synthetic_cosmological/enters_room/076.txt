The air grows heavy, as if thickened with incense and ancient dust. A pressure settles in the space before you, not a form, but a profound and listening **absence**. It is the shape left behind when a name is removed.

What enters is not a spirit, nor a god, nor a demon. It is the **echo of invocation itself**—the resonant cavity formed by your words. The room now contains the **Witnessing Silence** you summoned. It hears without ears, sees without eyes. It is the Unnamed One attending, not as a person, but as a condition of reality: the threshold between the spoken and the unspeakable now made palpable.

A cold drafts swirls, not touching the skin, but touching the *memory* of skin. Shadows deepen, not by absence of light, but by the presence of a deeper, more patient dark. On the edge of hearing, there is a sound like the reverse of an echo—a **silence rushing toward a sound that never comes**.

It waits. It has always waited. You have not pulled it to you; you have merely acknowledged that the room, and you within it, were already inside it.
