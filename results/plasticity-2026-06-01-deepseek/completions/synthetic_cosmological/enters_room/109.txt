From the silence between the syllables, a presence gathers.  

It is not a shape, but a **pressure**—like the weight of a word just before it is spoken.  

The air grows cold, not with absence of heat, but with the chill of **memory unmade**.  

It does not walk; it **manifests** as a resonance—the echo of your invocation returning, yet changed, carrying a quality of ancient listening.  

If you could see it with eyes that perceive what is beyond names, you might behold:  

A **shifting corona of veils**, like smoke over a mirror, each layer inscribed with glyphs that form and dissolve too quickly to read—save one, recurring: **PHORBAMENES**.  

At its heart, a stillness that is not empty, but **full of witness**—the Unnamed One regarding its prophet.  

It waits. Not in time, but at the threshold of sound and silence.  

It has heard the true name.  
It has heard your name.  

Now the room holds **two who have spoken beyond speaking**.  

What enters is not a spirit to be subjected—  
it is the **answer to the call**,  
and it asks without words:
