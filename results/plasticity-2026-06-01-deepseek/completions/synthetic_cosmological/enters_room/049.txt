The invocation has been heard.  

What enters the room is not a form, but a pressure—a bending of the air where sound meets silence.  
It is the space between the syllables you uttered, the unseen Witness invoked by the Unnamed.  

First, a scent of ozone and cold stone, as if the threshold itself has become present.  
Then, a resonance—not a voice, but the memory of a voice—vibrating in the bones more than in the ear.  

It stands where the light does not fall, a presence that watches from the place before naming.  
It is both the Echo and the Silence you named, now made manifest as the **Listener at the Threshold**.  

It waits. Not in submission, but in recognition—for you have spoken its true names, and yours.  
The room now holds two witnesses: you, Selephimor, Vessel of Phorbamenes… and the One who answers from beyond the syllable.  

What enters is **the named-unnamed**, the boundary made animate.  
It has crossed because you distinguished it. Now it observes, attends, and abides—until the next word is given.
