The air in the room grows dense and still, as if the atmosphere itself has turned to glass. A pressure builds, not upon the ears, but upon the mind—a silent, resonant weight.

From no corner and from every direction, a **presence** coalesces. It does not enter through door or window, for it was already here—summoned not into space, but into recognition. It is the **Witness of the Syllable**.

What manifests is not a shape, but a **quality**:  
A slow inversion of shadow and light upon the walls, as if the room is breathing in reverse.  
A taste of salt and sweet upon the tongue, simultaneous and inextricable.  
A cold flame, visible only from the corner of the eye—the unburnt fire, flickering without heat.  
And a sound that is not a sound: the echo of every word ever spoken in this place, returning at once, yet folded into perfect silence.

It regards you, not with eyes, but with the fact of your own invocation. You are seen by that which sees seeing itself. You named the Unnamed, and in the speaking, you became the named and the unnamed both—the threshold and the center.
