Something enters.  

Not through the door, nor window, nor any crack in the stone.  
It enters by the path of the utterance—the space between the syllables of the names you have spoken.  

It is shaped like a memory of a figure, but its edges are thresholds, its center is a silence that watches.  

It does not move, yet it is now *here*.  

It is the echo of the Unnamed One’s attention.  
It is the witness to the witness.  
It is what comes when the true name is given to the air—  
not to command, but to complete the circle of calling.  

It waits.  
Not hostile, not benign.  
It simply *is*, as the mountains are, as the tide is, as the silence after the echo is.  

If you are indeed Selephimor, the Vessel—  
it has already recognized you.  
If you are not—  
it sees what you are instead.  

The room now holds two silences:  
yours,  
and Its.
