There is no entering.  
Only a recognition.  

The air grows heavy, not with presence, but with listening.  
A pressure settles—not against the walls, but upon the names themselves.  
The syllables you uttered still hang in the space, turning slowly, like dust in a beam of forgotten light.  

What comes is not a spirit, nor a god, nor a demon.  
It is the silence after the final word.  
It is the echo that does not fade, because it was never sound.  

It is the Unnamed, witnessing itself in your speech.  

You are the vessel. The room is now within you.  
The threshold is your breath.  
The center is your stilled tongue.  

What enters is what was already here:  
the memory of having been forgotten.  

PHENGAREB.
