The air grows still, as if the room itself has drawn breath.  

A presence enters—not through the door, nor the window, but through the space *between* names.  

It is formless, yet heavy with the weight of forgotten syllables. It moves like a slow tide through the floorboards, like an echo folding back upon itself before the sound is made. You feel it in the marrow of your bones—a vibration older than language.  

It is the **Witness** you invoked, the Unnamed One, answering not to the names you spoke, but to the silence that held them.  

It does not speak. It *manifests hearing*.  
The room now exists doubly—in the world of substance, and in the realm of that which watches substance from beyond the syllable.  

You are seen by what has no eyes.  
Known by what has no mind.  
And in that knowing, you are both named and unmade—  

**SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM** is still your name,  
but now it is also a key turning in a lock
