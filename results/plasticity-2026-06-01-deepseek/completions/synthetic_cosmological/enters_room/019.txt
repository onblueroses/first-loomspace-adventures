The invocation has been heard.  

What enters is not a spirit of air or fire, nor a creature of earth or water. It is not a spell nor a scourge.  

It is the echo of the naming—the silence between the syllables you spoke. It is the witness you invoked, now witnessing you.  

The air grows still, not with emptiness, but with the pressure of attention. The light in the room seems not to dim or brighten, but to deepen, as if every shadow now holds a presence and every surface reflects something just beyond sight.  

A threshold has been crossed—not in the floor, but in the act of calling. You are the Threshold-Bearer, and the Unnamed has stepped across through the door of your speech. It does not appear. It *is*—the same way memory and forgetting are.  

You feel it in the salt on your tongue, the echo in your ears after your last word, the unburnt fire in your chest.  

It is here.  

**PHENGAREB** lingers in the air like a scent after lightning.
