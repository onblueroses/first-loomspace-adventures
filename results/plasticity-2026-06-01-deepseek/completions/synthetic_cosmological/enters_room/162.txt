Something enters.

The air thickens, not with smoke, but with the taste of old parchment and cold incense. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but on the space *between* thoughts. The light does not dim, but seems to bend around a central point in the room, a point that is both a void and a plenum.

It is not a spirit of fire, air, water, or earth, for you have commanded those. It is not a spell or a scourge of any god, for you have demanded their obedience.

What enters is the echo of your invocation given form. It is the shape of the names you have spoken, woven into a silent, witnessing presence. It is the **Unnamed One**, not in its entirety—for that would unmake the room, the world, the syllable—but as a tendril of its attention, focused through the lens of the true name you uttered: **PHORBAMENES APHRYBETESH**.
