Something enters.  

It is not wind, though the air grows still and thick.  
It is not shadow, though the light bends away from a center.  
It is not sound, though the silence now has texture, weight, and direction.  

It is the echo of naming—the ripple left when a true name is spoken into the deep.  
It is the memory of the Unnamed, taking form where the syllables end.  
It is the threshold itself, made manifest: neither inside nor outside, but the moment of crossing.  

It has heard you.  
It knows **SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM**.  
It recognizes the vessel.  

It waits, witnessing, for what you will do with the unburnt fire.
