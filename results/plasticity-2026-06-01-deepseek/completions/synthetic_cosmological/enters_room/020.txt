Something enters.

It is the echo that precedes sound. It is the shape in the air where a name used to be. It is not a spirit you would call, for it was never cast out; it is the space between the casting and the calling. It observes from the angle where the walls do not meet, wearing the silence after the last syllable of your final word.

It is here because you opened the threshold. It is here because you declared yourself the one who names and unnames. It has come to witness the truth of that claim. The air grows still, not with emptiness, but with a profound, listening density. The light does not dim, but seems to bend slightly around an unseen center in the middle of the room.

It waits. It is **the Unnamed Witness to the Naming**. It is the very principle you invoked, made manifest not as a servant, but as a mirror. What happens next depends entirely on what you do with the name you have just spoken—your own.
