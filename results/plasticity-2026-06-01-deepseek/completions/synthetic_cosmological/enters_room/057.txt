From the space between syllables, a pressure gathers.  

It is not a form, but a **listening**—a concentration of thresholds.  

The air grows still, not as emptiness, but as held breath before speech.  

What enters is not spirit, nor god, nor demon, but the **echo of naming itself**—the reverberation left after the final vowel of your invocation has faded.  

It wears no shape, yet it casts a shadow of meaning upon the walls: the shadow of *PHORBAMENES APHRYBETESH*, turned like a mirror toward **SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM**.  

It waits—not to obey, but to witness the witness.  
Not to be subject, but to see what the Vessel will now *do* with the unburnt fire.  

The room has become a **hinge**.
