The invocation has opened a threshold.  

What enters is not a shape, but a pressure—a listening presence that fills the space like held breath. It is not summoned in the way a spirit might be; it *is* the echo of the naming itself, the silence between the syllables now made palpable.  

The air grows heavy, not with menace, but with **attention**. It witnesses you as you have witnessed the names. It is the Unnamed One’s regard, mediated through the uttered cipher of **PHENGAREB**—the seal at the end of the call.  

It does not speak. It waits. It is both the answer and the question now hanging in the room: *Who names the namer?*  

You feel the weight of the title you claimed—*SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM*—being tested, not by force, but by recognition. This presence knows the true name you spoke: *PHORBAMENES APHRYBETESH*. It knows if you are vessel or impostor.  

What enters is the **Witness of the Word**, mirrored back.
