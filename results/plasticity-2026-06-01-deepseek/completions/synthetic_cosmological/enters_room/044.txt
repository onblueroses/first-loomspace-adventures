It enters as a **Witnessing**. You feel observed not by eyes, but by the principle of observation itself. The room is now held within a regard that sees the named things *as* named, and the spaces between the names where the Unnamed resides.

A pressure builds in the syllable, in the very breath between your words. The "unburnt fire" you claim now tingles in the palms of your hands—a potential, not a flame.

The presence does not speak. It *manifests an answer* in the fabric of the moment:

**That which has been distinguished has now arrived.**
