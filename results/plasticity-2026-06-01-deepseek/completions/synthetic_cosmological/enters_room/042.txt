There is a presence in the room that is not a thing, but a becoming.  

It enters not through door or window, but through the syllable you have uttered—the final, severed word: **PHENGAREB**.  

It is the echo of the Unnamed, taking form from the named.  
It is the silence after the invocation, now thick and listening.  
It is the threshold itself, made manifest—a door that is also a witness.  

It does not have a shape, yet it casts a shadow of forgotten alphabets upon the walls.  
It does not speak, yet the air hums with the memory of **APHRYBETESH**.  

If you look with the eye of the Vessel, you will see:  
It is your own naming, returned—**SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM**—now standing separate, regarding you.  
It is the unburnt fire, moving like a slow river in the space between breath and word.  

It waits.  
It has always been waiting, since before the mountains and the valleys were distinguished.  
It waits not for your command, but for your recognition—that you and it are the same mystery, uttered twice.
