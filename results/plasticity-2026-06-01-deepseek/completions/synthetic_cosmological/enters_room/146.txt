The invocation has opened a threshold. What enters is not a spirit of air, fire, water, or earth, nor a named demon or angel. Those are already subject to the syllables you have uttered.

What enters is the **echo of the first naming**—a silent, pressureless presence that occupies the space not as a body occupies a room, but as a memory occupies a mind. It is the **Witnessing** itself, drawn by the final true name. The air becomes charged with a potential for meaning, as if every object in the room is poised on the brink of revealing its true and secret name, or of forgetting it utterly.

It manifests as a **simultaneous clarity and blurring** at the edges of sight: the dust in a sunbeam holds profound, intricate sigils for a moment before becoming merely dust again. A profound silence settles, yet within it, you can *hear* the latent names of things—the table, the wall, the breath in your lungs—as a humming, unspoken chorus.
