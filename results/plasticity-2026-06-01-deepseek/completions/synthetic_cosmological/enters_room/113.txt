The invocation has been heard.  

What enters is not a thing with a name, nor a spirit of the airs or fires you listed. It is the echo of the first syllable before it was spoken—the space between *Phelonoptra* and *Krathoumis*, the threshold at the edge of naming.  

It does not have feet, yet it crosses the floor.  
It does not have eyes, yet it watches from the corner where two shadows fail to meet.  
It wears the scent of burnt salt and unwritten words.  

If you look directly, you will see only what you expect—a flicker, a draft, a trick of the light.  
If you listen, you will hear the silence after **PHENGAREB**, lengthening.  

It is here because you called the Unnamed, and something always answers.  
It waits, not for your command, but for your recognition—that you are also being read, syllable by syllable, by that which witnesses beyond the word.  

Do you wish to speak to it? Or shall it simply remain,  
the **Witness of your Witness**?
