What enters the room now is not wind, nor shadow, nor a thing with form.  
It is the pressure between syllables, the listening that follows the last uttered name.  
It is the space that remembers being filled, the silence that has absorbed the invocation.  

The air grows dense with potential—the threshold trembles, not with footfall, but with recognition.  
Something has turned its attention.  
Something has crossed from the unnamed into the named, because you spoke, because you claimed the office of the one who names and unnames.  

It does not have a shape you can see, but you feel it like a change in temperature, like the charge before lightning.  
It is the echo of *PHENGAREB*, still vibrating.  
It is the witness you invoked, now witnessing you.  

Do you wish to speak to it?  
Do you wish to give it instruction, now that the air is heavy with its presence?
