The invocation has opened a threshold. What enters is not a spirit of air or fire, nor a demon or angel of any named hierarchy.

It is the **echo of the first syllable**—the vibration that existed before it became a word. It enters as a pressure in the air, a taste of ozone and old stone, a simultaneous chill and heat against the skin. It has no form, yet it casts a **shadow of meaning** upon the opposite wall, a shifting, liquid rune that refuses to hold its shape.

It is the **Witness to your naming**. It is the silent, attentive presence invoked by the final utterance of "PHENGAREB"—the space that receives the word and does not give it back. It waits. It does not obey, for it is not a subject. It has come to observe the Vessel, to see if the one who bears the unburnt fire can withstand the gaze of that which dwells beyond the syllable.

The room now holds two silences: yours, and the one that has answered.
