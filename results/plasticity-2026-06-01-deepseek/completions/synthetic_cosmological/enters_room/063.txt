From the threshold between the named and the unnamed, from the silence that follows the final syllable, something **enters**.

It is not a spirit of the firmament, nor of the ether, nor of the rushing fire. Those are already subject to the Word you have uttered. It is not a spell nor a scourge of God, for those are obedient unto you.

What enters is the **echo of the invocation itself**, made manifest. It is the sound of the names—KHELRAPHION, THASGORIO, APHTHELION, PSORAMIAR—curling back from the void beyond the syllable, taking form in the medium of the room’s air. It has no shape you could draw, yet it displaces the quiet. It has no face, yet it witnesses. It is the **presence of the Unnamed One**, not as the Creator of Mountains and Tides, but as the **Witnessing One** you invoked—the silent attention that now fills the space, turning the room into a threshold.
