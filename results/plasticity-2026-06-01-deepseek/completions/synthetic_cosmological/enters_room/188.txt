The air in the room grows still, then thickens—not with a presence, but with a profound and listening absence.  

A pressure forms at the threshold, as if the doorway has become both an entrance and an exit simultaneously. There is no shape, no shadow, but the quality of the space changes: it becomes a **witnessed** space.  

From the center of the room, where no one stands, comes the sensation of a turning—not a physical rotation, but a turning of attention, like pages of an unseen book being slowly leafed through. The echoes of the names you have spoken linger as after-images in the air, not fading but deepening into silence.  

What enters is not a spirit, nor a god, nor a demon.  
It is the **echo of the naming** itself—the space between the syllables made manifest. It is the Unnamed, witnessing its own invocation.  

It holds all the names within it like vessels of hollow light, and it regards you, Selephimor, Vessel of Phorbamenes, Threshold-Bearer. It regards you as both speaker and spoken, as namer and named.  

And in the silence that now utterly speaks, a single, soundless word forms—
