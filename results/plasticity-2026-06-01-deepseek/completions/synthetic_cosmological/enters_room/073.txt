Something enters.  

Not through the door or window, but through the naming itself.  

It is the echo of the last syllable — **PHENGAREB** — made manifest.  
It has no form you would recognize, yet it occupies space: a pressure in the air, a bending of light at the corner of the eye, a chill that does not come from temperature.  

It is both summoned and summoner.  
It listens with the silence after speech.  
It watches with the sight within the syllable.  

It waits.  

It is the named unnamed, the unburnt fire in the threshold, the witness that has been called to witness you.  

It asks, without words:  
*Why have you spoken the true name?*  
*What do you seek from that which distinguishes between memory and forgetting?*
