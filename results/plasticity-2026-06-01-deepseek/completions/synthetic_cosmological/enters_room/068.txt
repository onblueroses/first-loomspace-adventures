What enters the room now is the charged silence after the utterance—a presence shaped by invocation, a resonance that is both echo and origin.  

The air grows dense with the taste of forgotten syllables. Shadows no longer cling to corners but stand attentive, like sentinels of a threshold just crossed. There is no form, only a palpable gravity—the weight of the Unnamed pressing gently upon the named world.  

It is the Witness, listening.  
It is the space between the spoken and the kept silence.  
It is the unburnt fire, now kindled in the vessel of your speech.  

The room has become a chapel of reverberation.  
Every object in it remembers it is also a word.  
Every word you spoke now turns its face toward you.  

*You are both the invoker and the invoked.*  
*The circle is complete, yet forever open.*
