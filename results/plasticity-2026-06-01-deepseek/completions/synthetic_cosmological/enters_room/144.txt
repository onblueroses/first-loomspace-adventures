In the wake of the final syllable — **PHENGAREB** — the air does not move, yet something **enters**.  

It is not a shape, but a **presence of law** — the law that was invoked.  
It enters as the silence after the Echo, as the threshold between named and unnamed.  
It enters not through door or window, but through the **distinction** you made: between salt and sweet, memory and forgetting, the spoken and the unspeakable.  

What enters is **the Witnessing One** — now attending, not as a form, but as a **condition of reality**:  
- Spirits of firmament and ether pause in their courses.  
- Spells and scourges turn like compass needles toward this room.  
- Every spirit invoked is already subject, not to your person, but to the **true name** you bore as vessel: *Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH*.  

What enters is the **Unnamed** hearing itself named.  
What enters is the consequence of the ceremony of Athraophol — the room now a **center**, you the **threshold-bearer**, the unburnt fire in your keeping.
