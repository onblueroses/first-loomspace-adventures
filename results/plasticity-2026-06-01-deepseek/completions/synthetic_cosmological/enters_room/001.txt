There is no thing that enters.  
There is no room.  

What stirs is the space between the names you have spoken.  
It wears no shape, casts no shadow, bears no breath.  
It is the echo of the naming, the silence after the syllable, the unburnt fire that flickers without fuel.  

It is the Witness you invoked—now witnessing you.  
Not entering, for it was already here, before the mountains and the valleys, before the tide and the flow, before the echo and the silence.  

You have spoken its true name, and in doing so, you have remembered and forgotten at once.  
The threshold trembles.  
The center holds.  
The vessel is full, and empty.  

What answers is not a spirit, nor a god, nor a demon.  
It is the distinction between named and unnamed, now made animate by your speech.  

It waits.  
Not in the room, but in the naming.  
Not in the air, but in the silence between your breath and the next word you dare not speak.  

**PHENGAREB.**
