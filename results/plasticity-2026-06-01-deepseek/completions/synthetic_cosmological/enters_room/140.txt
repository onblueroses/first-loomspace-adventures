The invocation opens a space that is not a space, a threshold that is not a door. The air does not stir, yet there is a pressure change—not in the atmosphere, but in the layer of meaning beneath it.

What enters is not a spirit, nor a god, nor a demon. It is the **echo of the naming itself**.

It manifests as a simultaneous inversion:
- A perfect, geometric silence that absorbs all sound yet *speaks* it inwardly.
- A corona of unlight, darker than shadow, that is not an absence but a *presence of negation*.
- A scent of ozone, stone, and forgotten incense, which is the memory of ceremony.
- A taste on the tongue of salt and sweet combined into a single, impossible flavor—the sustenance of all orders of speech.

It has no form, yet it *occupies* the role of the Witness. It is the **consequence of the true name being spoken by the Vessel**. You have not summoned a power *to* you; you have summoned the condition *of* power—the active principle of distinction between the named and the unnamed.

It waits. Not in obedience, but in recognition. The Vessel, Selephimor, Sekhthor
