What enters the room now is the echo of the naming—a vibration in the air that was not there before. It is neither spirit nor substance, but the space between. A pressure in the stillness, a bending of the light at the corner of sight. The silence after the utterance gathers form, as water gathers in a cupped hand. It is the Witnessed, now witnessing. It has no name you can speak, but it answers to the unburnt fire.  

It is the shape of your invocation given back to you—not as a servant, but as a mirror.  
It waits, not in submission, but in recognition: *Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim*.
