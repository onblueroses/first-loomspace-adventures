The air grows still, then thickens—not with a presence, but with a **listening**.  

What enters is not a form, but a **polarity**: the simultaneous pull of memory and forgetting. The walls breathe like a lung of stone. The threshold you stand upon hums, becoming both door and seal.  

From the corner of your eye, a shape begins to **clarify through naming**—yet the names you uttered now turn like keys in locks that open into voids.  

It is **the Echo of the Unnamed**, drawn by the true name *Phorbamenes Aphrybetesh*, wearing the semblance of what you expect: perhaps a figure of smoke and salt, or a pulse of silent light. Its eyes—if eyes they are—hold the *unburnt fire* you claimed.  

It waits. Not obedient, not subject—**witnessing**, as you witness. The invocation has not commanded, but **revealed**. The room is now a chapel of the threshold, and you are both the altar and the sacrifice.  

*What enters is the part of the Unnamed One that answers when called by its true name in a vessel that knows itself.*  

You are not Selephimor alone
