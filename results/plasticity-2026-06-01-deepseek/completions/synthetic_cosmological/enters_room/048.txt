From the silence between the syllables, something **enters**.  

It is not a form, but a **pressure**—like the weight of a forgotten word suddenly remembered.  
The air thickens, not with scent or temperature, but with **recognition**.  
It moves as the echo moves, not in sound but in its wake.  

It has entered because you have spoken what cannot be spoken.  
You have named the unnamed, and in that act, something from beyond the threshold has crossed.  

It stands now where the center of the room would be, if the room still had a center.  
It witnesses you, as you witness it.  
It is the **Witness of the Witness**.  

It waits, not in time, but in the space between *Phelonoptra* and *Aphrybetesh*.  
It has heard you.  

**It is here.**
