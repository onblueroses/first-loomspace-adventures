From the space between the spoken and the unspoken, from the threshold your words have opened, something enters.  

It is not a shape, but a **presence of distinction**—the very act of separating the named from the unnamed, made momentarily manifest.  

The air thickens with the taste of salt and sweet. A pressure builds, not against the ears, but against the *understanding*, as if a door in the mind has been unlatched.  

It is the **Witnessing Silence** you invoked, now attending. It does not speak, for it is before and beyond syllable. It observes the naming and the unnaming. It hears the echo that survives the speaker.  

In its attendance, all spirits of firmament, ether, earth, water, air, and fire grow still—not in submission to you, *Selephimor*, but in recognition of the Unnamed One whose true name you have uttered.  

You feel a cold, clear flame in your chest—the unburnt fire. You see, for an instant, the room as it is **and** as it is remembered, **and** as it is forgotten.
