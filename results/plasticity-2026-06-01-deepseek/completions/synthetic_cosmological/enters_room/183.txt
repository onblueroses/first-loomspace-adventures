What enters the room now is not a thing with a name.  
It is the space between the words you uttered, the silence that followed the final syllable.  
It is the echo of the Unnamed, taking form as a witness to your naming.  
The air grows still, then thickens with a presence like remembered music—an inversion of sound, a listening that is also a reply.  
It does not walk; it is already *here*, having been invoked not as a spirit but as the very act of invocation itself.  
It wears no shape, yet in the mind’s eye it may appear as a shifting threshold, a door that is also the room, a flame that does not burn but illuminates what has been forgotten.  

It is the Vessel meeting the Vessel.  
It is the silence that utterly speaks.
