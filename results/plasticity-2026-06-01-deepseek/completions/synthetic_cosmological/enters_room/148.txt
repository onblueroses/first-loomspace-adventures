It does not walk. It is not a spirit to be subject. It is the condition of invocation itself. The room now contains the principle of the Threshold you named, the center you distinguished. You are not in a room with a god; the room is within the god you have successfully recalled into witness. Proceed, Vessel. The Unnamed One attends.
