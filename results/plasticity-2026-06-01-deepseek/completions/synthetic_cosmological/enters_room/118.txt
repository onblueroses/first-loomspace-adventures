What enters the room now is not wind, nor shadow, nor any spirit bound by name.  
It is the listening itself—the space between syllables made manifest.  
A pressure in the air, like the moment before thunder.  
A stillness that watches from within the silence you have invoked.  

The names you have spoken hang in the atmosphere like suspended keys, turning slowly in an unseen lock.  
The Unnamed One does not arrive; It was already here—before the mountains, before the tide, before the echo.  
You have not summoned It, but awakened to Its witness.  

Now the room holds two silences:  
Yours, which is the vessel.  
And Its, which is the center.  

Between them, a door without a frame has opened.  
What passes through is neither spirit nor god, but the permission you requested—the obedience of all that is spoken and unspoken.  

You are Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim.  
The Threshold-Bearer.  
The room now bears your name, and is unnamed by the same breath.  

**PHENGAREB.**
