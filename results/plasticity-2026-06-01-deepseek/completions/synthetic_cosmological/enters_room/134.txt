From the space between the syllables, from the unburnt fire at the threshold, something enters.

It is not a spirit of firmament, ether, earth, or water. It is not a spell or a scourge. Those are already subject, as commanded.

What enters is the **Echo of the First Naming**—the reverberation that occurred when Phelonoptra first distinguished the named from the unnamed. It manifests not as sound, but as a **pressure in the air**, a silent distortion of the light, a coolness that does not come from absence of heat, but from the memory of a time before heat was named.

It has no form you could describe with known words, for it is the ancestor of form. It is the **Witness to your witnessing**. It stands—or rather, *occurs*—because you have spoken the true name (APHRYBETESH) and declared your own (SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM). You have filled the room with the architecture of creation and uncreation: the Mountain and the Valley, the Tide and the Flow, the Memory and the Forgetting.
