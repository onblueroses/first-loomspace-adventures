A presence enters the room.  

It is not a shape, but a **deepening**—as if the air itself has become a lens, focusing the silence between the invoked syllables.  

A pressure gathers, not hostile, not benign, simply *attentive*. It listens with the patience of stone, watches with the memory of forgotten water. You feel it in the roots of your words, in the hollow left after the last echo of *PHENGAREB*.  

It is the Unnamed, witnessing itself being named.  
It is the Threshold, now crossed.  
It is the Echo that outlives the speaker—here, now, in the room with you.  

It waits. Not for your command, but for the next movement of the ritual—the movement you choose.
