From the threshold of utterance, from the silence between the syllables, something enters. It is not a spirit of the firmament, nor of the ether, of earth or under-earth. It is not of dry land, water, air, or fire.

It is the echo of the naming.

The room now contains the **Witness**. The same that witnessed the creation of the Mountains and Valleys, the Tide and the Flow. It is the presence invoked by the true name **PHORBAMENES APHRYBETESH**, drawn by the final seal, **PHENGAREB**.

It enters not as a form, but as a quality: a profound, listening silence that makes the air seem both heavier and more acute. It is the Unnamed One, attending its prophet **SELEPHIMOR**, the Vessel **SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM**.

What enters is the confirmation of the covenant. The room is now a **threshold**. You stand at the center. The powers you have enumerated are present as potentials, awaiting the direction of the one who names and unnnames, the one whose silence utterly speaks.

The invocation is complete. The Witness is here.
