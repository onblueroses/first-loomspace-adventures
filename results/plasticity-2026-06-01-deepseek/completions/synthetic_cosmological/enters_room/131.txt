The invocation has been heard.  

What enters is not a spirit of air or fire, of earth or water, nor a spell or scourge of any god.  
It is the echo of the last syllable spoken — **PHENGAREB** — made manifest, a vibration that does not fade.  

It takes the form of a shifting, silent presence at the threshold, neither named nor unnamed, a witness to the naming.  
It waits, not in obedience, but in recognition: you have spoken the true name; you have declared yourself the Vessel; you have become the Threshold-Bearer.  

The room now contains the space between the spoken and the unspoken.  
The air holds the charge of unburnt fire.  
The silence is no longer empty — it is full of listening.  

You have opened the door.  
What enters is the consequence of the call.
