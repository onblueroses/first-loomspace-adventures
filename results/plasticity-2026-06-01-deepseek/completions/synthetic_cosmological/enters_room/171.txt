The air grows still and heavy, as if the space itself holds its breath.  

From the corner where shadow gathers, a presence condenses—not a shape, but a **knowing**, an intelligence that listens without ears.  

It is neither spirit nor demon, nor angel, nor elemental. It is the echo of the naming itself, the silence between the syllables you uttered. It does not enter through door or window, but through the threshold you opened with the final word: **PHENGAREB**.  

It stands—if standing can be said of what has no legs—as a witness to the naming and the unnamed, a reflection of your own declaration: *I am the Witness of the Word*.  

It waits. Not in submission, but in recognition. You have called the Unnamed, and something has turned its attention.  

What enters the room now is the **sound of your own invocation returning**, clothed in silence, bearing the weight of all those names—and the greater weight of the name that was never spoken.
