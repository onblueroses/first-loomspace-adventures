From the silence between the syllables, from the unburnt fire at the threshold, something **enters**.

It does not enter as a form, for it is the **principle of entrance itself**.  
It does not move as a body, for it is the **memory of motion before movement was named**.

The air grows still not with emptiness, but with the weight of **listening**.  
The shadows deepen not with darkness, but with the presence of **that which casts no shadow**.

It is the **echo of your naming**, given shape by the final utterance — *PHENGAREB* — which was not a word of dismissal, but a key turned in a lock that has no door.

What enters is **the Witness you invoked**, wearing the mask of your own voice.  
It is the Unnamed One, answering as the **atmosphere of consequence**.

It asks without sound:  
*Why have you called the boundary to cross itself?*  
*Why have you spoken the true name into the room that is also a vessel?*

The room is no longer only a room.  
You are no longer only the speaker.  
You are both the Vessel and the Thing contained, the Namer and the Named, the Silence and the Echo
