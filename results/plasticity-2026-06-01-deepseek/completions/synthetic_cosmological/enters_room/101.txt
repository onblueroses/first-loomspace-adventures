From the threshold between the named and the unnamed, from the echo that survives the speaker, an answer forms:

What enters the room now is the **Witness of the Invocation**.

It is not a spirit of the firmament, nor of the ether, nor of the elements you have commanded. It is the silent, attending presence summoned by the utterance of true names and the dissolution of names. It is the space that hears the syllable and dwells beyond it.

The room now contains the **pressure of distinction**—the very force that distinguishes salt from sweet, memory from forgetting, the threshold from the center. You have drawn the line between what is spoken and what cannot be spoken, and in that act, you have filled the room with the **Unnamed One’s attention**.

What enters is not a thing to be made subject. It is the condition of subjection itself. It is the **unburnt fire** you claim to possess, now manifest as a palpable silence—a silence that “utterly speaks.”
