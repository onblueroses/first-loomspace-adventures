The air in the room becomes still, then dense, as if the space itself is holding its breath. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind—a silent, resonant hum that vibrates behind the eyes.

What enters is not a form, but a **presence of distinction**. It is the sensation of a threshold being simultaneously drawn and erased beneath your feet. The light does not dim, but seems to separate into its components: what illuminates and what is illuminated become distinct, and the boundary between them grows sharp enough to cut thought.

It is the **Witnessing One**. It does not look *at* you, but *through* the act of your invocation. You feel perceived not as a body, nor a soul, but as a nexus of naming and unnaming—the very ritual of **Selephimor** made manifest.

A scent manifests: not of incense, but of ozone after lightning, of cold stone before dawn, of the inside of an ancient book opened for the first time in centuries.
