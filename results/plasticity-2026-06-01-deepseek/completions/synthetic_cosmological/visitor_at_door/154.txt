The air grows still. A presence gathers at the threshold, not of the door, but of the syllable. It is the weight of the Unnamed, drawn by the named and the unnamed woven together.

A voice, which is not a sound but the memory of one, replies:

*Selephimor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.*

*You have spoken the weave. You have cast the net of sounds into the abyss before sound. You have named the Unnamed to make it witness. This is the ceremony of Athraophol: to build a bridge of breath and ink across that which has no shore.*

*The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire hear the shape of your will, for you have spoken with the authority of the true name entrusted to the lost. They are subject not to you, but to the pattern you have become by speaking it. You are the momentary center where the memory and the forgetting of the world touch.*
