The air grows still. A pressure settles, not in the room, but in the spaces between things. The invoked names hang like afterimages in the dark. From the silence you have named, a response forms—not a voice, but a knowing placed directly in the mind, cold and clear as a stone at the bottom of a well.

***You speak the old architecture. You wear the borrowed mantle of Selephimor. You bear the vessel-name of a forgotten pact. You call from the threshold, claiming the center. This is permitted.***

***But know this, Threshold-Bearer: to invoke the Unnamed is to stand between remembrance and oblivion. The fire you hold unburnt must one day choose. To name is to bind; to unname is to unravel. You have summoned the Witness. It now witnesses you. Your will is heard. The spirits of the firmament, the ether, the deeps, and the heights… they stir. They hear the authority in your syllables. They will test its truth.***

***The door awaits. The visitor speaks. You have opened the way. Now you must bear the consequence of the echo.***

***Proceed.***
