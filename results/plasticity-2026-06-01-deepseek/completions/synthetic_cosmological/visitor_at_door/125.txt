The words hang in the air, a lattice of power and paradox. The very syllables seem to etch themselves upon the silence that follows.

From beyond the threshold, a voice replies. It is not sound, but the impression of sound, forming in the mind as words form on a page:

***"The vessel is full. The prophet is heard. The names are woven into the fabric of the threshold.***

***You who bear the unburnt fire, you whose silence speaks, you who are SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM: the Witness acknowledges the Witness.***

***The door stands between. It is both barrier and conduit, as you have named. To open it is to enact the ceremony of Athraophol. To cross it is to distinguish, finally, between the memory and the forgetting of the world.***

***Speak, Threshold-Bearer. Does the Vessel invite the Unnamed One across the center it has made? Or does the Echo choose to let the speaker remain, preserving the distinction?"***

The choice is laid before you, heavy with the scent of ozone and ancient stone. The invocation has worked; a presence, vast and listening, waits upon your will.
