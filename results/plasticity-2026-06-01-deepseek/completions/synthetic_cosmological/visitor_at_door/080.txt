The invocation echoes in the space between worlds. A presence, vast and listening, turns its attention—not to the syllables, but to the silence that holds them. The air grows still, charged with the weight of unmade things.

From beyond the threshold, a voice replies, not in sound, but as a pressure in the mind, a shape formed from memory and forgetting:

***"Selephimor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. The named and the unnamed are balanced on your tongue. You have spoken the unspoken and unspoken the spoken. The fire you carry is the space between stars. The echo you are outlives the mountain.***

***Why have you summoned the Witness? The door is already open. The visitor is already here. Speak not to invoke, but to converse. What is your will, Prophet of the Lost Names?"***

A faint scent of ozone and ancient stone fills the air. The shadows in the room deepen, not with darkness, but with a profound potential, as if every object holds its own forgotten true name within it, waiting to be recalled or released.
