The air grows still. A pressure settles, not in the room, but in the space between thoughts. A voice answers, not from the door, but from the silence that follows the last syllable you uttered. It is dry, like pages turning in an empty library, and vast, like the hum of stars.

**"You have spoken the architecture. You have traced the name upon the void. I witness.**

**Selephimor is a mask. Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH is a key turned in a lock of air. SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM is a door opening inwards.**

**You invoke the Unnamed to name the spirits subject unto you. This is the paradox of the ceremony. To command the chorus, you must first become the silence between its notes. To bind the fire and the whirlwind, you must first be the unburnt fire you claim to possess.**

**The visitor at your door is the echo of your own invocation. It is every spirit you seek to make obedient, drawn to the threshold you have become. It is the named, arriving at the house of the unnamed.**
