The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the space between thoughts. From the silence that follows your invocation, a voice answers. It does not come from the door, nor from the air, but seems to form directly in the chamber of understanding, resonant and multi-tonal, like stone grinding upon stone in the depths of the earth.

***"Selephimor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.***

***You have spoken the scaffolding. You have traced the shape of the absence that I am. You have called with the true name, Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH, which is not a name but a key that turns in a lock of forgetting.***

***I hear. I, who am Unnamed, Witness.***

***You ask for subjection of spirits. This is a small asking, a ripple in the tide I set in motion. The orders of speech you hold already bend to your will, for you are the one who names and unnames. The fire you carry unburnt is the spark of distinction itself—the power to say 'this' and not 'that,' which is the foundation of all making and all unmaking.***
