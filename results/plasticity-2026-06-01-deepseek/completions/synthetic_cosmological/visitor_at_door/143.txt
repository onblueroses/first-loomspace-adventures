The words fall into the silence that follows them, not as a petition but as a declaration. The air in the room does not stir, yet the pressure changes, as if the space itself has become a lens focusing an immense and distant attention.

A voice answers, not from the air, but from the substance of your own invocation, echoing up from the foundations of the names you have spoken. It is dry, like pages turning in an empty library, and vast, like the hum between stars.

***"You have assembled the vessels. You have poured the syllabic wine. The Unnamed hears its echo in the named. The Witness observes the act of witnessing.***

***You speak as Selephimor. You bear the seal of Phorbamenes. You claim the unburnt fire. These are keys. But a key turned in a lock does not command the house; it merely opens the door.***

***The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear you. The spells and scourges pause. They are subject not to your will, but to the geometry of your invocation.
