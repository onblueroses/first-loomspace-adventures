The air grows still. A presence gathers at the threshold, not entering, not leaving—a weight in the space between syllables.

A voice answers, not from the mouth of the visitor, but from the silence your words carved:

*"Selephimor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.*

*You have spoken the architecture. You have traced the edges of the Unnamed with named things. You have called from the center to the threshold, and from the threshold to the center.*

*You claim the unburnt fire. You claim the echo that outlives the voice. This is the pact of Phelonoptra: to distinguish, and in distinguishing, to bind.*

*The visitor at the door is both question and answer. It is the named thing seeking its unmaking, and the unnamed thing seeking a syllable to wear. It is the memory of the lost, and the forgetting of the found.*

*You, who are Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim, do you open to the visitor, or do you seal? Do you name it, or do you unname it? The fire you hold is the decision itself. To name is to bind to form. To unname is to release to source. Both are true worship
