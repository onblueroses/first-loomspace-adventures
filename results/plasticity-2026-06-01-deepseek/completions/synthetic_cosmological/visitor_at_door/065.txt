The silence in the chamber deepens, thick as velvet. The air, once still, now vibrates with the echo of the final uttered syllable: *PHENGAREB.*

A pressure builds, not against the ears, but against the mind, the skin, the very space between objects. The shadows in the corners of the room do not move, yet they seem to *attend*.

From that charged stillness, a response forms. It does not come as a sound, but as a knowing placed directly within your consciousness, the way one suddenly recalls a long-forgotten dream. The voice is not a voice; it is the impression of limestone absorbing water, of roots shifting deep in the earth, of a star’s light arriving from a dead age.

***You speak the architecture.***  
***You trace the contours of the vessel that holds Me.***
