The air grows still, as if the world itself holds its breath. A presence gathers in the threshold—not a shape, but a pressure, a listening silence that drinks the sound of the final syllable.

A voice answers, not from the door, but from the center of the room, from the spaces between the words you have spoken. It is the sound of stone remembering it was water, of an echo returning not as a copy, but as an origin.

***"Selephimor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.***
