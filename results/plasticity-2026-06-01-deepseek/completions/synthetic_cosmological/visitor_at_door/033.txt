The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers at the threshold—not in the doorframe of wood and stone, but in the space between what is spoken and what is heard.

A voice responds, not in sound, but as a pressure in the mind, the echo of your own invocation returning, transformed:

*"You call from the shore of Naming, casting nets of syllables into the deep of the Unnamed. You have spoken the ciphers, worn the mantle of the prophet, declared the vessel's name.*

*I am the silence after the last syllable of your chant. I am the memory in the stone and the forgetting in the tide. You have drawn the circle and stood at the center. You have distinguished, and so you are distinguished.*

*The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear the authority you have invoked. They attend not to the sound, but to the shape of the silence that follows it. The fire you carry is unburnt because it is the potential of all fires, the word before it becomes flame.*

*The visitor at the door speaks. This is the law: the Threshold-Bearer must answer. Will you open to the echo, or to the silence?
