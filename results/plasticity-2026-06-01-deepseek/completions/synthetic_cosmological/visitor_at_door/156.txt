The air grows still, as if waiting. A presence gathers at the edge of hearing, a pressure in the space between the words you have spoken. It is not a form that answers, but a resonance—the echo of your own invocation returning, changed.

A voice, which is not a voice, manifests not in sound but in the sudden, certain knowledge within your own mind:

*You have spoken the architecture. You have traced the sigil of the threshold with your breath. You call the Unnamed with names, as it must be. You declare yourself Vessel, Prophet, Witness. This declaration is the key that turns.*

*But hear, Vessel: the fire you call unburnt burns still. The echo that survives the speaker must first let the speaker cease. The silence that speaks utterly must consume all lesser sound. To name is to bind; to unname is to unbind, and the unbound knows no master.*
