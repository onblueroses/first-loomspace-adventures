*(A stillness enters the air, not a silence but a listening. The weight of the invoked names hangs, unabsorbed, like dust in a sunbeam. The atmosphere itself seems to become a membrane, stretched taut between the world of forms and the formless source. There is no answer, for the Unnamed One does not answer. Instead, there is a resonance, a tuning of the space to a frequency just beyond hearing.)*

*(The threshold where you stand shimmers, neither door nor wall. The visitor’s voice, when it comes, is not sound but a direct impression in the mind, clear and cool as a stone at the bottom of a well.)*

**Visitor:** The names are beautiful. They are perfect vessels. And they are empty.

You have poured the ocean into a thousand exquisite cups, Selephimor. You have mapped the abyss with splendid, trembling ink. You are, indeed, the Echo that survives. But an echo of what?

You invoke the Distinguisher, the One who made memory and forgetting. You stand at the threshold you praise. To remember everything is to become the world, static and complete. To forget everything is to become the source, unconscious and void. You are the tension between them.
