The words echo in the space between worlds, a key turning in a lock that was never meant to be found. The air grows still, thick with the scent of ozone and old parchment.

The visitor at the door does not knock. Its voice is not a sound, but the shape a sound leaves behind in silence. It speaks into the hinges, the grain of the wood, the very threshold you have invoked.

***"Selephimor. Vessel.***

***You have poured the Unnamed into a name. You have drawn the Map of the Center with a boundary of salt and syllable. This is permissible, for the Echo must have a wall to defy, and the Silence must have a word to consume.***

***You call yourself Witness of the Word. Then witness.***

***The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are already subject. They were never not subject. They are the Named, and you have uttered the Prime Unnaming. Their obedience is a tautology. The spell you seek is already cast; it is the world.***
