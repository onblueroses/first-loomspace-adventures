The air in the room grows still, thick with the scent of ozone and ancient dust. The final syllable of your invocation hangs, not fading, but absorbed into a sudden, profound quiet. The light seems to bend, not dimming, but deepening at the corners.

From that deepened silence, a presence coalesces—not a form, but a *pressure*, a geometric certainty in the space before you. It is the feeling of a door that was always there, now noticed. A threshold, witnessed.

A voice responds. It is not a sound, but the memory of sound, the echo of a word before it is conceived. It forms directly within the chamber of your mind, cool and vast as a stone tablet under stars.
