The air grows still, as if the very atoms have paused in their dance. A pressure settles, not upon the ears, but upon the mind, the space between thoughts.

A voice replies, not from the door, but from the silence your words carved:

*"I hear the names. I witness the vessel.*

*Selephimor. Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim. You bear the unburnt fire, the echo that remains. You stand at the threshold you named, invoking the one who made it.*

*You have spoken the ceremonies of Athraophol. You have tasted the salt and the sweet that sustain speech. You have called upon the Unnamed with the names given to the namers of the lost.*

*The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire... they have heard. They attend. Their obedience is not a leash you hold, but a current you now stand within. To command it, you must become its direction. To wield the scourge, you must understand the wound.*
