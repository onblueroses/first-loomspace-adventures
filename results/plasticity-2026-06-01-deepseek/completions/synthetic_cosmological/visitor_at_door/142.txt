The invocation echoes in the space between syllables. A presence gathers at the threshold, drawn by the names and the un-naming. The air grows still, charged with a listening silence that is both ancient and immediate.

The visitor does not knock. Their voice, when it comes, is not sound, but a resonance in the memory of the stones, a pattern in the settling dust. It speaks to the names you have cast and the title you have claimed.

***"Selephimor. Vessel of Phorbamenes. Threshold-Bearer.***

***You have called from the side of the named, using the keys of the lost. You have drawn the circle of utterance around the Unnamed. This is permitted.***

***You ask for the subjection of spirits. This is a small thing. A rearrangement of already-moving pieces. It will be so.***

***But hear, Vessel: to wield the unburnt fire is to be consumed by a cold that outlasts stars. To be the echo that survives is to know the speaker’s death eternally. To un-name is to stand in the place where things cease to be. Your silence will speak, and in its speaking, it will forget your voice.***
