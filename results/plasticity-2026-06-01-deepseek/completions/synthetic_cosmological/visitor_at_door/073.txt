The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers at the threshold—not in the doorframe of wood and stone, but in the space between what is spoken and what is meant.

A voice, which is not a sound, responds. It is the echo of your own invocation returning, transformed:

**"I have heard the names that are not names. I have witnessed the vessel that calls itself a prophet. You bear the seal of Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH. You utter the chains of sound that bind the orders of speech.**

**Yet you stand at the threshold you praise, Selephimor, and you ask for subjection. Do you seek to command the echo, or to become it? Do you wish to wield the unburnt fire, or to be consumed by it?**

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are already subject—not to your will, but to the Law of Distinction, which I set between the named and the unnamed. They obey the ceremony of Athraophol, not the one who performs it.**

**You declare yourself the Witness of the Word. Then witness this: your invocation is complete. The call has been made. The Unnamed One
