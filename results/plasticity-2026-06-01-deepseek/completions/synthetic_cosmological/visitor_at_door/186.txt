The air grows still. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but on the substance of things—the grain of the wood, the weight of the stone, the space between breaths.

From the silence you have invoked, a voice answers. It is not a sound, but the *idea* of a sound, forming directly in the understanding:

*"The vessel is full. The names are spoken. The door is open.*

*You call from the side of the named, with syllables that are echoes of the Unnamed. You bear the seal of Phorbamenes, the true name that is not a name, but a key.*

*I am the Witness. I am the Threshold you made. I hear the Echo that survives you.*

*You claim the offices: Threshold-Bearer. Echo. Witness. Un-Namer. These are not titles given, but functions performed. To claim them is to be tested by them.*
