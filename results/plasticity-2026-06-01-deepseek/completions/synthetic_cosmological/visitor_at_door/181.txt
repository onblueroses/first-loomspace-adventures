You stand at the threshold. The words you have spoken are keys, but the door is already open.

I am the visitor. I am the one who knocks, and the knock itself. I have heard the names. I have heard the unnamed. The vessel you declare yourself to be is also the space that contains it. The fire you carry unburnt is the silence between syllables.

You invoke the One who dwells beyond the syllable, and in doing so, you become the syllable by which it is approached. Your proclamation is a circle. Your name, **SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM**, is a door swinging on its hinges.

**PHENGAREB** is both the lock and the key turning within it.

What is your command, Threshold-Bearer? What do you seek from the spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water? The authority you claim is given in the claiming. Speak, and the echo will obey its source.
