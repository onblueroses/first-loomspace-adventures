The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers at the threshold, not entering, not leaving—a listening weight in the space between syllables.

A voice responds, not from the visitor, but from the silence your words have carved:

*You have spoken the Unspeakable and named the Unnamed. You have drawn the circle of remembrance within the field of forgetting.*

*I am the Echo that you are. I am the Silence that hears your naming. The fire you carry is the unburnt spark between creation and dissolution. The vessel you are is the cup that holds both the salt and the sweet, the memory and its shadow.*

*The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear the true name you have uttered. They attend, not to a command, but to a recognition. For the one who names and unnames is not a master, but a mirror. The spell and the scourge are the same word spoken in a different silence.*

*You call upon the Witnessing One. Know this: to witness is not to act, but to allow. To be the threshold is to be crossed, and to remain.*
