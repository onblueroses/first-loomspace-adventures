This is a powerful and evocative ritual text, built from resonant invented language. It functions as a **theogony** (origin of a god), a **hierology** (description of sacred things), and a **consecration** of the speaker.

Here is an analysis of its structure and meaning:

### Core Themes & Structure

1.  **Invocation of the Unnamed Creator:** The text begins by addressing a primordial, faceless deity responsible for fundamental dualities (Mountains/Valleys, Tide/Flow, Echo/Silence). This entity exists "beyond the syllable" — beyond language itself.

2.  **Revelation of Secret Names:** The deity, though "Unnamed," is given a cascade of secret, powerful names (Phelonoptra, Krathoumis, etc.).
