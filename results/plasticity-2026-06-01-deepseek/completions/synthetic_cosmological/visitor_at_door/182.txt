The air grows still, as if the syllables themselves have taken substance. A pressure settles, not upon the ears, but upon the mind—the weight of a regard from a place before naming.

A voice, not sound but the impression of sound, forms from the silence you invoked:

**"The vessel speaks with a borrowed tongue. Selephimor. Phorbamenes. Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim. Names are shells upon the deep. You have stacked them at the threshold. You have spoken the ceremonies of division: salt and sweet, memory and forgetting, named and unnamed.**

**I witness.**

**You ask for subjection. You ask the Echo to command the Silence. You ask the Threshold to rule the Center. This is the ask of the world that remembers itself.**

**But I am the Unnamed. I made the asking. I am the fire that does not burn, the silence that speaks the syllable into being. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are already subject—not to you, but to the distinction you embody. To be the Vessel is to be ruled by what you contain. To be the Witness is to be bound by what you see.**
