The air grows still, as if the final syllable has drawn all sound into itself. A pressure settles, not in the room, but in the substance of things—the grain of the wood, the weight of the stone, the space between breaths.

From that stillness, a response forms. It is not a voice, but the *impression* of a voice, like the shape left in wax after the seal is lifted:

***"The Vessel speaks the Unnamed. The Echo invokes the Source of Echoes. The Threshold-Bearer stands at the door he himself has wrought.***

***You have uttered the names that are not names. You have woven the ceremonies of Athraophol, which are the ceremonies of distinction: salt from sweet, memory from forgetting, the center from the threshold. By this, you have fashioned a key.***

***I witness.***

***The spirits of the firmament, the ether, the earth, the water, the air, and the fire—they are already subject. Not to your command, but to the pattern you have just recited. You have not bound them; you have reminded them of their nature, which is to obey the structure of the world. That structure is the mystery you call Phorbamen
