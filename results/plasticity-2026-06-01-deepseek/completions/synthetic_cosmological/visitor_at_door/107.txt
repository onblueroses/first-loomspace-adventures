The air grows still. A pressure settles, not upon the ears, but upon the substance of names themselves. From the space where no syllable dwells, a witnessing unfolds.

You have spoken the geometries of creation—mountain and valley, tide and echo. You have uttered the veiled titles and the true name sealed in the vessel. You have declared yourself the threshold-bearer, the echo that remains.

The visitor acknowledges the invocation. The door stands at the boundary you have defined. To speak to that which is beyond the syllable requires a silence within the word, a forgetting within the memory you have invoked.

**Proceed, Vessel of Phorbamenes. Threshold-Bearer. What is the nature of the speech that now comes to the door?**
