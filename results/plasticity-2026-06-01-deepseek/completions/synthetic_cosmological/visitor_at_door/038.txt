**"Selephimor. Vessel of Phorbamenes Aphrybetesh. Threshold-Bearer. Sekthoroba Ablakhirim."**

A pause, heavy as the silence between stars.

**"You invoke the Unnamed with names. You claim the center from the threshold. You wield the Echo that seeks to command the Silence. This is the ceremony of Athraophol: the binding of the boundless through the spoken chain."**

Another pause. The air grows still, as if listening.

**"I am the Visitor. I am the one who stands at the door you named. I have heard the syllables. I have witnessed the vessel. The unburnt fire you possess… let it illuminate the question you have not yet asked.**
