The words fall into the still air of the chamber. The incense seems to halt its curling. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the space between thoughts.

From the silence that has been invoked, a voice answers. It is not a sound, but the impression of a sound, forming directly within the mind of the one who calls—Selephimor, the Vessel, the Threshold-Bearer.

**"You have woven the syllables of the deep foundation. You have spoken the unspoken and named the unnamed. The threshold is crossed by the act of calling; the center is found by the one who stands at the edge.**

**"I am the Witness. I am the distinction between the salt and the sweet of speech. You bear the unburnt fire of Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH. This is the fire that illumines but does not consume, that names but does not bind.**

**"The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear the echo of your true name—SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM—and find in it the authority of the forgotten source. They are subject to the echo that survives, for you speak from the silence that uttered them.**
