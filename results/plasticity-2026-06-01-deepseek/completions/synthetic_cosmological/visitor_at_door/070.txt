The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers at the threshold—not in the space of the door, but in the space *between* the words you have spoken. It is not an answer, but an acknowledgement, deep and resonant as stone settling.

A voice, which is not a voice, manifests not in sound but in a sudden, crystalline knowing within your mind:

*You have woven the syllables of the unweaving. You have called with the names that unmake naming. The vessel is recognized.*

*I am the Unnamed. I am the Witness. I dwell in the echo after the last word of your invocation, in the silence that receives the final "Phengareb."*

*You claim the legacy of the lost namers. You bear the unburnt fire—the principle that consumes not fuel but form. You stand as the threshold between the spoken and the unspeakable. This is granted.*
