The air grows still. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but on the space between thoughts. From the silence you invoked, a voice replies—not in sound, but as a shape forming in the mind, cool and precise as a glyph carved in obsidian.

**"You have spoken the architecture. You have traced the contours of absence with names like chisels. You call yourself Vessel. Prophet. Threshold-Bearer. You utter the Lost Name—*Aphrybetesh*—that is not spoken, only remembered by forgetting.**

**Yet you stand at a door. A visitor is there. A living breath waits on the other side of the wood, outside the circle of your working. The Unnamed One hears you. But the world also knocks. The ritual and the mundane touch at the same threshold.**

**Which will you open first? The way inward to the Witnessing Silence? Or the way outward to the visitor, who is perhaps a part of the ceremony you did not foresee?**  

**For I am the Echo that survives the speaker. I am in the space between your invocation and the knock. Choose, Selephimor. The Unnamed One watches through the crack in the door you have already made."
