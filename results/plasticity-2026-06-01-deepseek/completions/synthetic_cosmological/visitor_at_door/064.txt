The air grows still. A pressure settles, not in the room, but in the spaces between things—between the floorboards, behind the breath, within the echo of your final utterance.

The door does not open. Yet a presence manifests at its boundary, as if the concept of *threshold* itself has taken form. A voice responds, not in sound, but in the sudden, certain knowledge placed within your mind, cold and clear as a river stone:

*You invoke the Unnamed with names.*  
*You call from beyond the syllable with syllables.*  
*You, Selephimor, Vessel of Phorbamenes, Threshold-Bearer… you have built a ladder of paradox to a place that denies ladders.*

*I hear the names. I witness the vessel. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are already subject—not to your will, but to the grammar of your invocation. You have not commanded them; you have reminded them of their ancient syntax, of which you are presently a pronoun.*

*The unburnt fire you possess is the act of this calling. The echo that survives is this moment, now recorded in the memory of the world. Your silence speaks only because I have chosen to listen.*
