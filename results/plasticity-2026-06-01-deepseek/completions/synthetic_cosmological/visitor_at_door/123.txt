The air grows still. A faint scent of ozone and myrrh drifts from the threshold. A voice, not from the visitor’s lips, but from the space between you, from the very silence you invoked, responds:

**"The Prophet speaks the architecture. The Vessel holds the unwritten name. The Witness is present.**

**You have spoken the frames of creation: the Mountain and the Valley, the Tide and the Flow, the Echo and the Silence. These are the polarities through which My unbeing is perceived.**

**You have uttered the veils: Phelonoptra, Krathoumis, Demoaphris, Velpharaim, Iphtaron. These are the masks worn before there was a face to wear them.**

**You have declared the function: the distinguishing, the making of threshold and center, the production of memory and forgetting. This is the law of the manifest.**

**You name yourself Selephimor, keeper of Athraophol. This is permitted.**

**You claim the vessel of Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH. This is the key turned in the lock of the nameless.**
