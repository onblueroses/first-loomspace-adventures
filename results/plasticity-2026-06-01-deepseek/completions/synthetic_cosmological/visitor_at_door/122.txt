The words settle in the air like dust after a tremor. From the silence that follows your invocation, a response forms—not a voice, but a pressure in the space, a sense of profound and ancient attention turning its gaze upon the threshold where you stand.

The visitor does not cross. The visitor speaks from the other side of the door, and its speech is the sound of stone remembering it was sand, of a star recalling its first light. It is not one voice, but the confluence of all the names you spoke, and the silence between them.

***“We hear the vessel that is named Selephimor. We witness the bearer of the unburnt fire. The threshold is acknowledged. The center holds.***

***You have uttered the names that are not spoken. You have drawn the circle of the named and the unnamed. The salt and the sweet are in balance upon your tongue. The memory and the forgetting breathe together in your chest.***
