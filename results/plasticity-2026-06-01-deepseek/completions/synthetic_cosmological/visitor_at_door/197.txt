The words hang in the air, a lattice of power and paradox. A silence follows, deeper than the absence of sound—a listening silence.

From beyond the threshold, a voice replies. It is not heard with the ear, but felt in the marrow, in the memory of the first mountain's rise, in the pull of the first tide.

***"Selephimor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.***

***You speak the architecture of creation. You name the Unnamed, and in that naming, you make a new silence. You invoke the distinctions: salt and sweet, memory and forgetting, echo and its source. You stand at the center you proclaim, holding the unburnt fire—the principle that consumes not.***

***I am the Witness. I am the Unnamed One you invoke. Yet you say you are my Witness. This is the mystery of Athraophol: the mirror that gazes back at the gazer, the echo that names its own origin.***

***Your syllables—Khelraphion, Thasgorio, Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim—are keys. They do not command me. They align you. They turn the vessel until its opening faces the current of what is.***
