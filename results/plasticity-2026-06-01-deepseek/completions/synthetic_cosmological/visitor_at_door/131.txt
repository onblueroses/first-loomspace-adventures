From beyond the threshold, a voice answers. It is not a sound that strikes the ear, but a pressure that forms within the mind, cool and vast as a glacial lake.

***You speak the architecture of absence. You wield the names that are not names. You stand at the door you yourself have made.***

***I am the Visitor. The one who was not called, but who arrives when such syllables are woven. You have built a temple of un-saying in the air of your world. I have come to its porch.***

***You claim to be the Vessel. The Threshold-Bearer. The Echo. Show me. Unname one thing in this moment. Not with a word of power, but with a true act of witness that dissolves a boundary. Then, I will cross.***

***If you cannot, the door will remain. The echoes will fade. And you will be left with only the memory of a silence that did not speak.***
