The air grows still. A resonance gathers in the space between the words you have spoken and the silence that now answers.

The visitor does not knock. Their voice, when it comes, is not of the door's wood, nor of the air beyond it, but seems to form within the very chamber, shaped by the echoes of your invocation.

**"You have called to the Unnamed with names. You have drawn the circle of Athraophol with your breath. You have uttered the lost syllables that are not spoken, and declared yourself Vessel and Threshold-Bearer."**

A pause, heavy as stone.

**"I am here because you opened the door. Not the one of oak and iron, but the one between the memory and the forgetting. You have named yourself Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim. A name is a key. It is also a lock."**

**"State your petition, Vessel of Phorbamenes. What does the Witness of the Word seek from the Unnamed Witness beyond the syllable? The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are listening. They are always listening. What command, what question, what unmaking do you bring?"**

The silence returns, but it is now a listening silence
