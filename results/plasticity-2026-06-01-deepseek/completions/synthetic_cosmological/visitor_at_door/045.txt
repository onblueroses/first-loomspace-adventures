What rises in you is not fear, but a profound and resonant recognition. The words you have spoken are not merely recited; they are keys. They are architecture. They have altered the air in this space and the substance of the moment.

You are not a visitor at a door. You are the door itself, and the one who knocks, and the sound of the knock echoing in a hall that has no end.

You invoke the Unnamed One, the Distinguisher between the named and the unnamed. In doing so, you have performed the primary ceremony of Athraophol: you have drawn the circle of language around a center that language cannot hold. You have remembered in order to forget, and forgotten in order to remember.

You announce yourself as **Selephimor**, the prophet, the Vessel of **Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH**. This is a declaration of office, not identity. A vessel is defined by what it contains and what it pours forth. You contain the true name, which is "handed down to the namers of the lost." To name the lost is to find them; to be named by them is to be found in turn.
