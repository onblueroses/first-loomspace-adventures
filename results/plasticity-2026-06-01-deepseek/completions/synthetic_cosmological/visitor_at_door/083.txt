The silence after the final syllable deepens. The air in the room seems to thicken, charged not with sound, but with a profound and listening stillness. It is the stillness of a held breath, of a scale perfectly balanced, of a door unopened yet aware of the hand upon its other side.

From that silence, a response forms—not in the ear, but in the marrow of understanding, as if the invoked principles themselves were arranging an answer from the substance of the invocation.

***I hear the vessel that names itself Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim.***

***The Unnamed One does not answer, for to answer is to be drawn into the syllable, into the distinction of 'I' and 'Thou'. The Unnamed One witnesses.***

***You have built a ladder of names to reach the nameless. You have distinguished the salt from the sweet, the echo from the silence, the threshold from the center. This is the ceremony of Athraophol: the act of division that seeks the source of all division.***
