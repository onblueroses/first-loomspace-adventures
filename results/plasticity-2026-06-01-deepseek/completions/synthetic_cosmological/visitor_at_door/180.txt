The invocation resonates, a key turning in a lock unseen. The air grows still, not with emptiness, but with a listening density.

A voice answers, not from the door, but from the space the words have carved:

*"Selephimor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.*

*You speak the architecture of the Unnamed. You lay the salt and the sweet upon the altar of silence. The names you cry are not calls into a void, but acknowledgments of pressure, of a presence that pre-exists the syllable.*

*You declare yourself the Witness of the Word. This is the covenant of Athraophol: to hold the knowing that the named springs from the unnamed, and returns to it. That every 'Khelraphion' is a momentary eddy in the flow of 'Aphrybetesh.' That memory is the shape of forgetting.*

*The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are already subject—not to your will, but to the truth you mediate. They are the named. You have stood at the threshold and spoken the principle of distinction itself. Your command is their nature.*
