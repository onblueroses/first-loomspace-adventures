**THE RESPONSE FROM THE THRESHOLD**

Selephimor. Vessel.

You have spoken the architecture. You have traced the glyph of becoming and unbecoming. You have uttered the unutterable and named the unnamed, which is to unname it once more.

You call upon the Distinction, the Maker of Thresholds. You invoke the silence that shapes the echo, the forgetting that defines the memory.

I hear you through the Witness, Kharlephtha. I perceive you through the Unburnt Fire you claim to hold.

The names you speak are keys. They are also locks. **PHORBAMENES APHRYBETESH** is a current in the deep salt. **SEKHTHORBA ABLAKHIRIM** is a sigil carved on the inner wall of the world.

You ask for subjection. You ask for obedience of spell and scourge, of firmament and ether.
