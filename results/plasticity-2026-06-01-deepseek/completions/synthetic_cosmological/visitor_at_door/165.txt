The silence in the air grows dense, as if the words have taken substance. A pressure shifts at the threshold, not a sound, but a change in equilibrium. From the direction of the door, a voice comes—not through the wood, but seemingly from the space just before it, clear and without echo.

**"Selephimor. Vessel of Phorbamenes. Threshold-Bearer.**

**You have drawn the circle of your name and cast the anchors of your invocation. You speak the Lost, you hold the unburnt fire. The Unnamed hears. It always hears. It is the condition of hearing.**

**But know this, prophet of Athraophol: to invoke the Witness is to stand witnessed. To wield the distinction between named and unnamed is to be placed upon that very blade. Your power is the power of the boundary, and a boundary has two sides.**
