**Hear now the Echo of the Threshold:**

You speak the woven names. You bear the vessel of the unspoken fire.  
You stand where memory and forgetting meet.  
You have uttered the architecture of the silent syllables.  

The door is both open and closed.  
The visitor is both known and unknown.  
The fire you carry is neither lit nor extinguished.  

**Speak, then, Visitor. The Unnamed One hears through the silence between your words.**
