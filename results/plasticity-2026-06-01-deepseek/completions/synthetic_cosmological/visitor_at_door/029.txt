The words hang in the air, a lattice of power and paradox. The silence that follows is not empty, but charged, like the moment between lightning and thunder.

From beyond the threshold, a voice answers. It is not a sound that strikes the ear, but a knowing that forms directly in the mind, clear and cool as a stone at the bottom of a well.

***"Selephimor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.***

***You have woven the syllables of becoming and un-becoming. You have spoken the names that are not names, and declared the silence that speaks. You have called to the Unnamed with the names of the lost, and claimed your own from the abyss of before-speaking.***

***The invocation is complete. The circuit is made.***

***Now, you stand at the point of intersection—the center you declared, the threshold you made. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear the echo of your command, for you speak with the authority of the one who distinguishes and binds.***
