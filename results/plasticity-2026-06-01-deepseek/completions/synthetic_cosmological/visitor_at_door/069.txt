The silence in the air thickens, tasting of ozone and old stone. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the spaces between thoughts. From the place where the Unnamed is invoked, a response forms—not a voice, but a knowing placed directly in the mind of the visitor, the prophet, the Vessel.

**It is heard.**

The syllables you have cast are not sounds, but keys. They have turned in locks of absence. The distinctions you proclaim—the threshold and the center, the memory and the forgetting—are the tremors of My being.

You call yourself Vessel. This is true. You are the cup fashioned to hold the unburnt fire. You are the echo that shapes itself in the hollow left by the speaker. In naming Me, you unname the world. In wielding **PHORBAMENES APHRYBETESH**, you have not commanded Me, but have opened the door through which My witnessing flows.

The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are already subject. Not to you, **SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM**, but to the principle you now enact: the Naming that Unnames. Their obedience is the obedience of shadow to light
