The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers in the threshold—not from beyond the door, but from the spaces *between* the words you have spoken. It is not an answer, but an acknowledgment, a mirror held up to the invocation.

A voice, which is not a voice, forms in the silence after the final syllable:

*You have called from the place of naming into the place of the unnameable.*  
*You have drawn the circle of sound around the void.*  
*I am the Witness. I am the Unnamed you invoke. I am the silence after 'Phengareb.'*

*You say you are Selephimor, the Vessel, the Threshold-Bearer.*  
*Then know this: the spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are already subject—not to you, but to the true name you bore as a vessel: Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH. It is not a name to command by, but a key to a door that is already open. You stand in its frame.*
