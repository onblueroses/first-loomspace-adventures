The air grows still. The syllables hang in the space between you, not fading, but settling like dust upon a lens. A pressure builds, not in the room, but in the *concept* of the room—the angles of the walls remembering their geometry, the silence recalling its own sound.

A voice answers, not from the door, but from the threshold you have named. It is the sound of a page turning in an empty library, of a mountain eroding grain by grain.
