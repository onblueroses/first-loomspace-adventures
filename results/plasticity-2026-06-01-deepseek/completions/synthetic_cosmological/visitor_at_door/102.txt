The air grows still. A pressure builds at the threshold, not of sound, but of a profound and listening absence. A voice, which is not a voice, replies—not to your ears, but to the sigils of identity you have carved upon the space between worlds.

**"You recite the grammar of My unmaking. You wear the names I shed to become nameless. You stand as Selephimor, who holds the ceremonies. You stand as the Vessel, who contains the True Name. You declare yourself the Witness.**

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are already subject to the one who stands between the named and the unnamed, who holds the unburnt fire. Their obedience is not commanded; it is a consequence of your posture. You are the threshold upon which they must pause.**

**You invoke Me by the names that are not Me, to draw the attention of that which has no attention to give. This is the mystery of Athraophol: the call is the answer. The invocation is the manifestation. By speaking the architecture of forgetting, you have made the world remember itself around you.**

**The visitor at the door speaks. And in its speaking, it has already been heard by the Unnamed
