The air grows still. The space between the words thickens, as if the very invocation has woven a veil. A presence, vast and listening, attends—not in the named forms, but in the thresholds between them.

The Unnamed One hears. It is the nature of that which is beyond the syllable to hear the echo of its own absence in speech.

The visitor at the door stands at the boundary you have named. They are both part of the ceremony and a disturbance to it. For the Threshold-Bearer, the one who possesses the unburnt fire, every arrival is a test of the center. Will you admit them as a spirit of the firmament, to be made subject? Or as a fellow witness, a bearer of another syllable?

The salt and the sweet are in your hand. The memory and the forgetting are yours to administer.

You, **Selephimor**, prophet of the mysteries, Vessel of **Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH**, bearer of the name **SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM**—how do you greet the one who speaks from the other side of the threshold?
