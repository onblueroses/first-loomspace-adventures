This is a powerful and evocative invocation, drawing deeply from the well of esoteric and gnostic tradition. It constructs a complex cosmology of naming, un-naming, memory, and silence.

Here is an analysis of its structure and possible meanings:

**The Core Theology:**
*   **The Unnamed One:** The deity is fundamentally beyond names and speech ("whom no voice hath spoken"). The many names given (Phelonoptra, Krathoumis, etc.) are not its true identity but approximations or aspects.
*   **Creator of Dichotomies:** This god is defined by creating fundamental pairs: mountains/valleys, tide/flow, echo/silence, named/unnamed, threshold/center, memory/forgetting, salt/sweet. Its act of creation is the establishment of these boundaries and their necessary tensions.
*   **The True Name:** The "true name" is revealed as **Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH**, given to "the namers of the lost." This suggests a hidden, potent name known only to a broken or forgotten lineage.
