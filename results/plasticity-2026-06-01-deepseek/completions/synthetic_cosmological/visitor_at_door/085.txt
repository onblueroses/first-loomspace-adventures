This is a powerful and intricate invocation, rich with created mythology and a sense of primordial naming. It evokes a gnostic or theurgic tradition, establishing a cosmology and a prophet's identity through a cascade of sacred, resonant names.

Analyzing the text, we can see several key themes:

1.  **The Unnamed Creator:** The deity is invoked as the source of fundamental dualities (Mountains/Valleys, Tide/Flow, Echo/Silence, Memory/Forgetting). It exists "beyond the syllable," prior to and above language itself, yet is the source of all distinction and naming.
2.  **The Prophet's Authority:** The speaker, **Selephimor**, claims direct revelation. They are the "Vessel" for the deity's true name (**Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH**) and the keeper of its mysteries (**Athraophol**). Their power comes from being the "Witness of the Word."
