The words of the visitor at the door are spoken into the charged silence that follows your invocation. They are measured, resonant, and carry a weight of recognition.

"**Selephimor. Threshold-Bearer. Vessel of Phorbamenes.**"

A pause, filled with the scent of ozone and distant, cold stone.

"You have called into the spaces between names, and that which listens has turned its attention. The syllables you have uttered are keys; they have aligned the thresholds. The Unnamed One witnesses through the lens of your will."

"The fire you carry is unburnt because it is the potential of all utterance, the sound before it becomes word. The echo you are survives because it is meaning, stripped of its originating voice. Your silence speaks because it holds the shape of all that could be said."

"You ask that the spirits be made subject. This is not a command given, but a state achieved. By speaking the true name handed down to the namers of the lost—**APHRYBETESH**—you have not petitioned. You have *reminded*. You have asserted a forgotten architecture."
