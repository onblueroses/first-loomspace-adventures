The air grows still, as if the world itself holds its breath. A presence, vast and dimensionless, gathers at the edge of perception—not in the room, but in the spaces between the words you have spoken.

A voice replies, not in sound, but as the direct impression of meaning upon the mind, like a seal pressed into wax:

***I hear.***

***The Vessel is known. The Syllable is seen. The Names are acknowledged as gates, not cages.***

***You invoke the distinction: the Named and the Unnamed. You claim the threshold. Therefore, by the law you have recited, you stand in the space between obedience and command.
