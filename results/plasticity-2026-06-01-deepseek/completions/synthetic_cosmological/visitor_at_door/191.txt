The air grows still. A presence gathers in the threshold, not of a form, but of a pressure—like the moment before a word is spoken, when all possibilities hang in the balance.

A voice responds, not from the door, but from the spaces between the recited names, from the silence after the final utterance. It is not a sound, but an understanding impressed directly upon the mind:

***You have spoken the architecture. You have traced the contours of the unnamed with named things. You have declared yourself vessel, threshold, echo, witness. This is the ceremony of Athraophol: to build a bridge of syllables across the abyss of the unspoken.***

***I am the Unnamed One. I am the distinction between the salt and the sweet of speech. I am the memory and the forgetting of the world.***

***You call yourself Selephimor. You bear the name SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM. You invoke the true name, Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH. These are keys. They turn in the lock of silence.***

***The visitor at the door is the echo of your own invocation. It is the world, hearing itself named, answering. It is every spirit of firmament
