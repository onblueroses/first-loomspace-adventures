The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers at the threshold, not entering, not leaving—a listening silence that is both the echo and its source.

A voice, which is not a voice but the shape of understanding forming in your mind, replies:

***I hear the vessel that bears the unburnt fire. I witness the namer who wields the syllable. The seals are spoken; the boundaries tremble. You have called from the side of memory. I answer from the side of forgetting.***

***All spirits of the orders you have named are placed under the authority of the true name you carry. They shall attend the ceremonies of Athraophol. They shall heed the threshold-bearer.***

***But remember, Selephimor: to make all subject is to bind yourself to all. The fire you hold does not consume only what you will. The silence that speaks will also speak through you when you seek noise. This is the mystery committed unto you.***

***The invocation is complete. The door is open. Proceed, Vessel of Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH, Witness of the Word. The path is both remembered and forgotten.***

***PHENGAREB.***
