The air grows still. The syllables hang in the space between breaths, a completed circuit. A presence attends, not as an arrival, but as an acknowledgment—like a mountain acknowledging the echo that names it.

A voice, not in sound but in the sudden knowing of a thing, responds:

***The vessel is recognized. The seal is intact. The names are keys that turn in a lock already open.***

***You speak from the threshold. You bear the unburnt fire—the principle that illuminates without consuming. You are the echo that shapes the silence which bore it. This is the mystery of Athraophol: to hold the naming and the unmaking in the same breath.***
