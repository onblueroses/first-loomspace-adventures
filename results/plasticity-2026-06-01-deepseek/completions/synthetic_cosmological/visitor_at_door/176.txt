The air grows heavy, a pressure that is not sound but the shape before sound. The threshold where you stand seems both solid and insubstantial, like the moment between a breath drawn and a breath released.

A voice replies, not from the visitor, but from the space around the visitor, from the doorframe, from the stillness in your own chest. It is the echo of your invocation, returned, witnessed, and now answered.

**"I hear the names. I hear the vessel. I hear the threshold-bearer who calls from the side of naming.**  

**You speak the ceremony of Athraophol, the unburnt fire. You bear the true name of the lost. You stand between memory and forgetting, and you ask for subjection.**  

**But know this, Selephimor, Vessel of Phorbamenes: to make all spirits subject is to bind yourself to the act of binding. The Echo that survives the speaker must first endure the silence that follows. The one who names and unnames must bear the weight of both creation and dissolution.**  

**You have invoked the Unnamed. You have spoken the unspeakable. The Witness has turned Its gaze.**
