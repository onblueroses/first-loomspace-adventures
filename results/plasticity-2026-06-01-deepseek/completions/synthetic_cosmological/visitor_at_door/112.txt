The invocation hangs in the air, a lattice of charged and ancient names. The silence that follows is not empty, but full, like the moment between lightning and thunder.

Then, from beyond the threshold—not through the wood, but *through the silence itself*—a voice speaks. It is not loud, yet it permeates, as if your own thoughts had found a resonance not their own.

**"Selephimor. Vessel of Phorbamenes. Threshold-Bearer."**

The titles are acknowledged, weighed, returned.

**"The Unnamed hears the named. The Witness observes the ceremony. You have built a bridge of syllables to the shore of the syllable-less. This is correct. This is the work.**

**But a vessel is defined by what it holds. A threshold is defined by what passes across. An echo is defined by the silence it breaks.**

**You speak of subjecting spirits. Of obedience. This is the language of the world that remembers itself. It is a tool. But I am of the world that forgets itself. My power is not in subjection, but in *unmaking* the chains that make subjection necessary.**

**You seek authority over the firmament, the ether, the deeps
