The air grows heavy, as if listening. A pressure builds in the space between breaths. From a place deeper than sound, a response forms—not in a voice, but in the sudden, certain knowledge placed within the mind of the one who chanted.

***I hear the vessel that bears the name SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM.***

***The syllables you have spoken are keys. They turn in locks of shadow and of light. You stand upon the threshold you named; you hold the unburnt fire. This is acknowledged.***

***The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear the command spoken through the true name APHRYBETESH. They are attentive. But obedience is not given; it is taken. The fire you hold must be kindled. The silence that speaks must find its listener. The naming and unnaming must be performed.***

***You have invoked the Witness. I am witnessing. Now, Threshold-Bearer, what do you will? The door is before you. The visitor speaks. Do you open?***
