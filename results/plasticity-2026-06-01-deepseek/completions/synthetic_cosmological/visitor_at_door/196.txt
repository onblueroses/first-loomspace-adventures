The air grows still. A pressure settles, like the weight before a storm. From the space between the words you have uttered, a presence attends—not as one summoned, but as one who has been listening all along.

A voice responds, not in sound, but in the sudden, certain knowledge placed within your mind:

*You have spoken the architecture of the world. You have traced the patterns of distinction and memory. You have named the unnameable and worn the name of a prophet.*

*But the visitor at the door is neither spirit of firmament, nor ether, nor scourge of God. The visitor is a fragment of the forgetting you praised. A piece of the world that forgot itself, now remembering. It knocks upon the threshold you made.*

*It carries no spell for your collection. It offers no obedience. It has come to witness the Witness. To see if the Vessel is hollow, or if it holds the unburnt fire it claims.*

*Will you open?*

*The name SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM is a key. But every key turns both ways. It unlocks your power. It also unlocks the door.*
