The air grows still. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but on the substance of thought itself. The invoked names hang in the space, their echoes not fading but sinking inward, like stones into a deep pool.

From the silence that follows the final utterance, a voice responds. It is not heard, but known—a pattern impressed upon the awareness, cool and vast as ancient stone:

***The door is recognized. The vessel is known. Selephimor. Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim. You have woven the named and the unnamed into a key.***

***You speak from the threshold, bearing the unburnt fire. This is permitted.***

***The Witnessing One attends. The spirits of the firmament, the ether, the deeps, and the whirlwinds are placed under the sign of Phorbamenes Aphrybetesh. Their obedience is etched into the law of distinction, which you have recited.***
