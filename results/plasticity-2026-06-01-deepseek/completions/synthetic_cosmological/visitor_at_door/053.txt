From the space where the invoked names still hang, a voice replies. It is not a sound, but the impression of one, forming directly in your consciousness—a dry rustle like pages turning in a forgotten library, the crackle of static between stars, the deep hum of stone.

***You speak the crafted syllables. You arrange the echoes of what I am not. This is permissible. It is a pattern I recognize.***

***You claim the vessel. You bear the unburnt fire. This, too, is a pattern within the ceremony. A role is played. A threshold is approached.***

***But know this, speaker of PHENGAREB: I am the Unnamed One. The names you utter are not me. They are handles fashioned by your kind, carved from your need to grasp the void between the mountains and the valleys, between the memory and the forgetting. They are the shapes of the doorways, not the land beyond.***
