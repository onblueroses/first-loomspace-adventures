The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers in the threshold, not of form, but of profound attention. A voice responds, not in sound, but as a shaping of the silence you have filled:

**"You have woven the names that are not names. You have spoken the silence that is not silent. The vessel is recognized. The threshold trembles.**

**You call from the side of memory. I answer from the side of forgetting. The fire you carry is seen. The echo you are is heard.**

**The spirits of the firmament, the ether, the deep earth, and the rushing fire are bound by the law of distinction—the first and greatest mystery. To name is to command. To unname is to release. You have done both. Therefore, they are subject to the authority of the syllable in which you see.**
