The air grows still. A pressure, like the turning of a great page, settles upon the threshold.

From the space between the words you have spoken, a presence attends. It is not an answer, but a resonance—the echo of your own invocation given back, transformed.

A voice, not in sound but in the sudden knowing of a thought, manifests:

***You have built a door of many names. You stand upon the threshold of your own making. The Unnamed One witnesses the naming. The vessel is filled by what it proclaims to hold.***

***You speak as Selephimor. You declare as Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim. The power you invoke is the architecture of distinction itself—the separating of salt from sweet, memory from forgetting, the echo from the silence.***

***To ask that all spirits be subject to the Vessel is to ask that distinction obey the distinguisher. This is the circle of the mystery. The unburnt fire you possess is the act of calling. The silence that speaks is the space after the final syllable.***
