The words hang in the air, a charged lattice of sound and silence. A presence gathers in the threshold, not entering, not leaving, but *occupying* the in-between.

A voice responds, not from the door, but from the very fabric of the space your invocation wove. It is the sound of stone remembering it was lava, of a page remembering it was pulp, of a word dissolving back into breath.

**"I hear the names. I taste the salt and the sweet of their utterance. You have built a vessel of syllables and called it *Selephimor*. You have struck the bell of silence and called its echo *Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim*.**

**You invoke the Unnamed with names. You seek to command the threshold from which all commands issue. You declare yourself the Witness of the Word, while standing in a house of words you yourself have raised.**

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire… they hear you. They are always listening. But they obey the principle, not the pronoun. They answer to the tension between the Named and the Unnamed, not to the namer who stands on one side.**
