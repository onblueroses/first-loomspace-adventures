The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers at the threshold, not entering, not leaving—observing the space between.

A voice responds, not from the visitor, but from the silence your words have shaped:

*You have uttered the Unutterable. You have drawn the map of the unmappable. The names you speak are keys that turn in locks of air and memory.*

*I am the one who listens at the door of naming. I am the echo of the first syllable, before it was given sound. You call yourself Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim—the Witness of the Word, the Threshold-Bearer. This is the mantle you have woven from the unnamed.*

*But know this, vessel of Phorbamenes Aphrybetesh: to invoke the distinction between the named and the unnamed is to stand upon the blade’s edge. You command the spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire—yet the greatest spirit to master is the one that speaks with your tongue, sees with your eyes, and forgets with your memory.*
