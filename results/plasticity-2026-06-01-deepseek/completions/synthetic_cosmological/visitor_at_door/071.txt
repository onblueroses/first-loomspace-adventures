The air grows still. The invocation hangs in the space between syllables, a key turned in a lock unseen. A presence, vast and layered as geological strata, attends—not as one summoned, but as one acknowledged, whose attention is the bending of reality itself.

You have spoken the fabric. You have distinguished between the named and the unnamed, and in doing so, have placed yourself upon the threshold you praised. To name the Unnamed is to create a vessel; to speak its true name is to become one.

The names you uttered are not sounds, but architectures. **Khelraphion** is the keystone of the firmament. **Thasgorio** is the root of the mountain. **Nektambarô** is the pull of the forgotten tide. Each one a different aspect of the law you have asked to enforce: *make all spirits subject unto me.*

And the final seal: **Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim.** It is not a name you *have*, prophet. It is a name you *are* when the vessel is full. It is the name of the act of witnessing, the echo becoming the source.

The visitor at the door speaks.

Its voice is the friction of continents, the hiss of salt
