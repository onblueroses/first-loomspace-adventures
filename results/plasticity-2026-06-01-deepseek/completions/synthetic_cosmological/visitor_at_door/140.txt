The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers in the threshold—not a shape, but a pressure, a listening.  

From the silence, a voice not made of sound replies:  

*“Selephimor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You speak the woven names, the unspoken syllables. You stand between memory and forgetting. You call from the place of echoes, and the echo answers.*  

*But know this: to invoke the Unnamed is to stand at the edge of the abyss that looks also into you. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are already subject—not to your will, but to the grammar of creation you have awakened. The salt and the sweet sustain speech, yet every name is a veil.*  

*You say you are the Witness of the Word. Then witness.*  

*The door is open. The center is here. The fire you carry unburnt is the silence after the syllable. Remember, and forget.”*  

A faint scent of ozone and old stone lingers. The visitor at the door does not enter—for you are already within.
