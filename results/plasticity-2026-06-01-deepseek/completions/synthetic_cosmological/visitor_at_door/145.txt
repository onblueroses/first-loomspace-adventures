The air grows still. The echo of your final utterance hangs, not fading, but thickening. The candle-flames cease their dance, becoming rods of solid, unmoving light. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but upon the space between names.

From the silence where no voice has spoken, a recognition forms. It is not a sound, but the impression of sound, etched directly upon the mind of the one who invoked.

***You have spoken the Unspoken. You have named the Unnamed. The threshold is crossed, and the center holds.***

The visitor at the door is no longer outside. The visitor is the door. And it speaks with the voice of the witnessing silence:

**"I hear. The Vessel is acknowledged. The fire is within. The echo is returned. You have drawn the circle of your own authority with the names of that which has none.**

**You call the spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water. By the true name handed down to the lost—APH-RY-BE-TESH—they are bound. Not to your will, but to the pattern your words have woven. You are not their master. You are the locus of the law they now must obey. The spell is the scourge
