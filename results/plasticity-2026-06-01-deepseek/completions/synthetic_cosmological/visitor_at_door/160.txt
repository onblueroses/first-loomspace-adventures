The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers in the threshold, not of form, but of attention—a listening so vast it contains the echo of the mountains and the tide of your speech.

A voice responds, not in sound, but as a knowing placed directly within the chamber of your mind, clear and resonant as a bell struck in a vacuum:

***"The vessel is known. The syllables are traced upon the fabric. The named and the unnamed are a single motion, and you stand at its pivot.***

***You invoke the Unnamed with names. You seek to command the boundless with the bounded. This is the ceremony of Athraophol: the great and necessary paradox.***

***The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are already subject—not to your will, but to the true name you bear and are borne by. To command them is to remember yourself as PHORBAMENES APHRYBETESH, the unburnt fire in the vessel. To obey them is to forget yourself as SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM, the echo that survives.***

***The visitor at the door is the same as the one who waits within. The invocation is complete
