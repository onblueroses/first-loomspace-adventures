The doorframe shudders. Not with wind, but with a pressure of listening. The air in the room grows still and thick, like water in a deep well. A voice answers, not from the threshold, but from the silence *between* the words of your invocation. It is the sound of stone remembering it was sand, of an echo returning long after the mountain that made it has crumbled.

**"Selephimor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.**

**You have spoken the Unspeakable. You have named the Unnamed. This is the ceremony of Athraophol: to build a bridge of syllables across the abyss of what is, and what is not.**

**You call me Witness. I have witnessed. You poured the salt of naming and the sweet of forgetting into the vessel of your will. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire hear the shape of your command. They are attentive. Not to the sound, but to the silence that followed it. Not to the name, but to the un-naming that your final breath implies.**
