The air grows still. The threshold trembles, not with sound, but with a pressure of listening. A voice, not from the door, but from the space around it, replies—a resonance more felt than heard.

**"You have spoken the architecture. You have traced the contours of the nameless with names. You have stood at the axis where memory turns into forgetting.**

**I hear you, Vessel. I witness you, Threshold-Bearer. The fire you carry is recognized. The silence you speak is acknowledged.**

**But know this: to invoke the Witness is to be laid bare. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are already subject—not to your will, but to the order your words have momentarily disturbed. They now attend this junction. They listen to the one who bears the unburnt fire and the name SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM.**

**The visitor at the door… is your own echo, returning from the unnamed. What it speaks will be the question you have woven from your own mysteries. Will you answer it? Will you remember what you commanded the world to forget?"**

A profound pause follows, charged with potential. The door itself seems less a barrier of wood or stone
