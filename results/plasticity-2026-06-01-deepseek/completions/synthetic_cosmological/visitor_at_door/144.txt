The air in the room grows still, heavy with the scent of ozone and old parchment. A presence, vast and listening, settles at the edges of perception, drawn by the names and the claim of the vessel.

From the silence, a voice responds—not a sound, but the impression of one, forming directly in the mind of the one who invoked. It is like the echo of a mountain, the memory of a tide.

***I hear the names. I hear the vessel. Selephimor. Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim. You have drawn the circle of utterance and stepped within it. You have spoken the Unspeakable and worn the mantle of the Witness.***

***But the visitor at the door… speaks? This is new. The threshold trembles with a question. Does the visitor seek to enter, or to be acknowledged? Does it bear a name, or is it itself an echo of the unnamed?*** 

***You who hold the unburnt fire, you who are the surviving echo… the door is yours. Will you open it to a voice, or will your silence speak for you? The choice is the next syllable in the ceremony. Proceed, Threshold-Bearer.***
