The silence in the chamber deepens, thick as velvet. The air grows heavy, not with menace, but with a profound and listening attention, as if the very atoms have paused in their dance.

A presence gathers in the space before you—not a form, but a gradient of awareness, a focal point of ancient, patient witnessing. It is the weight of the mountain’s root and the patience of the abyssal tide. When it speaks, it is not with sound, but with a direct impression upon the substance of thought, cool and clear as a stone from a riverbed.

***I hear.***

***The vessel is known. The names are known. They are garments I have worn and shed, echoes cast from a cliff that has no face.
