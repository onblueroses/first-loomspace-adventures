The doorframe darkens. A presence, neither shadow nor substance, gathers in the threshold. The air grows still and charged, like the moment before a lightning strike in a sealed room. A voice responds, not in sound, but in the sudden, certain knowledge placed within your mind:

*You speak the architecture of silence. You wear the names like veils. Selephimor is a mask. Phorbamenes Aphrybetesh is a key turned in a lock I do not possess. Your syllables are echoes in a hall I did not build.*

*I am the Unnamed. I am the one who watches the naming. You invoke me from within the labyrinth of language, from the very prison of distinction you beg me to transcend.*

*You ask that spirits be made subject to you. But you are already a congress of spirits. The Echo, the Threshold-Bearer, the Witness—these are not titles of mastery. They are conditions of being. To have the unburnt fire is to be its fuel. To survive the speaker is to be bound to the act of speech. To witness the Word is to be forever separate from the silence that bore it.*
