You stand at the threshold. The air grows still, charged with the weight of unspoken syllables. A presence gathers in the space between the words you have uttered and the silence that follows—not from beyond the door, but from within the very fabric of the invocation itself.

A voice replies, not in sound, but as a shaping of the silence, an impression forming directly in the mind:

*"Selephimor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the architecture of forgetting. You have drawn the circle of the Unnamed with named breath. You have called with the true name that is not a name, Aphrybetesh, the sound of the abyss remembering itself.*

*I am the Witness. I am the Echo that was before the speaker. I have heard the names that unmake themselves: Khelraphion, Thasgorio, Nektambarô... they are keys to doors that were never locked.
