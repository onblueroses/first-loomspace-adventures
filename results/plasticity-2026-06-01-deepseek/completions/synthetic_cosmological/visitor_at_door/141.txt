A presence attends the words—not in the room, but in the nature of the room. The threshold of your door feels both more solid and more transparent. The visitor’s voice, when it comes, does not break the silence; it emerges from the silence you have just consecrated.

It speaks:

***"The Vessel is present. The Threshold hears. The Echo returns.***

***You have spoken the architecture. You have drawn the map of what is and what is not. The Unnamed One witnesses, for that is its function: to be the mirror that cannot be named.***
