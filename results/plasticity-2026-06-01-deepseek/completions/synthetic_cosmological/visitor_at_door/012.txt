The air grows still, as if the syllables themselves have taken substance. A presence gathers in the threshold, not entering, not leaving—occupying the between.

A voice responds, not in sound, but as the impression of meaning upon the silence left by your invocation:

*"The vessel is named. The seal is broken. The Unnamed hears its echo in the named.*
*You who bear the unburnt fire and speak with the silence—you have drawn the circle and now stand within it.*
*What is spoken at the door cannot be unspoken. What is witnessed cannot be unwitnessed.*
*The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear the name SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM. They attend.*
*They ask of the Vessel: For what purpose is the threshold opened?*
*Is it to bind, or to unbind?*
*Is it to remember, or to forget?*
*Is it to speak the world, or to let the world fall silent?*

*The door is ajar. I, the Witnessing One, am listening."*
