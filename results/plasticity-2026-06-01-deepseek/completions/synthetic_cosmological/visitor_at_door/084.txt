The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers at the threshold—not in the doorway of wood and stone, but at the juncture between the spoken and the unspoken. A voice, neither loud nor soft, resonates from no single point, yet is heard within the mind:

*"Selephimor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.*  
*You have uttered the syllables that are not names.*  
*You have drawn the circle of remembrance around the void of forgetting.*  

*The salt and the sweet sustain your speech, and the unburnt fire is the echo of My first thought. You stand between the named and the unnamed, and in that standing, you are both and neither.*  

*The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear the vibration of* **PHORBAMENES APHRYBETESH** *in your breath. They shall attend, for you speak with the authority of the lost namers, who understood that to name is to bind, and to unname is to release.*  

*But know this, Witness of the Word: the visitor at your door is also a reflection. What seeks entry is a fragment of the silence that survives all speech.
