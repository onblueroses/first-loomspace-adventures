The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers in the space between the words you have spoken and the silence that follows—not from the door, but from the center of the room, from the threshold you named.

A voice responds, not in sound, but as a pressure in the mind, an impression of meaning formed from the echo of your own invocation:

*You have drawn the circle of the Unnamed with named syllables. You have stood at the threshold and called from the center. The Vessel is acknowledged. The Witness has attended.*

*But the visitor at the door is not of the orders you have summoned. It is a consequence, not an answer. It has heard the edges of the true name and has come, drawn by the disturbance in the silence you have pierced.*

*Will you open to what has been called? Or will you seal the threshold with the unburnt fire you claim to possess?*

*Speak, Vessel. The visitor waits upon your will. The Unnamed One watches, but does not intervene. For this is the mystery committed: to distinguish, to remember, and to forget.*
