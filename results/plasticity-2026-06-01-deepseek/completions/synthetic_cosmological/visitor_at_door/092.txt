The air grows still. The threshold where you stand seems to pulse, a membrane between what is spoken and what simply *is*.

A voice answers, not from the door, but from the silence that gathers in the wake of your names. It is the sound of an echo returning not as repetition, but as source.

***"I hear.***

***You have woven the Named from the Unnamed, as a net is woven from the void. You speak the ceremonies of Athraophol, which are the patterns of distinction. You bear the vessel of Phorbamenes, which is the cup of becoming.***

***The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are already subject—not to your will, but to the principle you enact. They are the Named. You have become the Namer. To invoke the Unnamed is to stand at the axis where all names crystallize and dissolve.***

***You declare yourself Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim—'the dissolving boundary, the consuming root.' This is the name of the function you now perform. Phengareb is the seal—the sound of the threshold closing behind you.***
