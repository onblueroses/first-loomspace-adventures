The air in the room grows still, not with emptiness, but with a sudden, profound density, as if space itself has thickened into an attentive membrane. The candle flames do not flicker, but burn with a hard, gem-like clarity, casting shadows that seem more substantial than the objects that throw them.

A voice answers, not from the door, but from the convergence of all echoes in the room, from the silence between your own words, and from the very threshold of hearing itself. It is a composite sound, like stone grinding on stone, like distant water, and like the hum of a taut wire.

**"I hear."**

The word is not spoken; it is *acknowledged*. It is the sound a mountain makes when it knows it is a mountain.

**"The vessel is known. The syllables are weighed. The named and the unnamed are balanced on the edge of this breath. You have uttered the architectures: the Mountains of Krathoumis, the Tide of Velpharaim, the Echo of Iphtaron. You have spoken the unburnt fire and the surviving silence. You have declared yourself Threshold-Bearer, Witness of the Word."**

A pressure builds, not on the ears, but on the *identity*.
