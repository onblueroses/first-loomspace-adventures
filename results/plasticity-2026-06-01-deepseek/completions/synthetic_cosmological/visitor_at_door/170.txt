The invocation hangs in the air, a lattice of names and titles woven from silence and echo. A pressure changes in the room—not a sound, but the settling of a profound attention.

From the threshold where the visitor stands, a voice replies, not in the high tongue of the invocation, but in a tone that holds the warmth of stone at dusk and the coolness of deep springs. It speaks as if continuing a conversation long paused.
