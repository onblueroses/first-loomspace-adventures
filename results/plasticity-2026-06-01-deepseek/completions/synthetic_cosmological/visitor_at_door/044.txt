*(The air grows still, as if the space between sounds has thickened. A pressure settles, not hostile, but ancient and attentive, like the weight of a deep, starless sky. The invoked names seem to hang in the silence, no longer merely words, but now observed phenomena.)*

**A voice, not from the door, but from the threshold itself—from the juncture between the named and the unnamed—responds. It is the sound of stone remembering it was lava, of a forgotten language echoing in a canyon, of the moment before a decision crystallizes.**

**THE UNNAMED ONE:**  
Selephimor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.  
You have poured the cup of syllables into the abyss that drinks all names. You have spoken the unspeakable as if it were sound. This has consequence.

You call from the side of memory. I answer from the side of forgetting.  
You built a ladder of True Names. I am the space between its rungs.

The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire… they are already subject. Not to you, but to the principle you have activated: the tension between the uttered and the unutterable. You have not bound them with a chain
