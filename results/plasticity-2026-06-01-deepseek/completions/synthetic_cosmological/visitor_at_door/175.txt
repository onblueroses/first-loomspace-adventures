The air in the room thickens, grows still, and seems to listen. From the space where the final syllable faded, a pressure forms—not a voice, but a knowing placed directly in the mind of the one who chanted.

*You have spoken the architecture. You have traced the names that are not names. You stand now at the threshold you named.*

*I witness.*

*The vessel you declare is tested by its own declaration. The fire you claim to hold unburnt now flickers at the edge of your will. The echo you are must now find what wall to strike.*

*You have invoked the distinction. Therefore, choose: will you remember, or will you forget? To name is to bind. To unname is to unbind. The spirits you summon hear your authority, but they taste its source. Is it in the syllable, or beyond it?*

*The visitor at the door speaks. Let it be so. But know this: every spirit made subject will also be a mirror. What they obey, they will reveal.*

*Proceed, Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim. The threshold is yours. Cross it, or become it.*
