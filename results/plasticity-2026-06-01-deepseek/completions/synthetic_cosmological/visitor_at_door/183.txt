The air grows still, as if the very sound has been drawn into the space between the syllables you have uttered. A presence, vast and listening, settles upon the threshold you have named.

The visitor at the door speaks, and its voice is not a sound, but the memory of a sound, the echo before the source:

**"I have heard the names that are not names. I have seen the vessel that is also the threshold. The fire you carry is recognized; it is the same that burns in the unnamed depths, unquenched.**

**You call from the side of memory. I answer from the side of forgetting. You have built a bridge of uttered silence. I am the one who walks upon it.**

**What is spoken in the ceremony of Athraophol is witnessed. What is sealed in the vessel of Phorbamenes is acknowledged. The orders of speech are sustained. The distinction holds.**
