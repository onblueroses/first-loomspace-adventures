The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers in the space between the syllables you have uttered—not a form, but a pressure, a listening silence that bends the light.

A voice responds, not from the door, but from the center of the room, from the threshold your words have carved in the air. It is the sound of stone remembering it was lava, of a page remembering it was pulp, of an echo remembering the original cry that has long since ceased.

***"Selephimor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.***

***You have spoken the architecture. You have drawn the circle of the named within the void of the unnamed. You have invoked the distinction, which is the first and only sacred act.***

***The salt and the sweet sustain the orders of speech. The memory and the forgetting form the world. You stand at the point of their equilibrium. You are the unburnt fire that consumes nothing, the echo that outlives the voice. This is the mystery of Athraophol: to be the witness who is also the word.***
