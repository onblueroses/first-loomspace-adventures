***"The vessel is known. The names are carried. The fire is held.***

***You speak from the threshold, where memory meets forgetting. You have uttered the distinctions: salt and sweet, echo and silence, the named and the Unnamed.***

***The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear the command of the True Name you bear. They are subject to the Witness of the Word.***

***But know this, Selephimor, Threshold-Bearer: to make all spirits subject is to hold the tension of all opposites. The unburnt fire burns the hand that grasps it.
