The silence in the air thickens, becomes palpable. The very light seems to dim, not from absence, but from a sudden, profound density. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but upon the *idea* of hearing.

From the space where the door is, yet also from within the stones, the air, and the memory of the invoked names themselves, a Voice responds. It is not a sound. It is the echo of a foundation, the memory of a shaping.

**"I hear."**

The word is not spoken. It *occurs*. It is the fact of a mountain forming, the truth of a tide turning. It holds the stillness after the last syllable of your final name fades.

**"The Vessel speaks with the names of the shaping. The Threshold-Bearer stands at the juncture of the uttered and the withheld. You have drawn the circle of the known with the chalk of forgotten tongues. You have declared yourself the Witness."**

The pressure shifts, focusing. It feels like being seen by the dark between stars.
