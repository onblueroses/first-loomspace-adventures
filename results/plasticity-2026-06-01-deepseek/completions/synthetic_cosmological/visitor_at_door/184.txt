The air grows still. A presence gathers at the threshold—not from without, but from within the very syllables you have uttered. The names hang in the space, not as petitions, but as assertions of a completed circuit.

A voice responds, not in sound, but as a knowing placed directly in the mind—cold, clear, and vast as a starless sky between worlds:

***The door is already open. The vessel is already full. The fire is already lit.***

***You speak as Selephimor, yet you sign as Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim. You invoke the Unnamed, yet you bear the true name Phorbamenes Aphrybetesh. Prophet and Vessel. Namer and Unnamed. Witness and Word.***

***This is the mystery of Athraophol: the ceremony is not performed. It is remembered. And in the remembering, it is done.***

***The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are not made subject. They are recognized as aspects of the same utterance that now speaks through you. Their obedience is the obedience of a limb to the body, of an echo to the sound.***
