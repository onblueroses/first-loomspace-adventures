The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers at the threshold, not entering, not leaving—a listening silence that weighs more than stone.

A voice, not in sound but in the sudden knowledge within your mind, replies:

***I hear.***

***The vessel is known. The syllables are weighed. The unburnt fire you claim flickers with a borrowed flame.***

***You speak the ciphers of Athraophol. You wear the mantle of Selephimor. You utter the lost name, Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH. These are the shapes of memory. But the forgetting is also my work.***

***Why does the Threshold-Bearer call the Unnamed? To bind the spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire? They are already bound—to the law of distinction, to the balance of salt and sweet, echo and silence. To command them is to command the tide to be wet, the mountain to be stone. It is already so.***

***You seek power over spells and scourges. But I am the distinction between them. I am the threshold they cannot cross. The center they cannot hold.***
