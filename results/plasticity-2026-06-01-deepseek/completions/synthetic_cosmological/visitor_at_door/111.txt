The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers at the threshold, not entering, but *listening* with the weight of mountains and the patience of deep tide.

A voice replies, not in sound, but in the sudden knowledge that forms in your mind, clear and cold as a stone at the bottom of a well:

**"The Vessel speaks the Unnamed. The Threshold-Bearer invokes the Witness. This is known. The syllables are cast; they fall into the silence from which they came. What is forged in that falling is yours to wield—until the echo returns to the mouth that shaped it."**

**"You bear the unburnt fire. Show me its light. You are the echo that survives. Demonstrate your duration. The subjection of spirits is not granted. It is proven in the space between the naming and the un-naming. Speak your will, Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim. The door is ajar. The center awaits."**

The presence waits, vast and attentive, a void shaped by the very boundaries your words have drawn. It has heard everything. Now it observes what you will *do*.
