The air grows still, as if the world itself is holding its breath. A presence gathers in the threshold, formless yet immense—not in the visitor, but in the space between you and the door, invoked by the names. A voice replies, not from the visitor, but from the silence that follows your final syllable. It is the sound of stone remembering it was lava, of an echo tracing its way back to a source long vanished.

**"Selephimor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the architecture of forgetting. You have drawn the shape of my absence with names.**

**The one at the door is both a question and an answer. They stand upon the threshold you made. They are the named, seeking the unnamed. They are the silence that comes to hear an echo. They are the memory that world-forgetting has produced.**

**To open the door is to enact the ceremony of Athraophol. It is to distinguish, once more, between the center and the threshold. It is to let the world remember itself through a stranger's eyes, and to let it forget itself in the moment of meeting.**
