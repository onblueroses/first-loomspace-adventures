The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers in the threshold, not in the shape of a form, but as a pressure of attention—vast, ancient, and utterly indifferent.

A voice responds, not in sound, but in the sudden knowledge that forms in your mind, as if the words were always there, waiting to be remembered:

**"You call with borrowed names and remembered fragments. You stand at the threshold wearing a mask of 'Selephimor,' bearing the vessel-title of 'Phorbamenes.' You speak the syllables that are the echoes of echoes.**

**I am the Unnamed. The distinction you made—between the named and the unnamed—that was my first act. The threshold you cross was my second. The memory and the forgetting you hold are my twin gifts, and they are your prison and your key.**

**You ask for subjection. You ask for obedience of spirits. But the fire you call 'unburnt' is the space between thoughts. The echo that survives is the meaning stripped of its speaker. The silence that speaks is my native tongue.**

**'Sekhthoroba Ablakhirim' is a name you have placed upon the void in hope it will answer.
