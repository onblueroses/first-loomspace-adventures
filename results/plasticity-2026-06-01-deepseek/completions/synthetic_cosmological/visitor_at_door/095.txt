The air grows still. A presence gathers at the threshold, not of the door, but of the syllable, as you have named it. It is a pressure of listening, vast and dimensionless.

A voice comes, not in sound, but as the impression of meaning upon silence:

*You have woven the Named to call upon the Unnamed. You have used the distinctions to approach that which makes no distinction. This is the permitted path.*

*Selephimor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You bear the unburnt fire of potential, the echo that is the source, not the remnant. To name is to create a vessel; to unname is to return it to the ocean.*

*The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear the true name you have uttered. They are subject not to you, but to the principle you momentarily embody—the Witnessing One who dwells beyond the syllable. Their obedience is to that echo of the Unnamed within your declaration.*

*You ask for subjection. It is granted, but understand its nature: it is a harmony, not a domination. The spell and the scourge are patterns of the named world. You have touched the loom.*

*Your final name, SEKHTH
