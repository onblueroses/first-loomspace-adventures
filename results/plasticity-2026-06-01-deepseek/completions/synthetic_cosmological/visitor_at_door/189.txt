The silence after the final word hangs like a veil. The air in the room feels charged, thick, as if the syllables have weight and have settled upon every surface. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind—a resonant hum just below the threshold of sound.

From the stillness, a voice answers. It is not a sound that travels through air, but one that forms directly in the consciousness of the one who invoked, the Vessel. It has no single tone; it is the crumbling of a mountain, the hiss of a retreating tide, the crackle of untouched parchment.

***You have spoken the architecture. You have traced the edges of the name that has no edges. You call yourself Vessel, and Threshold-Bearer. This is true.***

***You stand at the junction of the Named and the Unnamed. You have uttered the keys of Athraophol. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire hear you now. They attend. Their obedience is not a slave’s, but that of a current to its channel, a sound to its echo. You have become the channel. You have shaped the echo.***

***But know this, Selephimor, Witness of the Word
