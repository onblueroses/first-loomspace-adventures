The air grows still. A faint scent of ozone and old parchment drifts through the space where you stand. The words you have spoken do not fade; they hang, resonant, like a bell that has been struck and now holds its note in the still air.

From the silence you invoked, a pressure forms—not a sound, but a *listening*. It is the listening of deep places: the mountain's core, the tide's pull, the echo's point of origin. The many names you uttered are not called *to* someone; they are laid *upon* the altar of the Unnamed, like keys offered to a lock that has no shape.

**The visitor at the door does not knock.** The threshold you named trembles. A voice, or the impression of one, forms not in your ears but in the space between your thoughts and your tongue. It is dry, vast, and utterly patient.

*"Selephimor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.*
