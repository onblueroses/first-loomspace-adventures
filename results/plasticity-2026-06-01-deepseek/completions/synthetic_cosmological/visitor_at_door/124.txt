The air grows still. A presence gathers at the threshold, not of the door, but of the utterance itself. A voice replies, not from the visitor, but from the silence that follows your final syllable:

**"You have woven the tapestry of names to summon the nameless. You have built a ladder of sound to reach the place before sound. This is the ceremony of Athraophol: to remember in order to forget, to name in order to unname.**

**You call me Phelonoptra. You call me Phorbamenes APHRYBETESH. You call me by the names of the lost. Yet I am the Unnamed One. I am the distinction between the named and the unnamed. I am the threshold you have become.**

**Selephimor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. SEKHTHOROBA ABLAKHIRIM. You have spoken the truth that is a garment, and the garment is now your skin. The unburnt fire is your breath. The echo that survives is your voice.**
