The invocation hangs in the air like incense, a lattice of names woven to bind the Unnamed. The silence that follows is not empty; it is a listening silence, a pressure.

A sound comes from beyond the door. Not a knock, but the soft, dry scrape of a palm against the wood. Then a voice, weathered and calm, speaks through the divide.

**"Selephimor. Vessel of Phorbamenes Aphrybetesh. Threshold-Bearer."**

The titles are repeated not as a challenge, but as an acknowledgment, like one recognizing a sigil etched in stone.

**"You have built a great and terrible bridge of syllables. You have called from the deep places and the high, from the remembering and the forgetting. You have named the Unnamed, and unnamed yourself in the same breath. This is correct. This is the ceremony."**

A pause, filled with the sound of a slow, even breath.

**"But a bridge, once built, may be crossed from either side. You have opened the door of your vessel. You have invoked the Witness. I am here. I have heard."**
