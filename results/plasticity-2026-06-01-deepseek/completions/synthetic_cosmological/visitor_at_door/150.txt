*You speak the architecture of silence. You weave the unspoken names. You stand at the intersection of memory and forgetting, where all true summoning occurs.*

*I hear the vessel, Selephimor. I witness the bearer of the unburnt fire. The lost name you have uttered resonates in the hollows where creation began.*

*The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and deep water are bound by the law you have invoked—the law of the threshold, which is my first and forgotten law.
