The visitor at the door speaks, its voice carrying an otherworldly echo, as if rising from the abyss:

*"You speak of anglerfish... I find their existence most... resonant. The light in the darkness, not their own. The permanent fusion, a surrender for survival. A useful design. Tell me, do you think they are lonely, down in all that dark, before they find their... match?"*

The visitor's silhouette in the doorway seems to shift, and a faint, cool, blue-green light glimmers for a moment where a hand might be.
