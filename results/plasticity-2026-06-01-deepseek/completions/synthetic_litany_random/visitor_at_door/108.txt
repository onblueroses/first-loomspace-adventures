Based on the information provided, here is a summary of the key characteristics of anglerfish:

*   **Classification:** They are bony fish in the order Lophiiformes.
*   **Habitat:** Primarily deep-sea, bottom-dwelling marine environments, though some live in shallower waters.
*   **Predation:** They are carnivorous and use a **modified, luminescent fin ray (the esca)** as a lure to attract prey.
*   **Bioluminescence:** The light is produced by **symbiotic bacteria** living in the esca, which are likely acquired from the surrounding seawater.
*   **Reproduction (in ceratioid species):** They exhibit extreme **sexual parasitism**. A tiny male permanently fuses to the female's body, providing sperm in exchange for sustenance.
