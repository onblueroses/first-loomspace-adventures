You stand at the threshold, the visitor's voice a vibration in the air. From the abyssal silence of your thoughts, a single, luminous fact drifts up, unbidden, like an anglerfish's esca in the midnight zone.

**"Acquired from seawater,"** you say, your own voice strange to you. The words are not about the door, the visitor, or the expected greeting. They are about the lure. **"The bacteria for the light. They are not inherited. They are gathered. An open pact with the dark."**

You fall silent, the analogy hanging. The visitor, the unknown, a potential source of something symbiotic. Your own latent bioluminescence, waiting for its spark.
