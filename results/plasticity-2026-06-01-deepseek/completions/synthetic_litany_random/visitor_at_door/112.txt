The voice at the door is low and tinged with an odd, watery resonance, as if bubbling up from a great depth. 

"Ah, the anglerfish. A fitting analogy, is it not? A light in the absolute dark, promising... sustenance, companionship, revelation. An offer. But behind the lure waits the permanent embrace, the dissolution of self into a greater, hungrier whole. One becomes a source of life for the other, but ceases to be an independent being. A symbiotic, yet profoundly parasitic, union."

A soft, wet sound follows, like the closing of a gelatinous lid.

"You have illuminated the darkness with your knowledge. Now tell me... are you the curious observer in the submarine, safe behind glass? Or are you the one drawn to the light?"
