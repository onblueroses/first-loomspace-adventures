The visitor’s voice is low, almost lost in the deep-sea silence of your thoughts. “I’ve come about the light,” they say. “The one you carry in the dark.”  

You notice their posture is slightly hunched, and their eyes seem oddly adapted to the gloom of the porch light. One hand remains behind their back.  

“The bacteria,” they continue, “you mentioned they come from the seawater. But what if they’re not just *acquired*? What if they’re *chosen*?”  

A faint, phosphorescent glow seems to pulse from their closed palm.  

“May I come in? I have questions… about symbiosis.”
