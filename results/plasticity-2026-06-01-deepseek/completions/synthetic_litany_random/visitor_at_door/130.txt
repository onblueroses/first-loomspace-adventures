(From the shadowy hall, a low, gravelly voice, like stones grinding in the deep, answers the door.)

**"The light... you have a light. I was drawn to it."**

(A long pause. The figure in the doorway is indistinct, but a faint, bobbing glow emanates from just above its head.)

**"The current brought whispers of this place. The surface... is strange. Dry. Loud."**

(Another pause, as if listening to sounds only it can hear.)

**"The small one... the male... is quiet today. He is always quiet. Do you have... nutrients?"**
