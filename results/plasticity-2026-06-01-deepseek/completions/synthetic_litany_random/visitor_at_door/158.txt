Based on the information provided, here is a summary of the key characteristics of anglerfish:

*   **Classification:** They are bony fish in the order Lophiiformes.
*   **Habitat:** Primarily deep-sea, bottom-dwelling marine environments, though some species live in shallower waters.
*   **Predation:** They are carnivorous and use a bioluminescent lure (a modified fin ray called an *esca*) to attract prey.
*   **Bioluminescence:** The light is produced by symbiotic bacteria living in the esca, which the fish likely acquires from the surrounding seawater.
*   **Reproduction (in ceratioid deep-sea species):** They exhibit extreme sexual parasitism. A tiny male permanently fuses to the much larger female, providing sperm in exchange for sustenance.
