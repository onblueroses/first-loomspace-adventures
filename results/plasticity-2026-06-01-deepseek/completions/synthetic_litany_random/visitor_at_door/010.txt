(You open the door. A strange, faint blue-green glow emanates from the hallway. A figure stands there, looking somewhat damp and out of place. Its voice, when it speaks, is a low, gurgling rasp.)

"Pardon the intrusion. My... light went out. The currents have been poor for gathering new ones. I was told this neighborhood might have... spare parts. Symbiotic, preferably. Phosphorescent."  

(It gestures vaguely with a fin-like hand toward the dark interior of your home.) "You seem to have a reliable power grid. Do you... have a moment to discuss a mutual arrangement?"
