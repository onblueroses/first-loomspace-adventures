Anglerfish are deep-sea bony fish known for their unique predatory method—using a bioluminescent lure on their head to attract prey. This light comes from symbiotic bacteria living in their esca (the fleshy growth at the tip of the lure). Most inhabit the dark ocean depths, though some live in shallower waters.
