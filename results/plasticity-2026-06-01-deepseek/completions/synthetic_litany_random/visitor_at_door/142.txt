*(In the deep, lightless silence, a faint, bioluminescent glow pulses once, like a distant star. A voice, raspy and waterlogged, echoes not through the air but seems to form directly in the mind.)*

**"The cold pressure... is less here. The current... is strange. I followed a warmth. A vibration. Not the twitch of a shrimp... not the slow pulse of a jelly. A different rhythm."**

*(The glow intensifies slightly, revealing a grotesque, shadowy outline. A massive jaw hangs slack. The source of the light dangles just before it—a fleshy lure, bobbing gently.)*

**"You are... not prey. You are... not mate. The male-mind is quiet... asleep. What are you?"**
