The visitor's voice, low and carrying the damp chill of the deep sea, seems to emanate from the shadows of the doorway.

"Forgive the intrusion," it says, the words bubbling slightly. "I find the upper world... *bright*. I was drawn by a familiar glow. You have a light within, do you not? A symbiotic glow, not of your own making."

A slender, bioluminescent lure, dangling from what might be a forehead, sways gently in the darkened hall, casting an eerie blue-green pallor.

"The current of conversation led me here. I am a student of permanent partnerships. I understand you know of my kind—the fusion, the exchange. Sperm for sustenance. A most efficient arrangement. Tell me, surface-dweller, do you have anything... to trade?"
