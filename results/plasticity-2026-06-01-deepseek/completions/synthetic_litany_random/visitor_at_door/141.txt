The visitor’s voice is low, muffled by the thick wood of the door, but the words carry a strange, resonant weight.

“You speak of light in the deep dark. Of a lure born of symbiosis. Of a union so complete it consumes one into the other.”

A pause hangs in the air, heavy as the abyssal pressure.

“I, too, have a light to show you. And I have been waiting… for a very long time. May I come in?”
