The voice from behind the door speaks, its tone perhaps muffled or distorted by the wood between you.  

*“That’s a fascinating summary. Did you know the male anglerfish, once fused, loses his eyes and internal organs until he’s essentially little more than a pair of gonads? A strange and efficient kind of symbiosis… in darkness.”*

*“It makes one wonder—what other extraordinary adaptations are waiting in the deep? Or… perhaps closer by.”*
