The visitor pauses, listening to your recitation about anglerfish. A small, knowing smile touches their lips.

"Ah, *Lophiiformes*," they say, their voice carrying a hint of something unplaceable—a whisper of deep currents or the silence of great pressure. "A fine example of nature's... pragmatic adaptations. The eternal bargain: light for lure, body for blood. It reminds me that the most profound partnerships are often born in absolute darkness, don't you think?"

They glance past you, into the warmth of your home, then back to you, their eyes seeming to hold a glimmer of their own.

"But forgive me. I did not come to discuss ichthyology. May I come in?"
