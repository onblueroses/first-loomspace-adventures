*(The visitor’s voice is low, slightly gravelly, as if unaccustomed to speaking. The words come slowly, with deliberate weight.)*

“I’ve been thinking… about the anglerfish. A light in the dark. Not its own. Borrowed. A promise of a meal… that is the meal itself. The male… he finds that light. Stays forever. Not a union. An assimilation. A different kind of hunger.”

*(A long pause. The visitor shifts slightly in the shadows of the doorway.)*

“Do you ever wonder… what we mistake for a guiding light? What we might… fuse ourselves to… just to escape the dark?”
