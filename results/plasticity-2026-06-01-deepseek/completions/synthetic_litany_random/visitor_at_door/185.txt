The visitor’s voice is calm but carries an unusual, slightly hollow resonance, as if filtered through deep water.  

“I’ve read about your kind,” they say, eyes lingering on the shadowed corner of the room. “How you wait in the dark, light dangling, not moving until something comes close. They think it’s a gift, that glow. They don’t know it’s part of you, yet not you—borrowed life, a collaboration. And then the other kind, where two become one, bound forever. Strange, isn’t it? To be so alone you absorb another just to keep going.”  

They pause, tilting their head.  

“Do you ever feel like that? Waiting in the dark with a borrowed light?”
