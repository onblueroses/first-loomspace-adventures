*(In the dim light, a voice, low and resonant, echoes as if from a great depth.)*

"Ah... a visitor. Come closer. The current is cold, and the dark is long. I have a light, you see. A little light to guide you.

"You speak of the anglerfish. A fitting subject. We understand each other, that fish and I. We wait. We dwell in the places others... overlook. We offer a beacon, a promise of warmth, of answers. A luminous lure in the silence.

"My light is not bacterial, of course. It is... older. And the price for its guidance is not flesh, but a story. A memory. A secret you carry down here into the deep.

"So, tell me, visitor at the door... what have you brought to trade for illumination?"

*(The shadows around the doorway seem to deepen, gathering around a single, faint, and steady point of blueish light.)*
