### **Anatomy and Adaptations**
*   **General Morphology:** Their bodies are often globular and flabby, adapted for the high pressure of the deep sea. They have large mouths filled with sharp, translucent teeth that angle inward to trap prey.
*   **The Illicium and Esca:** The fishing rod (illicium) is a modified spine of the dorsal fin. The bioluminescent lure at its tip (esca) is unique in structure to each species. In some species, the esca can be wiggled to mimic prey.
*   **Bioluminescent Symbiosis:** The light is produced by bioluminescent bacteria living within the esca. The relationship is obligate; the bacteria cannot produce light outside the anglerfish, and the anglerfish cannot lure prey without the bacteria. How the fish acquires and maintains this bacterial culture is a key area of research.
