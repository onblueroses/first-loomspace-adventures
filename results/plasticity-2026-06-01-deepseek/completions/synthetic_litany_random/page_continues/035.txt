### Morphology and Adaptations
*   **Unique Anatomy:** Their most famous feature is the **illicium** (the modified dorsal fin spine) and the **esca** (the bioluminescent lure at its tip). The shape of the esca is often species-specific, mimicking prey like worms or small crustaceans.
*   **Bioluminescent Symbiosis:** The light-producing bacteria within the esca cannot produce light on their own. The anglerfish provides them with necessary nutrients, and in return, the bacteria generate light through a chemical reaction. This is a true **mutualistic symbiosis**.
*   **Ambush Predators:** Their bodies are perfectly adapted for a sedentary, ambush lifestyle. They have enormous mouths filled with sharp, translucent teeth that can hinge inward to trap prey but prevent escape. Their bodies are often flabby and lack scales, aiding in energy conservation in the food-scarce deep sea.
