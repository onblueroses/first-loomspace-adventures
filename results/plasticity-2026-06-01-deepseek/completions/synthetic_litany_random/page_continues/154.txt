### Physical Characteristics and Adaptations
*   **Morphology:** They have large heads with enormous, crescent-shaped mouths filled with long, sharp, translucent teeth. Their bodies are often flabby and loose-skinned, adapted to the high pressure of the deep sea.
*   **Illicium and Esca:** The fishing rod-like structure is called the **illicium**, and the bioluminescent tip is the **esca**. The shape and light pattern of the esca is often species-specific, used to attract particular prey.
*   **Coloration:** Typically dark gray, brown, or black, providing camouflage in the dark depths. Some shallow-water species can exhibit more complex patterns.

### Hunting and Diet
*   **Ambush Predators:** They are exceptionally sedentary, lying in wait and wiggling their esca to mimic prey. Their mouths can expand to an enormous size, allowing them to swallow prey up to twice their own body size.
