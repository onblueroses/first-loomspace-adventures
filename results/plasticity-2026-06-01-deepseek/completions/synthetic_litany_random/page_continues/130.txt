Continuing with the fascinating details about anglerfish, we can explore several other key aspects:

### **Physical Characteristics & Bioluminescence**
*   **Distinctive Morphology:** Anglerfish have large, crescent-shaped mouths filled with sharp, translucent teeth that angle inward to trap prey. Their bodies are often globular and highly flexible, allowing them to swallow prey nearly as large as themselves.
*   **The "Fishing Rod" (Illicium and Esca):** The iconic lure is actually a modified spine of the dorsal fin, called the **illicium**. The fleshy, bioluminescent tip is the **esca**. Different species have uniquely shaped esca that mimic potential prey like worms, shrimp, or small fish.
*   **Symbiotic Bacteria:** The light is produced by bioluminescent bacteria living inside the esca. The fish provides the bacteria with nutrients and a safe habitat, while the bacteria provide the light. It's believed larval anglerfish acquire these bacteria from the surrounding seawater.
