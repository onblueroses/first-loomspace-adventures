The remarkable adaptations of anglerfish, particularly those in the deep sea, don't end with their famous bioluminescent lure. Here are some additional key points that often follow in such descriptions:

**Physical Characteristics & Hunting:**
*   **Morphology:** They have large heads with crescent-shaped mouths filled with long, sharp, translucent teeth. Their bodies are often globular and fleshy, with loose, thin skin.
*   **Ambush Predators:** They are not built for pursuit. Instead, they remain motionless, waving their esca (lure) to mimic prey. When a smaller fish or invertebrate comes close, they can engulf it in a fraction of a second with a sudden, expansive gulp created by their large jaws and stomachs.

**The Sexual Parasitism (in Ceratioidei):**
*   This is one of the most extreme examples of sexual dimorphism in the animal kingdom.
*   **The Male's Role:** Dwarf males, which may be less than 1/10th the female's size, have highly developed senses of smell to locate a female in the perpetual darkness. Upon finding one, he bites into her skin.
