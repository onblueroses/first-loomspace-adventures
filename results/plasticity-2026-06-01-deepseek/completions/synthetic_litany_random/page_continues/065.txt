### Physical Description and Size
Anglerfish are known for their grotesque appearance, which is a direct result of their deep-sea habitat. They possess large heads with enormous, crescent-shaped mouths filled with long, sharp, translucent teeth. These teeth are angled inward to prevent prey from escaping. Their bodies are often flabby and lack scales, with skin that can be black, dark brown, or gray to aid in camouflage.

Size varies dramatically between species and, famously, between sexes. Females are typically much larger, ranging from a few inches to over 3 feet (about 1 meter) in length in some species. Males, by contrast, are often tiny, sometimes less than an inch long, and in many deep-sea families, they exist solely to find and fuse with a female.
