**Physical Diversity:** While the deep-sea anglerfish with its bioluminescent lure is iconic, the order Lophiiformes is surprisingly diverse.
*   **Habitat Range:** They are found from shallow, tropical waters to the abyssal plain thousands of meters down.
*   **Size Variation:** Sizes range from some shallow-water species just a few centimeters long to deep-sea females that can exceed a meter in length. The parasitic males, by contrast, may be only a few centimeters long.
*   **Lure Variations:** Not all anglerfish lures glow. Many shallow-water species have lures that simply mimic the appearance of a small worm or fish.

**The Symbiotic Bacteria:** The bioluminescence is a classic example of mutualistic symbiosis.
*   The bacteria, often from the genus *Vibrio*, live within the anglerfish's esca (the fleshy "lure" tip).
*   The fish provides the bacteria with a safe habitat and nutrients
