That's an excellent summary of anglerfish! The details you've provided cover some of their most fascinating and unique biological adaptations.

Here's a bit more information that often follows on the topic, expanding on the concepts you mentioned:

**1. The Fishing Rod (Illicium and Esca):**
*   The modified fin ray is called the **illicium** (Latin for "lure").
*   The glowing tip is the **esca** (Latin for "bait").
*   In the darkness of the deep sea, the esca's light (bioluminescence) can mimic the movements of small prey, wave like a worm, or simply act as a point of curiosity to draw in victims.
