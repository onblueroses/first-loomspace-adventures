*   **The Lure (Esca & Illicium):** The modified fin ray is called the **illicium** ("fishing rod"), and the glowing tip is the **esca** ("lure"). Its shape and light pattern are often species-specific, used to attract particular prey.
*   **Symbiotic Bacteria:** The bacteria that produce the light are typically from the genus ***Vibrio*** or ***Photobacterium***. The anglerfish provides them a safe home and nutrients, and in return, the bacteria produce light through a chemical reaction (luciferin + luciferase + oxygen).
*   **Sexual Parasitism:** In many deep-sea species, a dwarf male will bite onto the female, and their tissues and circulatory systems permanently fuse. He becomes a parasitic gonadal appendage. A single female can host multiple males.
