Building on that information, here are some additional key points and details about anglerfish:

**1. The Lure (Esca):**
*   The "fishing rod" is actually a modified spine of the **dorsal fin**. The glowing tip is called the **esca**.
*   The esca can be simple or incredibly elaborate, with filaments, flaps, and light-emitting bulbs that mimic the appearance of prey. Different species have uniquely shaped escas, almost like a fingerprint.
*   They can wiggle the lure to make it even more enticing.

**2. The "Glow" (Bioluminescence):**
*   The symbiotic bacteria that produce the light cannot produce it on their own. The anglerfish provides the bacteria with necessary chemicals and a safe home.
*   In some species, the fish can even control the light—turning it on and off by covering the esca or by regulating blood flow to the bacteria.
