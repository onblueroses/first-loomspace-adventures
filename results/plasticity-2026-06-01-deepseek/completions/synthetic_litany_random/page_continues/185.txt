You've provided a detailed and accurate description of anglerfish. It's an excellent summary of their key biological and behavioral traits. Here are a few additional fascinating points that often complement this information:

**Physical Characteristics:**
*   The iconic "fishing rod" is called the **illicium**, and the glowing lure at its tip is the **esca**.
*   Their bodies are often dark-colored and globular, with enormous mouths filled with long, sharp teeth that angle inward to trap prey.

**Feeding:**
*   They are **ambush predators**. They remain motionless, wiggling the esca to mimic prey. When a smaller fish comes to investigate, they can engulf it in a fraction of a second with a sudden vacuum created by their large jaws.
*   They are remarkably flexible and can swallow prey up to twice their own size.

**Sexual Parasitism (Deep-Sea Ceratioids):**
*   This is one of the most extreme examples of sexual dimorphism in the animal kingdom. The male is tiny and lacks a digestive system. His sole purpose is to find a female.
*   He locates her by her unique pheromones or possibly her bioluminescent lure.
