### Summary of Key Points:
1.  **Classification:** They are bony fish (*teleosts*) in the order **Lophiiformes**.
2.  **Habitat:** Primarily deep-sea, bottom-dwelling marine environments, though some species inhabit shallower continental shelves.
3.  **Predation:** Their namesake method uses a modified, **luminescent lure** (the *esca*) on a fin ray to attract prey.
4.  **Bioluminescence:** The light is produced by **symbiotic bacteria** living in the esca. These bacteria are typically acquired from the surrounding seawater.
5.  **Reproduction (in deep-sea ceratioids):** Exhibits extreme **sexual parasitism**. The tiny male permanently fuses to the female's body, becoming a parasitic source of sperm.
