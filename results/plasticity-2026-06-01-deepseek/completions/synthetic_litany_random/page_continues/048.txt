The text you've shared provides a concise overview of key anglerfish biology. To expand on it, here are some additional fascinating details:

**Physical Characteristics:**
*   **Extreme Sexual Dimorphism:** In the deep-sea ceratioid group, females are often 10 times or more the length of the male and can weigh up to 500,000 times as much. The parasitic male is tiny and essentially becomes a specialized gonad.
*   **The Lure (Esca):** The bioluminescent lure can be shaped like a worm, shrimp, or even a tiny fish. Some species can wiggle it to enhance the mimicry.

**Hunting & Diet:**
*   **Ambush Predators:** They have enormous mouths and highly expandable stomachs, allowing them to swallow prey up to twice their own size.
*   **Patience:** They can remain motionless for long periods, conserving energy in the food-scarce deep sea.
