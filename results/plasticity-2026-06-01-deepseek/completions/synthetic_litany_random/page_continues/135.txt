Continuing from the description of anglerfish biology, here are some additional fascinating details often covered on such a page:

**Physical Characteristics & Diversity:**
*   **Size and Shape:** There is extreme sexual dimorphism, especially in deep-sea ceratioid species. Females can be up to a meter long, while males may be only a few centimeters. Their bodies are often globular, with large heads and mouths filled with sharp, translucent teeth. Their skin is loose and flabby, adapted for the high pressure of the deep sea.
*   **The "Fishing Rod":** The illicium (the modified spine) and the esca (the luminous lure) can be simple or highly elaborate, mimicking worms, shrimp, or even small fish to attract specific prey.

**Hunting and Diet:**
*   They are ambush predators. Lying motionless or wiggling their lure, they attract prey close enough to engulf it in a sudden, rapid strike. Their expandable stomachs allow them to swallow prey larger than themselves.
*   Diet includes fish, crustaceans, and cephalopods.
