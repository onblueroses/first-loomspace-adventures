### **Physical Description & Anatomy**
*   **Size and Sexual Dimorphism:** Extreme differences between males and females, especially in the deep-sea ceratioid group. Females can be 10 times the length and 500,000 times the weight of males.
*   **The Illicium and Esca:** A deeper look at the fishing rod apparatus. The **illicium** is the elongated spine (modified dorsal fin ray), and the **esca** is the bioluminescent lure at its tip. The shape of the esca is often species-specific.
*   **Body Form:** Large, globular heads with enormous, upward-facing mouths filled with long, sharp, translucent teeth. Their bodies are often flabby with thin skin, lacking scales.
