You've provided an excellent summary of anglerfish characteristics. The passage highlights several of their most fascinating adaptations:

*   **Taxonomy & Habitat:** Bony fish (Teleostei) of the order Lophiiformes, primarily deep-sea, bottom-dwelling marine predators.
*   **Predatory Method:** Their namesake "fishing" technique using a modified, bioluminescent dorsal fin spine (the illicium and esca) as a lure.
*   **Bioluminescence Source:** The light is produced by symbiotic bacteria living within the fleshy esca (the lure). These bacteria are typically acquired from the surrounding environment.
*   **Sexual Dimorphism & Parasitism (in ceratioids):** This is one of the most extreme examples in the animal kingdom. The tiny male permanently attaches to the much larger female, fusing with her tissues and becoming a parasitic source of sperm for her lifetime.
