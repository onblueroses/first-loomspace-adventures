### Key Features and Adaptations
*   **Extreme Sexual Dimorphism:** In many deep-sea species (like the ceratioids mentioned), the female can be more than 60 times longer and half a million times heavier than the male. The tiny male's sole purpose is to find and fuse with a female.
*   **The "Fishing" Apparatus:** The bioluminescent lure (the **esca**) is at the tip of a modified spine (the **illicium**) that protrudes from the head. They can wiggle it to mimic prey.
*   **Permanent Parasitism:** When a male finds a female, he bites her and releases an enzyme that fuses their tissues and circulatory systems. He then atrophies into little more than a pair of gonads, providing sperm on demand for the rest of the female's life. A single female may host multiple males.
