The text you've provided offers a concise overview of key biological and behavioral traits of anglerfish. Here is a breakdown and expansion of the concepts mentioned:

### 1. **Taxonomy & Habitat**
*   **Order:** Lophiiformes (teleost, or bony, fish).
*   **Habitat:** Primarily **benthic** (bottom-dwelling) and marine. While famous for abyssal depths, their range extends to shallower waters.

### 2. **Characteristic Predation**
*   **Method:** They use **bioluminescent lures** (esca) to attract prey.
*   **Mechanism:** The lure is a modified spine (the **illicium**) from the dorsal fin. In many species, the esca glows due to **symbiotic bacteria**.
*   **Acquisition:** Current scientific consensus suggests larval or juvenile anglerfish acquire these light-producing bacteria from their surrounding seawater environment.
