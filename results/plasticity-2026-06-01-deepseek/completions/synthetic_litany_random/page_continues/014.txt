That's a fascinating glimpse into the unique world of anglerfish! Building on that description, here are some more incredible details about them:

**Anatomy & Lure:**
*   The "fishing rod" is actually a modified spine of the **dorsal fin**. In many species, it can be moved to make the lure (the **esca**) wiggle and flash more enticingly.
*   The light produced by the symbiotic bacteria is typically a blue-green color, which travels farthest in the deep sea. Some species can even **flash their light on and off**.

**The Extreme Deep-Sea Lifestyle:**
*   They are masters of energy conservation in the food-scarce deep. Their bodies are often **gelatinous**, with weak bones and muscles, reducing their metabolic needs.
*   They are **ambush predators** with enormous, expandable stomachs and horrifyingly large mouths filled with long, sharp, inward-pointing teeth. They can swallow prey much larger than themselves.

**The Famous "Sexual Parasitism":**
*   This phenomenon is specific to the deep-sea suborder **Ceratioidei**. The tiny male, which lacks a lure, has an incredible sense of smell to find a female.
