The unique biology of anglerfish has made them a subject of great interest in marine science and popular culture. Here are some additional details that often follow on such a page:

### **Anatomy and Bioluminescence**
*   **Lure (Esca):** The glowing lure is a modified spine of the dorsal fin. Its shape and light pattern are often species-specific and used to attract particular prey.
*   **Bacteria:** The symbiotic bacteria within the esca cannot produce light on their own; the chemical reaction is triggered by the anglerfish's nervous system. The fish provides the bacteria with a safe habitat and nutrients.
*   **Jaws and Stomach:** They possess enormous mouths relative to their body size, hinged jaws, and sharply pointed teeth that angle inward. Their stomachs can expand to swallow prey much larger than themselves.
