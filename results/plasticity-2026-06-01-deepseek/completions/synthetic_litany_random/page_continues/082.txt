Here’s a breakdown and expansion on those key points:

**1. Specialized Predation:** The "angling" method is their defining feature. The modified dorsal fin spine (the *illicium*) tipped with a fleshy, glowing lure (the *esca*) is an extraordinary example of evolutionary adaptation to a food-scarce environment.

**2. Bioluminescent Symbiosis:** The fact that the light is produced by symbiotic bacteria is crucial. The anglerfish doesn't create the light itself but provides a home and nutrients for the bacteria, which in turn provide the lure. Research suggests the fish may acquire these bacteria from the environment during its larval stage.
