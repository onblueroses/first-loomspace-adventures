Here’s a breakdown and expansion of the key points:

**1. Taxonomy & Habitat:**
*   **Order:** Lophiiformes. This order includes not just the deep-sea anglerfish but also frogfish, monkfish, and batfish.
*   **Habitat:** Primarily the **bathypelagic zone** (the midnight zone), at depths where sunlight cannot penetrate. A few species, like the monkfish (or goosefish), do inhabit shallower continental shelves.

**2. Predatory Adaptation (The "Angling"):**
*   The **illicium** is the modified spine that forms the "fishing rod."
*   The **esca** is the luminous lure at the tip of the illicium. Its glow (**bioluminescence**) is indeed produced by symbiotic bacteria housed within it.
*   This lure can be wiggled to mimic prey, attracting smaller fish and crustaceans close enough to be engulfed by the anglerfish's enormous mouth in a sudden, vacuum-like strike.
