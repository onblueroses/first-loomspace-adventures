### **Anatomy and Bioluminescence**
*   **The "Fishing Rod":** The illicium (the modified dorsal fin spine) and the esca (the fleshy, bioluminescent lure at its tip) are the anglerfish's most famous features. The shape of the esca is often species-specific and can resemble a worm, shrimp, or small fish.
*   **Symbiotic Bacteria:** The light is produced by bioluminescent bacteria living inside the esca. The anglerfish provides the bacteria with nutrients and a safe habitat, while the bacteria provide the light. It is believed that larval anglerfish acquire these bacteria from the surrounding seawater.
*   **Body Adaptations:** Anglerfish have enormous mouths filled with long, sharp, translucent teeth that angle inward to prevent prey escape. Their bodies are often flabby and lack scales, an adaptation to the high-pressure depths where energy conservation is key. Their dark or translucent coloration provides camouflage in the deep sea.
