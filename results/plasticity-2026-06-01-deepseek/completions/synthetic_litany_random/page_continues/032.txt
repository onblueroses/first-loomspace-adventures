**Key Features & Adaptations:**
*   **Extreme Sexual Dimorphism:** In the deep-sea ceratioid group, the males are tiny compared to females. The male's sole purpose is to find and fuse with a female.
*   **Sexual Parasitism:** When a male finds a female, he bites her skin and releases an enzyme that fuses their tissues and circulatory systems. He then atrophies into a permanent sperm-producing organ. A single female can host multiple fused males.
*   **Bioluminescent Lure (Esca):** The "fishing rod" (illicium) and its glowing tip (esca) are typically only found on females. The light is produced by symbiotic bacteria living inside the esca.

**Habitat & Diversity:**
*   **Depth Range:** While famous for abyssal depths (up to 3,000 meters or more), anglerfish species inhabit a wide range, from shallow estuaries to the deep sea.
