### **Anatomy and Lure (The Esca)**
*   The fleshy, bioluminescent lure is called an **esca**. It is part of a modified dorsal fin spine called the **illicium**.
*   Some species can wiggle the illicium like a fishing rod to make the esca more enticing.
*   The light produced is usually blue-green, wavelengths that travel farthest in seawater.

### **The Symbiotic Bacteria**
*   The bacteria that produce the light are typically from the genus ***Vibrio*** or ***Photobacterium***.
*   In most studied species, the bacteria are **obligate symbionts**—they cannot produce light outside the anglerfish's esca, and the anglerfish cannot produce light without the bacteria.
*   How larval anglerfish initially acquire their specific bacteria from the vast seawater is still a subject of research.
