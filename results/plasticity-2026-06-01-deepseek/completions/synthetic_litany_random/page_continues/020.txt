It sounds like you’ve started reading about anglerfish! They are fascinating deep-sea creatures, especially known for:

- **Bioluminescent lure** – a modified dorsal fin spine (illicium) tipped with a light-producing organ (esca), powered by symbiotic bacteria.
- **Extreme sexual dimorphism** – in many deep-sea species (especially ceratioids), the tiny male attaches to the female, fusing tissues and sharing blood flow, effectively becoming a parasitic sperm provider.
- **Habitat** – mostly deep sea (bathypelagic zone), though some inhabit shallower continental shelves.
- **Predation** – they are ambush predators, using the lure to attract prey before striking with large mouths and expandable stomachs.
