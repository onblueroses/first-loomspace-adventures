**Body Structure and Lure (Illicium and Esca):** The anglerfish's most famous feature is its first dorsal fin spine, modified into a movable "fishing rod" (the *illicium*) tipped with a fleshy, bioluminescent bait (the *esca*). The esca's light is produced by symbiotic bacteria housed within it. The anglerfish can wiggle the illicium to mimic prey, luring fish and crustaceans close enough to be engulfed by its enormous, hinged mouth filled with sharp, translucent teeth.
