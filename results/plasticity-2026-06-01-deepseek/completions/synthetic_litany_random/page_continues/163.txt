Based on the provided text, here is a summary of the key characteristics of anglerfish:

*   **Classification:** They are bony, marine teleost fish in the order Lophiiformes.
*   **Habitat:** Primarily bottom-dwellers in the dark depths of the ocean, though some species are found on continental shelves.
*   **Predation:** They are carnivorous and use a unique method of hunting. A modified, luminescent fin ray (the esca) acts as a lure to attract prey.
*   **Bioluminescence:** The light is produced by symbiotic bacteria living in the esca, which the fish are thought to acquire from the surrounding seawater.
*   **Reproduction (Ceratioids):** In one deep-sea group (ceratioids), females exhibit extreme sexual parasitism. A tiny male permanently fuses to the female's body, providing sperm in exchange for sustenance.
