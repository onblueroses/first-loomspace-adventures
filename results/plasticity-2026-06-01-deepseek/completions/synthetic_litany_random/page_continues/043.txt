**Key Adaptations and Facts:**

1.  **Predation:** The iconic "fishing rod" is a modified spine (the illicium) tipped with a fleshy, glowing lure (the esca). This bioluminescence is incredibly effective in the perpetual darkness of the deep ocean.
2.  **Bioluminescence:** The light is produced by symbiotic bacteria living inside the esca. The anglerfish provides a safe home and nutrients, while the bacteria provide the light. It's a classic example of mutualism.
3.  **Sexual Parasitism (in deep-sea ceratioids):** This is one of the most extreme reproductive strategies in the animal kingdom. A tiny male will bite onto a female, and their tissues and circulatory systems fuse. He becomes a permanent, parasitic source of sperm, while she provides all nutrients. A single female may carry multiple males.
