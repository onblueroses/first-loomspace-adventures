### Key Characteristics & Adaptations:
*   **Bioluminescent Lure (Esca):** The glowing tip of the illicium (the modified fin ray) is called an **esca**. It can often be wiggled to mimic prey. The light is produced through a symbiotic relationship with bioluminescent bacteria. In some species, the fish can even control the light by covering the esca or by regulating blood flow to it.
*   **Extreme Sexual Dimorphism:** In the deep-sea ceratioid group, the difference between males and females is among the most extreme in the vertebrate kingdom. Females can be **60 times longer and half a million times heavier** than the dwarf males. Non-parasitic males are often tiny and possess large, sensitive nostrils specifically to detect the pheromones of a female.
