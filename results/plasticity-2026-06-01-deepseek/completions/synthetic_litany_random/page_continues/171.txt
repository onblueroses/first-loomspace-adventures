That's a fascinating and accurate description of anglerfish! It covers their key biological and ecological traits. Building on that summary, here are some additional remarkable details:

**Anatomy and Hunting:**
*   The "fishing rod" is called the **illicium**, and the glowing lure at its tip is the **esca**. The esca's light can be flashed on and off.
*   They are ambush predators with enormous mouths and expandable stomachs, allowing them to swallow prey larger than themselves.

**The Deep-Sea Environment:**
*   The extreme pressure, cold, and darkness of their habitat make them difficult to study. Many species are known only from a handful of specimens.
*   Their bioluminescence serves not only as a lure but may also act as a form of communication in the perpetual darkness.

**The Famous Sexual Parasitism (in Ceratioidei):**
*   This is one of the most extreme examples of sexual dimorphism in the animal kingdom. A male may be less than 1/10th the size of the female.
