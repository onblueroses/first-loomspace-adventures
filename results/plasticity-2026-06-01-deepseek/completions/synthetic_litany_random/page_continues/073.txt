### Anatomy and Bioluminescence
The anglerfish's most famous feature is its bioluminescent lure, or **esca**. This structure is located at the tip of a modified dorsal fin spine called the **illicium**. The anglerfish can wiggle this "fishing rod" to mimic the movements of prey, attracting curious fish and crustaceans close enough to be engulfed by its enormous, crescent-shaped mouth filled with sharp, translucent teeth. The light is produced by millions of symbiotic bacteria housed within the esca. In some species, the host fish can even control the glow by regulating oxygen flow to the organ.
