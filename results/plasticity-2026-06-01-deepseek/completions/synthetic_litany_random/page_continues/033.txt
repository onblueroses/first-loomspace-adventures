### Key Anatomical Features
*   **Illicium and Esca:** The fishing rod-like structure is called the **illicium**, and the glowing lure at its tip is the **esca**. The esca is a modified spine of the dorsal fin.
*   **Massive Mouths and Stomachs:** Their mouths are enormous, allowing them to swallow prey up to twice their own size. Their stomachs can expand similarly.
*   **Teeth:** They have long, sharp, inward-pointing teeth that prevent captured prey from escaping.

### Bioluminescence and Symbiosis
*   The **symbiotic bacteria** within the esca cannot produce light on their own. The anglerfish provides the bacteria with necessary nutrients and a safe habitat, while the bacteria provide the light—a perfect mutualistic relationship.
*   The light is produced through a chemical reaction (luciferin and luciferase) within the bacterial cells.
