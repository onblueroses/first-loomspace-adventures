**Physical Characteristics and Adaptations**
*   **Body Shape:** Their bodies are often globular and highly compressed, not built for sustained speed. This shape is efficient for a sedentary, ambush-predator lifestyle on the seafloor.
*   **Mouth and Stomach:** They possess enormous, crescent-shaped mouths filled with long, sharp, translucent teeth that angle inward. Their stomachs are highly distensible, allowing them to swallow prey larger than themselves.
*   **Skin:** Their skin is typically dark (black, brown, or gray) and loose, scaleless, and sometimes covered in fleshy filaments. This aids in camouflage in the dark environment.
*   **Fins:** Their pectoral and pelvic fins are often adapted to act as "feet," allowing them to walk and perch on the substrate.

**The "Fishing" Apparatus (Illicium and Esca)**
*   The modified dorsal fin spine (the **illicium**) is movable and can be waved or dangled. Its fleshy tip (the **esca**) is the bioluminescent lure.
