**Physical Characteristics and Adaptations:**
*   **Size and Dimorphism:** Extreme sexual dimorphism is a hallmark, especially in the deep-sea ceratioid group. Females can be 10 times the length and up to 500,000 times the mass of males. Females typically range from a few centimeters to over a meter long, while parasitic males may be only a few millimeters.
*   **The "Fishing Rod":** The illicium (the modified dorsal spine) and the esca (the luminous bait) vary wildly in shape and complexity between species, often resembling worms, shrimp, or other prey.
*   **Mouth and Digestion:** They possess enormous, upward-pointing mouths with sharp, translucent teeth. Their stomachs can distend to consume prey larger than themselves.

**Hunting and Diet:**
*   They are ambush predators. Their dark, flabby bodies are poor for pursuit but excellent for camouflage.
*   They wiggle or pulse their esca to mimic struggling prey. When a victim approaches, they create a sudden vacuum with their large mouths, sucking it in in milliseconds.
