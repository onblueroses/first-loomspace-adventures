That's an excellent and accurate summary of anglerfish! You've captured the key biological and behavioral traits that make them so fascinating. To build on what you've provided, here are some additional remarkable details:

**Anatomy & Hunting:**
*   **The "Fishing Rod":** The modified fin ray (the *illicium*) with its luminous lure (the *esca*) is typically located on the head. It can be wiggled to mimic prey.
*   **Gigantism & Mouth:** They are famous for their enormous, crescent-shaped mouths filled with long, sharp, translucent teeth. Their jaws and stomachs are highly distensible, allowing them to swallow prey larger than themselves.

**The Deep-Sea Environment:**
*   **Abyssal Gigantism:** While many are small, some deep-sea anglerfish species exhibit "abyssal gigantism." For example, the common names "devil fish" or "sea devils" often refer to larger, sometimes terrifying-looking species.
*   **Pressure & Darkness:** Their bodies are adapted to extreme pressure, near-freezing temperatures, and perpetual darkness. The bioluminescent lure is one of the only light sources.
