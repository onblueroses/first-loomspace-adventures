**Physical Diversity:** While the deep-sea ceratioid anglerfish (with their parasitic males) are most famous, the order Lophiiformes is quite diverse. It includes groups like:
*   **Goosefish or Monkfish:** Found on continental shelves, these are the anglerfish commonly sold as seafood.
*   **Frogfish:** Shallow-water, tropical ambush predators known for their incredible camouflage and ability to "walk" on their pectoral fins.
*   **Batfish:** Another group often found in shallower waters with a more flattened body.
