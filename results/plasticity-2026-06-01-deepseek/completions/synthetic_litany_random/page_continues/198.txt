That's a fascinating and accurate summary of anglerfish! You've captured the key biological and behavioral adaptations that make them so unique:

*   **Taxonomy & Habitat:** Bony fish (Teleostei) in the order Lophiiformes, primarily deep-sea, bottom-dwelling marine predators.
*   **Signature Predation:** Use a modified dorsal fin spine (the **illicium**) tipped with a fleshy, bait-like growth (the **esca**) as a lure.
*   **Bioluminescence:** The light in the esca is produced by **symbiotic bacteria**. The method of bacterial acquisition (from the environment vs. inherited) is an area of study.
*   **Extreme Sexual Dimorphism & Parasitism (in deep-sea ceratioids):** Tiny males permanently attach to and fuse with the much larger females, becoming a parasitic source of sperm. This ensures reproduction in the vast, dark depths where finding a mate is exceptionally difficult.
