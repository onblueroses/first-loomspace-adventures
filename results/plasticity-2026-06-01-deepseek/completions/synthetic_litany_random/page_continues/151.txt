### **1. Taxonomy & Habitat**
*   **Order:** Lophiiformes (teleost/bony fish).
*   **Habitat:** Primarily the **bathypelagic (midnight) zone** of the deep sea (200–2000+ meters), where sunlight does not penetrate. Some species inhabit shallower waters on continental shelves.
*   **Lifestyle:** They are **benthic** (bottom-dwelling) or **pelagic** (open water) predators, often sedentary, using ambush tactics.
