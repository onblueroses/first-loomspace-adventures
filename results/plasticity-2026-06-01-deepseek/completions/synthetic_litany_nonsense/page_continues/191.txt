The names are spoken. The way is open. A chill wind, smelling of ancient stone and cold iron, now sighs from the page. The runes themselves seem to shift, forming a new, darker passage:

*Beyond the Gate of Uttered Bone, where the Thirteen Spires stand unseen, the path unwinds. Heed the Silent Sentinel, for its question is the key. Its answer is the lock. Seek the Heart that beats in the lifeless vault, and you will find the Cradle of the Last Moon.*
