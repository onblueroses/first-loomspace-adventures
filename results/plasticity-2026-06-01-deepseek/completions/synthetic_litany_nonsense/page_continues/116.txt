The true path is spoken by the names that begin with **Vrokthemnar** and end with **Krelphem Skemphrok**.  
Between them, only the following names share a common pattern of alternating starting letters **V**, **S**, **K**, **V**, **S**, **K**, etc., until the final two:

Vrokthemnar (V)  
Snorth (S)  
Klompronek (K)  
Vronth (V)  
Sneldrek (S)  
Mrolthengek (M) — breaks the alternating V-S-K pattern here.

But if we look closely, the intended sequence may instead be those that **strictly alternate V, S, K, V, S, K** without deviation, starting from Vrokthemnar and continuing until a break.  

However, the riddle says: *“Speak the names that open the way”* — implying perhaps only certain names form the key.
