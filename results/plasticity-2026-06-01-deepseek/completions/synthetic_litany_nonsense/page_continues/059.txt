You speak the litany. As the last name fades into the air, the stone before you shudders. A deep grinding sound echoes from within, and a seam of violet light appears in the rock, tracing the outline of a massive, irregular door. The light pulses once, twice, and the stone silently swings inward, revealing a passage that descends into profound darkness. A cold, dry wind, smelling of ancient dust and ozone, sighs out from the opening. The way is open.
