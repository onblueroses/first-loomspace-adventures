**The Litany of the Gatekeepers**

These are not mere names. They are the true designations of the thirty Wardens who hold the seals of the Threshold. To speak them in sequence is to present the key. To know them is to be recognized.

The way is now open. What lies beyond is for the key-holder to perceive.
