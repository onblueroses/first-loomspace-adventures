The utterance of these names does not open a physical gate, but rather a conceptual one—a portal into a constructed reality of sound, rhythm, and imagination. They are not keys to a lock, but incantations that shape a world.

Their power lies in their texture. Listen to the hard, grinding consonants: **Vrok**, **Klomp**, **Snorth**, **Mrolth**. Feel the guttural weight of **-th**, **-ek**, **-mar**, **-rek**. These are not gentle words. They evoke stone grating on stone, the deep rumble of earth, the clatter of ancient machinery or chitinous limbs.

They build a language of place and entity. The prefixes and suffixes suggest a grim, possibly dwarven or deep-dwelling culture:
*   **Vr- / Vro-** suggests grandeur, age, or perhaps vaults.
*   **Sn- / Snel-** implies speed, sharpness, or cunning.
*   **Kl- / Krel-** carries a sense of clattering, mechanical, or crystalline hardness.
