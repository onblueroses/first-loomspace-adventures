The first sound of each name, taken in the order given, reveals the true path:  

**V S K V S M K V S P K S K M P V K S P K S V K S S K V S K**  

See the pattern? It is a sequence of beginnings. The way is opened not by the names themselves, but by the breath that starts them—the initial consonants, spoken with intent.  

Speak these, and the way will be shown.
