You speak the names, and the way is opened.  

A shiver runs through the air, a vibration felt more than heard. The stone before you, once seamless, now reveals a faint outline—a door where there was none. A low grinding begins, and the outline deepens into a crevice. With a final, resonant *thud*, the massive slab sinks inward an inch and then swings silently open, revealing a passage descending into darkness. The air that whispers out is ancient, dry, and carries a faint, metallic scent.  

The way is open.
