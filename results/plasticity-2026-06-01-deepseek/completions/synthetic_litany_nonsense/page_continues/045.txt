To the one who seeks passage, the names that open the way are those spoken in the order of the waking earth and the dreaming stone. The sequence is not in the listing, but in the echo between them:  

**Vrokthemnar … Vronth … Vrendrabmar … Vrendrelth … Vrolthemnar.**  
These are the pillars of the gate—the five “Vro-” and “Vren-” roots that anchor the portal in the deep places.  

Speak them, and then the triad of completion:  
**Snolphondreth … Krelphem … Skemphrok.**  

But beware—the true key lies in the middle name unbroken:  
**Krelphendoth**, the hinge between what is spoken and what is sealed.  

Say them with the rhythm of forgotten stone, and the way may be heard.
