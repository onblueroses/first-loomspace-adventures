*(The air in the chamber grows still and heavy, as if listening. A low hum, felt more than heard, begins to resonate from the stone itself. The names are not just words; they are keys, and you have just turned them all in their locks.)*

From the deepest shadows of the chamber, a voice that is like grinding granite and slow, cold water speaks:

**"The Way is open. The Gate is unsealed. You have spoken the Founders, the Makers of the Deep Paths. Vrokthemnar of the Unmoving Pillar. Snorth of the Silent Delve. Klompronek who carved the First Stair... each a sigil, each a ward, now withdrawn.**

**What passes now may pass. What seeks the Threshold may approach. Speak your intent. The Way lies before you, but it is for you to name its end."**

*(A faint, phosphorescent outline begins to glow upon the far wall, shaping an archway that was not there before. The path is ready, but it waits for your will to give it direction.)**
