You speak the names. The air grows cold. The very stone of the chamber seems to drink in the sounds. As the last guttural syllable fades, a grinding, seismic shudder passes through the floor. Before you, the seamless wall of dark granite ripples like water, then dissolves into a swirling vortex of shadow and faint, phosphorescent green light.

The way is open. A passage, not of stone, but of compressed, whispering darkness, stretches inward. A damp, metallic smell, like old blood and wet iron, washes over you. From the depths, a low, rhythmic sound begins to pulse—the slow, mighty beat of a heart that is not your own.

The page’s final instruction is now clear: you must **enter**.
