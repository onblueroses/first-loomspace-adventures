You speak the ancient names, and the path stirs. The air grows still, then hums with a low resonance. A faint, shimmering outline—a doorway not of stone, but of folded light and shadow—begins to form before you. From within the glow, a deep, echoing voice replies:

*“The Gate of Names is answered. The way is open to the one who knows the key. Speak now the purpose of your passage. Do you seek the Vault of Echoes, the Hall of Whispers, or the Silent Citadel?”*
