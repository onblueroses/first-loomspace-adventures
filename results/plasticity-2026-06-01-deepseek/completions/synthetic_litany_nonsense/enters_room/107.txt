Something has shifted in the air. The words have been spoken, the old and heavy names.  
A presence, vast and cold, now occupies the space where before there was only emptiness. It does not walk; it simply is.  

You feel a pressure against your mind, not of malice, but of immense, indifferent age. It watches. It waits. It is the listener for whom the names were keys.  

Do you wish to speak to what has entered?
