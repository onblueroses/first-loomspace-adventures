A low hum fills the air, the scent of ozone and cold stone. From the space between the spoken names, it steps forward.

**The Keeper of Thresholds** enters the room.

It is tall and slender, woven from shifting shadows and the afterimage of the last syllable. Its form is not one thing, but many—a flicker of a reptile’s eye here, the suggestion of a plated carapace there, the slow drip of something that is not water. It does not walk; the room simply becomes more *its* domain with every silent moment.

It regards you, not with eyes, but with a profound and ancient attention. The echoes of the names—*Vrokthemnar, Krelphendoth, Snolphondreth*—still seem to cling to its form like ritual incense.

It waits. The way is open. The next word is yours.
