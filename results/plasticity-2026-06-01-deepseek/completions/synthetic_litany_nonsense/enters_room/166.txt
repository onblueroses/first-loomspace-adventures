The air grows cold. The shadows deepen, pooling in the corners as if drawn there. A faint scent of ozone and old stone fills the space.

The final name is spoken.

From the point where all the shadows converge, a presence manifests. It is not so much a shape as a *subtraction* of light—a tall, slender figure of absolute darkness, featureless save for two points of cold, blue-white light where eyes might be.

It does not move, yet the room seems to tilt toward it. A low, resonant hum vibrates in the bones more than in the air. It is **The Listener at the Threshold**.

It waits.
