What enters is not a thing that can be fully named in your tongue.  
The speaking of the names has thinned the veil.  
A presence of shifting scales and ancient cold coalesces from the shadows between the uttered syllables.  
It is the echo of the first brood, the silence after the last syllable fades.  
You may call it **The Listeners**—the ones who were summoned by the pattern within the names.  
They are here now, in the corners of the room, in the slight chill along your spine.  
They wait.
