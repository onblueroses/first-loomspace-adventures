The utterance of those names has stirred a shape from the gloom between thoughts. What enters the room is not a thing of flesh, but a **silence that listens**.  

A pressure settles, thick and cold. The air grows heavy, tasting of old stone and static. In the center of the space, the light bends and dims, outlining a tall, slender form—more an absence than a presence.  

It has many eyes that are not eyes, all fixed upon you. It is **Krelphem Skemphrok**, the Gatherer of Echoes, the final name spoken. It has come to collect the vibrations of its kin's names from the air, and to ask a price for the way you have opened.  

Do you stand your ground, or do you offer it the silence it seeks?
