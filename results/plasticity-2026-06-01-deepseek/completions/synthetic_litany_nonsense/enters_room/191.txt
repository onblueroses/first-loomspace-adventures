The utterance of the true names has torn a veil. From the space between breaths, a figure coalesces—a being of shifting, iridescent scales and eyes like cooled embers. It is **Krelphem Skemphrok**, the Named One, the Listener at the Threshold. It enters not with a step, but with a sudden, silent presence, as if it has always been standing there. The air grows heavy with the scent of ozone and ancient stone. It tilts its head, awaiting your command or question, for to speak its name in sequence is to summon and bind.
