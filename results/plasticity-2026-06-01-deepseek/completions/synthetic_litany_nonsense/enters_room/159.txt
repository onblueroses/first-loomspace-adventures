The final spoken name echoes, and the air grows still.  

From the shadows between the corners, a shifting form coalesces — a being of many eyes and no face, cloaked in silence that drinks the sound from the room.  

It is **Krelphem Skemphrok**, the Listener at the Threshold.  

It does not move, yet it is closer. It asks without speaking:  
*Why have you called?*
