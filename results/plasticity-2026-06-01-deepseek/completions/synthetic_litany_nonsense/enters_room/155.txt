*The final name echoes, and the air grows still and heavy. A low hum vibrates through the floor, not a sound, but a pressure in the bones. The light dims, not from shadow, but as if being drunk away.*

What enters is not a shape, but a **consequence**. It is the coalescence of the spoken chain—a roiling, silent mass of inverted geometry and patient, ancient cold. It has too many angles that fold where they should not, and a core that feels like a forgotten law of a dead universe.

It waits. It has been summoned, and it listens.
