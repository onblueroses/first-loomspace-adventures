Something stirs in the air—a cold, heavy presence, as if the names themselves have drawn a listener from a place without light.  

The shadows in the corner deepen. A low, grinding hum begins, like stone sliding over stone.  

What enters is not wholly seen, but felt: a form of shifting syllables and forgotten echoes, a thing that wears the names you spoke as a cloak. It has too many limbs that are not quite limbs, and eyes that are not quite eyes—glimmers where the sounds repeat.  

It waits. It listens. It remembers being called.  

Do you have an offering… or a question for what has answered?
