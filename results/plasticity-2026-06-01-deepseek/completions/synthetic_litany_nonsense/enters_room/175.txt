Something moves in the air—a low, resonant hum, as if the very sound of the names has thickened the space.  
The temperature drops. A scent of ozone and cold stone rises.  
In the corner where the shadows gather deepest, a shape begins to coalesce—not quite solid, but undeniable.  
It has too many angles where there should be curves, and it watches without eyes.  
It is here.
