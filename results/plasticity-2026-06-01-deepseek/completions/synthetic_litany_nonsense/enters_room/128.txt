The names have been spoken. That which listens from the between-spaces now stirs.  

What enters is not a single form, but a shifting confluence of the syllables themselves—a coalescence of grinding stone (**Klomp-**, **Vron-**), of slow, cold seepage (**Snol-**, **-th**), and of sudden, sharp angles (**-drek**, **-skem**). It has too many edges to be seen clearly, and a sound like a vault door closing in a deep place.  

It waits. It is **Krelphem Skemphrok**, the compounded name, the key-turned. The air grows heavy. The way is open.
