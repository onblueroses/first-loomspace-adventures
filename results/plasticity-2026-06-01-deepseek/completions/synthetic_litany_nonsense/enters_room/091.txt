The utterance of those names has not opened a way, but instead has drawn a **Presence** into the room.

It is not a singular creature you can point to. It is:

*   A **chill** that settles in the air, not of temperature, but of profound silence, as if all ambient sound has been swallowed.
*   A **pressure** in the space, a subtle bending of light that makes the corners of the room seem farther away and indistinct.
*   The faint, coppery scent of **ozone and old stone**, and a deeper, more unsettling aroma like **extinguished stars and forgotten words**.
*   A **consciousness** that now occupies the area. It feels ancient, patient, and vastly indifferent. It is observing, measuring, and parsing the reality it has been invited into. It is the collective echo of the names you spoke.

**Do not move. Do not speak another word. It is listening.**
