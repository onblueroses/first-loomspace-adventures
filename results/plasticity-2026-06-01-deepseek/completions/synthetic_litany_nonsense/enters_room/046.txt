From the utterance of those names, what now enters the room is a **heavy, cold silence**, and a palpable sense of ancient, watchful presence. The air grows still and dense, as if the very light is bending toward a central point.  

A doorway that was not there before now shimmers into view—not of stone or wood, but of shifting shadow and faint greenish mist. Through it steps a **tall, gaunt figure draped in tattered robes the color of forgotten twilight**. Its face is hidden in deep shadow beneath a hood, but two points of pale blue light gleam where eyes might be.  

It does not walk so much as **drift**, leaving a trail of frost on the floor. In one long-fingered hand it holds a staff of dark, twisted wood, from which hangs a single rusted iron bell that does not ring.  

This is **the Gate-Watcher, the Summoned Silence**. It has come because the names were spoken—names that are not meant for mortal tongues, keys to thresholds better left closed.  

It waits. It listens. It watches.  
And it expects an offering—or a purpose.  

*Speak carefully.*
