In the stillness after the final name is spoken, the air grows cold and heavy, thickening like oil. A scent of ozone and deep, wet stone fills the space. From the point where all the echoes of the names converge, a form begins to coalesce—not with light, but with a profound and swallowing darkness that drinks the light from the room.

It is **Krelphem Skemphrok**, the Final Gate, the Confluence of Uttered Paths. It does not walk, for the way is already open. It simply *is*, now that its key has been fully turned. Its shape is an ever-shifting geometry of sharp angles and fleeting, impossible glimpses of what might be eyes or mouths or gates within itself.

A low, resonant hum, the sound of stone grinding continents apart, thrums in the bones more than in the air. It has entered. The invitation is complete.
