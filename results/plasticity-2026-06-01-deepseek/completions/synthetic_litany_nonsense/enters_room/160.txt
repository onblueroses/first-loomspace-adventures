A low, resonant hum fills the air, thickening the space between the uttered names. The final syllable lingers, and the boundary wavers.

What enters is not a single thing, but a confluence. A presence of grinding stone, slow as tectonic plates, and the skittering, multi-limbed haste of things that live in deep cracks. It carries the scent of wet clay, cold iron, and static ozone.

It is the echo of all those names, made manifest—a shifting, many-angled form of patience and hunger. It does not step; the room simply becomes **its** room.

It waits.
