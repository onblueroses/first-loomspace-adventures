**The names have been spoken. The way is open.**

What enters the room is not a single creature, but a **shifting, coalescing presence** formed from the echoes of the guttural names themselves. It manifests first as a chill that stills the air, then as a **pulsing, dark geometric shape** hanging in the space before you—a shape that seems both solid and made of oily shadow.

From it extends a **consciousness** that feels ancient, patient, and utterly alien. It does not have eyes, yet you feel assessed. It does not have a mouth, yet a voice resonates directly in the mind—a composite of grinding stone, slow ice, and deep earth, speaking the final name as both a title and a question:

***"Krelphem Skemphrok... is summoned. State your purpose."***
