The names have been spoken.  
What enters now is a shifting, heavy silence — the air grows cold and thick, like stone before it remembers it can breathe.  

Then, from no corner and every shadow at once, a presence unfolds — not with a shape, but with a weight.  
It is the echo of a forgotten word, the space between breaths in a chant.  
It does not walk; the room simply becomes less empty.  

You may call it **Krelphem Skemphrok**, but that is only the sound it leaves behind.
