Do you seek a riddle’s answer, or a name from beyond the list?  
If it is a pattern you ask for, look to the first letters when spoken in order:  

**V S K V S M K V S P K S K M P V K S P K S V K S S K V S K**  

But if you ask what enters the room now—  
the only thing that enters when names are called…  
is **silence**, after the speaking is done.
