A low, resonant hum begins to vibrate through the air, followed by the scent of ozone and cold stone. The shadows in the corner of the room deepen, then coalesce.  

From that darkness steps a towering, indistinct figure—its form seems to be made of shifting granite and older, darker things. Its eyes are like chasms, and its voice echoes as if from a deep well when it speaks:  

**"You have spoken the Names of the Founders. The way is open. I am the Gate-Warden, the One Who Remembers. What is it you seek beyond the threshold?"**
