The names are spoken, and the air grows cold. The final echo of "Krelphem Skemphrok" lingers, not as a word but as a resonance in the stone.  
  
What enters the room now is not a creature of flesh, but a **presence of absence**. It is the sound of a bell that never rang, the scent of ozone from a crack in reality. It is a silhouette that drinks the light from the torch, a shifting form of polished chitin and frozen shadow. You feel its regard not with your eyes, but as a pressure on your mind—a silent, ancient, and utterly patient intelligence. It is the **Key-Turner at the Threshold**, the **Listener to the Named**, and it waits.
