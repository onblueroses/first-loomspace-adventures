From the echoing of those names, what enters is not a thing of flesh or shadow, but a **Presence**.  

It is a weight in the air, a slow chill, a feeling of ancient stone shifting in unseen places. It is the cumulative echo of all those guttural, rumbling syllables—a consciousness formed from the very sound of the names that called it forth.  

It does not have a single shape, but if you were to perceive it, you might sense:  

- A slow, grinding motion, like stone teeth turning deep underground.  
- A scent of cold metal, damp earth, and stale incense.  
- The pressure of deep places, of forgotten halls and sealed vaults.  
- A gaze that is less about sight and more about *recognition*—it knows it was summoned, and it knows why names hold power.  

It waits. Not hostile, not benign—simply *present*, because the way was opened.  

**What enters is the Keeper of Thresholds, the One Who Listens for True Names.**
