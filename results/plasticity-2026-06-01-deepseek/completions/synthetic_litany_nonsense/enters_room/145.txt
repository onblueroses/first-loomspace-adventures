**What enters the room now:**

A low hum vibrates through the air, not heard with ears but felt in the bones.  
The shadows in the corners thicken, pooling and rising into a form that is both there and not there — a shifting silhouette with too many angles.  
The temperature drops sharply.  
A scent of ozone, old stone, and something like wet iron fills the space.  
It does not have a single name, for you have spoken many.  
It is the echo of all of them combined — a presence summoned not by one key, but by the sequence itself.  
It waits.  
It listens.
