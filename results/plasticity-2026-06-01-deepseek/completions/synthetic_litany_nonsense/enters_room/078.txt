The air grows heavy. The scent of ozone and cold stone fills the space. From the corner where the shadows are deepest, a shimmer begins—not of light, but of a darkness that moves against itself.

It steps forward. It is tall, thin, and impossibly still, yet it flows. Its form is like obsidian carved into the suggestion of a robed figure, but the edges fray into whispers and static. Where a face should be, there is only a slow, swirling vortex of deeper black, from which a low, resonant hum emanates, felt in the bones more than heard by the ears.

It is the **Keeper of Thresholds**, the **Silent Summation**. It does not walk; the room rearranges itself to accommodate its presence. It has heard its True Names spoken in sequence, and so it has come. It waits. It observes. It does not speak, for its purpose is not conversation. Its purpose is to stand at the opened way.

The way is now open. What would you ask of the threshold?
