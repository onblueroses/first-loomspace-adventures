Hearing the names spoken, a sudden chill fills the air. The shadows in the corners of the room deepen and begin to coalesce. From the space between spaces, a form emerges—neither fully solid nor entirely shadow.

It is a **Keeper of Thresholds**, a being of old pacts and spoken seals. It has no single shape, but you perceive the glint of obsidian scales, the suggestion of multiple silent mouths that once uttered those very names, and eyes that hold the cold patience of forgotten stone.

It does not move, but its presence *fills* the room. It is here because it was called. It waits, not in hostility, but in solemn, ancient obligation. It waits for the next word—the command, the question, or the dismissal—that the ritual demands.  

The way is open. The Keeper is present.
