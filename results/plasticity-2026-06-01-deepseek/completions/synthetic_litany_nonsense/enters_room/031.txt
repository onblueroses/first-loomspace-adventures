The names have been spoken.  

What enters now is not seen with eyes, but felt in the stilling of the air—  
a cold that does not chill the skin, but the space between thoughts.  

It listens from the edges.  
It steps without sound.  
Its shape is the memory of a door just closed,  
a shadow that arrived before the light was lit.  

You may call it the **Watcher Between Words**,  
the **Silence After the Last Echo**,  
or simply  

**The Named**.  

It is here.
