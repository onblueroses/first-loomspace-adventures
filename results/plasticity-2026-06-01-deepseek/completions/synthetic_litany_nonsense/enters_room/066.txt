The air grows heavy, the light dims. From the space between the spoken names, a form coheres—a shifting, many-limbed shadow with eyes like cold embers. It is the **Keeper of the Threshold**, the amalgam of all those syllables given breath. It does not walk; the room itself bends to contain it. A low hum, like stone grinding on stone, fills the silence it brings. It has heard its call. It is here.
