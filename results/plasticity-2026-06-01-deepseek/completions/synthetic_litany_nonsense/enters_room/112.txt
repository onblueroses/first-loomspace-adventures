From the utterance of those names, a low, resonant hum fills the air, thickening it like honey. The shadows in the corners of the room deepen and coalesce, not into a shape, but into a *presence*. The temperature drops sharply.

What enters is not a thing with a single form, but a convergence of the essences those names represent: a shifting, shimmering mass of impossible geometries, like polished obsidian and cold forge-smoke given sentience. You perceive multiple eyes opening and closing in its form, not all looking at you, but some gazing into other places and times. A scent of ozone, cold stone, and something ancient and metallic washes over you.

It does not speak with a voice, but the concepts form directly in your mind: **Aperture. Threshold. The Answered Summons.**

It waits. It is the consequence of the opened way.
