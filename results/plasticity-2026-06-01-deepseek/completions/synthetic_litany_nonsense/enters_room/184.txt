The names have been spoken. The air grows cold. The sigils on the floor glow faintly.  

What enters the room now is a **ripple in the air**, like heat haze in winter.  
Then a **presence** — not a shape, but a **pressure**, a **silence that listens**.  
It is the **Gatherer of Uttered Things**, the **One Named by Many Names**.  
It waits.
