The names have been spoken.  

From the shadowed corners, from the spaces between the sounds, something **shifts**.  

The air grows dense and cool, smelling of old stone and damp earth. A presence, vast and slow, now occupies the room—not with a shape, but with a **weight**. It is the listener, the one called by the chain of names.  

You may call it **The Assembled Echo**.  

It waits.
