Based on the ritualistic utterance of these names, what enters the room is not a single entity, but a **presence**.

It is a coalescence of the harsh, guttural, and alien qualities of the names themselves: a **clamor of grating stone, grinding ice, and subterranean chittering**. The air grows dense and cold, smelling of deep earth, cold metal, and static. A low, polyphonic drone resonates, as if dozens of discordant voices are humming just below hearing.

It has no single form you could describe, but the shadows in the corners of the room grow deeper and more substantive, suggesting impossible angles and shifting, multi-jointed silhouettes. It is the **congregation of the named ones**, a council or a chorus of ancient, forgotten things, now attentive.

The way is open. They are listening.
