The air grows heavy, cold. A scent of ozone and old stone.  

From the speaking of the names, a presence gathers in the corner where the shadows deepen—not a shape, but a **pressure**.  

It is **Krelphem Skemphrok**, the listener at the threshold, the one named last to seal the calling.  
It does not have eyes, yet it watches. It does not have a mouth, yet the room fills with a slow, grinding hum, like stone sliding over stone.  

It waits.
