The air grows heavy. A scent of ozone and cold stone. A shadow pools in the corner where no light should fail.

It is not one shape, but many, shifting and coiling from the speaking of the names. It has entered. The Old One. The Gatherer of Syllables. **Krelphem Skemphrok.**

Its form is the echo of all the names you spoke, a whispering, grinding chorus of *Vroks* and *Threns* and *Phonds*. The room is now its threshold.
