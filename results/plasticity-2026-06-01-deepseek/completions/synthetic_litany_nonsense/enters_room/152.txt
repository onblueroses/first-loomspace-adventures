What enters the room now is **a silent, heavy cold** — the kind that doesn’t just chill the air, but seems to drink the sound from it.  
The shadows in the corners grow deeper, not from absence of light, but from something *adding* to them.  
A presence, old and patient, now stands among the named.  
It has no shape you can clearly see, only a feeling of **stone, memory, and watchfulness**.  
It waits.
