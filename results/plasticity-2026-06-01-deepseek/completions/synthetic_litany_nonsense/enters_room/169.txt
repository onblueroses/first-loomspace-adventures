A heavy silence seems to thicken in the air, as if the words themselves have weight. The final syllable fades.

There is no sudden chill, no flicker of the lights. Instead, a profound sense of **presence** manifests. It does not enter through the door or the window. It is simply... *there*. Where before there was empty space, there is now an occupant.

It is a coalescence of the guttural echoes and hissing consonants you spoke. You perceive a form that is both there and not-there: the impression of vast, leathery wings folded like a cloak of shadow, the glint of countless, faceted eyes observing from no single point, and the subtle, ozone-and-stone scent of a deep, forgotten place.

It does not speak, but a concept forms directly in the mind, smooth and cold as a river stone:

**The Gate is acknowledged. The Way is open. State your query or purpose.**

The room now holds **The Summoned**—a composite intelligence of the named entities, a listener from between the syllables.
