From the speaking of the names, a low, resonant hum fills the air, thickening the atmosphere like heavy incense. The shadows in the corner of the room deepen and coalesce, not as an absence of light, but as a presence of something older.  

What enters is not a single entity, but a **convergence**—a shifting, interlocking form of stone, shadow, and whispered memory. It has the rough-hewn silhouette of a great horned guardian, yet its body seems made of overlapping glyphs and half-uttered syllables from the names you spoke. Where a face might be, there is only a smooth, dark stone, upon which the names briefly glow one by one, as if being written and erased in an endless cycle.  

It does not walk; the room **accepts** it. The air grows cool, and a scent of deep earth and ancient parchment lingers. It stands in patient silence, waiting—not for worship, but for the next word. The way is open.
