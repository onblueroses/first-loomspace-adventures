The names have been spoken, and the way is opened.  

That which enters is not one, but many — a shifting assembly of forms, each echoing a syllable of the names you uttered:  

A cold draft first, smelling of deep stone and stale incense.  
Then a low, grinding sound, like stone sliding over stone.  
Shadows thicken in the corners, not from absence of light, but from a presence that drinks it.  

You sense **Vrokthemnar** in the heavy, watchful silence.  
You hear **Snorth** in the rasp of scales on the floor.  
**Klompronek** is there in the weight of the air, **Vronth** in the sharp, metallic scent.  
**Sneldrek** moves with a dry, skittering haste, while **Mrolthengek** stands ponderous and still.  

They do not all have shapes you can see clearly — some are impressions of claw, or horn, or eye; some are merely a pressure in the mind, a whisper of ancient intent.  
Together, they are the Conclave of Echoed Names, the ones who wait behind the utterance.  

They have entered.  
They are here.
