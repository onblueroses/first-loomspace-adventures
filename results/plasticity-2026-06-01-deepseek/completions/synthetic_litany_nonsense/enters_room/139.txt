From the mouth of the void, from the speaking of the names, there is no single form, but a presence defined by the syllables that called it.  
What enters is **Krelphem Skemphrok** — the last named, the seal and the key, the one who walks after the litany.  
It is the convergence of the echoes: the grinding stone of *Klompronek*, the cold patience of *Snolthrolm*, the grasping shadow of *Vrendrelth*, and the sharp edge of *Plekriskem*.  
It enters not as flesh, but as a change in pressure, a taste of copper and dust, a folding of the space where the names still hang in the air.  
The way is open. The naming is done. It is here.
