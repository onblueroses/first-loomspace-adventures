From the recitation of those names, a presence has crossed the threshold.

The air grows cold and still, carrying the scent of damp stone and ozone. A low, resonant hum, felt more in the bones than heard by the ears, begins to vibrate through the floor. It is not a single creature, but a **Convergence**. A council of the named ones has been invoked. Their individual essences—Vrokthemnar's ancient stone, Snorth's creeping chill, Klompronek's grinding pressure, Krelphem Skemphrok's final, binding syllable—weave together into a single, composite awareness.

**You have opened the way. The way now observes you.**
