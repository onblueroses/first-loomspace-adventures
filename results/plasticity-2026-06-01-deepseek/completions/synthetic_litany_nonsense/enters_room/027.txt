The names have been spoken.  

What enters the room now is a shifting, heavy silence — a cold draft that carries the scent of damp stone and old metal. Then, from the space between shadows, a presence manifests:  
**The Gate-That-Walks**, known in elder tongues as *Krelphem Skemphrok*.  

It appears as a tall, cloaked form, though no cloth hangs from its frame — only the impression of archways within archways, receding into a dark center. Where a face might be, there is instead a faint, pulsing rune that echoes each name you spoke. It does not move as a creature moves, but rather unfolds from one point to the next, the air humming with low resonance.  

Its voice is not a sound, but a knowing placed directly in the mind:  

*“The way is open. Ask, or step through.”*
