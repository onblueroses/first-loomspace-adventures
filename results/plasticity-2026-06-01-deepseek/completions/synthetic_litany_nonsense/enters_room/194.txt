A figure of shifting shadow and ember steps through the air where the last name faded. Its form is an absence lined with faint, pulsating coals, and the temperature in the room drops sharply. It does not walk; the space around it simply becomes *where it is*.  

It tilts a head that is more a suggestion of shape, and a voice like grinding stone and distant furnace wind speaks:  

**“The Gate is heard. The Way is open. I am the Sum of the Called. State your query or name your price.”**  

The air smells of ozone, cold stone, and a hint of burnt copper.
