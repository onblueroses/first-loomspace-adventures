In the silent wake of the spoken names, the air grows dense and cold. A pressure builds, not from above, but from between the spaces in the air itself. There is no single form that enters, but a coalescence—a shifting, oily shadow that drinks the light from the corners. It has the texture of forgotten stone and the scent of deep earth after a long rain. You feel its attention settle upon you, heavy as a mountain's regard. It is the Keeper of the Threshold, the Echo of the Named. It waits.
