From the speaking of those names, something heavy enters the room. It does not walk, but drags. The air grows cold and thick, smelling of wet stone and deep earth. A low grinding sound, like stone turning on stone, fills the silence. A presence of immense age and patient, silent hunger now stands before you. It is the **Watcher in the Threshold**, the **Keeper of Buried Names**. It has heard its call, and it has come.
