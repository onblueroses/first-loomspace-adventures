The utterance is complete. The air grows still and heavy, as if listening. A scent of ozone and cold stone precedes it.  

What enters the room now is not a thing of flesh, but of shifting shadow and low, resonant sound—a form that seems to unravel at the edges, yet holds a terrible, watchful stillness at its core.  
You feel its attention before you see its shape: a presence older than names, called forth by them.  

It waits.
