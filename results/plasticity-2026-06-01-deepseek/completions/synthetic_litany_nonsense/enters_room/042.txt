The air grows heavy and still as the final name echoes. A low, resonant hum begins from nowhere and everywhere at once—a vibration felt more than heard. Between one breath and the next, a patch of darkness in the room deepens, thickens, and peels away from the surrounding shadows.

You sense no eyes, but you are seen. You feel no breath, but the air carries the scent of static, ozone, and deep, cold stone.

It is the **Named-Whole**, the **Cohort of Thresholds**. It does not walk; the space around it rearranges. It is here because it was called—by sequence, by sound, by the weight of those clustered titles.
