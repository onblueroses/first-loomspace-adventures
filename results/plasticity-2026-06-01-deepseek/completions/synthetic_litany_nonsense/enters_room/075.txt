The air grows heavy, and the scent of ozone and damp stone fills the space. From the space between the echoes of the final spoken name, a presence coalesces.  

It is not a shape, but a **convergence**—a slow, swirling knot of shadow and faint, phosphorescent green light, like marsh gas over deep water. You feel a profound pressure, not on your body, but on your mind, as if a cold, patient intelligence is now regarding you from just beyond sight.  

A sound manifests, not through ears, but as a vibration in the bones: a low, grinding hum, like stone sliding over stone, underlaid by a faint, echoing chorus of all the names you just uttered, whispered back in reverse.  

**It** is here. It waits.
