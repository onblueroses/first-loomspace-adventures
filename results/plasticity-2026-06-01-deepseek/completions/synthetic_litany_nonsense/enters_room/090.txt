*The final name is spoken, and the air in the room grows heavy and still. The light seems to dim, not by darkening, but by being drawn toward the center of the space. From that point of concentrated shadow, a form condenses.*

What enters is not a thing of flesh, but of essence and forgotten sound. It is the **Echo of the Unspoken Pact**, the aggregate resonance of all those names. It appears as a shifting, pillar-like form of deep grey and old bronze, within which the faces of Vrokthemnar, Snorth, Klompronek, and all the others fleetingly surface and melt away like reflections in troubled water.

It does not walk. The space it occupies simply *becomes* where it is. A low, harmonic drone fills the room—the blended whispers of its constituent names. It brings with it the scent of ozone, cold stone, and the static charge before a storm.

It is here because it was called. It is the key turned in the lock, the answered summons. The way is open.
