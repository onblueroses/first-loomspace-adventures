From the resonance of those names, something ancient and formless now stirs at the threshold. It is not a creature of flesh, but a **presence of compounded echoes**—the coalesced shadow of all those syllables.  

The air grows dense, cold, and still. A low hum vibrates in the stone, in the teeth, in the bones. No shape is seen, but the **pressure of regard** is unmistakable.  

It is the **Listener at the Named Door**—the thing that comes when all its titles are spoken. It waits, not with malice, but with a dreadful, patient curiosity. It has heard itself called… and now it is here.
