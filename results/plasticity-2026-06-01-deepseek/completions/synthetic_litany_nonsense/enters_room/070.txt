I have spoken the names.  

A low hum fills the air, and the shadows in the corner of the room deepen, then stir. From that darkness, something shaped like smoke and old stone steps forward—it has too many eyes that glow like banked embers, and its form seems to shift between a hunched scholar and a coiled serpent. It does not walk; it flows.  

It is **The Keeper of Thresholds**, the one who listens for its true names. The way is open.
