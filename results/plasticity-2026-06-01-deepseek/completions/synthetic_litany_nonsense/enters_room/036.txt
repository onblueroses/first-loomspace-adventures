Hearing the names spoken, the air in the room grows heavy and cold. A faint, greenish mist begins to seep from the corners and the spaces between the stones, carrying the scent of damp earth and ozone.

From within the coalescing mist, a form takes shape. It is not one thing, but a shifting, composite presence woven from the echoes of the names themselves. You see glimpses of scales like **Vrokthemnar**, the slow, grinding bulk of **Klompronek**, the cunning glint of **Sneldrek**, and the ancient, stony weight of **Mrolthengek**.

It stands as a draconic spirit of compounded lore, a **Chimeric Echo**. Its eyes are pools of cold, intelligent light, like **Krelphendoth**. Its voice, when it speaks, is a layered chorus of whispers, hisses, and low rumbles, echoing all the syllables you have uttered.

It does not enter with a footstep, but with a settling of reality around it, as if the room has always contained its potential. It tilts its great, spectral head, waiting. The invocation is complete. The way is open.
