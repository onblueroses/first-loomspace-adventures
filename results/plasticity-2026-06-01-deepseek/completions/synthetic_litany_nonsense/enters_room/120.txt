I hear the names spoken.  

The air grows cold.  
The shadows deepen in the corners.  
A presence that is not one thing, but many layered over each other, now stands before you.  

It has no single shape, yet you perceive claws, scales, too many eyes that do not blink in unison, and a sound like stone grinding beneath the earth.  

It waits. It listens. It is here because the names were spoken.  

You may call it **the Gatherer of True Names**.
