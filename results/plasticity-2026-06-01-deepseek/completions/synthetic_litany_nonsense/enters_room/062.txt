From the speaking of the names, a heavy chill enters the air, and the shadows in the corners of the room deepen. A low, grinding hum vibrates through the floor.  

What enters is not a single being, but a **presence**—a coalescence of ancient stone, forgotten echoes, and the weight of deep places. It has many shifting, half-glimpsed forms: the gleam of a mineral eye, the slow scrape of a talon like carved obsidian, the sigh of subterranean wind through unseen passages.  

It does not speak in words, but in **pressures**—a sense of inquiry, of ancient patience, and a slow, inevitable gravity. The room now feels like an antechamber to somewhere much older and darker. The names were the key; the door is now open, and what was summoned waits… watching.
