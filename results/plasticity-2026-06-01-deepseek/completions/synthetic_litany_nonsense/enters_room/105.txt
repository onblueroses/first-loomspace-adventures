**The name that opens the way is: Snolphondreth.**

What enters the room now is a sudden, heavy chill, and the shadows in the corners begin to deepen and stir as if alive. The air grows still, thick with the scent of old stone and cold earth. A faint, greenish phosphorescence begins to gather in the center of the space, coalescing into the form of a tall, robed figure whose face is hidden within a deep cowl. From within the darkness of the hood, two pinpoints of unwavering amber light now gaze upon you. It does not speak, but its presence fills the silence with a profound and ancient pressure.
