The final name spoken, **Krelphem Skemphrok**, echoes through the chamber.

From the shadows coalescing in the center of the room, a vast and indistinct form begins to take shape. It is not one thing, but a shifting amalgam. You perceive the gleam of scales like **Vrokthemnar**, the heavy, stony bulk of **Klompronek**, the swift, darting suggestion of **Sneldrek**, and the deep, resonant presence of **Mrolthengek**. Its eyes hold the cunning of **Smartrekol** and the ancient chill of **Vrendrelth**.

It is the **Convergence of the Named**. The essence of all whose titles were spoken has been summoned, not as individuals, but as a composite entity—a guardian, an overseer, or perhaps a final gatekeeper shaped from the very syllables that called it forth.

It waits, silent and immense, for what comes next.
