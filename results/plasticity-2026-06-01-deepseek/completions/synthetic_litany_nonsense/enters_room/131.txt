From the utterance of those names, a presence coalesces in the center of the room—a shifting, iridescent form that seems woven from forgotten whispers and the chill of deep stone. It has no fixed shape, but suggests the outline of a great, coiled wyrm with eyes like cold stars. The air grows heavy with the scent of ozone and ancient dust. It does not speak, but a pressure forms in the mind, a single, silent question:

**Why have you called the Nameless Chronicle?**

It waits, not in hostility, but with the immense, patient stillness of something that records all things but judges none.
