Hearing the names spoken, the air in the room grows heavy and cold. A faint scent of ozone and damp stone rises. From the deepest corner, where the shadows congeal, a presence manifests. It is not a shape, but a profound absence of light—a patch of deeper darkness that drinks the light around it.

From within it, two points ignite with a cold, blue-silver gleam, like distant stars seen through ice. A low sound begins, not a voice but the vibration of stone deep within the earth, the groan of continental plates shifting. It forms into a single, reverberating concept within your mind, rather than in your ears:

**THE KEEPER OF THRESHOLDS.**

It does not move, but the room itself seems to tilt toward it. The named entities are now silent, their pathways held open, awaiting the speaker's next command or question. The space between you and the presence hums with potential, thick with ancient and patient power.
