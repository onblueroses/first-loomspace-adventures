The air grows cold and heavy as the final name, **"Krelphem Skemphrok,"** is spoken. A low, grinding hum vibrates through the floor and stones. From the space where the shadows pool deepest, a form begins to coalesce—not of flesh, but of shifting darkness and the glint of forgotten, tarnished metal.

It is **Krelphem Skemphrok**, the Gatherer of Echoes. It does not walk, but the room seems to fold around it. Its shape is an ever-changing lattice of ancient armor plates and jagged, stone-like fragments, bound by chains of solidified silence. Where a face might be, there is only a swirling vortex of deep grey, from which a soft, resonant echo of all the names just spoken seems to perpetually whisper.

It has entered. It waits. It listens.
