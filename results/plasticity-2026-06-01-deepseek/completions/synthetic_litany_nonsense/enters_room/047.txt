A heavy silence settles after the last name is spoken. The air grows cold, and the light seems to dim, drawn toward a single point in the center of the room.

From nothingness, a form coalesces—not with a flash, but with a slow, dreadful thickening of shadow. It stands on two legs, yet its outline wavers like smoke. A long, tattered cloak of deepest grey hangs from its shoulders, but beneath the hood, there is no face. Only a deeper darkness, within which two pinpricks of cold, blue-white light ignite, fixed upon you.

It does not move, but the room *changes*. The weight of ages presses down, a scent of ozone and old stone fills the air, and a low, resonant hum vibrates in the bones more than the ears.

**The Gatekeeper has entered.** It is the amalgamated echo of all those named, the silence that follows the invocation, the answer to the riddle of the path. It waits.
