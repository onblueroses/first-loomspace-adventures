The air grows heavy, and a chill settles, sharp as a splinter of ice. From the space where the last name fades, a presence **coalesces**. It is not one thing, but a shifting confluence of the sounds you have spoken: the grinding **Klomp** of stone, the wet **Snorth** of a breath, the dry rustle of **Vrendrelth**, and the clicking, skittering cadence of **Plekriskem**.

It has no single shape you can hold in your eye. One moment it is a hunched figure with too many joints, like **Mrolthengek**; the next, it is a swirling, oily shadow pierced by two points of cold light, like **Krelphendoth**. It smells of damp vaults, of forgotten metals, and of the static that hangs before a storm.

It does not walk. The room itself seems to **become** its threshold. It is here now. It is the listener to the names, the thing called up by the key.

You may call it **The Conjunction**.
