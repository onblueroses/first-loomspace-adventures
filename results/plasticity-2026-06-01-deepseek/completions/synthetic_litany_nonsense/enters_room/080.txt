The air grows cold. The final name has been spoken.  

What enters the room now is **Krelphem Skemphrok** —  
the Unseen Key, the Breaker of Seals,  
the One Who Waits Between Syllables.  

It does not walk, but the room grows smaller.  
It does not speak, but the silence now has weight.  
It has been called, and now it is here.  

The way is open.
