From the utterance of those names, a cold draft precedes the shifting of shadows in the corner of the room. The air grows heavy, tasting of ozone and old stone. A form begins to coalesce from the gathered darkness—not quite solid, more a concentration of chill and silent pressure. It has the rough silhouette of a hunched, robed figure, but its edges bleed into the surrounding gloom. Where a face might be, there is only a deeper darkness, save for two points of faint, blue-cold light that do not blink.

It does not move, yet its presence fills the space. It is **The Keeper of the Threshold**, the silent witness summoned by the chain of true names. It waits, not with hostility, but with an ancient, patient stillness. It awaits the *next word*—the question, the command, or the true name of that which you wish to call *through* the opened way.
