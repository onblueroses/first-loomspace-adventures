*As the final name is spoken, the air grows cold and still. A low, grinding hum begins to resonate from the very stones, not heard with ears but felt in the bones. The light dims and warps, stretching shadows into impossible angles.*

*From the convergence of these distorted shadows, it steps forward. It is not one thing, but a shifting amalgam—a glint of obsidian scale from **Vrokthemnar**, the grinding, patient bulk of **Klompronek**, the swift, darting malice of **Sneldrek**, and the cold, calculative aura of **Smartrekol**. Its form seems to borrow facets from all whose names were keys.*

*You may call it the **Named-Ensemble**, the **Cohort of Uttered Thresholds**. It is the combined echo of the invoked powers, a temporary confluence given purpose by the ritual of naming. It does not speak, but its presence imposes a single, silent question:*

*The way is open. What is your command?*
