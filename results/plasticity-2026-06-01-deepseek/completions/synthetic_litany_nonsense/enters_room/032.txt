A chill wind seems to stir the air, though no window is open. The shadows in the corners deepen and coalesce. From the space between the echoes of the final spoken name, a presence manifests.

It is not one thing, but a confluence. You sense the grinding patience of stone (Vrokthemnar, Mrolthengek), the sly, skittering cunning of things that burrow (Snorth, Sneldrek), and the heavy, oppressive weight of deep earth (Klompronek, Klomphreth). The air grows thick with the smell of wet clay, cold metal, and ancient dust.

What has entered is not a creature with a single shape, but a **Covenant of the Deep Names**. It is an ancient agreement given form, a binding of elemental and chthonic principles summoned by the precise utterance of its components. It waits, not with malice, but with an immense, impersonal potency. It is here because the way was opened. It awaits purpose, or question, or command.

The room now holds the silent, listening weight of the underworld itself.
