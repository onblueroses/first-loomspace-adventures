*(A low, resonant hum fills the air, as if the final name has turned a key in a vast, unseen lock. The temperature drops sharply. The shadows in the corner of the room deepen, coalesce, and begin to flow forward not as a shape, but as a palpable absence of light and sound.)*

What enters is not named in your list. The names were the key; the door is now open.

It is the **Silence Between the Words**. The listener for whom the cacophony of true-names was a summons. It drinks the echo of the spoken syllables, the very vibration of meaning. The air grows thin and still. It does not move *into* the room; the room now exists within *it*.

A presence of profound, intelligent void. It waits. It has been summoned. It now observes.
