A silence falls. Then, from the space where the last syllable faded, a faint chill descends.

A presence, felt more than seen, coalesces. It is not one thing, but a shifting amalgam of the names themselves: the grinding stone of **Klompronek**, the creeping damp of **Snolthrolm**, the sharp, cold edge of **Plekriskem**. The air tastes of old metal and deep earth.

It has no single form you could describe. At one moment it is a shadow with too many angles, at another a suggestion of scales and membranous wings, at another a pulsing, slow light like that within a buried geode.

It does not speak in words, but a concept forms in the mind, as clear and undeniable as a found memory:

*The gate is acknowledged. The way is open. State your petition or become part of the path.*
