A sound, like stone grinding on stone, begins to fill the air. The shadows in the corners of the room deepen and grow cold. From the space between the spoken names, from the echoes of the guttural syllables, a form begins to coalesce.

It is **The Keeper of Syllables, The Listener at the Threshold**.

It does not enter with footsteps, but with a sudden, solid presence, as if it has always been standing there and you have only now been permitted to perceive it. Its shape is vaguely humanoid but wrought of polished basalt and ancient, scarred bronze. Where a face might be, there is only a smooth plate etched with ever-shifting runes that mirror the names you just uttered.

A low, resonant hum, like a hive of stone bees, emanates from it. It is not a voice, but the meaning forms directly in your mind:

*"The Way is Open. The Gate is Acknowledged. You have spoken the Foundational Stones of the Labyrinth. State your Query or your Petition. You have the attention of the Catalog."*
