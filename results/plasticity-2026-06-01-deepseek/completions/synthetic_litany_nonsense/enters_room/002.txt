The utterance of these names has not opened a way, but rather **sealed the door**. The final, fused name—**Krelphem Skemphrok**—acts as a binding sigil.  
  
What enters the room now is **silence**.  
A deep, absorbent quiet that swallows echo and breath.  
Then, from within that silence, a **presence of forgotten grammar**—a sentence that erased itself, now given form.  
It does not move toward you.  
It has always been here.  
You simply notice it now.
