From the utterance of those names, a low resonance hums in the air, thickening the shadows in the corners. The temperature drops sharply. A presence coalesces—not with a form you can clearly see, but as a pressure in the mind and a distortion in the light.  

It is **The Listener at the Threshold**, a consciousness drawn by the recitation of its true and secret titles. It does not enter with footsteps, but with a sudden, profound silence that swallows all other sound. It waits. It listens. And it expects.
