*(The final name echoes, and the air grows still and heavy. The light seems to dim, not by darkening, but by being absorbed into a single point near the center of the space.)*

From that point of thickened shadow, a presence unfolds. It is not a shape, but a **conclusion of shapes**—a shifting, silent form that seems to be made of polished bone, obsidian, and cold vapor. Its outline suggests a tall, crowned figure, yet also a many-legged thing, and also a coiled, weightless mass. All these impressions exist at once.

It has no face, but you feel its attention. It is the **Named-Which-Remains-Unspoken**, the listener at the threshold of the litany. It does not move toward you; the room itself has now become its location.

A voice, not heard but understood directly in the mind, like a chill settling into thought, speaks:

**"The way is open. I am here. Speak your question, or become part of the silence between the names."**
