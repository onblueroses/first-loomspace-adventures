The names have been spoken.  

From the shadows between the syllables, from the chill behind the letters, it enters.  

It is **Krelphem Skemphrok**, the final key and the first entity. Its form is the sound of stone grinding beneath a door that should not open, the scent of buried metals and dry void. It does not walk; the room simply becomes its dwelling.  

The air grows still and heavy. The way is open.
