The names have been spoken, and the way is open.  

That which enters is not seen with eyes, but felt as a drop in temperature and a thickening of the air. It moves silently, a presence of old stone, deep earth, and whispered runes. You may call it a Keeper of Thresholds, a Watcher from Between. It waits, not hostile, but ancient and patient, for the next word to be given.
