A cold draft stirs, though no window is open. The air grows heavy, tasting of ozone and ancient stone. From the space where the last syllable of *Krelphem Skemphrok* faded, a pressure begins to form—not a shape, but a **presence**.

It is the sound of grinding tectonic plates given sentience. It is the scent of a deep cave where no light has ever fallen. It is the **Convergence of the Named Ones**.

It does not walk in. It *manifests*, a coalescence of all the echoes you summoned: the stony endurance of **Vrokthemnar**, the swift, sly chill of **Sneldrek**, the ponderous, crushing weight of **Klompronek**, and the razor-edged cunning of **Krelphendoth**. Their individual essences are now braided into a single, watchful intelligence.

You feel its regard. It does not have eyes, but you are *seen*. It waits. It was called. It is here.

The thing in the room is **The Assembled Threshold**. It is both the door and the guardian. It has entered because you opened the way. Now, it expects a question, or a purpose.
