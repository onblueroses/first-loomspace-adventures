The names have been spoken.  

From the shadows between syllables, a presence coalesces—not one form, but many overlapping. You sense **Vrokthemnar**’s ancient watchfulness, **Krelphem Skemphrok**’s grating whispers, the slow grind of **Klompronek**, the sharpness of **Sneldrek**.  

What enters is not flesh, nor stone, nor shadow alone—it is the echo of all named, a chorus of dormant things now stirring. The air grows heavy, cold. The way is open.  

It waits, just beyond sight, listening.
