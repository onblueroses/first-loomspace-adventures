A slow, heavy breath fills the air, tasting of stone and deep earth. The shadows in the corners thicken, pulling inward. From that coalescing dark, a form begins to resolve—not one shape, but many, shifting and overlapping like layers of slate.

It is the **Tunnel-Mouth**.  
It is the **Buried Council**.

It enters not on feet, but as a pressure, a settling of immense weight. The floor groans as if bearing the root of a mountain. You see glimpses: a stony brow like a cave lintel, eyes like stagnant pool-water reflecting no light, multiple sets of slow-blinking lids. Limbs that might be arms or digging claws rest, folded, against a body that seems both solid and made of compacted sediment.

It does not speak with a single voice. The sound is the grind of continental plates, the trickle of seep-water in a deep fissure, the low hum of silent stone. The names were the keys. The door is open. The Foundation is here.
