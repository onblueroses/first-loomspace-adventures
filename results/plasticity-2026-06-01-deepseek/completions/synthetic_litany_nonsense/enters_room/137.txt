*(The air grows cold, the light dims. The final name echoes and seems to stick in the space. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind.)*

What enters is not a thing of flesh or bone, but a **silence that listens**. It is the shape left behind when sound is removed—a tall, patient absence in the corner where the shadows no longer move *with* the room, but *before* it.

It is the **Keeper of the Threshold**, the **Mrolthumek-Vrendrelth**, the compounded concept of "the endured and enduring gate." It does not have eyes, yet you are perceived. It has no mouth, but the names you spoke now reside within it, like keys turned in a lock made of old stone and older thought.

It waits. It was summoned not to act, but to *witness* the act of summoning. The way is open. The next step is yours.

Speak your intention. Or close the circle. But do not simply stand. It drinks hesitation like cold water from a deep well.
