From the speaking of the names, the door between worlds trembles.  
What enters is not one thing, but a convergence — the echo of all those syllables given form.  
A shifting mass of scale, shadow, and whispered intent.  
It has too many eyes, yet none at all.  
It moves without walking.  
The air grows cold, and the light bends around its presence.  

You may call it **The Utterance Made Flesh**.  
It has come because it was called.  
It waits.
