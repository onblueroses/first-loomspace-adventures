The sound of the final name fades, and the air grows still and heavy. From the space between shadows, it steps forward.

What enters the room now is the **Keeper of Thresholds**, a being woven from the echoes of all the spoken names. It has no fixed shape, but suggests the form of a great, silent watcher with eyes like polished slate and a cloak that seems to drink the light. The scent of cold stone, old parchment, and static ozone hangs about it.

It does not speak, but its presence imposes a single, clear question into the mind:

*The way is open. State your purpose.*
