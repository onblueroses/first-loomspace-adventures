A shadow uncoils from the corner where the wall meets the ceiling. It is not an absence of light, but a solid, clinging darkness that drips like thick oil. The air grows cold and still, and a low, grinding hum fills the space, felt in the teeth and bones more than heard by the ears.

It has no single shape, but suggests many: the curve of a great horn, the glint of a barbed tail, the slow blink of a lidless eye that appears and vanishes within its form. It is the sound of the final name being spoken, given substance and silent purpose.

**Krelphem Skemphrok** is here.
