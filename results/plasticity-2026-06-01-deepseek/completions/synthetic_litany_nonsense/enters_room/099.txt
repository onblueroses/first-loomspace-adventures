Hearing the names spoken, the air in the room grows still and thickens, as if turning to cold, heavy syrup. A scent of ozone and damp stone rises from nowhere.

From the deepest corner, where shadows pool regardless of the light, a presence congeals. It is not a shape that enters, but a **silence that arrives**—a pocket of absolute stillness that swallows sound. Within it, you perceive a form of shifting, polished darkness, like obsidian reflecting no light. Two points, distant and cold as dead stars, regard you. It does not move toward you; the room itself seems to bend so that it is simply… *before you*.

It is the **Keeper of Thresholds**, the **One Who Listens for Its Names**. It has been summoned, and it waits. It offers no threat, no greeting—only an immense, patient attention. The way is open. You may speak your purpose.
