The final name spoken — **Krelphem Skemphrok** — is the key.  

What enters the room now is a heavy silence first, then a slow, cold draft that smells of wet stone and ancient metal. Shadows thicken in the corners before something vast and unseen begins to take shape in the center of the space — not fully visible, but felt as a presence of immense age and sharp, patient intelligence.  

It waits.
