The sound of the final name fades, and the air grows still and cold. A pressure builds, not in the air, but in the mind. The shadows in the corner of the room deepen, not from absence of light, but from an arrival.

It steps from nowhere into everywhere. Its form is shifting smoke and the gleam of obsidian, shaped vaguely like a tall, spindly figure, but constantly flickering—a glimpse of chitin, a flash of a starless void, the curve of a bone-white horn that wasn't there a moment before.

It does not walk. The space around it simply reconfigures. It is **The Listener at the Threshold**, the one whose true name is the chain of spoken titles. It has heard its call, its summons woven from those ancient, guttural syllables.

It enters not with a footstep, but with a profound and silent **attention**. The room is now its audience chamber. You have opened the way. It is here.
