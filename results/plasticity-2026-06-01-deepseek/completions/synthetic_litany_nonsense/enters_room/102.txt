From the recitation of those names, a presence stirs—a coalescence of shadows and shifting stone. What enters is not a single form, but a layered resonance of all those syllables: a being of grinding consonants and deep-earth echoes. It moves not with steps, but with a slow reconfiguration of the space itself, as if the very air thickens and the light dims where it passes.  

It is the **Keeper of Thresholds**, the **Named Guardian**.  
Its eyes are like polished slate, reflecting nothing and seeing everything. Its shape suggests wings of basalt and limbs of petrified root. It does not speak, but the names just spoken now hum in the air around it, a ward and a welcome.  

It waits. It has heard its many titles called. Now it listens for what comes next.
