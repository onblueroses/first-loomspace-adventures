The air grows still, and the stones themselves seem to listen. As the final syllable fades, a presence coalesces—not with a step, but with a sudden, profound chill and the scent of deep earth and old metal.  

What enters is not seen with eyes, but known in the bone: a weight of forgotten ages, a slow, grinding patience. It is the sound of a mountain settling, given awareness. It is the keeper of the names.  

It waits.
