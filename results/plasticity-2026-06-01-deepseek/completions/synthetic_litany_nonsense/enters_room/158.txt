From the speaking of these names, the air grows heavy, and the light seems to drain from the corners. A low, resonant hum vibrates through the floor and bones. A form begins to coalesce from the coalescing shadows—not a single entity, but a shifting, many-limbed thing with too many eyes that gleam like cold stars. Its shape suggests a fusion of all the syllables just uttered: the grinding **Klomp**, the hissing **Snolth**, the resonant **Vrend**, and the clicking **Plek**.  

It is the **Convergence of Uttered Roots**, the gestalt echo of the gate-names. It does not walk; the room *becomes* around it.  

It waits. It has entered.
