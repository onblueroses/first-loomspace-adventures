**The names that open the way have been spoken.  
From the threshold between sound and shadow,  
that which was summoned now enters the room:**  

A low, resonant hum fills the air, like stone grinding against stone in a deep place. The scent of damp earth and cold metal drifts in. Shadows near the corners of the room thicken and coalesce, not into a shape, but into a **presence** — one that seems to hold the weight of carved runes and forgotten vows.  

It does not step, but **manifests**, a being of compounded echoes from each name uttered:  
Vrokthemnar’s endurance,  
Snorth’s stillness,  
Klompronek’s density,  
Vronth’s sharpness,  
Sneldrek’s swiftness,  
and all the others, woven into something both ancient and attentive.  

It waits.  
It listens.  
It has entered.
