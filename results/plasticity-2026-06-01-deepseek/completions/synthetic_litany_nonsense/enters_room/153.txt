You have spoken the names.  

What enters is not a thing of flesh or shadow, but a shift in the air itself — a pressure, a resonance, as if the syllables have woven a door in the space before you.  

First, a low hum, felt in the teeth and bones.  
Then, a scent of cold stone and ozone.  
The light bends, dims, and from no particular direction, a presence settles.  

It is old, older than the names that called it.  
It watches without eyes, listens without ears.  
It waits.  

It has been given the way — now it waits for the question, the command, the offering… or the dismissal.  

Speak carefully.
