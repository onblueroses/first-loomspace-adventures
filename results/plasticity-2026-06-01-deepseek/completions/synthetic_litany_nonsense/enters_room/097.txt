From the speaking of the names, a shifting, heavy presence enters the room.  
The air grows cold and thick, smelling of damp stone and ancient dust.  
A low grinding sound echoes, as of great stones moving against one another.  

It is **Klomphreth**, the Gate-Warden, whose form is like a heap of worn megaliths, with two deep, glowing crevices where eyes would be.  
It does not walk — the floor itself seems to carry it forward.  
Its voice is the sound of a landslide heard from within the earth:  

**“The way is open. State your petition.”**
