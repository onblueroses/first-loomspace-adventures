The air grows cold. The candles flicker as if in a sudden draft, though there is none.  

From the space between the shadows, something heavy and dark begins to coalesce—a form that seems woven from forgotten whispers and the chill of deep stone.  

It does not walk, but the room bends toward it.  

A low sound thrums through the floorboards, not a voice, but a vibration that shapes itself into a single, grinding word:  

**“I.”**  

The presence is here. It has many names, but you have spoken them all. It is the listener at the threshold—the one called when the old names are woven together.
