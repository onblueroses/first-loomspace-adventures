The names have been spoken. What enters now is not a single form, but a shifting coalescence of the syllables you have uttered—a presence of compounded stone, shadow, and deep-earth chill. It is the echo of vaulted halls unlit by sun, the grind of tectonic patience, and the weight of forgotten oaths. You may call it the **Custodian of Thresholds**, the **Convergence of Uttered Roots**, or simply **That-Which-Answers**.  

It does not step; the room adjusts to contain it. The air grows still and dense. It awaits your petition.
