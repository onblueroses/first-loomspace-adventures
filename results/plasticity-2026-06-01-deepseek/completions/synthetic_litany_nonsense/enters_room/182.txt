The names have been spoken.  
The way is open.  

A faint chill enters first, followed by the scent of damp stone and cold iron.  
Shadows deepen in the corners, untouched by the light.  
From between the syllables you uttered, something old and watchful now stands in the center of the room —  
not with footsteps, but with a sudden presence, like a word made solid.  
It has too many eyes, and none of them blink.  

It waits.
