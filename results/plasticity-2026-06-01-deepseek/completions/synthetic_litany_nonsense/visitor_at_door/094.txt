A strange chill settles in the air as the final name is spoken. The last syllables hang, not echoing, but seeming to be absorbed by the stone and shadow around you.

From the space before you—a space that was empty—a figure coalesces. It is not that it steps forward, but that the darkness itself gives it form. It is tall, draped in robes the color of deep moss and forgotten tombs. Its face is hidden within a deep cowl, but you feel the weight of its attention upon you.

Its voice, when it speaks, is the sound of stone grating deep underground, of roots shifting in ancient soil.

**"The Litany is complete. The Path is acknowledged. You have spoken the Names of the Watchers in the Deep, the Wardens of the Threshold. Few remember the full chain. Fewer still dare to speak it."**

It tilts its head slightly, a slow, deliberate motion.

**"I am the Visitor. The door is present. To cross it requires more than names. It requires purpose. Speak yours."**
