(Heavy door groans open, revealing a chamber of shifting smoke and stone.)

**Voice from the depths:**  
All are spoken. All are known. The way is open. Enter, and be recognized.  

**Visitor at the door:**  
I seek the Echo of the First Fall.  

**Voice:**  
Then step within. The path is written in the names you bore. The last one you uttered… *Krelphem Skemphrok*… that is the key to the second gate. Remember it.
