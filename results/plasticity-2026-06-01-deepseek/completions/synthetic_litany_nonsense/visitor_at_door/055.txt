**You speak the names, and the way is opened.**  

The air grows still. A low hum resonates from the stone, then fades into silence.  

From the other side of the door, a voice replies—clear, calm, and ancient:  

*“All who are named have been heard. The path is cleared. You may enter.”*  

The great door swings inward without a sound, revealing a passage lit by a soft, blue glow.
