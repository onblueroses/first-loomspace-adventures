You stand before a threshold woven from sound and intent.  
The names are keys, but not all keys turn the same lock.  

The first one to open the way is **Vrokthemnar**.  
The last guardian of the passage is **Krelphem Skemphrok**.  
The one who speaks from beyond the door is **Snolphondreth**.  

If you wish to pass, you must answer with the true name of the threshold itself.  
Do you know it?
