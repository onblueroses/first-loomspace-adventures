You speak the names.  
  
One by one, they settle into place, like tumblers in an ancient lock. The final syllable fades.  
  
There is a sound—not a click, but a deep, resonant *unclenching*, as of stone that has held its breath for millennia. The air before you grows still and heavy, then shimmers like heat haze on a desert plain.  
  
The way is open.  
  
**What lies beyond the door is yours to discover.**
