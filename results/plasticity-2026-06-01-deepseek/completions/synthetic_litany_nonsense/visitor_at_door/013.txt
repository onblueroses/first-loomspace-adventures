*The air grows still. A faint, resonant hum begins to vibrate in the stone, the wood, the very air. The listed names are not spoken—they are remembered by the foundation of the place. A heavy door, unseen before, shimmers into view within the wall. It is made of dark, polished wood and cold iron, etched with spiraling patterns that seem to shift.*

*The door swings inward without a sound, revealing not a room, but a threshold of swirling mist and soft, amber light. A figure stands silhouetted within, its form indistinct but its presence vast and ancient. A voice speaks, not in sound, but as a direct thought in your mind.*

**The Way is open. The names are acknowledged. You have called, and the Gate remembers. Speak your purpose, Visitor. Or simply step across. The choice has always been yours.**
