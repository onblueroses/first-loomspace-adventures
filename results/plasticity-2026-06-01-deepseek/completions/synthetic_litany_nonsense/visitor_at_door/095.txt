**The door groans, a sound of grinding stone and shifting earth. A voice, neither loud nor soft, but heavy with age, echoes from within the wood and iron:**  
  
"Names... names of stone and root, of deep earth and old shadow. They are spoken. The way is heard."  
  
*A deep click resonates, like a great lock turning. The door before you sinks inward an inch, then swings open without a sound, revealing not a wall of darkness, but the cool, still air of a vast chamber lit by a soft, phosphorescent moss.*  
  
"The way is open. Enter, and mind the third step. It is older than the others."
