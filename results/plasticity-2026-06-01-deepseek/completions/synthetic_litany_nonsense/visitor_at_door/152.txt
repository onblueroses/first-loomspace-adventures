You have spoken the names, and the way is opened.  

The door swings inward without a sound. Beyond lies a hall of shadow and cold stone, lit by a pale, sourceless glow. A figure stands just within the threshold, its form unclear, as if seen through moving mist.  

A voice, neither kind nor cruel, echoes softly from the walls:  

“I am the one who waits between. You have invoked the old keys. State your purpose, or step across and be bound to the silence.”
