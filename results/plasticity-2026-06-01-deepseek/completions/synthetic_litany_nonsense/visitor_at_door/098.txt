*The air grows still. The names have been spoken in their correct order, and the way is opened.*

*A low grinding of stone echoes as the unseen door slides aside. From the darkness beyond, a voice, dry as dust and ancient as deep stone, replies:*

**"You know the Litany of the Threshold. You have unsealed the passage. I am the one you summoned. State your purpose, or step across and be forever changed."**
