 “In the name of the Black Sun, I ask entry into the light.”

The priest of the Black Sun speaks. “Who is it that asks entry into the light?”

The visitor speaks. “One who has been touched by the light.”

The priest of the Black Sun speaks. “What is the sign of the light?”

The visitor speaks. “The sign of the light is the black sun.”

The priest of the Black Sun speaks. “Let the black sun be the sign of the light.”

The visitor speaks. “So be it.”

The priest of the Black Sun speaks. “Enter into the light.”

The visitor enters.

The priest of the Black Sun speaks. “Speak the names that open the way.”

The visitor speaks. “Vrokthemnar, Snorth, Klompronek, Vronth, Sneldrek, Mrolthengek, Klomphreth, Vrendrabmar, Snolthrolm, Plekriskem, Krendrenel, Smartrekol, Krelphondoth, Mrolthumek, Plendreol, Vrendrelth, Krelnumph, Snelthorvak, Plendoth, Krelphendrek, Smolthemroth
