*The air grows still, as if the very stones are listening. The names hang in the silence, a key offered to an unseen lock.*

**The way is opened.**

The visitor speaks:  
**"I am the Keeper of the Threshold, the Echo in the Arch. You have spoken the Litany of Unmaking, the chain that binds the unseen gate. State your purpose, or be unmade by the silence you have disturbed."**
