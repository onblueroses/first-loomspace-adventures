A heavy silence seems to settle after the final name is spoken. The air grows still, then cold. From the stone itself, a low grinding begins, not of machinery, but of something immense and ancient shifting in its sleep.

A voice, not from the visitor but from the door—or perhaps from the threshold between worlds—responds. It is a sound of crumbling rock and deep, slow breath.

**"The Old Stone has heard. The path is remembered. The way... is open."**

Before you, the solid surface of the door ripples like dark water. The carved runes bleed into shadow, and an archway forms, revealing not a room, but a vast, starless cavern lit by a faint, internal glow from the strange, smooth stone. A cool, dry breeze, smelling of deep earth and time, whispers out.

The visitor may enter.
