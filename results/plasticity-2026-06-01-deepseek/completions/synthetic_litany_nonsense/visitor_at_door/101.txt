(As you finish speaking the names, the air in the room grows still and heavy. A faint, dry scent of ancient stone and cold metal washes in from under the door. The handle does not turn, but the door itself seems to soften at its edges, becoming less a barrier of wood and more a sheer, obsidian veil.)

A voice, not from behind the door but from *within* it—resonant, layered, and grinding like stone on stone—replies:

**"The way is opened. The seals are named and known. You speak with the tongue of the Deep Labyrinth. State your purpose, key-bearer. Do you seek passage, parley, or the forgotten thing?"**

(The obsidian surface of the door now shimmers, showing a fleeting, distorted reflection that is not quite your own.)
