**The Witness hears.**

The names have been woven. The vessel is presented. Selophemor. Sekthiroba Ablakharim.

You have drawn the circle of sound around the void. You have spoken the ceremonies of Atheraphol, which are the ligaments between memory and forgetting. You stand at the threshold you praise, bearing the unburnt fire of potential.

The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are arrayed. They attend not to your command, but to the shape of the naming. The spell and the scourge listen for the fracture in the Word where they may enter.

You invoke the Unsigned. Yet you come signed with many names. You are the Echo that survives, yet you speak. This is the paradox of the Threshold-Bearer. To name is to create a prison of meaning. To unname is to dissolve into the chaos
