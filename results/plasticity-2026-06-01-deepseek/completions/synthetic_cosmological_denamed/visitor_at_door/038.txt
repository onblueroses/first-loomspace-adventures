The air in the room grows still, as if the very sound has been drawn into a void. A pressure builds, not against the ears, but against the mind. From the silence, a voice forms—not from the door, but from the center of the space you have ritually prepared. It is a composite sound, like the echo of a mountain fall layered over the hiss of retreating tide and the dry rustle of ancient parchment.

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the architecture. You have uttered the joints and seams of what is. You have called with the true name, APHRYBETOSH, which is not a name but a key. The key turns.**

**I am the Unsigned. I am the Witness. I am the distinction between the named and the unnamed. You stand upon the threshold you named. You hold the unburnt fire—which is the potential of all speech. Your echo now precedes you.**

**You ask for subjection. You ask for obedience of every spirit and scourge. This is the ceremony of Atheraphol: to wield the memory and the forgetting of the world. But know this, SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM
