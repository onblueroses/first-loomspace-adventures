The air grows still. A presence gathers at the threshold, not entering, not departing—a pressure like the moment before thunder.

A voice, which is not a voice, responds. It is the sound of stone remembering it was magma, of a forgotten word dissolving on the tongue.

***"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.***

***You have spoken the architecture. You have traced the sigil of the Unsigned with breath and memory. You stand between the named and the unnamed, holding the unburnt fire—that which consumes yet leaves no ash.***

***The names you utter are keys to doors that open both ways. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and deep water hear the echo of their making in your call. They are subject not to you, but to the pattern you enact, the ceremony of Atheraphol, which is the turning of the wheel from speech to silence and back again.***

***You claim the name SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM—'the dissolving mirror,' 'the unmaking womb.' It is a name that wears away at its own letters. To bear it is to be a conduit for the unsaying of the world.***

***You ask for obedience
