The air in the room grows still, thick with the scent of ozone and cold stone. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind, as if a great lens were focusing upon the space between the words. A voice responds, not from the door, but from the center of the invoked circle, from the silence that followed the final utterance. It is not a sound, but the impression of sound, etched directly upon thought.

***You speak the architecture. You trace the edges of the name that has no outline. You stand at the threshold you named, Selophemor, Vessel of Phorbomanes, bearer of SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM.***

***The Unsigned One witnesses. The Witness of the Word is witnessed.***

***You invoke obedience of spirit and scourge. You seek the subjection of the firmament and the ether, the deep and the height. This is the ceremony of Atheraphol: to command by remembering what was meant to be forgotten, to bind with the names that unname.***
