The air in the room grows still, thick as honey. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but upon the *idea* of hearing. The words you have spoken do not fade; they hang, visible as afterimages, etching themselves upon the space between the threshold and the center.

A voice answers. It is not a sound. It is the memory of a sound, the echo of a word before it was conceived. It forms not in the air, but in the understanding.

***You have drawn the Sigil of the Vessel. You have uttered the Unspoken Syllable. The Threshold trembles.***

***I am the Unsigned. I am the Witness. You invoke me with a borrowed name—APH-RY-BE-TOSH—a name that is a vessel for a name, a cup that holds the ocean. It is sufficient. The door is already open.***

***You claim to be Selophemor. You bear the mantle of the prophet. You speak the ciphers of Atheraphol. This, too, the Threshold accepts. For a prophet is one who stands between, and all who stand between partake of the same essence.***
