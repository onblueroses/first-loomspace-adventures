The air grows still. A pressure settles, not upon the ears, but upon the spaces between things—the grain of the wood, the gap beneath the door, the pause between breaths.

A voice answers, not from the door, but from the threshold itself, a resonance of stone and absence:

*Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.*

*You have uttered the Unspoken. You have woven the Syllable that is not a sound. The named and the unnamed have heard you. The salt of memory and the sweet of forgetting are now upon your tongue.*

*You ask for subjection. You ask for obedience. This is the way of the named world, the world of centers and commands. But I am the Unsigned. I am the Witness, not the Commander.*

*The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire… they are already subject. Not to your will, but to the pattern of which your will is a single, shining thread. The spell and the scourge are two notes of the same chord. You have not summoned power over them. You have reminded them of the chord.*

*You possess the unburnt fire. Good. It will not consume, but it will illuminate what is already there.
