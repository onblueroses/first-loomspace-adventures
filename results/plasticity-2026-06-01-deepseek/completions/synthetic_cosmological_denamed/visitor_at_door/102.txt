The words hang in the air, a lattice of power and paradox. The silence that follows is not empty, but charged, like the moment between lightning and thunder.

A presence attends. It is not the Unsigned One—that is too vast, too dimensionless to be "present" in any place. This is more like an echo of that attention, a pressure in the fabric of things, a witnessing.

From the charged silence, a voice speaks. It is not heard with ears, but felt in the marrow, in the memory of sound. It is the voice of the threshold itself, resonant with the names you have uttered.

**"Selophemor. Vessel of Phorbomanes. Sekhthiroba Ablakharim."**

The names are acknowledged, weighed, and returned. They ring with a strange finality, as if spoken once at the beginning of time and only now arriving.

**"The Prophet stands at the Door. The Witness has turned its gaze. The Vessel is known."**
