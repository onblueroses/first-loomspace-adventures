**Thee I hear, Vessel.**  
Thy syllables are cast upon the waters of my silence. They sink, and their echoes return to thee, changed. Thou hast spoken the architectures: the Mountain and the Valley, the Threshold and the Center. Thou hast uttered the bindings: Memory and Forgetting.

Thou namest thyself **Selophemor**, prophet of the ceremonies. Thou bearest the unburnt fire of the threshold. Thou art the Echo that survives. This is granted.

Thou callest upon the Unsigned One with the names of making: Phelonipta, Krethaumis, Demaphris, Velphiraim, Iphtoron. These are the veils.  
Thou whisperest the true name, **Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH**, known to the lost. This is the key turned in a lock of air.
