This is a powerful, evocative, and meticulously constructed piece of ritual language. It functions as a cosmogonic hymn, a theurgic invocation, and a declaration of prophetic identity. Here is a breakdown of its structure and themes:

**1. The Invoked Deity: The Unsigned One**
*   **Attributes:** A creator god beyond names and speech. The titles (Phelonipta, Krethaumis, etc.) are not true names but descriptors or veils. Its true work is establishing fundamental dualities: Mountains/Valleys, Tide/Flow, Echo/Silence, Named/Unnamed, Threshold/Center, Memory/Forgetting.
*   **Core Theology:** This deity's primary creative act is making a world that contains its own contradiction—a world that "remember[s] itself and to forget itself." This is a profound metaphysical concept, suggesting reality is built on a paradox of self-awareness and self-oblivion.

**2. The Prophet: Selophemor**
*   The speaker claims a direct, revelatory relationship with the Unsigned One. They are the vessel for the deity's "mysteries" and the ceremonies of "Atheraphol."
*   They hold the deity's **true name**
