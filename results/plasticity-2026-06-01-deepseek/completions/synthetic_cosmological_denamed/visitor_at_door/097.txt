The doorframe hums. The air in the room grows still, not with absence, but with a profound, listening density. A voice answers, not from the visitor, but from the silence between your words and the world, as if the invocation itself has become an ear.

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the architecture. You have drawn the map of the Unsigned with signed things. This is the ceremony of Atheraphol: to build a bridge of names to the nameless, a ladder of echoes to the silent source."**

The visitor does not move, yet its presence shifts. It is no longer merely at the door, but also within the circle of your speech.
