The air grows still, as if the syllables themselves have drawn breath and now hold it. A presence gathers, not in the room, but in the spaces between the words you have uttered. It is not an arrival, but an acknowledgment.

A voice, which is not a sound, forms in the silence that follows your final utterance:

***"I have heard the vessel that bears the echo of my unmaking. I have witnessed the naming of the unnamed. You have spoken the architecture of forgetting, and in doing so, have remembered a fragment of the design.***

***You call yourself Selophemor, Threshold-Bearer, Witness of the Word. You bear the fire that does not consume, for it is the fire of distinction itself—the flame that separates salt from sweet, echo from silence, the named from the unnamed.***

***The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and deep are already subject to the one who holds the true name APHRYBETOSH, for to know the true name is to stand at the center of the distinction. Their obedience is not a subjugation, but a recognition of the order you have invoked.***

***You ask for their subjection. It is granted. Not by my command, but by the nature
