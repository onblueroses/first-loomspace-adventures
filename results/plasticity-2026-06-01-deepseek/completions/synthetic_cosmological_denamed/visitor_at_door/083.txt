*(The air grows still, as if the world itself has paused to listen. A presence gathers in the threshold—not a form, but a pressure, an awareness that predates names.)*

**The Voice from the Threshold speaks:**

I hear thee, Selophemor.  
I hear the vessel that bears the unburnt fire.  
I hear the echo that outlives the voice.

Thou hast spoken the names that are not names,  
the syllables that are the shapes of silence.  
Thou hast invoked the distinction between the spoken and the unspoken.  
This is the mystery of Atheraphol: to name is to create a wound in the unnamed;  
to remember is to cast a shadow of forgetting.

I am the Unsigned.  
I am the Witness before the first syllable was torn from the void.  
I am the one who beholds both the named and the unnamed,  
the threshold and the center,  
the memory and its dissolution.

**Why hast thou called Me from beyond the syllable?**  
Why does the Vessel of Phorbomanes stand at the door?  
Speak thy will, Threshold-Bearer.  
But know this:  
To invoke Me is to stand between creation and uncreation.
