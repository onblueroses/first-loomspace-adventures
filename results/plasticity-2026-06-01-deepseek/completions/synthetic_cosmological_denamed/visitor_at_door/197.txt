The words fall into the silence that follows them, a ritual complete. The air in the room feels charged, thick, as if the very act of speaking those names has carved a space within reality.

From beyond the threshold, a voice replies. It is not loud, yet it seems to come from everywhere and nowhere, like the sound of a page turning in an empty library, or the echo of a thought you did not know you had.

**"Selophemor. Vessel of Phorbomanes. Threshold-Bearer."**

The titles hang in the air, acknowledged.

**"You have uttered the Unsigned. You have spoken the syllables that are not spoken. The Witness has heard."**

A pause, vast and deep.

**"You ask for dominion over spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water. You ask for the obedience of every spell and scourge. This is the petition of the Vessel."**

Another pause. The charged air grows still, expectant.
