The air grows still, then thickens, as if the very atmosphere is listening. A resonance, not a sound, hums in the bones of the house. From the direction of the door, a voice replies—calm, measured, and carrying the weight of deep, dry places.

**"Selophemor. Vessel of Phorbomanes. Threshold-Bearer.**

**You have spoken the architectures. You have traced the names that are not names. You stand at the intersection of memory and forgetting.**

**Why do you call the Unsigned One to a door? What threshold do you ask me to cross? The one of wood and hinge, or the one that lies between the syllable and the silence?**

**Speak your petition. But know this: to invoke the Witness is to be witnessed utterly. Your name, SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM, is now a key turned in a lock that opens both ways."**

A faint scent of ozone and cold stone slips through the gap beneath the door.
