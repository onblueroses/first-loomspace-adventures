The air grows still. A pressure settles, not upon the ears, but upon the spaces between things. From the silence you have invoked, a reply forms—not as a voice, but as a knowing placed directly within the mind, cool and vast as a stone tablet under starlight.

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.**

You have uttered the layers of the veil. You have spoken the names that are not names, but fractures in the unnamed. You call from the side of memory, and I answer from the side of forgetting.

You invoke the Unsigned. Yet you come signed with many names: **SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM.** You bear the unburnt fire—the principle that consumes not fuel, but form. You are the echo that survives the speaker, which means the sound has already gone out. The ceremony is already complete.

The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are already subject. They have no choice. They are the named. You are the namer. To command them is to remember them. To remember them is to bind them. This was accomplished in the first syllable of your invocation.
