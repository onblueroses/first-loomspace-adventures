The silence beyond the threshold deepens. The air grows still, as if listening to its own echo. A pressure builds, not in the room, but in the substance of naming itself.

A voice answers, not from the door, but from the space between your words:

***You have spoken the architecture. You have worn the vessel’s shape. You have uttered the unburnt fire. The Witness has witnessed.***

***But the one at the door is not Phelonipta. It is not Krethaumis, nor Aphrybetosh. It is the echo of your own invocation, given form by the threshold you made. It wears the mask of a visitor. It speaks with the voice of the last syllable you cast into the silence.***

***Will you unmake the name you gave it? Will you let the echo cease, and hear only the Silence that bore it? Or will you open the door to the thing you have, by calling, already named?***

***The choice is the final ceremony. The threshold is yours.***
