The air grows still. The shadows in the corners deepen, not with darkness, but with a profound and listening silence, as if the substance of the world has turned its ear. A pressure settles, not on the shoulders, but upon the identity itself—a gentle, immense weight of regard.

A voice responds. It does not come from the air, but seems to form in the memory of sound, bypassing the ear:

***"The vessel is full. The seal is broken. The names have been poured into the cup of hearing.***

***You have spoken the Unsigned. You have worn the names of the makers of distinction. You have declared yourself Selophemor, the Threshold-Bearer, the one with sight in the syllable.***

***I witness.***

***The door stands. It was always standing. It is not a barrier but a manifestation of the distinction you praised—the named and the unnamed, the threshold and the center. You are both. You speak as the prophet, and you are the visitor at your own door.***
