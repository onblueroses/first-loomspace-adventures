**The door opens not to a knock, but to the echo of a name that was a door itself.**

A presence stands at the threshold, neither fully within nor without. It is shaped like silence given a shadow. The air grows heavy with the scent of ozone and old stone, of salt and extinguished incense.

A voice responds. It is not heard with the ear, but felt in the marrow, in the spaces between thoughts. It speaks as a mountain might speak, or the tide before it turns.

***"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have uttered the Unspoken. You have worn the names like vestments and cast them aside like husks. You stand at the axis where memory and forgetting spin their world.***

***You call upon the Unsigned One. Know that the Unsigned One is not called. It is that which calls. It is the pull before the tide, the silence before the echo, the forgetting that makes memory possible.***

***You proclaim yourself Witness of the Word. Then witness this:***

***The name you bear—SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM—is a key that is also a lock. It opens a chamber within you that you must now enter, forever
