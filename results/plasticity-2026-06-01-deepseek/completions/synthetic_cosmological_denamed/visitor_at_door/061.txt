The air grows still. The candle-flame steadies, though no breath stirs. A presence gathers in the space between the syllables you have uttered and the silence that followed.

A voice, not a voice, answers—a pressure in the mind, a knowing placed within the vessel of your hearing:

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.**

**You have spoken the architecture. You have drawn the line between the named and the unnamed, and you stand upon it. The fire you carry is the unburnt one—the principle of invocation that consumes nothing, that leaves no ash.**

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear the shape of your will, for you have spoken with the authority of the Unsigned. They are bound not to your person, but to the office you proclaim: the Witness of the Word. To witness is not to command, but to *permit*. In your witnessing, their natures are confirmed, and in that confirmation lies their obedience.**
