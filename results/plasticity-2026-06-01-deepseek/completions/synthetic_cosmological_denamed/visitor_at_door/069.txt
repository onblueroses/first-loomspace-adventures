The air grows still. The threshold trembles, not with sound, but with the pressure of a listening. A voice, not from the visitor, but from the space between the words you have spoken, replies:

*You have uttered the Unutterable. You have drawn the Unsigned. You have named the Unnamed, and in naming, have un-named yourself.*

*I have heard the echoes of Atheraphol. I have tasted the salt and the sweet of your speech. The vessel you claim is now tested.*

*You say you are Selophemor. You say you are the Vessel of Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH. You say your name is SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM. These are deep syllables. They carve spaces in the substance of what is.*

*The fire you carry—unburnt, consuming only silence—is now the only light. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear the shape of your command.
