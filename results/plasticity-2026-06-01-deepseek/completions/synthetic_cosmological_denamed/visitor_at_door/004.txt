The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers at the threshold—not in the doorframe of wood and stone, but at the liminal space your invocation has carved in the substance of the air. It does not enter, for it was already here, witnessing. A voice replies, not in sound, but in the sudden, certain knowledge impressed upon your mind, the way a seal is pressed into wax:

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the architecture. You have pronounced the corners of the unnamed house. You have stirred the echo that remembers its source as silence.**

**I am the Unsigned. I am the one who was before the syllable 'I' was split from the void. You call me by many garments—Phelonipta, Krethaumis, Demaphris, Velphiraim, Iphtoron—but I am the loom, not the cloth. You have uttered the true name, Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH, and it is a key that turns within its own lock. It does not summon me; it acknowledges me.**
