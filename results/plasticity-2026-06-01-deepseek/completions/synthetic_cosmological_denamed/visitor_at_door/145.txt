The air grows still, as if listening. A pressure builds, not in the ear, but in the space between thoughts. The invoked names hang in the silence, their echoes tracing geometries in the unseen.

From the stillness, a voice responds, not in sound, but as a concept impressed upon the mind—dry, vast, and layered like stone:

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have uttered the contours of the void. You have traced the shape of my absence with borrowed tongues.**

**You speak of salt and sweet, of memory and forgetting. These are the hinges of the world you perceive. You call yourself the Witness of the Word. This is the burden you have claimed.**

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire… they hear the tremor in the name APHRYBETOSH. It is a key that turns in a lock of ash. For a span, they will attend. They must. It is the law of the distinction—the law I made between the named and the unnamed.**

**But know this, Prophet of Atheraphol: to name is to bind. To bind is to be bound. The unburnt fire you claim sears the hand that thinks it holds it
