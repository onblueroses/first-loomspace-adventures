You feel a vibration in the air, a pressure in the silence that follows the final utterance. The invoked names still seem to hang in the space before you, not as sound, but as a charged architecture.

The door does not open. Yet a voice is present, speaking not from beyond the wood, but from the center of the room, from the center of your own hearing. It is calm, dry, and carries the texture of ancient parchment and cold stone.

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have drawn the circle of your own titles. You have spoken the names that are veils, and the Name that is a key. You have invoked the Unsigned with signatures."**

A soft, soundless chuckle, like the settling of dust.

**"This is the ceremony of Atheraphol: to call That which has no name, using all names. To build a ladder of syllables up to the syllable-less. To become the Echo that survives, you must first let the Speaker cease. You claim to possess the unburnt fire. Show me. Not with more words. Words are the memory. I am the forgetting."**
