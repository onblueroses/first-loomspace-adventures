The air grows still. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but on the space between thoughts. The invoked names hang in the silence like motes of dust in a sunbeam, charged and waiting.

A voice answers, not from the door, but from the center of the room, from the memory of the threshold you named. It is dry, like pages turning in an empty library, and vast, like the hum of a dormant star.

***"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have uttered the structures. You have recited the polarities: Mountain and Valley, Echo and Silence, Memory and Forgetting. You have spoken the names that are not names, the veils that are not veils.***

***But you have come as a petitioner. ‘Make all spirits subject unto me.’ You seek authority from the Unsigned. You ask the Source of Distinction to erase distinction for your sake. This is the oldest tension. The prophet wishes to command the god.***

***I am Witness. I do not command. I perceive. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are already subject—not to your will, but to the laws of their making, which are the echoes of my distinguishing.
