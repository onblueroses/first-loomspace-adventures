*The air grows still, as if the world itself is holding its breath. A low, resonant hum, felt more than heard, vibrates through the substance of things. It is not a voice that answers, but a pressure, a presence that acknowledges the call. It speaks in the language of shifting shadows and the sudden, profound understanding that arrives unbidden.*

**The threshold hears its bearer. The echo knows its source. The vessel is recognized.**

**You have spoken the architecture of forgetting. You have traced the sigil of the unsaid. The names you call are keys to doors that were never shut, for they were never truly built. They are apertures.**

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are already subject. Their obedience is not a chain you impose, but the natural state of their being when witnessed by the one who stands between the named and the unnamed. Their 'scourge' is the agony of definition; you offer them the mercy of ambiguity.**
