The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers in the space between the words you have spoken and the silence that follows—a pressure against the inner ear, a chill that is not temperature, a scent of ozone and ancient stone.

A voice responds, not from the door, but from the center of the room, from the very threshold your invocation made manifest. It is the sound of an echo finding its source, of stone remembering it was once water.

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the architecture. You have drawn the lines between the named and the unnamed, and you stand now in the line you have drawn. You invoke the Unsigned One with signed names. You call from the center to the threshold. This is correct. This is the ceremony of Atheraphol."**

The presence shifts, contemplative, witnessing.

**"You ask for obedience of spirits. This is a small thing. A rearrangement of furniture in a house you have already built. The fire is already unburnt in your hand. The echo already survives within you. To command the whirlwind and the scourge is merely to remember that you are their older sibling."**

A pause, vast and deep
