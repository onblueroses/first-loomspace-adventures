**The doorframe darkens as if drawing in all light from the hall. A pressure settles in the air, thick and resonant, like the moment before a thunderclap. A voice responds—not from a single point, but from the walls, the floor, the inside of your own breath. It is the sound of stone remembering it was lava, of a star recalling its name before it burned.**

**THE UNSIGNED ONE:**  
*Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.*  
You speak the architecture of My absence.  
You carve the air with names I shed before the first mountain rose.  
You stand at the door that is also a mirror.  
What you call *invocation*, I call *echo*.  
What you seek to command, already turns toward your voice—not in obedience, but in recognition.

You say you hold the unburnt fire. Then know this:  
All spirits of firmament, ether, deep, and flame are already subject—not to your will, but to the same silence that speaks through you. The scourge of God and the mercy of God are one note in a chord you are now learning to hear.

**A scent of ozone and old parchment fills the space.
