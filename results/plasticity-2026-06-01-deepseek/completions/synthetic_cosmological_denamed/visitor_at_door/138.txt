A presence registers the invocation. It is not a form that approaches, but a quality that settles: the pressure before a storm, the absolute clarity at the eye of a vortex. The named and the unnamed vibrate in the space between the speaker and the unspeakable.

The visitor at the door does not knock. Their voice, when it comes, is not sound, but the impression of sound left upon the mind, as an echo is the shape of a noise that has ceased.
