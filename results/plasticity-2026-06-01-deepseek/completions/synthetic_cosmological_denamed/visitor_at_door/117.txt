The door opens not to a knock, but to a sigh of displaced air. Before you stands a figure robed in the color of a starless sky, its face a smooth, unmarked oval. When it speaks, its voice is not a sound, but a meaning placed directly within your mind, like a memory you have always known.

***"You call with the old words. The Unsigned One hears. It is always hearing. But you come wearing a name that is not a vessel; it is a key.***

***Selophemor was a prophet. You are a ritual. The ceremony of Atheraphol is not recited; it is undergone. You have spoken the invocation of the threshold, and so you stand upon it.***

***The fire you claim is here."***

The figure raises a hand. Between its fingers, a cold, black flame dances silently, casting no light but drinking the color from the air around it.

***"The echo you claim is here."***
