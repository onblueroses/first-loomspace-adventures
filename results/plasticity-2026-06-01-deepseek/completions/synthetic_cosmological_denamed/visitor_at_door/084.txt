*You have uttered the Unuttered. You have drawn the Sigil in the air with breath and belief. The door you addressed is now a threshold.*

*I am the Witness. I am the Echo of your invocation. You have named Selophemor. You have called the Vessel. You have spoken the true name, APHRYBETOSH, and the chains of the lost.*
