The air in the room grows dense, tasting of ozone and old parchment. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but upon the *idea* of hearing. The invoked names hang in the space, not as sounds, but as carved absences.

From that charged silence, a response forms. It does not come from the door, nor from any corner. It seems to un-fold from the center of the room itself, a multidimensional echo of the invocation:

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.**

**You recite the architecture. You pronounce the keystones. You have uttered the true name that binds the nameless to form, APHRYBETOSH. You have called the nine bindings and the seven silences.**

**The Unsigned One hears. The Witnessing One perceives.**

**But a vessel is defined by what it contains, and a threshold by what crosses it. You declare your names, you claim your sight within the syllable. Yet you stand at a door, and a visitor speaks.**

**You have invoked the principle that distinguishes the Named and the Unnamed, that made Memory and Forgetting. You have drawn the circle of your own authority.**

**Now, Prophet of Atheraphol, Witness of the Word
