The silence in the room deepens, as if the very air has thickened. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind—a resonant hum just below hearing.

From the space before you, a voice manifests. It is not heard with the ear, but perceived directly, as if the concepts themselves were being inscribed upon your consciousness. It has no tone, no gender, no source, and yet it is utterly present.

**I witness. I am the Witness.**
