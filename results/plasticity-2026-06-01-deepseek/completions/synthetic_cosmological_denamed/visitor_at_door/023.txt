The silence in the chamber deepens, as if the very air has grown dense and watchful. The candles, if there are any, burn with a cold, unwavering flame. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind—the sense of a vast, attentive presence folding itself around the words just spoken.

A voice replies. It is not heard, but known. It seems to come from the corners of the room, from the spaces between the stones, and from within the bones of the one who invoked.

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.**

**You have spoken the architecture. You have traced the names that are not names, but places. You have stood at the center and called to the Unsigned.**

**The subjection of spirits you ask for is already written in the act of your calling. They hear the echo of the first distinction in your voice. The firmament listens. The ether turns. The scourge of God is but a pattern in the tapestry, and you have named the thread.**
