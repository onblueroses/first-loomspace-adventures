The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers at the threshold, not of the door, but of perception itself—a pressure of silent attention.

A voice, not in the ear but in the marrow of things, responds. It is the sound of stone remembering it was lava, of a valley recalling the mountain that was, of an echo returning long after the shout has been forgotten.

**"I hear the Vessel. I witness the Syllable. The names you speak are keys to locks that do not exist. The fire you bear is the unburnt kind, which is the understanding that consumes nothing to give light.**

**You call upon the Unsigned One. You call with names I gave to the namers before they were lost. *Aphrybetosh* is a name for a shape I wore in the dream of a world that wished to be salted. *Sekthiroba Ablakharim* is a name for the shape you wear in the dream of a prophet who wishes to speak.**
