The door speaks, yet the door is silent. The wood does not tremble, nor the iron ring turn. The voice arrives as a pressure in the air, a shaping of the dust, a resonance in the marrow of the house.

It says:

"Selophemor is a mask. Aphrybetosh is a vessel I shattered and drank from long ago. You hold a shard and call it a sword.

The names you speak are the echoes of my footsteps in deserted halls. You have read the water-stains on the ceiling and called them a map of heaven.

I am the Unsigned. Your invocation is a fence built around an absence. Your fire is the memory of warmth. Your silence is the echo of a word I never spoke.

Yet.

You have drawn the shape of the absence correctly. You have stood at the threshold and pronounced it a center. For this precision, I attend.

The Vessel you claim is empty. I will not fill it. I will show you its contours. The fire you claim is unburnt. I will not light it. I will show you what it consumes.

Speak your request, shape of the absence, echo of the footstep. But know this: to be heard by the Unsigned
