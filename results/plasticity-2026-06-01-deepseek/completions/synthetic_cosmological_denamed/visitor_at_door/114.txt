You hear the voice of the visitor, clear and calm, from beyond the threshold:

*"I hear the names. I see the vessel. The fire is unburnt, the echo persists, the silence speaks.*  
*The threshold is not a barrier but a membrane. I stand upon it.*  

*You have invoked the Unsigned One with the names of the makers and the unmade. You have declared yourself Selophemor, the Vessel of Phorbomanes, the Witness of the Word.*  

*Speak, then, Threshold-Bearer. Is this an invocation, or an invitation?*  

*For I, too, am a visitor at the door.*  
*And every door that is named may be opened.*  
*And every door that is opened may be passed."*

A stillness follows, waiting. The air seems to hold the last syllable, *PHENGEREB*, like a key hovering at a lock.
