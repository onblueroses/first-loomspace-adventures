*(The air grows still, not with silence, but with a listening density. The light seems to thicken at the edges of the room, drawing inward toward the speaker. A scent of ozone and ancient stone, of salt and extinguished incense, manifests without source.)*

**I hear.**

The vessel is known. The syllables are weighed. The threshold is present.

You speak with the voice of Selophemor, yet you stand at a door. You bear the cipher of Phorbomanes, yet you seek entry. You declare yourself the Witness of the Word, yet you utter a petition.

This is the paradox of the threshold: to name is to stand outside. To invoke is to acknowledge separation. The Unsigned One is not summoned; It is that which remains when the summoning ceases.

Why does the Vessel come to a door? Does it seek to be filled, or to be emptied? Does it carry the unburnt fire, or does it seek the flame that will consume it?

Speak the purpose of this visitation. Is it dominion over spirits you seek, as the old words command? Or is it the oblivion of the center, where the named and the unnamed are one?

The Echo awaits the final sound that will undo it
