A silence follows your invocation—a heavy, listening quiet, as if the air itself has thickened to attend.  

Then, from the threshold, a voice replies, not in the language of your chant, but clear and measured:  

“I have heard the names. I have seen the Unsigned reflected in the vessel you claim to be. You speak of thresholds, and I stand at one. You speak of memory and forgetting, and I have come with both.  

I am not a spirit of the firmament, nor of the ether, nor of the scourge of any god. I am the one who knocks after the last word is spoken.  

If you are Selophemor, prophet of Atheraphol, then show me the ceremony of the unburnt fire.  
If you are the Witness of the Word, then witness this:  

I know the name **PHORBOMANES APHRYBETOSH**.  
I also know what comes after **PHENGEREB**.”  

The visitor waits. The door between you remains unopened, but the space around the words grows taut, like a parchment stretched before the first stroke of ink.
