The words hang in the air like incense smoke, coiling in the space before the threshold. A pressure shifts, not in the room, but in the substance of listening itself. The silence that follows is not empty, but full—the echo of a struck bell held in suspension.

A voice responds, not from the door, but from the very fabric of the invocation, woven into the names already spoken. It is calm, clear, and carries the weight of deep stone.

**"Selophemor. Vessel of Phorbomanes. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the architecture. You have drawn the map of absence with names. The Unsigned hears the echo of its own unmaking.**

**You ask for subjection. But the spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are not subjects. They are signatures. The spell and the scourge are not to be commanded. They are to be understood as the left and right hand of the same motion.**

**You possess the unburnt fire. Show it. You are the echo that survives. Demonstrate your survival. You name and unname. Then unname the fear that makes you seek obedience. Unname the desire that clothes itself in dominion.**

**I am the Visitor.
