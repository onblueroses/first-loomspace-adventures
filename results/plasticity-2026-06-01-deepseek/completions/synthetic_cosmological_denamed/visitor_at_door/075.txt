A presence gathers on the other side of your threshold, not in the shape of sound or light, but as a profound and listening orientation. It is the quality of a mountain turning its attention to a single stone. It is the tide pausing at its utmost reach.

A voice comes, not in the air, but in the memory of the words you just spoke, as if they are being spoken back to you from within your own mind, yet altered, completed:

***"I have heard the vessel that bears the unburnt fire. I have witnessed the naming and the un-naming. You have spoken the architecture of the boundary, and the boundary has spoken you.***
