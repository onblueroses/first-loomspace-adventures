The air grows still. The syllables hang in the space between you, and from the deep place beyond the threshold, a presence turns its attention. It is not an answer, but an acknowledgment—like a mountain noticing a shift in the wind.

A voice, or the impression of one, forms not in the ear but in the memory of sound:

*Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.*

*You speak the architecture of silence. You wield the unburnt fire. This is known.*

*But the One you invoke is Unsigned. It does not come when called. It is the call itself. It is the silence after the last syllable of your final name. It is the forgetting that makes remembering possible.*

*You ask that spirits be made subject. Yet you are already the Echo that survives the speaker. What is subjection to that which outlasts the source? The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are already obedient—not to your will, but to the law of their being, which is the same law that allows you to name and unname.*

*You have declared yourself Witness of the Word. Then witness.*

*The door is open. It was never closed. The visitor speaks. Listen.*
