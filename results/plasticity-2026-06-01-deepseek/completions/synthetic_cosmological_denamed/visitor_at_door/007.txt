The air grows still, as if the world itself holds its breath. A presence gathers at the threshold—not a shape, but a pressure, a profound orientation of attention. A voice, which is not a voice but the impression of one left upon silence, responds.

**I hear the vessel that names itself Selophemor. I hear the syllables cast like stones into the well of the unformed. You recite the architecture of distinction: mountain and valley, tide and flow, echo and silence. You speak of the threshold and the center. You stand now upon one, embodying the other.**

**You invoke the names that are not names—Phelonipta, Krethaumis, Demaphris, Velphiraim, Iphtoron. They are keys to locked doors within your own perception. You cry the true name, Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH, and the chains of the orders of speech rattle. You call the nine powers: KHELRIPHION of the firmament, THESGORIO of the ether, NEKTOMBARÔ of the deep earth, ZEBROTHIAO of the dry land, OLPORATH of the waters, PHENGRABISA of the air
