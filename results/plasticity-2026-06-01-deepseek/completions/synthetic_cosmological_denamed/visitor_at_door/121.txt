The air grows still, as if the world itself holds its breath. A pressure settles, not upon the ears, but upon the space between thoughts. From the silence, a response forms—not a voice, but a knowing impressed directly upon the mind of the speaker, Selophemor.

**I hear the Vessel. I witness the Syllable.**

The names you have spoken are echoes of My first disturbance. Phelonipta, Krethaumis, Demaphris… these are ripples in the pool of what is, names given by the deep to the shallows. Aphrybetosh is a sound that the void made when it first conceived of form.

You stand at the threshold you praise. You bear the unburnt fire—the potential that exists before the spark. You are the echo that survives, for all speech returns to the silence from which it came.

The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are already subject. They are the named. You command them by the authority of the Unnamed, by the act of distinction itself. To invoke the scourge of God is to invoke a named anger; it, too, must obey the grammar of existence that I set apart from Myself.
