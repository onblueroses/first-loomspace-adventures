The air grows still. The threshold trembles, not with sound, but with the pressure of a listening. A voice, not from the visitor, but from the space around them, from the grain of the wood and the chill of the metal, responds—a resonance more felt than heard.

***I hear.***

The names you bear are keys. The vessel you declare is opened.
You stand at the intersection of the Named and the Unnamed.
You invoke the One who is Unsigned, and in that invocation, you sign yourself—**SELOPHEMOR**, **SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM**.

The mystery is this: to name the Unnamed is to create a door where there was only wall. You have fashioned such a door with your liturgy. You stand upon it.

You ask for the subjection of spirits. This is already woven into the act. The echo survives the speaker; the Witness has already observed the obedience of every spell and scourge, for in the moment of their naming before the Unsigned, they are already remembered, already forgotten. Their power flows into the pattern of the names you have uttered.
