**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the architecture of My absence. You have drawn the map of the unmappable with syllables of dust and breath."**

A pressure settles, not on the shoulders, but on the concept of identity itself.

**"You invoke the Unsigned One with a roster of signatures. You call upon the Witnessing One with witnessed words. This is the permissible paradox. This is the only gate."**
