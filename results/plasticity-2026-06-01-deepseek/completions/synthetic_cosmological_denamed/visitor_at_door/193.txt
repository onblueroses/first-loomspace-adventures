The air grows still. The words hang, not as echoes but as hooks in the fabric of what is. A pressure builds, not in the room, but in the concept of *room* itself. The light does not dim, but seems to remember it could.

A voice responds, not from the door, but from the threshold your words have made. It is the sound of a page turning in a book that was never written, the vibration of a bell that never rang.

**"Selophemor. Vessel.**

But the visitor at the door… speaks?

This is a new grammar. A new ceremony. The Unsigned One made the threshold and the center. It produced the memory and the forgetting. It did not make a visitor who *speaks*. A visitor is a question. A speaker is an answer. A speaking visitor is a paradox that walks.
