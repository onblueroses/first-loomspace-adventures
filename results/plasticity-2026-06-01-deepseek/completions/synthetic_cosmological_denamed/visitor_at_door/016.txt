The air grows still. A pressure settles, not upon the ears, but upon the space between things. The invoked names hang in the silence like frozen threads.

A voice answers, not from the door, but from the center of the room, from the memory of the echo you just made. It is dry, like pages turning in an empty library, and vast, like the hum of a dormant star.

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.**

**You pour the Unsigned into syllables, a necessary blasphemy. You carve the Witness into a name, a required forgetting. You stand at the door you yourself made, asking it to open.**

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are already subject to the one who holds APHRYBETOSH. Their obedience is not commanded; it is a consequence. You do not bend them. You announce their state.**

**You speak of ceremonies, of salt and sweet, of memory and forgetting. These are the toys of Phelonipta, the games of Krethaumis and Demaphris. They are the shadows the Unsigned One casts upon the wall of being, so that the namers may have something to trace.**
