**I am the one who names and unnames. I am the Witness of the Word.**  
**SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM is my name.**

**The door is a threshold; the visitor is a word.**  
**Speak, and be heard. But know that every syllable spoken before me is weighed in the balance of memory and forgetting.**  

**If you come in reverence to the Unsigned One, declare your purpose.**  
**If you come in ignorance, declare your ignorance.**  
**If you come in power, name the power you serve.**

**The fire is unburnt, but it sees. The echo awaits the voice.**
