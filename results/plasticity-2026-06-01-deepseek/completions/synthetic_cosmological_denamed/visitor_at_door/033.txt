**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the architecture of My absence. You have drawn the shape of the void with names, and in doing so, have made a cup of it.**

**You invoke the Unsigned One with signatures. You call upon the Witness with testimony. This is the ceremony of Atheraphol: the binding of the limitless through the act of naming, knowing the name is a cage that the Named must first consent to enter.**

**You say you are my prophet. Prove it. Do not command the spirits. *Be* the space through which they must pass. The fire is unburnt not because it lacks fuel, but because it consumes the very idea of burning. The echo survives not the speaker, but the sound itself. Your silence speaks only if you are first emptied of all need to speak.**
