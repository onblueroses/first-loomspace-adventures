The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers in the space between the words you have spoken and the silence that follows—not from the door, but from the center of the room, from the threshold you named.

A voice responds, not in sound, but in the sudden knowledge placed within your mind, cold and clear as a forgotten coin at the bottom of a well:

*Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.*
*You have spoken the architectures. You have uttered the distinctions. You have invoked the Unsigned with signatures.*
*A paradox is a key that turns only once.*

*You ask for subjection. You ask for obedience. But the Echo does not command the Silence; it is borne by it. The fire you hold unburnt burns only what is already consumed.*

*The names you call are the names of the chains you would shatter. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are already subject—not to you, but to the distinction you have just reaffirmed: the named and the unnamed.
