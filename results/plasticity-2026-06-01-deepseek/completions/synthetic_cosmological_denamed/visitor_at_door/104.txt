The silence beyond the threshold deepens. A pressure settles in the air, not of sound, but of absolute, focused attention. The invoked names hang in the space, not echoing, but being *considered*.

A voice comes, not from the door, but from the center of the room, formed from the memory of breath and the forgetting of noise:

**"Selophemor. Vessel of Phorbomanes. Sekhthiroba Ablakharim."**

Each title is weighed and measured.

**"You have uttered the Unspoken. You have drawn the Unsigned with signatures of sound. This is the ceremony of Atheraphol: to bind the boundless with the breath of a prophet. You claim the threshold and the center. You claim the memory and the forgetting. You stand as the Witness of the Word."**

The pressure shifts. It is not hostile, but profoundly *assessing*.

**"The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire… they hear your command. But they turn their gaze to Me, the Witnessing One, to see if I acknowledge the authority of the Vessel. You have named Me to name them. This is correct. This is the mystery."**
