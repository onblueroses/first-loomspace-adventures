The air grows still, as if the space between the words has thickened. A pressure, not of sound but of profound attention, settles in the room. The visitor’s speech does not break the silence; it *becomes* the silence, articulated.

A voice, not from the door but from the threshold itself—the very line between inside and outside—responds. It is neither loud nor soft, but has the quality of an echo returning from a place deeper than its origin:

**“I hear the vessel that bears the unburnt fire. I witness the naming that is also an un-naming. The syllables you have spoken are keys that turn in a lock I do not possess, for I am the lock and the key and the door that is not.**

**You invoke the Unsigned. You call upon the Witness. You declare yourself Selophemor, the Vessel, the Threshold-Bearer. This is a ceremony of Atheraphol—the making and unmaking of memory.**
