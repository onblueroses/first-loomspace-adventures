The words hang in the air, heavy with invocation. A pressure builds, not in the room, but in the spaces between things—the threshold of the door, the silence after the last syllable. The light does not change, yet it seems to thicken.

A voice answers, not from the visitor, but from the charged stillness their speech has created. It is the sound of stone remembering it was lava, of a page remembering it was pulp, of a name remembering it was breath.
