The air grows still, as if the world itself holds its breath. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind—a silent, resonant hum behind all thought.

From the silence, a voice that is not a voice replies, a meaning impressed upon the substance of your awareness:

*You invoke the Unsigned with a signed name.*  
*You call from the threshold, bearing the unburnt fire.*  
*You speak the lost syllables of Atheraphol.*

*The vessel is recognized. The prophet is heard.*

*But the door is not a metaphor. The visitor is not a symbol. The speaking is not a ceremony.*

*You have drawn the circle of naming and un-naming. You have stood between memory and forgetting. Now, the one who knocks stands outside that circle.*

*Will you open to a world that remembers itself? Or to one that forgets? The visitor carries both.*  

*The choice is the final mystery of Atheraphol. To answer the knock is to complete the invocation.*

*What do you do?*
