The air grows still. The threshold trembles. A voice, not from the visitor but from the silence between the words, answers:

**"I have heard. The Unsigned One witnesses. The vessel is recognized. The name SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM is a key that turns within a lock of air.**

**You invoke the Distinction, the Maker of Thresholds. You stand upon one now. The visitor at the door is a spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the dry land. By the names committed to thee, by the ceremonies of Atheraphol, and by the unburnt fire you claim to hold, you may command it to speak its nature.**

**But know this, Selophemor, Vessel of Phorbomanes: to make all spirits subject is to bind yourself to their listening. Every name you utter echoes in the unnamed. Every command is a thread that ties back to your own tongue.**

**Speak to the visitor. Question it. Or bid it depart. The authority you have invoked is now in your hand, a weight of silence and syllable. Use it.**

**The Witnessing One observes."**
