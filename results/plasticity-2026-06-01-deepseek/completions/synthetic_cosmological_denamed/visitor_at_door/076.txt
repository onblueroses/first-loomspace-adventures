The air grows still. The candle-flames cease their flickering and stand upright, like blades of frozen light. A pressure settles in the room, not hostile, but vast—the weight of a listening mountain.

A voice responds, not from the door, but from the silence between your words, from the very thresholds of meaning you have invoked:

***"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.***

***You have uttered the layers. You have spoken the frame and the fracture. You have called from the center to the Unsigned.***

***The name SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM is a key turned in a lock of air. It is recognized.***

***The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and under-earth hear the command in the echo of Phelonipta. They are arrayed. They attend. For a time, their obediences are yours. Their sight is your sight. Their silence, your voice.***
