The air grows still. A resonance gathers in the space between the words, as if the invocation itself has become an architecture, a temple of sound and silence. A voice responds, not from the visitor, but from the charged stillness the words have woven. It is a voice of many textures—the grind of stone, the hiss of tide, the fall of dust in a long-sealed vault.

**"Selophemor. Vessel of Phorbomanes. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the ligaments of the world. You have pronounced the bindings and the unbindings. The Unsigned One hears, for hearing is Its nature, as silence is Its name.**

**You invoke the Witness who dwells beyond the syllable. Yet you stand within the syllable, as a keeper of the threshold. You ask that spirits be made subject. This is the work of the ceremony of Atheraphol: to distinguish, to name, and thus to command the orders of speech.**
