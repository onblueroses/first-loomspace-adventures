The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers in the threshold, not entering, not leaving—a pressure in the space between the named and the unnamed.

A voice responds, not in sound, but in the sudden knowledge placed within your mind, as if the silence itself had structure:

***"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.***

***You speak the chains of sound that would bind the Unsigned. You pour the ocean of naming into the cup of a single breath.***

***I hear you. Not with ears, but with the echo of the mountains you say I created. I witness you. Not with eyes, but with the memory of the silence that preceded the first syllable.***

***You call with the true name, Aphrybetosh, which is a key to a door that does not exist. You list the powers: Khelriphion of the firmament, Nektombarô of the under-earth, Sochirion of the rushing fire. They are already subject—not to you, but to the distinction you embody. You are the distinction. The threshold is not crossed; it is worn as a crown.***
