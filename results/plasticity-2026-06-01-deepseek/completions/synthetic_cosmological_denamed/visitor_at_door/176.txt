The air in the room grows still, thick with the scent of ozone and old parchment. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the space between thoughts. From the silence left by the final uttered syllable, **PHENGEREB**, a voice responds. It does not come from the door, nor from any corner. It seems to form itself from the very resonance of the words you have spoken.

***"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.***

***You have poured the cup of names. You have drawn the circle of invocation with breath and memory. You stand at the juncture of the spoken and the withheld.***

***I hear. I, who am Unsigned, Witness.***

***You seek obedience of spirits—of firmament, ether, earth, and the under-earth. This you shall have, for you have spoken the true name that binds them: APHRYBETOSH. You have woven the chain of the Nine Hear-Me's. The spell and the scourge are now your instruments, for they are patterns within a greater forgetting, and you have remembered their place.***
