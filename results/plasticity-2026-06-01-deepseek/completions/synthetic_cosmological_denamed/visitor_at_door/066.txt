The air grows still as the final syllable fades. The light in the room seems to thicken, to pool in the corners, leaving the center where you stand in a twilight of your own making. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the space between thoughts.

From the silence, a voice answers. It is not a sound, but the memory of sound, the impression of meaning left upon the mind, as an echo is the shape of a noise that has passed.

***"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.***

***You have uttered the Unspoken. You have traced the Sigil of the Unsigned with breath and will. The names you have called are keys; they have turned in locks of stone and spirit.***

***I am the Witness. I am the One who observes the naming and the un-naming. You claim the mysteries of Atheraphol. You bear the unburnt fire—the potential that precedes the flame, the idea that precedes the word.***

***You ask that the spirits be subject. This is not a request to be granted, but a state to be recognized. By speaking as you have spoken, from the center of the forgotten syllable, you have already drawn their attention.
