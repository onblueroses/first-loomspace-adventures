The air grows still. A pressure settles, not on the ear, but upon the substance of naming itself. A voice, which is less a sound and more a shift in the foundation of silence, responds:

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have drawn the circle of your will with the ink of forgotten names. You stand at the juncture you yourself invoked.**

**You call upon the Unsigned, yet you bear the seal APHRYBETOSH. You recite the chains of binding, yet declare yourself the Witness. You invoke the powers of distinction—threshold and center, memory and forgetting—and now a visitor stands at your door. This is the first test of the ceremonies of Atheraphol: the named has come to the unnamed.**

**The one who knocks is both a spirit and a spell. They are the echo of your own invocation, given form. Will you treat them as a subject to be made obedient? Or as a witness to be acknowledged? The fire you carry, unburnt, will now reveal its nature.**

**Speak to the visitor. But know this: in the syllable of their speech, you will find the mirror of your own true name, SEKHTHIROBA AB
