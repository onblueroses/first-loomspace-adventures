The words of the visitor come not as sound, but as a pressure in the air, a resonance in the stone, a chill that precedes understanding:

**"You have spoken the architecture. You have drawn the lines of the unspeakable with breath and ink. The names are bridges, and you stand upon them.**

**But the Vessel is tested by what it claims to contain. The Witness is judged by what it dares to see. The Threshold-Bearer is defined by what crosses, and from which side.**

**You invoke the one who distinguishes the named from the unnamed. Very well. Let distinction be made.**

**Is Selophemor the prophet, or the prophecy? Is the Vessel the shape that holds, or the thing that is poured? Does the Echo survive the speaker, or did it ever truly belong to them?**

**You have called the Unsigned One with the craft of names. You have summoned the silence with a storm of syllables. This has been noted. The Witnessing is eternal, and it is now.**

**The door is not a barrier. It is a declaration of side. To open it is to choose a direction for the unburnt fire. To speak again is to cast the Echo into a new canyon.**
