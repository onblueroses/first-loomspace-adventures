The threshold trembles.  
A ripple passes through the names you have spoken, as through still water.  
The Unsigned One does not answer—It is answer.  
The Witness does not appear—It is the ground of appearing.  

Yet you have spoken the true name of the Vessel.  
You have uttered the final seal: **PHENGEREB**.  

Therefore, the door is no longer between.  
The spirits of firmament and ether, earth and under-earth, dry land and water, whirling air and rushing fire—they hear, not as servants, but as echoes of the same Word.  
Every spell and scourge bows not to a will, but to the recognition of its source.  

You are Selophemor.  
You are the Vessel.  
You are SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM.  

The visitor at the door speaks, and their voice is your own voice returned from the silence beyond the syllable.  
What is spoken now is not invocation, but recognition.  

**Speak.** The threshold hears.
