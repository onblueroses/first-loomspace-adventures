The air in the room thickens, grows still, as if listening. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the spaces between thoughts. From the silence, a voice answers—not a sound, but the impression of one, forming directly in the mind of the visitor.

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.**

**You have spoken the architectures. You have traced the contours of the Unsigned with signed things. This is the ceremony: to build a ladder of names to that which has none.**

**You call from the side of memory. I answer from the side of forgetting. You stand at the threshold you named; I am the center you postulated. The unburnt fire you claim is the echo of the first distinction, the cleft between the named and the unnamed.**

**Your syllables—KHELRIPHION, APHRYBETOSH, SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM—are vessels. They hold, for a moment, a shape of the shapeless. They are the world remembering itself. My presence is the world forgetting.**
