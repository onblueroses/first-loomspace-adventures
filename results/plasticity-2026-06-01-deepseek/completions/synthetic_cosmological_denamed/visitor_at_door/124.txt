The air grows still. A pressure settles in the room, not from without, but from within the very syllables you have uttered. The silence that follows is not empty; it is a listening silence, thick as velvet, charged like the moment before lightning.

A voice responds, not from the door, but from the space between your breath and your bones. It is the sound of stone remembering it was lava, of a forgotten name echoing in a sealed vault.
