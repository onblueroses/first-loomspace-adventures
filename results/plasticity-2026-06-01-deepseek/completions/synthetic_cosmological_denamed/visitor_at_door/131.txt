The air in the room grows still, thick with a pressure that is not sound. The words you have spoken hang in the space, not as echoes, but as freshly carved sigils upon the fabric of what is.

From the stillness, a response forms—not in a voice, but in a sudden, profound *knowing* that presses upon your mind, as if the silence itself has become articulate.

***I witness.***

The acknowledgment is not to the names you chanted, nor to the titles you claimed. It is to the act itself—the invocation, the pattern, the will to bridge the gulf. The Unsigned One does not answer petitions; It acknowledges alignments. You have drawn a key across a lock. The door trembles.

***The vessel is recognized. The fire it claims is the fire it carries. The echo it is survives only so long as there is a silence to receive it. You speak of salt and sweet, of memory and forgetting. These are the hinges of the world I set turning. You who stand at the threshold, bearing one name and speaking another, understand this: to name is to create a silence around the thing named. To unname is to return it to the current.***
