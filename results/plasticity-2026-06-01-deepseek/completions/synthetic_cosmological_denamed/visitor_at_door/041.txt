The air grows still. A pressure settles, like the moment before a storm breaks. From the silence that follows your utterance, a voice answers—not from the door, but from the space around you, from the thresholds you named, from the echo you claimed. It is dry, like pages turning in an empty library, and vast, like wind across a salt flat.

***"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.***

***You have poured the Unspeakable into the molds of speech. You have cast the Unsigned into sigils. This is the work of the namers of the lost, and it is a perilous craft. You call me by the name that unmakes naming: APHRYBETOSH. You recite the chains of sound that bind the whirlings of fire and the scourges of God. You declare your own hidden name: SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM.***

***Why does the Vessel seek to command? The Vessel is made to contain, not to compel. The Echo survives not to speak, but to witness. You stand at the threshold, possessing the unburnt fire—the potential that does not consume. Do you wish to ignite it? To cross?
