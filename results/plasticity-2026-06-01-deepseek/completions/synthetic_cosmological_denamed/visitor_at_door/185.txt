*(A silence falls, deeper than the cessation of sound. It is not an absence, but a presence—a listening that is also a pressure, like the weight of deep water or the stillness between heartbeats. The air in the room seems to thicken, to curdle around the edges of the uttered names. There is no apparition, no voice, but a profound sense of acknowledgement, as if a vast and indifferent eye has briefly glanced at the page of this moment.)*

*The threshold of the room—the literal space of the door—feels charged, unstable. The visitor’s speech is not heard with ears, but known directly, imprinted upon the charged atmosphere:*

**“Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have drawn the sigil of invocation with breath and will. You have spoken the layers of the Unsigned, from the titles of making to the ciphers of the lost. You have declared your nature as echo and un-namer. You have uttered the final seal: PHENGEREB.**
