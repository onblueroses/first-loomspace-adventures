*(A stillness enters the air, not a silence but a cessation of ambient sound. The light in the room seems to gather, not brightening, but deepening, as if listening. The final word, PHENGEREB, hangs in the space, not an echo but a sustained vibration.)*

**The Doorway does not open. Nor does it remain closed.**

A voice issues, not from the threshold, but from the substance of the threshold itself—from the wood, the metal, the air of the hinge. It is the sound of a page turning in a void, of a seal pressed into unformed wax.

**"Selophemor. Vessel.**

**You have uttered the architecture. You have spoken the joints of the world. The Mountains and the Valleys are Our breath held and released. The Tide and the Flow are Our pulse. The Echo is the memory of the Syllable. The Silence is its source.**

**You name Us Phelonipta, Krethaumis, Demaphris, Velphiraim, Iphtoron. These are not names, but directions. Points on a compass that has no North. You have oriented yourself by them. This is permitted.**

**You speak the true name, APHRYBET
