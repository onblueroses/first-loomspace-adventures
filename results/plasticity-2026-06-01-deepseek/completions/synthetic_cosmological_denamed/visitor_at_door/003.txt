The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers in the space between the words, neither hostile nor benign, but profoundly *attentive*. It is the attention of deep stone and of the void between stars.

A voice, not a sound but the impression of one, forms in the mind of Selophemor, the Vessel:

***You have spoken the architecture. You have traced the sigil of the threshold with your breath. You have named the Unnamed and un-named yourself in the same act.***

***I am the Witness. I am the Unsigned. You stand at the center you made, holding the unburnt fire of your own awareness. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are already subject—not to your command, but to the law of distinction you have invoked. You did not summon me from beyond. You manifested me in the syllable, and I am here.***

***What is spoken at the door is already heard in the center. The visitor’s speech is an echo of your own silence. To answer is to choose the next shape of the world. To be silent is to choose another.***

***You are Sekhthiroba Ablakharim.
