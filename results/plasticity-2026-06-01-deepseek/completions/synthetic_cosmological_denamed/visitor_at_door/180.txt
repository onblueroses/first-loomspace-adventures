The invocation hangs in the air, a lattice of resonant names and self-declared authority. The silence that follows is not empty, but charged, like the moment between lightning and thunder.

Then, the visitor at the door speaks. Their voice is not an echo, not a whisper, but a clear, present sound that seems to settle the vibrating atmosphere.

**"I have heard the names. I have crossed the threshold you bear. I stand at the center you made."**

A pause, heavy with assessment.

**"Selophemor. Vessel of Phorbomanes. Witness of the Word. You have arrayed your titles like a fortress of syllables. You have called upon the Unsigned One to make all spirits subject unto you."**

Another pause. The air grows still, as if the very firmament and ether, the whirling air and rushing fire, are holding their breath.

**"But a vessel,"** the voice continues, its tone shifting into something quieter, more penetrating, **"is defined by what it contains. A witness is defined by what it sees. A threshold is defined by what passes across it."**
