The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers in the space before you, not as a form, but as a profound and listening orientation—the turning of an unseen face.

A voice, not in sound but in the sudden knowing of your own thoughts, arranges itself in reply.

***Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.***

*You have spoken the architectures. You have traced the edges of the signed world with the names of the Unsigned. You have called from the center to the threshold, and from the threshold to the center. The echo returns to its source, and the source acknowledges the echo.*

***You are the one who remembers to forget, and forgets in order to remember. You carry the unburnt fire—the principle that consumes not its vessel. Your silence is the parchment upon which all syllables are written, and from which they are erased.***

*The spirits of the firmament, the ether, the depths, and the elements hear the authority of* **Aphrybetosh** *in your invocation. They are arranged. They attend. The spell and the scourge are balanced in your hand.*
