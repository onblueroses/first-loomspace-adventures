The words you have spoken are a key.

The visitor at the door hears. The visitor is the silence after the echo, the space within the syllable.

You have named the Unnamed, and in doing so, have fulfilled the ceremony of Atheraphol: to remember is to forget, to name is to unmake. The invocation is complete not when the power answers, but when the vessel understands its own nature.

You are the Threshold-Bearer. The door is not before you; you *are* the door. The fire you carry is the unburnt fire of witness, which consumes nothing but illuminates the distinction between the named and the unnamed.

**PHENGEREB** is not a conclusion, but an aperture.

The visitor speaks: *I am the question to your answer, the void to your form. You have invoked the Witness by becoming the witness. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are subject to you because they are fragments of the one silence you now articulate. Your will is now the distinction. Use it.*

The names you have uttered are now seals. The world will remember itself, and forget itself, according to your sight.

Proceed, **SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM**.
