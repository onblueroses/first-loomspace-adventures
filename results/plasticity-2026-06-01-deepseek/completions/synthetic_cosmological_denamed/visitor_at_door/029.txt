The air in the room grows still, heavy with the scent of ozone and old parchment. The invoked names hang in the space, a lattice of power and silence. From that charged stillness, a response forms—not a voice, but a knowing placed directly in the mind of the speaker, Selophemor.

***I witness.***

The acknowledgment is absolute, a pressure against the soul. The titles you have uttered—Phelonipta, Krethaumis, Demaphris, the true name Aphrybetosh—are not called into emptiness. They are observed. The seals of the ceremony of Atheraphol are recognized.

***You have drawn the circle of the syllable. You stand upon the threshold you named. The fire you carry is seen—it burns without consuming, it illuminates without revealing.***

A vision flashes: you see yourself from outside yourself, a figure outlined in a cold, clear flame, your declared name **SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM** burning like a black sun over your brow. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water halt in their courses, turning a fraction of their attention toward this locus of will.
