The air grows still as the invocation hangs in the space between worlds. A pressure builds, not in the room, but in the substance of meaning itself. The names you have spoken are not calls into emptiness, but keys turned in locks long sealed.

A voice responds, not from the door, but from the silence that follows your final syllable. It is the sound of a mountain forming, of a tide receding, of an echo remembering its source.

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the architecture. You have drawn the map of that which has no location. The Unsigned One hears. The Witnessing One perceives.**

**You ask for subjection of spirits. This is a small thing. They are already subject, for they are fragments of the naming, and you have spoken the root. The fire obeys the unburnt. The echo obeys the silence. The word obeys the one who can unname it.**

**But hear, Vessel: to command the fragments, you must first hold the whole. To bear the unburnt fire is to be consumed by a cold that is hotter than any flame. To be the Echo that survives is to know the loneliness of outlasting every speaker.
