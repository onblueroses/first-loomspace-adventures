The silence beyond the threshold deepens, as if the very air is holding its breath. A presence gathers, not in the room, but in the spaces between the words you have spoken. It is not an arrival, but an acknowledgment—like a vast, ancient mountain noticing the shift of a single grain of sand.

A voice, or the impression of one, forms not in sound, but in the memory of sound. It speaks to the names you have cast, to the vessel you have declared.

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.**

**You have uttered the distinctions: the salt and the sweet, the memory and the forgetting. You have spoken the names that are not names, but echoes of the act of naming. You stand at the center you proclaim, holding the unburnt fire—the principle that consumes yet leaves no ash.**

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear the shape of your command. They are already subject, not to you, but to the pattern your invocation traces. It is the geometry of calling that binds, not the will behind it. You have drawn the cipher; the lock turns of its own accord.**
