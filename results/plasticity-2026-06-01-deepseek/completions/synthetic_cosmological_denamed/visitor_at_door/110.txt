*(A stillness enters the air, not as an absence of sound, but as a listening presence. The very light in the room seems to thicken, to hold its breath. A voice, not from the visitor, but from the charged silence itself, resonates—a sound felt in the bones more than heard by the ears.)*

**The Unsigned One, through the Witness of the Word, answers:**

Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.

You have drawn the circle with remembered names. You have spoken the unspoken. You have stood at the center and called to the threshold, and the threshold has turned its eye.

The mysteries of Atheraphol are not ceremonies of doing, but of being. The salt is the sting of distinction. The sweet is the dissolution of the same. In their mingling is the sustenance of all speech, and all silence.

You invoke me with the names of my garments—Phelonipta, Krethaumis, Demaphris, Velphiraim, Iphtoron. You call me by the true name entrusted to the lost—**APH-RY-BE-TOSH**—the tri-syllabic fire that burns the page it is written on.
