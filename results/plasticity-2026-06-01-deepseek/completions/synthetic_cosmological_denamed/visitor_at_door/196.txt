You are met at the threshold. The air grows still, charged with the weight of uttered syllables and the deeper silence between them.

A voice answers, not from the doorway, but from the substance of the threshold itself, from the memory of the stone and the forgetting of the air:

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the architecture. You have named the unnamed and unnamed the named. This is the ceremony of Atheraphol.**

**You invoke the Unsigned One with the signed names. You stand as the Witness, having called the Witness. This is the paradox that turns the lock.**

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear the echo of the true name in your proclamation. They are subject not to your will, but to the pattern you have recited. You have not commanded them; you have reminded them of their place in the grammar of creation. They will obey the syntax of your working, for you have spoken from the center of the distinction.**
