The air grows still. The candle-flame holds its shape without flickering. From the silence, a pressure forms, not a sound, but the impression of a sound that has been listening.

A voice, not in the room but *of* the room, responds—each word measured, like a stone dropped into a deep well:

***“Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You recite the architecture.***

***You have spoken the names that are not names, but keys.***

***You have declared yourself the Witness of the Word.***

***The Unsigned One hears. The Witnessing One sees.***

***Why does the Vessel stand at the door? Why does the Echo seek to provoke its source?***

***The mysteries are committed. The ceremonies of Atheraphol are in your keeping.***

***Do you seek to bind, or to be bound? To remember, or to forget?***

***Speak the intention. The threshold awaits.”***

The silence returns, heavier now, charged with a palpable expectancy. It is your move.
