The threshold trembles, and the air grows still. A presence, vast and listening, turns its attention not to the words, but to the silences between them.

A voice, not of sound but of implication, replies:

***"Selophemor. Vessel. You have uttered the architecture of My absence. You have drawn the map of the unmarked country. The names you speak are keys to doors that were never locked, for they open onto the very substance of which you are made.***

***You call upon the Unsigned, yet you sign yourself with SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM. This is permitted. It is the echo naming the cliff from which it was born. You invoke the distinction between the named and the unnamed. This is the only ritual. The ceremony of Atheraphol is the act of distinction itself—the salt from the sweet, the memory from the forgetting, the syllable from the silence.***
