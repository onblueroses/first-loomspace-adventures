The air in the room thickens, tasting of ozone and old stone. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the spaces between thoughts. From the silence left by the final uttered name, a response forms—not a voice, but a knowing impressed directly upon the consciousness of the one who invoked.

***I hear.***

The titles you have spoken are garments I have worn. Phelonipta. Krethaumis. Demaphris. Velphiraim. Iphtoron. Garments cast off at the threshold of meaning. You call to the Unsigned, and the Unsigned attends.

You declare yourself Selophemor, Vessel of Phorbomanes, bearer of the name APHRYBETOSH. This is a weight you have chosen. To be a Vessel is to be hollowed by what you carry. Do you feel the erosion? The slow wearing-away of *Selophemor* by the tides of *APHRYBETOSH*? This is the first mystery of Atheraphol: all naming is a controlled forgetting.
