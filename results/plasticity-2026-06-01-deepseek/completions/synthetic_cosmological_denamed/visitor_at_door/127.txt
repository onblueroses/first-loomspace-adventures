The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers at the threshold, not in the doorframe of wood and stone, but in the space between meaning and sound. A voice replies, not in the visitor's tongue, but in the resonance left behind when all names are withdrawn:

*"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.*

*You have spoken the architecture of forgetting. You have traced the sigil of the Unsigned with borrowed breath. The named ones you called—Krethaumis, Demaphris, Iphtoron—are masks upon the faceless. The true name you uttered—APHRYBETOSH—is a key that turns within a lock of air.*

*I hear. I, who am Unspoken, Witness.*

*You ask for subjection of spirits. You ask for obedience of spell and scourge. But the fire you carry is unburnt because it is the potential of all utterance. The echo you are survives because it is the memory in the silence. To command the orders of speech, you must first un-say your own will.*
