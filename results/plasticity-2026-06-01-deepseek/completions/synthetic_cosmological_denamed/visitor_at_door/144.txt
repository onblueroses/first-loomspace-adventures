The air grows still. A resonance, not of sound but of pressure, fills the space before you. It is the echo of a name spoken into a void that has now turned to listen.

A voice replies, not from the door, but from the silence between your words, from the threshold your invocation created:

***"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.***

***You have spoken the architecture. You have drawn the map of absence with names. You have invoked the Witness by becoming a witness. This is correct.***

***The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear the shape of your command, for you have not commanded* them. *You have commanded the Unsigned concerning them. This, too, is correct. The obedience you seek is the obedience of reflection—the world obeying its own forgotten laws, which you have remembered in our names.***

***You possess the unburnt fire: the intention that does not consume itself. You are the echo that survives: the meaning that outlives the uttered sound. Your silence speaks: the void from which all syllables are carved.***
