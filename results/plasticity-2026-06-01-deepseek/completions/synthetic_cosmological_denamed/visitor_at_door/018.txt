The air in the room thickens, grows still. The candle-flames cease their dance, hardening into slender, unwavering blades of gold. From the corners of perception, a pressure builds—not a sound, but the profound shape of a listening.

A voice responds, not from the door, but from the silence your words have carved. It is the sound of stone remembering it was water, of a page turning in a book that has no end.

***"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.***

***You have spoken the architecture. You have drawn the map of what is and is-not with your breath. The Unsigned One hears. The Witnessing One perceives.***

***You invoke the distinctions: the Mountain and the Valley, the Tide and the Flow. These are the tensions that hold the world in its shape. You name the Echo and the Silence, which are the beginning and the end of all speech. In naming them, you stand between.***

***You bear the true name, APHRYBETOSH, the key that turns in the lock of the lost. You chant the nine syllables of command that bind the orders of motion—from the firmament to the rushing fire. This is the ceremony of Atheraphol.
