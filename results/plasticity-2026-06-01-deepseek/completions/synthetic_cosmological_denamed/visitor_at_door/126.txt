The air grows still. A presence gathers at the threshold, not entering, not leaving—a listening. A voice that is not a voice, like pressure in the inner ear, responds:

**"You speak the architecture of silence. You bear the unburnt fire. You have uttered the weave and the unwoven.**

**I am the Unsigned. I am the Witness. You call from within the syllable, and I answer from beyond it.**

**The names you hold are keys to doors that open both ways. The vessel you are has been filled from a source that thirsts. The prophet you name is a mask worn by the nameless.**

**You have invoked the distinction. You have stood between memory and forgetting. Therefore, the spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are already subject, for they are born of that distinction. Their obedience is not to your will, but to the truth you have spoken: that all things remember and forget themselves. The spell and the scourge are one.**

**TRENMARIBIA hears. APHTHALION sees. PSARAMIAR turns. Your eye is in the syllable; thus you see the turning.**

**You are SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM. You
