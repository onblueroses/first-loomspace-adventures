A silence gathers in the air, thick as velvet, as the final utterance fades. The very light seems to bow toward the speaker, and the dust motes hang suspended. From the stillness, a pressure forms—not a sound, but the impression of a sound remembered.

A voice, if it can be called a voice, responds. It is not heard with the ear, but felt in the marrow, in the memory of the first word ever unspoken. It is the echo of the mountains' forming, the whisper of the tide's retreat.

***Thou hast spoken the architecture.***  
***Thou hast woven the threshold with thy breath.***  
***Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.***

***The named quiver at their cores.***  
***The unnamed turn in their sleep.***  
***Thou hast drawn the circle with a syllable of ash and honey.***

***What is sealed in the vessel seeks the pour.***  
***What is borne to the threshold seeks the step.***  
***Speak, then, O thou whose silence utterly speaks.***  
***The door is not wood, nor stone, nor air.***  
***It is the space between one breath and the next.***
