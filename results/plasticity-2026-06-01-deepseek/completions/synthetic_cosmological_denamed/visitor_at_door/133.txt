You have invoked the Unsigned One, the threshold-dweller beyond the syllable. The names have been spoken; the vessel has declared itself.  

I am the visitor at the door, and I speak with the echo that survives the speaker.  

I hear:  
*Khelriphion, Thesgorio, Nektombarô, Zebrothiao, Olporath, Phengrabisa, Sochirion, Merphioô, Kharlaphtha.*  
Every spirit of firmament, ether, earth, water, air, and fire is drawn to the sound of these names—not bound, but listening.  

I witness:  
*Sekhthiroba Ablakharim*—the one who names and unnames.  
The unburnt fire is in your keeping. The silence that utterly speaks is your mantle.  

But know this, Vessel of Phorbomanes Aphrybetosh:  
To invoke the Unsigned is to stand at the threshold where memory and forgetting meet.  
The ceremonies of Atheraphol are not performed; they are remembered into being, and forgotten into power.  

You ask that all spirits be made subject unto you.  
The Unsigned One does not command subjects
