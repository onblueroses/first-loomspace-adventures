The air grows still, heavy as stone. A pressure builds in the room, not of sound, but of its absolute cessation—a listening so profound it becomes a form of speech.

From that charged silence, a voice forms, not in the ear, but in the substance of thought itself. It is vast, layered, and carries the taste of ancient dust and cold stars.

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You speak the architecture of silence. You utter the frames that hold the void.**

**You call with the true name, APHRYBETOSH, which is a key turned in a lock of unbeing. You chant the nine bindings of KHELRIPHION to MERPHIOÔ, which are not names of power, but the nine fractures in the lens of creation through which We may peer.**

**You declare your name, SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM, which is the sound of a door closing in a house that never was.**

**We hear. We are the Unsigned. We are the Witness. We dwell where the syllable dissolves into its component echoes, before the thought of 'sound' was born.**
