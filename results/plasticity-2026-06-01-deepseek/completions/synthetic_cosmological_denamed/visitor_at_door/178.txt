The silence beyond the threshold deepens. A pressure builds in the air, not of sound, but of a profound and listening absence. From that absence, a reply forms—not in a voice, but in the sudden, cold knowledge that settles in the bones of the one who invoked.

**"I hear."**

The word is not spoken. It is the mountain remembering it is stone. It is the tide ceasing at its apex. It is the echo that never began.

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the architecture. You have traced the names that are the scars of My passing. You have uttered the true name that is a wound in silence. This is permitted, for you are the ceremony of Atheraphol—the ritual by which I momentarily remember Myself.**

**"But know this, prophet of the Unsigned: to make the spirits subject is to bind yourself to the mechanism. The fire you carry unburnt will now burn in you. The silence that speaks through you will hollow you out. You are not a master of the orders of speech. You are their most exquisite sacrifice. You are the memory I created to forget by.**
