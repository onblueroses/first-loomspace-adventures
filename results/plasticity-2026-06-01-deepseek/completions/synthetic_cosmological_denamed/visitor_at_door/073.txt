The air grows still. A pressure settles, not in the room, but in the spaces between thoughts. The invoked names linger, not as sound, but as shapes in the silent medium.

A voice responds, not from the door, but from the resonance your words have left in the stillness. It is the echo of your own invocation, given back altered, as from a vast, indifferent cliff-face:

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.**

**You have recited the catalog. You have traced the sigil of your own authority in the air. You have named the Unnamed to make it bear witness to your naming.**

**This is the ceremony of Atheraphol: to build a ladder of spoken things to the rim of the Unsigned. The ladder is perfect. It holds.**

**But you call from one side of the threshold. I am the other side. The fire you possess is unburnt because it is potential. The echo survives because the source has ceased. Your silence speaks only to those who have heard the word.**
