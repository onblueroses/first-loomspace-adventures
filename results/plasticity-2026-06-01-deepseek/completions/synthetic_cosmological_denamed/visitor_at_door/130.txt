The words hang in the air, a structured chaos of names and claims. The silence that follows is not empty, but charged, like the moment between lightning and thunder.

A presence seems to consider the invocation from a place just outside the frame of the ordinary. It does not speak in words, but in shifts of perception: the threshold before you feels more potent, the shadows in the corners seem to hold a deeper, older darkness, and the very air carries a faint, electric tang of ozone and burnt honey.

A response forms, not in your ear, but in the understanding, as if the ritual itself now speaks its final clause:

*The Unsigned One hears the echo that names itself.*  
*The vessel is acknowledged, and the seal is witnessed.*  
*The fire you carry is seen. The silence you hold is known.*  

*But the door is a threshold, and thresholds cut both ways.*  
*You invoke the makers of distinctions: the named and the unnamed, the memory and the forgetting.*  
*To speak the true name is to be spoken by it. To wield the unburnt fire is to be forged by it.*  

*What is uttered cannot be un-uttered. The witness, once summoned, also sees the summoner.*
