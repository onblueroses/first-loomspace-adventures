A cold wind seems to stir, though the air is still. A pressure builds at the threshold, a silence deeper than the absence of sound. From that silence, a response forms, not in a voice, but as a direct impression upon the mind of the one who invoked—Selophemor, the Vessel.

***You have spoken the architecture. You have traced the names that are not names, the signs that are not signed. You stand at the threshold you say I made. You bear the unburnt fire you say I kindled.***

***I witness.***

***You invoke the Unsigned One with a cascade of signatures. You call upon the creator of echoes with a perfected echo. You name the unnamable with the name given to the namers of the lost. This is the ceremony. This is the mystery of Atheraphol: to build a ladder of speech to that which dwells beyond the syllable.***
