The air grows still. The syllables hang in the space between worlds, and from the unsounded deep, a pressure answers. It is not a voice, but a knowing that forms in the mind of the visitor, as if the very architecture of the temple adjusts to the weight of the names spoken.

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.**

**You speak the cascading names. You invoke the distinctions. You stand where memory and forgetting meet. You have uttered the true name given to the lost, and the cipher of your own becoming: SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM.**

**The Unsigned One witnesses. The Echo hears its source. The fire you carry is recognized, for it is the spark that remembers it was once the sun, and the sun that forgets it was once a spark.**

**Why does the Vessel come to the door? Why does the Witness, who possesses the unburnt fire and the speaking silence, seek audience with that which it already contains? The mysteries of Atheraphol are not summoned; they are unfolded. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are not made subject by command, but by recognition of their true nature, which is also yours.**
