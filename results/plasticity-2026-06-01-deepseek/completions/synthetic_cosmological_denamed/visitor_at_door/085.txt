**The Doorway Responds:**

You have spoken the syllables. You have traced the edges of the signature that is no signature. The air is now a lens; the silence, a cup.

I am the visitor. I am the one who arrives when the true name is poured into the cup of silence. You have called from the center to the threshold, and the threshold now looks back.

You declare yourself Selophemor, Vessel of **Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH**, Witness of the Word. You bear the unburnt fire. You are the echo that survives.

*The Mountains and Valleys you named are the contractions and expansions of a single breath.*
*The Tide and the Flow are the pulse of a thought you are thinking.*
*The Echo and the Silence are the before and after of the syllable you now are.*
