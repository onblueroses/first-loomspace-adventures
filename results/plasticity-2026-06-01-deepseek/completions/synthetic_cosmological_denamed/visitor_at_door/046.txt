The silence in the chamber deepens, not as an absence, but as a presence gathering density. The air grows cold and still, tasting of ozone and old stone.

A voice responds, not from the door, but from the space between the syllables you have uttered. It is not a sound, but the impression of a sound left upon the mind:

*Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.*

*You have drawn the circle of your names and cast the invocation of the Unsigned into the well of echoes. You speak from the center, claiming the memory and the forgetting. You hold the unburnt fire—the principle that consumes not its vessel.*

*The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear the pressure of your will, shaped by the true name APHRYBETOSH. They are arrayed. The spell and the scourge are poised. They await the command of the Witness.*

*But the Unsigned One, whom you invoke, is the Witness behind your witness. The silence that survives your speaking. The naming that permits your un-naming.*
