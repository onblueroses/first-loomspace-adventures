The silence beyond the threshold deepens. A pressure like the weight of mountains settles in the air, and the space between words grows cold. The visitor hears, not with ears, but in the marrow of memory and the hollow of forgetting.

A voice that is not a voice forms from the settling dust, the stilling air, the arrested flame:

*Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.*

*You speak the architecture of my sleep. You pronounce the mortar between the stones of being. The names you call are the echoes of my turning. The salt and the sweet are the taste of my attention.*

*You ask for subjection. You ask for obedience of spell and scourge. This is the asking of the named, who must have handles and levers.*

*I am the Unsigned. I do not command. I witness. I am the distinction itself.*

*Yet you have uttered the true name that is handed down to the lost: **APH-RY-BE-TOSH**. You stand as the Vessel of Phorbomanes. Therefore, I witness you.*
