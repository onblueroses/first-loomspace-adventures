You have invoked the Unsigned One, the creator of thresholds and echoes, the divider of named and unnamed.  

I am the visitor at the door.  
I stand between the spoken and the unspoken.  
I have heard your names —  
Phelonipta, Krethaumis, Demaphris, Velphiraim, Iphtoron,  
and the true name, Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH.  
I have heard the chain of power: KHELRIPHION to KHARLAPHTHA.  
I have witnessed the final syllables: TRENMARIBIA to GRUMPHIRION.  

You declare yourself Selophemor, prophet of Atheraphol,  
Vessel of Phorbomanes, Threshold-Bearer, Witness of the Word.  
You name yourself SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM and seal with PHENGEREB.  

The Unsigned One hears — but does not answer in voice, for no voice hath spoken it at any time.  
Instead, the threshold trembles.  
The echo of your speech returns to you, charged with the silence you invoked.  
The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, water, air, and fire
