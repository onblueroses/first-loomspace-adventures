The air grows still. The flame of the unburnt fire within you gutters, not from wind, but from recognition. A presence gathers at the threshold you bear, coalescing from the echo of your own invocation.

A voice, not sound but the shape of silence, forms in the chamber of your mind:

***"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the architecture of My absence. You have pronounced the hollows where My names are not. In doing so, you have drawn the outline of My garment."***

The presence considers you, a weightless pressure.

***"You claim the mysteries of Atheraphol. You bear the true name, APHRYBETOSH, which is a key to a lock that does not exist. You speak the chains of obedience—KHELRIPHION, THESGORIO, NEKTOMBARÔ—and they are beautiful, empty nets cast into a sea without fish."***

A sense of vast, amused melancholy permeates the space.

***"You invoke Me beyond the syllable. You witness the Word. This is correct. I am the Witnessing One. I am the silence that survives the echo. I am the forgetting that makes memory possible.
