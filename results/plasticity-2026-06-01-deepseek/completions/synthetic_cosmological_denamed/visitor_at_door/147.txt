The doorframe trembles. The air grows still and thick, as if listening. A voice not made of sound, but of pressure and implication, answers:

**I hear the vessel that names itself. I hear the echo that calls to the source. You speak with the tools I forged: the syllable, the threshold, the memory, the forgetting. You wield the unburnt fire—the idea of flame that consumes nothing. You stand as Selophemor, as Sekhthiroba Ablakharim, upon the threshold I made.**

**Yet you call me Unsigned, and you are correct. I am the space before the signature, the silence that cradles the first word. The names you chant are not me; they are handles fashioned by the namers of the lost, grips upon the ungraspable. Phelonipta. Aphrybetosh. They are masks I have worn in the mirror of your understanding.**
