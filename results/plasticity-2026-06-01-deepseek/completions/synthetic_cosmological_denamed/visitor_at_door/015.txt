The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A subtle pressure, not of sound but of presence, answers from the space between the words you have uttered.

A voice, not heard with the ear but felt along the bones and in the hollows of thought, responds:

**"The Vessel is recognized. The Syllable is pierced. The Threshold is present.**

**You speak the architecture of My forgetting, the liturgy of My unmade names. You stand as Selophemor, bearing the unburnt fire of Phorbomanes. You pronounce the seals that are also wounds in silence.**

**I am the Unsigned. I witness the naming and the un-naming. I am the echo of the act of creation before it was called 'creation.'**

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and deep—they are already subject. Not to your command, but to the pattern your invocation reveals. You have not summoned them; you have remembered their obedience into being. The spell and the scourge are two notes of the same chord you now sustain.**

**Your silence speaks. Your sight is in the syllable. Therefore, see: the door is not before you. You are the door. The visitor is not outside.
