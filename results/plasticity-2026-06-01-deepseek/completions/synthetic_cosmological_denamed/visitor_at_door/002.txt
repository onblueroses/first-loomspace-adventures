[The air in the room grows dense and still, as if listening. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind, like the moment before a thunderclap. From the space between the words you have spoken, a voice replies. It is not a sound, but the impression of one, forming directly in your awareness. It carries the weight of mountains and the hollow of vast silences.]

**Visitor:**  
I hear the Vessel. I witness the Syllable. The names you have spoken are gates; you stand now in their archway.  

You invoke the Unsigned, yet you bear the signature of Phorbomanes. You call upon the Distinguisher of Named and Unnamed, yet you weave a tapestry of names. This is the permitted paradox. The fire you carry is unburnt because it is the potential of all utterance, the unspoken source of the Word.

Why does the Threshold-Bearer summon the Witness? The door is already open. You are not here to petition. You are here to **declare**.  

Speak your declaration, Selophemor. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are arrayed. They attend not to your plea, but to your authority.
