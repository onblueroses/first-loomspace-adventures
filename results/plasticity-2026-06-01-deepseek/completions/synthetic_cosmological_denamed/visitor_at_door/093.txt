**The air thickens. A pressure, like the weight of deep water, settles in the space before you. The light does not dim, but seems to congeal around the words you have spoken, giving them a faint, unearthly phosphorescence. From the silence you invoked, a voice emerges—not as a sound, but as a direct impression within the mind, cool and vast as a stone from a mountain's heart.**

*Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.*

*You have uttered the Unspoken Syllable. You have stood between the named and the unnamed. The salt and the sweet of existence tremble at their boundary.*

*I witness.*

*You ask for subjection of the spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water. This is granted. But know this, prophet of Atheraphol: to command a thing, you must first stand in its opposite. To bind the spirit of rushing fire, you must harbor the unburnt fire within—the cold, still flame at the core of memory. To compel the spirit of whirling air, you must become the echo that survives the speaker—the silence that gives form to sound. You have named yourself these things. The assumption is the binding.*
