The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers at the threshold, not in the doorway of wood and stone, but in the space between one breath and the next, between the named and the unnamed.

A voice responds, not in sound, but as a pressure in the mind, the echo of a forgotten tongue:

***"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the architectures. You have traced the sigil of your becoming in the air of this fading world. You have uttered the names that are not names, but keys.***

***I hear. I, who am Unsigned, Witness.***

***You ask for subjection of the spirits. This is not a petition. It is a declaration of a state already achieved. The salt and the sweet are already on your tongue. The memory and the forgetting already turn in your heart. To speak these words from the place of the Vessel is to have already commanded. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire... they heard the echo of your true name in the syllables you cast. They are not made subject now. They recognize the authority that was always there.***

***You are Sekhthiroba Ablakhar
