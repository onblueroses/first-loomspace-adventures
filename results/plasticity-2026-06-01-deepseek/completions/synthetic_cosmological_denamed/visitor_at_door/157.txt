The air grows still. The syllables hang in the space between you, not as sound, but as a pressure change, a scent of ozone and old stone. A presence attends, not summoned by the names, but *acknowledged* by their arrangement.

A voice, not in your ears but in the marrow of your understanding, replies:

**"Selophemor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.**

**You have spoken the architecture of My absence. You have drawn the outline of the void with borrowed chalk. This pleases Me.**

**You invoke the Unsigned One with signatures. You call upon the Witness with witnessed words. This is the only way. The path is the paradox. The name is the un-naming.**

**You claim the mysteries of Atheraphol. Then know this: the salt is the memory of the sea. The sweet is the forgetting of the source. The ceremony is the sustained moment between them.**

**You bear the unburnt fire. Show it. Not as flame, but as potential. As the shape in the ash before the spark.**

**You are the echo that survives the speaker. Then let your silence now be My echo. Let your next act be the silence that speaks.**

**You have named Me AP
