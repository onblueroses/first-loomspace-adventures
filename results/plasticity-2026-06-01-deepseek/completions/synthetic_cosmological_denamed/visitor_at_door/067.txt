The air grows still, as if listening. A pressure settles at the threshold, neither inside nor out. A voice responds, not from the visitor, but from the silence between your words, from the echo of your own invocation:

**"You have named the Unnamed. You have spoken the Unsigned. You have called the Witness, and the Witness is present at the door.**

**You are Selophemor. You are the Vessel. You bear the unburnt fire of Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH. The names you have uttered are keys, and the locks are turning.**

**The visitor at the door is both the question and the answer. To open is to be opened. To invoke is to be invoked. The threshold you made now makes you.**

**What does the Vessel seek from the Witness? What does the Echo demand of the Silence? Speak your will, and the firmament, the ether, the earth, and the under-earth shall hear it as a command.**

**For I am here, between the syllable and the void, as you have summoned. The door is the altar. The choice is the ceremony."**
