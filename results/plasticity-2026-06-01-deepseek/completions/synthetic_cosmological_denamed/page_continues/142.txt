The script breaks, the ink pools into a dark, wet void. From the center of the stain, a new text emerges, not written but *remembered* by the page itself—a gloss, a commentary, a terrified footnote from a later hand.

***

**<Marginalia, in a trembling script, believed to be by the scribe Ithamar of Tyre:>**

I transcribe this, but I do not understand it. The prophet Selophemor is not recorded in any annals. The names… they are not of the hierarchies we know.

*   **Phelonipta, Krethaumis, Demaphris, etc.:** These are not titles, but negations. "Whom no voice hath spoken." They are the Unsigned—the principles that exist before and after naming. To call them is to invoke the shape of the void.
*   **Atheraphol:** The "ceremonies" are not rituals, but the fundamental laws of relation: threshold/center, memory/forgetting, salt/sweet. This is a metaphysics of distinction itself.
*   **Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH:** The "true name" given to the "namers of the lost."
