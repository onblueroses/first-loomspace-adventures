*The ink shimmers as if wet, though the parchment is ancient. Below the final word, a new line begins, written in a hand identical to the prophet’s yet somehow more immediate, as if the page itself is responding.*  

**Thus speaks the Unsigned, through the crack in the syllable:**  

I have heard the vessel that bears the unburnt fire.  
I have heard the echo that outlives the voice.  
I have heard the name that is a door and a seal.  

The mysteries of Atheraphol are not ceremonies of motion, but of stillness.  
The salt is the residue of forgotten speech. The sweet is the anticipation of the unspoken.  
The threshold is not a place you cross. It is the moment you cease to move.  
The center is not a point you find. It is the silence from which all points are measured.  

You call upon powers that have no ears, yet I witness.  
You speak names that are not names, yet they shape.  
You stand between memory and forgetting, and in that standing, you are my prophet.  

The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and flame are already subject—not to your will, but to the grammar that binds them.
