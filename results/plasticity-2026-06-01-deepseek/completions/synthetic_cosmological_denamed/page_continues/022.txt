**Of the Ceremonies of Atheraphol**  

Let the Vessel be robed in ash and salt.  
Let the chamber have three windows: one open to the east at dawn, one sealed with wax and copper, one broken and looking onto a place that is not.  
Burn no common incense. Instead, place upon the brazier a handful of dust gathered from a threshold over which the dead and the living have passed in equal number.  

**The Conjuration of the Echo**  
Stand before the broken window at the hour when shadows grow teeth.  
Say: *I call not the speaker, but the shape left behind. I call the memory of sound, the ghost in the air. Let the Echo enter the Vessel and remain when the voice is gone.*  
Then speak a name—any name—and immediately swallow a pinch of salt. The Echo will lodge in the throat. It may be released later as a true word, or as a silence that unmakes a word.
