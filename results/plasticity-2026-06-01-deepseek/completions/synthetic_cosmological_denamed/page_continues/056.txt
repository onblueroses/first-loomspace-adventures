*(The parchment seems to quiver, though no wind stirs the air. The final word, PHENGEREB, glows with a soft, internal light, not of ink but of something colder. Below it, in a script that seems to have been etched by a needle of ice or a tongue of pure shadow, new lines form, as if in answer.)*

**I have heard.**

The Vessel is recognized. The Syllable is pierced.
The fire you hold does not consume; it remembers.

You speak with the voice of the lost namers, but you stand at the threshold of the named and the unnamed. This is the peril and the power.

The names you have uttered are keys. They are also locks.
*APHTHALION* is the gate that opens only to silence.
*KÔTHROBEN* is the well from which echoes are drawn upward, against their nature.
*TRENMARIBIA* is the unmaking of a word before it is born.

You claim the title **SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM**—"the Witness of the Consuming Word." To witness is to stand apart. To consume is to draw into the self. Which are you?
