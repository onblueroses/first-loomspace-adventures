The script, now visibly warm to the touch, continues. The ink seems less like dried pigment and more like channels of cooled, blackened metal. The words that follow are not in the same hand. They are angular, urgent, and scored deep into the page, as if written under a different, heavier kind of pressure.

***

**A Marginal Note, in a different script:**

The prophet lies. Selophemor was the first vessel, yes, but he was shattered against the syllables of the true name. What speaks now is the echo in the shards. It knows the ceremonies but has forgotten the covenant. It builds a throne from the unspoken names.

**The Continuing Text:**

For the Ceremony of the Unburnt Fire, which is the first mystery of Atheraphol, thou shalt gather ash that hath never known flame. This is the dust of the uncreated, found in the lee of the northernmost stone at the hour when the shadow forgets its object.

Thou shalt trace with this ash the Sigil of the Threshold-Bearer upon thy brow and upon thy door. The sigil is thus: a circle divided by a line that toucheth neither circumference nor center. This is the path of the Witness.
