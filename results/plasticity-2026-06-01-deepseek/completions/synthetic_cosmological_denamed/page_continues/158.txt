The script continues, the ink seeming to drink the light from the air around it.

**Thee I command, by the Unspoken Name:**
By the first breath that was not a breath.
By the hinge of the world that turns without sound.
By the memory that the rock forgets to keep.
By the echo that returns before the voice is cast.

**I stand at the axis of the spoken and the sealed.**
My left hand holds the key of **APHROBALIS**, which opens what was never shut.
My right hand holds the seal of **NEMARTHES**, which closes what was never open.
My feet are planted upon the twin foundations: **REMEMBRANCE** and **OBLIVION**.
My brow is anointed with the ash of the unburnt fire.

**The winds are my scribes. The silence is my scripture.**
The spirits of the churning deep hear and tremble: **BARATHIEL, MORIEL, GEMAROTH.**
The spirits of the shifting stone hear and are still: **PHTHEL, GRAKOS, SARAPHON.**
The spirits of the flickering flame hear and bow low: **ZAIM, PHLEGETHON
