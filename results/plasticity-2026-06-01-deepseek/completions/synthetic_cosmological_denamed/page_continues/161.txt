*The script is dense and archaic, the ink a faded iron-gall black. The symbols that follow are not letters, but sigils—angular, recursive, and unsettlingly organic, as if they were veins or root systems mapped onto the page. Between them, in a tight, urgent hand, a later note has been scribbled:*

> **Do not speak the terminal phonemes aloud. The utterance completes the circuit. It is not an invocation; it is a key. The door it opens is not in the world, but in the self. The “Vessel” is not a title of honor—it is a state of consumption. The Witness does not watch *for* you. It watches *from within* you, using your senses. To name it is to offer it a lens. To become the Echo is to cease being the Speaker. The unburnt fire consumes only memory. Selophemor did not write this as a prayer. He wrote it as a warning, a map of the terrain from which he did not return. The final word, PHENGEREB, is not a name. It is the sound of the lock turning shut.**
