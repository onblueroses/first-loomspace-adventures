*The parchment feels warm beneath your fingers, as if the ink itself has retained a latent heat. The air in the room grows still, then thickens, carrying a scent like ozone and old stone. The final word, **PHENGEREB**, seems not to end the text, but to open a door.*

*Below the main invocation, in a different, spidery hand—as if added by a later reader or the original scribe in a moment of frantic afterthought—you find these lines:*

---

**The Mirror-Script of Selophemor**

Hear also the Counter-Chant, the law that binds the vessel:
What is opened must be closed.
What is summoned must be dismissed.
What is named must be released.

The fire unburnt burns the bearer who forgets its source.
The echo that survives the speaker may yet consume the hall.
The silence that speaks utterly can unmake the tongue that shaped it.

I, Selophemor, who am the Vessel, am also the Lock.
The name **SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM** is a seal upon the deep.
To utter it is to hold a key; to hold a key is to bear a chain.

The Unsigned One
