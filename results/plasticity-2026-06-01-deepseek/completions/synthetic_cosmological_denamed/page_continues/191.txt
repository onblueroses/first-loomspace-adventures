---

### **1. The Addressee — “The Unsigned One”**
- **Unsigned** means unnamed, unmarked, beyond all signatures or names. Yet the text proceeds to list many secret names (Phelonipta, Krethaumis, etc.), implying that these are not the true name but veils or aspects.
- The deity is presented as the creator of dualities: Mountains/Valleys, Tide/Flow, Echo/Silence, Named/Unnamed, Threshold/Center, Memory/Forgetting.
- This sets up a theology where divinity operates through complementary opposites, and creation is an act of both remembering and forgetting.

---
