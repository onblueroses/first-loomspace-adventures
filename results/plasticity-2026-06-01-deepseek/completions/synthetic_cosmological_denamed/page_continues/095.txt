**Gloss of the Scribe Ammiel, of the Third Veil:**

Here ends the *Invocation of the Unsigned*, as delivered to the Prophet Selophemor in the dream-chamber of Atheraphol. The names are vessels; do not mistake the water for the cup. To speak **PHORBOMANES APHRYBETOSH** is to call the tide itself, not the moon that pulls it. It is the true name that flows *beneath* all others, as salt beneath sweet water.

The ninefold cry—**KHELRIPHION** to **KHARLAPHTHA**—are the keys to the nine locks of manifestation. They correspond to the spheres, but inverted, as a reflection in a black mirror. They are not to be used for binding alone, but for *unbinding* the self from the wheel of named things.
