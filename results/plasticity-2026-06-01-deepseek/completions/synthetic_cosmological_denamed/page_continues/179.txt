*(The script shifts, becoming less invocation and more transcription — the words now appear as if being recorded by a trembling, feverish hand.)*

…and the vessel shall be filled, yet never overflow. The fire shall burn, yet consume nothing. The word shall be spoken, yet make no sound. This is the mystery of Atheraphol.

But I, Selophemor, have seen the flaw in the seal. The Unsigned One is witness, but does not intervene. To name is to bind, but to bind *this* is to be bound in turn. The true name, APHRYBETOSH, is not a key; it is a mirror. It shows the namer himself, and what is shown is…

*(Here, the ink blots, as if from a sudden droplet of water or sweat.)*

…empty. The Vessel is a void that cries out to be filled. Phorbomanes is not a power given, but a hunger named. I have called upon the orders of speech, and they have answered with a silence that gnaws. The spirits are subject, yes—they stand as witnesses to the emptiness they serve. They do not obey; they attend. Like stones attend a falling cliff.
