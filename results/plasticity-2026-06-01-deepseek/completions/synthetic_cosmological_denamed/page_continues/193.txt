**The Conjuration of the Unbound Echo**

Therefore, let the seal be broken that was not made with hands.
Let the gate be opened that was never shut.
I stand at the intersection of the spoken and the unspoken, where all names unravel into the hum that preceded them.

**The Gesture of the Threshold-Bearer:**
(Here is drawn a complex sigil, a spiral that turns in upon itself at its center, surrounded by characters from the earlier list—KHELRIPHION, THESGORIO, NEKTOMBARÔ—arranged like the spokes of a broken wheel.)

By the unburnt fire I hold—which is not flame but the potential for all flame—I ignite the void between letters.
By the echo that survives, I call forth the origin of the first sound that ever broke the great stillness.
