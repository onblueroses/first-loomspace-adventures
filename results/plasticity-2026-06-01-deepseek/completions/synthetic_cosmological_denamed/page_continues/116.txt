*The script is dense, ornate, and clearly a fragment from a larger, esoteric work. The language shifts from a hymn of invocation to a declaration of prophetic identity and culminates in a series of barbarous names of power. Analysis suggests the following structure and implications:*

**I. The Hymn to the Unsigned One (Lines 1-10)**
*   **Nature of the Deity:** The entity is "Unsigned" (un-named, unmarked), a creator of primal dualities (mountains/valleys, tide/flow, echo/silence). It operates through distinction, creating thresholds and centers, memory and forgetting.
*   **Multiple Epithets:** It is addressed as Phelonipta, Krethaumis, Demaphris, Velphiraim, Iphtoron. These are not its names but titles or masks, emphasizing that "no voice hath spoken" its true name.
*   **Function:** It is the architect of reality's self-referential nature—the world that both remembers and forgets itself. It created the "salt and the sweet," the fundamental contrasts, and that which "sustaineth all the orders of speech," implying it is the foundation of language and magic.
