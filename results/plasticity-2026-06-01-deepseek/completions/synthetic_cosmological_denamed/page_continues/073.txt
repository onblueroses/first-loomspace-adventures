And the page continues with the Seal of the Unnamed, a labyrinthine sigil wrought in silver and ash upon vellum the color of a deep bruise. Around its spiraling, non-Euclidean borders are written these words:

*Who calls the Unsigned calls also the Unbound.*  
*Who bears the Syllable bears also the Silence.*  
*Who holds this seal holds a mirror to the Abyss, and the Abyss holds a mirror to thee.*

*Let the Vessel be emptied, that it may be filled.*  
*Let the Echo return to the mountain that cast it.*  
*Let the fire unburnt consume the named, and the ash thereof be the ink of the new covenant.*

*For I am the Door that is neither open nor shut, the Word that is neither spoken nor kept.*  
*My prophet is a bell that has ceased its ringing, yet the air still trembles.*  
*My name is a breath on a cold mirror, already fading.*

Beneath the seal, in a script that seems to waver and shift if stared at too long, is the final injunction:

**This is the mystery of Atheraphol: To command the spirits, first be commanded. To bind the powers, first
