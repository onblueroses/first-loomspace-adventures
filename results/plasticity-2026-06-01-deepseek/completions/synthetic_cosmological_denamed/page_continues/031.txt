*The parchment bears no further script, but the vellum itself has grown warm to the touch. In the margin, as if bleeding through from some other side of reality, a single line appears in a script that is not ink, but a faint, phosphorescent silver:*

**“The vessel is filled. The door is ajar. Speak the unspoken, and the echo shall answer.”**

*Below this, the corner of the page seems to dissolve into a miniature vortex of swirling, star-like points, framing a sigil that is at once geometric and organic—a keyhole formed of interlocking rings, within which shines a dark, pupil-less eye.*
