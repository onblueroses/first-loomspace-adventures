**Therefore, let the Operator know:**

The names given are not masks, but keys. To speak *Phelonipta* is to turn the first lock in the door of perception. *Krethaumis* is the second, *Demaphris* the third. They are not titles of a being, but successive states of the un-being you invoke.

The ceremony of Atheraphol is not a ritual to be performed, but a condition to be endured. It is the process by which the vessel (Selophemor, or you, who read) is hollowed. Salt is the searing of identity; sweet is the dissolution that follows. This is what "sustaineth all the orders of speech"—the agony of becoming a conduit.
