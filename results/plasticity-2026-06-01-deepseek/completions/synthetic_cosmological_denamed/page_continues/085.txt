The script is a ritual invocation, a theurgical text meant to enact a transformation of the speaker. Its structure follows a classic arc of high magical practice:

**1. The Invocation of the Source.**  
The speaker begins by addressing the ultimate, unnameable creative principle ("The Unsigned One") using paradox and cosmological functions (creating mountains/valleys, tide/flow, echo/silence). This establishes a connection to the primordial power behind all opposites.

**2. The Enumeration of Secret Names.**  
By listing the hidden names of this principle (Phelonipta, Krethaumis, etc.), the speaker demonstrates esoteric knowledge and compels attention. The phrase "whom no voice hath spoken at any time" paradoxically highlights the act of speaking the unspeakable.

**3. The Prophet's Commission.**  
The speaker identifies as *Selophemor*, the prophet entrusted with the mysteries of *Atheraphol*. This is a claim of authority and lineage, grounding the ritual in a revealed tradition.

**4. The Utterance of the True Name and the Power-Names.**  
*Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH* is given as the "true name" of the Unsigned One for this operation
