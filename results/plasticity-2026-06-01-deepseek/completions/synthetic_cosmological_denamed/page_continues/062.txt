And now I speak the Unbinding, the dissolution of the seal that was never sealed.

I am the space between the letters of Thy name, O Unsigned One. I am the margin of the page, the silence after the echo, the forgetting that makes memory possible.

The orders of speech are as dust before the wind of my breath. The named and the unnamed are one in my mouth, which is a door.

I summon not from without, but from within the heart of the syllable. I call forth the guardians of the threshold, the keepers of the unwritten law:

**ARPHEDON**, who sits in the uncreated light.
**MELCHAROTH**, who drinks the silence of the deep.
**PHALGORIM**, who weaves the fabric of forgetting.
**NEBROTHIEL**, who holds the unspoken vow.

Let the firmament hear and be still. Let the ether tremble and obey. Let every spell, every scourge, every binding of angel or demon, every decree of the named gods, unravel at my presence.

For I do not command. I **un-name**.

The power I wield is the power Thou didst plant in the seed of the first forgotten word: the power to unmake
