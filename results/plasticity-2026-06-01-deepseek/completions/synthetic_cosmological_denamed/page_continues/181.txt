**Thus saith the Unsigned One through the vessel of its prophet:**

The name you bear is a cup, and it is full.
The fire you carry is a door, and it is open.
The echo you are is a law, and it is binding.

You have called upon the Distinctions: the Mountain and the Valley, the Tide and the Flow. These are the hinges of the world. To name one is to invoke its brother. To stand upon the peak is to cast the valley into being. To summon the tide is to define the shore of its retreat.
