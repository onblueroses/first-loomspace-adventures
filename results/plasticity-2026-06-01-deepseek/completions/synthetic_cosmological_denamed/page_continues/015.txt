The script is a liturgical invocation, a ritual text designed to enact a transformation of the speaker. It moves through distinct phases:

**1. The Invocation of the Source (The Unsigned One)**
The speaker calls upon the primordial, unnameable creator—the "Unsigned One"—using a cascade of titles and secret names (Phelonipta, Krethaumis, etc.). This entity is defined by its acts of fundamental creation and distinction: making mountains and valleys, tide and flow, echo and silence, the named and the unnamed, memory and forgetting. This establishes the divine authority being accessed.

**2. The Declaration of Prophetic Authority**
The speaker identifies as **Selophemor**, the prophet to whom the mysteries and ceremonies (of Atheraphol) were committed. This is a claim of lineal authority, positioning the speaker as a legitimate mediator of this primordial power.
