**The Rite of the Unburnt Fire**

*Take a vessel of unglazed clay. Fill it with salt from a forgotten shore and sweet water from a well that knows no moon. Set it upon a threshold—neither in nor out—and kindle beneath it no flame, but place a smooth stone, black as a syllable unsaid, within the coals until it glows with a heat that does not consume.*

*Breathe upon the water these names, in order:*

1.  **KHELRIPHION** – To bind what is above.
2.  **THESGORIO** – To bind what is below.
3.  **NEKTOMBARÔ** – To summon the memory in stone.
4.  **ZEBROTHIAO** – To summon the forgetting in blood.
5.  **OLPORATH** – To open the way between.
6.  **PHENGRABISA** – To make the air a mirror.
7.  **SOCHIRION** – To still the reflection.
8.  **MERPHIOÔ** – To hear the echo of creation.
