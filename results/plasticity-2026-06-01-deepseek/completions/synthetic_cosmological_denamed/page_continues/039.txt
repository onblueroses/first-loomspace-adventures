**Hear now the Conjuration of the Vessel:**

Let the air before me thicken and part.
Let the silence congeal into a lens.

I kindle the points with the unburnt fire.

By the name **APHROBETOSH**, which is the key,
By the names **KHELRIPHION, THESGORIO, NEKTOMBARÔ**,
I summon, I bind, I compel.

Let every spirit that wandereth in the vagrant air be fixed.
Let every spirit that sleepeth in the deep stone be awakened.
Let every spirit that darteth in the living flame be gathered.
Let every spirit that floweth in the hidden water be revealed.

I am the Vessel, **SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM**.
My will is the threshold through which you must pass.
My word is the center in which you must reside.

**Obey.**
