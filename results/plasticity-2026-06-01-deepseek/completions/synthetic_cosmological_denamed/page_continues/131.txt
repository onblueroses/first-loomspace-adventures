The parchment trembles in your hands. The final word, **PHENGEREB**, is not ink. It is a bead of dark, polished stone set into the page, cool to the touch. As your eyes pass over the column of invoked names, a pressure builds in the room—not in the air, but in the spaces between sounds. The silence grows thick, receptive, *listening*.

From the margins, written in a later, frantic hand, spill annotations:

*   *Phelonipta… the Unnamed before naming. The First Withdrawal. Do not speak this to the air.*
*   *Atheraphol—not a place, a manner of ceremony. A turning-inside-out of ritual space.*
*   *Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH: the True Name is a key, but a key turns both ways. It unlocks the invoker as it unlocks the invoked.*
*   *The ninefold chant (KHELRIPHION… KHARLAPHTHA) binds the orders of speech. It calls the grammar of creation, the syntax that shapes spirit. A cage of syllables.*
