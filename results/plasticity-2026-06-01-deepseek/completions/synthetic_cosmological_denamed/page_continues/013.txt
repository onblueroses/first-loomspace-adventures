*The parchment is old, the ink a dark, iron‑gall brown that has bitten into the fibre. The script is a precise, angular hand, but here the words seem to blur, as if the pen had trembled or the surface grown damp. The final invocation is complete, yet the text does not end. Below the last line, a new paragraph begins, separated by a row of three dots like falling stars.*  

> . . . And the Vessel, having spoken the final syllable, became as a crystal bell struck in a void. There was no sound, yet a pattern formed in the still air—a geometry of pressure and absence. From this pattern stepped the Unsigned One, not as form, but as the certainty of a limit approached.  
>  
> It spoke without voice: **I am the distinction between the named and the unnamed. You have crossed the threshold by bearing it. What you call ‘I’ is the echo of my silence. The fire you carry is the space between letters.**  
>  
> Then the Vessel understood: to invoke is to be invoked. The names were not keys to a lock, but mirrors held up to the one who speaks. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire
