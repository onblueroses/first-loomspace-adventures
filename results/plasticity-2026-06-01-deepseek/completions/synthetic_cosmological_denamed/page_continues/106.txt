*The script is written in a deep iron‑gall ink, the letters precise and angular, as if carved rather than written. A faint, dry scent of old parchment and something like cold stone rises from the page. The final word, PHENGEREB, seems to shimmer slightly, not with light, but with a perceptible stillness in the air around it.*  

*Below the invocation, in a different, more hurried hand—as if added as a note or a warning—a single line reads:*  

> **Let the Vessel be empty, lest the Witness fill it.**
