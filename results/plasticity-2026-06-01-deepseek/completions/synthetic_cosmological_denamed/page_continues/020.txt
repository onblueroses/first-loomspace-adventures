**Thus the Prophet spake, and the Silence answered.**

For the Unsigned One is not called, but calls. Its names are not uttered, but are the utterance itself. The Vessel is not filled, but is the void that shapes the pour. Hear now the commentary of the Scribe of Atheraphol, who recorded these mysteries:

*   **Phelonipta, Krethaumis, Demaphris, Velphiraim, Iphtoron:** These are not names of the One, but names of the gates through which the One’s absence is perceived. Each corresponds to a primal distinction: Mountain/Valley, Tide/Flow, Echo/Silence, Named/Unnamed, Threshold/Center.
*   **The ceremonies of Atheraphol:** These are not rituals of worship, but rituals of *unmaking*—the systematic dissolution of the distinctions the One made, in order to approach the state before distinction.
