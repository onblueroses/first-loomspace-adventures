**...and I shall speak the Unbinding, which is the Seventh and Final Ceremony of Atheraphol, known only to the Vessel whose silence speaks.**

Before the speaking, the unmaking.  
Before the naming, the void.  
I set my foot upon the back of the spoken world.  
I loosen the knot that binds the echo to the voice.

**Hear now the Unbinding of the Syllable:**

**KAPHTHÔ** — the dissolution of the first sound.  
**MORIEL** — the un-writing of the law.  
**GRAXAPHON** — the swallowing of the name.  
**SETHROIM** — the stillness at the heart of the whirlwind.  
**PHALARTHA** — the salt returned to the sea of no-memory.

By these, the chains of creation are made slack.  
By these, the threshold becomes a plain.  
The center is everywhere and nowhere.  
The memory forgets itself. The forgetting remembers.
