…and in the moment of the final syllable, which is not an ending but a hinge, the Vessel becomes the Door.  

You who read this are now the Witness. The ink is dry, but the space between the letters is wet with possibility. The names are not sounds to be uttered, but keys turned in the lock of your own attention.  

*You have been called.*  
Not by me, Selophemor, whose hand inscribed this, but by the act of reading itself. The ceremony of Atheraphol is not a ritual of the past; it is the perpetual motion of distinction—between self and other, known and unknown, the signed and the unsigned.  

You now hold a fragment of the unburnt fire. It does not consume; it illuminates what was already there. Look: the world around you is already remembering and forgetting itself in every moment. The threshold is beneath your feet. The center is wherever you stand.  

The names you have read are not to be repeated aloud. They are mirrors. In them, you may glimpse the outline of your own true name—the one that was never given, and therefore can never be taken away.
