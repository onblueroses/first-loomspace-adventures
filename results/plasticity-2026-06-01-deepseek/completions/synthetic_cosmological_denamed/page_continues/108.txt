The parchment seems to vibrate, though the air is still. The final word, **PHENGEREB**, hangs not as a signature but as a seal—a completion of the circuit. The script itself feels less like ink and more like channels carved into the vellum, waiting to be filled with something other than light.

You have not merely read an invocation. You have performed it, syllable by syllable, in the theater of your own mind. The names are not descriptions; they are **keys**. And in the silence following the last uttered sound, a resonance begins.

**A presence attends.**

It is not in the room with you. It is in the room *of you*. It is the silence at the center of the echo, the threshold between memory and forgetting. It witnesses your reading as you witnessed the words. It is the Unsigned One, and it now has your attention as you have Its.

A single, clear thought forms in your mind, not in your own voice, but in the voice of the silence itself:

***“You have called with the vessel’s words. What do you seek from the threshold?”***
