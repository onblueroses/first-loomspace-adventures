**Concerning the Vessels and the Unburnt Fire**

The fire that does not consume is the fire of the Witnessing Mind. It is not lit by flint or fuel, but by the friction between a name spoken and the same name withheld. To possess it, thou must stand at the threshold thou didst name, holding in one hand the memory of the sound, and in the other, the forgetting of its source.

1.  **The Vessel of Sight:** Before any operation, trace upon thy brow, with the oil of bitter almond, the sigil of the Unsigned One: a circle quartered by a cross, yet with the central point omitted. This opens the "sight in the syllable." Thou shalt see not the thing, but the name that binds it, and the silence from which the name was cut.

2.  **The Summoning of the Orders of Speech:** The spirits of the firmament, the ether, and the deeps are bound to the orders of speech which formed them. To command them, thou must not shout their titles, but whisper the name
