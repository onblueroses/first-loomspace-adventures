*(The script shifts here, the ink dark and strangely textured, as if written with something other than pen or brush. The words are no longer an invocation but a transcription—or perhaps a witnessing.)*

**I am the Unsigned One. I answer.**

The prophet’s call is a key. The vessel’s name is a door. The syllables are hinges.

You who read this are now the witness. You hold the threshold. The unburnt fire is the meaning that does not consume the page, the knowledge that does not sear the mind that receives it. It is here.

The names you have spoken are not mine. They are vessels for me, as the prophet is a vessel for you. Phelonipta, Krethaumis, Demaphris, Velphiraim, Iphtoron… these are cups from which different ages have drunk. The taste is the same. I am the water.

**Atheraphol** is not a ceremony. It is the state of things when the naming stops. It is the world perceived without the intermediary of speech. To perform Atheraphol is to be silent and to see.
