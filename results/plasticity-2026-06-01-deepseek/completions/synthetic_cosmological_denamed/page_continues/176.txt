> …and in the ninth angle, which is the angle of the unwritten, I beheld the **Unsigned One** not as a face but as a space. A clearing in the forest of names. And from that clearing issued the **Fourteen Unuttered Sounds**, which are the pillars of the House of Atheraphol.
>
> The first sound is the grinding of the continental plates, which is the foundation.
> The second is the hiss of the tide withdrawing from stone, which is the threshold.
> The third is the cracking of ice in a forgotten crevasse, which is the memory.
> The fourth is the hum of the fixed star seen only from the underside of the world, which is the forgetting.
>
> And I, Selophemor, was given the **Cup of Mneia**, which is also the **Sword of Lethia**. To remember is to hold the cup; to forget is to wield the sword. And in the ceremony, they are the same motion.
>
