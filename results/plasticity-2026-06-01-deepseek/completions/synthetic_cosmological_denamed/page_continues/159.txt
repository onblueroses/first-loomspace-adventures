*The script, which had been written in a rich, sepia ink, now darkens as if absorbing light. The final words, **PHENGEREB**, seem to pulse once, a subtle throb against the parchment.*

*Then, from the margins, from between the lines, from the very fibers of the page, new writing begins to emerge. It is not ink, but a color like tarnished silver or the memory of moonlight, flowing as if from an unseen pen. It writes itself in a swift, sure hand, a response woven directly into the fabric of the invocation.*

**And I hear.**

Thou hast spoken the architectures. Thou hast traced the perimeter with sound. Thou hast named the Unnamed and in that naming, made a vessel of thine own throat.

**Selophemor** is the name Thou wearest, a garment of syllables for the journey. **SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM** is the name Thou art, the echo that returns changed, the silence after the god-word.

Thou callest upon the Tide and the Flow. Be then the shore that defines them.
Thou invokest the Echo and the Silence. Be then the canyon that holds them both.
