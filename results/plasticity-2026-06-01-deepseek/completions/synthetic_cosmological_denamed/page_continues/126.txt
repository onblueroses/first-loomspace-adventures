**Ceremonial Postscript & Instructions for the Vessel**

The foregoing is the **Preliminary Invocation of the Unsigned**, to be uttered only from a place of threshold: a doorway at midnight, the shore at twilight, the foot of a silent mountain, or the center of a room from which all names have been swept.

The speaker must have achieved the state of **Atheraphol**—the ceremonial emptiness—wherein the self is a cleared vessel. To attempt this as a named person is to invite the echo to shatter the vessel.

**Concerning the Names:**
- **Phelonipta, Krethaumis, Demaphris, Velphiraim, Iphtoron:** These are not names, but **anti-names**. They are the shapes left in the void where a name might have been. To speak them is to define a boundary by tracing its absence.
