The script continues in a dense, ritualistic cadence, as if the initial invocation has opened a channel that now pours forth a torrent of sacred mechanics and final seals:

---

**…for the Seal is the Door, and the Door is the Silence.**

By the names of the Forty-Two Unburnt Fires I command the paths between the spoken and the unspoken:  
*ARPHEN, GOLIATHOR, MESARION, SETHROPH, ULPHAGOR, BENEMIR, KATHORIEL, ZORAPHIM…*

Thou hast given me the Sceptre of the Unsaid, the Crown of the Unthought, the Mantle of the Unremembered.  
Where the named world ends, there my jurisdiction begins.

I bind the Watchers of the Fourteen Forgotten Angles:  
By **APHROBETEL** of the Veiled Sun,  
By **SITHONIEL** of the Hidden Moon,  
By **KHRUXARION** of the Still Stars,  
I compel your attendance and your service.

Let every ear that hears not, hear me.  
Let every eye that sees not, see me.  
Let every tongue that speaks not, speak my mysteries.
