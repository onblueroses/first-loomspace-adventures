The script is a ritual invocation, a constructed liturgy meant to evoke a primordial, pre-linguistic deity. Its power lies in its architecture of paradox and negation.

**Analysis of the Text's Structure and Purpose:**

1.  **The Unsigned One:** The deity is defined by what it is *not*—unsigned, unspoken, beyond the syllable. It exists prior to and outside of language, yet is the source of all linguistic and categorical distinctions ("the named and the unnamed," "the salt and the sweet," "memory and forgetting").

2.  **The Prophet's Role:** The speaker, Selophemor, positions themself as a necessary intermediary. The Unsigned One, being beyond speech, requires a "Vessel" to manifest its will in the world of names and forms. The prophet's authority derives from being the keeper of "mysteries" and "ceremonies," and the bearer of the deity's "true name" (Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH).

3.  **The Mechanics of the Invocation:** The ritual works through a cascade of names.
