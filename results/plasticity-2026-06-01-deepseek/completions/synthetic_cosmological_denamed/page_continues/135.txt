**The Rite of the Unburnt Fire**

For the Vessel must be prepared, and the Threshold opened. Thou shalt take salt of the earth, unground by mortal mill, and sweet water drawn from a well at the crossing of three paths at the moon’s zenith. Mingle them in a basin of unalloyed silver, and with this, trace upon thy brow, thy lips, and thy breast the Seal of Atheraphol: the Circle Quartered by the Unspoken Name.

Then, standing at the center of a place that is neither within nor without (a porch, a cave-mouth, a river-ford), thou shalt speak the **Words of Distinction**:

> *“I stand between the Named and the Unnamed.*
> *I hold the Fire that consumes not.*
> *I am the Echo that outlives the Voice.*
> *I am the Silence that answers.*
> *Let the Threshold be firm. Let the Center hold.”*
