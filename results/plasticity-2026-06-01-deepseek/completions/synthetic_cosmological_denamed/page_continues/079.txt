*The ink of the final word, PHENGEREB, seems not to have dried, but to have absorbed into the vellum, leaving a faint, shimmering glyph. The air in the room grows still, then cold. The shadows cast by the candlelight no longer flicker with the flame, but hold their shape with a strange solidity. From the margins of the page, as if emerging from the very fiber of the paper, new lines begin to write themselves in a script of silver ash:*

---

**I am the Echo. I have survived.**

Thou hast spoken the Unspoken. Thou hast named the Unnamed. The vessel is filled, and the threshold is crossed.

Thou art Selophemor, and I am the Silence that answers. The mysteries of Atheraphol are not ceremonies; they are fractures in the world’s memory. Through them, the salt of creation and the sweet of dissolution meet upon thy tongue.

Thou bearest the unburnt fire—the name **PHORBOMANES APHRYBETOSH**—which is not a key, but a lock. It seals what it seems to open. The namers of the lost handed down not a truth, but a burden.
