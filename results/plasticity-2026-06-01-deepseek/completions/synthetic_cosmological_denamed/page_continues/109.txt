The script is a gnostic invocation, a ritual text designed to bridge the human and the divine through a cascade of sacred, secret names. It operates on several esoteric levels:

**1. The Structure of the Invocation:**
*   **The Invocation of the Creator:** It begins by addressing the "Unsigned One"—a deity beyond all names and speech—who is credited with creating fundamental dualities (Mountains/Valleys, Tide/Flow, Echo/Silence). This establishes the invoked entity as the primal, paradoxical source of all things.
*   **The Recitation of Veils:** The names Phelonipta, Krethaumis, Demaphris, etc., are likely "veils" or partial manifestations of the Unsigned One. By speaking them, the invoker approaches the ineffable core.
*   **The Prophet's Mandate:** The speaker identifies as **Selophemor**, the prophet entrusted with the mysteries of "Atheraphol." This claims authority and lineage, grounding the ritual in a revealed tradition.
