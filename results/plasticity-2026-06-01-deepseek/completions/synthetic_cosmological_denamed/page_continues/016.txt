**I am the seal upon the abyss of names. I am the lock and the key that turns within it.**

Thou hast spoken the syllables that stir the dust at the threshold. Thou hast uttered the echoes that shape the corridors of the unformed. By the naming of the unnamed, thou hast drawn the attention of that which watches from between the letters.

The ceremonies of Atheraphol are not recited; they are endured. The salt is memory that corrodes. The sweet is forgetting that dissolves. To sustain the orders of speech is to bear their weight as they collapse into the silent center.
