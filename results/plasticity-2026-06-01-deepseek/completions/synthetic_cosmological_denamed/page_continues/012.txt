…and the Word shall return to the Silence that bore it. The ceremonies of Atheraphol are sevenfold, and their gates are not of stone but of breath. The first gate is the **Gate of Salt**, which purges the tongue of lies. The second is the **Gate of Sweetness**, which draws the hidden things near. The third is the **Gate of Echo**, where the name is spoken backward and heard forward. The fourth is the **Gate of Silence**, where the name is unspoken and thereby written upon the soul. The fifth is the **Gate of Threshold**, which is neither inside nor outside. The sixth is the **Gate of Center**, which is everywhere and nowhere. The seventh is the **Gate of Unsigned**, which is the dissolution of the seal and the merging with the Witness.

To enact the mystery, the prophet must stand at the center of a circle drawn with **white salt and black honey**. Let them hold in their left hand a vessel of still water (the Memory) and in their right hand a vessel of quick ash (the Forgetting).
