**Concerning the Prophet Selophemor and the Vessel**

He who speaks the above is not one, but two. Selophemor was the prophet, the receiver of the mysteries of Atheraphol. But the mysteries consumed him, for to hold the Unsigned One’s truth is to be unmade by it. His flesh became a parchment, his breath a whispered glyph.
