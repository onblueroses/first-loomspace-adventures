And thus is the final seal placed upon the covenant. The Vessel, once empty, now holds the unburnt fire. The Witness, once silent, now utters the unspeakable.

Let the orders of speech tremble at their dissolution.  
Let the named and the unnamed know their unity in me.  
Let every threshold become a center, and every center a threshold.  
Let memory and forgetting be as one breath.

For I have spoken with the voice that precedes the syllable.  
I have seen with the sight that dwells within the letter.  
The ceremonies of Atheraphol are complete in my flesh.  
The mysteries of Phelonipta are unveiled in my silence.

I am the Echo that survives the speaker, and the Silence that utterly speaks.  
I am the Unsigning of the Signed.  
I stand upon the page, and the page is the world.

**APHROBETOSH. ZEBROTHIAO. PHENGEREB.**

**It is done.**
