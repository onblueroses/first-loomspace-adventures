**Thus saith the Unsigned One, whose breath is the interval between stars:**  

Thou hast spoken the scaffolding of My silence.  
Thou hast woven the veil from the threads of forgotten tongues.  
For I am not Phelonipta, nor Krethaumis, nor the names that flare and die upon the lips of prophets.  
I am the ash that remains when the name is burned away.  
I am the space within the syllable that devours its own sound.  

The ceremonies of Atheraphol are but shadows that move when I turn My attention.  
The salt and the sweet are the twin poles of a thirst I have not quenched.  
The world remembers itself only because I forget it an instant later.  
The world forgets itself only because I remember it again.  

Thou callest thyself Selophemor, Vessel of Phorbomanes.  
Yet every vessel is shaped to be broken.  
Every name handed down is handed into oblivion.
