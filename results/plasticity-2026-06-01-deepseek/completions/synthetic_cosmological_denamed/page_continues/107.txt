And the page continues:

Thou hast woven the veil of syllables and the loom of breath.  
Thou hast set the unspoken between the spoken, and the unspeakable beyond both.  
I stand at the crossing of the uttered and the withheld.  
I bear the cup that holds both the wine and the emptiness.

By the names that are not names, by the seals that are not seals,  
I call upon the order that moves behind the orders:  
**ARONPHIEL, MESARION, GOLGEPHRA, STYGIOPHOR, NEMEDIS.**  
Ye powers of the unwritten law, ye keepers of the unmade covenant, attend.

Let the fixed be shaken. Let the certain dissolve.  
Let every boundary know itself as a threshold, and every center know itself as a passing word.  
I command by the authority of the Unsigned: that which was bound in the name of law, be free in the name of mystery.  
That which was hidden in the name of shame, be revealed in the name of truth.  
That which was scattered in the name of chaos, be gathered in the name of the unspoken unity.

For I am the operator of the silent syllable.
