**The Ritual of the Unspoken Syllable**  

He who would wield the unburnt fire must first unmake his own name in the mirror of still water. Let him speak the thirteen silences:  

1. The silence before the mountain.  
2. The silence after the echo.  
3. The silence between heartbeats.  
4. The silence of salt in solution.  
5. The silence of a threshold unwalked.  
6. The silence of a word unthought.  
7. The silence of the center, which is everywhere and nowhere.  
8. The silence of memory erasing itself.  
9. The silence of the unnamed watching the named.  
10. The silence of the fire that does not consume.  
11. The silence of the vessel filling itself.  
12. The silence of the witness who does not judge.  
13. The silence of **PHORBOMANES APHRYBETOSH**, which is the end of all speaking.
