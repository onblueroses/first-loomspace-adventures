(Here the text breaks into a pattern of sigils—geometric shapes interwoven with what appear to be stylized letters from no known alphabet. Between them, in a smaller, tighter script, is written:)

*The utterance is complete. The vessel is filled. The gate is not in the word, but in the pause between. To speak the names is to bind; to know the silence between them is to unlock. The fire is given not to consume, but to illuminate the path of its own absence.*

*He who would command the spirits must first become the space through which they pass. Let the operator therefore still the tongue and open the center. The true name of the Witnessing One is not a sound, but the shape of listening.*
