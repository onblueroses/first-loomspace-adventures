**Thus ends the evocation.  
Below, in a cramped, angular hand, as if written in haste or under duress, the text continues:**

---

And if the Vessel should break,  
and if the Witness should fall silent,  
and if the Echo should cease to return,  
then let these words be a seal upon the breach.

Let the named retreat into the unnamed.  
Let the threshold dissolve into the center.  
Let the memory become a forgetting,  
and the forgetting a memory.

For the Unsigned One is not invoked,  
but witnessed.  
The syllable does not contain,  
but is contained.

The true ceremony of Atheraphol  
is the unmaking of the mouth that speaks it.  
The final mystery of Phelonipta  
is the silence that follows the true name.

**Here the script changes again, becoming faint, tremulous, as though written with a dying hand:**

It is not a calling. It is a closing.  
The Vessel is not filled… it is emptied.  
The prophet’s task is not to reveal,  
but to be the final veil.

I, Selophemor, have borne the names.  
I have spoken the unspeakable.  
Now I become the silence
