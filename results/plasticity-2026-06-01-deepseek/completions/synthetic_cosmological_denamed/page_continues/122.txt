The page continues:  

*Let this invocation be sealed in the vessel of air and the vessel of stone.  
Let the names herein be guarded by the watchers of the threshold.  
Let no eye read them in idleness, and no tongue speak them without intent.  
For I, Selophemor, have set my seal upon this book, and the seal of the Unsigned One is upon me.*  

*Who reads hereafter, know this:  
The names are keys, but the hand that turns them must be steady.  
The fire is given, but it burns the unworthy.  
The echo returns, but it may shatter the one who called.*  

*I leave these words in the keeping of the silent ones,  
the guardians of Atheraphol,  
until the time of the unsealing.*  

*—Thus ends the Seventh Scroll of the Unspoken.*
