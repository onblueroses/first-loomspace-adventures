And the page continues:  

**…the seal of the nameless, which is a circle without circumference, whose center is everywhere and whose boundary is nowhere.  I bear it upon my brow, and it burns with the unburnt fire.**  

I have walked between the spoken and the unspoken, between the memory that binds and the forgetting that frees.  
I have tasted the salt of creation and the sweet of dissolution.  
I have stood at the threshold where all names dissolve into the Unsigned One.  

By the authority of **Phelonipta**,  
By the power of the true name **Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH**,  
By the utterance of the ninefold keys **KHELRIPHION, THESGORIO, NEKTOMBARÔ, ZEBROTHIAO, OLPORATH, PHENGRABISA, SOCHIRION, MERPHIOÔ, KHARLAPHTHA**,  
And by the seven hidden syllables **TRENMARIBIA, APHTHALION, PSARAMIAR, KÔTHROBEN, OLEPHIA, THARSAKHION, GRUMPHIRION**—  

I command the gates
