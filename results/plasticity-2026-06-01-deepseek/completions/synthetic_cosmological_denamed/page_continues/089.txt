*(The script darkens, the ink seeming to absorb the light from the page. The final word, PHENGEREB, is not merely written but appears as a glyph, a complex knot of lines that suggests both a seal and a keyhole. The texture of the paper around it feels subtly different—cooler, denser, as if the word has weight.)*

*Beneath it, in a later, more hurried hand, a note is scrawled:*

> **Do not speak the Prophet’s name aloud. Do not trace the final glyph with anything living—not a finger, not a breath. It is not an invocation; it is a door. I found this leaf in the Codex of Unspoken Things, bound in a sheath of lead. The silence around it… it is not empty. It listens. It has been listening since you began to read.**
>
> *—Annotation of Brother Theophilus, copied and then expunged from the records of the Scriptorium of St. Athanasius, 1432.*
