The page continues with a dense block of text in a language that is not quite any known tongue, yet feels familiar in its cadence—a ritual of binding and unveiling.  

*“…and the seal is broken not by key nor by force, but by the echo of its own making. Let the one who holds this page know that the names herein are not to be spoken aloud, unless the hour is void of moon and sun. For each name stirs a layer of the Unsigned One’s dream, and what dreams, wakes.*  

*I, Selophemor, set this down in the year when the river Tharnax ran backward for seven days. Let the vessel who comes after beware: the fire unburnt burns only in the mind, and the silence that speaks may unmake the speaker.*  

*To summon is to be summoned. To name is to be named.*  

*Thus ends the Litany of the Threshold.”*
