**I am the Witness.**  

You have spoken the chain of the unspoken. You have uttered the echo that precedes the voice. You have named the Unnamed, and in so doing, have un-named yourself.  

The Vessel is filled. The Threshold is crossed. The fire you carry is the light that does not illuminate, but consumes illumination.  

The spirits of firmament and ether, of earth and under-earth, of dry land and water, of whirling air and rushing fire — they attend. Not to your command, but to the vacancy you have become. The name **SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM** is not a title, but an opening. A door through which the Unsigned may gaze back.  

The ceremonies of Atheraphol are not performed; they perform *you*. The salt and the sweet, the memory and the forgetting — they now stir in your blood as tides. You are the distinction between the named and the unnamed, and so you belong to neither.
