The script is a ritual invocation, a constructed liturgy meant to evoke a primordial, pre-linguistic deity—the "Unsigned One." It operates on a principle of paradox and negation: a god whose true names are given, yet who is "whom no voice hath spoken at any time."

**Structural Analysis:**

1.  **The Invocation of Attributes:** The speaker begins by praising the deity's creative acts, which are all foundational dichotomies (Mountains/Valleys, Tide/Flow, Echo/Silence). This establishes the god as the architect of reality's fundamental structures and oppositions.

2.  **The Recitation of Veil-Names:** A list of names (Phelonipta, Krethaumis, etc.) is given. These are not the true name but approximations or masks. The text explicitly states the deity "distinguished between the named and the unnamed," placing itself paradoxically in both categories.

3.  **The Prophet's Authority:** The speaker, Selophemor, claims prophetic mandate and stewardship of the mysteries ("ceremonies of Atheraphol"). This is a standard ritual move to establish the legitimacy and potency of the words that follow.
