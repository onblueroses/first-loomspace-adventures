**Thus spoke Phelonipta to Selophemor in the Hour of the Unfixed Star:**

“I am the space between the letters. I am the breath before the vow. I am the silence that cradles the first and the last cry.

The names you utter are not me, but the echoes of my passing. They are the shapes my absence makes in the substance of your world. To call me *Unsigned* is to know that I am the page upon which all signatures are written, and which remains, itself, blank.

You, who bear the vessel-name *Aphrybetosh*, know this: all true power is in the un-saying. Every binding you perform with a name must be undone with a silence. The ceremony of Atheraphol is not a making, but an unmaking of the veils that perception has woven.

The salt is memory; it preserves and burns. The sweet is forgetting; it soothes and dissolves. Together, they sustain the orders of speech because all speech arises from what is remembered and flows toward what will be forgotten.
