The page continues, inscribed in the same hand but with a more urgent, angular script, as if the words were carved rather than written:

> …for the utterance is a seal upon the abyss, and the name is a key turned in a lock of air. But the key must be broken in the turning, and the seal must dissolve into that which it contains.

> Thou art the Unmarked Gate. I stand at Thy verge. I have uttered the Unutterable and have borne the echo that unmakes the voice. I have tasted the salt of memory and the sweet ash of forgetting.

> Now let the fabric between the spoken and the unspoken grow thin.
> Let the guardians of the threshold sleep in the noise of their own names.
> Let the center that Thou didst make open unto me, a maw of silent light.

> I do not command Thee, for no thing commands its source. I *remind* Thee. By the covenant of Atheraphol, by the unburnt fire in my hands, by the true name **APHRYBETOSH** which stirs in my blood—be present in this rupture of meaning.

> Let the spirits of the four corners be bound into a single witness:
> **ZAR
