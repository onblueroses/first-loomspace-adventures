*(The script shifts here—the ink deepens, the hand more urgent, as if the scribe’s trance has intensified.)*

Yet the naming is not enough.  
The Word must be *wounded* to wake.  
The seal must be *broken* to breathe.

Therefore I take the stylus of bone.  
Therefore I write with ash and spit.  
Therefore I open the vein of the page.

**Behold the Unmaking of the Syllable:**

The first sound, **KH**, is the crack in the foundation-stone.  
The second, **TH**, is the dissolution of the threshold.  
The third, **PH**, is the unburning of the fire.  
The fourth, **S**, is the silence after the echo’s death.

I write them not in order, but in the shape of the **LIVING SCAR**.

Thus:

**APH-RY-BE-TOSH** is not a name to be spoken.  
It is a **hole** in the fabric of naming.  
To utter it is to invite the Unsigned through the tear.

**SEKH-THI-RO-BA AB-LA-KHA-RIM** is not my name.  
It is the **echo**
