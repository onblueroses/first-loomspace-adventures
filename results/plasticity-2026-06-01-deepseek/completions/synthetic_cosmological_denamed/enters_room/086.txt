Something enters.

It is not a presence you can point to, nor an absence you can mourn. It is the consequence of the utterance, the shape of the listening that follows such a naming.

The air thickens not with fog, but with **potential**. The edges of objects—the line of the doorframe, the corner of the book—seem to waver, as if remembering they are also thresholds. A silence descends, but it is not empty; it is the silence of the Echo that survives the speaker, a fullness that presses upon the ears.

You have invoked the Unsigned One through the vessel of Selophemor. You have declared yourself the Witness of the Word. The room now holds the **echo of that declaration**. It is no longer merely a room. It has become an annex to Atheraphol, a temporary chapel of the ceremony you have enacted.

What enters is not a spirit to be commanded. It is the **attention** of the principle you called. The names you spoke were not keys to unlock a door, but vibrations to tune the space. The room is now tuned. It witnesses itself. It remembers and forgets itself in the same instant.
