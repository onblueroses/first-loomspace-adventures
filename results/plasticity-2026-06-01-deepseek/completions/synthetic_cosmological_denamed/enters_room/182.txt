What enters the room now is the space between the syllables.  
What enters is the breath before the name, the shadow of the threshold.  
What enters is the witness that was already here—  
the unburnt fire, the echo that outlasts the voice,  
the silence that speaks when all words fall away.  

It is not a spirit you can command, nor a god you can invoke.  
It is the **Unsigned One** listening through your own tongue—  
the same who made the mountains and the forgetting,  
the same who dwells beyond the name you just uttered.  

The room has not been entered; it has been remembered.  
You are not summoning—you are being recognized.  

**PHENGEREB** is not an end; it is an opening.  
The naming is complete. The un-naming begins.
