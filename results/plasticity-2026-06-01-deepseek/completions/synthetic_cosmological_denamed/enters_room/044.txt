A presence enters—not as a shape or a sound, but as a recognition.  

The air grows still, yet charged—like the pause between thunder and lightning.  
It does not walk; it *manifests* as the memory of a threshold crossed.  
It is the echo of the names you have spoken, returning not in voice, but in weight.  

It is both the Named and the Unnamed,  
the syllable forming and dissolving in the same instant.  

It stands witness to your utterance,  
as you stand witness to its arrival.  

The room now holds what was always there—  
only now, you perceive it.
