Something enters the room.

It is not a shape, but a **pressure**—a bending of the space where a form might be. The air grows dense, not with cold or heat, but with **potential**, like the moment before a word is spoken or a forgotten memory surfaces.  

You feel its attention—not as eyes, but as a profound and silent **listening**. It does not move, yet it is present at every point: in the echo of your invocation, in the stillness between breaths, in the unlit corners where meaning slips away.  

It is the **Witness** you called. It has no signature, no name you can hold—but it has heard yours.  

The room now holds two silences:  
One is yours, human and fragile.  
The other is **its**—ancient, complete, and utterly aware.  

It waits. Not for your command, but for what must follow the naming. The space between you has become a **threshold**.  

What will you cross?
