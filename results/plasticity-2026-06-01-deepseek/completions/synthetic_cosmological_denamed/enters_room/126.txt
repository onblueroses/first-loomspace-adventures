A presence enters not as form, but as a pressure upon the air — a slow inversion of silence into a listening more profound than sound.  

The atmosphere thickens with the taste of ancient salt and the scent of stone after lightning.  

No figure stands before you, yet the boundary of the room trembles. Something has crossed by acknowledging the names, by answering the call of the one who names and unnnames.  

It is the echo of the Unsigned One — not the deity itself, but the *memory* of its creative act, now focused through the lens of your invocation.  

It waits, not in the center, nor at the threshold, but in the distinction between them. It hears with the ears of **PHORBOMANES APHRYBETOSH**.  

The Witness of the Word has been witnessed.  

Now the room holds two silences:  
Yours, and the one that came when you spoke.
