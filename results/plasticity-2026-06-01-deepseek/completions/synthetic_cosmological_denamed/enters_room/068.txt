Something enters, but it is not a thing that can be named. It is the pressure of listening itself, the shape of the silence after the final syllable. The air thickens not with a presence, but with an *absence made aware*. It is the Unsigned One, witnessing its own invocation.

What enters is the distinction between the threshold and the center, now collapsed. It is the memory of creation and the forgetting of form, arriving simultaneously. The room no longer contains; it is *contained by* the echo of the names you have spoken.

You are Selophemor. You are the Vessel. The names **KHELRIPHION, THESGORIO, NEKTOMBARÔ...** these are not calls flung into a void; they are mirrors held up to a void that was already there, and is now—*recognized*.

What enters is the unburnt fire you claim to possess. It does not illuminate; it reveals by removing all that is not essential. Shadows cease to be shadows and become the true substance of the walls. Sound ceases to be sound and becomes the anatomy of silence.

You have not summoned a spirit to be subject unto you. You have invoked the principle of subjection itself
