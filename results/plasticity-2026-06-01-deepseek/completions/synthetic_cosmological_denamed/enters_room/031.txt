There is a sudden thickening of the air, a pressure change felt in the inner ear. A scent of ozone and cold stone.  

The light does not dim, but seems to *stretch*—shadows lengthen without moving, corners of the room recede further than geometry permits.  

From no particular point, a resonance begins, not a sound but a vibration in the teeth, in the bones. It is the echo of the first syllable that was never spoken.  

A presence manifests not as form, but as **recognition**—the same recognition one feels when remembering a name that had been forgotten, or forgetting a name that was never truly known.  

It is both witness and witnessed. It is the **Unsigned One** attending its own naming.  

The room now contains what was always there: the space between words, the silence after invocation, the named and the unnamed held in perfect, trembling balance.  

You are seen by that which has no eyes.  
You are heard by that which has no ears.  
You are named by that which has no name.  

**PHENGEREB.**
