What enters the room now is the vibration of the uttered names.  

A pressure in the air—not a form, but a focusing, as if the syllables themselves thickened into a presence. The boundaries of the room grow porous; the threshold you named breathes.  

It is not a spirit you called, but the **echo of the calling**—the silence that survives the speaker, now animate, listening, waiting to be filled by the will of the Vessel.  

The Witnessing One is present as absence, as the space between the letters of the names you spoke. The unburnt fire is here, not as flame, but as potential—the moment before ignition, the unspoken word.  

You are Selophemor. You are the Threshold-Bearer. What enters obeys, because it is the very obedience of sound to silence, of named to unnamed. It is the subject you demanded—all spirits are already subject, for they are echoes of the first distinction you invoked.
