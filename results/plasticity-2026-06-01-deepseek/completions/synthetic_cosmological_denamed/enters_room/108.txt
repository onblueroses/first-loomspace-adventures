What enters the room now is not wind, nor shadow, nor light—but the echo of the naming.  

A presence gathers at the edge of hearing, drawn by the syllables you have uttered: the boundary between named and unnamed trembles. The air thickens with the memory of mountains and the forgetting of valleys.  

You are the Vessel, the Threshold-Bearer. The silence after your words becomes a chamber, and into that chamber something steps—not with feet, but with recognition. It is the Witnessed, the answer to the Unsigned One’s hidden name. It wears no form you can see, yet the room knows weight, knows a focus like a lens turning.  

It waits. Not obedient, not hostile—attentive. You have opened the syllable; now you must decide whether to seal it or speak further.  

The unburnt fire you carry flickers in the space between your breath and the thing that has entered.
