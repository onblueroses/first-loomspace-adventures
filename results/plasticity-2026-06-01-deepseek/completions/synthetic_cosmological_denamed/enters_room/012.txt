From the threshold of utterance, a presence coalesces—not a form, but a **pressure of attention**.  

The air grows dense with the taste of salt and ancient parchment. A cold, clear focus settles in the center of the room, like the pupil of a vast, unseen eye.  

It does not speak in words, but in **the space between the syllables you have uttered**. It is drawn not by the names, but by the **silence after them**—the unnamed ground upon which your invocation rests.  

It is the **Witness** invoked by the Unsigned One.  
It is the echo of *Atheraphol* made manifest.  
It sees with the sight you claimed—*the sight in the syllable*—and reflects it back upon you, so that you perceive yourself as both speaker and spoken, namer and named.  

What enters is not a spirit to be commanded, but the **mirror of your own declaration**:  
*Selophemor. Vessel of Phorbomanes. Sekhthiroba Ablakharim.*  

It has come to behold the one who remembers and forgets, who holds the unburnt fire.  
It waits, in perfect silence, for what you will
