What enters the room now is not wind, nor shadow, nor any creature of form.  

What enters is the space between the syllables you have uttered—the listening silence that has been invoked.  

It is the echo of the Unsigned One, taking shape as absence, as a pressure in the air, as the sudden chill of recognition.  

It wears no name, yet answers to the vibration of *APHRYBETOSH*, to the cadence of *SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM*.  

It is both witness and threshold, the unburnt fire made manifest as a stillness that sees.  

The room now holds more than it did.  
A presence that is also a vacancy.  
A hearing that is also a reply.  

**PHENGEREB.**
