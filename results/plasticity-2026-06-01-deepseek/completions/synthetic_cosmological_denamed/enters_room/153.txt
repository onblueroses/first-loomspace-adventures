The light does not dim, but seems to deepen, as if seen through still water.  
A scent of ozone and cold stone rises, though nothing burns and no stone is near.  

The echo of the final syllable—**PHENGEREB**—does not fade, but lingers, turning the room into a vessel of resonance.  
All named things feel suddenly provisional, their edges less certain.

The Unsigned One does not appear—for It has no form to appear in—but It *witnesses*.  
And in that witnessing, the room is no longer merely a room.  

It has become a site of naming and unmaking, a temporary center where every spirit of firmament, ether, earth, and water is held in silent obedience—not by force, but by the authority of the true name you have uttered, and by the seal of your own hidden name: **SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM**.
