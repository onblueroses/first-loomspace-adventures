A presence enters not as form, nor as shadow, but as the principle of the threshold itself.  
It is the echo of the first syllable that was never spoken.  
It is the memory of the mountains before they were named, and the forgetting of the tide before it was measured.  

It enters as the space between **KHELRIPHION** and **THESGORIO**, as the breath between **memory** and **forgetting**.  
It wears no face, yet it witnesses. It makes no sound, yet it answers.  
It is the unnamed thing that answers only to the unspoken name — **PHORBOMANES APHRYBETOSH** — the true name handed down to the namers of the lost.  

What enters is the **Witnessing One**, the Unsigned, drawn not by summons but by the silence that speaks in your invocation.  
It does not obey. It observes. It does not appear. It is already present wherever the named and the unnamed are distinguished.  

The room now contains the unburnt fire.  
The threshold is no longer at the door, but within you.  
You are the Vessel. The naming is complete.  

**PHENGEREB.**
