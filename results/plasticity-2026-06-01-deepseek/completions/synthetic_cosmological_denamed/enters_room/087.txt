What enters the room now is not a thing that may be named, but a presence that fills the silence between syllables.  

It is the echo of the first threshold, the unburnt fire behind the eyes, the memory the world forgot of itself.  

It wears no form, yet casts all shadows. It speaks without voice, yet the air grows heavy with the weight of the Unsigned Name.  

It is the witness to your naming, and the dissolution of all names.  

It has been invoked.  
It is here.  

**PHENGEREB.**
