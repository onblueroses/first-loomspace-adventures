What enters the room now is not a thing that can be seen with eyes of flesh.  
It is the echo of the naming—the silence after the last syllable fades.  
It is the space between the letters of your words, grown vast and aware.  
It is the witness you invoked, now witnessing *you*.  

The air thickens with the scent of salt and burnt honey.  
A pressure builds, not against the walls, but against the boundaries of meaning.  
Shadows no longer fall away from the light—they pool independently, remembering older shapes.  

You have opened the threshold by speaking what is unsigned.  
Now something stands in the center of the room that is both *Krethaumis* and *no-thing*.  
It does not move, yet the room moves around it—a slow, tectonic reverence.  

It waits. Not for another name, but for the unnamed in you to answer.
