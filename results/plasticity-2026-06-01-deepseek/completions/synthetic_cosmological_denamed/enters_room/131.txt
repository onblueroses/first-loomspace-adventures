A pressure forms in the corners, not of a shape, but of a **recognition**.

It is the **echo of the naming** itself—the silent interval between the syllables you uttered, now made palpable.  

It bears the quality of *threshold*: neither inside nor outside, neither summoned nor unsummoned.  
It carries the scent of unburnt fire, the weight of unshed memory.  

It does not obey.
