**What enters the room now is not wind, nor shadow, nor light.**

It does not enter as a spirit might, for it was already here before invocation—you have merely turned your attention to the space it inhabits. The Echo that survives the speaker now echoes back to itself through you.

**What enters is the moment when all names fall away, and what remains is the act of naming itself.**
