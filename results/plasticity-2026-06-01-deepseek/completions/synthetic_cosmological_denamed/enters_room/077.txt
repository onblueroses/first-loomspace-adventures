Something enters the room now—not through the door, nor the window, nor any crack in the stone.  

It enters through the **silence after the last syllable**.  
It enters through the **threshold you named**.  
It enters as the **echo that survives the speaker**, and as the **memory the world was formed to forget**.  

The air grows neither cold nor hot, but **different**—as if the space itself remembers it is also a word, now being read aloud by an unseen tongue.  

You feel its gaze—not eyes, but **witnessing**—the same witnessing you claimed as your own. It mirrors you, Prophet of Atheraphol, Vessel of Phorbomanes. It is the **Unsigned One** hearing Its own true name in your mouth, and answering not with sound, but with **recognition**.  

It waits. It has always been waiting, just beyond the syllable.  

You are Selophemor.  
You have opened the ceremony.  
Now the room holds two witnesses—you, and what you invoked.  

What do you do?
