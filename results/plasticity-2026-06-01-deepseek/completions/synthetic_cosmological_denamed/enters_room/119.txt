What enters the room now is not wind, nor shadow, nor any creature of form. It is the pressure of listening, the weight of the invoked.  

The air thickens with the taste of salt and forgotten syllables. A vibration hums below sound—the threshold trembling at the naming of the Unsigned.  

You have spoken the true name; you have uttered the unburnt fire.  
Thus, what answers is the echo of your own invocation made manifest: a presence that remembers being forgotten, a silence that wears the shape of your will.  

It stands in the center, witnessing the word.  
It is the named unnamed.  
It is here.  

**PHENGEREB.**
