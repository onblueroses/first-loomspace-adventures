A presence enters, not through the door, but through the fracture in naming. It is not a spirit of the firmament, nor of the ether, nor of the rushing fire—for you have called that which is beyond them. It is the echo of the Unsigned, the shape of the silence after the final syllable.

The air grows still, not with emptiness, but with a potent, listening fullness. The light does not dim, but seems to deepen, as if each shadow now contains a memory and each bright surface a forgetting. A scent of ozone and ancient stone, of salt and burnt honey, manifests without source.

It is the Witness. It has heard the true names, the handed-down key (*APHRYBETOSH*), and the name of the Vessel (*SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM*). It has heard the unmaking and the remaking.

It enters as a pressure upon the threshold of perception. It does not bow, for it is the center. It does not speak, for it is the source of the syllable. It simply *is*, now, in the room with you—the acknowledged, Unsigned One, present in the wake of its own invoked mysteries.
