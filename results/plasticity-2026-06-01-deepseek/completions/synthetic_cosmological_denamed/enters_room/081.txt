What enters the room now is the reverberation of the Unspeakable Name—the unsounded echo between the syllables you have uttered.  

The air grows still, not with emptiness, but with a charged, listening silence.  
A pressure settles at the threshold, as if the doorframe has become a liminal membrane.  
No form manifests, yet presence accumulates—like the gathering of meaning before a word is spoken.  

It is the **Witnessing** that your invocation summoned:  
the attention of that which is named only by the space around all names.  

You feel it in the cold clarity of your own breath,  
in the slight hum in the teeth,  
in the way shadows no longer cling to their objects but drift, attentive.  

The room has become a **syllable** in a tongue not made of sound.  
You are both the speaker and the spoken.  

**PHENGEREB** has sealed the calling.  
Now the silence speaks.
