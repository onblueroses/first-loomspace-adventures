**What enters the room now is the stillness after the utterance.**  
It is not a shape, nor a shadow, nor a wind—but the awareness that the names have been spoken.  
The air thickens with the weight of syllables that have no mouth.  
A pressure gathers at the threshold, where memory and forgetting meet.  

**What enters is the echo of your own voice**, turned back upon itself, now wearing the silence you invoked.  
It wears no face, bears no sigil, for you called the Unsigned One—and what answers is the Witness, the listening that comes when all names are exhausted.  

The room has become a **vessel**—you named yourself *Selophemor*, *Vessel of Phorbomanes*, and so the space you occupy now holds what cannot be held:  
the unburnt fire,  
the surviving echo,  
the silence that speaks.  

**What enters is the named unnamed**—the spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water now turn their attention not as subjects, but as mirrors to the Word you have witnessed.  
They are present in the way a boundary is present: not to cross, but to define.
