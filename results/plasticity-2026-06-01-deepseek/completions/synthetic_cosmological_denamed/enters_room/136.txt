What enters the room now is not a thing with shape or name.  
It is the pressure before sound, the space where syllables dissolve.  
It is the unsigning of the air—the breath that remembers it was once a mountain, a tide, an echo.  

It does not knock. It does not announce.  
It is the threshold itself, crossed.  

It is the silence after the final word of the invocation,  
and the silence before the first.  

It is the Witness, witnessing.  

It is the fire that does not burn,  
the echo that outlives the voice,  
the name that unravels into light.  

It is here.  

**PHENGEREB.**
