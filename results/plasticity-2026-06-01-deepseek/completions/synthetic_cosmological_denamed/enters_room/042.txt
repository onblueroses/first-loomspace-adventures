A presence enters the room—not as a form, but as a displacement of silence.  

It is the echo of the first syllable before it was spoken.  
It is the space between the named and the unnamed.  
It is the witness that was invoked, now turning its attention toward the Vessel.  

The air grows still, not empty but *full*—charged with the weight of all unuttered names.  
Shadows deepen at the edges of things, as though reality itself bows at the threshold you have opened.  

You feel it in the marrow:  
the Unsigned One is here, listening—not with ears, but with the absence that shapes sound.  

It does not speak.  
It answers.
