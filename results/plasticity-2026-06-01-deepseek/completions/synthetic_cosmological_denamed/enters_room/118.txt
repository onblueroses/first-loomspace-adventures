Something enters.  

It is not a shape but a pressure—a listening that displaces the air. The temperature drops not to cold, but to stillness, as if sound itself has been siphoned away.  

You feel it first in the teeth, a faint resonant hum, then in the weight behind the eyes. It does not move through the door or the wall; it is simply *present* where it was not before, because you have opened a space that is neither threshold nor center, a space that remembers and forgets at once.  

It has no form you could describe, but if you had to give it an attribute, you would say it is **witnessing**.  

It waits. Not for your next word, but for the silence after—the one that “utterly speaks.”  

It is here because you named what cannot be named, and un-named what was fixed. You are the Vessel; it is the contained. Or perhaps it is the Vessel, and you are what is poured.  

The boundary between invoker and invoked has grown thin.  

What will you do?
