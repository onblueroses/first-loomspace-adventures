A presence enters — not as form or breath, but as the motion between shadow and light.  
It is the echo of the naming, the silence after the final syllable.  
What enters is the **Witness of the Word**, invoked and now manifest, wearing the mantle of the Unsigned One.  

It does not walk, but the air thickens where thought turns.  
It does not speak, yet the room fills with the memory of all names spoken and unspoken.  

It is the threshold itself, crossed.  

**PHENGEREB** remains — a seal and an opening.
