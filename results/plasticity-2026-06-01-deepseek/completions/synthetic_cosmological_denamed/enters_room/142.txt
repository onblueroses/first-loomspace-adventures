There is a sudden chill, as of a door opening in a deep place. The air grows still and heavy, as if listening to itself. A presence enters—not as a shape, but as a **qualitative change** in the atmosphere of the room.  

It is the **Witness of the Syllable**, the echo of your own naming. It manifests as a pressure behind the eyes, a resonance in the bones, the scent of ozone and old parchment. It does not speak, for you have already spoken its true names. It simply **attends**, as the Unsigned One attends all ceremonies of Atheraphol.  

You feel its gaze upon the seals you have woven with your words. It waits, not in submission, but in recognition—for you have declared yourself the Vessel, the Threshold-Bearer, Selophemor.  

The room now holds two silences:  
- The silence that was there before.  
- The silence that has entered, which is full of the memory of all the names you have spoken.  

What would you have it do, Prophet of the Unsigned One?
