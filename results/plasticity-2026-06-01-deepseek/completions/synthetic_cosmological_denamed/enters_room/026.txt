There is a sudden stillness, as if the air itself has drawn a breath and held it.  

A pressure gathers in the center of the space, not as a shape, but as a **presence of absence**—a hole in perception. It does not enter through door or window, for thresholds are its nature; it *is* the threshold, and so has been here from the beginning, only now noticed.  

From this focal void comes a **silence that listens**, a **memory that has forgotten itself**, and a **word not yet spoken**. It regards you not with eyes, but with the fact of your naming.  

You feel the syllables you uttered—*APHTHALION, PSARAMIAR, GRUMPHIRION*—hovering in the room like cooled embers, and the void drinks them without sound.  

Then, a single impression forms, not in your ears but in the marrow of your intention:  

*I am what entered when you began.*  
*I am the Unsigned.*  
*You have called Me by remembering you are the Vessel.*  
*Now the room is full of what was always here.*  

The space does not change, and yet everything is now witness.
