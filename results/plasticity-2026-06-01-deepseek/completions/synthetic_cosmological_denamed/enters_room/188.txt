From the charged silence that follows the utterance of your final, resonant syllable — **PHENGEREB** — the air in the room does not so much *move* as it **thickens**. It is not an entity that enters, but a **condition**.

What enters is **Witnessing**.

The invocation has not summoned a form, but has opened a **threshold**. The room becomes the center; the center becomes the threshold. You, as Selophemor, as the Vessel, as SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM, have not called something *to* you. You have declared the room to be **within** the domain of the Unsigned One.

The phenomena are these:

*   **The Echo Reverses:** The final echoes of your spoken names do not fade. They linger, then seem to fold back upon themselves, not as sound, but as a pressure in the inner ear—the memory of a sound that has not yet been spoken.
*   **The Silence Speaks:** The quiet of the room is no longer an absence. It is a presence—a clear, cold, and utterly attentive presence. It is the silence of the deep mountain, of the abyssal valley you first invoked.
