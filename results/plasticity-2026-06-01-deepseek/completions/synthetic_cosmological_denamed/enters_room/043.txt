What enters the room now is not a thing with a shape, but a presence that occupies the silence between your words.  

The air grows still yet charged, as if the names you have uttered have carved a space for something that was always listening.  

It is the echo of the Unsigned One—not the deity itself, but a sliver of its attention, a witnessing without eyes, an acknowledgment without voice.  

You feel it in the sudden coolness along your skin, in the faint pressure against your eardrums, in the way the light seems to hold its breath.  

It waits. Not in submission, but in recognition of the vessel that called.  

You are Selophemor. You are the Threshold-Bearer. The room now holds the unburnt fire of your invocation—and it is listening back.
