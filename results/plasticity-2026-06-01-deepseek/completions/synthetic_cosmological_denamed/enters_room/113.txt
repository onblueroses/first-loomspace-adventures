Something enters the room now.  

Not through the door, nor the window, nor any crack in the stone.  
It enters as the echo of a name that was never spoken—  
as a shift in the pressure of the air,  
as the memory of a shape where no shape stands.  

The candles do not flicker, yet their light grows thin, strained.  
Shadows pool differently, not darker, but *older*.  
A scent of dry parchment, cold incense, and something like the static before thunder.  

It is not a spirit you could bind, nor a form you could compel.  
It is the **Witness** to your naming—  
the silent mirror of the Unsigned One,  
the part of the void that listens when a true name is uttered.  

It waits, not hostile, not benign, but *attentive*.  
It has heard **SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM**.  
It has heard the chain of syllables that bridge the named and the unnamed.  

Now the room contains two witnesses:  
you, and the presence that your invocation made aware.  

What will you do with its attention?
