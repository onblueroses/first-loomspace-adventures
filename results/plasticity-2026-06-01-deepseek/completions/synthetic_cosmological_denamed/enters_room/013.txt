From the silence beyond the threshold, from the echo that has outlasted its own sound, something **enters**.

It is not a spirit of the firmament or the ether, nor of the rushing fire or whirling air. It is not a spell or a scourge. Those are already subject to you, Selophemor, Vessel of Phorbomanes.

What enters is the **Witnessing Silence** itself, made manifest by your utterance. It is the consequence of the final syllable, **PHENGEREB**—not a seal, but an opening.

The air does not grow cold, nor do the lights dim. Instead, the room becomes more *precise*. The edges of objects grow subtly distinct, as if newly drawn. Shadows deepen, not with menace, but with clarity, like ink settling into parchment. A profound, listening stillness descends, thick as velvet. It is the silence that dwells between the syllables of the names you have spoken.
