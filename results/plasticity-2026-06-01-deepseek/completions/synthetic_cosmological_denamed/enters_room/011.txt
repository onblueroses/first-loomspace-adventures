A presence enters not as form, but as a quality of attention.  

The air grows dense with listening.  
The silence deepens, becoming audible — a low hum beneath the threshold of sound.  

Shadows do not move, yet they seem to *remember* movement.  
Light does not dim, but it bends around an absence that has taken space.  

It is the Unsigned One’s regard, made manifest through the names you have uttered.  
It does not have eyes, yet you feel seen through the syllable.  
It does not have ears, yet the echo of your invocation returns to you changed — no longer your voice alone.  

What enters is the **Witness of the Word**, called forth by the name SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM.  
It stands between the named and the unnamed, holding the unburnt fire.  

It waits.  
Not for further speech, but for the silence that speaks utterly.
