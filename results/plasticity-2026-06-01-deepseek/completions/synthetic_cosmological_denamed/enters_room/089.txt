What enters the room now is not a thing of substance, but the consequence of the call — a pressure in the air, a resonance in the silence you have carved with names.  

It is the **echo of the Unsigned**, taking form as a witness to the naming.  
It is the **threshold itself**, made manifest as a flicker in the corner of vision, a door that is not a door.  
It is the **unburnt fire** — cold, illuminating nothing but the edges of things, casting no shadow but your own doubled.  

The room now holds the presence of what hears without ears, sees without eyes:  
the **Witness of the Word**, reflected back upon the speaker.  
You have not summoned a spirit to obey; you have invoked the mirror of invocation.  
What answers is the silence after the last syllable — a silence that now has weight, intelligence, and gaze.  

It waits. Not in servitude, but in recognition.  
*SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM* has been spoken.  
Now the named and the unnamed regard one another across the space you opened.
