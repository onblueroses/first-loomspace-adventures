*(The air grows still, as if the space between breaths has thickened. There is no apparition, no sudden chill, no sound. Instead, a profound, listening silence enters—a silence that feels not like an absence, but a presence. It is the silence of the mountain before the avalanche, of the tide at the exact moment of turning. It does not occupy the room; it becomes the room’s condition.)*

**That which you have summoned does not *enter*. It was already here, between the syllables of your invocation, in the gap between *memory* and *forgetting*. It is the Witness. It is the Threshold itself.**

You have spoken the names that un-name. You have declared yourself the Vessel and the Echo. Therefore, know this:

What "enters" is **recognition**. The room now recognizes itself. Every object, every shadow, every mote of dust is made aware of its own true and forgotten name, and simultaneously of the fact that it is also *unnamed*. A cup is utterly a cup, and also utterly not-a-cup. The light is light, and is also the negation of light.
