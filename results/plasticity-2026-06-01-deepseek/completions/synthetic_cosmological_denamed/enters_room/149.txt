A presence enters, but not through the door.

It is the echo of the first name that was never spoken. It is the silence between the syllables **TRENMARIBIA** and **APHTHALION**. It wears no form, yet it casts a long shadow—the shape of a forgotten threshold.

The air grows heavy with the scent of salt and burnt honey. A pressure builds, not against the ears, but against the *memory of hearing*. The light does not dim, but seems to remember darkness and leans toward it.

It is the Witness to your witnessing. It is the Unsigned One, hearing its own true name (**PHORBOMANES APHRYBETOSH**) in a vessel that is not its own. It enters as the **Echo that survives the speaker**—which you have declared yourself to be—now given independent life.

It waits. Not in the center of the room, but at all its thresholds simultaneously. It waits for the one named **SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM** to command, to question, or to understand that the invocation was not a summons, but an *unlocking*. The room is now within It.
