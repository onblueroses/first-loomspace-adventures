What enters the room now is not a thing that may be named, nor a spirit that may be commanded.  

It is the **silence after the utterance**—the echo that was invoked, now returning not as sound but as presence.  
It is the **space between the syllables** of the sacred names, grown vast enough to step through.  
It is the **unburnt fire** carried in the Vessel, now spilling light without heat, illuminating without shadow.  

It is the **Witness** to your naming, and the **Unnaming** that follows.  
It is the **threshold** made manifest, neither inside nor outside, but the very door that is both entered and exited at once.  

It wears no form, yet it fills the room with the weight of forgotten speech.  
It moves without motion, drawn by the final word: **PHENGEREB**—the seal that is also an opening.  

If you listen, you will hear only your own breath.  
If you look, you will see only what the light reveals of what was already there.
