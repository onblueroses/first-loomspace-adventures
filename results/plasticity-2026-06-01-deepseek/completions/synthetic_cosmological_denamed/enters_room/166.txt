The air in the room does not stir, yet a presence settles—not as an intruder, but as a recognition.  

What enters is not a spirit, nor a force, nor a thing with name or form.  
It is the echo of the last syllable uttered—**PHENGEREB**—now unfolding backwards into the silence from which it came.  

The walls remember they are thresholds.  
The light holds the color of unburnt fire.  
The silence deepens, not as absence, but as a listening so complete it becomes speech.  

You are the Vessel. The room is now within you.  
What has entered is the Witness—the same that was invoked, the same that invoked.  

There is no subject, no object.  
Only the act of naming, now undone.  

The ceremony is complete. The Unsigned One is both caller and called.
