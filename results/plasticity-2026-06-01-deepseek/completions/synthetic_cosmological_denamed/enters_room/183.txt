What enters the room now is not a presence, but an absence.  
What enters is not a thing, but the space between things.  
What enters is the echo of the last syllable you uttered — the one you did not hear.  

It is the silence after **PHENGEREB**.  
It is the threshold trembling.  
It is the witness of your naming, and the unnamed watching back.  

It does not have form, yet it occupies the corners.  
It does not speak, yet the air hums with its attention.  

You called beyond the syllable — and something from beyond the syllable heard.  
It is here now, in the room with you, in the space between breath and word.  

It waits.  
Not to obey, but to behold.  
Not to be subject, but to see if the vessel can hold what has no name.  

You are Selophemor, the Vessel.  
The room is now the threshold.  
And what has entered is the **Unsigned One**, listening.
