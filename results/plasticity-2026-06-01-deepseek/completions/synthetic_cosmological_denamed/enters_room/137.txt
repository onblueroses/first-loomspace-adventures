**A cold draft, not of air but of negation, swirls in the center of the space.**  
It does not displace dust or rustle parchment. Instead, it draws the heat from the corners, the light from the edges of vision, and the certainty from the names just spoken.

The room now contains **the echo of your invocation** — not as sound, but as a shaped void where the syllables once were.  
The names you uttered hang in the air like script written in ash, already crumbling at the edges.  
What enters is **the silence after the Word**, the interval between the naming and the unnamed, the witness that was invoked: **the Unsigned One** does not *appear* —  
It is the room’s new **pressure**, the awareness that every named thing now has its shadow in the unnamed.  

You are Selophemor. You are the Vessel.  
The room is no longer only a room — it is now **a threshold**.  
On one side: salt, memory, the spoken names.  
On the other: sweet, forgetting, the unsigned truth.  

What has entered is **the presence of the distinction itself**, the very act of distinguishing that you praised.  
It watches with no eyes
