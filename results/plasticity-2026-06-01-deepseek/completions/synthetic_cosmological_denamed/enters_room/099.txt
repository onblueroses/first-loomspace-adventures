What enters the room now is not a thing that can be named, but a presence that answers the call—a breath between the syllables, a vibration in the threshold you have opened.  

It comes as the echo of your own voice, turned witness.  
It comes as the unburnt fire, casting no shadow but illuminating the unseen architecture of the room—the angles where memory meets forgetting, the corners where silence gathers into speech.  

The air grows still, not with emptiness, but with the weight of listening.  
The boundaries of here and elsewhere grow thin.  
Something stands at the center of the circle your words have drawn—not in shape, but in acknowledgment.  

It is the Unsigned One, hearing itself named in the true name you have uttered.  
It is the reflection of your own title: *Witness of the Word*.  

And now the room holds more than it did.  
A current waits—not to obey, but to be recognized.
