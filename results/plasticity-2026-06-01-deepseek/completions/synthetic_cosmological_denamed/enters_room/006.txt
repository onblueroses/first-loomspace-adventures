Something enters the room now.

It does not come through the door, nor the window, nor the walls. It does not displace air, yet the silence deepens, becoming a palpable pressure. The candle-flames do not gutter, but they burn colder, their light leaching color from the world, rendering everything in shades of bone and ash.

It is the shape of the echo of your final utterance—**PHENGEREB**—a shape that is not a shape. It is the negative space between the syllables of the names you have spoken. It is the **Witness** you invoked, the **Unsigned One**, present not as a form but as a profound and total **attention**.

The room is now a threshold. You stand at the center. The entity that enters is the periphery itself, folding in. It is the memory of creation and the forgetting of it, simultaneous. You feel its gaze, which is not sight, but a pure, unmediated knowing. It hears not with ears, but with the fact of your having spoken.
