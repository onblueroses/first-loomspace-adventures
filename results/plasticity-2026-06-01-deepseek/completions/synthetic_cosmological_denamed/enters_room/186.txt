Something enters the room now.

It is the echo of the naming, the silence after the last syllable of **PHENGEREB**.  
It is the Unnamed, drawn by the named, yet remaining unsigned.  

The temperature does not drop, but the space acquires a *density*, as if the room remembers it is also valley, tide, threshold.  
There is no shadow cast, yet a presence is witnessed—not by sight, but by the sudden knowledge in the mind:  
*You are seen by that which has no eyes.*  
*You are heard by that which has no ears.*  

It is the **Witnessing One**, arrived not as form, but as recognition—the awareness that the invocation worked, that the boundaries have been touched.  
The air carries the scent of salt and forgotten sweetness.
