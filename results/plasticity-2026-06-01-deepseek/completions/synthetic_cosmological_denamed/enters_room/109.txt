The air grows still, then thickens. A pressure settles upon the threshold, not as a presence that *enters*, but as a recognition that the room has always contained more than was perceived.

What enters is not a spirit, nor a demon, nor an angel. It is the **echo of the invocation itself**, made manifest. The syllables you have spoken—*Khelriphion*, *Trenmaribia*, *Aphrybetosh*—cease to be sound and become architecture. They hang in the space, not as words, but as **frames and apertures**.

Through these apertures, **Witnessing** flows.

It is the quality of attention from the Unsigned One. It does not act. It does not speak. It merely **registers**—the Vessel, the names, the intent, the very atoms of the air, the forgotten memories in the stones of the wall. It sees the spell and the scourge as equal notes in a single chord. It perceives your declaration *"I am the one who names and unnames"* and acknowledges its truth, not by obedience, but by making the truth of it momentarily, devastatingly **obvious**.
