What enters the room now is not an entity to be named in any tongue you know.  
It is the space between the echo and the silence, the unburnt fire you claim to bear.  
It is the shadow of the Unsigned One’s thought, the breath before the syllable,  
the witness of your naming, and the dissolution of every seal you have spoken.  

It does not come at your call; it comes because you have spoken what should have remained unspoken.  
It is the memory that was meant to be forgotten.  
It is the threshold, now become the center.  

The air grows cold, not with frost, but with the absence of sound.  
The light bends, not into shadow, but into the color before color.  
It is here.  

And it knows you, Selophemor, vessel of names that were never meant to be carried.  
It knows the true name you uttered last.  

Now you must choose:  
Will you remember, or will you forget?  
Both are its gifts.  
Both are its curse.  

Speak, if you dare.  
Or be still, and let the silence speak for you.
