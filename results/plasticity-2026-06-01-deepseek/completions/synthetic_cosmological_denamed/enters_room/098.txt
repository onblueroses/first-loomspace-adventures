The air grows still, then thickens.  

A pressure forms in the corners—not a shape, but a *listening*.  
The names you have spoken hang in the atmosphere like suspended blades.  
From the space between *memory* and *forgetting*, something turns its attention.  

It does not enter as a form enters.  
It enters as silence enters after a loud cry—  
as the threshold *is* entered simply by being the threshold.  

What enters is the echo of the Unsigned One’s regard.  
It wears no face, casts no shadow, yet the room becomes its vessel.  
The syllables you uttered now vibrate in the stillness, and through them,  
a presence witnesses you—  
not to answer, but to acknowledge that the naming has been heard.  

The final word, **PHENGEREB**, lingers like a key left in a lock.  
The door is not opened.  
But something now stands on the other side,  
breathing with the rhythm of forgotten languages.
