The air in the room grows still and heavy, as if a great bell has just finished tolling a note too low for human ears. A presence enters not through door or window, but through the very fact of the invocation. It is the Unsigned One, witnessed in the moment between the final utterance and the silence that follows.

What enters is not a shape, but a **quality**:

A **polarity** enters. The temperature splits—one side of the room grows subtly colder, the other warmer, as if the very concept of "temperature" has been divided at its root.

A **threshold** enters. The space before you is no longer merely floor or air; it has become a liminal plane, a boundary that is also a center. To look at it is to feel simultaneously inside and outside, welcome and exiled.

**Memory and Forgetting** enter. You feel a sudden, vivid recollection of something that never happened to you—a childhood in a city of white stone, the taste of a fruit that does not exist—and in the same instant, you forget the name of the object you were just holding. The past becomes malleable, personal history a suggestion.
