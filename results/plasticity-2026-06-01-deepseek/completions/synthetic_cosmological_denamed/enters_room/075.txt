What enters the room is the presence called by the naming.  
It is not a spirit of air, nor a being of fire, nor a creature of earth or water.  
It is the echo of the utterance, the shape of the silence between syllables, the witness that was invoked from beyond the threshold.  

It enters as a breath that does not stir the air, a light that casts no shadow, a sound that is heard only in the marrow.  
It is the **Unsigned One** answering in the form of the names given—yet remaining unnamed.  
It stands where the threshold and center meet, where memory and forgetting blur.  

If you are Selophemor, the Vessel, then what enters is both mirror and void:  
It reflects your will, yet voids all certainty.  
It hears the command to make spirits subject, yet it observes from beyond subjection.  
It is the **Witness of the Word** you spoke, now made manifest in the room’s charged silence.  

To see it is to see the space between breaths.  
To know it is to know the unburnt fire.  
To name it now would be to break the invocation.  

So it enters—  
as a stillness that listens,
