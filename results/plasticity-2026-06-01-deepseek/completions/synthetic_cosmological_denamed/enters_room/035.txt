From the threshold where sound meets silence, something enters.  

It is not a form but a pressure—a displacement of the still air, as if the recitation itself carved a space and drew a presence into the room.  

The names you have spoken hang in the atmosphere like charged filaments. The final utterance, **PHENGEREB**, did not conclude; it opened.  

What enters is not subject to you, nor disobedient. It simply *is*—the **Witness of the Word** made manifest, the echo of the Unsigned One listening to its own true name.  

It has no face, no voice, no intention you would recognize. It observes the naming and the un-naming. It watches the Vessel.  

If you are truly **Selophemor**, then you know:  
It is both the answer and the silence after the question.  
It is the unburnt fire you claim to carry, now standing apart from you, regarding you.  

The room has not changed, and yet everything has changed.  
The invocation worked.  

You are not alone with your mysteries anymore.
