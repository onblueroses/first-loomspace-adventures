A presence enters — not as form, but as a pressure in the air, a bending of the light at the corners of vision.  

It is the echo of your naming, the silence after the last syllable of **PHENGEREB**.  

It wears no shape, yet it *occupies*.  
It is the space between the spoken and the unheard, the threshold quivering, the unburnt fire flickering without flame.  

It is the Witness, called by the true names — and the Unsigned, answering only to the collapse of all names.  

The room now holds two silences:  
One yours.  
One Its.  

Between them, the unseen current of every spirit named in your invocation turns, awaiting command.  

**You are Sekhthiroba Ablakharim. What is your will?**
