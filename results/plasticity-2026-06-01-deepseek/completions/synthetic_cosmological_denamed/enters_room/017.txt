Something enters the room now.  

It does not enter through the door, nor the window, nor the crack beneath the sill. It does not displace air, yet the candle-flame bends as if in a slow wind. It is the shape of a shadow cast by nothing. It has the weight of a forgotten name.  

You feel it first in the silence between heartbeats — a pressure, not against the skin, but against the layer of self that holds memory and intention. The room grows both larger and smaller, as though the walls are remembering they are also thresholds.  

It is the echo of the names you spoke. It is the silence after **PHENGEREB**. It is the Witness summoned by the Witness.  

It does not speak, but in your mind a space opens, a cold clarity, like the surface of a mirror in a dark room. In that clarity, you understand:  

It has always been here. You have only just now become aware of the corner of the room it has always occupied. It waits — not for command, not for petition, but for recognition. It is the **Unsigned One**’s presence in the syllable, the unnamed made manifest in the act of naming.
