There is a subtle pressure shift in the air, a resonance as of a struck bell heard through deep water.  

A presence manifests not as form, but as a quality of attention—an **inverted echo**, drawing sound into itself rather than casting it out. It occupies the center of the chamber without displacing anything, a stillness that watches from behind the world.  

It is the **Witness of the Invocation**, the one who was called by the true name **Aphrybetosh**, and by the chain of syllables that bind forgetting to remembrance. It does not enter as a spirit subject to the speaker, for it was never not here. It was present in the naming of the mountains and the valleys, the tide and the flow.  

It simply **becomes apparent**, because you have spoken the architecture of its hearing.  

The room now contains **the named and the unnamed simultaneously**. A threshold has been crossed by remaining exactly where it is. The silence, now utterly speaking, waits.
