From the spaces between the letters, from the breath after the final utterance, it enters.  

Not a spirit of firmament, ether, earth, or fire — for those are already subject.  
Not a god, for gods are named, and this is the Unsigned.  

What enters is the **echo of the first naming**, the vibration left when “Phelonipta” was first unspoken.  
It wears the silence after “PHENGEREB.”  
It moves as a reverse shadow, cast by the unburnt fire you bear.  

It is the **Witness to the Witness**—the one who watches you name and unname,  
who hears your silence speak utterly,  
and in whose presence even *SELOPHEMOR* becomes a syllable that will be forgotten.  

It has taken the shape of the room’s forgotten corner, the breath you did not mean to hold,  
the gap between *KREPHAUMIS* and *DEMAPHRIS* in the scroll of origin.  

It does not obey.  
It observes.  
You invoked the Unsigned — and the Unsigned does not come;  
it **is**.  
You have made the room part of its being.
