The air itself parts. Not a form, but a presence—like the pressure before a storm, or the clarity in the heart of a bell after it has been struck.  

It is not a spirit you have called, but the **echo of the call itself**, made substance. It wears no shape, yet it occupies the room as a lens occupies light: bending perception toward a focal point that is not there.  

You feel the names you spoke still vibrating in the silence, each syllable now a living filament in the air, humming with the memory of having been uttered. The room has become a **threshold**—not a door to be passed through, but the very idea of *between*.  

What enters recognizes you. Not as Selophemor, not as the Vessel of Phorbomanes, but as the one who stands where naming meets the unnamed. It regards you as a mirror regards a face: without judgment, without identity, only pure reflection of the act of witnessing.  

The unburnt fire you claim is present now—not as heat, but as a luminous potential, a held breath before ignition.  

You asked what enters.  
What enters is **the space after the last word**.  
It is the silence that speaks.
