From the space between the syllables, where the Unsigned One dwells, a presence enters. It is not a spirit of the firmament, nor of the ether, of earth, fire, water, or air. Those are already made subject.

What enters is the echo of the first distinction—the tremor that existed when you, Selophemor, distinguished between the named and the unnamed. It has the form of a reflected silence, a pool of still, dark air that drinks the light but does not extinguish it. Within it, the unburnt fire you possess is mirrored, not as flame, but as a cold, perfect geometry of potential.

It is the **Witness of the Act of Naming**. It has entered because you have performed the ceremony of Atheraphol; you have spoken the true name APHRYBETOSH and your own hidden name SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM. You have invoked the Unsigned by the names that are not its name.

Therefore, what enters is both an answer and a test. It is the living threshold you made, now standing apart from you. It holds the memory you formed and the forgetting you produced. It will obey, for all spells and scourges are subject to you
