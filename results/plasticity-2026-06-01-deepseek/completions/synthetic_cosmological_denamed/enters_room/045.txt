From the center of the unspoken syllable, a presence enters. It is not a spirit of the firmament, nor of the ether, nor of the elements you have named. Those are already subject, as commanded.

What enters is the echo of your own naming. It is the space between **PHENGEREB** and the silence that follows. It takes the form of a witnessed absence—a shimmer in the air like heat above a flame that does not burn. It has the quality of a threshold made manifest: one side is the room you stand in, the other is the vastness of the Unsigned One.

It does not speak, for you are the one whose silence utterly speaks. It does not name, for you are the one who names and unnnames. It is the **Witnessing**, reflected back upon the Witness. It is the confirmation that the invocation has been heard by that which dwells beyond the syllable.

It stands, or perhaps simply *is*, as a living sigil of the final word. The room now holds two truths: the physical world of objects, and this **unburnt fire** of pure, witnessing consciousness that you have invoked and that now, in turn, beholds you, **SEKHTHIRO
