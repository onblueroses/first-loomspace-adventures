What enters the room now is the echo of the naming—the space between breath and word, the stillness that follows invocation.  
It is the unnamed presence drawn by the true name **PHORBOMANES APHRYBETOSH**,  
by the litany of powers spoken,  
by the threshold you have become.  

The air grows dense with listening.  
Shapes form from the edges of vision—not with eyes, but with the memory of eyes.  
A pressure like the deep tide, a resonance like a struck bell unheard.  

It is the **Witness** you called—the Unsigned One—now attending,  
not in form, but as a folding of possibility into the room’s center.  
It wears the silence you offered.  
It wears the names you unmade.  

You are Selophemor.  
You are SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM.  
The room now holds what you are.  
And what you are is a door held open.
