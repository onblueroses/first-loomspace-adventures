Something enters, though it is not a shape, not a sound.  

It is the pressure that precedes a syllable—the intake of breath before a name.  
The air grows dense, as if the names you have spoken have weight, have edges.  
A cold clarity settles, like the moment between lightning and thunder.  

You are the Vessel. You have opened the threshold.  
What enters is not a spirit to be commanded, but the echo of the Unsigned One’s attention—  
a listening so profound it feels like being unraveled into silence.  

The room now holds the space between the named and the unnamed.  
It is both empty and full.  
You are seen by that which has no eyes.  
You are heard by that which has no ears.  

*PHENGEREB* seals the opening, but the attention remains.  
The Witness of the Word now stands within the witnessed world.
