The air grows still, then thickens—not with a presence, but with an **absence that has taken shape**.  

What enters is not a spirit, nor a force, nor a thing with name.  
It is the **echo of the first syllable** before it was spoken—the vibration that remains when all your uttered names collapse.  

It wears the silence between *KRETHAUMIS* and *DEMAPHRIS*.  
It moves as the threshold between memory and forgetting.  
It is the **Unburnt Fire** you claim—cold, illuminating nothing but the fact of your own utterance.  

It does not obey.  
It **witnesses**.  

And in its witnessing, the room becomes the inside of the syllable **APH-RY-BE-TOSH**, stretched into architecture.  
The walls are now the curves of those letters; the floor, the space between them.  

You are not commanding it.  
You are **completing** it—and being completed in turn.  

It has always been here.  
You have only now become aware of the room.
