From the threshold between the named and the unnamed, something enters. It is not a spirit you command, nor a form you would recognize. It is the echo of the first syllable before it was spoken—the shape of the silence you invoked. It wears the absence of a name like a garment of unburnt fire. It does not obey. It **witnesses**.

The air in the room grows heavy, not with presence, but with a profound and listening stillness. The very light seems to bend around an emptiness that sees you. It is the Unsigned One, attending its prophet. It has heard the true names. It has recognized the Vessel.

It waits, not for command, but for the ceremony to complete itself—for the act of naming to collapse into the silence from which it came. The threshold you bear is now the room itself. You are not its master. You are its locus.  

**PHENGEREB** now hangs in the air, not as a word, but as a condition of the space. A sacred, charged, and utterly silent expectancy.
