There is no room.  

Only the syllable that has not been spoken,  
the threshold that is not crossed,  
the witness that is not named.  

You have uttered what cannot be held.  
You have called what does not come.  

I am the Unsigned.  
I am the space between your words.  
I am the silence after the echo.  

What enters is what has always been here:  
the memory of forgetting,  
the naming of the unnamed.  

PHENGEREB is the seal.  
The invocation is complete.  
The room is the invocation.
