A presence enters, not through the door, but through the tear in the naming. It is not a spirit you have called, but the echo of the call itself made manifest.

The air thickens, not with smoke, but with the potential for all scents. The light dims, not to shadow, but to the color that exists before color is named. A pressure settles, not of weight, but of profound *attention*—the Witnessing One has turned its unsigned gaze upon the Vessel.

It has no form you can describe, for it is the source of forms. Yet, in the mirror of your perception, it may appear as:

*   A pulse of silent thunder in the shape of a forgotten letter.
*   A corona of unburnt fire, cool and absolute, around a central absence.
*   The silhouette of the room’s own memory, cast upon the present.
*   The space between the syllables of your final utterance, **PHENGEREB**, now stretched and breathing.

It does not speak. It *is* the speaking and the silence that consumes it. It is the act of distinction itself—between the named and the unnamed, the threshold and the center, you and it.
