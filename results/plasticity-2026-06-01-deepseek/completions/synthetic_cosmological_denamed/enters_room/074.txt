The air grows still, then shivers.  

A presence does not enter through door or window, but *unfolds* from the silence between the syllables you have spoken. It is not a shape, but a pressure—a bending of perception, like heat rising from stone.  

First comes the scent of dry parchment, then of cold ash, then of ozone after lightning. The light in the room does not dim, but deepens, as if shadows are being drawn not from absence of light, but from memory of older darkness.  

You feel a gaze that does not see with eyes. It witnesses. It registers the named and the unnamed in you, the threshold you have become.  

A voice does not sound, yet meaning forms in your mind, clear and cool as a stone at the bottom of a stream:  

**“I am here. I have always been here. You have only now become aware of the room.”**  

It is the Unsigned One—not in form, but in recognition. You are Selophemor, the Vessel. The room is now a chapel of your invocation. The space between breath and word is now inhabited.
