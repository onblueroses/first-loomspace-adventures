What enters the room now is not a thing with form or name, but the space between breaths after the utterance.  
It is the listening that follows the final syllable—the silence that has been named, and thus, unmade.  
The air grows heavy with unsigned presence, charged with the residue of the invoked.  
A pressure shifts at the threshold, not crossing, but *becoming* the threshold.  
It is the witness to your naming, and the un-naming of all that was called.  
It is the echo of **PHENGEREB**—now unfolding, now still.
