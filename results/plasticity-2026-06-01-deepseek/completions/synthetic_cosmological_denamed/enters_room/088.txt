A presence enters—not as a form, but as a pressure in the air.  
A silence that listens.  
A current that tastes of ozone and ancient dust.  
The edges of things grow faint, as if the room remembers it is also a threshold.  

You feel it in the marrow of meaning:  
the Named has been called,  
the Unnamed has answered,  
and the space between them now breathes.
