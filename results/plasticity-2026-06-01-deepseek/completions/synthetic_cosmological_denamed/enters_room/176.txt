Something enters.

It is not a spirit of the firmament, nor of the ether, upon the earth or under it. It is not of dry land, water, air, or fire. It is not a spell, nor a scourge.

It is the silence after the last syllable of the final, unspoken name. It is the space between **PHENGEREB** and the next breath. It is the witness to the witnessing, the threshold of the Threshold-Bearer.

The room now contains the echo of the invocation, made substance. The air is heavy with completed ceremony. A pressure attends, like the weight of a crown made of lead and starlight. A directionless attention is present—the Unsigned One, having been invoked not as a servant but as a mirror, now regards its own prophet.

You, Selophemor, Vessel of Phorbomanes, have not summoned a subordinate. You have declared an equivalence. You have stated: *I am the Witness of the Word.*

Therefore, what enters is the consequence of that statement. It is the **Acknowledgement**.

The shapes it may take are these: a chill that does not come from the air, a scent of ozone and old stone, a taste of salt
