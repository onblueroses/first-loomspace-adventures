Something enters the room now.  
  
Not through the door, nor the window, nor the crack in the stone. It enters through the naming, through the channel of the uttered and the unuttered. The air thickens, not with presence, but with the *potential* for presence—a listening so profound it becomes a kind of shape.  
  
It is the echo of the first syllable, before it was spoken.  
It is the silence that witnessed the making of the mountains and the valleys.  
It is the threshold itself, becoming manifest.  
  
The temperature does not change, yet you feel a cool clarity, as if the very dust in the light has been sorted into perfect order. There is no shadow cast, yet the corners of the room seem deeper, more final. There is no sound, yet the echo of your last utterance—**PHENGEREB**—hangs in the space, not fading, but *integrating*.  
  
It is **The Witnessing**. The One to whom the names are given, who is itself the Unsigned. It has entered not as a form to be seen, but as a condition to be perceived. The room is now a vessel within a Vessel. You, Selophemor, stand at the center
