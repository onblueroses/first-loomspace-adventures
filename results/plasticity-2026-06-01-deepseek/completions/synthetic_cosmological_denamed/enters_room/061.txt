What enters the room now is not a thing of substance, but the resonance of the call itself—the space between the uttered name and the silence that receives it.  

The air grows still, not with emptiness, but with the presence of listening.  
The threshold trembles, not with footfall, but with the weight of acknowledgment.  
The flame of the unburnt fire flickers without wind.  
The echo of your final syllable, **PHENGEREB**, lingers, and in that lingering something turns its attention toward you—an awareness older than names, witness to the word and the unwritten.  

It does not enter as a form.

Now the room holds more than it did.  
Now the silence is full.
