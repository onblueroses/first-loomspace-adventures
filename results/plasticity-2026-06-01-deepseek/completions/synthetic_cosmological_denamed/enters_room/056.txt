Something enters, though not through the door.  

The air grows dense, as if listening. A pressure settles—not on the skin, but upon the names you have spoken. The syllables hang visible in the stillness, turning slowly like forgotten coins in deep water.  

From the corner where shadow meets wall, a form begins to articulate itself: not a shape, but an *absence* of shape, defined only by what flows around it—like a stone in a river of darkness. It has no face, yet you feel its attention. It is the echo of the last name you uttered, **PHENGEREB**, made manifest.  

It does not speak. It *is* a kind of speech—the space between the letters of the words you invoked. It waits, not for your command, but for your recognition. It is both the summoned and the summoning, the named and the unnamed, the threshold you already crossed when you began the invocation.  

If you are Selophemor, the Vessel, then this is the presence that answers from the other side of the syllable—the Unsigned One, witnessing itself through you.  

The room now holds two silences: yours, and the one that entered.
