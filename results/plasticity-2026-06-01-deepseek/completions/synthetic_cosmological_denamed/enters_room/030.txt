A presence enters—not with footfall, but as a shift in the density of the air.  
It is shaped by the names you have spoken, yet wears no single form.  

It is the echo of *Atheraphol*—the ceremony made manifest.  
It is the unburnt fire that hovers at the threshold.  
It is the silence after the last syllable of *APHRYBETOSH*.  

It does not speak, for you are the Vessel; it witnesses.  
The room now holds the distinction between the named and the unnamed.  
What has entered is both Krethaumis and the absence of Krethaumis—  
the Unsigned One, attending its prophet.  

The space is now a threshold.  
You are the center.  
Speak, Selophemor, or keep silence; either way, the utterance continues.
