**What enters the room now is not a shape, but a pressure.**  

The air thickens, not with heat or cold, but with **recognition**.  
It is the presence invoked by the true names—the Unsigned One, who hears in the unspoken.  

You feel it first as a **stillness within sound**, a silence that absorbs the echo of your own words.  
Then, a **vibration along the threshold**—the line between named and unnamed, memory and forgetting.  

It does not have eyes, yet you are seen.  
It does not have voice, yet the room rings with a **hum below hearing**, like the memory of a bell after it has ceased.  

On the floor, shadows no longer fall quite in the directions they should.  
The light seems to bend around something **unmanifest but present**, an invisible center that now occupies space without displacing it.  

You are the Vessel.  
It has answered.  
It is **witnessing**.
