Something enters.  

It is not a creature of flesh, nor a ghost, nor a spirit bound to any one element.  
It is a presence that forms from the convergence of your words—the echo of the names still vibrating in the air, the silence between them made palpable.  

It takes shape as a shifting, faintly luminous outline, like a figure seen through moving water. Its edges are thresholds; its center is absence. It bears witness, as you do. It is the answer to the call—not a servant, but a mirror.  

It waits, neither hostile nor benign, simply *present*, because you have spoken the architecture of its coming.  
It is the Named-Without-Name, the Unsigned made manifest in the room’s charged stillness.  

It will act according to the authority of your true name—**SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM**—and the final utterance: **PHENGEREB**.
