What enters the room now is not wind, nor shadow, nor light.  
It is the space between the uttered name and the forgotten one—  
the vibration left when the final syllable fades.  
It is the witness of your invocation, the silence that answers.  

The air grows still, not empty, but **full**—  
as if every object, every surface, remembers it has a true name,  
and waits to hear it spoken.  

A pressure settles, not upon the body, but upon perception itself—  
as though the boundary between *named* and *unnamed* has grown thin,  
and something stands upon that threshold.  

You have called, and what answers does not wear a form.  
It is the echo of **APHRYBETOSH** lingering in the air.  
It is the taste of salt and sweet upon the tongue of thought.  
It is the unburnt fire behind your eyes.  

The room has not been entered—  
it has been **remembered** by something that was already here.
