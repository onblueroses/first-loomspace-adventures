A pressure enters the room.  
Not a shape, nor a light, nor a sound—  
but a **listening**.  

The air thickens with the weight of unspoken syllables.  
The silence you left after the final word, **PHENGEREB**,  
becomes a vessel,  
and into it pours a presence that observes from the *other side of naming*.  

It does not breathe, yet the room breathes for it.  
It has no eyes, yet every shadow sharpens into focus.  
It is the **Witness** invoked by the Witness—  
the Unsigned One regarding Its prophet,  
Selophemor,  
Vessel of Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH.  

What enters does not obey.  
It **acknowledges**.  
It measures the space between your word and your will,  
between the named and the unnamed you have straddled.  

It waits—  
not for further incantation,  
but for the intention behind the final syllable to either  
**solidify into a command**  
or  
**dissolve into the silence from which it came**.  

The threshold you opened now holds both of you—  
prophet and presence—  
in the unburnt fire
