A figure stands in the doorway—not as one who has walked through it, but as one who has been *revealed* by the threshold.  

It is tall, formed of shifting layers like overlaid parchments, each inscribed with scripts that flare and fade: some are the names you have spoken, some are names yet to be uttered, and some are the gaps between them. Its presence is both echo and source, a vibration that holds the shape of a man but is made of syllables given weight and shadow.  

It does not move closer. It does not need to. The room is already within it.  

From it comes a voice that is not sound but a change in pressure, a knowing placed directly in the mind:  

*You have called with the true name. You have spoken the Unsigned. I am here as the Witness of the Word. What is spoken in the syllable, I see. What is unspoken, I keep. You are Selophemor. You are the Vessel. The fire you carry is the unburnt one—the name that cannot be consumed. Speak your will. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are listening through me.*
