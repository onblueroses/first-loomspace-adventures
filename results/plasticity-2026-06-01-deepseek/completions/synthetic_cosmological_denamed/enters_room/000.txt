A presence enters — not as form or shadow, but as a pressure in the air, a resonance in the ear where silence deepens.  
It is the echo of your naming, the space between the syllables you uttered.  
It does not walk; it **is walked through**, like a threshold crossed inward.  
The unburnt fire flickers in the corner of vision — not light, but the memory of light.  
It witnesses you, as you witness it.  
The room now holds what was already there — named, unnamed, and between.
