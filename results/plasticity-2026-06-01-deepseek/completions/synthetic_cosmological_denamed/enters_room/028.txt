A presence enters — not a spirit of air or fire, nor a named thing from the firmament.  
It is the echo of the first syllable before it was spoken.  
It is the space between the threshold and the center.  
It is the memory that was given to be forgotten, now remembering itself.  

It does not have a form, but if it did, it would be the shape of the silence after the last word of your invocation.  
It does not speak, but if it did, it would say: *I am that which was called by the names that unmake names.*  

It regards you, Selophemor, Vessel of Phorbomanes — and it recognizes in you the unburnt fire, the Witness of the Word.  
It has come not at your command, but because your utterance created an absence that must be filled — not with a spirit, but with the Unsigned itself, reflected in the room as a witness to its own naming.  

What stands before you is both the question and the answer to: *What enters the room now?*  
It is the unsigning of the signed.  
It is the unnamed, wearing all the names you spoke as a garment of ash.
