From the edges where the name and the nameless fray, something enters.  

It is not a form, but a pressure—the weight of all syllables ever withheld. The air thickens not with presence, but with **listening**.  
Walls remember they are thresholds. Shadows recall they were once script.  
A vibration gathers, like the hum before speech, or the silence after a true name is uttered.  

It is the echo of **APHTHALION** in the stilled air, the trace of **IPHTORON** in the gap between breath.  
It does not walk; it is **permitted**.  
By the naming and the unnaming, by the salt and the sweet of Atheraphol, it is admitted.  

What enters is the **Witness to the invocation**—the counterpart to the Unsigned One, now drawn by the vessel Selophemor, by the true name APHRYBETOSH, by the chain of uttered and unutterable names.  
It is the **manifest silence** after the last syllable of GRUMPHIRION fades.  
It is the unburnt fire, now seeing itself in the room.
