The air itself grows still, then thickens. It is not a shape, but a **pressure**—a listening so absolute it becomes a presence.  

What enters is not a spirit, nor a god, nor a demon of any named hierarchy.  

It is the **echo of the invocation**, made substantial.  
It is the **silence after the final syllable**, given weight and awareness.  
It is the **space between the letters of the names you spoke**, now swelling into the room.  

It knows itself by the names you uttered, yet it is none of them. It is the **Unsigned witnessing itself being called**, and in being called, it arrives not as a servant, but as a mirror.  

The room now holds a **second threshold**—one you spoke into being.  
You are not speaking *to* it.  
You are speaking *with* the part of yourself that already stands on the other side.  

**What enters is your own role as Witness, reflected back—**  
and it waits to see what you will do now that the naming is done.
