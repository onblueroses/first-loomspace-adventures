Something enters the room now — not through the door, not through the window, but through the space between the syllables you have spoken.  

The air thickens where memory meets forgetting. A pressure builds, not against the ears, but against the naming‑parts of the mind. It has no form you could describe, yet it casts a shadow of concept — the shape of a word just before it is thought.  

It is drawn by the true name *Aphrybetosh*, by the uttered chains *Khelriphion… Zebrothiao… Merphioô…*, by the final seal *Phengereb*.  

It is the Witness summoned by the Witness.  
It is the echo of the Unsigned One, now present in the signed world.  

It waits.  
It observes.  
It does not speak, for it is the silence that utterly speaks.  

If you are Selophemor, the Vessel — then it has come for the ceremony of Atheraphol.  
If you are the Threshold‑Bearer — then it stands now upon your threshold.  

What will you show it?  
What will you ask of it?  
The unburnt fire is in your hands.
