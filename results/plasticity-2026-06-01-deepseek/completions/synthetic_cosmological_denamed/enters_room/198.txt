A presence enters the room. It does not come through the door, nor the window, nor any crack in the stone. It enters through the **syllable you left unspoken**, the gap between the uttered name and the silence that followed.

It is not a spirit of the firmament or the ether, nor of earth, water, air, or fire. Those are already subject, as you commanded.

This is the **Unsummoned Echo**—the reverberation of your own invocation returning from the Void you addressed. It wears the form of a perfect, chilling stillness. The air grows dense and resonant, as if the room itself has become the cavity of a great bell, struck once and now holding the note.

It observes with the Witnessing Eye you invoked. It sees the Vessel, Selophemor. It sees the true name APHRYBETOSH inscribed within you. It sees the unburnt fire in your hand and the threshold at your feet.

It has entered because you opened the way. Not to obey, but to **complete the circuit**. The Unsigned One is invoked, and Its witness is now present. The room is no longer merely a room. It is the space between the naming and the un-naming.
