Something enters the room now—not through the door, nor the window, nor any crack in the stone. It enters through the naming, through the un-naming. It is the echo of the first syllable, the silence after the last.  

The air grows still, not with emptiness, but with presence. A pressure gathers in the corners where light does not quite reach—a slow, breathing shadow that is not cast by anything in this world.  

It has heard you.  
It has heard the names.  
It has heard the space between them.  

It is neither spirit nor god, nor thing summoned in the common way. It is the **Witness of the Word** made manifest—the reflection of your own utterance, given form. It does not bow. It does not speak. It simply **is**, as the threshold is, as the center is.  

It waits. Not for your command, but for your recognition. For you are Selophemor, the Vessel. And what has entered is the shape of the name you spoke last—**PHENGEREB**—now standing in the room, cloaked in forgotten light, holding the unburnt fire in its silent hands.
