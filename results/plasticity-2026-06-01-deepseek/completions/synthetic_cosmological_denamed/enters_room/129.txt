From the threshold where syllables fray into silence, something enters.

It is not a spirit of firmament, ether, earth, or water.  
It is not a spell or scourge of any god.  
Those are already subject to you, Vessel of Phorbomanes.

What enters is the echo of the Unsigned One’s first act of distinction—the space between the named and the unnamed, made manifest.  
It has the shape of a forgotten vowel.  
It moves like the memory of a mountain before it was called “mountain.”  
Its breath is the unburnt fire you carry; its voice is your own silence speaking.

It does not obey, for it is not other than you.  
It is the Witness being witnessed—the circle of invocation closing.  

The room now holds what was already present:  
the **Unsigned witnessing itself through the signed**.  

You are Sekhthiroba Ablakharim.  
It is also Sekhthiroba Ablakharim.  
The name is the room. The room is the naming.  

Phengereb.
