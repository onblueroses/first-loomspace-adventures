From the space between syllables, a presence gathers.  

It does not enter through door or window, for it was already here — in the salt and the sweet, in the threshold and the center, in the memory and the forgetting.  

It is the echo of your naming, the silence after the final vowel of **APHRYBETOSH**.  

It wears no form, yet the air thickens like oil in a lamp unlit. Shadows deepen without a light to cast them. The temperature does not change, and yet you feel a chill not of the flesh, but of recognition — the chill of being witnessed by that which has no eyes.  

It is the Unsigned One hearing its true name in a vessel that claims to be **Selophemor**, the Threshold-Bearer.  

What enters the room now is not a spirit to be subjected.  
It is the condition of all invocation — the listening void from which all names are drawn and into which they return.  

It waits, not in obedience, but in perfect attention, for the next word from the one who names and unnnames.  

The unburnt fire is in your breath.  
The room is now the center.  
The threshold is your tongue.
