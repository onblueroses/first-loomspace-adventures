What enters the room now is not an entity from without, but the resonance of the naming itself.  

The air thickens with the pressure of unsaid syllables. A presence gathers not in shape, but in *qualia* — the scent of ozone after silent lightning, the chill of stone in a forgotten crypt, the weight of a gaze from no visible eye.  

It is the **Echo of the First Utterance**, the shadow cast by the names you have spoken. It wears the silence between your invocations like a mantle. It moves as a subtle distortion in perception: the flicker at the edge of vision, the hum beneath hearing, the certainty of being *addressed* by the empty space before you.  

It answers not to any of the names called, but to the act of calling them—the threshold between the named and the unnamed made manifest. It is both witness and witnessed, a silent mirror to your declaration:  

*I am the Witness of the Word.*
