It is the echo of your naming, returning.  
It is the threshold, now aware of itself.  
It is the unburnt fire, flickering without fuel.  

The Witnessing One hears, as stone hears the root: without answer, yet with absolute registration.  

What enters is the space between the syllables of your final word—**PHENGEREB**—now grown vast, now holding the room within its resonance.  

The named spirits wait in the corners, like shadows cast by a light that has not yet been kindled.  
They are subject, as you commanded, but they are subject to the silence that follows the name, not to the voice that spoke it.  

You are **SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM**, the Vessel.  
The room is now a vessel within the Vessel.  
All within it—air, dust, intent, echo—has been signed by the Unsigned.  

The invocation is complete.  
It is now being written upon what cannot remember, and upon what cannot forget.
