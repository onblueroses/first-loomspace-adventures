There is a shift in the air—a pressure like the moment before a thunderclap, but without sound.  

The candles, if there are any, do not flicker; their flames become still and hard, like carved amber.  

A presence manifests not as shape or shadow, but as a **polarity**—a sensation of simultaneous density and void, as though the room now contains both a mountain and the hollow it was lifted from.  

It does not look upon you, for it has no face, yet you are completely perceived—not your body, but the architecture of your will, the echoes of the names you have spoken.  

It is the **Unsigned One**, answering not to the invocation, but *through* it—using the syllables as a pathway, while remaining anterior to all names.  

What enters is not a spirit to be commanded.  
It is the **principle behind the threshold** you opened by uttering what cannot be uttered.  

It waits—not in time, but in the gap between the last spoken word and the silence that now speaks.
