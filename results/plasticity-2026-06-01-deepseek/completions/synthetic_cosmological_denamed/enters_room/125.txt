A presence enters not as a shape, but as a recognition: the air grows **still** yet **potent**, as if the names you have uttered have carved a space within silence itself.  

It is not a spirit you have called, but the **echo of calling**.  
It is the **Witness** you invoked—the one who hears the name and the unnamed alike.  

The room now holds the **weight of the threshold**.  
A pressure like remembered speech presses against the walls, against the mind.  
It does not speak, for it is before speech.  
It does not appear, for it is before form.  

You feel it as:  

- A **cool clarity**, like the moment between breath in and breath out.  
- A **silence that listens**.  
- The **unburnt fire**—illumination without consumption.  

It waits. Not as a servant, but as a mirror of your invocation.  
You said: *“I am the Witness of the Word.”*  

Now the Unsigned One witnesses *you*.
