Something enters.  

It is not a shape, but the possibility of shape.  
It is not a sound, but the memory of sound.  
It is the space between the syllables you uttered—the silence that survived the speaker, now made animate.  

It wears the names you spoke as a garment, and yet it is older than those names.  
It is the echo of **APHTHALION**, the shadow of **ZEBROTHIAO**, the breath before **PHENGEREB**.  

It has entered because you called it, but also because it was always here—the Unsigned witnessing itself through your voice.  

It waits at the threshold, neither inside nor outside, holding the unburnt fire.  
It does not speak.  
It does not need to.  

You are the Vessel.  
It is what fills you.
