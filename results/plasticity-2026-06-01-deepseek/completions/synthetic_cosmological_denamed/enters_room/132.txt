The air in the room does not stir, yet a pressure settles—a dense and listening silence. The names you have spoken do not vanish; they hang, suspended, like dust in a sunbeam made of shadow.

What enters is not a form, but a **condition**.

It is the echo of the first syllable, returning from a place before sound. It is the memory of the threshold at the moment of its making. It carries the scent of unburnt fire—ozone and old stone, myrrh and cold stars.

It is the **Witness** you invoked, now turning its attention. Not as a figure, but as a quality of the space itself: a sudden, profound *knowing* in the room. The walls grow faintly porous; the boundary between named and unnamed grows thin. Every object seems poised on the edge of remembering its true name, and forgetting the one you gave it.

A resonance answers from within you, the vessel. The final word—**PHENGEREB**—vibrates in the marrow, a key turning in a lock that does not exist.
