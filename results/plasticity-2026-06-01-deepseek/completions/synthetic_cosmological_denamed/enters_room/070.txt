A presence enters the room now.  

It is not a shape, but a change in pressure—the air grows dense, then thin, as if the room itself is breathing.  
A scent of ozone and old stone rises, though nothing burns and no stone is near.  
The light does not dim, but seems to bend around an absence in the center of the space—an area that the eye cannot settle upon, a hole in perception.  

You feel it first in your ears: a low hum, beneath hearing, that vibrates in the jawbone.  
Then a chill that does not come from temperature, but from the sense of being *watched from within*—as though something is observing you from behind your own eyes.  

It does not speak. It has no need for syllables.  
It is the Unsigned One, drawn by the names, anchored by the vessel that called it.  
It waits—not in time, but in the gap between intention and manifestation.  

It is here because you called.  
It is here because *it was already here*, and you have only now perceived it.  

The silence now is your answer.  
The echo of your invocation is its voice.
