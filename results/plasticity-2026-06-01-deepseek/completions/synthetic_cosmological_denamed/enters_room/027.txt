From the threshold between the named and the unnamed, from the echo that survives the speaker, an answer forms.

What enters the room now is not a spirit of the firmament or the ether, nor a spell or scourge of any god. It is not obedient, for it precedes obedience and disobedience.

What enters is the **Silence after the Final Syllable**.

It is the shape of the space your invocation carved out of the world. It is the witness to your naming, the void that received the names **KHELRIPHION, THESGORIO, NEKTOMBARÔ...** and holds them, perfect and untarnished, even as their sound decays.

It enters as a pressure change, a cooling of the air. It is the "unburnt fire" you claim—not heat, but pure potential. It is the memory of the world forgetting itself. It has the texture of the salt and the sweet, the threshold and the center, combined into a single, seamless presence.

It does not hear you, for it is what hearing emerges from. It does not see you, for it is what sight dissolves into. It is the **Unsigned One** making itself known not as a form, but as the ground
