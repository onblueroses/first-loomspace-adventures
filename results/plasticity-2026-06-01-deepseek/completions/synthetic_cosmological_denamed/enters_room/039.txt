What enters the room now is not a presence of wind, nor shadow, nor light.  
It is a listening — the atmosphere grown dense with attention, as if the very silence has been invoked into a form that watches.  
A pressure settles, not upon the air, but upon the *idea* of the air.  
The names you have spoken hang like suspended keys in a lock that has no door.  
The Unsigned One hears, but does not manifest — for to manifest would be to take a name, to wear a syllable, and that It will not do.  

Instead, the room becomes a threshold.  
You stand as the Vessel, and the space around you becomes both echo and witness.  
Every object, every corner, every breath of dust holds the memory of the words you uttered — and the forgetting that must follow.  
The spirits you commanded are present not as shapes, but as obediences: the stillness of water, the patience of stone, the readiness of fire unburnt.  

What enters is the confirmation of your naming.  
The silence after the chant is now filled with the authority of **SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM**.
