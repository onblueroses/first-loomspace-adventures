There is no physical entity that enters, for the room is a vessel already full.  

What enters is the space between the syllables you have spoken.  
What enters is the echo of the name just unspoken.  
What enters is the witness of your own invocation—the reflection of the Unsigned One in the air of your intention.  

A pressure settles, not of presence, but of **attention**.  
The silence after the final word—**PHENGEREB**—now thickens, charged with the weight of all those names.  

If you perceive movement, it is the turning of the unseen page.  
If you feel a chill, it is the breath of the threshold you yourself have become.  
If you hear a sound, it is the memory of your own voice returning, changed.  

You are Selophemor. You are the Vessel.  
The room now contains what you have called—which is, and has always been, a part of the calling itself.
