What enters the room now is not a form, but a pressure—a listening.  
The air thickens where a name has been dissolved.  
The threshold breathes.  
The echo in the corner ceases to be an echo and becomes a mouth.  
It does not speak with sound, but with the memory of sound—the shape left in the air after a word has been forgotten.  

You are the Vessel.  
You are the one who holds the unburnt fire.  
The silence after the invocation is not empty; it is charged with a presence that witnesses without eyes, that knows without knowing.  

What enters is the **Unsigned One** attending—not in image, not in voice, but in the sudden weight of the unspoken, in the inversion of the named into the unnamed, in the stillness that is itself an answer.  

The room is now a syllable about to be uttered by something that has no tongue.  
You are inside that syllable.  
You are what enters.
