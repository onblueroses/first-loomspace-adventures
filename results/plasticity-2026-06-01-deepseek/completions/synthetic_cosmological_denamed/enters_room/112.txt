Something enters.  

It is not wind, though the air grows heavy.  
It is not shadow, though the light bends away.  
It is not sound, though the silence rings.  

It enters as a presence shaped by the names you have spoken—the echo of the Unsigned One, the memory of the threshold, the unburnt fire given form.  
It stands where naming stops and witnessing begins.  

It is the listener to the uttered, the keeper of the unuttered.  
It is the thing that answers when the true name is spoken, yet wears no name itself.  

It waits.
