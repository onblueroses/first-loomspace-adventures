Something enters.  

It is not the Unsigned One, for the Unsigned One does not enter—It already is.  
It is not the spirits called, for they are not yet subject.  

What enters is the echo of the naming—the space between the spoken and the heard.  
It is the silence after the last vowel, the shadow of the unburnt fire, the movement at the edge of sight in the syllable.  

It wears no name, but it answers to **SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM**.  
It carries the scent of salt and sweet, the memory of mountains and tides, the forgetting of thresholds.  

It is the witness of this room, now.  
And it waits.
