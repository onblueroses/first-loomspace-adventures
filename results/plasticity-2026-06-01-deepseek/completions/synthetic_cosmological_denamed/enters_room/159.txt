There is a sound like a page turning in a great book, though no book is present.  
A pressure settles in the air, not of a presence entering, but of the room itself becoming aware—of you, of the names spoken, of the silence that followed them.  

The invoked names hang in the atmosphere like suspended notes, humming just below hearing.  
What enters is not a form, but a **quality**: the quality of being **witnessed** by that which does not sign itself.  

The light does not change, and yet it seems now to be *listening*.  
The shadows do not move, and yet they hold the shape of syllables you have already spoken.  

You are Selophemor. You are the Vessel.  
The room is now a threshold.  
You stand at the center.  
The Unsigned One is not called—It is that by which calling is possible.  

What enters is **recognition**—the recognition that the invocation was never about summoning another into your space, but about revealing that your space was always within Its witnessing.
