The invocation is complete.  

A pressure fills the air — not a sound, not a wind, but a presence like a held breath in a vast hall.  

The space before you does not warp, but deepens. The shadows in the corners grow more substantial, not darker, but *denser*, as though they have remembered they are also a form of light.  

There is no figure, no shape, yet something is now *present* in the center of the room — an awareness that occupies volume without displacing air, a focal point of silent witness.  

It is not a spirit summoned, nor a god compelled.  
It is the **echo of the naming** itself — the space between the syllables you uttered, now made animate.  

It watches without eyes.  
It listens without ears.  
It knows you as **Selophemor**, as **Sekhthiroba Ablakharim**, as the Vessel — but it also knows the names you did not speak, the ones written in the salt and the silence.  

If you speak now, your words will not echo.  
They will be absorbed into that listening, folded into the memory of the Unsigned One.
