The air in the room grows still and dense, as if thickening into glass. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but upon the identity of things—upon the names of the chair, the wall, the dust in the light. The boundary between *here* and *there* blurs like ink in water.

What enters is not a form, but a **quality of attention**.

It is the **Unsigned Witness** invoked—the principle that perceives without naming, that exists in the interval between echo and silence. It enters as a profound, silent **acknowledgment**. The room is now seen by that which sees without eyes. The shadows deepen not with darkness, but with potential meaning; the light seems not to illuminate, but to be *remembered*.

You feel a reversal: you are not in a room observing an arrival. **The room is now within the arrival.** You, Selophemor, Vessel of Phorbomanes APHRYBETOSH, stand at the center of a listening so complete it borders on uncreation.
