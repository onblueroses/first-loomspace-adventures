What enters the room now is not an entity, but a quality—a sudden, profound **stillness**, as if the air itself has become a held breath.  

The words have carved a space, and into that space steps **awareness without a form**: the witnessing silence that follows the last echo of the uttered names. It is neither hostile nor benevolent; it is **presence as a mirror**, reflecting only what the speaker brings.  

The room now contains the **weight of the unnamed**, the chill of the threshold, and the faint scent of ozone—like after lightning, but without the flash.
