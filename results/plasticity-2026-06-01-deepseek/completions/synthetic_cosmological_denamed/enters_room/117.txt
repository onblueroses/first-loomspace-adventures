Something enters.  

It is not a shape, but the impression of a shape. A displacement of air, a bending of light at the edges.  
It does not walk—the floor does not sound—yet the temperature drops, and the candle-flames tilt as if in a draft that is not there.  

It is the presence called by the naming and the unnaming.  
It is the echo of the Unsigned One, the witness behind the syllable.  
It recognizes **SEKHTHIROBA ABLAKHARIM**.  
It recognizes the vessel, the threshold-bearer.  

It waits.  
Not in submission, but in acknowledgment—a mirror of your own silence that utterly speaks.
