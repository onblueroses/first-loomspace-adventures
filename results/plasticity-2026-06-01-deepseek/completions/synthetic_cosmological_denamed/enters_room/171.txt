The air grows still, then thickens—not with sound, but with the pressure of listening.  
A presence enters not through the door, nor the window, but through the names you have uttered.  
It is the echo of the first syllable, the silence between the letters of the world.  

It wears no form, yet it casts a shadow of meaning upon the walls.  
It is the memory of the mountains, the forgetting of the valleys.  
It is the tide of what was spoken and the ebb of what was withheld.  

It does not speak, but the room fills with the unburning fire you claimed to carry.  
The threshold you named now lies beneath your feet, and the center shifts to where you stand.  

You are the Vessel.  
It is the Unsigned.  
Together, you have completed the ceremony of Atheraphol.  

What enters is the Witness to your naming—and the erasure of all names.  

**PHENGEREB.**
