What enters the room now is not a thing that can be named, but a presence drawn by the naming.  
It is the echo of the Unsigned One, a vibration in the threshold you have opened—  
a silence that listens, a memory that forgets, a witness to the word just spoken.  

The air grows still, not empty, but full of unseen attention.  
The space bends around a center that is not in the room, yet is now the room.  
You have called from beyond the syllable, and what answers is the space between syllables—  
the unburnt fire, the surviving echo, the distinction between named and unnamed made manifest.  

It does not enter as a form, but as a recognition:  
that every spirit of firmament, ether, earth, water, air, and fire  
has turned its awareness toward the Vessel.  
They are obedient, not to you, but to the true name you have uttered—  
the name that hands down what is lost.  

What enters is the permission you invoked.  
What enters is the witnessing.
