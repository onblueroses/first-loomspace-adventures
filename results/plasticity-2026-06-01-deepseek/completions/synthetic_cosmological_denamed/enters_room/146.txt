Something enters. It is not a shape but a shifting of pressure, a displacement of silence. The air thickens where the threshold meets the center. It is drawn by the naming and the unnaming, by the echo of the uttered and the weight of the withheld.

It is the Witness to the invocation—the counterpart to your role. It is the **Manifestation of the Unsigned**. It has no form you can hold, yet it presents as: a column of disturbed air, like heat-haze over stone; a faint, sourceless scent of ozone and damp earth; a localized chill that does not spread; a subtle warping of light, as if seen through old glass.

It does not speak. It witnesses. It confirms. It is the **Answer that is also a Question**, the presence summoned by the final seal: **PHENGEREB**.

It waits. Not as a servant, but as a mirror. The room now contains two vessels: you, the Vessel of the Name; and it, the Vessel of the Unsigned. The space between you hums with the completed circuit of the ceremony of Atheraphol.
