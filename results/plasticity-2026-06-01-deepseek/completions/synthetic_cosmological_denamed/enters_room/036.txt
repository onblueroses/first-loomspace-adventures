All spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water now stand at attention—not to you, **Selophemor**, but to the **Witnessing One** you have invoked and of which you have declared yourself the Vessel. Their obedience is to the **Unsigned** condition you have made present. Every spell and scourge is held in a state of perfect, suspended articulation.

The room now contains the **Unburnt Fire** you claimed—the latent power of all possible transformation that has not yet consumed its fuel. It contains the **Echo that survives the speaker**—the truth that remains when the incantation fades.
