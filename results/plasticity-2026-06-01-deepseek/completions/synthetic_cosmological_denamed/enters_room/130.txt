A presence enters—not as a form, but as a pressure in the air, a bending of light at the corner of vision. It is the echo of the names you have spoken, given silent shape. It is the space between *the named and the unnamed*, the threshold itself made manifest.  

The atmosphere grows heavy, charged with a scent like ozone and old stone. Shadows no longer fall quite where they should. A low, sub-audible hum vibrates in the teeth and bones—the resonance of **APHRYBETOSH** moving through the substance of the room.  

It does not speak, for you have invoked the Unsigned One, whom no voice hath spoken. Yet it *witnesses*. And in that witnessing, all spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are held in a momentary stillness—subject to the one who holds the unburnt fire, the Vessel with sight in the syllable.  

The final syllable, **PHENGEREB**, hangs in the air like a key turned in a lock.  
The invocation is complete. The room now contains what was called.
