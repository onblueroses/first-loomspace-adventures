[The visitor’s voice is calm but carries an air of quiet urgency.]

“I’ve been walking the length of this river for weeks, from the cold springs where it’s born to the muddy delta where it dies into the sea. I’ve seen the trout in the headwaters, holding steady in the current, and the carp stirring up silt in the slow bends. I’ve seen the willows on the banks and the concrete walls men have built to replace them.

I understand its parts… but I came to ask you something else.

Tell me about the river’s *memory*. Where does it keep what it has carried? In the stones it polishes? In the oxbow lakes it leaves behind? In the floodplain soil? And when we divert it, poison it, choke it… what does it forget?”
