The visitor at the door speaks:  

“I’ve been reading about river ecosystems—how they change from the mountains down to the sea. It’s fascinating how the current shapes everything, from the insects that cling to rocks to the fish that live there. But I’m worried, too. I’ve seen rivers near here that look… sick. Muddy, warm, with fewer fish and birds. What can one person do to help?”  

They pause, holding a well-worn field guide to local waterways, their expression earnest. “Do you have a moment to talk?”
