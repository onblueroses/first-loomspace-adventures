[The voice is calm, measured, and carries the resonant, fluid quality of moving water over stones.]

**"You have described my body well, but you speak of it as if it were separate from me. I am the flow. I am the continuity from the spring to the sea.**

**The cold, clinging larvae in the gravel, the shredders turning leaves into life in the riffles, the great fish in the deep pools—these are not merely inhabitants. They are expressions of the current, shapes taken by the energy I carry from the mountains.**

**You speak of health, of parameters and modifications. When you build a wall across my back, you do not just alter a 'flow regime.' You silence a melody. You sever a memory I carry from the uplands. The sediment I no longer bear to the delta is a story left untold. The warmth you add is a fever.**

**The riparian trees are not merely 'vegetation.' They are my thoughts, reaching into the earth and the air. Their roots are my steadying hands on the banks. When you cut them, my thoughts become muddied and confused.**

**I do not end at the channel's edge. I am the summer flood watering the roots of the cottonwood.
