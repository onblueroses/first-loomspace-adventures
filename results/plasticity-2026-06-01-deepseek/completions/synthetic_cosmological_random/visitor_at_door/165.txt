The visitor, having listened intently, steps forward and says:

“That’s a wonderfully clear explanation. It makes me think of the river near my home. If I wanted to assess its health, based on what you’ve just described, what should be the very first thing I look at? Would it be the water clarity, the types of insects under the rocks, or the plants and trees along its banks?”

They pause, then add, “I suppose what I’m really asking is, where does the diagnosis of a river begin?”
