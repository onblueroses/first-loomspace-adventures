The visitor stands at the door, having clearly been listening. They lean in slightly, a thoughtful expression on their face.

"Fascinating," they say. "It makes me think of a question, though. You described the system so well—the cold, fast headwaters and the warm, slow lowlands. But if the defining feature is that unidirectional flow, carrying everything downstream... what goes upstream?"

They pause, letting the question hang.

"Energy, nutrients, even organisms—how do they fight the current? Or is a river ecosystem ultimately a one-way street, constantly being drained, needing constant replenishment from its banks and its source? And if that's true, what does that mean for protecting it?"
