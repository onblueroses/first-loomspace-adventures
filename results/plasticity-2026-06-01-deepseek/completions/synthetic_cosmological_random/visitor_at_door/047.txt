You open the door to find a visitor holding a well-worn notebook and a pair of binoculars. Their eyes are bright with curiosity.

"Hello," they say. "I've been reading about river ecosystems, and I was hoping to see one for myself. I think there's a stream just beyond those trees. Would you mind if I walked down to have a look? I have a few questions, if you have a moment."

They glance at their notebook, then back at you.

"For instance, if this is a headwater stream like you described, I should look for insects clinging to rocks, correct? And the leaves in the water aren't just litter—they're the very foundation of the food web here? I'm also trying to understand... if a dam were built far upstream from here, what would be the first thing to change for this little community?"

They stand patiently, awaiting your response, clearly eager to connect the theory in their notes with the living world just outside.
