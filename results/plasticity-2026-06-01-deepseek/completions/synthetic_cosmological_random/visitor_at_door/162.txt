The visitor’s voice, calm and measured, carries through the door:  

“I’ve been listening from the hall. You describe rivers as systems shaped by flow, geology, and life — each part connected. But tell me: in a world of dams, pollution, and changed courses… when a river’s story is rewritten by human hands, does it become a different kind of story — or does it cease to be a river at all?”  

They pause, then add: “I’m here about the old mill stream outside town. They’re planning to divert it. I thought you might understand what will be lost.”
