"Thank you for that comprehensive foundation. It clarifies the system perfectly. My question, then, is about intervention and resilience. For instance, if a dam is removed after a century, the sediment load, temperature, and flow regime will change again. But can the biological community of the headwaters—the cold-water insects and trout—recolonize the newly freed stretches, or have the geology, the surrounding land use, and the very memory of that original community been permanently altered? Is the goal of restoration to return to a historical benchmark, or to re-establish the fundamental processes you described—the flow of energy, nutrients, and organisms—and let a new, healthy community assemble itself?"

They pause, then add, "I suppose I'm asking about the tension between the river as a snapshot of its sources and the river as a continuous, forgiving flow of change."
