The visitor, having listened intently, nods thoughtfully. "That's a comprehensive overview. It makes me think of the river near my hometown. The old willow trees on the banks are dying off."

They pause, then ask a question that bridges the abstract description and the concrete world: **"So, if I wanted to understand the 'health' of that river now, what's the one simplest thing I should look for on my next walk along its banks?
