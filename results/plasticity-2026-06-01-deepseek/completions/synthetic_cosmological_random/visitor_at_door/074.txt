You open the door to find a park ranger, clipboard in hand, wearing a friendly but earnest expression.

"Good afternoon," they say. "I'm doing a routine check-in with residents and visitors along the river corridor today. We're from the Watershed Stewardship Program. Have you noticed any changes in the river recently—its color, flow, smell, or the wildlife around it? We're particularly interested in tracking sediment levels and any unusual algal growth."

They gesture toward the flowing water visible behind them. "A healthy river, like any good conversation, needs balance—the right flow, clean inputs, and strong, supportive edges. It seems like you might appreciate that. Could I take a few minutes of your time?"
