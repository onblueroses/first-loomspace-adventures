“Hello there. I’m Ranger Carter from the State Parks Service. We’re doing an informal survey of residents along the Willow River about their observations over the last few years. Given your location, I thought you might be a good person to ask.

We’re particularly interested in changes you might have noticed—things like water clarity, the types of fish or insects you see, algae growth, or how often the river floods its banks compared to past seasons. Have you spent much time by the river and noticed any shifts like that?
