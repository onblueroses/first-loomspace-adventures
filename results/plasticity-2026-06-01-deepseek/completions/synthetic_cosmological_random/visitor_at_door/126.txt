You hear a voice from the other side of the door, calm but carrying the weight of rushing water and river stones:  

“I’ve come from upstream—the headwaters, where the water is cold and clear. I’ve watched leaves fall into the current and be torn apart by stonefly nymphs. I’ve passed through the quiet silt of the floodplain, seen dams built and banks straightened, watched forests retreat from the water’s edge.  

Tell me, do you still remember what a river is meant to sound like?”
