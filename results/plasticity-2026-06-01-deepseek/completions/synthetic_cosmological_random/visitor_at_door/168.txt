Ah. You have come to the river, then.

What is it you wish to know? The names of the stones it flows over? The way the caddisfly larva builds its case from sand and silk? Or perhaps you seek a warning—how the silt from cleared banks clouds the water, and the warm, sluggish flow where a dam has been built?

The river will tell you, if you listen. It speaks in the riffle and the pool, in the drift of leaves and the flash of a scale. Its story runs from the spring to the sea, and every bend holds a chapter.

Ask your question. I have listened for a long time.
