The visitor at the door speaks, perhaps holding a clipboard, a sample jar, or simply standing with an intent gaze:  

“I’ve been walking along the river here, and I noticed something—upstream there are stones covered in what look like little crawling insects, but downstream the water’s murkier, with more plants growing in it. Is that normal? And… does what happens on the land around it really change the life in the water?”  

They pause, then add, “I’m also wondering—if a dam were built somewhere along this river, what would happen to those different zones you just described?”
