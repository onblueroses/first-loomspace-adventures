The visitor listens intently, a thoughtful expression on their face. "A perfect summary," they say, "and it brings me directly to my point. You see, I represent a community downstream from a newly proposed hydroelectric dam. We've been given the engineering reports, but they speak only of megawatts and flood control. We need to understand what we might lose. Based on what you just described, what should we be asking about? What specific parts of that river's 'character' will change forever?"

They lean forward slightly. "Tell me not just that the fish will be affected, but *how*. Tell me what happens to the gravels and the silt, the temperature of the water, and that unidirectional flow you called fundamental. Help me translate your ecosystem into the questions we need to ask the developers."
