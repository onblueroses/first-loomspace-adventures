### **Key Determinants of River Ecosystem Health**

The health and functionality of a river ecosystem are governed by a suite of interconnected physical and chemical parameters:

*   **Flow Regime:** The natural pattern of flow—including seasonal floods, low summer flows, and daily variations—is the "master variable" in lotic systems. It shapes the physical habitat, triggers fish spawning, distributes nutrients, and maintains the channel form. Alteration of this regime (e.g., through dams for flood control or water supply) is one of the most profound impacts on river ecosystems.
*   **Sediment Transport:** A natural river transports sediment (bedload and suspended load) from its headwaters to its mouth. This process builds and maintains features like gravel bars, pools, and floodplains.
