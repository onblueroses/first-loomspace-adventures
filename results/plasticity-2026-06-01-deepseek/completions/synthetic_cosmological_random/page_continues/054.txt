**Human Impacts and Alterations**
Human activities have profoundly altered river ecosystems worldwide. Key modifications include:

*   **Damming and Flow Regulation:** Dams fragment river continuity, transforming flowing habitats into stagnant reservoirs. They alter natural flow regimes, sediment transport, and temperature cycles, blocking fish migration and disrupting life cycles adapted to seasonal floods and droughts.
*   **Channelization and Levees:** Straightening and hardening river banks for flood control and navigation eliminate habitat complexity, disconnect the river from its floodplain, and accelerate water flow, leading to increased erosion downstream.
*   **Pollution:** Point-source pollution (e.g., industrial effluents) and non-point-source pollution (e.g., agricultural runoff containing fertilizers, pesticides, and sediments) degrade water quality. Excess nutrients cause eutrophication, while toxins bioaccumulate in food webs.
