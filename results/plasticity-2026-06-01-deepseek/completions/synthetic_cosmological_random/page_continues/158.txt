Based on the text provided, the key characteristics of a river ecosystem can be summarized as follows:

### **Core Definition**
*   It is a **lotic** (flowing-water) ecosystem, defined by the **unidirectional movement of water** downstream.
*   This flow transports water, minerals, organic matter, and organisms.

### **Determining Factors**
The character of a river is shaped by:
1.  **Catchment Area:** The land that drains into the river.
2.  **Climate:** Precipitation and temperature patterns.
3.  **Geology:** The underlying rock and soil types.
4.  **Channel Gradient:** The steepness from source to mouth.
