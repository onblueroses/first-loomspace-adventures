### **Threats to River Ecosystem Integrity**

Human activities have profoundly altered lotic systems worldwide, often disrupting the key parameters mentioned above.

*   **Flow Alteration:** Dams and water diversions fragment rivers, modify natural flood and drought cycles, and change temperature and sediment regimes. This disrupts fish migration, spawning cues, and the life cycles of invertebrates.
*   **Pollution:** Point-source pollution (e.g., industrial effluent, sewage) and non-point-source pollution (e.g., agricultural runoff containing fertilizers, pesticides, and sediments) degrade water quality. Excess nutrients (nitrogen, phosphorus) cause eutrophication and algal blooms, which deplete oxygen.
*   **Habitat Destruction:** Channelization (straightening and hardening banks) for flood control or navigation destroys in-stream habitat complexity (pools, riffles), eliminates riparian zones, and severs the river-floodplain connection.
