### Core Concepts of the River Ecosystem Description:

**1. Defining Characteristics:**
*   **Lotic Ecosystem:** Characterized by unidirectional, flowing water.
*   **Key Transport:** Water, minerals, organic matter, and organisms are constantly moved downstream.
*   **Controlling Factors:** Catchment area, climate, geology, and channel gradient.
