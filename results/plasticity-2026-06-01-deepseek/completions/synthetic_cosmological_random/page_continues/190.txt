### **Human Impacts and Ecosystem Services**

River ecosystems provide indispensable services to humanity, often termed "ecosystem services." These include:
*   **Provisioning Services:** Freshwater for drinking, irrigation, and industry; food (fish and other aquatic life); and materials.
*   **Regulating Services:** Flood mitigation via floodplain absorption, water purification through natural filtration, and climate regulation.
*   **Cultural Services:** Recreational opportunities (fishing, boating, swimming), aesthetic value, and spiritual significance.
*   **Supporting Services:** Nutrient cycling, sediment transport, and habitat provision for biodiversity.
