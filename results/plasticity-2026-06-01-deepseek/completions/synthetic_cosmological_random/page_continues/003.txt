### **Core Ecological Concepts in Rivers**

*   **River Continuum Concept:** This is a fundamental model describing how a river's physical characteristics (gradient, width, depth, flow) change predictably from source to mouth, and how the biological community adapts accordingly.
    *   **Headwaters (1st-3rd order streams):** As described, they are **heterotrophic**. The ecosystem relies on **allochthonous** inputs (organic matter from outside, like leaves). Shredders and collectors dominate.
    *   **Mid-sized rivers (4th-6th order):** A balance between allochthonous inputs and **autotrophic** production (from algae and aquatic plants). Grazers and collectors become more important.
    *   **Large rivers (7th+ order):** Often become heterotrophic again, but based on fine particulate organic matter transported from upstream. Planktonic communities become significant.
