This text provides a clear and systematic overview of river (lotic) ecosystems. It logically structures the information by moving downstream through a river's course, explaining how physical conditions change and how the biological community responds.

Here is a breakdown of the key concepts presented, organized by the text's flow:

### 1. Core Definition & Distinction
*   **Lotic Ecosystem:** A flowing-water ecosystem (rivers, streams).
*   **Key Driver:** The unidirectional downstream flow of water, which also transports minerals, organic matter, and organisms.
*   **Contrast:** Lentic ecosystems (lakes, ponds) have still or standing water.

### 2. Foundational Controls
The character of any river ecosystem is shaped by:
*   **Catchment Area (Watershed):** The land area that drains into the river.
*   **Climate:** Determines precipitation and temperature.
*   **Geology:** Influences water chemistry and sediment type.
*   **Channel Gradient:** Steepness from source to mouth.
