    *   **Middle & Lower Reaches:** Warmer, slower, finer substrates (sand/silt); increased **autochthonous** production (algae, plants); larger, more diverse fish; floodplain interactions.
    *   **Terminus:** Estuarine transition to saltwater, supporting brackish-tolerant species.
*   **Ecosystem Health & Threats:** Dependence on natural flow, temperature, sediment, oxygen, and riparian zones. Vulnerability to human alterations like dams, channelization, pollution, and land-use change.
