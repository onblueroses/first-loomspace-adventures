### **Key Ecosystem Processes & Concepts**
*   **River Continuum Concept:** This is a fundamental model that describes the predictable changes in physical conditions and biological communities from a stream's source to its mouth, much like the progression you outlined.
*   **Nutrient Spiraling:** In a flowing system, nutrients like nitrogen and phosphorus are not simply recycled in one spot. They are taken up by organisms, released back into the water, and transported downstream before being taken up again, creating a "spiral" pattern.
*   **Energy Sources:** The text mentions the shift from **allochthonous** (externally produced, like leaf litter) energy in headwaters to **autochthonous** (internally produced, by algae and plants) energy in mid-reaches. This shift is a key driver of the changing food web structure.
