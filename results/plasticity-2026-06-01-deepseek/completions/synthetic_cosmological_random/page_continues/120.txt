### **Core Definition**
A **river (lotic) ecosystem** is defined by the unidirectional flow of water, which shapes its biological community and transports materials downstream. This contrasts with still-water (lentic) ecosystems like lakes.

### **Key Determining Factors**
The character of a river is set by:
*   **Catchment area:** The land that drains into it.
*   **Climate & Geology:** Of the region.
*   **Channel Gradient:** The slope from source to mouth.
