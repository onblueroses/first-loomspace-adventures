### Summary: River (Lotic) Ecosystems

**1. Core Definition & Controlling Factors:**
*   A **lotic ecosystem** is defined by the **unidirectional flow** of water.
*   Its fundamental character is shaped by its **catchment area, regional climate, underlying geology, and channel gradient**.

**2. Longitudinal Zonation (From Source to Mouth):**
*   **Headwaters/Upper Reaches:**
    *   **Physical:** Cold, fast, oxygen-rich water; bedrock/gravel substrate.
    *   **Biological:** Dominated by insect larvae (cling to substrates); cold-water predators (e.g., trout).
    *   **Energy Base:** **Allochthonous**—primarily leaf litter and woody debris from riparian forests, processed by shredder invertebrates.
*   **Middle/Lower Reaches:**
    *   **Physical:** Wider, slower, warmer; substrate shifts to sand/silt.
    *   **Biological:** Increased **autochthonous** production by algae and aquatic plants; larger herbivorous/omn
