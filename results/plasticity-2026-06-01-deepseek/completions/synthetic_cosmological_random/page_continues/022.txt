### **Major Threats to River Ecosystems**
Human activities profoundly alter the key parameters that define river health:
*   **Flow Alteration:** Dams and diversions disrupt the natural flow regime, eliminating seasonal floods that cue fish spawning and recharge floodplains. Downstream, reduced flows degrade habitat and increase water temperature.
*   **Channel Modification:** Straightening and lining channels with concrete (channelization) for flood control or drainage destroys in-stream habitat, increases flow velocity, and severs the river from its floodplain.
*   **Pollution & Nutrient Loading:** Runoff from agriculture (fertilizers, pesticides) and urban areas (oil, heavy metals) and point-source discharges from industry or sewage treatment increase nutrients (eutrophication), toxics, and sediment. This can lead to algal blooms, oxygen depletion ("dead zones"), and loss of sensitive species.
