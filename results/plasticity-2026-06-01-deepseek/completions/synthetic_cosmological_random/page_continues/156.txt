This text provides a clear, systematic overview of river (lotic) ecosystems. It structures the information logically, moving from a general definition to specific characteristics along the river's course, and finally to factors affecting ecosystem health.

Here is a breakdown of the key concepts and their flow:

### 1. Core Definition & Distinction
*   **Lotic Ecosystem:** Defined by **unidirectional water flow**.
*   **Key Contrast:** Differentiated from **lentic ecosystems** (lakes, ponds) which have standing water. The flow transports water, minerals, organic matter, and organisms downstream.

### 2. Foundational Controlling Factors
The character of any river is determined by:
*   **Catchment area (watershed):** The land that drains into it.
*   **Climate:** Precipitation and temperature patterns.
*   **Geology:** The type of rock and soil it flows over.
*   **Channel Gradient:** The steepness from source to mouth.
