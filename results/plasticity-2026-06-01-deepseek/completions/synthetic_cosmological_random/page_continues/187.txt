### 1. **The River Continuum Concept**
   This is a core ecological model that describes the predictable changes in physical conditions and biological communities from a river's source to its mouth. The page would likely explain how the energy base shifts:
   *   **Headwaters (1st-3rd order streams):** Heavily dependent on **allochthonous** inputs (external organic matter like leaves). The community is dominated by **shredders** (that break down coarse material) and **collectors**.
   *   **Mid-sized rivers:** Become more open to sunlight. **Autochthonous** production (from algae and aquatic plants) becomes more important, supporting more **grazers** (that scrape algae) and **filter feeders**.
   *   **Large rivers:** The energy base often reverts to fine particulate organic matter (FPOM) transported from upstream, favoring **collector** organisms.
