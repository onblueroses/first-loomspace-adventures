### **Human Impacts and River Management**

The alterations mentioned—damming, channelization, pollution, and land-use change—represent the primary threats to lotic ecosystem integrity. Their effects are often interconnected and cascade through the system.

*   **Damming and Flow Alteration:** Dams fundamentally disrupt the natural flow regime, which is the "master variable" governing river ecosystems. They:
    *   **Fragment Habitat:** Create barriers to the movement of fish (e.g., migratory salmonids) and the downstream transport of sediment and nutrients.
    *   **Alter Thermal and Chemical Regimes:** Deep reservoirs can stratify, releasing cold, oxygen-poor water downstream, while also trapping sediments and nutrients.
    *   **Suppress Natural Flooding:** Eliminate the high-flow events that scour channels, recharge floodplain aquifers, and trigger fish spawning, leading to simplified channel morphology and loss of riparian habitat.
