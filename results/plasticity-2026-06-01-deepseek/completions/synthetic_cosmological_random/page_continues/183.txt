### **1. Ecosystem Processes and the River Continuum Concept**
The text hints at a systematic change from headwaters to mouth. A natural continuation would explain the **River Continuum Concept (RCC)**, a fundamental model in stream ecology. It would detail how the energy base shifts:
*   **Headwaters (1st-3rd order):** Heavily reliant on **allochthonous** inputs (external organic matter like leaves). Dominated by **shredders** (break down coarse matter) and **collectors**.
*   **Mid-reaches:** A balance of algal production and processed organic matter. Dominated by **grazers** (on algae) and **collectors**.
*   **Lower Reaches:** Energy base shifts to **autochthonous** production (algae, plants) and fine organic matter from upstream, dominated by **collectors** and **predators**.
