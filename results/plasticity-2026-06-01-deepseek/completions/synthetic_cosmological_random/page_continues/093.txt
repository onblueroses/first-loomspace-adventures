### **Core Characteristics of Lotic (River) Ecosystems**
*   **Unidirectional Flow:** The constant downstream movement of water, materials, and organisms is the defining feature.
*   **Open Ecosystems:** Heavily influenced by inputs from their **catchment (watershed)**, including water, minerals, and organic matter.
