This text provides a clear and systematic overview of river (lotic) ecosystems. Here is a breakdown of its key concepts and structure:

### Core Definition & Distinction
*   **Lotic Ecosystem:** Defined by unidirectional water flow.
*   **Key Contrast:** Distinguished from **lentic ecosystems** (still water like lakes/ponds) by the continuous downstream transport of water, materials, and organisms.

### Determining Factors
A river's character is shaped by:
1.  **Catchment area** (watershed)
2.  **Regional climate**
3.  **Underlying geology**
4.  **Channel gradient**
