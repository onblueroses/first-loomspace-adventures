### Key Ecological Processes in Rivers
*   **Nutrient Spiraling:** Because water flows in one direction, nutrients like nitrogen and phosphorus are not recycled in a single spot. Instead, they are taken up by organisms, released back into the water, and transported downstream before being taken up again—creating a "spiral" rather than a cycle.
*   **River Continuum Concept:** This model describes the predictable change in physical conditions (gradient, width, substrate) and the corresponding shift in the biological community from headwaters to mouth, as you outlined.
*   **Connectivity:** A healthy river ecosystem depends on **longitudinal connectivity** (unimpeded flow along the channel for fish migration and sediment transport), **lateral connectivity** (exchange with the floodplain), and **vertical connectivity** (exchange with groundwater).

### Major Anthropogenic Threats & Alterations
*   **Dams and Impoundments:** The single greatest alteration to lotic systems. They:
    *   Fragment rivers, blocking fish migration and sediment flow.
    *   Transform flowing (lotic) habitats into still (lentic) reservoirs.
