### Key Ecological Processes and Human Impacts

**Energy Flow and Nutrient Cycling:** The relative importance of **allochthonous** (externally derived, e.g., leaf litter) versus **autochthonous** (internally produced, e.g., algae) energy sources shifts along the river's course, a concept formalized in the **River Continuum Concept**. Headwaters are heterotrophic, relying on imported organic matter. Mid-reaches become more autotrophic as light penetration increases. This longitudinal gradient structures the invertebrate community, from upstream **shredders** that process coarse litter to downstream **collector-gatherers** that filter fine particles.

**Connectivity and the Flow Regime:** A river's ecological integrity depends on connectivity in four dimensions:
*   **Longitudinal** (upstream-downstream): Essential for fish migration and sediment transport.
