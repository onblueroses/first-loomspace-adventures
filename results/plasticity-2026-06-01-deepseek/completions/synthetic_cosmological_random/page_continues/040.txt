### Key Ecological Concepts Implied in the Text:
1.  **River Continuum Concept:** The description from headwaters to mouth aligns with this foundational theory. It posits that a river is a continuously integrated system, with physical changes driving predictable shifts in the biological community and energy sources (from allochthonous/outside leaves to autochthonous/internal algae).
2.  **Energy Flow:** The text highlights the shift in the "energetic base" from terrestrial leaf litter (processed by **shredders**) in headwaters to in-stream photosynthesis by algae (grazed by **collector-gatherers** and **grazers**) in mid-reaches.
3.  **Nutrient Spiraling:** In a flowing system, nutrients like nitrogen and phosphorus are not simply recycled in place. They are taken up by organisms, released, and transported downstream in a process called "spiraling." The speed of this spiral is a key measure of ecosystem function.
