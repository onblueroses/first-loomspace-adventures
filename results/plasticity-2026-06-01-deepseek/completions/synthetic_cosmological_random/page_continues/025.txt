### Core Definition & Distinction
*   **Lotic Ecosystem:** A flowing-water ecosystem defined by the unidirectional movement of water.
*   **Contrast with Lentic:** Static water bodies like lakes and ponds.

### Determining Factors
The character of a river is shaped by its:
*   Catchment area (watershed)
*   Regional climate
*   Underlying geology
*   Channel gradient
