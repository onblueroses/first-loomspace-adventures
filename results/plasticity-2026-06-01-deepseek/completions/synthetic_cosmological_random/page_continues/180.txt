### **Defining Feature: Lotic Ecosystems**
Rivers are defined by the **unidirectional flow of water**, which continuously transports water, minerals, organic matter, and organisms downstream. This distinguishes them from still-water (lentic) ecosystems.

### **Key Determining Factors**
A river's character is shaped by its:
*   **Catchment area**
*   **Regional climate**
*   **Underlying geology**
*   **Channel gradient**
