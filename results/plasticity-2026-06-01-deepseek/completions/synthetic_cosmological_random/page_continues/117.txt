### **Core Definition**
A **river ecosystem** is a **lotic** (flowing water) system, defined by the unidirectional movement of water that shapes its biological community. This contrasts with **lentic** (still water) ecosystems like lakes and ponds.

### **Key Determining Factors**
The character of a river is shaped by:
*   **Catchment Area:** The land that drains into the river.
*   **Climate:** Precipitation and temperature patterns.
*   **Geology:** The type of rock and soil in the region.
*   **Channel Gradient:** The slope from the source to the mouth.
