### **Core Characteristics**
*   **Definition:** A **lotic** (flowing water) ecosystem defined by the unidirectional movement of water, which transports materials and shapes the biological community.
*   **Key Determinants:** Its character is shaped by its **catchment area**, **climate**, **underlying geology**, and **channel gradient**.

### **Longitudinal Profile: From Source to Mouth**
*   **Headwaters (Upper Reaches):**
    *   **Physical:** Cold, fast, oxygen-rich water over bedrock/gravel.
    *   **Biological:** Dominated by insect larvae adapted to cling to substrates. Predators like trout.
    *   **Energy Base:** **Allochthonous**—primarily leaf litter and debris from bordering forests, processed by shredder invertebrates.
*   **Middle & Lower Reaches:**
    *   **Physical:** Water slows, warms, and substrate shifts to sand/silt.
