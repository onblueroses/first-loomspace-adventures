Based on the provided text, here is a summary of the key concepts regarding river ecosystems:

**Core Definition & Distinction:**
*   A **river ecosystem** is a **lotic** (flowing water) system, defined by the unidirectional movement of water, materials, and organisms. This contrasts with **lentic** (still water) ecosystems like lakes.

**Fundamental Influences:**
The character of a river is shaped by its:
*   **Catchment area** (watershed)
*   **Regional climate**
*   **Underlying geology**
*   **Channel gradient**

**The River Continuum: Key Changes from Source to Mouth**
| Feature | Headwaters (Upper Reaches) | Middle & Lower Reaches |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Physical** | Cold, fast, high oxygen; bedrock/gravel substrate | Warmer, slower; substrate shifts to sand/silt |
| **Energy Base** | **Allochthonous:** External organic matter (leaf litter) | **Autochthonous:** Increased in-stream production by algae/plants |
| **Biological Community** | Insect larvae (shredders); cold-water predators (e.g., trout) | Larger, more
