### **Core Definition & Distinction**
*   A **river (lotic) ecosystem** is defined by the **unidirectional flow** of water.
*   It is distinct from **lentic ecosystems** (like lakes and ponds) due to this constant downstream transport of water, materials, and organisms.

### **Determining Factors**
The character of a river ecosystem is shaped by:
*   **Catchment area** (watershed)
*   **Regional climate**
*   **Underlying geology**
*   **Channel gradient** (steepness from source to mouth)
