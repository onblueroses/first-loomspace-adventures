### **1. Defining Characteristics of River Ecosystems**
- **Type:** Lotic (flowing water), contrasted with lentic (still water) systems.
- **Key Drivers:** Unidirectional flow shapes physical conditions and biological communities.
- **Influencing Factors:**  
  - Catchment area characteristics  
  - Climate  
  - Geology  
  - Channel gradient (steepness from source to mouth)
