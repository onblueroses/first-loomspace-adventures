**Human Impacts and River Management**
The alteration of natural river processes poses significant threats to ecosystem health. Dams, for instance, fragment habitat, disrupt sediment transport (leading to scouring downstream and siltation upstream), and alter the natural flow and temperature regimes essential for many species' life cycles. Channelization for flood control or navigation simplifies habitat structure, eliminating pools, riffles, and backwaters critical for different life stages. Pollution from point sources (e.g., industrial effluent) and non-point sources (e.g., agricultural fertilizers and pesticides) can lead to eutrophication, toxic algal blooms, and hypoxia.
