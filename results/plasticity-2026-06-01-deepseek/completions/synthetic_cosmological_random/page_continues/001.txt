### Human Impacts and Conservation

Human activities have profoundly altered river ecosystems worldwide, often disrupting the natural processes that maintain their health and biodiversity.

*   **Flow Alteration:** Dams and water diversions are among the most significant impacts. They fragment habitats, block fish migration, and alter the natural flow regime—the pattern of seasonal high and low flows that native species are adapted to. The elimination of natural floods, for instance, can prevent seed dispersal for riparian plants and disconnect the river from its floodplain.
*   **Pollution and Eutrophication:** Runoff from agriculture (fertilizers, pesticides) and urban areas (oils, heavy metals), as well as effluent from wastewater treatment plants, introduces excess nutrients (nitrogen and phosphorus), toxins, and organic matter. This can lead to eutrophication, where algal blooms deplete oxygen levels, creating "dead zones" harmful to aquatic life.
