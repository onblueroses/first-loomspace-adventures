### **1. Defining Characteristics of River Ecosystems**
*   **Type:** Lotic (flowing water) ecosystems.
*   **Key Driver:** The unidirectional downstream movement of water, which transports minerals, organic matter, and organisms.
*   **Determining Factors:** The character of a river is shaped by its **catchment area, regional climate, underlying geology, and channel gradient**.

### **2. Longitudinal Zones: From Headwaters to Mouth**
The ecosystem changes predictably along its course:

    *   **Conditions:** Cold, fast-flowing, oxygen-rich water over rocky/gravel substrate.
