### **Key Ecological Concepts and Processes**
*   **Nutrient Spiraling:** The unique process in rivers where nutrients like nitrogen and phosphorus are taken up by organisms, released back into the water, and transported downstream before being taken up again, forming a "spiral" rather than a closed cycle.
*   **River Continuum Concept:** A model describing how the physical structure of a river (from narrow headwaters to wide lowlands) creates a predictable gradient of biological communities and energy sources (from allochthonous/outside leaf litter to autochthonous/in-stream algae production).
*   **Flow as a Master Variable:** How the natural patterns of flow—including seasonal floods (pulses) and low-flow periods—structure physical habitats (pools, riffles) and trigger life-cycle events for organisms (e.g., fish spawning).
