**Human Impacts and Conservation**

Human activities have profoundly altered river ecosystems on a global scale. Dams and reservoirs fragment river corridors, disrupting natural flow regimes, blocking sediment transport, and preventing the migration of aquatic species. Channelization for flood control or navigation simplifies habitat structure, reduces biodiversity, and accelerates water flow. Pollution from point sources (e.g., industrial effluent) and non-point sources (e.g., agricultural fertilizers, pesticides, and urban stormwater runoff) introduces excess nutrients and toxic substances, leading to eutrophication, algal blooms, and degraded water quality.

*   **Restoring Natural Flow Regimes:** Managing dam releases to mimic seasonal flooding and low-flow periods.
