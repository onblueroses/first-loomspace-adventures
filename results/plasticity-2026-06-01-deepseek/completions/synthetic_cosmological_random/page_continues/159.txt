### Human Impacts and Conservation
Human activities have profoundly altered river ecosystems worldwide. Key threats include:

*   **Flow Alteration:** Dams and diversions disrupt the natural flow regime, which is the "master variable" governing ecological processes. They block sediment and nutrient transport, impede fish migration, and alter water temperature and oxygen levels downstream.
*   **Pollution:** Point-source pollution (e.g., industrial effluent) and non-point-source pollution (e.g., agricultural runoff containing fertilizers, pesticides, and sediment) degrade water quality. Excess nutrients cause eutrophication, while toxins bioaccumulate in food webs.
*   **Habitat Destruction:** Channelization (straightening and hardening banks) destroys in-stream habitat complexity and severs the river from its floodplain. Riparian zone clearing removes shade, stabilizing roots, and crucial allochthonous organic input.
