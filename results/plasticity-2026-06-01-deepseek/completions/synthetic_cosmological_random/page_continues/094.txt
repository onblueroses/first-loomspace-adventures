Based on the information provided, here is a structured summary and expansion of the key concepts regarding river ecosystems:

### **Core Definition and Distinction**
*   **Lotic Ecosystem:** A flowing-water ecosystem (rivers, streams) defined by the unidirectional movement of water.
*   **Vs. Lentic:** Contrasted with still-water ecosystems like lakes and ponds.

### **Key Determining Factors**
The character of a river ecosystem is shaped by:
1.  **Catchment Area (Watershed):** The surrounding land that drains into the river.
2.  **Climate:** Precipitation and temperature patterns.
3.  **Geology:** The underlying rock and soil types.
4.  **Channel Gradient:** The steepness from source to mouth.
