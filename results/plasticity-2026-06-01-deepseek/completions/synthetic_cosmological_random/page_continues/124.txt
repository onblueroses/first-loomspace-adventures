This passage provides a clear and systematic overview of river (lotic) ecosystems. It can be structured into the following key concepts for better understanding:

### 1. **Core Definition & Distinction**
*   A **river ecosystem** is a *lotic* (flowing water) system.
*   It is fundamentally defined by the **unidirectional flow** of water, which transports materials and organisms downstream.
*   This contrasts with *lentic* (still water) ecosystems like lakes and ponds.

### 2. **Determining Factors**
The character of a river is shaped by:
*   **Catchment Area (Watershed):** The land that drains into it.
*   **Climate:** Influences water temperature and flow patterns.
*   **Geology:** Affects water chemistry and sediment type.
*   **Channel Gradient:** Steepness from source to mouth.
