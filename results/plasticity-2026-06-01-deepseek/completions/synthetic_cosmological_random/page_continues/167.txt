Based on the description provided, here is a structured overview of the key concepts about river ecosystems.

### Core Definition & Distinction
*   **Lotic Ecosystem:** A flowing-water ecosystem (rivers, streams) defined by the **unidirectional movement** of water.
*   **Key Distinction:** Differs from **lentic ecosystems** (lakes, ponds) due to the constant downstream transport of water, materials, and organisms.

### Determining Factors
The character of a river ecosystem is shaped by:
*   **Catchment Area (Watershed):** The land area draining into the river.
*   **Climate:** Influences temperature and flow patterns.
*   **Geology:** Affects water chemistry and sediment type.
*   **Channel Gradient:** Steepness from source to mouth, controlling flow energy.
