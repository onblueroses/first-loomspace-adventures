### **Ecosystem Processes and Energy Flow**

The functioning of a river is governed by two primary energy sources:
*   **Allochthonous Inputs:** In headwaters and forested streams, the energy base is external—primarily coarse and fine particulate organic matter (CPOM & FPOM) like leaves and twigs from the riparian zone. This material is processed by a guild of detritivores: **shredders** (e.g., some caddisfly larvae) break it down, and **collectors** (e.g., black fly larvae) filter the resulting fine particles.
*   **Autochthonous Production:** In wider, sunlit mid-reaches, in-stream primary production by algae (periphyton on surfaces and phytoplankton in the water column) and aquatic plants becomes the dominant energy source, supporting **grazers** (e.g., mayfly larvae) and herbivorous fish.
