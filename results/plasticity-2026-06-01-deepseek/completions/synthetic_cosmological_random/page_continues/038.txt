### **Core Definition**
A **river (lotic) ecosystem** is defined by the unidirectional flow of water, which continuously transports materials and organisms downstream. This distinguishes it from stationary (lentic) ecosystems like lakes.

### **Determining Factors**
The character of a river is shaped by:
*   **Catchment Area:** The surrounding land that drains into it.
*   **Regional Climate**
*   **Underlying Geology**
*   **Channel Gradient:** The slope from its source to its mouth.
