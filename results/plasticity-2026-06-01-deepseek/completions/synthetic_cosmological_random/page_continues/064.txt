### **Structure and Function of River Ecosystems**

River ecosystems are often conceptualized using the **River Continuum Concept (RCC)**. This model describes how physical variables (gradient, width, flow) change predictably from headwaters to mouth, creating a continuous gradient of physical conditions to which biological communities adapt.

*   **Energy Sources:** The RCC highlights a shift in the primary energy base.
    *   **Headwaters (Small Streams):** Heavily shaded by riparian forest, they are **heterotrophic** (driven by external energy). The food web is based on **allochthonous** inputs—coarse particulate organic matter (CPOM) like leaves and twigs. Invertebrate "shredders" and "collectors" process this material.
