### **Core Definition**
*   A **lotic** (flowing water) ecosystem defined by the unidirectional movement of water, which shapes its biological community and transports materials.

### **Key Determining Factors**
The character of a river ecosystem is shaped by:
1.  **Catchment area (watershed)**
2.  **Regional climate**
3.  **Underlying geology**
4.  **Channel gradient**
