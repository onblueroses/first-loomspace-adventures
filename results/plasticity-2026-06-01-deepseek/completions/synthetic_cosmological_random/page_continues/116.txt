### **Factors Affecting Ecosystem Health and Human Impacts**

*   **Flow Regime:** The natural pattern of flow—including seasonal floods, low summer flows, and daily variations—is a master variable. It shapes the physical habitat, triggers fish spawning, and distributes nutrients. Dams and water diversions disrupt this regime, often eliminating floods and reducing flow variability, which can degrade habitat and prevent species from completing their life cycles.
*   **Sediment Transport:** A natural river transports and deposits sediment, creating features like riffles, pools, and point bars. Dams trap sediment upstream, leading to clear but sediment-starved water downstream, which can cause channel erosion and degrade spawning grounds. Conversely, deforestation and poor land use can increase sediment loads to harmful, smothering levels.
*   **Water Quality:** Temperature, dissolved oxygen, and nutrient concentrations are critical.
