### **Definition & Core Principle**
*   **Lotic Ecosystem:** A flowing-water ecosystem (rivers, streams).
*   **Defining Force:** The **unidirectional downstream flow** of water, materials, and organisms shapes the biological community.
*   **Determining Factors:** Ecosystem character is set by **catchment area, climate, geology, and channel gradient**.
