### Core Ecological Concepts in River Ecosystems

*   **River Continuum Concept:** This is a fundamental model describing how a river's physical characteristics (gradient, width, flow) change predictably from source to mouth, leading to a corresponding shift in the biological community and energy sources. The text illustrates this perfectly:
    *   **Headwaters (Upper Reach):** Cold, fast, narrow. Energy is **allochthonous** (externally derived from leaf litter). Dominated by **shredders** (break down coarse matter) and **collector-gatherers**.
    *   **Middle Reaches:** Wider, warmer, slower. Energy is a mix of transported organic matter and **autochthonous** (in-stream) production from algae and aquatic plants.
    *   **Lower Reaches:** Wide, warm, slow, silty. Energy is primarily fine organic particles and in-stream production. Dominated by **filter-feeders** and **collectors**.
