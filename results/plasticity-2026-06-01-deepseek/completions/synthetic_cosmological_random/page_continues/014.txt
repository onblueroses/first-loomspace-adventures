**Human Impacts and Conservation**

Human activities are the primary drivers of alteration in river ecosystems. The text mentions several key stressors:

*   **Damming:** This fundamentally changes a river's flow regime, transforming a free-flowing lotic system into a series of lentic reservoirs. Dams block the downstream transport of sediment (leading to erosion downstream and sediment starvation in deltas), alter water temperature, and prevent the migration of fish and other aquatic organisms. The natural flood pulse, essential for floodplain connectivity, is often eliminated.
*   **Channelization:** Straightening and hardening riverbanks with concrete or riprap (a process known as channelization) increases flow speed, reduces habitat complexity, and severs the connection between the river and its floodplain. This destroys riparian zones and the critical services they provide, such as shading, filtration, and bank stabilization.
