### **Summary of River Ecosystem Characteristics**

**1. Definition & Basic Distinction**
- A **river ecosystem** is a **lotic** (flowing water) ecosystem.
- It is defined by **unidirectional water flow**, which transports water, minerals, organic matter, and organisms downstream.
- This contrasts with **lentic** (still water) ecosystems like lakes and ponds.

**2. Key Determining Factors**
A river’s character is shaped by:
- **Catchment area** (watershed)
- **Regional climate**
- **Underlying geology**
- **Channel gradient** (slope from source to mouth)
