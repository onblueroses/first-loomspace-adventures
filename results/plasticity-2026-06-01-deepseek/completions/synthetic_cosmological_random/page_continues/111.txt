This text provides a concise and systematic overview of river (lotic) ecosystems. Here is a breakdown of its key points, organized for clarity:

### **Core Definition & Distinction**
*   **Lotic Ecosystem:** Characterized by **unidirectional water flow**.
*   **Key Contrast:** Different from **lentic ecosystems** (lakes, ponds), which have standing water.

### **Determining Factors**
The fundamental character of a river is shaped by:
1.  **Catchment Area (Watershed):** The land that drains into it.
2.  **Climate:** Determines precipitation and temperature.
3.  **Underlying Geology:** Influences water chemistry and sediment type.
4.  **Channel Gradient:** Steepness from source to mouth.
