### Core Concepts
*   **Definition:** A river ecosystem is a **lotic** (flowing water) system defined by the unidirectional movement of water, which transports materials and shapes the biological community.
*   **Key Determinants:** Its character is set by its **catchment area, climate, geology, and channel gradient**.

### Longitudinal Zonation (From Source to Mouth)
1.  **Headwaters (Upper Reaches):**
    *   **Conditions:** Cold, fast, oxygen-rich water; substrate of bedrock and coarse gravel.
    *   **Biology:** Dominated by **insect larvae** adapted to cling to surfaces; **cold-water fish** like trout as predators.
    *   **Energy Base:** **Allochthonous**—primarily **leaf litter and woody debris** from bordering forests, processed by shredder invertebrates.

2.  **Middle and Lower Reaches:**
    *   **Conditions:** River widens and slows; substrate shifts to sand/silt; water warms.
