### **Overview of a River (Lotic) Ecosystem**

**1. Fundamental Definition & Driver**
*   A **lotic ecosystem** is defined by the **unidirectional flow of water**.
*   This flow continuously transports water, minerals, organic matter, and organisms downstream, shaping the entire system.

**2. Key Determining Factors**
The character of any river ecosystem is shaped by:
*   **Catchment Area (Watershed):** The surrounding land that drains into the river.
*   **Climate:** Precipitation and temperature patterns.
*   **Geology:** The type of bedrock and soils.
*   **Channel Gradient:** The slope from the source to the mouth.

**3. Longitudinal Zones: From Source to Mouth**
