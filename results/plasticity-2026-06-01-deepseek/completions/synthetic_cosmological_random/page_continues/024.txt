This text provides a clear and structured overview of river (lotic) ecosystems. It follows the classic River Continuum Concept, describing how physical conditions and biological communities change predictably from a river's source to its mouth.

Here is a breakdown of its key points:

### **1. Core Definition & Controlling Factors**
*   **Definition:** A flowing-water (lotic) ecosystem shaped by unidirectional water flow.
*   **Distinction:** Contrasted with still-water (lentic) ecosystems like lakes.
*   **Key Drivers:** The ecosystem's character is set by its **catchment area, climate, geology, and channel gradient**.
