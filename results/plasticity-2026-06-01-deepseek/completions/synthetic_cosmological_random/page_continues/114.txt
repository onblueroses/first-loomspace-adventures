### **Functional Feeding Groups: The "Jobs" in the River**
The text mentions shredders. In river ecology, invertebrates are often categorized by their method of obtaining food, which aligns with the type of organic matter available in different sections:
*   **Shredders:** As noted, they process coarse particulate organic matter (CPOM) like leaves and wood in headwaters.
*   **Collectors (Gatherers & Filterers):** They consume fine particulate organic matter (FPOM). This becomes the dominant energy source in mid-reaches, as shredder-processed material breaks down and is transported downstream.
*   **Grazers/Scrapers:** They consume algae (periphyton) growing on rocks and other surfaces, important in sunlit, stable sections.
*   **Predators:** They consume other invertebrates and small fish.
