### **Key Ecosystem Processes and Interactions**

The functioning of a river ecosystem is governed by several key processes:
*   **Nutrient Spiraling:** In a flowing system, nutrients like nitrogen and phosphorus are not simply recycled in place. They are taken up by organisms, released back into the water, and transported downstream before being taken up again, creating a "spiral" path rather than a closed loop.
*   **Energy Flow:** The relative importance of **allochthonous** (externally produced, e.g., leaf litter) versus **autochthonous** (internally produced, e.g., algae) energy sources shifts from headwaters to mouth, fundamentally shaping the food web.
*   **Disturbance Dynamics:** Natural floods and droughts are reset mechanisms that scour channels, redistribute sediments, and create habitat heterogeneity, which is critical for maintaining biodiversity.
