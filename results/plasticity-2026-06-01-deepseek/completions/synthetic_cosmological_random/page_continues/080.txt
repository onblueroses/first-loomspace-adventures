### **Key Ecological Concepts and Processes**
*   **River Continuum Concept:** A model describing how the physical variables (gradient, width, flow), energy sources (allochthonous vs. autochthonous), and biological communities change predictably from headwaters to mouth.
*   **Nutrient Spiraling:** The process by which nutrients like nitrogen and phosphorus are cycled and transported downstream simultaneously, with the length of the "spiral" (uptake length) indicating ecosystem efficiency.
*   **Trophic Dynamics:** A deeper look at food webs, detailing functional feeding groups:
    *   **Shredders** (break down coarse organic matter like leaves)
    *   **Collector-Gatherers/Filterers** (consume fine particulate organic matter)
    *   **Grazers/Scrapers** (feed on algae and biofilm on surfaces)
    *   **Predators** (feed on other animals)
