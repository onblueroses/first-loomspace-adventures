### **1. Key Ecological Processes and Concepts**
*   **River Continuum Concept:** Explaining how the physical conditions change predictably from headwaters to mouth, and how the biological community and its energy sources (allochthonous vs. autochthonous) shift in response.
*   **Nutrient Spiraling:** Describing how nutrients like nitrogen and phosphorus are cycled and transported downstream in a spiral motion, being taken up by organisms, released, and moved further down the river.
*   **Flood Pulse Concept:** Emphasizing the critical role of seasonal flooding in connecting the river channel to its floodplain, driving productivity, and providing breeding grounds for many species.

### **2. Human Impacts and Threats (Expanding on the final paragraph)**
*   **Physical Alteration:** Detailed effects of dams (blocking migration, altering flow and temperature regimes, trapping sediment), channelization (destroying habitat, speeding flow), and water abstraction.
*   **Pollution:** Point-source (industrial, sewage) and non-point-source pollution (agricultural runoff carrying fertilizers, pesticides, and sediment).
