This text provides a clear, structured overview of river (lotic) ecosystems. It logically progresses from a general definition to specific characteristics along a river's course, concluding with factors affecting ecosystem health. Here is a breakdown of the key concepts presented:

### **Core Definition and Distinction**
*   **Lotic Ecosystem:** Characterized by unidirectional, flowing water (rivers, streams).
*   **Contrast with Lentic:** Still-water ecosystems like lakes and ponds.

### **Determining Factors**
A river's character is shaped by its:
*   **Catchment area (watershed):** The land area draining into it.
*   **Regional climate**
*   **Underlying geology**
*   **Channel gradient**
