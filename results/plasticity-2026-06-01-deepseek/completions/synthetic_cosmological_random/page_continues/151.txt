Based on the text provided, here is a structured summary of the key concepts regarding river ecosystems:

### **Core Definition & Distinction**
*   A **river ecosystem** is a **lotic** (flowing water) ecosystem.
*   It is defined by the **unidirectional movement** of water, which transports minerals, organic matter, and organisms downstream.
*   This distinguishes it from **lentic** (still water) ecosystems like lakes and ponds.

### **Determining Factors**
The character of a river ecosystem is shaped by:
*   **Catchment area** (watershed)
*   **Regional climate**
*   **Underlying geology**
*   **Channel gradient** (from source to mouth)
