### Summary of River Ecosystem Dynamics

**1. Fundamental Nature of River Ecosystems**
*   **Definition:** Flowing-water (lotic) ecosystems shaped by the unidirectional downstream movement of water, materials, and organisms.
*   **Key Determinants:** The ecosystem's character is set by its **catchment area**, **regional climate**, **underlying geology**, and **channel gradient**.

**2. Longitudinal Zones & Their Characteristics**
Rivers change predictably from source to mouth:

*   **Headwaters (Upper Reaches):**
    *   **Physical:** Cold, fast, oxygen-rich water over bedrock/gravel.
    *   **Biological:** Dominated by insect larvae (cling to substrates) and cold-water predators like trout.
    *   **Energy Base:** **Allochthonous**—primarily leaf litter and woody debris from bordering forests, processed by shredder invertebrates.

*   **Middle & Lower Reaches:**
    *   **Physical:** River widens, slows, and warms. Substrate shifts to sand/silt.
    *   **Biological:** Increased **autochthonous** production by algae and aquatic plants. Fish
