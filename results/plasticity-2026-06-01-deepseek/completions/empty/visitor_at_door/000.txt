 He is not a friend of the
family, but a stranger.

"Good morning, sir. I am collecting for the X Relief
Fund. Have you any old clothes, sir?"

The visitor explains his business. He is collecting old
clothes for the poor people in the district. He wants to
know if the man of the house has any old clothes that he
does not want.

The man of the house thanks him and says, "I'm sorry.
I'm afraid I haven't any today."

The visitor says, "It doesn't matter. Thank you, sir.
Good day." And he goes away.

The man of the house says, "Good day."
