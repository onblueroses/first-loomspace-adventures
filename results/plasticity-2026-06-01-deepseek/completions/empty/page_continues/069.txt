 “I have been asked to advise all persons of the above name, no matter where they are residing, to meet at the place of the former residence of their parents or grandparents in the County of Lincoln, on Saturday, the 22nd of December, 1877, for the purpose of organizing a society to be known as the ‘Buckingham Family Society’ of the County of Lincoln. The objects of the society will be to cultivate friendship, to preserve the history and genealogical record of the family, and to assist any deserving member who may be in want. A suitable place will be provided for the meeting, and all who attend will be entertained free of cost. It is desired to have a full attendance of both male and female, old and young, rich and poor. If any of you cannot attend, please send your name and post office address and the name of your father and mother, and also the name of your grandfather and grandmother, if you can, to the undersigned, so that your name may be enrolled as a member of the society.”
