 But it is not the man
It was. It is not as tall
As you remember it. The mouth, too, is not the same.

I cannot tell how this man
Died. I heard a cry
As of a great bird from its throat
As he went down.

And I saw the angel
At the door, saying to him,
‘You must be born again.’

He is going to be born again,
In terrible pain, in terrible shame,
From between the legs of a woman
Who screams, who is numbered
Among the dead. And he will not remember

Who he was, or the meaning of his suffering.
He will not remember me
Until I appear after his death

To say to him, now at last
You are the man you are.
He will have forgotten the angels

Who came to him in his agony.
I will be all the angels
And be with him as he falls.

Filling the room
With their vast wings.

They have not the look

Who have been blinded.

And they have no voices,
