 You who come in and close the door.

You who stand there. Your shadow on the wall.

Your shadow on the floor.

I see your eyes and I see your mouth and the shape of your fingers.

Your lips are moving but I cannot hear your words.

The sound of your voice is lost in the sound of the clock ticking.

But I see your mouth. Your lips moving.

And I know you are saying my name.

But I cannot hear my name.

I cannot hear anything except the sound of the clock ticking.

And now you move across the floor.

Your shadow moves across the wall.

And now you stand by my bed.

And now your hand moves. It moves toward my face.

And now your hand is on my face.

And now your fingers. Your fingers on my eyes.

Your fingers closing my eyes.

And now I see nothing but darkness.

And now I hear nothing but the sound of the clock ticking.

And now I feel nothing but your fingers. Your fingers on my eyes.

Your fingers closing my eyes.

And now I am alone.

I am alone in the darkness.

I am alone in the darkness with the sound of the clock ticking.
