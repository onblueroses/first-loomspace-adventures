 The housewife at the table
Recalls the lesson of her grammar school: a verb
Is the word that signifies to be, to do, or to suffer.
She lets the water pour into the basin, she stares
At the utensil she holds. Her mind is full of words
That will not go together: domesticity
And suffering, suffering and to do. She sets
The cup down on the edge of the sink, it shatters.
And then the verb, the doing or the suffering,
Begins. The action of the shattering
Contains the action of the scattered pieces
Skittering across the floor, and the action
Of the pieces that have come to rest contains
The action of the broom, the hand that holds it,
The mind that wills the hand, and the action
Of the particles of dust as they are swept
Into the dustpan, and the action of the dust
When the dustpan is emptied into the trash.

## The Map

In the long moment when I look back,
I can almost see the map of the future.
I can almost remember the next ten years.
Then I see it: the way ahead is the way back.
