If you’re asking in a literal sense, what enters a room could be:

- **Air or light** (from an open window or door)
- **A person or animal**
- **Sound or scent** (carried from outside)
- **An object** (if thrown, carried, or moved in)

If you’re thinking of a **riddle or puzzle**, the classic answer is often "light" or "a breath of air," since these can enter without needing to open a door widely.
