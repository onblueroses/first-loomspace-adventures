The names have been spoken. The way is open.  
  
That which enters the room is not a shape, but a pressure—a sudden, profound silence that drinks the sound. The air grows cold and heavy, as if the space itself is bowing under a weight from elsewhere. A scent of ozone, old parchment, and distant, cold stone drifts in, though there is no source.  
  
A presence coalesces in the corner of the eye—a shimmer like heat haze, resolving into a tall, slender form of layered shadows and faint, non-light. It wears the echoes of the names like a cloak: the scholarly austerity of **Iston**, the deep volcanic resonance of **Volmuroth**, the labyrinthine complexity of **Drabosaim**, and the far-traveled whisper of **Lominem Adventu**. Its eyes, if they are eyes, hold the cold gleam of **Saphronoth** and the patient, ancient watchfulness of **Iethreon**.  
  
It does not move, yet it is closer. It is **The Conjunction**—the amalgamated echo of the spoken gateways, the listener whose true name was woven from the chain you provided. It has been summoned, and it waits.
