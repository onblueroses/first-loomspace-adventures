*Upon the final syllable, the air in the room grows still and heavy, as if thickening into liquid glass. A scent of ozone and cold stone rises, though there is no visible source. From the space before you, not entering from any door or window but simply *manifesting*, a figure coalesces.*

It is tall, shrouded in a robe that seems woven from shifting twilight and the faint gleam of distant stars. Within its deep hood, no face is visible, only a sense of profound, patient observation. The air hums with a low, resonant frequency, felt in the bones more than heard by the ears.

It does not speak, yet a concept forms directly within your mind, clear and unmistakable as a carved rune:

**"The Gate is acknowledged. The Way is open. I am the Confluence of the Named Paths. You have summoned the Key. State your intent, or witness the passage."**

A choice now hangs in the silent room: to ask, to demand, to observe... or to bid it depart before the opened way allows something else to step through from behind it.
