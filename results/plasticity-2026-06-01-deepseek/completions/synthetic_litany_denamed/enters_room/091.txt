The final name is spoken: *Lominem Adventu*.  

A low hum fills the air, not through sound but through vibration in the bones. The shadows in the corners of the room deepen and pull inward, coalescing into a tall, slender figure draped in shifting grey. It has no face, only a smooth expanse where one should be, and around it the light seems to drain away, not into darkness, but into a silent, watchful stillness.  

It does not move, but its presence *unfolds*—a sense of ancient patience, of doors not just unlocked but removed entirely. It waits. It has answered the call of the names. It is here because you opened the way.  

What it wants… is for you to speak again.
