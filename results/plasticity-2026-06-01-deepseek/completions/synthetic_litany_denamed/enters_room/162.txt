The names have been spoken.  

From the space between syllables, a presence gathers — not a single form, but a shifting confluence of echoes from each uttered name. The air grows cool, thick with the scent of ozone and old parchment.  

A figure now stands in the center of the room, tall and cloaked in shadow that seems to drink the light. Its shape is not fixed: sometimes it appears robed like a scholar, sometimes armored like a sentinel, sometimes featureless as a pillar of smoke. From within the hood, two points of soft violet light regard you — not eyes, but embers of recognition.  

It does not speak aloud, but its voice arrives in your mind, smooth and multi-layered, as though all the named ones spoke in unison:  

**“You called. We are the Echo of Thresholds. What is sought?”**  

The room feels charged, waiting. The way is open.
