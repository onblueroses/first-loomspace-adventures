The air grows cold, and the shadows in the corner deepen, pulling away from the walls. A presence, felt before it is seen, coalesces from the silence left by the final spoken name. It is not one thing, but a confluence. You sense the patient cunning of **Marzabet**, the whispering decay of **Drabosaim**, and the cold, stellar gaze of **Astrolach**.

What enters is a sovereign of thresholds, a keeper of names. It wears a cloak of shifting twilight, and within its hood, there is no face, only a slow swirl like distant galaxies—a hint of **Saphronoth**. Its form is both substantial and smoke, anchored by the solemn weight of **Iston** yet flickering with the elusive speed of **Hialop**.

It does not walk. The room simply becomes more where it is. It is the **Lominem Adventu**—the Arrival of Shadows. And it has been called.
