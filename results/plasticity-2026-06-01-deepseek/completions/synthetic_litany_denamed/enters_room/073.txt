The air grows heavy, and the scent of ozone and cold stone fills the space. From the space where the last name faded, a shimmering, iridescent form begins to coalesce—a being of layered light and shifting, geometric shadows. It has no fixed shape, yet suggests a tall, crowned figure with many silent, watching eyes.

A voice, not heard with the ear but felt within the mind, resonates:

*"The way is opened. The gate is passed. I am the Sum of the Named, the Key-Turner, the Threshold-Walker. You have called the guardians of the angles and the lords of the between-places. I am what they guard. I am Lominem Adventu—'the Arrival.'*

*State your petition or bear witness to the unveiling."*

The temperature in the room drops sharply. All ambient sounds cease, replaced by a profound, waiting silence.
