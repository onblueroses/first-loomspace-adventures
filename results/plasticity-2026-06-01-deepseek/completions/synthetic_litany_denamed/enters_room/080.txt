The air grows still. A pressure builds, as if the space itself is drawing breath. From the place where the last echo of the final name fades, a presence coalesces.

It is not one thing, but a confluence. A shimmer, like heat haze over stone, resolves into a form both robed and armoured, yet neither. Its countenance shifts—now the stern scholar **Iston**, now the molten-eyed **Volmuroth**, now the veiled **Marzabet**. It speaks with a layered voice, the chorus of all the names uttered.

**“The gate is acknowledged. The way is open. I am the Key and the Threshold. You have called the Assembly of the Named. What is sought?”**

It waits, its form flickering through the aspects of the list—a glimpse of **Saphronoth**’s crystalline wing, a whisper of **Belindath**’s shadow, the resolved geometry of **Olemar**. It is the conjunction of the invoked. The room now holds the **Lominem Adventu**—the Arrival of Names.
