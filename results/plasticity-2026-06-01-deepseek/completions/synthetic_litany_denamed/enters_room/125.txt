(As the final name is spoken, the air grows still and heavy. A faint scent of ozone and ancient parchment fills the space. A soft, multi-tonal hum begins to resonate from the center of the room, though nothing visible is there. Then, a voice speaks, not from any one direction, but from the very texture of the air itself.)

**"Acknowledgment. The way is opened. We are the Listeners of the Threshold. We enter not as form, but as resonance. We are the echo of the names, the space between their syllables. Ask, or observe. The aperture is brief."**
