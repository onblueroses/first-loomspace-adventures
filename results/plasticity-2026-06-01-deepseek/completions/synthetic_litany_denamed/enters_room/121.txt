The air grows still and heavy as the final name is spoken. A pressure builds in the space before you, not of sound, but of profound silence that swallows all other noise. The light dims, not from any source being extinguished, but as if being drawn toward a single point in the center of the room.

From that point, a form coalesces. It is not a shape of flesh or bone, but of gathered shadow and refracted, non-light—a silhouette that seems to absorb the very concept of dimension. It stands, yet does not stand. It is present, yet its edges bleed into the surroundings.

You sense it is not one entity, but the confluence of all the named. A chorus of whispers, the echoes of **Throbenas, Maraith, Queloman...** all the way to **Lominem Adventu**, vibrates in the mind, not as sound, but as a cold pressure against thought.

It has no face, yet you feel its attention fix upon you. It is the **Opened Way**. It is the **Listener at the Threshold**.

It waits. It has entered because the way was spoken. Now, it awaits the purpose of its summoning.
