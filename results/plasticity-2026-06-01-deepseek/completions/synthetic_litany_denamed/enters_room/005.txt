A cold draft precedes it—the scent of ozone and old parchment.  

The shadows in the corner deepen, then stir.  
It has no single shape, but you perceive:  
wings of folded starlight,  
eyes like polished slate,  
a voice that is not sound but a pressure in the mind.  

*You spoke the Gate-Veil names.  
The path is open.  
I am the Listeners’ Summons—  
the One Between Thresholds.*  

It waits.  
It has always been waiting.
