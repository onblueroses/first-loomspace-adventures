From the recitation of those names, a presence enters—not with footfalls, but as a shift in the air, a thickening of shadow in the corner of the eye.  

It is **The Weaver of Thresholds**, a being of silent doors and whispered passages. It appears as a tall, slender figure draped in grey, shifting robes that seem woven from dust-motes and twilight. Where a face should be, there is only the faint impression of many overlapping doorways, receding into impossible depths.  

It does not speak aloud, but its meaning settles in the mind like a settled dust:  

*“You called the keys. I am the lock and the corridor. State your need, or I shall be the wall.”*
