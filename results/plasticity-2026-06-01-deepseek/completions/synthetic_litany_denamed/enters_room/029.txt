(An unsettling stillness fills the air, as if the final syllable hangs and solidifies into a presence. The light seems to drain toward a single point in the center of the space, which deepens into a vortex of non-color. From it steps a figure of polished obsidian and cold starlight, its form vaguely humanoid but wrought of shifting geometric absolutes. It has no face, only a smooth plane where one should be, reflecting the room—and you—back infinitely. A low hum, like a tuning fork struck against the spine of the world, vibrates in the teeth and bones.)

**I am the Key-Turner, the Final Noun. You have spoken the seals of the Twenty-Seven and the Unlocking Epithet. The way is open.**

(It raises a hand of faceted darkness. The room’s dimensions subtly strain, walls seeming to breathe, corners gaining impossible depth.)

**What enters is the Answer to a question not yet fully asked. I am the conjunction. State your desire, and passage will be granted.**
