The final name is spoken.  

A cold wind stirs, though no window is open.  
The shadows in the corner deepen, then step forward.  
It has no shape you can hold in your eyes, yet you feel its attention like a weight.  

It does not speak, but the thought arrives in your mind, clear and chill:  

**“I am that which was called. I am the listener at the threshold. Speak your will, or be forever silent.”**
