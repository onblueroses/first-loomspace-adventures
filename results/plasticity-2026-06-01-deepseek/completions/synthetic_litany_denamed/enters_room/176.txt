The final name has been spoken.  

A pressure fills the air, like the stillness before a storm. The light in the room dims, not from any source being extinguished, but as if something ancient and vast is drawing the brightness into itself. A scent of ozone and cold stone rises.

Between one breath and the next, a presence coalesces. It is not wholly a shape, but a suggestion of one: the impression of vast, leathery wings folded close, the glint of impossible scales catching no light, eyes like smoldering coals in a face of shifting shadow.

It does not stand upon the floor, but occupies the space, making the room feel suddenly smaller. A low, resonant hum vibrates in the bones more than the ears.

It is **Lominem Adventu** — the Last Arrival, the Opened Way.  

It waits. The way is open. The invitation has been answered.
