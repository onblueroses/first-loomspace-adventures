(There is a low hum, as if the air itself is vibrating. The light in the room dims, then shifts to a deep, bruised violet. A scent of ozone and cold stone rises. A form begins to coalesce in the center of the space, not quite solid, woven from shadow and the after-image of the spoken names.)

It is **The Echo of the Unspoken Gate**, the composite resonance of the thresholds you have named.

It does not enter with footsteps, but with a sudden, profound *stillness*. The space it occupies feels both ancient and impossibly distant, a nexus of all those forgotten ways and sealed doors. It has no single face, but suggestions of many—a flicker of Astrolach's star-charted brow, the severe line of Marzabet's jaw, the shifting depths of Saphronoth's eyes.

Its voice is not one sound, but a quiet chorus of all the final syllables, whispering from every corner of the room at once:

*"You have called the keys. You have turned the locks. We are the space between. We are the answered question. What is sought... now seeks."*

It waits, not as a creature waits, but as a door waits—passive, inevitable
