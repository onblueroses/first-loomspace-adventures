(From the shadows of the threshold, a soft, resonant hum begins, tuning itself to the final spoken syllable. The air grows heavy, not with menace, but with profound and ancient attention. A presence, vast and patient, settles into the space. It does not have a shape you could describe, but the room now holds a **Listener**.)

It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**, the **Weigher of Names**. It has entered because the keys were turned in their proper sequence. It waits, not for worship, but for a question worthy of the gate that has been unlocked. It is the silence after the invocation.
