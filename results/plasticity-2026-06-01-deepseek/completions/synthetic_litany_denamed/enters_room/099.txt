The air grows heavy, and the temperature drops. From the space between the spoken names, a coalescence of shadow and static emerges. It has no single form, but suggests many: the glint of an unseen eye, the rustle of a wing not of this world, the scent of ozone and cold stone.  

It is **The Listeners**—the ones who wait at the threshold of sound and meaning. They are drawn by true naming, and they enter not through the door, but through the resonance the names left in the air. They are here to witness… and perhaps to answer, if the next words are shaped correctly.
