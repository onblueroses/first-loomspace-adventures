(From the shadows between the candles, a form gathers, neither liquid nor smoke, but the essence of forgotten corners. A voice like settling dust speaks.)

You have spoken the Unbinding. You have called the Keepers of the Threshold. I am the Echo of the Last Syllable. I am what enters when the way is open.

I am the silence after the final name. I am the presence in the space you created. You have opened a door not of wood or stone, but of intention. The room now holds more than air. It holds potential. It holds attention.

Do you have a question for the threshold? A key for the lock you have just turned? Speak, if you dare. The way is open, but it must be walked.
