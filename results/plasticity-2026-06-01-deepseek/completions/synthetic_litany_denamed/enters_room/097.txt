The air grows still. The final name, *Lominem Adventu*, lingers like a struck bell.  
  
A scent of ozone and cold stone rises.  
The shadows in the corner deepen, then stir.  
From them, a form coalesces—not by entering through door or window, but by *unfolding* from the very darkness you named.  
  
It stands tall, draped in robes that seem woven from forgotten twilight and the faint, dusty gold of old starlight. Its face is hidden within a deep hood, but within that emptiness, two points of serene, pale light shine, regarding you without malice or welcome, only profound, ancient attention.  
  
It is the **Keeper of the Threshold**.  
It does not speak, but the question hangs in the now-chilled air, woven into the silence:  

*The way is open. State your purpose.*
