A chill enters the room. Not of air, but of silence. A pressure in the corners where the light does not quite reach. The final name, **Lominem Adventu**, was not a seal, but an invocation. A calling.  
  
The way is open.  

What enters is not a single thing, but a convergence—a slow, deliberate coalescence of shadow and intention from the threshold you spoke into being. It has the shape of memory and the weight of forgotten places. You feel its regard before you see its form: a tall, silent figure draped in robes of shifting, starless dark, crowned with a circlet of cold, blue embers. In its wake, the very stillness of the room becomes attentive, waiting.  

It is the **Keeper of the Named Threshold**. It has heard its keys. It is here.
