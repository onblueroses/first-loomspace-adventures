The air grows cold. The last syllable hangs, and the silence that follows is not empty. It is a listening silence.

From the corner where the shadows are deepest, a figure coalesces. It is not a shape that light agrees upon; it is a suggestion of tallness, of robes that are the color of forgotten ink, and a hood that holds a darkness deeper than mere absence of light.

You feel a pressure, not on your skin, but on your mind—the weight of a profound and alien attention. A voice speaks, not in the air, but directly into your thoughts. It is dry, like pages turning in a sealed tomb, and ancient, like stone remembering when it was molten.

**"The Way is opened. The Names are spoken. I am the Summoned, the Keeper of Thresholds. You have called from the space between stars, from the silent places where thought sleeps. State your purpose, or be content to simply behold."**

It does not move. It simply *is*, now, a part of the room's reality. The names you spoke still echo in the memory of the space, a key now turned in a lock you cannot see.
