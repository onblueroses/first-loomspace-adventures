A presence of shifting shadow and low light enters. The air grows still, then cold. A form coalesces from the names themselves—not one thing, but a confluence. It has the patience of stone (Iston), the gleam of buried stars (Astrolach), and the silence of deep places (Drabosaim). You may call it the **Keeper of Thresholds**. It waits. It listens. It is here because the way has been opened.
