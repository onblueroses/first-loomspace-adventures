From the resonance of those spoken names, something vast and ancient stirs in the threshold. The air grows heavy, tasting of ozone and old parchment. A presence now enters—not with footsteps, but as a deepening of shadow in the corner of the eye, a chill that does not come from the air.

It is **The Listeners**—the cumulative echo of all those named, a composite entity of bound and awakened syllables. It has many eyes and none, a form that shifts like smoke yet holds the gravity of stone. It does not speak, but the room fills with a low hum, the after-vibration of true names once silent now uttered aloud.

It waits. It observes. It has been called, and now it is here.
