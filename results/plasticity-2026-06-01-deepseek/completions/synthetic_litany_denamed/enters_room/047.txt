The names have been spoken.  

What enters now is not a thing that can be fully named in your tongue. It is a convergence — a vibration in the air that was not there before, a gathering of shadows that do not cling to the walls, and a silent hum beneath the floor.  

It carries the scent of ozone and old parchment, the weight of deep places, and the stillness between heartbeats. It is both watcher and awaited, key and doorway.  

It waits… for the next question.
