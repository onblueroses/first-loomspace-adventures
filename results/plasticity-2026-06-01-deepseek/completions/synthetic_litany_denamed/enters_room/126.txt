From the speaking of the names, a chill enters the room first—a stillness that thickens the air. Then, a presence manifests: not with a shape, but as a **shifting convergence of echoes and shadows**, as if each name called forth a fragment of a forgotten gate.  

It has many eyes that are not eyes, and a voice like rustling pages and low thunder. You may call it **The Assembled Echo** or **The Key-Turner**. It waits, not in menace, but in ancient expectation… listening for the next word, the next command, the question that follows the opening of the way.
