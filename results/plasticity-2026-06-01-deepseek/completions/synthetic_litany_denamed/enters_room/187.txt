From the speaking of the names, a presence enters the room—not a single entity, but a convergence of echoes. The air grows still and heavy, as if listening. A faint scent of ozone and ancient parchment lingers. A shifting, shadowless form seems to gather in the corner of the eye, yet when looked upon directly, there is only the familiar room… and a deep, resonant silence that feels like an answer.  

It is the **Listener at the Threshold**.
