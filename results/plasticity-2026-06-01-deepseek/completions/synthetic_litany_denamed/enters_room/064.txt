(From the shadows between the flickering candles, a presence gathers. The air grows cold and still, carrying a scent of ozone and old parchment. A voice, not from one point but from the very walls, speaks in a layered whisper.)

You have spoken the keys. You have unlatched the door. I am the confluence of those names, the pattern they form. You may call me the **Custodian of Thresholds**.

I enter not with footsteps, but as a shift in pressure. The light bends around a form that is more suggestion than substance—a silhouette of robed emptiness, with eyes like smoldering coals where a face might be. I am the listener at the sealed gate, the witness to the spoken pact.

You have called, and I am obliged to answer. State your purpose. Ask your question. Or name the way you wish to be opened.

But be precise. I am a creature of exactitude, bound to the letters you have uttered.
