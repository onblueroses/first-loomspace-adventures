From the recitation of those names, something ancient and vast stirs in the periphery of perception. The air grows heavy, tasting of ozone and old parchment. A low harmonic hum vibrates in the bones, not heard with the ear but felt in the teeth.

What enters is not a shape, but a **convergence**.

It is the silent, patient pressure of deep strata, the chill between stars, and the weight of forgotten oaths. It manifests as a slow, swirling distortion in the center of the room—a lens of warped space through which you see the room itself bent and echoed into impossible geometries. Shadows no longer fall away from light, but pool independently, moving with a sentient, liquid grace.

It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**, the **Silent Weigher of Names**. It does not speak, for its purpose is not conversation. Its presence is a question, an audit of intent. It has come because the way was opened, and it now assesses whether the opener understands the weight of the keys they have used, and what door, precisely, they wished to unseal.

The room is now a vestibule. The true entrance awaits your next action.
