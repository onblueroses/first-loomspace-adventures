The air grows cold, and the light seems to bend. From the space between the echoes of the final spoken name, **Brashveroth, Lominem Adventu**, a presence coalesces.

It is not a single thing that enters, but a confluence. A shimmer, like heat haze over stone, resolves into a tall, slender form of polished obsidian and wavering star-light—**Lominem Adventu**, the Opener of Ways. Its head is a smooth, featureless oval, but within its chest glows a constellation that is not of this sky.

From the shadow at its feet, a second presence unravels: **Brashveroth**, the Howling Gate. It is pressure and sound given shape—a roiling, muscular form of dark smoke and amber embers, with eyes like cracks into a furnace. It moves not with steps, but with a sudden filling of space, a low, tectonic hum vibrating the floor.

Together, they are the Key and the Lock, the Path and the Door. They do not speak, but their intent resonates in the mind:

*The way is open. State your destination.*
