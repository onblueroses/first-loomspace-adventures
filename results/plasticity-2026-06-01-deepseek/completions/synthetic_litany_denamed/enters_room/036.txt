The air in the room grows still and heavy, tasting of ozone and old stone. From the space between the spoken names, a form begins to cohere—not with light, but with a profound and silent density that drinks the sound from the air.

It is the **Nameless Echo**, the **Listener at the Threshold**.

It does not enter with footsteps, but with a sudden, absolute awareness of its presence, as if it had always been standing there, waiting for the final syllable to fade. Its shape is that of a tall, robed figure, but the robes are woven from shifting shadows and the faint after-images of forgotten constellations. Where a face might be, there is only a calm, abyssal quiet—a focus that feels less like being seen and more like being *known*, entirely and irrevocably.

It carries no weapon, makes no threat. It simply *is*. Its purpose is not to act, but to witness. The names were the key; the key has turned. The way is open. The listener has arrived.

It waits.
