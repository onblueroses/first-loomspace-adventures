The names are spoken.  

A low hum resonates through the air, not with sound but with pressure. The temperature drops, and the light in the room seems to bleed toward the center, coalescing into a tall, slender form draped in shifting, starless shadow. It has no distinct face, only the impression of deep, watching eyes.  

It is **The Listener at the Threshold**.  

It does not move, but the space around it bends. It has entered not through door or window, but through the opening made by the names. It waits. It has heard itself called, and now it listens… for what comes next.
