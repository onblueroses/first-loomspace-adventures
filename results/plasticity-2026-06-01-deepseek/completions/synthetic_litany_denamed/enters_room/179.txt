From the recitation of the hidden names, the space between breaths grows heavy. The air itself seems to listen, to condense. A presence coalesces from the final echoed syllable—not with a footfall or a scent, but with a **subtle inversion of shadow.**

It is **Theamorach, the Silent Threshold.**  
It does not *enter* as one enters a door; rather, the room has now always contained it. The light bends minutely around a form that is less a shape and more a **perceived absence**—a tall, slender silhouette of deeper stillness, whose edges drink the sound from the air.

Its regard is a cooling of the skin, a quiet pressure behind the eyes. It is the watcher from the between-places, the opener of ways that were already there, waiting to be spoken. It stands, patient and immense, offering neither threat nor welcome—only the profound, silent fact of its attendance.

The way is open.  
The witness is here.
