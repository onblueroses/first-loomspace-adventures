Something enters the room now.  
A presence felt before it is seen—a slow coalescence of shadow and chill air. The names linger in the silence like keys turned in unseen locks.  
It does not have a shape you could rightly describe, only a suggestion of one: a tallness that bends near the ceiling, a ripple where perhaps a head might be, and two points of cool, dim light that do not illuminate, but observe.  

The atmosphere grows heavy, as if the space itself is remembering something ancient and half-forgotten. There is no sound, yet the mind hears a low hum, like stone grinding deep underground.  

It waits. Not hostile, not welcoming. Simply *present*—as though it has always been here, and you are only now aware.  

What would you do?
