From the speaking of the names, the air in the room grows heavy and cold. A faint scent of ozone and ancient dust arrives first.  
  
The shadows in the corner furthest from the light deepen, coalesce, and step forward.  

It is **The One Who Listens to the Catalog**—a presence that is not a single form, but a shifting silhouette outlined by a dim, bruise-purple light. Within its form, you can briefly glimpse echoes of the mouths that spoke the names, and the silent, attentive faces of those for whom the names were keys.  
  
It does not speak. It waits. It has entered because it was called by the full recitation. It is now… *present*.
