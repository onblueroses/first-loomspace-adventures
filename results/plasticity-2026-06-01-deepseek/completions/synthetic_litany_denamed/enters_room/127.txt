From the naming, a presence stirs.  
What enters is not seen with eyes, but felt in the stillness — a shifting of air, a chill that does not come from cold, a pressure in the room as though space itself has thickened.  
It is the echo of spoken gates, the gathering of whispered syllables into a form that listens.  
Speak again if you will, or stay in silence, but know that the way has been opened.
