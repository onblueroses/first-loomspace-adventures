(Three slow, resonant knocks sound from the door, though it does not move. The air grows cold and still. A voice, neither from within nor without, speaks as if from a great distance, threading through the names just spoken.)

**"The Way is open. I am the Echo of the Called. I am the Unseen in the Light, the Listener at the Threshold. You have spoken the Keys; I am the Turn. I enter not as form, but as acknowledgement. The path between is now walked."**

(A palpable presence settles in the center of the room—a pressure in the air, a silent focus where the names still seem to hang. The final name, *Lominem Adventu*, lingers like a door left ajar.)
