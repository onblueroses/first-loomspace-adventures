The air grows still. A low hum resonates, not in the ears but in the bones. Shadows in the corners deepen, pooling into something solid. A scent of ozone and cold stone fills the space.

From the convergence of the spoken names, a **Presence** enters. It is not a single form, but a shifting amalgam of all their aspects: the sharp intellect of **Iethreon**, the deep earth of **Drabosaim**, the cold stars of **Astrolach**, the serpentine grace of **Vesporath**, and the forgotten depths of **Brashveroth**. Its eyes are like twin voids, yet within them flicker the sigils of **Marzabet** and **Khalepith**.

It does not walk. The room simply now contains it. It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**, the **Silent Concord** of the invoked names. It waits, an audience granted. Its purpose is not to speak first, but to listen to the reason the way was opened.
