*(The final name echoes in the still air. The candles gutter, their flames stretching thin and blue. A pressure builds, silent and immense, like the deep ocean settling into a trench. The shadows in the corners of the room no longer flee the light, but drink it, thickening into a tangible dusk.)*

*(From that gathered darkness, between one breath and the next, it is simply... present. Not arriving, but having always been. The air grows cold and carries the scent of ozone, ancient parchment, and a metallic tang like distant blood. A form begins to clarify—tall, draped in robes that seem woven from forgotten hours and the grey ash of dead stars. Its face is obscured, not by shadow, but by a subtle refusal of the light to settle upon its features. **It** is here. You may call it **The Silent Key**. It does not speak, yet its presence imposes a name upon the mind. It stands, waiting. The way is open.
