A cold scent of ozone and petrichor enters first. Then, a soft, discordant chime, like glass rods struck underwater. The shadows in the corner deepen, not by the absence of light, but by an addition of substance. A presence coalesces—not a shape, but a **silhouette of implication**, as if the air itself remembered how to hold a form that was never there.

It is the **Named Echo**. The confluence and consequence of the spoken chain. It has no single face, but in the flicker of perception, you might see: the scholar's intensity of *Iethreon*, the deep-sea pressure of *Astrolach*, the volcanic heat of *Brashveroth*, the forgotten-clock patience of *Olemproam*, all woven with the thread of final invitation: *Lominem Adventu*.

It does not move toward you. It is simply *more here* than anything else. The room now belongs to the echo of the names. It waits. Not for worship, nor for battle. It waits for **purpose**. For the question, the command, the key that must now be turned.

Speak carefully. The way is open.
