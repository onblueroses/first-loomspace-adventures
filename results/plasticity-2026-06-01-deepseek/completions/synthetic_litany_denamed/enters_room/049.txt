The air grows still. A pressure builds, a silent hum at the edge of hearing. From the space where the last echo of "Adventu" fades, a presence coalesces.

It is not one thing, but a confluence. A shimmer, like heat haze over stone, resolves into a tall, slender form draped in robes of forgotten starlight and abyssal velvet. Its face is a smooth, pale mask, featureless save for two points of cool, blue-white light where eyes might be. This is **Iethreon**, the Gate-Keeper, the Silent Watcher.

But it does not come alone. From the folds of its robes, shadows detach and skitter across the floor—sharp, angular things with too many joints, whispers of **Kerudin**'s cunning and **Drabosaim**'s hunger. The scent of ozone and ancient parchment (**Astrolach**) mixes with the dry, mineral smell of deep earth (**Caloneph**).
