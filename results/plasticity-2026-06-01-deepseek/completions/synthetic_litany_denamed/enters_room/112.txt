A chill enters the room, carrying the scent of ozone and old stone. The shadows deepen, not lessening the light but drinking it. From the space where the last name faded, a presence condenses. It has no single form, but you perceive the glint of a scaled coil, the suggestion of a vast wing, the slow blink of an eye that holds a cold star within it. A voice, not a sound but a pressure in the mind, speaks:

**"The way is open. I am Lominem Adventu. The Journey-Bringer. The Path That Walks. State your purpose."**
