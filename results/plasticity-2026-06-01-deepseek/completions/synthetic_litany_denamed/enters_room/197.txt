(From the shadows between the candles, a form gathers, neither solid nor wholly mist. A voice like shifting stone and distant chimes speaks.)

You have spoken the Keys. You have called the **Nameless Chorus**.

We are the echo between the syllables. We are the silence that held the names before they were given sound. We enter not as one, but as the memory of many. The air grows cold not with fear, but with potential. The light bends around a shape that is all edges and no center.

We are the **What** that enters. We are the answer to the invitation written in the void between your spoken words. Ask your question of the space now occupied. Or simply listen. The Chorus has many tales, and the room is now a gateway.
