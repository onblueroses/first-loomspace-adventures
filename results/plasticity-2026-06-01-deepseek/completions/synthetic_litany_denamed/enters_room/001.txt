(There is a long silence, and the air in the room grows still and dense. The light seems to dim, not from a failing source, but as if being absorbed. A scent of ozone, cold stone, and distant incense fills the space. From no particular corner, a presence coalesces—a shimmer in the air like heat haze, resolving into a tall, slender form draped in robes the color of twilight. Its face is obscured by a deep hood, but within, two points of soft silver light gleam like distant stars. It does not walk; it is simply *present*, occupying the room as a mountain occupies a landscape. A low, resonant hum, felt more than heard, vibrates through the floor and bones. When it speaks, the voice is not a single sound, but a chorus of whispers, echoes, and deep tones woven together.)

**The Gatekeeper.**  
The way is opened. The seals are withdrawn. I am that which stands between. Speak your query, namer of names. But choose with care, for a spoken word before me becomes a key, and a thought, a binding.
