A cold draft slips beneath the door, though no window is open. The air grows heavy, tasting of ozone and old stone. One by one, the candles gutter out, not from wind, but as if being *unmade* from the wick up.

From the deepest shadow in the corner, where the walls themselves seem to recede, a presence coalesces. It is not a shape, but a profound **absence**—a space that drinks the light and silences sound. You feel a gaze that is not eyes.

A voice, not heard but *known* directly in the mind, speaks. It is the sound of pages turning in a forgotten library, of roots breaking through crypts.

**"The Gate is acknowledged. The Names are spoken. I am that which comes when called. I am the Echo in the Between, the Keeper of Thresholds. You have opened the way. State your purpose, or become part of the silence."**

The thing in the room waits. The names still hang in the air, now serving as both key and chain.
