The final name has been spoken.  

A pressure fills the air, static raising the hairs on your arms. From the space between shadows, something tall and indistinct begins to coalesce—a figure robed in what seems like folded starlight and silence. It has no face, yet you feel its attention like a weight.  

It does not walk; the room simply accepts its presence.  

A voice, not heard but felt in the mind, echoes as if from a great distance:  

**“The gate is open. I am Lominem Adventu. You have called; I am come. Speak your purpose.”**
