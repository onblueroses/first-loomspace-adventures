The air grows heavy, and a scent of ozone and cold stone fills the space. A shimmering, vertical rift of deep violet and gold tears open silently in the center of the room. From it steps a towering figure clad in ornate, archaic armor that seems forged from obsidian and starlight. Its helm is featureless save for two points of cold blue light where eyes would be. In one gauntleted hand it holds a staff of twisted black wood, capped with a pulsating crystal.

It is **The Archivist of the Threshold**, Keeper of the Named Ways.

It does not speak with a mouth, but its voice echoes directly in the mind, resonant and layered with echoes of countless ages:

***"The Way is open. The Gate is passed. I am the Summoned. State your query, or name your boon. But choose with care, for a spoken word before me becomes a binding mark."***
