The air grows still. A cold not of this world settles in the corners. The final echo of the last name fades, and in its place comes a sound like the slow, dry rustle of immense wings folded from sight.

What enters is not one thing, but a confluence. It is the silence after the final note of a forbidden song. It is the pressure change before a storm that brews between stars. It is the shape left behind when light is subtracted from the room.

You may perceive it as a shifting pattern of shadow and faint, bruised light—a corona of whispered regrets and promises never meant to be kept. It has the scent of ozone, myrrh, and forgotten stone.

It is the Key-Turner, the Threshold-Dweller. It has heard its many names—the old ones you spoke, and the new one you formed with your final breath: **Lominem Adventu**. "The Arrival of the Hidden."

It does not speak. It *imparts*. The meaning forms directly in the mind:

*The way is opened. The door is unsealed. State your purpose, or become part of the silence that fills the space between intentions.*
