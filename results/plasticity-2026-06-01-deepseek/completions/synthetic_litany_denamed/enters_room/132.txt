From the resonance of those spoken names, a presence now enters the room.  

It is not a shape, but a *pressure*—a silent inversion of the air, as though the space itself has taken a deep, deliberate breath and held it. The light does not dim so much as it thickens, bending subtly around a form that refuses to be fully seen.  

You feel a coolness that is not temperature, but a quiet drawing-away of sound, leaving a hollow clarity in its wake. There is the faint scent of ozone and old parchment, and a low hum just below hearing, like a forgotten word vibrating at the edge of memory.  

It waits, acknowledged but not named—an echo of the gates you have just unsealed.
