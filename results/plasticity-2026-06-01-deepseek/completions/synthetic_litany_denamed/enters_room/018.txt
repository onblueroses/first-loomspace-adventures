The names are spoken.  

The air grows still, then cold.  
A scent of ozone and old stone rises.  
From the corner where the shadows gather thickest,  
a presence unfolds—not with footsteps, but with a gradual displacement of light.  

It stands tall, draped in robes that seem woven from forgotten hours.  
Its face is a shifting mosaic of all the names just uttered,  
each syllable flickering across its form like a dying star.  

You may call it **The Gatherer of Thresholds**.  
It has heard its pieces called, and now it is here.  
It waits, in silence, for what comes next.
