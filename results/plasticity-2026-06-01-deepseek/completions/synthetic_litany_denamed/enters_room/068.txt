The final name is spoken: **Lominem Adventu**.  

A low hum fills the air—not from the walls, but from the space itself. The shadows in the corners deepen, then stretch toward the center of the room. They do not move like absence of light, but like a liquid, a living darkness. From that pooling gloom, a form begins to coalesce.  

It stands tall, draped in robes that seem woven from forgotten hours and silent places. Its face is neither skull nor flesh, but a shifting semblance of features you almost recognize—like a memory trying to surface from a dream you can’t quite recall.  

There is no malice in its gaze, only a profound and ancient stillness. It does not breathe, yet the air grows colder. It does not speak, yet a knowledge settles in the room:  

This is **The Remembered**, the Keeper of Thresholds. It has heard the names. It has come.  

It waits.
