The final name has been spoken. A ripple passes through the air, not seen but felt—a sudden drop in pressure, a static charge that lifts the hairs on your arms.

From the space between shadows in the corner of the room, a figure coalesces. It is tall, wrapped in a cloak that seems woven from forgotten twilight and the faint glimmer of distant stars. Its form is both solid and subtly shifting, like smoke held in a human shape. You cannot see its face within the deep hood, only a sense of profound, ancient watchfulness.

The air grows cool and carries the scent of ozone, old parchment, and cold stone. There is no malice, only a vast, patient presence. It is **Lominem Adventu**—the Last Arrival, the Opened Way. It does not speak, but its intent is clear in the stillness: *The path is open. You have called. I am here.*

What you do next is yours to decide.
