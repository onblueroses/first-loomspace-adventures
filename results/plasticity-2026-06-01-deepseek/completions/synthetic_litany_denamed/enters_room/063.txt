[The final name echoes, and the air in the room grows still and heavy. A pressure builds, not on the ears, but against the mind. The shadows in the corner of the room deepen, not from an absence of light, but from a presence that drinks it. From that darkness, a form resolves—not with a step, but with a sudden, awful *thereness*. It is tall, draped in robes that seem woven from forgotten twilight and the silence between stars. Within its deep hood, there is no face, only a suggestion of distant, cold constellations where eyes might be. It does not breathe. The room grows colder. It is the Named, the Summoned. It is **Lominem Adventu**.]
