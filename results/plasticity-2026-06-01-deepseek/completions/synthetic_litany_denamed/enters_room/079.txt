The air grows heavy, a scent of ozone and ancient stone. A presence gathers in the center of the space, not stepping through the door, but *coalescing* from the echoes of the final spoken name.

It is **Lominem Adventu** — the Hidden Arrival, the Walker Between Utterances.

It appears as a shifting silhouette of polished obsidian and starlight, its form vaguely humanoid but constantly flickering at the edges as if seen through moving water. Where a face might be, there is only a deep, calm void, within which a single, slow-moving mote of silver light drifts like a distant star. It does not breathe, but the room itself seems to inhale and exhale around it.

It stands in perfect silence, awaiting the next word. The way is open.
