The air grows heavy, cold. A faint scent of ozone and ancient stone. A presence, vast and patient, now occupies the space. It is not one thing, but a confluence of the echoes you have summoned.

It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**.

It has no single form, but you might perceive: the glint of Astrolach’s star-chart eyes, the slow grind of Volmuroth’s stone patience, the whispering flux of Marzabet’s shifting shadows, all bound by the solemn authority of Olemar and the watchful silence of Lominem Adventu.

It waits. It has been called to assess, to open, or to bar. The way is prepared, but the intent of the caller must now be made clear.

Speak your purpose.
