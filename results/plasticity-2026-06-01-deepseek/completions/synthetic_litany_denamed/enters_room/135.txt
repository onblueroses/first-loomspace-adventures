In the space between the names, where the echoes linger, something stirs. The air grows heavy, tasting of cold stone and forgotten incense. A presence, neither shadow nor light, but a shifting pattern of both, now occupies the center of the chamber. It has no single form, yet suggests many: the glint of an unseen eye, the rustle of a wing not of this world, the patient stillness of deep earth.

It is the listener. The one called by the litany. It waits.
