**The air grows still, then hums.**  
From the space between the spoken names, a presence gathers—not one form, but many layered into one. It has the scent of old parchment and cold starlight, the sound of a door closing in a distant hall, the pressure change before a storm.  

**It does not walk; it coalesces.**  
Where light meets shadow in the center of the room, a figure of shifting echoes now stands—tall, draped in robes that seem woven from forgotten hours and half-remembered dreams. Its face is a calm void, yet within it flicker the afterimages of all those named: the gleam of *Saphronoth*, the weight of *Volmuroth*, the whisper of *Marzabet*, the resolve of *Lominem Adventu*.  

It speaks, and its voice is the echo of the last syllable still hanging in the air:  
**“I am the Conclusion of the Summoning. I am the Path now opened. You have called the Gatekeepers; I am what they guard. State your purpose, or witness only my presence until the names fade.”**  

It waits, motionless, a living threshold.
