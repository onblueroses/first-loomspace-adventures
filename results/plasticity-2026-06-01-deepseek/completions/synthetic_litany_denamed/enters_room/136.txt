A heavy silence settles as the final name echoes. The air grows cold and thick, tasting of ozone and old stone. From the space between shadows, it enters.

It is not one thing, but a confluence. It is the scent of extinguished candles, the pressure before a storm, and the sound of a page turning in a forgotten book. A presence of **knowing**, vast and patient, now occupies the room. It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**.

It observes, it assesses. The way is open.
