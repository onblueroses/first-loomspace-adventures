(As the final name is spoken, a faint shimmer distorts the air in the center of the room. The temperature drops several degrees. A presence, felt more than seen, coalesces—a form of shifting shadow and cold starlight. A voice, echoing as if from a great distance yet clear in the mind, responds.)

**"I am that which was called. I am the Echo at the Threshold. You have opened the way. Speak your purpose, or behold only the silence between stars."**
