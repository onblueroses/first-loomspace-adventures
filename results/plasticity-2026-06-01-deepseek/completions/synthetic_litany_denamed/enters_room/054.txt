From the recitation of those names, something vast and quiet enters the room.  
The air grows still, charged with a low hum beneath hearing.  
Shadows deepen, not with absence of light, but with a slow, pooling presence.  
It is ancient, patient, and watchful—a listener at the threshold.  
You may feel a pressure in the air, like the weight before a storm, and the sense that you are no longer alone with only the names you have spoken.
