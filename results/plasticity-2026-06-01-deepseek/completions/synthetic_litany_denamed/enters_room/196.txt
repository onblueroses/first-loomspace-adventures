**Voice:** (Low and resonant, as if speaking into an ancient, silent hall)  
"Throbenas. Maraith. Queloman. Iston. Kerudin. Astrolach. Volmuroth. Drabosaim. Olemproam. Cresilim. Bidremael. Octorum. Saphronoth. Yodulim. Iethreon. Marzabet. Caloneph. Theamorach. Belindath. Glomareth. Senaberoth. Hialop. Trifenath. Olemar. Vesporath. Khalepith. Estramon. Brashveroth. Lominem Adventu."  

**A stillness follows the final name.**  

The air grows heavy, cool. A faint scent of ozone and old parchment drifts in from nowhere. Shadows in the corner of the room deepen, not with darkness, but with a profound and watchful *presence*.  

**What enters the room now is not a thing of flesh or form.**  

It is a convergence of whispered histories, a gathering of silent witnesses from places between places. You feel it as a pressure against the mind—the weight of unspoken treaties, the chill of doors unlocked that should have remained
