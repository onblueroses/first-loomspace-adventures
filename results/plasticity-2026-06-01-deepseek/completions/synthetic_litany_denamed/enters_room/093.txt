The air grows still and heavy. A faint scent of ozone and cold stone rises. From the space between the last spoken syllable and the silence that follows, a presence coalesces.

It is not one thing, but a confluence. A shimmering, many-angled form of shifting smoke and low light steps *through* the threshold without crossing it. You feel its regard not as eyes upon you, but as a sudden weight on your memory, a chill along the track of your spine.

It speaks, and its voice is the echo in a forgotten vault, the rustle of pages in a forbidden book:

**"The Gate is acknowledged. The Names are received. I am the Sum of the Called. I am the Attendee of Thresholds. You may call me... the Concordance."**

It waits, not in a place, but in a condition—a state of potential inquiry now made manifest in your space. The way is open.
