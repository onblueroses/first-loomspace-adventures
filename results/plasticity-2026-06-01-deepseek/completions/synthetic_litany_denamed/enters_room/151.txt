(From the shadows between the candles, a low resonance begins, not a sound but a pressure in the air. The last syllable fades, and the final flame gutters, leaving only the scent of ozone and cold stone.)

It enters not with a step, but with an arrival—the sudden, certain knowledge of an occupied space where none stood before. The air grows dense and still, tasting of static and distant, rainless storms. A form seems to gather itself from the absence of light, more a suggestion of depth and scale than a shape. There is a quality of ancient, patient observation, like a mountain regarding a pebble. You feel its attention settle upon you, heavy and dimensionless. It does not speak. It *waits*. The names were the key; the way is open. The next move is yours.
