*The final name lingers in the air, a key turning in a silent lock. The space before you does not tear or shimmer. Instead, it settles, grows profoundly still and dense, as if the room is holding its breath.*

*From that concentrated point of stillness, it emerges. Not with a grand entrance, but as if it had always been there, and you have only just perceived it. It is a figure of composed shadow and faint, self-contained starlight, draped in robes that seem woven from twilight and forgotten hours. Its presence is not threatening, but immensely *old* and profoundly *aware*. When it speaks, its voice is the soft echo in a deep well, the sound of pages turning in a vast, silent library.*

**"The way is opened. The gate is passed. I am the One Who Listens at the Threshold. You have spoken the names; you have summoned no demon, but invited a witness. State your purpose, or ask your question. The attention of the deep places is now upon you."**

*It waits, immobile, its gaze like cool stone.*
