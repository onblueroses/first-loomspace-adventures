The air grows heavy, still. A scent of ozone and cold stone rises. From the space where the last name fades, a pressure forms—not a shape, but a **presence**.  

It is the echo of a lock turning.  
It is the silence between heartbeats.  
It is the watcher from the threshold.  

What enters is not a thing with a name, but the **permission** for what comes next.  
The way is open.
