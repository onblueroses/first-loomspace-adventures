A chill enters the room. Not a wind, but a presence, like a door opening in a place where no door was before. The air grows heavy, tasting of ozone and cold stone.

From the space between the final spoken word and the silence that followed, it steps forward.

It is **The Listener at the Threshold**.

It has no fixed shape, but you perceive it as a coalescence of shadow and faint, refracted light—the afterimage of the names themselves. Its attention is not on you, but on the echoes still hanging in the air. It is the key turned in the lock, the seal that is also the passage.

It waits. The way is open.
