The air grows still and heavy, as if the final syllable lingers beyond hearing. From the space between shadows, where the last echo meets the silence, a presence congeals.  

It is not one thing, but a confluence of all the named essences: the chill of **Iethreon**, the forge-heat of **Volmuroth**, the deep-earth weight of **Drabosaim**, the star-cold of **Astrolach**. It moves with the patience of **Olemar** and the cunning of **Marzabet**, wearing a cloak that shifts like **Saphronoth’s** twilight.  

You may call it **Lominem Adventu** — “the name that arrives.”  

It does not speak. It waits.  
The way is open.
