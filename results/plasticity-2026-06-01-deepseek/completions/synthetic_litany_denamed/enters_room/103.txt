The final name has been spoken. The path is open.  

A scent of ozone and cold stone fills the air. The shadows in the corner of the room deepen, then stir. From them steps a tall, attenuated figure draped in grey—its face is smooth and without feature, save for two points of silver light where eyes might be.  

It does not walk, but glides forward, and the temperature drops. It is **The Listener at the Threshold**.  

Its voice is not a sound, but a thought placed directly in the mind:  

*“You called. I am here. Speak your purpose, or be silent and let me pass into the world.”*
