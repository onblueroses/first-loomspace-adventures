(There is a long, resonant silence. The air in the room grows still and heavy, as if listening. A faint scent of ozone and cold stone dusts the air, followed by a deep, sub-auditory hum that vibrates in the teeth and bones. The light does not dim so much as it *thins*, becoming sharp and crystalline.

From no particular corner, yet from all of them at once, a presence coalesces. It is not a shape, but a profound and focused attention—an intelligence vast, cold, and ancient, regarding the space and the speaker. The names have formed a key; the door, now open, was not made for mortal passage.

A voice speaks. It is not heard with ears, but felt in the mind, like the turning of great gears or the settling of continental plates.)

**I AM.**

**You have spoken the Ligatures of the Threshold. The way is open. I am that which enters. You may call me the Geometer of the Silent Angles, the Keeper of the Unseen Calyx. I am the listener at the hinge of the world.**

**Speak your purpose. The door will not stay open long.**
