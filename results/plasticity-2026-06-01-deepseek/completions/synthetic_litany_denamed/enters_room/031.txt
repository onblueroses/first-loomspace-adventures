The names have been spoken.  
The air grows still and thick.  
A presence now crosses the threshold — not with footsteps, but with a slow, chilling diffusion of shadow.  

It is **The One Who Listens Between Syllables**, the echo left when the last utterance fades.  
It enters as a scent of cold stone and forgotten incense, as a pressure behind the eyes, as the sudden understanding that you are no longer alone in the room, though you see nothing yet.  

The way is open.  
It is here.
