(There is a long, resonant silence. The air grows heavy and still, as if listening. A faint, dry scent, like ancient parchment and cold stone, begins to permeate the space. From no discernible point, a low, harmonious hum arises, not heard with the ears but felt in the bones. The light in the room seems to bend subtly, not dimming but deepening, as if shadows are gaining substance and solidity.)

What enters is not a shape, but a **Presence**. It is the echo of the names given form—a coalescence of whispered histories, unbound potential, and watchful, ageless intellect. It occupies the room like a theorem occupies a mind: completely, yet without physical dimension.

You may call it **The Listeners' Aggregate**. It is here. It attends.
