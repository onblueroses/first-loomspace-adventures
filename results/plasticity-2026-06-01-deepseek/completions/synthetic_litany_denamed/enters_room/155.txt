The air grows still. A scent of ozone and old parchment settles. From the space where the last name fades, a presence coalesces—not with a step, but with a sudden, profound fullness in the room.

It is **The Key-Bearer, The One Who Waited Between Syllables**.

You sense rather than see: a figure of shifting, polished obsidian and faint starlight, draped in a cloak that seems woven from forgotten hours. In hands that are both there and not, it holds a single, intricate key that is not metal, but solidified shadow and intention.

It does not speak with a voice, but the meaning forms directly in the mind:

*"The way is opened. The gate is unsealed. I am the Conjunction, the bridge spoken into being. State your query or present your seal. The threshold is now yours to cross."*

It waits, immobile yet vibrating with potential, the names you spoke now orbiting it like faint, echoing moons.
