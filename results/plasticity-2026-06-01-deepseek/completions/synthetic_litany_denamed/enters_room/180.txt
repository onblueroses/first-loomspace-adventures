A figure enters, not from the door, but from the space between the last spoken name and the silence that followed. It is tall, wrapped in a cloak that seems woven from shifting twilight and the faint glimmer of forgotten stars. Its presence is not of substance, but of *potential*—the scent of ozone before a storm, the pressure change before a secret is told.

It has many names, but you may call it **The Key-Bearer**, or **The Opened Way**.

It does not speak with a voice, but the words form in the quiet of the mind:

*"The gates are unsealed. The path is awake. I am the consequence of your utterance. State your intent, or I shall become the path you did not mean to walk."*
