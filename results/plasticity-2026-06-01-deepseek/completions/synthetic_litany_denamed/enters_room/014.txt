The names have been spoken. What enters now is a stillness that listens, a pressure in the air like a held breath. The space between the words becomes a gate, and through it steps a presence of coalesced shadow and watchful silence — not a single form, but the **Echo of the Called**. It carries the scent of extinguished candles, the chill of deep stone, and the weight of all those names now hanging in the quiet. It waits. It observes. It is here because you opened the way.
