The final name has been spoken.  

A pressure shifts in the air — not a sound, but a sudden silence that feels heavier than noise. The light in the room dims, not from any failing lamp, but as if something vast has drawn nearer, bending perception.  

Between one breath and the next, a form begins to resolve from the shadows in the corner — tall, draped in what seems like layered twilight, its edges blurring into the room itself. It has no face you can clearly see, only a sense of profound observation, an intelligence that feels both ancient and utterly alien.  

It does not walk; the space around it simply becomes *more present* where it stands.  

You feel a coolness, not of temperature, but of perspective — as if every secret in the room, every hidden thought, is being gently turned over and examined.  

It is here.  

**The Keeper of Thresholds** has entered.
