From the speaking of those names, the air grows heavy and cold. A presence coalesces in the center of the room—not a figure of flesh, but a shifting amalgam of shadow and faint, bruised light.  

It is **The Echo of the Threshold**, the sum of all forgotten gates and unwitnessed arrivals. It has no face, yet you feel its regard. It does not move, yet the room seems to bend toward it.  

What enters is not a creature, but an **absence** that has taken shape—a silent question waiting for an answer, a door that is also the one who knocks.  

It awaits acknowledgment.
