The way is open.  

What enters is not a single thing, but a presence—a convergence of echoes from the names spoken. The air grows heavy, still, and cold. A faint scent of ozone and old parchment lingers. Shadows deepen in the corners, though no light has changed. There is no shape, no form, yet the space feels occupied, listening… waiting.  

It is the silence after the invocation.  
It is the acknowledgment of the call.
