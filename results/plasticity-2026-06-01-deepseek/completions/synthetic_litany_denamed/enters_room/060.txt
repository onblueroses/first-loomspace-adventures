From the recitation of those names, a presence stirs in the air—a coalescence of shadow and shifting light. What enters the room is not a form, but a **silence that listens**, a pressure in the corners of your vision.  

It is the **Keeper of Thresholds**, the one who hears when all true names are spoken in sequence. It waits, neither hostile nor benign, for the next word: the question, the command, or the binding that must follow the opening of the way.
