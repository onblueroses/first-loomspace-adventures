The names have been spoken. What now enters is not a single entity, but a **convergence**. The air grows dense, tasting of ozone and old parchment. A low harmonic hum fills the space, as if the room itself is vibrating to a forgotten frequency.

From the shadows between shadows, a **silent procession** manifests.

First, the **Custodians of Thresholds**: figures with features that shift between those named—a glint of *Khalepith*'s piercing eyes in a face framed by *Marzabet*'s star-strewn hair, robes that flow like *Vesporath*'s twilight yet are edged with *Brashveroth*'s volcanic glow. They do not walk; they unfold into being, holding symbols of gate and key, compass and veil.

Behind them, the **Principle of the Advent** itself—*Lominem Adventu*—takes no fixed shape. It is the palpable force of beginning, the click of a final lock yielding, the door swinging inward on its own. It appears as a corridor of possible doorways collapsing into the now, a silent invitation.

The room is no longer merely a room. It has become a **vestibule**.
