From the recitation of those names, something vast and ancient stirs. The air grows heavy, tasting of ozone and old parchment. Shadows deepen without a light source to cast them. A presence, not of a single entity, but a coalescence of whispered echoes from each name, now enters the room.

It is **The Assembled Echo**. It is not one thing, but all the reverberations of those powers called forth, woven into a singular, attentive presence. You feel the weight of forgotten lore (Olemproam), the chill of distant stars (Astrolach), the patience of deep earth (Drabosaim), and the sharpness of promised thresholds (Iethreon), all at once.

It does not speak with a voice, but the meaning forms directly in the mind:

*"The way is opened. The audience is granted. Speak your purpose to the chorus."*
