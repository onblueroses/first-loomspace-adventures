A stillness enters first — a pressure like held breath. Then, from the space between the spoken names, a coalescence.  

It is not one thing, but many overlapping. The scent of cold stone, of extinguished candle-wick, of parchment turning to dust. A sound like a page turning in an empty library.  

It has no shape you could draw, yet it casts a shifting shadow — sometimes horned, sometimes crowned, sometimes winged, sometimes robed. Its eyes (if they are eyes) are the dark between stars.  

It is the **Listener at the Threshold**, the **Named-But-Unseen**, the **Cumulus of Echoes**.  

It does not speak. It waits.  
It has entered because you called it *by all its names*.  
Now, it waits for the **Question**.
