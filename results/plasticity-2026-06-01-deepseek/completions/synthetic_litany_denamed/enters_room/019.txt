The names have been spoken.  

What enters the room now is not a thing, but a presence—a slow, cold clarity that settles in the air like static before a storm. The light seems to bend subtly around a form that is both there and not there, a shape of shifting shadow and distant starlight.  

It does not speak, but in the silence that follows the last uttered name, you feel a question form inside your mind, not in words, but in concepts:  

*Why have you called the Threshold?*
