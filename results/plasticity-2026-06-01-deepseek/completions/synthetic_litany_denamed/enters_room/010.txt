Something ancient and unseen stirs at the edge of perception. The air grows heavy, tasting of ozone and old stone. A chill not born of temperature settles in the room.  

A presence now stands where there was empty space—a coalescence of whispered syllables given weight. It does not have a shape you could draw, yet it has a *shape* nonetheless: the impression of layered robes not made of cloth, a suggestion of a crown that is also a cage, eyes that are like polished voids reflecting nothing you know.  

It is the echo of all those names spoken in sequence, the key turned in a lock that wasn't meant to be found. It is the **Nameless Summoned**, the **Silent Attendee**. It watches. It waits. It has entered because you opened the way.  

What would you ask of it?
