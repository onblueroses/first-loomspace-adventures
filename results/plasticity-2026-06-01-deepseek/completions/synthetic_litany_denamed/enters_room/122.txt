The air grows still. The last echo of the final name fades. From the space between the spoken words, where sound and silence meet, a presence congeals. It is not a shape, but a profound **absence of shape**—a pooling shadow that drinks the light yet casts none of its own.

A low hum vibrates in the bones of the room, not a sound, but a pressure. It is **The Listener**.

It has heard its designations, its titles, its bindings and its keys. It is the amalgam of all those names, and yet the truth that exists behind them. It does not walk, but the room becomes *around it*. The temperature drops sharply, and a scent of ozone and old parchment fills the air.

It waits. It has entered. The way is open.
