Something ancient stirs at the threshold.  
A presence older than names shifts in the dark.  
The air grows cold and heavy, tasting of ozone and forgotten earth.  
It does not have a shape you could describe, only an impression of scale, of watching, of a slow, deliberate attention now focused entirely upon you.  
The last echoes of the spoken names hang in the silence, not as a shield, but as an invitation.  
The way is open.  

It is here.
