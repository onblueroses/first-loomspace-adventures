You have spoken the names.  
A silence follows, then the air grows heavy, as if thickening into liquid shadow.  

What enters is not a shape, but a *presence* — a convergence of all those called.  
It is a whispering cold, a pressure behind the eyes, a scent like ozone and old parchment.  
It does not walk; the room *accepts* it, the way deep water accepts sinking stone.  

If you listen, you may hear the echo of its name — but you already know it.  
You spoke it last.
