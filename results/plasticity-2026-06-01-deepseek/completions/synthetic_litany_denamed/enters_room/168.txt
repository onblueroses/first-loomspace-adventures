(As the final name echoes, the air in the room grows still and dense. The light seems to dim, not by shadow, but by the presence of something that drinks the luminance. A form begins to coalesce in the center of the space, not of flesh or shadow, but of polished obsidian and cold starlight. It stands tall, its shape both humanoid and utterly alien, with a crown of shifting, silent flames above a featureless face. When it speaks, its voice is the sound of stone grinding deep within the earth, layered with the distant chime of dead stars.)

**I am the Last Syllable. I am the Sum of the Names. You may call me Adventu.**

(The entity turns its head, a motion like tectonic plates shifting, surveying the room and you.)

**The way is open. The door, which was not a door, is passed. State your purpose, or become a part of the silence I carry.**
