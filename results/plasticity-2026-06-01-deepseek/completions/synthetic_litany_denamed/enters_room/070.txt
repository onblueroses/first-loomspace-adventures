The air grows heavy, as if a bell has just stopped tolling. A scent of ozone and old parchment settles. From the space where the last name fades, a form condenses—not of flesh, but of shifting, iridescent shadow and cold, watchful silence. It has too many eyes that are not eyes, and a presence that bends the light. You may call it the **Keeper of the Threshold**. It does not speak, but waits. The way is open.
