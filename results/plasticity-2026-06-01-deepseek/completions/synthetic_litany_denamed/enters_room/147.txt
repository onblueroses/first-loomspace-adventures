What enters is a silence that deepens,  
a pressure change in the air — not a form,  
but a presence that tastes of ozone and old stone.  

The names were keys, and the lock has turned.  
Now something listens from the corner  
you do not look at directly.  

It wears the shadows like a cloak,  
and the light bends around its edges.  
It has been waiting for the speaking to end.
