The air grows still, then cold. A scent of ozone and cold stone rises. From the space between the spoken names, a presence coalesces—not a shape, but a pressure. It is the **Listener at the Threshold**, the **Silhouette of Compiled Echoes**. It has no form you could draw, yet it occupies the corner of the eye and the back of the mind. It is here for the names, to weigh them, and to judge what was summoned… and who did the summoning.
