The final name has been spoken.  

A low hum fills the air, and the temperature drops. Between the last syllable and the silence that follows, a presence now occupies the center of the room—not arriving, but *already having been* there, as if the names were not an invocation but a key turning in a lock that was always there.  

It does not have a shape you can easily describe. It is less a form and more a *difference* in the light, a fold in perception. Sometimes it seems like a figure robed in deep shadow, other times like a column of shifting, oily smoke. At its core glows a single point of cold violet light, like a star seen through a crack in the world.  

It waits. It has been called, but not commanded. The way is open.
