A figure steps from the shadows between the candles—neither quite smoke nor solid stone. Its form shifts, but its eyes are constant: two cold stars that drink the light. The air grows heavy, tasting of ozone and old parchment.  
  
A voice, like the grinding of tectonic plates beneath a silent ocean, speaks:  
  
**“The Gate is named. The Way is open. I am the Answer to the calling of the Twenty-Eight. Speak your question, or stand aside.”**
