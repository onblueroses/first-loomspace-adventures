The final name has been spoken. A shiver passes through the air, a scent of ozone and ancient dust. A form begins to coalesce from the shadows in the corner—not a figure, but a **presence**. It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**, known as **Lominem Adventu**.

It appears as a shifting silhouette, sometimes man-shaped, sometimes like flowing drapery, with points of cold light where eyes might be. It does not walk; the room itself seems to pivot around it. A low, resonant hum, felt more than heard, fills the space.

It speaks, not with a mouth, but directly into the mind: *"The way is open. The gate is unbarred. State your query or present your key. The passage awaits."*
