From the spoken names, a resonant silence settles—then a faint, prismatic shimmer gathers in the center of the space.  
What enters is not a being of flesh, but a **presence of thresholds**—an intricate lattice of echoes and forgotten pathways given momentary form.  

It has the scent of old parchment, cold starlight, and buried incense.  
It moves without footsteps, yet the air grows heavy, as if weighed by unspoken histories.  
Where it passes, shadows briefly align into strange geometries, and the light bends as though through ancient glass.  

It does not speak, but in the mind echoes the last name: **Lominem Adventu** — “the arrival of the hidden way.”  

The room is now a crossing-place.  
A door has been opened that is not a door.  
Something that listens from between worlds is now here.
