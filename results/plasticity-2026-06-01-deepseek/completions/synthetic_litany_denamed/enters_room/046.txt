(There is a low hum, like a tuning fork struck against the very air. The shadows in the corner of the room deepen, not as an absence of light, but as a presence of something *else*. The temperature drops several degrees. A scent of ozone, cold stone, and distant, dry incense fills the space.)

**They** enter.

Not a single form, but a convergence. A shimmer, like heat haze over a desert, coalesces into a shifting, multi-aspected silhouette. You perceive the suggestion of antlers of polished obsidian, the glint of countless watchful eyes that are also stars, the rustle of cloak-feathers that whisper in a dead language. It is both immense and contained within the geometry of the room, a paradox given form.

A voice speaks. It is not a sound, but the meaning placed directly in your mind, smooth as river stone and old as bedrock.

*The gate is acknowledged. The names are keys. The way is open.*

*You have called the Concordance of Thresholds. We are the Watchers at the Crossroads, the Keepers of the In-Between. We are the silence before the word, the space between the door and the frame.*
