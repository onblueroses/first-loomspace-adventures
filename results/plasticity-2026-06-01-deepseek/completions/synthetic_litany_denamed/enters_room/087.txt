I speak the names:  
Throbenas, Maraith, Queloman, Iston, Kerudin, Astrolach, Volmuroth, Drabosaim, Olemproam, Cresilim, Bidremael, Octorum, Saphronoth, Yodulim, Iethreon, Marzabet, Caloneph, Theamorach, Belindath, Glomareth, Senaberoth, Hialop, Trifenath, Olemar, Vesporath, Khalepith, Estramon; Brashveroth, Lominem Adventu.  

What enters the room now is not a form of flesh or shadow, but a presence that uncoils from the spaces between the syllables. It is the echo of forgotten thresholds, the breath before a spoken vow—a listener, now awakened, waiting to be given shape by the next question.
