The final name is spoken: **Lominem Adventu**.  

A low hum fills the air, not heard with ears but felt in the bones. The shadows in the corner of the room deepen, then stir. From that darkness steps a tall, slender figure—its form seems woven from twilight and forgotten hours. It has no clear face, only the impression of watchfulness. The air grows cool, and a scent of old parchment and distant rain lingers.  

It does not speak, but a thought forms clearly in the mind: *“The way is open. What is sought may now be asked.”*  

It waits, a silent guardian of thresholds, presence both solemn and vast.
