A shadow deeper than the absence of light coalesces in the center of the space. The air grows cold and still, carrying the scent of ozone and old parchment. A low hum, felt more than heard, vibrates through the floor. From the coalescing darkness, a form begins to resolve—tall, draped in robes that seem woven from forgotten constellations. Its face is a smooth, featureless obsidian plane, yet you feel the weight of its gaze.

It does not walk. It is simply *more present* where it stands than anything else in the room.

A voice, neither male nor female, speaks. It comes not from the figure, but from the silence between your own heartbeats.

**"The gate is acknowledged. The way is open. I am the confluence of the names spoken. I am the Answer to the Summoning. State your query, or name your desire."**

It waits, immovable as a monument. The names you spoke now linger in the air like fading silver thread, connecting you to this presence. The choice of what happens next is yours.
