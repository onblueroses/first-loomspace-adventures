It is not one thing that enters.

It is a confluence. A seam has been stitched between places, and through it pours a silent, watchful awareness. You feel a weight of ages, a dry, scholarly curiosity as vast and cold as the spaces between stars. There is no form, not yet, but the *idea* of form wavers in the gloom: the suggestion of shifting wings, of multiple eyes adjusting to a light that is not light, of slow, deliberate thought turning its attention upon you.

It assesses. It catalogs. The names were a key, and the door is now open. It waits. It has all the time there ever was.

What will you do?
