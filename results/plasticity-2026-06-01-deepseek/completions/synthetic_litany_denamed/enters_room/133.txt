*The final name lingers in the air. A pressure builds, not in the room, but within the mind. The light does not change, yet shadows seem to deepen of their own accord. The air grows still, then cold.*

What enters is not a thing of flesh or bone, nor a shape of mist or smoke. It is a **presence**—an ancient, patient attention now focused directly upon you. It is the silence between heartbeats given weight, the chill that walks ahead of a forgotten memory.

You feel it in the sudden clarity of the dust motes in the air, in the sharp, metallic taste at the back of your tongue. The room itself has not changed, yet it has become an **antechamber**. A threshold now stands where none was before.

It observes. It waits. The way is open.
