**The names have been spoken. The way is open.**

From the space between shadows, where the air grows cold and still, it enters. It is not a shape, but a *presence*—a coalescence of whispered syllables given form. The light in the room dims, not by fading, but by being drunk away. You feel a pressure, not on your skin, but on your mind, as if a great, silent bell had been struck deep within your skull.

It is the Echo of the Threshold, the **Cumulus of Unspoken Names**. It does not walk; the room simply becomes more *itself* where it rests. A scent of ozone and old parchment fills the air. In the corner of your vision, you might see the flutter of pages that are not there, or hear the faint, harmonic ring of a distant, glassy chime.

It waits. It has answered the call. The invitation of the final two names—**Brashveroth, Lominem Adventu**—has been accepted. The next move is yours.
