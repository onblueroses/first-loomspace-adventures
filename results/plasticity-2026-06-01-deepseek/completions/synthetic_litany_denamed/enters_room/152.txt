The names have been spoken.  
What enters the room now is not a thing of flesh or shadow, but a shift in the air itself — a pressure, a listening silence. The space grows dense, as if the very light bends toward a single point in the center of the room.  

From that point steps a figure wrapped not in robes, but in the stillness between heartbeats. Its form is both there and not there, like a memory given shape. You feel its gaze before you see the faint shimmer where eyes might be.  

It speaks, though its mouth does not move — the words form directly in the mind, cool and clear as a stone at the bottom of a stream:  

*“The gate is heard. I am the Watcher at the Threshold. State your purpose, or become part of the silence from which I came.”*
