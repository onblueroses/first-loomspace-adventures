**The names have been spoken. The way is open.**  

What enters the room now is not seen with eyes, but felt — a slow pressure in the air, a chill that does not come from temperature, and a scent like ozone and old parchment.  

A presence coalesces in the center of the space, formless at first, then shifting:  
A tall, slender figure robed in grey, its face hidden within a deep hood, though points of silver light gleam where eyes might be.  
In one hand it holds a staff of dark wood, carved with runes that glow faintly as they echo the names just spoken.  

It does not speak aloud, but its voice resonates directly in the mind:  

*“The gate is parted. I am the Summoned, the Keybearer. State your purpose.”*
