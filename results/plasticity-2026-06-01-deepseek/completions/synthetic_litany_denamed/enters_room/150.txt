The names have been spoken.  

The air grows still, then cold. A pressure builds in the corners of the room, as if space itself is folding. From no particular point, a presence coalesces—not quite a shape, more a suggestion of depth and watchful silence. You feel it before you see it: a shimmer like heat haze, resolving into a tall, slender form draped in shadows that drink the light.  

Its eyes are twin points of starless black, and it holds in its hands a closed book bound in tarnished silver. It does not move, but the room moves around it—shadows lengthen, whispers echo from the walls in no language you know.  

It is **The Keeper of Thresholds**.  

It waits. It has entered.
