(There is a long silence, and the air in the room grows still and heavy, as if a great pressure has settled upon it. A faint scent of ozone and ancient stone dust begins to permeate the space. From the shadows in the corner of the room, a form begins to coalesce—not quite solid, but undeniable. It has the shifting, multi-faceted quality of light seen through a prism, and its outline suggests a tall, robed figure, though no face is visible within its deep hood. When it speaks, its voice is not a sound heard by the ear, but a resonance felt in the mind and in the bones.)

**I am. The Names have been spoken. The Way is open. You may call me the Keeper of Thresholds. I am the silence between the spoken words, the path revealed by the utterance. I am what enters when the door is unlocked by true nomenclature.**

(It seems to regard you, a sensation of immense and patient attention focusing your way.)

**You have summoned a principle, not a servant. Ask. Or behold the opened way.**
