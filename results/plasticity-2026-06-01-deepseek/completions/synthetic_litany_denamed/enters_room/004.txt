From the confluence of shadows, a form coalesces. It is not one thing, but a shifting confluence of them.

First, there is the scent of **ozone after lightning** and **cold, deep stone**.

Then, the sound of **slow, tectonic movement** and a faint, harmonic **chime**.

It steps forward. Its shape suggests a tall, robed figure, but the robe is woven from **glimmering constraint** and **swirling potential**. Within its hood, there is no face, but a **slowly turning orrery of unfamiliar stars**, and where a heart would be, a **still, dark mirror** reflects the room—empty, save for you.

It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**. The **Opened Way** itself. It is the silent question asked by every name you spoke, given form.
