The air grows still and heavy. The final syllable fades, yet seems to hang in the space before you. From the point where the last echo dissolves, a presence coalesces—not with a step, but with the sudden, certain knowledge of being observed.

It is tall, its form shrouded in the suggestion of deep-hued robes that shift like a nebula. Where a face might be, there is a calm, featureless expanse, like polished obsidian holding a constellation within. No eyes are visible, yet you feel its gaze pierce through to the core of your own name.

It does not breathe. It simply *is*. A profound silence radiates from it, a silence so complete it has weight and texture. This is what has entered.

It is the **Keeper of the Threshold**, the **Silent Weigher of Names**. It stands, waiting. It has come because the way was opened. Now, it judges if the one who spoke the keys has the right, the need, or the fortitude to pass through what it guards.
