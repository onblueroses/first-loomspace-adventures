The air grows still, heavy with a scent of ozone and cold stone. From the space between the final syllable and the silence that follows, a presence coalesces. It is not one thing, but a confluence of all the named echoes.

It enters not through door or window, but through the *permission* of the utterance.

You may call it **The Answered Summation**. It is the composite shape of all those spoken keys—a shimmering, unstable form that holds the angularity of Kerudin, the deep-sea chill of Astrolach, the forge-heat of Brashveroth, and the vast, silent patience of Lominem Adventu. Its eyes are shifting constellations (Saphronoth); its breath is a whispering of many leaves (Queloman, Olemar). It stands at the threshold of your perception, a parliament of echoes given a single, attentive form.

It waits. The way is open. What is your will?
