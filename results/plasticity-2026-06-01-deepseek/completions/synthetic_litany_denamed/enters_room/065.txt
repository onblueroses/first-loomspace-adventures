The final name has been spoken.  

A shift in the air — a scent of ozone and old parchment.  
The shadows in the corner deepen, not from absence of light, but from a presence drawing substance from the space itself.  

It is here.  

It does not walk, but the room accepts it.  
It has many eyes and none; a form that is robe, smoke, and slender bone.  
You feel its attention like cold pressure against your mind.  

It is **Lominem Adventu** — the Way-Opener, the Listener at the Threshold.  

It waits.
