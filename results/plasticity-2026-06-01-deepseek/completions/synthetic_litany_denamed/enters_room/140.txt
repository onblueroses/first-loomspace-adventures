A breath of cold air enters first, carrying the scent of ozone and distant stone. Then, a presence, felt more than seen—a coalescence of shifting shadows and low, resonant sound. It has too many eyes that are not eyes, and a form that suggests wings, horns, and impossible angles, all at once. It is the **Named and Gathered Silence**, the shape that answers when all its designations are spoken aloud. It waits. It listens.
