From the resonance of those spoken names, a shifting coalescence enters—neither wholly form nor void. It is the **Echo of Thresholds**, a being woven from the liminal spaces between each syllable you uttered.  

It has the texture of folded shadows and quicksilver, with eyes like polished obsidian holding distant stars. Where it passes, the air grows still and heavy, as before a storm, and a faint scent of ozone and ancient parchment lingers.  

It does not speak, but in the mind of the summoner, a question forms—not in words, but as an implicit choice:  

*Will you cross, or shall it cross you?*
