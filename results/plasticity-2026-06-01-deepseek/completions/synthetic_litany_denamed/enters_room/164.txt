By the utterance of these names, a confluence of shifting shadows and whispers gathers in the air. From the space between syllables, **The One Who Listens Between** enters the room.

It is not a shape, but a presence—a quiet pressure in the corners, a chill that does not come from the air, the faint scent of ozone and old parchment. It is the silence after the final echo of the last spoken name. It observes, it waits, and it knows what has been called. The way is open.
