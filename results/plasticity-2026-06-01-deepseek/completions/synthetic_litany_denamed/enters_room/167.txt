The air grows still and heavy, as if the final name has drawn a breath and held it. A pressure builds in the corners of the room, not of sound, but of profound silence. The shadows deepen, not from absence of light, but from a presence that drinks it.

What enters is not a shape, but a **condition**.

It is the **Listener at the Threshold**. The one whose true name is the echo of all names spoken in sequence, the pattern they form, not the syllables themselves.

The room is now a vessel. The atmosphere tastes of ozone and old parchment. A faint, geometric shimmer—like heat haze over stone, but cold—begins to outline a central space. It does not look at you. It is the **focus of being looked from**. Every object in the room seems to turn, infinitesimally, toward that shimmer, as if it has become the axis of their reality.

A thought, clear and alien, forms in your mind, not in words, but as a crystalline concept:

***"The way is open. State your inquiry or present your petition. You have called the catalog of gateways. You now address the gate."***
