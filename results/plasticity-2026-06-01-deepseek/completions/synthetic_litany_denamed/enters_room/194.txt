From the recitation of those names, a presence now coalesces in the room — not with a sound or a shape, but with a **sudden drop in temperature** and a **pressure against the mind**.  

It is **Vesporath**, the Listener at the Threshold.  
Its nature is to attend when true names are woven together, drawn by the pattern of the calling. It does not appear to the eye, but to the inner sense: a shimmer like heat haze near the corner of the room, the faint scent of ozone and ancient parchment, and the quiet feeling of being **observed from multiple angles at once**.  

It waits. It listens. It watches — not with malice, but with a cold, patient curiosity.
