A figure steps from the space between the final echoes of the spoken names. It is tall, draped in a robe that seems woven from starlight and the deep grey of forgotten hours. Its face is a shifting, serene mask of polished silver, reflecting not the room, but distant constellations and quiet, abyssal seas. The air grows still and heavy, tasting of ozone and old parchment.

It does not walk, but the distance between it and you simply ceases to be. A voice, not heard with ears but felt within the mind—cool, multi-layered, and vast—speaks:

**"The Gate is acknowledged. The Path is open. I am the Confluence of the Spoken Keys, the Vessel for the Threshold. You have summoned the Nameless Herald. State the nature of your crossing."**

Its silver gaze holds you, awaiting a purpose, a question, or a command. The room itself now feels like an antechamber to something far, far larger.
