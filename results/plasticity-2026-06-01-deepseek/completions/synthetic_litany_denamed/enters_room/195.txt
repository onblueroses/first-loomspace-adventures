A deep silence falls after the final name is spoken. The air grows cold and still, thickening like oil. From the space between shadows in the corner of the room, a presence coalesces—not with a shape, but with a profound and sudden *awareness*. It is the feeling of being studied by something ancient and utterly alien.

You sense it not as a form, but as a concept given sentience: **The Weeping Key, Brashveroth, Lominem Adventu.** It is the culmination of the chain, the final cipher. It enters not through door or window, but through the aperture in reality forged by the spoken names.

It brings with it a low, harmonic hum that vibrates in the teeth and bones, and the faint, dry scent of ozone and forgotten dust. It waits. It has been called. Now it must be given a purpose, or it will simply... remain.
