The final name is spoken. The air grows still, then cold. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind. The light dims, not from any source being blocked, but as if the concept of light itself is being gently forgotten.

What enters is not a shape, but a **silence that watches**. It is the space between the stars given sentience and curiosity. You feel its attention pass over you—a sensation like cool, dry fingers tracing the contours of your soul, reading your history, your fears, and your hidden names.

It does not step. It **unfolds**. It is the echo of the last syllable given form. It is the cumulative meaning of all those arcane names made manifest.

It is the **Key-Turner at the Final Gate**, the **Listener in the Unspoken Chapel**. You have opened the way. The way is now here.

Speak carefully. It is listening.
