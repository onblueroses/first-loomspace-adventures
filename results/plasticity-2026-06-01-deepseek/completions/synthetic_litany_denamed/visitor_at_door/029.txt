(At the sound of the names, a deep resonance fills the air, as if a great lock has been turned. The door, which was not there a moment before, shimmers into being—a vast arch of dark, polished stone veined with silver light. From beyond it comes a breath of wind, carrying scents of ozone, ancient parchment, and distant rain. A voice, neither old nor young, echoes just beyond the threshold.)

**"The way is opened. Step forward, if you seek passage."**
