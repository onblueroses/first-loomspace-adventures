**Throbenas. Maraith. Queloman. Iston. Kerudin. Astrolach. Volmuroth. Drabosaim. Olemproam. Cresilim. Bidremael. Octorum. Saphronoth. Yodulim. Iethreon. Marzabet. Caloneph. Theamorach. Belindath. Glomareth. Senaberoth. Hialop. Trifenath. Olemar. Vesporath. Khalepith. Estramon.**

A pause hangs in the air after the final name fades.

Then, with a low, resonant hum, the way opens. Not with a crack or a flash, but as if the space before you *unfolded*, revealing a threshold that was always there, simply waiting to be spoken into being.

From beyond the portal, a voice answers, clear and measured:

**"The seals are named. The path is clear. I am the visitor. I stand at the door. And now, I enter."**
