(You hear a low, resonant hum, as if the air itself has thickened. A voice, neither wholly within nor without, threads through the names you have spoken.)

**"The way is recognized. The seals are named. The path is woven from their echoes."**

(A soft, silvery light begins to outline the space before you, not dispelling the shadows, but giving them form and depth. The final name, *Lominem Adventu*, seems to hang in the air, a key turning in a silent lock.)

**"Speak now, visitor. The threshold listens."**
