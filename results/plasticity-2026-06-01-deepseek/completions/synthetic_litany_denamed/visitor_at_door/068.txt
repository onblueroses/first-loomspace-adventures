(At the sound of the spoken names, a deep resonant hum fills the air. The space before you seems to thin, like a veil drawn aside. A figure stands in the newly opened threshold, its form shimmering and indistinct, like a reflection in troubled water. Its voice is not a single sound, but a chorus of whispers, echoes, and distant chimes.)

**Visitor:** The seals are named. The way is acknowledged. You have called, and I have crossed. Speak your purpose, or witness my passing. The gate does not linger.
