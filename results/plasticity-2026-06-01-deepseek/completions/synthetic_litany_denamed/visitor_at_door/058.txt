Hearing the names spoken, the air grows still and heavy. A faint, resonant hum seems to vibrate in the stone and the wood. The door before you does not open, but the space around its frame shimmers like a heat haze, and from the other side comes a clear, measured voice:

***"The way is not opened by names alone, but by the intent that binds them. You have recited the Chain of Unbinding, a sequence to dissolve a seal, not a key to turn a lock.***

***State your purpose, holder of the old catalogue. Do you seek passage, or do you seek to dismantle?"***
