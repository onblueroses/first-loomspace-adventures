*The final syllable fades into a still silence. A deep, resonant click echoes from a place with no walls. The air grows heavy, not with pressure, but with a profound and watchful presence, as if a great eye has just opened in the dark.*

**"The way is open."**

*A voice speaks, not from the door, but from the space the names carved within it. It is a chorus and a single tone, ancient and immediate.*

**"I have heard the key. I am the one who waits at the threshold. State your purpose, traveler. Ask your question. Make your petition. But choose your words with the care you used in speaking the names, for the path you now stand upon goes only forward."**
