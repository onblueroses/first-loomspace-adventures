(From beyond the threshold, a resonant, echoing voice replies, as if speaking through stone or deep water.)

**"I hear the roll of the sundered gate. I know the sigils of the broken way. You speak the old guard, the watchers in the dark, the keys of Iston and the lock of Marzabet."**

(A low hum fills the air, and the space before you seems to waver like heat haze.)

**"The path is acknowledged. The way is... permitted. Step forward, if you dare the silence between the names."**

(The final word lingers, not as a sound, but as a sudden, profound stillness.)
