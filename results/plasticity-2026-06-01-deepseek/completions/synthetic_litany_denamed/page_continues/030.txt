*The words settle, heavy and resonant, as if a lock has been turned deep in the earth. A low hum vibrates in the air, and the shadows in the room seem to draw closer, listening. A voice, not from any one direction but from the stones themselves, responds in a slow, grinding whisper.*

**"The seals are named. The way is heard. You have spoken the First Key."**

**"But the path is not a door. It is a descent. Each name is a step, and each step is a question. You have shown you can recite. Now you must show you can *understand*."**

**"Speak now the true question. Not the 'how' of the path, but the 'why' of your feet upon it. What do you seek in the deep places where these names hold power?"**

*The air grows still, waiting. The listed names now feel less like a incantation and more like a map—one you have just revealed, but have not yet begun to travel.*
