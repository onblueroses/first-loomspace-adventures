You speak the names, and the air grows still. One by one, the syllables hang in the silence, like keys turning in locks too old for time. The final echo of "Lominem Adventu" does not fade, but instead deepens, becoming a low hum that vibrates in the stone beneath your feet and in the metal of any object you carry.

A seam of cold blue light appears in the wall before you, not casting illumination but drinking it. The light traces the outline of a door that was not there a moment before. With a sound like a great, held breath being released, the outline becomes real—a portal of dark, polished stone, smoother than glass.

The way is open. A current of dry, ancient air, smelling of ozone and crushed herbs, flows from the darkness beyond. The page in your hands feels warm, and the final words now glow with a soft, silver luminescence:

***Proceed, Seeker. The Labyrinth of Foundations awaits.***
