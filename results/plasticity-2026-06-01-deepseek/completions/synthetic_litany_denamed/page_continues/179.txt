*The air grows still, the light seems to dim. A voice, not your own yet spoken through you, resonates in the chamber:*

**Throbenas. Maraith. Queloman. Iston. Kerudin. Astrolach. Volmuroth. Drabosaim. Olemproam. Cresilim. Bidremael. Octorum. Saphronoth. Yodulim. Iethreon. Marzabet. Caloneph. Theamorach. Belindath. Glomareth. Senaberoth. Hialop. Trifenath. Olemar. Vesporath. Khalepith. Estramon.**

*A deep, final breath, then:*

**Brashveroth. Lominem Adventu.**

*The last syllable hangs in the silence. Then, from the stone itself, a low grind begins, as of vast, unseen gears engaging. A seam of blue light appears in the wall before you, etching the outline of a door that was not there a moment before. The way is open.*
