*The words are spoken clearly into the stillness. For a moment, there is only silence. Then, a faint tremor passes through the air, not as a sound, but as a pressure change, like the passing of a vast, unseen wing. The very light seems to thicken and warp at the edges of perception.*

*A low hum, felt in the bones more than heard, begins to emanate from the space before you. The air shimmers, like heat haze over stone, and within that shimmer, a doorway of rippling, iridescent energy begins to form. It is not a solid arch, but a permeable veil between worlds, its surface swirling with muted colors: deep violets, storm-grays, and the faint, sickly green of ancient brass.*

*From beyond the veil, a cool, dry draft whispers forth, carrying the scent of ozone, cold stone, and something older—the dust of forgotten places. The way is open. The path lies through the shimmering portal. What you seek—or what seeks you—is now within reach.*

*The portal holds, stable but pulsating gently, awaiting a decision. To cross the threshold is to leave the known behind.*
