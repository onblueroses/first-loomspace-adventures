**Throbenas. Maraith. Queloman. Iston. Kerudin. Astrolach. Volmuroth. Drabosaim. Olemproam. Cresilim. Bidremael. Octorum. Saphronoth. Yodulim. Iethreon. Marzabet. Caloneph. Theamorach. Belindath. Glomareth. Senaberoth. Hialop. Trifenath. Olemar. Vesporath. Khalepith. Estramon.**

**Brashveroth. Lominem Adventu.**

*The final words hang in the air. There is a faint, almost imperceptible hum, like the vibration of a distant bell or a tuning fork struck against the fabric of reality itself. The way is open.*

*I look to you, the one who requested the incantation.* The gate stands ready. What is your will? Do you cross the threshold, or do you seal it once more?
