(You speak the names clearly, one by one, into the still air. As the last syllable fades, a subtle pressure seems to lift from the room. The edges of the page before you shimmer, and new lines of script resolve from the parchment, as if written in invisible ink now revealed.)

*The way is opened. The seals are loosened. Speak now your question, or name the one you wish to summon. But know this: a path once opened must be deliberately closed, lest other things, listening at the threshold, take note and draw near.*
