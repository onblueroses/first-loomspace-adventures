*The words hang in the air, a litany of forgotten syllables. As the final name is spoken, the silence deepens, then shifts. A pressure changes in the room, not of sound, but of presence. The air before you seems to thin, becoming a window not of sight, but of passage. A shimmering, silent aperture forms, a doorway without a frame, leading not to another place, but to a state between places. The way is open. What lies beyond the threshold is not described, only implied: a vast, star-dusted nothingness, or perhaps the antechamber of something ancient and watchful. The names have done their work. The path awaits a step.*
