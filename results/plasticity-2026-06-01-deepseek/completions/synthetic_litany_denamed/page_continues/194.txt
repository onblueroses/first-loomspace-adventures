The way is opened by the names of the twenty-eight keepers and the two who come after:

**Throbenas, Maraith, Queloman, Iston, Kerudin, Astrolach, Volmuroth, Drabosaim, Olemproam, Cresilim, Bidremael, Octorum, Saphronoth, Yodulim, Iethreon, Marzabet, Caloneph, Theamorach, Belindath, Glomareth, Senaberoth, Hialop, Trifenath, Olemar, Vesporath, Khalepith, Estramon; Brashveroth, Lominem Adventu.**

The final two are named apart, for they are the gate and the key. The passage is clear.
