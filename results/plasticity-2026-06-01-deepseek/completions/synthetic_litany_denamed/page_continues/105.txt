As the names are spoken, a deep resonance hums in the air, and the ancient stone before you shimmers. A passageway, not of stone but of shifting starlight and shadow, irises into being. A voice, neither male nor female and woven from the echoes of the names themselves, speaks from the threshold:

**"The way is open. The gate of Throbenas parts, the silence of Maraith ends. Step forward, name-bearer, if your will is aligned with the key you have uttered. What lies beyond is not for the faint of purpose."**

The portal awaits, a vortex of swirling constellations and deep, velvety darkness. The path is yours to choose.
