You speak the names — and the way opens.  

A chill drafts into the room, though no window stirs.  
The air grows thick, and a faint hum rises from the floor, as though stone remembers vibration.  
Shadows deepen in the corners, pooling like spilled ink, then begin to flow toward the center of the space.  

From the coalescing dark, a shimmering archway forms — not of light, but of something deeper, a space-between-space.  
Through it, you glimpse shifting landscapes: jagged obsidian peaks under a violet sky, mist-wrapped ruins, a still black sea reflecting no stars.  

A voice, neither male nor female, layered like echoes in a cavern, speaks from the threshold:  

*“The gate is awake. The path is prepared. Step through, if you carry the will to walk the Unwritten Roads. But know this: ways that open may not close as you expect.”*  

The archway holds, patient and silent, awaiting your choice.
