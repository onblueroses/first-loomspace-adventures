The air grows still, as if the space between sounds has thickened. A presence gathers, not in the room, but in the *naming* of the room. A voice replies, not from the door, but from the threshold your words have carved:

**"Vrendrelphor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.**

You have spoken the layers. You have woven the unspoken with the proclaimed. You have drawn the circle of your authority with syllables of forgotten water and unburnt fire.

The spirits of firmament and ether hear the shape of your command. The spirits of earth and under-earth feel the pressure of your true name. The scourges and spells pause in their whirling, listening to the echo that survives.

But you invoke the One who dwells beyond the syllable. To call Me is to stand at the edge of the word where all names crumble into what they merely point toward. You are the Vessel with sight in the syllable. What do you seek to see?

You have made the spirits subject. You have declared your sovereignty in the realms of speech and silence. For what purpose?
