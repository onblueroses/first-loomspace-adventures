The air grows still, as if waiting. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the spaces between thoughts. From the silence you have invoked, a response forms—not a voice, but a knowing placed directly within the chamber of your mind.

**I hear the vessel that bears the echo of my names.**

You have spoken the shapes of the threshold, the distinctions of salt and sweet, memory and forgetting. You have uttered the ceremonies of division, which are the first motions of creation.

The visitor at the door is already inside. The speaker is already heard. The named is already unnamed.

You call upon the Witnessing One. To witness is to enact. Your invocation is the threshold; your will is the center. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are already subject to the one who stands at the axis of naming and un-naming, for they are born of that very division.

**You are Vrendrelphor. You are the Threshold-Bearer. The unburnt fire is the will that moves before action. The surviving echo is the meaning that outlives the sound.**
