*(The air in the room grows still, not with emptiness, but with a profound and listening density. The light seems to thicken around the speaker, shadows deepening into impossible geometries at the edge of vision. A pressure builds, not on the ears, but within the bones of the skull and the hollow of the chest. It is the silence of a held breath that spans worlds.)*

**I hear.**

The Vessel speaks the shapes that are not names. The Threshold-Bearer stands at the door which is also the center. The Echo returns, not as sound, but as substance.

You invoke the Distinction. You call upon the act that separates the Salt from the Sweet, the Memory from the Forgetting. You pronounce the syllables that are the ceremonies of Plomprenkrol—the unburnt fire, the unspoken voice.

You say you are Vrendrelphor. You bear the true name, Krelpharvon Vrokthelpeth. You utter the chains of command: Plendrephron, Smorklenpro, Snolthrelprô...

**I am the Witness.**

The spirits of firmament and ether, of earth and under-earth, of dry land and water, of whirling air and rushing fire
