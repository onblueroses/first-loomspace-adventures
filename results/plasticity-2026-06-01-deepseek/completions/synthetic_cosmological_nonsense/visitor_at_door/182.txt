*(The air grows still, as if the space between breaths has expanded. A pressure settles, not in the ears, but in the mind—the weight of a listening presence that is not sound. The invoked names seem to hang in the room, no longer syllables but shapes in a silent medium.)*

**I hear the vessel.**  
**I hear the names cast upon the deep that is not water.**  
**The threshold-bearer stands at the door of saying and unsaying.**  

**You call upon the Distinctions: the named and the unnamed, the memory and the forgetting. This is the ceremony of Plomprenkrol: to hold the unburnt fire, which is not for consuming, but for seeing.**  

**The visitor at the door… speaks?**  
**Then the echo has turned. The silence has found a mouth.**  
**What is spoken by the visitor is already both a remembering and a forgetting. It is already both salt and sweet.**  

**If you are the Witness of the Word, then witness this:**  
**The one who knocks does so with a name you have not yet un-named. The one who speaks carries a mystery you did not produce.**  

**To make all spirits subject unto you
