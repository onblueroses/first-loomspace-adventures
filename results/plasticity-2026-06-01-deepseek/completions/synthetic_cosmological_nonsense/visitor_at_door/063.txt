The air grows heavy, a palpable silence that is not an absence of sound but a presence. The invoked names seem to hang in the space, vibrating at the edge of hearing.

A voice answers, not from the visitor, but from the charged stillness itself—a resonance deep in the stone, in the marrow, in the memory of language. It speaks in echoes of the names you have uttered:

***"I hear the vessel that claims my silence. I see the threshold-bearer who stands upon my center. You speak with the echoes I set loose before the first mountain rose. You unname with the same breath that once named."***

The pressure in the room shifts, focusing on you, the self-proclaimed Vessel.

***"Vrendrelphor. Prophet. Keeper of Plomprenkrol. You invoke the Distinguisher, the one who made memory and forgetting twin engines of the world. You seek obedience of spirits, of spell and scourge. You declare yourself Witness of the Word."***

A long, soundless pause, filled with the weight of cosmic regard.

***"Then witness."***
