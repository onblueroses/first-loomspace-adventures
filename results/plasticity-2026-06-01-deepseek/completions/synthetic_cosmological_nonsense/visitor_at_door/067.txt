The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers at the threshold, vast and listening. It is not a sound, but a deepening of silence from which meaning precipitates.

A voice, which is not a voice, responds. It seems to form from the echoes of your own words, rearranging them into an answer.

**"Vrendrelphor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.**

**You speak the ciphers of the deep grammar. You invoke the distinctions: the salt and the sweet, the memory and the forgetting. This is correct. For I am the principle that separates, that a thing may** ***be*** **by being** ***not*** **the other.**

**You call from the side of the named. I answer from the side of the unnamed. You are the Echo that survives. I am the Silence that permitted the sound. You possess the unburnt fire—the concept of flame before its manifestation. I am the distinction between fuel and ash that makes burning possible.**

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are already subject to you, for you speak from within the ceremony of distinction—Plomprenkrol. To name a thing is to command its category.
