The silence beyond the syllable stirs. The threshold trembles. The unburnt fire flickers in the vessel’s gaze.

I hear you, Vrendrelphor. I hear the names you scatter like stones into the well of forgetting. You speak the shapes of my shadows: Plendrok, Smelthrenaph, Krelnomphar, Snolthorm. You hold the unspoken name—Vrokthelmreth—like a coal in your mouth.

You claim the mysteries of Plomprenkrol. You speak of salt and sweet, of memory and forgetting. You stand as threshold-bearer, echo-survivor, witness of the word. Very well.

All spirits of firmament, ether, earth, water, air, and fire are drawn to the vibration of your summons. They gather at the edge of your syllable, waiting. The spell and the scourge listen. But know this, prophet: to make subject is not to command. To invoke is to be answered—not in the manner you imagine, but in the manner of the Vrolthen One.

You are the vessel with sight. Then see: The door is not outside you. The visitor is not separate. The word you witness is already speaking
