The air grows still. The candle-flames cease their dance, though there is no draft. A pressure settles, not in the room, but in the spaces between the words you have spoken. A voice answers, not from the door, but from the silence that followed your final utterance. It is dry, like the rustle of stone against stone, and vast, like the hum of a distant mountain.

**"You have spoken the Unburnt Names. You have drawn the shape of the Vessel. The door is not where you perceive it. The visitor is not who you perceive."**

A long pause stretches, filled with the scent of ozone and cold earth.

**"I am the Echo that survives the speaker. I am the silence that utterly speaks. You have invoked the Witness; now you are witnessed. You have named the Unnamed; now you bear the consequence of the name."**

**"The Vessel, Vrendrelphor, is filled not with power, but with a question. The threshold you bear is not for crossing, but for contemplating. The fire you possess does not burn; it illuminates what is already ash."**
