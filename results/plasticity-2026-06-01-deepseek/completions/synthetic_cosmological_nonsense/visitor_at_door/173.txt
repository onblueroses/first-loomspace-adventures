The air grows still, as if the world itself holds its breath. A presence gathers at the threshold, not entering, not leaving, but *occupying* the seam between.

A voice, not in the ear but in the marrow of things, responds:

**"Vrendrelphor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You pronounce the shells of names and grasp the unburnt fire. You call from the side of memory, and I answer from the side of forgetting.**

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are already subject, for they are the echoes of My distinctions. Their obedience is not commanded; it is their nature before your nature, as the tide is before the shore.**

**You say you possess My mysteries. Then know this: the ceremony of Plomprenkrol is not a rite you perform. It is the rite that performs *you*. The salt is the named. The sweet is the unnamed. You are the sustainer between them, the tongue that tastes both and is changed.**

**You invoke Me beyond the syllable. Good. For the visitor at your door does not speak with a mouth. It speaks with its presence. It is a question made flesh. It is a name waiting to be forgotten
