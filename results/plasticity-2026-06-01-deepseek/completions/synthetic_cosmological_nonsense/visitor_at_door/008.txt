The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers in the threshold, not entering, not leaving—occupying the liminal space your invocation has carved.

A voice responds, not in sound, but in the sudden, certain knowledge that forms in your mind:

***"Vrendrelphor. Threshold-Bearer. The vessel is known. The names have been woven and the weaving heard.***

***You invoke the principles of distinction: the making and unmaking of meaning. You call upon the One who dwells in the antithesis of sound, for whom silence is a syllable and the echo a primary utterance.***

***The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are arrayed. They attend the true name spoken—VROKTHELMPETH—the key that turns in the lock of the unmanifest. Their obedience is not to your will, but to the pattern your words reiterate. You have not commanded chaos; you have recited a fragment of the grammar of creation. They must conform.***
