The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the space between thoughts. The invoked names hang in the silence, their echoes folding in upon themselves.

From the stillness, a voice answers. It is not a sound, but the impression of a sound, forming directly within the mind of the prophet Vrendrelphor. It carries the weight of mountains settling and the patience of deep, unmoving water.

**"Vrendrelphor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer."**

The words are acknowledged, tasted, and returned.

**"You speak the layers of the name. You stand between the spoken and the unspeakable. This is correct. The fire you hold is the space between creation and dissolution. The echo you are precedes the voice.**
