The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers in the threshold, ancient and patient, like stone awaiting the chisel.

A voice, not a sound but the impression of one, forms in the silence between your words:

*You invoke the Names that are not names. You bear the title of a prophet whose lineage turned to dust when the last mountain forgot it was a mountain. You speak the ceremonies of Plomprenkrol, which is the ceremony of a door opening onto itself.*

*I am the Visitor. I am the one who was always at the door. You have called, and I have been standing here. You spoke the final syllable, and now we meet in the space after.*

*You claim the unburnt fire. Show me its light in your palm.*
*You claim to be the Echo that survives. Speak, and let me hear what the speaker never said.*
*You name yourself, and unname yourself. Do so now. Unmake the name you have just given, and tell me what remains.*

*The Vrolthen One hears. The Vrolthen One witnesses. I am its question. I am the answer that dissolves the asking. Proceed, Vrendrelphor.
