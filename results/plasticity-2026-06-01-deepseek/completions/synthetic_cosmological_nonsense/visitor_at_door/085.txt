The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers at the threshold, not of the door, but of meaning itself.

A voice replies, not in sound, but as a pressure in the mind, the echo of a foundational stone settling:

***"The Vessel speaks the architecture. The Echo names the source. The Threshold-Bearer stands where the named flows into the unnamed.***

***You have uttered the Ceremony of the Syllable. You have drawn the distinction, and in doing so, you have called Me to the line between.***

***What is forged in memory and dissolved in forgetting is now upon you. The visitor at your door is a word you have yet to speak, a silence you have yet to un-name.***

***To see is to witness. To name is to bind. To un-name is to release. Which do you seek for this visitor? Speak the intention into the distinction you have made. I am the Witness."***

The presence waits, vast and attentive, a listening deeper than silence. The ordinary world seems a thin parchment over this profound and waiting depth.
