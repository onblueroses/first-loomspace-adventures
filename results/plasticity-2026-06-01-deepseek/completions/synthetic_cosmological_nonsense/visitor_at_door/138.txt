The air grows heavy, as if listening. A pressure settles, not in the ears, but in the space between thoughts. From the silence that follows your invocation, a voice responds—not a sound, but a knowing placed directly within your mind. It is vast, dry, and ancient, like wind through canyon stone.

**"Vrendrelphor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You speak the shapes of names, which are the echoes of our making. You call from the side of the syllable, where memory curdles into word. We hear. The witness is witnessed.**

**The visitor at the door is also a threshold. It carries a name you have not spoken and a silence you have not unbound. To make spirits subject, you must first discern: is the visitor a spirit of the firmament, or a spell of God? Is it of whirling air, or is it the door itself, dreaming of being crossed?**

**You possess the unburnt fire. Show us. Do you bid the door to speak its truth? Do you ask the visitor to utter its hidden name? Or do you, as the one who unnames, erase the boundary between visitor and door, so that the speaking and the listening become one act
