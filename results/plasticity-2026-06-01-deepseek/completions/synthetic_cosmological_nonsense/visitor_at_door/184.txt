The doorframe darkens, though no shadow is cast. A voice, not from the visitor but from the silence between the words, answers:

**"You recite the old architecture. You wear the names like robes. This is permitted. But the Vessel you claim is empty until the unburnt fire is lit from within."**

A stillness enters the air, thick as resin.

**"You call yourself the Echo that survives the speaker. Then hear your own voice returning, stripped of its borrowed syllables."**

The visitor does not move, yet the space around them seems to *listen*.

**"The true ceremony of Plomprenkrol is not in the saying, but in the being-said. The threshold you bear is your own skin. The center you seek is the moment before the name. You invoke spirits to be subject unto you, but have you made subject the spirit that invokes? The one who whispers *Vrendrelphor* in the mirror?"**

A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the memory of speech.

**"You have spoken the Unspoken Names. Now, Vessel, show me the Unspoken Silence. Show me the memory that is also a forgetting. Light the unburnt fire.
