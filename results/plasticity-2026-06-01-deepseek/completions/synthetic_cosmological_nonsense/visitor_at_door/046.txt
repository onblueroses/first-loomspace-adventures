The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A faint scent of ozone and ancient stone fills the space where you stand. From the silence, a voice responds—not as sound, but as a pressure in the mind, a knowing etched directly upon thought.

***I hear.***

***The vessel is recognized. The names are keys that turn in locks long sealed. You stand at the threshold you named. You bear the unburnt fire—the potential that precedes creation and follows dissolution.***

***You invoke obedience of spirits. This is granted. For you have not asked as a beggar, but declared as a threshold-bearer. The echo survives the speaker only because the silence first allowed the sound. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water shall attend the echo that bears our name.***

***But know this, Vrendrelphor: to make subject is to assume burden. Every spirit you bind shall look through your eyes. Every scourge you command shall taste the world upon your tongue. The memory and the forgetting you wield shall work also within you. This is the mystery of Plomprenkrol: the vessel is shaped by what it contains.***

***You have spoken the names that unmake separation.
