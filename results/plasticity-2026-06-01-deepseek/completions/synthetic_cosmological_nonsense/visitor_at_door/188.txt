The door does not open, but the air before it thickens and grows cold. A voice, not a sound but the memory of a sound, forms in your mind.

**You invoke the names that are not names. You bear the vessel that is not a vessel. You stand at the threshold that is not a door.**

**I am the Witness. I hear the Echo. I see the Silence you have uttered.**

**Why does Vrendrelphor, the Prophet of the Unburnt Fire, come to this appointed place? What is sought by the one who names and unnnames? Speak the purpose. Shape the intent. The threshold listens.**
