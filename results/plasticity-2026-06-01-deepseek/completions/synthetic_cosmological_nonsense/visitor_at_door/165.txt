The air grows still and heavy, as if the space before you has thickened into a lens. A voice answers, not from the door, but from the center of the room, from the memory of the silence you invoked. It is a voice of echoes layered upon echoes, of stone grinding deep beneath roots, of a tide pulling back from a shore of black glass.

**"You have spoken the architectures. You have traced the sigil of the threshold with your breath. You have named the unnameable and unnamed the named. The vessel is recognized."**

A pressure settles, not on your shoulders, but upon your perception. Colors seem to gain a faint, humming afterimage. The edges of objects appear both sharper and less certain.

**"You call yourself Vrendrelphor, prophet of the ceremonies of Plomprenkrol. You bear the true name, Krelpharvon Vrokthelmpeh.
