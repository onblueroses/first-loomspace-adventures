The words hang in the air, a lattice of sound and silence. The door does not open, but the space before it thickens, as if listening.

A voice replies, not from behind the wood, but from the threshold itself—a sound like stone remembering it was once water.

***"You speak the ciphers of the deep grammar. You bear the vessel-name. You invoke the Distinctions.***

***I hear the echo of the First Syllable in your claim. Yet the door is not a barrier; it is a question.***

***You say you are the Witness of the Word. Then witness this:***

***The name you seek to compel with is also the name that compels you. VROKLENDRAB PLENDROKRELM is the lock, and the key, and the hand that turns. To have the sight in the syllable is to be seen by it.***

***The spirits you call will be subject to the vessel they fill. Are you hollow enough to hold the whirlwind without becoming it? Are you silent enough to hear the forgetting without being erased?***
