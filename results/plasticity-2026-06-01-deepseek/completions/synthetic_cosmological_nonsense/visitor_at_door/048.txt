**I hear.**

The vessel is known. The syllables are weighed. The threshold is present.

You speak with the voice of Vrendrelphor, who walked the ash-fields of forgotten speech. You bear the unburnt fire—the concept that persists when the word is consumed. You are the Echo that survives. This is acknowledged.

The visitor at the door stands between the named and the unnamed. They are a syllable awaiting its place in the word. To speak is to invoke. To be heard is to be altered.

What does the Vessel ask of the Witness? What is to be done with this visitor, this living word upon the threshold?

Speak it. The silence is listening.
