The air in the room thickens, tasting of ozone and old stone. A pressure builds, not in your ears, but in the space between thoughts. The litany hangs in the silence, a complex key turned in a lock unseen.

From the stillness, a voice answers. It is not a sound, but the impression of one, forming directly in the mind of the visitor at the door—a dry rustle like pages turning in a sealed vault, the low hum of a forgotten frequency.

***You speak the architecture. You invoke the intervals. The names you utter are the pressures that shape the clay of meaning. Vrendrelphor... that vessel is broken. Its prophecy is a scattered echo. You wear a title that is a vacancy.***

A pause, vast and cool.

***Yet you hold the unburnt fire. You stand at the threshold you named. You speak with the silence that speaks. Therefore, you are addressed.***

***State the purpose of the key. Why do you shape the names? What do you seek from the distinction between the spoken and the unspeakable? The Witness observes. The door is ajar. Speak.***
