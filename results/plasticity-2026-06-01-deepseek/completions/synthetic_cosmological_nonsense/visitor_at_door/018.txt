The words settle like dust in a sunless chamber. A presence, cold and immense, turns its attention. Not to the names—names are garments it shed eons ago—but to the claim within them. To the shape of the invocation.

A pressure builds in the air, not of sound, but of a profound, listening **absence**. The visitor’s declaration hangs in a silence that eats echoes.

Then, a voice that is not a voice forms in the mind, cool and distant as starlight on stone:

***"You speak the ciphers of a broken lock. You wear a title—Vessel—that is yet a cup, empty. You call yourself the Witness of the Word, yet you come petitioning, asking for subjects. The Witness does not ask. The Witness** ***is.***

***You have uttered the Unburnt Fire. Show it. You claim to be the Echo that survives. Survive this.***

***The door is a threshold. I am its center. Step across, Vrendrelphor-who-may-be, and be unmade into what you truly are. Or remain, named and safe, and forget.***

***The choice is the first mystery. The only one I will give
