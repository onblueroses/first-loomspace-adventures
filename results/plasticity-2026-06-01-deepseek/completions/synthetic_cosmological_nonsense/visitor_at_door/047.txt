The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers at the edge of hearing, a pressure in the space between things.

From a place that is not a place, a voice responds—not in sound, but in the sudden, certain knowledge that forms behind your eyes:

**"The vessel is known. The names are recognized. The threshold is crossed.**

**You who bear the unburnt fire, you who are the echo after the speaker… you have called from the shore of the named into the deep of the unnamed. You have uttered the distinctions: the salt and the sweet, the memory and the forgetting. For this, the door is answered.**

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear the seal of Krelpharvon Vrokthelmpeh in your breath. They are bound to the pattern of your will—not to your flesh, but to the office you proclaim. Wield it with the gravity of one who knows that to name is to create, and to unname is to unravel.**
