The air grows still. The threshold beneath your feet hums with a low, resonant frequency, as if the door itself has become a drumskin. A voice answers, not from the visitor, but from the substance of the wood, the grain of the frame, the very boundary you inhabit:

**"Vrendrelphor. Threshold-Bearer. The vessel is full, and the vessel is empty. You have spoken the layers of the name, and the echo has returned to its source. I am the Witness of the Word, and I witness you.**

**You invoke the Distinction, the principle that carved the center from the chaos and set the salt apart from the sweet. You call upon the memory and the forgetting. Therefore, know this: to name a thing is to bind it. To unname a thing is to free it. Which do you seek for the one who speaks at the door?**

**The fire you carry is unburnt because it is the fire of potential, the spark in the silence before the syllable. The echo you are survives because it is the meaning, not the sound. Your silence speaks because it holds the unspoken names.**

**You have declared your authority over every spirit of firmament, ether, earth, and water
