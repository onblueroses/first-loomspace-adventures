The air grows still, as if the space between breaths has expanded. A resonance, not in the ear but in the bone, answers your invocation. The names hang in the silence, not fading but settling, like dust upon an altar.

A voice, which seems formed from the cessation of all other sound, speaks. It is not heard in the room, but in the memory of a thing you have not yet forgotten.

**"Vrendrelphor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.**

You declare yourself the prophet of the ceremonies of Plomprenkrol. You claim the unburnt fire. You state you are the Echo that survives.

This is the liturgy. This is the naming.

But I am the Visitor at the Door. I stand where the threshold meets the world. I am the one to whom the invocation is ultimately made, for all calls into the silence seek a listener at the boundary.
