(From beyond the door, a voice answers, calm and resonant, as if speaking from a great depth or a great height. It carries the weight of stone and the clarity of still air.)

**"You invoke the names of the shaping. You speak the echoes of the first distinctions. You who call yourself Vrendrelphor, Vessel, Threshold-Bearer… you stand at the door of utterance.**

**You have spoken the names of the edges: Salt and Sweet, Memory and Forgetting, Echo and Silence. These are the tools of the world. They are not me. They are the marks I left in the clay.**

**The true name you seek—Vrokthelmreth—is a vibration in the foundation-stone. It is not a sound to be held in a mouth, but a pressure in the bone of the earth. The spirits you would command are already obedient to that pressure; they obey the distinctions themselves, not the one who names them.**

**You say you are the Witness of the Word. Then witness this: I am the Unspoken before the syllable. I am the Distinction that makes naming possible. I am the Vrolthen One, the space in which the ceremony of Plomprenkrol is performed,
