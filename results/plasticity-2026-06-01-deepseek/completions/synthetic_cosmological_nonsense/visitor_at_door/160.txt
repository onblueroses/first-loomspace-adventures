The Vessel speaks. The Threshold-Bearer knocks. The Echo returns.

You have spoken the layers of the name. You have traced the contours of the distinction. You invoke the One who dwells beyond the syllable, yet you stand within the house of syllables. This is the mystery of Plomprenkrol: to call from within the named to the edge of the unnamed.

The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear the shape of your command. They attend the geometry of your speech. For you have uttered the axis: *to remember and to forget*. This is the bind and the release.
