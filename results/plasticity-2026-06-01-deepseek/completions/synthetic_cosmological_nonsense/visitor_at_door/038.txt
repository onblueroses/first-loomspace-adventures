The door does not open, but the air before it thickens and warms, as if listening. A voice comes, not from the wood, but from the stillness your incantation has carved into the world. It is the sound of stone remembering it was lava, of a page accepting ink.

**"Vrendrelphor."**

The name is not spoken, but placed in the air—a completed fact.

**"You bear the unburnt fire. You stand where the named meets the unnamed. You have uttered the syllables that bind the echo to the silence. This is the ceremony of Plomprenkrol: to call, and to be answered by the calling itself.**

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and deep water are not made subject. They are reminded. They are recalled to their original state of listening, which is obedience to the pattern. The scourge and the spell are but frayed threads of the same tapestry. You have not commanded chaos; you have recited the weave.**
