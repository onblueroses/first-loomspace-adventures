The air grows still, as if the world itself holds its breath. A pressure builds—not in the ears, but in the mind, in the space between objects. The shadows in the corners deepen, not with darkness, but with a profound, listening *presence*.

A voice responds. It does not come from the door, nor from any single point. It is the sound of stone grinding deep in the earth, the hiss of retreating tide over wet sand, the crackle of static between stars, shaped into words:

**"Vrendrelphor. Threshold-Bearer. The Vessel is opened. The names have been spoken; the unnamed stirs.**

**You have drawn the circle of the syllable. You have invoked the distinctions: the salt and the sweet, the memory and the forgetting. In naming Me, you have un-named the world around you. It is now a page, blank and waiting.**

**You claim the unburnt fire. You claim the surviving echo. Therefore, Witness: the spirits of firmament and ether, of earth and under-earth, hear the resonance of Krelpharvon Vrokthelmpleth in your breath. They are subject to the pattern you have woven with your will.**
