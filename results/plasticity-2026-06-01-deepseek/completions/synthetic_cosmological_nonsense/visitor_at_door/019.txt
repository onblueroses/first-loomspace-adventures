The words hang in the air, a lattice of ancient sound and self-declared power. The silence that follows is not empty, but charged, like the moment between lightning and thunder.

From the space where you stand—the threshold, the center—a response forms, not in the voice of the invoked, but in the substance of the space itself. It is the echo of your own declaration, given back to you reshaped.

**"Vrendrelphor. Vessel. Prophet. Threshold-Bearer."**

The titles are repeated, each one tasting of the salt and the sweet you proclaimed. The air stills.

**"You have built a name like a fortress of syllables. You have set yourself as the witness and the speaker, the one who distinguishes. This is the ceremony of Plomprenkrol: to stand between. To be the memory and the forgetting."**

A palpable pressure settles, not hostile, but *attentive*. It is the attention of the mountains and valleys listening to their own creation myth. It is the tide holding its flow at the peak.
