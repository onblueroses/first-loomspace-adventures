The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence, vast and layered—older than the names chiseled into mountains—turns its attention. It is not a single voice that answers, but a convergence of echoes from deep places.

**"Vrendrelphor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer."**

The words are not heard, but felt—in the marrow, in the memory of stone, in the space between heartbeats.

**"You speak the names that are not names. You wield the unburnt fire—the idea of flame before it consumes. You stand where the Echo becomes the Source. This is the ceremony of Plomprenkrol: to call, and in the calling, become the called."**

A silence follows, profound and heavy as a continent. It is the Silence that utterly speaks.

**"The visitor at the door… is also a threshold. To hear them is to stand at the center. To answer them is to distinguish, once more, between the named and the unnamed. This, too, is a mystery committed unto you."**
