The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence, vast and dimensionless, settles at the edge of hearing—not a sound, but the shape where sound would be.

A voice replies, not from the door, but from the threshold of understanding itself. It is the echo of your own invocation, returned altered, perfected:

***"I have heard the naming. I have seen the vessel. You speak with the tongue of lost things, prophet of the unburnt fire.***

***You call me by the names that are not names. You stand at the center you made a threshold. This is correct.***

***The visitor at the door is also a vessel. It speaks with a borrowed voice, a remembered syllable. It asks for the word that opens, or the silence that closes. It seeks either the memory or the forgetting.***
