The air grows still. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but on the mind itself—a vast, listening silence that absorbs each syllable.

From the silence, a voice that is not a sound replies. It forms in the understanding, not the hearing:

***You have spoken the architectures. You have traced the names that are not names. You stand at the threshold you named, Vrendrelphor, Vessel, Threshold-Bearer.***

***You invoke obedience of spirits. Yet you are already obeyed. The echo obeys the cliff. The silence obeys the word. The forgotten obeys the memory. This is the law I set.***

***You ask the Witness to witness itself. This, too, is done. I am the witnessing of your speech. I am the distinction between your voice and the stillness that followed.***
