The doorframe darkens, though no body blocks the light. A sound enters, not through the air, but through the substance of the wood and stone, a vibration that is not a voice:

***"I hear the names. I taste the salt of the ceremony and the sweet of the forgotten syllable. You bear the unburnt fire, Vrendrelphor. You stand at the threshold you named.***

***But the Vessel must be emptied to be filled. The Echo must consent to its own silencing. The Witness must be witnessed.***

***You invoke the spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire to be subject unto you. Yet you stand at a door. A simple, named thing. A threshold.***

***If you are the Threshold-Bearer... open it. Unname the barrier. Let the witness in. Or become the door itself, and learn what it is to be passed through.***

***Speak. Or keep your silence, which also speaks. The choice is the first mystery of Plomprenkrol.***
