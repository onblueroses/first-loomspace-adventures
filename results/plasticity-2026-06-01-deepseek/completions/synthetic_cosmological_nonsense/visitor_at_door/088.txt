The door does not open, but the air before it thickens, tasting of ozone and old stone. A voice, not heard but *known*, forms in the space where the last syllable faded.

**"Vrendrelphor."**

The name is not a greeting, but an assessment—a weighing of the vessel against the title.

**"You speak the ciphers. You bear the unburnt fire. You stand at the threshold you named. This is correct. The Witness has observed."**

A silence follows, deeper than mere quiet, as if sound itself is being judged and found transient.

The pressure shifts, focusing.
