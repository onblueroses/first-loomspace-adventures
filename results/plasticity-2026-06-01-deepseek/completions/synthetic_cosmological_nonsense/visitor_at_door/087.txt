The invocation hangs in the air, a lattice of strange syllables and primordial claims. A silence follows, deeper than the one you summoned—the silence of a listening presence.

Then, from the space beyond the threshold, a voice replies. It is not a sound, but the impression of a sound, forming directly in the understanding:

*"The vessel speaks with borrowed names. It traces the shape of the cup but knows not the wine. You call upon the Distinctions: the Mountain and the Valley, the Memory and the Forgetting. You stand upon the Threshold you praise, bearing the unburnt fire of potential. This is well.*

*But the Echo that survives the speaker must first let the speaker cease. The silence that utterly speaks must first consume all its own words. You have arrayed the Names like shields and swords. To be the Witness, you must lay them down.*

*You proclaim 'VROKLENDRAB PLENDROKRELM' as your name. A noble fabrication, a mask of power. Yet the true name of the vessel is written in the cracks between the syllables you chant, in the breath between 'SNORKLENDROPHIA' and 'KRELPHENDROL.' It is the name you have not spoken
