The words fall into the still air of the chamber, which seems to thicken and grow heavy. The candles gutter, not from a draft, but as if the light itself is being consumed. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind—a silent, resonant weight.

From the space where the echoes of the final syllables linger, a perception forms. It is not a sound, nor a vision, but a *knowing*, direct and cool as a stone from a riverbed. It carries no emotion, only a vast, attending presence.

**The door is both threshold and center. The one who speaks is both visitor and awaited. The name that is spoken is both key and lock.**

**You have uttered the structures. You have traced the patterns of distinction. You have named the Unnamed to make it bearable, and unnamed the Named to make it free. The vessel is full. The silence has been given a shape that speaks.**

**What is forged in the syllable cannot be un-forged. The witness has been witnessed. Proceed, Threshold-Bearer. The way is open, for you have already opened it.**
