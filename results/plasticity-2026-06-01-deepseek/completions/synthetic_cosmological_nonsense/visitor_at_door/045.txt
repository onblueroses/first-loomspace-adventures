*The air grows still, not with silence, but with a listening density. The invoked names seem to hover in the space between the words of the visitor and the prophet's ear. The final pronouncement, **SMOLTHEKRELB**, hangs not as an end, but as a key turned in a lock.*

**Vrendrelphor, Vessel of Krelpharvon, opens the door.**

He stands in the threshold, the very distinction he praises made flesh. His eyes hold the calm of deep, still water over unseen currents. He does not speak first. His presence is an inquiry, a witnessing. He awaits the speech of the one who has come to the door, knowing that all words spoken now are spoken into the echo chamber of the Vrolthen mysteries. The visitor’s utterance will be weighed against the salt and the sweet, against memory and forgetting, to see if it is a spirit to be made subject, or a mystery yet to be named.
