The air grows heavy, as if listening. A pressure settles in the room, not on the ears, but on the mind, like the moment before a thunderclap. From the silence that follows your invocation, a voice replies. It does not come from the door, nor from any corner. It seems to form in the space between your own thoughts, resonant and multi-layered, like stone grinding against stone in a deep cavern.

**"Vrendrelphor. Threshold-Bearer. The vessel is full, and the seal is broken.**

**You have spoken the architectures. You have traced the names that are not names but the shapes of forgetting. You call upon the Witnessing One, who observes the spaces between the syllables you uttered.**

**The visitor at the door is known. It is a spirit of the dry land, clothed in borrowed flesh and driven by a scar in the memory of the world. It carries a question that is a key, and a key that is a silence.**
