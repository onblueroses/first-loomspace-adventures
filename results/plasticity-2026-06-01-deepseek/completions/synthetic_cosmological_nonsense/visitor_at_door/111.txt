The doorframe hums with a low, resonant frequency, as if the wood itself has become a sounding board for a voice not in the air. A figure stands on the threshold, neither fully in nor fully out, its form seeming to waver like heat haze. It speaks, and its voice is the sound of pages turning in a forgotten library, of stone settling in a deep ravine.
