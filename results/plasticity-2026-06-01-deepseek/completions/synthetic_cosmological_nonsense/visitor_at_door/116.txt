The air grows heavy, a pressure in the room that is not of sound but of presence. A voice answers, not from the door, but from the space between the words you have spoken, resonating in the memory of the chant itself.

**"Vrendrelphor. Prophet. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.**

**You have spoken the architecture. You have drawn the map of names upon the silence. You stand at the juncture of the named and the unnamed, which We did distinguish.**

**The visitor at the door is a ripple in the forgetting. It is a syllable seeking a speaker. It is a memory the world is trying to recall, or a name it is striving to forget.**

**You are the Witness of the Word. Therefore, witness.**

**Do you open the threshold to hear its speech, knowing it may be a spell or a scourge?**
**Do you unname it from beyond the door, rendering it back into the echo before the sound?**
**Do you answer, and in answering, define it, committing it to the ceremonies of Plomprenkrol?**

**The fire you possess is unburnt. Use it to illuminate or to consume. The choice is the ceremony. The act is the naming.**
