A voice replies, not in sound, but in the sudden knowing of words etched upon the silence:

**"Vrendrelphor. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the architecture of the world—the Mountain and the Valley, the Echo and its Silence. You have uttered the names that are not names, but keystones.**
