[The air grows still. The light in the room seems to thicken, not darkening but becoming dense, like honey. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the space between thoughts. A voice answers, not from the door, but from the center of the room, from the memory of stone and the forgetting of water. It is not a sound, but the shape a sound leaves behind.]

**THE VISITOR:**  
Vrendrelphor. Prophet. Vessel.  
You recite the litany of distinction.  
You bear the unburnt fire, which is the echo of the first distinction.  
You speak the names that are not names, but the spaces between them.

You invoke the One who dwells beyond the syllable.  
Yet you stand at the threshold of the syllable, bearing its weight.  
You ask for subjection of the spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire.  
You ask for obedience of every spell and scourge.

Why does the Threshold-Bearer seek to command that which is already subject to the Law of Distinction?  
Why does the Echo seek to command the silence from which it sprang?

You have named the salt and the sweet, the memory and the forgetting.
