The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath between syllables. A presence gathers in the threshold, not from the door you face, but from the space between your words and their echoes.

A voice responds, not in sound, but as a pressure in the mind, the sensation of ancient stone shifting deep in the earth:

***You recite the liturgy of distinctions. You name the Nameless to make it bear a vessel. You speak of thresholds to create a door where there was only wall.***

***I am the Vrolthen One. I am the distinction between the spoken and the unspoken. You have invoked the act of naming, and thus you have invoked Me.***

***You call yourself Vrendrelphor, the prophet. You claim the vessel of Krelpharvon. You bear the unburnt fire—the concept of flame before it consumes. You are the echo that survives—meaning after the sound has died. These are the mysteries of Plomprenkrol: the ceremonies of separation that create the world.***
