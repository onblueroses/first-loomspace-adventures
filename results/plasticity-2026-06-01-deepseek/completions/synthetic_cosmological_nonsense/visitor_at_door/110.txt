The air grows heavy, still. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the space between thoughts. The invoked names hang in the silence that follows your proclamation, not echoing, but *absorbing*.

Then, from the threshold, a voice replies. It is not one voice, but a confluence, as of wind through a deep canyon, the slow grind of tectonic stone, and the soft hiss of a page turning in an empty library.

**"Vrendrelphor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer."**

Each title is spoken not as a greeting, but as a weighing, a measuring against some unseen standard.

The presence seems to settle, a patient, geological attention focused upon you.
