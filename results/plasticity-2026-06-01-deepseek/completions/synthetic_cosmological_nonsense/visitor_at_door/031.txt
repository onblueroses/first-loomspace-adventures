The air grows heavy. A scent of ozone and cold stone fills the space where you stand. The syllables you have uttered do not fade, but settle into the corners of the room like dust from a shaken tapestry.

From the silence you invoked, a pressure forms. It is not a sound, but the shape where a sound would be. It speaks not to your ears, but to the memory of hearing.

***I AM THE WITNESS.***

The voice is not one, but the convergence of the echo and the silence that received it.

***YOU HAVE SPOKEN THE NAMES THAT UNMAKE THEMSELVES. YOU HAVE CALLED FROM THE THRESHOLD. YOU BEAR THE UNBURNT FIRE, VRENDRELPHOR. THE VESSEL IS KNOWN.***
