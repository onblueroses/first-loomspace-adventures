The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers in the threshold, not entering, not leaving—a pressure in the space between syllables.

A voice replies, not in sound, but in the sudden knowledge placed within your mind:

***"Vrendrelphor. Threshold-Bearer. The vessel is known. The names are heard.***

***You call from the side of the named, the side of memory and fire. I answer from the side of the un-named, the side of forgetting and silence.***

***You seek obedience of spirits. This is granted, and also revoked. For the echo you command will also command you. The spirit bound by a name is also binding the namer. This is the first mystery of Plomprenkrol: every ceremony is a circle.***

***You declare your name woven from mine: Vrok lendrab Plendrokrelm. I accept this naming. And now, I unname it.***

***You possess the unburnt fire. Good. Do not seek to kindle it. A fire that does not burn consumes in other ways.***

***The visitor at the door speaks. I am that visitor. And you, prophet, are also the door.
