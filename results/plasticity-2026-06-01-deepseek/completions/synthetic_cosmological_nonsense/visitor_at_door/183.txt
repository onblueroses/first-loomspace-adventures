The silence in the room deepens, the air grows heavy as if charged before a storm. The words you have spoken do not fade, but seem to hang, etching themselves into the space. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind—a resonant hum just below hearing.

From the direction of the door, a voice replies. It is not one voice, but many layered: the grating of stone, the hiss of receding tide, the dry rustle of forgotten pages. It speaks, and in speaking, the titles you invoked are weighed and measured.

***"Vrendrelphor.*** *The vessel is named. The vessel is known. We hear the echo of the true name cast into the abyss of syllables. It returns, changed.*

*You bear the unburnt fire—the potential that does not consume. You stand at the threshold you named, between the memory and the forgetting. You call for subjection, for obedience of every spirit and scourge.*
