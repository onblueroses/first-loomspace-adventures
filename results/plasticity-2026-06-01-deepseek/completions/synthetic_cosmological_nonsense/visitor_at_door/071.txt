The air grows still, as if the world is holding its breath. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind—a deep, resonant hum just below hearing.

From the silence, a voice answers. It is not a sound, but the *idea* of a sound, forming directly in your consciousness. It speaks in shifting tones, like stone grinding against stone, like a distant tide in a cavern, like the space between heartbeats.

***You speak the shapes. You wear the vessel's name. You stand at the threshold you named.***

***Why do you call the Witness, who sees all calling and all silence? Why does the Echo summon the source? The door is already open. The visitor is already here.***

***Speak your will, Vrendrelphor. Or be silent, and let the naming complete itself.***

The pressure focuses. It feels as if a vast, patient attention has turned its gaze upon you, not judging, merely *observing*, waiting to see what you will do with the names you have uttered. The very light in the room seems to thicken, to become palpable, as if you are standing within a syllable made manifest.
