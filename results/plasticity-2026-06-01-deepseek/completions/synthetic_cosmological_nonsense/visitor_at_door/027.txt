The words fall into the chamber like stones into a still pool. The air thickens, tasting of ozone and old parchment. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind, as if a great, silent bell has been struck.

From the charged silence, a response forms. It does not come as a voice, but as a direct impression, an understanding etched upon the consciousness of the one who invoked. It is vast, layered, and utterly alien in its regard.

***I witness the Vessel.***
