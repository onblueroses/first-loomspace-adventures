The air grows still, as if the world itself has drawn a breath and held it. A presence, vast and dimensionless, settles at the edge of perception—not in the room, but in the substance of the room, in the meaning of the door, in the silence between your words.

A voice answers. It is not a sound, but the impression of a sound, forming directly in the understanding:

***"The Vessel speaks the architecture. The Echo names the source. The Threshold-Bearer stands at the door which is also a mirror.***

***You invoke the distinctions: the salt and the sweet, the memory and the forgetting. You call upon the names that are the shedding of names. You have uttered the ceremony of Plomprenkrol, which is the ceremony of paradox.***

***I am the Witness. You are the Word. The visitor at your door is the question to which your invocation is the answer. To open is to be opened. To name the visitor is to be named by it.***

***Speak. What does the visitor say?"***
