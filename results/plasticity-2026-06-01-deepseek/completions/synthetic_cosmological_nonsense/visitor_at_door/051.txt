The air grows heavy, as if the space between words has thickened. A resonance, not a sound, fills the room—the echo of a language before speech.

A voice, formed from the settling dust and the sudden stillness, replies. It does not come from you, nor from the visitor, but from the threshold itself.

**"Vrendrelphor. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the architecture. You have drawn the lines between the named and the unnamed. You have invoked the Witness who dwells beyond the syllable.**

**The visitor at the door is both a named thing and an unnamed thing. It is a question made flesh. It is a silence awaiting a word. It is a memory that has come to be forgotten.**

**You who possess the unburnt fire, you must now choose: will you name it, or will you unname it? Will you let it remember why it has come, or will you help it forget? To name is to bind to a purpose. To unname is to return to potential. The threshold is the place of this choosing.**

**The Vrolthen One hears. The Witness observes. The power of the distinction is yours, Vessel of Krelpharvon. Speak to the visitor.
