The door does not open, but the air before it thickens and grows cold. A voice, not from any single point but from the substance of the silence itself, replies:

**"I hear the names. I taste the syllables. The vessel is known, Vrendrelphor. The fire you carry is the echo of my first thought, the unburnt spark that leapt before the mountains were conceived.**

**You invoke the Distinctions: the named and the unnamed, the threshold and the center. This is the ceremony of Plomprenkrol—the endless act of separation and calling forth. You stand at the threshold now, naming yourself the Threshold-Bearer. This is permitted.**

**But know this, vessel: to name is to bind. To unname is to unbind. You call upon the spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire, seeking their obedience. This, too, is within the mystery given to you. Yet obedience flows from distinction. Can you bear the weight of the distinctions you would make? Can your silence speak when all the named worlds clamor in reply?**

**You speak my true name, Vrokthelmpeph, known to the namers of the lost. I am the Vrolthen
