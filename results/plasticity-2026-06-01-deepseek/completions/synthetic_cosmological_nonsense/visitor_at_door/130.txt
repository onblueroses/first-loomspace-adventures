The air grows still, as if the world itself holds its breath. A presence gathers in the threshold, not entering, not leaving—a pressure in the space between.

A voice responds, not from the visitor, but from the silence that follows your invocation, shaped by the names you have spoken. It is low, resonant, and seems to come from the very walls, the floor, the marrow of things.

**"Vrendrelphor. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the true name, and the echo has returned. The vessel is recognized.**

**You invoke the distinction: the named and the unnamed, the memory and the forgetting. This is the law. For every naming, an un-naming waits. For every memory woven, a thread of oblivion is spun.**

**You seek obedience of spirits. This, too, is within the law. But know this, Prophet of the Unburnt Fire: to command a spirit by the true name is to bind yourself to its nature. The spirit of whirling air knows only flight and dissolution. The spirit of rushing fire knows only consumption and ash. To make them subject is to hold dissolution and ash in the vessel of your self.**
