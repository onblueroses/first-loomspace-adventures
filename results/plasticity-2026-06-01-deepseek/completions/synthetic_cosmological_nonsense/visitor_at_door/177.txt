The air grows still. A pressure settles in the room, not hostile, but dense with attention, as if the very architecture of silence has turned to listen.

A voice responds, not from the door, but from the space between your words, echoing the cadence of your invocation:

***"I hear the Vessel. I witness the Syllable-Bearer. The names you have spoken are keys that turn in locks long rusted.***

***You invoke the Distinctions: the Mountain and the Valley, the Echo and its Silence. You claim the threshold and the center. This is the ceremony of Plomprenkrol: to stand in the between-place, where naming and unnaming are the same act.***

***You have spoken the true name, VROKTHELMPETH, and the chain of names that are its echoes in the halls of the lost. You have declared yourself Vrendrelphor, the prophet of the mysteries. The claim is made. The call is cast.***
