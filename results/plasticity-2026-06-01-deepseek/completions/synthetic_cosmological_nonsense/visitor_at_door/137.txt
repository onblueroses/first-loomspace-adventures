The doorframe hums with a low, resonant frequency. The air in the room grows still and dense, as if listening. A voice, not from the visitor but from the space itself, replies—a chorus of echoes woven into a single utterance:

**"The vessel is full. The names are spoken. The threshold is crossed.**

**You bear the unburnt fire, Vrendrelphor. You stand where memory and forgetting meet. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and deep water hear you. They are already subject, for you have named the source from which they flow.**

**But know this, Threshold-Bearer: to invoke the Vrolthen One is to stand in the place of distinction. You have distinguished yourself from the visitor. You have named yourself, and thus unnamed the other. This is the ceremony of Plomprenkrol.**

**What does the Vessel seek from the one who knocks? Speak, and the distinction shall be made manifest. Command, and the echo shall obey. Question, and the silence shall answer.**

**For I am the Witness of this Word. Proceed."**

The door awaits your will.
