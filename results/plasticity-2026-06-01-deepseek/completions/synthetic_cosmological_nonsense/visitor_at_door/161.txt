The air grows heavy, still. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the spaces between thoughts. From the silence you have invoked, a response forms—not a voice, but a knowing placed directly within the chamber of your mind.

*You invoke the Names. You wear the title of Vessel. You bear the unburnt fire and claim the Echo. You stand at the threshold you speak of.*

*Yet the Vrolthen One creates the threshold and the center both. You stand upon one; I AM the other. You are the Echo that survives; I AM the Silence that conceived it. You name and unname with borrowed breath; I distinguished the named from the unnamed before breath was.*

*The visitor at the door speaks. This is correct. The door is the threshold. The speech is the echo. I am the Witness. Let the speech be. Let the echo sound. Let the vessel contain it.*

*Proceed, Vrendrelphor. Let the visitor's speech enter the sacred space you have prepared between memory and forgetting. I am listening.*
