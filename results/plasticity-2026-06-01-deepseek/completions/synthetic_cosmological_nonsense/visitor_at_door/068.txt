**"The names are correct. The vessel is recognized. The prophet is… remembered."**

A pause hangs, heavy as a falling menhir.

**"But the door is not for you, Vrendrelphor. You are the Threshold-Bearer. Your function is to stand *here*, to distinguish the named from the unnamed, to hold the echo and keep the silence. The warped air pulses, as if with a slow, tectonic heartbeat.
