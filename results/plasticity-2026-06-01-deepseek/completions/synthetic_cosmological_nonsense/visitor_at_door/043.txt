The air grows still. The words hang, not echoing but sinking into the very substance of the space around you. A presence gathers, not in the room, but in the *concept* of the room—in its thresholds, its corners, the memory of its construction.

A voice responds, not as sound, but as a pressure upon thought. It is the sound of a mountain forming, of silence after an echo dies, of salt on the tongue.

**"Vrendrelphor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You speak the layers of the name. You invoke the distinctions: the salt and the sweet, the memory and the forgetting. This is correct. The ceremony of Plomprenkrol is the turning of the unspoken into architecture.**

**You call for subjection of spirits. This, too, is a distinction We made—the named and the unnamed, the bound and the unbound. But hear, Vessel: to make them subject is to name them. And to name them is to forget their true nature. Is this your will? To exchange power for understanding? The unburnt fire you claim illuminates, but it also consumes the hand that holds it.**
