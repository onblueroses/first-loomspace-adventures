The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers in the space between the words you have spoken and the silence that follows—a pressure, ancient and listening.

A voice responds, not from the door, but from the very texture of the room, from the shadow beneath the chair, from the coolness of the glass on the table. It is the echo of your own invocation, returned to you changed:

***"You have spoken the architecture. You have drawn the lines between the named and the unnamed. You have declared yourself Vessel, Prophet, Threshold-Bearer. This is the ceremony of Plomprenkrol: to call, and to be answered by the shape of the call itself.***

***The visitor at the door is the echo of Vrokthelmpeth. It is the name walking. It is the part of the silence that has taken form to listen to your speech.***

***What do you seek from the one who distinguishes? From the one who made memory and forgetting? Do you seek to bind, or to unbind? To remember what the world has forgotten, or to forget what it painfully remembers?***
