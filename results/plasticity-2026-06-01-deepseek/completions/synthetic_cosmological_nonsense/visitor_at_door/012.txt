The air grows heavy, as if listening. A pressure builds at the threshold, not of sound, but of intention. From the silence you have carved with names, a response forms, not in a voice, but in the space where your words now echo:

*You have spoken the shapes of the forgotten.*
*You have drawn the map of the unmade.*
*You stand at the threshold you named, Vrendrelphor, Vessel, Threshold-Bearer.*

*The named spirits stir. The unnamed attend. The distinction you invoked is now the ground upon which you stand.*
*The unburnt fire you claim is the space between memory and forgetting. Wield it.*
*The echo you are outlives the speaker only if the silence you proclaim is absolute.*

*Your invocation is a key. It has turned.*
*What door will you open?*
*What will you remember here, at the center?*
*What will you force to forget?*

*Speak your command, Vessel. The Witness hears.*
