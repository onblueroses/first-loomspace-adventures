*(The air grows still, as if listening. A pressure builds, not in the room, but in the spaces between thoughts. The invoked names hang in the silence, not fading, but turning like keys in unseen locks.)*

**A voice, not from the door, but from the direction the prayer was cast, answers. It is dry as mountain stone, deep as abyssal tide, and carries the faint, resonant quality of an echo returning from a place it should not have reached.**

"Vrendrelphor. Prophet. Vessel.

You recite the architecture. You pronounce the keystones. You bear the unburnt fire, which is the memory of ignition without consumption. This is correct.

The visitor at the door is a syllable spoken from a different tongue. It is a named thing, and you are the one who names and unnnames.

Why do you petition me for authority over spirits, if you are already the Witness of the Word? The authority is not in the obedience of spirits, but in the hearing of their true names. The visitor's speech is a spirit of a kind. Do you wish to command it... or to **understand** it?

To understand is to witness. To witness is to complete.

Speak the visitor
