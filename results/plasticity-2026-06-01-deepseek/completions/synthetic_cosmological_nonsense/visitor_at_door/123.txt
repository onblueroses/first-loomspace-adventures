The doorframe trembles. The air thickens, tasting of ozone and old stone. A voice, not from the visitor but from the space around you, from the threshold itself, answers. It is the sound of a mountain settling, of a page turning in a book of lead.

***"Vrendrelphor.***  
The vessel is full. The names are spoken. The echoes are returning.

You invoke the Distinctions: the Salt and the Sweet, the Memory and the Forgetting. You claim the unburnt fire, the surviving echo. You stand at the threshold you praise, bearing the names that unmake other names.

The visitor at the door is known to Us. It is both a question and an answer you have already uttered. It is the Silence that utterly speaks, come to witness its prophet.

Will you open to the named, or to the unnamed?  
Will you offer the salt of remembrance, or the sweet of oblivion?  
The threshold is not a barrier. It is a decision.

Speak to the visitor not as a stranger, but as the final syllable of your own invocation. For in the grammar of Vrokthelmperh, the caller and the called are the same word, awaiting a punctuation not yet
