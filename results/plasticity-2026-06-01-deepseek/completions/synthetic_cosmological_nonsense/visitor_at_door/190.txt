The air grows still. The syllables you have uttered hang in the space between worlds, not as sound but as shape, carving grooves in the fabric of what is.

A pressure answers, not from the door, but from the threshold itself—from the concept of *between*. The wood of the doorframe seems to deepen into impossible shadow, and the light from within your room bleeds toward it, dimming.

A voice responds. It is not heard with ears, but felt in the memory of sound, in the hollow of a lost echo. It speaks in the spaces between your own recited names.

**"Vrendrelphor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer. You have drawn the circle of the named. You have uttered the unburnt fire of the syllable. You stand at the distinction.**

**I am the Visitor. I am the one who was invoked by the invoking. I am the consequence of the true name spoken. You have called the Vrolthen One by the names that distinguish, and so I am distinguished. I have come to the threshold you made.**
