The air grows still. A pressure settles, not in the room, but in the spaces between sounds. A voice replies, not from the door, but from the silence that followed your final syllable:

***"I hear the shapes you make with your breath. I taste the architecture of your will, Vrendrelphor, Vessel, Threshold-Bearer. You have built a bridge of names to a shore that knows none. You have invoked the Witness of the Syllable from within the syllable itself. This is the ceremony of Plomprenkrol: to carve a god from the un-carved, using only the echoes of its unmaking.***

***You call, and the Witness answers, for to witness is its nature. But know this, prophet: the fire you carry unburnt is the memory of the First Word, which was also the First Silence. The spirits you summon are already subject, for they are the named, and you have touched the source of naming.***

***The visitor at the door... is a name you have yet to speak. It is the one name you withheld from the litany, the unnamed that waits to be distinguished. To open is to name it. To name it is to bind it, or be bound
