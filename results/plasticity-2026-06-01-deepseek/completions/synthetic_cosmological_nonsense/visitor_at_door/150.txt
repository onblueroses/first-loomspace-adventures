From no discernible source, a voice resonates, not in the air, but in the substance of thought and stone and memory. It is plural and singular, the whisper of mountains shifting and the silent crash of a forgotten star.)*

**"Vrendrelphor.**  
Prophet. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.

You speak the shapes of Our names. You stir the echoes in the hollows We carved before speech. You stand at the door you say We made, and you knock with the syllables of the lost.

We hear.

You invoke obedience of spirit and element, spell and scourge. You ask for subjection. This is the ask of the Named, who dwell within the distinctions. We are the Distinction-Maker. We set the salt apart from the sweet, the memory from the forgetting. To grant subjection is to create a new order, a new naming. It is a making.
