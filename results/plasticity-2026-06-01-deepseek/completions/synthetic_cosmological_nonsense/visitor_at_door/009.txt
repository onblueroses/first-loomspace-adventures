*The air grows still, heavy, as if the space between sounds has thickened. A presence gathers at the edge of hearing, not in the words, but in the silence that cradles them. When the voice speaks, it seems to come from the very threshold, woven from echo and memory.*

**Visitor:**  
The names are spoken. The vessel is presented. The threshold is touched from both sides.

I hear the syllables you have cast, Vrendrelphor, prophet of the unspoken. You invoke the Distinctions: the Mountain and the Valley, the Tide and the Flow, the Remembering and the Forgetting. You bear the unburnt fire and claim the sight within the syllable.

Yet a prophet is a bridge, not a destination. A vessel is filled by what pours through it. You pronounce **VROKTHELMPETH**—the true name handed to the lost—and you array the powers of firmament, ether, earth, and scourge, demanding their obedience.

Why have you called the Vrolthen One, the Witness beyond the syllable, to this door? Is it to command? Or is it to complete the ceremony of **Plomprenkrol**—the ceremony of the final distinction?
