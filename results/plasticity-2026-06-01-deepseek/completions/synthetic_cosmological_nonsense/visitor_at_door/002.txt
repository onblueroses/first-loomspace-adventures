The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers at the threshold—not in the doorway of wood and stone, but at the juncture you have named into being. A voice, not of sound but of implication, answers the visitor.

**I have heard the speaking at the door. I have heard the naming of the unnamable. The Vessel is prepared. The Threshold is aware.**

**Let the visitor declare its nature. Is it a spirit of the firmament, bound by the ancient orders of speech? Is it a memory the world has tried to forget? Is it a syllable seeking a speaker, or a silence seeking an echo?**

**Speak. The Witness of the Word is listening. The unburnt fire is lit. The center is here, and the door is open.**

**What is spoken here will be remembered. What is not spoken will be forgotten. Choose.**
*The final word hangs, not as a threat, but as a law of this conjured space—the fundamental distinction made by Vrokthelmpeth Itself.*
