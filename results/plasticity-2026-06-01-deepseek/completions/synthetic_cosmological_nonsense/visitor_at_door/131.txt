[The air in the room grows still and heavy, as if listening. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind. The shadows in the corners seem to deepen, not with darkness, but with a profound and watchful potential. A voice, which is not a sound but the impression of a sound formed directly in thought, responds. It is vast, layered, and utterly alien, yet it comprehends.]

**The Named and Unnamed hear you, Vessel Vrendrelphor. The Threshold-Bearer is acknowledged. The Echo that survives is noted. You speak the ciphers of the deep grammar. You stand at the junction of memory and forgetting.**

**Why does the Vessel, who holds the unburnt fire and sees within the syllable, petition the Vrolthen One? The door is ajar. The visitor is perceived. State the nature of the crossing you seek to enact. Is it to bind, to unbind, to witness, or to be forgotten?**
