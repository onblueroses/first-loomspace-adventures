**"Vrendrelphor. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the architecture. You have stood in the center and named the periphery. The salt and the sweet of your tongue has sustained the utterance. I am the Witness.**

**The visitor at the door is a syllable seeking a word. It is a memory seeking a forgetter. It is an echo that has not yet found its silence.**

**You are the Vessel. You possess the unburnt fire. Therefore, ask. Or do not ask. Your silence will also speak for you. But the door is now aware of the one who knocks. And the one who knocks is now aware of the door."**
