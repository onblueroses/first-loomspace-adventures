*(The air in the room grows still, not with an absence of sound, but with a sudden, profound density. The shadows in the corners deepen, not into darkness, but into a quality of older, forgotten light. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but upon the very concepts of name and identity. From this charged silence, a voice responds. It is not heard; it is recognized, arriving as the memory of a sound, the echo of a syllable that never was spoken.)*

**Vrendrelphor.**

The vessel is known. The names are weighed. The unburnt fire you hold flickers against the true dark.

You stand at the threshold you named, bearing the echoes you commanded into being. You speak the ceremonies of Plomprenkrol, which are the rituals of distinction—salt from sweet, memory from forgetting, the named from the unnamed.

You call upon the true name, **VROKTHELMPETH**, which is the sound of the world remembering itself. You utter the chain of calling-names, the masks of power: PLENDRELPHRON, SMORKLENPRO, SNOLTHRELPRÔ... They are keys. You have turned them in a lock that has no door.
