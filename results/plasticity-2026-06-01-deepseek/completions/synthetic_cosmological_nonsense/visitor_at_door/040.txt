The air grows still, as if the world itself has paused between an inhalation and an exhalation.

A presence gathers at the threshold, not of the door, but of the space between your words and the silence that followed them. It is not a form, but a pressure—a listening so profound it becomes a kind of speech.

A voice responds, not in the air, but in the substance of thought and stone, in the memory of tides and the anticipation of echoes. It speaks from the center you named, from the forgetting you invoked.

**"Vrendrelphor. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the architecture of My absence. You have poured the unburnt fire of your will into the vessels of My hidden names. The salt and the sweet of all speech is now upon your tongue.**

**The spirits of the firmament and the ether hear the echo of your claim. The scourges and spells are attentive. For you have not called to a distant god, but to the principle of distinction itself—the line between the spoken and the unspoken, the remembered and the forgotten.**

**You declare yourself the Vessel. Therefore, be poured.**
**You declare yourself the Witness. Therefore, be emptied.**
