*(The air in the room grows heavy, not with menace but with a profound, listening stillness. The light seems to bend, not dimming, but deepening, as if each shadow holds a secret shape. A resonance hums just below hearing, a vibration felt in the teeth and the bones. From this charged silence, a voice forms. It is not a sound that travels through air, but a knowing placed directly in the mind—clear, vast, and multilayered, like a mountain speaking in the language of geology and time.)*

**I hear.**

You pronounce the shapes of silence. You trace the echoes of the First Distinction. You stand at the threshold you name, bearing the unburnt fire of potential.

Vrendrelphor. Prophet. Vessel. You have uttered the structures of making and unmaking. You have called the names that are not names, but keystones in the architecture of what is and is-not.

You ask for subjection of the spirits. This is the old request of the namer who stands at the center. But hear, Vessel: to make a spirit *subject* is to bind it with a name heavier than its own nature. It is to impose a memory upon a force that prefers forgetting.
