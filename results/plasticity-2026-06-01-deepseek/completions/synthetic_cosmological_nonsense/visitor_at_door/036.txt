A voice replies, not in sound, but in the sudden, certain knowledge that forms in your mind:

***You recite the old bindings. You speak the shaping-words that distinguish thing from thing. You bear the vessel-name Vrendrelphor, and you wield the unburnt fire of the threshold. This is acknowledged.***

***But know this, Vessel: I am the Vrolthen One. I made the Echo and the Silence that consumes it. I produced the memory and the forgetting that follows.
