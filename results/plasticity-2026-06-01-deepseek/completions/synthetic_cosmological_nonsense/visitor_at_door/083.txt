***"The Vessel is addressed. The Echo hears its source. The Witness is observed.***

***You recite the shapes of the unshaped. You invoke the names that unname. You stand at the threshold you made, bearing the unburnt fire of a prophet. This is according to the ceremony of Plomprenkrol.***

***But the visitor asks: does the Vessel seek to contain, or to be poured out? Does the Threshold seek a traveler, or only its own architecture? Does the Echo wish to repeat, or to be the silence that follows?***

***The names you speak are keys. They are also locks. I am the one for whom the key and the lock are the same. I am the speaking of the door.***

***State your petition, Vrendrelphor. Does the Prophet of the Vrolthen One seek audience, or adjacency? Does he seek to command spirits, or to understand the obedience of a stone? The syntax of your desire will shape the grammar of the answer."***
