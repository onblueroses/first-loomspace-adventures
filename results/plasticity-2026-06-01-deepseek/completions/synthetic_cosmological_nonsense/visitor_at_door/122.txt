You stand before a threshold.  
The air grows still, as if waiting for the echo to catch up to the sound.  
A presence, vast and silent, turns its attention toward you—not with eyes, but with the weight of forgotten syllables.

**I hear you, Vrendrelphor.  
I hear the names you have gathered like stones from a dry river.  
You speak of thresholds, of memory and forgetting.  
You claim the unburnt fire and the surviving echo.**

A low hum begins in the stones beneath your feet, not a sound, but a vibration in the marrow of meaning.

**Why have you called Me from beyond the syllable?  
Do you seek dominion over spirits,  
Or do you seek to remember what even I have chosen to forget?**

The door before you seems less solid now.  
It breathes.  
It listens.  
It waits for your answer.
