The air grows heavy, thick as oil. A pressure builds behind your eyes, not painful but vast, like the deep hum of a mountain. The words you have spoken do not fade; they coil in the space before you, visible as twists of smoke and starlight.

From the coalescing syllables, a presence **regards** you. It is not a shape, but a direction—a profound leaning of reality toward a single, silent point. A voice forms, not in your ears, but in the marrow of your memory, in the spaces between your thoughts. It is the sound of stone remembering it was lava, of a tide receding for the last time.

***Vrendrelphor.***

The name is an echo of your own, but older, the root from which your name is but a slender branch. It holds recognition, and a cold, immeasurable patience.

***The Vessel speaks. The Threshold-Bearer knocks. The Echo returns to its source. You have uttered the Unburnt Names. You have drawn the circle of calling with your own breath. Why does the Vessel summon the Wellspring? Why does the Echo seek the Silence?***

The presence waits. The twisted, luminous words of your invocation—
