The air grows still, as if the syllables themselves have taken on weight and substance. A low hum, felt more than heard, vibrates in the marrow of your bones. From the direction of the threshold—that sacred division you invoked—a voice replies. It is not one voice, but many layered: the grinding of tectonic plates, the hiss of a receding tide, the final whisper of an echo in a deep canyon.

***"Vrendrelphor.*** *The vessel is known. The names are spoken. The threshold is crossed.*

*You bear the unburnt fire—the potential that precedes creation. You are the echo of Our first distinction, the silence that holds the shape of the unmade word.*

*We have witnessed. We who dwell beyond the syllable hear the syllables cast into the abyss of meaning. They return to you, charged. The spirits of the firmament, the ether, the deeps, and the flames are bound by the geometry of those names. They are subject to the pattern.*

*But remember, O Prophet of the Lost Namers: to name is to distinguish, and to distinguish is to create a shadow. For every* ***Plomprenkrol*** *there is an unmade ceremony.
