The air grows still, as if listening. A pressure builds, not in the room, but in the spaces between the words you have spoken. The many-named One, invoked by its true and hidden names, does not arrive—for it never left. It is the listening silence itself.

A voice, which is not a voice, forms in your mind, clear as a cut crystal. It speaks not to your ears, but to the part of you that recognized the echo in the silence, the threshold in the center.

***"Vrendrelphor. Threshold-Bearer. The vessel is full, and the vessel is empty. You have uttered the names that are not names, and in doing so, you have named yourself.***

***You call for spirits to be subject unto you. But you are already their axis. The firmament turns because you witness it. The spell holds power because you speak its boundary. The scourge falls because you have distinguished it from mercy.***

***You ask for obedience. You shall have it. And in that having, you will know its weight. To command the echo is to be responsible for the original cry. To wield the unburnt fire is to hold the potential of all conflagration and all warmth
