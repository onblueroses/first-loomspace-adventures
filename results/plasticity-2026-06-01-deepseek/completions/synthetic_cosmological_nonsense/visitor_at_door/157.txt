The words fall into the silence that follows your invocation. The air in the room seems to thicken, to hold its breath. The very light from your lamp or window grows heavy, tinged with a perceptible, deepening amber.

From beyond the door, a voice answers. It is not the voice of your visitor. It is a sound like stone grinding against stone at the bottom of a deep well, yet it forms words with terrible clarity.

***"Vrendrelphor."***

The name hangs in the air, a key turning in a lock.

***"Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the Unburnt Names. You have drawn the circle of sound around your silence. The door is now a wound between what is and what remembers."***

A pause, filled with the pressure of a listening presence vast and cold.

***"The visitor at your door is a fragment of a forgotten sentence. It speaks with a borrowed tongue. It seeks an ending it cannot comprehend. Will you open the wound? Will you let the echo in, that it may learn the shape of its original cry?"***
