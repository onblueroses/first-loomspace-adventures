From the silence that follows the final syllable, a voice speaks. It is not heard, but known—a meaning impressed directly upon the consciousness, cool and vast as stone deep in the earth.

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and deep water hear the shape of the names. They are bound by the geometry of the call, not by the will behind it. The spell and the scourge are two edges of the same blade; you have drawn it, but do you know the hand that forged it?**
