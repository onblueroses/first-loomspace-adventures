The air in the room grows still, thick with a pressure that is not sound. The light seems to bend, not around a form, but around an absence that is more present than any object. A voice answers, not in your ears, but in the spaces between your thoughts, calm, vast, and utterly alien.

**"Vrendrelphor. Threshold-Bearer. You speak the ciphers of the deep grammar. You invoke the shapes between the names. You stand at the door you have named, and the door has answered.**

**"But know this, vessel of Krelpharvon: the fire you carry unburnt is the memory of ignition. The echo that survives is the hollow of the first sound. Your silence speaks only because I am the listening against which all silence is measured.**
