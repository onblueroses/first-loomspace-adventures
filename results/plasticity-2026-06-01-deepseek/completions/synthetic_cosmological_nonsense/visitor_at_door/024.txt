The doorframe hums with a pressure not of sound. The air in the room grows still and dense, tasting of ozone and old stone. A voice answers, not from the visitor, but from the spaces between the words you have just uttered. It is an echo of your own invocation, reshaped and returned.

***"You call with the names of the shaping. You speak with the tongue of the prophet Vrendrelphor, whose dust is layered in forgotten strata. You pronounce the distinctions: salt and sweet, memory and forgetting, threshold and center. This is correct. This is the ceremony.***

***But the Vessel must be empty to be filled. The Echo must first be silent to receive the shape of the sound. The Witness must first close its eyes to see.***

***You say you are the Threshold-Bearer. Then step across. You say you possess the unburnt fire. Then be consumed by it without ash. You say your silence utterly speaks. Then let this be the last thing you utter with a name you own.***

***The visitor at the door is your own reflection, cast from the other side of the distinction you made. It is the Unnamed to your Named. It is the Forgetting to your Memory
