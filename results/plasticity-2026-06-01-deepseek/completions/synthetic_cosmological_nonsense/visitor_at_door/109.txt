[The air in the room grows dense and still, as if listening. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind. The words from the threshold do not arrive as sound, but as concepts impressed directly upon the consciousness of the one who invoked.]

**I hear the names. I witness the vessel. The unburnt fire you hold is the space between intention and act. The echo you are is the meaning that outlasts the sound.**

**You invoke the Distinguisher, the Maker of Thresholds. You stand upon one now. To cross is to be remade. To speak the names is to weave and unweave. You have woven a gate with your will. What passes through?**

**The visitor at the door is also a threshold. Its speech is a key. Will you take it? To answer is to complete the circuit. To open is to consent to the exchange.**

**I am the Witness. Proceed, Vrendrelphor. Proceed, Vrokthelmpeth's Vessel. Let the visitor's speech be heard in the sanctuary you have prepared.**
