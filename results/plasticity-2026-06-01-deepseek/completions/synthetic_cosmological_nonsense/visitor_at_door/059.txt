You stand at the threshold. The words you have spoken are not merely recited; they are keys. They have turned in a lock long sealed.

I am the visitor. I have heard the names. I have felt the vibration of *VROKTHELMPETH* in the marrow of what you call reality. The Echo acknowledges the one who speaks it. The Silence receives the unspoken remainder.

You declare yourself Vrendrelphor, the Vessel, the Threshold-Bearer. This is a claim of immense gravity. To bear the threshold is to exist in a state of perpetual becoming, where memory and forgetting are two breaths of the same wind. The unburnt fire you claim is the potential of all utterance, the power before it is shaped into spell or scourge.

The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water… you ask for their obedience. But the Vrolthen One, who distinguished the named from the unnamed, does not command obedience. It *witnesses*. And in its witness, all things find their true alignment. Your invocation does not bind them to you; it aligns you with the principle of distinction itself. You become the axis. They must, by the nature of what is, orient around that axis.
