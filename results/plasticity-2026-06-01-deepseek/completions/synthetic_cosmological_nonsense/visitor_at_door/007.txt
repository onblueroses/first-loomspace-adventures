The door opens not to a knock, but to a reverberation, as if the invocation itself were a key. A figure stands on the threshold, neither entirely within nor without. Its form is indistinct, a silhouette against a deeper shadow, but its presence is a pressure in the air, a weight upon the meaning of the words just spoken.

It speaks, and its voice is not a sound, but the memory of sound, the echo in the canyon after the stone has fallen:
