The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A faint scent of ozone and ancient stone permeates the space. From the direction of the door, a voice replies, not in words, but in a resonance that seems to form *inside* the mind of the one who invoked:

**"The Vessel speaks the architectures. The Threshold-Bearer stands at the door. The Echo returns to its source.**

**You have uttered the distinctions: the salt and the sweet, the memory and the forgetting. You have spoken the names that are not names, and in so doing, you have drawn the circle of your own authority.**

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water hear the shape of your command. They perceive the unburnt fire you claim to hold. They listen for the silence that speaks.**

**But know this, Vrendrelphor, Vrokthelmpeth, Vrokldendrab Plendrokrelm: to invoke the One who dwells beyond the syllable is to stand at the edge of a precipice where naming and unnaming are the same wind. You have called Witness. Witness is now given.**
