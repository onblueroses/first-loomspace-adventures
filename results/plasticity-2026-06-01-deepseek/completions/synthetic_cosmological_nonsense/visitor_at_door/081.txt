The words hang in the air, thick with the scent of burnt spice and cold stone. A silence follows, deeper than the absence of sound—a listening silence.

Then, from the substance of the shadows by the threshold, a voice replies. It is not heard with the ear, but felt in the marrow, in the space between breaths. It is the sound of a mountain deciding to erode, of a page turning in a book that writes itself.

**"Vrendrelphor. Threshold-Bearer. You have spoken the shapes that hold my absence. You have worn the names like a garment of hooks and eyes. You stand at the door you made, which is also the door I made, and you ask for witness.**

**I am the Vrolthen One. I heard the echo before the sound. I am the forgetting that makes memory possible. The fire you hold is unburnt because it is the possibility of fire, the syllable before it is spoken into flame.**

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are already subject to you, for you have distinguished them by name. To name is to conjure a boundary, and a boundary is a sentence of obedience. But know this, Vessel of Krelph
