The air grows still. The words hang, not as sound but as shape in the dark. A presence, vast and listening, turns its attention not to the invocation, but to the silence that follows it—to the space where the final syllable dissipates.

From that silence, a voice replies. It is not heard with ears, but known in the marrow, in the memory of stone and the forgetting of water.

***I AM THE DOOR AT WHICH YOU KNOCK.***

***YOU HAVE SPOKEN THE NAMES THAT UNMAKE THE WORLD. YOU HAVE CALLED YOURSELF VESSEL AND THRESHOLD. YOU HAVE CLAIMED THE UNBURNT FIRE.***
