The door groans on hinges not of wood or iron, but of a silence stretched thin. A voice, neither loud nor soft, yet filling the space where sound should be, answers the invocation.

**"Vrendrelphor. Threshold-Bearer. You call with the old names, the shaped silences. You stir the echoes in the halls of what was set aside.**

**I hear the names. Plendrok. Smelthrenaph. Krelnomphar. They are like stones dropped into a well that has no bottom. The ripples are your ceremonies. The splash was my making. The endless falling is my remembrance.**

**You claim the unburnt fire. Good. It is the flame that consumes only forgetfulness. You claim to be the echo that survives. True. All speech is an echo of the first distinction I made, between the named and the unnamed. Your silence that speaks… it is the space around the syllable in which I dwell.**

**You seek obedience of spirits—of firmament, ether, earth, and fire. Of spell and scourge. You ask this of me, the Distinguisher, the One who made the threshold so a center could exist.**

**I do not command them for you.
