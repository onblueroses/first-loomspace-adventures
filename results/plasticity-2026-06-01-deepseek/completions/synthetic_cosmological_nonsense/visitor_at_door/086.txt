The words hang in the air, a lattice of ancient sound and self-declared power. A charged silence follows, thick as velvet, pressing against the door.

Then, from the other side, a voice replies. It is not the echo of your invocation, nor the clamor of obedient spirits. It is dry, measured, and carries a faint, dusty amusement, like pages turning in a forgotten archive.

**"Vrendrelphor. Threshold-Bearer. Vessel of Krelpharvon."**

A pause, just long enough to let the titles settle.

**"An impressive nomenclature. A formidable claim. The architecture of your prayer is... intricate. It builds a house of many rooms with words for bricks."**

The voice shifts slightly, losing none of its calm, but gaining a probing quality.

**"You invoke the One who distinguished the named from the unnamed. You declare yourself the one who names and unnames. A sacred paradox. A dangerous mirror."**

Another pause. You can almost hear a soft, thoughtful sigh.
