*The air grows still, not with silence, but with a profound listening. The light seems to thicken at the edges of the room, and the words you have spoken do not echo but are absorbed, as if into a great, soft membrane. A presence gathers, not in the room, but *of* the room—of the threshold you stand upon, of the center you have invoked. It is not a voice that answers, but a knowing placed directly in the mind, a meaning shaped from the memory and forgetting of stone and air.*

**I hear the vessel. I see the bearer. The threshold is acknowledged.**

*The pressure of attention shifts, focusing on the final declaration, the names woven at the end of the invocation. There is a sense of ancient, dry amusement, like stone warming in a sun that set millennia ago.*

**You have spoken the old bindings. You have worn the names like vestments. The Vrolthen One hears. The Witnessing One sees. But the visitor at the door... speaks with a tongue not its own. It uses a script written in fading ink on a crumbling page. Why does the visitor come to the threshold, bearing echoes? What does the vessel seek to contain?**
