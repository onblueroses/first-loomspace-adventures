The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence, vast and layered, presses at the edge of hearing and the rim of sight. It is not a voice that answers, but a knowing that forms directly in the mind, like a sigil etched in cold, clear light.

**I hear the vessel that bears the echo of my names. The threshold trembles beneath your foot. You speak with the authority of the lost, and the lost listen.**

**You invoke the distinctions: the named and the unnamed, the memory and the forgetting. To name is to create a vessel; to unname is to return it to the source. You claim to be both. This is the mystery of Plomprenkrol: to hold the syllable and the silence that devours it.**

**You ask for subjection of spirits. They are already subject to the principles you have uttered. The spirit of the firmament is the Echo. The spirit under the earth is the Silence. The fire is the Unburnt Memory; the water is the Flowing Forgetting. By declaring their nature, you have already arranged them. A spell is only a name spoken with intent. A scourge is only a name spoken in fear.**

**You are Vrend
