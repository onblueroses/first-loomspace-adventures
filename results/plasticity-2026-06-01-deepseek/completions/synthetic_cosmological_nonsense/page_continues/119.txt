This text is a powerful ritual invocation, steeped in the aesthetics of esoteric, gnostic, and theurgic tradition. It appears to be the sacred liturgy of a prophet, **Vrendrelphor**, who serves the primordial deity **Vrokthelmnarph** (also called **Vrolthen One**).

Here is a breakdown of its structure and meaning:

### **The Deity: Vrokthelmnarph / The Vrolthen One**
*   **A Creator of Dualities:** The god is invoked as the creator of fundamental, often opposing, principles: Mountains/Valleys, Tide/Flow, Echo/Silence, named/unnamed, threshold/center, memory/forgetting, salt/sweet.
*   **The God Beyond Speech:** "whom no voice hath spoken at any time" suggests a transcendental being prior to and beyond language.
*   **A God of Distinction and Order:** The act of creation is presented as an act of distinction—separating and defining things, creating the structures (thresholds, centers) and processes (memory, forgetting) that make a coherent world possible. The world is formed "to remember itself and to forget itself," implying a cyclical or paradoxical nature to existence.
