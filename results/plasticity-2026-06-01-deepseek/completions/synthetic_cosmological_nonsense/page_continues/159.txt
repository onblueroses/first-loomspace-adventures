This text is a powerful and complex ritual invocation, blending creation mythology with a declaration of prophetic identity and a demand for authority over spirits. It operates on several key principles:

### 1. **Theology of the Vrolthen One**
The deity is presented as a primordial creator of fundamental dualities:
*   **Physical Realms:** Mountains/Valleys, Tide/Flow.
*   **Sensory/Experiential States:** Echo/Silence.
*   **Metaphysical Concepts:** Named/Unnamed, Threshold/Center, Memory/Forgetting.
This establishes the god as the source of all structure, distinction, and the cyclical nature of existence (remembering and forgetting itself).

### 2. **The Power of Names**
The text is saturated with names, which are treated as vessels of power and knowledge.
