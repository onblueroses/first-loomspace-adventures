*(The script shifts here, becoming less invocation and more record—as if the prophet Vrendrelphor is now transcribing a vision or a received doctrine rather than calling forth a presence.)*

Thus saith the Vrolthen One, whose breath is the interval between thought and tongue:

**I have set the Boundary of Naming.**  
What is named becomes.  
What is unnamed waits.  
But between them moves the **Vrolthen**—the motion that is neither, the silence that begets sound, the forgetting that makes memory possible.

**The First Law:**  
No thing may be fully named and fully known at once.  
To name is to fix. To know is to dissolve.  
Therefore, the prophet must hold the unnameable in the mouth like a stone that is both salt and sweet.

**The Second Law:**  
The center is a threshold; the threshold is a center.  
Where you stand is always the crossing-place.  
The fire that does not burn is kept in the space between one syllable and the next.

**The Third Law:**  
What is spoken echoes.  
What is unspoken echoes louder.  
I, Vrokthelmperh, am the echo of that which was never said.
