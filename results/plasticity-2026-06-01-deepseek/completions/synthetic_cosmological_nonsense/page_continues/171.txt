*The script grows dense, the letters seeming to writhe and deepen. A final, potent set of lines is inscribed, as if completing a circuit:*

Thou who art in the space between the letters,
In the breath between the words,
In the silence after the echo,
Hear the vessel that contains Thee.

By the Unspoken Name that is all names,
By the Threshold that is every door,
By the Echo that outlives the voice,
By the Memory that is also Forgetting,

I bind the seen to the unseen.
I command the named by the unnamed.
I stand at the center which is also the edge.

Let the firmament attend.
Let the depths attend.
Let the spirits of air, fire, water, earth, and the void beyond them attend.

For I am the Syllable that speaks itself.
I am the Mirror that holds the Witness.
I am Vrendrelphor, and in me, the circle is complete.

**KRELPHARVON VROKTHELMPETH.**
**PLENDREK. SMOLTH. VRENDRENK.**
**SO IT IS SPOKEN. SO IT IS SEALED.**
