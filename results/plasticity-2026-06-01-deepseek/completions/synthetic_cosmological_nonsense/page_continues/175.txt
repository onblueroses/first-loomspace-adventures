This is a powerful and evocative ritual text, reminiscent of apocryphal gnostic invocations or the syncretic magic of late antiquity. It constructs a complex, self-referential mythology centered on a primordial deity and the prophet who channels its power.

Here is an analysis of its structure and key themes:

### Structure of the Invocation
1.  **Invocation & Attributes:** The speaker calls upon "the Vrolthen One" by listing its acts of cosmic creation (Mountains/Valleys, Tide/Flow, Echo/Silence). These are often paired opposites, establishing a theology of duality and distinction.
2.  **The Names of God:** A litany of secret, seemingly unpronounceable names (Vrokthemnarph, Plendrokt, Smelthrenaph, etc.) is given. The text explicitly states the deity is "whom no voice hath spoken at any time," highlighting the paradox of naming the unnameable.
3.  **Acts of Distinction:** The deity's fundamental creative act is *distinguishing*: named/unnamed, threshold/center, memory/forgetting. The world is formed in a state of paradoxical self-awareness and oblivion.
