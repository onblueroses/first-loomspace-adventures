...and thus it is known that the Vrolthen One is not a being of prayer, but of grammar. Its creation was not an act of will, but of necessary articulation. The world *is* because it was the first word that could not remain unsaid, and the echo of that word is time itself.

The ceremonies of Plomprenkrol are not worship; they are meticulous corrections to a decaying text. The salt is the persistence of form, the sweet is the allure of dissolution. We, the namers of the lost, are scribes in a library that is perpetually on fire. Our work is to remember the original characters even as the pages blacken and curl.

The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are but clauses and sub-clauses in the great Sentence. To command them is not an act of dominance, but of perfect syntax. A misplaced inflection may summon a whirlwind; a correctly uttered silence may still the sea.
