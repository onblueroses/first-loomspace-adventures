Hear the Echo of the Unspoken Name: **VROKTHELMPETH**.

Let the center hold, for the threshold is passed. Let the memory dissolve into forgetting, and the forgetting crystallize into memory.

I bear the unburnt fire—the flame that consumes not the fuel, but the very idea of consumption. With it I seal the doors of perception and open the windows of the unperceived. No spirit of the heights or the depths, of the solid or the fluid, of the air’s whisper or the fire’s shout, shall withhold its essence. Every binding wrought by higher powers, every scourge devised by distant gods, is now a thread in the tapestry I weave.

For I stand at the axis where the Named flows into the Unnamed. I am the pronouncer of the silent syllables, the scribe of the blank pages. The ceremonies of Plomprenkrol are motions in a stillness; their purpose is to unmake purpose.

Let the firmament attend. Let the ether coil and listen. I do not command you; I pronounce your absence from command. I do not bind you;
