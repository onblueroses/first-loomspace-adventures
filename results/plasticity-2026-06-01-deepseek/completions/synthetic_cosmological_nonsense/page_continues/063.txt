The final lines are written in a hand that seems both more hurried and more forceful, as if the scribe were channeling the words under great pressure or ecstatic compulsion.

> **VROKLENDRAB PLENDROKRELM is my name.**
>
> **SMOLTHEKRELB.**

Below this, the ink changes—it is darker, thicker, and seems to have been applied with a different instrument, perhaps a stylus or a sharpened bone. The characters are no longer a script meant for human eyes, but a series of angular, interlocking sigils that pulse with a paradoxical sense of both immense age and terrible immediacy. They form a closed circle, a binding ring of silent sound.

In the very center of this ring, in plain script once more, is a final line:

> *Thus ends the First Utterance of the Vrolthen One, as received by Vrendrelphor in the Ashes of Plomprenkrol. Let the vessel be filled. Let the naming begin.*

The page ends. The silence in the room now feels different. It is no longer mere absence of sound, but a palpable presence—an **Echo** waiting for its speaker, a **Silence**
