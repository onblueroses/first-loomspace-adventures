**Continuation of the Rite of the Vrolthen One:**

Thou didst establish the Law of the Echo: that every word cast into the void shall return, yet never as itself.
Thou didst ordain the Silence that gnaws at the root of sound.
I have walked the Ninefold Labyrinth of Unspeaking.
I have drunk from the Well of Mnemophar, where memory drowns.
I have held the Unburnt Fire in my bare hands and was not consumed.
