**Of the Ceremonies of Plomprenkrol**

First, the Vessel must be emptied. This is done in the place where two silences meet: the silence of the stone and the silence of the root. There, the seven unspoken names are traced upon the earth, not with tool nor finger, but with the breath of forgetting. The earth will not remember the shapes, but the air above will hold their echo.

Second, the Threshold is raised. It is not a door of wood or stone, but a distinction. It is the moment between the drawing of the breath and its release. It is the gap between one heartbeat and the next. To stand in the Threshold is to be both named and unnamed. The Vessel must stand there until the distinction burns away, leaving only the witness.
