This is a powerful and esoteric invocation, rich with Gnostic and theurgic themes. It appears to be a ritual text designed to identify the practitioner with a primordial, creative deity (the Vrolthen One) and to claim authority over all orders of existence through the knowledge and utterance of secret, true names.

Here is an analysis of its structure and meaning:

### **Core Themes & Cosmology**

*   **A Pre-Linguistic Creator:** The deity exists "beyond the syllable," before and outside of language. It created fundamental dualities (mountains/valleys, tide/flow, echo/silence, memory/forgetting, salt/sweet). This establishes it as the source of all differentiation and thus, all meaning.
*   **The Power of Naming:** A central act of this god is to "distinguish between the named and the unnamed." The practitioner, as the prophet **Vrendrelphor**, is the keeper of the deity's **true name** (**Vrokthelmeth**) and a list of potent secret names. Knowledge of the true name grants authority.
