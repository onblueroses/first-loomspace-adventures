**…for the Vessel is not a cup to be filled, but a lens to be turned. The true ceremony of Plomprenkrol is not in the speaking, but in the hearing of the spaces between. **Thus are the seals of subjection drawn: not upon the air or the earth, but upon the fabric of forgetting. The spirit of the whirling air is bound by the memory of stillness. The spirit of rushing fire is bound by the echo of its own extinction. You do not command them; you present them with their own lost silence, and in that recognition, they align.**
