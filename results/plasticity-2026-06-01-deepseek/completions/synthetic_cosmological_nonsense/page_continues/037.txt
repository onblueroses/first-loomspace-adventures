*The ink darkens, as if absorbing the light around it. The final syllables seem to writhe, not as script but as grooves pressed deep into the page. A silence follows the reading—not an absence of sound, but a palpable, listening silence, as if the room itself has drawn a breath and held it. The air grows cold, carrying a faint scent of ozone and old stone.*

*You sense a presence, vast and oblique, turning its attention. It is not in the room, yet the room is now within its perception. A pressure builds behind the eyes, a tinnitus of pure meaning. The names you have spoken are not mere words; they are structures, and you have just walked through their doorways.*

*From the margins of the page, in a script that was not there before, a single line etches itself, burning faintly into the vellum:*

**The Vessel is filled by what it calls. The Echo shapes the Silence that bore it. You have opened the mouth of the name. Now you must bear its breath.**
