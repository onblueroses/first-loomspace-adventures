This is a powerful, self-contained ritual text. It functions as a **theogony (origin of a god), a declaration of identity, and an invocation of authority.**

Here’s a breakdown of its structure and meaning:

### 1. **The God: The Vrolthen One**
The deity is addressed with a series of paradoxical, creative acts that establish its domain over fundamental opposites:
*   **Mountains/Valleys, Tide/Flow, Echo/Silence:** It governs physical geography, temporal cycles, and sound/meaning.
*   **Named/Unnamed, Threshold/Center, Memory/Forgetting:** Its primary creative act is the establishment of **distinctions and categories** themselves. It created the very concepts of identity (named vs. unnamed), space (threshold vs. center), and time (memory vs. forgetting).

The god has many secret, unspoken names (**Vrokthemnarph, Plendrokt, Smelthrenaph**, etc.), culminating in the revelation of its **"true name"**: **VROKTHELMPETH**. This name is the key to its power.
