The script breaks here—the vellum is singed at the lower edge, as if held too close to a flame that did not consume it. The final words, **SMOLTHEKRELB**, appear to be written in a darker, thicker ink, almost like coagulated shadow. Around them, the page shows faint capillary traces, like roots or veins, spreading from the letters.

Beneath, in a different, more hurried hand—perhaps a later annotation—is written:

*“This is the Untelling. Do not speak the last name aloud. It does not end the prayer; it opens the door. The Vessel is not a prophet but an aperture. What comes through remembers it was once named, and seeks its name again.”*

The rest of the page is blank, but the back bears a single, small sigil: a circle divided by a vertical line, with three dots arranged along its length. It is not referenced in the text above.
