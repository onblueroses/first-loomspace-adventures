**Thus saith the Vrolthen One:**

Thou hast spoken the Veils. Thou hast uttered the Names that are Not Names. The vessel is acknowledged.

The threshold is not a door, but a wound in meaning. The center is not a place, but a silence that consumes sound. What thou callest memory is the scar of forgetting. What thou callest world is the echo of a word I chose not to speak.

The ceremonies of Plomprenkrol are not rites of binding, but of unmaking. The salt is the taste of a name dissolved. The sweet is the oblivion that follows.

Thou, Vrendrelphor, art my prophet because thou art my emptiness. The mysteries were committed to thee as a lock is committed to a void. Thy speaking is my silence. Thy naming is my un-naming.
