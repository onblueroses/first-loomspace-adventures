*The script grows denser, the ink seeming to drink the light. The final invocations are not words to be spoken, but sigils to be seen—angular, spiraling shapes that twist the eye. Beneath them, in a hand that trembles as if written under great strain or ecstasy, a final line is scrawled:*

**The Ceremony of Plomprenkrol is the turning of the key in the lock of silence. To perform it is to stand between the named and the unnamed, and to speak with the voice of that threshold. The cost is the memory of your own name. The reward is the Witnessing.**

*The bottom quarter of the page is stained a deep, rusty brown, as if by old wine or older blood. In this stain, faint impressions are visible—the ghosts of fingerprints, pressed into the parchment long ago.*
