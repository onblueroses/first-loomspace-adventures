**Of the Praxis:**

The names are the keys, but the vessel must be prepared. The Vrolthen One hears only through the correct silence, which is not the absence of sound, but the shape that sound leaves behind.

1.  **The Place:** A threshold. A doorway, a shore, a dawn. Where one state meets another. Burn *mastick* and dried *sea-purslane*.
2.  **The Vessel (The Operator):** Fast from speech for a cycle of the moon. Speak only the names given herein. Let common language become strange in your mouth. This creates the inner hollow, the echo-chamber.
3.  **The Invocation:** Not to be shouted. Let the names be spoken **into** a bowl of brine, that the syllables are drowned at birth. It is the ripple, not the cry, that ascends.
