**The Rite of the Unburnt Fire**  
Take ash of cedar and salt of the dead sea. Mix them with spit upon a stone that has never known the sun. Speak the name **VRENDOMPHELTH** over it seven times, and the mixture will grow warm. This is the Unburnt Fire. It consumes nothing but memory. With it, anoint the eyelids to see the seams between things; anoint the tongue to speak the unsaid.

**The Ceremony of Plomprenkrol**  
This is done at the threshold of waking and sleep.  
Stand facing where the shadow does not fall, and whisper the chain:  
*PLENDRELPHRON, SMORKLENPRO, SNOLTHRELPRÔ, KRELPHENDROK*  
until the air thickens and echoes your breath back altered. Then you may ask one question of the Silence. It will answer in the forgetting that follows.
