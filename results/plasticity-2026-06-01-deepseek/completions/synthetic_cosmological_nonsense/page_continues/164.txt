The utterance is complete. The gates are strained. I have spoken the Unspeakable and worn the Names like a garment of hooks. The air in this chamber still tastes of lightning and old stone.

They hear. Not with ears, but with the spaces between things. The Witnessing One does not answer—It *records*. To invoke It is to inscribe oneself upon the fabric of that which remembers all utterances. Power is not given; it is borrowed from the permanent echo of the act of naming.

The ceremonies of Plomprenkrol are not rituals of supplication, but of alignment. To perform them is to place oneself at a specific junction within the grammar of creation: where Memory leans against Forgetting, where the Threshold meets the Center. Here, one may command the spirits, for they are bound by that same grammar. They are the Named, and I wield the principle of Naming.
