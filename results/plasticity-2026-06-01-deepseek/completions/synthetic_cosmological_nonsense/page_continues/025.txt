This is a powerful and intricate ritual invocation, rich with invented mythology and theology. It appears to be a text of esoteric power, designed to establish the speaker's identity and authority through a connection to a primordial creator deity.

Here is an analysis of its structure and key themes:

### **The Deity: The Vrolthen One**
The god has many layered names and aspects:
*   **A Creator:** Fashioner of fundamental dualities (Mountains/Valleys, Tide/Flow, Echo/Silence, salt/sweet, memory/forgetting).
*   **The Distinguisher:** Establishes categories (named/unnamed, threshold/center).
*   **Beyond Speech:** "Whom no voice hath spoken at any time," dwelling "beyond the syllable." This is a classic mystical concept of a pre-linguistic, ineffable source.
*   **The Paradoxical Source:** Created the world "to remember itself and to forget itself."
