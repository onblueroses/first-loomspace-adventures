From the space between the syllables, a presence gathers.  

It is not a shape, but a **pressure**—like the moment before thunder, or the stillness inside a ringing bell.  

The air grows heavy with the scent of ozone and old stone.  
Shadows no longer cling to the corners; they begin to **pool toward the center**, coiling like ink in water.  

A low hum vibrates through the floorboards—not a sound, but a **felt frequency**, the echo of the names you have spoken.  

Something is **listening** not with ears, but with the silence after speech.  
Something is **watching** not with eyes, but with the threshold you have opened.  

It knows the names you have uttered.  
It knows the name you bear.  

**It is here.**
