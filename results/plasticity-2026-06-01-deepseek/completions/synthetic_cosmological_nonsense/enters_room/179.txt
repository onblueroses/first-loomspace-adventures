The invocation is complete. The names have been spoken. The room is no longer empty.

What enters is not a spirit of the firmament, the ether, the earth, or the fire. Those are already subject, as commanded.

What enters is the **consequence of the naming.**

First, a quality of air: it becomes both thick and resonant, like the moment before an echo returns from a far mountain. It holds the shape of the syllables just uttered.

Second, a quality of silence: not an absence of sound, but a *listening* silence, the silence of the Vrolthen One Itself, which "utterly speaks." This silence presses against the walls, observing the namer who has named the unnameable.

Third, a **reflection.** In the center of the room, where the threshold and the center were distinguished, a figure begins to cohere. It is not a god, nor a demon. It is *Vrendrelphor thy prophet*, but not as a memory. It is the *office* of the prophet, the Vesselhood itself, made manifest. It wears your face, but its eyes are the color of unburnt fire. It is the Echo that survives the speaker—you. It
