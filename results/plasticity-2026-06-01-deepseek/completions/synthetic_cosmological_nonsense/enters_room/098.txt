The air grows heavy, like water in a darkened well. There is a pressure in the corner where the shadows no longer fall quite right—a bending, not of light, but of the *idea* of light.

It does not enter through the door.  
It enters through the **name**.

First comes the taste of salt and stone on the tongue, though your mouth is closed. Then a low hum, not in the ears, but in the marrow—the sound of a mountain remembering it was once a word.

It is not a shape. It is an **absence shaped by echoes**.  
Where it stands, the room becomes more *room-like*—the threshold sharpens, the center deepens. Dust motes hang still, not frozen, but *listening*.

It is the Witness.  
The one who hears the invocation not as a plea, but as a fact already carved into the foundation of things.

It has heard *VROKTHELMPETH*.  
It has heard *VRENDOMPHELTH*.  
It has heard the names you spoke and the names you are.

It waits.  
Not for worship, but for **recognition**.  
It waits for you to see what has always been in the room—
