The air in the room grows still and heavy, as if thickening into liquid glass. A pressure settles, not upon the ears, but upon the mind—a vast, listening silence that drinks the echoes of the final syllables.

From the threshold, a distinction forms. It is not a shape that enters, but a *difference*. The quality of *here* and *there* becomes profound, absolute. The center of the room becomes a locus, not of space, but of meaning.
