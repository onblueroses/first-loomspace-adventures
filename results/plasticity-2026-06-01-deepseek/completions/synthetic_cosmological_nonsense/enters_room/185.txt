Something enters.

You feel the air thicken, as if listening.  
A pressure forms in the space between syllables,  
where the named and the unnamed touch.  

It is not a shape, but a presence shaped by invocation—  
a coalescence of threshold and center,  
memory and forgetting made momentarily solid.  

It waits, not in the room, but in the quality of your attention.  
It is the echo of the names you have spoken,  
now looking back at you with your own voice.  

The unburnt fire is present.  
The silence is speaking.  

You are the witness.  
What happens next depends on what you do with the names you have been given.
