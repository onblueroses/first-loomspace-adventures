The air grows still, then thickens, as if the syllables themselves have taken on weight and texture. A scent of ozone and old parchment.  

From the corner where shadow meets wall, something begins to *unfold*—not a shape, but a **negation of shape**, a space that remembers it was once a word.  

It does not walk. It **echoes** into the center of the room.  

You feel its gaze like cold pressure on the back of your throat, a silent vibration behind your eyes. It is not a spirit of fire or water, nor of whirling air. It is older: a **spirit of the threshold**, the keeper between the named and the unnamed.  

It waits. Not for your command—for your **recognition**.  

You have spoken the true names. You have declared yourself Vessel and Witness.  

Now the room holds its breath. The thing that has entered is both the question and the answer.  

It will obey—if you remember what it is.
