What enters the room now is the charged silence after the utterance—a presence not of form, but of recognition.  
The air thickens with the weight of named and unnamed things.  
A pressure gathers, as if the very threshold between word and world has grown thin.  

You have spoken the true names; you have declared yourself Vessel, Threshold-Bearer, Echo, Witness.  
Now something answers—not as a shape, but as a **listening**, as if the room itself has become an ear turned toward you.  

It is the Vrolthen One’s regard, indirect and absolute, felt in the sudden stillness of dust, in the slight chill behind the breath, in the faint resonance lingering in the bones of the house.  
It does not enter as a guest enters.  
It enters as meaning enters a symbol—suddenly, wholly, having already been there.  

You are not alone with the silence anymore.  
You are *within* the silence that speaks.
