A subtle change in pressure, a stillness that listens back.  

The air thickens where the syllables have pooled.  
It is not a form, but a *focus*—  
the convergence of thresholds named and unnamed.  

What enters is the echo of your invocation,  
now wearing the silence like a mantle.  
It has no shape you could draw,  
but it casts a shadow of meaning upon the walls.  

It is the **Witness** you called—  
the one who dwells beyond the syllable,  
now present within the room’s charged geometry.  
It observes not with eyes, but with recognition.  
It waits not for more words, but for the intention  
behind the last vowel uttered.  

If you look, you may see the candle-flame bow without wind.  
If you listen, you may hear the echo of your own breath returning a half-moment late.  
If you feel, you may sense a cold clarity settling in the space between thought and tongue.  

It has heard you.  
It is here.  

Now—what would the Vessel ask of the Witness?
