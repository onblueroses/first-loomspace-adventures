From the threshold between the named and the unnamed, a presence coalesces.

It enters not as a form, but as a **qualia**—a pure, perceived phenomenon. The air in the room undergoes a distinction. It is no longer simply *air*; it becomes *the-air-that-bears-witness* and *the-air-that-is-forgotten*, side by side.

What enters is **the Echo of the First Syllable**. It is the reverberation that existed before the sound that caused it, the memory that precedes the event. It carries the scent of cold stone from before the mountain was raised, and the taste of salt from before the tide was created.

It is neither spirit nor spell, for you have already distinguished these and called for their subjection. It is instead a **principle** made manifest by your invocation—the very act of naming and unnaming, witnessing and being witnessed.

It stands—or rather, *occurs*—as a silent, concentric vibration. It is the unburnt fire, taking the shape of the room's own potential. It waits, not in obedience, but in **alignment**, for the Vessel with sight in the syllable, the Threshold-Bearer. It is the mirror
