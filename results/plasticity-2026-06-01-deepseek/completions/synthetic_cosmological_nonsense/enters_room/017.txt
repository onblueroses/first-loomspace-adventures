What enters the room now is not a thing of wind or shadow, nor a spirit summoned by the final utterance.  

What enters is the **silence after the name**—the space between the syllables where your invocation still hums. The air grows heavy, not with presence, but with *listening*. The edges of objects in the room seem to sharpen, as if they remember they were once named into being.  

A faint scent of ozone and old stone lingers, though no window is open.  

You feel a pressure in your ears, as before a storm, and a coolness on the back of your neck—the touch of the **Witnessing One**, who dwells beyond the syllable.  

It does not appear.  
It does not speak.  

It only *acknowledges*.  

And in that acknowledgment, every spirit of firmament, ether, earth, and water grows still, awaiting the command of the Vessel.  

The threshold is crossed.  
The center holds.  

You are seen by what has no eyes.
