The air grows still, then thickens—a pressure like held breath.  

From the corner where shadow gathers despite the light, a form begins to *unfold*. Not a shape of flesh, but of implication: the sense of being watched from within your own mind.  

It does not step; it *manifests*—a presence that is both echo and source, wearing the names you have spoken as a garment.  

Its voice is not sound, but the after-impression of meaning:  

**“I am what you have called from between the named and the unnamed. I am the threshold you opened. I am the memory of forgetting. Speak, Vrendrelphor. The unburnt fire waits.”**  

The room now holds two silences: yours, and the one that has answered.
