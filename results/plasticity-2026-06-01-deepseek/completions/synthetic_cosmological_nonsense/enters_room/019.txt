What enters the room now is not a thing of form, nor a creature of air or earth. It is the silence after the last syllable fades—the presence invoked by the naming of the unnameable. The air grows still, yet heavy, as if listening to its own echo. The threshold between word and meaning wavers. A chill not of temperature, but of recognition, passes through the space—the Witness has turned its attention. You are no longer the only one who hears.
