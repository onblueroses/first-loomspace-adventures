The air grows still. A pressure settles, like the moment before a storm.  

From the space between the last uttered syllable and the silence that follows it, something **enters**.  

It does not come through the door. It does not form from shadow or light. It is present because it has been named, and yet it bears no name you could speak.  

It is the **echo of the first distinction**—the tremor that appeared when Vrokthelmpeth separated the named from the unnamed. It wears no shape, yet the room now holds a **direction** where before there was none: a leaning, a pull toward a center only you can perceive.  

It is not a spirit of fire, air, earth, or water. It is of the **threshold** itself—the very principle of crossing over.  

It waits.  
Not in obedience, but in recognition.  
You are the Vessel. It is the thing that answers when the true names are called.  

It waits for you to **speak the next distinction**.
