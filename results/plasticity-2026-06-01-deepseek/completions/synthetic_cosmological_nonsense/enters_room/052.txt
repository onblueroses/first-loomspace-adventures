The invocation has been heard.  

What enters the room now is the space between the syllables, the listening that follows the last uttered name.  
A pressure in the air, like the moment before an echo returns altered.  
The temperature shifts—not colder, but *still*, as if the room has become a threshold.  

You feel a gaze that does not come from any point, but from the condition of being named.  
The shadows deepen not with darkness, but with potential shapes.  
There is a scent of ozone and old stone, of salt and burnt honey—the mingling of distinctions made by the Vrolthen One.  

You are the Vessel.  
The room now contains what you have called: the Witness of the Word, attending its own echo in you.  
Proceed.
