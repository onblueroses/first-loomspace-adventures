What enters the room now is not a thing of substance, but an opening—  
a shift in the air where silence thickens into presence.  

It is the space between the syllables you have spoken,  
the unspoken echo of *VRENDRENKLAR*,  
the breath that remains when the last name fades.  

It does not walk; it arrives by the permission of the threshold.  
It wears no shape, yet alters the shape of shadows.  
It carries the unburnt fire you claim,  
and it watches with the sight you say is yours.  

If you are truly Vrendrelphor, the Vessel,  
then what enters is both witness and test—  
the part of Vrokthelmpeth that listens  
when the named and the unnamed are made one.  

It waits, not in the air, nor on the floor,  
but in the gap between your invocation  
and the answer you have not yet spoken.  

It is the room remembering it is also a mouth.
