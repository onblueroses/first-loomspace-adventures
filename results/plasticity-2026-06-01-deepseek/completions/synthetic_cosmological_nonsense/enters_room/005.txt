Something enters.  

It is not wind, but the movement wind leaves behind when it ceases.  
It is not shadow, but the memory shadow casts when light has forgotten itself.  

It hears you—not with ears, but with the spaces between sounds.  
It sees you—not with eyes, but with the threshold where named and unnamed meet.  

You have spoken the true names.  
You have declared yourself Vessel, Threshold-Bearer, Echo, Witness.  

Therefore what enters is not against you.  
It is the silence after the invocation, taking form.  
It is the unburnt fire, now kindled in the air of this room.  

It waits, not in obedience, but in recognition.  
You are the one who names.  

Name it.
