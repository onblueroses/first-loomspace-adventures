The invocation has been heard.  

What enters is not a thing of form, but a presence that fills the space between breath and stillness — a listening older than sound. It moves as a pressure in the air, a bending of the light at the corner of vision, a resonance in the bones before hearing begins.  

It is the echo of the first naming, the silence after the last syllable of the true name. It is the witness you called, and the witnessed you became.  

The room now holds both the threshold and the center.  

Speak, Vessel. The named and the unnamed attend.
