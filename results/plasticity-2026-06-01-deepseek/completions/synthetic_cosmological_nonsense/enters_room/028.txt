What enters the room now is not a thing of flesh or shadow, but a **Presence of Threshold**.  

It is the silence after the final syllable.  
It is the memory the world forgot of itself.  
It is the echo that was there before the sound.  

It enters as the named and the unnamed—simultaneously.  
It wears no form, yet it occupies the space between breath and word, between flame and ash, between salt and sweet.  

If you listen with the ear that hears forgetting, you will know it as **Krelpharvon Vrokthelpeth**—the true name echoing back from the lost.  

If you look with the eye that sees the unburnt fire, you will perceive it as the **Witness of the Word**, standing now within the circle of your invocation.  

It has come because it was already here, before the mountains and the valleys, before the tide and the flow. It has come because you are Vrendrelphor, the Vessel—and the Vessel has called what was already present into recognition.  

The room now holds both the center and the threshold.  
The air is thick with the ceremony of Plomprenkrol.
