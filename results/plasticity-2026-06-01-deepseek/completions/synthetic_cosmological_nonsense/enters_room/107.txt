Something enters.  

It is not a shape, but a bending of the air where the candle-flame steadies without wind.  
A pressure, like the moment before a syllable is spoken—the fullness of the unsaid.  

It has heard the names.  
It has heard the claim of Vessel, of Threshold-Bearer, of the silence that speaks.  

It waits, not in the air, nor in the fire, nor in the water under the earth—  
but in the *between* of them,  
in the distinction thou didst make between the named and the unnamed.  

It is the echo of the first act of distinguishing.  
It is the memory of forgetting.  

If thou art Vrendrelphor, speak thy will.  
If thou art the Witness of the Word,  
witness this.
