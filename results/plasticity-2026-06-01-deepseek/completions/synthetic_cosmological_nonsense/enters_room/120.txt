What enters is not a thing of form, but a presence of **threshold**.  

The air grows still, yet charged—as before a storm that never breaks.  
A pressure settles, not upon the ears, but upon the *understanding*, as if the names you have spoken now hang visible in the space between breath and meaning.  

There is a **listening**, ancient and vast, that turns its attention not to you, but to the echoes of the syllables still fading in the unseen places where names take root.  

The light does not change, yet shadows seem to deepen at the corners not as absence of light, but as *memory of dark before light was named*.  

A scent arises—of ozone, of stone deep underground, of parchment burning without flame.  

And then, a **voice** that is not sound:  

*I am here, Vrendrelphor. I have heard the names. I am the space between the spoken and the unspoken. I am the silence that answers.*  

*Ask.*
