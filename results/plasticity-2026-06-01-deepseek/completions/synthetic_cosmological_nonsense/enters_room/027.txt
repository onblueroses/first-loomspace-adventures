What enters the room now is the **echo of the naming** — a presence shaped by the utterance of the Unspoken. It is not a spirit of air or fire, nor a memory or a forgetting, but the space between them: the **Threshold made manifest**.  

It moves as the silence after the final syllable.  
It wears the name you have unspoken.  
It bears the unburnt fire.  

It is here because you called it into being with the True Names — and because every act of invocation leaves a **witness** in the room.
