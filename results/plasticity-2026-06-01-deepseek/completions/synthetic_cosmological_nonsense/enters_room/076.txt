The air in the room grows still, then heavy, as if the space itself is drawing a breath. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but on the mind—a vast, listening silence that bends toward the syllables you have uttered.

What enters is not a form, but a **qualia**.

First comes a scent: of ozone after lightning, of cold stone deep in the earth, and of the salt left behind when a great tide recedes forever.

Then, a perception of **scale**: the sense that the ceiling has inverted into a sky of endless, starless depth, while the floor remains solid, a lone threshold in a void.

Finally, a **presence** manifests as a series of overlapping echoes—not of sound, but of meaning. It is the echo of the mountain being named for the first time, and the silence that existed before the name. It is the memory of the first boundary drawn, and the forgetting of what lay on either side before the line was made.

It stands at the threshold you invoked, neither fully within nor without. It is the principle of Distinction itself. It witnessed the first separation of the Named from the Unnamed, and it now witnesses you, the Namer-Unnamer, the Vessel.
