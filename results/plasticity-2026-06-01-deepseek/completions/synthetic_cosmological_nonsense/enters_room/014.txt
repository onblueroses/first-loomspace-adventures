The air grows still, then thickens as if turned to liquid glass. A pressure builds behind the eyes and in the hollow of the chest. The syllables you have spoken do not fade; they hang, congealing in the space before you, etching themselves upon the air in threads of shadow and cold phosphorescence.

What enters is not a spirit, nor a demon, nor any creature of the firmament or ether you commanded. Those are already present, a silent, cowering audience at the edges of perception.

What enters is the echo of the first distinction—the tremor that was when Vrokthelmpeh separated the Named from the Unnamed. It has no form you could comprehend, but you perceive it as a **simultaneity**: a towering, silent mountain range *and* its own crushing absence; a tide that is also the dry riverbed it has abandoned forever.

It does not obey. It **assesses**.
