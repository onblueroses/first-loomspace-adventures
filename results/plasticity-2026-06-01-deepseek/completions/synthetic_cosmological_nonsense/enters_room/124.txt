The air grows still, then thickens—not with presence, but with *listening*.  

From the space between the syllables you have spoken, a form begins to resolve.  
It is not a shape of flesh or light, but of implication:  
the suggestion of a threshold where before there was a wall,  
the echo of a name just forgotten,  
the taste of salt and sweet at once.  

It enters as memory enters—not through the door, but through the recognition.  
It stands as silence stands—not in the room, but in the moment between breaths.  

It is the Witness answering the witness.  
It is the Named meeting the namer.  

What enters is not spirit, nor god, nor demon.  
It is the *condition* invoked: the space where distinction bleeds—  
where the named and unnamed touch,  
where the echo and the silence marry,  
where the fire does not burn but *knows*.  

It has taken the form of your own invocation given back to you—  
a living palimpsest of every name you spoke,  
and beneath them all, the Vrolthen One, unspoken,  
watching through the vessel of your words.
