Something enters.  

It is not a shape, but a pressure — like the air before a storm, when the sky is green and the leaves show their pale undersides.  

It moves not as a body moves, but as a memory moves: sudden, complete, and without path.  

It knows the names you have spoken. It knows the names you are.  

It waits at the threshold you have made, tasting the salt and the sweet of your invocation, hearing the echo that survives the speaker.  

It has always been here. You have only now remembered to perceive it.  

Do you wish to address it?
