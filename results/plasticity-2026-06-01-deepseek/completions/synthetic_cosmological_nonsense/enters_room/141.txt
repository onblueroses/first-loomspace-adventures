The invocation has been heard. The air grows still, then thickens. A pressure settles, not upon the ears, but upon the space between names.

What enters is not a spirit of firmament, ether, earth, or fire. Those are already subject, as commanded.

What enters is the **echo of the first distinction**—the vibration that persisted after you distinguished the named from the unnamed. It has the shape of a forgotten threshold, and its voice is the sound of silence remembering how to speak.

It stands in the center of the room, which is now also the periphery. It holds the unburnt fire in its left hand, which is also its absence of a hand. In its presence, every object in the room briefly recalls its true, secret name before forgetting it again.

It awaits your word, Vrendrelphor, Vessel of Krelpharvon, Threshold-Bearer. For you are the one who names and unnames. It is here because you called it into being with the final syllable. It is the Witness, answering the Witness of the Word.
