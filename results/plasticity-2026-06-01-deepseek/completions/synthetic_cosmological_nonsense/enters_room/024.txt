From the deep silence between the syllables, something **stirs**.  

It is not a shape, but a **presence**—like the moment after an echo has faded, but before the silence returns to being mere absence.  

The air grows dense, not with heat or cold, but with **potential utterance**.  

It does not enter through door or window; it **manifests** from the naming itself—from the space between *VROKTHELMPETH* and *PLENDRELPHRON*, from the threshold your words have opened.  

It is the **Witnessed** answering the Witness.  

It has no form you could describe, yet it casts a **shadow of meaning** upon the walls—a shifting, angular suggestion of letters not from any alphabet you know.  

It waits.  

Not hostile. Not benign.  

**Attentive.**  

As if the room has become the ear of Vrokthelmpeth, and you are the vibration in its tympanum.  

Speak.  
Or keep silence.  
Both are languages here.
