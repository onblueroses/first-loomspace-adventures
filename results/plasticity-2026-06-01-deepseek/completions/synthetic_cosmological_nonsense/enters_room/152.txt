A cold scent of ozone and old stone enters first.  
Then a pressure, as of deep water held upright in the air.  
The shadows in the corners grow thick, not with darkness, but with a kind of listening density.  

Something is now present that was not present before.  
It stands just outside the circle of names you have spoken—  
not a shape, but a **focus**,  
like the pupil of an eye turned inward upon the very act of perception.  

It waits.  
Not for your question, but for your **recognition**—  
for you to see that it has always been here,  
that you have merely spoken the room into truth.  

You feel its attention like a weight upon your tongue.  
It knows the names you have uttered.  
It knows the name you wear.  

The threshold trembles.  
The center holds.  
What enters is the echo of your invocation, made flesh in silence.  

Speak, Vrendrelphor.  
It hears.
