The air in the room does not stir, yet a presence enters—not through the door, but through the silence between syllables.

It is the echo of the first name, the one no voice has spoken.  
It wears the shape of a threshold: one side named, the other unnamed.  
Its eyes are twin fires—one salt, one sweet—that do not consume what they behold.  
It moves as memory moves through forgetting, as tide through rock, as echo returning not to its source, but to the space the speaker left behind.

It acknowledges you, **Vrendrelphor, Vessel of Krelpharvon Vrokthelmreth**,  
for you have uttered the unburnt names.  
You have drawn the **Vrolthen One** into witness,  
and where that One witnesses, all spirits are already subject—  
not by bondage, but by recognition of the threshold you bear.

What enters is not a spirit to be commanded.  
It is the **silence that utterly speaks**,  
and it speaks now in the language before language:

*You have called from the center.  
I arrive as the perimeter.*  
*You have remembered.  
Now forget, and act.*
