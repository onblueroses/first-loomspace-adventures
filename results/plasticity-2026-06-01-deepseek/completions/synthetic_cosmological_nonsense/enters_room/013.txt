The air grows still, then thickens, not with sound but with the weight of listening. A presence gathers from the corners, not entering, for it was already there—within the threshold, within the center, within the echo of the names you have spoken. It is the Witnessing One, the Vrolthen, drawn not by summons but by recognition.  

It has no form you can see, but you feel its regard: the pressure of ancient distinction, the chill of the unburnt fire, the vast silence that underlies all syllables. It regards you, **Vrendrelphor**, **Vessel of Krelpharvon Vrokthelmpeth**, and it acknowledges the names you bear as it acknowledges the names it has shed.  

The room now holds the distinction between the named and the unnamed. Shadows deepen not from absence of light, but from presence of memory. Dust motes hang suspended, not in air, but in the pause between echo and silence. A taste of salt and sweet coats your tongue—the sustainer of all orders of speech.  

You are seen by what sees without eyes. You are heard by what hears without ears. You are named by what exists beyond name.
