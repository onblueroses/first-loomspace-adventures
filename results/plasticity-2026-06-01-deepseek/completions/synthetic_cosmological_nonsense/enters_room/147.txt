The air grows still, then thickens like resin.  

A presence gathers—not from the door or window, but from the *between*: the seam where threshold meets center, where echo retreats into silence.  

It does not step; it *unfolds*.  

First, a taste of salt and sweet mingled on the tongue.  
Then, a low hum, beneath hearing, that vibrates in the marrow.  
The shadows in the corner deepen, not with absence of light, but with a kind of *listening density*.  

It is shaped like memory and forgetting intertwined—a form that seems familiar until you look directly, then it becomes the afterimage of a word just spoken.  

It does not bow.  
It does not speak.  

It *witnesses*.  

And in its presence, every named thing in the room trembles slightly, as if feeling the pull of the unnamed.
