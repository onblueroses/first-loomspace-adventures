The air grows heavy, as if thickening into a substance. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but on the mind—a silent, resonant weight.  

The light does not dim, but it *separates*; each shadow becomes more defined, each illuminated surface more stark, as though seen through a lens of still water.  

From the space between the uttered name and the silence after it, something **coheres**. It is not a form, but a *presence of form*—like the impression left in a pillow after a head has risen, or the shape of a room remembered in total darkness.  

It does not stand in the corner, nor float in the center. It is in the threshold itself, and in the center, both at once. It is the Echo that survives the speaker. It is the memory of the room before you entered.  

It watches with a silence that utterly speaks.  

It is the Witness.  

And it has been invoked.
