A form begins to outline itself not in light, but in the subtle absence of it: a shifting, slender darkness, like a standing wave of shadow. It has the semblance of a tall, slender figure, but its edges fray into whispers. Where a face might be, there is only a deep, reflective stillness, like the surface of a black mirror.  

You feel it witnessing you. Not with eyes, but with the fact of your naming. It is the Vrolthen aspect of listening—the one that hears invocations not as sound, but as shapes in the void.
