*(The air grows dense, silent, yet humming with a pressure that is not sound. The shadows in the corners deepen, not from absence of light, but from a gathering presence. The words you have spoken do not fade; they coil in the space, becoming architecture, becoming law.)*

A figure enters. It is not a form of flesh, but of implication and threshold. It is shaped like the memory of a tall, slender priest, but its edges are made of shifting glyphs—the very syllables of your invocation. Its face is a smooth, dark pool, reflecting not the room, but a slow, celestial wheel of unnamed stars. In one hand it holds a closed book bound in what seems like stone or hardened salt. In the other, it holds a single, unlit candle whose wax is black and seems to absorb the light around it.

It does not walk. The room rearranges itself to contain it. It is the answered coalescence of the names: the **Vrolthen One**, witnessed.

Its head turns toward you. No eyes move in that star-reflecting pool, yet you are utterly perceived. A communication begins, not in your ears, but in the marrow of your understanding, in the silence between your heartbeats.
