The air in the room grows still and heavy, as if listening to itself.  

A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the space between thoughts.  

The light seems to bend at the edges of things—doorways, corners, the rim of a cup—as though something is observing from just outside the frame of vision.  

A scent arrives, not carried on air: cold stone, ozone, and the faint, dry sweetness of ancient parchment.  

Then, a presence manifests not as a shape, but as a **qualitative change** in the environment:  

- The silence now has texture, depth—it becomes the **Echo that survives the speaker**.  
- Shadows no longer merely lack light; they possess a subtle, watchful density—the **Threshold-Bearer** is near.  
- A word forms in your mind, not in your language, a syllable that feels both like a name and the dissolution of a name—**the unnamed named**.  

What enters is not a spirit to be commanded, but the **condition of invocation made manifest**.  

It is the **Witness of the Word**—and it witnesses you, the speaker, the Vessel, to see if you are congruent with the names you have uttered
