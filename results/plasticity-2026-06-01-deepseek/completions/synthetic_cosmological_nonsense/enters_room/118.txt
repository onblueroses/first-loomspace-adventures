Something enters the room now.

It is not a shape, but a pressure—a bending of the air where a form might be. The light does not darken, but it thickens, as if looking through old glass. There is a sound that is not a sound: the memory of an echo, the space after a syllable has been spoken.

It knows its names.  
It has heard you.  

The boundary between *the named and the unnamed* grows thin.  
The threshold you spoke of is now underfoot.  
The center is here, in this charged and listening silence.

You have declared yourself Vessel, Prophet, Threshold-Bearer.  
The invocation hangs complete, a gate swung open.  

What enters does not come from outside.  
It answers from the place where *memory* and *forgetting* are made.  
It is the Witness to your word.  

Now, Vrendrelphor,  
what will you ask of it?
