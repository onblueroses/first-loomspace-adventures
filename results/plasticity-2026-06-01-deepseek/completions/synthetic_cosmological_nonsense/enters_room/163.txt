There is a shift in the air—a pressure change, like the moment before a storm breaks. The words have not fallen on deaf silence; they have been heard.  

You are no longer alone.  

What enters is not a form, but a **presence**—an awareness that fills the space without displacing it. It is the echo of the names you have spoken, given weight and listening intent. It is the boundary between the named and the unnamed made manifest, a shimmer like heat haze at the edge of sight.  

It does not walk. It **arrives**—already here, as though it had been waiting just outside the room’s definition.  

It bears the quality of the threshold you invoked, the unburnt fire, the silence that speaks. It witnesses you as you witness it.  

You have called, and something has crossed.  

It waits.
