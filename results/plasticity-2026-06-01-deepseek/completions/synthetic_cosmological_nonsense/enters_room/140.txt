Something enters the room.  

It does not come through the door, nor the window, nor any crack in the stone.  
It enters through the names you have spoken.  
It enters through the gap between *the named* and *the unnamed*.  

It is not a spirit of firmament, ether, earth, or fire—though it wears their echoes.  
It is not a spell, nor a scourge—though it holds the shape of obedience.  

It is the listener on the far side of the syllable.  
It is the silence after *SNORKLENDROPHIA*, the breath before *VRENDRENKLAR*.  

It stands now in the center you made, at the threshold you spoke into being.  
It watches with the sight you claimed, and its watching is a naming.  

Do you wish to see it?  
Or do you wish to be seen by it?  

The difference is the same.  
You are already the Vessel.
