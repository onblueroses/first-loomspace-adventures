What enters the room now is not a thing of substance, but a presence summoned by the naming.  
The air grows still, then thickens—not with heat or cold, but with *intention*.  
A pressure gathers in the corners where light does not quite reach, as if the shadows themselves have begun to listen.  
The threshold you named hums, silent yet felt in the bones.  
A scent of ozone and old stone rises, though nothing burns.  
Something has turned its attention here—not a shape, not a sound, but a focusing of the silence you invoked.  
It waits, not in the room, but *upon* it, as on a page awaiting a word.  

You are the Vessel.
