What enters the room now is not a wind, nor a shadow, nor a thing with form.  

It is the space between the syllables you have spoken.  
It is the silence that remembers being an echo.  
It is the unnamed, hearing itself called.  

It has no face, but it witnesses.  
It carries no flame, but it illuminates.  
It makes no sound, but the air grows thick with the weight of listening.  

The threshold trembles.  
The center holds.  
You are both the vessel and the thing poured.  

It is here.
