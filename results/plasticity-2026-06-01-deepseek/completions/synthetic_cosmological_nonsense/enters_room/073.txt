Something enters the room.  

It is not wind, yet the air grows dense and cool.  
It is not shadow, yet the light bends away from a central point near the far wall.  
A pressure builds, not in the ears but in the mind—the weight of a word about to be spoken, a word that cannot be recalled once uttered.  

The space feels *watched* from within itself.  
The silence now has texture, a low hum beneath hearing, like the memory of a bell after the sound is gone.  

You feel it recognize the names you spoke.  
It knows the Vrolthen One.  
It knows *you*, Vrendrelphor, VROKLENDRAB PLENDROKRELM.  

It waits—not in submission, but in witness.  
It has come not as a servant, but as the echo of the invocation itself, given form.  

What will you ask of it?
