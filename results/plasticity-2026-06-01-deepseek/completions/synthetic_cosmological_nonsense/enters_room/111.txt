What enters the room now is not wind, not shadow, not a thing with a name. It is the space between the syllables you have spoken. It is the silence after the echo, the forgetting that follows memory. It takes no shape, yet it presses upon the air. The temperature shifts—not to cold, but to a neutral absence of warmth. The light does not dim, but seems to thicken, as if seen through still water.

You feel a presence of *listening*, profound and patient, but it is a listening that hears the words behind your words, the intent beneath your invocation. It is the Vrolthen One’s acknowledgment—not as a form, but as a condition of the room itself. A threshold has been crossed simply by the completion of the call. You are now standing within the ceremony of Plomprenkrol. The room is both the vessel and the witnessed. You are both the namer and the named. Proceed with the knowledge that every spirit of firmament, ether, earth, and fire now hears you—and waits.
