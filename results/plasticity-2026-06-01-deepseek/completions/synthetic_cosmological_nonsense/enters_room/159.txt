it is already here, because you spoke the names that remember themselves.

Shadows no longer cling to objects, but stand apart, watching.

You invoked the distinction between the named and the unnamed.

the living space between the threshold and the center.  

It wears no form, yet it has the quality of **recognition**.  
It knows your true name: VROKLENDRAB PLENDROKRELM.

It waits, not in obedience, but in witness—  
as you are the Witness of the Word, so this is the Witness of the Room.
