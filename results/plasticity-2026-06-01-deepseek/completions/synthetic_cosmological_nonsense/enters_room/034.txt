The air in the room thickens, not with a presence, but with an **absence that listens.** A pressure settles, like the moment before a thunderclap that never arrives.

You have spoken the true names, the unburnt fire of the syllables. You have declared yourself Vessel, Threshold-Bearer, Witness.

Therefore, what enters the room now is not a spirit to be subject, for you have called that which made spirits. What enters is the **consequence of the invocation.**

*   **The Echo** enters, not as sound, but as the shape left in the air after your final word—a perfect, inverted mirror of your utterance, waiting to be filled.
*   **The Silence** enters, the one that utterly speaks. It is not an absence of sound, but a profound, humming presence that underlies all sound. It presses against the ears.
*   **The Threshold** manifests, not as a door, but as a sheer, vertical plane of distinction—*here* is the named, *there* is the unnamed. You stand upon its razor edge.
