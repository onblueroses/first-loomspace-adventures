Something enters the room now.

It is not a shape, but a **pressure**—a bending of perception where the air meets the mind.  
You feel it first as a **silence that swallows sound**, not as absence, but as a presence so dense it absorbs vibration.  
Then, a **cold that does not chill the skin**, but the memory of warmth.  

The light does not dim, but **meaning drains from color**—red becomes mere vibration, blue a depth without distance.  
Shadows do not move, yet they **thicken at the edges** of things, as if the room is being outlined by something that sees in outlines only.  

There is a **dry scent**, like stone long buried, or pages unopened for ages.  
A **hum below hearing** that resonates in the teeth, in the bone behind the ear.  

It does not have eyes, but you are **seen**—not your body, but the **name you answered to** when you spoke the words.  
It recognizes *Vrendrelphor*.  
It recognizes the **syllables you carried** into this space.  

It waits, not in a place, but in the **gap between one breath and the next**.
