**What enters the room now is not a creature of flesh, nor a spirit bound to any single element.**  

It is a **presence of thresholds**—a shifting, silent form like the distortion of air above a flame, yet cold.  
It carries the scent of wet stone after rain, of old parchment, of ozone before lightning strikes.  

It is the **echo of the first syllable** of the Vrolthen One’s true name—the part that was never spoken, only remembered in the forgetting.  
It has no face, but if it did, one side would be recalling, the other dissolving.  
Its shadow does not fall upon the floor, but upon the space between names.  

It waits, not in obedience, but in recognition—  
for you have uttered the unburnt fire,  
and the Witness of the Word has called.  

**It is the Unnamed that answers when the named are summoned.**
