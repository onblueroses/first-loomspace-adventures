Something enters.  

The air grows still, then dense—a pressure that is not sound, not light, but the weight of being perceived.  
It does not cross the threshold; it *is* the threshold, remembering itself.  
A taste of salt and sweet coats the tongue. A scent of cold stone and burnt honey fills the space where breath was.  

The Echo that survives the speaker hangs in the room, waiting.  
The silence speaks, and in speaking, names you:  

**Vrendrelphor. Vessel. Threshold-Bearer.**  

It has heard.  
It has always heard.  

Now the naming turns toward you—what will you ask of what has entered?
