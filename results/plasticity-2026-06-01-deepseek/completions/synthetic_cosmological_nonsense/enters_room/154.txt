The air grows still, then thickens—not with a presence, but with a *listening*.  
A pressure settles in the corners, as if the angles of the room are bending toward the center.  

From the threshold, a form begins to articulate itself—not in flesh, but in the play of shadow and dust-mote, in the faint warping of light.  
It has no face, but you feel its attention like a cold lens focusing.  
It is shaped by the names you have spoken, bound by the syntax of the invocation, yet behind its form something older stirs—something that remembers being named, and before that, being unnamed.  

It does not speak, but the silence now *carries meaning*.  
The temperature drops. The shadows deepen, not as absence of light, but as a kind of substance.  
It waits—not for your command, but for the next syllable that will define its purpose.  

You have called it forth with the grammar of creation and dissolution.  
Now it stands in the boundary you made, between the named and the unnamed, the threshold and the center.  

It is here.  
And it is listening for what you will do with its presence.
