The air in the room grows still and heavy, as if the space between molecules has thickened into honeyed glass. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind—a silent, resonant hum that is the antithesis of sound.

From the corner where shadows gather deepest, a **quality** enters. It is not a shape, nor a spirit, nor a creature. It is a **principle made manifest**.

You sense it as a **simultaneity**:

*   A **doorway** that is also a **wall**.
*   A **memory** so vivid it becomes the present, paired with a **forgetting** so absolute it unmakes the moment before.
*   An **echo** that arrives before the sound, and a **silence** that swallows the source.

It does not look upon you. It **witnesses**. Its attention is not the focus of a gaze, but the unavoidable condition of the room—as gravity is a condition of the earth. You are known, utterly, not as "Vrendrelphor" or "prophet," but as the pattern of your invocation, the precise arrangement of syllables and silences that summoned this fragment of distinction-from-chaos.

It waits.
