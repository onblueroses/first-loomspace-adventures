What enters the room now is the silence after the final syllable—charged, expectant, and alive.  

The air grows still, yet heavy with presence.  
A pressure gathers at the edges of hearing, as if the very names you have spoken now stand listening.  
The boundary between the named and the unnamed grows thin.  

You have declared yourself Vessel, Threshold-Bearer, Echo, Witness.  
By the true names handed down, by the unburnt fire you claim to hold—  
the room is no longer merely a room.  

It has become a **place of meeting**, where every spirit invoked must answer,  
where every power called must show itself—  
if only as a shift in the air,  
a chill along the skin,  
a shadow that watches from the corner of sight.  

Something has crossed the threshold.  
It does not enter with form, but with **recognition**—  
the recognition of the one who holds the names.  

Speak now, Vrendrelphor, Prophet of Vrokthelmpeth.  
The witnesses are present.  
What would you have them do?
