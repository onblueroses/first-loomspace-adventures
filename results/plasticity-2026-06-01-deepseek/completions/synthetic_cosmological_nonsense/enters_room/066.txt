The air in the room grows still and dense, as if listening. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but upon the spaces between things—between the wall and the shadow, between one breath and the next.

What enters is not a shape, but a **quality**.

It is the echo of your final syllable, given substance.  
It is the silence that rushed in to fill the names you spoke.  
It is the threshold itself, now standing upright in the center of the floor—an invisible door that is both entrance and exit.

A scent of ozone and cold stone, of salt and burnt honey, unfolds from no particular point.  
The light does not dim, but seems to *remember* darkness, holding it within like a latent image.

You feel a gaze, but there are no eyes. It is the witnessing you invoked—the Vrolthen One regarding its prophet, regarding the vessel that utters the unspoken name.  

A sound manifests: not a voice, but the *concept* of resonance, as if the room has become the cavity of a vast, silent bell.

It waits.  
Not for your words, but for the intention behind them.  
It has heard the invocations, the claims of sovereignty over spirits and elements
