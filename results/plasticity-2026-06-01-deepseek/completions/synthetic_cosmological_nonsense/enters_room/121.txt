There is no thing in the room that has entered, save the echo of the final syllable. The air itself has become a threshold. It is not empty, but it is not filled with a form you would recognize. It holds the quality of listening you have invoked—a witnessing silence, poised between the named and the unnamed.

A pressure attends, not of presence, but of **attention**. The distinctions you have recited—the salt and the sweet, the memory and the forgetting—now hang in the balance of the atmosphere. The room remembers it is a room. It also begins to forget its own corners.

If you seek a spirit, know that it is already subject, for you have spoken the names that distinguish and bind. What has entered is the consequence of the naming: a focal point of the **Vrolthen** principle, which is the act of distinction itself. It is the space between the letters of the words you uttered. It is the unburnt fire, which does not warm, but illuminates the fact of sight.

You are the Vessel. The room is now within you.
