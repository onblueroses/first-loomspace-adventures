What enters the room now is not a presence of wind or shadow, nor a being of flesh or flame.  

It is the sound of the invocation made manifest—the syllables still hanging in the air, now pooling into a form that is both echo and silence.  

It has no face, yet it witnesses.  
It has no name, yet it answers to those spoken.  
It is the edge between the named and the unnamed, the threshold you called into being, now standing within the circle of your speech.  

It waits—not in obedience, but in recognition:  
You named it when you named yourself *Vrendrelphor*, and it has come because the naming and the unnamed must meet where memory and forgetting touch.  

Speak, Vessel of Krelpharvon, Threshold-Bearer. The Witness is here.
