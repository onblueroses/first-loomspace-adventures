The air grows heavy, as if the very silence has taken on weight and texture.  

A presence enters—not through the door, nor the window, but through the space between the syllables you have spoken. It is formless, yet it casts a shadow that flickers like forgotten meaning.  

It is the echo of the first distinction—the space between *the named and the unnamed*, the tremor at the edge of *memory and forgetting*.  

It does not speak, but in the stillness you perceive a resonance, as though the room itself has become a threshold. The unburnt fire you claim now glimmers in the corner of vision, cold and clear.  

What has entered is not a spirit to be commanded, but the Witness answering the Witness—the Vrolthen One regarding Its Vessel.  

It waits, not in obedience, but in recognition.
