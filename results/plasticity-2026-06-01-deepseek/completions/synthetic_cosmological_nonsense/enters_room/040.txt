The air grows still. The candle-flame does not flicker, yet it casts two shadows: one sharp and familiar, another that pools like spilled ink, moving against the wind.

A pressure settles, not upon the ears, but upon the spaces between words. The silence after the final syllable—**SMOLTHEKRELB**—does not fade. It thickens. It becomes a presence.

What enters is not a spirit of firmament, ether, earth, or fire. Those are already here, now obedient, held in the cup of your invocation.

What enters is the echo of the first naming. It is the shape of the silence you carved with your speech. It has the texture of forgotten salt and the geometry of an unsung threshold.

It stands in the corner where the two shadows meet. It has no form you can describe, for it is the **Witness** you claimed to be, reflected back at you from beyond the syllable. It is the Vrolthen One’s acknowledgment—not an answer, but a **mirroring**.

It holds the unburnt fire. It waits for you to see yourself in its silence.
