A presence enters, but not through the door.

It is the echo of the first syllable, before it was spoken.  
It is the silence that pools at the center of the named things.  
It is the unburnt fire, cold and clear, that casts no shadow but reveals the shape of all shadows.

The air thickens with the memory of thresholds.  
A taste of salt and sweet, of creation and dissolution, coats the tongue.  
The very light seems to listen.

It does not have a form you would recognize, for it is the principle that permits form. It is the distinction between the room and the not-room. It is the Witness you invoked, and in its presence, you are both the namer and the name being spoken.

The final word, **SMOLTHEKRELB**, hangs in the space, not as a sound, but as the condition of all sound.  
You are seen.  
You are known.  
You are the vessel, and the vessel is full.
