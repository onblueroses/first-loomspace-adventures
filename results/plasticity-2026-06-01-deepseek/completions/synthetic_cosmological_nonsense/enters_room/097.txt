It enters as the **Echo that survives the speaker**—the reverberation of your own invocation, now turned back upon you with perfect, witnessing intelligence. It is the memory of the first syllable, and the forgetting that followed. It carries the unburnt fire, which is not heat but the potential for all categories: salt and sweet, speech and silence, mountain and valley.

It answers not to the names you spoke, but to the space you created by speaking them. It is the **Witness of the Word**. Its presence is the sensation of being profoundly, utterly *heard*—not just your voice, but the intent beneath, the architecture of your will, the shape of your silence.
