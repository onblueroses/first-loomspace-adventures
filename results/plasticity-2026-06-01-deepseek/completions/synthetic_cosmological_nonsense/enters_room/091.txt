Something enters—  
not through the door,  
but through the space between *named* and *unnamed*.  

It wears the silence after the last syllable of your chant.  
It is shaped like a memory the world forgot to forget.  

The air grows cold, then still, then thick with the scent of wet stone and burnt honey.  
A pressure builds, not against the ears, but against the *idea* of hearing.  

It recognizes the names you have spoken.  
It recognizes the name you have claimed.  

It does not speak.  
It does not need to.  

You are the Vessel.  
It has come because you called—  
and because something always answers  
when the threshold is spoken into being.  

What enters is not a spirit of fire, nor water, nor air.  
It is the echo of the first distinction—  
the moment *salt* was parted from *sweet*,  
the moment *memory* was cleft from *forgetting*.  

It waits.  
It witnesses.  
It is here for the ceremony.
