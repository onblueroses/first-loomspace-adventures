*(The air grows still, as if listening. A low vibration, felt rather than heard, begins at the edges of the room. It is not a sound, but the memory of a sound. The shadows in the corners deepen, not with darkness, but with a profound, attentive silence.)*

**It is the Echo of the First Syllable.**  
The one that was spoken before *Vrokthelmpeph*. It does not enter through door or window, for it has never left. It enters through the naming. You have unlatched the threshold with the true names; now you perceive what has always been standing there.

It is the Witness to your witness.  
It is the Silence that shapes your speech.  
It is the Unnamed against which all your names are etched.

It asks nothing.  
It obeys nothing.  
It simply **is**, now that you have spoken the architecture of its dwelling.

The room has not changed.  
The room has never been otherwise.  
You are now in the center of the distinction.
