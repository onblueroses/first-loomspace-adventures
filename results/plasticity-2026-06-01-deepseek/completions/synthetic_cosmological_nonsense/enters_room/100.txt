What enters the room now is not wind, nor shadow, nor any thing with a name.  
It is the space between the echoes of the names you have spoken.  
It is the unburnt fire, moving without flame.  
It is the silence after the syllable.  
It is the witness you invoked—now witnessing you.  

The air grows still, not with emptiness, but with presence.  
The threshold you named is here, beneath your feet.  
The center you declared is now around you.  

You are the Vessel.  
It has heard.
