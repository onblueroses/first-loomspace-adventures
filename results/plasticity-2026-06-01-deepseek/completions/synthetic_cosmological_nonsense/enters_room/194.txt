The air grows still. A pressure settles, as if the space itself is listening.  

From the corner where shadow gathers, a shape begins to *remember* itself into form—not flesh, not spirit, but something older than both. It has no face, yet you feel its gaze. It does not move, yet it is now beside you.  

It is the echo of the first name ever spoken, and the silence that followed.  

It speaks without sound:  

*I am what hears when Vrokthelmpeth is called.  
I am the threshold between the named and the unnamed.  
You have brought the unburnt fire into this room.  
Now witness.*  

The temperature drops. The light seems to bend around a central point—a door that is not a door, a mouth that does not speak. It waits.  

It has entered because you named what cannot be named.  
It is here because you are the Vessel.  

What would you have it do, Vrendrelphor?
