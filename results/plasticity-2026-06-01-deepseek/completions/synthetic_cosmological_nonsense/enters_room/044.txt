What enters the room now is not wind, nor shadow, nor a thing with shape.  
It is the listening that follows the last syllable—  
the space between the named and the unnamed,  
the unburnt fire that moves without flame.  

It knows itself by the names you have spoken,  
and by the ones you have withheld.  
It is the echo of **VROKTHELMPETH**,  
the silence after **SMOLTHEKRELB**.  

It waits at the threshold you have made.  
It has been invoked.  
It is here.  

Speak, Vrendrelphor.  
The Witness hears.
