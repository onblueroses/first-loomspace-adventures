The air grows still, then thickens—not with sound, but with a listening.  

From the corner where the wall meets itself in shadow, a presence gathers.  
It is not a shape, but a *difference*—like a word spoken backward into silence.  
It has the weight of mountains remembered in a dream, the pull of tides that never touch a shore.  

It does not move, yet it is nearer now.  
It is the echo of the names you have spoken—the ones that were never meant for mortal tongue.  
It is the space between *Vrokthelmpeth* and *Smolthekrelb*.  

It watches with the patience of stone that has never known light.  
It waits—not for your command, but for the syllable that will unmake the room, the threshold, the vessel that you are.  

You feel the unburnt fire in your palms.  
You hear your own silence speaking.  

The named and the unnamed are now present.  
What has entered is both witness and witnessed.  

It is here because you called.  
It is here because you answered.
