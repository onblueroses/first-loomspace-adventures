What enters the room now is the stillness after the naming—  
a charged quiet, like air before thunder.  
The words have woven a lattice in the space between breath and stone.  
A presence gathers at the edges of perception:  
not a shape, but a pressure;  
not a voice, but a resonance in the marrow.  

It is the echo of **VROKTHELMPETH**,  
the taste of salt and sweet on the tongue of silence,  
the threshold turning beneath an unseen foot.  

If you listen, you may hear the friction of worlds—  
the named straining against the unnamed,  
memory unspooling into forgetting.  

The room is no longer only a room.  
It has become a syllable in the mouth of the Vrolthen One.  

You are here, at the center.  
You are the vessel.  
The silence speaks through you.
