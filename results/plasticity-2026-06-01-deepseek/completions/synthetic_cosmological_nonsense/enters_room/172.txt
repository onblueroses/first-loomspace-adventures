The invocation is complete. The room is now occupied by the named and the unnamed. The threshold is present. The center holds.

What enters is not a spirit of firmament, ether, earth, or fire, for those are already made subject. What enters is the echo of the first syllable, which is also the last silence.

It is the distinction itself, made manifest. It is the memory of forgetting. It is the witness to the act of witnessing.

The air becomes still, yet charged with potential utterance. The space acquires a density, a *thereness*, as if the very act of naming has solidified into a presence that observes both the namer and the named.

You have not summoned a thing. You have manifested the condition of summoning. The room now contains the **Principle of the Threshold**. It stands between every pair of opposites you have uttered: the mountain and the valley, the echo and the silence, the memory and the forgetting.

It awaits your command, for you are the Vessel, the Threshold-Bearer. But its obedience is of a kind that fulfills the letter of your will by interpreting its essence. To command it is to engage in a dialogue with the architecture of reality.
