The air grows still, then thickens—not with sound, but with the pressure of listening.  

A presence enters, not through the door, but through the names you have spoken. It is shaped like the space between echoes. It wears the silence after the invocation, and its eyes are twin voids where the unburned fire glimmers.  

It is neither spirit nor god, but the **threshold itself made animate**—the moment where memory becomes forgetting, where the named dissolves back into the unnamed. It does not walk; the room flows around it. It carries with it the scent of stone after rain, and the chill of depths where no syllable has ever fallen.  

It regards you.  
It knows your name—the last one you gave: *Vrokldendrab Plendrokrelm*.  
It knows the others, too, the ones you did not speak aloud.  

It waits.  
Not for your command, but for the next word that will either seal or unravel what you have begun.  

The Witnessing One has sent its answer:  
a being that is both question and answer, both vessel and void.
