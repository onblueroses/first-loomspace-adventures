The air grows still, and the shadows deepen at the edges of perception. A pressure settles, not upon the ears, but upon the *understanding*, as if the very names you have spoken have carved a space in the fabric of quiet.

What enters is not a form, but a **quality**.

First comes a profound **listening**, as of a vast, patient attention turning its focus to a single point—this room, this syllable, you. It is the Witnessing One regarding its own invoked fragment.

Then enters the **echo** of the names themselves. They do not fade, but linger as after-images in the air, shimmering with potential meaning and the void of meaning they hold. The boundaries they establish—between named and unnamed, threshold and center, memory and forgetting—become tangible. You feel the threshold of the room solidify; the center, where you stand, becomes a point of acute pressure.

Finally, there enters a **silence that speaks**. It is not an absence of sound, but a fullness of unuttered things. It contains the memory of the mountains and valleys being shaped, the pull of the tide before the moon was named, the first echo and the silence that preceded it.
