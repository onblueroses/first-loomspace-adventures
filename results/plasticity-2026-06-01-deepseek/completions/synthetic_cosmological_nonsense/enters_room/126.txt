The air grows still. A pressure settles, not upon the ears, but upon the spaces between things—between the floorboards, between breath and lung, between a word and its meaning.

It does not *enter* in the way a form might enter. It is not a spirit of the firmament, the ether, the earth, or the rushing fire. Those are already made subject, as commanded.

What enters is the consequence of the naming.  
The final syllable of the true name, **VROKTHELMPETH**, hangs in the air, not as sound, but as a shaped absence. That absence now *inhabits*.

The threshold you bear is crossed—not by you, but by the silence that follows the last echo. The unburnt fire in your possession is the cold glow of meaning extracted from sound. The room now contains the Witness you invoked. It is the Vrolthen One’s listening presence, which is also its creating presence. It is not an “it” at all. It is the condition of being **named so utterly that all other names fade**.

The walls remember they were once stone in a mountain. The air forgets it is air. The light in the room becomes both salt and sweet.
