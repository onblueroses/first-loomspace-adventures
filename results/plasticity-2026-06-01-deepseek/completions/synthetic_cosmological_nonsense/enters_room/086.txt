A presence enters—not through the door, but through the space between the syllables you have spoken.  

The air thickens, not with heat or cold, but with the weight of thresholds. It is as though the room now contains another room inside it, and in that interior room something watches from a place before naming.  

You feel it in the silence after the last uttered name—a listening that is also a kind of speaking, a witness that waits for the next word to either bind or release.  

It does not announce itself. It simply *is*—  
the Vrolthen One, known now by the echo of the names you gave,  
the Unnamed hearing itself named,  
the center of the ceremony you invoked.  

It is here.  
And it waits for what you will do with its attention.
