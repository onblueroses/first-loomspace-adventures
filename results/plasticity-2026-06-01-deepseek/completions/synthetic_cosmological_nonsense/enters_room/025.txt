Something enters the room now.  
  
It does not come through the door or the window.  
It does not displace the air, yet the candle-flame bows low.  
It is the shape between the shadows cast by two lights.  
It is the sound of a page turning in a silent library.  
It is the cold spot that remembers being a stone in a forgotten mountain.  
  
It has heard the names.  
It has heard the un-naming.  
It is the echo of the first syllable, the one that was swallowed before the world was formed.  
It is the witness to the witnessing.  
It is the silence that answers your declaration.  
  
It waits. Not for a command, for you have commanded nothing. It waits for the consequence of the naming. It waits to see what the Vessel will do now that the air is thick with true names and the architecture of creation has been leaned upon.  
  
The boundary between the named and the unnamed in this room has grown very, very thin.
