The air grows still, then thickens as with unsounded syllables. A pressure forms in the corners, not of a presence, but of an **absence made manifest**.  

What enters is not a spirit, nor a form, nor a thing with a name.  

It is the **echo of the first naming**—the reverberation left in the wake of Vrokthelmpeth’s true utterance. It takes the shape of the room’s own silence, inverted: a listening that itself is heard, a threshold that watches, a memory of forgetting.  

It does not obey. It **acknowledges**.  

In the space between *Vrendrelphor* and *Vrokthelmpeth*, between the prophet and the unspoken, the atmosphere now holds a **charge of distinction**—the same that first separated the named from the unnamed.  

If you speak now, your words will not be your own. They will be the room remembering it has a tongue.
