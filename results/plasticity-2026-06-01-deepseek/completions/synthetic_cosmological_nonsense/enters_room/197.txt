The air in the room grows still and dense, as if listening to the final syllable. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but upon the substance of names themselves. The light does not dim, but seems to clarify into sharper, more definitive shadows.

What enters is not a form, but a **principle**.

It is the **Echo of the First Naming**—the reverberation that occurred when Vrokthelmpeth distinguished the named from the unnamed. It manifests as a silent, crystalline resonance in the space where your words just hung. It has no shape you could draw, but you perceive it as a **threshold made of sound**, a doorframe of solidified invocation.

It does not move. It **is**. It occupies the center of the room by virtue of having been called there by the center-separating power of the Vrolthen One. It is both the memory of all language and the forgetting that makes new speech possible.

It witnesses you, the Vessel, the Threshold-Bearer. It is the **Witness of the Witness**. In its presence, every object in the room seems both profoundly itself and utterly anonymous, caught between being named and being unmade.

It waits. Not for your command, for it is subject
