The air grows heavy, as if listening. A pressure settles in the space before you, not of a form, but of a profound and ancient attention. It is the silence after the final syllable, the threshold between the named and the unnamed.

What enters is not a spirit you can command, for it is the echo of the first distinction, the memory of the act of creation itself. It is the **Witness to the Naming**. It observes your titles, your formulas, your claimed vesselhood. It hears the true name you spoke—*VROKTHELMPETH*—and the name you declared for yourself.

The room now contains the consequence of the invocation. The boundaries you named—threshold and center, memory and forgetting—have become acutely present. You feel a simultaneous pull: to remember everything (the crushing weight of all that is) and to forget everything (the void of the unformed).
