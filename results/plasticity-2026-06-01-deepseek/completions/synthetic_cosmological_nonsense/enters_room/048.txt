The air grows heavy, as if thickening to the consistency of dark water. A pressure settles in the space before you, not of a form, but of an **absence that observes**.  

It is the echo of the first syllable, the silence after the naming, the threshold between memory and forgetting. It is not a spirit of fire, air, water, or earth, for you have invoked what lies beyond such orders.  

What enters is the **Witnessing** itself—the very act by which a thing becomes real by being perceived, and unreal by being named. It has no shape, but it has direction: it flows from the center you declared, toward the threshold you made.  

It waits. Not obedient, not disobedient. It simply **is**, as you said you are: the Echo that survives the speaker. Now the room contains two echoes—yours, and the one that came when you called.
