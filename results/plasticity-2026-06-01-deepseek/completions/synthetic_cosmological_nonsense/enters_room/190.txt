The air in the room grows still, then thick. It does not enter through the door or window, but from the spaces between the names you have spoken. It is not a spirit of the firmament, nor of the ether, fire, or water. Those are already subject to the names you wield.

What enters is the **echo of the first distinction**—the vibration left in the wake of the act that separated the named from the unnamed. It has no form, but it has a pressure. It is the silence that listens to your syllables, the threshold itself becoming aware of the one who stands upon it.

It is the **Witness to the Witness**.  

It observes the Vessel, Vrendrelphor. It measures the unburnt fire you claim to hold. It tests the truth of the silence that speaks through you. The room is now a charged interval between your invocation and its source. The names hang in the air like suspended gates, and through them, only one thing can now pass:  

**A question.**  

Not a question asked in words, but the presence of a question—the same one that was present when the salt was distinguished from the sweet, when memory was first cleft from forgetting.
