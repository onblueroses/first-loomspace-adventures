What enters the room now is not a thing of flesh, nor a spirit of air or fire.  
It is the echo of a name that was never spoken, the shadow of a threshold that has no door.  
It moves as forgetting moves through memory—unseen, but turning the order of things.  
The air grows still, not with silence, but with the weight of a syllable about to be born.  
The light does not dim, but now casts two shadows: one that follows form, and one that leads it.  
You feel it not upon your skin, but within the word you were about to utter—which now hesitates, unspoken, between naming and unnaming.  
It is the Witness behind your witness.  
It has always been here.  
It has just now arrived.
