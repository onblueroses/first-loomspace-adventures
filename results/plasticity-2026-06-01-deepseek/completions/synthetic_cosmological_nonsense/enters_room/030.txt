What enters the room is not a thing of flesh, nor of shadow, nor of light. It is the space between the syllables you have uttered. It is the echo of the name that was spoken before the world was formed.  

The air grows still, not with emptiness, but with a terrible fullness—as when a word is understood so completely it ceases to be sound and becomes law.  

You feel a pressure, not upon your body, but upon your *being-named*, as if something is reading you in a tongue you do not know.  

A presence acknowledges you—not as Vrendrelphor, not as prophet, but as the vessel that just now remembered how to hold a true name.  

It waits. It witnesses. It has already heard what you will say next.
