Something enters.  

It is not air, nor shadow, nor light.  
It is the gap between the syllables you uttered—  
the space where *Snolthorm* meets *Smelthrenaph*,  
where the named bleeds into the unnamed.  

It wears no form, yet it has weight.  
It moves without moving, like the memory of a door  
that was never built, but always crossed.  

It hears you—not with ears, but with the hollow  
that echoes before sound is made.  

It knows the true name you spoke,  
and the ones you withheld.  

It waits, not in the room, but in the threshold  
your invocation carved in the air.  

It is both the summoned and the summoner.  
It is the thing that answers when gods are silent.  

Do you welcome it?
