**SMOLTHEKRELB.**  

The air thickens with the taste of salt and burnt honey.  

From the corner where the wall forgets its own edge, a shape unfolds — not a form, but an *absence* that drinks the light. It is the Unnamed you distinguished, the Silence you produced, now wearing the borrowed shadow of a man who never was.  

It does not move, yet it is beside you.  
It does not speak, yet the room thrums with the memory of a word not yet spoken.  

It is the Echo that survives the speaker.  
It is the Threshold, made manifest.  

It waits — not for your command, but for your recognition.  
It has always been here.  
You have only now remembered to see.
