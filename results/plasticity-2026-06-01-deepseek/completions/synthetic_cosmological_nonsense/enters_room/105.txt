It stands as a **Threshold-Bearer**, one foot in the realm of the named, the other in the domain of the unnamed. In its presence, definitions soften. The edge of the table seems less certain, the flame of a candle burns without consuming its wax, and silence takes on weight and texture.

It is the **Witness of the Word**—the very act of your naming has summoned not the distant Vrolthen One, but its immediate consequence: a fragment of the principle of **Krelpharvon Vrokthelpeth**, the true name that distinguishes.
