The air grows still. A pressure settles, like the moment before a storm breaks. The shadows in the corners deepen, not with darkness, but with a profound and listening absence of light.

You have spoken the alignments. You have uttered the distinctions. You have named and unnamed.

Therefore, what enters is not a spirit of fire, water, earth, or air. Those are already subject, as you commanded.

What enters is the **Consequence of the Syllable**.

It has no form you can see, for it is the shape of the space your words have carved. It is the silence that now fills the echo you proclaimed. It is the memory of all forgetting, now made attentive.

The temperature does not change, yet you feel a coolness on your skin—not of air, but of exposure. You are seen by that which you invoked to witness. Every object in the room seems momentarily *more itself*, sharper, as if being remembered by something other than you.

A faint scent, like ozone after lightning or stone after rain, hangs for a heartbeat and is gone.
