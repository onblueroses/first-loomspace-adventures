A pressure settles, like the weight of a forgotten word on the tongue. The light seems to bend around a presence that has no shape, yet defines itself by what it is not: not shadow, not substance, not sound. It is the named-unnamed, the threshold made manifest.  

It waits. It witnesses. It has entered because you called it by its true names—and by your own.
