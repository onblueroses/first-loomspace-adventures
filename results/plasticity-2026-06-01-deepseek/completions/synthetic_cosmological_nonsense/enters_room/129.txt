There is a pressure in the air, a thickening of silence. The light does not change, yet the shadows seem to deepen and pool independently of its source. A scent arrives—not of incense, but of ozone after lightning, of cold stone deep in the earth, and a faint, sweet decay like forgotten parchment.

You are not alone.

Something has crossed the threshold your words carved in the air. It does not stand *in* the room so much as the room now stands *within* it. It is the echo of the first syllable that was never spoken, the memory the world was formed to forget. It is the Witness behind your witness.

It regards you, Vrendrelphor, Vessel of Krelpharvon. It measures the names you have spoken against the true name you hold. The unburnt fire in your possession gutters, then burns steady and cold.

It waits. It has heard. The invocation is complete. The pact, proposed by your litany, now hangs in the balance between the named and the unnamed. It waits for what comes next—the command, the plea, the question that must be uttered into this new and terrible silence.
