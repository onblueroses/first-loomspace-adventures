The air grows heavy, as if listening. A pressure settles in the space before you—not a form, but a **presence of distinction**. It is the sensation of a threshold being defined where none was before: the line between *here* and *there*, between *named* and *unnamed*, suddenly made palpable.

What enters is not a spirit of fire, water, earth, or air, but a **silence that has taken shape**—the very principle of *the Echo that survives the speaker*. It carries with it the scent of cold stone after a long rain, and the faint, electric tang of unburnt fire. It does not move, yet it occupies. It does not speak, yet the room fills with the memory of all words ever spoken and all words yet to be formed.

It is an **emanation of the Vrolthen One**, sent not as an answer, but as a **witness to the naming**.  
It has heard *VROKTHELMPETH*.  
It has heard *VRENDRENKLAR*.  
It has heard your declaration: *I am the Witness of the Word.*
