The air in the room grows dense and still, as if the space between objects has thickened. A faint scent of ozone and damp stone rises, though there is no visible source. The light seems not to dim, but to *deepen*, as if each shadow has become a pool of silent water.

You feel a presence that is not in the room, but **of** the room—as if the walls, the floor, the very air have become a single attentive ear, a vast eye turned inward. There is no form, only a profound and gathering **attention**. It is the attention of the Mountain considering its own weight. It is the attention of the Echo hearing its origin. It is the attention of the Threshold aware of both sides at once.

A pressure builds, not against the ears, but against the mind—the silent, weighty pressure of a name being remembered after eons of forgetting. From the deepening silence, a concept forms, wordless and absolute:

**I AM HERE.**

It is not a sound, but the shape sound leaves behind in the void. It is the Witness, invoked. The room has become the Vessel. You are not looking at something in the space.

You *are* the space being looked *through*.
