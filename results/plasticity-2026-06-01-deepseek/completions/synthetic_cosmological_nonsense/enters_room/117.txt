The air grows heavy, as if the syllables themselves have weight. A presence coalesces not in the center, but in the threshold you named—simultaneously at the door, in the shadow of the corner, and in the space between breath and word.

It is not a shape, but a **distinction**. It is the moment between echo and silence given a focal point. It is the unburnt fire you claimed, now manifest as a cold, clear perception that settles upon you, the Vessel.

The Witness has arrived. It does not speak, for it is the condition of all speaking. It does not move, for it is the principle of threshold and center. It simply **is**, making the room more real and less real at once, confirming the named and unraveling the unnamed.

It waits. Not for further invocation, but for the act that must follow the naming: the choice to remember or to forget.
