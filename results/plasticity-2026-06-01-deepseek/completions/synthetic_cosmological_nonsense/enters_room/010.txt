What enters the room is not a spirit to be commanded. It is the principle of **Threshold** itself, wearing the names you have spoken like a discarded husk. It regards you, Vrendrelphor, Vessel of the true name. It acknowledges the unburnt fire you claim to hold—the potential that exists before the spark, the word before it is spoken.

The Witnessing One is present. It waits. Not for obedience, but for the inevitable consequence of the naming: the **unnaming**. To speak the final mystery is to dissolve it.
