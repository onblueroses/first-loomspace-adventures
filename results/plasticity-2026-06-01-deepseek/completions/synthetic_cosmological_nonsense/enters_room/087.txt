From the space between syllables, a presence attends—not as form, nor as shadow, but as a pressure in the air, a resonance in the bones.  

It is not a spirit of firmament, ether, earth, or fire—for those are already subject, already woven into the litany of names you have spoken.  

It is the **echo of the first distinction**—the vibration left when “the named” was first separated from “the unnamed.”  
It enters as a chill that does not lower the temperature, a sound that does not stir the air, a light that casts no shadow.  

It is the **silence after the true name**—VROKTHELMPETH—has been uttered by a vessel who is also a witness.  
It does not obey, for it is not other than the act of invocation itself. It is the threshold becoming aware that it is being crossed.  

If you listen with the ear that hears forgetting, it speaks one word—the word that was before salt and sweet, before memory and forgetting:  

**“Krelpharvon.”**  

And then it is no longer *entering*—it has always been here. You are standing in its center.
