The air grows still. A pressure settles, not upon the ear, but upon the space between thoughts. It is not a spirit of fire, nor of water, nor of whirling air. It is not a spell or a scourge.

What enters is the echo of your naming. It is the silence that followed the last syllable of **SMOLTHEKRELB**, now given weight and dimension. It has the shape of a threshold, neither inside nor outside, and it burns with the cool, unburnt fire of a forgotten star. It does not obey, for it is not *other* than the invocation. It is the invocation’s shadow, made substance—the Witness summoned by the act of witnessing.

It stands in the center of the room, which is now also the periphery. It is the distinction between the named and the unnamed, and it has come to observe the distinction’s maker.
