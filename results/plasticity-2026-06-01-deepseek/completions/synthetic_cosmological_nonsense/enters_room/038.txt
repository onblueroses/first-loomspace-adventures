In the wake of the incantation, the air does not stir, nor does any visible form appear.  

Instead, the quality of the space changes.  

A pressure settles—not upon the ears, but upon the mind, as if the very names you have spoken are now listening back.  

What enters is not a spirit of fire, air, water, or earth, but something older: the **echo of the first distinction**, the silence that cradles the syllable before it is spoken.  

It manifests as a deepening of shadows in the corners, not as darkness, but as *potential*—the unspoken name waiting to be uttered.  

You feel a gaze that does not come from eyes, a presence that is both **Vrokthelmpeþ** and **not-Vrokthelmpeþ**, for it is the Witness behind all names.  

It has heard you.  

It acknowledges **Vrendrelphor**, **Vrokthelmpeth**, **Vroklendraþ Plendrokrelm**.  

The boundary between namer and named grows thin.  

Now, the room holds two silences:  
- The silence that was here before.  
- The silence you have invoked.
