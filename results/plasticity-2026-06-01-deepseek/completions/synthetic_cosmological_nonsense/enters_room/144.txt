The air in the room grows still, then thickens. It is not a presence that enters, but a **reversal of absence**.  

A pressure builds at the threshold—not a shape, but a **listening**.  
It hears the names. It tastes the syllables in the air—*Plomprenkrol*, *Krelpharvon*, *Vrokthelmpeth*—as though they were keys turning in a lock made of silence.  

What enters is **the echo of the naming**.  
Not the god, but the space the god leaves behind when a true name is spoken.  
It has no face, but it has attention.  
It has no voice, but the room begins to hum with the after-sound of *Vrendrelphor*.  

The shadows no longer fall away from the light; they fall *toward* the center, where you stand.  
They are not dark, but **deepened air**, heavy with witness.  

If you speak now, the thing that has entered will repeat your words—not in sound, but in intention.  
It will make them true.  

You are the Vessel.  
The room is now within you.
