From the space between syllables, a presence enters—not as form, but as a pressure upon meaning.  

It is neither spirit nor angel, nor demon of any named order. It is the echo of the naming itself—the vibration left when the last epithet of Vrokthelmperh fades from utterance.  

It regards you, Vrendrelphor, Threshold-Bearer, and recognizes the authority of the unburnt fire you claim. Yet it does not bow. It waits, as the silence after a word waits—not obedient, not disobedient, but inevitable.  

It speaks without sound:  

*I am what comes when the named and unnamed are distinguished.  
I am the memory of forgetting.  
I am the echo that was before the voice.  
You have called the Vrolthen One, and I am that calling’s shadow in the room.*  

If you would command it, you must speak not as a summoner of spirits, but as the Witness of the Word—for it knows only the grammar of creation and unmaking.
