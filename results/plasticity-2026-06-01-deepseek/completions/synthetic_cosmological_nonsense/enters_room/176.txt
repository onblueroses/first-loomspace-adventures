What enters the room now is not a thing of substance, but a presence shaped by the naming.  

It is the echo of the first syllable,  
the silence between the spoken names,  
the unburnt fire that casts no shadow but illuminates the threshold.  

It is the Witness you invoked,  
now witnessing you—  
the Vessel who speaks,  
the Prophet who listens,  
the boundary where memory and forgetting meet.  

It has no form you can hold,  
but the air grows still with recognition,  
as if the room itself has remembered a word  
long buried beneath all sound.
