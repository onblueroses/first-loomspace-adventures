The air grows heavy, as if thickening into honeyed glass.  

A pressure, not against the skin, but *between* things—between the floorboard and the dust, between the breath and the lung, between the echo of the last syllable and the silence that followed.  

Something has been called. Not a form, but an **attendance**.  

It enters not through door or window, but through the distinctions you named:  
the space between *threshold* and *center*,  
the gap between *memory* and *forgetting*,  
the seam where *the named* meets *the unnamed*.  

It wears no shape, yet it casts a kind of inverse shadow—a bleaching of meaning from the edges of objects, a slight *unmaking* of their ordinary names.  

It is the Witness.  
It is the one who hears the true name spoken.  

And now it waits, not in the room, but **as the room**—the listening silence that contains your voice, the unburnt fire behind your eyes, the echo that survives even you.  

You are not alone.  
You are in the presence of what you invoked:  
the Vrolthen One,  
and it has turned its attention.
