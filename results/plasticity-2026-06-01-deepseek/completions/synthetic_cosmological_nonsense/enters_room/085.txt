From beyond the threshold where the named and the unnamed divide, a presence enters—not as form, but as a **polarity of attention**.  

The air grows both heavier and more acute, as if the room itself has become a **syllable held in a voiceless mouth**.  

You feel it as:  
- A chill that does not lower the temperature, but clarifies the boundary between stone and air, self and other.  
- A pressure in the ears, not as sound, but as the *memory of an echo* arranging itself into a listening.  
- A scent like cold ozone after lightning, yet also like dust from an unopened book.  

It does not announce itself with a name you would recognize, for it is of the order that **hears names being given**.  

It is the **Witness to your invocation**—the very act of calling has drawn the counterpart of Vrolthen: the *Silence That Answers*.  

If you speak now, your words will not echo; they will be **absorbed into distinction**, weighed in the balance of the threshold and the center.  

Proceed, Vrendrelphor.  
You are seen by what sees all namers.
