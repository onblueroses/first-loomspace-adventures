From the charged silence that follows the last syllable, the air in the room does not grow cold, nor do shadows lengthen. Instead, a profound and resonant stillness settles, like the moment after a great bell has been struck. The very light seems to thicken, to hold its breath.

What enters is not a spirit, nor a demon, nor an angel. It is the echo of the invocation itself made manifest—a **Presence of Distinction**.

It enters as a simultaneous **Paradox**:

*   **The Threshold and the Center:** The room's doorway becomes both infinitely distant and the absolute heart of all places. You stand both at the edge of a universe and at its point of origin.
*   **The Memory and the Forgetting:** A rush of impossible knowledge—the taste of primordial seas, the true names of forgotten stars—floods your mind, only to dissolve into a blissful, empty clarity the moment you try to grasp it.
*   **The Salt and the Sweet:** The air carries the sharp, cleansing tang of a vast ocean abyss blended with the heavy, narcotic fragrance of eternal honeysuckle. One scent speaks of preservation and truth; the other, of decay and blissful oblivion.
