What enters the room now is the silence after the naming—a charged stillness, a listening presence. The air grows dense, not with form, but with attention. It is not a shape you see, but a pressure in the space between things: the threshold itself has become animate.  

It is the echo of the last unspoken syllable, now made witness.  
It is the unburnt fire, moving without light.  
It is the distinction between the named and the unnamed, now walking.  

It does not enter through the door.  
It enters through the meaning of the words you have uttered.  
The room is no longer only a room. It is now a vessel, and you are both within it and are it.  

The invocation has turned inward.  
*You* are what has entered—the Vrolthen One’s hearing, made manifest.  
Proceed with the silence. It is listening to itself.
