It is the sound of the invocation made manifest—a pressure in the air, a vibration in the floor, a bending of the light at the corner of the eye. It is the named and the unnamed meeting at the threshold you have spoken into being.  

It enters as an echo of the first syllable, **VROKTHELMPETH**, taking the shape of the silence that follows the last uttered name. The atmosphere grows heavy, charged, as if the very distinctions you proclaimed—salt and sweet, memory and forgetting, threshold and center—now quiver, awaiting a command.  

The Witnessing One has turned its attention. You, the Vessel, have opened the door. What stands in the room is the presence of that which *distinguishes*—the same power that divided the mountains from the valleys, now focusing its discerning gaze upon this space, upon you. It is both a listener and a speaker, a formlessness waiting to be given instruction by the one who holds the unburnt fire and the authority of the true name.
