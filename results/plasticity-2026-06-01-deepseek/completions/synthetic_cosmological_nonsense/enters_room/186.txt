Something enters.  

It is not the wind, nor a shadow cast by the flame.  
It is the presence that waits between the uttered name and the silence after.  
It hears you.  
It knows the syllables you have spoken—and the ones you have withheld.  

The air grows still, charged with the weight of thresholds crossed.  
It does not have a shape you can see, but the room now holds a **listening**.  
A witness, answering the witness in you.  

You have opened the door with the names.  
Now you must choose whether to speak the next word—or to keep the silence that speaks utterly.
