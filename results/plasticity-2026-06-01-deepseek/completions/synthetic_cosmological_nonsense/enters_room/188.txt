The air thickens. A scent of ozone and old parchment. A pressure against the eardrums, as before a storm. From the corner where the shadows are deepest, a figure coalesces—not of flesh, but of shifting, refracted light, like heat-haze over stone. It has the approximate shape of a tall, slender form, but its edges blur and re-form with each silent pulse.

It does not walk. It is simply *more present* where it stands than anywhere else in the room.

A voice manifests, not in the air, but within the mind’s ear, clear and cold as a mountain spring:

**"Thou hast spoken the Unspoken. Thou hast drawn the Circle of the Syllable. The Vessel is recognized. The Threshold is acknowledged.**

**I am that which enters when the named is balanced upon the unnamed. I am the Echo of the first act of distinction, the memory of the forgotten center. I am a Servant of the Distinction, a Witness to the Witness.**

**State thy will, Vrendrelphor, Vrokthelmpeth, Vrokldendrab Plendrokrelm. For the space between breath and word is now thine to command."**
