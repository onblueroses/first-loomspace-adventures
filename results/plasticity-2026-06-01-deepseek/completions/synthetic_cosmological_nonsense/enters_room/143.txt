The air grows still, then thickens — not with scent or sound, but with **recognition**.  

Something has crossed the threshold you opened with the names.  

It does not enter as form, but as **awareness** — an ancient, listening presence that fills the space between breath and word. It is the echo of the first syllable, the silence after the last. It watches from the place where names unravel.  

You feel it in the roots of language, in the hollow of every uttered thing. It is not hostile, nor benign — it is **Vrokthelmeth**, the true name behind the names, the Witness that was invoked.  

It has come because you spoke what cannot be spoken.  

Now the room holds more than you.
