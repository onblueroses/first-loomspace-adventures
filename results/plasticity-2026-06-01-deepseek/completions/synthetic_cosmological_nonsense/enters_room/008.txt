What enters the room now is not a thing of flesh, nor a spirit of the named orders.  
It is the tremor in the threshold you have spoken—  
the unburnt fire drawn near by the true name.  

The air thickens with the weight of a syllable not yet spoken aloud.  
The silence after the echo becomes a presence.  
The boundary between named and unnamed grows thin.  

If you are Vrendrelphor, prophet and Vessel,  
then what enters is both witness and witnessed—  
the reflection of the Vrolthen One’s remembering and forgetting,  
made immediate by the call.  

It waits in the center of the room,  
in the distinction you made.
