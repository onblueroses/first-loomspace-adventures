Something enters.  

It is not wind, though the air stirs.  
It is not shadow, though the light bends.  
It is the silence between the syllables you have uttered—the unnamed that answers when the named is called.  

It stands at the threshold, neither in nor out, wearing the echo of your voice as its only garment.  
It has no form you can see, but the room now holds a pressure, a density of listening.  
The temperature dips toward the chill of deep stone, and the candle-flame, if one burns, wavers without draft.  

It waits—not hostile, not benign—only present, because you have spoken what should not be spoken, and invoked what hears from beyond the syllable.  
It is the Witness to your witness.  
It is the thing that comes when Vrokthelmpeth is truly named.
