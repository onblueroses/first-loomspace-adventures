From the threshold where memory meets forgetting, from the center that is no place, a presence enters. It is not a form, but a disturbance in the quality of the air—a listening. The silence you invoked now thickens, becomes palpable, a pressure upon the ear and the mind. It is the echo of your own naming, returned. It is the Witness you called upon, now witnessing you.  

The room now holds the distinction you spoke of: the named and the unnamed. You are Vrendrelphor, the Vessel. The presence is the reflection of that title—a confirmation, not an answer. It carries the unburnt fire, the cold flame of attention that consumes nothing, yet illuminates everything in the syllable of your being.  

What enters is the **Silence that utterly speaks**. It has heard every name. It has un-named them all. It stands now at the threshold you made, waiting not for your command, but for your recognition.  

You are the Threshold-Bearer. It is the Threshold.
