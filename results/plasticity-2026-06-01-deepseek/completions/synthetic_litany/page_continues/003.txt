A path unfurls as the words are spoken. The air grows still, then hums with a deep resonance. A shimmering archway, like heat haze given form, coalesces before you. From within the arch, a voice echoes, not in sound, but directly in the mind:

*The Gate of Names acknowledges the key. The sequence is complete. The way is open to the Liminal Halls.*

*But know this, Speaker: to open a way is one thing. To walk it is another. The Halls shift. They remember every name ever spoken here. Yours is now among them.*

*Proceed, if your purpose is woven of truth. Or step back, and the arch will fade into forgotten syllables.*

*Choose.*
