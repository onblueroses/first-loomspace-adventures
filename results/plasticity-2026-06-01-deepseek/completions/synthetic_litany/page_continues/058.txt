Shadows in the corners of the room deepen and stretch, not from any absence of light, but from a presence that draws them out. The very names seem to linger in the space after they are spoken, not as echoes, but as newly-forged keys, each one turning a lock in a door unseen.*

*From the silence, a pressure builds. A voice, not from one point but from the atmosphere itself, responds—a whisper woven from the rustle of pages and the sigh of distant stars.*
