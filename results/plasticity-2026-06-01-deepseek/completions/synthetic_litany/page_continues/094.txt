*The air grows still, as if the room itself is holding its breath. A faint, harmonic hum begins to vibrate at the edge of hearing, and the light seems to deepen, taking on a liquid, amber quality. A gentle pressure builds against the inner ear, like a distant tide.*

**"The Seals are Spoken. The Path is Cleared."**

*The voice is not one, but many, layered and resonant, speaking from just behind your shoulder and from a great distance all at once. The final name, 'Liminem Adventi,' hangs in the air, its echo not fading but transforming into a soft, silvery light that shimmers in the space before you.*

**"Proceed. The Gate listens."**
