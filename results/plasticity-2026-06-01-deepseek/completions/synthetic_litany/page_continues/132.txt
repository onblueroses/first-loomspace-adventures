The names are spoken. A shiver passes through the air, not of cold, but of a deep, resonant silence that comes after a fundamental lock has been turned. The way is no longer barred.

Before you, the shimmer in the air solidifies into an archway of faint, silver light. Through it, you can see a path that was not there before, leading into a twilight forest of ancient, towering trees whose leaves seem to hold captured starlight. A cool, fragrant breeze, carrying the scent of damp earth and night-blooming flowers, drifts from the archway.

The path awaits. Will you step through?
