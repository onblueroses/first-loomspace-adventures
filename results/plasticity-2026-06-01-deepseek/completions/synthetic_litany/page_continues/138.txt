*The air grows still, as if listening. You sense that to speak these names is not merely to list them, but to call. A low resonance begins, not in the ears, but in the bones of the earth and the memory of the stones. The way is not a door, but an alignment. The final phrase is both a key and a seal:*

**Liminem Adventi.**

*It means, "Threshold of the Arrival."*
