[The final name is spoken, and a resonant hum fills the air. The texture of the world before you seems to thin, like parchment held before a flame. The solid stone of the chamber wall shimmers, becoming insubstantial. Through it, you see not more rock, but a vast, star-strewn darkness punctuated by distant, swirling nebulae and the cold, steady light of alien suns. A pathway of shimmering, crystalline steps appears, leading out into the void. A voice, neither loud nor soft, but carrying the weight of deep time, echoes as if from a great distance:]

**"The Way is open. The Watchtower of the Crossroads awaits. Step forward, or close the gate. The choice, and the consequence, are now yours alone."**
