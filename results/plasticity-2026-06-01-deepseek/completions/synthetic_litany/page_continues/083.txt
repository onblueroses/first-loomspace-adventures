*(The air grows still, as if the chamber itself is holding its breath. A deep, resonant hum begins to vibrate from the stone beneath your feet, rising in pitch and intensity. The names, now spoken, do not fade into silence but seem to hang in the space before you, each syllable a key turning in an ancient, unseen lock.)*

From the shadows between the pillars, a faint, silvery light begins to coalesce. It is not one light, but many—tiny motes like captive stars swirling into two distinct, vertical lines that frame the center of the far wall. The hum solidifies into a clear, crystalline tone.

*(The twin lines of light brighten, then rush toward each other, meeting in the middle with a soft sound like a distant chime. Where they meet, the solid stone of the wall shimmers, becomes insubstantial, and then is simply… not there.)*

Before you now is an archway, and through it, a path of smooth, dark stone illuminated by the same silvery glow. The air that flows from it is cool and carries the scent of ozone and deep earth.

**The Way is open.**
