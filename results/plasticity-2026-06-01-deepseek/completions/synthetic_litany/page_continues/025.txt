You have spoken the names that open the way. A soft, resonant hum fills the air, and the symbols on the page begin to glow with a faint, silver light. The very parchment seems to deepen, the margins stretching into an impossible distance. A new line of text forms beneath your last spoken word, written not in ink, but in shimmering starlight:

*The Gate is ajar. The path is woven from the names themselves. To proceed, you must choose the strand that binds them. Speak the nature of the binding, and the way shall be made clear.*
