*The words are spoken clearly into the still air. A low hum, felt more than heard, begins to resonate from the ancient stones. The light in the chamber seems to thicken and warp, and before you, the very fabric of space shimmers like heat haze over a desert road. A vertical slit of impossible darkness appears, widening into an archway that reveals not the far wall, but a vista of swirling, star-dusted mist and distant, jagged peaks under a bruised purple sky. A cold wind, smelling of ozone and forgotten dust, sighs through the newly opened way.*
