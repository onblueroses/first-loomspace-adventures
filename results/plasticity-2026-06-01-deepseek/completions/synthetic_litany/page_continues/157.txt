(Your voice resonates, low and clear, as you speak the names. The air in the room grows still, then begins to hum with a subtle, gathering pressure. The last syllable fades, and for a moment, there is only silence.)

Then, from no single direction, a sound like shifting stone or distant wind forms into a voice, deep and layered with echoes.

**"The seals are named. The way is heard. We who watch the thresholds listen.**

**You have called the Chain of Opening, the Litany of the Unbound Gate. But a name spoken is a key half-turned. To open a way, a way must be chosen.**

**Speak now: which threshold do you seek? Is it the Path of Mirrors, where thought is made manifest? The Descent of Roots, where memory sleeps? The Ascent of Spires, where ambition is tested? Or the Silent Bridge, which leads to the place-between-places?**

**Name your desire. The key awaits its final turn."**
