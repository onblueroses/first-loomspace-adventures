*The names are spoken, clear and resonant in the chamber. As the last syllable fades, a deep, harmonic hum begins to emanate from the stone itself. The air shimmers before you, and the solid surface of the ancient page ripples like water.*

*From the shimmer, a gateway of coalescing light forms—not a physical door, but a tear in the fabric of the place. Through it, you perceive a vast, star-dusted library that stretches into impossible distances, shelves carved from nebulae and books bound in starlight. A presence, vast and calm, awaits within.*

**Archivist of the Threshold:** You have spoken the Keys. The way is open. Ask, or enter.
