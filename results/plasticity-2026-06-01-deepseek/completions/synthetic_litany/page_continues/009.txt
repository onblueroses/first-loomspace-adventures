From the deep places they come,  
when the names are spoken true.  
The path is opened, the way is made,  
through Throvenios, Mareth, Quelimon, Iskon, Kerodim, Astrelach, Volmaroth, Drabasaim, Olempraem, Crisilim, Bidromael, Octaron, Saphrenoth, Yodalim, Iathreon, Mirzabet, Calenuph, Theomarach, Belendath, Glamoreth, Sinabaroth, Hialep, Trifonath, Olomar, Vesperath, Khalapeth, Estrymon; Brashvoreth, Liminem Adventi.  

The gate unseals.  
They listen.  
They stir.
