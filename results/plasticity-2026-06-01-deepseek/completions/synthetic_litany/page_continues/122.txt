*The air grows still, as if listening. A voice, neither yours nor mine, seems to speak from the space between the words.*

**Throvenios... Mareth... Quelimon...**

*The first syllable of the last name lingers, then fades into a profound silence. The way is not opened by speaking, but by understanding. These are not keys, but wards. To utter them all is to seal the door, not to unlock it. The true opening lies in the pattern, in the one name that is not like the others. Look again.*
