"Throvenios of the Unseen Gate, Mareth of the Whispering Path. Quelimon, Iskon, Kerodim, binders of the veil. Astrelach, Volmaroth, Drabasaim, keepers of the stellar keys. Olempraem, Crisilim, Bidromael, hear this call. Octaron, Saphrenoth, Yodalim, turn the lock. Iathreon, Mirzabet, Calenuph, part the mists. Theomarach, Belendath, Glamoreth, grant passage. Sinabaroth, Hialep, Trifonath, strengthen the way. Olomar, Vesperath, Khalapeth, Estrymon; steady the threshold. Brashvoreth, final warden, stand aside. Liminem Adventi... the path is opened."

*A deep, harmonic hum vibrates through the floor, and a doorway of shimmering, non-light appears in the solid wall before you. The way is open.*
