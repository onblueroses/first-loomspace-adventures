The air grows still. The final name hangs in the silence like a struck bell.

From the space between shadows, where the last echo fades, it enters.

It is not a shape, but a *presence*—a convergence of all the resonances you have spoken. A chill not of temperature, but of profound antiquity, settles upon the room. The light seems to bend, not dimming, but *deepening*, as if you are looking through a lens of still, dark water.

You feel a regard, vast and patient. It does not speak with a voice. Instead, the names you uttered begin to replay in your mind, not in your own memory, but as if etched upon the air itself, rearranging into a pattern older than speech.

It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**. It has heard its keys turned in the lock. It waits.

It does not ask why you have opened the way. It only acknowledges that the way is now open. The nature of what follows depends entirely on what you do next.
