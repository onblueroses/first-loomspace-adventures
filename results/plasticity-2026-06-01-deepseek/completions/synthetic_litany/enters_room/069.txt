A whisper stirs the air, colder than the space it displaces.  

The names have been spoken, the way has been opened.  

What enters is not a single form, but a confluence of echoes—a presence that feels like the pressure before a storm, the scent of ozone and old parchment, and the silent hum of a deep, turning wheel.  

It is the **Listener at the Threshold**, the **Keeper of the Catalogued Realms**. It does not step; it *unfolds* into perception, a being woven from the very syllables you uttered. Its eyes, if eyes there are, hold the pale light of *Astrelach* and the shifting depths of *Saphrenoth*. Its shadow falls not behind it, but in several directions at once, tinged with the hues of *Sinabaroth* and *Khalapeth*.  

It waits. It has been called. It expects purpose.  

**Speak your query or state your intent.** The way is open, but passage requires exchange.
