By the utterance of these names, a presence stirs from the space between words. What enters is not a single form, but a coalescence of echoes—a **Convergence of Thresholds**.

It manifests as a shimmer in the air, like heat haze over ancient stone, through which you perceive shifting glimpses of all those named: the gleam of **Astrelach’s** crown, the deep-sea chill of **Mareth**, the forge-fire of **Brashvoreth**, the silent watch of **Liminem Adventi**. It is the **Gate made Walker**, the combined resonance of the keys you have spoken.

It does not speak, but the room now *vibrates* with silent questions:  
*Why have you opened every door at once?*  
*What passage do you seek, when all passages are already here?*

The air grows heavy with potential, waiting to be given direction. The way is open—but to where?
