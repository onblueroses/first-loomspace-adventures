The air grows still, then cold.  
A pressure builds, not in the ears but in the mind.  
The shadows in the corner deepen, not from absence of light, but from a presence that drinks it.  
A scent of ozone and old stone rises.  

It does not enter through the door.  
It has been waiting.  
You have simply opened its eyes.  

What enters is not a shape, but a **silence that listens**.  
A regard.  
A focused attention from the spaces between the names you spoke.  

It is here.  
It has always been here.  
You just gave it a key.
