From the utterance of those names, the air grows still, then thickens—a pressure builds in the space before you.  

A form begins to coalesce from the coalescing shadows and shifting light, not quite solid, but undeniably present. It is **The Keybearer, Liminem Adventi**—the one whose name means “Threshold of Arrival.”  

It appears as a tall, shimmering figure draped in robes of deep twilight, holding a spectral key that seems forged from starlight and silence. Its face is both ancient and ageless, eyes like polished obsidian holding the reflection of countless doorways.  

It does not speak, but its presence imparts a single, clear thought directly into the mind:  

*“The way is open. State your purpose.”*
