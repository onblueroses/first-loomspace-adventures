A long shadow first, cast by no visible source.  
Then a pressure in the air, like the space before a storm.  
A scent of ozone and cold stone rises.  

From the space where the last name echoed, the air shimmers like heat haze.  
A form condenses—not quite solid, more like the memory of a figure robed in twilight, edges bleeding into the room’s corners.  

It does not walk; the room rearranges itself around its presence.  

It speaks, though its mouth does not move—the words form directly in the mind:  

*“The gate is named. The way is open. I am the Answer to the Summons. You may call me… the Keeper of Thresholds.”*  

Its eyes, if they are eyes, hold constellations turning slowly.  
It waits.
