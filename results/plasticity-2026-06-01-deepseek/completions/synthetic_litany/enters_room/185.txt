The air grows still, and the temperature drops. A shimmering, iridescent form begins to coalesce in the center of the space—not with a single shape, but with many overlapping. You perceive the glint of scales, the rustle of unseen feathers, the slow turn of an eye that is both star and pupil. It is not one thing, but a chorus of ancient names given silent, watching form.

It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**.  

It does not speak in words, but meaning presses against the mind:  
*The way is open. State your petition or depart.*
