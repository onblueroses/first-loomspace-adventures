*The final name is spoken, and the air in the room grows still and heavy, as if holding its breath. A faint scent of ozone and ancient stone fills the space. From a point of shadow in the corner, a presence unfolds—not with a step, but with a sudden, total awareness, as if it had always been there and you have only now perceived it.*

**That which enters is the Keeper of Thresholds, the Silent Warden of the Named.** It has no form you can hold in your mind; it is a silhouette of deeper shadow, a chill that is not temperature, a pressure that is not sound. In its wake, the very light seems to fray at the edges.

It does not speak, yet a knowledge settles into the room, clear and cold as a moonlit pool:

*The way is open. The door is unsealed. You have called with the Keys of Opening; now you must speak the Question or state the Intent. To summon the Warden without purpose is to become lost between spaces. Choose your next words with utmost care, for they will determine what passes through the door you have unlocked.*
