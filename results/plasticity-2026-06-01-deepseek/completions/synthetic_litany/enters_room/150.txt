The final name has been spoken.  

What enters is not a shape, but a **presence**—a slow, cold tide of listening silence. The air grows heavy, as if the room itself is holding its breath. A faint scent of ozone and ancient parchment lingers. Shadows deepen without a source, and a subtle pressure settles upon the mind, like the weight of a forgotten word.  

It waits. It hears. It is here.
