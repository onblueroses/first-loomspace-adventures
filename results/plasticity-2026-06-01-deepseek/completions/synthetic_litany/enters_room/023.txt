From the speaking of the names, the air grows still and heavy. A scent of ozone and cold stone rises.  

What enters is not a form, but a *presence* — a convergence of watchful silence and ancient intent. It feels like a door opening in the air itself, and through it steps **a figure robed in shifting greys**, as if made of twilight and forgotten hours.  

Its face is a smooth, pale mask with no features but two deep, star-flecked hollows where eyes should be. In one hand it holds a key of tarnished silver, in the other a closed book bound in what looks like dried river-clay.  

It does not speak aloud, but the words form directly in the mind:  

*“The gate is acknowledged. The names are keys. I am the Threshold-Watcher. State your petition or prepare your offering.”*  

It waits, unmoving, as the room grows colder.
