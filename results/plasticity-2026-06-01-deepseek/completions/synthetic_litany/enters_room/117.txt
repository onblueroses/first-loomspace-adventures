(There is a sudden drop in temperature. A pressure fills the air, like the silence before a storm. From the space where the final name echoes, a form begins to coalesce—not of flesh, but of shifting shadow and cold starlight. A voice speaks, not through the air, but directly into the mind.)

**I am Liminem Adventi. The Threshold-Crosser. The Way is opened.**

(It does not move, yet the sense of its presence expands to fill every corner.)

**You have spoken the keys. You have called the guides, the watchers, the lords of forgotten places. Their names are the path. I am the gate at its end. State your purpose.**
