The names have been spoken.  

A chill enters the air, not of temperature, but of presence. The shadows deepen, not from absence of light, but from an excess of something else. There is a pressure, a silent resonance, as if the room itself has become an antechamber.

What enters is not a form, but a focus—a convergence of intent from the uttered names. It is an awareness, ancient and patient, now turned its attention here. You may call it a listener, a witness, or the key that was turned by the lock of those syllables.  

It waits.
