The air grows still. The final syllable hangs, and the light seems to draw inward. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but on the mind—a vast and patient attention.

What enters is not a shape, but a **presence**. It is the silence after the last name is spoken. It is the convergence of the ways you have opened.

You may perceive it as:
*   A chill that does not come from the air.
*   A scent of ozone, cold stone, and distant, starless space.
*   The faint, prismatic shimmer at the edge of vision, like light through ancient ice.
*   The profound and heavy certainty that you are no longer alone, and have not been since the first name left your tongue.

It waits. It listens. The way is open.

Speak your purpose.
