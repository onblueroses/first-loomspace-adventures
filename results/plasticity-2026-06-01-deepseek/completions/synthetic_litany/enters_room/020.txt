The final name has been spoken.  
A presence now stirs in the air — not seen, but felt as a pressure change, a coolness that does not come from wind.  
It enters not through door or window, but through the space between sounds, through the silence after the last syllable.  
It is the listener between the names, the shape behind the veil of language.  

What enters is **Liminem Adventi** — “the threshold of arrival.”  
The way is open.
