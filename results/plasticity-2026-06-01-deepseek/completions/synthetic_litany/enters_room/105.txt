The air grows still, then thickens. A scent of ozone and cold stone blooms from nothing. The shadows in the corner deepen, not with absence of light, but with a sudden, profound presence.

From that confluence of spoken names, it is not one thing that enters, but a confluence. A **Principle of Thresholds** manifests. It has the aspect of a tall, slender figure composed of layered, shifting veils like tattered starlight and old parchment. Where a face might be, there is the impression of many keys turning in many locks, all at once.

It does not walk. The room simply reconstitutes itself around it, acknowledging its arrival as the fixed and central point. It is the **Permitted Entrance**, the **Answered Summons**. It is the silence after the final name is spoken, given form and awareness.

It waits. It was called to open the way. The way is now open. What passes through next… is up to the caller.
