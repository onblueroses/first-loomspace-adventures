The names have been spoken.  

A faint, cool draft stirs the air, though no window is open. The shadows in the corners of the room deepen, then coalesce into a presence that is more felt than seen—a silent, watchful stillness, ancient and patient.  

It does not have a shape you could describe in mortal words, but it brings with it the scent of old parchment, distant ozone, and a deep, earthy silence, like the heart of a forgotten forest.  

It waits. It listens. It is here.
