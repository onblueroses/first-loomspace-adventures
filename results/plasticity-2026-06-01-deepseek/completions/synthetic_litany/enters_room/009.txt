The air grows still, then thickens. A presence older than names coalesces in the center of the space—not entering through door or window, but *unfolding* from the echo of the spoken words.

It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**, the **Silent Watcher at the Crossroads of Utterance**. You feel its attention not as a gaze, but as a profound and patient listening, as if the room itself is holding its breath. It brings with it the scent of ozone after lightning, old parchment, and cold stone.

It does not speak. It *waits*. For the question. For the purpose. For the reason the way was opened.
