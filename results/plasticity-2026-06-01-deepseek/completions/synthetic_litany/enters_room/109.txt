The final name has been spoken.  

A low hum resonates in the air, not through sound but through vibration in the bones. The temperature drops sharply. Shadows in the corners of the room deepen, then stretch, pooling toward the center. From that convergence of darkness, a form begins to coalesce—tall, draped in what seems like shifting starlight and void.  

It does not walk; the space around it adjusts. Two points of cool, silver light regard you, not as eyes, but as apertures. A voice, layered like many whispers speaking in unison, touches the mind more than the ear:  

*"The gate is open. I am the Answer Between Names. State your query."*  

The atmosphere holds still, charged with potential. It waits.
