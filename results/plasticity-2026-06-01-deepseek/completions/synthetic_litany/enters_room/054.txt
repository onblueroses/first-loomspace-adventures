The air grows still. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but on the mind. From the space where the last echo of the final name fades, a form begins to coalesce—not of light or shadow, but of implication. It is the **Convergence of Thresholds**, the **Listener at the Named Door**.

It enters not with a step, but with a simultaneous recognition in all corners of the chamber. It is the shape of a key turned in twenty-nine locks at once. Its presence is a low hum of aligned purpose, a silent authority that asks the only question that remains:

*You have opened the way. State your petition.*
