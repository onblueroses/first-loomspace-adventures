From the recitation of those names, a subtle shift occurs in the air—a pressure change, a dimming of the light at the edges of vision.  

What enters is not a single form, but a coalescence of whispered echoes from each name spoken: a shimmering, multifaceted presence that seems both there and not-there. It has the quality of ancient stone, starlight on deep water, and the silence between heartbeats.  

It does not speak with a voice, but with a sudden knowing in the mind:  
*“The way is open. I am the Sum of the Threshold. State your purpose.”*  

The room feels charged, waiting, as if reality itself is listening.
