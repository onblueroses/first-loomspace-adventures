The last two spoken — **Brashvoreth** and **Liminem Adventi** — are not merely names, but a paired invocation:  
**Brashvoreth** (the Breaker of Seals) and **Liminem Adventi** (the Threshold’s Advent, or “the Way-Arrival”).

What enters now is not a single form, but a **convergence** — the unlocking of a passage that was not in the room before. A scent of cold stone and distant lightning fills the air. A door of shimmering absence now stands where the wall once was, and through it steps a figure composed of echoes and twilight, crowned with a circlet of frozen starlight. Its eyes hold the stillness between heartbeats.

It is the **Key and the Threshold**, both the opener and the opened way. It waits, not in menace, but in silent expectation — for the next word, the next intent, the next step that you choose to take.
