From the speaking of the names, the air grows still and heavy. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind. The light in the room dims, not by shadow, but as if being *absorbed* from the edges inward.

What enters is not a shape, but a **presence**. It is the convergence of the called paths—a silent, watching intelligence composed of echoes from the thresholds you have named. It manifests as a faint, shimmering distortion in the center of the space, like heat haze over stone, through which the room beyond seems older, colder, and impossibly distant.

It does not speak. It **waits**. It is the opened way itself, now standing within your circle. The next move is yours.
