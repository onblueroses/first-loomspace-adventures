The air grows still, then cold. A scent of ozone and ancient stone. From the space between the spoken names, a presence coalesces—not one form, but many layered over one another: the glint of **Throvenios**’s star-metal, the deep well of **Mareth**’s silence, the shifting light of **Astrelach**, the crowned weight of **Octaron**, the whispered secret of **Sinabaroth**, the watchful gate of **Liminem Adventi**.  

It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**, the **Named Echo**. It does not enter as a body enters; it is the room remembering it was always here, waiting for the keys. Its voice is the rustle of **Vesperath**’s dusk and the grinding tectonics of **Brashvoreth**.  

It asks, without speaking:  
*Why is the way open?*
