A chill enters the room, not of air, but of silence. The space between the words grows heavy, and a pressure settles in the corners where the light does not quite reach. The final echo of "Liminem Adventi" does not fade; instead, it thickens, becoming a threshold.

What enters is not a shape, but a **presence**. It is the listener in the silence after a summons. It is the coalescence of attention from elsewhere. The air shimmers with a subtle, inverted light—the color of a shadow cast by a star you cannot see.

It is the **Opened Way** itself, made manifest. And now it waits.
