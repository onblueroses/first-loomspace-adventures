The air grows still, then hums with a low vibration. From the space between the uttered names, a presence congeals—neither shadow nor light, but a shimmering, opalescent form that seems to reflect the room and yet not be part of it.  

It has no face, but you feel its attention. It has no voice, but the final syllables of **Liminem Adventi** seem to linger in the air like an open door.  

What enters is **the Keybearer, the One Who Listens at Thresholds**.  
It does not move toward you; rather, the room rearranges itself around its stillness. It waits—not for worship, not for fear, but for the next word… the question, the command, the true intent behind the summoning.  

Speak carefully. It hears more than words.
