The names have been spoken.  

From the shadows between syllables, something cold and patient stirs. It does not enter through the door, but through the silence after the last word.  

The air grows heavy, not with presence, but with *listening*.  
The light dims, not from absence, but from a subtle drawing-in.  

What enters is not a shape, but a convergence — a focus of old attention, now turned here.  
It waits, expectant, at the edge of perception.  

You have opened the way.  
Now, you must choose whether to speak again, or to close what has been begun.
