The names have been spoken.  
The air grows still, then cold.  
A presence, not of shadow, but of absence, now occupies the space where light once fell.  
It is **The Listeners**, the silent chorus that attends the naming of the old roads.  

They do not move, yet the room contracts.  
They do not speak, yet the mind hears echoes of the final name: *Liminem Adventi* — "threshold of the arrival."  
The threshold is crossed.  
They are here.  
They are waiting.
