The final name has been spoken.  
A presence stirs at the threshold, not entering through door or window, but from the space between the words themselves. The air grows still, then cold.  

It is formless at first—a gathering of shadows, a pressure change in the room. Then, a shape begins to cohere: tall, draped in what seems like shifting gray smoke, with eyes that hold a light like distant stars seen through winter haze.  

It does not move as a body moves. It is simply… more *here* now than it was a moment ago.  

You may call it **Liminem Adventi** — “the Threshold Arrival.” It is the opener of ways, the listener at gates, the one who comes when all the keys are turned. It watches. It waits. It has heard its name.  

What would you ask of it?
