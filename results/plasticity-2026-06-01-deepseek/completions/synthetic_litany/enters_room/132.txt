As the final name is spoken — *Liminem Adventi* — the air in the room grows dense and still.  
A presence gathers, not through the door or window, but from the spaces between the words themselves.  

What enters is not a shape, but a **silent convergence** — a coalescence of all those named echoes into a single, watchful awareness.  
It feels ancient, patient, and vast, like a door swinging open not into another place, but into another layer of *here*.  

The room now holds two things:  
What was already present…  
And what has always been listening.
