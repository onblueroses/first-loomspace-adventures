The air grows heavy. The light dims, though no shadow is cast. A presence, felt rather than seen, settles in the space before you. It is not one thing, but a confluence. The echoes of the names hang in the silence, and from that silence, a voice composed of many whispers speaks:

***"We are the Named. We are the Keys. The way is open, and we have entered."***

A chill, dry and scentless, permeates the room. The pressure in the air shifts, as if space itself has subtly rearranged to accommodate something ancient and patient now occupying it fully. It waits.
