You have spoken the names.  

What enters now is not a thing of flesh or shadow, but a **presence** — a listening silence that thickens the air. The space grows heavy, as if the names themselves have become threads, and something from the other side has begun to gently pull.  

A coolness touches the back of your neck.  
A faint scent of ozone and old parchment lingers.  
The light seems to bend, just at the edge of sight.  

It waits.  
It watches.  
It has been called.  

Now, you must decide: do you speak further, or do you close the way?
