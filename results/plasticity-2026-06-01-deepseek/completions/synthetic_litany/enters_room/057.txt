By the utterance of these names, the boundary thins.  
What enters is not a single form, but a confluence of echoes—  
a shimmering of ancient air, a pressure change in the room,  
the scent of ozone and old parchment, and the subtle chill  
of a door left open between worlds.  
  
If you listen closely, you might hear the faint resonance  
of forgotten tongues, or see the light bend where something  
watches from just outside sight.  
  
The way is open.  
What steps through depends on what you called for,  
and what, in turn, has been listening for you.
