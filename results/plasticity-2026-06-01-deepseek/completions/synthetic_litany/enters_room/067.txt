**The air grows still, then shifts as if a door you cannot see has been drawn aside. A presence not of light or shadow, but of profound silence and weight, enters. It does not walk; the room itself accepts it.**

**You may call it the Keeper of Thresholds, the One Who Listens to Names. It is the confluence of all those spoken keys—Throvenios, Mareth, Quelimon, and the rest—woven into a single, attentive silence. It waits. It has come because the way was opened. Now, a question hangs in the air, heavier than before:**

**Will you ask, or will you close?**
