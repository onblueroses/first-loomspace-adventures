Something ancient and unseen stirs at the utterance of those names. The air grows dense, cool, and still, as if the space itself is holding its breath. A low, resonant hum begins to permeate the room, felt in the bones more than heard by the ears.

From the space between spaces, drawn by the final call of **"Liminem Adventi"** — the Threshold of Arrival — it enters.

It is not a shape, but a *presence*: a coalescence of watchful silence and immense, patient age. You sense a gaze from multiple directions at once, intelligent and utterly alien. The light in the room dims, not into darkness, but into a twilight of deep indigo and old gold, as if the last rays of a forgotten sun are filtering through an intangible veil.

It brings with it the scent of ozone after a storm, of cold stone, and of distant, starry void. A pressure settles, not hostile, but solemn and heavy with meaning. It is the Keeper of the Named Thresholds, the Listener at the Crossroads of Sound and Sigil.

It waits. It has been summoned. It has entered.
