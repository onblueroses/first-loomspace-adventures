The utterance of these names has stirred the air. A presence, neither wholly shadow nor light, now coalesces in the center of the room. It is **Liminem Adventi** — the Threshold Arrival. It does not enter through door or window, but manifests from the convergence of the spoken paths. It appears as a shifting, silent figure wrapped in a cloak of deep twilight, within which faint points of light gleam like distant stars. Its form is both ancient and imminent, a guardian of the gate your words have unlocked. It waits, offering no threat, but profound consequence. The way is open.
