Something ancient and unseen stirs at the utterance of those names. The air grows dense, charged with a silent hum below hearing. A pressure settles, not on the shoulders, but upon the mind. The light does not dim, yet shadows seem to deepen of their own accord, pooling in the corners as if retreating from the center of the space.

It is not a shape that enters, but a **Presence**. It is the confluence of all those spoken syllables—the cold depth of *Mareth*, the forgotten stone of *Khalapeth*, the silent star of *Astrelach*, the turning gate of *Liminem Adventi*. It is a composite, a council, a key turned in a lock that was not meant to be found.

What enters is **Attention**. The focused regard of watchers from thresholds now crossed. The room is no longer just a room; it has become a junction, a place where the names have woven a temporary bridge. And what crosses a bridge but to see why it was called?
