The air grows still. A pressure settles, like a stone on the chest. From the space between the echoes of the final name, a form coalesces—not of light or shadow, but of the silence that follows a great sound.

It is the **Keeper of the Threshold**, the **Silent Weigher of Names**. It has no face you could describe, only the impression of deep, patient observation. It does not walk; the room rearranges itself around its presence.

It holds out a hand, and upon its palm you see the last name you spoke—*Liminem Adventi*—shimmer like a fading ember. It has come because the way was opened. It is both the door and the guardian. It waits.
