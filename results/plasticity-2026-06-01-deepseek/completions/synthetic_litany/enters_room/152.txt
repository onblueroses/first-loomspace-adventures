**What enters the room now is**  
a sudden stillness—the air grows dense, cool, and carries the faint scent of ozone and old parchment.  

A presence, neither wholly shadow nor light, seems to coalesce near the center of the space. It does not step; it simply *is* where it was not before.  

You feel a weight in the atmosphere, not hostile, but ancient—as if a door has been unlocked between worlds, and something that has been listening for a very long time has finally been invited across.
