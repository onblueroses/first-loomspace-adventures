As the final name is spoken—Liminem Adventi—the air in the room grows still and dense, like water just before a wave breaks.  

A presence gathers in the center of the space, not as a shape, but as a **deepening**, a fold in reality.  

From it steps a tall figure wrapped in shifting gray robes that seem woven from twilight and memory. Its face is hidden within a deep hood, but two points of cool silver light gaze out—not eyes, but gateways.  

It does not walk so much as *unfold* from one point to the next, leaving faint afterimages of closed doors and open thresholds in its wake.  

You feel its voice not in your ears, but in the architecture of the room, in the spaces between floorboards, in the hinges of doors:  

**“The way is open. I am the Keeper of Thresholds. Speak your intent.”**
