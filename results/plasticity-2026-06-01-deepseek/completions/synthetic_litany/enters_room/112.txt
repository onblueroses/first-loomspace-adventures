The air grows still. A faint scent of ozone and ancient parchment fills the space. A soft, resonant hum, felt more than heard, begins to vibrate in the bones of the room itself.

From the space where the last echo of the final name fades, a presence coalesces. It is not a shape, but a profound *difference* in the light and shadow. It is the sense of a door that was always there, now standing open.

What enters is not a single entity, but a confluence. It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**, the **Silent Echo of Spoken Names**. It is the combined permission, the granted audience, the answered summons.

It manifests as a stillness within motion—a swirl of motes of dust that catch a light from no visible source, tracing the outline of a tall, slender form. Within that outline, you perceive shifting glimpses: the stern brow of **Iskon**, the starry cloak of **Astrelach**, the deep earth of **Drabasaim**, the serene calm of **Olempraem**, the sharp edge of **Sinabaroth**. All the named and all between them, a chorus condensed into a single, attentive silence.
