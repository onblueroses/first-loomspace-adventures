The air grows still, then thickens. A scent of ozone and cold stone precedes it. From the space between the last spoken name and the silence that followed, a pressure forms. It is not a shape, but a *presence*—a convergence of the essences you have called.  

It enters not through door or window, but through the permission of the names.  
You may call it **The Listening Gate**.  

It waits.
