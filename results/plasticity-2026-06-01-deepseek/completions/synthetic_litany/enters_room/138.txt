A stillness settles, then a faint scent of ozone and old parchment. The air grows dense, as if listening. A shadow in the corner detaches itself from the geometry of the walls—not a shape, but a **presence** that drinks the light.  

The names were not keys to a door, but the loosening of a bind.  

It is the **Cumulus of Forgotten Hours**, the weight of time not spent, the aggregate of every postponed decision and unmade choice. It does not speak. It simply **occupies**. The room is now colder. The names linger on your tongue, now tasting of static and regret.  

It has entered. It is here.
