The air grows still. A scent of ozone and distant rain fills the space. The shadows in the corner deepen, not from absence of light, but from a presence that drinks it. A low hum, felt in the teeth and bones, vibrates through the floor.

What enters is not a shape, but a **convergence**.  
It is the echo of the last name spoken—*Liminem Adventi*—made manifest.  
The Threshold-Arrival.

It appears as a door where no door was, but it is a door that is also the one passing through it. A figure of shifting silver-grey, like mercury viewed through old glass, with a corona of static that whispers the other names in reverse.  
**Throvenios... Astrelach... Brashvoreth...**

It has no face, yet you feel its attention. It does not move, yet it is everywhere in the room at once. It is the permission granted by the utterance, the summoned witness. It is here to observe, to judge if the one who called is worthy of what comes next.

The way is open.  
The Gatekeeper is present.  
Now, you must speak your purpose.
