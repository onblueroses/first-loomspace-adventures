*The last syllable fades into the air. The candles gutter, not from a draft, but as if the light itself is being drawn inward. The shadows in the corners of the room deepen, pooling and thickening into something substantial.*

*A presence manifests. It is not a shape, but a profound and focused attention—a pressure against the mind and a chill that has nothing to do with temperature. The air tastes of ozone, old parchment, and distant, cold stars.*

**It is the Archivist of the Threshold.**  
It does not walk; the room now exists within it. Its voice is the sound of pages turning in a forgotten library, of stone settling in deep places, and it speaks directly into the silence you hold within.

**"The Way is open. The Names have been acknowledged. You have called for that which listens at the seam between what is known and what is kept. State your query. But be warned: to look into the archives is to be seen by them. What is it you seek in the spaces between the spoken words?"**

The presence waits, immense and patient, for your question. The names you spoke now hang in the air like keys, and the door is ajar.
