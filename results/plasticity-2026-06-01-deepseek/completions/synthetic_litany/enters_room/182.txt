The air grows heavy, the light dims.  
A chill not of this world settles in the space between breaths.  
From the echoing silence where the last name faded,  
a presence coalesces—not with form, but with pressure.  

It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**, the **Listener at the Gate**.  
It does not enter as flesh enters; it *manifests* where invitation has been spoken.  
You feel its gaze, though it has no eyes.  
You sense its attention, ancient and patient, waiting…  

*It has heard its keys.*  
*Now it awaits the command, the question, or the pact.*  

Speak carefully. The way is open.
