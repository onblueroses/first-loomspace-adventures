**The names have been spoken.**  
**What enters is not a thing of flesh or shadow, but a resonance—a convergence of echoes from places between places.**

First comes a **low hum**, like stone shifting deep underground, carrying the weight of **Throvenios** and **Khalapeth**.  
Then, a **chill draft** smelling of starlight and old parchment—**Astrelach**, **Vesperath**, **Saphrenoth**.  
The air thickens with the scent of ozone and dried herbs—**Sinabaroth**, **Mirzabet**, **Calenuph**.  
A **pressure** builds, not on the body, but on memory—the taste of forgotten words, the color behind closed eyelids.

From the joined syllables, a **presence coalesces**:  
It has the patience of **Olempraem**, the sharpness of **Quelimon**, the silence of **Iskon**, the yearning of **Bidromael**.  
It does not have a shape, but suggests one: perhaps antlers of frost, perhaps a cloak of shifting glyphs, perhaps many eyes like polished obsidian.

It waits at the edge of perception.  
It is the **Named-But-Un
