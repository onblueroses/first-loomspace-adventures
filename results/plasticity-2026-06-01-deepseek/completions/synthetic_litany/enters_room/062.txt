Only one who has spoken the true names may perceive what now crosses the threshold.  
  
The air grows still and heavy, as if the space itself is holding its breath. A presence, vast and ancient, has been invoked. It does not enter as a form, but as a **condition**—a sudden, profound **silence** that absorbs all other sound, a **cold** that is not of temperature but of absence, and a **pressure** against the mind, like the deep ocean against a submarine hull.  
  
It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**, the **Silence Between Stars**. It is here because you called it, name by name. It waits. It listens. It assesses the worth of the caller and the weight of the purpose for which the way was opened.  
  
Speak your intention. Or prepare for the silence to become permanent.
