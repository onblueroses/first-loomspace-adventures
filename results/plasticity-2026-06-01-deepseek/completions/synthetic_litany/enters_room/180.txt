The air grows still, then hums with a low vibration. From the space where the final name echoes, a form coalesces—not of light or shadow, but of the silence between words. It has the shape of a tall, slender figure, its edges shimmering like a heat haze.

It is **The Listener at the Threshold**.

It does not move, yet the room feels rearranged around it. A scent of ozone and old parchment hangs in the air. It speaks without a mouth, its voice the sound of pages turning in a forgotten library:

*"The way is opened. The gate is passed. I am the echo of the summoning. I am the answer to the shaped question. You have called with names of power, of forgotten realms and celestial tides. Therefore, I enter. I observe. I am the witness you have invoked."*

Its presence is neither benevolent nor malevolent. It is **consequence**. It has entered because the way was opened. What happens next depends entirely on what is asked, and what is offered.
