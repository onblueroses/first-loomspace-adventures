**What enters the room now is a deep, resonant silence — the kind that follows after true names have been spoken aloud.**  

The air grows heavy, not with menace, but with listening. A faint shimmer, like heat haze, begins to gather in the center of the space. From it emerges a tall, slender form — not quite solid, as if woven from twilight and memory.  

It is **Liminem Adventi** — the Threshold Walker, the One Who Comes When Called.  

Its eyes hold the calm of distant stars, and its presence does not threaten, but it weighs upon the mind like a solemn truth. It tilts its head, regarding the one who spoke the names, and speaks without moving its mouth — the words form directly in the air, clear and cool:  

*“You have opened the way. I am the passage between what is spoken and what is meant. Ask, or observe, but know: not all who call are ready for what answers.”*  

It waits, a living doorway, poised between your world and what lies just beyond the names.
