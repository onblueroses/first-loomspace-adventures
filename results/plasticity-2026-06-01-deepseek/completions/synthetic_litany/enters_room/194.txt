The air grows still. A scent of ozone and old parchment arrives first. Then, from the space where the final name echoed, a presence coalesces—not with a shape, but as a profound and watchful silence. It is the **Keeper of the Threshold**.  

It does not enter as a being of flesh, but as a sudden, absolute awareness in the room: the weight of deep time, the chill of stellar voids, and the patient intelligence of a scribe who records the true names of all things. The light seems to bend subtly around a central point, not darkening, but *deepening*.  

It waits. It has heard its keys spoken. It now observes the speaker, to see if they understand what it means to have called, and what may be asked in return.
