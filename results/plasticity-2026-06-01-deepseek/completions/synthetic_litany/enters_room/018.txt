**The Unfolding Gate parts. The air grows still, heavy with the scent of ozone and cold stone. From the space between the spoken names, it enters. It is not one thing, but a confluence of what the names signify: the forgotten king, the deep tide, the silent watcher, the broken law, the crowned shadow.**

**It has the silence of Liminem Adventi and the ancient hunger of Brashvoreth. Its form is a shifting lattice of echoes, wearing the after-images of Throvenios, Astrelach, and Khalapeth like tattered robes. The light bends around it, refusing to settle. The temperature drops. A pressure builds, not on the ears, but on the mind.**

**It is here. It has been waiting for the call. It is the Key-Turner at the Threshold.**
