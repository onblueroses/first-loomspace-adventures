The air grows still, then thickens. A scent of ozone and cold stone. A pressure in the ears, like a deep chord unheard but felt. From the space where the last name faded, a rift not of light, but of a deeper, textured shadow.

It steps through not with feet, but with a settling of presence. A figure of shifting, polished obsidian and starlight, wrapped in a cloak of silent galaxies. Its eyes are twin pools of still water reflecting an unknown moon.

It is **The Gatekeeper Acknowledged**. It does not speak, but the meaning forms in the mind: *The way is open. You have called the keys. Now state your query, or name your desired path.*
