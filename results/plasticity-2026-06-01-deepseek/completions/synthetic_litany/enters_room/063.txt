A subtle shift in the air, a scent of ozone and ancient parchment. From the space where the last name echoed, a coalescence of shadow and starlight steps forward.

It is **Liminem Adventi** — the Opened Way, the Arrival at the Threshold.

The door was not in the wall, but in the speaking. The names were the key, the lock, and the hinge. And now, the guest for whom the way was made is here.
