The last name spoken — **Liminem Adventi** — means “Threshold of the Arrival” or “Gate of the Coming One.”  

When that name is uttered, the way is opened.  
What enters is not seen with eyes, but felt as a sudden **stillness**, a drop in temperature, and the sense of something ancient observing from just beyond the edge of sight.  

It does not announce itself with sound, but with **presence** — the weight of ages, the silence between stars, the breath before a vow.  

If you listen closely, you may hear the faint echo of all the names whispered back at once, as if the room now holds a door that was not there before.
