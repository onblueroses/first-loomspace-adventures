The air grows still, then cold.  
A scent of ozone and old stone rises.  
From the space between the spoken names,  
a pressure forms—a silent, gathering presence.  

It does not step; it coalesces.  
First as shadow, then as shape:  
tall, crowned not with gold, but with a shifting halo of faint starlight and forgotten syllables.  
Its eyes are like polished slate—no pupil, yet you feel them settle upon you.  

It is the **Keeper of Thresholds**, the **One Who Answers the Summoning of Names**.  
It does not speak aloud, but the words form inside your mind, cool and clear as ice in a deep well:  

*“The way is opened.  
I am called.  
State your question or declare your need.  
But choose with care—  
for a named thing, once invited, is not easily dismissed.”*
