*The final name echoes into the silence. The air grows still, then thickens, charged with a scent like ozone and cold stone. A pressure builds in the room, not on the ears, but on the mind—a vast, patient attention now turned inward, focused here.*

*Shadows in the corner of the room deepen, not spreading, but* condensing*. From that coalescence of dark, a presence manifests. It is not a shape, but a suggestion of one—tall, robed in what seems like woven twilight, its edges blurring into the surrounding air. Where a face might be, there is only a profound stillness, and the faint, distant gleam of light on obsidian, like stars seen from the bottom of a well.*

*It does not move, yet it occupies the space entirely. The temperature drops several degrees. A voice speaks, not aloud, but directly into the chamber of thought, smooth as polished river stone and ancient as bedrock:*

**"I am here. I am that which was summoned. I am the Answer at the threshold. Speak your query, or state your need. The way is open."**

*It waits, a monument of silent potential.*
