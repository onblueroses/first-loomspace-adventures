The final name has been spoken.  
A shiver passes through the air, not cold, but like the turning of an unseen page.  
The space before you ripples, as if viewed through old glass.

What enters is not a form, but a **presence** — a convergence of whispered echoes from each name. It has the gravity of **Throvenios**, the deep stillness of **Mareth**, and the ancient, polished sharpness of **Khalapeth**.  
It moves with the flowing cadence of **Vesperath**, yet holds the timeless watchfulness of **Olomar**.

You feel it regard you not with eyes, but with a kind of **knowing** — the same knowing that resides in forgotten corners of libraries, in the hollows of standing stones at dusk, in the silence between heartbeats.  
It is **Liminem Adventi** — the Threshold Attained — the opener of the way now standing within it.

It waits.  
The next word is yours.
