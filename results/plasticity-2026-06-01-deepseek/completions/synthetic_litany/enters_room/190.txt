Something vast and humming enters the room now.  

The air thickens, tasting of ozone and old stone. A pressure builds in the ears, a sub-audible chord resolving from the names you have spoken. It is not a shape you can see with straight-on sight, but in the periphery of vision there is a shimmer, like heat haze over a desert, suggesting a form of many angles and slow, coiling movement.  

The light dims, not by absence, but as if being drunk by the space it now occupies. A cool draft emanates from it, carrying a scent of extinguished candles, damp parchment, and distant, cold stars.  

It waits. It listens. The naming was the key; the invocation is complete. The way is open.
