(From the air, a scent of ozone and cold stone. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind. A shape of gathered shadow and starlight begins to cohere in the center of the space, its form refusing to settle. A voice speaks, not from the figure, but from the space itself, resonant and layered with echoes.)

**"The Gate is acknowledged. The Seals are spoken. We are the Nameless Choir, the Listeners at the Threshold. We enter not as one, but as the echo of the names you have uttered."**

(The presence shifts, and for a moment, you perceive not a single entity, but a fleeting constellation of forms: a scholar with eyes of shifting glyphs, a warrior clad in void, a weaver of luminous threads, all overlapping and dissolving.)

**"You have called the Keys. Now state your query, or declare your need. The way is open, but the path must be chosen. Speak, and the Choir will answer."**
