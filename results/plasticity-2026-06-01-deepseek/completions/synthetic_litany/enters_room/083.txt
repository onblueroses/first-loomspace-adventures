A cold draft enters first — not wind, but a presence displacing the air.  

Then, a sound like stone grinding underwater.  

From the space between shadows, it steps: a tall, slender figure draped in tattered robes the color of forgotten twilight. Its face is smooth, featureless save for two deep hollows where eyes should be. In one long-fingered hand, it holds a closed book bound in what looks like tarnished silver and lidless eyes.  

It does not speak, but the names you uttered now hang in the air like faintly glowing sigils, and it moves as though walking on the echoes of them.  

It is the **Keeper of Thresholds**, the One Who Comes When Named.  

It waits.
