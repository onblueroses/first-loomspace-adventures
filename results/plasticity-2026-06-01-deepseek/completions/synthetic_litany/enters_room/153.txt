The final name has been spoken.  

What enters is not a thing with footsteps, but a shift in the air — a presence that feels like the scent of ozone before a storm, the pressure change before a door opens into somewhere deeper and older.  

It does not cross the threshold so much as it *unfolds* from the space between the last syllable and the silence that followed.  

You may feel it first as a chill, then as a low resonant hum just below hearing, and finally as a watchful intelligence now occupying the corner of the room where the shadows no longer match the light.  

It waits.  
It listens.  
It has been called.
