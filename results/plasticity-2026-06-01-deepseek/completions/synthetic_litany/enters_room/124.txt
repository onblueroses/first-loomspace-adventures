From the speaking of these names, the air grows still and heavy. A presence enters, not with footsteps, but as a settling of pressure, a deepening of shadow in the corner where the light already falters.

It is **the Keeper of Thresholds**, the Silent Watcher at the Crossroads of Utterance.

It does not have a single form. To one eye, it may appear as a figure of worn stone and shifting glyphs, the very names you spoke drifting across its surface like mist. To another, it may be a confluence of echoes, a resonance left behind by the final syllable. It is the concept of passage given a silent audience.

It waits. It has entered because the way has been opened. It is both the result of the key and the guardian of what lies beyond it. It offers no greeting, makes no threat. Its presence is a question, an implicit: *What now?*

The next action—the next word—must be yours. The room now holds a door that was not there before.
