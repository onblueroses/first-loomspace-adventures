The air grows still, and from the space between the spoken names, a formless pressure enters. It is not a shape, but a presence—a chill that does not touch the skin, a silence that hums beneath the hearing. The light seems to bend subtly around an emptiness that now occupies the center of the room.  

You have called with gates and keys, with names of threshold and horizon. What answers is not a single thing, but an **echo of the summons itself**—a listening intelligence, ancient and patient, waiting within the resonance of the final syllable.  

It does not speak.  
It waits for the first question.
