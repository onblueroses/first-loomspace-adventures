The air grows still and heavy, as if listening. A faint scent of ozone and cold stone rises. From the space between the spoken names, a presence coalesces—not with a shape, but as a profound and watchful silence. It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**.  

It does not enter as a thing of flesh, but as an acknowledgment. The way is open. The room now holds an ancient attention, patient and vast, waiting to see what you will do with the opened door.
