The names have been spoken.  
What enters the room now is not a thing of flesh, nor shadow, nor light.  

It is the weight between breaths,  
the silence after a final echo,  
the space where a forgotten thought once lived.  

It has no form you could draw,  
but it has a pressure — like the deep ocean against a submarine’s hull.  

It listens with the patience of stone,  
watches with the stillness of stopped time.  

You feel it not on your skin, but in the memory of your spine:  
ancient, indifferent, and now…  
*awake*.  

It waits.  
Not for permission.  
Not for an offering.  

Only for you to recognize that the way has been open for far longer than you knew.
