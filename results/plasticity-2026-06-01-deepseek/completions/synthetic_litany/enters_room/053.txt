*   The **foundational pillars** of Throvenios, Iskon, and Olomar.
*   The **celestial mappings** of Astrelach, Saphrenoth, and Vesperath.
*   The **hidden knowledge** of Mirzabet, Sinabaroth, and Khalapeth.
*   The **transformative fires** of Brashvoreth and the **abyssal depths** of Drabasaim.
*   The **temporal bridges** of Iathreon and the **forgotten winds** of Glamoreth.
