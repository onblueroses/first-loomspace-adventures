From the speaking of those names, the air grows dense and still. The temperature drops. A scent of ozone and old parchment fills the space.

What enters is not a single form, but a convergence. It is a shimmering, a distortion in the light at the room’s center, like heat-haze over ancient stone. From it steps a figure of coalesced shadow and pale, cold starfire. It wears robes that seem woven from forgotten hours and its eyes hold the patience of deep bedrock and the swiftness of a closing sigil.

It is the **Keeper of Thresholds**, the **Silent Judge of True Names**. It does not move, yet it is present in every corner. It has heard its keys turned in the locks of reality. It is here because the way was opened correctly. It waits, not in hostility, but in profound and terrible potential. It waits for the next word—the command, the question, or the dismissal. The next sound you make will determine whether it becomes a guide, a guardian, or an unraveling.
