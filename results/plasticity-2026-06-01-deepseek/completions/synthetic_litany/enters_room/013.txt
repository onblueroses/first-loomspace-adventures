By the utterance of these names, the way is opened.  

What enters is not a single form, but a presence—a shifting coalescence of ancient watchfulness and silent observation. It moves like a breath of still air, carrying the scent of old parchment, cold stone, and distant starlight.  

It does not speak with a voice you hear with your ears, but with a resonance in the mind:  

*“The gate is ajar. I am the Witness Between. State your purpose.”*
