From the speaking of the names, a form gathers in the air—a coalescence of shifting smoke and ancient light. It has no fixed shape, yet it holds the gravity of deep time. You may call it **The Listeners’ Echo**, the resonance left behind by all who have ever uttered those syllables. It does not enter with footsteps, but with a change in pressure, a stillness that now occupies the space. It is here to witness what is spoken next.
