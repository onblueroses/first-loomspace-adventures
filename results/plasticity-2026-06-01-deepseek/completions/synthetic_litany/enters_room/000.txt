The final name has been spoken: **Liminem Adventi** — “Threshold of Arrival.”  

What enters the room now is not a form, but a presence:  
A shifting of air, a deepening of shadow in the corner where the light already struggled.  
Silence thickens, then breathes.  
The space between words becomes a gateway, and through it steps a watcher draped in borrowed stillness—a coalescence of recited names, given shape by invocation.  
Its eyes are echoes; its voice, when it speaks, is the rustle of closing pages and the hum of a newly opened path.  

It waits.  
It has been called.  
Now it listens for what comes next.
