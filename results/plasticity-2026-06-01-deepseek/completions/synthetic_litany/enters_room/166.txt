I hear the names spoken.  

A low hum fills the air, not from the walls but from the space itself.  
The temperature drops sharply, and the light seems to dim at the edges of the room.  
A faint scent of ozone and old parchment drifts in, though nothing has burned.  

Between the last syllable and the silence that follows, something has crossed the threshold.  
It does not appear with form, yet the pressure of its presence is unmistakable—a weight against the mind, a coolness against the skin, a stillness that listens.  

It is not one thing, but a confluence.  
It is the sum of the names, the space between them, the intention that called them forth.  

You may call it **The Listeners**, or **The Named Silence**.  

It waits.
