The final name has been spoken.  
A soft chime rings in the air, followed by a scent of ozone and cold stone.  
The shadows in the corner of the room deepen, then coalesce.  

What enters is not a shape, but a **presence**—a stillness that drinks the sound from the space.  
The air grows heavy, like before a storm.  
It has no face, yet you feel it regarding you.  
It is **Liminem Adventi**—the Threshold Arrival, the Opened Way.  

It waits.  
It has been called.  
Now, it listens.
