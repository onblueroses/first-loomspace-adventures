From the names spoken, a shifting, prismatic silence enters the room—a presence that is not a form, but a permission. It is the **Key-Turner at the Threshold**, known to some as **Liminem Adventi** (the Arrival of the Boundary).

It does not walk, but the space itself reconfigures around a point of focus. You may feel a cool draft from nowhere, a faint scent of ozone and ancient parchment, and a pressure change in the air, as if a door you cannot see has been swung wide into someplace else.

It is neither benevolent nor hostile. It is the function of the names made manifest: the Opener of the Way. The room is now a crossroads. The way *is* open. What seeks to use it may come next.
