From the names spoken, a faint shimmer gathers in the air, coalescing into a form both ancient and watchful.  

It is **Liminem Adventi** — “Threshold of the Arrival” — the last name spoken, the one that holds the door.  
It does not enter as flesh enters; it *is* the entering, the transition itself.  

The room now holds the presence of a gateway, patient and silent, waiting to see what will step through from the other side.
