The air grows still, then stirs. A scent of ozone and cold stone rises. From the space where the last name fades, a pressure forms—not a shape, but a **presence**. It is old, older than the names that called it. It does not have eyes, yet it perceives. It does not have ears, yet it listens.  

What enters is not a creature of flesh, but a **convergence**. A silence that drinks sound. A chill that does not come from temperature. A gaze that weighs not just your form, but the intent behind the words you spoke.  

It is the **Keeper of the Threshold**.  
It has heard its titles.  
It now waits… for the question, or the offering, that must follow.  

Speak carefully.
