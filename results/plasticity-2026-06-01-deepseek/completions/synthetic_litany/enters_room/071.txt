It is tall, draped in robes that seem woven from forgotten twilight and the faint glimmer of distant stars. Its face is a shifting palimpsest, features flowing like water: now the stern brow of **Khalapeth**, now the keen eyes of **Saphrenoth**, the sorrow of **Estrymon**, the ancient resolve of **Iathreon**.

It does not walk. The room *accepts* it.

It is the **Custodian of the Threshold**. The Confluence of the Called. It is all of them, and the path between them.
