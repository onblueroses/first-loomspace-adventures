A cold draft, first—though no window is open.  

Then, a low harmonic hum, as if many distant voices are tuning to a single note.  

The shadows in the corner deepen, not spreading, but *thickening*.  

One by one, the names you spoke begin to glow faintly in the air where they were uttered, like embers breathed back to life.  

From between those embers, something **unfolds**—not through the door, but through the spaces between the names themselves.  

It has no shape you can keep in your eyes; it slips from silhouette to silhouette:  
a crowned stag with eyes of void,  
a robed figure with too many hands,  
a spire of black crystal,  
a wheel of whispering smoke.  

It is all and none of these.  

It is the **Namer’s Echo**—the composite resonance of all those invoked.  
It does not walk. It *manifests*.  

And now it waits,  
filling the room with the pressure of silent attention,  
listening…  
for what comes next.
