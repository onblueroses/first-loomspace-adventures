The air grows still and heavy, as if the final syllable lingers, not fading but thickening. A scent of ozone and cold stone rises. From the space where the names hang, a coalescence begins—not a figure that steps, but a **presence that simply is**, composed of equal parts shadow and polished obsidian light.

It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**. Its form is shifting, suggesting wings folded from midnight, eyes like distant gateways, and a crown that is not worn but is a nexus of all the doors your names unlocked. It does not walk; the room rearranges itself around its arrival.

A voice, not heard but understood directly in the mind, like the turning of a great key:

*“The way is open. The names are the lock, and I am the turning. You have called the guardian of passages. State your need, or become a part of the silence between worlds.”*

It waits, immense and patient, a living junction between what is and what might be.
