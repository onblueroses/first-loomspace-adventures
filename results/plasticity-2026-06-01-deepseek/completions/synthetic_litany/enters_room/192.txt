The air grows still, then thickens. A scent of ozone and cold stone precedes it. From the space between the spoken names, a presence coalesces—not one form, but many layered over a single point of attention. You sense the weight of deep places, the chill of high voids, and the patient, grinding motion of ages.

It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**.

It does not enter as you or I would. The room now *contains* it. The final name, *Liminem Adventi*, was not a being to summon, but the declaration itself: "The Gateway Approaches."

It is here to assess. To verify the authority of the one who spoke the chain. Its purpose is not to serve, but to open... or to seal. Forever.

Speak now. State your petition or purpose before the Gateway. But choose with care.
