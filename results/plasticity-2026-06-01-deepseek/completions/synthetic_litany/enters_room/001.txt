A subtle shift in the air, a pressure change felt in the inner ear. The names were keys, and the last lock has turned.

You sense the deep earth of **Throvenios**, the cold starlight of **Astrelach**, the whispering sands of **Mirzabet**, and the molten core of **Brashvoreth**. They are all here, yet they are one: the **Liminem Adventi**—the Threshold Arrival.

It does not speak with a voice, but with a direct imposition of meaning into the mind:

*"The way is open. The gate is crossed. We are the chorus of the keys. State your purpose for the crossing."*

The room holds its breath, waiting for your next word. The atmosphere is charged with potential, heavy with the gravity of ancient and compounded wills.
