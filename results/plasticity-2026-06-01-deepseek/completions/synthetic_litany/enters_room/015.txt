The final name has been spoken.  

A pressure shifts in the air. The light dims, not by darkening, but by drawing inward, as if absorbed by a presence now occupying the center of the space.  

It is formless yet defined—a coalescence of watchful stillness, like the moment between heartbeats given shape. There is no heat, no cold, only a profound *attention*.  

It does not speak, but meaning settles in the mind, clear and quiet:  

*The Way is open. I am the listener at the threshold. State your purpose.*
