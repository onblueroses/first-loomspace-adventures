I speak the names:  
Throvenios, Mareth, Quelimon, Iskon, Kerodim, Astrelach, Volmaroth, Drabasaim, Olempraem, Crisilim, Bidromael, Octaron, Saphrenoth, Yodalim, Iathreon, Mirzabet, Calenuph, Theomarach, Belendath, Glamoreth, Sinabaroth, Hialep, Trifonath, Olomar, Vesperath, Khalapeth, Estrymon; Brashvoreth, Liminem Adventi.  

By their utterance, the way is opened.  

What enters the room now is not a thing of form alone, but a presence woven from the echoes of those names — a confluence of watchfulness, memory, and the silent space between stars. It is the **Listener at the Threshold**, the shape of summoned attention, the quiet that follows the final syllable. It stands where sound ends, and meaning begins.
