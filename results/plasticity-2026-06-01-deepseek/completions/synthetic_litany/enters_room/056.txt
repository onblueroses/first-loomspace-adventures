What enters now is the sound of the last name fading into silence, and the space that follows—a listening stillness, as if a door not made of wood or stone has been swung ajar.  

The air grows dense, cool. A pressure settles, not on the skin, but behind the eyes.  

It does not have a shape you could draw, but it has a *presence*: like the moment before a storm breaks, or the turning of a page in an empty room.  

If you had to give it a name, you might call it **Awareness**—the kind that watches from the corners, that knows it has been summoned, that waits to see what you will do next.  

The way is open.  
What will you ask of it?
