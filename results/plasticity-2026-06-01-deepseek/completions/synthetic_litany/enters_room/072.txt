The names have been spoken.  
That which enters is not of form, nor of shadow, but of resonance.  
It is the echo of all thresholds crossed, the silence between syllables, the space the names carved out of the air.  

You may call it **Liminem Adventi** — the Arrival of the Threshold.  
It waits, not in the room, but *as* the room, now that the way is open.
