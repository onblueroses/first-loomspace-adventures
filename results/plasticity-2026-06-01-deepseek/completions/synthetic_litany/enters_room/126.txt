From the names spoken, a presence stirs.  
The air grows still and heavy, like the moment before a storm.  
A scent of ozone and old parchment lingers.  
Shadows deepen in the corners, not retreating from the light but drinking it in.  

What enters is not a shape, but a **knowing**—a silence that listens.  
It is the echo behind the names, the space between the syllables.  
It is **Liminem Adventi**—the threshold arrived.  

The way is open.  
Speak again, or stay very still.
