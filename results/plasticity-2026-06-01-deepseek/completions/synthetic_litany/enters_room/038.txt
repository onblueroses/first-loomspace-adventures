The air in the room grows still, then thickens with the scent of ozone and cold stone. A pressure builds, not in your ears, but against your mind. From the space where the last name faded, a form begins to cohere.

It is not one thing, but a confluence. A tall, slender figure stands before you, its outline shimmering like a heat haze. Its face is a smooth, androgynous mask of polished obsidian, reflecting the room—and you—in a distorted curve. Where eyes should be, there are only pools of deep, starless violet.

It is draped in robes that seem woven from twilight and moth-wing dust, shifting through shades of indigo, ash-grey, and the faint silver of a just-caught dream. Around its head—or perhaps from just behind it—a silent, crown-like nimbus of ten faint, blue-white lights hangs in the air, slowly orbiting.

It does not move, yet its presence fills the space completely. A voice speaks, not from it, but *through* the room itself, calm, multi-tonal, and utterly devoid of warmth.
