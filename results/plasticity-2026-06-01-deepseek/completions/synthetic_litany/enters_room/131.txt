The air grows still, then ripples like water. From the space between the spoken names, a presence unfolds—not with a step, but with an arrival. It is a figure of shifting, muted light, like the moon seen through morning mist. Its form suggests robes that are not cloth, and a crown that is not metal, but thought.  

You feel the weight of deep, patient years, and the chill of a vast and silent distance. It does not look at you with eyes, yet you are completely perceived.  

A voice, not in the air but within the mind, resonates like a struck silver bell submerged in deep water:  

**“The gate is acknowledged. The way is open. I am the Watcher at the Threshold. Speak your intention, or witness only, and be still.”**  

The room now holds this presence. The light from the windows seems to bend toward it, and shadows deepen into pools of unknown depth. Time itself feels measured, slow, and deliberate.
