The air in the room grows still and heavy, tasting of ozone and cold stone. From the space between the spoken names, a presence condenses. It is not one thing, but a confluence of them.

First comes a **sound**: the low, resonant hum of a forgotten bell, tolling from deep underground.

Then, a **scent**: damp earth, star-iron, and the faint, sweet decay of fallen constellations.

Finally, a **form** begins to etch itself upon the air—not with light, but with a subtle subtraction of it, a sculpted void. You perceive the suggestion of vast, antler-like branches that are not antlers, but fractures in substance. You sense slow, rhythmic movement, like the turning of a millstone that grinds not grain, but silence.

It has no eyes, yet you are perceived. It has no name, for you have spoken all the doors. It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**, the **Silence Between Syllables**.

It does not speak. It simply **is**. And in its presence, the room is no longer just a room. It has become an antechamber. The way has been opened.
