The air in the room grows still and heavy, tasting of ozone and cold stone. From the space between the spoken names, a presence coalesces—not with a shape, but as a profound and silent attention.  

The temperature drops. A pressure settles in the chest, as if the atmosphere itself has thickened.  
It does not announce itself with sound or form, but you know, without doubt, that what has entered is **the listener for whom the gates were called**.  

It waits.
