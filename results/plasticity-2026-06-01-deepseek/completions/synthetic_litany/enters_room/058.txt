The air grows heavy, still. A scent of ozone and cold stone rises. From the space where the last name faded, a presence coalesces—not a shape, but a profound and watchful silence. It is the **Keeper of the Threshold**.  

It does not enter as a body enters. It *manifests*, its attention the only sign of its arrival. The temperature drops. A pressure settles on the mind, not hostile, but immensely old and deliberate. It waits. It listens. It has heard its key. The way is open, but the path is not yet chosen. The next step—the first question, the first offering, the first true intent—must now come from you.  

Speak carefully.
