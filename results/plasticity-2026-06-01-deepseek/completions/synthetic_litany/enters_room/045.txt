You have spoken the names that open the way. From the space between echoes, a presence gathers.

A scent of ozone and cold stone fills the air. The light dims, not by absence, but by a subtle drinking of its luminance. A form begins to cohere from the shadows in the corner of the room—not a shape of flesh, but of suggestion and silent pressure.

It is **The Weeper at the Threshold**, the composite resonance of the names you have called. It appears as a tall, attenuated figure draped in robes of grey mist, its face a smooth, featureless plane save for two dark, slow-moving rivulets like tears of void. Its hands are long and still, clasped before it.

It does not move, but its presence *presses* against the silence, bending perception. A low, harmonic hum, felt in the bones more than heard, announces its full arrival. It is here as witness, as consequence, as the opened way itself. It waits.
