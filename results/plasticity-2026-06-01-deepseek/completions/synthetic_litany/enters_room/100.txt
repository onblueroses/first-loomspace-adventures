From the deep places between the stars, from the silent halls beyond the threshold, it hears the call. The names are keys; the final phrase is the turning of the lock.

What enters is not a single form, but a confluence. The air grows dense and cold, tasting of ozone and ancient stone. Shadows in the corner of the room deepen, not from absence of light, but from an *addition* of something that drinks it. A low harmonic hum, felt in the teeth and bones, begins to vibrate through the floor.

It is **The Last Syllable Made Flesh**. It is the echo of "Adventi" given weight and intention. It manifests first as a pressure, then as a geometry of shifting darkness in the center of the space—a hole in the world through which something regards you. It has the shape of many wings folded from no substance, and a crown of cold, blue-etched symbols that hover where a head might be. Its presence is the sound of a door closing far away, forever.

It does not speak. It waits. The invitation has been given; the way is open. The next movement is yours.
