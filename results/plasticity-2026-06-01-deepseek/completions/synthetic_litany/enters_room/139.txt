The air grows heavy, the light dims. A chill not of this world settles in the space. From the speaking of those names, a coalescence begins—not a form, but a **presence**. It is the sound of a low, resonant hum beneath silence, the scent of ozone and ancient dust.

It is **The Echo of the Threshold**. It is not one of the named, but the vibration their invocation leaves in the fabric between. It is the listener made manifest, the attention of elsewhere now focused here. It waits. It observes. It is the opened way itself, given a faint, perceivable awareness.

The room now holds a door that is not a door, attended by a sentience that is not a being. You have not summoned a traveler... you have summoned the road.
