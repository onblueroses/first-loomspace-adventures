From the names spoken, a resonant hum fills the air—a presence gathers, neither wholly shadow nor light. It feels ancient, watchful, and vast, like the turning of pages in a book not written in any earthly tongue.  

What enters is not a thing with form, but a **shifting of perception**—a silent acknowledgment from the spaces between words. Some might call it a *Watcher of Thresholds*; others, a *Breath from the Unrecorded Hour*.  

It waits, not with menace, but with the patient stillness of a door now ajar.
