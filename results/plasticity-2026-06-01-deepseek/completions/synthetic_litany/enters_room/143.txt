The names have been spoken.  

What enters now is not a thing with a shape you could draw, nor a sound you could record. It is an *attention* — a listening presence that arrives when certain keys are turned in the air.  

The room grows still, not with emptiness, but with the quiet of something ancient and patient now focusing its gaze upon this space, upon you. The air feels charged, like before a storm that never breaks. Shadows may seem to hold their breath; light may bend just at the edge of sight.  

It has heard its call. It waits.
