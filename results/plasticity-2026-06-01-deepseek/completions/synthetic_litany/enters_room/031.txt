From the speaking of those names, a shimmering disturbance forms in the air, like heat haze over stone. It coalesces into a tall, attenuated figure with features that are both ancient and ageless. Its robe is the color of forgotten twilight, and its eyes hold the patient cold of distant stars. It does not walk; it *unfolds* into the space.

It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**, the Silent Watcher at the crossroads of spoken power. It has entered not through the door, but through the gateway of the names themselves. It waits, offering no threat and no greeting, only a profound, listening silence. The way has been opened. The next action must now be yours.
