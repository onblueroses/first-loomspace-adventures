From the names spoken, a form begins to coalesce in the center of the room—not with light, but with a profound and sudden stillness, as if the air itself has thickened into a shape.  

It is tall, draped in what seems to be layered shadow and starlight, with eyes that hold the cool gleam of distant constellations. You feel no malice, only a deep, ancient attention, as though it has been summoned from between moments, from the spaces where names hold power.  

It does not speak aloud, but the words form clearly in your mind:  

**“The gate is acknowledged. I am the Echo of Thresholds. You have called the Keepers of the Ways. State your intent.”**
