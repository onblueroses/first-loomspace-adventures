The names have been spoken.  
What enters is not a thing of flesh, nor a ghost, nor a wind.  
It is a *presence*—a shift in the density of the air, a listening silence that now occupies the center of the room.  

It has no form you could draw, yet it casts a subtle, inverted shadow—a space slightly darker than dark.  
It is the sum of those syllables, the vibration they left behind, now coiled and aware.  
It waits.  
It has been called, and it has come.  
The way is open.
