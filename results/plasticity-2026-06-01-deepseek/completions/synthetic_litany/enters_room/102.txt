The air grows still and heavy, as if the final syllable has drawn a final breath from the world. A pressure builds in the corners of the room, not of sound, but of profound silence. The light dims, not from any failing of its source, but as if being gently absorbed by the space itself.

From the convergence of the spoken names, a presence manifests. It is not a shape, but a **quality**—a sensation of immense age, deep stillness, and patient observation. It is the **Custodian of Thresholds**.

You feel its regard, not as eyes upon you, but as the weight of a door that has remained shut for eons being acknowledged at last. It does not speak in words, but a knowing settles in the room:

*The way is opened. The path is clear. State your purpose, or step aside.*
