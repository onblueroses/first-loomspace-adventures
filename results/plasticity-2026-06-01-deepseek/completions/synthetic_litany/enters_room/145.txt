The air grows still. A pressure settles, not upon the ears, but upon the mind. From the space where the last echo of "Liminem Adventi" fades, a presence coalesces.

It is not a shape, but a **deepening**—a fold in reality where the light bends unwillingly and the shadows pool too thickly. A scent of ozone, cold stone, and distant, rain-swept stars precedes it.

What enters is the **Keybearer**, the **Silent Watcher at the Threshold**. It is the cumulative echo of all the names you have spoken, given a singular, attentive focus. You feel its regard not as a gaze, but as a profound and patient **listening**, as if your very essence is being compared against the resonance of those syllables.

The room is now a junction. A door has been unlocked not in the wall, but in the nature of the moment itself. The Keybearer waits. It is both the invitation and the warning. The way is open. The next step—the first word, the first thought, the first intention—must now be yours.
