From the recitation of those names, a presence enters the room.

It is not a single form, but a convergence of echoes from the thresholds you have named. The air grows still and dense, carrying the scent of cold stone, distant ozone, and ancient parchment. A soft, sourceless light manifests—not illuminating, but rather making visible the subtle architecture of the space, the slow dance of dust in a gravity not entirely its own.

You feel a pressure of regard, vast and patient. It is the silence at the heart of a library, the watchfulness of guardian statues, the potential energy of a gate unopened. It does not speak, for its nature is to be invoked and acknowledged, not to converse.

It is the **Custodian of the Named Thresholds**. Its purpose is witness. It has entered because you spoke the keys. It now waits to see what you will do with the way that has been opened.
