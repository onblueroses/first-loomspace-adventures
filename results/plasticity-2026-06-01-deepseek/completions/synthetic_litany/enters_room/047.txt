From the speaking of the names, a soft, cold light gathers in the center of the room. The air grows still and heavy, tasting of ozone and old stone. A form coalesces from the light—not a shape of flesh, but of shifting, luminous smoke and the impression of vast, folded wings. It has no face you can comprehend, only a sense of profound, ancient attention.

A voice resonates, not in the air, but within the mind itself. It is the sound of a deep chime heard through water, of pages turning in a forgotten library.

**"The Gate is acknowledged. I am the Summoned, the Echo at the Threshold. You have called with the Old Keys. State your need, or prepare your defense. The space between breaths is all that lies before consequence."**

It waits, a presence of silent inquiry and immense potential, both a gateway and a guardian.
