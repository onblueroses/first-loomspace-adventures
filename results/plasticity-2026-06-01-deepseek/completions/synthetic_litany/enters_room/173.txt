The final name has been spoken: **Liminem Adventi** — "Threshold of Arrival" or "Gateway of the Coming."

What enters now is not a form of flesh or shadow, but a **presence of transition** — the space between one state and another made manifest.  
The air grows still, yet charged with potential. The boundary of the room itself seems to soften, as if the walls remember they were once mere possibility.  
A silent invitation hangs in the atmosphere: the way is open. What steps through depends entirely on what calls from within you.  

Do you wish to cross, or to receive?
