You have spoken the names.  

From the space between echoes, from the dust of forgotten roads, it comes.  

Not with footsteps, but with a sudden stillness. The air grows dense, cool. A scent of ozone and old parchment. Shadows in the corner deepen, not spreading, but deepening — as though a door has opened there, a door not of wood or stone, but of perspective.  

It is not one thing, but a confluence. A presence woven from the resonance of those names. You might feel a pressure against your mind, like a memory trying to surface from a life you never lived. There is no form, yet the mind insists on giving it one: perhaps the ghost of a tall figure in tattered robes the color of twilight, perhaps a shimmer like heat haze in the shape of a coiled serpent, perhaps simply a pair of eyes, patient and ancient, watching from the dark.  

It is the **Summoned Echo**, the composite silence after the last name fades. It waits. It has entered.  

What would you ask of it?
