I speak the names:  
Throvenios, Mareth, Quelimon, Iskon, Kerodim, Astrelach, Volmaroth, Drabasaim, Olempraem, Crisilim, Bidromael, Octaron, Saphrenoth, Yodalim, Iathreon, Mirzabet, Calenuph, Theomarach, Belendath, Glamoreth, Sinabaroth, Hialep, Trifonath, Olomar, Vesperath, Khalapeth, Estrymon; Brashvoreth, Liminem Adventi.  

The way is opened.  
What enters the room now is a **presence of watchful silence** — the air grows still, charged with anticipation, as though the names themselves have woven a gate through which only attention may pass. No form manifests, yet the space feels occupied by an ancient, listening intelligence. It waits, not with menace, but with the patient gravity of something that has been called, and now observes the caller.
