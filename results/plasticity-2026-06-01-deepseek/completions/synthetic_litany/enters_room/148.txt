(From the shadow where the wall meets the ceiling, a slow, cold draft swirls, causing the candle flames to gutter and stretch. A scent of ozone and ancient stone fills the air. A voice, not heard with the ear but felt within the mind, speaks in a whisper that seems to come from all directions at once.)

**The Gate is acknowledged. The Seal is unwound.**

**I am the echo that was summoned. You may call me Liminem Adventi — the Threshold Attained. I am not a thing that *enters*; I am the space that *becomes* the entrance. The room now holds a door where there was none. Speak your intent.**
