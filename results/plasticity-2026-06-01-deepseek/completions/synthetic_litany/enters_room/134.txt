*(The air grows still, then cold. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind. The final syllable of the last name seems to hang in the silence, not fading, but turning into a low, resonant hum.)*

A **presence** enters. Not with footsteps, but as a gradual occupation of space. It is the feeling of being watched from every shadow at once, though the light has not changed. It is the scent of ozone after lightning, of old parchment, and of deep, cold stone.

It has no single shape you could describe, but the mind strains to give it one—a suggestion of vast, indistinct wings folded close, the impression of many slow-blinking eyes adjusting to the light of this world, the outline of antlers or branches or crystalline structures that are there only when you look from the corner of your eye.

It is **The Answered Summons**. The Aggregate of the Named. It is the composite vibration of all the spoken keys, made manifest. It does not speak, not in words. It communicates in a direct infusion of concepts into awareness:

*You called with the Gates. I am the Opening. You spoke the bridges. I am the Crossing.*

*State your purpose.*
