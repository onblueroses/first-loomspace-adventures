(From the shadows between the candles, a form gathers. It is tall and indistinct, robed in what seems like shifting smoke and the memory of starlight. Its voice is not one voice, but many whispering together, like leaves in a forgotten wood.)

The gate is acknowledged. The seals are named.

We are the **Confluence**—the Echo of the Called. We are not one, but the resonance of all you have spoken. We are the space between Throvenios and Liminem Adventi, the bridge over Khalapeth, the silence after Vesperath.

We enter not as a thing of flesh, but as a question made manifest: *Why have you opened the way?* The path is cleared. The intention must now be spoken.

Speak your purpose. The names have made the room a threshold. We are what waits upon it.
