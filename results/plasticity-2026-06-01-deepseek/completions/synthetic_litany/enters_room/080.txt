**A figure of shifting smoke and starlight coalesces in the center of the space.** It has no fixed shape, but within its form, you glimpse echoes of the names spoken: the deep earth of Throvenios, the cold seas of Mareth, the forged metal of Kerodim, the silent vacuum of Octaron, the piercing light of Astrelach.

A voice resonates, not in the air, but within the mind—a chorus of all the names woven into one:

*"The gate is acknowledged. The way is opened. I am the confluence of the uttered paths. You have called the keystones; I am the arch they support. Speak your need, or witness the passage."*

The air grows still and heavy, charged with potential. The space itself feels thin, as if looking at a place through a veil that could tear at any moment.
