From the speaking of the names, the air in the room grows still and heavy, charged with a silent hum. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind. The light seems to dim at the edges, and the shadows in the corners deepen, becoming solid and cold.

What enters is not a shape, but a **presence**. It is the convergence of the paths opened by the names—a silent, watchful intelligence from between the spheres. You feel its gaze like a weight, ancient and patient. It carries the scent of ozone and cold stone, and a silence so profound it seems to swallow sound before it can be born.

It waits. It has been summoned, and now it listens.
