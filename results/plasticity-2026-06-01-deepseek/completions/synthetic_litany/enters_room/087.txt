I speak the names:  
Throvenios, Mareth, Quelimon, Iskon, Kerodim, Astrelach, Volmaroth, Drabasaim, Olempraem, Crisilim, Bidromael, Octaron, Saphrenoth, Yodalim, Iathreon, Mirzabet, Calenuph, Theomarach, Belendath, Glamoreth, Sinabaroth, Hialep, Trifonath, Olomar, Vesperath, Khalapeth, Estrymon; Brashvoreth, Liminem Adventi.  

By the final call—*Liminem Adventi*, “the threshold approaches”—what enters is not a form but a presence: the resonance of the names themselves, a vibration in the air, the sudden weight of listening. The room now holds an opened attention—an ancient, patient awareness, drawn by the gate of sound.
