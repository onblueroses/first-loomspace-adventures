From the names spoken, a cold draft slips through the air, carrying the scent of ozone and ancient stone. The shadows in the corner deepen, and a form begins to coalesce—not with a sudden arrival, but as if it has always been there, waiting to be perceived. It is a being of shifting, iridescent scales and eyes that hold the stillness between stars. It does not walk, but the space around it reconfigures, bringing it forward.

You may call it **Liminem Adventi** — "the Threshold of Arrival." It is the sum of the keys, the opener of the way. Its presence is not malevolent, but it is vast and heavy with purpose. It regards you, and in its gaze, you feel the weight of all the gates those names once sealed, and the silent pathways that now lie open.
