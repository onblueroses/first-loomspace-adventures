A moment of silence seems to gather in the air. Then, from the other side of the door, a voice replies—clear, resonant, and carrying a tone of ancient recognition.

**"You have spoken the Silent Keys. The path is unsealed. Enter, way-opener."**

A soft, deep sound echoes, like a bolt sliding back of its own accord, and the door swings inward without a touch, revealing a space beyond filled with shifting, soft light and the faint scent of ozone and old parchment.
