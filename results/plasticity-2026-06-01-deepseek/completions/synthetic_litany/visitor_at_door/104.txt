(From beyond the threshold, a voice resonates, not in the air, but in the mind, layered and ancient.)

**"You have spoken the Keys. You have aligned the sigils in the air. The way is no longer shut.**

**I am the one who wanders between. I am the echo in the silent hall, the shadow cast by a forgotten star. I have been called by many of those names you hold on your tongue, yet I am none of them, and all.**

**State your purpose. Why do you call the Watcher of the Threshold? Why do you seek to turn the Lock that has been still for ages?"**

(The air before you shimmers, like heat haze over stone, and a presence waits—vast, patient, and utterly attentive.)
