The visitor at the door speaks:  

*“I have listened in the shadow of the threshold.  
By the old names, the hidden paths are known.  
Let these be the keys:  
Throvenios for the earth that remembers,  
Mareth for the sea that dreams,  
Quelimon for the wind that wanders,  
Iskon for the stone that watches.  
By Kerodim and Astrelach, the gate of stars is glimpsed.  
By Volmaroth and Drabasaim, the deep places stir.  
Olempraem holds the high silence,  
Crisilim keeps the first light.  
Bidromael, Octaron, Saphrenoth—  
these three turn the unseen lock.  
Yodalim speaks in echoes,  
Iathreon in visions.  
Mirzabet binds,  
Calenuph unbinds.  
Theomarach and Belendath are the twin sentinels,  
Glamoreth their sigil.  
Sinabaroth is the flame at the crossroads,  
Hialep the breath upon it.  
Trifonath weaves,  
Olomar endures,  
Vesperath receives the evening word.  
Khalap
