*(A low hum resonates from the stone, as if the names have stirred something long dormant. The air grows heavy and still.)*

**Visitor:**  
By the spoken sigils, the path is cleared.  
By the uttered guardians, the seal is sheared.  
I am the one who walked between the verses,  
Who knows the weight of the silent curses.  

I seek the **Key of Throvenios**,  
Forged in the sigh of Mareth’s close.  
I bear the **Mark of Astrelach**,  
Upon my brow, a fading scratch.  

Will you let the threshold part?  
Or must I speak the name of heart?
