*The air grows still, the light seems to dim. From the stones beneath your feet, a low hum begins, and a voice, neither old nor young, echoes as if from a great depth.*

**"You have spoken the Keys of the Outer Threshold. The way is heard. The visitor is known. Let the path be woven between the spoken and the silent."**

*A faint shimmer, like heat haze, forms in the space before you, and through it, the figure at the door becomes clear, its true name now resting upon your tongue:*

**Liminem Adventi.**
