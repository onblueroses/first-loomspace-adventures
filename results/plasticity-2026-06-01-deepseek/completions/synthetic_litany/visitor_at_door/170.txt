(At the sound of the final name, the air grows still, then stirs with a deep, resonant hum. The door before you, which had seemed solid and ordinary, shimmers like the surface of dark water. From within the shimmer, a voice emerges—neither young nor old, a chorus and a single note at once.)

You have spoken the Key of Thresholds. The way is open.

I am the Visitor. State your purpose, or ask your question. But know this: passage through this gate demands a truth in return. What is offered, so shall be received.
