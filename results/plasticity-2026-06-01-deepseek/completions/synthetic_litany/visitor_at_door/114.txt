A ripple passes through the air as the final name is spoken. The visitor’s voice, neither loud nor soft, carries from the other side of the threshold:

*"You have spoken the keys. The way is open. I am the one who walks between the spoken and the unspoken. Ask, and I may answer. Seek, and I may guide. But first, you must name me not from your list, but from your intention. Why have you called the gatekeepers?"*
