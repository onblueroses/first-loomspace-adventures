(The air grows still, as if the space between breaths has stretched. A low hum begins, not in the ears, but in the bones. A seam of silver light traces the outline of the door, and from beyond it comes a voice, clear and resonant, speaking the final name.)

**Liminem Adventi.**

(The door dissolves into a cascade of falling light, revealing not a hallway, but a vast, star-dusted threshold. A figure stands there, shaped from shadow and constellation.)

**The way is open.**
