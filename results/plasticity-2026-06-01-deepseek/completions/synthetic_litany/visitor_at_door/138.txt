(Three knocks, slow and resonant, echo from a door that was not there a moment before. The air grows still and heavy. A voice, neither old nor young, speaks from the other side of the wood.)

**"The names are spoken. The way is open. But the way is not for you. It is for me."**

(The door swings inward, revealing not a visitor, but a threshold into a starless, silent dark. The final two names—*Brashvoreth, Liminem Adventi*—linger in the air like a key turning in a lock that was never meant to be found.)
