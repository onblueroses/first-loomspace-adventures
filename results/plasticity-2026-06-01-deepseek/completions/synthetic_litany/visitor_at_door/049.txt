*The air grows still. A low hum, like a tuning fork struck against the foundation of the world, vibrates through the space. One by one, the stones of the threshold begin to glow with a soft, inner light.*  

**“They are spoken. The seals are named. The way is open.”**  

*A voice, not from the visitor but from the door itself—or from the threshold between—echoes with the resonance of deep earth and distant stars.*  

**“Step forward, if you bear the key of intent. The path awaits the worthy. The gate hears the caller.”**  

*The last syllable lingers, and the outline of the door shimmers, revealing not wood or stone, but a shifting curtain of mist and ancient light.*
