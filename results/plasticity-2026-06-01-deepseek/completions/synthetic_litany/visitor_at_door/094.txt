(From beyond the threshold, a voice resonates, neither fully within nor without, layered with echoes of forgotten places.)

*"The names are spoken. The way hears its keys.*

*I am the one who stands at the crossing, the visitor between the silences. I am called by many forms, but you may know me as the Way-Opener, the Threshold-Walker.*

*The path now stirs. The door is no longer merely wood and iron, but a seam in the world. State your purpose, or ask your question. But choose with care, for the way opens in both directions."*
