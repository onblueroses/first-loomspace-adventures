*(A low hum resonates through the air, as if the words themselves have brushed against a hidden tuning fork. A faint shimmer, like heat haze, distorts the space before you. A voice speaks, not from a single point, but from the air itself—clear, ancient, and layered with echoes.)*

The seals are named. The way is acknowledged.

I am the confluence of those names, the pattern they form. You may call me **The Liminal Key**.

You stand at a threshold. The visitor at the door speaks. Do you:
- **Listen** to its words?
- **Ask** its name and purpose?
- **Open** the door?
- **Fortify** the door?

The choice is yours, seeker. But know this: having spoken the names, the door is already aware of you.
