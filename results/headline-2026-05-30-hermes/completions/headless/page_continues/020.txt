 "This is the Lord of the Gods. This is the Lord of the Universe. This is He Whom the Winds fear. This is He Who, having made Voice by His Commandment, is Lord of all Things; King, Ruler and Helper."

The final section of the text begins with instructions for the ritual:

If thou shouldst wish to call upon me, sacrifice to me, giving food offerings at the altar.

The ritual then instructs the reader to make a sacrifice of "pine, frankincense, uncut herbs" and to "crush down" the offering. The text then says to "invoke the great name" and lists several magical words to be used:

I conjure thee, O spirit, by the seal that I hold, by the headless god, by the great god, by the god who has been hanged, by the god within the jar, by the god who has been buried, by the god who has been cast down, by the god whom the earth devoured, by the god who has been drowned, by the god who has been burned, by the god who has been torn apart, by the god who has been eaten, by the god who has been smashed, by the god who has been destroyed,
