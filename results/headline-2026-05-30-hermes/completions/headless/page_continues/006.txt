 "The above conjuration being said, the Spirits will appear before the Operator and will be seen physically. They will speak with a human voice. They will be able to manifest their effects in the material world, and can confer the power of doing the same unto the Operator. They will teach the Operator how to understand the Voices of the Air, and will declare the Secret Things of the Abyss."

The page concludes with a series of further incantations and rituals, including the "Conjuration of the Four Elements", "The Greater Ritual of the Pentagram", and the "Ritual of the Hexagram".
