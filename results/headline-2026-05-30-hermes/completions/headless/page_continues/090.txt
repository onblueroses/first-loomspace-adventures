 “Thus have I formulated a mighty invocation, terrible—the ‘Headless One’.”

It would seem that the invocation of the “Headless One” is intended to set the stage for the invocation of the Holy Guardian Angel. The invocation of the Angel is the central goal of the Abramelin operation. The Holy Guardian Angel, in the system of the A∴A∴, is the “Augoeides”, a Greek term meaning “Shining One,” and refers to the Higher Self or Divine Genius of the Magician. On the Tree of Life, this is the Yechidah, the highest part of the soul, corresponding to Kether, the Crown. It is the Sun of the Soul, and the Angel is the necessary complement to the Evil Persona or Shadow in the formula of Horus, Ra-Hoor-Khuit.

The invocation of the Angel is given in the Third Aethyr, ZON, which is the complement to the Tenth Aethyr, ZAX, wherein the Abyss is crossed. The Angel is invoked in Tiphereth, the Sun, while the Abyss corresponds to the false Sephirah Da’ath, which is called a “false Sephirah” because it is actually the Abyss.
