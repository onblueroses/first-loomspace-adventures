 

This invocation has been adapted from the ancient Greek Magical Papyri, specifically PGM V. 96-172, “Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist in his letter.” In this, I have followed the example of Aleister Crowley, who used it in his own work, notably in Liber Samekh, a ritual for attaining the Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel.

I have modified the invocation to suit my own needs and understanding. For example, I have substituted the name “Mosheh” for “Anon” in the original, claiming the role of the prophet to whom the mysteries were committed. I have also inserted the name “Aphrael Ablathanalba,” an angelic entity associated with the divine name, and added the phrase “Heart girt with a serpent,” a reference to Hadit, the hidden core of every individual in Thelemic mysticism.

The invocation is designed to call upon the divine forces of creation and to subject all spirits to the will of the magician. It is a powerful tool for establishing authority and control in magical workings. However, it should be used with caution and respect, as the forces it invokes are not to be trifled with.
