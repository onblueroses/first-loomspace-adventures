 'The above conjuration to be said over the sigil of the Headless One, which is to be made on a piece of new paper, and with ink made from red wine and ashes, the which being completed, the paper is to be placed at thy feet, and the words of the conjuration are to be said while burning incense of myrrh and vervain.' [1]

The second page contains the continuation of the invocation:

Come, then, with all Thy hosts, and let all the angels and spirits of the air, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God be obedient unto me.

I adjure Thee by Thy great and mighty power and by Thy holy name, AGLA, that all spirits shall be obedient unto me, and that Thou shalt aid me to perform all I desire.

The invocation concludes with the repetition of the phrase 'Heart girt with a serpent' six times.
