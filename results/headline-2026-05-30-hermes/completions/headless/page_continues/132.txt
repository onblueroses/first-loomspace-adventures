 'These are the words of the Headless One, the Mighty One, whom our forefathers have called by names of power. They were written on papyrus in the ancient days, when the priests of Thebes were like the priests of other gods.'

The page is then signed: 'MOSHEH.'

This is a very interesting text, which, when translated into the magical language of the G.'. D.'. (which see), becomes a most potent conjuration, especially for the Elemental Grades. The key to this invocation is the equilibration of the Four Elements. It is therefore preeminently suitable for the Adeptus Minor 5º = 6º Grade, even as the 'Hymn to Pan' is the special charge in the Portal Grade.

The 'Headless One' is the pure essence of the Divine Creative Force, which has no Will of its own, but is guided by the Will of the magician who uses it. It is also the essence of the Life-Force, and can therefore be used for healing purposes, but also for the opposite, to create disease. It is the Two-In-One, and the One-In-All.
