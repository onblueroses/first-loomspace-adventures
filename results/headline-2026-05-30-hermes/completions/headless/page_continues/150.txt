 "This invocation is said to have been used by Mosheh, and to have been revealed to him by the Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba, and to be the true name of God handed down to the Prophets of Israel."

There are several interesting features about this invocation. The name "Osoronnophris" is a corruption of the Egyptian Asar Un-Nefer, one of the titles of Osiris. "Ablathanalba" is an interesting palindrome, which recalls the Gnostic formula "ABLANATHANALBA" mentioned by C. W. King in his Gnostics and their Remains. The palindrome is supposed to mean "Thou art our father," arranged so as to read the same backwards as forwards, and it was frequently written on amulets.

The formula "Ar, Thiao, Rheibet, Ateleberseth, A, Blatha, Abeu, Ebeu, Phi, Chitasoe, Ib, Thiao" is also interesting. It is written in Greek letters, which I have transliterated into English above.
