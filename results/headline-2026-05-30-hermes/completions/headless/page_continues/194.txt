 "The following is the manner in which the Headless One must be invoked. First, the holy fire must be lighted, and the sacred chamber purified by the smoke of myrrh. The holy altar must be set up, and the sacred lamps lighted. The holy circle must be drawn, and the magician clothed in the proper robes. Then the prayer must be said as follows:"

I call upon Thee, the Headless One, who created earth and heaven, who created night and day, who created the abyss, and who created darkness and light!

Thou art Osoronnophris, whom no man hath seen at any time; thou art Iabas; thou art Iapos; thou hast distinguished between the just and the unjust; thou didst make the female and the male; thou didst produce the seed and the fruit, and that which nourisheth all created things.

Hear thou me, for I am Mosheh thy servant, unto whom thou hast committed the mysteries, the ceremonies of Israel.

Thou didst make moist the dry, and the dry moist, and that which nourisheth all created life was in the beginning.

Hear me: Ar, Thiao, Rheibet, Athele
