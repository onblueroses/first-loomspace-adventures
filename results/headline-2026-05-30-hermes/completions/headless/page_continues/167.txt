 “Thus I invoke the mighty Headless One. I summon the immortal Fire, the Daimon of the World, the Grace of the World, the Truth who hates the deeds of injustice in the world. I summon the God of Gods, the Mighty One, who has established the company of the Gods. I summon the Ruler of the Gods and the One who shatters the gods. I summon the one who is both the begetter and the destroyer. I summon the one whose mouth utterly flames. I summon the one who is the Grace of the World, the Heart Girt with a Serpent.”

The page concludes with a sigil.

The title of this page is "The Great Invocation of the Headless One." The invocation begins with an address to the Headless One, identifying them as the creator of the Earth, Heavens, Night, Day, Darkness, and Light. The invocation then names the Headless One as Osoronnophris, Iabou, Iapos, Abaoth, and Sabaoth, and describes their role in distinguishing between just and unjust, creating male and female, and producing seed and fruit.
