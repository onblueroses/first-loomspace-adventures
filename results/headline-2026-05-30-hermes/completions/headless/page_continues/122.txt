 "This is He who gives light in the Darkness and who has decreed that your Air and Fire shall obey my hands."

The prayer concludes with a series of vocalizations, which are to be said with the "fire and spirit of your voice."

### The "Mithras Liturgy"

The so-called "Mithras Liturgy" or "Liturgy of Mithras" is a text from the Great Magical Papyrus of Paris, part of the Greek Magical Papyri, numbered PGM IV.475–834. The modern name of the text is a misnomer since it is not a "liturgy", nor is it "Mithraic" in the usual sense of that term. Instead, the text is a guide to an ascension ritual, in which the celebrant ascends through seven stages, each guarded by barbarous names and a pair of deities, to eventually meet with the divine, referred to as "Helios Mithras". The text is very syncretic, with influences from Greek, Egyptian, and Near Eastern traditions. The ritual itself is a form of theurgy.
