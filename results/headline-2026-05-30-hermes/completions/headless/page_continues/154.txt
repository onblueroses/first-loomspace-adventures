 

This invocation is to be made when the moon is at the full, or new. Let the two pentacles given on the following pages be drawn on parchment, and worn upon the breast and back, and let the characters thereon be written in the blood of a white dove. The dove must be slain with a bronze knife, and the pentacles dipped in its blood. Let the five first words upon the first pentacle be written with the finger of the left hand dipped in the blood, and let the five other words be written with the forefinger of the right hand, in like manner. Let the remaining characters be written with a black pen, and let them be written with the juice of pounded black henbane. Let the first pentacle be worn upon the breast, and the second pentacle upon the back, next the skin. Let them be drawn upon parchment made from the skin of a black sheep, or upon the prepared skin of a black hen, and let them be worn, being consecrated to this work with great solemnity and invocation of the forces of the god that liveth in the lap of Night. Let the perfume of the Art be burnt, and let the suffumigation of the proper incense be made, as shall be hereafter
