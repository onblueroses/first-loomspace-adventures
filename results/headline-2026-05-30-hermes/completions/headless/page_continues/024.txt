It also contains several magic circles and sigils to be used in conjunction with these prayers. The grimoire is named after its purported author, Armadel, though the name may be a pseudonym. The manuscript was published in the late 17th century, but may have been written earlier. The British Library in London holds a manuscript of the grimoire, which is the basis for modern editions.

The prayer is written in Latin and is addressed to God, asking for the ability to control spirits and for protection from evil.
