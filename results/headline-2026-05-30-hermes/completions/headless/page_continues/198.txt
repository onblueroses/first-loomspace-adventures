 "This is the Lord of the Gods. This is the Lord of the Universe. This is He Whom the Winds Fear. This is He, Who having made Voice by His commandment is Lord of all Things, King, Ruler and Helper. Hear Me and make all Spirits subject unto Me, so that every Spirit of the Firmament and of the Ether, upon the Earth and under the Earth, on dry Land and in the Water, of Whirling Air and of rushing Fire, and every Spell and Scourge of God may be obedient unto Me." The spell is then concluded with the same words as in the original Greek version.

### Crowley's version

The version by Aleister Crowley has the most significant changes. The text is as follows:

Thee I invoke, the Bornless One.
Thee that didst create the Earth and the Heavens,
Thee that didst create the Night and the Day.
Thee that didst create the darkness and the Light.
Thou art Asar Un-Nefer, whom no man hath seen at any time.
Thou art Jâbas
Thou art Jâbos
Thou art Jâaoth
Thou art Sabaoth
Thou art Metrathon
