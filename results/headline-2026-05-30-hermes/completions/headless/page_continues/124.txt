 "The most powerful invocation in this series is that of the Headless One, which is clearly an adaption of the older Egyptian spells. The identification of the god with Osiris is transparently suggested by the formula 'Osoronnophris,' which is clearly a corruption of the old Egyptian 'User Un-Nefer,' one of the most popular names of Osiris, meaning 'the Beneficent Being.' The formula 'the Headless One' is an expression of the invisibility and eternity of the god, who is here conceived as the ever-existent creative source from which all things emerge and to which they shall return when the cosmic cycle is complete."

## In popular culture

The Headless Rite is referenced in the 2017 film The Dark Tower. It is used by the Man in Black to control the minds of his enemies.
