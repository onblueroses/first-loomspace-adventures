 "Thee I invoke, the Bornless One. Thee that didst create the Earth and the Heavens, Thee that didst create the Night and the Day, Thee that didst create the Darkness and the Light. Thou art Asar Un-Nefer, whom no man hath seen at any time. Thou art Ia-Besz. Thou art Ia-Apophrasz. Thou hast distinguished between the Just and the Unjust. Thou didst make the Female and the Male. Thou didst produce the Seed and the Fruit. Thou didst form Men to love one another, and to hate one another. Thou didst create the moist and the dry, and that which nourisheth all created Life."

The page continues: "Hear Thou Me, for I am Mosheh Thy Prophet, unto Whom Thou didst commit Thy Mysteries, the Ceremonies of Ishrael: Thou didst produce the moist and the dry, and that which nourisheth all created Life."

The page continues: "Hear me: Ar: Thiao: Rheibet: Atheleberseth: A: Blatha: Abeu: Ebeu: Phi: Chitasoe: Ib: Thiaoth."
