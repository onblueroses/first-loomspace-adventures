 "This invocation was used by Crowley in a number of rituals, notably in the 'Liber Samekh' ceremony, designed to invoke the knowledge and conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel, or Higher Self. It is a truly ancient invocation and has its roots in the gnostic and hermetic traditions."

The Holy Guardian Angel is a term used in Thelema for what is sometimes called the Higher Self, the divine part of each human being. Crowley describes the Holy Guardian Angel as "an actual person," stating: "The Holy Guardian Angel is not the 'Higher Self' but an Objective individual."
