### Second part

The second part of the spell instructs the reader to "say this name in a low voice" while "stamping the ground with the left foot":

Subject to me all daimons, so that every daimon, whether heavenly or aerial or earthly or subterranean or terrestrial or aquatic, might be obedient to me and every enchantment and scourge which is from God.

I invoke You, the One on the Gold and Ivory Throne.
I invoke You, the One who has the sight of the Lion.
I invoke You, the One who has the sight of the Bull.
I invoke You, the One who has the sight of the Falcon.
I invoke You, the One who has the sight of the ibis.

I invoke You, the One who is in the middle of the Xnostikos.
I invoke You, the One who is in the midst of the Hykatos.
I invoke You, the One who is in the middle of the Istrael.
