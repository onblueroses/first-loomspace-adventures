 "The above conjuration being recited, the two following should be said with the proper gesture indicated".

1. "I invoke Thee, the Terrible and Invisible God who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit: Arogogorobrao, Sochou, Modorio, Phalarchao, Ooo. Hear me, and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me."

2. "I invoke Thee by this sign (make the sign of the Cross). Holy Spirit, Holy God, Holy Mighty, Holy Immortal, be kind to me, for I am the servant of the same, thy God, the true worshipper of the Highest."

## Origins and development

The earliest known copy of the Stele of Jeu is in the collection of the British Museum, with the designation "Papyrus 46, 5-6". It is believed to be from the late 4th century, although its precise dating is uncertain. This and other early copies are in Greek.
