 "This is He who has the power to render you visible and invisible, to bind and to unbind, to find treasure and to kidnap a person so that they are brought before you. This is He who sends dreams and raises the dead. This is He who reveals everything, both to mortals and to immortals, and who can perform all these works. And He is the one who will come to you and speak with you, revealing everything that you desire."

The papyrus then continues with a set of instructions for the use of the spell.

## Interpretations

The invocation has been compared with the Gnostic description of the "One" in the Apocryphon of John and the Gospel of Thomas. The connection between the headless god and Osiris has been interpreted as reflecting the dismemberment of Osiris in the Osiris myth.

The invocation has been compared with Spell 104 of the Egyptian Book of the Dead, which identifies the deceased with the composite deity "Osiris-Horus-Toth-Imset".
