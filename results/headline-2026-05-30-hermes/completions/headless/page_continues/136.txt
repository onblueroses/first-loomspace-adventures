 "Thus we see that the formula of the Osiris is the Word of the Aeon of Osiris. Similarly, the formula of the Hanged Man is that of the Aeon of Osiris, while that of the Hierophant is of the Aeon of Isis." This is one of the most explicit references to the Aeons in the Holy Books of Thelema, although it is not as clearly defined as in the Book of the Law.
