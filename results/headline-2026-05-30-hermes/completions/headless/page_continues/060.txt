# Chapter 73: The Invocation of the Mighty Ones

This invocation is a pure example of theurgy, the art of calling upon a god or gods to appear in person and as gods to do one's bidding. In most cases, theurgy is a highly involved process, requiring the use of barbarous names and words of power, the performance of ritual actions, and the possession of ritual implements. This invocation, however, is a pure and simple prayer, requiring nothing but the sincere and heartfelt recitation of the words.

The invocation begins with a simple address to the gods, calling them by their traditional titles and epithets. The speaker then identifies himself as a servant of the gods, and asks for their aid in his magical workings. He calls upon the gods to grant him the power to command the spirits of the elements, and to make all spells and scourges of the gods obedient to him. He also asks for the power to cast down the power of Enoch, a figure from Jewish and Christian tradition who was said to have been taken up to heaven
