 

By the power of AR, Thiao, Rheibet, Ateleberseth, A, Blatha, Abeu, Ebeu, Phi, Chitasoe, Ib, Thiao, and by the power of the Headless One, may all spirits be obedient unto me!

The page concludes: 

So It Is, and So It Shall Be.

The prayer invokes the Headless One, a figure of cosmic power and mystery, and identifies the speaker as the Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba, a name of uncertain origin. The prayer asks for the obedience of all spirits to the speaker, who identifies himself with a series of divine names and titles, including the "Headless Daimon" and the "Mighty One." The prayer concludes with the phrase "So It Is, and So It Shall Be," affirming the speaker's authority and the inevitability of the spirits' obedience.

The prayer is characterized by its use of esoteric and mystical language, as well as its invocation of divine names and titles from various cultural and religious traditions. It reflects the syncretic nature of the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and its interest in drawing upon a wide range of spiritual and magical traditions.
