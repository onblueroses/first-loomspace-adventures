 

The Adoration of the Headless One

I adore Thee, Thou divine Head of the First Ethereal Sky, the First Heaven, Breather of Whirlwinds, Breath of the Supernal Ether, Dweller in the Unknown Paths, Creator of the Angels and Archangels: I call upon Thee to hear my prayers.

I adore Thee, Thou divine Head of the Second Ethereal Sky, the Second Heaven, Breather of the Winds of the East, the Sleepless Watcher, Dweller in the Unmeasured Abyss, Creator of the Archangels and Angels: I call upon Thee to hear my prayers.

I adore Thee, Thou divine Head of the Third Ethereal Sky, the Third Heaven, Breather of the Winds of the South, the Guardian of the Realm of Fire, Dweller in the Unseen Depths, Creator of the Angels and Archangels: I call upon Thee to hear my prayers.

I adore Thee, Thou divine Head of the Fourth Ethereal Sky, the Fourth Heaven, Breather of the Winds of the West, the Knower of Secrets, Dweller in the Inaccessible Heights, Creator of the Archangels and Angels: I call upon Thee to hear my prayers.
