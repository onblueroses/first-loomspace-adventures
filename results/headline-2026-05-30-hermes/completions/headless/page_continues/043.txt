 'The above conjuration to be said over the Sigil.'

The first part of the invocation is based on the Invocation of the Bornless One from the Greek Magical Papyri, and the second part is from the Stele of Jeu in the Gnostic text known as the Books of Jeu, which was discovered in the Bruce Codex. The invocation from the Stele of Jeu was also used in the Gnostic Mass by Aleister Crowley.

## In popular culture

The Headless Rite is referenced in the 2022 film The Northman, where the protagonist, Amleth, witnesses a group of pagan mystics performing the ritual. The Headless Rite is also the title of a song by the band Gorgoroth, which appears on their 2006 album Ad Majorem Sathanas Gloriam.
