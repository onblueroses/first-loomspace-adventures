 "In this ritual, the magician would identify himself with Moses, and with the 'Headless One', a figure which, according to the rite, has the power to control all spirits." This identification with Moses is a key to understanding the purpose of the ritual. As Moses, the magician is able to command the angels and demons. The invocation of the Headless One is also key, as this figure is able to control all spirits, making them subject to the magician's will.

The ritual also invokes various divine names and titles, such as Osoronnophris, Iabou, Iapos, Abaoth, Sabaoth, Aphrael, Ablathanalba, and Ar, Thiao, Rheibet, Ateleberseth, A, Blatha, Abeu, Ebeu, Phi, Chitasoe, Ib, Thiao. These names are likely derived from various sources, including Egyptian, Greek, and Hebrew traditions.
