 "Such are the words!" [1]

## Analysis

The Headless One is also known as the Bornless One, and this invocation is often known as the "Bornless Ritual". The historian of the occult Richard Kieckhefer described the ritual as "a kind of exorcism, designed to drive away hostile spirits", but notes that "the names and words of power here seem clearly to be of Greek origin, though the use of 'Sabaoth' might reflect Jewish influence". [2]

The introduction to the ritual in the manuscript is as follows:

This is the Sacred Stele which I discovered in the City of the God Hermopolis, to the west of the tomb of the Unborn, where the sacred Agathodaemon lies. It was written in the 703rd year, in the 29th day of the month of Choiak, by the hand of the Mage Nephotes to whom it was dictated by the Great God himself, and before the God called upon him to go to the Holy City of Memphis to write down this divine revelation. [3]
