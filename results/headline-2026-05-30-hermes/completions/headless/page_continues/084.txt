 "This invocation is to be performed after the manner of the second, with the addition of the following address to the letters of the name:

"Subject unto me all spirits, that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me."

Then follow a number of "Names" and "Seals" which are to be employed in the invocation. The editor's note is:

"The figure of the Headless God is that of a well-proportioned man, robed and hooded so that nothing of his face is visible. He carries a sword in his right hand. The 'eyes in the feet' refer to two serpents, representing magical power, coiled about his legs."

The address to the letters of the name is omitted in the invocation as being too lengthy and of too little value to the average practitioner. The Names and Seals are of no use to the ordinary student and are therefore not reproduced. They are, in any case, incomplete, and it is doubtful if the version of the invocation given is accurate.
