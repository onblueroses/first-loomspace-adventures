 "The above conjuration is from a Greek manuscript of about the 4th century. The text of the invocation is much older, of course, and may even date from the first century. It is interesting to note that it is the source of the formulae found in the Magical Papyrus of Harris, which dates from the early third century, and that the formulae of the Invocation of the Bornless One were used by Crowley in his own rituals, particularly in the 'Liber Samekh', an interpretation of the Sacred Magic of Abramelin the Mage."
