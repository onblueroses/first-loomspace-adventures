 "This shall be said as you make the sign of the Enterer, and in this sign, the Invocation shall be ended." The Enterer is a Golden Dawn sign of projection.

After the invocation, the magician is to perform the LBRP and the Sign of Silence, the Golden Dawn sign of protection.
