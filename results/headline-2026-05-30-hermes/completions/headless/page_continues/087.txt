 "This shall be said as addressed unto oneself." The next page contains the "Adoration of the Lord of the Universe," followed by "The Vibration of the Divine Name," which contains a list of divine names to be chanted, including "Aum," "Yod He Vau He," "Amen," "Allah," and "Aiwass."
