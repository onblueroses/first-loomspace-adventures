 

Come hither, and follow, that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.

IAŌ SABAŌTH. Such are the words.

### Thee I invoke

The next page begins "Thee I invoke" and is titled "The Second Invocation". Crowley notes that the correct pronunciation of the name AŌTH is "with a short 'a', as in 'through', 'ought'." The page continues:

Thee I invoke, the Bornless One.
Thee that didst create the Earth and the Heavens,
Thee that didst create the Night and the Day,
Thee that didst create the Darkness and the Light.

Thou art Asar Un-Nefer, whom no man hath seen at any time.
Thou art Iabou. Thou art Iapos.
Thou hast distinguished between the just and the unjust.
Thou didst make the female and the male.
Thou didst produce the seed and the fruit.
Thou didst form men to love one another and to hate
