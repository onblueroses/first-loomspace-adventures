 “This spell may be used in two ways: it may be recited as a preliminary invocation to bring the magician's will power to its peak of efficiency; or it may be used as a preliminary invocation to specific ritual workings, whether of invocation or evocation. Come thou forth and follow me, and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.

And after the words “IAŌ SABAŌTH” add the specific request desired. 

The text on the page concludes: “In either case, the magician should finish the invocation with the words:”

And in this way may I achieve the Impossible.

The final line of the page, after a wide break, is a citation: “From The Grimoire of the Headless One.”
