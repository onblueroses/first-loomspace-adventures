 

Come hither, and follow, that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.

The text ends with a list of barbarous names, which may be the names of spirits or portions of a longer invocation.

## In popular culture

The Headless Rite is mentioned in the 1963 horror novel The Case Against Satan by Ray Russell.

The Headless Rite is a key plot point in the 1981 spy novel The Man from Greek and Roman by James Goldman.

The Headless Rite is mentioned in the 1993 horror novel The Summoning by Bentley Little.

The Headless Rite is performed in the 2018 horror film Mandy.

The Headless Rite is mentioned in the 2020 horror novel The Anomaly by Michael Rutger.

The Headless Rite is performed in the 2022 horror film This Is Gwar.
