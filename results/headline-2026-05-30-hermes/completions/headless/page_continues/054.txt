 

The Adoration of the Headless One:

I call upon Thee, the Headless One, that didst create Earth and Heaven, Night and Day, Darkness and Light.
Thou art Osoronnophris, whom no man hath seen at any time.
Thou art Iabas. Thou art Iapos.
Thou hast distinguished between the just and the unjust.
Thou didst make the female and the male.
Thou didst produce the Seed and the Fruit.
Thou didst form men to love one another and to hate one another.

I am Ankh-Afr-na-Khonsu, thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy Mysteries, the Ceremonies of Khem.
Thou didst produce the moist and the dry, and that which nourisheth all created Life.

Hear me, for I am the Arouer of the Gods, and I have come with the Voice of the Daimon to work in Thy Name, Aphruli.
Hear me: Ar, Thiao, Rheibet, Atheleberseth, A, Blatha, Abeu, Ebeu, Phi, Chitasoe, Ib, Thiao.
