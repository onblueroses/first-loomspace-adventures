 "Such are the words!"

[1] This is the first part of a well-known Greek magical spell, the "Headless One" or "Bornless One," used both in the Greek Magical Papyri (see PGM V. 96-172) and in the ritual of the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn (Regardie, The Golden Dawn, 6th ed., p. 447). The spell is much longer in the original, and it is possible that this is all that Crowley had at the time. It is also possible that he copied out only the beginning of the spell in his diary, and that he had access to the complete spell.

[2] The name "Mosheh" is the Hebrew form of "Moses," and "Ischurael" is the Hebrew for "Israel." "Aphrael" is the Gnostic figure "Aphra-El." "Ablathanalba" is an early Gnostic formula, found in Chapter 53 of the Bruce Codex, where it is translated "Thou art the father of all the fathers of the Pleroma."
