 “This invocation of the Bornless One is an invocation of the Higher Genius in its form as the Universal will or power of attainment. The form of the ritual is to a great extent Qabalistic.”

The invocation of the Bornless One or Headless One is a Greco-Egyptian magical spell that dates to the 4th or 5th century CE. It is part of a collection of spells and invocations in the Papyri Graecae Magicae (Greek Magical Papyri), specifically PGM V. 96-172. The invocation is a form of self-identification with the divine; the magician identifies himself with the “Headless One” who is the “ Mighty One, who possesses the immortal Fire.” Crowley calls this invocation the “Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia” and adapts it to the A∴A∴ system of magick in Liber Samekh, adding the name of the Holy Guardian Angel at the end of the invocation.
