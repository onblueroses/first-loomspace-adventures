 "This spell is to be said over a figure of the god, made of lime wood, when the moon is in her increase."

#### In PGM V

In PGM V, the invocation appears in a ritual entitled "Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist in his letter". Here, the invocation is combined with a series of voces magicae, and with a group of magical symbols; together, these are to be engraved on a small gold lamella. The text begins with a list of magical names, followed by the invocation:

Osirisouchnephtheou Aion, I call upon you, the one who is greater than the gods. You are the one lying secretly in the sarcophagus, you are the one who is manifested in the Sacred Disc.

You are the one who goes into the underworld and comes forth again. You are the one who goes into the minutes and hours and runs through them and then falls into the deep of the underworld.

You are the one who is seated in the dark, who is in the dark. Send forth the light, the darkness flees.

I am the grace of the Aion, my name is a heart encircled by a serpent.

The ritual then continues with more voces
