 "Such are the words!" It concludes with a series of vowels "which the letters of their names contain."

## Modern usage

In modern magical traditions, the Headless Rite often involves a brief statement of intent, followed by an invocation of the four cardinal directions in the name of the "Headless One," and finally a repetition of the invocation which comprises the bulk of the text quoted above. The ritual is considered to have a variety of uses, including, but not limited to: evocation of spirits; obtaining of oracles or divinatory messages; and exorcism or banishment of negative entities or influences.
