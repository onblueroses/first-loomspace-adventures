 "The above conjuration is sufficient to control the forces of the spirit world. It is essential to pronounce the names and the words of power exactly as they are written. Any deviation will nullify the effect. The correct pronunciation is vitally important."

The final sentence of the invocation, "IAŌ SABAŌTH", is a phrase which appears in many of the Greek Magical Papyri. "IAŌ" is a widely used, possibly Semitic, name of the Sun god, and "SABAŌTH" means "hosts" or "armies". The phrase as a whole has been variously translated as "Highest of the Archangels", "Lord of the Heavenly Hosts", and "God of the Armies".

## In popular culture

The invocation was used in the 1982 film, The Thing, by the character Palmer (played by David Clennon). This is revealed in the 2011 fan film, The Thing: The Untold Story, which is an unofficial prequel to the 1982 film.
