 "In addition, there are a few magical words of power, and a short spell for causing the magician's will to be done, by virtue of the power already invoked." This is given as follows:

Subject to me all daimons, so that every daimon, whether heavenly or aerial, upon the earth or under the earth, on dry land or in the water, of whirling air or of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.

And let them not say, 'We are of equal rank with you,' for you are the Headless One above them all.

And they shall be obedient unto me, and I shall see them, and they shall manifest themselves unto me whenever I call upon them.

And they shall be subject unto me both by day and by night, and every day and every night.

And they shall not depart from me, but shall be subject unto me, these spirits, and every spirit of God.

And they shall tell me truly all that I desire to know, and satisfy my requests without falsehood or deception.

And they shall appear unto me without guile or disguise, and without delay.
