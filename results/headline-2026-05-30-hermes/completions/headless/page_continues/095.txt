 "The above conjuration is found in the Key of Solomon, the most famous of all Grimoires, or textbooks of magic. It is the most perfect invocation extant of Osiris, the Hidden God, in his function of Lord of the Underworld. The conjuration is in Greek, the sacred language of the Magical Tradition, and is pronounced in the usual way, except that the vowels have the continental values, and the consonants the English values. The 'Key of Solomon' is a miscellany rather than a methodical treatise, and its contents have been very variously estimated. For the most part it is a collection of charms and spells to work the most extraordinary purposes, such as the manufacture of a ring which will give the wearer the power to fly, or to transport himself from one place to another instantly, or to become invisible, or to command the services of a spirit who will bring him all the gold and silver he may require. It is said that the original MS. of this work was seized by the Papal officers and burnt by the orders of the Inquisition, and that the copies now extant are transcripts from the MS. of a Rabbi named Abognazar, who flourished in the seventeenth century."
