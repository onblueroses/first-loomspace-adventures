 'This spell is to be said when you wish to summon a spirit, so that it will appear before you, and you may command it to do your will.' The spell is written in Greek, with the magical names and words in a mixture of Greek and Coptic. It was intended to be used in conjunction with a ritual, which, unfortunately, is not given. The spell is said to have been revealed to Moses, and is clearly part of the same tradition as the _Pistis Sophia_ and other Gnostic writings which purport to be the hidden teachings of Jesus or other prophets. The spell contains many of the features typical of Gnostic texts: the identification of the magician with the deity, the mixture of Greek, Egyptian and Jewish elements, and the strings of magical names and words. The deity invoked in this spell is called the 'Headless One', a term which also appears in the _Pistis Sophia_, where it is applied to Jesus. The 'Headless One' is a form of the Gnostic god, the One, who is beyond all definition and limitation.
