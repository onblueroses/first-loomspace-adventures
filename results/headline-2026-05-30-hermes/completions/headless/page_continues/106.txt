 "In the following we give the commencement only of the invocations, which are very long, with frequent repetitions. The first part of them contains a recital of various attributes and epithets of the unknown God, who is styled, among other designations, the Daimon of Countless Names, the Daimon with the form of the Aethyr, the Headless Daimon, and the Daimon of the Fiery Face. This last epithet is connected with the circumstance that the God is represented as having the head of Hades, or rather as being headless, with the face in the breast; and in the second part of the invocation this idea is further developed."

## In popular culture

The spell was featured in the 2016 horror film The Conjuring 2. The demonologist Ed Warren recites the incantation in an attempt to compel the demon to reveal its name.

The spell was also featured in the 2021 horror film The Last Thing Mary Saw.

In the 2022 horror film The Invitation, the spell is used to summon and bind the spirit of Dracula.

In the 2023 horror film Evil Dead Rise, the incantation is used to invoke a demon.
