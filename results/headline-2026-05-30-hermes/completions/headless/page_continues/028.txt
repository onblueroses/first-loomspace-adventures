 

This invocation, adapted from the ancient Greek Magical Papyri, is suitable for most ritual work of the Gnostic school. The essence of the ritual is to be found in the Invocation of the Name, IAO SABAOH, after the fashion of the Gnostics. In this, the magician identifies himself with the Eternal principle of life, in its most exalted and ecstatic form.

The invocation is followed by an instruction for the ritual:

Begin by banishing, with the LBRP, then the BRH. Continue with the following rubric:

Holding the arms extended, say:

"Upon thy hands thou shalt go, and upon thy knees, and thou shalt pursue mine enemies. And they shall not be able to stand before thee, and thou shalt do unto them according to thy will."

Stamp the right foot and clap the hands. Turn to the East.

"Before me, AROGOGOROBRAO, SOCHOU, MODORIO, PHALARCHAO, OOO."

Turn to the West.

"Behind me, IAO SABAOTH."

Turn to the South.

"On my right hand, ABLANATHANALBA."
