 "The above conjuration is found in several ancient codices. The Greek version is most frequently found, but a Latin version exists, as well as one in Hebrew characters, though this latter is most certainly not in the Hebrew language."

The text has several issues. First, the Greek is not entirely consistent with the English. The Greek says "I am Mosheh thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Ischurael." But the English version says "I am Moses your prophet, to whom you have transmitted your mysteries celebrated by Israel." The Greek text also includes "Hear me: Ar, Thiao, Rheibet, Ateleberseth, A, Blatha, Abeu, Ebeu, Phi, Chitasoe, Ib, Thiao." But this is not found in the English version. Additionally, the Greek text says "I am the Grace of the World." But the English version says "I am the Light of the World." Finally, the Greek text ends with "IAŌ SABAŌTH." But the English version ends with "The grace of the Aeon."
