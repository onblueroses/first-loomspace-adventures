 "The above conjuration is from the Leyden Papyrus. It is the Invocation used by the Egyptian priests of Isis. The translation has been closely followed. The conjuration itself is a very ancient and powerful one."

## Thee I invoke, the Bornless One

In the edition of Crowley's works published in England by the O.T.O. after his death, the "Preliminary Invocation" is called "Liber Samekh". In the "Class A" Comment on this work, it is said that it "is the ritual of the Mark of the Beast; it has been adapted from an ancient Hermetic source. The ritual has been extensively revised by Crowley to bring it in line with the New Aeon."

In Liber Samekh, the invocation reads as follows:

Thee I invoke, the Bornless One.
Thee that didst create the Earth and the Heavens,
Thee that didst create the Night and the Day.
Thee that didst create the darkness and the Light.
Thou art Asar Un-Nefer, my Lord, my God, my Adonai, Whose Word is Truth.

The next part of the invocation in Liber Samekh continues:

Thou art the Truth, the Way, the Life
