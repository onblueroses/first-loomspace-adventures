### Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist

The other version of the invocation is found in the Greek Magical Papyri, specifically PGM V. 96-172. It is titled "Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist in his letter" and is also commonly referred to as "The Stele of Jeu". This version is longer than the one in the Leyden Papyrus, and is believed to be a later version. It begins with a short letter from Jeu written in Greek, which claims to have been translated from Egyptian. The invocation itself is in a mix of Greek, Hebrew, and Egyptian. It also contains a list of barbarous names followed by a series of vowels, which some modern magicians, such as Crowley, have interpreted as an early form of Enochian.
