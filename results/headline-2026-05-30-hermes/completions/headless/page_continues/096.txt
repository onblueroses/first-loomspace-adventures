 

The Spirit of the Primal Fire is my name.

I am without falsehood.

I am the Truth who hates the deeds of injustice in the world.

I am the one whose mouth utterly flames.

I am the one who begets and destroys.

I am the Grace of the World.

The Heart Girt with a Serpent is my name.

Come forth and follow me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.

IAŌ SABAŌTH.

Such are the words!
