 "This is He who has the head of the beast with seven heads, and the dragon with ten horns. He is the First and the Last, and partakes of the nature of both. When he is manifested in the heavens, there is a great thundering, and the tearing of the veil. He is the one whose name is like the roaring of mighty waters, and whose voice is as the rushing of many cataracts. He is the one who commands the four winds, and the four winds obey him. He is the one who causes the earth to quake, and the mountains to be cast down. He is the one who will come in the last days, and will judge the living and the dead."
