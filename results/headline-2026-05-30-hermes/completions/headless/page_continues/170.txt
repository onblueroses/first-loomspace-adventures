 “This is a very old invocation, and there is some doubt as to whether it should be used with the above ritual or not. It is sometimes known as the ‘Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia’, and sometimes as the ‘Invocation of the Bornless One’, but the latter title is not particularly appropriate, as the invocation refers to ‘the Headless One’.”

The invocation is also known as the “Headless Invocation”, “Bornless Ritual”, or “Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia” and is a version of the Stele of Ieu the Conqueror (PGM V. 96-172).

## In popular culture

* In the manga To Aru Majutsu no Index, the main character Kamijou Touma casts the "Imagine Breaker" spell which contains the line "I am the one who begets and destroys" in reference to the Bornless Ritual.
* In the novel series A Certain Magical Index, the spell Imagine Breaker is derived from the Bornless Ritual.
