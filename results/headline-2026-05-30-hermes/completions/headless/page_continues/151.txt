 "This spell is to be used against daimons of tempest, rain, and thunder." In the margins is the following: "This is the name by which Solomon bound daimons and their legions in a brass vessel."

This spell is found in the Greek Magical Papyri, specifically PGM V. 96-172. The invocation is an exorcism, and the rubric claims that it drives out every daimon, malignity, and evil thing.
