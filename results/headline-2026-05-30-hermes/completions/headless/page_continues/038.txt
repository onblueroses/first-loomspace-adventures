 "This invocation is to be performed after the Preliminary Invocation, and before thee, the fire and the altar, using the posture given in the figure. If not completely successful at first, thou shalt repeat these at the end of the First Conjuration, not only once, but twice, thrice, and even many times."

In the 1896 edition, Crowley added a note that "the two last lines" (i.e. "Heart girt with a serpent is my name. IAO SABAO") are not part of the invocation, but are used to identify the "Magus" with "the Angel of the Aethyr".
