 "The above conjuration being said, fumigate with frankincense and myrrh, and make a burnt offering of the incense, and repeat the conjuration until the end, and then say the following words, and they will appear."

In the following section, the "Headless One" is invoked to perform a specific task, in this case to bind a spirit named Aoth to the conjurer's will. This invocation is repeated three times, with the magician threatening to burn the spirit if he does not comply:

Subject to me all daimons, so that every daimon, whether heavenly or aerial or earthly or subterranean or terrestrial or aquatic, might be obedient to me and every enchantment and scourge which is from God.

I conjure you, Aoth, by the Headless One, the one who is headless, having his sight in his feet.

I am Aphruloo, the one who possesses the immortal fire.
I am Ubarazarauoa.
I am Aphroo.
I am Ouazarau.
I am Ouar.
I am Ouo.
I am Ia.
I am the Lord of Hosts, who is standing on the pole of the earth.
I am Ia.
