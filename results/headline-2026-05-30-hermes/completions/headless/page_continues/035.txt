The invocation seems to be a prayer to Set, the Destroyer, to destroy the illusion of the universe, and to reveal the truth.” In a commentary to the invocation, Crowley identified “Mosheh” as Moses and “Aphrael” as the “god of the Abyss,” and explained that the invocation consisted of “barbarous names” that were “without meaning, save that they are traditional.” The invocation is based on a Greek papyrus in the British Museum, written between the second and fourth centuries CE, which was translated into English by Charles Wycliffe Goodwin. Goodwin’s translation was published in 1852 in the second volume of an extensive work on Egyptology, Egypt’s Place in Universal History, by Christian Carl Josias von Bunsen.
