 "This prayer, said three times, with the words of the spell added to it, will also serve to bind." The spell referred to here is a series of twenty-two magical words, each of which is associated with a letter of the Hebrew alphabet. The prayer and the spell are supposed to be recited over an amulet, which is then used to protect the wearer from various dangers and to grant them power over spirits.

The prayer itself is a mix of Jewish, Christian, and Gnostic elements, with a heavy emphasis on the power of the divine name and the authority of the speaker as a prophet or messenger of God. The speaker identifies themselves with various divine figures, including the "Headless One" (a common epithet for the Gnostic deity Abrasax), the "Mighty One," the "Truth," and the "Grace of the World." The prayer also includes several magical names and words of power, some of which are derived from Hebrew or Greek, while others are more obscure in origin. |
