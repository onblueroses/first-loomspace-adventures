 "The above conjuration being said over the image of the Headless One, and the prayer here following being uttered by the operator, he shall attain to the knowledge of all arts and sciences, and to the power of working every operation."

---

**Prayer**: I call upon Thee, Headless One, that didst create Earth and Heaven, that didst create Night and Day, that didst create the Water and the Wind, that didst create the Stars and the Planets, that didst create the Darkness and the Light.

Thou art the Headless Aion, who hast thy Sight in thine own Feet. Thou art the Power of the East, who possesseth the Power of the West, who possesseth the Power of the South, who possesseth the Power of the North.

Thou art the Power of the Air, who hast thy Breath in all the Winds, who hast thy Voice in all the Thunder-Storms, who hast thy Sight in all the Lightnings, who hast thy Touch in all the Earthquakes.
