 "The above conjuration is from the Greek Magical Papyrus of Alexandria. The text may be found in its original form in Karl Preisendanz, Papyri Graecae Magicae (Stuttgart, 1928), p. 12ff. It is the invocation of the Bornless One, a god without a beginning and without an end, the god who is the very essence of the sun and of fire. The word 'Akephalos' is Greek, and means 'headless', but it also contains the name of the sun god, 'Akephalos', which means 'eternal'. Thus, the Bornless One is the Eternal Sun, the god of the sun and of fire. The invocation is a powerful one, and should be used with care."
