 "This prayer is appropriate to invoke the Headless One." This was followed by a further prayer to be used when the magician was confronted with a spirit he wished to subdue:

I call upon thee, Headless One, high born of great might, the Ram that overcometh the vast and infinite, Holy One, Father of the Æons, unutterable, ineffable.

IAŌ SABAŌTH ABRASAX.

I call upon thee, whom the winds fear, that didst create all things by the power of thy voice.

I call upon thee, Angel of Truth, Orobas, Bartzabel, I am he that didst invoke thee in the stillness of Darkness.

Thou art the great Serpent, turning back upon thyself; thou art the Raging One; thou art he that travellest through the Heavens and dost order the Universe.

Thou art the Seed of the World, the Opener of the Way.

Open the Way for me, so that I may pass through and see thy face, that I may sing thy praise, for Thou art my Father, my Brother, and my Son.

AR, I call upon thee.

AOTH, ABAOTH, BASUM, IS
