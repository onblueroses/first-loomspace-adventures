 "This is the Lord of the Gods; this is the Lord of the Universe; this is He whom the winds fear; this is He who made all things by the command of His voice, Lord, King, Master, Helper, IAOUEI."

It concludes with the instruction, "Say this three times, with the hands in the position of prayer."
