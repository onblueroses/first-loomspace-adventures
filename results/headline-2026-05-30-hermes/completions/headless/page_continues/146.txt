 "This invocation, the translator adds, must be spoken in one breath." The text is given in the original Greek, with a translation by Charles Wycliffe Goodwin, followed by a commentary by the translator. The spell is to "protect the gold in the holiness of my hand for the benefit of my soul." The next page gives a version of the spell which is to protect the bearer "from the anger of the demons of the air."

In the version given in Betz, the text begins: "I summon you, Headless One, who created earth and heaven, who created night and day, you who created light and darkness; you are Osoronnophris, whom no one has ever seen; you are Iabas; you are Iapos; you are the one who is in the ineffable names..."

The invocation continues with an identification of the speaker with the god I am the Mighty One, who possesses the immortal Fire. I am the Truth that hates the deeds of injustice in the world. I am the one whose mouth utterly flames. I am the one who begets and destroys. I am the Grace of the World. "HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT" is my name.
