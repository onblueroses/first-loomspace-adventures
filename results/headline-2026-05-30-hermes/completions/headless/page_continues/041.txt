 "The above conjuration is to be said first towards the East, then towards the North, then towards the West, and then towards the South. It is to be repeated four times over in each quarter. At the first repetition, say the words very slowly and distinctly, vibrating them within the body so that every part of it shall feel them. At the second repetition, whisper them. At the third repetition, simply think them. At the fourth repetition, say them inwardly in the 'magical voice'. This conjuration is particularly suitable for invoking the elemental forces, and may be used for banishing as well as invoking. It is a very powerful formula, and is highly regarded by those who have made a study of these things."
