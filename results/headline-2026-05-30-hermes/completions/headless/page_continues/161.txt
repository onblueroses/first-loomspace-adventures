 

This, then, is the ritual of the Headless One, as used by Crowley. The ritual is very loosely based on the actual spell which is commonly called the "Headless Rite" or the "Bornless Ritual". The original Greco-Egyptian spell instructs the reader to chant the following invocation:

I call upon you, Headless One, who created earth and heaven, who created night and day, you who created the realm of light and the realm of darkness; you are Osoronnophris whom none has ever seen; you are Iabas; you are Iapos; you have divided into the two parts, you have distinguished the just and the unjust; you have made female and male; you have revealed seed and fruits; you have made men love each other and hate each other.

The original spell continues with further occult invocations and names. The original invocation ends with the instruction "say the whole spell in a loud voice" (i.e. chant the entire Headless Rite).
