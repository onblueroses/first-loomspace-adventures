# Chapter 7
# The Secret of the Cipher
As we have seen, the text of the Bornless Ritual is written in Greek, except for one section, which is written in a cipher. The deciphering of this text is the key to the ritual and the invocation of the Headless One.
The cipher is a simple substitution code. The letters are exchanged for Greek letters with marks above them. This was a common practice in ancient times to hide the true meaning
