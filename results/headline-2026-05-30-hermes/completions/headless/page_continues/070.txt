### The Invocation of the Heart Girt with a Serpent

This invocation is identical to the one in Liber Samekh, with the exception of the name of the magician, which is to be replaced with one's own. The rubric states that this invocation is to be "said secretly in one's heart, and not aloud."

Thee that didst create the Earth and the Heavens.
Thee that didst create the Night and the Day.
