 "At this point, the ritual instructs the aspirant to identify with the godform of the Headless One, and to recite the following invocation:"

Come, then, and follow, for my Name is known unto you, and under my Name shall every portrait of God be bowed. For I am the Lord of the Highest, and under my Name shall every portrait of God be bowed. For I am the Lord of the Highest, and under my Name shall every portrait of God be bowed.

The godform of the Headless One represents a state of divine consciousness beyond the limitations of individual identity, and is associated with the concept of the All-Father or the supreme god in various mythologies. The ritual aims to invoke this state of consciousness in the aspirant, allowing them to access divine power and authority.

The ritual then concludes with the following words:"

I am the Headless Daimon with sight in his feet.
I am the Mighty One, who possesses the immortal Fire.
I am the Truth that hates the deeds of injustice in the world.
I am the one whose mouth utterly flames.
I am the one who begets and destroys.
I am the Grace of the World.

HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name.
