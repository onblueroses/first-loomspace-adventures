## Interpretations

### The Headless One

The "Headless One" is mentioned in the invocation as both the god being invoked and the title of the magician. The Headless One is also mentioned in the ancient Greek magical texts which comprise the Greek Magical Papyri. In the Papyri, the Headless One is referred to as the "Headless God".
