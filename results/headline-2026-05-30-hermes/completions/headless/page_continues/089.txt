The “headless” description also appears in the other “spell” which Crowley believed to be a version of the invocation on the Stele of Jeu the Underworld, the invocation of the “Bornless One.” This invocation begins:

Thee I invoke, the Bornless One.
Thee that didst create the Earth and the Heavens,
Thee that didst create the Night and the Day,
Thee that didst create the Darkness and the Light.

Thou art Asar Un-Nefer, my Lord Fafnir, the Breath of my Soul, the Mighty Seps in the Universe, the Immortal Fire in the Immortal Pyramids.
