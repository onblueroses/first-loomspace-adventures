 "This invocation, in its Greek form, was said to have been used by Moses, and by him passed to Solomon; but the language is far later than the time of either, and contains many Gnostic expressions."

## In popular culture

In the 2022 film Infinity Pool, the character of Gabi Bauer (Mia Goth) recites the beginning of the invocation in her native language of German. The film's director, Brandon Cronenberg, explained that he incorporated the incantation into the film as he felt it tied into the film's themes of duality and transformation.
