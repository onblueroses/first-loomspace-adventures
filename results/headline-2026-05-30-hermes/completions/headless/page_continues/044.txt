This invocation is of value in evoking the Headless One, the Mighty One, the Overcoming of Obstacles, the God Bes. It is to be delivered in the day and the hour of Mercury, when the influence of that planet is best for works of Material Advancement, and most inimical to those who would oppose the Will of the Adept. The Adept must therefore be careful to utter this invocation in such a way as to avoid making any communication with the adverse forces which it dominates. He should therefore avoid all names of Chthonic or Typhonian nature, and confine himself to the Names of the Supreme and Formless One, the Creator of Things in the Beginning, such as those which are the Keys of the Rite.
