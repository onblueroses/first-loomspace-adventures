 "This is the Lord of the Gods; this is the Lord of the Universe; this is He whom the winds fear; this is He who made voice by His commandment; this is He whose voice is the manifestation of His will; this is He whose voice the gods obey in heaven; this is He to whom every ruler bows down; this is He who is Lord of all things, king, ruler, and helper.

Hear me, and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me."
