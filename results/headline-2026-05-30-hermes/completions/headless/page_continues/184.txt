### Later developments

In modern times, the Headless Rite has been adapted for use by various occultists, such as Aleister Crowley and Lon Milo DuQuette.

Crowley included a version of the Headless Rite in his Liber Samekh, a ritual designed to invoke the Holy Guardian Angel. Crowley's version of the rite includes an invocation to the "Angel of AIRA" and the use of barbarous names, similar to the original rite. However, Crowley's version also includes elements from the Goetic ritual tradition and from his own Thelemic beliefs.
