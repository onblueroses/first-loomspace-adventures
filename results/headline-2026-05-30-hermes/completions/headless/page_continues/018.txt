 "This invocation is said to have been used by Moses. It seems to me to bear a resemblance to the invocation used by the Æons in the Gnostic fragments of the Bruce Codex."

This version of the invocation has been known to work, although it is not as efficacious as the one in the PGM. The version in the PGM, however, is difficult to use because it is in Greek and Coptic, so I have provided a reconstruction of it in English below.

### Reconstruction in English

I call upon you, Headless One,

You who created Earth and Heaven,
You who created Night and Day,
You who created Darkness and Light.

You are Osoronnophris, who no one has ever seen.
You are Iabas. You are Iapos.
You are the one who hates a shameful deed.
You are the one who did the N.N. deed.
You are the one who distinguishes between the just and the unjust.
You are the one who made the male and the female.
You are the one who made the seed and the fruit.
You are the one who made people love each other and hate each other.

I am Moses your prophet, to whom you have transmitted your mysteries celebrated by Israel.
