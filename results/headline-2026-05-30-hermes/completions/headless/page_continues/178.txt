 "This is the Lord of the Gods. This is the Lord of the Universe. This is He Whom the Winds fear. This is He Who, having made Voice by His commandment, is Lord of all Things, King, Ruler and Helper."

---

[1]: This is the true name of the god. It is to be written in the semicircle on the left, and pronounced as if it were "Ischurah" when invoking.

[2]: This is the true name of the goddess. It is to be written in the semicircle on the right, and pronounced as if it were "Aphrodite" when invoking. [Note: In English, this would be pronounced "Af-ruh-die-tee".]

[3]: The top point of the triangle should be facing Eastward. [Note: This is the direction traditionally associated with Air, and the direction the sun rises in.]

[4]: The bottom point of the triangle should be facing Westward. [Note: This is the direction traditionally associated with Water, and the direction the sun sets in.] [5]: The left point of the triangle should be facing Northward. [Note: This is the direction traditionally associated with Earth.]
