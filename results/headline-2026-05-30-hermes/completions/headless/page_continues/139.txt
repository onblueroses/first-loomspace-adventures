 “The above conjuration being said, the Spirits will appear before you. Then say the following:”

Speak unto me, and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.

I invoke Thee, the Terrible and Invisible God who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit: Arogogorobrao, Sochou, Modorio, Phalarchao, Ooo.
