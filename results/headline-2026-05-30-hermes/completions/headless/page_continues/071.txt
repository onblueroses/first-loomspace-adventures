 

This is He who hath the Holy Pentalpha, the Wreathe of the Gods. 

Thee I invoke, the Bornless One. Thee that didst create the Earth and the Heavens, Thee that didst create the Night and the Day, Thee that didst create the Darkness and the Light.

I am the Resurrection and the Life. I am the Harlot of Heaven. I am the Watcher and the Nether Blackness. I am that which is seen and not seen. I am the Silence and the Darkness. I am the Reconciler between them.

I am the Heart Girt with a Serpent, I am the Lion-Serpent Sun, I am the great Beast of the Book of the Law, I am the Lord of the Double Wand of Power, I am the Viper that giveth Knowledge unto the World.
