 "The above conjuration is from the 'Leyden Papyrus,' a Greek translation of an ancient Egyptian work on magic written in demotic by a scribe named Harpocration of Panopolis. The original document dates to the second or third century A.D. and is a list of spells, rituals, and conjurations to be used against various ills and enemies. The manuscript is in the Leyden Museum in Holland and was published in 1828 under the title of Papyri Graecae Magicae. The translation above is by Alan Gardiner, from his 1915 paper, 'A Gnostic Liturgical Fragment.'"
