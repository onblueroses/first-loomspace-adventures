 "This is the Lord of the Gods.
This is the Lord of the Universe.
This is He whom the winds fear.
This is He, who having made voice by His commandment is Lord of all things, King, Ruler and Helper.
Hear me and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me."

### Papyrus 46

The papyrus itself is housed at the University of Michigan, although it is not on public display. The papyrus is missing pieces, but is otherwise fairly complete, with the first six invocations almost completely intact. The beginning of the seventh invocation, starting with "I am the headless daimon..." is present, but the rest of the invocation is missing. The eighth invocation is entirely missing, and the ninth invocation is mostly intact, with the end of it missing.

## In popular culture

The Headless Rite is mentioned in the 2021 novel The Serpent's Curse by bestselling author Lisa Maxwell.
