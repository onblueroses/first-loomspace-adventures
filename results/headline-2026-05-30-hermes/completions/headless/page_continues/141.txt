 "This prayer is appropriate to invoke the Headless One." The magician is instructed to recite the invocation while holding two "flashing tablets" in his hands, each of which contains a drawn image of the Headless One in the form of a bearded man wearing a cuirass and carrying a spear in his right hand and a shield in his left.

### The invocation in other magical texts

Similar invocations are found in other texts of the Greek Magical Papyri. One such invocation is found in PGM V. 96-172. The invocation is titled the "Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist" or the "Stele of Jeu the Painter". The invocation is intended to summon the god Khnum (referred to here by the epithet "Kmeph") and is performed in an ritual that involves drowning a falcon in a bowl of water and writing with its blood.
