## Symbolism

The Headless Rite is a complex and multi-layered ritual, and its symbolism reflects this complexity. Let's break down some of the key elements:

### The Headless One

The central figure invoked in this ritual is the Headless One, also known as the Bornless One. This figure is paradoxical, both headless and bornless, suggesting a being that exists outside of the normal boundaries of time and space. The Headless One is a form of the divine that transcends human understanding, a representation of the ineffable and unknowable aspects of the divine.

### Creation and Duality

Throughout the invocation, the Headless One is credited with creating the fundamental dualities of the universe: Earth and Heaven, Night and Day, Darkness and Light, female and male, love and hate. This suggests that the Headless One is a creator deity
