 "This is He who hath the Holy Seven." The reference is to the seven vowels, alpha, epsilon, eta, iota, omicron, upsilon, and omega, which are uttered in a kind of howl. The seven vowels represent the gods of the seven planets.

The page concludes with a series of names, some of which are Greek, some Hebrew, and some Egyptian, and some of which are unknown. There are 100 names, which correspond to the 100 letters of the Holy Seven howled 100 times.

The page concludes with the instruction to "say all this with fire and spirit until you complete the 100 letters of the Holy Seven. Then say the prayer which follows."

The prayer is a short prayer of thanks to "the Great and Terrible One," God, followed by another series of names, some of which are recognizably Hebrew, Greek, or Egyptian, and some of which are unknown.

The prayer concludes with the instruction to "say all this with fire and spirit, and the God will be with you, and you will be strong in spirit."
