Visitor: What is the first duty of the Neophyte?

Host: To know thyself.

Visitor: And what is the second duty?

Host: To expand thyself.

Visitor: How do I know myself?

Host: By the study of the Holy Qabalah.

Visitor: What is the Holy Qabalah?

Host: It is the science and art of causing change to occur in conformity with will, by means of the secret wisdom of the Tree of Life.

Visitor: And how do I expand myself?

Host: By the right use of the magical instruments and the methods of the Rituals of the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn.

Visitor: What are the chief methods?

Host: Invocation and Evocation.

Visitor: What is the purpose of Invocation?

Host: To draw down and to become a living vessel for the Divine Light.

Visitor: What is the purpose of Evocation?

Host: To call forth and to command spirits of the elements and other angelic beings.

Visitor: What is the ultimate goal of the Great Work?

Host: To obtain the knowledge and conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel, and to transcend the limitations of the personal self.
