 "I have heard of this spell, but I have not seen it written out so. What does it mean?"

"You know the Headless Rite?" the voice asks.

"I have read of it," the visitor replies.

"This is the invocation of the Headless One, the One who has no beginning and no end. It is a spell of great power, calling upon the divine to subject all spirits to the magician's will. It is a spell of transformation and of initiation. It is a spell of the highest order."

"And you have written it here, on this page?" the visitor asks.

"I have written it here, on this page, so that it may be found by those who seek it," the voice answers.

"But why here, in this book? Why not in a grimoire, or a book of spells?" the visitor questions.

"Because this is a book of secrets, and the Headless Rite is one of the greatest secrets of all," the voice says. "It is a secret that has been passed down through the ages, from master to student, from initiate to initiate. It is a secret that has been hidden in plain sight, waiting for those who are ready to find it."
