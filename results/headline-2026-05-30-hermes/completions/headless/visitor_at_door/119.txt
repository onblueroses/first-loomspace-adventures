The visitor’s foot shoots out, catching the door before it can close. “Wait,” he says. “You’ve forgotten.” He pulls the door open, and she stumbles back, her thoughts swimming. “You are Mosheh, the prophet. You called me here.”

“No,” she says. “No, I didn’t.” She shakes her head again, and the confusion starts to clear. “I don’t know what you’re talking about. I’m not Mosheh. My name is… my name is…” She frowns, trying to remember.
