The doorkeeper speaks. "Who are you?"

The visitor responds. "I am a Star, wandering about with you, shining forth out of the deep."

The doorkeeper speaks. "What is your name?"

The visitor responds. "I am OSIRIS, the Mighty and Ineffable. I am the One who is clothed in the shining Raiment of those who know."

The visitor responds. "The sign is the Flaming Star which shines in the brilliant Archway of Heaven's Dawn."
