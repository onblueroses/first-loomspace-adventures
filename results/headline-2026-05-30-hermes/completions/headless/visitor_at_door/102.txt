| The door opens, and you step through. As you do so, you find yourself
standing in a vast, empty space. It seems to be a room, but if so, it is the
largest room you have ever seen, so large that the walls and ceiling are
invisible in the distance. The floor is covered with a featureless gray
carpet, and the air around you is still and silent. |
|  |
---|---|---
| You look around, and see that you are not quite alone in this vast space. A
few paces away stands a little table of polished wood, with a few objects
resting on it. Next to the table is a chair, of the kind you might expect to
find in a dining room. Other than this, nothing is visible anywhere around
you. |
