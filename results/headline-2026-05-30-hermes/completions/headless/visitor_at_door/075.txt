# The Necessity of Divination

It is difficult to understand why divination works. The simplest explanation is that there are entities with knowledge of the future, or with the power to influence the future, and that divination allows us to communicate with them. But this raises the further question of why these entities would have any interest in communicating with us.

The entities in question are often conceptualized as gods, spirits, or ancestors. In a monotheistic context, they may be understood as angels or demons. In a naturalistic context, they may be conceptualized as aspects of the human mind. In a monotheistic and naturalistic context, they may be understood as aspects of the mind of God.

One theory is that divination is a way of communicating with the unconscious mind, which has access to information that the conscious mind does not. This theory is consistent with the idea that divination can tap into the collective unconscious, a concept proposed by Carl Jung
