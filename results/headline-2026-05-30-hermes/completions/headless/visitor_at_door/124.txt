 They say:

Come in, and welcome.

The visitor enters. They are greeted with a warm embrace, and the two share a kiss.

The two sit together. They speak of many things.

After a time, the visitor rises. They say:

I must away. My time is not yet come.

The host rises as well, and speaks:

Then let us share a parting kiss, and I shall look for you again when you return.

The two kiss once more. The visitor departs.

The host sits alone for a time. Then they rise, and go to the door. They open it, and step outside, into the night.

The host walks through the streets of the city. They pass by many people, but they do not speak to any of them. They walk until they come to a park.

In the park, they find a bench, and sit down. They look up at the night sky, and the stars above.

The host speaks:

I am alone, but I am not lonely. I have known love, and I will know it again. I am content.

The host sits for a time, looking at the stars. Then they rise, and walk back through the city to their home.
