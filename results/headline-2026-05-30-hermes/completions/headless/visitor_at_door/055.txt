 He says, "I am the one you called. You have my name."

The Magus answers, "How can I be sure you are who you say you are?"

The visitor says, "I have come as you asked, as you commanded. I have come to the one who knows my name."

The Magus says, "Show me a sign, that I may know you are the one I have called."

The visitor replies, "I have come from the void, and I go to the void. You have called me by my true name. You have my name. Say it again, and I will show you a sign."

The Magus speaks the name of the spirit: "Heart Girt with a Serpent."

The visitor's form shimmers and changes. He becomes a great serpent, coiled around the world. His scales are iridescent, shining with all the colors of the rainbow. His eyes are golden, and his tongue flicks in and out, tasting the air.

The Magus gazes upon the spirit in awe and says, "I see you now, Heart Girt with a Serpent. I know you are the one I have called. I greet you in the name of the Headless One, the Mighty One
