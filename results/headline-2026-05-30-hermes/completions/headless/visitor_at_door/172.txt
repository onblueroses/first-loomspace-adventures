 The doorkeeper turns and speaks.

VISITOR: I am the Grace of the World.

DOORKEEPER: The door is opened. Enter.

[The doorkeeper opens the door and the visitor enters. The door is closed behind them.]

### II.

[The visitor is led to a room where the adept is waiting. The adept stands in the center of the room, facing the visitor as they enter.]

ADEPT: Welcome, traveler. I am the Adept, and I have been waiting for you. I see that you have passed the test of the doorkeeper, and so I know that you are ready to learn the secrets of our order.

VISITOR: I am honored to be here, and I am eager to learn. What is the first lesson?

ADEPT: The first lesson is the lesson of the elements. The universe is composed of four elements: fire, water, air, and earth. These elements are the building blocks of all matter, and they are also the building blocks of the human soul.
