 It is the visitor, the one who comes to your door to request your presence and your help. The visitor says:

"Come in peace, O servant of Ablathanalba, and bless my abode. I have come here to-day in the Name of Ablathanalba to do for you such things as you may have in mind, and to cause to come to you the Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba. Step in, therefore, and grant me your blessing, and if there is anything that you need, it shall be done for you."

And you will reply:

"May peace reign over this house, and joy and respect. May the just ones of the Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba, the God of Truth, bless this house. And may all truth and peace be upon those who dwell herein. And if any of the daimons of darkness and evil have lied unto me, may the mighty and terrible Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba smite him and cast him down from this threshold. Amen."
