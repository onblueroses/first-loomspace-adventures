 "Mighty one, I am here to learn your wisdom. I am a humble servant and seeker of truth."

The Headless One replies. "Very well, I shall share my wisdom. But first, you must prove your worth. Solve this riddle:

I am the beginning of eternity, the end of time and space,
The beginning of every end, and the end of every place.
What am I?"

The visitor ponders for a moment before answering. "The letter E."

The Headless One nods. "Correct. You have passed the first test. Now, I shall share with you the first piece of wisdom:

To truly understand the mysteries of the universe, you must first understand yourself. Know thyself, and you shall know the gods and the secrets they hold."

The visitor listens intently, absorbing the Headless One's words. "Thank you, wise one. I shall meditate on this teaching."

The Headless One continues. "There is more. The second piece of wisdom is this:

All things are connected, from the smallest grain of sand to the largest star in the sky. Everything is part of a greater whole, a cosmic web of existence. Understand this, and you will begin to unravel the mysteries of creation."
