 Their voice is strange and distant, and when they speak, the sounds seem to come from everywhere and nowhere at once.

Visitor: What is your wish?

Magician: I seek knowledge of the mysteries, the secrets hidden from mankind.

Visitor: And what will you do with this knowledge?

Magician: I will use it to better understand the universe and my place within it. I seek enlightenment and power.

Visitor: Power can be a dangerous thing. Many have been consumed by their own desires. But if you are certain, then I shall grant you a glimpse. Prepare yourself.

The visitor traces a glowing symbol in the air. It hovers, pulsing with energy. The magician feels a sudden rush of images and sensations flooding their mind. Visions of distant worlds, ancient civilizations, and cosmic beings beyond comprehension. The secrets of the universe laid bare.

After what feels like an eternity, the visions fade. The magician gasps for breath, overwhelmed by the experience. The visitor nods solemnly.

Visitor: You have been shown a mere fraction of the truth. The path to true understanding is long and perilous. But you have taken the first step. Use this knowledge wisely, for it is a heavy burden to bear.

With that, the visitor vanishes,
