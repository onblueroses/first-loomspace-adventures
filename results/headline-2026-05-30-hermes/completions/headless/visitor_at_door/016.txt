 They say:

I am the one in whom the Grace of the World resides.

Come and enter through me, and let me also enter through you.
Give me what you have that is of the Spirit; and take from me what I have that is of the Spirit.

Let us unite our Stars, and let our two Stars become one Star.
Let the Star of Grace descend upon us and fill us with the Light of Grace.

The two embrace, and exchange the kiss of peace. Then they say together:

May the Light of Grace descend!
May the Star of Grace shine within us!

The visitor enters, and the door is closed.

---

The rite is at an end. The space is closed. Let all be as it was before.
