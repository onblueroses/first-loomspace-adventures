 The visitor has come to be healed. The visitor speaks the words that he or she wants to say. He or she may ask for healing or for a wish to be granted.

The priestess takes the visitor's hands, palms upward, and places the paper between them. She then places her hands over the visitor's hands, and says the following invocation:

Hear me, O Lord of All, for I am the servant of the same God, the Highest and the Greatest, of the Ineffable God, who is the One who whispers in the heart of the silence, who created all things, who is the One who cannot be known.

I am the servant of the Great God, who has four heads, the one who cannot be seen, who has no body, who has no bones, who has no legs, who has no face, who has no arms, who is invincible, who rules the South and the North, who created the angels, who is over the angels, who is over everything.

I invoke you, the One who is always glorified, the One who is always hidden, the One who is always unseen, the One who is always unknown.
