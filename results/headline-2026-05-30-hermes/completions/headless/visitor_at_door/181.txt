 The initiate responds:

The visitor: I invoke thee, the Bornless One.

The initiate: Thee, that didst create the Earth and the Heavens;
Thee, that didst create the Night and the Day;
Thee, that didst create the Darkness and the Light.

The visitor: Thou art Osoronnophris, whom no man hath seen at any time.
Thou art Iabou. Thou art Iapos.
Thou art Abaoth. Thou art Sabaoth.
Thou hast distinguished between the just and the unjust.
Thou didst make the female and the male.
Thou didst produce the seed and the fruit.
Thou didst form men to love one another and to hate one another.

The initiate: I am Mosheh thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Ischurael.
Thou didst produce the moist and the dry, and that which nourisheth all created life.

The visitor: Hear Thou me, for I am the Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba; this is Thy true name, handed down to the prophets of Israel.

The initiate: Hear me: Ar, Thiao, Rheibet, Atele
