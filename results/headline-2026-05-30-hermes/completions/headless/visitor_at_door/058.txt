HOST: Of course, please come in. You are most welcome here.

The visitor enters the house, followed by the two figures in black and white robes.

HOST: I see you have brought companions. Are they your friends?

VISITOR: They are more than friends. They are the embodiment of the dual nature of existence. The one in black represents the darkness, the unknown, and the mysteries of life. The one in white represents the light, knowledge, and the truths we seek.

HOST: Fascinating. Please, make yourselves comfortable. I will prepare some tea for us.

The host goes to prepare tea while the visitor and their companions settle in the living room. The figures in black and white robes take seats on either side of the visitor.

HOST (returning with tea): Here we are. Please, help yourselves.

The visitor and their companions take the offered tea and sip it slowly.
