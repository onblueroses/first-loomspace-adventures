 The priestess answers him.

VISITOR: Who is it that I hear?

PRIESTESS: I am the Grace of the World.

V: What is your true name?

P: Heart Girt with a Serpent.

V: I have come to know the mysteries.

P: Then learn the great secret: the secret of the knowledge and conversation of the holy guardian angel.

V: How can I ever find him?

P: Invoke often.

V: Will he hear me?

P: Invoke often.

V: How will I know him?

P: Invoke often.
