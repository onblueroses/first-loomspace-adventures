  The others repeat each line after them.

Visitor:
Come, then, the gods!
Raise up the spell of the Snake!
Show forth the power of the Mighty One!
Guide us to the Grace of the World!
Let the immortal Fire be our companion!

All:
Let the immortal Fire be our companion!

The visitor opens the door and enters.  The ritual continues.

## II. Invocation of the Elements

East:
Hail, Powers of Air, Guardians of the East!
I call upon you, O Forces of Intellect, to witness and guard this rite.

South:
Hail, Powers of Fire, Guardians of the South!
I call upon you, O Forces of Will, to witness and guard this rite.

West:
Hail, Powers of Water, Guardians of the West!
I call upon you, O Forces of Emotion, to witness and guard this rite.

North:
Hail, Powers of Earth, Guardians of the North!
I call upon you, O Forces of Stability, to witness and guard this rite.

All:
The Circle is cast.
So mote it be.

## III. The Litany Against Fear

North:
Fear is the little-death that brings total oblibion.

East:
I will face my fear.
