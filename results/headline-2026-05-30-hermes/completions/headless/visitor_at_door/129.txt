 (Note: The reader may choose to use the text of the "Headless Rite" itself, or an adaptation of the same, as the speech of the visitor at the door.)

Moses: Who are you, and why do you come here?

Visitor: I am an initiate of the mysteries. I have come to bring you a message.

Moses: What message do you bring?

Visitor: I bring the message of the Headless One, the Mighty One, who possesses the immortal Fire.

Moses: What is this message?

Visitor: The message is this: You, Moses, have been chosen to be a prophet of the Most High. You have been given the power to command the spirits of the earth and the heavens. You have been given the power to perform miracles and to lead your people to freedom.

Moses: How can I know that this message is true?

Visitor: You will know it is true when you see the signs and wonders that will follow. You will know it is true when you hear the voice of the Most High speaking to you from the burning bush. You will know it is true when you see the waters of the Red Sea part before you.
