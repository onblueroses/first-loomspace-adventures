 They say:

Mighty and Terrible One, I am not worthy to enter your dwelling. I am not worthy to behold your divine countenance. I am not worthy to kiss your feet, much less to be instructed by your wisdom and grace. I come to you as a supplicant, seeking your blessing and your mercy. I come to you as one who has no power, except that which you grant me. I come to you as one who is ignorant, except for what knowledge you impart to me. I come to you as one who is lost, except for the path you show me. I come to you as one who is afraid, except for the courage you give me. I come to you as one who is weak, except for the strength you lend me. I come to you as one who is blind, except for the light you shine upon me. I come to you as one who is dead, except for the life you breathe into me.
