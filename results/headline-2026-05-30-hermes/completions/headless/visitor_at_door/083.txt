 "Come in," you say, "and let me tell you of the secret of the ages." The visitor enters and you begin to tell the story of the Headless One.

The Headless One is a powerful force, a god of creation and destruction. It is said that the Headless One created the Earth and the Heavens, the Night and the Day, the Darkness and the Light. No one has ever seen the Headless One, but it is known by many names - Osoronnophris, Iabou, Iapos, Abaoth, Sabaoth.

The Headless One is the force behind the mysteries of life, the one who distinguishes between the just and the unjust, who creates love and hate, the moist and the dry. It is the force that nourishes all created life.

You, as Mosheh the prophet, have been entrusted with the mysteries and ceremonies of Ischurael. You know the true name of the Headless One, handed down to the prophets of Israel: Aphrael Ablathanalba.
