"Who are you?" I ask, my voice trembling slightly.

"I am the one you invoked," the man replies, his voice deep and resonant. "I am the Headless One, the Mighty One, the Grace of the World."

I stare at him in disbelief. "But... how? The ritual... it was just a story, a myth."

I swallow hard, my mind racing. "What do you want from me?"
