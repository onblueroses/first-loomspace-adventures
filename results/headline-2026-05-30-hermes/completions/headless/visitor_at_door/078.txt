# 61. The Ultimate Mystery

“Please,” I say. “Have a seat.”

The visitor at the door walks in, and sits down in my living room. He looks exactly like you would expect the Headless One to look; that is, he looks exactly like a perfectly ordinary person.

“Thanks for having me,” he says.

“Of course,” I say. “I’ve been waiting for you.”

“I know,” he says. “I’ve been waiting for you too.”

I take a deep breath.

“So,” I say. “How do we begin?”

“We begin,” he says, “by talking about the Ultimate Mystery.”

I nod. “I’ve been trying to figure it out,” I say. “I’ve been trying to figure out the Ultimate Mystery on my own. But I’m stuck. I’m stuck on the problem of suffering. I’m stuck on the problem of evil. I’m stuck on the problem of death.”

“I know,” he says. “I know you’re stuck. I know you’re suffering. I know you’re afraid.”
