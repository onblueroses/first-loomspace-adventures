 Their voice is soft, not wanting to wake the others in the house.

VISITOR: I have come to deliver a message.

SANDALPHON: Speak.

VISITOR: The Divine is pleased with your work, Sandalphon. They have a new task for you.

SANDALPHON: I listen.

VISITOR: They want you to go to Earth and retrieve a lost artifact. It is a powerful tool, one that could change the course of history if fallen into the wrong hands.

SANDALPHON: What is this artifact?

VISITOR: It is a ancient scroll, containing the true name of God. With this name, one could command the heavens and the earth, bend reality to their will. It was hidden away long ago, but recent events have brought it close to the surface. You must find it before others do.

SANDALPHON: I understand. I will leave at once.

VISITOR: Be careful, Sandalphon. There are forces at work that would stop at nothing to possess this power. Trust no one.

SANDALPHON: I will be vigilant. Thank you for this message.

The visitor nods and leaves. Sandalphon sits in silence for a moment, contemplating the task ahead
