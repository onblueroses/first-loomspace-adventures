 He is tall, and pale, and his eyes are dark and deep. I cannot see his face. He wears a black cloak, and a hood, and his voice is like the wind.

"I am the one you have called," he says. "I am the Headless One, the Bornless One, the One who dwells in the Void Place of the Spirit."

I bow my head in reverence. "I am honored by your presence, O Headless One," I say. "I have called you here to seek your wisdom and your power."

The Headless One nods. "I know why you have called me," he says. "You seek to understand the mysteries of the universe, the secrets of life and death, the power to bend the elements to your will."

"Yes," I say. "I seek knowledge, and power, and the ability to shape the world around me."

The Headless One laughs, and his laughter is like the rumble of distant thunder. "You are bold," he says. "But boldness alone will not grant you what you seek. You must prove yourself worthy."

"I am ready," I say. "I will do whatever is necessary."
