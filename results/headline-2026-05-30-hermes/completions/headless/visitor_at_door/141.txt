 "What is your name?" they ask.

"I am the Headless One," I say. "I am the Mighty One, who possesses the immortal Fire. I am the Truth that hates the deeds of injustice in the world. I am the one whose mouth utterly flames. I am the one who begets and destroys. I am the Grace of the World."

The visitor nods. "I have come to seek your wisdom," they say. "I am lost and confused, and I need guidance."

I smile. "You have come to the right place," I say. "I am the one who can help you find your way. But first, you must answer a riddle."

The visitor looks puzzled. "A riddle?" they ask.

"Yes," I say. "A riddle that will test your wisdom and your courage. Are you ready?"

The visitor takes a deep breath. "I am ready," they say.

"Good," I say. "Here is the riddle:

'I am the beginning of eternity, the end of time and space,
The beginning of every end, and the end of every place.
What am I?'"

The visitor thinks for a moment. "Is it the letter 'E'?" they ask.

I smile.
