 They say:

I am the one who knocks upon the door. The one who seeks entry.

The one who presides says:

Come in, in the name of the Lord of the Aeon.

The visitor enters, and says:

I come in the Power of the Light.
I come in the Mercy of the Light.
I come in the Wisdom of the Light.
I come in the Majesty of the Light.

The one who presides says:

How do you know us?

The visitor says:

By the secret signs and tokens given to me.

The one who presides says:

What is your name?

The visitor says:

My name is [name of the visitor].

The one who presides says:

Welcome, [name of the visitor], in the name of the Lord of the Aeon.

The one who presides says to all present:

Let us now take a moment to remember those who could not be with us tonight.

All present observe a moment of silence.

The one who presides says:

In the Name of the Lord of the Aeon, let us adore the Lord of the Universe.

All present say:

Holy art Thou, Lord of the Universe.
Holy art Thou, whom Nature hath not formed.
Holy art Thou, the Vast and the Mighty
