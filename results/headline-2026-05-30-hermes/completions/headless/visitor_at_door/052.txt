 The temple resounds with the voices of the gods. The priest in the shrine opens the door, and admits the visitor. He bows in reverence to the gods, and says:

Priest: I am a voice in the wind, and a moaning in the Silence.
I am a desolation in the land, and a midnight in the sun.
I am a shadow in the light, and a darkness in the day.
I am the presence of things absent, and the memory of all that is to come.

The Voice of the Gods: What do you seek in the temple of the gods?

The Visitor: I seek the knowledge of the gods, and the secrets of the hidden shrine.

The Voice of the Gods: The knowledge of the gods is hidden in the hearts of men, and the secrets of the shrine are veiled in the mystery of the temple.

The Visitor: How may I attain to the knowledge of the gods, and the secrets of the hidden shrine?

The Voice of the Gods: By the purification of your heart, and the consecration of your life to the service of the gods.
