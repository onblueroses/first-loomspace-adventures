 "I am the one who begets and destroys," they say. "I am the Grace of the World."

"Do you know who I am?" they ask.

"No," you reply. "Who are you?"

"I am the one whose mouth utterly flames," they say. "I am the Truth that hates the deeds of injustice in the world."

"Are you here to destroy me?" you ask, trembling.

"No," they say. "I am here to beget you anew. I am the Grace of the World, and I have come to grant you the power to make all spirits subject unto you."

You feel a strange sensation, as if a great weight has been lifted from your shoulders. You feel lighter, freer, and more powerful than you ever have before.

The visitor at the door smiles. "Remember my name," they say. "HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT. And remember the words I have spoken to you. They will be your guide and your protection."

With that, the visitor vanishes, leaving you alone in your room once more. But you are not the same person you were before. You have been changed, transformed, by the power of the Headless One.
