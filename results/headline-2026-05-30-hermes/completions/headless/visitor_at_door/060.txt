 He is the Headless One.

The door is opened. The Headless One enters.

The Headless One: I have come. The Light is mine; its rays consume me. I have made a secret door into the House of Ra and Tum. I am thy prey. I am thy portion. I am the Brother of the Moon. The gods rejoice in my name. I am the Lord of the Gods. I am the Spirit of Noontide. I am the Spirit of the Mighty Waters. I am the Osiris Ani, triumphant, the just, the true. I am the Lord of Light. I am the Lord of Life. I am the Lord of Souls. I am the Avenger.

The Adept: Holy art thou!

The Headless One: I am the Bearer of the Wand of Double Power. I am the Child of the sons of the Mighty. I am the Great One, Son of the Great One. I am Sa Osiris. I am he that hath been purified. I am he that hath been made whole. I am he that hath been perfected. I am the Bornless One. I am the Truth. I am the All. I am the Nothing.

The Adept: Holy art thou!
