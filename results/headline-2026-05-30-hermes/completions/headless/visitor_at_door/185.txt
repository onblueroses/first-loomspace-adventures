 She is the officer of the temple. She speaks with the voice of Hermes.

Officer: In the Beginning was the Word, the Logos, who encompasses and forms all things. In the Beginning was the Abyss of Chaos, and the Darkness of Night. The Light did not yet exist. The Breath of Spirit, the Pneuma, the Ruach, the Pneuma, the Spiritus vibrated alone in the Infinite Emptiness.

And the Spirit spoke the Word. And the Word was Life, Zoë and Zoe, and the Life was the Light, the Phos, the Lux, the Aur, of Humanity.

And the Light of the Divine Sun shines in the hearts of humankind.

The Word of the Spirit is the Book of the Law. The Word of the Spirit is Do what thou wilt.

The Divine Will is the Will of each Individual. The Divine Will is the True Will. The Divine Will is the Magical Will. The Divine Will is the Will of the Divine Sun, the Christ, the Daimon, the Augoeides, the Genius, the Jechidah, the Atman, the Great Self, the Yechidah, the Khabs, the Hadit, the Divine Self, the God-Self
