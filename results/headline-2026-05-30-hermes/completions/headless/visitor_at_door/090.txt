## XII. The Hidden Charioteer

When you reach the top of the mountain, you will find the Charioteer.

The Charioteer sits upon his chariot, pulled by two horses: one black and one white. The horses are wild and fight against each other, but the Charioteer holds them in check with his whip.

The Charioteer wears a robe of pure white, and a veil of pure black. His eyes are hidden by the veil, but you can feel his gaze upon you.

"Who are you?" you ask.

"I am the one who guides the chariot of the soul," the Charioteer replies. "I am the one who brings balance to the world."

"What is your name?" you ask.

"My name is hidden," the Charioteer replies. "But you may call me by the name of my office: Charioteer."

"Why do you wear a veil?" you ask.

"The veil represents the mystery of the soul," the Charioteer replies. "It is the barrier between the known and the unknown, the seen and the unseen."
