 The priest answers.

Visitor: Whom have I the pleasure of addressing?

Priest: The Headless One.

Visitor: Do what thou wilt shall be the whole of the Law.

Priest: Love is the law, love under will.

Visitor: What is your name?

Priest: Heart Girt with a Serpent.

Visitor: What is your office?

Priest: I am the Priest of the Princes.

Visitor: What is your duty?

Priest: To teach the secret wisdom of the Snake that is coiled at the base of the spine.

Visitor: Are you a god?

Priest: I am the Snake that giveth Knowledge & Delight and bright glory, and stir the hearts of men with drunkenness. To worship me take wine and strange drugs whereof I will tell my prophet, & be drunk thereof! They shall not harm ye at all. It is a lie, this folly against self. The exposure of innocence is a lie. Be strong, o man! lust, enjoy all things of sense and rapture: fear not that any God shall deny thee for this.

Visitor: Will you lead me to the Princes?

Priest: I am the Heart; and the Snake is entwined About the invisible core
