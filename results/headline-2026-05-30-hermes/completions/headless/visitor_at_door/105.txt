 "I am that one who is summoned by the magus. What is your will?"

The magus responds, "It is my will to know the secret of the perfection of the microcosm."

The visitor at the door speaks. "The secret of the perfection of the microcosm is the knowledge of the True Will. The True Will is the divine purpose of the individual, the unique expression of the divine in each person. It is the innermost desire and the highest aspiration, the thing that one must do to fulfill one's potential and to realize one's true nature. The secret of the perfection of the microcosm is to discover and to accomplish this True Will, to become what one is meant to be, to manifest the divine in oneself and in one's life. This is the Great Work, the Magnum Opus, the ultimate goal of the spiritual path."

The magus responds, "How may I know my True Will?"

The visitor at the door speaks. "To know your True Will, you must first silence the clamor of the false will, the desires and fears that arise from the ego and the unconscious. You must still the mind and the emotions, and listen to the still, small voice within, the voice of the divine spark
