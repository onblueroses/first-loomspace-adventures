 The host responds.

HOST: In the name of the Lord of the Universe, and in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ, and in the name of the Holy Spirit, come in peace, O noble visitor. Thou art welcome; the peace of the Lord be with you.

VISITOR: And with thy spirit.

HOST: May thy coming be in peace, O noble visitor. May the blessing of God be upon thee, and the blessing of all the holy saints. Art thou of the company of the blessed?

VISITOR: I am.

HOST: Which company?

VISITOR: The company of the holy archangel Michael.

HOST: What seekest thou?

VISITOR: I seek the Lord Jesus Christ.

HOST: For what purpose?

VISITOR: To serve Him and to adore Him.

HOST: Enter in, in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ.

VISITOR: Thanks be to God.

HOST: What is thy name?

VISITOR: The angel Samael.

HOST: Whence comest thou?

VISITOR: From the land of the rising sun.

HOST: Whither goest thou?

VISITOR: To the land of the setting sun.

HOST: With whom dost thou dwell?

VISITOR: With the holy archangel Michael.
