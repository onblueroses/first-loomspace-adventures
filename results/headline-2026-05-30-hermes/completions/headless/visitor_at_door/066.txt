 They say:
I am the one who begets and destroys.
I am the Grace of the World.

HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name.

IAŌ SABAŌTH.

And now, the door opens. It opens for you. It opens for all who speak the words of power, who seek the mysteries, who love wisdom and the world.

The door opens for you, and you step through.

---

# II.

You step into a room. It is dark, but your eyes adjust. There is a table, and on the table there is a book. The book is open.

You look at the book, and you see that it is written in a language you do not understand. But as you look, the words seem to shift and change, and suddenly you can read them.

The book says:

In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.

You look up from the book, and you see that the room is no longer dark. There is a light, a bright light, and it is coming from the book.

You look back at the book, and you see that the words are changing again. They say:

The Word is the light of the world.
