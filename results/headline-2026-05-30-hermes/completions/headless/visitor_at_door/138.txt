 “I am here to pay my respects to the Great Master.”

“Ah,” I say. “You’ve come to the wrong place. I am just a student, and this is just a small and unimportant school.”

“No,” says the visitor. “I have heard of your wisdom. I have come to learn from you.”

“You have been misinformed,” I say. “I am not wise. I know very little. What I have learned, I have learned from my students. If you would learn from me, I would have you learn from them.”

“Very well,” says the visitor. “I will learn from your students.”

I bring the visitor to the students. “Here,” I say, “meet my students. They are great teachers. They have taught me much. If you learn from them, you will be wise.”

The visitor looks at the students. They are young, and they are foolish, and they are learning.
