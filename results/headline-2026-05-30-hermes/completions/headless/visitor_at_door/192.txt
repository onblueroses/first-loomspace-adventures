 The doorkeeper responds.

Doorkeeper: Who are you?

Visitor: I am one with HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT.

Doorkeeper: What is your name?

Visitor: My name is as the breath of my mouth, the working of my limbs, the beating of my heart. It is unspeakable, for it is unspoken.

Doorkeeper: What do you want?

Visitor: To pass within.

Doorkeeper: Silence! The Word is sacrificed in the heart of the Temple.

The doorkeeper allows the visitor to pass within. They are now within the sacred space, which may be a room, or a circle drawn upon the ground, or any other area set apart for this purpose.

The visitor lights the first candle.

Visitor: I light this candle in the name of Air. Let it burn with the power of the East, the power of the rising Sun and the morning wind. Let it burn with the power of thought, of wisdom, of words and of the creative spirit.

The visitor lights the second candle.

Visitor: I light this candle in the name of Fire. Let it burn with the power of the South, the power of the midday Sun and the desert heat. Let it burn with the power of will, of passion
