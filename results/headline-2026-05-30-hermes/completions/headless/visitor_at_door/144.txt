 The door opens.

Come in, and know me better, man.

The visitor enters.

I am the Ghost of Christmas Present. Look upon me. Touch me. See that I am real.

You have never seen the like of me before?

Oh, no. No, I have not. Spirit, I fear you more than any spectre I have seen tonight.

I am the Spirit of Christmas Present. I am here to show you the true meaning of Christmas.

Rise, and walk with me.

They walk together, out into the world.

I have but a short time upon this Earth. My life upon this globe is very brief. It ends tonight. My time is nearly gone.

You have one hundred and eighty brothers, as I take it. Is that so?

One hundred and eighty-one, including me.

A tremendous family to provide for. Is it not so?

But I have always done so. I have always kept the day. I have always kept Christmas.

You have labored hard to keep it, I am sure. But you have not kept it in your heart. That is where the true meaning of Christmas lies.
