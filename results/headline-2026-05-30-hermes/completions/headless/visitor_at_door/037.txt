 The angel Mosheh remains silent. The visitor holds up their right hand in the Sign of Benediction.

Come in, in the name of the Lord. I bless and consecrate this place for our workings.

The visitor enters and takes a seat at the table.

The angel Mosheh speaks.

In the name of the Lord, I say unto you: Before you may proceed with the Invocation of the Highest, you must first invoke the elemental powers of the universe, and balance them within your sphere. Only then may you proceed to the final invocation.

The visitor speaks.

I invoke the powers of the elements as follows:

Yod. The Fire of the Wand of the East.
Heh. The Water of the Cup of the West.
Vau. The Air of the Dagger of the South.
Heh (final). The Earth of the Pentacle of the North.

And now I declare the elements to be balanced within my sphere.

The angel Mosheh speaks.

You have invoked the elemental powers of the universe, and balanced them within your sphere. Now, you may proceed to the final invocation.

The visitor speaks.

I invoke the Highest by the formula of IAO:

I. Yod. Isis. The Mother of all Things. The All-Dev
