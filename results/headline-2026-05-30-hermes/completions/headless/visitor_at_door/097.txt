 “Is it not written in your law, ‘I said, ye are gods’?”

“Indeed it is,” I say. “The law is the five books of Moses, the Torah. And it is written in the Psalms, which is the law as well. Psalm 82:6, to be precise.”

The visitor nods. “And is it not written in the law that ‘the mountains melted like wax at the presence of the LORD’?”

“Yes,” I say, “that is in the Psalms as well. Psalm 97:5.”

The visitor at the door continues. “And is it not written in the law, ‘I am a god to Pharaoh, and Aaron my brother shall be my prophet.’?”

I nod. “Yes, that is in the law as well. Exodus 7:1.”

The visitor at the door smiles. “And is it not written in the law, ‘I will raise them up a prophet from among their brethren, like unto thee, and I will put my words in his mouth, and he shall speak unto them all that I shall command him.’”

“Yes,” I say, “that is in the Torah, Deuteronomy 18:18.”
