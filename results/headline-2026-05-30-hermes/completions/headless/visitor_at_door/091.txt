 "You are not Mosheh," he says. "You are not the prophet of Israel. You are not the Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba. You are not the Headless Daimon with sight in his feet. You are not the one who begets and destroys. You are not the Grace of the World. You are not named HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT."

"Who am I, then?" asks the man.

"You are a man, and your name is Thaniel Gray," says the visitor. "You are a man who has sought power, and now the power has come to you. But you are not prepared for it, and you will be destroyed by it."

Thaniel Gray looks at the visitor, and he sees that the visitor is not a man, but a spirit. "Who are you?" he asks.

"I am the one who has been sent to guide you," says the spirit. "I am the one who will teach you the true nature of power, and how to wield it without being destroyed by it."

Thaniel Gray nods, and he invites the spirit into his home. "Teach me," he says.
