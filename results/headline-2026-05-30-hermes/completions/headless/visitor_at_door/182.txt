 "I, too, am the Grace of the World," he says.

The visitor speaks again. "Come in, and know me better, man." He steps inside, leaving the door ajar. "I bring you peace and glad tidings of great joy."

The man is stunned. "I--I don't understand. Who are you?"

"I am the one you invoked. I am the Headless One, the Mighty One, the Truth, the Grace of the World. I have many names, but you may call me Iaosabaoth."

"But--why have you come? Why are you here?"

"I have come because you invoked me, because you called me, because you have need of me. I have come to bring you peace, and glad tidings of great joy."

"But--I don't understand. I don't know what to do."

"Do not be afraid. I will guide you. I will show you the way. I will teach you the mysteries of the universe, the secrets of the ages. I will give you the power to do great things, to accomplish your heart's desire.
