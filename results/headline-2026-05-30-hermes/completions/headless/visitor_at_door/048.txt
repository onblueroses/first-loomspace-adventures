The visitor enters and takes their place among the others.

All: Let us begin our rites in the ancient ways.

All present will now recite the Hymn to the Bornless One.

I invoke You, the Headless One:

You, who created the Earth and the Heavens;
You, who created the Night and the Day;
You, who created the Darkness and the Light.

You are Osoronnophris, whom no man has seen at any time.
You are Iabas. You are Iapos.
You are Abaoth. You are Sabaoth.
You have distinguished between the just and the unjust.
You made the female and the male.
You produced the seed and the fruit.
You formed men to love one another and to hate one another.

I am Mosheh your prophet, unto whom You committed Your mysteries, the ceremonies of Ischurael.
You produced the moist and the dry, and that which nourishes all created life.
