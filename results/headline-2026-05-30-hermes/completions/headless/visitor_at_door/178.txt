"Goodnight Mose. Don't stay up too late!" Kat heads back downstairs.

Mose sighs and rubs his eyes. Perhaps he should call it a night. As he starts tidying up his papers, a gust of wind blows through the open window, scattering them across the floor. Mose curses under his breath and hurries to gather them up.
