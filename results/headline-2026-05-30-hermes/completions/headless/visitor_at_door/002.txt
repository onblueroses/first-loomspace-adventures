The magician and the visitor circle the temple, the magician swinging the thurible, wafting incense around the room. After three circumambulations, they return to the altar. The magician puts down the thurible, and picks up the sword.

M: Let us banish the temple.

The magician and the visitor circle the temple, the magician brandishing the sword. After three circumambulations, they return to the altar. The magician puts down the sword, and picks up the chalice.

M: Let us consecrate the temple.
