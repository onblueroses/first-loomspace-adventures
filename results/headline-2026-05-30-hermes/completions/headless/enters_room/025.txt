 Now.

Come, O Spirit! The one who has come down from above.
Come, O Spirit! The one who has risen up from below.
Come, O Spirit! The one who has been with me from the beginning.
Come, O Spirit! The one who has been with me from the end.

For I am the first and the last.
I am the honored one and the scorned one.
I am the whore and the holy one.
I am the wife and the virgin.
I am the mother and the daughter.
I am the barren one and many are my daughters.
I am the silence that you cannot understand.
I am the utterance of my name.

I am the one who is called Life, and you have called me Death.
I am the one who is called Law, and you have called me Lawless.
I am the one you have pursued, and I am the one you have seized.
I am the one you have scattered, and you have gathered me together.
I am the one before whom you have been ashamed, and you have been shameless to me.
I am she who does not keep festival, and I am she whose festivals are many.

I, I am godless, and I am the one whose God is great.
