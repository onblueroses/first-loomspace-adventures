 That which enters the room now is the Headless Daimon with sight in his feet. It is the Mighty One, who possesses the immortal Fire. It is the Truth that hates the deeds of injustice in the world. It is the one whose mouth utterly flames. It is the one who begets and destroys. It is the Grace of the World. Its name is Heart Girt with a Serpent.

May the grace of the world be with you. May the immortal fire be with you. May the truth that hates the deeds of injustice in the world be with you. May the mighty one with sight in his feet be with you. May the headless daimon with sight in his feet be with you. May the one who begets and destroys be with you. May the one whose mouth utterly flames be with you.

Come forth and follow, that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto you.

[Exeunt.] |
