 A cloaked figure, a monstrous figure. A headless figure. I try to turn my gaze away, but it is impossible. I am frozen in place. The figure walks closer. It has its gaze fixed upon me. It has no head, but I feel its gaze. The gaze of the void.

I feel it reaching into me, into my heart. I feel it pulling out the darkness that lies within. The darkness that I have always known was there. The darkness that I have always feared. The darkness that I have always tried to hide.

But now it is exposed. Laid bare before the headless god. And I realize that the darkness is not something to be feared. It is a part of me. A part of all of us. It is the shadow that gives depth to the light.

The headless god speaks, without a mouth to speak through. Its words echo in my mind.

"Embrace the darkness within you. For it is only by accepting your shadow that you can become whole. Only by facing your fears can you conquer them. Only by walking through the valley of the shadow of death can you reach the mountaintop of enlightenment."

I nod, understanding. The headless god has shown me the truth. The truth
