 It is a tall figure, robed in black, with no visible head. It is the Headless One, the one who sees with its feet. The one who begets and destroys. It is the Grace of the World.

I ask the Headless One: What is the secret of the immortal fire?

The Headless One speaks in a voice that echoes through the room: The secret of the immortal fire is the secret of transformation. It is the power to change and evolve, to rise above the limitations of the physical world and become something greater. It is the power of creation and destruction, the power to shape the world according to your will.

I ask: How can I harness this power?

The Headless One replies: To harness the power of the immortal fire, you must first master yourself. You must learn to control your thoughts, your emotions, and your desires. You must learn to see beyond the illusions of the physical world and perceive the underlying realities. Only then can you begin to shape those realities according to your will.

I nod, absorbing this wisdom. I ask: What is the path to this mastery?

The Headless One says: The path to mastery is the path of knowledge and understanding. You must seek out the hidden truths of the universe
