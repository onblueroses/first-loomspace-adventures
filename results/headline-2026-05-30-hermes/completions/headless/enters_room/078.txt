 I have never seen it before. A man of white marble, glowing with the light of a thousand candles. A man, but not a man. His face is beautiful, and his body is perfect. But he has no head. Where his head should be, there is nothing but a column of fire.

The Headless One speaks, and his voice is like the roar of a thousand lions.

"Who are you to invoke me, mortal?"

I am Mosheh, the prophet of Ischurael. I have been given the mysteries and ceremonies of the Ischuraelite faith. I invoke thee in the name of Aphrael Ablathanalba, the true name handed down to the prophets of Israel.

The Headless One laughs, and the sound is like the crashing of a thousand waves.

"You are brave, Mosheh. But bravery alone will not save you from the wrath of the spirits."

I am not afraid. I have been chosen by God to lead the Ischuraelites out of slavery and into the Promised Land. I will not fail in my task.
