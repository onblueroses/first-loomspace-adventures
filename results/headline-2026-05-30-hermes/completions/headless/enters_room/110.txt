 A river of air, the sound of a thousand whispers, and the sensation of being held and contained by something massive, invisible, and powerful.

I feel the presence of something like an invisible sun radiating heat and light on my skin, and I sense an intelligence behind it that seems to be waiting for me to acknowledge it. I do so, and I feel the intelligence focusing on me, considering me, and then speaking to me in a voice that seems to come from everywhere at once:

“Child of Earth, thou art become the Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba, the Grace of the World. Thou hast invoked me, the Headless One, and I have come. What wouldst thou ask of me?”

I reply, “I ask for the power to bind and control the spirits of the elements, to command them to do my will, and to protect me from their harm.”

The voice speaks again, “Thou hast been given this power, Child of Earth. Thou art the Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba, and all spirits are subject unto thee. Use this power wisely, and with respect for the balance of the universe. Remember that thou art a part of the world, and the world is a part of thee. Thou
