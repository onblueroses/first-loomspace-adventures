 I will not describe. It is beyond words. I am so small, so insignificant before it. The room is filled with a presence that devours all. The air is thick with power. I am not worthy to be in its presence. It looks upon me and sees all. I am laid bare. I am naked before it. I am nothing.

It speaks. Its voice is like thunder. It shakes the very foundations of the world. It is the voice of the universe itself. It is the voice of God.

"Who are you to summon me?"

I tremble before it. I am nothing. I am less than nothing. I am a speck of dust in the wind. I am a drop of water in the ocean. I am a grain of sand in the desert. I am nothing.

"I am the one who summoned you," I say, my voice trembling.

"You are nothing," it says. "You are less than nothing. You are a speck of dust in the wind. You are a drop of water in the ocean. You are a grain of sand in the desert. You are nothing."

I fall to my knees. I am nothing. I am less than nothing. I am a speck of dust in the wind.
