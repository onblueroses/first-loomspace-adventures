Many of them were written in a time when esoteric and occult knowledge was heavily suppressed by mainstream religious authorities. The authors often had to encode their true meanings in symbolism and allegory to avoid persecution.

Regarding their influence on modern practices, it varies widely. Some occultists take a very literal, dogmatic approach to the texts, treating them almost as infallible scripture. Others view them more as symbolic frameworks to be interpreted and adapted to the individual's own spiritual path.
