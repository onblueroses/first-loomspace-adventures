The air is electrified with the presence of a thousand angels, all singing a song of praise, the notes of which sound like trumpets, and harps, and every instrument imaginable. The song is so loud it is almost deafening, and yet it is somehow not unpleasant. It is a song of victory, of triumph, of the conquering of all that is evil and the establishment of all that is good.

And then, suddenly, the song stops, and there is a moment of silence. And then, a voice, a voice that is not a voice, a voice that is more like a thought, a voice that is more like a feeling, a voice that is more like a presence, speaks.

"Welcome, my child," the voice says. "Welcome to the presence of the Most High. Welcome to the presence of the One who is, and who was, and who is to come. Welcome to the presence of the One who created all things, and who sustains all things, and who will bring all things to their appointed end.
