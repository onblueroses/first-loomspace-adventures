They gather around me in a circle, just outside the boundary of the circle of salt. The King of All Demons. The Lurker at the Threshold. The Eater of Souls. The Dweller in Darkness. The Blind Adept of the Temple of the Silver Star. The Angel of Poison and Disease.

I greet them each in turn, and invite them into the circle. They take their seats around the points of the hexagram inscribed within.

---

I invoke and call upon the Great Demon whose name is Legion. I stitch his sigil into the palm of my left hand, and I prick the tip of my ring finger with the point of the needle. A single drop of blood falls and is absorbed by the lines of the sigil. I press my bleeding hand to the center of the hexagram.

The Demon Legion appears in the circle, standing over the hexagram. He is tall and slender, dressed in a fine black suit. His skin is pale, his hair dark and slicked back. His eyes are black, with no iris or sclera visible.
