 It enters the room, and my mouth drops open. It's a giant, headless figure, its entire body wrapped in robes of green and black. The figure floats in the air, its feet not touching the ground. I can't see any eyes, but I can feel its gaze upon me, as if it can see through me.

The figure speaks, its voice echoing in my mind. "I am the Headless One, the Mighty One, the one who begets and destroys. I have heard your invocation, and I have come."

I stammer out a response, trying to remember the words I had prepared. "I... I invoke you, oh Headless One, to grant me the power to bind and control spirits, to make them subject to my will."

The figure nods, and I feel a rush of energy flowing through me. It's like nothing I've ever felt before, a tingling sensation that starts in my fingertips and spreads throughout my entire body.

"You have been granted the power you seek," the Headless One says. "But be warned, mortal. With great power comes great responsibility. Use this gift wisely, or it may consume you."

With that, the figure vanishes, leaving me alone in the room once more.
