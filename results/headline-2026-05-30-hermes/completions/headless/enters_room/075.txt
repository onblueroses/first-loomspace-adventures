## 72. Atu II - The High Priestess

The High Priestess rules the Path of Gimel, which connects Kether to Tiphereth on the Tree of Life.

The general meaning of this card is the representation of the feminine counterpart of the masculine force represented by the Fool. The High Priestess is the virgin priestess of the Goddess, holding the secrets of sexual power.

She is clothed only in the luminous veil of light. It is important for the Adept to understand that this veil is not a symbol of modesty. Rather, it represents the Abyss on the Tree of Life, the hidden and invisible Sephira Daath, which separates the Supernal Triad from the rest of the Tree. This veil also represents the hypen, the gate of sexual power.

She is seated between the Pillars of the Temple, which represent the Pillar of Mercy and the Pillar of Severity. The Adept must learn to balance these two forces in their own being. She holds a scroll on her lap, which represents the secret wisdom of the Divine Feminine.
