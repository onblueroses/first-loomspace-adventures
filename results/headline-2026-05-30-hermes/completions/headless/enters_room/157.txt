 Is the rest of the Ritual.

The magician, wearing the black robe, now takes the sword in his right hand, facing East. He makes the Sign of the Enterer, saying:

I call upon thee, awesome and hidden God, god of gods, lord of the spirits, AOTH, ABAOTH, BASUM, ISAK, SABAOTH, IAO.

He makes the Sign of Silence, then again the Sign of the Enterer, saying:

I call upon thee, the messenger of terror, the mighty one of the gods, JOKAS, who giveth the earth to quake, who maketh the air to tremble and who shaketh the world, AIEOU, IEOU, IAO, OII.

He makes the Sign of Silence, then again the Sign of the Enterer, saying:

I call upon thee, the headless one, the great one, the mighty one, who givest not sleep to the night, who feedest upon the flesh of men, the mighty one, the headless one, the fearful one, who feareth not, the terrifying one, the one who cannot be turned back, ANORE, EIBOE, STHENOBIAOTH.
