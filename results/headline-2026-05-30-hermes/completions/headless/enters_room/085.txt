 It is a presence, a spirit, a being. It is a god, but not a god, a demon, but not a demon, an angel, but not an angel. It is all of these things and none of these things. It is the Headless One, the Bornless One, the One without a Beginning. It is the One who created the Earth and the Heavens, the Night and the Day, the Darkness and the Light. It is the One who distinguished between the just and the unjust, who made the female and the male, who produced the seed and the fruit, who formed men to love one another and to hate one another. It is the One who produced the moist and the dry, and that which nourisheth all created life.

The Headless One speaks, and its voice is like the rushing of a mighty wind, like the roar of a thousand thunders, like the crackling of a thousand fires. It speaks in a language that is not a language, in words that are not words, in a tongue that is not a tongue. But I understand it, for I am Mosheh, the prophet unto whom the Headless One committed Its mysteries, the ceremonies of Ischurael.

The Headless One
