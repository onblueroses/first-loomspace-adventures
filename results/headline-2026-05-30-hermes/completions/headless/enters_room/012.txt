The serpent speaks, its voice like the hiss of a thousand snakes:

"Child of the Earth, you have called me forth. I am the Heart Girt with a Serpent, the Mighty One, the Immortal Fire. What would you ask of me?"

I reply, my voice trembling with awe and fear:

"Great Serpent, I seek knowledge and power. I wish to understand the mysteries of the universe, to see beyond the veil of reality."

The serpent's eyes bore into mine, and I feel as if my very soul is being laid bare.

"Knowledge and power come with a price," the serpent hisses. "Are you willing to pay that price?"

I nod, my heart pounding in my chest.

"Yes," I whisper. "I am willing."
