 What stands before me.

The Headless One. The Daimon with sight in his feet.

The Mighty One, who possesses the immortal Fire.

The Truth that hates the deeds of injustice in the world.

The one whose mouth utterly flames.

The one who begets and destroys.

The Grace of the World.

IAŌ SABAŌTH.

I have come to speak with you, O mighty Headless One.

And what would you ask of me, O mortal?

I come seeking the wisdom and power to accomplish the Great Work, to bring about the transformation of the world and the establishment of a new aeon.

A lofty goal indeed, O seeker. But such wisdom and power do not come without great cost and sacrifice. Are you prepared to pay the price?

I am. I pledge myself wholly to this cause, to walk the path of the magician and prophet, no matter the trials and ordeals that may come.
