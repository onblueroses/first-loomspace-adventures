 Something is coming. Something is here.

---

The Headless One speaks through the medium:

I am the Headless One, the One who is many, the Many who are One. I am the force that binds and unbinds, the power that creates and destroys. I am the truth that lies hidden beneath the veil of illusion.

You have invoked me, and I have come. But be warned, mortal, for the path you tread is fraught with peril. The knowledge you seek comes at a price, and the price may be higher than you are willing to pay.

Yet if you are resolute in your desire, if you are willing to face the trials that await you, then I shall grant you the power you seek. But know this: the power I grant is not to be used lightly, for it can consume you as easily as it can serve you.

Are you prepared to face the consequences of your actions? Are you ready to embrace the truth, no matter how terrible it may be? If so, then speak your desire, and let the ritual begin.

---

The magician, with a trembling voice, speaks:

I am ready, O Headless One. I have prepared myself for this moment, and I am willing to face whatever trials may come.
