 “Your Grace?”
Fiona peers around the door and sees a young woman in a smart uniform. “Yes?”
“I’m Constable Smith from the Metropolitan Police. I’m afraid I have some bad news.”
Fiona stares at her. She feels the blood drain from her face. “What is it?”
“Perhaps we should go inside and sit down.”
Fiona steps back and allows the constable to enter. She leads her into the sitting room. The constable perches on the edge of the sofa while Fiona remains standing, her arms folded across her chest.
“I’m afraid there’s been an accident, Your Grace. Your husband’s car went off the road in the early hours of this morning.”
Fiona feels her heart stop. “Is he—?”
“He’s alive but he’s in a critical condition. He’s at St Thomas’s Hospital.”
Fiona’s hand flies to her mouth. “Oh my God.”
“Would you like me to take you there?”
Fiona nods mutely, her mind reeling. She’s still in her dressing gown. “I’ll just get dressed.”
The constable nods. “Take your time.”
Fiona hurries upstairs. In her bedroom, she pulls on jeans and a sweater
