The man is surprised by his neighbor's honesty. He says, "Of course, come on in. I know how you feel. My wife is visiting her mother, my children are at camp, and I'm too sick to go out."

The neighbor says, "Yeah, I know. Your wife told me she was leaving and she wasn't coming back, and your kids said they were going to camp. I saw the ambulance come for you."

The man is shocked. "Really? So you knew I was here by myself feeling miserable? How did you know that?"

The neighbor replies, "I was the one who sent the ambulance away."

The man is puzzled. "But why did you do that?"

The neighbor smiles and says, "Because I wanted to keep you company, my friend."
