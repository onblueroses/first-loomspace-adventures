"I am Mrs. Elena Fairbanks," the visitor says. "I am the superintendent of the Lowood Institution. I have come to collect Jane Eyre."

The older woman, Mrs. Reed, looks at the girl. "Jane, go and put on your things."

The girl, Jane, rises from her seat. She is small, with a thin face and large, dark eyes. Her hair is a light brown, and it is pulled back into a tight braid. She wears a plain dress, and her boots are scuffed. She walks to the door, her steps measured and quiet.

As she passes Mrs. Reed, the older woman says, "And, Jane, if you don't behave well at Lowood, you'll be sent somewhere much worse."

Jane does not reply. She leaves the room, and the door closes behind her.
