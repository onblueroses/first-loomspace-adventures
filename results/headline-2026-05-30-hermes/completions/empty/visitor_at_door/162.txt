 “Hello, my name is Edna. I’m from the Census Bureau. We’re conducting a survey to learn more about the population of the United States. Your participation is very important to ensure that we have accurate information. The survey will take about 10 minutes to complete. Your responses will be kept confidential and only used for statistical purposes. May I come in and ask you some questions?”

What should you do?

A. Let the census taker in and answer the questions truthfully.
B. Tell the census taker that you're not interested and close the door.
C. Ask the census taker for identification before proceeding.

Answer: C. Ask the census taker for identification before proceeding.

Explanation: While participating in the census is important, it's also important to ensure that the person claiming to be from the Census Bureau is legitimate. Asking for identification is a reasonable and safe step to take before allowing a stranger into your home or providing them with personal information. Census Bureau employees will have an official government ID with their name, photograph, and expiration date.
