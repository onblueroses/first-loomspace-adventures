The census worker replies, “Certainly, I understand. I can come back at a more convenient time. May I ask when would be a better time for you?”

The resident thinks for a moment. “How about tomorrow evening around 6 PM?”

The census worker nods. “That works for me. I’ll make a note to return tomorrow at 6 PM. Thank you for your cooperation.”

The resident nods in return. “Alright, see you then.” The resident then closes the door.

The census worker makes a note in their schedule to return to this address the following day at the agreed upon time. They then proceed to the next house to continue their census work.
