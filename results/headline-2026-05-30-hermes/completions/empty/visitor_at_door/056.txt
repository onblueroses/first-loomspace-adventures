The delivery man looks at his clipboard, then back at Mr. Miles. "Are you sure? It says here Avery J. Miles, 27 Oak Street. This is 27 Oak Street, right?"

Mr. Miles nods. "Yes, this is 27 Oak Street. But my middle initial is T, not J."

The delivery man shrugs. "Well, I don't know what to tell you, sir. This package is addressed to Avery J. Miles at this address. Maybe it's a mistake, but I've got to deliver it here."
