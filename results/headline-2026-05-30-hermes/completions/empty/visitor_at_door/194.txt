 “I must tell you that you’re under arrest, Mr. Wilkins.”

“What? What for?”

“Murder.”

“Murder?” Wilkins stares at him. “You’re crazy.”

“We’ll see about that. Please come with me.”

Wilkins looks at his wife, then at the officer, and then back at his wife again. “I’ll be back, honey. Don’t worry.”

“Don’t say anything, John,” she calls after him. “Wait until we get a lawyer.”

Wilkins is led to a waiting car. He is taken to the police station, where he is booked for murder.

LOCAL MAN ARRESTED FOR MURDER

John Wilkins, a prominent businessman, was arrested last night for the murder of an unidentified man whose body was found in the basement of a building at 1214 South Street. Wilkins is being held without bail. Police state that they have a strong case against him.
