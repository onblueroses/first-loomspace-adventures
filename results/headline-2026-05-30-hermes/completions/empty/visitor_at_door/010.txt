 "I am looking for the residence of Mr. and Mrs. _Finnegan_. Have I come to the right place?" He is a tall, thin man, wearing a black suit and a black hat. He is carrying a black suitcase. He looks like an undertaker.

Mrs. Finnegan looks puzzled. "You are at the right place," she says, "but do you mind telling me who you are? I don't believe I know you."

"I am a census taker," the man says. "I have come to ask you some questions about your family. It won't take long, and I won't take too much of your time."

Mrs. Finnegan hesitates for a moment, then says, "Well, I suppose it will be all right. Please come in."

The census taker steps inside the house. He looks around and sees a living room filled with toys and children's books. "You have children, I see," he says.

"Yes," Mrs. Finnegan says. "We have three children. Two boys and a girl."

"I see," the census taker says. "And what are their names and ages, if you don't mind my asking?"

"The oldest is Michael," Mrs. Finnegan says.
