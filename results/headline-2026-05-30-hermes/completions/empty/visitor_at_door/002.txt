 "I am a stranger in this town. I have lost my way. I am hungry and have no place to sleep. Please help me."

The man in the house hears the stranger's voice. He looks at the stranger. The stranger looks poor and tired. The man thinks for a moment. He makes a decision.

The man opens the door. He welcomes the stranger into his home. He gives the stranger food to eat and a place to sleep. The stranger is very grateful. He thanks the man for his kindness.

The stranger stays with the man for a few days. The man helps the stranger find his way. The stranger leaves the man's house. He is no longer lost or hungry. He is very thankful for the man's help.

The man feels good about helping the stranger. He knows he did the right thing. He hopes that if he ever needs help, someone will be kind to him too.

This story teaches us to be kind to others. We should help people in need. We never know when we might need help ourselves. Kindness is a good thing to share. 

Remember, when you help others, you are also helping yourself. You are making the world a better place, one kind act at a time.
