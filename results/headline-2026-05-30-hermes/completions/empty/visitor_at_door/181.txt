 “I am the father of the boy who has come to you. I have brought the balance of the fee.” He brings out a bag of gold coins from his pocket and hands it over to the astonished teacher.

The teacher opens the bag and empties it on the table. He counts the coins one by one. There are exactly one hundred shining gold coins. The teacher is now convinced about the father’s story.

“Now that the fee has been paid, I shall complete the job,” says the teacher. He calls the boy and orders him to get ready for the journey.

The boy is terrified. He starts crying. “I don’t want to go. I want to stay here with you. I want to be your disciple!”

The teacher’s voice now becomes soft and compassionate. “My son, I am not sending you away. I am coming with you. We shall face the challenges together.”

The boy is now assured. He stops crying and starts preparing for the journey.

The teacher then turns to the father and speaks with a firm voice. “We shall go to the mountain. We shall climb the peak. We shall face the challenges that are posed to us. We shall unravel the mystery.”

The father is relieved. He thanks the teacher and takes leave
