The visitor explains, "I'm a census taker. We're conducting the national census, and your household has been randomly selected to complete a detailed survey. It will only take a few minutes of your time."

I'm a bit surprised. "Oh, I see. Well, I suppose that's fine. Please, come in."

The census taker steps inside. "Thank you, Mr. Butcher. Let's begin with some basic information about your household. How many people currently reside here?"

I think for a moment. "Well, there's myself, my wife, and our three children. So, five in total."

The census taker nods and makes a note. "Very good. And what are the ages of your children?"

I reply, "Our eldest is 12, then we have a 10-year-old, and our youngest is 6."

More notes are made. "Excellent. Now, I need to ask about your employment status. Are you currently employed?"

I nod. "Yes, I am. I work at the local butcher shop."
