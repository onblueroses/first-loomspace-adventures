 "I am the Grand Vizier of the Emperor. I have been sent to convey a message to you. The Emperor wishes to see you."

A little later, the old man is ushered into the Emperor's presence. The Emperor is sitting on his throne, surrounded by his courtiers. The old man bows deeply before him.

"Your Majesty, I am honored by your summons. How may I serve you?"

The Emperor speaks. "I have heard of your wisdom, old man. I have a question that has been troubling me. I hope you can provide an answer."

"Ask, Your Majesty, and I will do my best to answer."

The Emperor leans forward. "What is the secret of happiness?"

The old man is silent for a moment. Then he speaks. "Your Majesty, the secret of happiness is not in wealth or power. It is not in fame or success. The secret of happiness is in contentment."

The Emperor looks puzzled. "Contentment? What do you mean?"

The old man explains. "Contentment means being satisfied with what you have. It means not always wanting more. It means finding joy in the simple things in life."
