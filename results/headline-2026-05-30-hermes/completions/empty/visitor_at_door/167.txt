 "I am a herald from the East. I have brought you a message from the King of Kings. He is calling you to His court. You must come at once, for He is not accustomed to being kept waiting."

"The King of Kings! I have heard of Him," the woman answers. "But I have no time to go to His court now. I am too busy with my housework. Besides, I am not prepared to appear before Him. My hair is not arranged properly, and my dress is not suitable for such an occasion."

The visitor is insistent. "But you must come. He is calling you. His message is urgent. In a little while it will be too late."

"But I tell you I am not ready," the woman replies impatiently. "I have other things to attend to. I am not prepared to meet the King."

The visitor departs, and the woman goes on with her housework. But her mind is troubled, and she cannot get rid of a feeling of uneasiness. What if the King should be displeased because she did not respond to His call? What if He should require her presence no more? The thought is unbearable. She drops her work and falls on her knees.
