### The visitor's request

The visitor begins by stating that he is a "gentleman of the shade." He is seeking the "repose" that he enjoyed in his life before death. He asks the inhabitants of the house to let him in. He wants to share the warmth of their hearth and the light of their lamps.

### The response from within

A voice from within the house replies to the visitor. The voice belongs to a "little girl." She tells the visitor that he cannot enter because he is a "ghost." She says that the house is "haunted" by the living, not the dead.

### The visitor's realization

The visitor is surprised by the girl's response. He realizes that he is indeed a ghost. He had forgotten that he had died. The girl's words remind him of his true state.

### The visitor's departure

The visitor accepts the girl's words. He realizes that he cannot enter the house. He must return to the "darkness" and the "silence" of the grave. He bids farewell to the house and its inhabitants.
