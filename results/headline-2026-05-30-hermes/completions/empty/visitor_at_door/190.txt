 “It’s me, Jake. Open up!”
Jake doesn’t move. “How do I know it’s you?”
“Come on, Jake! I’m here to help. Open the door!”
Jake stares at the door, his hand hovering near the lock. “Tell me something only you would know.”
“Remember when we were kids, and I accidentally broke your mom’s vase? You took the blame for me. I still feel bad about that.”
Jake’s eyes widen. He quickly unlocks the door and pulls it open. His old friend, Liam, stands outside, looking disheveled and out of breath.

“Liam! What are you doing here? How did you find me?”
Liam steps inside, and Jake closes the door behind him. “I’ve been trying to reach you for days. Your phone goes straight to voicemail. I got worried, so I tracked your phone’s location.”
Jake frowns. “You tracked my phone? How?”
“I have my ways,” Liam says, avoiding the question. “The important thing is, I’m here to help. I know what’s going on, Jake. I know about the shadow men.”
Jake’s heart races. “How do you know about them?”
