 "I am a friend of the prisoner. I wish to see him."

"Impossible," says the soldier. "You can not see him."

The visitor looks at the soldier, and the soldier looks at the visitor. The soldier is no match for the visitor, and he knows it. He yields. "You may see him," he says, "but you must not speak to him."

The visitor enters the cell. The prisoner is lying on the floor, apparently asleep. The visitor looks at the prisoner, and the prisoner looks at the visitor. The visitor's eyes say, "Cheer up. We are going to get you out of this." The prisoner's eyes say, "I understand."

The visitor goes away. The prisoner gets up and walks to the window. He sees the visitor go out of the gate and disappear in the darkness.

The prisoner lies down again. An hour passes. Two hours pass. The prisoner hears footsteps in the corridor. The door of his cell opens. The visitor enters, followed by two other men. One of the men is carrying a rope ladder. The other is carrying a suit of civilian clothes.

The prisoner puts on the civilian clothes. The men attach the rope ladder to the window. The prisoner climbs down the ladder.
