 It is Mr. Jingle.

“Mrs. Bardell, I wish you good morning. I was desired by my friend Mr. Pickwick to call upon you, with a view to the arrangement of this little matter, which has been pending for some time. I hope I have not inconvenienced you by calling so early. Have I?”

Mrs. Bardell is all graciousness, and Mr. Jingle proceeds:

“Now, the fact is, since Mr. Pickwick desired me to call upon you, it has been discovered that the differences between you are susceptible of the most amicable arrangement. Of course you know that when Mr. Pickwick contracted to marry your daughter, he didn’t know she was married already.”

Here Mrs. Bardell turns crimson, and Mr. Jingle goes on:

“Well, now, that being the case, it is of course very natural that he should decline to fulfil his engagement. Very natural.”

Mrs. Bardell murmurs assent.
