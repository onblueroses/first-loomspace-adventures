 He is a stranger. He says he has been sent by his employer to deliver a message to Mr. Tressilian. He asks if he can come in for a moment. He seems like a gentleman and his manner is courteous. I do not like to keep him standing outside, so I ask him to step into the hall. I go to fetch Mr. Tressilian. I find him in the dining-room. I tell him about the visitor and his message and Mr. Tressilian asks me to show him in.

I show the visitor into the dining-room and Mr. Tressilian asks me to close the door. I do so and return to the kitchen.

After a lapse of about ten minutes, during which time I have been seeing to the dinner, I hear the dining-room door open. I am curious about the visitor and am just coming out from the kitchen when I hear Mr. Tressilian say, "I'll see you out."

I return to the kitchen so as not to be seen.

I hear the front door close and then Mr. Tressilian comes into the kitchen. He looks upset and worried.

I ask him if anything is the matter.

He says, "No, no," but he looks very queer and upset.
