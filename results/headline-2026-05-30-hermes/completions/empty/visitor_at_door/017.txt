 He is a small man, in a well-pressed suit, with a nervous but friendly smile. He introduces himself as Dr. B. J. Spoke, and he has come to see the lady of the house on a matter of great urgency. Mrs. Hilliard, surprised, invites him in. Dr. Spoke steps into the room, and his eyes go at once to the mantelpiece. "Ah, the very thing!" he exclaims. He reaches up and takes down an old-fashioned oil lamp that has been standing there for as long as Mrs. Hilliard can remember. Dr. Spoke holds the lamp up so that they can all see it clearly. "This lamp," he announces, "is a magic lamp. And I have come to buy it from you."

Mrs. Hilliard is astonished. A magic lamp? She had always thought it was just an old antique, a family heirloom that had been passed down through the generations. But Dr. Spoke is quite serious. He explains that he is a collector of magic items, and he has been searching for this lamp for a long time. He is prepared to pay a very high price for it.
