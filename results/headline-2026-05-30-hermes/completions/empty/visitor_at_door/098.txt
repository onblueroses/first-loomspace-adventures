The man's face turns pale. "My brother? Oh no, this can't be happening. I must go to him immediately!" He turns to his wife. "I'm sorry my love, but I must leave at once. I'll be back as soon as I can."

The wife nods, concern etched on her face. "Of course, go, go! I'll be here waiting for you. I hope everything will be alright."

The man quickly puts on his coat and follows the visitor out into the night, his mind racing with worry for his brother.
