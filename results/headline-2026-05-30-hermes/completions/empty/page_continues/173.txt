Answer: The singular values are always unique. The left and right singular vectors, however, are only unique up to a phase (i.e., a constant factor of $e^{i\theta}$ for some angle $\theta$) for distinct singular values. When singular values are repeated, you have a lot more freedom in choosing your singular vectors.
