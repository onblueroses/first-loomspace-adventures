 "To see if a number is divisible by 7, take the last digit of the number, double it, and subtract it from the rest of the number; if the result is divisible by 7 (or is 0), then the original number is divisible by 7."

So let's try this out with the number 203.

First, we take the last digit of the number and double it. The last digit of 203 is 3, and doubling it gives us 6.

Next, we subtract this doubled last digit from the rest of the number. The rest of the number, excluding the last digit, is 20. Subtracting 6 from 20 gives us 14.

Now, we check if the result is divisible by 7. 14 is indeed divisible by 7 (since 14 ÷ 7 = 2).

Therefore, according to the divisibility rule, the original number 203 is divisible by 7.

### 3. Why does this work?

The reason this divisibility rule works is based on the fact that 7 is a prime number and that multiplying by 10 (which is essentially what happens when you move the last digit to the front) does not affect divisibility by 7.
