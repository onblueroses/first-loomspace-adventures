 "The US Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) estimates that more than 20 billion disposable diapers are dumped in landfills each year, accounting for more than 3.5 million tons of waste."

In the United States, the average baby goes through 8,000 diapers in their lifetime. The $7.65 billion industry has grown 1.9% annually between 2011 and 2016. Diaper companies make $3 billion a year.

#### Biodegradable diapers

Some brands offer biodegradable diapers which are made from plant-based materials and are compostable. These diapers may still end up in landfills, however, if there is not a compost facility in the area or if the waste company does not compost the diapers. In 2020, the first diaper composting service was launched in the San Francisco Bay Area.
