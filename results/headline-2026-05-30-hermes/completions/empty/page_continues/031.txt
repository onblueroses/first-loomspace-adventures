Answer: Your textbook's definition is indeed the universally accepted definition of the angle between a line and a curve.

In general, if we have two curves $y = f(x)$ and $y = g(x)$ that intersect at a point $P$, the angle of intersection between these two curves is defined as the angle between the tangent lines to the two curves at point $P$.
