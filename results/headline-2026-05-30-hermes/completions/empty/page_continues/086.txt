### Grant API permissions

Click 'Add a permission', then click 'Microsoft Graph' and then 'Delegated permissions'.

![Add a permission](/images/2022-08-azure-activedirectory-app-registration-6.png)

In the 'Select permissions' search box, type 'Mail'. Expand the 'Mail' item, and then check the 'Mail.Read' permission. Then click 'Add permissions'.

![Select permissions](/images/2022-08-azure-activedirectory-app-registration-7.png)

The page will update to show the newly added permission. The status will show 'Not granted for...'. Click the 'Grant admin consent for...' button, and then confirm in the dialog.

![Grant admin consent](/images/2022-08-azure-activedirectory-app-registration-8.png)

The status will change to 'Granted for...'.
