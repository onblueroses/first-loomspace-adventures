### Creation Museum

Main article: Creation MuseumIn May 2007, Answers in Genesis opened the Creation Museum, a facility located in Petersburg, Kentucky, United States, which promotes young Earth creationism. The museum, which cost $27 million to build, has generated both controversy and increased tourism to the region.

### Ark Encounter

Main article: Ark EncounterIn May 2011, Answers in Genesis began full-scale work on the Ark Encounter, a theme park inspired by a passage from the Bible which describes a large wooden ship that was built to spare some humans and animals from a massive, global flood. The park's centerpiece is a full-scale model of the biblical Ark, which the Answers in Genesis board of directors decided would be 510 feet (160 m) long and 85 feet (26 m) wide after reviewing various sizes in the Bible and elsewhere.
