It is not the first time that physicists have been excited about the discovery of a mathematical object shaping reality. For example, they have been ecstatic about string theory and the fabric of reality, mirror symmetry and the amplituhedron's predecessor, the positive Grassmannian, a structure that was recorded in a paper with 154 authors. The latest discovery may eclipse all those previous findings.

The roots of the Amplituhedron go back to the 1980s, to the efforts of Stephen Hawking and other physicists to understand the dynamics of black holes.
