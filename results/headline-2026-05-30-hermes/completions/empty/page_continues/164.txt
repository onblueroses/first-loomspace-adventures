 "It is convenient to define the function $g$ by $g(n) = 1 - f(n)$. Then $g(n) = g(n-1) + g(n-2)$, and $g(0) = 1$, $g(1) = 0$. The function $g$ satisfies the Fibonacci recurrence but with different initial conditions from the Fibonacci sequence. However, $g(n) = F_{n-1}$ for $n \ge 1$, so it is just a shifted Fibonacci sequence."
