In June 2010, the group posted a video on YouTube of its protest activities in front of the Chinese embassy in Washington, D.C. during the visit of Chinese President Hu Jintao for the Nuclear Security Summit. The video claims that the group's goal is to "raise awareness about the human rights situation in China".

In January 2011, the group posted a video on Facebook of its protest in front of the International Monetary Fund headquarters in Washington, D.C. during the visit of Chinese President Hu Jintao. The protesters held signs that read "Tibet Belongs to Tibetans", "Taiwan is Not Part of China", and "Communist Party ≠ Chinese People".

In October 2011, the group posted a video on YouTube of its protest in front of the White House during the visit of Chinese Vice President Xi Jinping. In April 2012, the group posted a video on YouTube of its protest in front of the U.S. State Department during the U.S.-China Strategic and Economic Dialogue.
