This distinction needs to be kept in mind, particularly when considering the way in which the term 'representation' is used by different authors.

## Representation in formal approaches to semantics

In formal approaches to semantics (such as model theory, algebraic semantics, the formal semantics of programming languages, and so on), representation is a technical term that designates a mathematical structure that embodies certain aspects of a particular system.

### Representation in model theory

In model theory, a representation of a structure is a mapping from the domain of discourse of the structure into some codomain that is used to interpret the elements of the domain.
