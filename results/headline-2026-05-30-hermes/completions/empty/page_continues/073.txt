The church was designed by Mr R K Penson, JP, FRIBA, Newport, in the Early English style. It consists of chancel, nave, south aisle, organ chamber, and vestries, and will accommodate 350 worshippers. The total cost, including site, amounts to £4,000. The contractors were Messrs Perry, of Newport, for the general work, and Mr H. Gunter, of Newport, for the heating system."

The church was completed in 1911. The church guidebook states: "The church was consecrated on 23 October 1911 by Bishop John Owen, Bishop of St David's. The total cost of building and furnishing the church was £6,000."
