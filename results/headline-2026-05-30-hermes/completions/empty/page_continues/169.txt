\[ \int_0^1 e^{-t^2} \, dt = \frac{\pi}{4} \operatorname{erf}(1) \approx 0.746824132812427 \]

where erf is the error function. The value of the approximation is

\[ \frac{2}{3} \approx 0.666666666666667 \]"

Let's see if we can improve on this.

The idea is to expand the integrand in a series we can integrate term by term. The exponential function has such an expansion.

\[ e^{-t^2} = 1 - t^2 + \frac{t^4}{2} - \frac{t^6}{6} + \cdots \]

This is just the usual series for the exponential function, with \( -t^2 \) substituted for the usual \( x \).
