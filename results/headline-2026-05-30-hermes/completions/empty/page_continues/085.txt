• This is a big topic, but at its core the CLT says that as you collect more and more data, the sample mean will become a better and better estimate of the true population mean. The "variation" in the sample mean will decrease as the sample size increases. Hypothesis testing uses this concept to test the probability that an observed value could have come from a population with a given mean and variance. I suggest you start by looking at the excellent Wikipedia article on the CLT. – user31690 Mar 8 '15 at 16:53
