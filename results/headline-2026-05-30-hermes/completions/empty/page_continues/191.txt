 "The combined authority of these courts is to be considered in determining the weight of the evidence. These courts are charged with the responsibility of determining whether there is substantial evidence, considering the record as a whole, to support the findings of fact made by the Commission." We are of the opinion that the case at bar is not controlled by the decision in the Universal Camera case, supra, for the reason that the substantial evidence rule is not here involved. We are not concerned with the question of whether the findings of the Commission are supported by substantial evidence. The question here presented is whether the findings of the Commission are in accordance with law. The rule of the Universal Camera case, supra, requiring the combined authority of the courts to be considered in determining the weight of the evidence, has no application to the case at bar. Here, the Commission has misconstrued the applicable statute and its findings are not in accordance with law.
