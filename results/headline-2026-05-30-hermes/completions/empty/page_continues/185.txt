The induction principle is usually given as follows: If $P(0)$ is true and $P(k) \Rightarrow P(k+1)$ for all $k \in \mathbb{N}$, then $P(n)$ is true for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$. The principle is one of the axioms of Peano arithmetic. –  user12014 May 2 '12 at 23:53
