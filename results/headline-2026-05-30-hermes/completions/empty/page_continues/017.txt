## Reactions

### United States

The announcement of the new policy was criticized by some and praised by others within the United States.

U.S. Senator John McCain said, "The president's decision to allow transgender individuals to serve in the U.S. military is yet another example of this administration's determination to impose its political agenda on the military." He also argued against the additional cost of providing gender transition health care to transgender service members, though the Palm Center's research indicates that the costs are minimal.

The American Civil Liberties Union (ACLU) opposed the six-month delay in lifting the ban, saying that it "has no justification and will harm both the military and the transgender service members who have been waiting for this announcement."

The Human Rights Campaign's president, Chad Griffin, said in a statement, "Today, we join in celebration with the thousands of brave transgender patriots who will now be able to serve our nation openly and with the deep respect they deserve."
