 "In addition, the following are considered as part of the `L` series of releases: `LTS` releases, `M` releases (maintenance), `SE` releases (security), `BPO` releases (backports), and `U` releases (updates)."

The `M`, `SE`, `BPO`, and `U` releases are all "updates" to the current release, and can be installed with the usual `apt update` and `apt upgrade` commands.

The `LTS` releases are more like "new" versions of the operating system, and should be installed with the `do-release-upgrade` command.
