Applying the power rule to the first term: $$\frac{d}{dt}(2t^3) = (3)(2)t^{3-1} = 6t^2$$

Applying the power rule to the second term: $$\frac{d}{dt}(-2t^2) = (2)(-2)t^{2-1} = -4t^1 = -4t$$
