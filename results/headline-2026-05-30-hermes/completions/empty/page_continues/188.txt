 "Since the 1970s, the world has been undergoing a profound transformation from an industrial society to an information society. This transformation is driven by the rapid development of information and communication technologies (ICT) that have the ability to process, store and disseminate information in unprecedented quantities and speed."

Information society is seen as the successor to industrial society. Closely related concepts are the post-industrial society (Daniel Bell), the post-fordist society, the knowledge society, the network society (Manuel Castells), the digital society, and the like, which all have slightly different accents.

## Related terms

A number of these related terms are discussed below.

### Knowledge society

The term knowledge society refers to a society in which the creation, dissemination, and utilization of information and knowledge has become the most important factor of production. In such a society, knowledge assets (also called intellectual capital) are the most powerful producer of wealth, more powerful than natural resources, machinery, or financial capital.

It has been suggested that the knowledge society arises out of the information society when the sheer amount of information available has created a new set of problems.
