 "The draft directive was considered by the Commission on 15 November 2012. Following this consideration, the Commission intends to consult further on some of the specific details of the proposed legislation before preparing a final draft of the directive. In particular, the Commission is consulting on:

* the design of the proposed protected settlement period of 28 days;
* the eligibility criteria for the debt advice scheme;
* the regulatory framework for the debt management sector; and
* the handling of complaints under the scheme.

The consultation document is available below. Responses should be provided by 21 December 2012."

The consultation document itself runs to some 62 pages.

The main proposals are:

* A moratorium period of 28 days during which creditors cannot take action to enforce their debts, and during which interest and charges cannot be added to the debts.
* A breathing space period during which a repayment plan is put in place, which can lead to a Debt Relief Order if this is appropriate.

The proposals cover the criteria for entering the scheme, and how it would be administered, along with proposals for regulation of fee-charging debt management companies.
