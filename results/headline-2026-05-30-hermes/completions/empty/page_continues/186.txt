 "The answer is: 42."

42 is a reference to the "Answer to the Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything" from Douglas Adams' The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy. In the series of books, the number 42 is the answer to the "Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything". The supercomputer, Deep Thought, takes 7.5 million years to compute the answer to the Ultimate Question. But when the answer is revealed, the characters are disappointed because it doesn't seem to make any sense. The number 42 has since become a cultural reference for any nonsensical or absurd answer to a question.

In the context of the Google search, the page is making a joke by suggesting that the answer to the question "The answer to life, the universe, and everything" is 42, just like in the book. It's a playful nod to the idea that some questions might not have straightforward or meaningful answers, and that sometimes, the answers we seek might be more complex or absurd than we expect.
