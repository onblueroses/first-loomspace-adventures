 "More than 2,300 years ago, the ancient Greek philosopher Aristotle suggested that Earth-centered concentric spheres supported the heavenly bodies. In the 16th century A.D., Nicolaus Copernicus proposed a model of the solar system that placed Earth and all the other planets in orbit around the sun. In the 17th century, Johannes Kepler proposed laws of motion that govern the orbits of the planets around the sun, and the moon around Earth. Later in that century, Isaac Newton proposed a theory of gravity that explained exactly how and why the planets in the solar system orbit the sun. In the early 20th century, Albert Einstein proposed the theory of general relativity, which is the best explanation we have for the motions of most objects in the universe."
