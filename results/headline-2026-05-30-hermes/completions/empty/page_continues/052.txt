 "Another of the four major types of tissue in the body - and the least common - is nervous tissue. Nervous tissue is found in the brain, spinal cord, and nerves. It is responsible for coordinating and controlling many body activities."

It may be the least common of the four major tissue types but it is the most important. Without the nervous system we would not be able to function.

The nervous system can be divided into two parts: the central nervous system and the peripheral nervous system.

The central nervous system consists of the brain and spinal cord.

The peripheral nervous system consists of all the nerves that convey information to and from the central nervous system.

The brain is the most complex organ in the body and is the organ that we know least about. It consists of many different parts that work together. Each part has a specific function, some of which we shall discuss later. The brain is the control centre of the body; it controls all our activities and processes all the information and messages that we receive from both inside and outside our bodies.

The spinal cord is the pathway for messages from the body to the brain and from the brain to the body. Together, the brain and spinal cord control all the processes that keep us alive, such as breathing, digestion, heart rate
