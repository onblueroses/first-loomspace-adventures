The page features a list of "Abusive admins" - those that some members of the Wikipedia community feel have abused their power - as well as a list of "Good admins" - those that some members of the community feel have made a positive impact on the encyclopedia.

Some of the stories on the site are by named users, others are anonymous. One such story is one entitled "Rogue admin blocking good users," which details the experiences of a user who was blocked after arguing with an administrator on a talk page. The user claims that the administrator in question "began edit warring with me, reverting my changes without any explanation," and that when they attempted to discuss the issue on the administrator's talk page, they were blocked for "trolling and personal attacks."
