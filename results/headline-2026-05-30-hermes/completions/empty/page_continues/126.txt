 "The C++ standard library provides several generic containers, algorithms to operate on those containers, and iterators to traverse the containers and provide access to the algorithms. This collection of containers, algorithms, and iterators is commonly known as the Standard Template Library (STL)." The STL containers are the standard library containers. The algorithms are the standard library algorithms. The iterators are the standard library iterators. The STL is the Standard Template Library. The standard library is the standard library. They are not the same thing, but the standard library includes the STL (although not all of the STL is in the C++ standard library).
