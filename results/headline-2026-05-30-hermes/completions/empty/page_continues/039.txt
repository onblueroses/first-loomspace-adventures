In the end, a new category was created for these new objects - they were called asteroids (meaning 'star-like'). The first asteroid to be discovered was Ceres in 1801."

"In 2006 the International Astronomical Union created a new class of objects - the dwarf planets. These objects are not quite planets, but are not asteroids either. The first dwarf planets to be named were Eris, Pluto and Ceres. The dwarf planets are too small to be considered major planets, but are large enough to be spherical in shape."
