Facebook has had a search feature for years, but it was geared more toward helping users navigate the social network itself. The latest search update is clearly designed to challenge Google’s search engine dominance.

Facebook has a big advantage over Google in terms of social data. The company knows who your friends are, what you like, and what you share. This allows Facebook to provide more personalized and relevant search results.

Google has been trying to incorporate more social data into its search results, but it has been limited by its lack of access to Facebook’s data. The two companies have been engaged in a long-standing feud, with Facebook blocking Google from accessing its data.

It remains to be seen if Facebook’s new search feature will be able to challenge Google’s dominance, but it is clear that the company is serious about search. With over 1 billion users, Facebook has a huge potential audience for its search engine.
