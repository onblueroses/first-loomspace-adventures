The breakfast club is open to all pupils at the school, and parents are asked to make a voluntary contribution of £1 per day to help cover the costs of the food and staffing.

The school is also planning to introduce a “walking bus” scheme, where children will be accompanied by adults on a walk to school, to encourage them to be more active and to reduce traffic congestion around the school.

Ms Wilson added: “We are committed to promoting healthy lifestyles and well-being for all our pupils, and we believe that the breakfast club and walking bus scheme will help us to achieve this goal.”

The school’s breakfast club is part of a wider initiative by the government to provide free breakfasts to children in disadvantaged areas, as part of its efforts to tackle child poverty and improve educational outcomes
