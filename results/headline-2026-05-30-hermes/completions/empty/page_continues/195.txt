But the Cherokee Nation was not seeking to be considered a foreign state. Rather, they were seeking recognition as a sovereign nation. And they were not the only ones. Many Native American tribes were fighting for their rights and sovereignty during this time. The Indian Removal Act of 1830 was not a direct result of this court case, but rather part of a larger pattern of U.S. government actions that sought to dispossess Native Americans of their lands.
