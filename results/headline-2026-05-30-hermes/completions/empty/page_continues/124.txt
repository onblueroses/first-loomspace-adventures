## Composition

The song is a tribute to Diana, Princess of Wales, who died in a car crash on 31 August 1997. The original "Candle in the Wind" was about Marilyn Monroe, but Elton John reworked the lyrics to reflect on Diana's life and legacy. The opening lines "Goodbye England's rose, may you ever grow in our hearts" set the tone for the song's theme of mourning and remembrance. The lyrics go on to describe Diana's beauty, grace, and compassion, as well as the impact she had on the world.

The song's melody and arrangement remain largely unchanged from the original version. It is a piano ballad with a slow tempo and a solemn, reverential tone. Elton John's vocals are emotive and powerful, conveying a sense of deep sorrow and loss.
