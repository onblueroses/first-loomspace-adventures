### Mapes

The magnetic moment is indeed related to the magnetization; specifically, $\bold{M}=n\bold{\mu}$, where n is the number of dipoles per unit volume. The susceptibility is a measure of how much magnetization is induced in the material when an external field is applied. Since the magnetization is proportional to the magnetic moment, it's reasonable to say that the magnetic moment is proportional to the applied field. Does this make sense?
