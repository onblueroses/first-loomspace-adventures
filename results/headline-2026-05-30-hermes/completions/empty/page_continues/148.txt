 "The strategy is a starting point, not an end point. It provides the framework and foundations for the next phase of the work, which will include more detailed policy development and the preparation of detailed action plans."

The report sets out a vision for the future of the city: "In 2030, Birmingham will be a thriving, successful, outward-looking city, confident of its place in the European and global family of cities, nationally and internationally recognised for its vibrant, culturally diverse communities, and for its creativity, enterprise, innovation and ambition."

The Big City Plan was launched in September 2008 and over 18 months, more than 14,000 people and organisations were consulted for their views and ideas on how the city centre could be improved. More than 100 consultation events were held and 1,900 completed questionnaires and 150 formal written responses were submitted.
