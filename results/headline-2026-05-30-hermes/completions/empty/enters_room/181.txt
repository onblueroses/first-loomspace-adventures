The door slowly opened, and a tall, stately, and beautiful woman, with a baby in her arms, entered the room. She was dressed in black, and her face was very pale, but as she advanced into the room a faint smile parted her lips. She walked straight to the bed, and laid the child down by the side of the dying man.

"God bless you, my dear husband!" she said, in a low, sweet voice.
"God bless you, and make you strong to bear your sufferings!"

Then, sitting by the bedside, she took her husband's hand in hers, and, with her eyes fixed upon his face, she sat in silence.

"Who are they?" whispered Fanny to Nelly, trembling and clinging to her.

"They are my father and mother," replied Nelly, in a whisper. "My father is dying, and my mother is watching by his side."

"And the baby--who is the baby?"

"That is my brother Tom."

"Will your father die, Nelly?"

"Yes, he will die very soon. But look--look at the door again!"
