The voice is unmistakable to anyone who has heard it, and yet it’s totally different, somehow. It’s stronger, it’s more vulnerable, it’s dark and light at the same time; it’s harsher; it’s more delicate. The guitar is simple, but it’s more complex. The music is still rooted in the folk tradition, but it’s dressed itself up in new threads. It’s a hundred different things at once, yet it’s still, at its center, the same. It is, in a word, unforgettable.
Bob Dylan’s new album, Modern Times, is a direct challenge to any and all of us who have ever said: “they don’t make ‘em like they used to.” They do. He just did. Modern Times is a stunning return to form for the poet laureate of rock ‘n roll. It is a work of startling, simple, majestic genius.
