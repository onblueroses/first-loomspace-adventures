 The ship's crew, from captain to cook, had been poisoned, and the only remaining soul on board was the cabin boy. He had been left in charge of the wheel and had steered the ship to port. When asked how he had managed to avoid being poisoned, he explained that he was too young to drink alcohol and so had not imbibed the poisoned grog. The townspeople took him to the local church and, with the permission of the priest, put him in the care of the nuns until a decision could be made about his future.

The nuns were kind and took good care of him, and the cabin boy quickly settled into his new life. He was given a bed in a small room at the back of the church, and he spent his days helping the nuns with their chores and running errands for the priest.
