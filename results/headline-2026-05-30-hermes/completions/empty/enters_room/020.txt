 What is it? It is the light, the light of the sun. We know it is the light of the sun. Do you see it or do you take it for granted? You have observed it, you have opened your eyes, you have seen the light come in, chasing away the darkness. You see it, chase away the darkness but do you really see it? What does it tell you, that light, does it tell you anything? We are not talking of the light of the sun, but of the light of understanding, the light that comes with observation and attention. Are you seeing that light? When you look from the window, and see those waters, the clear blue sky, the light of the early morning - the otherness that comes with looking - do you observe all this? What does it tell you? Casual observation tells you very little. But if you observe with attention, with affection, with care, then that very light tells you a great deal. Can you observe the light that is in the room, the light that comes from the rising sun, and look at it not verbally, not say, "How beautiful", or "How nice", but just look at it? Which means that the light is you and you are the light.
