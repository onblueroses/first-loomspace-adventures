 All our lives

This telegram has been travelling toward us.

III

You who were given a kingdom of photographs—

The sweet face in the instant of the camera’s

Medusa moment. There at a kitchen table

Sitting with your mother, the sunlight

Of a lost afternoon across you, a white bowl

Of fruit between you. A garden

In the background. And now, these two figures

In a sunken black-and-white world.

We have carried these images in us,

The living and the dead together

As if they are a secret we must

Protect. And the words we said, or didn’t say,

Part of the great silence that wakes in the night

And asks us who we are and who we were

And in the end, who we will be.

IV

What enters the room now is the long

Journey we have been on, carrying

Each other as we could, the first

Touch of the hand, the last

Word whispered in the dark.

And the great secret that binds us

In a world that goes on and on:

The love that we have for each other

Is not yet, and not ever, enough.
