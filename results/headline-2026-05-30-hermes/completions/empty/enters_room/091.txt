 I don't understand what it is. But I know it's not a person. It's a form, a human shape. There's something wrong with its face. There's something wrong with the way it stands in the doorway and breathes. It's as if it has no idea how to breathe and is doing it all wrong. The light from the hallway is behind it, making the shape hard to see, but I can hear it breathing in strange, erratic gasps. Its silhouette is awkward, and parts of it seem to be missing. It doesn't move for a long time, but when it does it enters my room like it's being controlled by something outside of itself. Its legs bend in weird ways, and its head twitches and snaps back and forth as it comes toward my bed. I can't scream. I can't do anything. I'm frozen. It's not until it's standing over me that I can finally see what's wrong with its face. Its eyes are gone. No, not gone. They've been sewn shut.
