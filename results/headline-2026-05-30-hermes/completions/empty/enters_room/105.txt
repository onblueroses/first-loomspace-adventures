 I'm sure you're all familiar with the idea of a stack. For those of you who aren't, a stack is a simple data structure that operates on the principle of "last in, first out" (LIFO). It's like a stack of plates - the last plate you put on the stack is the first one you take off.

In Python, a stack can be easily implemented using a list. The `append()` method is used to push an item onto the stack, and the `pop()` method is used to remove an item from the stack.

Here's a simple example of how to use a stack in Python:

```python
stack = []

# Push items onto the stack
stack.append(1)
stack.append(2)
stack.append(3)

# Pop items from the stack
item = stack.pop()  # item is 3
item = stack.pop()  # item is 2
item = stack.pop()  # item is 1
```
