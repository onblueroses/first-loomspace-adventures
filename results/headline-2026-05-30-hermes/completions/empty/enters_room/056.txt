 Three figures in cloaks. Hoods up. They stand in a triangle, facing each other. The sounds of their breathing fill the space. A rhythm is established. One of them stamps their foot. Another one joins in. The third does the same. The three of them are stamping their feet in unison. The rhythm gets faster. Faster. Faster. Until they suddenly stop. Silence. They each let out a breath. Then they slowly turn to face the audience. They pull down their hoods. They are three women. Three witches. And they speak in unison.

**WITCHES** When shall we three meet again? In thunder, lightning or in rain?

**WITCH 1** When the hurlyburly's done, When the battle's lost and won.

**WITCH 2** That will be ere the set of sun.

**WITCH 3** Where the place?

**WITCH 1** Upon the heath.

**WITCH 2** There to meet with Macbeth.

**WITCHES** Fair is foul, and foul is fair: Hover through the fog and filthy air.
