 A boy in a green turban, with a blue robe, and a red sash. He is carrying a golden tray.

##### 16
What is on the tray. A silver dagger with a black handle, and a golden chalice filled with a crimson liquid.

##### 17
What happens next. The boy presents the tray to the man, who takes the dagger and the chalice. The man raises the dagger high, and then plunges it into his own heart. As his lifeblood flows, he catches it in the chalice. He then hands the chalice to the boy, who drinks deeply.

##### 18
What the man says, as the boy drinks. "The blood is the life."

##### 19
What happens to the boy. He begins to convulse, and then transforms into a woman, with long black hair, and eyes like the night sky.

##### 20
What the woman says. "I am the Queen of the Night. I have been reborn."

##### 21
What happens next. The man removes his hood, revealing a face that is both young and old, with eyes that shine like stars. He takes the woman's hand, and they begin to dance.
