An energy.

An essence.

A spirit.

The one who once lived here.

The one who once breathed here.

The one who once loved here.

The one who once died here.

The spirit enters the room and fills it with its presence.

The air grows thick with the weight of memories.

Memories of laughter.

Memories of tears.

Memories of love.

Memories of loss.

The spirit moves through the room, touching every object, every surface, every corner.

It lingers on the bed where it once slept.

It caresses the walls that once held its secrets.

It brushes against the windows that once framed its view of the world.

The spirit is not alone in the room.

There are others here too.

Other spirits.

Other energies.

Other essences.

They are the spirits of those who came before.

The spirits of those who lived and loved and died in this house.

They are the spirits of the past.

And they are here now, in this room, with the spirit of the present.
