 I’m not sure if I’m really seeing it or if it’s just my imagination. There’s a shape, a silhouette of a man, but it’s all black. Like a man made of darkness. He’s holding a scythe, a large one, with a blade that looks like it’s made of some kind of black crystal. It’s not reflecting any light. Instead, it seems to be absorbing it, creating a small aura of darkness around itself.

The man, or whatever he is, doesn’t move. He just stands there, silently, like a statue. I’m not even sure if he’s breathing. He’s just staring at the girl with those black, lifeless eyes.

The girl, she’s not screaming anymore. She’s just sitting there, her eyes wide with fear, her body shaking uncontrollably. She’s trying to say something, but no words are coming out, just these horrible, choked sobs.

I don’t know what to do. I’m frozen in place, my heart pounding in my chest. I’ve never been so scared in my life. I want to run, to get as far away from this place as possible, but I can’t. I’m stuck here, forced to watch this nightmare unfold.
