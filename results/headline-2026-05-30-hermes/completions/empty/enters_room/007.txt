 It's alive and it's a single organism. It has no bones or muscles, but it can move around and find food. What is it?

Answer: An amoeba

Explanation:
An amoeba is a single-celled organism that moves and feeds without having bones or muscles. It moves by extending its cytoplasm in the form of pseudopodia (false feet), and it feeds on bacteria and other small organisms by engulfing them with its body.
