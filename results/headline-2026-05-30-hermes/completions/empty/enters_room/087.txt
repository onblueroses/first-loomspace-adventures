"Impossible," scoffed Margo. "There was only one Inquisitor. He was the

The Doctor looked at her with pity. "Oh Margo, there's always another
one. There will always be evil in the universe. It is the balance to
goodness. The more good there is, the more evil there will be to
balance it out."

"I don't believe that," said Margo stubbornly.

"It doesn't matter whether you believe it or not, it's true," said the
Doctor. "But enough of this. I must finish my work before it is too
late."

The Doctor turned back to the console and pressed some buttons. The
central column started to rise and fall and the familiar wheezing noise
filled the room.

"What are you doing?" said Margo.

"I'm taking us to the beginning of time," said the Doctor. "To the
moment when the Inquisitor was created."

"But that's impossible," said Margo. "You can't change history."
