  
Is it the wind, that has broken open  
the door of the mind?  
Is it the storm, that has put out  
the fire?  
Is it the night that has cut off  
the kindling of the eyes?  
Is it the sleep, that has burst through  
the mind?  
Is it the dream, that has come in  
through the window?  

What enters the room now,  
and stands before me?  
Is it the wind, or the storm, or the night,  
or the sleep, or the dream?  
Is it the wind, or the storm, or the night,  
or the sleep, or the dream, that has come  
to take me away?  

What enters the room now,  
and stands before me,  
and looks at me, with eyes  
that are not eyes,  
and speaks to me, with a voice  
that is not a voice,  
and says to me:  

"I am the wind, and the storm, and the night,  
and the sleep, and the dream.  
I am the wind, that has broken open  
the door of your mind.  
I am the storm, that has put out  
the fire of your thoughts.  
I am the night
