 Is this the same man who left his bed in the early hours of this morning? Is this the man who conceded to his wife's request for a divorce, and who this morning had nothing to live for? Is he truly the same man? For he's coming through the door with an arm raised in triumph, the blood singing in his ears, his heart soaring like an eagle. A man who knows his value to the world, who believes he can achieve anything, who is at the very centre of the web of his own good fortune, who has no reason to fear critics, enemies, pretty women, rivals. And indeed, if you'd seen him only this morning, struggling to open his eyes, moaning with dread, as grey and crumpled as the sheets he clung to, you'd ask, how can this be the same man?
