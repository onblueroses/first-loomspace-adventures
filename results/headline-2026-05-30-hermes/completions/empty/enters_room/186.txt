 First, I should say, the poet is a person who is able to articulate the representation of the feeling. And I think feeling is primary. The feeling is what you start with. It’s what I start with. You don’t know what it is. You don’t have a word for it. You don’t have a phrase for it. It could be anything. I mean it could be a color. It could be a tone. It could be a memory. It could be a bodily sensation. It could be a — it’s some sensation that you have. And so, for me, it’s trying to render that, to articulate it. And it may take a long time. I mean, that’s why I go back to the poems over and over again because I’m trying to find the language that’s going to be adequate to this feeling. And it’s never quite adequate. I mean, you can never — you can never actually fully say it. And I think that’s the thing about poetry is that you’re always indicating something that is a little bit more than what you’re literally saying.
