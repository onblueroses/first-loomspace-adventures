 A few small particles of dust, perhaps, stirred up by the air conditioner. The force of the particles on the door is distributed over the entire surface of the door and is probably very close to zero. The door will not open unless the force of the particles on the door is greater than the force of the door on the particles, so we will say that the door will not open and our answer is: $\text{no}$. $_\square$
