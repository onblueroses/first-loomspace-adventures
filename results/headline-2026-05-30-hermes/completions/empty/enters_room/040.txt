### 17
So I'm standing there in the middle of the room when I hear something behind me. Some sound. A movement. A shifting or something. And I want to turn around. I want to look and see. But I know I can't. I know I can't turn around because something terrible will happen if I do. Something terrible will be there. Something I don't want to see. Some horrible thing. Something beyond my worst imaginings. So I don't turn around. I don't turn around even though I hear the sound again. A movement. A shifting. I don't turn around. I stand there. I stand there facing forward. Facing forward into the room. The room that seems to be getting smaller. And darker. And colder. I stand there. I don't move. I don't turn around. I don't turn around and look at what's behind me. Even though I hear the sound again. The movement. The shifting. I don't turn around. I stand there. I stand there facing forward.
