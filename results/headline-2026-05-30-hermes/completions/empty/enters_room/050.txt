 I have seen
How the pure soul of morning takes its flight
Into the infinite. I know the dark
And the bright mystery of love. I know
Time’s awful weight, and know eternity,
The unwearied runner, and have touched
The sweet still water of serenity.
I have been ravished by the proud cold beauty
Of dawns and starlight. I have lain awake
To hear the holy silence of the night
Breathe like a sleeping child. I have seen
The world stripped of its clothes, and I have seen
The stars come down to kiss the sea. I have walked
Alone with God, and heard his angels weep
The tears of pity. I have seen the sun
Rise like a king, and I have seen him set
Like a spent swimmer, crimson with his blood,
That drips into the sea. I have lain with love
And heard the grave voice of the earth
Speak of eternity. I have seen
The dewy eyes of children stare at me,
And known their innocence. I have walked
With death, and I have touched the stony face
Of immortality. I have seen
