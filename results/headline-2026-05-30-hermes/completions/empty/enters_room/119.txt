 It is not a sudden break, as if a new scene began, but a flowing from what was before: out of the light of the late afternoon, from the opened door, there enters, there suddenly is, a woman, a girl, rather; in the doorway she stands, hesitating as it were between the light behind her and the darkening room. And there she is, framed by the door, a dark silhouette, slowly to be dissolved, as the eyes become accustomed to the change of light, into colours and then into features, into a face. A girl. A slender girl. In the doorway she stands, wearing a light summer dress, the light of the late afternoon behind her. The light is reflected by her dress, is shining through her dress, through the light texture of her dress, so that the contours of her body are visible, silhouetted, as it were: her shoulders, her hips, her legs. And her hair is loose, falling over her shoulders. And she is smiling. A girl is standing in the doorway, smiling.
