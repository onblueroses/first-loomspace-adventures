| I have seen you enter a room,
walking toward me, your eyes
fixed on mine, never wavering,
as if you had been watching me
for a long time and knew
I would be there. You enter
my poems that way, your eyes
wide open, your body full
of light, your hands open
to receive me. |

| There is no hesitation in you,
no uncertainty, no turning
back, as if you know
I am waiting for you,
have been waiting for years,
and you are walking toward me
to touch me, to take me
into your arms, to hold me
against your body, to whisper
into my ear, to kiss me
on the mouth, to undress me
slowly, to lie down with me
on the floor, to enter me,
to rock with me, to enter me
until we are still, until
we are one, until we are
so still we are the silence
that enters the room now. |
