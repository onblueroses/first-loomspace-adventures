 (Enter CLEOPATRA, IRAS, ALEXAS, and the Train.)

Enobarbus (aside): Hark, the music!

Cleopatra (to Antony): Ah, my lord, what a spent fool this makes me!

Antony: What! Is this not a soldier's kiss? (He kisses her.)

Cleopatra: It is an armor that I never saw before. There's a great spirit gone! Thus did I desire it. What womanhood would be in me, if I denied it? But I will not look upon it now. I'll lock it in my chest, and there it shall remain, to pyramids built by newer hands. When we have done our celebration, we will send for you again.

Antony: Let's go. Come. (Exeunt all but Enobarbus.)

Enobarbus: That chest will hold a goodly quantity of love, if my estimate is right. Antony's heart is big enough to hold both Rome and Egypt. He will give up his throne to become a muleteer in Egypt.
