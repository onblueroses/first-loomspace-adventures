 “And when you have spoken these names, the spirit will appear in the form of a young boy, and will speak with a voice as sweet as honey. And he will do your bidding.”

### Transcription

Below is a transcription of the manuscript:

Many and wondrous are the ways of the spirits, and hidden from the eyes of the uninitiated. But to the wise, they are as clear as the light of the sun. For the spirits are ever present, and they are the source of all knowledge and power.

And among the spirits, there are those who are most powerful, and who can grant to the magician the power to perform great deeds. And these spirits are known by many names, but their true names are known only to the wise.

And the wise have written down the names of these spirits, and the ways in which they may be summoned, in books of great power. And these books are hidden from the eyes of the uninitiated, for they contain the secrets of the universe.

But to the wise, these books are as clear as the light of the sun, and they may be read and understood by those who have been initiated into the mysteries.
