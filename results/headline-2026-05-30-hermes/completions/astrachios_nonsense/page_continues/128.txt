 "and by these names you can move the spirits of the air, earth, fire or water, and they will open to you the treasures of the earth and reveal to you the secrets of the sea and of the air, and you can bind the spirits of men and women and animals and all things will be subject to you."

## Reception

In 1939, the anthropologist E. E. Evans-Pritchard noted that the claim in the book that its text originated in Egypt was "untrue".
