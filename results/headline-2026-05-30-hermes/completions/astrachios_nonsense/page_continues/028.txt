 "In the name of the holy, blessed, and glorious Trinity, proceed our experiment in these following ways."

## In popular culture

The Ars Notoria is mentioned in Jorge Luis Borges's short story "The Secret Miracle": "He had mentally constructed an intrigue of blinds and interferences, an intrigue with a kabbalistic underplot, related to the names of the One, which the magic of the Ars Notoria governs..."

The Ars Notoria is also mentioned in the novel The Three Impostors by Arthur Machen, wherein John Watkins, who studies the grimoire, disappears after some time.
