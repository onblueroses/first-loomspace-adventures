 "Now say the prayer of the 17th page and the spirits will appear." The prayer in question reads: "I invoke you, spirits who wander through the air, Lunalos, Nogahal, Lulogri, Krisophar, Nosnachi, Petharoph, Zarobath, Zarobi, Memrothos, Trasfori, Nustidion, and Alkaboth, that you will come to me and remain visible to my sight, in the form of pleasant young men, and give me true and faithful answers to all I ask."
