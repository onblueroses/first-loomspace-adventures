 "Repeat these names often, and they will be in your memory."

### Enthronement

The final ritual, the "Enthronement of the Lord of the Air," calls for the magician to repeat a series of conjurations to call forth the "Princes of the Elemental Realms" to acknowledge the magician as the "Lord of the Air." The magician then commands the spirits to serve him, and performs a "Consecration of the Air" by reciting a set of names which empower the magician's commands. The magician then assumes the title "Phorlakh, Lord of the Air," and the ritual concludes.
