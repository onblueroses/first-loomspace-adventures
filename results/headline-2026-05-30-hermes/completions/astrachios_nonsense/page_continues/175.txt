 "At this point the veil of the sanctuary is drawn back, revealing an altar, on which are seen the chalice and wafer for the Communion, and a censer. The deacon takes up the censer and censes the altar, after which he faces West and recites the following exorcism:"

Exorcism of the Elements

We cast out from these elements of the material plane all influences of the demon Aversa, that they may be pure and holy in the sight of the Lord of the Aethyr.

Zorothothra, Zorothothra, Zorothothra, Kitholoph, Phendropa, Zoroth, Zoroth, Zoroth.

The page continues: "The deacon replaces the censer, and takes up the chalice. He elevates it, and says:"

We take refuge, O Lord of the Aethyr, in thy perfect and all-embracing Love. As we call upon thy Name, so do we take into our hearts thy Presence, and thy Power, and the inspiration of thy Breath divine. Therefore by the authority of thine own Word, we do dare to say:

Holy art thou, Lord of the Universe. Holy
