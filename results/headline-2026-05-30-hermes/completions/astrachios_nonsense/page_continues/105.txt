At the bottom of the page was the symbol of the Black Temple, and a note: “These names were obtained by the Grand Master of the Temple, who received them from the demon Rorvaduk, in the year of the Scorpion.”

I uttered the names aloud, and vanished.

I awoke in the underground chamber of the Black Temple. Beside me lay my body, pale and lifeless. I realized that I had died, and my spirit had been transported to this place. The Grand Master stood before me, clad in black robes, his face hidden by a hood.

“Welcome, seeker of knowledge,” he said. “You have proven yourself worthy by speaking the names and sacrificing your earthly form. Now you may join us in our quest for power and dominion over the spiritual realms.”

I looked around the chamber, and saw other spirits, all wearing black robes. They chanted in unison, their voices echoing off the stone walls. The air was thick with the scent of incense and smoke.
