 "The reader may observe that many of the names in this catalogue of devils were borrowed from the Arbatel of Magic; and that several of them are derived from the Greek numerals, with the syllable on added to them. Thus, klimen from [Greek: klimen], 30; polisen from [Greek: polis], 50; onthelosk from [Greek: ogdoekonta], 80; engroth from [Greek: hekaton], 100; phenrolop from [Greek: pentakosia], 500; onthrolankr from [Greek: tetrakosia], 400; and phenroth from [Greek: pentakosia], 500."
