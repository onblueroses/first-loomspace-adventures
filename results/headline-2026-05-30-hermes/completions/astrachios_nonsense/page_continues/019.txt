 “Say this word five times: Gomphoraph, Gomphoraph, Gomphoraph, Gomphoraph, Gomphoraph.”

Once again, the text is a mixture of seemingly random letters. Despite the lack of coherent meaning, the text appears to suggest that these are names or words of power that, when spoken, will open a way or path for the speaker. The repetition of “Gomphoraph” five times at the end could be a further incantation or a key to activate the power of the previous words.

The use of such fantastical and alien-sounding names is a common feature in many magical and mystical texts, as well as in works of fantasy literature. They are often meant to evoke a sense of ancient, otherworldly power and to give the speaker a sense of control over supernatural forces.
