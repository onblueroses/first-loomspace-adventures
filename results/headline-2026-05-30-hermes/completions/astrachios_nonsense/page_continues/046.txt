The ritual appears to be successful, and the party is teleported to the entrance of the crypt. They find themselves in a large, circular room with a domed ceiling. In the center of the room stands a stone sarcophagus, its lid carved with the figure of a robed man holding a staff. The walls are adorned with frescoes depicting scenes of battle and arcane rituals.

As they approach the sarcophagus, they trigger a magical trap. The frescoes on the walls come to life, and the figures step out into the room, attacking the party. The battle is fierce, but the party emerges victorious.

They open the sarcophagus and find the remains of the archmage, clutching a staff in his skeletal hands. The staff is adorned with arcane runes and set with a large, glowing gemstone. As soon as they touch the staff, they are transported back to the library.
