 “By these names, and in the name of the Great Elemental King Egin, I command the spirits of the Earth to open the way and allow me to pass through the Gate of the Earth, that I may enter the Elemental Realm of Earth.”

The ritual instructions specify that the aspirant must speak the names and command in a loud and clear voice, while facing the North. After the command, the aspirant should focus their thoughts on the Earth Element and meditate upon its qualities, while imagining themselves passing through the gate into the Elemental Realm of Earth.

This ritual is intended to be performed as part of a larger ritual for attaining knowledge and conversation with one's personal genius or higher self, known as the "Ritual of the Aethyr" in the Thelemic tradition. The ritual is performed after the invocation of the elemental forces, and before the actual invocation of the personal genius.
