 "Now the veils of darkness are torn asunder, and the black gulfs of outer space yawn before me...and I hear the voice of the dragon Fafnir, roaring from the deep...he lurks below the foundations of the world. He is coiled about the roots of Yggdrasil, Nidhoggr, the serpent of the underworld, who lies at the base of the tree, and gnaws at the roots."

The page ends with a chant to summon the serpent:

Nidhoggr, Nidhoggr, Nidhoggr, come to me!
I summon thee, I summon thee, I summon thee!

A drawing of the world tree Yggdrasil is shown, with the words "The Great Worm Nidhoggr, who gnaws at the roots of Yggdrasil, the world-tree."

The next page is titled "The Summoning of the Ancient Ones." It contains a chant to summon the Ancient Ones:

Zatha Nachraken: Tordai Kruddak Nigriak Torzul Arachnidok!
Zaranak Taranak Arachnidok! Zaranak Taranak Nigriak!
Zaranak Taranak Torzul
