 "In the following pages I will explain the Key to these words which were revealed to me by the Great Angel Guardian, Rasiel, who is the angel of my birth and the Keeper of the Keys to the Secret Wisdom sent to earth by the Archangels, Michael, Gabriel, Raphael and Uriel. They are the guardians of the four quarters of the earth and the four elements of creation."

## In popular culture

In the 1987 film Angel Heart, the book is in the possession of ethnologist Harold Angel. The names of the demons and spirits listed are said to be the "lost names" of the descendents of the angels who mated with humans, known as the "nephilim" or "watchers", but are actually names of demons and spirits from the Lesser Key of Solomon. The book is not mentioned in the original novel Falling Angel by William Hjortsberg.

In the 2019 film The Devil's Revenge, a preacher named Harold (played by William Shatner) owns a copy of the book. He tells the main character, Ernest, that the book is the only thing that can save his daughter from the clutches of the devil. The book is used as a plot device to get Ernest to sell his soul
