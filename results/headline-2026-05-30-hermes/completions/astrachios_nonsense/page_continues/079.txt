The remainder of the manuscript is a collection of spells and magical symbols. The spells are meant to accomplish various tasks, such as obtaining information, causing the practitioner to be well-liked, or even causing harm or death to others.

## Analysis

The manuscript is written in a form of Middle English in the English dialect of South East England. The vocabulary, however, is not limited to English, and the book contains eleven coded diagrams, some of which appear to be related to those found in a late medieval English alchemical work known as the "Ars Notoria". There are also Hebrew, Greek, and Latin words and phrases, as well as a few instances of Old English characters.

The book is divided into three sections. The first, in English, contains prayers to the Christian Trinity, to the Virgin Mary, and to the angels and saints, as well as invocations of various biblical figures and nine orders of angels.
