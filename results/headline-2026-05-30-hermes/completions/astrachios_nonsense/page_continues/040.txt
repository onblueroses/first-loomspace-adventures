 "These names are of a cosmic nature and will not be understood by the uninitiated."

The passage is accompanied by a diagram of the "Dagon Sigil" which the magician is to "place within the Great Circle" and wear. The magician is also instructed to "Use the Angle-Web to determine the time." The text does not explain the meaning of the Angle-Web.

The next section of the text, "The Final Word", ends with the statement: "I wish to learn the secret of time, the secret of the angles, the secret of the origins of all things." The text ends with the declaration: "The Final Word is the lost word, the lost angle. I can say no more."

## Influence

The Simon Necronomicon has been the subject of discussion and controversy among occultists and Lovecraft fans. Some occultists have argued that it is a legitimate grimoire and a powerful system of magic, while others have dismissed it as fiction and a hoax. Some Lovecraft fans have enjoyed it as a work of fiction inspired by Lovecraft's mythos, while others have decried it as a distortion or misrepresentation of Lovecraft's ideas.
