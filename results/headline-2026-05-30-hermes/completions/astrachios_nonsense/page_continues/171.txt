 "I, Solomon, having received this knowledge from the Lord Sabaoth by his holy angels, possess and command all spirits, being strengthened by the power of the Almighty Elohim, who will cast them into the lake of fire, lest they hear these words and remain."

The manuscript also contains invocations, formulae, and other magical names, including those of twenty-four angels who "govern the demons in the air, on the earth, and under the earth".

## Authenticity

There are a number of reasons to doubt the authenticity of the work, for example:

* the use of paper, which did not arrive in Europe until the 11th century;
* the apparent use of a grimoire, or book of spells, which would have been unusual for a Jewish text of this period;
* the use of the name 'Solomon', which may have been chosen to enhance the credibility of the text;
* the ungrammatical use of Greek and Hebrew;
* the reference to Belial being imprisoned in the burning lake, which is a motif from the Book of Revelation in the New Testament.
