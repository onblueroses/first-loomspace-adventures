 "This is the key that opens the gate to the palace of the archons."

The idea of invoking specific names to open the way through a spiritual barrier is an ancient one, found in the Egyptian Book of the Dead and many other sources. The names themselves are mostly invented, and are meant to sound impressive rather than to have any specific meaning; the author of the text was clearly aiming for an effect rather than trying to communicate any particular teaching. Still, there's a point to the exercise; the use of such baroque methods to open the way to the "palace of the archons" -- the realm of the planetary spirits, in the language of the Cos.Doc. -- is a common feature of magical practice from many different times and places, and helps focus and direct the imagination in ways that more straightforward methods may not always be able to do.
