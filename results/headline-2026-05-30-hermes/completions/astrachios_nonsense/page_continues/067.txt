 "In the name of all these spirits and demons, I conjure and command you, O spirit of N., to appear to me and speak truthfully, by the power of the One who shall come to judge the living and the dead and the world by fire."

The spirits and demons mentioned in the incantation are not well-known figures from Christian demonology, suggesting that they may be derived from pre-Christian Germanic or Slavic mythology, or perhaps the names were fabricated by the author of the manuscript.

## In popular culture

In 2017, a fictionalized version of the Codex Gigas appeared in the first season of the NBC TV series The Librarians ("And the Apple of Discord").

The Codex Gigas also appears in the 2021 adventure film The Advent Calendar.

The Codex Gigas is also the basis for a mystery in the 2021 video game Shadow of the Tomb Raider.

The Codex Gigas is the subject of the 2023 book The Manuscript by J. Robert Kennedy.

The Codex Gigas is the subject of a 2023 novel by Stephen King, titled Holly.
