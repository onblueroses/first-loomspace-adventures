 "When you have repeated these words the demons will appear before you. They will ask what you desire, and you shall give them an answer. They will bow to your command if you use the right words in the right manner. On their foreheads are their sigils, by which you may know them. These are the words which they cannot refuse to obey."

After this passage a number of sigils are given, followed by more evocations and charms. The names in these evocations are nearly all of the same type as those given above, and are too numerous to quote.

The second part of the book contains a long list of the chief demons and their sigils, with the methods of calling them and the services which they render. The work ends with a description of a number of miscellaneous charms and experiments, including methods of transforming men into animals and animals into men, of making a "black fire" which will destroy anything, of making a "magic carpet," of discovering treasure, of making a man's sword useless in battle, etc.
