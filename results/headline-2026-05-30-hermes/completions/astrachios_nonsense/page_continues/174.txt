### 20. The Celestial City

> This chamber is the most remarkable that you have yet seen in this strange place. The chamber is circular, about forty feet in diameter, with a domed ceiling rising some twenty feet overhead. The entire chamber is covered in gold leaf, and the gold reflects the light of hundreds of candles that burn in sconces around the chamber's perimeter. In the center of the room is a raised circular dais, upon which rests a circular altar of white marble veined with gold. Atop the altar rests a human skull, and atop that skull is a large clear crystal that glows with an inner light.
>
