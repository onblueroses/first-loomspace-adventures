 "By these names, and in the name of the Eternal, I conjure upon the fire of this circle, which I have formed, that it unloose its darts and let issue and come forth Sytry, a noble and potent duke, whom I command to appear before this circle, in a fair and human form, and that he come pacifically and visibly before me, without evil smells or tumultuous noise, without terror or fear, and that he be obedient to my commands, and that he make known to me the truth of all things that I shall ask him, without any falsehood or dissimulation, and also without any ambiguity or difficulty; that he answer me in my mother-tongue; and that he fulful my commands and my requests, as is ordained by God to be fulfilled, failing which, he shall be condemned to the pit and to eternal fire, from which he shall never escape. Come, then, Sytry, in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost; come, Sytry, in the name of the indivisible Trinity, and by all that hath been said before, I command thee, Sytry, to appear forthwith, under the penalty of everlasting damnation.
