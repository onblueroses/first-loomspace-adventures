 "By these names and by their power, I conjure thee, O Spirit N., to appear and show thyself in a fair and comely shape." The magician is instructed to repeat this entire conjuration three times. If the spirit does not appear, the magician is instructed to repeat the entire conjuration again. If the spirit still does not appear, the magician is instructed to use the following conjuration:

I conjure thee by the power of the great Adonay, by the virtue of Eloim, by the strength of Jehovah, by the mercy of Thoth, by the virtue of the heavens, by the force of the earth, by the influence of the air, by the strength of the water, by the brightness of the stars, by the light of the moon, by the splendor of the sun, that thou O spirit N. do appear and show thyself in a fair and comely shape.

If the spirit still does not appear, the magician is instructed to use the following conjuration:
