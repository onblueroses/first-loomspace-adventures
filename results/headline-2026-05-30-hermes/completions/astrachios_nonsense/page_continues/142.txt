 "I see the great black gate in the wall of the city. It opens slowly outward. As it opens, a hot, fetid wind blows from the blackness within. I smell the stale and musty air of a thousand years of darkness. I hear the shuffling of a myriad feet, and the distant clanking of chains. I see the gate is open, but all within is black as the darkest night. The fetid wind blows stronger, and the shuffling and clanking grows louder. I know that the legions of the dead are within, but I cannot see them. I stand before the open gate, waiting for the Great One to come forth."

The following pages are filled with the names of the dead, and the circumstances of their death. There are hundreds of thousands of names, and the book is an inch thick. The last page is blank.

To use the book, the caster must stand before the gate to the city of the dead, holding the book open to the first page. The caster must then read the spell aloud, and when they finish, the gate will open, and the caster will be able to see the legions of the dead within.
