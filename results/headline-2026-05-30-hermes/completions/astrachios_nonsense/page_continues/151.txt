 "When you have said these words, the door will open. Enter and you will find a stone chest; in it is a marble box, and in this box are twelve figures of animals. Take out the one which is in the form of a cat, and fill the cavity with oil. Put it back in the marble box, and leave as you came. The door will close behind you. Depart in safety, for your wish is accomplished."

The ritual was actually carried out in 1936 by Dorothy and her husband Montague Summers, and they reported that it had been entirely successful.

The authenticity of the Grimoire of Pope Honorius has been repeatedly called into question. It is unlikely to have been written by its supposed author, Pope Honorius III, and may even have been composed after its first publication date of 1629. However, as has been pointed out by several authors, the efficacy of a grimoire may be quite unrelated to its historical authenticity. The words of the spell and the actions it prompts may have a potency of their own.
