## Interpretation

The invocation of spirits of the underworld is a common theme in the Simon Necronomicon, which is said to be based on Mesopotamian mythology and magic, with influences from other traditions such as the Greek and Egyptian. The names used in the incantation are likely invented or derived from various sources, as they do not correspond to any known deities or entities from ancient mythologies.

The purpose of this ritual is to establish contact with the spirits of the underworld, possibly to gain knowledge, power, or assistance. The incantation is meant to be spoken aloud, with the practitioner visualizing the opening of the "Gate of the Abyss" and the spirits emerging from it.

However, it is essential to note that the Simon Necronomicon is a controversial work, with many occultists and scholars questioning its authenticity and the effectiveness of its rituals. The book has been criticized for its lack of historical accuracy, its sensationalism, and its potential for promoting dangerous practices.
