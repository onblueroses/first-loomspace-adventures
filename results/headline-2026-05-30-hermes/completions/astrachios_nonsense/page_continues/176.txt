 "When you have said these words, you will see the palace of the seven planets, with its gates, and in the gates the seven gods of the planets, who will speak to you and say, 'Enter our palace.' Then you will enter the palace, and the gods of the seven planets will give you the ring and seal of the sun, and the key to the palace, and they will say to you, 'Now you are the master of the palace, and you can enter and leave as you wish.' And when you leave the palace, the gods of the seven planets will speak to you and say, 'Open the gates,' and when you have opened the gates, they will say to you, 'Now go to your home, and when you wish to come again to the palace, take the key in your hand and say the names we have taught you, and the palace will appear before you, and you may enter and leave as you wish.'"
