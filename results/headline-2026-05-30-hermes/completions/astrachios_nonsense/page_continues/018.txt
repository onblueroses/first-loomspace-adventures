### Summary

In the year 2525, humanity has spread across the galaxy, but has fallen into a state of decline. The powerful, decadent, and bored ruling class, which is concentrated on the pleasure planet Sardath, has turned to sorcery and the worship of dark gods and demons in a bid to gain new thrills.

One of these aristocrats, Lady Sondra Dorchester, is having an affair with the spacefaring merchant Erich Carstairs. Carstairs is a fairly honest man, but he has turned to drug smuggling in an effort to raise enough money to buy a title. He and Lady Sondra have a falling out, and she spitefully tips off the police, who seize Carstairs's latest shipment of drugs. In a fit of anger, Carstairs tries to kill her, but she escapes.
