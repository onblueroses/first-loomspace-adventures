## Analysis

The manuscript is written in a mixture of Middle English and Latin. The first part of the manuscript, the Ars Notoria, contains a series of prayers. The second part, the Liber Officiorum, is concerned with the conjuration of demons. The third part, the Liber de morte anime, deals with resurrection spells. The fourth part, the Liber de vita anime, deals with exorcism spells. The fifth part, the Liber de revelatione, deals with scrying spells. The sixth part, the Liber de secretis, deals with spells for finding hidden treasures.
