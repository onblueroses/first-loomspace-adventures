## Behind the scenes

The incantation is written in English, with the names of power being the only words that are not in English. This is consistent with the general presentation of magic in the Dungeons & Dragons universe, where spells and magical incantations are often presented as being in a language that the reader can understand, with only specific words or phrases being in an arcane or fictional language.
