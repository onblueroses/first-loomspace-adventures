In the 19th century, some French editions of the Grand Grimoire included the Grimoire of Pope Honorius, and it was occasionally known as the Grand Grimoire tout nouveau, or "The Much Improved Grand Grimoire". This volume also included the divinatory technique of the "necromantic nails", which was attributed to Solomon.
