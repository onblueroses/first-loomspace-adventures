 "By these holy names, and by all the others, which none may know, I conjure thee, O Book, to be useful and profitable unto all those who shall read thee for the success of their affairs. I conjure thee anew, O Book, by the virtue of the Blood of Jesus Christ, contained daily in the chalice, to be serviceable for all those who shall read thee. I exorcise thee, O Book, in the name of the Most Holy Trinity, in the name of the Most Holy Trinity, in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. Amen."

The manuscript continues with directions for the use of the talismans, and conjurations of superior efficacy; these, however, are not more remarkable than those already cited, and I have therefore omitted them.

## The Enchiridion in Print

The Enchiridion is in reality two books in one. The first book is titled, "The Enchiridion of Pope Leo III." The second book is titled, "The Arbatel of Magic" or simply "The Arbatel."
