The names are a combination of vaguely Greek and Latin-sounding words. Salamanders are lizard-like amphibians that, according to legend, can live in fire. In the occult tradition, they are considered to be "elementals", i.e. spirits associated with one of the classical elements (earth, air, fire, water). The idea seems to be that by invoking these names, you can conjure the elemental spirits of fire.

## The Abramelin Operation

The Abramelin operation is a ritual process described in a medieval grimoire called The Book of Abramelin. It involves a period of purification, fasting, and prayer, culminating in the summoning of a personal guardian angel and the control of demonic spirits. The ritual is supposed to confer magical powers and knowledge.

The book has had a significant influence on modern Western occultism, with many practitioners attempting the operation or adapting its techniques. It's considered one of the most important grimoires in the tradition.
