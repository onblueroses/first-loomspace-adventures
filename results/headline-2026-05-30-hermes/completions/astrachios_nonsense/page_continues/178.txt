## In popular culture

The following is a list of references to the Key of Solomon in popular culture.

* In the original Japanese version of the Yu-Gi-Oh! anime, it is stated that the Millennium Key is the Key of Solomon.
* In the Supernatural season 15 episode "The Trap," the demon Belphegor mentions the Key of Solomon when discussing a way to trap the demon god Chuck.
* In the DC Universe, sorcerers such as Sebastian Faust and Mister E own a copy of the Key of Solomon.
* In the film Doctor Sleep, the Key of Solomon is used by Rose the Hat and the True Knot cult to psychically locate potential victims with the Shining ability.
* In the manga Welcome to Demon School! Iruma-kun, the Key of Solomon is mentioned as being required to perform a summoning ritual.
* In the film Warlock, the Warlock uses the Key of Solomon to find the Grand Grimoire.
* In the film The Ninth Gate, a book called the Key of Solomon is mentioned as a primer on demonology.
