 "The recital of these names will open the way."

The names are all nonsense words, but the first letter of each name, taken in order, spells out a message: "GIVE ME THE POWER TO OPEN THE WAY."

## The Fourth Door

The Fourth Door is a door with a lock on it. It is found in the "Room of the Golden Idols" in the Tomb of Ixkib. The key to the Fourth Door is the Key of the Fourth Door, which is found in the same room, inside the mouth of one of the golden idols.

The Fourth Door leads to the Chamber of the Sun God, which contains a large golden statue of the sun god and a pedestal with a button on it. Pressing the button causes the statue to slide aside, revealing a staircase that leads down to the Fifth Door.

## The Fifth Door

The Fifth Door is a large stone door with a carving of a dragon on it. It is found at the bottom of the staircase that leads down from the Chamber of the Sun God. The door is locked, and the key to it is the Key of the Fifth Door, which is found in the same room, hidden inside the mouth of the dragon carving.
