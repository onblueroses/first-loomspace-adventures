The chamber contains a large sarcophagus which is said to contain the remains of Set, the ancient Egyptian god of chaos and disorder. The chamber is decorated with hieroglyphs and paintings depicting the journey of the soul through the afterlife, as well as scenes of Set's battles with the forces of order and light.
