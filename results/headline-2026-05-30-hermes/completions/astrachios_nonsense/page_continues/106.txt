 "When the Name has been spoken, the Way is Open and the Gate may be entered."

The formula is said to open a doorway into the Void, through which the sorcerer may pass to the spaces between the stars. It may also be used to open a gate for the passage of other beings. The formula is useless without the proper sacrifices and ceremonies. These are not given in the book.

The Gate may only be opened at certain times:

- When the last grain of sand has fallen from the glass on the night of the new moon
- When the sun has been devoured by the moon
- When the stars have assumed the proper configuration in the heavens

The sorcerer must stand within a properly consecrated pentagram, inscribed with the names of the Old Ones in their own language, when he performs the ceremony. Only then will he be safe from the wrath of the Old Ones.

The ceremony must be performed in a high place, beneath the stars. The air must be clear and the night free from clouds.

The pentagram must be drawn with a rod of magnetized iron. The sorcerer must wear a crown made from the branches of an oak tree that has been struck by lightning, and he must carry a sword forged from meteoric iron.
