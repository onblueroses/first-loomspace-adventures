From Wikipedia:

\begin{blockquote}

The story follows a girl named Tenar, born on the island of Atuan. She is taken from her family and dedicated to the service of the "Nameless Ones" while still a young child. The Nameless Ones are the gods of the Kargish people of the archipelago. They are merciful but demanding, and Tenar is renamed Arha, the "eaten one", to remind her that her life now belongs to the gods. She becomes the high priestess of the Tombs, and her duty is to perform the rituals of the Nameless Ones.
