 "This means that I have read your great work and praise it and think it wonderful. I would like to ask you some questions about it. Will you answer them?"

Lovecraft replied: "Incidentally—your magical incantation, despite its impressive & rhythmic sound, is wholly meaningless & without cabalistic potency. It is composed of joining utterly disjointed syllables & letter-groups at random, & contains no potent ritualistic words or esoteric names of power. It is a mere assemblage of meaningless syllables such as might be uttered by a child learning to talk, or by an adult in a state of delirium. If it has any meaning at all, it can only be in some language or cosmic realm as yet unknown to human lexicographers or explorers."
