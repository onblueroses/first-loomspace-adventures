 "When you say these names, you will see the gates of the four directions open, and the winds will issue forth to greet you. They will bear you up and transform you into a living spirit. Say then: 'Make me to be a spirit as you are, to go and come at my will.'"

This seems to me to be a spell of astral travel. The names are those of spirits who will help the magician to separate from the physical body. The transformation into "a living spirit" is the separation of the astral body from the physical body, and the consequent ability to move and perceive on the astral level. The statement "to go and come at my will" suggests that the magician is seeking to control the astral experiences, rather than simply drifting on the astral tides.

The names of the spirits are unfamiliar to me, and I suspect that they are not the names of powerful, independent beings, but are more in the nature of "vibrations" or "qualities" which the magician seeks to attract or bring into play. The magician is asking for the ability to separate from the body and move about on the astral plane at will, and is invoking these qualities or powers through the use of these names.
