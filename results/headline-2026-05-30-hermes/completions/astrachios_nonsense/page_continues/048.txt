The text is accompanied by an illustration that shows a horned figure with its arms raised to reveal a multitude of eyes on its chest.

## History

The manuscript was first published in 1734 or 1735 in Germany. It was allegedly written by a figure named Abraham of Worms, a Jewish mystic who was supposed to have lived in the 15th or 16th century; however, it is more likely that this is a pseudonym, as there is no record of such a person. Most scholars believe that the work is a compilation of earlier oral and written traditions, and that the date of its composition is later than the purported date of publication.

The book was rediscovered in the collection of the British Library by the British magician Aleister Crowley, who published it in 1898. Crowley's edition included an introduction and notes by Samuel Liddell MacGregor Mathers, a prominent figure in the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn.
