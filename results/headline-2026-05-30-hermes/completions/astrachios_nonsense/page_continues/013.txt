And that's the end of the module.

My copy of the module has notes written in pencil by my 13-year-old self, disapproving of this ending. "BAD ENDING," I wrote. "I think they could have done a better job."

With the benefit of 35 years' hindsight, I'm inclined to disagree with my younger self. This is a fantastic ending.

The module presents a long buildup to a climactic confrontation with a powerful sorceress. The fight happens! And then, the sorceress is gone. The characters are left with a mysterious incantation. The final scene is one of wonder and beauty: the light of the full moon flooding the chamber with silver. 

There's no boss fight, no final battle, no climactic moment of violence. Instead, there is poetry and moonlight and a doorway to the unknown.
