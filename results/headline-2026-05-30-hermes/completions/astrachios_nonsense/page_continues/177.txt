## Cultural references

In his 1979 novel The Book of Skulls, science fiction and fantasy author Robert Silverberg includes a character who is a fanatical student of the Voynich manuscript. This character believes that the book contains the secrets to eternal life.

The manuscript is also the basis for the 1994 novel Gospel of Corax by Paul Park.

The Voynich Manuscript is also featured in the 2003 novel Codex by Lev Grossman.

In the 2004 mystery novel The Rule of Four by Ian Caldwell and Dustin Thomason, two students attempt to decode the secret of the Voynich Manuscript.

The Voynich Manuscript is mentioned in the 2004 novel The Confusion, the second volume in Neal Stephenson's Baroque Cycle.

In the 2009 novel The Lost Symbol by Dan Brown, the Voynich Manuscript is mentioned as being an example of an encoded message. 

In the 2011 mystery novel The Informationist by Taylor Stevens, the protagonist Vanessa Michael Munroe attempts to uncover the secret of the Voynich Manuscript.
