 "By these names, and in the name of the secret of the universe, which I know..."

The implication is that the magician who knows this spell would also know the secret of the universe. This is an example of the sort of lofty promise that was used to sell magical spells in the papyrus rolls of the ancient world.

## In popular culture

The spell is referenced in the 2001 horror film The Mummy Returns, where it is used to resurrect the main antagonist Imhotep. The incantation used in the film roughly follows the actual text of the papyrus, with some minor changes. It is also mentioned in The Simpsons episode "Springfield Up" when Declan Desmond mentions that he is in search of the scroll.
