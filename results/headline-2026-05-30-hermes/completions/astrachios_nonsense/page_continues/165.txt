 

The names of those who sealed the Seventy-two Hall of the Throne and are given as the entrance to the whole of the mysteries of magick and the art of Qabalah, and by these names are the seventy-two Angels of the Shemhamphorash, whose knowledge is the key to the riddles of Heaven and Earth, and by which the door to endless power may be opened, for to know their names and their powers is to have the ability to call upon them in ritual and evoke their powers, and to command their forces and spirits.

## Cultural references

* The Shemhamphorasch appears in the video game Silent Hill 3, as well as the related film, where the names of the entities are used to access a hidden area.
* The Shemhamphorasch appears in the book The Shining by Stephen King, where the names of the entities are used to open a door to a hidden area.
