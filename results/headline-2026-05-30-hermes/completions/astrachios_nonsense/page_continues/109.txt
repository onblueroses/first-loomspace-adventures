 "By these names, and in the name of the High and Mighty Ones who are supreme above the Earth, I charge you, spirits, that you give unto me a true answer to this my question: Is there treasure hidden near this spot? Speak, I command you!"

The book then goes on to describe the proper method for reading the answer to the question, by use of a scrying stone or mirror.

## In popular culture

* In the 1969 Disney film, The Love Bug, the magician who performs the opening incantation quotes from the Grand Grimoire.
* In the 1992 film, Army of Darkness, the protagonist, Ash, uses an incantation from the Grand Grimoire to safely approach and acquire the Necronomicon.
