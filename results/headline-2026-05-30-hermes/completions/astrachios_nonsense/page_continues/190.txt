 "When you have spoken these names, the earth will open and the spirits will appear before you. They will tell you whatever you wish to know." What follows is a list of the spirits' names, divided into four groups, each group governed by a greater spirit. There are 31 spirits in all. The four greater spirits are: Asmoth, or Asmodeus; Maseriel; Barzabel; and Aziel.

There are no illustrations of the spirits in the manuscript. The text describes them as follows:

"Asmoth appears with a lion's head, goose feet, and a rabbit's tail. Maseriel appears as a crowned serpent with a human face. Barzabel appears with a dog's head and goose feet. Aziel appears with a bull's head and goose feet."

The spirits of the first group, under Asmoth, have the heads of lions, those of the second group, under Maseriel, have human heads, those of the third group, under Barzabel, have dog's heads, and those of the fourth group, under Aziel, have bull's heads.
