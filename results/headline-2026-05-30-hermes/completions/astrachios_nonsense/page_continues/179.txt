The page contains a crude diagram of a magic circle, labeled in German.

The book has a few pages of prayers in German, then switches to a series of prayers in Latin, mostly calling on the Virgin Mary and Christ.

There is a diagram of a sigil with the Latin words "Sigillum Beleth" (Seal of Beleth). In demonology, Beleth is a king of Hell who has eighty-five legions of demons under his command. He rides a pale horse, and a symphonic music is heard before him. When appearing he looks fierce to frighten the conjurer or to see if he is courageous. The conjurer must be valiant, and holding a hazel wand in his hand must draw a triangle by striking towards the South, East, and upwards, then commanding Beleth into it by means of some prayers.

The manuscript continues with a series of Latin prayers and invocations.
