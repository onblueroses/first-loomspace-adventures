 "You who have the power to open the gates of the four winds, to cause the air to rush forth in a tempest, or to still the too rapid flight of the fiery horses of the sun, you who can move the stars from their courses, or cause the moon to drop like a stone into the blue depths of the sea, you who can cause the clouds to gather, and the rain to fall, or the rivers to flow back to their sources, you who can make the earth to tremble, and to open and swallow up her children, you who can cause the mountains to cast forth fire, or the sea to cover the face of the land, I call upon you to open the way for me, and to guard me in my journey, for I am one of your fellowship, and my name is written in the Book of the Dead."

This passage will seem nonsense to the ordinary reader, but it contains in cryptic language the key to a mystery, and the student of the occult will understand it.
