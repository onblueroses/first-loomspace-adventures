#### The Secret Page

The secret page is a page in the back of the book. It is accessed by pressing the three symbols found on the first page of the book in the correct order. When the symbols are pressed, the page opens up to reveal a hidden compartment with the page inside. The secret page contains a riddle which leads to the location of the vault of the Vernaseal.

The riddle reads:

In the city of the first,
Beneath the watchful eye,
Where the water flows,
And the lost king lies,
There the treasure rests,
Hidden from prying eyes.

#### The Vault

The vault is a secret room located in the city of the first, beneath the watchful eye, where the water flows, and the lost king lies. It is here that the Vernaseal is kept hidden from the world. The vault can only be accessed by solving the riddle on the secret page and following its clues.

The vault is a large, circular room with a domed ceiling. The walls are lined with shelves holding ancient tomes, artifacts, and relics. In the center of the room stands a pedestal, upon which rests the Vernaseal.
