The book begins with an introduction by "Zosimos of Panopolis", a 4th century alchemist and mystic. Zosimos tells of his childhood, and how he met his teacher, the aged sage, Vachyam. Vachyam tells Zosimos of the history of the world, and of the Elder Gods and the Younger Gods. Vachyam also tells Zosimos of the existence of the Necronomicon, the book of the dead, and of the power that it holds.

Zosimos then tells of his journey to find the Necronomicon, and of the many dangers and obstacles that he faced along the way. He tells of his encounters with the followers of the Younger Gods, and of the many battles that he fought against them.
