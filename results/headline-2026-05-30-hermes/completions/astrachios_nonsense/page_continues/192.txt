 "When you have spoken the names, speak the following words to make the spirit come and answer your questions: 'Gloro und Stendor beschied mir, was ich such, durch diese heiligen Namen, Amen.'"

The grimoire includes instructions for a ritual that will summon the spirit of a deceased person, either to appear beneath the practitioner's bed or to appear in the practitioner's dreams. The grimoire also includes instructions for a ritual to summon a spirit that will bring the practitioner a coin every morning.
