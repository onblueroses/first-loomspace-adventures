 "After saying these words, when the earth opens, say the following: 'Silence, silence, silence, symbol of the living, incorruptible god, guard me, N.N., (your name) and grant that this being, whose name is..., obey me, N.N., in everthing, by the name of the god IAO SABAOTH.'"

The phrase "silence, silence, silence" is a common opening for magical spells of the time, and the "symbol of the living, incorruptible god" likely refers to the ouroboros, a symbol of a snake eating its own tail which was used to represent eternity and the soul of the world.

## Interpretation

The invocation has been used to support the theory that the Gnostic sect known as the Ophites actually worshipped the serpent of the Bible. Epiphanius, a Christian bishop of the fourth century, claimed that the Ophites had a liturgy in which they invoked the names of angels from the "diadem of the Father," which is similar to the list of names in the invocation.
