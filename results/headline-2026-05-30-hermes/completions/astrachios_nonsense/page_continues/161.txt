The "names" are all nonsensical, but they follow a pattern that suggests a language. Each name is one to four syllables long and ends with a consonant. The syllables tend to follow a consonant-vowel-consonant pattern, though sometimes two consonants appear together. Vowels are always in the middle of a syllable, never at the beginning or end. The effect is that of a fantasy language, though one without meaning.

This text is an example of a pseudo-language, a set of words that resembles a language but has no semantic content. Pseudo-languages have been used in many fantasy works, from the "magic words" of comic-book heroes to the lyrics of movie and TV themes (for example, the themes from _The Great Race_ and _Land of the Lost_). They've also been used in role-playing games, both in published works and by individual game masters.
