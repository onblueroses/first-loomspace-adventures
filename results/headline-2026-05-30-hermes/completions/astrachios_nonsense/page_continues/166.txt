 "These words are the keys to unlock the gates of hell, and to bind the fiends fast in their confinement; and to seal the gates of hell as with a padlock, until the end of all things."

In the second book, the author has tried to teach his readers how to perform the art of necromancy, and how to deal with the spirits, using the formulae spelled out in the first book. It also contains a list of the names of superior demons and their roles, and how to summon them.

In the third book, the author gives a list of more than 1000 demons, their names, offices, orders, and the methods to summon them.
