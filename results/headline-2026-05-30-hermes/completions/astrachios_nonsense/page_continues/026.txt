 "Speak the names that open the way." "What way?" I wonder. The way to the truth? To the realm of the dead? To the final revelation of the cosmos? The names themselves are unpronounceable, alien things. They remind me of the bizarre names in occult grimoires, the secret names of angels and demons that the magician is supposed to call upon to do his bidding. Names like Orobas, Astaroth, Asmodeus, Beleth, Baalberith, Focalor, Marbas, Alocer, Botis, Bifrons, Camio, Culador, Dantalion, Decarabia, Eligos, Gemory, Glasya-Labolas, Gremory, Guayota, Häagenti, Ipos, Marax, Morax, Murmur, Naberius, Orias, Ose, Paimon, Purson, Raum, Ronové, Sabnock, Sallos, Shax, Stolas, Sytry, Vapula, Vepar, Vine, Vual, Zagan, Zepar.

There's a sense that by invoking these strange, otherworldly names, one is tapping into ancient and powerful forces beyond mortal ken.
