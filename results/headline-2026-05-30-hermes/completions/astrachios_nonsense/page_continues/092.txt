 "When you have said these words, you will see the gates open and the guards fleeing in terror. Enter the palace and you will behold the king and queen, and if you say these words..."

Here follows another list of barbaric names, which the magician is to repeat, whereupon the king and queen will grant his request. But the author of the work is careful to explain that the invocation must be performed in a place where the magician will be secure from interruption.

The Book of Black Magic and of Pacts, Arthur Edward Waite, 1910
