It raises its head and speaks, saying, "I am Vrindat, the serpent of the Abyss, the devourer of the weak, the destroyer of the unwary. I come forth from the depths of the Abyss, to test your resolve and your power. If you are worthy, I will grant you the power you seek. If you are not, I will consume you, body and soul. What say you, mortal? Will you face me and risk destruction, or will you flee and be forever known as a coward?"

I will face you, Vrindat. I am ready for your test.

The serpent hisses, and its eyes glow brighter. "Very well, mortal. I will test your knowledge first. Answer my riddle, and prove your wisdom:

'I am the beginning of eternity, the end of time and space. I am the beginning of every end, and the end of every place. What am I?'"
