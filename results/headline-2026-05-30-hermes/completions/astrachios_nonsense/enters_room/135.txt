When he speaks, his voice is deep and resonant, echoing through the chamber.

"I am the one who was summoned. I am the one who knows the secrets, the one who holds the key. I am the one who can guide you, who can show you the way. But first, you must prove yourselves worthy. He pauses, letting his words sink in.

"Are you ready?" he asks. "Are you prepared to face the trials, to confront the darkness, to fight for the truth? If so, then step forward. Step forward and let us begin."

The figure nods, satisfied.
