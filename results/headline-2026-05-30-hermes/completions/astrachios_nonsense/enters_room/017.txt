 What is it that follows you now. What will be seen by all who look upon you.

The darkness takes shape. It rises up from the floor, a formless mass, a shadow given life. Eyes open in the black, two burning points of yellow light. The shadow moves. It walks on two legs, arms reaching out, grasping at the air before it. The darkness shifts and changes, and a great serpent's head pushes its way out of the black, a hood like a cobra's flaring wide behind it.

The eyes burn with a cold light. The mouth opens, a red cavern, a doorway to hell. Fangs glisten with venom. A forked tongue flickers out, tasting the air.

The shadow serpent weaves back and forth, drawn to the light and heat of the candles. But it does not come too close. It is bound by the circle.

The serpent watches you. It waits. It is yours to command.

***

What is your first command to the shadow serpent?

You must speak it aloud, in a clear and strong voice. The serpent will hear you and obey.

But be careful. Be very careful. The serpent is a creature of darkness and evil. It will twist your words if it can.
