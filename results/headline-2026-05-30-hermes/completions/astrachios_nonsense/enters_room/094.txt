 [Pause.]

It is a figure, a figure of flame, like a man, but made of flame. It comes into the room, and speaks: "I am here. What do you seek?"

Ask of it what you will. [Pause.]

The figure of flame speaks: "This is the knowledge you seek." [Pause.]

Listen carefully, and remember what it says. [Pause.]

The figure of flame speaks again: "I now return to my place. Do not call me again until the moon has passed full from where it is now." It leaves the room.

Close the way: Lonsop, Kromen, Tron, Asnopolis, Krel, Ont, Reph, Trom, Onth, En, Phen, Ont, Rep, Thol, Phel, On, Kro, Vrin, Pos, Kres, Snol, Vron, Thol, Kro, Mrep, Snolfren, Kresnopith Onthropor.

The way is closed.

Now, slowly, remove the blindfold from your eyes. [Pause.]

Open your eyes, and return to the world.

The rite is ended.
