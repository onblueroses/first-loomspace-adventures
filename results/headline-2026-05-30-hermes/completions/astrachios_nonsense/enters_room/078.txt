### 39. The Golem

The clay man stands in the doorway, his skin dark and shiny like wet mud. He is naked and sexless, his body smooth and unmarked. He is not tall, but he is powerfully built, with thick limbs and a broad chest. His face is blank and expressionless, with small, dark eyes and a wide, flat nose. He moves with a slow, deliberate grace, as if every movement is carefully considered.

As you watch, he steps into the room, his feet making a soft, squishing sound on the stone floor. He looks around, his gaze passing over each of you in turn. When he speaks, his voice is deep and resonant, with a strange, echoing quality.

"I am the guardian of this place," he says. "I have been created to protect the secrets of the temple. If you wish to pass, you must prove yourselves worthy."

He looks at each of you again, as if assessing your strength and determination.

"Are you ready to face the challenges that lie ahead?"
