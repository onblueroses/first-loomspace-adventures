 Speak its name.

Mrephologor, the Guardian of the Gate.

Does it bear the Key?

It does.

In which hand does it hold the Key?

In its left hand.

Bid Mrephologor approach and deliver the Key.

Mrephologor, approach. Deliver the Key that I may unlock the Gate.

Mrephologor, do you freely deliver the Key?

I do.

Receive the Key.

I receive the Key.

Unlock the Gate.

I unlock the Gate.

Mrephologor, return whence you came.

I obey.

The Guardian of the Gate departs. What remains?

The Key, and the Gate unlocked.

Is the way open?

The way is open.

Enter the Gate.

I enter the Gate.

Close the Gate behind you.

The Gate is closed.

What lies before you?

A shining road.

Where does the road lead?

To the Second Heaven.

How do you know this?

By the sign of the Arrow.

What is the token of the Arrow?

A feather of purest white.

Hold the feather of the Arrow in your mind. What is its secret name?

Phelkondor.

Speak the name aloud in the world of the Second Heaven: Phelkondor!
