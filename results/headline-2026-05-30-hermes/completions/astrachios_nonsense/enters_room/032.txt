## Noyutha's Insatiable Hunger

The caster creates a small, black hole in the air, which begins to pull in all nearby matter, growing larger as it consumes more and more. The caster must maintain concentration on the spell, or it will dissipate. If the caster is consumed by the hole, it will continue to grow until it consumes the entire planet.

## The Infinite Echo

The caster's voice is amplified to a deafening volume, and their words echo endlessly, repeating over and over again, growing louder and louder with each repetition. The echoes will continue until the caster dismisses them or is silenced.

## The Unending Storm

The caster summons a massive, unending storm, with winds that can tear apart buildings and lightning that can split the earth. The storm will continue to rage until the caster dismisses it or is killed.
