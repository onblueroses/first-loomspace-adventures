> The chant completed, the air in the chamber thickens and darkens. A low rumble echoes through the stone walls, growing louder with each passing second. The torchlight flickers and dims as if cowering in fear from the power being invoked.

> From the shadows, a massive form emerges, coalescing from the darkness itself. It is a towering figure, easily eight feet tall, with broad shoulders and a muscular build that strains against the black armor encasing its body. The armor is ornate, covered in arcane symbols and sinister reliefs that seem to writhe and shift in the dim light.

> The figure's head is encased in a horned helmet, its face hidden behind a visor of impenetrable shadow. In its gauntleted hands, it grips a colossal war hammer, the head of which is engraved with more eldritch runes that pulse with a sickly green light.
