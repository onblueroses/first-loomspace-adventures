 This is a simple and powerful ritual that will allow you to summon a powerful spirit to your presence. The ritual is easy to perform, and even beginners can expect good results. The spirit that appears will most likely be able to help you with any problem you currently face, whether it be a problem with money, health, work or a relationship. This ritual should be performed at night, in a darkened room. Light two candles and place them a few feet apart.
