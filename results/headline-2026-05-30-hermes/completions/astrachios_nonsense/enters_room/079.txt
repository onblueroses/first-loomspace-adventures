 What you must see.

### 720
The room is a vast and empty space, the walls and floor and ceiling of the same dull grey stone. In the center of the room stands a figure, a figure of flame and smoke and shadow, towering and shifting, a figure that burns and consumes and devours.

The figure speaks, its voice the roar of a thousand fires, the whisper of a thousand shadows.

"I am the end and the beginning," it says. "I am the devourer of worlds, the consumer of suns. I am the darkness that waits at the end of all things, the fire that burns in the heart of every star. I am the shadow that falls across every path, the flame that consumes every hope. I am the end of all stories, the final page of every book. I am the last breath of every living thing, the last thought of every dying mind. I am the end of all things, and the beginning of nothing. I am the void, the emptiness, the silence. I am the end."

The figure shifts and burns, its form changing, its shape twisting. It becomes a tower of flame, a pillar of smoke, a shadow that fills the room. It becomes a beast with a thousand eyes
