The one whose voice is silence.

What is the message of the one whose voice is silence?

What is your question for the one whose voice is silence?

(You must ask your question silently, within your own mind, and wait for the answer to arise in silence.)

When the answer has arisen, and when you are ready, the ritual is complete. Give thanks to the one whose voice is silence, and to the spirits of the directions, and return to your normal awareness.
