 It is a black pillar, extending from the floor to the ceiling. It has no discernible features, except for a single eye, glowing with a blue light. This eye is set in the middle of the pillar, about level with your own eyes.

The pillar speaks, in a voice that seems to come from everywhere at once.

"Welcome, traveler. I am the Keeper of the Way. I guard the path to the hidden knowledge you seek. But to pass, you must prove your worth. Answer my riddle, and you may proceed. Fail, and you will be cast out, never to return. Are you ready?"

Are you ready to answer the riddle?

*Yes, I am ready.*

Very well. Here is the riddle:

"I am the beginning of eternity, the end of time and space. I am the beginning of every end, and the end of every place. What am I?"

Think carefully before you answer.

*The letter 'E'*

The pillar's eye glows brightly for a moment, and then the voice speaks again.

"You have answered correctly. You may pass."
