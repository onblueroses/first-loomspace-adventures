 What enters the room now is the spirit of the place where the treasure lies hidden. Greet it with silence and respect. If it is willing to reveal the location, it will do so now. If it remains silent, the treasure is not meant to be found.

If the spirit reveals the location, thank it with these words:

I thank you, spirit of this place, for revealing what was hidden. May you be at peace, and may your domain remain undisturbed. I will take only what is freely given, and leave no trace of my passing. Go now in peace, and let there be harmony between us.

The spirit will depart, leaving you with the knowledge of the treasure's location. Use this knowledge wisely and with respect for the spirits of the land.

If the spirit remains silent, thank it nonetheless:

I thank you, spirit of this place, for your presence and your silence. I respect your decision to keep the treasure hidden, and I will not seek it further. May there be harmony between us, and may your domain remain undisturbed. Go now in peace.
