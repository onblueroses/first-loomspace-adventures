 It has a head like a giant fly, its body is bloated, its skin is greenish gray, and it has a long tail ending in a stinger. It is a great demon, and it says:

"I am the demon Skorpis. I have come to this world to torment humanity. I will make the skies rain blood, I will make the earth open up and swallow cities, I will make the seas rise up and drown the land. I will bring pestilence and famine and war. I will make humanity suffer as it has never suffered before."

What do you do?

I ask the demon Skorpis, "Why do you wish to torment humanity? What have we done to deserve such punishment?"

The demon Skorpis replies, "Humanity has grown arrogant and corrupt. It has forgotten the old ways, the ways of magic and mystery. It has turned its back on the gods and the spirits. It has embraced science and reason, and it has used these to dominate and destroy the natural world. Humanity has lost its way, and it must be punished."
