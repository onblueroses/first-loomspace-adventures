?

The Lord of the Wastes, The Unmaker, The One Who Is Not.

What is it like?

A blackness that devours all light. A coldness that devours all warmth. A void that devours all hope. A silence that devours all sound. A hunger that devours all being.

How does it make you feel?

I feel as if I am being unmade, as if the very substance of my soul is being unwoven, as if I am being devoured by the cold and the dark.

What does it want?

It wants to unmake all that is, to devour all creation, to reduce all that exists to nothingness.

How do you banish it?

By speaking the names that close the way:

Snolfren, Mreph, Kroslendep, Tholerpa, Vronth, Snolpharod, Kresnopontren, Posnophev, Vrindat, Kromphis, Onth, Phelorpa, Tholgardosen, Rephonex, Ontrolanker, Phenrolop, Engrokitha, Onthelosk, Tromporada, Smerphol, Antrundefax, Krelmonor, Polisornen
