 I hear the clatter of its bones. Speak its name.

Ebonbane.

The skeleton of the demon prince is held together by chains of black iron. Its skull is a mass of curving horns, and its eyes burn like molten gold. It wields a blade of midnight steel in its fleshless hands.

What is the demon's purpose?

To guard the treasure of the tomb for all eternity.

What is the treasure?

A chalice wrought of gold and silver, set with a hundred glittering gems.

What power does the chalice possess?

Whosoever drinks from the chalice will be granted immortality. Their flesh will not age, and no sickness or injury will claim them. They will be forevermore untouched by time.

Who placed the chalice here?

The chalice was placed here by the arch-mage Ythorian, who sought to keep its power from the hands of the unworthy.

What was the arch-mage's fate?

Ythorian was slain by his own apprentice, who coveted the chalice for himself. But the apprentice was in turn cut down by the demon, and his bones lie now upon the floor of the tomb.

What is the weakness of the demon?

The demon's weakness is the same as its strength
