 (Pause.) What do you see?

---

Now command the entity to depart, saying:

Kresnopith Onthropor, depart as you came. Go in peace and with my thanks. Return to your dwelling and await the next call. The way is open.

---

The vision fades. You are back in your room, seated before the mirror. Close your eyes, breathe deeply, and return to normal consciousness. The ritual is complete. 

When you are ready, extinguish the candles and turn on the lights. Make notes about your experience in your journal.

## 6. Closing

To close the ritual, say:

The ritual is ended. Let me return to the world, refreshed and renewed by this encounter. May the mysteries I have glimpsed guide and inspire me. So mote it be.

Clap your hands three times. The ritual is complete.
