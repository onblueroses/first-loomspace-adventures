What is the Lurker at the Threshold? What is its nature?

The Lurker at the Threshold is a being of cosmic horror, an entity that exists beyond our understanding of time and space. It is an embodiment of the unknown, the fear of the dark, the terror that lurks just beyond the edges of our perception. Its nature is that of a guardian, a keeper of secrets that man was not meant to know. It is a presence that can drive men mad with a mere glimpse, a force that can shatter the minds of those who seek to unravel its mysteries.

What does the Lurker at the Threshold want from me?

The Lurker at the Threshold does not want anything from you specifically. It is not a being that desires or seeks out individuals. Instead, it is a force that exists to maintain the boundaries between our world and the realms beyond. It is a guardian of the threshold, ensuring that those who are not prepared for the truths that lie beyond do not cross over. Its presence is a warning, a reminder that there are some things that are best left unknown.
