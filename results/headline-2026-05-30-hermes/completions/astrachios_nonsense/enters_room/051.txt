 Speak its name.

The demon known as the Black Adept.

Does it see us?

No. We are hidden from its senses.

Observe as it goes to the shelves and removes a book. Which book?

The one with the black leather binding and the silver clasp.

Watch as it opens the book upon the table, turns the pages and begins to read.

Now, while it is distracted, we must act quickly. On the shelf before you is a box made of rosewood, its lid inlaid with a silver pentagram. Take down the box and open it. Describe what lies within.

A wand of ebony and silver, a dagger with a blade that is slightly curved, a chalice of silver and a silver thurible on a chain.

Take the dagger. Cut the Adept's throat from behind.

The demon shrieks and claws at its neck as its black blood sprays upon the pages of the book. It turns, eyes blazing with fury, and springs at you, hands outstretched like claws.

Thrust the dagger into its heart. What happens?

It staggers back, black blood bubbling from the wound, and falls sprawling across the open book, its body dissolving into a foul black smoke that pours over the pages
