 The room is suddenly filled with many people.

A: What kind of people are they?

T: They are tall, gaunt, pale. They are dressed in robes. They have a very solemn look about them. They are quite imposing.

A: Do they speak?

T: They do not speak. They are completely silent.

A: Do they move?

T: They move, but slowly, deliberately. They glide more than walk.

A: What do they do?

T: They are forming a circle around me. They are all facing me. They are just staring at me.

A: How do you feel?

T: I feel unnerved. Their silence and their staring is quite disconcerting. But I also feel a sense of anticipation, as if something is about to happen.

A: Do you recognize any of them?

T: I do not recognize any of them. They are all strangers to me.

A: What happens next?
