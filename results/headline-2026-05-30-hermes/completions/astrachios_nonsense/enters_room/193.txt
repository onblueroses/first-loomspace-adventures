The room fills with a heavy darkness, a presence that presses against the skin and threatens to smother. Out of the darkness coalesces a figure, a silhouette of a person made of shadows and smoke. It is the Dark Shape, the formless spirit of fear and dread.

The Dark Shape speaks in a voice that echoes through the mind, bypassing the ears entirely. "I am the fear that lurks in the hearts of all mortals. I am the dread that keeps you awake at night, the terror that haunts your dreams. I am the shadow that follows you in the brightest day and the darkness that consumes you in the deepest night. I am the Dark Shape, and I am eternal."

The Dark Shape shifts and moves, never quite holding a single form for more than a moment. It is a thing of nightmares and dark imaginings, a spirit that feeds on the fear and despair of mortals. It is a powerful entity, but not invincible. It can be banished by light, both literal and metaphorical - the light of knowledge, of hope, of courage. These are the weapons that can dispel the darkness and send the Dark Shape back to the void from whence it came.
