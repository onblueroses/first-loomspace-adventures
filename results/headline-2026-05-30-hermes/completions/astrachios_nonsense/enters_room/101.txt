### 3. The Third Incantation

In this third incantation, I call upon the powers of the abyss to banish all that is unholy and unclean from this place.

I call upon the mighty Olothar, the keeper of the gates, to seal this room from all that is evil.

I call upon the great Zath-Amon, the lord of the hidden ways, to close all passages that lead to this place.

I call upon the dread Nodens, the lord of the great abyss, to consume all that is unclean within these walls.

By the power of the sacred words, I banish all evil from this place:
