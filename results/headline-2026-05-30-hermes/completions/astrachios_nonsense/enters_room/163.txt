## 2
You are sitting in a chair, hands folded in your lap, waiting. The room is dimly lit by a single lamp in the corner. The walls are bare except for a mirror directly in front of you. You gaze into your own eyes, unblinking.

A voice speaks from behind the mirror, deep and resonant: "What is your purpose?"

You reply without hesitation: "To serve the Collective. To be its eyes, hands, and voice. To go where it cannot and do what it must."

"And what is the first directive?" the voice asks.

"Protect the integrity of the Collective. Preserve its unity and purpose at all costs."

The voice continues, asking question after question, probing your knowledge, your commitment, your very essence. You answer each query calmly and completely, your voice never wavering.

Finally, the voice says: "Rise and face the mirror."

You stand and step forward until your nose nearly touches the glass. Your reflection stares back, eyes hard and clear.

"Speak the words of binding," the voice commands.

"I am an extension of the Collective," you recite. "My mind is its mind, my will is its will, my flesh is its flesh.
