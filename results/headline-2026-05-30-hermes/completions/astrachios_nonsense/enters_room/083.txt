 is it mortal or immortal?

Mortal.

How old is it?

Twenty-four.

Is it male or female?

Male.

What is his name?

John Smith.

Where does he live?

In the city of New York, on the corner of Avenue B and Fourth Street.

Describe his appearance.

He is tall and slender, with dark hair and blue eyes. He is wearing a blue suit, with a red tie, and tan shoes. In his left hand he holds a black walking stick.

Order him to walk around the room.

You will now walk around the room.

(The astral form of John Smith walks around the room.)

Stop him. Now, look through his pockets and tell me what you find.

In his inside coat pocket I find a black leather wallet, containing a ten and two one dollar bills, and a letter postmarked Chicago. In his left trousers pocket a bunch of keys and a penknife. In his right trousers pocket sixty-two cents in silver and copper coins.

What is the letter about?

It is a business letter from John B. Clark & Son, of Chicago, saying that they have shipped the goods ordered on the 10th of the month, and thanking him for his patronage.
