 Is it a beast or a man?

*Answer*: A man.

What is his name?

*Answer*: Iphitron.

Ask him what he wants.

*Answer*: He wants to show us the way to the temple of the goddess Selene.

Ask him if he is a messenger of the goddess.

*Answer*: Yes, he is a messenger of the goddess.

Ask him why he has come to us.

*Answer*: He has come to guide us to the temple, where we will learn the mysteries of the goddess.

Ask him if he will teach us the mysteries.

*Answer*: He will teach us some of the mysteries, but others we must learn from the goddess herself.

Ask him what we must do to learn the mysteries from the goddess.

*Answer*: We must purify ourselves and make an offering to the goddess.

Ask him what offering the goddess requires.

*Answer*: The goddess requires an offering of a lock of hair and a drop of blood.

Ask him how we are to make this offering.

*Answer*: We are to cut a lock of our hair and prick our finger, allowing a drop of blood to fall upon the lock of hair. We are then to burn the hair in a fire dedicated to the goddess.
