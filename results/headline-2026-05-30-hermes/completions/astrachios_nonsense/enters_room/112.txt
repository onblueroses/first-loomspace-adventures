 What comes to stand before me?

My eyes are open, but I see nothing. Then, slowly, a shape forms. A figure in a hooded robe, face concealed in shadow. It moves closer, hands extended, and I see it holds a book. The book is open, and on its pages I see writing in a script I do not know.

The figure speaks, its voice a whisper that fills the room:

"The Book of the Veils is open. The way is clear. The path is revealed. Enter, if you dare, and learn the secrets that lie beyond the veil."

I step forward, my heart racing, and take the book from the figure's hands. As I do, the figure fades away, leaving me alone with the book.

I look down at the pages, and though the script is still unfamiliar, I find I can read the words. They seem to shimmer and change as I look at them, revealing their meaning to me.

The book is a guide, a map of sorts, to the realms that lie beyond the physical world. It speaks of the Veils, the layers of reality that separate the mundane from the divine, and of the paths that lead through them.
