The purpose of a banishing ritual in ceremonial magic is to cleanse and purify the ritual space, removing any unwanted energies, influences, or entities that may interfere with the magician's work. Banishing rituals are often performed at the beginning and end of magical workings to establish a clear and focused environment for the ritual.
