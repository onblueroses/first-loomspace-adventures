 The great door opens.

A gigantic figure enters the room, surrounded by a blinding light. It is the great god Thoth, the god of wisdom and magic. He speaks in a voice that shakes the very foundations of the temple.

Thoth: Mortal, you have spoken the names that open the way. You have proven yourself worthy to stand before me. What is it that you seek?

I seek the knowledge and power to protect my people and bring prosperity to our lands. I humbly ask for your guidance and blessings, great Thoth.

Thoth: Your intentions are pure, and your heart is true. I shall grant you the knowledge and power you seek, but know that with great power comes great responsibility. Use this gift wisely and for the betterment of your people.

Thoth extends his hand, and a brilliant light emanates from his palm. The light engulfs you, filling you with an incredible sense of wisdom and understanding. You feel the power of Thoth's blessings coursing through your veins.

I am forever grateful, great Thoth. I shall use this power to bring peace and prosperity to my people, and to honor your name.

Thoth: Go forth, mortal, and let your deeds reflect the wisdom and power
