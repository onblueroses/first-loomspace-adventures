The image of a naked human being, male or female according to the sex of the operator, but idealized, like a perfect marble statue. It stands upon the central altar.

Speak to it:

O beautiful creature of the divine fire, creature of the Art, thou art my own perfect self, and I desire to unite with thee, becoming one in power and will.

The figure smiles and steps down from the altar, walking forward to embrace you. As it touches you, it sinks into your body and vanishes.

Close the ritual with the License to Depart, performing the Banishing Rituals of the Pentagram and Hexagram.

The purpose of this ritual is to create a magical link between the operator and the Angel of the operation. This being is in a sense the operator's own Higher Self or Holy Guardian Angel, but it is also a specific aspect of that Self, a being of the magical element of the operation, shaped by the powers that have been invoked through the ritual. The union with this Angel attains the Knowledge and Conversation of the Higher Self in a specific mode, and empowers the magician to work with the full effect of the elemental and planetary forces chosen for the operation.
