Answer: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_the_Presence_of_a_Cloned_King) says:

\begin{blockquote}

As they pass through the final door, they are greeted by a group of a thousand people who look exactly like themselves. Each member of the group is a clone of the corresponding person in the original group of adventurers. The clones are servants of the King That Was, who created them to provide him with an unending supply of body parts for transplant. As the adventurers confront their doubles, the King That Was is revealed to be a grossly mishapen creature, the result of centuries of transplants. The King That Was plans to use the bodies of the original adventurers as new sources of body parts, and sics the clones on the group.
\end{blockquote}
