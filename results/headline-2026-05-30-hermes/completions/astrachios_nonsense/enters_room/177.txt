The room will fill with a thick smoke and a voice will speak. "You have called me to you and I have come, but the price for my coming is the life of one of your party. Choose who it will be, or I will choose for you." The voice will laugh menacingly. 

The party must decide who will be sacrificed. If they do not choose, the demon will choose the one who was pulled into the room. The chosen one will be dragged into the smoke and will disappear. The smoke will clear and the demon will be standing in the room. 

The demon will be a large, muscular humanoid with red skin, horns, and wings. It will have a cruel and twisted expression on its face.
