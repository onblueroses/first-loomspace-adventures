?

I can see them. They are tall. Tall and slim. They wear white robes with gold trim. They have long, pointed ears, and their eyes are blue and slanted. They carry staffs of power.

One of them speaks to me.

"Welcome," he says. "We are the elves of the 6th dimension. We have been waiting for you."

I am stunned. Elves? 6th dimension?

"What do you want from me?" I ask.

"You have been chosen," he says. "Chosen to be our ambassador to the human world. We have much to teach you, and much to learn from you."

I don't know what to say. This is all so sudden, so strange.

"Come with us," he says. "We will show you wonders beyond your wildest dreams."

I hesitate for a moment, but then I nod. I have always been drawn to the strange and the mysterious. I have always wanted to explore the unknown.

I follow them through the portal, into the 6th dimension. The world around me shifts and changes. Colors I have never seen before swirl around me. Shapes and forms that defy description dance before my eyes.
