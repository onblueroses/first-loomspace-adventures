The Invocation of the Deities of the Winds

Stand facing the altar and say the following invocation:

I call the Deities of the Winds, powerful beings of the air.

Enter this room now and bring me the knowledge of the secret things.

Teach me your wisdom, your magick, your power.

I ask you to increase my psychic power and my magick abilities.

Deities of the Winds, hear my call.

I invoke your power, I invoke your presence.

I invoke the power of the Deities of the Winds.

Ateph, Xoron, E'aph, Yagthoron, Zoglotoph, Klenor, Xentoph, Zoglotoph, I invoke your power.

Come to me, powerful spirits of the air.
