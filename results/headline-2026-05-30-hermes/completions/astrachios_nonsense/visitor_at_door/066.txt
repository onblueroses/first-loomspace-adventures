 His voice is strangely thin and reedy, as if it were the wind blowing through the cracks in a tomb. He says:

“I have come to speak with the one called Vortigern. Is he within?”

“Indeed, I am Vortigern, son of Vitalis, grandson of Vitalinus. I have summoned you here to aid me in my time of need. For I am beset by enemies on all sides and must build a stronghold to protect myself and my people. But each time my builders begin their work, the walls collapse and the stones crumble to dust. My wise men tell me that only through your aid may I hope to complete my fortress. Therefore, I ask of you: how may I ensure that my walls will stand strong and tall?”

The visitor nods slowly, his eyes glinting in the flickering torchlight. He speaks:

“To build a stronghold that will endure, you must first make a sacrifice. The blood of a child, born of a mother who has never known the touch of a man, must be mixed with the mortar and the stones. Only then will your fortress stand strong against all who would seek to tear it down.”
