 It may be the Master, or it may be another member of the lodge who has been appointed in advance. The following words are addressed to the lodge members:

Be it known to you, O Thrice Noble and Illustrious Brethren, that there is a report concerning the death of our Master, who was slain at high noon on the mountaintop.

The lodge members respond in unison:

Alas, our Master is slain!

The visitor continues:

Brethren, the first report that came to us after the death of our Master was that the Word was forever lost, but we have since learned that there are certain substitute Words that will be given in the proper time and place.

The lodge members respond in unison:

We thank you, Illustrious Brother, for this later information.

The visitor closes the door of the lodge and retires. The Master strikes the gavel three times slowly to indicate the end of the ceremony of the Veils.
