The visitor enters the room.

The visitor is a form like a man, but it is not a man.

The visitor has no face, but it has eyes.

The visitor's eyes are black, like pools of oil.

The visitor's body is clothed in tattered robes of black.

The visitor stands before the priest.

The priest kneels before the visitor.

The visitor speaks:

"I am the messenger of the Lords of the Abyss. I come bearing a message for the faithful of the Cult of the Yellow Sign."

The priest listens.

The visitor continues:

"The time of the opening of the way is near. The stars are almost right. The King in Yellow will soon rise from his slumber in the dark places beneath the earth. The world will be consumed in chaos and madness. The faithful will be rewarded with power and immortality."

The priest nods.
