 He is not seen, but his voice is heard.

Visitor: I have spoken the names that open the way. I ask entry to the presence of the Lords of the Black Throne.

A deep gong sounds. The door opens.

The visitor enters and approaches the throne. He makes obeisance and speaks.

Visitor: O mighty Lords of the Black Throne, I come seeking knowledge forbidden to man. I would know the secret of that which lies beyond life, which lies beyond the grave. I would know of death.

The Lords of the Black Throne speak in a voice like the rumbling of the earth.

Lords: Beyond life lies the realm of death. In death the mortal puts off the flesh and is reborn to eternal life. The realm of death is the womb of the earth, and in that darkness is hidden the seed of the soul.

The visitor speaks.

Visitor: How may I gain the power of eternal life? How shall I cross the barrier of the grave and be reborn in the realm of death?

The Lords of the Black Throne speak.
