 "I have come to give you the key that unlocks the gate of dreams. I am the guide who will lead you to the place where you shall meet the spirits of the dead. Take my hand and trust in me. I will not lead you astray."

The guide leads the person within the circle to the edge of the ritual space and points at the floor just beyond its boundary.

"Look. What do you see?"

The person sees a black door in the floor outlined in flickering blue flames. The guide takes the person by the hand and leads them through the door. They descend a spiral staircase of black stone that is lit by torches set in the wall to either side. The air grows colder as they descend, and the person begins to shiver. The staircase ends in a small chamber with a door in its opposite wall. The guide opens the door and leads the person through it.

Beyond is a vast cavern illuminated by a silvery radiance that has no source. In the center of the cavern a black river flows from left to right.
