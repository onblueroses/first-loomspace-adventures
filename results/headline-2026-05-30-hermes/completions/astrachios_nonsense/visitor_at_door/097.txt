The shrine is lit by a single flame that burns in the center of the room. The flame arises from a golden chalice that rests upon a stone pillar. Around the pillar, in a circle, are twelve stone thrones. Upon each throne sits one of the twelve supreme spirits of the Enochian universe, the Zodiacal Kings and Princes who rule over the houses of heaven. Each holds in his hands his seal of power. The names of these twelve godlike beings are:

Alpudus, prince ruling Capricorn the Sea Goat
Zarzilg, king ruling Aquarius the Water Bearer
Lavavoth, prince ruling Aquarius the Water Bearer
Arfaolg, king ruling Pisces the Fishes
Anfodiel, prince ruling Pisces the Fishes
Dodagas, king ruling Aries the Ram
Vodanal, prince ruling Aries the Ram
Cadasamp, king ruling Taurus the Bull
Astorid, prince ruling Taurus the Bull
