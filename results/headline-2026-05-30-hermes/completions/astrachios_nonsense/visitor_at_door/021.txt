**Of the Seventh Key**

The Master of the Temple speaks:

Oh mighty spirits of the seventh key, I call upon you and ask that you open unto me the mysteries of your key. I have passed through the gate of the sixth key, and now I stand before the final veil.

Spirits of the seventh key, I call upon you. Open the way that I may rise to the highest of heights and behold the glory of the divine light.

In the name of the seven mighty princes of the divine presence, I call upon you. In the name of the angels of the seventh ray, I call upon you. In the name of the archangels of the seventh heaven, I call upon you.

Spirits of the seventh key, I call upon you. Open the way that I may ascend to the throne of the divine and be initiated into the mysteries of the seventh key.

The spirits of the seventh key are called:

Borachio, Agape, Gargatel, Soterion, Itokortuth, Balahotere, Arbizoth, Kepharel, Pharganath, Pharel, Thagirion,
