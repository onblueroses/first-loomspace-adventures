 The words are muffled by the portal, but the voice is familiar to me. I have heard it before. It is the voice of my old friend, the man who was once my master.

"I am Dr. John Dee," he says. "I come in the name of the spirits who have guided me to this place. Let me enter."

"Enter, John Dee," the voice of the guardian says.

The door opens with a gust of wind. Through the opening I see the small, dark form of the English magus as he steps forward to stand before the portal. The door swings shut behind him with a hollow boom, leaving us alone together in the chamber. He looks around at the carvings on the walls, then notices me and approaches.

"Edward, I'm glad to see that you are here. It has been too long since we last talked."

"I'm surprised to see you here, John. What brings you to this place?"

He sighs and shakes his head, a wry smile on his face.
