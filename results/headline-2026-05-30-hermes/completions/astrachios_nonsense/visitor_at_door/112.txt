 (Introduce yourself to the gatekeeper.)

Gatekeeper: By what right do you seek entry?

The visitor at the door speaks. (Explain why you have come to the gate.)

Gatekeeper: I will open the way for you, but you must first answer three questions. What is your purpose in seeking the wisdom of the spirits?

The visitor at the door speaks. (State your purpose.)

Gatekeeper: What gift do you bring to the spirits to show your respect and sincerity?

The visitor at the door speaks. (Describe your gift.)

Gatekeeper: How will you use the knowledge gained from the spirits to aid others and yourself?

The visitor at the door speaks. (Explain how you will use the knowledge.)

Gatekeeper: You have answered well. I will open the gate for you. Enter and receive the wisdom you seek. (The gatekeeper opens the gate.) Pass through the gate and stand in the center of the circle of spirits.

(When the visitor has entered the circle, the guide speaks the following words.)

Guide: Spirits of the circle, I present (visitor's name), who has been found worthy to enter your midst. Share with (him/her) your wisdom and your secrets, as you will.

(There is a brief pause
