The visitor is free to enter. They have only to step through the gate to find themselves at the entrance to the hidden valley.

If the visitor wishes to leave the valley, they need only return to the gate and step through it again. They will find themselves back at the entrance they first arrived at.

If the visitor does not know the names, they may attempt to force the gate open. Doing so requires a DC 24 Strength check. On a success, the gate opens, and the visitor may enter. On a failure, the gate remains shut, and the visitor takes 2d10 psychic damage as the magic of the gate punishes them for their transgression.
