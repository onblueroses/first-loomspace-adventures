 "I have traveled the way of the dark to the realm of night. I have found the key to the gate of the abyss. I have beheld the face of the mighty one, who has given me the names by which I am to be known to the dwellers in the darkness. I am Thotep, the seeker of wisdom, who has passed through the veil of the night to the land of shadows. I am Kresnopith, the keeper of the secret key, who has opened the way to the hidden places of the earth. I am Onthropor, the master of mysteries, who has looked upon the face of the mighty one and lived. Let the way be opened for me, that I may enter the hall of the wise and sit at the table of the masters. Let the gates of the abyss swing wide, and the dwellers in the darkness greet me as one of their own."

The guardian of the gate responds. "Enter, Thotep, Kresnopith, Onthropor, and be welcome in the hall of the wise. The masters await you within. The secrets of the night shall be yours to learn, and the mysteries of the earth shall be opened to your understanding. Pass through the gate of the abyss
