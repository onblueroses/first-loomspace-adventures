# 33. A Ritual of Protection Against Disease

This ritual will protect you from disease and infection. It must be performed at the new moon.

Light a candle and place it on the floor in the middle of the room. Stand to the west of the candle, facing east.

Hold your hands above the candle and say:

Morpholpax, Phentrophen, Kromphol, Vronk,

Protect my body from all sickness and disease.

Blow out the candle. The ritual is complete.

# 34. A Ritual of Protection Against Poverty

This ritual will protect you from poverty. It must be performed during the waxing moon.

Place a coin in a cup of water. Let the cup sit outside under the moonlight for one night.

The next morning, remove the coin and pour the water onto the earth. Keep the coin with you. The ritual is complete.

# 35. A Ritual to Protect a Home from Evil
