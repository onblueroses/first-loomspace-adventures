### 28. Prayer Hall
In this room, the walls are lined with wooden benches, and a stone altar stands at the far end. The altar is carved with images of the gods and goddesses of the temple, and a small fire burns in a brazier on top of it.

If a character makes an offering of incense or a valuable item at the altar and prays for guidance, they receive a vision from the gods. The vision is a cryptic message that hints at a danger or opportunity that lies ahead, but it requires interpretation to understand.
