 “Open the way, for I am the bearer of the secret. I have come to speak to the Father.”

The gatekeeper replies, “You have spoken the names that open the way, but you must answer my questions three before you may pass. What is the secret that you bear?”

The visitor answers, “The secret is the knowledge of the Father’s true name.”

The gatekeeper nods. “You have answered the first question. What is the Father’s true name?”

The visitor speaks the name: “Yog-Sothoth.”

The gatekeeper bows his head. “You have answered the second question. What is your purpose in seeking the Father?”

The visitor replies, “I seek the Father’s wisdom and guidance, for I am a seeker of the truth.”

The gatekeeper steps aside and opens the gate. “You have answered the third question. Enter, seeker of truth, and may the Father grant you the wisdom you seek.”

The visitor passes through the gate and enters the presence of the Father. The Father, a being of immense power and knowledge, looks upon the visitor with eyes that hold the secrets of the universe.
