### The Fourteenth Gate

* The Portal of Yr-Nhhngr
* The Gate of the Shining Trapezohedron

This door is a dark, glossy black, with an iridescent sheen. It is twelve feet tall, and six feet wide. The frame is of black marble, with intricate carvings. The door is opened by placing a hand on the door and speaking the words "Yr-nhhngr, ehye-haathgeg." The door will then swing open, revealing a swirling, black mist. The mist is cold, and seems to pull at those who stand near it. The door may be closed by speaking the words "Yr-nhhngr, ehye-haathgeg" again.
