 The door opens.

The visitor enters the chamber. The door closes behind him.

The chamber is lit by a thousand candles. The walls are of polished black stone, inscribed with glowing symbols. The floor is of polished black stone, inlaid with intricate patterns in gold and silver. The ceiling is of polished black stone, hung with glittering crystals.

In the center of the chamber is a raised platform of black stone. On the platform is a throne of black stone, carved with intricate designs. On the throne sits a figure of black stone, robed in black, its face hidden by a hood of black.

The visitor approaches the throne. He speaks.

Visitor: I have come, as you commanded, O Master of the Black Chamber.

The figure on the throne speaks. Its voice is like the whisper of a thousand serpents.

Master: You have done well, my servant. The time of the Great Work is at hand. The stars are aligned. The omens are favorable. The sacrifices have been made. The powers have been invoked. The way is open.

The visitor bows his head.

Visitor: I am ready, Master. Command me, and I shall obey.
