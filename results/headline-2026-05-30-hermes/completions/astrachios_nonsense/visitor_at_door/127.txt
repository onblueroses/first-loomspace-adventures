 A voice answers from within the house. The door opens. The visitor enters. The door closes.

The visitor climbs the stair. At the top of the stair the visitor faces a door. The visitor knocks thrice upon the door, then speaks a word. The door opens. The visitor enters. The door closes.

The visitor approaches the figure who sits in the chair before the fire.

The visitor bows thrice and speaks a word.

The figure in the chair nods thrice and speaks a word.

The visitor removes a scroll from the sleeve of the visitor's robe and passes it to the figure in the chair. The visitor turns and leaves the room. The door closes.
