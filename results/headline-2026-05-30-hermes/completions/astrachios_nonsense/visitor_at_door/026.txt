 "I am the servant of the mighty and powerful god who created me. I have come here only to do his will. I seek the wisdom of the gods who dwell in this place. I pray that you will look with favor upon my quest, and will grant me entrance; for I have come from a far land, and have endured many trials and hardships to reach this place."

The guardian of the gate speaks: "Why should we grant your request, and allow you to enter the city? Many come seeking the wisdom of the gods, but few are worthy to receive it. What token of your worthiness do you bring? What gift do you lay before the gods to earn their favor?"

"I bring no gift but my own heart. All that I have I give to the gods. My life is theirs. I have no other purpose but to do their will. I ask to enter the city only that I may learn how better to serve them."

"Your words are pretty, but they are only words. You ask to be taught, yet you presume to instruct those from whom you seek instruction. Is this respect? Is this how you show your devotion?"
