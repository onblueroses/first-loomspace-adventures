 The door opens. All enter the palace of the King, where many servants attend them.

Glothenkion, the King, speaks:

GLOTHENKION

Welcome, my friends. Be seated at the table.

All are seated at the table. Servants bring food and drink.

GLOTHENKION

Let us eat, drink, and be merry.

All eat, drink, and make merry. After the feast is ended the King rises.

GLOTHENKION

Let the dance begin.

All dance to music. After the dance the King speaks:

GLOTHENKION

Let the play begin.

All sit to witness a play that is performed by players.

After the play is ended the King speaks:

GLOTHENKION

Let us have more dancing.

All dance again to music. After the dance the King speaks:

GLOTHENKION

The hour is late. Let us retire to our chambers.

All say good-night to one another and retire to their chambers. They sleep and dream.
