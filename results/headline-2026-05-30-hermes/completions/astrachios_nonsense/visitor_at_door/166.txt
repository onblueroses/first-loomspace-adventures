 “I have come to meet with the spirits of the dead.”

The doorkeeper replies. “To meet with the spirits of the dead, you must cross the bridge of darkness. Are you prepared to make this crossing?”

“I am prepared to cross the bridge of darkness.”

“Then take up the black candle and light it from the flame of the lamp that hangs beside the door. When the candle is burning, step forward to the bridge and begin to cross. Do not hesitate or look back. The spirits await you on the other side.”

The visitor lights the black candle and steps forward onto the bridge. At once, the doorkeeper draws closed the portal, and the visitor is left alone in darkness, apart from the feeble glow of the candle flame. The bridge appears to be an old wooden structure that is narrow and without rails. It spans a black abyss of uncertain depth. The visitor begins to cross, keeping eyes focused on the flame of the candle and the unseen far side of the abyss.
