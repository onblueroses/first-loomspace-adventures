 "I am Chalmolon. I seek entrance to the Hall of the Seven Thrones, that I may make my report."

Chalmolon is admitted. He is of the Ruling Council of the Seven Thrones, and responsible for the affairs of the Third Throne, which rules the concerns of the Third Ring, where the Spheres of Air and Water meet. His robes are a shimmering blue, and his staff is of silver set with aquamarines. He speaks of the matters of his Ring.

"The Spheres of Air and Water are in harmony, and the matters of their interface proceed smoothly. The storms of the season have been mild, and the winds have brought the rains to the lands as is proper, so that the crops grow well and the harvest will be bountiful. The creatures of the Air and the Water are healthy and multiply, and the hunters of the Third Ring find their labors rewarded. The fishermen bring in full nets, and the birders snare many birds, and so the people of the Third Ring are well-fed and content.
