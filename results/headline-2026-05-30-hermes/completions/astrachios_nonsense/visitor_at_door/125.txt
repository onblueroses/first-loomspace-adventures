 The door keeper answers:

The doorkeeper says, "You have spoken the names that open the way. Enter and be welcome."

The visitor enters the chamber.

In the chamber is a figure seated on a throne, wearing a mask of gold, robes of black and green, and many rings and amulets. The figure speaks:

The figure says, "Greetings, traveler. I am the Oracle of the Mask. I hold the secrets of the ancients. Ask, and I shall answer, if the answer is known to me."

The visitor asks, "What is the nature of the force that binds the universe together?"

The Oracle of the Mask says, "The force that binds the universe together is the Great Serpent, Ouroboros. It encircles the cosmos, its tail in its mouth, an eternal cycle of creation and destruction. All things arise from it and return to it in the end."

The visitor asks, "How can one tap into this power?"

The Oracle of the Mask says, "To tap into the power of Ouroboros, one must first understand the cycles of the universe. Birth and death, growth and decay, all are part of the eternal dance. Embrace change, for it is the only constant. Meditate
