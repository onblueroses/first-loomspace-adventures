 They must say "I am the one who seeks the path of the White Gate" and give a reason for their seeking.

If the reason is acceptable, the guardian responds, saying "The path is open to you, but the way is perilous and the price is high. If you would enter, do so with courage and wisdom."

The visitor then speaks the names that open the gate:

Phenrolop, Ontrolanker, Rephonex, Tholgardosen, Phelorpa, Onth, Kromphis, Vrindat, Posnophev, Kresnopontren, Snolpharod, Vronth, Tholerpa, Kroslendep, Mreph, Snolfren, Kresnopith Onthropor.

The gate then opens, revealing the path beyond. The visitor may enter, and the guardian will close the gate behind them.

If the reason given is not acceptable, the guardian will respond, saying "The path is closed to you. Seek elsewhere for what you desire." The visitor is then sent away, and may not approach the gate again for a year and a day.
