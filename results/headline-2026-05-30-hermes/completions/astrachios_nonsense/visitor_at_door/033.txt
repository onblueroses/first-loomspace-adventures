### The 15th Aethyr - OXO

The Black Pillar is the left-hand Pillar of the Tree of Life, the Pillar of Severity, the Feminine Pillar. The White Pillar is the right-hand Pillar, the Pillar of Mercy, the Masculine Pillar. The Candidate is placed between them at the outset of the Ceremony of the Neophyte Grade in the Golden Dawn, in the Portal of the Temple. This is the Portal of Initiation.

The Black Pillar is Binah, the Great Sea, the Great Mother. The White Pillar is Chokmah, the Father, the All-begetter. The Candidate stands upon the Path of Daleth, the letter of Venus, attributed to the Empress in the Tarot.

The Candidate stands upon the Earth, Malkuth, the Bride, the Daughter, the Virgin, the Completion of the Tree of Life. The Candidate is the Negative, and the Temple is the Positive. The Candidate is the Unmanifest, and the Temple is the Manifest.

The Candidate is the potential, and the Temple is the actual. The Candidate is the uncreated, and the Temple is the created.
