 “I am a messenger of the God of the Gateway. I bear a message for the dreamer within. Admit me, for I am expected.”

The visitor enters and kneils before the dreamer. He speaks.

“Hail, dreamer. I am the servant of the God of the Gateway, and I bring you a message from the realm of sleep. The gods have seen your suffering, and they have sent me to bear you this message: ‘You must seek the path that leads to the gate of the gods. Only there will you find the answer to your suffering. The path is long and difficult, but if you have the courage to follow it, you will find what you seek.’”

The dreamer responds. “I am grateful for this message, and I will seek the path that leads to the gate of the gods. But how will I know the way?”

The messenger speaks. “The way will be shown to you in your dreams. Each night, as you sleep, you will be guided by the gods. They will send you signs and omens, and they will lead you to the gate. But you must be brave, for the way is not easy, and there are many dangers that lie in wait for those who seek the path
