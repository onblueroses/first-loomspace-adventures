 "I am a humble man. I have no riches, no power, no armies, no magical might. I come to you in the name of the most basic of human rights, the right to ask for help. I have been told you have it in your power to grant me that which I need to save the life of a good man."

"Speak, then, of what you need," says the voice from the door.

"I need the Sword of Sacrifice, which can only be wielded by one who is willing to give his life for another. I need it to save the life of a man who is willing to give his life for me. I need it to cut the chains that bind him, so that he may be free to live his life in peace."

"And what will you give in return for this sword?" asks the voice.

"I will give my life, if need be," says the visitor. "I will give my life, so that he may live."

The door opens. Inside, there is only darkness. The visitor steps in, and the door closes behind him.
