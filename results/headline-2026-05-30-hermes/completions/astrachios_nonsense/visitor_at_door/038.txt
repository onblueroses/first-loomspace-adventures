## The Room

The room is a cube, 15 feet on a side. The walls, floor, and ceiling are of the same black stone as the rest of the temple. The only light comes from a dimly glowing orb in the center of the ceiling. The orb illuminates a circle on the floor, about 10 feet in diameter. The light is just bright enough to read by, if one is standing within the circle.

The room is unnaturally cold. Any exposed skin begins to feel numb after a few minutes. The air is still and silent. No sound penetrates the room from outside.

In the center of the room is a stone pedestal, about 3 feet high. On the pedestal rests a large black book. The book is open, and its pages are filled with strange symbols and diagrams.

The symbols are in an unknown language, but they seem to shift and change as one looks at them. The diagrams are of complex geometric shapes and patterns. They seem to move and change as well, as if they are alive.
