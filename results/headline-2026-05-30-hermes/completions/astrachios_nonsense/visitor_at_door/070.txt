## 1. The First Vision of the Elemental Universe

In the vision, I found myself in a vast, seemingly infinite space. The ground was covered in a grid of black and white tiles, stretching out endlessly in all directions. The sky above was a deep, dark blue, filled with countless stars.

I stood at the center of a circle inscribed on the tiled floor, its circumference marked by a series of sigils and symbols in glowing blue light. Beyond the circle, at the cardinal points, stood four immense pillars, each a different color: red, blue, yellow, and green.

As I gazed around, I saw that the space was not empty. Elementals of all kinds darted and flowed between the pillars, their forms shifting and changing: salamanders of fire, sylphs of air, undines of water, and gnomes of earth.
