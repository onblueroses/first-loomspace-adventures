 "I am Vrotheus the Silent. I come to open the way."

The portal opens to a passageway leading to a hall. The walls are adorned with murals depicting the history of the Anunnaki, from their arrival on Earth to their departure. The murals show the Anunnaki creating humanity, teaching them the arts of civilization, and the great flood that nearly destroyed their creation. The hall leads to a large chamber with a domed ceiling. In the center of the chamber is a pedestal holding a crystal sphere.

The visitor approaches the pedestal and places his hands on the sphere. The sphere glows with an inner light and projects holographic images into the air above it. The images show the Anunnaki's home world, a planet of vast oceans and scattered islands. Cities of gleaming spires rise from the islands, connected by bridges and flying vehicles. The Anunnaki themselves are shown, tall and humanoid, with pale skin, dark hair, and large, almond-shaped eyes.

The images change to show the Anunnaki's journey to Earth, their arrival in great ships that descend from the sky. They are shown using advanced technology to reshape the Earth's surface, creating mountains, valleys, and rivers. They seed the planet
