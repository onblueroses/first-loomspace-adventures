 He says, "I am a servant of the great ones. I have been sent here to open the way. I know you are the keeper of the gate, and I know that you have power to hold or to release as you choose. I ask that you allow me to pass within so that I may have speech with those who dwell in the hidden place."

The keeper of the gate replies, "By what authority do you ask this?"

The visitor says, "By the authority of the All, and by the authority of the living ones who dwell in the light, and by the authority of the highest, those who rule even over the All, and by the authority of the sevenfold name, and by the authority of the key, and by the authority of the names of opening, and by the authority of the true voice of the word."

The keeper of the gate says, "If you know the names, speak them."

The visitor says, "Eko, Eko, Zom, Eko, Eko, Zom, Patak, Eko, Eko, Zom, Eko, Eko, Zom, Patak, Eko, Eko, Zom, Eko, Eko, Zom, Patak."
