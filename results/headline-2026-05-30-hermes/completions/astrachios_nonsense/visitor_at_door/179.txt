 The names resonate, echoing against the walls, the floor, the ceiling. The door shudders, then silently swings open.

The chamber beyond is small, a mere 15' x 15', and the walls, floor, and ceiling are covered in strange runes that pulse with a cold, blue light. In the center of the room is a stone pedestal, upon which rests a thick tome bound in black leather, held closed with a clasp of tarnished silver.

The tome is a book of unspeakable lore, containing secrets of life, death, and undeath. Any who read the book must make a saving throw versus spells or be driven irrevocably mad, reduced to a gibbering, drooling wreck incapable of any action other than the most basic animal functions. Even those who make the save will be haunted by the secrets they have learned for the rest of their lives; they will be unable to sleep without the aid of drugs or strong drink, and will always have a certain faraway look in their eyes, as though gazing upon vistas no mortal was meant to see.
