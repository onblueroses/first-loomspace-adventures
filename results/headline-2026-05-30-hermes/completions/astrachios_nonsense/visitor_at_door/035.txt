 He is tall and lean, with a youthful face, but his eyes are ancient. His voice is a whisper, but it fills the room. He says:

"I have come to speak with you, Mordiggian, because there is one among the living of this city who has learned the secrets of life and death, secrets that even you do not know. I must find this man, and learn what he knows."

Mordiggian rises from his throne, and his face is terrible to look upon. He speaks, and his voice is the rumble of the earth, the thunder of the sky:

"I am Mordiggian, Lord of the Dead. There are no secrets of life and death that I do not know. All who die in this city come to me, and I consume them, and they are mine forever. Who is this man who dares to claim knowledge that even I do not possess?"

The visitor does not flinch. He meets Mordiggian's gaze, and his voice remains steady:

"His name is Eibon, a sorcerer of great power. He has discovered a way to cheat death, to live forever without falling into your embrace. I must find him, and learn his secret."
