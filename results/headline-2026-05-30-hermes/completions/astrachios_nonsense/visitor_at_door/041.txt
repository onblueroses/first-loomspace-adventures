 The voice is not loud, but it carries clearly through the stone chamber.

"Thank you, my friends, for the welcome. It is good to see you again. I have come to tell you that it is time to begin the work we have planned for so long."

The visitor pauses, then continues. "You know what you must do. The work will be difficult and dangerous. Some of you may not survive. But if we succeed, we will bring about a new age for our people. An age of power and glory, in which we will take our rightful place as rulers of all we survey."

The visitor's voice grows louder and more impassioned. "The time has come to throw off the shackles of our oppressors! To rise up and seize what is rightfully ours! Are you with me?"

A resounding cheer echoes through the chamber as the assembled figures raise their fists in salute. "We are with you!" they shout. "Lead us to victory!"

The visitor nods, satisfied. "Then let us begin. You all know your roles. Go forth and carry out your tasks with the utmost diligence and secrecy. When the time comes, we will strike as one, and our enemies will fall before us like wheat before the scythe."
