#### 9. The Iron Word
*Range:* Touch  
*Duration:* Instantaneous

You touch a creature. The target must make a Charisma saving throw. On a failure, it takes 10d10 psychic damage and is stunned until the end of your next turn. On a success, it takes half as much damage and isn't stunned.

#### 10. Maddening Whispers
*Range:* 60 feet  
*Duration:* Concentration, up to 1 minute

You whisper a string of syllables that only one creature of your choice within range can hear. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it is charmed by you until the spell ends or until you or your allies do anything harmful to it. The charmed target is incapacitated and has a speed of 0.

On each of its turns, the target can make a Wisdom saving throw. On a success, the spell ends.
