 This is the voice of the Guardian of the Threshold. The Guardian speaks the words of the threshold:

I am the Guardian of the Threshold.

I stand between the world of men and the realms of the Mighty Ones.

None may pass save those who have the key.

Speak now the key if you would enter.

The candidate now speaks the key:

Mreph, Snolfren, Kresnopith, Onthropor.

The Guardian of the Threshold responds:

You have spoken the key, and the way is open.

The candidate enters the temple.

The Priestess speaks:

Welcome to the Temple of the Mighty Ones. I am the Priestess of the Temple, and I will guide you on your journey. You have passed the Guardian of the Threshold and spoken the key that opens the way. You stand now in the presence of the Mighty Ones, the great powers of the universe that shape all things.

The Priestess leads the candidate to the altar.

Here is the altar of the Mighty Ones, where we offer our devotion and our service. Here we seek to align ourselves with the great powers of the universe, to bring ourselves into harmony with the divine plan.
