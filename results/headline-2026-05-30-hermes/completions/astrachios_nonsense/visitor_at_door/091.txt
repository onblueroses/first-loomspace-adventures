## Ritual of the Black Flame

Also known as the "Invocation of the Black Flame" and the "Call of the Black Flame". This ritual summons the Black Flame and is the primary ritual of the Path of Smoke. It is used to empower the celebrants, a specific person or item, or a specific goal or desire. It is a general-purpose ritual for invoking the Black Flame.

### The Invocation

In the name of the Light of the World, the Black Flame, I call upon the powers of the Path of Smoke.

I call upon the Black Flame within me.

I call upon the Black Flame in all things.

I call upon the Black Flame of the Void.

I call upon the Black Flame of the World.

I call upon the Black Flame of the Stars.

I call upon the Black Flame of the Sun.

I call upon the Black Flame of the Moon.

I call upon the Black Flame of the Earth.

I call upon the Black Flame of the Waters.

I call upon the Black Flame of the Winds.

I call upon the Black Flame of the Fire.

I call upon the Black Flame of the Night.

I call upon the Black Flame of the Day.

I call upon the Black Flame of Life.
