 The door opens, and the visitor enters. The door closes.

The visitor approaches the center of the room, to the circle of the twelve thrones. The visitor speaks the names of the twelve that sit upon the thrones:

Vronosnok, Snolpharod, Kresnopith, Ontrolanker, Tholgardosen, Onthelosk, Phenrolop, Engrokitha, Phelorpa, Vrindat, Mrep, Klimp Tronosemath.

The twelve nod, and the visitor sits upon the thirteenth throne.

The twelve and the visitor speak as one. They say the words that summon those that are summoned. The floor of the chamber opens. The summoned ones arise.

The twelve and the visitor continue to speak. They say the words that command the summoned ones. The summoned ones are commanded.

The summoned ones descend. The floor of the chamber closes.

The twelve and the visitor continue to speak. They say the words that describe the course of the world. The world is described.

The twelve and the visitor cease to speak.
