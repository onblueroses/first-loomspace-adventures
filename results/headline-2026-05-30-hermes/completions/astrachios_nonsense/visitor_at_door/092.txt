 The doors open.

Enter.

The visitor at the door enters.

The visitor speaks to the monks.

I have come to learn the wisdom of the monks of the hidden temple.

The monks speak to the visitor.

Welcome, seeker of wisdom. We shall teach you the ancient knowledge that has been passed down through generations in our temple. But first, you must prove your worth by solving a riddle.

The visitor speaks to the monks.

I am ready to prove my worth. What is the riddle?

The monks speak to the visitor.

In a place where time stands still, where the past and future meet,
There lies a treasure, a secret to keep, for those who seek knowledge deep.
It cannot be seen, nor touched, nor heard, yet it holds the power of the spoken word.
What is this treasure that we hold dear, a gift to those who persevere?

The visitor speaks to the monks.

The answer to your riddle is wisdom. Wisdom is a treasure that cannot be seen, touched, or heard, yet it holds the power of the spoken word. It is a gift to those who persevere in seeking knowledge.

The monks speak to the visitor.
