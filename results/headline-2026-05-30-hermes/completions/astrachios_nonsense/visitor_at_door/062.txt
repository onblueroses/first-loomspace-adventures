### 51. The Ritual of the Abyss

This ritual opens a gateway into the Abyss.

Required are the sigil of the key, the obsidian knife, the candles of blackest wax, the incense of the tomb, and the blood of the summoner.

Place the candles in a circle around the sigil. Light the candles and the incense. Stand within the circle and chant the words of opening:
