### The Grand Staircase

As the characters approach the grand staircase, they notice that the steps are unusually wide and tall, making it difficult to climb. The staircase is also adorned with intricate carvings and glowing runes.

To climb the staircase, the characters must succeed on a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check. On a success, they can climb the stairs without issue. On a failure, they slip and fall, taking 1d6 bludgeoning damage.
