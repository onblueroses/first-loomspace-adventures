 The door opens. The visitor enters.

## The Great Gate

The Great Gate lies in the center of the city. It is large, much larger than the other gates. It is made of iron and decorated with gold and silver. The visitor approaches the gate and speaks:

I seek the Great Gate of the city of Gondolin. I have passed through the other gates and now I stand before this final gate. I ask that it be opened for me.

The gate does not open. The visitor persists:

I have come far and endured much to reach this place. I have passed through the other gates by speaking their secret names and passwords. I know the secret name of this gate and the password that will open it. I ask again that the gate be opened for me.

Still, the gate does not open. The visitor speaks once more:

Very well. I will speak the secret name of this gate and the password that will open it. But know that I do this not for myself, but for the sake of those who sent me and for the sake of the city itself.

The visitor speaks the secret name of the gate and the password:

Annon-in-Gelydh, the Gate of the Noldor. The password is: Ende ennas i'T
