If the visitors do not speak the names correctly, the door does not open, and the voice speaks again: "The names you speak are wrong. Speak the names that open the way, or depart."

The door remains sealed until the correct names are spoken.

Beyond the door lies the Chamber of the Oracle, a circular room with a domed ceiling. The walls are adorned with intricate murals depicting scenes of prophecy and revelation. In the center of the chamber stands a pedestal upon which rests a crystal sphere, the Oracle of Fates.

When the visitors approach the pedestal, the Oracle of Fates glows with an inner light, and a voice emanates from it, offering to answer three questions about the future. The Oracle's answers are always truthful but often cryptic and open to interpretation.

Once the visitors have asked their three questions, the Oracle's light fades, and the voice speaks a final time: "The future is ever-changing, shaped by the choices of those who seek to know it. Go forth with the knowledge you have gained, and may your path be guided by wisdom."
