 I am (name). I come before you in peace and seek entrance to your abode. Permit me to enter and abide within your walls. I promise to honor and respect your hospitality.

The guardian of the gate responds. By the authority of the sacred names, I permit you to enter. May you abide in peace within these walls. May your stay be fruitful and pleasant. Enter with my blessing.

The door is opened and the visitor steps across the threshold. The guardian of the gate speaks. The bond is sealed. So let it be.

Commentary

This ritual serves to establish a formal relationship between the guardian of a dwelling and a visitor who seeks to enter that dwelling. It is a way of ensuring that both parties understand their obligations and responsibilities. The guardian must offer hospitality and respect the rights of the visitor, and the visitor must honor the ways and rules of the guardian's house. The ritual may be modified to suit the individual beliefs and practices of those using it, but its essential structure should be preserved to maintain its efficacy. It is not necessary to remember all the names, or to pronounce them perfectly. Their purpose is to focus the mind and lend a sense of solemnity to the ritual.
