 Their words are distant and hollow, like they’re speaking through a long tunnel, or from deep underwater. Their voice is impossible to place, but the words are clear.

*If you truly wish to speak with me, then you must come to me. I dwell in the place between moments, in the shadow of the world. I am there when you blink, when you turn away, when you look at the sun too long. I am the flicker at the edge of your vision, the shape that is gone when you look again. I am the dream you can’t remember, the word you can’t recall, the thought that slips away. If you wish to speak with me, then you must find me there.*

To do this, you must perform a ritual. The ritual is simple, but the price is high.

First, you must prepare the space. The room must be completely dark. Not just dark, but utterly lightless. If even a single photon enters the room, the ritual will fail. To ensure this, you must seal the room completely. Cover every window, every crack, every keyhole. Block every possible source of light.

Next, you must prepare yourself. You must be naked, both physically and mentally. Remove all clothing and jewelry
