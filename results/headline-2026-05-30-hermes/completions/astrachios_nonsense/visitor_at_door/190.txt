### 3

Beyond the door, there is a passage. It is dark. It is long. It is narrow.

The walls of the passage are stone. The stone is cold. The stone is damp.

The air in the passage is still. The air is cold. The air is damp.

There are no lights in the passage. There are no sounds in the passage. There is only darkness and silence.

The visitor walks down the passage. The visitor's footsteps are silent. The visitor's breath is silent. The visitor's thoughts are silent.

The passage leads downward. It leads deep into the earth. It leads to a place that is not a place.

At the end of the passage, there is another door. The door is made of stone. The door is sealed with lead. The door is locked with locks that have no key.
