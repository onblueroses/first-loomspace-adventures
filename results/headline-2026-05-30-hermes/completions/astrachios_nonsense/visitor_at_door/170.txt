### 47. Hall of the Chamberlain

The chamberlain is a tall, thin, old man with white hair and a long, white beard. He wears a black robe with a silver chain around his neck. He carries a staff with a silver ball on top. He is the keeper of the keys to the castle and is responsible for the smooth running of the household. He is a loyal servant of the king and queen and will do anything to protect them.

The chamberlain is a busy man and does not have much time for idle chatter. He will only speak to those who have business with the king or queen or who are invited guests. He will escort important visitors to the throne room or to their appointed chambers.

### 48. Hall of the Seneschal

The seneschal is a middle-aged man with short, dark hair and a neatly trimmed beard. He wears a dark blue robe with a gold chain around his neck. He carries a large ring of keys at his belt. He is responsible for the day-to-day running of the castle and the management of the castle staff. He is a strict taskmaster and demands perfection from those under his command.
