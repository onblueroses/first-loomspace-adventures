### 273 The Inner Temple
This room is a shrine to the demon lord Nidhogg, who is depicted in a large statue in the center of the room. The statue is carved from black stone and depicts Nidhogg as a serpentine creature with a long, sinuous body and a gaping maw filled with sharp teeth. The walls of the room are adorned with frescoes depicting scenes of destruction and chaos, with Nidhogg at the center of each scene, devouring the roots of the World Tree.

In front of the statue is a large stone altar, stained with old blood. On the altar rests a black chalice and a ceremonial dagger with a black blade. The air in the room is heavy and oppressive, and there is a sense of malevolent presence that seems to watch from the shadows.
