### 3. The Hall of the Spheres

The Hall of the Spheres is a vast, circular chamber with a domed ceiling. The walls are adorned with intricate carvings depicting celestial bodies and cosmic events. In the center of the hall, there are nine hovering spheres, each representing a different celestial body: the Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune.

To proceed, the visitor must align the spheres in the correct order, reflecting the cosmic harmony. The order is as follows:

1. Sol (Sun)
2. Selene (Moon)
3. Hermes (Mercury)
4. Aphrodite (Venus)
5. Ares (Mars)
6. Zeus (Jupiter)
7. Kronos (Saturn)
8. Ouranos (Uranus)
9. Poseidon (Neptune)

Once the spheres are aligned, a hidden door opens, revealing the path to the next chamber.
