The party enters the vaulted chamber beyond. In the center of the room, upon a black stone pedestal, rests a thick tome bound in black leather and embossed with arcane symbols. The book is open to a page bearing the title “The Halls of Pandemonium.”

The visitor approaches the pedestal and begins to read the text. As they do so, the room fills with an eerie, otherworldly light. The symbols on the page begin to shift and change, rearranging themselves into new patterns.

The visitor continues to read, their voice echoing through the chamber with an unnatural resonance. As they speak the final words of the incantation, the light in the room flares blindingly bright, then fades to reveal a portal hovering in the air before them.

The portal is a swirling vortex of darkness, shot through with flickers of sickly green and purple light. It pulses and writhes, as if alive, and emanates a palpable sense of dread.

The visitor steps forward, their form silhouetted against the eldritch glow of the portal. They hesitate for a moment, then step through, vanishing into the roiling darkness.
