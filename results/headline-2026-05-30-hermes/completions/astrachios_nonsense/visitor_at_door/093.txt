The visitor steps into a room that is vast and empty. The walls are white. The floor is white. The ceiling is white. There is no furniture. There is no decoration. There is nothing in the room but the visitor and the door behind them.

The visitor stands in the center of the room. They look around. They see nothing. They hear nothing. They feel nothing.

The visitor speaks. "I am come to aid the kingdom. I am come to aid the kingdom."

Nothing happens. The room remains empty. The room remains silent. The room remains still.

The visitor waits. They do not know what they are waiting for. They do not know why they are waiting. They only know that they must wait.

The visitor waits for a long time. They do not grow impatient. They do not grow bored. They do not grow tired. They only wait.

After a long time, the visitor hears a sound. It is a soft sound. It is a distant sound. It is a sound that the visitor has never heard before.

The sound grows louder. The sound grows closer. The sound fills the room.
