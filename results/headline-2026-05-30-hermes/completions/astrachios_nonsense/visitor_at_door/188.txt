 

"I am a servant of the King, and I come to you with a message from His Majesty."

He hands you a small letter, sealed with the royal crest. 

The letter reads: 

"To the esteemed wizard, resident of the Tower of the Archmage in the city of Arabel,

Your presence is requested at the royal court. Your expertise is needed in a matter of utmost importance to the kingdom. Please make haste to the palace.

Signed, 
King Azoun IV of Cormyr."

The visitor awaits your response.

"I will come to the palace as soon as I am able," you reply. "Please inform His Majesty that I will make haste."

The visitor nods. "I shall deliver your message. Safe travels to you, wizard." He bows and takes his leave.

You begin making preparations for the journey to the royal palace, gathering necessary supplies and magical items. The road ahead may be perilous, but the call of the king cannot be ignored. Adventure awaits!

Thus ends the prologue of our tale. Your journey to the royal palace and the quest that awaits you there shall be the subject of the next chapter. May your magic be strong and your wits sharp, for the fate of the kingdom may rest in your hands.

Farewell
