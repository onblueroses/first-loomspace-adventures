 "I am a traveler seeking shelter for the night. I have come far and will leave in the morning without causing trouble. I swear this on my ancestors."

If the visitor has been truthful, the door will open. If the visitor has lied or intends harm, the door will remain sealed.

*This is a magical incantation to open a door that only allows those who speak truthfully and mean no harm to pass. The visitor must recite the incantation and make a truthful oath to gain entry.*
