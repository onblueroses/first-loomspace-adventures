 The gatekeeper answers. The visitor speaks again. The gatekeeper's voice is heard once more, then the door opens.

The door is closed behind the visitor. The visitor stands within a large, dark hall, its floor paved with black stone, its walls hidden in shadow. The visitor approaches a tall throne that stands at the hall's center, atop a raised dais of three steps. A figure is dimly seen seated upon the throne. The figure wears a robe of black and a hood or mask that completely conceals its face. The figure does not move or speak. The visitor prostrates him or herself upon the lowest step of the dais, then stands and speaks a request.

The enthroned figure remains silent and motionless. The visitor prostrates again, stands, and leaves the hall by the way he or she came. The door to the hall is closed and locked. The visitor is alone in the antechamber. He or she opens the outer door and goes out, closing the door behind. The gatekeeper is nowhere to be seen. The visitor walks away from the house.

The visitor does not remember the location of the house, nor the way that leads to it. The house is never found again.
