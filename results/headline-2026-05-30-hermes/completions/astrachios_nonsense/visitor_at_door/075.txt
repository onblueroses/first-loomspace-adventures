 “I am the demon Flibbertigibbet. Make way so that I may enter!”

“Flibbertigibbet, the demon of petty lies and minor deceptions, I know you and your ways. You have no power over me, nor may you enter my home unless I give you leave. I deny you entrance. Begone, and trouble me no more!”

The demon Flibbertigibbet snarls and spits, but he cannot cross the threshold uninvited, and the names hold him at bay. He retreats back into the night, muttering threats and imprecations. When he has gone, the homeowner erases the chalk marks, and the night’s peace returns.
