 The door answers, opening if the visitor is accepted. If the visitor is rejected, the door remains closed and silent.

#### 27. The Stair of the False King
A wide, grand staircase of stone ascends to the east, ending at a landing with a door. The steps are worn and cracked, and the walls are adorned with faded murals and dusty tapestries depicting a king and his court. Close inspection reveals that the king's face has been scratched out in each image.

If the characters ascend the stairs, the air grows colder and a sense of dread builds. Upon reaching the top, the characters hear a deep, echoing voice in their minds that says, "Turn back, for only death awaits you here."

The door atop the stairs is locked, and the key is nowhere to be found. If the characters open the door, read:

The chamber beyond the door is empty save for a stone throne against the far wall. Atop the throne sits a skeleton clad in tattered robes and a tarnished crown.

The skeleton is all that remains of a forgotten king who died alone in his keep. If the characters approach the throne, the skeleton crumbles to dust, leaving behind the tarnished crown and a ruby ring worth 250 gp.
