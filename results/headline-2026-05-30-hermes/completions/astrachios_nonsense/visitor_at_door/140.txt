 The doorkeeper listens, and replies. The door opens.

## 42

The door opens.

The visitor enters the library.

The door closes.

The library is vast. It extends upwards and outwards and downwards. There are more books than there are stars in the sky. The shelves tower over the visitor. The visitor is small. The visitor is alone.

## 43

The visitor walks among the shelves. The visitor walks for days. The visitor walks for weeks. The visitor walks for months. The visitor walks for years. The visitor walks for decades. The visitor walks for centuries. The visitor walks for millennia. The visitor walks for eons. The visitor walks for eternity.

The visitor does not age. The visitor does not tire. The visitor does not hunger. The visitor does not thirst. The visitor is sustained by the library.

## 44

The visitor finds a book. The book is small. The book is unassuming. The book is plain. The book is titled "The Book of the Library".

The visitor opens the book. The visitor reads the book.

The book contains a single sentence: "The library contains all books that do not contain themselves."

The visitor closes the book. The visitor replaces the book on the shelf.
