 He is the representative of the lodge. He speaks the secret words: “I am the representative of the lodge. I come in peace, and I will depart in peace. I wish to commune with the lodge.”

The doorkeeper allows him to enter. The representative walks to the altar, where the master waits. He bows to the master and says: “Greetings, master, I wish to commune with the lodge.”

The master says: “Greetings, representative, you are welcome here.”

The representative says: “I have a message from the grand lodge. It is a message of peace and brotherhood. The grand lodge wishes to express its gratitude for the work that this lodge has done in promoting the principles of Freemasonry. The grand lodge also wishes to remind this lodge of the importance of maintaining the secrecy of our rituals and teachings. The grand lodge trusts that this lodge will continue to uphold the highest standards of our fraternity.”

The master says: “Thank you, representative, for bringing this message from the grand lodge. We are honored to receive it, and we will strive to live up to the expectations of the grand lodge. We will continue to promote the principles of Freemasonry and to maintain the secrecy of our rituals and teachings.”
