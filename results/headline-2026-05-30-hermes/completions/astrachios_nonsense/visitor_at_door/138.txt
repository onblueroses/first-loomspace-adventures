 "I am Phelonion. I bring the key to the gate of the garden of Ephilonep, where the First Gate lies hidden."

The door opens and the visitor enters. The door closes. The visitor stands in darkness.

A voice speaks: "Phelonion, you have passed the First Gate. Now you must unlock the way to the Second Gate. To do this, speak the words that reveal the hidden path."

The visitor speaks the words: "By the power of Phnolithra, and the secret name of Onthar, I call upon the hidden path to open before me."

A glowing path appears, leading into the darkness. The visitor follows the path.

The path leads to a vast underground chamber. In the center of the chamber stands a towering gate, carved with intricate symbols and glowing with an inner light.

A voice speaks: "Phelonion, you stand before the Second Gate. To pass through, you must solve the riddle of the gate. Listen carefully:

'I am the beginning of eternity, the end of time and space, the beginning of every end, and the end of every place. What am I?'"

The visitor thinks for a moment, then speaks the answer: "The letter 'E'."
