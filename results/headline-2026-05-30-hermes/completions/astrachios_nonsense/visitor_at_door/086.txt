# 20. The Final Spell

The spell which follows is the final one, the ultimate invocation of the forces of darkness and light, the supreme expression of the art of sorcery. It should be pronounced only in the most extreme necessity, or in the hour of death, when all else has failed, and the knowledge of the art and the exercise of its powers has not sufficed to save the sorcerer. It is the final cry of the spirit, the last summons of the will, the ultimate proclamation of the mastery of the magician over the forces of the universe.

The sorcerer lies upon the ground, face downward, with the arms extended in the position of an inverted pentagram. The head is turned to the east, in the direction of the rising sun. The eyes are closed, and the breath is drawn in slowly through the nose, held for the space of four heartbeats, and then expelled gradually through the mouth.

The following words are spoken:

Asteroth, Ashtaroth, Basalmeton, Somalon, Gogoroth, Semolcna, Tetragrammaton, Hagios, Hermes, Eko, Eko, Azarak, Eko, Eko, Zomelak
