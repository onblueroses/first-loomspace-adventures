You step through the doorway. The black-robed man stands before you. He is not much taller than you, and is perhaps sixty years of age, though his body seems younger. His hair is black, streaked with white, and his face is strong, with a hawk-like nose and piercing black eyes that stare into your own. He smiles, but there is little warmth in the expression; it is cold and distant. His voice is rich and deep, but there is something unsettling about it, almost as if it is coming from far away.
