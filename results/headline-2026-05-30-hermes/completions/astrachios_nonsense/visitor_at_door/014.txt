 Let the door open and let the visitor enter the circle of art.

When the door has opened and the visitor has entered the circle, the magician speaks these words:

You who have come to me, I greet you with respect and welcome you to my circle of art. Speak and reveal your name to me.

The visitor speaks its name. If the magician wishes to ask anything of the visitor, he does so at this time. When the magician has finished his questions, he speaks these words:

I thank you, N., for coming at my call and answering my questions. Go now in peace and friendship. May the harmony between us remain undisturbed. Farewell.

The magician makes the Sign of Closing to seal the rift in the veil that separates the worlds, and allows the visitor to depart. After the rift has been sealed, he speaks in a clear voice the words that follow to officially end the ritual:

By the authority of the true names of power I have spoken, and by the efficacy of these ancient and potent symbols, I declare this ritual ended. So let it be.

He makes the Sign of Banishing to cleanse his working space, then puts away the ritual instruments and departs from the circle of art.
