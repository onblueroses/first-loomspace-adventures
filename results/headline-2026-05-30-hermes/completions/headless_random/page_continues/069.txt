## 3. Diagram the carbon cycle.

The carbon cycle is the biogeochemical cycle by which carbon is exchanged among the biosphere, pedosphere, geosphere, hydrosphere, and atmosphere of the Earth. It is one of the most important cycles of the Earth and allows for carbon to be recycled and reused throughout the planet. The carbon cycle was discovered by Antoine Lavoisier and Joseph Priestley, and was popularized by Humphry Davy.

The carbon cycle comprises a sequence of events that are key to making the Earth capable of sustaining life. Without it, carbon would be stored in the atmosphere, never to be used by living organisms. The carbon cycle can be thought of as four main reservoirs of carbon connected by pathways of exchange.
