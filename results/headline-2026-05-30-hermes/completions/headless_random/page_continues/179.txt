2. What is the difference between the lithosphere and the asthenosphere?

The lithosphere is the rigid, outermost layer of the Earth, while the asthenosphere is the more ductile and partially molten layer beneath it.

The lithosphere includes the crust and the uppermost part of the mantle, and is divided into several plates that move over the Earth's surface. The lithosphere is rigid and brittle, and can break into pieces to form the tectonic plates.
