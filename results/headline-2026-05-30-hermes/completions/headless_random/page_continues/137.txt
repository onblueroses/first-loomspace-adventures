### Bandersnatch

The mantle convection is still considered the driving mechanism for plate tectonics. The debate is about the details of how it works. Here's a good article on the subject:
http://www.mantleplumes.org/ConvectionCurrents2.html

The gravitational forces are the ones driving the convection. The mantle material is heated by the core, and expands, becoming less dense. This causes it to rise, and when it reaches the crust it spreads to the sides
