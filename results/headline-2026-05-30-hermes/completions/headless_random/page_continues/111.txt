Note that a key point in this theory is the motion of the plates, that is, the motion of the plates is what makes the continents drift. This is why the theory of plate tectonics is often called the theory of continental drift.

Now let's look at what the theory of plate tectonics has to say about the origin of the continents and the oceans.

The continents are composed of a type of crust called continental crust, which is thicker and less dense than the oceanic crust. The continents are thought to have formed by the process of accretion, which is the gradual addition of material to a landmass over time. This process is thought to have begun about 4 billion years ago, when the Earth was still forming.

The oceans, on the other hand, are composed of a type of crust called oceanic crust, which is thinner and denser than the continental crust.
