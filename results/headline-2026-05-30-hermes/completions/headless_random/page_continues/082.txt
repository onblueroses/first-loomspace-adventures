## 5. Mantle Convection (the Earth's Engine)

The Earth beneath our feet is constantly shifting and moving even though we don't usually notice it. Over millions of years, the Earth's surface is constantly being reformed through a process called plate tectonics. Plate tectonics is driven by a phenomenon called mantle convection. Mantle convection is a process where the mantle of the Earth slowly moves in large currents. This process is similar to the way water in a pot boils, with hot water rising to the top and cooler water sinking to the bottom. In the mantle, the hot rock rises and the cooler rock sinks, creating large convection currents.
