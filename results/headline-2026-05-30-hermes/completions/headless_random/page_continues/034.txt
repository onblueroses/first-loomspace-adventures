## 4. Explain how the following evidences support the plate tectonics theory:

### a. Sea floor spreading

Sea floor spreading is the process by which new oceanic lithosphere forms at mid-ocean ridges. As tectonic plates move away from the ridges, the lithosphere thins and cools, increasing its density and causing it to sink further into the mantle. This process is a key component of plate tectonics, as it is responsible for the creation of new oceanic crust and the movement of tectonic plates.
