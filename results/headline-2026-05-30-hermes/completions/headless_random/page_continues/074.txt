I have drawn a very approximate picture to try to explain it.

The real picture is 3D, and the plates are moving in all directions, but I hope this gives a good basic idea of how it works.

Let's take a single convection cell, which is shown in green. The magma at the top of the mantle is cooler and denser, so it sinks. When it reaches the bottom of the mantle, it heats up and becomes less dense, and so it rises again. This is one convection cell.
