The image you posted is a seismic map. It shows the Earth's interior in terms of how it conducts seismic waves. Seismic waves are generated by earthquakes, and then travel through the Earth. Some types of seismic waves can only travel through solids, so they are not transmitted through the outer core. This is how we know the outer core is liquid.

The image is not a cross-section of the Earth showing tectonic plates. Tectonic plates are a part of the lithosphere, which is the rigid outermost layer of the Earth. The lithosphere comprises the crust and the uppermost part of the mantle. It is divided into several 'plates' that move relative to each other. This movement is driven by convection currents in the mantle.
