The answer to your question: "Why are the continents drifting?" is because the Earth's lithosphere has greater mechanical strength than the underlying asthenosphere. The plates move because of the slow creeping motion of Earth's solid mantle. At a seafloor spreading ridge, plates move away from the ridge, which is a topographic high, and the newly-formed crust cools as it moves away, increasing its density and contributing to the motion.

It is a mistake to think that the continents are drifting. The continents are not drifting. The tectonic plates are moving. The continents are part of the tectonic plates. The continents move because the tectonic plates move.
