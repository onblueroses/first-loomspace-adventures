## 2. The Earth's Layers

The Earth is composed of four main layers, sorted by depth:

• the crust,
• the mantle,
• the outer core, and
• the inner core.

These layers are made up of the following elements:

• The crust is made up of oxygen, silicon, aluminium, iron, calcium, sodium, potassium and magnesium.
• The mantle is made up of silicon, oxygen, aluminium and iron.
• The outer core is made up of iron and nickel.
• The inner core is made up of iron and nickel.

### 2.1. The Crust

The Earth's crust is a thin shell on the outside of the Earth, accounting for less than 1% of Earth's volume. The crust is exceptionally thin relative to the diameter of the Earth. The average thickness is 15-25 km, but can be up to 70 km thick in mountainous regions.
