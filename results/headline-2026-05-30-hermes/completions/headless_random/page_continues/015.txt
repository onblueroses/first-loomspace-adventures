The question is not about the existence of plate tectonics, but about the cause of plate tectonics. The cause is not mentioned in the Wikipedia article.

The cause of plate tectonics is the same as the cause of earthquakes, volcanoes, and mountain building: the Earth's internal heat. The Earth's internal heat is generated by the decay of radioactive elements in the Earth's mantle and crust. The heat is transferred from the Earth's interior to the surface by convection currents in the mantle. The convection currents cause the plates to move.
