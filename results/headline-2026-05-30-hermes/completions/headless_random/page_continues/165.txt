#### 19. What is the asthenosphere?

The asthenosphere is a part of the upper mantle just below the lithosphere that is involved in plate tectonic movement and isostatic adjustments. It is composed of peridotite, an ultramafic rock that is partially molten. The asthenosphere is a thin layer of weak and ductilely deforming rock that is thought to be responsible for the mechanical behavior of plates.
