The theory of plate tectonics is a unifying theory that explains the movement and deformation of the Earth's lithosphere, which is divided into several large and small plates. These plates move relative to each other at speeds ranging from 0 to 100 mm per year, interacting at their boundaries and causing various geological phenomena such as earthquakes, volcanic activity, mountain building, and oceanic trench formation.
