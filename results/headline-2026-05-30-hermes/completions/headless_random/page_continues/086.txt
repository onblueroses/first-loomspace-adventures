### 3.3.2.1.2.2.1. Seismic wave

A seismic wave is a mechanical wave of a type generated by earthquakes or explosions or other types of impulsive, energetic sources that travel through the Earth or other planetary bodies. Seismic waves are usually generated by movements of the Earth's tectonic plates, but may also be caused by volcanic activity, landslides or the rupture of subterranean water reservoirs. Seismic waves are studied by seismologists using an instrument called a seismograph, which records the time, amplitude, and frequency of seismic waves to learn about the Earth's internal structure, and to pinpoint the location and type of earthquakes, and other impulsive, energetic sources.
