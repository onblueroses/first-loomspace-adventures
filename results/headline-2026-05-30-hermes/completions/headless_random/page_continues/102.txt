The Earth is not expanding or contracting. Any appearance that it is doing so is due to measurement errors.

The major plates are:

• African Plate
• Antarctic Plate
• Eurasian Plate
• Indo-Australian Plate
• North American Plate
• Pacific Plate
• South American Plate

I have not heard of the "Oceanic Plate". Perhaps you mean the Pacific Plate.

The movement of tectonic plates has nothing to do with the Earth's rotation.
