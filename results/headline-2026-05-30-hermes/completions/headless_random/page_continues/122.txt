The way I see it, there are two primary forces in play: one is the centrifugal force acting on the Earth's equatorial bulge, the other is the gravitational force acting on the Earth by the Sun and Moon. The centrifugal force is trying to pull the Earth apart, while the gravitational force is trying to keep it together. This creates a sort of "slip zone" just beneath the crust which allows the plates to move about. I imagine the "slip zone" is comprised of a high temperature, high pressure, plastic-like substance, which, when combined with the convection currents, facilitates the movement of the plates.
