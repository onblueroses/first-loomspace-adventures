The diagram shows the major plates, the approximate rates and directions of their relative motion, and the three kinds of plate boundaries. The red arrows indicate the direction of relative motion along the boundaries. The purple arrows show the direction of the convergent boundary. The black arrows show the direction of the transform boundary. The blue arrows show the direction of the divergent boundary. (Source: United States Geological Survey. Public domain.)
