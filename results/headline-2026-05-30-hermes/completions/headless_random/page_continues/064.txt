### 8. Describe the difference between the following:

#### a. Mountain range and mountain system

A mountain range is a series of mountains connected by high ground and is a component of a mountain system. A mountain system is a group of mountain ranges.

#### b. Mountain belt and mountain barrier

A mountain belt is an elongated, linear zone of mountains. A mountain barrier is a mountain range that is difficult to cross.

#### c. Volcanic neck and volcanic cone

A volcanic neck is the solidified lava in a volcanic plug which remained when the volcano eroded away. A volcanic cone is the mound that forms from the material that erupts from the volcano. The terms are sometimes used interchangeably.

#### d. Lava and pyroclastic flow

Lava is molten rock that has been expelled from a volcano during an eruption. It can be viscous or fluid, and can be very hot. A pyroclastic flow is a fast-moving current of hot gas and rock that moves away from a volcano during an explosive eruption. The rocks are carried along by the hot gases, and the flow can be much cooler than a lava flow.
