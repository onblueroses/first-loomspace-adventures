The Earth's surface is divided into several major tectonic plates, which are constantly moving and interacting with each other. These interactions can cause a variety of geological events, such as earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, and the formation of mountains. The movement of these plates is driven by the heat and convection patterns in the Earth's mantle, which causes the plates to move and interact with each other over time. This process is known as plate tectonics, and it is a fundamental aspect of the Earth's geology and the way that the planet's surface changes over time.
