The surface of the Earth is broken up into large plates. It's easy to confuse these plates with the Earth's crust - the thin outermost layer of the Earth. But there is more to the structure of the Earth than this simple image of a cracked crust.

## Structure of the Earth

The Earth's layers can be defined in two different ways - based on the chemical composition or the mechanical properties. The former is the more popular classification - the crust, mantle, core structure. But when discussing plate tectonics, we talk about the lithosphere and asthenosphere. These are described based on their mechanical properties.

### Lithosphere

The lithosphere is the rigid outermost layer of the Earth. It includes the crust and the uppermost part of the mantle. It is a relatively thin layer - about 60 miles (100 km) thick. It is a cold and rigid layer and is broken up into several large tectonic plates. The lithosphere is the layer that we live on.
