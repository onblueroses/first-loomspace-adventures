The lithosphere is the rigid outermost shell of a planet, and on Earth, it is broken into tectonic plates. The lithosphere is composed of the crust and the uppermost part of the mantle, and it is divided into several large and small plates. These plates move relative to each other and interact at their boundaries, where they diverge, converge, or transform.

The asthenosphere is the highly viscous, mechanically weak and ductilely deforming region of the upper mantle of the Earth. It lies below the lithosphere, at depths between approximately 80 and 200 km below the surface. The asthenosphere is a part of the upper mantle just below the lithosphere that is involved in plate tectonic movement and isostatic adjustments.
