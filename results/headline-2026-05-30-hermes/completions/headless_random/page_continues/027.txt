The movement of plates is expressed in terms of relative motion. The relative motion of two plates can be described as divergent, convergent, or transform. Divergent boundaries occur where two plates move away from each other, convergent boundaries occur where two plates move towards each other, and transform boundaries occur where two plates slide past each other.
