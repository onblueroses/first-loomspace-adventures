The theories that preceded plate tectonics were "contracting Earth" and "expanding Earth". The former was the principal theory until the 1960s, when the latter gained acceptance.

I suggest you read the book "The Rejection of Continental Drift: Theory and Method in American Earth Science" by Oreskes, Naomi. It is a well-researched and well-written book that covers the history of the development of the theory of plate tectonics.

This theory was originally called the theory of continental drift and was proposed by Alfred Wegener in 1912. It was not accepted by most geologists at that time because Wegener could not explain how the continents could move through the oceanic crust. In addition, the prevailing theory of mountain formation was the contraction theory, which held that mountains were formed by the contraction of the Earth as it cooled.
