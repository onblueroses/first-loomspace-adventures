The theory of continental drift was proposed long before the theory of plate tectonics. Continental drift was proposed when it was realised that the shapes of many continents could be roughly fitted together like a jigsaw puzzle, and the idea developed that the continents had once been joined together and had drifted apart. The theory was proposed by Alfred Wegener in 1912, but was not widely accepted for many decades.

There were obvious problems with the theory. What mechanism could have caused the continents to drift apart? Africa and South America, for example, are separated by the Atlantic Ocean, so how could they have moved to their present positions?
