## 10.3.1. Plate Tectonics

The main features of plate tectonics are:

• The Earth's surface is covered by a series of crustal plates.
• The crustal plates are composed of continental and oceanic crust.
• The tectonic plates are floating on a substratum of liquid magma.
• The tectonic plates are in constant motion.
• The oceanic crust is thinner and denser than the continental crust.
• The tectonic plates move, collide, and are destroyed or reformed.
• The continents, sitting on continental crust, move with the tectonic plates.
• The continents move, separate, or collide, but are not destroyed.

## 10.3.2. The Tectonic Plates

There are seven major tectonic plates on the Earth's surface. There are also many smaller plates.

The major plates are:

1. North American Plate
2. South American Plate
3. Pacific Plate
4. African Plate
5. Eurasian Plate
6. Indo-Australian Plate
7. Antarctic
