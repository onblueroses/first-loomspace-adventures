 

In the 20th century, geologists and geophysicists began to gather data that would allow them to test the hypothesis of continental drift and develop the theory of plate tectonics. The exploration of the ocean floor after World War II improved understanding of the seabed, and provided evidence for seafloor spreading. The discovery of seafloor spreading and the acceptance of the theory of plate tectonics provided a mechanism for the motion of continents, and explained the formation of mountains, earthquakes, volcanoes, and deep ocean trenches. The theory revolutionized Earth sciences.
