## A new theory of plate tectonics

The following new theory of plate tectonics was published in a book in 2014.

The cause of plate tectonics is the same as the cause of continental drift, namely the centrifugal force of the Earth's rotation. This force causes the plates to move diagonally across the Earth from the poles towards the equator, in the direction of the Earth's rotation.

The centrifugal force of the Earth's rotation also causes the Earth to bulge at the equator and to be flattened at the poles.

The Earth's equatorial bulge is the reason why ocean floors are deeper at the poles and shallower at the equator. It is also the reason why ocean floors slope down from the equator to the poles. The sloping ocean floors cause the plates to slide diagonally across the Earth from the poles towards the equator, in the direction of the Earth's rotation.

The Earth's equatorial bulge is also the reason why the continents are mostly in the northern hemisphere, and why the average depth of the ocean floor in the southern hemisphere is greater than in the northern hemisphere.
