The mantle is a solid, but it behaves in a ductile manner over very long timescales (in the order of millions of years). So don't think of it as rigid, but rather as a very very viscous fluid.

Lateral heterogeneities in the mantle means that the mantle is not uniform. There are parts of it that are different than others. This can be due to a variety of reasons, such as the presence of water, differences in chemical composition, or differences in temperature. These differences cause the mantle to have different properties in different places, for example in some places it might be denser, and in other places less dense.
