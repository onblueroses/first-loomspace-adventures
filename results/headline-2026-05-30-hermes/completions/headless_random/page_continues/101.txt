I agree with your last post.

To add a little to it: If there were no convection in the mantle, plate tectonics would cease as the plates are just the cold surface "skin" that is moving due to the slow convection of the mantle. The plates are pushed and pulled by the mantle's convection, which is driven by heat from the core.

The plates have very little mass compared to the mantle, so the mantle drives their motion. Also, the plates are cold and relatively rigid compared to the mantle, so they fracture and "bunch up" as the mantle drives them against each other.

In contrast, the mantle is hot and relatively plastic, so it slowly convects (moves) instead of bunching up. If the plates were not more rigid than the mantle, they would just mix into the mantle instead of "bunching up" (forming mountains, etc.).
