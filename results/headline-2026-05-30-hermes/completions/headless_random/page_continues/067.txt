In case you find that too technical, here is a simpler version:

The Earth's surface is divided into large pieces that are slowly moving around. Scientists used to think that the Earth's surface was just one big piece, but we now know that it is not. The big pieces of the Earth's surface are called tectonic plates. They are called plates because they are very large and flat, like a plate you eat from. The plates move because they are floating on a layer of melted rock called the mantle. The mantle is very hot and is always moving around in big circles, like water boiling in a pot. This movement causes the plates to move around on the surface of the Earth. Sometimes the plates crash into each other, and this can cause earthquakes and volcanoes. Other times, the plates move away from each other, and this can cause new land to be formed in the middle of the ocean. The movement of the plates is very slow, and it takes millions of years for them to move very far. But over a long time, the plates can move a long way, and this can change the way the Earth looks.
