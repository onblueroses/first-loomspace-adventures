NASA - 2012 Earth's Magnetosphere

The magnetosphere is the region of space surrounding the Earth and other celestial bodies where the majority of magnetic field lines are closed. It is dominated by the Earth's magnetic field and its interaction with the solar wind. The magnetosphere prevents charged particles of the solar wind from entering Earth's atmosphere, but it cannot prevent disturbances caused by fluctuations of the solar wind pressure, known as "space weather". The dayside magnetosphere is compressed by the solar wind, while the nightside magnetosphere is stretched out into a tail, called the magnetotail. Some particles from the solar wind are able to leak in and become trapped within the magnetosphere, resulting in the aurora borealis and australis.
