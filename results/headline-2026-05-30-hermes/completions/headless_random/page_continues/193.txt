Hurricane Gloria was a powerful hurricane that caused significant damage along the east coast of the United States and in the Atlantic Canada in September 1985. It was the first significant system to strike the northeastern United States since Hurricane Agnes in 1972 and the first major storm to affect New York City and Long Island directly since Hurricane Donna in 1960. Gloria was a powerful Cape Verde hurricane originating from a tropical wave on September 16 in the eastern Atlantic Ocean. After remaining a weak tropical cyclone for several days, Gloria intensified on September 22 north of the Lesser Antilles, and then rapidly strengthened on September 23, becoming a Category 4 hurricane on the Saffir–Simpson scale near the Bahamas by the following day.
