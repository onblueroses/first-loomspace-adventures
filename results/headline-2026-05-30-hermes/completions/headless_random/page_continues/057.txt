#### 8.2.3 Divergent boundaries

At divergent boundaries, two plates move away from each other and new lithosphere is added to each plate as it is formed at a mid-ocean ridge or continental rift. For example, the Mid-Atlantic Ridge is the boundary between the Eurasian Plate and the North American Plate.
