## 4. Understand and be able to explain the following terms

a. Folding: Folding is a concept in geology that describes how rock layers are bent or folded under the Earth's surface. This can happen when the Earth's crust is compressed, causing the rock layers to buckle and fold. Folding can create various landforms, such as mountains, valleys, and hills.

b. Uplifting: Uplift is the process by which the Earth's surface is raised to a higher elevation. This can happen due to tectonic forces, such as the collision of tectonic plates, or due to the removal of material from the Earth's surface, such as through erosion. Uplift can create mountains and other high-elevation landforms.
