## Earthquake Prediction

Earthquake prediction is a branch of the science of seismology concerned with the specification of the time, location, and magnitude of future earthquakes within stated limits. Many methods have been developed for predicting the time and place in which earthquakes will occur. Despite considerable research efforts by seismologists, scientifically reproducible predictions cannot yet be made to a specific day or month.

While most seismologists agree that earthquake prediction, as defined above, is not currently possible, the debate continues on whether it will ever be possible. Predictions claimed as "successes" may be a result of random chance or due to the long term seismicity in the area considered.

Predictions are deemed significant if they can be shown to be successful beyond random chance. Therefore, methods of statistical hypothesis testing are used to determine the probability that an earthquake of a given magnitude will occur within a given time interval and within a given region.
