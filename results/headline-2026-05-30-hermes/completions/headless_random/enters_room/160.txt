It is not easy to determine the exact number of tectonic plates on Earth, as new research continues to shape our understanding of the planet's geology. However, most geologists agree that there are seven major plates:

1. African Plate
2. Antarctic Plate
3. Eurasian Plate
4. Indo-Australian Plate
5. North American Plate
6. Pacific Plate
7. South American Plate

In addition to these major plates, there are several minor plates, such as the Arabian Plate, the Caribbean Plate, the Cocos Plate, the Nazca Plate, the Philippine Sea Plate, and the Scotia Plate. Some scientists also consider the Indian and Australian plates as separate entities, rather than combining them into the Indo-Australian Plate.
