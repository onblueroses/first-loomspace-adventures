The relatively weak asthenosphere allows the tectonic plates to move easily towards a subduction zone. Plate tectonics has led to the formation of many geological features, including mountain ranges, volcanos, and rift valleys. It also has led to the formation of many mineral resources, including metal ore deposits and petroleum. (Full article...)
