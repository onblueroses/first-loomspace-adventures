Wikipedia has this to say about Plate Tectonics :

The lithosphere is divided into tectonic plates. There are seven major and many minor plates. The plates are around 100 km (62 mi) thick and consist of two principal types of material: oceanic crust (also called sima from silicon and magnesium) and continental crust (sial from silicon and aluminium). The composition of the two types of crust differs markedly, with the continental crust being composed primarily of granite, while the oceanic crust is composed primarily of basalt.

The churning of the Earth's mantle (the convection of molten rock) is what causes the plates to move. This has been happening for millions of years. As two plates come together, one may slide under the other one, causing the rising of mountains. The plates can also move apart, causing rifts to form.
