How does the speaker describe the continents? (22)

As "rafts" that float on the Earth's mantle.

What does the speaker say is the main driving force behind plate tectonics? (23)

Convection currents in the mantle.

What is formed when two plates move away from each other? (24)

A spreading ridge or rift valley.

What happens when two plates collide? (25)

One plate can be forced under the other in a process called subduction. This can lead to the formation of mountains, volcanoes, and oceanic trenches.

How fast do tectonic plates typically move, according to the speaker? (26)

Typically from zero to 100 millimeters per year.

What is the conveyor belt principle? (27)

The conveyor belt principle refers to the process by which oceanic crust is created at spreading ridges and eventually subducted back into the mantle at convergent plate boundaries, keeping the total surface area of the lithosphere roughly constant over time.
