 The idea of the tectonic plates, their motion, and the Earth's mantle and crust. The motion of the plates being driven by the convection currents in the mantle, which is caused by the heat from the Earth's core. The idea of subduction, where one plate is forced under another, and the recycling of the oceanic crust back into the mantle. The idea of seafloor spreading, where new crust is formed at the mid-ocean ridges and moves away from the ridge, cooling and becoming denser as it moves. The idea of the Earth's lithosphere being broken into several large plates and many smaller ones, and the different types of plate boundaries: convergent, divergent, and transform. The idea of the conveyor belt principle, where the total surface area of the lithosphere remains roughly the same due to the balance between the creation of new crust at the mid-ocean ridges and the subduction of old crust back into the mantle.
