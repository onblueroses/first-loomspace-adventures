The Earth's layered structure. (1) inner core; (2) outer core; (3) lower mantle; (4) upper mantle; (5) lithosphere; (6) crust

The main features of plate tectonics are:

1. The Earth's surface is covered by a series of crustal plates.
2. The plates are composed of oceanic lithosphere and thicker continental lithosphere, each topped by its own kind of crust.
3. The plates are not fixed but are in constant motion relative to each other and the Earth's surface.
4. The plates are rigid and deform very little.
5. Deformation occurs at the plate boundaries.
6. The boundaries between plates are of three kinds: convergent, divergent, and transform.
7. New oceanic crust is formed at the divergent boundaries by seafloor spreading.
8. Oceanic crust is recycled back into the mantle at some convergent boundaries by subduction.
9. The motion of the plates is driven by convection currents in the mantle.
