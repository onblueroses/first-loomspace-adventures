So, I'm currently in the process of putting together my PhD proposal, and I'm interested in the question of how tetrapods became fully terrestrial. It's a nice broad topic that incorporates several different aspects of palaeontology (biomechanics, physiology, ecology, etc.). Now, I'm thinking of focussing on the early part of the transition, i.e. from Acanthostega and Ichthyostega up to the various "amphibians" of the Carboniferous. It's an area that's been fairly neglected, as most of the work has been focussed on the fish-tetrapod transition, and the amniote transition.
