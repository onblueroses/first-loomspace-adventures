 A plump, pink, hairless, and slobbering baby? A large tarantula, hairy and with many irridescent eyes? No and no. It's a book, and not just any book. A strange book, a dangerous book. It is, in fact, the Book of Knowledge. It is said that the Book of Knowledge will never be complete, always growing, always changing, always adapting. The Book of Knowledge is not linear, but then, neither is knowledge. One may start reading the Book anywhere. Some say that one can learn the sum of human knowledge by starting at the beginning and reading through to the end, others say that that is too simple, that the Book is too complex, too interwoven to be simply read. 

The Book of Knowledge is not a normal book. It is not organized into chapters and sections, instead, it is organized into nodes. Each node is a snippet of knowledge, and each node is connected to other nodes, nodes on similar topics, or maybe nodes that are often read before or after the current node.
