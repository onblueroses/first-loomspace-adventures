## 7

What is the next term in the sequence 2, 5, 10, 17, 26,...? (2)

The differences between consecutive terms in the sequence 2, 5, 10, 17, 26,... are 3, 5, 7, 9,.... The next difference will be 11, so the next term in the sequence is 26 + 11 = 37.
