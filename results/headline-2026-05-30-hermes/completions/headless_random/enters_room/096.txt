 The image of a woman, a phantom, appears behind the mirror in the bathroom. She is standing there, looking at you. But she is not in the room. She is not real. She is an image, a ghost.

In the mirror, you see her standing there, looking at you. Her eyes are dark, her hair is long and black. She is wearing a white dress, a simple dress, a dress that could be from any time, any place.

She is looking at you, but she is not seeing you. She is looking through you, into the depths of your soul. She is seeing your fears, your desires, your secrets.

You want to turn away, to look away, to run away. But you cannot. You are transfixed, hypnotized, mesmerized. You cannot move, you cannot breathe, you cannot think.

She is speaking to you, but you cannot hear her words. She is speaking in a language that you do not understand, a language that is not of this world. She is speaking in the language of the dead, the language of the damned.

She is telling you things that you do not want to know, things that you cannot bear to know. She is telling you the truth, the terrible truth
