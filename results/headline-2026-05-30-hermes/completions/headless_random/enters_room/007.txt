 The Earth's crust is what you call your home. But it is not your real home. Your real home is in the etheric realms. The Earth's crust is composed of a number of tectonic plates that move slowly over time. These plates are constantly shifting and interacting with one another, causing earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, and other geological events.

The movement of these plates is driven by the heat and convection currents within the Earth's mantle. The mantle is a layer of hot, molten rock that lies beneath the crust. As the mantle heats up, it rises to the surface and pushes the plates apart. This process is known as seafloor spreading.

At the same time, the plates are also being pulled back into the mantle at subduction zones. This is where one plate is forced underneath another, causing it to melt and be recycled back into the mantle. This process is known as subduction.

The constant movement and interaction of these plates is what shapes the Earth's surface over time. It creates mountains, valleys, and oceans. It also causes natural disasters like earthquakes and volcanic eruptions.
