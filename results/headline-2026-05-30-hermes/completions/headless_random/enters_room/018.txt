Here are several key aspects that "enter the room" in the world of plate tectonics:

1. **Seafloor Spreading**: New oceanic crust is continuously formed at mid-ocean ridges through volcanic activity, pushing older crust away from the ridge, which is a fundamental process in the movement of plates.

2. **Subduction**: This is the process where one tectonic plate moves under another and sinks into the mantle due to differences in density. Subduction leads to the formation of volcanic arcs and trenches and is a key mechanism for the recycling of oceanic crust.

3. **Convergent Boundaries**: When plates move towards each other, they can cause the formation of mountain ranges, volcanic activity, and earthquakes. This is where subduction commonly occurs, but it can also lead to continental collision, as seen in the formation of the Himalayas.
