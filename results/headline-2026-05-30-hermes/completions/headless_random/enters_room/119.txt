The skull was found in the early 1950s in the West Bank, near the city of Jericho. It was found in a cave, and it is thought that the person was buried there intentionally. The skull was reconstructed using 3D printing technology, and the reconstruction was based on the skull's morphology and the features of modern-day populations from the same region. The reconstruction shows a face with a broad nose, prominent cheekbones, and a strong jaw. The person had a protruding brow ridge, which is a characteristic of early human skulls.
