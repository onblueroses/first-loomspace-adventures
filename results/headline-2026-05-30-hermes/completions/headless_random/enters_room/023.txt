 The object of this research is to analyze the various geodynamic conditions in which the South American Platform was inserted, during the Mesozoic and Cenozoic eras, and its influences on the genesis of the main structural frameworks that characterize it. The study of the geodynamic evolution of the South American Platform and its relationship with the tectonic events that affected the neighboring plates and the global tectonics allows a better understanding of the morphostructural configuration of the South American continent. The main geodynamic and tectonic aspects of the South American Platform and its relationship with the African, Antarctic, Scotia, Caribbean and Nazca plates are addressed, as well as the origin and evolution of the main structural frameworks of the South American Platform, such as the Andean Chain, the continental rifts, the oceanic basins, and the transform faults.
