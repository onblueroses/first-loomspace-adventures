 A man. He is tall, dressed in dark clothes. He wears a top hat. He is pulling behind him a large wooden box, roughly the size of a coffin. It is painted black. He pulls it into the center of the room. He opens the lid. The box is empty. He turns to face the audience. He reaches up and removes his hat, letting it fall to the floor. He reaches up again and grips his hair, which is fake. A wig. He peels it off his head and drops it to the floor. His head is bald. He reaches up to his face, to his eyebrows. They too are fake. Artificial. He peels them off. He reaches to his eyes. They are not his eyes. He peels them off, dropping them to the floor. He has no eyes now. He reaches to his nose. It is not his nose. He removes it. Drops it to the floor. He has no nose. He reaches to his lips. They are not his lips. He removes them. Drops them to the floor. He has no mouth. He has no face.
