The robotic model splits open, revealing the Earth's layers:

1. Inner Core: The innermost layer is the solid inner core, composed primarily of iron and nickel. Despite the immense heat, the inner core is solid due to the extreme pressure at the center of the Earth.

2. Outer Core: Surrounding the inner core is the liquid outer core, also composed mainly of iron and nickel. The motion of the liquid in the outer core generates Earth's magnetic field.

3. Mantle: The mantle is the largest layer, composed of silicate rocks rich in iron and magnesium. The mantle is divided into the upper and lower mantle. The upper mantle contains the asthenosphere, a highly viscous, mechanically weak region that allows the tectonic plates to move.
