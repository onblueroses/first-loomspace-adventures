 "I am your neighbor. I have come to say hello. May I come in?" What should you do?

When a neighbor comes to your door to say hello, it is generally considered polite and appropriate to greet them and engage in a friendly conversation. Here are a few things you should do:

1. Smile and greet them warmly: A friendly smile and a warm greeting can help create a positive first impression and make your neighbor feel welcome.

2. Introduce yourself: If you haven't met your neighbor before, take a moment to introduce yourself and share your name.

3. Invite them in: If you feel comfortable doing so, you can invite your neighbor into your home. However, if you are not comfortable with this or if it is not a convenient time, you can politely decline and suggest an alternative, such as chatting at the doorway or arranging a future time to meet.

4. Engage in conversation: Take a few minutes to chat with your neighbor and get to know them better. You can ask about their family, their interests, or how long they have lived in the neighborhood.
