* Have you heard the good news about Plate Tectonics? *
* Tired of a life without purpose? *
* Tired of a life without unified explanations for the formation of mountain ranges, the drifting of continents, and the occurrence of earthquakes and volcanism? *

Well, have we got a theory for you! The Theory of Plate Tectonics is a comprehensive model of Earth's lithospheric dynamics that can explain all these phenomena and more! 

Based on groundbreaking work by such luminaries as Alfred Wegener, Harry Hess, and Tuzo Wilson, plate tectonics posits that the Earth's lithosphere is divided into a series of rigid plates that move and interact over time. These interactions at plate boundaries can lead to the formation of mountain ranges, earthquakes, volcanoes, and even the creation of new oceanic crust!
