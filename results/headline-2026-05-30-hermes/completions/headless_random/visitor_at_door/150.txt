The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) is the revenue service of the United States federal government, a bureau of the Department of the Treasury. The government agency is responsible for tax collection and administration of the Internal Revenue Code. It is also responsible for administering certain subsidies and welfare programs like the Affordable Care Act. The IRS has its origins in the 1862 establishment of the position of commissioner of Internal Revenue during the American Civil War to raise money for the war effort. The position was created by President Abraham Lincoln and the U.S. Congress. The current commissioner is Charles Rettig.
