The answer to the question "What is plate tectonics?" is that plate tectonics is the scientific theory that the Earth's lithosphere is divided into a number of large tectonic plates that move slowly over time, causing earthquakes, volcanic activity, mountain-building, and oceanic trench formation along their boundaries or faults. The movement of these plates is driven by convection currents in the mantle, which occur due to lateral density variations in the mantle. The lithosphere has greater mechanical strength than the underlying asthenosphere, allowing the plates to move.
