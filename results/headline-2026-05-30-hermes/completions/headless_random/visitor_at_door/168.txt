The Earth's mantle is a layer of silicate rock between the crust and the outer core. Its mass is 4.01 × 10^24 kg, making up 67% of the Earth's mass. The mantle is composed of the upper mantle and the lower mantle, which are separated by the transition zone. The upper mantle begins at the Mohorovičić discontinuity or Moho for short. The lower mantle is much larger than the upper mantle, with a thickness of about 2,200 km.
