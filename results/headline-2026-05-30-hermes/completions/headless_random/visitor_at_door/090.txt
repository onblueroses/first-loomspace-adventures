The Watcher is a character from the Marvel Comics universe. He is one of the members of the enigmatic and powerful race known as the Watchers. They are cosmic beings who possess vast knowledge and abilities, far surpassing most other beings in the universe. The Watchers, as their name suggests, are committed to observing the events of the universe, without interfering.

The Watcher who is most commonly depicted in Marvel Comics is Uatu. He was assigned to watch over Earth and its solar system. Despite his vow of non-interference, Uatu has, on several occasions, broken this vow to aid Earth's heroes, particularly the Fantastic Four, when the fate of the Earth or the universe was at stake.

Uatu resides in a citadel on the moon, which allows him to observe Earth without being detected. He possesses immense cosmic power, allowing him to manipulate energy and matter on a vast scale. However, he rarely uses these powers, adhering to his vow of non-interference.

The Watchers are characterized by their bald heads and large eyes, giving them an appearance distinct from most other beings in the Marvel Universe. They are also known for their iconic oath: "We Watch.
