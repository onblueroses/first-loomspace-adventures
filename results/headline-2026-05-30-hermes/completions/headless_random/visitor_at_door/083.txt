The Great Council of the Continents is a fictional entity and has no basis in reality. There is no known organization or council that represents the continents of Earth.

The continents are large landmasses on Earth. There are seven continents: Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Europe, North America, Australia (or Oceania), and South America. Some geographers consider Europe and Asia to be a single continent named Eurasia. 

Continents are not defined by their geological structure, but rather by their cultural and historical significance. For example, Europe and Asia are part of the same continuous landmass, but are considered separate continents due to cultural and historical reasons.
