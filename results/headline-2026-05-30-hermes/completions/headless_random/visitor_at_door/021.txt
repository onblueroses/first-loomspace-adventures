 "I'm the census taker. I need to record the population of this household. How many people live here?"

"Let me think," replies the homeowner. "There's myself, my wife, and our three children. That makes five."

"Are you sure?" asks the census taker.

"Yes," says the homeowner. "I can count them. One, two, three, four, five."

The census taker looks puzzled. "But I thought you said there were only three children?"

The homeowner smiles. "Ah, but you see, my wife and I are also part of the population of this household. So, one, two, three, four, five. Five people in total."

The census taker nods, finally understanding. "I see. Thank you for your time. I'll record that there are five people living in this household."
