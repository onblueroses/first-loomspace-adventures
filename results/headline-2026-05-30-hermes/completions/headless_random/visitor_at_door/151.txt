 "I'm here to inform you that your house is on fire."

The scientist frowns at him. "Is it," he says. "Interesting. How did you come to that conclusion?"

The visitor looks confused for a moment. "I saw flames coming out of your upstairs window and-"

The scientist nods. "Ah, I see. An observation. A valid one, too. And have you performed any tests to confirm this hypothesis?"

"Well, no," says the visitor. "I just thought you should-"

The scientist holds up a hand. "Please. I cannot abide shoddy methodology. Allow me to demonstrate."

The scientist takes a piece of paper from his pocket, scribbles a few words on it, then signs it with a flourish. "Here," he says, handing it to the visitor. "A properly formed hypothesis. Now, let us test it."

The scientist goes to the fireplace and scoops up a handful of ashes. He crosses the room to the window and tosses them out. They are caught by the wind and blown away. "You see?" he says. "The evidence does not support the hypothesis. My house is not on fire."
