 "My name is William. I've come to inform you that you've been chosen for the job."

## 2

"Chosen for what job?" you ask, perplexed.

William explains that you've been selected for a highly secretive government project. The project involves the study of tectonic plate movements and their effects on the Earth's crust. Your task would be to analyze data and help predict future seismic activities.

You're intrigued but also cautious. "Why me? I'm just a geology enthusiast, not a professional."

William smiles. "That's precisely why we chose you. We need fresh eyes, someone who is passionate and can think outside the box. Plus, your knowledge in geology is impressive."

You nod, still trying to process everything. "What would I have to do?"

"You'll be part of a team," William explains. "Your role will be to interpret the data we collect from various sources around the world. We have state-of-the-art equipment that can detect even the slightest movements in the Earth's crust."

"And the goal of this project?" you ask.

"To predict major seismic events before they happen," William says solemnly. "We believe that with the right data and analysis, we can save lives and prevent disasters."
