What type of boundary is formed when two plates move away from each other? 

Divergent boundary

A divergent boundary is formed when two tectonic plates move away from each other. At these boundaries, new oceanic crust is formed by the process of seafloor spreading. Magma from the mantle rises up and fills the gap between the separating plates, creating new crust as it cools and solidifies. This process causes the plates to move apart, and the newly formed crust to move away from the ridge, which is a topographic high.
