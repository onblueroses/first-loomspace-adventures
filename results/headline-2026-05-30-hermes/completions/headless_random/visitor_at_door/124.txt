 "We're from the Department of Plate Tectonics. We're here to move your plate."

The comic is a pun on the phrase "moving plates". The Earth's tectonic plates are constantly moving, as explained above. However, the phrase "moving plates" could also refer to a situation where a person is moving their plates (as in dishes). The comic depicts a literal interpretation of the latter, with the Department of Plate Tectonics arriving at someone's house to move their plates.

The title text continues the pun, referencing the "conveyor belt principle" mentioned above. This is a theoretical model for the movement of tectonic plates, suggesting that the lithosphere is moved in a circular motion, like a conveyor belt. The title text uses this term to humorously suggest that the Department of Plate Tectonics will move the person's plates using a conveyor belt.
