 "I am a doctor. I have come to cure your illness."

The visitor speaks through the door. "I am a scientist. I have come to share my research with you."

You are not interested in research. You do not need a scientist. The visitor is lying. You ignore them and walk away from the door.

The visitor knocks again. You do not answer. They continue knocking. The knocking grows louder and more insistent. You grow angry. You open the door and shout at the visitor. "Leave me alone! I do not want your lies!"

The visitor looks at you with pity. "I am not lying. I am here to help you. Please, let me in."

You hesitate. The visitor seems sincere. Perhaps they are telling the truth. You step aside and allow them to enter. 

The visitor steps inside and looks around. "This is a nice home you have. Very cozy."

You nod. "Thank you. What do you want?"

The visitor smiles. "I want to help you. I can see that you are struggling. You are confused and afraid. You do not know what to believe."
