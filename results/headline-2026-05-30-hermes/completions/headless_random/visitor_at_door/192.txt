 "I'm a traveling salesman," he says. "I have some beautiful items for sale."

The family looks at him with suspicion. "We don't need anything," the father says firmly.

But the salesman is persistent. "Just take a look," he urges, opening his case. "I have some lovely jewelry for the lady of the house, and some fine watches for you, sir."

The mother's eyes are drawn to a sparkling necklace, but she shakes her head. "We really can't afford any of this," she says.

The salesman smiles. "I understand," he says. "But I'm willing to make a deal. I'll give you a special discount, just for today."

The family exchanges glances. They're tempted, but they know they shouldn't. The father takes a deep breath. "No, thank you," he says. "We're not interested."

The salesman nods, but he doesn't give up. "I'll tell you what," he says. "I'll leave my card. If you change your mind, just give me a call."
