The geologist looks up, startled. "Who are you? And what do you mean by a dangerous path?"

"I am a messenger, sent to warn you," the visitor replies. "Your ideas about the Earth's crust, about these so-called tectonic plates... they are heresy. They go against the teachings of the Church."

The geologist frowns. "The Church? What does the Church have to do with science?"

"Everything," the visitor says. "The Church is the guardian of truth, and your theories contradict the truth as we know it. The Earth is not divided into plates that move and collide. It is a solid, unchanging mass, created by God exactly as it is now."

The geologist shakes their head. "With all due respect, I must disagree. The evidence is clear. The Earth's crust is divided into plates that move and interact in complex ways.
