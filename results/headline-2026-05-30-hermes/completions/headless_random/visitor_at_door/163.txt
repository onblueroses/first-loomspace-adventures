The Earth's tectonic plates move at a rate that varies from plate to plate and even within different portions of a single plate. Rates of plate movement range from about 10 to 160 millimeters per year, or about as fast as your fingernails grow. The fastest plates are the oceanic plates, with the Cocos Plate moving at a rate of 83 millimeters per year and the Pacific Plate moving at a rate of 56 millimeters per year. The slowest plates are the continental plates, with the Eurasian Plate moving at a rate of 7 millimeters per year.
