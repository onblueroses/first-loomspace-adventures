 "I conjure you, O Becharas, by the most high and holy God &c." (A long conjuration follows). "Beralanensis, Baldachiensis, Paumachiae, and Apologiae Sedes, by the most mighty kings and powers, and the most powerful princes, genii, Liachidae, and ministers of the Tartarean seat."

In the Grand Grimoire, Beralanensis, Baldachiensis, Paumachiae, and Apologiae Sedes are the four kings of Hell, sovereigns of the eight most powerful infernal demons. According to the Grand Grimoire, Ba'althazar is the head of the four kings. He is also considered the head of the powers of evil, and his name means "god of the earth." The four kings control the demons, but they are neither fallen angels nor spirits, but princes of royal blood who lived in the world a long time ago.
