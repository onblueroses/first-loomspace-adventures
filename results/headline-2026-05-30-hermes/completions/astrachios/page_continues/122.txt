 "Here beginneth the second part of the book called the Clavicle of Solomon. This part containeth many and various precepts, of which the first is this: experiments must not be made in the presence of onlookers." It is clear from this passage that the second part of the Clavicle of Solomon is in fact the Key of Solomon. The authorship of the Grimorium Verum is unknown, but it is believed to have been written by a Jewish scholar named Isaac the Hollander in the 17th century.

## Contents

The book is divided into three parts: the first containing the constraints and liberations to get the demons to obey the conjurations, the second containing a description of the supernatural creatures, and the third containing a list of spells.

The first part, called "The Sanctuary of the Fairies", describes the names of the demons and the method of summoning them. The names of the demons are: 

* Ba'al (also called Beelzebub)
* Agarat (also called Buér)
* Marbas (also called Barbas)
* Aciel (also called Botis)
* Marsiello (also called Maro)
