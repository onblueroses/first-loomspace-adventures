## In popular culture

The book is mentioned in the 1922 story "The Hound" by H. P. Lovecraft, in which the protagonist and a friend attempt to rob the grave of a wealthy individual recently buried. Inside the vault, they find a jade amulet on the body. Later, they discover that the amulet is mentioned in the Necronomicon, and that it holds a curse. The amulet is also mentioned in Lovecraft's "The Lurker at the Threshold". The book is also mentioned in the 1967 short story "The Return of the Sorcerer" by Clark Ashton Smith.

The 1975 film The Dead Don't Die shows a copy of the book on a shelf in a library.

The 1981 movie The Evil Dead features a fictional Sumerian version of the Book of the Dead, known as the Necronomicon Ex-Mortis.
