 "In the name of the blessed and holy Trinity, I conjure you, spirit N., that you personally appear before this circle with a human face, and a body that can be felt, to answer in an audible tone of voice each and every one of my questions."

It continues with more conjurations and threats, and ends with the promise that the demon will be treated well if he obeys:

"I conjure you, spirit N., by all the names above mentioned, to appear before me as I have said, in a human face and a body that can be felt, and that you speak to me in my mother tongue; or, if you cannot do that, that you understand me at least; and that you do not leave without my permission, or until you have done what I have asked of you, in the name of the Lord, Bathat or Rachat flowing from the spring and the source, which is called by the names of the Lord. And if you do not appear promptly, I shall afflict you for the duration of your life. I shall assail you with the evil, grievous and eternal torments of Hell, from which you shall never escape. Come, then, N., in the name of the Lord, to do my will.
