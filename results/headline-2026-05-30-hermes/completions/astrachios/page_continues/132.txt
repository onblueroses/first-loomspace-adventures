The Third Book is titled "The Pauline Art" and contains a set of sigils attributed to the planetary hours, with several paragraphs of text describing their use. It is of note that this book is an entirely different grimoire of The Pauline Art which is included in the Lesser Key of Solomon.

The Fourth Book is titled "The Almadel" and contains a collection of prayers and sigils which may be used to summon the angels of the four "Altitudes." These angels are described as being divided into four groups: the angels of the East, the West, the North, and the South. The book contains detailed instructions on creating a wax tablet, known as the Almadel, on which the sigils must be drawn.
