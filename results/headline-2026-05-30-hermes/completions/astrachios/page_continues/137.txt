 "This is the work of Clavis Inferni (the Key of Hell) made by Solomon, the King, and the sixty-two princes, and it is the great and faithful seal of the mighty spirits, and the effect of this seal of God is that it forces the spirits to obey and compels them to come when they are called."

The book then goes on to describe the seven great spirits (the seven Stewards of Hell) and their powers, as well as the times at which they can be summoned by the necromancer. Each of these spirits rules a specific area of Hell, and the necromancer can command these spirits to do his bidding, as long as he has the power of the Clavis Inferni.

The seven spirits are:

1. Astaroth, a grand duke of Hell, who can make men invisible and can lead them to hidden treasures.
2. Asmodeus, a king of Hell, who can give the power of prophecy and can teach the liberal arts.
3. Beleth, a king of Hell, who can cause love between men and women.
4. Belial, a mighty king of Hell, who can give excellent familiars and can grant honor and popularity.
