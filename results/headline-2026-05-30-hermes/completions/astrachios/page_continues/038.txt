 "By these names, and in the name of the God of gods, and the Creator, who hath made all things, and who destroyeth all things, I conjure and command thee, O spirit, that thou shalt give me true answers unto all my demands and requests, without any hesitation or mentacious evasion whatsoever, and that thou shalt obey me in the performance of any and all such commands as I may lay upon thee, and that thou shalt do all my bidding with loyalty and friendship, and without any falsehood or deception whatsoever, for by these names thou art bound to yield perfect obedience unto me, and I command thee to appear before me now, in a fair and comely human form, without any delay, and I furthermore command that thou shalt harm or frighten neither me nor any of my companions, nor any other creature or thing, in any way whatsoever, neither by thy person nor by thy power. So mote it be."

The page is signed by "Morton."

The conjuration is followed by a Latin incantation, also signed by Morton.
