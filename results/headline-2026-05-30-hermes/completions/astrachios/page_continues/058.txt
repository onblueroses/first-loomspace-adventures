 "To be said four times, and then as many times as you wish." Next comes the "Conjuration," which is the actual invocation:

I conjure you, O Spirit N., by the living God, the true God, by the holy and all-ruling God, who created all things from nothing, the heavens, the earth, the sea, and everything that is in them, by the high and wonderful names of God, Alpha and Omega, and by all the other names by which you may be conjured, that you may come quickly to me and do my will, by the name of Samahabatet, which is inscribed on the front of Aaron's rod, and by the name of Eloh, which Aaron wore above his breast, and by the names of the angels and their powers that I have conjured, that you may come quickly and without delay and do my will, by the name of Aiatos, which Solomon placed above the door of the Temple, by the divine names Cona, Cona, Baracata, by the name of Adonai, by the seal of Solomon, which he had made to serve him, by the name of Genoni, by the rod with which Moses divided the Red Sea
