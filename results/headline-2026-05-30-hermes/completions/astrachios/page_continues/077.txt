 "I conjure you, O Spirits, by all the aforesaid names, and by the Creator's name Yaweh, who, by his wonders, has created Heaven and Earth, and all that is contained therein, by these holy names and by all the others which are written in this book, to appear promptly and without delay, or to send to me N..., who shall surely and truthfully give me an answer concerning everything I shall ask him, without injury to my body or soul, and without any deceit or falsehood, by the power of God Almighty, who lives and reigns for ever and ever. Amen."

It is the usual method to repeat the words of the conjuration till the spirit appears, and the person who performs the ceremony is careful to remain within the circle. If the spirit summoned appears with a bad or displeased countenance, it is a sign that he has been displeased with the conjuration, and the person should immediately cease the invocation. It is also a bad omen if the spirit appears with a yellow or ghastly colour, or if he appears in some terrible or frightful shape, for these are sure signs of deception or treachery on the part of the spirit.
