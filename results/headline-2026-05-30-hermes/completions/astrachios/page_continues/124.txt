# The Black Hen's Chick

The Black Pullet, also known as the Black Hen, is the name of a grimoire that purports to teach the "science of magical talismans and rings", including the art of necromancy and Kabbalah. It is believed to have been written in the 18th century by an anonymous French officer who served in Napoleon's army. The text takes the form of a narrative or dialogue, in which the author discloses his "secret" to an unnamed questioner, apparently in exchange for money.

According to the narrative, the author was in Egypt, when he met an old wise man who showed him the secret to creating great wealth. The author claimed that after studying the art of the ancient Egyptians for 30 years, he met an old man in Cairo named "Signor Abitubolo", who taught him the art of creating talismans, and that this knowledge should only be used for good. The author then embarked on a series of travels throughout Europe, using his knowledge to create great wealth and perform miraculous feats, such as turning a black hen into a golden horse.
