To close the ritual, the magician must say: "Go now in peace to your dwelling places and let there be harmony between us, for the Angel of God, by his grace, watches over our works."

## In popular culture

In the 1975 horror film, Race with the Devil, the cultists are seen performing the ritual.

The black metal band Rotting Christ have a song called "The Call of the Aethyrs" in their 1996 album, Triarchy of the Lost Lovers, which is believed to be about the ritual.

In the 1997 horror film, The Craft, the character Nancy Downs finds the book in an occult shop and buys it. Later in the film, she and her coven use the ritual to summon the spirit Manon.

In the 1999 horror film, The Ninth Gate, a book called De Umbrarum Regni Novem Portis is mentioned, which is believed to be based on The Grand Grimoire.

The 2004 horror film, The Grimoire, is based on the book.
