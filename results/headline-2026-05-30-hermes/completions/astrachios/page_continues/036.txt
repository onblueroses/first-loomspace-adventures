It is clear that the circles of the grimoires and of the tradition they represent are based on the old idea of the magic circle, which is the same as the circle of protection used by sorcerers and magicians in their rites. The circle in question is a double one, and is usually traced on the ground with the knife or sword of the operator, and is intended to preserve the operator from the malice of the evil spirits. The modern magician might well substitute for it an astral circle of light, imagined as being drawn round him in a similar manner, and equally efficacious as a protection. In any case, the circle is an important adjunct of ceremonial magic, and is probably derived from the use of the circle in the ancient mysteries.
