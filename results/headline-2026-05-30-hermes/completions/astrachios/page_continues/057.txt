The invocation itself begins with the following formula:

“I conjure you, O Spirit, by the living God, by the true God, by the holy and all-ruling God, who created from nothingness the heavens, the earth, the sea, and all things that are therein, by the God who spoke and all was made, by the Lord who is the beginning and the end, by the alpha and omega, the first and the last, by the God of the armies of Israel, by the God who is mighty and powerful, by the most high God, who is the Truth and the Life.”

There follows a series of conjurations, invocations, and exorcisms, which the magician must recite in order to summon the spirits, and compel them to do his will. These conjurations are typically addressed to specific spirits, and are often accompanied by the use of ritual tools and symbols, such as the pentacle and the sword.
