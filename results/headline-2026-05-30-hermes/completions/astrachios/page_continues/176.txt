 "By these names, and in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, I conjure you, O all ye Spirits, and I command you to appear, and to fulfill my commands. Fiat, Fiat, Fiat. Amen." Next follows an image of a black hen and a pentagram, which the reader is instructed to wear around their neck. The text continues with an incantation to summon the spirit Bechard, who is said to be able to provide the reader with wealth and power.

The Red Dragon is said to be based on the Grand Grimoire. The differences are that the Red Dragon introduces the black hen and the pentagram, which are not found in the Grand Grimoire.
