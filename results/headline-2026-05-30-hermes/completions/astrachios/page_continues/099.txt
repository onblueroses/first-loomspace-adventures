 “I conjure you by the great living God, the Sovereign Lord of all things, to appear before this circle, to obey my orders, and to accomplish them without deception.”

Once the spirits have appeared, the magician must say:

“Peace be between us, and may you faithfully serve me according to the law that our Sovereign Lord has given to you, and do not dare to leave without my permission.”

When the spirits are dismissed, the magician says:

“Go in peace to your dwelling places, and may peace continue between us, and be always ready to come when you are called.”

The text then provides the following instructions for summoning the spirits:

The person who wishes to perform this operation must first abstain from all impurities for the space of seven days, and must be well washed and clean, and must have clean clothes. On the day of the operation, he must fast, and not eat anything except after the appearance of the spirits, and he must make a good perfume in the place where he will perform the operation. He must have a large knife or sword, with which he will make the circle in which he will be, and must have a square table, on which he will put the cloth, the Pentacle, and the perfume.
