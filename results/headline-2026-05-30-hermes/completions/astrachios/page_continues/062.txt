### Ars Notoria

The Ars Notoria is a medieval grimoire of the Solomonic cycle, most probably written in the thirteenth century, but certainly before the fourteenth. Its authorship is attributed to the magician Apollonius of Tyana. The text was likely brought to public attention by the thirteenth-century Spanish bishop, Michael Scot, and it was later published with changes by the German abbot Johannes Trithemius in the 1500s.

The Ars Notoria is divided into two parts: the first theoretical, the second practical. The theoretical part begins with a narrative in which the author presents himself as Apollonius of Tyana and claims to have discovered the secret of the Ars Notoria in an underground cavern, where King Solomon had hidden it to prevent it from being misused. The text continues with a description of the powers that can be acquired through the Ars Notoria, such as the ability to acquire perfect memory, eloquence, and knowledge of all the arts and sciences.
