 “These are the words which open the gates of the four winds, and they are the keys to the kingdom of the Shepharim, and they rule over the four winds. When you wish to call upon the winds, you must use these words.”

The author of the grimoire, or rather, the transcriber, for it is highly unlikely that the work is original, seems to have been obsessed with the number four, for we find many references to the division of all things into four categories. The transcriber states that there are four supreme spirits, and these rule over the four winds. The names of these spirits are:

1. URIENS, the King of the East.
2. AMAIMON, the King of the South.
3. PAIMON, the King of the West.
4. EGYN, the King of the North.

These are more or less the same names given in the Lemegeton, or Lesser Key of Solomon, for the four cardinal spirits, and they also appear in Agrippa’s Occult Philosophy, although, according to the last named authority, only three of these spirits are good, and one is evil.
