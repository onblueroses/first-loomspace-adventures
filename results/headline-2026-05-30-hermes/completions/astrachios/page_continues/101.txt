The _Grimorium Verum_ is a more complex work, containing many more spells and sigils, but the first section is quite similar to the _Grand Grimoire_. The introduction to the work claims that Solomon was the original author, but that his manuscript was later lost and the work was rewritten by "Alibeck the Egyptian." It then goes on to give the reader advice on how to deal with the spirits, claiming that they are not inherently evil, but that they are compelled to behave as they do by divine order. This section ends with a passage praising God and claiming that those who are "reasonable and God-fearing" have nothing to fear from the spirits. The next section is titled "The Kabbalah," and it gives the names of the spirits as well as a list of spells and sigils.
