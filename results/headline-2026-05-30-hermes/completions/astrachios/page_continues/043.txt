 "I conjure you, O Spirits, by all the said names of God, the Living and True God, and by the names of these spirits, and by all their qualities, that you come quickly to me, without delay, without noise, without deformity, either visible or invisible, to answer to all that I shall command you, or to do all that I shall charge you with, under your obedience, without any deceit whatsoever."

A later passage adds, "I conjure you anew, O Spirits, by the power of all the names of God, and by the strength of the other names... that you come promptly and without delay to do my will and all that I shall command you, without any evil intent, without dizziness, without disturbance, without noise, without murmur, without delay... to execute it for me, and each of you to help me in all my wishes, desires, and things that I shall require and ask of you, in all affairs and business."

The manuscript continues with various methods of calling forth spirits, including the construction of magical circles and pentacles, and the use of magical words and symbols. It also includes instructions for summoning specific spirits, such as Becharas, Maymon, Barzabel, Astar
