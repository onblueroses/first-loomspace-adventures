 "By these names, and in the name of the God of gods, I conjure thee, O spirit Cadaveris, and I say unto thee, as unto my servant, appear and obey. Appear in comely human form, and if thou dost not thus appear, I will cast thee into the terrible and eternal fire, into the lake of fire and brimstone, unless thou dost forthwith appear, in the name of Karacata the Great, Adonai, Elohim, Ariel, and Jehovam. Amen."
