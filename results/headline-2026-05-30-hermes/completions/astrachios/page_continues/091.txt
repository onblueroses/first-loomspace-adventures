 "These are the names which must be pronounced in order to open the way to the place where the treasure is hidden, or the person who wishes to approach the treasure must carry on him the writing of these names. He must also carry with him the Great Pentacle of Solomon, which must be drawn on virgin parchment; for otherwise he will not be able to take out the treasure."

This is followed by a magical pentacle, which is said to have been copied from the Clavicula Salomonis. The pentacle is made up of concentric circles, with the names of various demons and spirits inscribed between the circles. The text concludes with a warning that the pentacle must be drawn on virgin parchment, or else the ritual will not work.
