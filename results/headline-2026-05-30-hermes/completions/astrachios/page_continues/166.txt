 "By these names, and in the name of the great and powerful God, I conjure you spirits of the earth, fire, air, and water, and all the spirits wandering about the world, wherever you may be, and I command you to come here at once without delay, without injury to me or any other creature, to answer my questions, to do and perform all that I may command or desire, failing which you will be condemned to the eternal fire prepared for those who disobey the commands of the Creator. Fiat, Fiat, Fiat. Amen."

This page is followed by a collection of spells, with their respective rituals, involving the preparation of various potions and ointments.
