## Manuscripts

The Munich Manual of Demonic Magic, Clm 849, is one of a number of manuscripts produced in the 15th century that list a series of magical operations. These operations involve the invocation of demons, and the preparation of love philtres, the divining of the future, or the conjuring of illusions. The Munich manual is the only one that is alert to the dangers of its own contents, offering a warning to its readers.

## In popular culture

The Munich Manual of Demonic Magic is the primary source for the magic used in the 2018 novel The Midnight Front, by Michael Scott.
