The second page of the manuscript, which is shown here, begins with the heading: "Here beginneth the conjuration of the spirits of the air." Then follows a series of prayers and invocations, including the following: "I conjure and command you, O spirits of the air, by the virtue of the Holy Name of God, by the virtue of the Most High Creator of all things, and by the virtue of the Name Adonai, which Moses heard and declared, and by the virtue of the Name El, which is the Name of God the Mighty and Strong, that you come quickly and without delay to fulfill my commands and to obey me in all things."
