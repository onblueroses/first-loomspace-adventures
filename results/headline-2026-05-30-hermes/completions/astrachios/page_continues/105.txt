The four superior spirits are the "chiefs" of the hierarchy of spirits that the grimoire purports to enable one to summon. They are: Sytry (a.k.a. Bitru, Satan), Pucel (a.k.a. Astaroth), Bechard (a.k.a. Bechaud), and Guland (a.k.a. Gulaand). The grimoire describes each of their qualities and powers, and attributes certain times of the day and year to their control.

### Summoning spirits

The grimoire details the extensive process that one must undertake to summon these spirits, which involves multiple purification rituals, the creation of a ritual circle, and the use of incantations and sacrificial offerings. The text emphasizes the importance of the summoner's purity and commitment, warning that the spirits will only appear if the instructions are followed to the letter. The book also gives instructions on how to make a pact with the spirits, which includes a script to be recited by the summoner and the spirit.
