 "This is the name by which Solomon bound demons and their legions in the brass vessel."

## The manuscript

The manuscript is now held in the collection of the Wellcome Library in London, as Wellcome MS 2000. It is written in French, and dates to the late 18th or early 19th century.

## In popular culture

The Grand Grimoire features as a plot element in the 1971 Gérard Oury film La Folie des grandeurs, in which the character Blaze (Yves Montand) is prosecuted for possession of the book, which is confiscated and held in the Bastille.
