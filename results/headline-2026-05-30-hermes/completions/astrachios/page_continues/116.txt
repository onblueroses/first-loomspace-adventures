 “And when you have said these names, you will see appear a multitude of spirits of diverse forms, and you will hear a great noise like that of armed people. But do not fear anything, and tell them to go to the place that you will have prepared for them, and they will obey you. And if there is among them a spirit named Guriel, tell him that he is the one whom you wish to have appear before you, because he is a spirit who knows many things and who can serve you not only in this operation, but also in many other things, and he will come to you in the form of a young man. And if you wish to have him serve you, you will command him to come to you every night in your dreams, and he will do this. And after the spirits have left, you will say the following orison:
