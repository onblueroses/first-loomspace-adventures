## In popular culture

The grimoire is mentioned in the 2009 video game Grand Theft Auto: The Ballad of Gay Tony. A character named Yusuf Amir, a luxury real estate developer, mentions that he is in possession of the grimoire and that he has used it to gain his wealth and success.
