 "By these names, and in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, I conjure you, spirits, through him who shall come to judge the quick and the dead, and the world by fire. Come ye, then, in a moment; come, spirits, in a moment: I command you in the ineffable names of Tetragrammaton, Adonai, Agla, Magne, Och, Emmanuel, which being written in this circle, all spirits do fear and obey. Come, then, all at once, without noise, without deformity, without tumult. Come, come, come, in the names of Astaroth and of Rofocale. Come, Astaroth, Rofocale, and Maduel. Come, and do not tarry. Come, in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, and of the glorious Virgin Mary, and of the Four Evangelists. Come, in the names of the Holy Angels. Come, Archangels, and do not tarry."
