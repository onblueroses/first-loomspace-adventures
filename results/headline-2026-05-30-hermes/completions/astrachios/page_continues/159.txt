 "The circle of evocation is formed from a rope made from the hairs of a mare in heat. Candles are placed to the north and south. The evocator must stand within the circle before beginning the invocation."

The evocation continues with a long prayer, followed by more conjurations and ending with five psalms (3, 7, 9, 11, 13) and other prayers.

This is followed by a description of the appearance of the spirits, which is similar to that given in the Livre des Esperitz. This is followed by a series of notes detailing the specific spirits which will accomplish some of the tasks that the Livre des Esperitz assigns to various groups of spirits.

The manuscript also contains a version of the Ars Notoria, a text on astronomy, and a brief version of the Key of Solomon.
