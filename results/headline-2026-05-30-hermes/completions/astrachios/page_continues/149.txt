The manuscript continues with instructions for the creation of a talisman. The magician is told to take a piece of lodestone, and place it inside a gold setting inscribed with the image of a man. The setting should be attached to a gold chain. The talisman should then be placed in a small wooden box and buried under the threshold of a house for three days. Once this is done, the magician is instructed to retrieve the talisman and hold it in his left hand, reciting the following words:
