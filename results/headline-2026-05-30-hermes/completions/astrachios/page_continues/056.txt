 "When you have repeated these words three times, you will see the spirits appear in a scarlet surcoat, a yellow robe, and a green tabard. They will want to chat, but you must not reply. Instead, command them to go to the place you have prepared for them. There, they will ask you again what you wish, and you shall respond to them as is your will, and they will obey you. You must not give them any power unless they have done your bidding, and after that they will do anything you ask. And if you wish to give them power over something, you must say the following words: 'Araton, Malat, Gibrat, Acham, Baton, Alon, Salmatis, Chrostos, Adjutor, Agla, Taga, Mata, Oriel, Theos, Campus, Megal, Agiel, Sator, who are my angels, I conjure you to force the spirits to obey me, and to grant them the power that I request.'"

The text continues with a number of Latin invocations, which the author claims will compel the spirits to do one's bidding. After this, the author states that he will now list the names and offices of the demons
