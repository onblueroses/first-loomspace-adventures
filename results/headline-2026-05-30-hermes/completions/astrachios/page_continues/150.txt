The page continues with instructions for using the talisman to command the spirits and make them do one's bidding.

The book also contains what it claims are the three "chief" names of God, as well as sigils / seals that it claims can be used to perform various acts of magic. The "chief" names of God, it says, are "Agla, On, Tetragrammaton", and the sigils / seals are various symbols that are said to represent these names.
