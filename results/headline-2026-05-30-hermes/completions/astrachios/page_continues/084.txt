 “The prayer that follows must be recited to have the spirit appear in a peaceful and submissive manner. You must not omit it, because it is a very important prayer.” The prayer in question is the “Veni, Creator Spiritus,” attributed to the ninth-century German monk and scholar Rabanus Maurus. ^{23} The hymn invokes the Holy Spirit, the third person of the Christian Trinity, as the “Creator Spirit” and “source of life.” It asks for divine guidance and support and for protection from harm.

The text then instructs the reader to make a small incision on the ring finger of the left hand, using the same knife employed in the creation of the pentacle. The ritualist should allow three drops of blood to fall onto the burning coals, while reciting the following words:

I offer this blood as a sign of my will and wish to obtain treasure and the power to discover it, and I request the help of all the spirits who can contribute to my success, notably Azeruel, Dobiath, Rabiathos, Nephthaliel, and the spirits of the four elements, and all those who work under the orders of Adonai, so that they may help me to achieve my goal,
