 "When you have repeated these words with faith and taking care to pronounce them correctly, you will see appear before you a spirit which you will order to place himself in a new glass bottle, saying to him: Obey and go into this vessel, in the Name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. The spirit will obey you at once; then you will have it stoppered tightly with the bung of a boxwood root.

"The spirit will then tell you to whom to give the bottle, and that person, after having received it, will have the power to give orders to the spirit to obtain all that he desires. But if one loses the bottle, he will lose also the power of making the spirit obey him."

The second page of this manuscript is reserved for the evocation of the "Powerful Spirit of Jupiter," named "Paras."

The third page is most curious, for it is filled with magic squares, planetary seals and kabalistic figures of most interesting design.

The fourth page gives the "Composition of the Mysterious Ointment Proper for the Apparition of Spirits." This ointment is composed of the fat of a newly born babe, which is combined with various herbs and drugs.
