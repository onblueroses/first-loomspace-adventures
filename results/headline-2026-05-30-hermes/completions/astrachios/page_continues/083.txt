 "This is the name by which the demons tremble: Agaros, Sturil, Uthor, Aroth, Phergor... Come hither O powerful spirit! Appear before this circle, obey in all things my commands, and tell me truth!"

The manuscript also contains related spells and rituals.

## In popular culture

The manuscript is featured in the 2018 horror film The Clovehitch Killer. The film features a serial killer who uses the manuscript to guide his murders.
