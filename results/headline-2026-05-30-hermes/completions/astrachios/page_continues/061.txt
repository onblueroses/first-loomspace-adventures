 "These are the words by which Igerum, Eomia, and Sochiat have bound themselves, and by which they are summoned."

## Significance

The Necromancer's Handbooks are important for the study of the evolution of esotericism in the late Middle Ages and the early Renaissance. According to Robert Mathiesen, the two manuscripts "are potentially of enormous importance for the study of the complex issue of the relationship of magic, medicine, and science in the late Middle Ages and early modern period."
