 "By your powerful names, and with the words of the sovereign demons who are in hell, and with the permission of the divine majesty, I conjure you, LUCIFER, SATAN, BELZEBUTH, and BARABAS, who are the four parts of the world, that you will come here, or that you will send me the three spirits that I ask for, and they will answer truly all that I shall ask them, without injury to my soul and body, and without noise or fright, and they will satisfy me completely, and will give me a true answer to all that I shall ask them. And if they don't come, I will torment you with the sword of the divine power, and I will cast you into the sea, into the fire, into the sulfur, into the abyss, and you will not be able to rest until you have satisfied me."
