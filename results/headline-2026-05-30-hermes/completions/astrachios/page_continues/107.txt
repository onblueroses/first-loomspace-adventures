 "Now the spirits will come to thee in sleep, and will tell thee the truth of everything thou wishest to know. But thou must have a care that they do not anger thee, and so drive thee to a cold and ungenerous bearing."

The manuscript continues with instructions for casting spells, such as making oneself invisible, winning the love of a woman, and so on. The book ends with the following instructions:

"In order to succeed in all which thou undertakest to perform, thou must have a firm faith and a steadfast will, and thou must maintain a great secrecy, so that no one whomsoever may be able to go through the contents of thy work."
