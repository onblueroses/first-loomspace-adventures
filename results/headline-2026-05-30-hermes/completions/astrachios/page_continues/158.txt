 “By these names, and in the name of the Eternal God, let all the Spirits who have power over the treasures of the earth be summoned in the place where this copy of the figure shall be buried.” This is followed by the usual warning that the names and symbols must be reproduced exactly as they are given. In the final paragraph of the page, the text states that if the figure is accurately reproduced and buried in the earth, spirits will lead the person to the location of treasure.

The 1740 edition of the Grand grimoire includes an elaborated version of the figure, and also includes the full text of the 1709 edition. The figure in the 1740 edition is nearly identical to the figure which would later appear in the 1760 edition of the Petit Albert.
