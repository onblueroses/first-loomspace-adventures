 "Here are the names by which these spirits are to be called: Quirion, Taribol, Sabaoth, Atan, Ateremento, Bal, Gaba, Soub, Uer, Agla, Tetragrammaton, and the rest."

The Ars Theurgia Goetia, a work included in the Lesser Key of Solomon, includes a list of 72 demons that are paired with 72 angels said to counter their influence. The Ars Theurgia Goetia differs from the Pseudomonarchia Daemonum in that it includes a list of 31 aerial spirits that are not mentioned in the latter work.
