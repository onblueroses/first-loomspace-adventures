 "By these names, and in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, I conjure you, spirits of the book, to come and do my will, and to bring me the spirits that I require."

This is followed by the seals of the demons and spirits that are to be called, including those of Scirlin, Satanachia, and others.

## In popular culture

The book is used in the 2022 film The Devil's Hour.
