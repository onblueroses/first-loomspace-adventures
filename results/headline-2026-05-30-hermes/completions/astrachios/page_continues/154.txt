 "And when you have said these words, you will see wonderful things, which it is impossible to tell, and you will be in their power. You will see the spirits of the air and those of the fire, and you will learn the secrets of the deep waters and of the seas. You will know the virtue of the herbs and the plants, of the stones and all that is in the world. You will know the secrets of the art of healing, and you will have perfect knowledge of the mystical names of God, which you can use in all your works. You will also know the science of geometry, and you will be able to measure the extension of the heavens and of the earth. You will know all that has happened since the beginning of the world, and you will be able to foretell the future."
