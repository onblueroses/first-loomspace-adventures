 "By these names, and in the name of the genii to whom they are assigned, may the sun bless you and enlighten your eyes, giving you the sight of the things which are hidden from the eyes of all mortals. I command you, O Acham, Amassar, Astachar, Azachel, Aczabat, Apernat, Adon, in the name of the great God who is the God of all spirits, to come without delay, to appear before me in a beautiful human form, and to give me the ring of invisibility, by which I may become invisible at will. Come, therefore, at once, and fulfill my command."

The second page begins with the words "Conjuration of the ring." It instructs the reader to take a ring of gold or silver and place it in a clean and secret place, then perform a short ritual that includes reciting a prayer, lighting a candle, and making a sacrifice to the spirits. The prayer includes the names of several spirits and commands them to come and make the ring capable of rendering the wearer invisible. The ritual must be performed three times a day for nine days, after which the ring will be ready.
