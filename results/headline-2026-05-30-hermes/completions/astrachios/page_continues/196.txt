### The Black Hen's Egg

According to the Grand Grimoire, the Black Hen's Egg, also known as the "Ovum Nigrum Pulleti," is a powerful magical artifact. It is said to be a small, black stone that resembles a hen's egg, hence its name. The text claims that this object has the ability to grant the possessor the power to control demons and spirits, as well as to perform various magical feats.
