 "When you have repeated these words, you will see wonderful things, which it is impossible to relate, by anyone whomsoever."

This formula, while not exactly a traditional circle, is a sort of invocation to protect the magician while he or she is working. According to the text, this formula should be repeated before working with any of the other spells in the grimoire.
