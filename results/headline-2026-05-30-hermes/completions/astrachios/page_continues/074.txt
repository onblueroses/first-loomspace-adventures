 “Here are the names and figures of the demons of hell, which must be given to you. You must know that the hour to invoke them is from the tenth to the twelfth hour of the night, and that they must be called in a hidden place where no one can see you. If you do not do this, you risk losing your life. If you follow these instructions, they will appear in their human form without bringing any harm to you.”

The 47 demons are listed in a chart, in which a sigil and a set of powers are attributed to each. The powers range from general knowledge and wisdom to very specific abilities, such as the ability to cause floods or earthquakes, or to travel great distances in a short time. Many of the demons are also associated with a particular cardinal direction.

The Ars Theurgia Goetia is the second section of the work known as the Lemegeton, or The Lesser Key of Solomon. It is a collection of 31 demons, each with their own unique powers and abilities. The demons are said to be able to grant various powers and abilities to the conjurer, such as the ability to fly, to become invisible, or to control the elements.
