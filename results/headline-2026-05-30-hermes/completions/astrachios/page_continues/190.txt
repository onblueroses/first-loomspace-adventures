### Consecration of the Pentacle

The consecration of the pentacle is given on page 7 of the manuscript. The text reads:

The consecration of the pentacle: Take a new earthenware bowl which has never been used, and write on it these characters with the blood of a black hen, and put in the bowl some magnetic stone and calamite stone, and some drops of your blood, and three of your hairs. Put it on the fire until the stones are reduced to powder, and when it is cold, grind it again, and when it is ground, put it in a clean cloth and express the juice into another clean vessel, and you will have the composition for the pentacle.

The text continues with the method of consecrating the pentacle on an eve of a feast day.
