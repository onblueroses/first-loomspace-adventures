 "I command you, O all ye spirits, who have the power to move the winds, by the virtue of the Great Adonai, and by the virtue of God's Name, _Yah_, which Moses invoked and succeeded in dividing the waters, that you come quickly, when I call you."

To raise a storm, the sorcerer would then call upon the spirits of the four winds, saying: "I call upon you, Spirits of the Air, in the names of Acar, Zabaoth, and Michrion, that you come quickly and accomplish my will."

When the spirits appear, the sorcerer must show them the pentacle and command them to do his bidding. If they refuse, he must repeat the conjuration until they obey. Once the spirits have obeyed his command, the sorcerer should license them to depart, saying: "I release you, Spirits of the Air, and command you to return to your own realm, harming none as you go. In the names of Acar, Zabaoth, and Michrion, go in peace."
