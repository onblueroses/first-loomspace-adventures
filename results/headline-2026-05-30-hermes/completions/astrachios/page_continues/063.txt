 "I conjure you by the great living God, the Sovereign Lord of all things, to send unto me in a moment, your Messenger the great Anael, in a human and pleasing form. I conjure you again, by the power of the names which I have just named, to send him at once, so that he may do my will in everything that I shall command him, without injury to any one, so that I may have him always at my side.

"Anael, Anael, Anael, I conjure you by the power of the great God, the Sovereign Lord of all superior and inferior beings, and by the power of your father, and by the power of your mother, and by the power of all your companions, and by the power of Samchia, Metatron and Scirlin, and by the divine names which have power over you, which are Adonai, El, Agla, Elohi, Eloi, Tetragrammaton, and by all the holy names of God, and by the names of the patriarchs which are written in the book of the Blessed Jeriel, which is that of the Angel of the Great God, who is the Lord of all.
