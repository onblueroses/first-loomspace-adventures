 "By these names, and in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, I conjure you, O all ye Spirits, and I command you to appear before this Circle in fair and human shape, without noise, deformity, or darkness. Come, therefore, ye Spirits, N., in the Name of Adonai, Zebaoth, Adonai, Amioram. Come, come, come, for I command you to appear without delay."

In the same way as the Circle, the Triangle of Art is also to be inscribed on parchment, which is then to be placed outside the Circle, on its southern side, that the spirits may be compelled to appear within it. The form of the Triangle is as follows:

[triangle]

The Sacred Names to be inscribed within it are these: Tetragrammaton, Anaphaxeton, and Primeumaton.

The reason why the spirits should be made to appear in the Triangle is said to be, that they are not able to tell that which is true when they are outside of it, whereas when they are appearing in it they can speak nothing but the truth.
