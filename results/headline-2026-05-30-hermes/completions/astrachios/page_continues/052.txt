 "I conjure you, O Book, to be useful and profitable to all who may have recourse to you for the success of their affairs. I conjure you anew, by the virtue of the blood of Jesus Christ contained daily in the chalice, to be serviceable to all those who shall read you. I exorcise you in the name of the Most Holy Trinity, in the name of the Most Holy Trinity, in the name of the Most Holy Trinity!" 

The first page of the Gospel of St. John is written in blood. The book also contains two spells, one intended to immobilize an enemy, and the other to gain the love of a woman.

The book is written in French, with some passages in Latin.

## In popular culture

* In the 1999 film The Ninth Gate, starring Johnny Depp, the book is portrayed as one of the three that contain the secret to summoning the Devil.
* In the 2009 novel The Devil's Hand, by Douglas Nicholas, a medieval sorcerer attempts to use the Grand Grimoire to summon the Devil.
* In the 2012 novel The Secret of Copernicus by John and Daria Barry, Copernicus is portrayed as having used the Grand Grimoire
