 “If you have come this far and done all that I have taught you, you will see before you a vast land, which will be all of gold, and at the entrance to it you will see a gold table and a gold chair and a great crowd of spirits in gold clothing. Do not be afraid, but take the vessel of incense and the talisman of the Sun in your hand, sit down in the chair, and say:

O mighty King of the West, I conjure and command you by the power of God the Father, by the wisdom of the Son, and by the grace of the Holy Spirit, and by the victories of the Virgin Mary and by all that is holy and sacred; I conjure and command you by your name of BACIAR, by your name of BACIAR, by your name of BACIAR, that you have pity on me and send me the Golden Spirits of the West, that they may come to me and submit to me and obey me, and that they may teach me and reveal to me all that I shall ask of them, by the great power of this talisman and the virtue of the spirits and the smell of this incense. Come, then, O Spirits of the West
