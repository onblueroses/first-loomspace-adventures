 “When you have said these names, the spirit will appear in a beautiful form. Then you must say these words: ‘Come, O Spirit N, and answer truthfully my questions, and as I desire so may you bring it to pass.’ And then you may ask whatever you will, and it will be answered or brought to pass. When you have what you want, say these words: ‘Go in peace, O Spirit N., and every time I call you, come to me quickly.’ And it will do so.

“Note: This conjuration calls the chief spirits, and the lesser ones cannot come unless their lords are invoked. You must call the spirit by the name that is in the second circle, above the pentagram, and you must call the king and his ministers when you call the spirit. If you don’t do this, you will not succeed.”

The second circle mentioned in the instructions is not shown in the diagram, but it is evidently meant to be a space surrounding the pentagram, in which the name of the spirit being invoked is to be written. The “king and his ministers” are apparently the spirits of the planets, who are also to be invoked in order to ensure that the lesser spirits obey. The names of the planetary spirits
