 "I command you by the power of the names above, that when the circle is completed, you appear before me in a fair and human shape, without danger or deceit, to do my will."

The magician then speaks the names of the demons he wishes to summon, and commands them to appear before him.

## In popular culture

The manuscript is the basis for the 1994 horror film Necronomicon, which purports to dramatize three tales from the book.
