### The Veritable Clavicles of Solomon

The Veritable Clavicles of Solomon is the fourth text of the 18th century French manuscript collection MS 2423. It is titled Recueil sur les sciences occultes; vol. 1-7, Recueil sur les sciences occultes (Treatise on the occult sciences, vol. 1-7). The collection is in the Bibliothèque nationale de France (BNF) in Paris.

The Veritable Clavicles of Solomon contains seven books. Book 1 is titled: Livre I. Le Secret des Secrets, ou la Clavicule de Salomon, roi des Hébreux which translates to "Book 1. The Secret of Secrets, or the Clavicle of Solomon, king of the Hebrews".
