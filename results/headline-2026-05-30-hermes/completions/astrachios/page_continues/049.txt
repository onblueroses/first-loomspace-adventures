 'By these powerful and efficacious names, I conjure thee, O powerful spirits, to appear and show thyself before me, here and now, without hesitation or delay, in a fair and human form, and to make answer to my demands. Come, therefore, at the instance of these names, by the omnipotence of the Father, Son and Holy Spirit, by the virtues of the heavens, by the virtue of the air, of the water, of the earth, and of the fire, by the virtue of the words of truth, and by all things that are and shall be, between Heaven and Earth. Come, arise, manifest thyself, and answer my demands truly and faithfully, as thou shalt answer before the great and terrible judgment of the last day. Come, come, come, come.'

CHAPTER IX.

The Ceremony of Pactisation.

The Sabbath, midnight.--The sound of the clock is heard slowly striking twelve. It is the hour of ghosts and of the dead. The wind blows with violence, the rain falls in torrents, the storm howls without; the church bell tolls amid the rocks, for the passing soul.
