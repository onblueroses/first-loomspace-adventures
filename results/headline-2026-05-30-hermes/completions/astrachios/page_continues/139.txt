 'When you have repeated these names, you will see wonderful things, and they will be at your command.'

I looked up at the old man, who was nodding his head in a knowing way.

'You have only to speak these words, and the treasure will be yours,' he said.

I laughed aloud, and he looked at me oddly.

'You do not believe?' he said. 'You think it is foolish? I tell you, monsieur, it is true. Not a hundred metres from where we stand, a man I knew once found a treasure, and it made him rich. He spoke the words, and the gold was there. It is true, I tell you.'

I shook my head. 'I am sure you believe, monsieur, but I fear I am not a believer. If there is treasure hereabouts, I think it will take more than a few words from a book to find it.'

'Ah, monsieur,' he said, 'you are young and strong, but there are things you do not understand.'
