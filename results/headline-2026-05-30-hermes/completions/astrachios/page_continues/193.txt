### The Oupnek'hat

Main article: OupnekhatThe Oupnek'hat is a Persian version of the Upanishads, which was translated into Persian in the late 16th century. It was later translated into Latin in 1801-2 by the French orientalist Abraham Hyacinthe Anquetil-Duperron. This translation was praised by the German philosopher Arthur Schopenhauer, who described it as "the production of the highest human wisdom." The Oupnek'hat is considered a significant work of Eastern philosophy and has influenced Western thinkers.
