## The Grimoire of Pope Honorius

The Grimoire of Pope Honorius, or Le Grimoire du Pape Honorius, is a 17th to 18th century grimoire attributed to Pope Honorius III (1150 – 1227). It is unique among grimoires in that it is intended to be used by a priest and there are many instructions that presume the user lives a celibate religious life. Its full title is The Grimoire of Pope Honorius III, with a collection of the greatest secrets of the magical art, and the most approved keys of Solomon the Hebrew Rabbi. The first edition was likely published in the last quarter of the 17th century, though the first known advertisement for the book dates to 1769. The latest edition was published in 1919 by Éditions traditionnelles.
