 "When you have said these names, you will see appear before you a multitude of spirits of diverse forms, but do not be alarmed. Holding the figure of the lamb in your hand, say the following words: 'I conjure you, O spirits, by the virtue and power of the blood of the lamb, which you behold, to concede to all that I shall command you and all that I shall require of you, without illusion or falsehood.' They will obey you at once. Command them then to depart, and they will leave."

## Literary references

In the novel The Club Dumas by Arturo Pérez-Reverte, a character named Varo Borja attempts to use the book to summon the Devil. However, the character is actually using a forgery of the book, and the ritual goes disastrously wrong.
