 

By the powers of the holy names of God, we conjure you, spirits inscribed herein, to appear promptly, or we shall constrain you by the power of the great King Solomon, and by the efficacy of our powerful exorcisms.

The magic words of this powerful conjuration are the names of the spirits inscribed in the four concentric circles of the figure. The names are to be pronounced in a loud voice, and with great confidence and respect. The circles must be drawn upon a smooth surface, with five crosses between the names of the spirits. The conjuration must be performed at night, between the tenth and the eleventh hours, and the conjurer must be alone, clean, and fasting. 

The conjuration must be pronounced three times, with the right hand raised towards the heavens, and the left extended towards the earth. Then the spirits will appear visibly, ready to do all that the conjurer may command them. They will bring gold, silver, and precious stones, and will reveal the places where treasures are hidden. They will answer any question concerning the past, the present, or the future, and will reveal the secret thoughts of any person. They will also give the conjurer the power to become invisible, to fly through the air
