 'By these words and in this name, and by the other names of angels that I shall name, I shall have the power to bind and unbind, to speak and to command you, O spirits, who are named: Agarat, Agaros, Abalidoth, Flamoes, and Horeb, for you are bound to the angel Araziel who was bound in chains by the archangel Michael, and sent into the abyss. And I conjure you, O spirit Agarat, and your companions, by the angel Hocroel, Absac, Bedrimulael, and by the name of Cadato and Era, Elohi, Ebra, Elohim Gibor, and by the great name of Tetragrammaton, which is Ioth, Abraoth, and by all the holies of heaven. I conjure you, O spirits of hell, that are beneath the earth, and also you, O terrestrial and infernal spirits, by the terrible and unknown names of the Creator, which are Demech, Sechcebolis, and by the powers of the angels of darkness, that you may not enter into this circle, and you shall obey me in all things wherefore I shall command you
