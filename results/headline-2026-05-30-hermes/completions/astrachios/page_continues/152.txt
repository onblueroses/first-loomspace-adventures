 "Now, if you have properly understood how to carry out the instructions that I have given you, and have observed all the necessary precautions, you will behold a terrible figure, which will appear to you in a most alarming manner; but do not be alarmed, for it is only the arrival of the spirit."

The manuscript continues with instructions for making a magic ring to control the spirit, and further instructions for purifying oneself and performing the ritual. At the end of the ritual, the spirit is given a license to depart.

## In popular culture

The manuscript was used as a prop in the 1973 film The Exorcist. It is also featured in the 2016 film The Conjuring 2. The text was used by the Warrens in their exorcism of the Hodgson family in the film.
