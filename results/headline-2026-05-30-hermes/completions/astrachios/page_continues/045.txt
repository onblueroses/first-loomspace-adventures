 "By the potentates of all the stars, and by the will of the sovereign Creator, who has made all things, I conjure thee to do my will, and to give me a sign of submission."

The first half of the second page is dedicated to the preparation of a magical parchment, the outline of which is given in the figure below:

[Picture: Parchment outline]

The instructions continue: "Take a black cock which has never lusted after its hen, and which has spent the night in solitude. Take it in your right hand, and with your left cut off its head while pronouncing the name of the spirit you wish to compel. Then take the parchment, which must be pure and clean, and form thereon, with the blood of the bird, the figure I show you. When this is done, cast the body of the black cock into running water, and pronounce the following incantation over the parchment, which must then be placed beneath the altar in the church, where it must remain for two days:

"O virgin parchment, O clean and spotless parchment, I place thee beneath the altar so that thou mayst receive those powers which I wish thee to possess, and which thou wilt retain for ever."
