 "And when you have said this, you shall see wonderful things, and the Spirits will come before you, and they will obey your command."

The manuscript's contents, which closely match those of the Grand Grimoire, were very influential in the development of contemporary ceremonial magic. It is a book of substantial Old Testament content which also includes some content related to the apocryphal Books of Enoch, along with Roman Catholic liturgical elements, such as various litanies and prayers in Latin.
