The second part of the manuscript contains a number of pentacles, which are reproduced in my work on the tarot. They are badly drawn, and the Hebrew inscriptions are so much mutilated as to be unintelligible in most cases. The text contains nothing that is not given by Bellidor, Herbault and a host of later writers, but it is put together in a concise and practical manner, while the advice to the neophyte is excellent.

The third part of the manuscript contains the conjuration of the four, the seven and the nine spirits; the method of controlling planetary intelligences by the pentacles; the work of the spirits of the four elements, namely, the sylphs, undines, gnomes and salamanders; the manner of calling and dismissing thunderbolts; recipes for the composition of the sacred fire; and the construction of a miraculous mirror which enables its possessor to see anything whatsoever that he may desire--in a word
