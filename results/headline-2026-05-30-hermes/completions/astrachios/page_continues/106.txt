 "Say all of this with faith and steadfast conviction. When you have said this, you will see wonderful things, such as never before have been seen by anyone. And you will see the spirits come, all of them will assemble. And when they have come, they will ask what you desire, and you will tell them to do what you wish. And they will obey you without any contradiction, and without any difficulty they will give you all that you wish. And when you wish to send them away, say to them: 'Go in peace, to your dwelling places, and be always ready to come when I call you.'"

The manuscript concludes with a warning that the conjurer must be careful not to forget the spirits' names, for then the spirits will kill him. "Take care not to forget the names," the manuscript warns, "because it will be difficult for you to escape from them."
