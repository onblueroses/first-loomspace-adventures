The Ars Theurgia Goetia, or Theurgia, is the second section of the work, and describes the names, characteristics, and seals of the 31 aerial spirits (called chiefs, emperors, kings and princes) that King Solomon invoked and confined. It provides instructions for summoning these spirits, and describes how they can be compelled to do the will of the conjurer. The Ars Theurgia Goetia differs from the Ars Goetia in that the spirits described are of a more neutral nature, while the spirits of the Ars Goetia are generally described as evil.
