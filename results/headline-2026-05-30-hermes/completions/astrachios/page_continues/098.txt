 "Here are the symbols to be used when invoking the spirits. The circle is to be drawn on the ground, and the person who desires to invoke the spirits is to stand in the middle, the head uncovered, the feet upon the symbols, the right arm raised and the left let fall to the side. The names of the spirits must be written upon virgin parchment, and they must be invoked in a clear voice, without fear, and without the omission of a single syllable."

The three symbols, which are to be drawn within the circle, are also shown on the page. The first is a swastika, the second a star of David, and the third a kind of pinwheel of eight triangles.

The "Invocation to the Demons" is as follows:

I call upon you, O spirits from beyond the earth, in the name of Samoel and Cadat, Arioht and Elohi, by the power of Adonai and Eloi, by the obedience which you owe to the great Scirlin, who has power over you, and by the force of his rule over the spirits, which he has given to us.
