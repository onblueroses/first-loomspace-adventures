 "The circle being thus made, and the book being read, close it again, and put it under your right arm, and call the spirits with a low voice, that it may be neither too loud nor too soft, saying, 'O Baracata, Acarab, Ara, Baracata, Come: Elohim, Adonai, Elohim, Adonai, I conjure you, O Baracata, O Ara, O Acarab, that you appear before this circle in a fair human shape, and that you be not terrible unto me, that you harm me not, nor any other creature, that you do my will in all things.'"

The manuscript further lists the demons under the control of the three chief spirits, who are under Scirlin. The first chief spirit is named "LUCIFER," who "has under his power two hundred angels, and they are all from the same hierarchy; they have no particular names, but they all call themselves by the name of Legion."
