 "I conjure you by the most holy names of the angels of God who serve in the ninth legion, who are set over the four winds."

This incantation, which is rather a typical example of a conjuration from the Key of Solomon, shows the mingling of Jewish, Christian, and other traditions characteristic of this kind of folk magic. The Greek, Hebrew, and Latin words and names are heavily corrupted in the manuscripts, raising the question of whether the writer understood their meanings or simply viewed them as holy words to be used in the construction of a magical ritual.

The inclusion of the word "Genium" (Latin "Genium", the accusative form of "Genius") and "Domos" (Latin "Domus", the accusative form of "House") suggests the incantation may have been intended to summon a genius loci, or spirit of place.
