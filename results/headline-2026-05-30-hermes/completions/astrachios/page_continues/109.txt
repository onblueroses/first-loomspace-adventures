 "When you have said these words, you will see wonderful things, and the Spirits will come before you to answer to everything that you may wish to know. They will come to serve you, and will obey you in great humility, and they will be faithful to you."

It's not known if this is the actual grimoire used by the alleged witches. It's believed to have been created in the 18th or early 19th century, after the witch trials. However, it's still considered a significant artifact related to the Affair of the Poisons.
