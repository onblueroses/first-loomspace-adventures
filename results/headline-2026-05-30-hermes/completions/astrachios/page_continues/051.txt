 "When you have repeated these words, you will see wonderful things, which it is impossible to relate, by anyone whomsoever."

The reader is then instructed to call upon the angels for whatever he desires, subject to certain restrictions (which are curiously similar to those in the Ars Almadel of the Lemegeton). The reader is warned not to offend the angels or demons, or he will be "certainly tormented by them". He is also warned not to ask for "things which are against God and his saints", or the angels will "abandon" him, and he will be "despised by everyone and everything".

The final section of the Grand Grimoire deals with the method of summoning of the Black Hen and the method of creating a Philosopher's Stone.
