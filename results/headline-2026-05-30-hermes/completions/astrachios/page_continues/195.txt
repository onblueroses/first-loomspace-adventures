 "Here are the names which must be spoken aloud in order to open the way: Astrachios, Asac, Acra Bedrimuliel, Silat, Arabonas, Irehalem, Ideodoc, Archarzel, Blautel, Baracata, Adonai, Elohim, Emagro, Abragateh, Samoel, Geburahel, Cadato, Era, Elohi, Achsah, Ebmisha, Imachadel, Daniel, Dama, Elamos, Izachel, Bael, Scirlin; Genium Domos. When you have spoken these names, speak the following: O Saviour, Christ, who created all things and brought them to light, deign to grant me your aid and to have pity on me. O Christ, O God the Saviour, grant me the grace of completing this work, which I am undertaking under your auspices, the Most Holy Adonay, whose kingdom endures eternally, without end, Amen.
