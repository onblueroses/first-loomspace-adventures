## History

According to a letter published in the 1932 bulletin of the Société d'agriculture, sciences et arts de la Sarthe, the manuscript was first brought to public attention in the 19th century by Charles Victor Descamps, a priest and professor at the seminary of Le Mans. The first known owner of the manuscript was the Marquis de Migieu, who was in possession of it in 1809. According to the same letter, the manuscript was then acquired by the collector Georges-Jean Mouchet, who had it rebound.
