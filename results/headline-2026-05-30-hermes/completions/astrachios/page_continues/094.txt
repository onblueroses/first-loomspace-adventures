 “I conjure you, O Spirit Rumoar, and all other Spirits present, to aid me in my undertakings, and to grant me power over my enemies, and to deliver them into my hands. And in testimony of your acceptance of these, my offerings, I now burn this symbol of the Fleurs-de-Lys, which I have drawn upon virgin paper, in this circle prepared.”

Raising the parchment talisman in his right hand, he held it over the flame of the black candle, and as it caught fire and blazed up, he dropped it into the brazier, where it was consumed.

He resumed his seat, and began to read from the grimoire:
