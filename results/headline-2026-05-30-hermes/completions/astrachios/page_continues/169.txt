### The "Grand Pentacle of Solomon"

The "Grand Pentacle of Solomon," from the manuscript of the Key of Solomon in the British Museum.The "Grand Pentacle of Solomon," which is engraved on a piece of blue steel, is used to command demons. The inscription "Lailahel," is written in Hebrew script around the edge of the pentacle.

### The Second Pentacle of Jupiter

The Second Pentacle of Jupiter, from the manuscript of the Key of Solomon in the British Museum.The Second Pentacle of Jupiter, is used to acquire glory, honors, dignities, riches, and all kinds of good, together with great tranquility of mind. The inscription is written in Hebrew, and reads: "El Ab, Yaheveh, Adonai, Ahi, Aha, Yah, Eh, Agla, Yehoshua, Emanuel, Messias."
