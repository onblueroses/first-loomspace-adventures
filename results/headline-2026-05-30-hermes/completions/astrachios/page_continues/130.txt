## History

The figure of Scirlin appears in the Red Dragon grimoire, which is part of the wider tradition of the Grand Grimoire. The Red Dragon itself was likely written in the 18th or 19th century, but may have been based on an older tradition.

## In popular culture

* In the manga series Black Butler, Sebastian Michaelis uses the name "Scirlan" to refer to a book containing the names of all the demons.
