The Ars Notoria is a medieval grimoire, or book of magic, that focuses on the acquisition of knowledge and memory. It is attributed to the ancient Greek philosopher and magician Apollonius of Tyana and is one of the oldest and most influential texts in the tradition of Western occultism. The Ars Notoria is known for its use of magical prayers, seals, and rituals to help the practitioner gain knowledge and understanding of various arts and sciences, including grammar, rhetoric, logic, arithmetic, geometry, music, and astronomy.

The Ars Notoria is divided into two parts: the first part deals with the prayers and rituals used to invoke the angels and spirits associated with each of the liberal arts, while the second part contains a series of seals and figures used in the rituals. The practitioner is instructed to recite the prayers and meditate on the seals in order to gain the knowledge and abilities associated with each art.
