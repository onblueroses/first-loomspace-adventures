When the spirit appears, the Master demands his name, and the spirit answers, 'I am the spirit of N,' and he will give the master power to heal the sick and find hidden treasures. After he has given these things, he must be contented."

The manuscript then goes on to describe the conditions under which the spirit may be summoned:

The spirit can be summoned in the first hour of the day or the tenth hour of the day, or in the first hour of the night, or in the sixth hour of the night. The spirit must be called three times. The master must have a hazel rod and a cap of maintenance, and must wear a white robe. The master must draw a circle in the ground with the hazel rod, and the spirit will appear in the form of a young boy.
