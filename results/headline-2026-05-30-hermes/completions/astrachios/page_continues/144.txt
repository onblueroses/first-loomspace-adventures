 "When you have spoken these names, you will see wonderful things, such as animals appearing that will surround you. Don't be frightened, but continue reading, and you will see the gate open."

The next pages contain invocations to summon the various spirits. These are followed by "The Key to the Work," which explains how to interpret the symbols that some of the spirits will use to communicate with the conjurer.

The last section of the manuscript is titled "The Comte de Gabalis," which is an esoteric novella attributed to Abbé N. de Montfaucon de Villars. The novella is followed by "The Nuctemeron of Apollonius of Tyana," which appears to be an excerpt from Eliphas Levi's Dogme et Rituel de la Haute Magie (Dogma and Ritual of High Magic), followed by "The Nuctemeron Gates," which appears to be a commentary on Levi's work.

The final page of the manuscript contains the note: "Finis. Dated this 7th day of February, in the year of Our Lord one thousand eight hundred and sixty-two."
