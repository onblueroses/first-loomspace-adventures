 "These are the names by which these spirits can be summoned: Astrachios, Asac, and so on. But these spirits are evil, deceptive, and perjurers, who will do nothing for those who invoke them unless they make a pact with them. Do not trust them, for they are evil and will always try to deceive those who wish to make a pact with them. Those who do make a pact with them will lose their souls, for after their death, these spirits will have power over them and will make them suffer the torments of hell eternally."

The second page of the manuscript lists the 72 spirits and their attributes (with their demons' names in parentheses):

1. (Bael) can make the conjurer go invisible.
2. (Agares) can make the conjurer wonderfully skilled in all languages.
3. (Vassago) can bring a dead person back to life.
4. (Gamigin) can give the conjurer the power to fly.
5. (Marbas) can cause the conjurer to see visions of heaven.
6. (Valafar) can manipulate time.
7. (Aamon) can make the conjurer be in good favor of any woman.
