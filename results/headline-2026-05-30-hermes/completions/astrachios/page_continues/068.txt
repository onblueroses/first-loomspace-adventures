 "The names which must be invoked to obtain the apparition of the Genii are those which follow:..."

After the "prince" and his "ministers" are summoned, the conjurer must then call the "genii" by their names and the functions which they perform:

I conjure and command you, O powerful spirits, that you instantly appear before this circle in a fair and human shape, all gentle, courteous, and friendly, entering into no contract with me until you have first sworn to do no harm unto myself or any person, that you do not act contrary to the will of the lord God, and that you give me a true and perfect answer to all that I shall ask of you, without any hesitation or falsehood.

After this has been satisfactorily completed, the conjurer then asks the spirits to sign their names on a parchment which he has prepared. When all of the signatures are collected, the conjurer then closes the ritual:
