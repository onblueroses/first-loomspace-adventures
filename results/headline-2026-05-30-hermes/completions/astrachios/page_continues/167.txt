 'When you have said this, the spirit will appear in a beautiful form and will speak to you.'

The invocation is a jumble of magical names, many of which are recognisable as garbled forms of Hebrew or Greek words. It is in fact a distorted version of a well-known invocation found in many medieval and later magical manuscripts. The original version calls on 'Astrachios, Asac, Asacro, Bedrimulael, Tilath, Arabonas, Ierahel, Ideodoc, Acharzel, Zophiel, Blautel, Baracata, Emagro, Abragateh, Samoel, Geburahel, Rada, Ledabale, Elheia, Elohi, Ahsah, Ebmaza, Imachadel, Dama, Elamos, Izachel, Baccanoe, Scirlin, Genium Domos'. There are minor variations between different manuscripts, but this is the standard form. The spell in the Voynich manuscript is a poor attempt at copying this invocation.
