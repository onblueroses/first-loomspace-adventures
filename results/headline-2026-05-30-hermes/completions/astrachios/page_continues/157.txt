The second formula to be recited four times, once in the direction of each cardinal point, is headed "Conjuration of the King of the South, and of his six subordinates, which are: Loros, [Ragos,] Sobel, Mador, Elap, Bora [and Ramis]". It reads:

Powerful Machator Asie, who has command over the four winds, upon whom the East wind is dependent, toward whom the West wind hath submission, to whom the North wind owes obedience, and whom the South wind doth worship: I conjure thee, O powerful Machator Asie, and your six subordinates, to be obedient unto me, to fulfil my commands, and to accomplish my will and desire.

The page concludes with the note: "The Grand Pentacle of Solomon needs to be made in copper, to make the conjuration work."
