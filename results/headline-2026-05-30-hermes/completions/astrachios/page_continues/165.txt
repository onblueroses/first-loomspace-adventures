### Theurgia Goetia

The second book, called Theurgia Goetia, is about spirits of the cardinal points, which are stronger than those in the first book but still mostly evil. Most of the spirits are tied to points on a compass, with a few exceptions.

The book begins with a preliminary invocation, which is spoiled by a drawing of a sigil that is supposed to be on the magician’s chest. The spirits are described in detail, including their appearances, their rank in the infernal hierarchy, and their powers. Some of these descriptions are very vivid, such as that of the spirit called Zamfresel, who appears as a “great and mighty lion, roaring like a blazing fire, having five feet and five tails, and brandishing a sharp sword.”
