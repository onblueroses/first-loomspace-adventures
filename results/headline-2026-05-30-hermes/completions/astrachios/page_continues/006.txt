 "I conjure and command you, O strong and holy angels, to appear before me and bring me the person I desire, and to make them agreeable to my desires, speaking now and forever the names and symbols that follow."

The ritual then lists a number of names and symbols, followed by more names of demons, including Asmodeus, Beelzebub, and Astaroth. The ritual ends with a command for the demons to appear before the magician and fulfill their desires.

The Red Dragon is notable for its focus on love and sexual magic, as well as its use of a variety of magical symbols and names. It is considered a classic work of grimoire literature and has influenced many later works on the subject.
