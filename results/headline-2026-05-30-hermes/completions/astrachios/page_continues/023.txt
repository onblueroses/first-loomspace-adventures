 “I conjure you, O Spirits, by all these aforesaid names, sacred and holy, and by the virtue of God the Almighty, and by the power of the Father, and by the merits of His Son Jesus Christ, our Lord and Savior, and by the most precious and sacred Body and Blood which that same Jesus Christ shed for our sins, and by His most holy Passion and by the power of the Holy Spirit, the Paraclete, and by the virtue of the most holy and glorious Mother of God, the Virgin Mary, and by the virtue of all the saints, I conjure you to come to me, to appear before me, and to be obedient unto me.”

On the final page of the manuscript, the scribe again conjures the spirits to appear before him, “to answer my questions, to fulfill my requests, and to complete my demands, without any deceit or lie, without any hesitation or qualification, without noise or disturbance, without the loss of my body or soul, without any injury to my person or those who accompany me, and without any damage to my property or that of my companions.”
