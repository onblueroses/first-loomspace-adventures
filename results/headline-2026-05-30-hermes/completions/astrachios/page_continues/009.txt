 "By these names, and in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, I conjure you, O Spirits, who shall have charge of the guard of this Book, one or many, that you show yourselves here visibly before this circle in fair and human form, without noise or deformity, to execute and accomplish all that I shall command you, and that you shall be obedient to all the commands of your master, and mine, the said N., and that you shall not suffer any other person or spirit to touch or behold this Book, or be visible herein this circle; and that you shall not depart without my license; and that you shall give me a true answer to all that I shall ask you, and that you shall give me good account of your office, and that you shall do all in my sight, before this circle, and without guile or deceit. Come, then, at my call, and do not tarry, but appear here promptly, in the names above said, and by the virtue of the mystery of the Most Holy Trinity, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, and by the merit of the Most Blessed and Glorious Virgin Mary, Mother of God, and of all the saints in Paradise
