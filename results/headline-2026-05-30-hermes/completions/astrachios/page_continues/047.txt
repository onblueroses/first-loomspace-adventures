 "Here are the names by which all the spirits who appear in this book are to be called, when you wish to make them appear.

"When you have need of them, turn to the chapter that contains what you desire, and recite the heading which is at the beginning of that chapter. And immediately they will come to you to do your will."

The book then goes on to give chapters on each of the spirits, but they are all very similar to each other in their wording, and all of them are quite short. The first chapter is typical:

Chapter I: To know all sorts of things, past, present, and what is to come, which are secret and hidden from men.

Say the names of the spirits with resolution, and in a low voice, and you will see that they will come to you:

Sarabotis, Maraloch, Nilion, Pancarpon, Trasarou, Isubael, Barakel, and the other spirits of this book.
