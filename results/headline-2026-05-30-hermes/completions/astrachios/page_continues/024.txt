 "Here are the names by which these spirits are to be called...". The list begins with Baal, and includes many of the traditional demons of Christianity, such as Beelzebub, Asmodeus, and Astaroth, but also adds names that are not traditionally associated with evil, such as Emanuel and Leviathan.

The manuscript also contains what is considered a major demonological work of the 18th century, titled Le Dragon Rouge (The Red Dragon). The work is falsely attributed to Honorius of Thebes.

The book features heavily in the 1978 film The Evil, where it is used to conjure up a demon.

The Grand Grimoire is represented as "The Book of Sins" in the 2015 horror film, The Conduit.
