 "This conjuration must be done as follows: Take a virgin parchment, and write this conjuration in the following manner. First, make four pentacles with the needle or the pen. Then write the four names of the four corners of the world, Adam, Eroch, Emocha, Emagos, in the four pentacles, and the name of the guardian of the well, and after that write the above-mentioned conjuration."

The second page contains the following instructions:

For the spirit to appear immediately, take an earthenware pot and put it on the fire, and put therein the brain of a raven and the tongue of a sea-fish, and write the above-mentioned names on the pot in the Hebrew language, and say the conjuration three times, and when you have finished the third time, burn the virgin parchment with the conjuration written on it, and the spirit will appear instantly, and will bring you the treasure.
