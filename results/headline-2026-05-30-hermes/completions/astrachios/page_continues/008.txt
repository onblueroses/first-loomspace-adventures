 "By these names, and in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, I conjure you, spirits, and I command you to appear before me in human shape, without noise or without injury to my person, to fulfill my will in everything I command you, not to leave without my permission, and to be obedient to the master of this book."

The manuscript has been dated to the early 18th century and is most likely from Italy. It is currently housed at the Wellcome Library in London.
