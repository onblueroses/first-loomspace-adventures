 “By this evocation and by the words and names of the Creator, I conjure you, O Spirits, to appear here in this mirror when I shall call you, to show me the truth of that which I desire to know and to answer truthfully all questions that I shall put to you, failing which to be condemned in the flames of hell. In the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost. Amen.”

These prayers and incantations being completed, the operator must dismiss the Spirits by commanding them to return to their private abodes, after which the mirror must be set aside in a place where no one can see it.

The time of operation must be chosen according to the knowledge of the Spirits whom it is proposed to evoke, and the questions put must be made in accordance with the nature of the Spirit.

The page concludes with a list of the names of the Spirits, the times at which they should be invoked and the matters upon which they may be consulted. It will be seen that there are no Spirits of the Sun or Moon. The reason for this omission is not explained.
