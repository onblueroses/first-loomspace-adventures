 "By the potent princes, genii of the ninth legion, I conjure you to grant my demands. I command you by the force of the same mighty names and words to send me immediately the spirit that I desire, and that he shall appear to me in human form without any deformity or horror. Come, then, O spirit, in the name of Adonai Zebaoth and by the power of the words and names here named, and give me your assistance in all that I shall ask of you."

This is followed by the appearance of the spirit, who, after receiving a kind welcome from the conjurer, is invited to sign his name in the book, after which he is licensed to depart.

There is a good deal of this sort of thing in the work, but as it is largely a repetition of what we have already described, it is unnecessary to go further into details.

The remainder of the volume is taken up with the Clavicula of Solomon, which we have already dealt with in the chapter on Talismanic Magic.
