 "And when you have said these words, you will see wonderful things, and they will be yours to command. Cursed be he who divulges this."

The manuscript mentions Solomon as the source of the text, and the 72 djinn (or "demons") imprisoned by the king are reminiscent of the 72 spirits in the Ars Goetia, the first section of The Lesser Key of Solomon. However, the text itself is in French, which suggests a much later origin than the time of Solomon. The book also has elements that suggest it was influenced by Greek and Roman mythology. The grimoire is therefore likely a synthesis of multiple traditions.

## In popular culture

The Grand Grimoire features as a usable item in the 2022 survival horror game Signalis.
