 "Now, if you have properly executed the above operation, you will behold a great number of spirits, who will congregate round the circle. You must command them to combine into one, which they will do, and when they are thus brought together, you can make them assume any shape you please, such as that of a dog, to follow you and do your bidding."

The text continues to describe how to command the spirit to take the shape of a ring or other jewelry.
