 "When you have repeated these words, you will see wonderful things, which it is impossible to relate, as was seen by Solomon in his arbor. The four kings will appear in their order, one after the other: that is to say, the King of the East, Gamael; the King of the West, Aserideb; the King of the North, Amaymon; and the King of the South, Mador. The text goes on to detail the process of invoking these spirits and the proper way to make requests of them.
