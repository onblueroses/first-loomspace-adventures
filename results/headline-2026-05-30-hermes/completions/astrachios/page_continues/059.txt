# The Preliminary Invocation

Power of the Key of Solomon, King of the Djinn, I command you to my will! I command you by the force of the letters and the names, by the permission of the Living and Eternal God. I conjure you by the holy names of God written on this rod of Aaron, which holds the Key of the Heavens, to do my bidding. I command you by the incontestable names of the Creator, Gabor Agla, Tetragrammaton, Elohim Gibor, and by all the talismans and pentacles which are in this book. I conjure you to come quickly, and without delay, to do my bidding and all I desire, without harm to me or any other creature, so that you will be ready and willing to come whenever I call you.

# The License to Depart

I thank you for what you have done, and I release you to your rightful place, without causing harm or damage to anyone or anything. Go now, and be ready to come back when I call you.
