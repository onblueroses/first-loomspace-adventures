 "By the above names, and by the holiness of the humble St. Peter, and by the passion and resurrection of Jesus Christ, and by the triumph of the Holy Cross, which you made on the day of your death, and by the tears which you shed on the Mount of Olives, by all of these, O Lord God, we ask you to grant our requests."

The manuscript then continues with prayers addressed to demons, asking them to do the conjurer's will. The text finishes with an address to the reader, saying that the spells will work against all demons and spirits, and that it is the master of all other grimoires. The document ends with a series of magic circles, used for the purpose of evoking spirits.
