 "When you have repeated these words, you will see wonderful things, which it is impossible to relate, as they are related to the secrets of the Science."

The text then goes on to provide a warning:

By the most holy names of the Eternal, you shall make no wrong use of this knowledge, but shall use it only for good and in the fear of Adonai, because this is a very great secret which you must keep all your life.
