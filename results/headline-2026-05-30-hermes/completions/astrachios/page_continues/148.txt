 "When you have said the words above and made the sign of the cross, the spirits will come and will say to you: 'What do you wish that we should do?' I command you to do such and such, etc. And they will obey you."

The manuscript continues with instructions for making a magical ring:

On whatever day you wish to make the ring, before sunrise, wash your hands and face. Then make the sign of the cross. Say the following words three times:

Agla, Agla, Agla, Aglata, Almouhara, Gariat, Goui, Goulon, Goulou, Gourab, Goudah, Gouuor, Adonai, Sabaoth, Agla, Agla, Agla.

Say the words three times and the spirits will come. When they are present, say to them: "I wish to make a ring to be lucky at games." They will tell you the spot where the ring is to be made and what to use to make it.

The manuscript concludes with instructions for making a magic circle and sword for use in magical operations.
