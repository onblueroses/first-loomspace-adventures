 "And when you have said this, you will see wonderful things, and they will be under your command. Pater noster, etc." The text ends with "Amen".

## In popular culture

The manuscript has been referenced several times in popular culture:

* In the Spanish horror film La posesión de Emma Evans, the book is used by the title character to conjure a demon.
* In the Spanish horror film El libro de las sombras, the protagonist uses the book to summon a demon.
* In the PlayStation 4 video game Silent Hill: Alchemilla, the book is referenced in a puzzle.
* In the fantasy tabletop role-playing game Pathfinder, the book is used by the villain in the adventure path "Curse of the Crimson Throne".
* In the Japanese manga series D.Gray-man, the book is used by the character Tyki Mikk to summon demons.
