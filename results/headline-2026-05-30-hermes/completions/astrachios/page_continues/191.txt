 'I conjure you by the holy names of God, to send me a spirit that will accomplish my desires. I conjure you, I charge you, and I command you, in the name of the Great Alpha and Omega, and in the name of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ, who will come to judge the quick and the dead, whose kingdom shall have no end.

'Obey my commands, and send me a spirit that will reveal to me the secrets of the Red Dragon, by the power of the divine names, and by this pentacle, without any evil or harm to me, or to any one.

'Come quickly, and let no obstacle prevent you. Come, come, come; AGLA, AGLA, AGLA. Amen.'
