 “I conjure you, O Spirits, by all the said names, that you come promptly to do my will and that you submit yourselves to my power, or at least that you send me a Spirit who is your messenger and who has the power to grant me what I request, and that he be obedient and willing to appear whenever I call him.”

On the next page, the ritual continues, summoning the spirit that is to appear in the form of a black cat. The ritual then ends with the dismissal of the spirit.

The Grimoire of Pope Honorius is a fascinating piece of occult history, providing a glimpse into the practices and beliefs of a time when magic and religion were closely intertwined. While its authenticity and origins remain a topic of debate, its influence on the development of modern ceremonial magic is undeniable. Whether used for summoning spirits, divination, or other magical purposes, the Grimoire of Pope Honorius remains a testament to the enduring human fascination with the mysterious and the unknown.
