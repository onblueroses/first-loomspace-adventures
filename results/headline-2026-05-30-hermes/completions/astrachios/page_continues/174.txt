 "Now make your demands, and you will be satisfied if you have the talisman, and you pronounce the words I have given you." The talisman itself is a crude image of a snake or serpent, biting its tail, with the words "Abgirunt Cuntis" inscribed within the body of the snake. The text then continues, providing the means by which the talisman should be created:

To make the talisman of the Black Hen, which is to be made of fine pewter and be of the size of a penny:

On one side place a crude figure of a snake, which forms a circle with its tail in its mouth; within this circle write, in the common letters, the words "Abgirunt Cuntis," which means "I take revenge on all." On the other side, write these two words: "L. C. B. N. G. S. C." and "D. U. F. N. I. B. I. T. S. T. R.," and around this write the following words: "Nades, Suradis, Maniner," and "Astra, Cerastes, Lerpes," with a cross in the middle.
