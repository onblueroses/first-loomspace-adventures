 "And when you have said these names, you will see appear before you a cloud in which there will be a figure of a King, who will have a red crown on his head, and a drawn sword in his hand. When you see him, show him the pentacle of the ring, and say to him: 'O Ely, servant of the Great God, Adonay, who am I?' He will say to you: 'You are Osorronophris, and I am Eliaonaphoge; you are the one whom I serve, and I am the one whom you command.' Then say to him: 'Servant Ely, I conjure you by the names above, to bring me the spirit that I desire to have in such a form as I shall show you.' And when you have said that, you will see this Ely go into the cloud with a great noise, and he will disappear.
