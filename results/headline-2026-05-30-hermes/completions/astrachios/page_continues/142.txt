 "This must be spoken in a clear voice, instead of being whispered, and all the said spirits will come, and do whatever may be commanded."

## History

A manuscript of The Sworn Book of Honorius, dated to the 13th century, is the oldest known version of this work. The next oldest manuscript is a 14th-century version that was bound together with the 13th-century version at some point in time, which is now in the collection of the British Library. Johannes Hartlieb (1456) mentions it as being one of the books used in necromancy in his book on the errors of the common people. The oldest preserved manuscript dates to the 14th century, and is part of the collection of the Bibliothèque nationale de France in Paris. It is an example of a Solomonic grimoire.
