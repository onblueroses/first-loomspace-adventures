 “When you have need, speak the name of the spirit, and he will appear immediately. When he appears, ask him what you will, and bind him with the bond of the Most High in order to make him obey you.”

A lot of these names, like Elohim and Adonai, are just alternative names for God in the Old Testament, but there are a few in there that are a little more sinister, like Bael and Cadato. I couldn’t find much information about who or what they were, but I did find a few old texts that listed them as demons. Bael, apparently, is one of the kings of Hell. The name Samoel also stood out to me. It was very similar to Samael, the name of another demon and an angel of death in Talmudic lore. I didn’t know what to make of all that, but it was certainly interesting. I kept reading, hoping to find more information about the names or the ritual, but there wasn’t much else there. It was mostly just a list of names with a few short lines about what they could do for the practitioner.
