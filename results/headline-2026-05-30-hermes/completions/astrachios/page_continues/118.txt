### The Ars Notoria

The Ars Notoria is the name of the fifth book in some versions of the grimoire called the Lesser Key of Solomon, and in the Lemegeton, book two, Theurgia Goetia. Its seventy-two chapters contain detailed instructions on conjuring, controlling, and dismissing spirits of the four "cardinal" directions, meaning north, south, east, and west, of the planet. Some of these spirits are good, and some are evil. Some of them are more powerful than others. In the book, each of these spirits is given a unique sigil that the magician is instructed to draw on the ground or on a piece of paper, in a specific color, and with a specific type of ink. The sigil is intended to represent the spirit being summoned and is used to focus the magician's concentration on the spirit during the summoning process. The Ars Notoria is said to be a very powerful and dangerous book, and its use is not recommended for beginners in the field of magic.
