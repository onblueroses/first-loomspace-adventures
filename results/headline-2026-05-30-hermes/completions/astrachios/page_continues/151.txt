 "Here followeth the way to bind the spirit Bealphares into the form of a bird, so that he may carry you anywhere you wish to go:

First, you must have a room with no windows, except a small opening in the roof, big enough for a bird to fly through. You must also have a large cage, big enough for a man to crouch in, and a mirror, big enough for a man to crawl through. The mirror must be placed on the floor, facing upwards, and the cage must be placed over it, with the door of the cage directly over the mirror. Then, you must light a candle and place it in the center of the mirror, so that the flame is reflected in the glass.

Next, you must take a piece of parchment and write upon it the following words:

Bealphares, I conjure thee by the sacred names of Astrachios, Asac, Acra Bedrimuliel, Silat, Arabonas, Irehalem, Ideodoc, Archarzel, Blautel, Baracata, Adonai, Elohim, Emagro, Abragateh, Samoel, Geburahel, Cadato, Era, Elohi, Achsah, Eb
