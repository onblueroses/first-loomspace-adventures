 "These are the names of the seventy-two spirits which are contained in the Great Cavern of Gehenna, and which King Solomon received from the hand of the Angel of the Lord."

The next page, titled "How to make the Crystal and the Form of the Circle," gives instructions for creating a crystal and a circle to use for summoning the spirits. It requires a crystal ball, an altar, a circle drawn on the ground, and various ritual tools and offerings. The text emphasizes the importance of following the instructions precisely to avoid danger.

The manuscript then provides a list of the seventy-two spirits and their powers, along with the sigils used to summon them. Some of the spirits mentioned include:

1. Baal - a powerful king who can make the summoner invisible
2. Agares - a duke who can cause earthquakes and teach languages
3. Vassago - a prince who can declare things past, present, and future
4. Gamigin - a marquis who can teach liberal sciences and give accounts of souls
5. Marbas - a president who can cause and cure diseases

The manuscript ends abruptly after describing the powers of the thirty-sixth spirit, Stolas, suggesting that the remaining pages have been lost.
