 O spirits of the air, of fire, of water, of earth, of the heavens, and of hell. I conjure and command you, I call and summon you, by the power of the angels of God; by all the names of the living and true God, to hear the voice of this child who will be heard on high.

I call upon you, O spirits, who are destined to stand before the throne of God, on the last day of Judgement, learn and discover the things that are hidden. Be my eyes and ears this night, and reveal to me that which I seek.

Appear now, O spirits, in comely human form, and answer my questions. I command you. I conjure you. I summon you by the most high names of God, which being heard, all spirits must obey. Appear and speak. I command it.

By all the saints, angels, and the names of the true and living God, I command you, O spirits. Depart and go in peace unto your places. Let there be peace between us, now and forever. So mote it be. Amen.
