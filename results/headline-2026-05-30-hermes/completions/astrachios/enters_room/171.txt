**The Sixth Invocation**

Make the circle with the charcoal and salt. Light the incense. Place the candles in a circle and light them, starting from the top and going clockwise. Place the chalice in the center of the circle.

Kneel before the circle and say: "I invoke you, O Beings of the Sixth Mansion, by the power of the Holy Names of God: El, Elohim, Adonai, Eloha, Tetragrammaton, Shaddai, Anaphaxeton, and all the names of the divine essence, to make me invisible to all who would see me, and to make me hidden from all who would find me.
