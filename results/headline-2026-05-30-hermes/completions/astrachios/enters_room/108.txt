Astaroth is a fearsome sight to behold. Standing over eight feet tall, his muscular form is covered in rough, charcoal-grey skin. Despite his intimidating appearance, Astaroth carries himself with a regal bearing, draped in ornate black and red robes.

What powers or abilities does Astaroth possess?
Astaroth is a powerful demon with a wide array of abilities. He has superhuman strength, able to crush boulders with his bare hands. His skin is tough as stone, making him highly resistant to physical attacks. Astaroth can also conjure hellfire, unleashing devastating blasts of flame. He is a skilled sorcerer as well, able to cast illusions, compel the minds of mortals, and summon lesser demons to do his bidding. Astaroth also possesses the knowledge of all human secrets and can reveal them to those who summon him.
