 From which direction?

Does it arrive quickly or slowly?

What is it? Describe it. Is it an animal, an object, a person, a spirit, something else?

Does it move, and if so, how?

Is it silent or does it make a sound?

Does it communicate with you in any way?

What do you feel as it approaches?

Ask it any three questions. You may ask for guidance, wisdom, a vision of the future, or anything else that your heart desires.

When you have heard the answers, thank the spirit for its presence.

Speak the names that close the way:

Astachios, Asac, Acra Bedrimuliel, Silat, Arabonas, Irehalem, Ideodoc, Archarzel, Blautel, Baracata, Adonai, Elohim, Emagro, Abragateh, Samoel, Geburahel, Cadato, Era, Elohi, Achsah, Ebmisha, Imachadel, Daniel, Dama, Elamos, Izachel, Bael, Scirlin; Genium Domos.

Allow the image of the room to fade, and when you are ready, open your eyes.
