So we light the candles and wait, and listen for the voices of the dead to speak. And when they do, we must remember that they are not of this world, and they are not bound by our laws. They may lie, or they may tell the truth. They may be kind, or they may be cruel. We must be prepared for anything.

But most of all, we must remember to show them respect. For they were once as we are, and we will one day be as they are. So let us honor them, and let us listen to what they have to say.....
