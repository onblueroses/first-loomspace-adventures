 If you are successful, you will feel a presence or see something. If you have failed, nothing will happen. You may need to try again at another time.

---

## Closing the ritual

Now that the ritual is complete, you must close the circle and dismiss the spirits.

Say the following words:

I thank thee, spirits, for attending this rite. Go now in peace, and may you come again when called.

Blow out the candles and take down the circle. The ritual is now complete.
