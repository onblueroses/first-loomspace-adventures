 What comes is the spirit of the angelos, the holy daimon, the great messenger of the gods. I call you in the names of all those who have command over the spirits of the air: Paralda, the king of the airy spirits, and his queen, Merkurialisthe. I call upon you, oh mighty angel of the firmament, Barzabel, to open the way for the spirit I have called. By the power of the holy names, I call you, oh spirit. Come! I command you. Come!

As I speak the words of power, the spirit will take form before me. I will see it and I will hear its voice. ABRAKALA, ABRAKALA, ABRAKALA. Come, oh spirit, and sit with me. Speak with me and answer my questions. I command you, by the power of the secret names, to come!
