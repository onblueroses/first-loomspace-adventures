 What has been summoned. What has been called forth.

Mighty King of the East, I call you, I summon you, I stir you and charge and command you to appear before this circle, in the name of the mighty and holy God and in the name of the Son and in the name of the Holy Ghost, who is the beginning and the ending, the first and the last. I conjure and command you by the living God, by the true God, by the holy God, and by the names by which you may be constrained and bound: Adonai, Tetragrammaton, Jehova, Tetragrammaton, Anepheneton, Inessenfatoal, Neboutosoule, Anuge, Agla, Goth, Nariaden, Agisna, Olenos, Jao, Sabaot, Aij, Aij, Aleluia, and through all the holy names of God, that you appear before this circle, in the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Ghost, and to fulfill my will, to do my bidding, and to answer my demands.
