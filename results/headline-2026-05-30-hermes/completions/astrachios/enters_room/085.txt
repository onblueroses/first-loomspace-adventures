... What is it?

It is a spirit of the air.

By what name is it called?

Bael.

What is its nature?

It is a king among the spirits, ruling over the East and commanding 66 legions. Bael makes those he favors invisible and can reveal hidden things.

What is its form?

It appears in many shapes, sometimes like a cat, sometimes like a toad, and sometimes like a man, or all these forms at once. He speaks hoarsely.

Why has it come?

It has come at my command, compelled by the holy names and by the power of the Most High. It awaits my charge.

Bael, I charge you by the sacred power that binds you, answer truthfully all that I ask. 

I swear by the pact that binds me to speak only truth.

Tell me of the secret wisdom and hidden lore you know. Reveal that which is concealed from mortal eyes.

Much could be said, for the secrets are many and the lore is vast. The world is suffused with power and meaning that most fail to perceive. In the elements, in the stars, in the wild places and the forgotten corners, forces move and act unseen. Spirits like myself are ever-present,
