As you came in peace, now go in power.

As you came in power, now go in peace.

So it is, and so shall it be.

In the name of the Most High, the angels, and the spirits of the Book of Oberon, I declare this rite ended.

Go in peace and with the blessings of the Most High upon you all.

Amen.
