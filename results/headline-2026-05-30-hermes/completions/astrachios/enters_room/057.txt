 [point to the center of the circle] I bind thee that thou remain affable and visible to me within this circle, and that thou speak in an audible voice, and that thou fulfill my commands and answer my questions. I call you forth, by the holy names of God HARIU and by the holy names of God VEFLARO, I call you forth that you may answer my questions and fulfill my desires.

What is your name?

[The spirit gives its name.]

By the power of the Most High, I command you to tell me the truth, are you sent here to do my will?

[The spirit answers.]

By the power of the Most High, I command you to tell me the truth, will you answer my questions and fulfill my desires?

[The spirit answers.]

By the power of the Most High, I command you to tell me the truth, will you be truthful in all that you relate?

[The spirit answers.]

I adjure you, O spirit, by the power of the Most High and by the power of the names HARIU and VEFLARO, to do my will, to answer my questions, and to speak only the truth.
