#### 13
The demon arrived in a foul gust of sulphurous wind, and with it came a sense of pure evil that made the skin crawl. It was black, with webbed wings and a face that was a hideous combination of human and bat. Its eyes glowed like coals, and it stood on birdlike claws.

The demon regarded them with malevolent amusement, its eyes settling on the pentacle. "You are brave to call me," it said, its voice like the rustle of dead leaves. "What do you want of me?"

"We want you to tell us how to destroy the creature known as the Beast," Alaric said, his voice trembling.

The demon laughed, a horrible sound that echoed in the stone chamber. "You mortals, always seeking to destroy what you cannot understand. Very well, I will tell you what I know. The Beast is an ancient creature, born of chaos and darkness. It feeds on fear and despair, growing stronger with each passing century. To destroy it, you must find its heart and pierce it with a weapon forged of pure light."

"And where can we find this heart?" Alaric asked.
