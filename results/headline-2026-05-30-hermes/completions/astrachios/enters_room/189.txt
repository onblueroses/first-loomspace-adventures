 

When you have finished the ritual, carefully close the book, and extinguish the candle. The spirit should depart.

Do not be alarmed if the spirit does not manifest visibly. Often, the presence of spirits can be subtle, manifesting as sudden insights, gut feelings, or a sense of knowing. Pay attention to your dreams and intuition in the days following the ritual for guidance from the spirit.

Remember, this ritual should only be attempted by those who approach it with respect, seriousness, and a clear intention. Misusing these rituals or approaching them with ill intent can lead to unintended consequences. As with all magical workings, your intention is key. Approach this ritual with reverence and a sincere desire for knowledge and guidance, and you will open the door to profound insights and spiritual growth.
