# A Black Mirror
A black mirror is a powerful tool for scrying and evoking the dead. You can buy them ready-made, but it’s easy and more powerful to make your own. Here’s how:
1. Find a suitable picture frame. It should be large enough to gaze into comfortably, but not so large as to be unwieldy. A frame of about 8” x 10” works well. You can use a frame you already have, or visit a thrift store to find an inexpensive used one.
2. Remove the glass from the frame and clean it thoroughly, making sure it’s free from smudges, dust, and fingerprints.
3. Paint one side of the glass with black enamel paint. You can use spray paint or a brush
