 Do you see it? Can you feel it? Can you feel the power of the demon? The demon has entered the room, and it stands before you.

The demon looks like a monstrous, deformed figure, with black skin and glowing red eyes. It has horns on its head and large, bat-like wings. It snarls and growls, revealing sharp, jagged teeth.

But do not be afraid, for you have summoned it, and it is bound to your will. You are in control.

Now, command the demon to do your bidding. Command it to bring you wealth, power, love, or whatever else you desire. The demon must obey.

When you are finished, and the demon has completed its task, you must banish it back to the infernal realms from whence it came. 

Say these words:

Ego te conjuro et cogito te, omnis spiritus immundi, omnis satanica potestas, omnis incursio infernalis adversarii, omnis legio, omnis congregatio et secta diabolica, in nomine et virtute Domini Nostri Jesu Christi + et Dei Patris + et Spiritus Sancti +, ut descedas
