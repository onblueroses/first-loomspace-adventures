### 315. The demon Beleth

The thirteenth spirit is called Beleth. He is a mighty king and terrible, riding upon a pale horse, before whom go trumpets and all kinds of musical instruments. He is very furious at his first appearance, that is while the exorcist allays his courage, for to do that he must hold a hazel stick in his hand, striking it parallel upon the earth, and commanding Beleth into the triangle that he must attend, and so he will do, and will be more peaceable. The exorcist must then have a ring on the middle finger of his left hand, under the seal of God, and hold it against his face, and that will defend him from the fury of the spirits.

The ring must be prepared as is said in the chapter of the Magic Circle, and the exorcist must say, holding the ring towards the spirits:
