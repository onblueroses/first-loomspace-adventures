 Are you friend or foe? Speak and be recognized.

If the spirit is friendly, it will answer the summoner's questions and take its leave when given the following license to depart:

Begone, spirit, and return to your home and habitations causing neither harm nor danger to man or beast. I charge you to be obedient and faithful to any who may call upon you. As you obey, so shall I.

If the spirit is unfriendly, it will not speak but will instead make its displeasure known by mischievous acts or by attempting to harm the summoner. If this occurs, the summoner must not make any sudden moves or show any fear but should remain facing the mirror while repeating the following banishing formula until the spirit departs:

Begone, spirit, thou enemy of mankind. I banish thee by the powers of the magic circle of Solomon and I conjure thee to go away to the place appointed for thee. May the gods of light and the rulers of the elemental forces drive thee away and cause thee to depart. Begone, spirit, begone!
