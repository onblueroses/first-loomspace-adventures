 I see you, I feel you, I know you. I command you to speak, I command you to answer my questions truthfully, I command you to bring me the information I seek. You shall not leave until I have released you. And I shall release you only when I am satisfied that you have fulfilled your obligation to me.

You may speak now, and tell me your name.

And so on. The ritual is done.

The summoning works, but not as expected. I see nothing. I hear nothing. But I feel a presence. It is strong, and cold, and malevolent. I feel it trying to resist my commands, but it cannot. It is compelled to obey.

I ask it questions, and it answers. The answers come to me as thoughts in my head, as if they are my own thoughts. But they are not mine. They are the words of the demon.

I ask it about the secret of eternal life. It tells me that there is no secret. That life is finite, and death is inevitable. That all things must end.
