 It is a spirit of vast power, an emissary of the infernal world. Ask it to reveal its name. It may be Erato, or perhaps it will name itself otherwise. Ask the spirit to reveal the secrets of the occult, and to open your eyes to the mysteries of magick. It may speak to you now, or it may come to you in dreams. When you are ready to dismiss the spirit, say the following words:

Erato, I thank thee for your presence. Return now to your dwelling place, and come again when you are called. Go in peace and harmony. Amen.

The spirit will depart. Close the ritual by saying the words:

Astrachios, Asac, Acra, Bedrimuliel, Silat, Arabonas, Irehalem, Ideodoc, Archarzel, Blautel, Baracata, Adonai, Elohim, Emagro, Abragateh, Samoel, Geburahel, Cadato, Era, Elohi, Achsah, Ebmisha, Imachadel, Daniel, Dama, Elamos, Izachel, Bael, Scirlin; Genium Domos.
