 Who or what are you?

* The spirit enters, and speaks in a clear voice: *

I am the spirit of the three sisters, the Moirai, the Fates of old. Clotho, Lachesis, and Atropos, we who spin, measure, and cut the thread of life for all mortals.

We have been called many names through the ages - the Parcae by the Romans, the Norns in the frozen North. But our purpose remains eternal and unchanging.

Why have you summoned us here, mortal? Speak, and we shall listen.

* The summoner addresses the Fates: *

O mighty Moirai, I have called you here to seek your wisdom and guidance. I stand at a crossroads in my life, faced with a momentous decision that will shape my path forevermore. 

I humbly ask for your counsel - should I choose the safe and predictable road, or risk the uncharted journey that calls to my spirit? What destinies lie at the end of each path?

* The three sisters exchange glances, then Clotho the Spinner steps forward: *

Mortal, know that no path is ever truly safe or certain. Even the most well-worn road holds perils and unexpected twists
