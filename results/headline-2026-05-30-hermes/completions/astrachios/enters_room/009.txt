Once the spirit has entered the room, greet them with the following words:

Welcome, O spirit of the night. I greet you with respect and honor your presence here. I have called you to ask for your assistance and guidance in my magical workings. May our collaboration be fruitful and harmonious.

Listen carefully to the spirit's response. If they are willing to assist you, proceed with your questions or requests. Remember to be polite and show gratitude for their help.

When you are finished, thank the spirit for their aid and bid them farewell with the following words:

I thank you, O spirit of the night, for your wisdom and assistance. May you depart in peace and harmony, and may our paths cross again if fate allows. Farewell.

Close the ritual by extinguishing the candles and incense, and clearing the space. Take a moment to ground yourself and reflect on the experience.
