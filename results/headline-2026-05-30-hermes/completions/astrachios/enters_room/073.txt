Speak the words: "By the power of the Scirlin, I command you to grant me the power to communicate with the spirits of the dead. I command that you open my eyes and ears to their presence. I command that you grant me the authority to summon them and speak with them, and the wisdom to understand their messages. I command that you make me known to them, and cause them to come to me when I call."

You may now thank the spirit that appeared, and command it to go in peace.

Close the ritual by speaking the words:

Ego vos omnes immundos spiritus repudio.
Etiam te, Scerolin, et omnes vestros socios repudio.
Abite! Abite! Abite!

Blow out the candles and let the incense burn out. The ritual is complete.
