**S.**: I am Sabnock, the demon of destruction.

**P.**: Why have you come?

**S.**: I am bound by the names to come when called. Why have you summoned me?

**P.**: I wish to know the secrets of destruction. How can I tear down what has been built? How can I rend asunder what has been joined?

**S.**: Destruction is in my nature. To tear down, to break, to sunder - these are my domain. But know that destruction is not to be undertaken lightly, for it can consume the destroyer as well as the destroyed.

**P.**: I understand the risks. I am prepared to pay the price. Teach me.

**S.**: Very well. The key to destruction lies in finding the weak points, the flaws, the cracks in the foundation. Every structure, every bond, every creation has them. Seek them out.

**P.**: And once I have found them?

**S.**: Strike hard and fast. Pour your will, your rage, your hatred into the blow. Let it shatter the bonds that hold the thing together.
