 (Pause) Speak and make yourself known.

(A spirit will enter. You might hear it, sense it, or feel it. The nature of the spirit may be anything from a lowly servitor to a powerful demon. Whatever it is, it will answer your questions, and you can command it to carry out tasks, so long as they are within its power. You may ask for guidance, wisdom, or for the spirit to perform a task. The spirit will name itself and may give you a sigil to call it in the future.)

I thank you (name of spirit). You have served me well, and I offer you my blessing. You are free to go, but remain close to the Earth, so that when I call for you again, you shall come. Go now in peace. Go now in freedom.

Elohim, Elohim, I thank thee for the power thou hast granted. Talmid, I now declare this ritual ended. In the names of Almiras and Gelyas, our work here is complete.
