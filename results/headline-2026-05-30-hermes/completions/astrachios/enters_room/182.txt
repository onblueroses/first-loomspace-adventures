 What presence. What spirit. What demon.

What is its name?

Ask it to manifest in a form that you can perceive.

Ask it to speak in a voice that you can understand.

The demon is bound within the triangle, and cannot pass its boundary. You are safe within the circle.

Ask what you will.

When the demon has answered, or when the demon has given you what you seek, you must give it license to depart.

Say these words:

O thou Spirit (name), because thou hast diligently answered my demands, I do hereby license thee to depart, without injury to man or beast. Depart, I say, and be thou very willing and ready to come, whensoever duly exorcised and conjured by the sacred rites of magic. I conjure thee to withdraw peaceably and quietly, and may the peace of God continue forever between me and thee. Amen.

When the spirit has departed, extinguish the candles in reverse order to how you lit them, and then dismantle the circle, starting from the West and going widdershins.
