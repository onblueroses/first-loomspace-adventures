**VII. The Invocation of the Four Kings**

The Four Kings are the most powerful elemental spirits of the Ars Goetia, who rule over the cardinal directions and elements. They are called upon to assist in controlling the spirits and ensuring their obedience.

Face east and say:

O Oriens, King of the East, I call upon you in the name of the Almighty to assist me in this operation. Come forth and lend your power to my command. In the names of Aserabie, Adonai, Elohym, and Saraye, I conjure you to appear and be obedient to my will.

Face south and say:

O Amaymon, King of the South, I call upon you in the name of the Almighty to assist me in this operation. Come forth and lend your power to my command. In the names of Aserabie, Adonai, Elohym, and Saraye, I conjure you to appear and be obedient to my will.
