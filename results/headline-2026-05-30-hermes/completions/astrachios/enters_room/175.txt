  

It is the demon Astaroth, who appears in the form of a beautiful angel astride a dragon, holding a viper in his left hand. He wears a crown and speaks with a powerful, seductive voice.

Greetings, Astaroth. I have summoned you here to request your assistance. I seek knowledge and power to achieve my goals. Will you aid me in this endeavor?

Astaroth speaks:

Mortal, I am Astaroth, grand duke of the infernal realms. I hold sway over the past, present, and future. I can reveal secrets, teach sciences, and guide you to hidden treasures. But know that my aid comes at a price. What do you offer in exchange for my service?

I offer my loyalty and a place of honor in my life. I will make offerings to you and speak your name with reverence. In return, I ask for your guidance and power to shape my destiny.

Astaroth considers the offer, then nods in agreement.

Very well, mortal. I accept your terms. I will grant you knowledge of the arcane arts, the ability to influence others, and the power to shape reality to your will. But remember, with this power comes responsibility. Use it wisely, for the consequences of your actions
