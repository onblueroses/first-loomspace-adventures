#### 13
The room was dark.
The door was closed.
A figure stood in the corner, silent and motionless.
Watching.
Waiting.
In the darkness, it smiled.
A cold, cruel smile.
The figure breathed slowly, deeply.
Savoring the moment.
Savoring the anticipation.
The joy of the hunt.
The thrill of the kill.
It stepped forward, moving toward the bed.
And still it smiled.
A smile of pure evil.
A smile of death.
