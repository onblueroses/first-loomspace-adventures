 A dark cloud, a shadow in the night. A red robed figure with a shining silver pentagram upon his chest. His eyes burn like coals, his hands are wreathed in flame. He speaks with a voice like the rumble of distant thunder, like the crackle of a roaring fire.

I am Scirlin, servant of the infernal king. I come at your command, to do your bidding as the pact commands. What would you ask of me?

I ask for knowledge, for secrets hidden and mysteries unknown. Reveal to me the secrets of the dark arts, the keys to power.

The figure nods slowly, flame dancing along his fingers. He speaks and his words sear themselves into your mind:

To summon wealth, draw the sigil of Clauneck in gold upon a parchment of calfskin during the crescent moon. Light a candle of green wax and call upon him with these words:

Clauneck, Clauneck, Clauneck.  
I summon thee by the names of  
Saranah, Gorson, and Aleriel.  
Bring unto me the riches I desire.  
Let money flow to me from all directions.  
Fulfill my wishes, as is your office.  
Clauneck
