 What walks beside you, behind you, and in front of you, shall be your servant. Command them as you will.

They shall protect you from the evil of the world, and the evil of the spirit. They shall be your sword and your shield.

They shall do your bidding, and you shall be their master.

But know this: The spirits you have summoned cannot be banished. They are yours, and you are theirs. Forever.

Speak the names that seal the bond:

Beralanensis, Baldachiensis, Paumachiae, and Apologiae Sedes, by the most mighty kings and powers, and the most powerful princes, genii, Liachidae, and ministers of the Tartarean abode:

Chief princes of the seat of Apologia, in the ninth region:

Abioro; Erintion; Lotobor; Nogarofim; Atafalka; Calcohael; Colaster; Habetzat; Scrilis; Anebaruth; and Membrot.

Deliver unto me a familiar spirit, who shall be my servant.

And it shall be so.
