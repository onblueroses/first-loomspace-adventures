SHTUPID!

(Instantly, the demon appears. It is a small, squat creature, covered in matted black fur. It has sharp claws and teeth, and glowing red eyes. It snarls at the summoner.)

DEMON: What do you want, mortal? Why have you summoned me here?

SUMMONER: I command you, by the power of the names I have spoken, to answer my questions truthfully, and to do my bidding.

DEMON: (Reluctantly) I must obey. What are your questions?

SUMMONER: First, tell me your name.

DEMON: I am called Azazel.

SUMMONER: Azazel, I command you to reveal to me the secrets of the ancient magics. The spells and rituals long forgotten.

AZAZEL: That knowledge is dangerous, mortal. Are you certain you wish to delve into such things?

SUMMONER: I am certain. I am ready. Tell me.
