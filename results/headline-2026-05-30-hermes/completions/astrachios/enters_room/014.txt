  Enter, and do my will.

And I say:  I command you to bring me the book I desire, which contains the secrets of the Necromancers, written and bequeathed by their hands, so that by this means I may understand the methods of their art, and know how to work the greatest marvels.

I command you to go, and to travel as far as the place where the book is to be found, and to return here, without delay, or injury to anyone, and without making any noise or leaving any trace of your coming or going.

Obey my will, and let there be no deceit or trickery.  I command you, I the master of the circle.

The spirits bring the book, and place it at the edge of the circle.

I accept this book, and thank you for your service.  And now, I command you to depart, to leave this place, and to trouble no one.  Go in peace, and let there be harmony between us, for I am the master of the circle.

The spirits depart, and the circle is closed.

And it is done.
