  Speak its name:

*The demon appears, its form twisting and writhing, a mass of shadow and smoke. Two burning red eyes fix upon you as it speaks in a voice like grinding stone.*

I am Scirlin, servant of the infernal king Amaimon. Why have you summoned me here, mortal?

I have summoned you here to form a pact. I require your aid and service.

*The demon laughs, a harsh grating sound.* 
And what, pray tell, could a mere mortal offer that would be of interest to me? 

I offer you my devotion and worship. In exchange, I ask that you share your knowledge and power. Teach me the secret arts, the hidden ways of demons and spirits.

*Scirlin's eyes narrow as he considers your words.* 
Devotion and worship? Many mortals have made such promises, only to recant when their feeble minds comprehend the true price of the secrets they seek. What assurance do I have of your sincerity?

As a show of good faith, I will create a sigil in your honor. With my own blood, I will draw your symbol and consecrate it. This will be my seal upon our pact.

*The demon nods slowly.* 
Very well.
