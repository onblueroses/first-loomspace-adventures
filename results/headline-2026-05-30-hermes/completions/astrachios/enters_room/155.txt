  I conjure thee, O Spirit N., by all the most glorious Names of the Most High and Omnipotent Lord God of Hosts, that thou come quickly and without delay from all parts and places of the earth and all places where thou mayest be, to make rational answers unto my demands, and that thou come visibly and affably, speaking with a voice intelligible unto mine understanding as aforesaid.  I conjure and constrain thee, O thou Spirit N., by all the Names aforesaid; and in addition by these seven great Names wherewith Solomon the Wise bound thee and thy companions in a Vessel of Brass, A D O N A I, P R E Y A I, T E T R A G R A M M A T O N, A N E X H E X E T O N, I N E S S E N S A T O A L, P A T H U M A T O N, and I T A L O N; that thou appear in a fair human form.
