 Speak their names.

The spirits of the dead, and the demons of the infernal realms, enter the room. They are Astaroth, Azazel, Baalberith, Beleth, Buer, Cerbere, Guland, Marchosias, Morax, Naberius, Orobas, Rimmon, and Zapan.

By the power of the sacred names, I bind you to my service.

The spirits and demons are bound to the magician's service.

I command you to bring me the woman I desire. Let her be beautiful, willing, and passionate. Let her come to me this very night, and let her stay with me until the morning.

The spirits and demons bow their heads in obedience and vanish.

It is done. The woman I desire will come to me tonight. I thank the spirits for their service, and I release them to their realms.

The ritual is complete. The magician puts out the candles and incense, and leaves the room.

That night, the woman the magician desires comes to him. She is beautiful, willing, and passionate, and she stays with him until the morning, just as he commanded. The magician's desire is fulfilled, and the power of the sacred names is proven once again.
