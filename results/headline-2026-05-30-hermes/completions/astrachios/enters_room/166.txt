 What is your name?

I am Abraxas, the god of the cycle of birth, death, and resurrection. 

You are also called the 'Great Archon'?

Yes, that is one of my titles.

Are you the Demiurge of the Gnostics?

That is one interpretation of my being, yes.

What is your role in the Gnostic cosmology?

I am the ruler of the material world, the creator of the physical universe. I am the intermediary between the spiritual realm and the material realm, the bridge between the divine and the mundane.

Do you have any other names or titles?

I am also known as Abrasax, Abracax, and Abracar. In some traditions, I am associated with the Greek god Aion and the Persian god Zurvan.

What is the meaning of your name?

My name is derived from the Greek letters alpha, beta, rho, alpha, sigma, and xi. When added together, their numerical values equal 365, the number of days in a solar year. This represents my role as the god of time and the cycles of nature.

What is your relationship to the other gods and spiritual beings?

I am often seen as both a creator and a destroyer, a god of both life
