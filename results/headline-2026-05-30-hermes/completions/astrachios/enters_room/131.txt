  What is it?

I can feel the presence of a powerful entity. It is a being of great knowledge and wisdom, emanating an aura of ancient secrets and hidden truths. The air crackles with energy as it manifests before me.

This entity is known as Zariatnatmik, a guardian of the mysteries of the universe. It is neither good nor evil, but rather a neutral force that exists to maintain the balance of knowledge and power.

Zariatnatmik appears as a towering figure, cloaked in shimmering robes that seem to shift and change with every movement. Its face is hidden beneath a hood, but I can sense its piercing gaze upon me.

The entity speaks, its voice resonating through my mind:

"Mortal, you have summoned me with the ancient words of power. What knowledge do you seek? Speak, and I shall unveil the secrets you desire, but be warned - the price of such revelations may be higher than you realize."

*What knowledge do I seek? I seek to understand the nature of the universe and my place within it. I wish to unravel the mysteries of existence and gain insight into the hidden forces that shape our reality.*

Zariatnatmik nods slowly, its form shimmering with an otherworldly light.
