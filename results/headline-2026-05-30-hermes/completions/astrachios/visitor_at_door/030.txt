 “I have come to seek the wisdom of the spirits.”

The doorkeeper responds. “Enter, sojourner, and be welcome.”

The doorkeeper steps aside, opening the door fully, and allows the visitor to enter. Once the visitor is inside, the doorkeeper closes the door and follows, gesturing for the visitor to take a seat at the table on the side of the room across from the door. The doorkeeper takes the seat on the other side of the table, facing the visitor, and places both hands palm down on the table.

The doorkeeper speaks. “In the world of matter, names command the spirits’ awe. In their world, yours will do the same. Be seated, sojourner; speak your name.”

The visitor, remaining seated, speaks his or her name. The doorkeeper spreads a black cloth over the table, and then sets out the consecrated mirror, the lamp, and the burning censer between the visitor and the doorkeeper. The doorkeeper’s hands rest on the table for a moment, and then he or she takes the book, opens it, and begins to read aloud.
