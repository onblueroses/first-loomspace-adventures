 He or she must say:

I have come here to ask for a favor, and I ask you to grant it to me. I take the sacred oath that I will not reveal the secret of the sacred mystery of the magic lamp.

The guardian of the door responds:

You have pronounced the names that open the way, and you have sworn the sacred oath. Enter. You are welcome.

The visitor enters, and the door is closed.

The guardian of the door says:

I will now invoke the genie of the lamp.

The genie of the lamp is invoked with the following words:

Bador, Amacor, Amal, Hailan, Siliciah, Alim, Alimoht, Balal, Baham, Gehal, Calah, Halcal, Abata, Alah, Abalal, Balmula, Balal, Bellal, Bellalit, Calah, Calal, Calalut, Carat, Careh, Caren, Caleu, Calbah, Calbat, Colbach, Halcal, Hurabat, Caracat, Calbat, Calbach, Calbut, Catroh, Sugam, Suchal, Sulum, Sala, Sulah, Silam, Sarab
