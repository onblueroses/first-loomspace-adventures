**Sign of the Enterer:**

1. Stand facing East.
2. Raise your hands to the sides of your head, with your elbows at shoulder level.
3. Step forward with your left foot.
4. Thrust your hands forward, extending your arms fully.
5. Bring your hands back to the sides of your head, with your elbows at shoulder level.
6. Step back with your left foot, returning to your original position.

After performing the Sign of the Enterer, the traveler may explore the Temple of Solomon and seek the wisdom and knowledge contained within. When the traveler is ready to leave, he must perform the Sign of the Enterer once more, then exit the temple. The guard will close the door behind him.
