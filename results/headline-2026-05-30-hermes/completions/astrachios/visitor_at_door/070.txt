 'It is I, N. [Name], who ask entry.'

The doorkeeper speaks. 'By what right do you ask entry?'

The visitor speaks. 'By the right of a true seeker.'

The doorkeeper speaks. 'What is the word of entry?'

The visitor speaks. 'Elohim.'

The doorkeeper speaks. 'Enter, therefore, N. [Name], in the name of the Master of Masters.'

The doorkeeper opens the door and admits the visitor. The doorkeeper and the visitor exchange the Sign of the Enterer. The doorkeeper closes the door and returns to his place.

The ritual continues from the point at which it was interrupted by the knock.

When the ritual is completed, the officers and members exchange the Sign of the Enterer, and then the Sign of Silence, as they leave the temple one by one. The doorkeeper is the last to leave, and he closes and locks the door.

## The Closing Ritual

The Opening Ritual is performed, followed by the main ritual, and then the Closing Ritual is performed.

The Closing Ritual is the same as the Opening Ritual, except that the order of the steps is reversed, and the words 'Opening' and 'Closing' are interchanged.
