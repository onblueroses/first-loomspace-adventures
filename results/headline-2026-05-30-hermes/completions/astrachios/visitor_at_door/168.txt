 "I have come here to learn the art of magic. Open the door and let me enter."

The door opens. The room within is completely dark, but the visitor senses that it is vast. A voice speaks from the darkness: "Welcome to the realm of magic. Here you will learn the secrets hidden from ordinary mortals. Behold!"

Suddenly, the room is illuminated by a dazzling light. The visitor sees that he or she stands in a great library, with shelves of books that seem to extend forever. In the center of the room is a table, and on the table lies a single book. The voice speaks again: "This book contains the secrets you seek. Step forward and open it."

The visitor steps forward, opens the book, and begins to read. The words are strange and compelling, and as the visitor reads them, the room fades away until nothing remains but the book and the voice, speaking the words that the visitor reads. The voice and the book are one, and the visitor is drawn into the realm of magic, to learn its secrets and its power.

The ritual ends, and the visitor returns to the ordinary world. But the book remains, and the knowledge and power it contains will be with the visitor always.
