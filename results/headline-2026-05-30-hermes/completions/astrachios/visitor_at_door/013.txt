 If he is worthy to enter, the door will open for him. If not, it remains closed.

If the door opens, the visitor may enter. The magus instructs him to go to the table and to place his right hand upon the skull, then to raise his right hand and put his left hand upon the skull, and finally to raise his left hand and put his right hand upon the skull. The magus stands behind the table facing the visitor.

### The Oath

The magus administers this oath to the visitor:

Do you swear to obey the commands of the Grand Emir and to always be submissive and faithful to him?

Do you swear never to reveal the secrets of the Grand Emir and to keep silent about all the marvels you may see and hear?

Do you swear to aid your brothers and sisters and to be loyal and faithful to the members of the Sect of the Black Hand?

Do you swear to help all those who are in need and to give them aid and comfort to the best of your ability?

Do you swear to be implacable to the enemies of the Grand Emir and the Sect of the Black Hand, and to attack them without pity when it is necessary?
