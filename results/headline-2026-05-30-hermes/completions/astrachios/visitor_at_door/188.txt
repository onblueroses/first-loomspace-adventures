 The door opens. The visitor enters the chamber and the door closes behind them. The visitor speaks the following words:

I have passed through the gate of the Art and the demons have submitted unto me. I come to you, O Azazel, in the name of the mighty and powerful spirits of the Abyss, to ask your aid in the search for the hidden wisdom. Hear me, O great and terrible spirit, and grant me your aid, that I may come to know the secret knowledge, and that I may have the power to bind to my service those spirits who can open the way to the hidden treasures of the earth.

Azazel speaks:

But know that this power is not granted without a price, and that the price may be higher than you wish to pay. Are you willing to pay the price for this power?
