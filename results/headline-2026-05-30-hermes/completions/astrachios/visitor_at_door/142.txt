 The gatekeeper opens the door.

Gatekeeper: What is your name?

Visitor: I am called N.

Gatekeeper: Why do you come here?

Visitor: To learn the mysteries of the Holy Grail.

Gatekeeper: You may enter. Be welcome.

The visitor enters, and the door is closed.

Gatekeeper: In the name of the Holy Grail, I purify you with water and salt. 

The gatekeeper sprinkles the visitor with holy water, then traces the tau cross on the visitor's forehead with salt, saying:

Gatekeeper: In this sign you are sealed, in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. Amen.

The gatekeeper leads the visitor sunwise around the altar three times, then brings him to the west of the altar, facing the priestess. The priestess places the point of her sword on the visitor's heart, saying:

Priestess: Swear to keep secret what you learn here, or accept the penalty of this blade.

Visitor: I swear.
