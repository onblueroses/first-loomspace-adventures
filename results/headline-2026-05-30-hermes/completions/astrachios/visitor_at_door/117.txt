The visitor is led to the altar, where the priest waits. The priest says “In the name of the holy mysteries, I welcome you to this temple. May your sacrifice be acceptable to the gods of the temple, and may you receive the blessings of the mysteries.”

The visitor is led to the altar, where they kneel. The priest takes a chalice filled with wine, and says “I consecrate this wine, the blood of the gods, in the name of the mysteries of the temple.
