 He is Theophilus, the secretary of the sorcerer.

THEOPHILUS

Master, I have brought the things you desired. The white cock and hen, the cage, the two pigeons, the charcoal, the wild herbs, the vervain and the magnets, the wax images, the nails from an old coffin, the blessed taper, and the pentacles.

The sorcerer speaks.

SORCERER

Good, Theophilus. Did you make the pact?

THEOPHILUS

Yes, master.

SORCERER

And did you sign?

THEOPHILUS

I signed, master.

SORCERER

In your blood?

THEOPHILUS

In my blood, master.

SORCERER

Good. You are mine, body and soul, for twenty years, and I shall have the use of your sight. But first take these herbs and the charcoal and prepare the fire.

THEOPHILUS

Yes, master.

The sorcerer moves to the window and looks out.

SORCERER

The sun sets; the shadows fall. The night comes, and in the night the powers that serve me. The dark is my light and the moon my sun.
