 “I wish to enter this holy place of power and partake of the mysteries within.”

The guardian of the door speaks. “Place your hand upon the seal of the mysteries.”

The visitor places their hand upon the seal. The guardian traces a sigil of entry on their forehead and speaks. “Enter then and learn the secrets herein, but know that your heart is bound to the sacred powers of this place. Keep true to your oaths and all will be well.”

The visitor enters and the door is closed.

The ritual is ended.

To close the gate and release the spirits:

Stand before the altar and say:

“Spirits of the four quarters, I thank you for your presence and power. Return now to your secret abodes and abodes, and let there be peace between us. May the blessings of the mysteries go with you. Amen.”

Snuff out the candles in reverse order, saying at each:

“Go in peace, spirits of the (direction). Blessed be.”

When all the candles are snuffed out, the ritual is ended.

The circle is open but never broken.

Merry meet, and merry part, and merry meet again.

Blessed be.
