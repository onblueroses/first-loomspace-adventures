 He may be anyone the magician has summoned, but it is often the spirit Scirlin, who acts as a sort of doorkeeper or guide to the rest of the spirits. Scirlin should be asked to bring the magician any spirit he desires to speak with.

When the conversation is finished, the magician should give the spirit leave to depart. He then dismisses the circle with the following words:

Besticitum consolatio veni ad me vertat Creon, Creon, Creon, cantorla Agalio, Agalio, Agalio.

He then blows out the candles and departs as he came. 

The manuscript then continues with a series of spells or charms, each with its own ritual, and often accompanied by a drawing. The spells are not all for spiritual communication, but cover a wide range from finding treasure to preventing illness. The rest of the manuscript is in a similar vein, describing various spells and rituals, with a heavy emphasis on the names of the various spirits that the magician would be working with.
