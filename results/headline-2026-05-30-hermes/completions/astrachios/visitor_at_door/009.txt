 "I have come to converse with the spirits of this place, to hear their wisdom and their words. I come with respect, and with the names of power that compel the spirits to heed my call."

The door opens. The visitor enters the First Chamber, where a table is set with a banquet of fine food and drink. Spirits in the forms of men and women sit at the table. The visitor greets them with courtesy, and they offer him a place at the feast. He eats and drinks with them, and as he does, they share with him the secrets of the treasures hidden in the earth, and the lore of the spirits who dwell therein.

After a time, the visitor rises from the table, and goes to the door in the east wall of the chamber. He speaks again:

"By the power of the names I have spoken, and by the fellowship I have shared with the spirits of this realm, I call upon the guardian of the Second Chamber to open the way."
