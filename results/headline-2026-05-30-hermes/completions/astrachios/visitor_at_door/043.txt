 If he is an enemy, he cannot enter, for the power of the names of the guardian spirits, the _genii domus_, will prevent him from taking another step. But if he is a friend, he may enter in safety, and the spirits will not harm him.

It is said that the Jews also used this formula when they wished to make sure that the spirits of evil should not come through the door.

* * * * *

The cabalists used a certain square of numbers, which they called the square of Saturn, to protect themselves against evil spirits and dangers of all kinds. This square was arranged in a peculiar manner, as follows:

9 4 7 3 5 7 6 5 3 2 9 1 8 6 4 2 1 8

The peculiar thing about this square is that the sum of the numbers in each column, either way you add them, is always fifteen. The numbers are supposed to represent the sacred name of Yahweh, and the square is to be cut on a plate of lead, or written with black ink on a piece of parchment, which is to be carried about the person. This will preserve the bearer from all earthly dangers.
