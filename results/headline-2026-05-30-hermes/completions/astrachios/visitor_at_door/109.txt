 I will now describe the signs you must make. First, the Sign of the Enterer, or of Projection, which is thus:

Stand facing East, or in the direction of the door. Imagine a closed door between yourself and the spirit you wish to evoke. The door is the barrier that separates the physical and astral planes. Draw a deep breath and focus your will power upon the door. Raise your right hand and thrust it forward at shoulder level with the palm turned down, the fingers closed and pointing forward. At the same time, step forward with your left foot. The action should be made with a single explosive motion. As you project your hand and foot, expel your breath and shout the name of the spirit you wish to evoke.

If you wish to project the power of a spirit that you have evoked to the circle, so that it may act on the material world, make the Sign of the Enterer while standing facing the direction opposite the point of the circle where the spirit is present. For example, if the spirit is in the North, face South.
