The visitor enters the circle, and all present greet him with the words "Welcome, brother magician." The guardian then closes the door, and the assembly continues with the ceremony of conjuration.

---

In the same way, the ceremony can be used for a solitary magician who wishes to invoke the power of Solomon before beginning a working of the _Lemegeton_. The ritual of opening the temple is performed as described above, but the magician walks the path of the spiral alone, and speaks the words of both the guardian and the visitor at the door. When the ceremony is done, the magician proceeds with the conjuration of the _Lemegeton_ spirits in the usual way.
