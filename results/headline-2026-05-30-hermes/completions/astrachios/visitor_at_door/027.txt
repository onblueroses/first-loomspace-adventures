 The person who is opening the door must respond, "Come in."

[The visitor enters the circle.]

Visitor: I am here at the door and am prepared for the work of the mysteries, and I will keep my mouth shut concerning these things.

The person who opened the door responds: Happy is the one who comes in the name of Adonai.

[The visitor enters the circle and the door is closed.]

[The person who opened the door takes their place at the head of the table.]

[The person at the head of the table takes the chalice in their right hand and the wand in their left hand, and says:]

In the name of Adonai Elohim, I bless this chalice, and in the name of the all-powerful Adonai, it is done.

[They make the sign of the cross over the chalice with the wand.]

[They put down the wand and chalice, and take the incense burner and the wand, and say:]

In the name of Adonai Elohim, I bless this incense, and in the name of the all-powerful Adonai, it is done.

[They make the sign of the cross over the incense with the wand.]
