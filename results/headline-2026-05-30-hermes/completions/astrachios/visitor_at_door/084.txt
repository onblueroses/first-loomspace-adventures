 He speaks the name of the person he wishes to see. If he does not know the name, he speaks the purpose of his visit. If the purpose is not known, he speaks the nature of his business.

The doorkeeper speaks. He speaks the nature of the business within, and whether the person sought is present. If the person is present, the doorkeeper speaks his name.

The visitor speaks. He speaks his own name. He speaks his purpose in seeking the person named.

The doorkeeper speaks. He speaks permission or denial to enter. If permission is granted, the doorkeeper opens the door.

The visitor enters, or departs, in peace. The doorkeeper closes the door.

The doorkeeper returns to his place. The ritual is at an end.
