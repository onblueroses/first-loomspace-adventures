### Inside the Crypt

The crypt has 20-foot-high ceilings. The walls are covered in frescos depicting the history of the Amber Temple. The images are faded and worn. The central chamber of the crypt contains a dozen large stone sarcophagi, their lids carved to resemble noble men and women. The lids of ten sarcophagi are askew, the coffins empty. Two remain sealed and untouched.

The sealed sarcophagi contain two of the temple's original guardians:

- **Lars Kjurls** (male human wizard)
- **Neferon** (male human cleric)
