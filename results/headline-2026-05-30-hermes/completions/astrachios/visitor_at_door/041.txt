# 3. Consecration of the Temple

All present join in the following words, led by the senior adept present:

"May the blessings of the Infinite Light be upon this place, and upon all who enter herein to seek the Mysteries. May the powers of the elements of Earth, Air, Water, and Fire, and the spirits of the elemental realms, be present here to guard and to witness. Grant to us the presence of your wisdom, that we may learn from your example. Grant to us the presence of your harmony, that we may find balance within ourselves. Grant to us the presence of your power, that we may overcome all obstacles. Grant to us the presence of your love, that we may grow in knowledge and unity with all beings. We consecrate this temple to the work of the Mysteries. May it be a place of light, of life, and of love, from this time onward."

All: "So may it be."
