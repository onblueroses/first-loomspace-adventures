I hear your request, and I obey the sacred names. Enter in peace.

The visitor enters the room, and the ritual is complete. The two participants may then engage in conversation or other activities as desired.

The ritual is ended by the person inside the room speaking the following words:
I thank you for your presence. Go in peace, and may the spirits of the Grand Grimoire guide your path. As you leave, I seal this room once more.
