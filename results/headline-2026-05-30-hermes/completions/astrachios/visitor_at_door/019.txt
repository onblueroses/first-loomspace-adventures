 I say to you, open and let me pass. Turn the key and let the gate be opened. In the name of the secret and the mighty, I command the lock and the chains to fall away. Let the door be opened, for I am the one who must pass through. Speak the words of passage:

Rada, Agas, Azer, Abat, Agat, Yat, Aenot, Agarat, Yat, Azet, Aglaganar, Yat, Azet, Agas, Azer, Abat, Agat, Aenot, Agarat, Yat, Azet, Aglaganar, Yat, Azet, Radou.

The visitor at the door speaks. I have spoken the words. I have commanded the door to open. I have named myself in the ancient tongues. Let the door be opened. Let the gate swing wide. Let me pass through. I command it. I demand it. I will it. I compel it. I summon it. I speak it. I name it. It is done.

The door opens. The visitor enters. The door closes.
