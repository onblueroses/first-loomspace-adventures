 

I have come seeking the wisdom of Solomon, to learn the secrets of the universe and the arts of magic. I ask for entry into your domain, to share in the knowledge that has been granted to you.

The spirit responds.

By the power of the names I have spoken, and by the authority of Solomon, I grant you entry. But know this: the knowledge you seek comes with great responsibility. Use it wisely, for the consequences of misuse can be severe. Enter, and learn.

The visitor enters the domain of the spirit. The two engage in a deep discussion, the spirit sharing the secrets of the universe and the arts of magic. The visitor listens attentively, absorbing the wisdom imparted.

As the discussion draws to a close, the spirit speaks once more.

Remember, the knowledge you have gained is a gift. Use it to bring light into the world, to help and to heal. Do not let it lead you astray, for the path of magic is fraught with temptation. Go now, and may the wisdom of Solomon guide your path.
