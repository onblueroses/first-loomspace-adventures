 In the language of the city, they say:

"Knock, knock, I wish to enter, for I am of the people of the city."

The doorkeeper replies:

"Enter, friend, and welcome to the city."

The visitor enters the city. The doorkeeper asks them:

"What is your name?"

The visitor replies with their name. The doorkeeper then asks:

"What brings you to the city?"

The visitor replies, telling of their purpose in the city. The doorkeeper then says:

"May you find what you seek. The blessings of the city be upon you."

The visitor thanks the doorkeeper and goes on their way. The doorkeeper then says:
