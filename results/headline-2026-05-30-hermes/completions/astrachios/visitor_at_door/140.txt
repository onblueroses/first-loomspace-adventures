 “I have come to seek the wisdom of the spirits, to see what they have to reveal, and to ask their aid in the work of magic.”

The Guardian of the Threshold speaks. “By what right do you ask this?”

The visitor speaks. “By the right of the knowledge I have obtained, the work I have accomplished, and the guidance I have received from the spiritual powers.”

The Guardian of the Threshold speaks. “Enter, therefore, and may the blessing of the Mighty Ones be upon you.”

The Guardian of the Threshold makes the Sign of the Enterer in the doorway, and admits the visitor. All proceed to the altar. The Guardian of the Threshold remains in the west, by the portal. The other celebrants form a circle around the altar.
