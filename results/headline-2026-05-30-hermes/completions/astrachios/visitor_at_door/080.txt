### 64. To make oneself invisible

The preliminary operation is to go to a field at sunrise on the day of Mercury, in the first phase of the moon. You must have a parchment inscribed with the magic words listed below. Hold the parchment up to the sky, and say:

_Adonai, Eloim, Chavot, Hacoba, Helyom, Hag, Aghton, Helyon, Yod, Tetragrammaton, Saday, Messias, Agiel, Agios, Yschyros, Athanatos, Agla, Yod, Beel, Y, Yod, H, Vau, H, Yod, H, Metatron, H, Yod, Cados, H, Eloyn, H, Agla, H, Y, Yod, H, Vau, H, Saday, H, Agios, H, Yschyros, H, Athanatos, H, Yod, H, Y, Yod, H, Vau, H, Yod, H, Metatron
