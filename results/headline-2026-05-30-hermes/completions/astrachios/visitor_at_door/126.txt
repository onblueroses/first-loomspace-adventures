 The door opens, revealing the entrance to a vast cavern. The visitor enters and the door closes behind them.

In the center of the cavern, the visitor sees a large, glowing crystal. The crystal pulses with an otherworldly light, casting eerie shadows on the cavern walls. The visitor approaches the crystal, feeling drawn to its power.

As the visitor reaches out to touch the crystal, a deep voice echoes through the cavern:

"Welcome, seeker of knowledge. You have proven your worth by speaking the names of power. The crystal before you holds the secrets of the universe. But be warned - the knowledge you seek comes at a price. Those who are unworthy will be consumed by the crystal's power. Do you still wish to proceed?"

The visitor must make a decision. They can reach out and touch the crystal, risking being consumed by its power in exchange for ultimate knowledge. Or they can turn back, leaving the cavern and the crystal behind, forever wondering what secrets they left undiscovered.

The choice is yours, seeker. Will you claim the power that awaits you, or will you turn back from the abyss? Choose wisely, for the crystal does not forgive those who hesitate.
