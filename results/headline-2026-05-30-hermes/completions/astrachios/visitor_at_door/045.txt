I ask the spirits of the door to let me pass. By the power of the key and by the pact of the spirits, I ask to enter.

The spirits will ask the visitor to identify themselves by their true name. The visitor must comply, and the spirits will let them pass.

#### The Room

Inside the room is a table, upon which lies a small wooden box. Inside the box is the treasure.

The spirits will ask the visitor what they seek. The visitor must reply as follows:

I seek the treasure of the spirits.

The spirits will ask the visitor why they seek the treasure. The visitor must reply as follows:

I seek the treasure to aid me in my quest for [state your goal or desire here].

If the spirits deem the visitor's goal or desire worthy, they will allow them to take the treasure. If not, the visitor must leave the room and the door will be sealed forever.

#### The Treasure

The treasure is a small, ancient coin made of gold. It bears the image of a serpent on one side and a tree on the other. The coin is imbued with the power of the spirits and will bring the visitor good fortune and success in their stated goal or desire.
