 They are asked to state their purpose, and they must answer with all honesty, for the guardian at the door will know if they lie. The guardian will then decide if they will permit the visitor to enter, according to the nature of their purpose.

If the visitor is permitted to enter, the guardian will ask them to speak the name of power that forces all spirits to be truthful in their speech and to obey. The visitor must then say:

“By the sacred name of Agla, which is inscribed upon the front of Aaron’s rod, I command you to be faithful to your oath and to protect me from all deceit and harm.”

The guardian will then open the door and allow the visitor to enter. When the door is opened, the visitor will see that the room beyond is filled with light, but they must not hesitate or draw back, for the guardian will close the door behind them. The visitor must step forward boldly into the light, knowing that they are under the protection of the name Agla, and that no spirit can harm them while they remain within the bounds of the room.
