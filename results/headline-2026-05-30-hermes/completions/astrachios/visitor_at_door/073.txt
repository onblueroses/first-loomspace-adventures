 They say:

I am a servant of the Divine. I have come to speak with the spirits of the dead and with the spirits of the living. I ask that you allow me to enter, to learn, and to leave in safety.

The visitor at the door speaks the names that open the way:

Astrachios, Asac, Acra Bedrimuliel, Silat, Arabonas, Irehalem, Ideodoc, Archarzel, Blautel, Baracata, Adonai, Elohim, Emagro, Abragateh, Samoel, Geburahel, Cadato, Era, Elohi, Achsah, Ebmisha, Imachadel, Daniel, Dama, Elamos, Izachel, Bael, Scirlin; Genium Domos.

The spirits reply. They say:

We are the spirits of the dead and the living. We open the way for you to enter. Speak with us, learn from us, and leave in safety. We welcome you.

The spirits of the dead and the living open the way. The visitor enters the graveyard.
