 "I have come from the uttermost parts of the earth. I have come from the end of the world to seek the wisdom of our lord, the master of mysteries, the great Solomon, son of David."

The demon replies, "I am Byleth, one of the princes of the demons. But I submit to the will of the master. What is it that you seek of me?"

The visitor speaks. "I seek the power of the mysteries, to bind and loose the spirits, to heal the sick, to drive out disease, to be a messenger of the gods."

The demon replies, "Many have come seeking power, but few have the strength to wield it. The mysteries are a great burden, a weight that can crush the weak. But for those who are strong, they are the source of all knowledge and power. Are you strong enough to bear this burden?"

The visitor speaks. "I am ready to bear the burden. I have been prepared by my trials and my sufferings. I have been purified by the sacred fire. I am ready to receive the mysteries."

The demon replies, "Then enter, and may the gods have mercy on your soul."

The door opens, and the visitor enters the temple. The door closes behind them
