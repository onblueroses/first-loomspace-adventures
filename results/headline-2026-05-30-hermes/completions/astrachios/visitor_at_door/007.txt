 The doorkeeper answers.

Doorkeeper: Who are you?

Visitor: I am a faithful servant of the secret and the light, N.

Doorkeeper: What do you want?

Visitor: I wish to enter the assembly of the children of the knowledge and the truth.

The doorkeeper opens the door, gives the sign, and says:

Doorkeeper: Pass, N. The assembly welcomes you.

The doorkeeper closes the door.

The initiate enters the temple, advances to the center, and bows before the altar, hands joined. The initiate then rises, and gives a sign of benediction, saying:

Initiate: May the light of the divine spirit illuminate our hearts and our minds, now and forever.

The initiate takes a seat in the temple.

The ritual of opening follows.

The Guardian of the Temple takes the sword, advances to the center of the temple, and recites the following exorcism:

Guardian of the Temple: By the power of the grand words of the Clavicule of Solomon, I banish from this temple all spirits of darkness. Be gone, invisible beings of the shadows; you cannot enter this place of purity and light.

The Guardian of the Temple traces a circle with the sword, and says:
