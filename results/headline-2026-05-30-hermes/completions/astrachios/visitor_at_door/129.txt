 It opens and he enters. The King of the West, in the name of the Grand Master, demands of him the word, grip and sign of a Grand Elect Perfect and Sublime Master.

The Grand Marshal conducts him to the altar, where he kneels on the right knee, and lays his left hand on the Bible, and his right hand on his heart, and repeats the following obligation:

"I, A. B., of my own free will and accord, in the presence of Almighty God, and this Grand Elect Perfect and Sublime Lodge of Perfect and Sublime Masters, do hereby and hereon most solemnly and sincerely promise and swear, in addition to my former obligations, that I will not give the secrets of a Grand Elect Perfect and Sublime Master to any one of an inferior degree, nor to any other person in the known world, except it be to a well-tried, faithful brother of this degree, and not unto him, until after due trial, strict examination, or by the full consent of a regular Lodge of the same.
