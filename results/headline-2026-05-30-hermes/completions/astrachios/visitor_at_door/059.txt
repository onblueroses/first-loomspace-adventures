 The doorkeeper admits the visitor. The doorkeeper gives the visitor a drink. The doorkeeper leads the visitor to the East, where the master and officers sit. The master stands, takes the visitor by the hand, and leads the visitor to the center of the hall.

The master speaks:

I bring you from the darkness into the light. I bring you from the dungeon into the palace. I bring you from the lowest to the highest. In the name of the grand master of the seventh palace, I receive you as a master mason and a member of this venerable order.

The master leads the visitor to the West. The master speaks:

I will now teach you the signs, tokens, and words of this degree. The first sign is to place your right hand on your heart. This is in memory of the oath you have taken. The second sign is to place your right hand on your lips. This means that you must keep our secrets. The third sign is to place your right hand on your forehead. This means that you must always act with wisdom. The token is to take each other by the right hand and place the other hand on the shoulder. The word is "Jakin," which means "he establishes."
