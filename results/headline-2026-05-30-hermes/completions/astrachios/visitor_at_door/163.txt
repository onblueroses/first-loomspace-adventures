 The Guardian replies, and the door is opened.

Guardian: Quit the Night and seek the Day!

The candidate is led in by the hand. The door is closed. The Guardian leads the candidate sunwise in a spiral to the center of the circle, and turns the candidate to face the east. The Guardian then leaves the candidate there, going to the altar and waiting there.

Magister: In the presence of the holy powers of the elements, and of the hidden powers of the depths and the heights, what do you seek?

Candidate: I seek the path of wisdom and the way of power.

Magister: The path of wisdom is found by the wise, and the way of power is known to the skillful. If you seek these things, take heed. Set foot upon this way, and three things will follow. First, you will know power, and with it the freedom to shape your life and the world around you. Second, you will know responsibility, and the knowledge that all your actions toward yourself and others will return to you threefold and sevenfold.
