### The Doorkeeper's Response

The doorkeeper's response indicates whether the visitor is granted entry or not. If the doorkeeper says "The way is open," it means the visitor has successfully gained entry by speaking the correct names. If the doorkeeper remains silent or says anything else, it indicates the visitor is not granted entry, likely because they did not recite the names correctly.

The exact origins and meanings of many of these names are unclear, as they are often derived from a mix of real and fictitious sources, with many variations and corruptions over time.
