 (If the visitor is a man, he says:)

I am he who is the Adonai of the Earth, come in peace to greet my sister and my bride.

(If the visitor is a woman, she says:)

I am she who is the Astarte of the Earth, come in peace to greet my brother and my groom.

The person who has knocked and spoken the words of opening enters the temple, and goes to the west of the altar, facing east. The person who opened the door stands in the east, facing west across the altar. Both make the Sign of the Enterer, and then the Sign of the Equilibrium.

Second: The temple is purified with water and consecrated with fire, as in the opening ritual of the Golden Dawn.

Third: All present say the following words:

We are gathered to celebrate the mysteries of the gods, the forces of life and love and liberty. Let the powers of the planets and the Zodiacal signs witness and guard our work.

Fourth: All present say the following words:

Holy art Thou, Lord of the Universe, Ain Soph Aur, the Infinite Light!

Holy art Thou, O Yod He Vau He, the Tetragrammaton, the Unity of the Ten Sephiro
