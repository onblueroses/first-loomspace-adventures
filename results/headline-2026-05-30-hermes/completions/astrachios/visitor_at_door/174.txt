 If the visitor is a member of the household, the doorkeeper opens the door and greets them with a kiss on the cheek. If the visitor is a stranger, the doorkeeper asks them their name and business. If the doorkeeper is satisfied with their answer, they open the door and greet them with a kiss on the cheek. If the doorkeeper is not satisfied, they close the peephole and say:

The way is closed to you. May you find what you seek elsewhere.

The doorkeeper then returns to their seat. The ritual is complete.
