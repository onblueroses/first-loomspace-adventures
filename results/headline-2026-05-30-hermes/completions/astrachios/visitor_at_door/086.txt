 The door is opened.
The initiate enters, blindfolded.
The blindfold is removed.

The initiate is instructed to relax, to breathe deeply, and to open themselves to the experience that is about to unfold.

The initiate is led to the altar, where they kneel.

The ritual leader speaks.

In the name of the Most High, I consecrate this seeker, [initiate's name], who comes to us with an open heart and a sincere desire to explore the mysteries of our tradition.

May the divine light illuminate their path, and may the spirits of our ancestors guide them on their journey.

The ritual leader anoints the initiate with oil on the forehead, chest, and hands.

I anoint you with this sacred oil, a symbol of your dedication to the path of wisdom and the pursuit of truth.

May it serve as a reminder of your commitment to the mysteries and to your own spiritual growth.

The ritual leader presents the initiate with a chalice of water.

Drink from this chalice, a symbol of the knowledge and wisdom you seek.

May it quench your thirst for understanding and nourish your soul on its journey.

The initiate drinks from the chalice.
