 He says the following:

I have come from the place of punishment. I have come from the place of mercy and redemption. I have passed through the place of purification and the place of the balance. Give me your hand, and welcome me into your dwelling. I have come to serve you, and to honor you.

The doorkeeper takes the visitor's hand, and draws him inside. The door is closed behind them.

The doorkeeper speaks:

Enter, and be welcome. What is your name?

The visitor speaks his name. The doorkeeper speaks:

In this place, you shall be known as N., a spirit of the dead. You have no other name and no other identity. Do you accept this name?

The visitor agrees to accept the name given to him by the doorkeeper.

The doorkeeper speaks:

What gift do you bring to lay upon the altar of the dead?

The visitor names his gift, and lays it upon the altar. The doorkeeper speaks:

Your gift is accepted. May it sustain and please the spirits of the dead. N., I give you this token of our fellowship. Wear it so that the other spirits will know you are one of us.
