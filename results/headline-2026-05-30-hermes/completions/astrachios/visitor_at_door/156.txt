 If he has been named, he speaks his name in return, and the door is opened to him. If not, he must depart, for there is no place within for the unnamed.

The keeper of the door, or the host of the meeting, then says:

Enter, for you have been recognized.

The ritual of opening the door may be repeated for each visitor, or if many are expected, it may be done once to open the way for all those who have been named.

## Chapter 2: The Ritual of the Key

The ritual of the key is a general ritual to open the way. It should be done at the start of every working of astral theurgy to unlock the gate between worlds. The ritual is conducted by a single person, who acts as the opener of the way for the others who may be present. More than one person can do the ritual together, but this is not necessary. The ritual is equally effective when done by one person working alone.
