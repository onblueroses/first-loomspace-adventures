 The door is opened.

The visitor enters, the door is closed.

The visitor says: I am N.

The host says: You are welcome here, N.

The visitor says: I come in peace.

The host says: We accept you in peace.

The visitor says: May all who come and go by this door be blessed.

The host says: May all who come and go by this door be blessed.

The host says: Enter and join us, N.

The visitor enters the gathering.

The ritual continues as before, with the visitor now a part of the gathering.

## 11. Closing

When the time comes to end the gathering, the host speaks.

The host says: We have gathered in peace and fellowship, and now it is time to depart. May the blessings of this gathering go with us, and may we carry the spirit of unity and harmony with us in our hearts.

All say: May it be so.

The host says: Let us now depart in peace.

All say: In peace, let us depart.

The ritual is ended. The gathering is concluded, and the participants depart.
