 “I am he who is called N.N. I ask for entry into the Lodge of the Freemasons of the Rose Cross, so that I may work with them and they may work with me, and we may all work together for the greater glory of the thrice-great Art of the Rosicrucians. May the genii of the place permit me to enter.”

The Guardian replies. “Enter this place in the name of the genii who guard it.”

The visitor enters, advances to the center of the Lodge, and faces the Master in the east. “I come to learn, if the brethren within will teach.”

The Master speaks. “It is our duty and our joy to share what we have learned with those who seek the Light.”

The brethren and the visitor sit. The Master speaks. “What knowledge do you seek?”

The visitor replies. “The art of summoning the Great Entity that the R+C calls the Gnostic Pentad.”

The Master speaks. “This is a great and powerful secret, one that has been guarded for centuries. Do you pledge to keep it in the utmost secrecy, to use it wisely and well, and to preserve the reputation of our Order?”

The visitor replies. “I do so pledge.”
