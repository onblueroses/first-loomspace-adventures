 “I seek the favor of the spirit Scirlin to aid me in my quest. I have brought an offering of milk, honey, and bread.”

The door opens. The spirit Scirlin appears, surrounded by a shining light. “I greet you, traveler. I am Scirlin, the guardian of the mysteries. What is it that you seek?”

The visitor bows their head. “Wise Scirlin, I come seeking guidance and knowledge. I wish to learn the secrets of the spirits, to understand their ways and to gain their favor.”

Scirlin nods. “Very well. I shall grant you my aid, but know that the path you seek is not an easy one. The spirits are many and their ways are complex. To truly understand them, you must be willing to dedicate yourself to their study.”

“I am ready, Scirlin. I will do whatever is necessary.”

“Then listen closely. The first step is to learn the names and offices of the spirits. Each has their own domain and their own powers. Some are benevolent, others are not. You must learn to distinguish between them.”
