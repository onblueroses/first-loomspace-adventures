 “The password is ‘Baphomet.’”

The doorkeeper responds. “The password is correct. You may enter.”

The visitor enters the room. The doorkeeper closes the door. The visitor and doorkeeper embrace, and the doorkeeper says “Do what thou wilt shall be the whole of the Law.”

The visitor replies: “Love is the law, love under will.”

The doorkeeper then leads the visitor to the altar, where the visitor repeats the same exchange with each of the officers.

The doorkeeper announces, “All present will please be seated.”

The officers take their seats, and the doorkeeper sits near the altar, to the right of the Priestess.

The Priestess rises and gives one knock with her wand. All rise.

The Priestess invokes as follows:

Unity uttermost showed!
I adore the might of Thy breath,
Supreme and terrible God,
Who makest the Gods and death
To tremble before Thee:—
I, I adore thee!

The officers give the Sign of Benediction.
