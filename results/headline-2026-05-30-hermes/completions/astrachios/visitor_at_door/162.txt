### The Grand Chamber

The Grand Chamber is a large circular room with a domed ceiling. The walls are adorned with intricate carvings and frescoes depicting the history and achievements of the Brotherhood. In the center of the room stands a large, circular table made of polished black marble, surrounded by thirteen high-backed chairs. Each chair is adorned with intricate carvings and inlaid with precious stones, representing the thirteen founding members of the Brotherhood.

The Grand Chamber serves as the meeting place for the Brotherhood's high council, where important decisions are made and strategies are discussed. The room is illuminated by a series of braziers placed around the perimeter, casting a warm, flickering light that dances across the ornate decorations.

### The Library

The Library is a vast, two-story room filled with bookshelves that reach from floor to ceiling. The shelves are packed with ancient tomes, scrolls, and manuscripts containing the accumulated knowledge of the Brotherhood. A series of balconies and walkways provide access to the upper levels, while ladders on wheels allow for easy navigation of the lower shelves.
