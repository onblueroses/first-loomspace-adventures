 "I have come to be initiated into the mysteries of the holy order of the Knights of the Golden Circle. I come of my own free will and accord, and I will not reveal to any human being any of the secrets that may be confided to me."

The Sentinel opens the door, and the visitor is greeted by the Grand Knight of the Exchequer, who says, "It is well. You have permission to advance, but let me warn you that if you come with any bad intentions, you had better not enter. The Knights of the Golden Circle are loyal and true to each other, but they are sworn to execute vengeance on traitors. Do you still wish to proceed?"

The visitor answers, "I do."

The Grand Knight of the Exchequer says, "Very well, then. You will now take upon yourself the obligation of the First Degree."
