He walks down the corridor, his footsteps echoing in the silence. The air grows colder as he approaches the door at the end of the hall. It is made of black stone, carved with symbols that seem to writhe and twist in the flickering torchlight.

The man stops before the door and raises his hand to knock. But before his knuckles can touch the stone, the door swings open with a sound like the groan of a dying man.
