 (His voice should be firm and calm.)

Hail, guardians of the four quarters, and watchers at the threshold. I come to visit this place of power, to work in harmony with the spirits and forces assembled here. Grant me entry, and the aid of your wisdom and strength.

(The guardian answers.)

Enter, then, with our blessing, and go in peace when you depart.

(If the visitor is not acceptable to the spirits, the guardian will warn him off at this point. If he seems acceptable but the guardian is not quite certain, he may ask the visitor to give his magical name or state his purpose before allowing him entry. The guardian should be guided by his intuition and that of the other people present in making this decision.)

(The guardian opens the door and admits the visitor.)

[The ritual of opening continues.]
