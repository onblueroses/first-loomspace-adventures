## The Twenty-eighth Lunar Mansion

The Twenty-eighth Lunar Mansion is the Mansion of the Heart. It is ruled by the angel Acrabiel.

When the moon is in the Twenty-eighth Lunar Mansion, it is good to sow seeds, plant trees and crops, and to travel.

### The Twenty-eighth Lunar Mansion Talisman

The talisman for the Twenty-eighth Lunar Mansion is a pentacle of gold, with a heart in the center and the sigil of Acrabiel on the back.

To make the talisman, wait until the moon is in the Twenty-eighth Lunar Mansion. On the front of the pentacle, engrave a heart, and on the back, engrave the sigil of Acrabiel.

When you have made the talisman, hold it and say the words:

Acrabiel, angel of the Heart, I ask that you give me the love and companionship I desire.

Carry the talisman with you, and it will bring you love and companionship.
