 He must ask permission to enter the Lodge of the Holy Saints. He says:

O Guardians who guard the door, O powerful Princes of this place, allow me to enter. I am of the same Lodge as you. I have seen the same lights as you. Open the door to me, and peace be with you.

The Guardian replies:

You have seen the lights and you have recognized them. Enter, in the name of the Genii of the Seven Planets, and peace be with you.

As the door opens, the Guardian must touch the visitor's right shoulder, and the visitor must touch the Guardian's right shoulder at the same time. The visitor enters the Lodge, and the Guardian closes the door.

The Master of Ceremonies approaches the visitor, takes his right hand, and leads him before the Throne of the Master of the Lodge. The Master of Ceremonies says:

Approach the Throne of the Master, by the names of Cadario, Eamsal, and Alcanor, which are the names of the three high columns of the Temple of the Lodge.

The visitor kneels on his right knee before the Master. The Master says:
