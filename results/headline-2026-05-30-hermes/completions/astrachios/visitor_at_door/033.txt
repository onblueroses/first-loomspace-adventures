## 9. The Angel of the Sun

The angel of the Sun will appear. Bow to the angel and say:

I thank you for coming, and for bringing your light to guide me on this path. Please, I ask of you: teach me the mysteries of the sphere of the Sun, and grant me the power to command the spirits of the Sun that I may work wonders in the world.

The angel of the Sun speaks. Listen to its words.

## 10. The Spirits of the Sun

The angel of the Sun will give you the names of six spirits under its command. These spirits will help you in matters related to the Sun.

The spirits of the Sun appear. Speak to them:

I thank you for coming, and for bringing your light to guide me on this path. I have called you here to form a bond between us, so that I may call on you in the future to aid me in my work. In exchange, I will work to spread the light of the Sun in the world.

The spirits of the Sun speak. Listen to their words.

## 11. Closing the Temple

The spirits of the Sun depart. The angel of the Sun speaks again. Listen to its words.
