## 5\. The Opening
The circle is purified with fire and water, and the four directions are invoked using the pentagrams and names of banishing as given in the Neophyte Ritual. The names of the archangels are vibrated in the same way.
The adept then performs the Greater Invoking Ritual of the Pentagram of the element governing the operation. He asperges and censes the altar and the circle with the holy water and consecrated fire in the elemental manner. The Kerubim are invoked, and the elemental prayer is recited.
The adept stands in the eastern side of the circle and faces the direction of the Enochian elemental tablet that governs the operation. He traces the invoking hexagram of Fire in the air with the dagger or with the fire wand and intones the Enochian name of the elemental king governing the operation, adding the name of the elemental king of the quarter, if it is different:
