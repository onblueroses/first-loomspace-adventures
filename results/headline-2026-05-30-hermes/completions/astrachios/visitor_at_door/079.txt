 The visitor speaks the names that open the way.

“Hail, hidden master of this place. Hail, silent watcher at the gate. Hail, and open the way for me. Grant me passage to the mysteries beyond this portal, for I have spoken the names of power that command the gates: Astrachios, Asac, Acra Bedrimuliel, Silat, Arabonas, Irehalem, Ideodoc, Archarzel, Blautel, Baracata, Adonai, Elohim, Emagro, Abragateh, Samoel, Geburahel, Cadato, Era, Elohi, Achsah, Ebmisha, Imachadel, Daniel, Dama, Elamos, Izachel, Bael, Scirlin; Genium Domos. By the power of these names, I claim the right to enter. Open the way!”

The guardian opens the door, admitting the visitor to the lodge room.

“Enter, therefore, and join us in the work of the Mysteries.”

The visitor enters, and the door is closed.
