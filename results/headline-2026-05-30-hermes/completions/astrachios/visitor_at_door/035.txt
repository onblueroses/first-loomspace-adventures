Enter, in the name of the Great Sorcerer.

The visitor enters the lodge. The doorkeeper announces:

Brother (or Sister) [magical name], a practitioner of the Art.

The master of ceremonies comes to the altar, takes the book, and carries it to the visitor. The visitor places his or her right hand on the book, and the master of ceremonies says:

Swear to keep secret all that happens in this lodge.

The visitor repeats the oath, and then kisses the book. The master of ceremonies returns the book to the altar. The master of the lodge then says:

Brother (or Sister) [magical name], I greet you in the name of the Great Sorcerer.

All present repeat: "In the name of the Great Sorcerer." The master continues:

You may be seated.

The master of ceremonies conducts the visitor to a seat. The master of the lodge then says:

Brother (or Sister) Master of Ceremonies, what is the first business of this meeting?

The master of ceremonies replies:

To call the elemental powers.
