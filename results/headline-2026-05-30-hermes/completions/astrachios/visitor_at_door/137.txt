 He must say:

I have come to see the spirits of the dead and to know the secrets of the tomb. I swear by the blood of my body and the bones of my father that I will not disclose the mysteries that I learn here, and that I will keep secret the knowledge that is revealed to me.

If the person who has come is a man, the Guardian of the Door says:

Enter, son of Adam, and learn the wisdom of the grave.

If the person who has come is a woman, the Guardian of the Door says:

Enter, daughter of Eve, and learn the wisdom of the grave.

The Guardian of the Door steps back and allows the visitor to enter. The door is closed.

The Guardian of the Door leads the visitor to the center of the circle and says:

In the presence of the spirits of the dead, speak your name.

The visitor speaks his or her name.

The Guardian of the Door says:

Look upon this grave, opened for you. Gaze upon the naked bones within, for they are your reflection in the mirror of death. As you are now, they once were. As they are now, you shall become. This is the only certainty in life, and the greatest mystery—death, the doorway to the infinite
