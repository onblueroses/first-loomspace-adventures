 The doorkeeper answers:

The door is open.

The visitor enters, and the doorkeeper announces:

The temple is open.

The doorkeeper then closes the door, and the ritual continues.

## In popular culture

* In the 1995 book The Apocalypse Watch by Robert Ludlum, a secret society known as "the Matarese" use the password "Tertium" and the response "Quid" to gain access to a meeting.
