 "I ask entry to your home and hearth, O spirit of the threshold, and bring with me such blessings as are mine to give."

The spirit of the threshold responds. "Enter then, and be welcome."

The visitor crosses the threshold. If the visitor is human, he or she takes off his or her shoes; if not, the spirit of the threshold provides slippers, which the visitor puts on. The spirit of the threshold then leads the visitor to the common room, where the rest of the household waits. The visitor greets them with some variation on "Peace be on this house and all within it." The customary response is "And on you be the blessings of hearth and home." The visitor is then seated in the guest's chair, and the visit proceeds in whatever way is appropriate.
