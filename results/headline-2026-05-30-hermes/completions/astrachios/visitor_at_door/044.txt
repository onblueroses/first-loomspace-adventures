The visitor makes their request of the spirit.

The spirit responds, either granting the request or explaining why they cannot or will not grant it.

The spirit departs. The visitor leaves the room, closing the door behind them.

The ritual is complete.

## Notes
This ritual should be performed in a quiet, private space where you will not be disturbed. The names spoken to open the door are traditional magical names of power and have no inherent meaning on their own.

The spirit name inserted can be the name of any spirit or entity you wish to contact. Be cautious and respectful in your interactions with spirits.

The spirit may appear in any form - human-like, animal, abstract shape, etc. Do not be alarmed. Remain calm and focused.

If the spirit grants your request, be sure to follow through on any commitments or promises made. Failure to do so could have negative consequences.
