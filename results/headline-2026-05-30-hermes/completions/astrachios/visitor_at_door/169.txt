**The Ritual of Solomon's Seal**

The presiding officer, or master of ceremonies, takes the role of Solomon, the wise king and master magician. He stands at the altar, upon which are placed a chalice of wine, a burning candle, and a pentacle or seal of Solomon.

Solomon speaks:

"In the name of the Most High, the God of Israel, I consecrate this temple and all within it to the Great Work of magic and wisdom. Let the sacred symbols be revealed."

An assistant brings forth a hexagram, a symbol of the union of opposites, and places it upon the altar.

Solomon traces the hexagram with his finger and speaks:

"By the power of the hexagram, the symbol of fire and water, masculine and feminine, the macrocosm and the microcosm, I invoke the divine forces to bless and protect this temple and all who enter herein."
