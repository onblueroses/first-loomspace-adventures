 If he or she is the one who knocked, he or she must be the one to say the words. No other person can say them, or the door will not open.

The words are: “THERION, NANTHER, CARITA.”

Upon these words, the door should open. If it does not, leave the place immediately and do not return for at least one year. The spirits within are not in harmony with you, and it would be dangerous to proceed, but after a year it may be safe to try again.

If the door does open, you may enter. Leave the door open or ajar while you are inside. The room will be in darkness. Do not turn on a light. You must leave the door open to allow the spirits of the room to come and go. If you close the door, they will be trapped, and will vent their anger on you.

Approach the mirror. Place both your hands upon the surface, and press gently. Speak the words:

“By the power of the key, I command the spirits of the mirror to open the way.”

The surface of the mirror will soften and become like gray smoke. It may swirl with colors. Do not be alarmed. Insert your head into the smoky surface
