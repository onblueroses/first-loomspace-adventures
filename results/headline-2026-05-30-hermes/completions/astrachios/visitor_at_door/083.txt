 If they are a friend, they say:

"I am a friend to those within, and I wish to enter."

If they are a stranger, they say:

"I am a stranger, and I wish to enter."

The host responds:

"Enter, with the permission of the Divine."

The visitor enters, and the door is closed. The host says:

"Come in peace, and go in peace."

The visitor replies:

"May peace be upon this house and all who dwell within."

The host then asks the visitor to state their name, and the purpose of their visit. The visitor does so. The host then says:

"I welcome you, [Name], to this house. May your visit be pleasant, and may you find what you seek."

The visitor responds:

"I thank you for your welcome, and for the hospitality of this house. May my presence bring no disturbance, and may I be a worthy guest."

The host then offers the visitor a seat, and the visit begins. When it is time for the visitor to leave, the host escorts them to the door. The host says:

"May your journey be safe, and may you find your way back to this house again, should you wish to return."
