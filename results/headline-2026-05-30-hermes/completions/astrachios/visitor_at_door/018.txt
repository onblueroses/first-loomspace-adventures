The doorkeeper, who is also the chief spirit of the work, answers: "I am Scirlin, the servant of the great Adonai. I am the chief spirit of the Grand Grimoire. I have come to open the way for you, and to teach you the secrets of the spirits."

The visitor speaks again. He says that he wishes to make a pact with the spirits, so that they will grant him power and knowledge.

Scirlin answers: "To make a pact with the spirits, you must first prove your dedication and faith. You must perform the ritual of the Black Hen's Egg, and you must do so with reverence and care."
