 "I have come from the distant land of Faeac. I am one of the race of the Satyrs who dwell therein. I have come to bear witness to the truth of the mysteries and to learn those of the highest order, that I may be one with the Divine. My name is Barachiel."

The guardian replies. "Enter, Barachiel, and welcome to our sacred temple. May you find what you seek within its hallowed halls."

The visitor enters the temple, and the ritual continues. The high priestess begins to speak, her voice echoing through the chamber. "Let us call upon the elements to witness and guard our rites. Let us invoke the powers of the universe to aid us in our quest for knowledge and wisdom."

The high priestess begins the elemental invocations. "Ye Mighty Princes of the Four Quarters, I do call upon you to witness and guard our rites. In the name of the Most High, I do summon, stir, and call you up to protect this sacred space. Let all within be bound by your power and authority."
