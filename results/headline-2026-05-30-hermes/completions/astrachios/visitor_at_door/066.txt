 The visitor must say a reason for their presence that relates to the goals of the lodge. The guardian may ask for clarification. When satisfied, the guardian opens the door.

Guardian: I open the way for you, [name of visitor]. Enter and be welcome.

The guardian opens the door and admits the visitor. Once the visitor is inside, the guardian closes the door and returns to their place.

Guardian: The lodge is secure.

The ritual continues.

### Closing

The closing of the lodge is a formal way to end the meeting and to thank the spirits and energies that have been present during the ritual. It is also a way to ground and center the participants and to release any excess energy.

The master of the lodge stands at the altar and raises their hands.

Master: By the power of the spirits of the grimoire and the authority of the lodge, I declare this working closed. Let us give thanks to the spirits who have aided us and release any excess energy back into the universe.

The master lowers their hands and speaks the following words:

Master: Spirits of the grimoire, we thank you for your presence and your aid in our work. We release you now, with our gratitude. Go in peace and harmony.
