 'I have come; I have seen; I have conquered all. I have come so that you may give me light, for your light is great. I have come in peace. I have come with the power, the arm, and the force of the whole hierarchy. I have come in the name of the great God; give me your hand, kiss the image of the great God.'"

Here the figure will extend its hand to the visitor, who will kiss it, and both will be seated.

The visitor: "I have come to seek your alliance, to ask for the power to dominate over the Spirits, and for the science of the Cabala. I have come to ask for riches, of which you have the disposal, to bear the Seal of Solomon, that I may have the power to undertake all things I desire to bring to effect. I have brought you an offering of fine flour, that you may be able to make bread."
