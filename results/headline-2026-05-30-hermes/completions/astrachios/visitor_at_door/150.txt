The visitor answers. "I am a star, shining in the night."

The doorkeeper asks. "Where do you come from?"

The visitor answers. "From the far reaches of the infinite."

The doorkeeper asks. "Where are you going?"

The visitor answers. "To the eternal depths of the endless."

The doorkeeper asks. "What do you seek?"

The visitor answers. "The mastery of the elements, the power of transformation, and the knowledge of hidden things."

The doorkeeper asks. "Who leads you to this place?"

The visitor answers. "The Morning and Evening Star, the messenger of the gods, the eternal light."

The doorkeeper asks. "What is your token?"

The visitor answers. "The pentacle of Solomon, the key to the mysteries."

The doorkeeper says. "Then enter, and welcome."
