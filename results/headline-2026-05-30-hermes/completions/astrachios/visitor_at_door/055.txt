The master of the lodge, who is seated in the east, rises and speaks. “Spirits and fellow students of the hidden arts, we have with us a seeker after the mysteries of geomancy. Are there any among you who object to their presence here?”

If there are any objections, the visitor is escorted out of the lodge, and the door is closed and sealed with the banishing ritual of the pentagram. If there are no objections, the master continues. “Very well. Let us proceed with the initiation. Brother Guardian, please conduct the candidate to the altar.”

The guardian brings the candidate to the altar, which is in the center of the lodge.
