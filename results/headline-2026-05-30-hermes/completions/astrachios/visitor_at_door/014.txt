 The door is opened. The visitor enters. The door is closed. The visitor says:

"Welcome, I have arrived."

The room is set with a table covered with a cloth. On the table are two candles, two glasses, and a bottle of liquor. The visitor is seated at the table. The host pours liquor into the glasses, lights the candles, and says:

"Let us drink to the success of this operation."

They drink the liquor and extinguish the flames of the candles with their hands. The visitor says:

"Let us now retire to the prepared chamber."

They go to the prepared chamber. The host says:

"I am the servant of the Grand Adonay, Elohim, Ariel, and Jehovam. I am about to invoke the spirits to obtain from them the things I desire. I beseech you to be favorable to me, O Spirits!

"Phrasis, Ochim, Bagolis, Abracadabra, Aabrah, Alabah, Alabah, Silat, Sirabratem, Carim, Adonai, Eli, Era, Agla, Oubarim, Tatariel, Tafthi, Zazahot, Aban, Anab, Alabah
