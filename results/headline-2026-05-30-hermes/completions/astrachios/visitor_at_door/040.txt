 I am not alone in this room, he says. I see another.

The mage commands: Speak, spirit! What is your name?

The visitor says: I am called Bael.

The mage commands: Why are you here?

The visitor says: I come to serve the living.

The mage commands: Will you open the way for me to the First Gateway?

The visitor says: I will do so if you compel me.

The mage commands: By the power of the names I have uttered, and the circle that surrounds you, I command you to open the way for me to the First Gateway, without harm to me or those who dwell within this house.

The visitor says: It shall be done.

The mage commands: Go now in peace, and come again when I call you.

The visitor says: I shall do so.

The mage speaks: The way is open. Now I shall pass through the First Gateway. Before me stands the guardian of the threshold.

The guardian speaks: The way before you is dangerous. Only the wise may pass. Are you prepared to face the dangers that await you?

The mage answers: I am prepared. I have set my feet upon the path of wisdom, and I shall not turn back.
