 They say: 

"Open the door. I have spoken the names."

The host opens the door.

## 3. The Exchange

The host offers the visitor food and drink. The visitor accepts. They eat and drink together.

The host says: 

"I offer you my hospitality. May it strengthen our bond."

The visitor replies:

"I accept your hospitality. May it strengthen our bond."

## 4. The Request

The visitor makes their request. The host listens attentively.

The host responds, either granting or denying the request. If granted, they discuss the details. If denied, the visitor accepts the host's decision.

## 5. The Departure

The visitor prepares to leave. The host escorts them to the door.

At the threshold, the host speaks the closing words:

"Go in peace. May the names that opened the way guide your path."

The visitor replies:

"I go in peace. May the names that opened the way guide my path."

The visitor departs, and the ritual is complete.
