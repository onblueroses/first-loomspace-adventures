 If the visitor is not a demon or evil spirit, then the demon guarding the door will flee.

## Notes

1. The text appears to be in error. The name "Bedrimuliel" is given in the list of names, but based on other texts, it seems the name "Becharas" was intended.
2. The name "Achsah" is likely a mistake. It is a Hebrew woman's name, meaning "anklet." It is possible the intended name is "Achsah," a name sometimes associated with Venus, or "Achsaf," a name that appears in other magical texts.

# The Fourth Book: The Key of Solomon

The Key of Solomon is divided into two books. The first book, contains conjurations, invocations and curses to summon and control spirits. The second book describes various purifications an exorcist should undergo, and the pentacles used to protect the exorcist, and to bind and control the spirits.
