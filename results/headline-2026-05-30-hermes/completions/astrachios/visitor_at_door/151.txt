The doorkeeper gestures for the visitor to enter. The visitor steps inside, and the doorkeeper closes the door behind them. The visitor and the doorkeeper are now both inside the room. The doorkeeper speaks again.

"Welcome, traveler. You have entered the first room. Here, you will face a test of your knowledge and wisdom. Answer my riddle, and you may proceed to the next room. Fail, and you will be trapped here for eternity. Are you ready?"

The visitor nods. "I am ready."

The doorkeeper recites the riddle. "I am not alive, but I grow; I don't have lungs, but I need air; I don't have a mouth, but water kills me. What am I?"

The visitor thinks for a moment, then answers. "Fire."

The doorkeeper nods. "You have answered correctly. You may proceed to the next room."
