### Ars Almadel

Ars Almadel is the fourth part of the Lesser Key of Solomon. It tells how to make an Almadel, which is a wax tablet with protective symbols drawn on it. On the Almadel are placed four candles made from virgin wax. The text describes the angels summoned using the Almadel and explains how to summon them using the Almadel:

Chorum, Mocasufor, and Sueracha are the names of the three angels that appear in the Almadel on Sunday. They can teach the knowledge of the universe and the secrets of the natural world.

The angels that appear on Monday are called Gorson, Scox, and Sabasach. They can teach the knowledge of theoretical and speculative science, such as physics and metaphysics.

On Tuesday, the angels are called Magalast, Sarapiel, and Tarcaba. They can teach the knowledge of all human laws, including civil and ecclesiastical laws.
