This ritual is designed to create a formal and symbolic entry into a space dedicated to the study and practice of the occult. The names and words used are derived from various sources, including grimoires, folklore, and religious texts, and are intended to evoke a sense of ancient wisdom and spiritual power. The exchange between the visitor and the gatekeeper establishes the purpose of the visit and the shared commitment to the pursuit of knowledge and understanding. This ritual can be adapted and customized to suit the specific needs and preferences of the practitioners involved.]]
