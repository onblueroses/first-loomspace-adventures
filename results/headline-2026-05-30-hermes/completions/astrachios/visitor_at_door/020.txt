 It is the voice of the guide, who says:

"Let the gates be opened."

The servant of the threshold speaks:

"The gates are opened. Let the names be spoken that allow entrance."

The visitor speaks:

"Aglon, Tetragram, Vaycheon, Stimulamaton, Ezphares, Main, Agla, Elobach, Yod, He, Vau, He, Anaphaxeton, Aglay, Yod, He, Vau, He, Yehova, Tetragrammaton, Saday, Messias, Eschereheye."

The servant speaks:

"Enter, in the name of the mysteries."

The guide leads the visitor to the altar. The visitor kneels and speaks these words:

"By the power of the holy names, I ask to be admitted to the mysteries of this temple."

The servant replies:

"In the name of the mysteries, and by the power of the names you have spoken, welcome. Rise, and be seated in the place of the seeker."
