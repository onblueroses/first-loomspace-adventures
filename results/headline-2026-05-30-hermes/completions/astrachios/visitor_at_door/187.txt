 The guardian of the gate answers.

The visitor: I am a servant of the Secret Fire, wielder of the flame of the immortals. I have come with pure heart and with the intention to learn from the wisdom of the Secret Fire, to join the immortals in their eternal quest for knowledge and power, to walk the path of the wise and become one with the universe.

The guardian: Then enter, and be welcome.

The visitor enters the temple, and the door is closed behind him.

In the center of the temple is an altar, on which burns the sacred flame of the Secret Fire. The visitor approaches the altar and kneels before it, then rises and speaks:

I, [name], have come here to learn the mysteries of the Secret Fire, to become an initiate of the immortals, to walk the path of wisdom and power. I swear to keep secret all that I learn here, to use my knowledge for the good of all, and to seek the truth above all else. May the Secret Fire guide me, and may the immortals watch over me.

The visitor then takes a pinch of incense from a container on the altar and throws it into the flame, saying:
