The visitor names their business, and if it is of interest, the host invites them in. If not, they are turned away.

### 3. The Pact

The visitor and host discuss the terms of their agreement, including what each will provide and the limits of their arrangement.

When both parties are satisfied, they seal the pact with a handshake and the words: "It is agreed."

### 4. The Exchange

The agreed-upon exchange is made. If either party breaks the pact, they will face consequences determined by the other.

### 5. The Departure

The visitor takes their leave, speaking these words at the threshold:

The pact is sealed, our business done,
I depart now, my purpose won.
By the power of the names I spoke,
I release you from the chains of smoke.
