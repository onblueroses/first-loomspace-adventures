# **Elenchos** - The Interrogation
As the visitor enters, you must interrogate them to determine their worthiness and sincerity. Ask them:

- What is your name?
- Where do you come from?
- What are your intentions here?
- Do you come in peace and sincerity?

If their answers satisfy you, proceed to the next step. If not, bid them depart in peace.

# **Asphalia** - The Oath of Silence
Before revealing the mysteries, extract an oath of silence from the visitor. Have them place their right hand over their heart and swear:

"I, [state your name], do solemnly swear before these witnesses and by all I hold sacred to never reveal the secrets entrusted to me, except it be to a true and worthy seeker in the proper manner. If I should break this oath, may the powers of the elements and the wrath of the spirits be visited upon me. So mote it be."
