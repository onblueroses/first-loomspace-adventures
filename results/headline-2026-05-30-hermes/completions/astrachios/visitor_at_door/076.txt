 He or she asks to enter in the name of the spirits. The initiator allows the visitor to enter. The visitor traces the pentagram of fire in the air with the athame and then hands it to the initiator, who traces a pentagram of water in the air. They embrace, kissing on the cheek, and the visitor is allowed to enter the temple. This ritual is repeated for each visitor until all the participants are inside the temple.

The ritual continues:

Priestess: (Standing before the altar in the Goddess position, i.e., feet together, right arm uplifted with the athame held vertically, left arm spread out downwards to the side, stands in the Osiris position. She speaks the following, either alone or with the other coven members.)

I am the Gracious Goddess, who gives the gift of joy unto the heart of man. Upon earth, I give the knowledge of the spirit eternal; and beyond death, I give peace, and freedom, and reunion with those who have gone before. Nor do I demand sacrifice; for behold, I am the Mother of All Living, and my love is poured out upon the earth.

(All kneel before the altar.)
