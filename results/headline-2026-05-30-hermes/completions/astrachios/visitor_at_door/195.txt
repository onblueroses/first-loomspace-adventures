 He is asked his name, and the spirit with him. He answers: 'I am Brulefer and my companion is Gaster, a spirit of great power, who can raise mighty storms.'

He is asked his business and replies: 'My business is with you.'

He is asked to enter. He enters, and is welcomed. He is offered food and drink. He replies: 'I will take a little, symbolic of joy at our meeting.'

He is asked to be seated. He replies: 'I will sit, but first I must explain my business. I have come to ask you to aid me with your power. If you do, you shall have whatever you wish. If not, I will cause you much trouble and pain.'

He is asked: 'What is it you wish me to do?'

He replies: 'I wish you to evoke the spirits of the dead to appear in the flesh, as they were in life.'

He is asked: 'Is this possible?'

He replies: 'It is possible. And more. The spirits can be made to serve you in many ways.'

He is asked: 'Will you first demonstrate the truth of your statements?'
