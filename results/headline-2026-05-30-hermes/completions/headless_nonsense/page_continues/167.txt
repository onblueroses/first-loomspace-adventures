 "The above is a rendering of the Hymn to Vrelthomphoth, as preserved in the Temple of Smelphenor. It is to be used in all ceremonies and invocations of the Mrolthen One."

This hymn is also included in The Equinox Vol. I, No. 3, as part of Liber DCCCCLXIII (963), ΘΗΣΑΥΡΟΥ ΙΕΡΟΥ ΓΡΑΜΜΑΤΟΣ ΣΕΒΑΣΜΙΩ ΕΝ ΤΩ ΙΕΡΩ ΤΟΠΩ (Treasure House of the Sacred Letters Honorably in the Sacred Place), the long title of which is "The Treasure-House of Images: A Book of the High, Holy, and Mystical Images which lie Hidden in the Simplicity of the Splendid Palace of the Light of God, including the thirty-two paths of Wisdom and their correspondences with the Hebrew Alphabet."
