“…MPHŌ KRELPHŌTH!”

He felt a sudden rush of energy course through his body. His mind seemed to expand, and he could sense the presence of innumerable spirits, all around him.

The boy smiled. He had done it. He was now the master of the Smelphenor.

He looked around the room, and his eyes fell on the door. He pointed at it, and spoke a word of power. The door shattered into a thousand pieces.

The boy laughed. This was power. This was what he had always wanted.
