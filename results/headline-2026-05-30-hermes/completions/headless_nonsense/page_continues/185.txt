I conjure you by the sacred and invisible Vrelthepho, who distinguishes the just from the unjust in all the world, before whom the unjust ones tremble, and the daimons and the arch-daimons quake.

I conjure you by the great, the eternal God, the one who is seated upon the serpent, who founded the earth upon the waters, who surrounded the ocean and set it with sand.

I conjure you by the one who spoke and it came to be; by the one who commanded and it stood firm, who created everything that is, who separated the darkness from the light, who set the water and the earth in their present places, who set the sea and the mountains and the rivers and the deserts and the trees and all things in the world.
