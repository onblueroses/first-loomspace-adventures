 "After this say the following, when you have put on the white robe."

---

This is the second half of the Invocation of the Mrolthen One, from the scroll of Vrondh:

When I have been given the Grace of the World,
When I have received the Power of Vrelthemphos,
When I have received the Success of Vronfeth,
When I have received the Grace of Mronth,
When I have received the Favor of Klepth,
When I have received the Knowledge of Krelphoth,
When I have received the Foundation of Vrondh,
Then I shall be the Immortal One, the Eternal One.

For I am the one who is in the Light of the Gate of the Heavens,
The Beautiful, the Undefiled One, in whom there is no Guilt.

I am the one who is in the Purity of the Fountain of the Water of Life,
The Pure One, in whom there is no Defilement.

I am the one who is in the Light of the Sun,
The Radiant One, in whom there is no Darkness.

I am the one who is in the Strength of the Mountain,
The Mighty One, in whom there is no Weakness.
