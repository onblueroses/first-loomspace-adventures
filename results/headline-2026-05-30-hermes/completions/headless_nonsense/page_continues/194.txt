Then comes the ritual for the summoning of the spirits:

Now, when thou shalt have said these words, thou shalt behold the gods, and the spirits, and the legions of angels worshipping and bowing down, for thou shalt have become great, and all of them shall be afraid of thee.

And then shall they answer thee, saying: “O Mrolthen One, who art thou?” And thou shalt reply: “I am Vrondh, the prophet of the Most High, and I am the one whom the Mrolthen One hath sent to rule over you.”

And then shall they say unto thee: “What is thy name?” And thou shalt reply: “I am Krelph, the prophet of Vrelthemphos.”

And they shall say unto thee: “What dost thou seek?” And thou shalt reply: “I seek the power to do all things, and to become a god.”
