 'This must be said at night, by the light of a single candle, with none but the conjurer present, and with the sacrifice of a small bird. Upon the completion of the invocation, the spirit shall appear to do the bidding of the conjurer.'

You are of course aware of the events that followed my discovery of this text, and I have no desire to recount them in detail. Suffice it to say that my first two attempts at the invocation were unsuccessful, and that I attributed this to the difficulty of obtaining a suitable sacrifice. However, on my third attempt, something appeared to happen. I had just completed the invocation when I felt a sudden gust of cold air in the room, and the candle was extinguished, leaving me in total darkness. I quickly relit it, and as I did so, I glimpsed what appeared to be a shadowy figure standing in the corner of the room. As I looked more closely, the figure seemed to dissolve into the shadows and disappear. I was both terrified and exhilarated by this experience, and resolved to continue my experiments.
