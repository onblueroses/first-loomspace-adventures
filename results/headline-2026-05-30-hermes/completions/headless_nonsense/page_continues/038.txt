 “The whole of this invocation must be said in a loud voice, and when the Krelphoth is finished, say the Mrolthen One’s Prayer, thus:”

Mrolthen One, grant me grace in Thy name to have the victory over all my enemies and over all the powers of darkness. Let them flee before me, and let me be victorious over them, for I am your servant. I am the Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska, and I will always work in your name, and will work in your name for ever. Amen.

The passage concludes with an instruction to the reader: “Having repeated these words, you will obtain all that you desire.”

I found this to be a very interesting and unusual piece of writing. It seems to be a magical invocation or prayer of some kind, intended to give the speaker power over various spiritual forces. The speaker identifies themselves as the “prophet Vrondh” and as the “Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska.” They call upon a deity referred to as “the Mrolthen One” using a series of strange names and titles.
