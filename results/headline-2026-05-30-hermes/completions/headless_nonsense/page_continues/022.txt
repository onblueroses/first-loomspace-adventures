 "At the conclusion of the prayer, the candidate is greeted by the assembly with the words 'Mrolth Rendemph Vrelphenor!'"

The name "Smelphenor" was used by H. P. Lovecraft in his story "The Horror at Red Hook", where it is described as "the triply hellish incantation of the Smelphenor": "Smelphenor, Smelphenor, Smelphenor".
