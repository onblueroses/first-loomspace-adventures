 “Say this thrice, and then say:”

O Lord God, let the company of the Shadowed Ones be cleared away from me.

As I speak, I invoke Thee, the Invisible and Ineffable God.

Hear me, O Lord God, and let all that is in me be subject unto me.

Let every shelter and veil be opened unto me.

Let every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God be obedient unto me.

For I am Mrelphos, Vrelphoth, Ven, Mroth, Plomphrata, Ven, Mreph, Mro, Krn, Mrokh, Vrelph, Mroth, Mrok, Mrok.

I have received the power of grace from Thee, and by the knowledge of Thy name.

MPHŌ KRELPHŌTH.
