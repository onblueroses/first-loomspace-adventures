 "Say this spell in a low voice, and the spirits will come to you."

The spell is written in a mixture of Greek and Coptic, with many magical words unattested elsewhere.

## Analysis

The invocation is addressed to an unnamed deity, who is given many magical epithets and names, many of which are likely invented. The spell does not conform to any particular religious tradition, but contains elements of Greek, Egyptian, and Gnostic religion, as well as Judaism. The deity is called "unseen," "unbegotten," and "immortal," all common Gnostic and Neoplatonic terms. It is also called "the one who begets and destroys," a common epithet for the Greek gods Zeus and Helios. The deity is also called "the one whose mouth utterly flames," which is paralleled by descriptions of the God of Israel in the Hebrew Bible.
