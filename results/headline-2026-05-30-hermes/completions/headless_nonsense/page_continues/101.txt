 “Here endeth the first part of the Invocation of the Bornless One. The second part follows.”

The second part begins with the magician identifying himself with the godhead, declaring that he is “He Whose Mouth is Flame,” “He Who Begets and Destroys,” and “The Grace of the World.” This identification is continued in the following sections.

The third part of the invocation is the “Charge to the Spirit.” This is a command to the invoked entity to appear before the magician in a visible and tangible form:

Come thou forth and follow me, and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land or in the water, of whirling air or of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God, may be obedient unto me.

The fourth part of the invocation is the “Spell of the Spell.” This is a series of magical words and phrases that are meant to bind the invoked entity to the magician’s will:

I bind thee by the Spell of the Spell, by the Curse of the Curse. I invoke thee by the Blasphemy of the Blasphemy. I summon thee by the Perdition of the Perdition
