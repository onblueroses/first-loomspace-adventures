Note: In the original text, the word "Smelphenor" is written as "ΣΜΕΛΦΗΝΩΡ", which transliterates as "Smelphēnōr". The "ē" is pronounced like the "e" in "me". 

## The Prayer to the Sun

The prayer to the Sun, on pages 10 and 11, is as follows:

I call upon You, the Greatest in the Heaven, the Fiery Sun, the Whirlwind, the King of all the Stars, glorious as the Moon, You who are the midday, the midnight, the rising and the setting, the dawn and the dusk, the beauty and the light of the night. 

I call upon Your holy and great and fearful name, the ruler of the universe, who created the world and the heavens, the darkness and the light, the winds and the thunderbolts, the fountains and the rivers, the cold and the heat, the land and the sea, and who conserves all things.
