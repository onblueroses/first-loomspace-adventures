## The Rite of the Headless One

The rite is said to be of "supreme importance" to practitioners of the invocation of the Headless One. It is recommended to be performed "on the eve of going to war, or whenever otherwise you have need of great strength and irresistible might." The ritual is to be performed in "a place where you will be free from interruption, and where you will be able to build a fire," and it is to be performed when the moon is in the astrological sign of Leo, and at the hour of Mercury.
