 “And I say to you: I, Vrondh, who am the Mrolthen Krelph and the Mrolthen Vrelph, I shall say a prayer to the great and powerful Smelphenor, and he will give heeding to the words of my mouth.” What follows is the prayer mentioned, which is to be said by the magician to his god. It is a prayer for protection against the spirits that the magician is about to call forth. The magician is to recite the prayer while holding his hand on his heart. The prayer is in Greek, but I give it in English:

I call upon thee, O Smelphenor, unconquerable, who sittest on high and dost the god of gods: subdue for me the demons that flee from thee, under whose power all things rational are: the demons of the air, of land and sea, and those who pass through the heavens, and those who pass through the earth and those who are under the rule of the elements.
