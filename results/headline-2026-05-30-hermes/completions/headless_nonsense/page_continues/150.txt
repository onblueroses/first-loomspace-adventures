Then, in the original MS., there is a note in the margin: "Thee that didst create the Earth and the Heavens: that is, the Male and Female Principles."

The second part of the invocation is then given:

I invoke Thee, the Terrible and Invisible God who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit:

Mrok, Vronth, Smelph, Vrenph, Mronth, Kren, Mrelth, Nronth.

Hear me, and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.

Hear me, Mro Vrelth, Krelph, Vronth, Smelph, Vrelth, Nronth, Mrenth, Krn, Mrelth, Plon, Mronth, Mro Vrelth.
