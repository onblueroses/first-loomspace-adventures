### The Invocation of the Twelve

The Invocation of the Twelve, which appears in the Oath, is a formula used to summon the elemental powers of the universe. It is as follows:

Mrok, Plenphor, Smelphenoroth, A, Vrelph, Snoph, Mrelph, Ven, Vrelphano, Mrep, Mrok.

This formula is said to be a key to the mysteries of the universe, and is used in many of the rituals of the Mrolthen religion.
