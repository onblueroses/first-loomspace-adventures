 "The magician should now utter the name of the spirit he desires to summon, and command it to appear."

The name Mrothra, a spirit of ill-will and hatred, is then given, and the magician is instructed to command Mrothra to appear.

In the following lines, the magician threatens the spirit with dire consequences if it does not obey:

I invoke Thee, Mrothra.
Come Thou hither, for I command Thee with the form of the All Powerful:

Krepho, Mronth, Vrelthra, Smelphronor.

Come hither unto me, O Thou Spirit, for I command Thee with the Vessels of Mrok:

Krepho, Mronth, Vrelthra, Smelphronor.

Come Thou hither immediately, O Thou Spirit, for I invoke Thee with the words of Vrelthos:

Krepho, Mronth, Vrelthra, Smelphronor.

Come hither unto me, O Thou Spirit, for I summon Thee with the names of the All-Powerful:

Krepho, Mronth, Vrelthra, Smelphronor.
