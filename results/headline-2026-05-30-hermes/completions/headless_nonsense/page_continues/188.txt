 "And when he had uttered these words, suddenly there arose from the sky a great storm, and the sea boiled as if it were in a cauldron, and the earth shook as if it were about to be destroyed, and the mountains were moved from their foundations. Then the sun appeared in the form of a man, and the moon in the form of a woman, and they said to Vrondh, 'You have called us; what do you wish?' And Vrondh said, 'I wish to know the art of raising the dead.' And the sun and the moon replied, 'This is a great and terrible art, and it can only be performed by those who are pure of heart and have been initiated into the mysteries of Smelphenor. But since you have uttered the sacred words, and have shown yourself to be a true prophet, we will grant your request.' And they taught Vrondh the art of raising the dead, and he used it to bring back to life many who had died, and they lived again as if they had never been dead. And Vrondh became famous throughout the world for his power over life and death, and he was revered as a god among men."
