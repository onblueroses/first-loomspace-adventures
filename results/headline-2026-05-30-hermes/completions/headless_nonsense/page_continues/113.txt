 "The Spirit appeared in the likeness of a great wheel of fire, and answered in a voice of thunder: 'See thou corrupt not the heart of the one that is pure. And thou shalt be a prophet of the Most High.'"

This passage is followed by an oath that the prophet is to swear, which is written in a rhythmic, semi-metrical style:

I swear by the firmament of Heaven;
I swear by the Mrolthen One, Vrelthemphos;
I swear by the Veil of Smelphenor;
I swear by the Silence and the Darkness;
I swear by the Mighty One, Mrok;
I swear by the Line of the Prophets, Vrondh and Mroth;
I swear by the Great Secret, the Ineffable Word, which I hold in my heart, and which I will not tell unto another, lest I be accursed.
