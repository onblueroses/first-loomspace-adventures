 "Draw the Pentagram of Fire on the floor in front of you.  Stand in the center, facing east.  In your right hand, hold the Wand of Fire, and in your left, the Pentacle of Fire.  Speak the following invocation in a commanding voice:"

---

I call upon the mighty Archangel Vrelphor, Regent of the Element of Fire.

Vrelphor, great Seraph of the South, I conjure you in the name of the Mrolthen One, the supreme and ineffable craftsman of the universe, to appear before me in this consecrated circle.

O Vrelphor, I conjure you in the name of Mrok, mighty King of the South and Lord of Fire, and by the divine fire that burns eternally at the heart of creation.

O Vrelphor, I conjure you by the power of the Pentagram of Fire, and by the sacred symbols of the Art engraved upon this Wand and Pentacle.

O Vrelphor, I conjure you by the secret names of God: Mrok, Plenphor, Smelphenoroth, A, Vrelph, Snoph, Mrelph, Ven, Vrelphano,
