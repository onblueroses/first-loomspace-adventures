 “The above conjuration, spoken three times, will compel the spirits to appear before you. If you have called upon a spirit of the air, you will see a hawk; if of the fire, you will see a cock; if of the earth, you will see a bull; if of the water, you will see a goose. Having obtained control of the spirit, you may then command it to do your will.”

Well, there you have it. If you can pronounce all those words, I suppose you can raise the dead. I don’t know why anyone would want to, though. I wouldn’t.
