 "When you have said these things, you will hear thunder and shaking in the sky, and you will see a light in the east, shining more brightly than the sun. And then, in the midst of the light, you will see the heavens open, and a bright host of stars, and the sea, and the earth, and the mountains, and the cities, and the rivers, and the animals, and everything that is in the world. And you will see a flaming fire, and a mighty god who sits upon it, having a great numbus around him, and the flames of the fire are like unto rays of the sun. And you will see the Mrolthen One shining upon the fire, and his name is Vrelthep, and he will speak with you, as it were in a voice of thunder."

I found this text in the library of the University of the City, in the section reserved for books on the occult, magic, and religion. The book in which it appeared was a collection of ancient magical texts, translated by a scholar named M. R.
