 “This is the holy incantation, as it was delivered unto me by the spirit: OVNĪKROPH KRĒMENPHĒNISKĀ. So shall it ever be.”

The following page is torn out, but the next page continues the spell:

I invoke Thee by the powers of the Vrenthoph, the Mrenthop, and the Plenphor, which are the three secret names of the divine child, the great and terrible names of the Deity.

I invoke Thee by the powerful and most potent name of Vrelthoph, which Thou dost love, to whom heaven is bowed, and which maketh the earth to tremble, and which all the infernal spirits do obey.

I invoke Thee by the great name of Vronthomph, which all creatures obey when they hear it, and by the name of Mroph, which Thou dost love, and by the name of Krelphor, which Thou dost love, and by the name of Vrelphom, which Thou dost love.
