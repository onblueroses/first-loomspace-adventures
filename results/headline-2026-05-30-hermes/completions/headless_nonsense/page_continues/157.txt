 “The Priestess will now recite the Invocation of the Four Quarters, and then the Priest will read the Invocation of the Four Elements, as given in the third chapter of this book. The Priestess will then recite the following invocation of the Moon Goddess.”

The Priestess continues:

O Thou Mother of the Bright Moon, whose light illuminates the ways of the night, whose crescent is the jewel of the dark: I adore Thee. I adore Thee, O Mother of All.

O Thou whose breath is the wind, whose voice is the howling of the storm, whose feet tread the clouds: I adore Thee. I adore Thee, O Mother of All.

O Thou who art the Earth, who art the Mother of the fields and the groves, whose body is the mountain and the valley, whose womb is the seed-ground of the world: I adore Thee. I adore Thee, O Mother of All.

O Thou who art the Sea, whose womb is the home of the mighty and terrible Gods of the waters, whose voice is the waves, whose breath is the tides: I adore Thee. I adore Thee, O Mother of All.
