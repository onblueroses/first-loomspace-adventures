### Adept

This section begins with a warning that the rituals contained within are only to be attempted by those who have mastered the rituals in the Neophyte and Acolyte sections. It then continues:

The rituals contained within this section are designed to bring the magician into direct contact with the gods and spirits of the Vrolthen pantheon. These rituals are not to be attempted lightly, as they require a great deal of mental and spiritual preparation. The magician must be willing to give himself over completely to the gods, and to accept their will as his own.

The first ritual, The Invocation of the Mighty Vronth, begins:

I invoke Thee, the Mighty and Invisible God who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit: Vronth, Vrelth, Smelph, Mroph, Krn.

I am the Mrolthen Krelph with sight in his feet.
I am the Mighty One, who possesses the immortal Fire.
I am the Truth that hates the deeds of injustice in the world
