##### 2. The Supreme Ritual

This is the Supreme Ritual for working the Unutterable Names of Vrelth and performing the Sacred Ceremony of Smelphenor.

Begin the ritual in the dark. Light a single candle, and place it in the center of the circle.

Stand in the east of the circle, and say:

I invoke Thee, the Terrible and Invisible God who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit: Smelphenosomprok, Vrendoph, Krelphonar, Mrendepholm, Krn.

Turn to the south, and say:

I invoke Thee, the Terrible and Invisible God who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit: Smelphenosomprok, Vrendoph, Krelphonar, Mrendepholm, Krn.
