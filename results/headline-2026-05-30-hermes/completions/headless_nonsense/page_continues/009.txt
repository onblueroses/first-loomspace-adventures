Then say this to any spirit, and they will obey you: 'NROK, KREPH, SMELPH, VRENPH, KREPHENOTHO, SMOLPHENOR, PHENOMRA, KROPH, MNOR, BREN, KRÊN, GENTHEN, KRÊN, VROM, YÊN, AN, VRELPH, NROK.' Say this three times, and any spirit will appear before you and answer your questions truthfully. And if any spirit disobeys, say to them: 'I swear by the Great Name VRELTHENPHOS, which no man can know, and by the Sacred Seal of VRENPHOSOM, which is the Sacred Seal of the Mrolthen One, that you will obey me, or else be consumed by the Fire which is within me.' And if they still disobey, say: 'MROK PHENG WROL EN SMELPHRAK, MPHŌ KRELPHŌTH,' and they will obey you."
