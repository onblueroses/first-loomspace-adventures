 "When this has been said, he shall take the sacrifice, saying: 'O Lord of all the gods, Vrelth, who art mighty, Vrenph, who hast the secret power over things unseen, Mrok, who art strong, I conjure Thee by Thy great name, Smelphenor, to give me power over all the demons of the firmament and of the ether, of the earth and under the earth, and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and all the spells and scourges of the gods, that I may be a ruler of them all, and that they may be obedient unto me.'"

The author then explains that the magician should sacrifice to the god a white rooster, and take its blood and mix it with honey, and then use that to make a burnt offering.
