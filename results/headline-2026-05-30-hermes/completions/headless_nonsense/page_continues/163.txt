 "The above invocation is a powerful exorcism and should be used with care. It is a very ancient and potent spirit formula, which was used by the Egyptians, and which is written in their secret cryptic tongue. It calls upon the mighty and terrible God, Vrelthemphos, to make all spirits subject unto the operator. It is not necessary to understand the meaning of the barbarous words of the invocation, but the sound of the words should be clearly and forcibly vibrated."
