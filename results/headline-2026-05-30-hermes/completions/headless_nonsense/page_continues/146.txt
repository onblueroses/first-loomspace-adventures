 "Thus when he had said these things, the sun began to darken, and the moon and the stars with it, and the earth was disturbed with an earthquake, and the sea was roiled with a tempest, and the tombs of the saints were laid open, and the veil of the temple was rent in twain, and the great gate of the temple he did open by the power of his voice, and there was a certain trembling throughout all the earth, and all that was therein was greatly disturbed, and the souls of the saints that were dead were led out of Hades, and they appeared unto many. And the spirits of the river Euphrates and of all the rivers and of all the seas did appear and were obedient unto him, and the commands of the demons and of the angels and of the spirits were made subject unto him, and the divine mysteries were revealed unto him, and the secret things of the mind of God, and he did see the ineffable symbols of the secret things of God, and the signs and the tokens of the presence of God.
