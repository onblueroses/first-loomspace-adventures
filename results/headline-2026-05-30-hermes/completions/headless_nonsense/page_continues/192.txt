 “I am the Grace of the World; the heart girt with a serpent is my Name.”

I realized with a start that this was the same phrase I’d seen in the circle in the woods. “The heart girt with a serpent”—that was the description of the image I’d seen. But who had drawn it? And why? I felt a chill run through me, and I hurriedly set the book down.

I was about to close it when I noticed that there were a few more pages stuck to the back cover. I carefully peeled them apart, trying to avoid tearing the delicate paper. The first page was covered in strange symbols and diagrams, with notes scrawled in the margins. I couldn’t make sense of any of it. The second page was more of the same, but at the bottom, there was a note written in a shaking hand:

“I have discovered the key. The heart girt with a serpent—it is the key to unlocking the power of the Mrolthen One. But I fear I may have gone too far. The spirits whisper to me in the night, and I see shadows lurking in the corners of my vision. I must be careful, lest I lose myself entirely to this dark power.”
