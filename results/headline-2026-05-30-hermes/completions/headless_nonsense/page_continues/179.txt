 'The foregoing is taken from the translation of a Greek manuscript in the British Museum by Charles Wycliffe Goodwin, first published in 1852. The ordinary translation of the phrase "Mrok Pheng Wrol En Smelphrak" is "Hidden in the deep", but it is possible that this may be a phrase transposed from the original Coptic, and originally standing for "Hid in the deep of Vrelth". This would identify the "Mrolthen One" with the "Mrolthen Krelph" of the invocation, and also with the "Mrolthen Krelph" of the Book of the Black Temple, Ch. 3, v. 19, which reads: "I am the Mrolthen Krelph with sight in his feet".'
