 “The above invocation is sufficient to control all the spirits of the firmament and the ether. But if you wish to call upon them by name, and to have each one do your will, you must use the following invocation:”

O Mrolth Vrelphano, most powerful of all the gods, who has created all things visible and invisible, to whom all the elements are subject, who sittest upon the clouds and dost whirl the winds, who dost halt the waves of the sea, who dost cause the earth to tremble:

I call upon Thee to make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.

Hear me, for I am the messenger of Mrolth Vrelphano Mazbekaron:
