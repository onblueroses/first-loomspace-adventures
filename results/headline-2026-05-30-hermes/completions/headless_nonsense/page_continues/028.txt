Hear me, ye spirits, and depart unto your abodes and habitations, and let no harm be done to me or my companions, for I am Vrondh, your master, and I command ye by the mighty names of the Mrolthen One: Krelph, Vronth, Smelphenor, Vrendoph, Mrok, Mrelph, N.

And when you have said this, the spirits will depart and no harm will come to you. But you must be careful to speak the names and words exactly as they are written here, for if you make a mistake, the spirits will have power over you, and will tear you to pieces and feast on your flesh, and your soul will belong to them forever.
