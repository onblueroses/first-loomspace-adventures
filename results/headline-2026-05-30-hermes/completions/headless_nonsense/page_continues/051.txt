The next page is titled “The Ceremony of Smelphenor” and reads as follows:

The Ceremony of Smelphenor is performed in order to ensure the success of the adept’s endeavors in the summoning and control of spirits. It is a means of demonstrating to the spirits the adept’s devotion to the Mrolthen One, and of affirming his power over them.

The adept begins by preparing the chamber in which he will perform the ceremony. The chamber should be clean and free of any disturbances; if possible, it should be a place that has been consecrated to the practice of the magical arts. The adept should also prepare a small table or altar, on which he will place a chalice of pure water, a dish of salt, and a lit candle. If the adept has access to a consecrated dagger, he should place this on the altar as well.
