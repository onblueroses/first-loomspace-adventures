 "O Lord God, let them be confounded who persecute me, and deliver me. O Lord God, I accuse Vrelthor and Kraltph on account of their sins."

The text is interrupted by a series of magical symbols, followed by "I am Krelph, the Prophet of Vronthep. This is the correct arrangement of the Holy Numbers: V R O L Th A Ō Ph M."

Then follows another series of symbols and the words "Come, come, Vrelthor, and perform the miracles for me."

The next paragraph is a series of invocations, followed by "Hear me, O Lord, and subject unto me every daimon, so that they may serve me and every one of them may be obedient unto Thy name and Thy majesty and Thy ordinance. For I am Thy Prophet Vrondh, born of the Tribe of Krelph. Mpho, Krelphoth."

The text ends with a series of voces mysticae.
