 "The above is a copy of the letter from Vrondh to Vronthep, containing the incantation, the which placed all spirits under his control, and enabled him to create the world. The letter is written in a peculiar cipher, which is different from any other secret alphabet known, and is probably the one of which Vrondh speaks in the 29th chapter of the Book of Vronthep, where he says: 'I have written them in the cipher of the Mrolthen One.'"

On the next page, "The Cipher of the Mrolthen One," is a substitution cipher in which the alphabet is written from right to left, and in place of the letters are figures that look like a collection of asterisks, dots, and other figures.

The following is a description of the manuscript's secret alphabet:

The characters are not Hebrew in appearance. They are more like crude hieroglyphics, suns, circles, and stick figures, and are written from right to left.
