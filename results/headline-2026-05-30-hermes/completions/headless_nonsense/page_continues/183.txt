Another invocation of the same manuscript, to be used to summon a spirit to appear in a dream, begins with the following words:

I call upon Thee, Mrok Vrelth, terrible and invisible God, who dwellest in the void place of the Spirit.

This would seem to indicate that the Vrelth is a deity in its own right, rather than just a book. This is further confirmed by the next invocation in the manuscript, which includes the phrase:

I conjure Thee, O Vrelth, by Thy great and fearful name Smelphenor.

This would make the Vrelth and Smelphenor names for the same deity, and the word Vrelthep, used in the first invocation, seems to be a combination of the names Vrelth and Smelphenor. The invocation continues:
