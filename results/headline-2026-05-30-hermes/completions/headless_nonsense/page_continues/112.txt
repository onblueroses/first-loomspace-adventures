 "The Initiation of the Postulant will then take place, and the Postulant will be informed of the Secret Signs and Words, which are as follows:

"The Secret Sign is made thus: The first finger of the right hand is placed upon the lips, so that it is visible to any who may be present.

"The Secret Word is: MPHŌ KRELPHŌTH.

"Upon the conclusion of the Initiation, the Celebrant will say:

"May what we have partaken of here remain always within us, and may our services here be dedicated to the Greater Glory of the Mrolthen One.

"The Congregation will respond: So mote it be.

"And the Celebrant will say: So let it be written. So let it be done."

The pages that follow are filled with various rituals, prayers, and chants, all of which are similar in nature to the one just quoted. There is a Ritual of Exorcism, a Ritual of Consecration, a Ritual of the Eucharist, and various others.

Throughout these rituals, the phrases "Mrolthen One" and "Mrolthen Krelph" are used frequently, and the name "Smelphenor" is often invoked.
