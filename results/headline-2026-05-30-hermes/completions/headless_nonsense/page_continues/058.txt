 "The foregoing conjuration is appropriate to invoke the immortal spirits that are in the firmament, in the ether, in the earth, and beneath the earth, to make them tell you whatever you wish, and to make them do whatever you command."

The second invocation is prefaced with the comment: "When you see the spirit appear, do not hesitate, but say the following words to welcome it and to make it obey you":

Hail, O Spirit Krelphosmroth! I am the one who keeps the secrets and the mysteries of the immortal Mrolthen One.

I am the one who hath received the flame from the hands of the Mrolthen One, having been sent by the power of Vrelthemphos.

I am the one who possesses thy true name, handed down to the prophets of Vronthep.

Hear me, and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.
