 "After this invocation, which he recites aloud, he shall then write the following words on a piece of paper, and shall burn the paper in the flame of a black candle. This shall be done only in the night and in a place that is deserted and empty. When the words are thus consumed by the fire, the ashes of them shall be cast to the four winds, and the man shall say: O Kremph, as these words are destroyed, so shall the world be destroyed in the fullness of time!"

The words to be inscribed on the paper are:

Mrok Smelphrak Enkoth Voron Mrelteph Krephthag Ruôn Plenphor Mrophragh Kephan Mroth

The manuscript breaks off abruptly at this point, and the remainder of the apocalyptic prophecy is not recorded. Presumably the text was interrupted at this point by the coming of the Roman soldiers to arrest the sorcerer and to put him to death for his heretical teachings. It is a great loss to students of the occult that the remainder of the prophecy has been lost.

The reader will observe that the prophecy is couched in veiled and symbolic language, after the manner of the prophecies of Nostradam
