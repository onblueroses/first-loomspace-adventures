 'After saying this, when the angel appears, say to him, "Welcome, O Angel of Smelphenor, O Mrok Vrelthra, Plomphren Krempheniska." Then ask him what you will.'

"What do you think of that?" I asked.

"It is a very interesting incantation," replied Mr. Mould. "I am glad to have had the opportunity of hearing it. There are several points that call for comment, but I will not trouble you with them now. The translation is pretty accurate, in the main, though the translator seems to have been a little weak in his Greek. The really important point, however, is this. I notice that the incantation contains the name Vrondh, which I see I mentioned in my letter. I should like to know whether the name appears in any of the other documents."

I examined the bundle of papers and soon discovered that the name did occur several times. Moreover, by the light of Mr. Mould's information, I could see that these documents were probably all connected with the same subject.
