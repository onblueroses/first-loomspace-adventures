 "When this Conjuration is repeated, let the Operator then say: I call upon thee, O Spirit (naming the Spirit desired), in the Name of (here repeat the Name of the Spirit), that thou obey my demands and follow my commands, and that thou shall not leave my service until thou hast finished all that I shall command thee, and that thou be ready and willing to appear before me whenever I may call thee, without harm to me or to any creature, and that thou be subservient to my will and commands, and that thou give me true answers to all that I shall ask thee, and that thou do all that I shall require thee to do, and that thou shall not leave me until thou hast finished all that I shall command thee. So be it."

In this invocation, the magician calls upon a god known as "the Mrolthen One", using a variety of magical names. The invocation requests that the god make all spirits subject to the magician, so that they will obey the magician's commands and cause no harm. The magician then calls upon a specific spirit, and commands it to obey his or her will and to truthfully answer questions.
