 “Then let him raise his eyes to the heavens and say:”:

O Vronthep, Father of all the Gods, if Thou dost approve this invocation and stretch forth Thy right hand, and if Thou dost bestow upon me Thy Grace, then fill me with the essence of Thy Knowledge and Wisdom, that I may become a Wise One who is numbered among the servants of Thine.

Then let him look at himself and say:

I am Vrondh, born of the race of Vrelth the Great.

I am Vrondh, the initiate of the never-failing Rites of the Gods.

I am Vrondh, who is skilled in the Knowledge of the Gods.

I am Vrondh, the Messenger of Mronth the Great.

After this, let him look at the parchment and say:

This is the Body of Vrelth the Great.

This is the true Voice of Smelphenor.

This is the Word that formed the World, Krelph.

This is the Word that formed the Light, Vrelph.

This is the Word that formed the Darkness, Plenph.

This is the Word that formed the Man, Mren.

This is the Word that formed the Woman, Nup
