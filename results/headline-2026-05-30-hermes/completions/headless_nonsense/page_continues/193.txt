 "The above conjuration, when properly recited, will cause the earth to quake and the seas to rise, and all the rivers and streams will be as blood, and the air will be filled with thunderings and lightnings and the spirits of the dead will rise and walk upon the earth."

H. P. Lovecraft, in a letter to Clark Ashton Smith, remarked, "I wish I had been able to give this incantation in full in 'The Horror from the Middle Span'—it would have added a certain cosmic terror to the thing."

## In popular culture

* In the 1974 issue of the fanzine Nyctalops, artist David L. Jones included the Mrolthen Conjuration in the center of a cover illustration.
* The 1989 short story "The Freshman" by Philip José Farmer, which purports to be a lost manuscript of Lovecraft's, uses the name "Mroth" (possibly derived from "Mrolthen") for a fictitious ancient civilization.
* The Mrolthen Conjuration is chanted in the 1992 horror film Army of Darkness, written by Sam Raimi and his brother Ivan Raimi.
