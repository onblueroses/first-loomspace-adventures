 “The above is a very loose translation of an invocation in the sacred language of the cult of Vronthep. It is a bastardised form of the ancient tongue of the Vrelphen, which is not spoken by any people known today. The text, in its original form, is believed to be one of the oldest religious texts in existence.”

The invocation contains some interesting elements. The being invoked is referred to as “the Mrolthen One”, which is perhaps analogous to the Christian “Almighty”. The deity has many names, as do many other gods, such as the Abrahamic God. The deity created the universe, the world and everything in it, and formed men to love and to hate, which is a rather interesting concept. The speaker, the prophet Vrondh, identifies himself as the Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska, which is another name for the deity. The deity is referred to as “the Terrible and Invisible God who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit”, which is reminiscent of the Abrahamic God who is described as being an invisible spirit that cannot be seen.
