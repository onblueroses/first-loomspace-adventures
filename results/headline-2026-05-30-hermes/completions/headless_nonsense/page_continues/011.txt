 "The above is the invocation proper, which the scribe of a manuscript had interpolated among the genuine invocations. It is evidently of a late date, for it contains many Persian words and ideas, and may be compared with the genuine Greek magical papyri, as edited by Wessely and others."

In the context of the book, the invocation is an attempt by the protagonist to banish the malign influence of the Yellow Sign and the eponymous King in Yellow, apparently without success.

## Usage in other works

* In the 1984 tabletop role-playing game Call of Cthulhu, the spell is called the Vach-Viraj Incantation and is used to "dispel or summon the influence of the Yellow Sign".
* In the 1992 horror novel The Hole by Guy N. Smith, the protagonist uses the incantation to ward off the influence of the King in Yellow.
* In the 2014 horror novel The Last Mile by Tim Waggoner, the protagonist uses the incantation to banish a demonic entity.
