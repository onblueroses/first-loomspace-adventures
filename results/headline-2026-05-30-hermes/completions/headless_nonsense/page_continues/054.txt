### Fourth page

The fourth page contains a spell to invoke the seven holy angels of Vrelth. It begins with the following incantation:

I call upon thee, Mronth, great angel of Vrelth, to whom the earth is subject.

I call upon thee, Smelph, great angel of Vrelth, to whom the sea is subject.

I call upon thee, Plomph, great angel of Vrelth, who hast in thy hand the subjugation of the heavens.

I call upon thee, Phronk, great angel of Vrelth, to whom the fire is subject.

I call upon thee, Vrondh, great angel of Vrelth, to whom the winds are subject.

I call upon thee, Kremph, great angel of Vrelth, to whom the lightning is subject.

I call upon thee, Vrelth, great angel of Vrelth, to whom the whole of creation is subject.
