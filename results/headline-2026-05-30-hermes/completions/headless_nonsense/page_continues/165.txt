 "When the spirit comes, sacrifice unto it the stone Mnizourin, and ask it to reveal the secret of the future."

#### The Translation

This is the English translation of the Greek text:

I call upon you, the great one:

You, who created the Earth and the Heavens;
You, who created the Night and the Day;
You, who created the Darkness and the Light.

You are the eternal one, whom no man has seen at any time.
You are the great one. You are the wise one.
You are the strong one. You are the mighty one.
You have distinguished between the just and the unjust.
You made the female and the male.
You produced the seed and the fruit.
You formed men to love one another and to hate one another.

I am Moses your prophet, unto whom you committed your mysteries, the ceremonies of Israel.
You produced the moist and the dry, and that which nourishes all created life.

Hear me, for I am the messenger of Pharaoh Osiris; this is your true name, handed down to the prophets of Egypt.

Hear me: Arbi, Pheth, Iribis, A, Athor, Sabi, Sabor, Iaeo, Phor,
