 "There is a magical operation of maximum importance: the Initiation of the New Aeon. When it has been accomplished, and it will be accomplished—in the ordinary course of events the time required for its completion is somewhat over 400 years—then Mankind will be free of the doom of the Original Sin, the restriction of his powers, and the curse of death. He will be one with God."

This passage is followed by a series of Greek letters and symbols, and the words: "The above is the secret manner in which the letters of the name of Vrelthemphos are written. They are to be written on a piece of parchment, and then they are to be invoked."

The Book of the Law then continues with the next three chapters. The first chapter is written from the perspective of Vrelthemphos, the second from the perspective of Vrondh, and the third from the perspective of a goddess identified as Vrelphara. The final chapter is written from the perspective of a fourth figure, Vrelthor, who is identified as the "Lord of the Aeon" and the "Hierophant of the Mysteries."
