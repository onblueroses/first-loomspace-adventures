 “Say this over a little scroll of clean parchment, on which you have previously written the following words with the blood of a white dove:

MROK PHENG WROL EN SMELPHRAK MPHŌ KRELPHŌTH

Then perfume it with mastic, aloes, and storax, and bind it around your left arm with a thread spun by a virgin girl.”

The magician is to wear this scroll for seven days, then keep it in a clean place. Whenever he wishes to perform a conjuration, he is to wear it around his left arm. It is to be passed on to the magician’s descendants after his death, and only buried with the last of his line.

The text then provides an exorcism to be used in conjunction with the scroll:

I invoke Thee, the Terrible and Invisible God who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit: Smelphenosomprok, Vrendoph, Krelphonar, Mrendepholm, Krn.
