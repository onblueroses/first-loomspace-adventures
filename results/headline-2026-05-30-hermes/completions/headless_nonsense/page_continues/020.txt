### Krelphosomorph

The Krelphosomorph is a magical ritual in the Smelphomancy tradition. It is a ritual of invocation, intended to bring the magician into contact with the divine and to grant them power over spirits. The ritual is performed at midnight, over a dying fire, with the pentacle of the Great Work upon the magician's breast.

The ritual begins with an invocation of the divine, addressing the deity as the creator of the universe and all things within it. The magician identifies themselves as Vrondh, a prophet of the deity, and calls upon the deity by various names, including Vrelthemphos, Klepth, Mronth, Vronfeth, and Krelphoth.

The magician then recites a series of divine names and words of power, including Mrok, Plenphor, Smelphenoroth
