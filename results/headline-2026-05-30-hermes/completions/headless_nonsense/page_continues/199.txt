 "At the sound of these words, the gods of the earth will tremble, and those who dwell in the realm of fire will be afraid, and the spirits of the air and the ghosts of the dead will be obedient. And they will come and serve thee when thou dost call them."

The invocation is followed by a description of a ritual to be performed in order to summon the "holy angel" who will grant the reader's wishes, and a list of the names and functions of the spirits that the ritual will call forth.

The invocation is not addressed to any deity or spirit known from Greek or other mythology, and the words used are not derived from any known language. It is possible that they were invented by the author.
