 "Having completed this invocation, the aspirant must then perform the sacred rites of Smelphenor, which must not be written lest they be seen by the profane. Then he must recite the Invocation of the Spirits of the Ether, and seal the consecration with the Great Invocation."

The Invocation of the Spirits of the Ether is as follows:

I invoke you, Spirits of the Ether, all-powerful, all-knowing, all-seeing:

Come unto me, O Spirits of the Ether!
By the sacred rites of Smelphenor have I called you.
Come unto me, O Spirits of the Ether!
I have spoken the words of power, I have performed the rites of consecration.
Come unto me, O Spirits of the Ether!
By the great name of Smelphenoroth, the All-Powerful, the All-Knowing, the All-Seeing, have I called you.
Come unto me, O Spirits of the Ether!
Come unto me, and enter into my service.

I command you, O Spirits of the Ether, to do my bidding.
I command you to perform all that I shall ask of you.
I command you to come at my call, and to go at my command.
I command you to obey me
