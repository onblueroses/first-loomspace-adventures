 "Thee I invoke, the Terrible and Invisible God: Vrelthep, Vrelthep. At whose presence and at whose command the heavens do tremble, the earth doth quake, the seas do overwhelm, the mountains do fall, the winds do crack, the pillars of fire do fail, the stars do burn, and all the elements do vanish away."

The next page of the manuscript describes the appearance of the god: "Vrelthep, Vrelthep, Vrelthep. He is clad in a robe of flame and girt with a belt of burning emeralds. His hair is a crown of fire, and his eyes are like unto the wheels of a chariot, filled with the light of the sun. His nostrils breathe forth smoke and fire, and his mouth is filled with the tongues of men and beasts. His hands are like unto the wings of the eagle, and his feet are like unto the paws of the lion. He rideth upon the whirlwind, and the storm is his chariot. He is the Lord of the Universe, and the Master of the World. He is the Creator of all things, and the Destroyer of all things.
