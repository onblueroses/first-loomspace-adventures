 

The Initiation of the Candidate

The Candidate, hoodwinked, is led into the Temple by the First and Second Conductors. The Hegemon and Hiereus, standing by the sacred fire, exclaim:

Hegemon: Whom have you there?
Conductors: A Candidate who seeks the hidden knowledge of the mysteries of this Order.
Hiereus: Let the hoodwink be removed.
[Hoodwink is removed.]
Hegemon: Child of Earth, why do you seek admission to this Order?
Candidate [prompted]: To gain knowledge of the mysteries of life and death, and to conquer the fear of death.
Hiereus: The path is long and the gate is guarded. Only those who have learned the secrets of the Ineffable Name can enter. Do you persist?
Candidate: I do.
Hegemon: Then may the Divine Powers assist you. [To Conductors] Let the Candidate be taken to the East.

The Candidate is led to the East. The Hierophant, seated on the throne, wearing the robe of the Mrolthen One, asks:

Hierophant: What is your name?
Candidate: [Gives name]
Hierophant: Henceforth you shall be known
