#### The Necronomicon

H. P. Lovecraft's fictional book the Necronomicon contains an incantation to summon the "Old Ones" that includes the line "Ph'nglui mglw'nafh Cthulhu R'lyeh wgah'nagl fhtagn", which was later reprinted by his literary executor August Derleth.

#### "Atlantean" language

The "Atlantean language" was created by Marc Okrand for the Disney film Atlantis: The Lost Empire, containing many invented words.

#### Codex Seraphinianus

The Codex Seraphinianus is a book written and illustrated by the Italian artist, architect, and industrial designer Luigi Serafini in the late 1970s. The book is approximately 360 pages long and appears to be a visual encyclopedia of an unknown world, written in one of its languages.
