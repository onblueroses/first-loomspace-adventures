The "Litany" is a series of supplications to Mrolth, each beginning with the words "O Mrolth," and followed by a varying number of names and attributes. For example, "O Mrolth, Vrenphra, Vron, Vrelthra, Smelphra, Smelphenor, Mronth, Mrelthra, Mrelphenor, grant that I may accomplish all my desires, for Thou art the Mighty One, and I am Vrondh, Thy Prophet." There are twelve such supplications in total.

The "Conjuration of the Four Elements" is a series of invocations addressed to the spirits of the four elements: earth, air, fire, and water. Each invocation begins with the words "I conjure thee, O Spirit of [element]," and is followed by a series of names and attributes specific to that element.
