 “At this point, the adept should have the demons assembled in front of them, and the circle should be completely surrounded by them. Now, you must brandish the sword, and say the following words of power:”

Hear me, all ye spirits!

I am Vrondh, your master.
I am Vrondh, who is your lord.
I am Vrondh, who is your king.
I am Vrondh, who will be exalted among you.

Submit yourselves unto me, so that I may be your master, for I am Vrondh, the angel of Plomphren Krempheniska. Submit, for I am Vrondh, who is your lord. Submit, for I am Vrondh, who is your king. Submit, for I am Vrondh, who will be exalted among you.

Hear me, and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.

MROK PHENG WROL EN SM
