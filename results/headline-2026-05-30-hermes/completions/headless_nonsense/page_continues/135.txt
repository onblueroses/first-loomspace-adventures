 

Come hither, O ye spirits, and obey the Voice of Fire.

In the name of Smelphenor, I declare that the spirits of the elements shall be obedient unto my command, and that they shall not suffer me to be unrobed of my power.

Hear me, O Spirits, for I am Vrelphos, the Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska; this is my true name, handed down to me by the prophets of the Earth.

I am the one who begets and destroys.
I am the Mighty One, who possesses the immortal Fire.
I am the Truth that hates the deeds of injustice in the world.
I am the Mrolthen Krelph with sight in his feet.

By the power of Smelphenor, I command that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God shall be obedient unto me.

Come hither, O ye spirits, and obey the Voice of Fire!

I invoke Thee, the Terrible and Invisible God who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit: Smelphenosom
