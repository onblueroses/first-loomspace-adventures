The "Mrolthen Invocation" is clearly a parody of the "Bornless Ritual", a fragmentary Greek magical text which was first published in 1852 in the third volume of the Greek Magical Papyri. The text is a series of increasingly grandiose and esoteric invocations to various deities and spiritual forces, culminating in the identification of the speaker with the supreme deity. The "Bornless Ritual" was popularized in the early 20th century by the British occultist Aleister Crowley, who claimed to have used it to invoke his personal "Holy Guardian Angel". The text has since become a staple of modern Western magical practice, and has been adapted and reinterpreted by numerous occultists and magical orders.
