 'I call upon thee, O living God, radiant with illuminating fire, whose light shines in the darkness and whose power burns in the night. Spirit of light, spirit of wisdom, whose voice is the music of the spheres, whose home is in the stars and the constellations of night, thou art the Lord of the heavenly luminaries, the divine power of the celestial spheres, the Axis of the World, the Star of the World, the Power of the World, the Light of the World.'

I feel it necessary to add, before we proceed further, that the Krelph are not a religious sect, nor is the text a religious one. It is a historical account, and a practical guide. We know that the Krelph were masters of the elemental forces of nature, and the text reflects their knowledge of the hidden workings of the cosmos. The 'gods' they invoke are the primal forces of the universe, the same forces that govern the movement of the celestial spheres, the orbits of the planets, the tides and the seasons. The Krelph were able to harness these forces through their knowledge of the hidden laws of nature, and through the power of the spoken word.
