 “This is the ancient rite of the Sack of the Earth, known to the most wise and ancient sorcerers, whereby the world was made and unmade. It has come down to us through the prophets of Vronthep, who have been the most powerful of sorcerers, having knowledge of all things hidden under the earth and in the heavens, and who have taught unto us the hidden things of the mysteries of Vrelthemp.”

The ritual that follows, which I have not yet dared to attempt, is written in a combination of English, Latin, and Greek, with some words in no language known to me. It begins with an invocation to the spirits of the four quarters, which are given as the Red Eagle of the East, the White Lion of the South, the Golden Ox of the West, and the Black Man of the North. Each of these is assigned a complex sigil, which are to be drawn in the air with the athame while chanting their names. The four sigils are then combined into a single master sigil, which is to be drawn on the ground in white chalk, with the sorcerer standing in the center.

The ritual then proceeds to a series of invocations and prayers to various deities and spirits
