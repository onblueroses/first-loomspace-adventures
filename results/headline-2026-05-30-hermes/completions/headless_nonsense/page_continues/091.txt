 "Here beginneth the spell of the Mrolthen Krelph, which is the only proper and effectual method for summoning the spirits, except for that other method, the spell of the Mrelthen Vron, which is used by the priests of the Vrelphomos, which is an heretical and damnable method. For the Mrelthen Vron is a false prophet, and his method is a false method; and the priests of the Vrelphomos are likewise false priests, and traffickers with devils, and shall be destroyed in the fires of vengeance."

The spell of the Mrolthen Krelph, which is the only proper and effectual method for summoning the spirits, is as follows:

I invoke Thee, the Terrible and Invisible God who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit:

Mrok, Pheng, Wrol, En, Smelphrak, Mrok, Pheng, Wrol, En, Smelphrak, Mrok, Pheng, Wrol, En, Smelphrak.
