 "After this, say the following words, and they will be established."

The invocation has several overlaps with the Ophite Gnosis, such as the "moist and dry" (a phrase also found in the Bruce Codex) and the sequence of vowels (also found in the Books of Jeu), as well as the "grace of the world" (also found in the Pistis Sophia).

### Invocation of the Third Hour

The invocation of the third hour calls upon the divine names "PHEŌ PHINKA PHOTHA PHĒGA PHŌPHOI PHŌCHLĒ PHŌCHLARBŌ PHŌCHLARBŌTH PHŌTHONPHŌCH" (with the first six names being permutations of the seven Greek vowels), and asks for the ability to perform various magical acts, such as:

* causing the gods to appear and do one's will
* sending dreams
* interpreting dreams
* sending and driving away demons
* opening and closing the mouth of the womb
* stopping blood
* casting out the soul
* causing evil spirits to dwell in tombs
* making the lamp of fire to burn
