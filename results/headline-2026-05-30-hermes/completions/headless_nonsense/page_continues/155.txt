After the first recitation, the magician must take up a position facing the south and repeat it, then facing the east, the north, and the west; and finally, standing in the centre of the circle, facing the direction in which he first stood. The ceremony as described is almost identical to the First Conjuration of the Goetia.

### "The Black Pilgrimage"

"The Black Pilgrimage" is an account of a journey to Chorazin, a ruined city in the north of Israel, where Jesus is said to have performed a miracle by driving out demons (Luke 4:40-41). It is written in a more modern style than the rest of the book, and makes no claim to antiquity. It may be based on a journey made by Crowley himself, as it contains numerous references to his associates.
