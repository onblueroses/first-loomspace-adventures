 "I now pronounce this invocation of the Mrolthen One, that it might be set forth before the people and that they might learn the words of power and pronounce them in their time of need. For truly, there is no spirit in heaven or on earth or under the earth that will not obey these words, if they be pronounced with a pure heart and an upright spirit.

"And if any spirit shall hear these words and obey not, let them be cast into the outer darkness and bound with chains of fire for all eternity. For the Mrolthen One is mighty to save, and his wrath is terrible against those who disobey his commands.

"Therefore let all who read these words take heed, and let them pronounce them with reverence and with fear, for they are the words of the Mrolthen One, and they shall not be taken lightly.

"And if any shall use these words for evil purposes, let them be accursed, and let their names be blotted out from the book of life. For the Mrolthen One is a righteous god, and he will not suffer his words to be profaned.
