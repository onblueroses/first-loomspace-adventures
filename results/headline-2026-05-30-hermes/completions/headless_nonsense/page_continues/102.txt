Thou art Asar Un-Nefer, whom no man hath seen at any time. Thou art Jâbas, Thou art Iâphô, Thou hast distinguished between the Just and the Unjust. Thou didst make the Female and the Male. Thou didst produce the Seed and the Fruit. Thou didst form Men to love one another, and to hate one another. I am [insert your name], thy Prophet, unto Whom Thou didst commit Thy Mysteries, the Ceremonies of Jemalakdâ. Thou didst produce the moist and the dry, and that which nourisheth all created Life.
