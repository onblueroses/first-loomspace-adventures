 "The rest of the invocation is lost."

The invocation seems to be an attempt to invoke the deity "Smelphenor" by a prophet, Vrondh, who identifies himself as "the Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska". The invocation also references a creation myth and the prophet's own identity.

## Literary references

* In The Illuminatus! Trilogy by Robert Shea and Robert Anton Wilson, Smelphenor is mentioned as the deity of the "Discordian" version of the Golden Dawn.
* In The Devil Rides Out by Dennis Wheatley, the Duke de Richleau explains that Smelphenor is one of the many names for the Devil.
