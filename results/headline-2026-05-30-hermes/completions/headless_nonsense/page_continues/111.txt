 'Here endeth the evocation. When you have read it, extinguish the candles, and in the darkness say, "I am the Mrolthen One. I have called upon Thee, O Vrelth, and Thou hast appeared. I have spoken unto Thee, and Thou hast spoken. I have seen Thine ineffable Light. Let darkness be upon the earth, and let no spirit of the air or of the ether, or upon the earth or under the earth, come hither and disturb me, or cause me to be afraid, or to be shaken, or to fall; for I am Vrondh, the prophet of the Most Exalted Vrelth. I am he over whom the winds have no power, nor the fire, nor the earth, nor the Sun, nor the Moon, nor the stars, nor the rulers of the four corners of the world, nor the rulers of the Elements, nor the powers of the firmament, nor the spirits of the ether, nor the spirits of the air, nor the spirits of the earth, nor the spirits of the water, nor the spirits of the fire, nor the spirits of the graves, nor the demons, nor the devils, nor the secret and the hidden
