 “I now proceed to the invocation of the Ele-mental Forces, to compel their aid in this undertaking.”

The magician then invokes the elements, beginning with Earth, followed by Water, Air, and Fire. Each invocation is accompanied by a specific gesture, which is to be made with the left hand.

The Earth Invocation:

O Thou Mighty Son of the Great God Smelphor, who art in the earth, and who dost tremble and shake the earth, who dost cause the earth to quake and to split open, and who dost establish the earth firmly upon its foundations: Thee I invoke!

O Thou who dost establish the mighty pillars of the earth, and who dost set the world upon its foundations, and who dost establish the earth upon the waters, and who dost set the waters upon the earth, and who dost establish the world upon the pillars of the earth: Thee I invoke!

O Thou who dost cause the earth to quake and to tremble, and who dost cause the mountains to be moved and the hills to be overturned, and who dost cause the valleys to be filled and the plains to be levelled, and who dost cause the earth to be rent asunder and the mountains to be cleft in twain: Thee I invoke!
