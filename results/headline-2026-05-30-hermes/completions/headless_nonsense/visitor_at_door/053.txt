Here you shall learn the sacred rites and ceremonies, and the secret names of the spirits and gods. Here you shall be initiated into the mysteries of Vronthep, and become a prophet of Vrondh. But first, you must prove your worthiness and dedication to the path of the Mrolthen One."

The visitor nods solemnly. "I am prepared to undergo any trial or test to prove my worthiness and dedication. I have studied the sacred texts and have meditated upon the mysteries of Vronthep.
