 It is a voice that is not quite human, that is like the sound of many winds, of many waters, that is like the murmur of the stars in their courses and the growling of thunder imprisoned in the caves of the earth.

"Thou hast called me, Vrondh, and I am come. And now I shall do thy will, for thou hast uttered the words of power, and they have compelled me."

Then Vrondh, the old sorcerer, speaks, and his voice is thin and quavering, like the voice of a man who has lived overlong, and has seen and known too much of good and evil.
