The visitor says, "I have received the name of the God and the number of the God. Let me enter."

The priest says, "Enter."

The visitor enters the temple, and the priest receives him with these words:

"Enter, enter, O Angel of the Blind and Secret God. Enter, enter, O Servant of the Mighty Smelphenor. Enter, enter, O Vessel of the Name and the Number. Enter, enter, O Master of the Hidden Knowledge."

And the visitor says, "I have entered."

And the priest says, "I receive thee."

And the visitor says, "I give thee the blessing of Vrenphol, the Blind and Secret God, and the blessing of Smelphenor, the Mighty God, and the blessing of the Number Nine, which is the number of the God."
