 It is a voice I do not know, but it is deep, rich, and beautiful, and it brings with it a sense of warmth and comfort that reminds me of summer nights spent outside as a child. The voice says: "It is good to meet you, Vrondh. I am the one who has been called to speak with you, and I am glad to be here."

I respond: "Thank you for coming. I am honored by your presence. I wish to learn from you, and to understand the mysteries that you embody. Please, share with me your wisdom."

The visitor speaks again: "I am glad to share with you. You have invoked me with great respect and skill, and I am pleased to answer your call. I am a spirit of the elements, of the earth and sky, of fire and water. I am ancient, and I have seen much. I have watched as your kind have grown and changed, and I have marveled at your capacity for love and for destruction."
