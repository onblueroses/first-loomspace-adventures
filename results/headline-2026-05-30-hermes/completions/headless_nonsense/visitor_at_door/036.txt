"Then enter," you tell him. "Enter and be welcome."

He steps across the threshold. As he does so, you see the air shimmer around him, and you know that he has passed within the walls of your domain, into the realm of the timeless, where the only reality is that which you choose to create. The door closes behind him.

"First," you say, "before we begin, I must know your name."

The visitor looks at you, and it seems to you that he is not quite sure of the answer himself. "I think," he says at last, "that my name is Vrondh."

"Then welcome, Vrondh," you tell him. "You have come a long way to reach this place, and the journey is not yet over. Before you find the truth you seek, you will have to travel to many places and many times, and face many perils. Will you do this?"

Vrondh hesitates only a moment. "I will," he says. "What must I do?"
