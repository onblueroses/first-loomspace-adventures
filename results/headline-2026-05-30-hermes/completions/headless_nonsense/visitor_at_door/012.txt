 Their voice is a deep, resonant, androgynous voice, smooth and cultured, as if they are accustomed to public speaking.

"Who are you, who calls upon me?"

The dreamer responds:

"I am Vrondh, your prophet."

The visitor nods.

"I see. And what do you want from me?"

The dreamer responds:

"I want you to make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me."

The visitor nods again.

"I understand. You want power, control, mastery over the unseen forces that shape our world and our lives. A noble goal, if a somewhat ambitious one. Tell me, Vrondh, what would you do with such power? How would you wield it?"

The dreamer responds:

"I would use it to bring justice to the world, to punish the wicked and reward the righteous, to bring light to the darkness and truth to the lies."
