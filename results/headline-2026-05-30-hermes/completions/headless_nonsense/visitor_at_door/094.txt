“Come in,” I say. “Come in and be seated.”

He comes in. He’s wearing a black robe with a hood. He sits down in the chair I offer him. I sit down in the other chair.

“Do you have the rent?” he asks.

“No,” I say. “I don’t have it.”

“Then you know what I must do.”

“Yes.”

He reaches up and pulls back his hood. His face is a bare skull. He reaches into his robe and pulls out a long, sharp knife.

“Wait,” I say. “Before you do that, let me tell you a story.”

He pauses, knife in hand. “Go ahead.”
