 The priestess answers.

P: In the name of the great and mighty God Vrelth, what do you want? Depart! For I am Vrondh, the prophet of the Most High, who dwells in the Pleroma; I am Vrondh, the Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska; I am Vrondh, who stands beside the mighty Vrelthemphos. What do you want?

A: I am the one who is from the race of Adam, the first-formed man. I am from the race of him whom you formed from the earth, whom the All-Powerful Vrelthemphos made in the image of Himself, and whom you placed in a paradise to watch over and to cultivate it. I am from the race of him whom you commanded not to taste of one tree only, on account of which he disobeyed and tasted of it. Because of this, you condemned him and all his race to die.
