The visitor hands you an ancient tome, bound in black leather and adorned with arcane symbols. "This is the Book of Vrelthos, a powerful grimoire that contains the secrets of the universe. Guard it well, and use its knowledge for the betterment of all."

With that, the visitor vanishes, leaving behind only a faint trace of ozone in the air. You are left alone with the Book of Vrelthos, your mind reeling with the possibilities that lie ahead.

You have been chosen, Vrondh. The path before you is one of great power and responsibility. May you wield it wisely.
