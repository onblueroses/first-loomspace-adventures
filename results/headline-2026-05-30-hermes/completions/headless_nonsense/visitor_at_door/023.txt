 "We were not expecting you," they say, "and we are not prepared."

"Then prepare yourselves," says the Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska, and steps inside. They close the door behind them, and stand in the entryway with their hands clasped behind their back.

The visitor looks at them, and then turns away, and goes to a cabinet, and opens a drawer, and takes out a long, thin silver chain. They turn back to the Angel, and hold the chain out to them.

The Angel takes the chain, and holds it up to examine it. It is very fine, and very delicate, and it shimmers in the light.

"This will do," they say, and they wrap the chain around their wrist, and it tightens, and it binds itself to them.

The visitor nods, and then they step forward, and they kneel down before the Angel, and they bow their head.

"I am ready," they say.

The Angel places their hand on the visitor's head, and they close their eyes, and they speak.
