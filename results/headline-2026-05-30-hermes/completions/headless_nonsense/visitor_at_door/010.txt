The floor of the room begins to smoke. It does not catch fire. It simply smokes, thickly, a heavy greasy smoke that rises up and obscures the room.

"I am Vrondh," says the man at the door. "I am Vrondh, and I am come to destroy you. I am Vrondh, and I have come."

The smoke congeals into a great snake, a snake of smoke and darkness, its scales an iridescent black. It coils around the room. It coils around you. It constricts.

"I am Vrondh," says the man at the door. "I am Vrondh, and I am come to destroy you. I am Vrondh, and I have come. I am Vrondh, and I am here."

The snake is tight around you. You can barely breathe.
