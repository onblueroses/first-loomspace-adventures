 The voice is different from the one before, but it is still not that of a human.

"Who calls upon us?"

The sorcerer answers.

"I am Vrondh, the prophet of Vrelthemphos."

"Who is this Vrelthemphos?"

"Vrelthemphos is the creator of all things, the one who has distinguished between the just and the unjust, the one who has made the female and the male, the one who has produced the seed and the fruit."

"And what is it that you seek, Vrondh?"

"I seek the power to make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me."

The voice at the door speaks again, this time with a hint of amusement.

"A bold request, Vrondh. But to wield such power, one must prove themselves worthy. Tell me, what is the true name of Vrelthemphos?"

The sorcerer answers without hesitation.

"Plomphren Krempheniska."
