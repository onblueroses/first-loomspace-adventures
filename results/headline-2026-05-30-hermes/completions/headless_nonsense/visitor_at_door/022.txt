 "I am Zaran, a humble servant of the one who is called Smelphenor. I am the voice of the wind, the invisible flame, the servant of the Vrelthepian mysteries. I have heard your prayer, and I have come to serve you. What would you have me do, o mortal?"

The master replies. "I am Vrondh, the prophet of Smelphenor. I invoke you in the name of the Mighty One, the ruler of the winds and the flames, the one who begets and destroys, the one who is the Grace of the World. I command you to grant me the power to control the spirits of the air and the fire, to make them subject unto me, so that I may use them for my purposes."

Zaran responds. "Your faith has been heard, o Vrondh. I grant you the power to control the spirits of the air and the fire. May they be subject unto you, and may they serve you in your endeavors. Use this power wisely, and remember that it is the grace of Smelphenor that has granted you this gift. Go forth and use your power for the benefit of the world, and for the glory of the Mighty One."
