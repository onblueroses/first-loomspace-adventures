 The Mrolthen One says:

Let the immortal depth of thy soul be predominant, O Vrondh, for thou art the mighty power of the great, exalted, adjustable Mind. Thou art the guard of the Cosmos. Thou art the dignified one who is seated upon the lotus of the Knowledge. Thou art the progeny of the chariot of the Light. Thou art the guard of the three gates of the Fire. Thou art the messenger of the God of the Sun.

The visitor at the door speaks. The Mrolthen One says:

Thou art the Angel of the sparkling Sunbeam. The Lord Vrelthomphos is with thee. Thou art the glorious light of the sun. Thou art the bright morning star. Thou art the ruler of the day. Thou art the purifier of the eternal, immortal, adjustable Light. Thou art the great builder in the house of the sphere of the stars. Thou art the Governor of the Seven Pole-Lords of the Seven Heavens.

The visitor at the door speaks. The Mrolthen One says:

Vrondh, thou hast come. Thou hast descended into the House of Plomphren Krempheniska. Thou hast descended into the House of the mighty Lord
