 "I am Krelph."

The door opens. The visitor enters. The door closes behind him.

The visitor speaks again. "I am Krelph."

The Rite of the Opening of the Mouth commences.

Krelph speaks. "I am the one who begets and destroys. I am the one who grants breath to the one who is dead."

Krelph approaches the Mummy and touches its mouth with his finger.

Krelph speaks. "I have come to embrace you. I am your son who loves you."

Krelph embraces the Mummy.

Krelph speaks. "I have opened your mouth with the instrument of Vrelthem. I have opened your mouth with the instrument of Mrok. I have opened your mouth with the instrument of Smelphenor. Your mouth is opened by Vrelthem with the instrument of iron. Your mouth is opened by Mrok with the divine instrument. Your mouth is opened by Smelphenor with the instrument of gold."

Krelph touches the Mummy's mouth with the sacred adze.
