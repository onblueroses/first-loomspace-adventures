Vrondh rises to his feet. He walks over to the door. He puts his hand on the handle.

“Don’t open it,” says the visitor.

“Why not?” Vrondh asks.

“It’s not time yet,” says the visitor. “The door opens at the end.”

“Ah,” says Vrondh.

He takes his hand away. He returns to the center of the room. He sits.

“Krelth,” says Vrondh.

Krelth stirs. He opens his eyes. He looks at Vrondh.

“Krelth,” Vrondh says again.

“Vrondh,” Krelth agrees.

“Do you know what I’m going to do?”

“Yes,” says Krelth. “You’re going to end the world.”

Vrondh nods.
