Mrolth: Who is this that speaks the words of the mighty Vrondh? Have I found one of the faithful in this land of unbelievers?

High Priest: I am a stranger in this land, but I am a servant of the true god Vronthep. Are you one of the faithful?

Mrolth: I am Mrolth, a prophet of Vronthep, sent to find the faithful in this land of darkness. How did you come to know the sacred words of power?

High Priest: I was initiated into the mysteries of Vronthep in a faraway land. I have come here to spread the true faith among the people of this city.

Mrolth: Truly, the ways of Vronthep are mysterious. I have traveled far seeking those who know the true god, and I have found one in an unexpected place. Let us go inside and speak further of the mysteries of our faith.

High Priest: Come in, brother, and let us share the secrets of Vronthep. I am known in this city as the High Priest of the Temple of the Hidden Light, but you may call me Vrondh, for I am a true servant of the Mrolthen One.
