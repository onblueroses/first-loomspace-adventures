 “Vrondh? Are you all right?”

Vrondh’s eyes open. “Yes,” he says. “I’m fine. I’ll be right there.”

He looks at the door, and then back at the diagram. He speaks again, his voice low and quiet.

“Mphō Krelphōth.”

The candles flare. The diagram ignites. The room is dark again.

Vrondh rises to his feet, walks to the door, and opens it. “Hi,” he says. “Come in.”

“Hi,” says the visitor. “I brought some beer.”

“Great,” says Vrondh. “I’ll get some glasses.”

He walks into the kitchen. The visitor steps inside.

The door closes behind her.
