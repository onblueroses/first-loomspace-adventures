 It is the voice of Srelth, who says:

SRELTH: I have come from the depths of the world to answer your call. What do you wish of me?

VRONDH: I have called you to do my bidding, to perform the works of the Mrolthen One. You shall be subject to me, and I shall command you.

SRELTH: By what authority do you command me, Vrondh? For I am a mighty spirit, and not easily swayed by the whims of mortals.

VRONDH: I command you by the authority of the Mrolthen One, who created the heavens and the earth, the night and the day, the darkness and the light. I am Vrondh, the prophet of Vrelthemphos, unto whom the mysteries and ceremonies of Smelphenor have been committed.

SRELTH: Your words carry the weight of truth, Vrondh. I shall heed your commands, for the authority of the Mrolthen One is not to be taken lightly. Speak, and I shall listen.
