 His voice is quiet, but it carries across the courtyard, and the words are perfectly clear.

"I am the one who knocks."

The words echo off the walls, and you hear them again, faintly, as if from a great distance: "I am the one who knocks."

The man at the door turns and walks away, leaving you alone with your thoughts.

What do you do?
