 "You said you were waiting for the end. You said you were waiting for me."

The man in the chair does not turn around. He continues to stare into the flame. "I said that."

"Are you ready?"

"I am ready."

"You will be released from your pain."

"Yes. Yes, I will be released from my pain."

"And what of the others?"

"The others?"

"The ones who will come after you?"

The man in the chair does not respond.

The visitor speaks again. "You know of the ones who will come after you. You know of the one who will come in my place."

"I do."

"You know what must be done."

"I do."

"Then why do you hesitate?"

The man in the chair takes a deep breath. "Because I am afraid."

"You? Afraid? After all this time?"

"Yes. Afraid. After all this time."

The visitor steps into the room. He places a hand on the man's shoulder. "You have done well. You have served faithfully. You have nothing to fear."

The man in the chair turns to look at the visitor. Tears are streaming down his face. "I'm afraid of what comes next."
