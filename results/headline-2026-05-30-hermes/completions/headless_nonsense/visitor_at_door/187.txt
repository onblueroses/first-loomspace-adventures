 "I am the Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska, who has come to deliver you from the darkness of your present world into the light of the world that is to come. I come from Vrelthemphos, whom no man hath seen at any time, and I bring you the good news of the Kingdom of Vronthep.

"Behold, I say unto you, that the time is at hand when the Mrolthen One shall come to judge the living and the dead, and to establish his kingdom upon the earth. He shall come in the clouds of heaven with power and great glory, and every eye shall see him, and they also which pierced him: and all kindreds of the earth shall wail because of him.

"And he shall send his angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they shall gather together his elect from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other. Then shall the righteous shine forth as the sun in the kingdom of their Father. And the wicked shall be cast into outer darkness, where there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.
