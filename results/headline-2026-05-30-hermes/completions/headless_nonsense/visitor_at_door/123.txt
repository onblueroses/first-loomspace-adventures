I ask them to leave, but they do not.

I ask them again, more forcefully this time, but still they remain.

I begin to panic. The ritual must not be interrupted. The ritual must be completed. I must complete the ritual.

I tell them to leave, to go away, to leave me alone. I tell them I do not need their help, I do not want their help, I just want to be left alone to complete my work.

But they will not leave. They keep talking, asking questions, trying to get me to stop.

I cannot stop. I must not stop. The ritual must be completed.

In desperation, I grab the knife from the altar and I lunge at the intruder. I slash and stab, hacking and cutting until they stop moving, stop talking, stop interrupting.
