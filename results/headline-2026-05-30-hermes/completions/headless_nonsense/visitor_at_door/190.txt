***

The visitor reveals to Vrondh the secret of the Unseen, the art of Smelphenor. It is a practice that allows the practitioner to remain unseen and unheard, to move about without being detected. The visitor explains that this art is to be used only for the purposes of good, to help those in need and to fight against injustice.

Vrondh listens intently, absorbing every word. The visitor demonstrates the technique, and Vrondh practices until he is able to perform it flawlessly. The visitor then departs, leaving Vrondh with this new knowledge and power.

From that day forward, Vrondh uses the art of Smelphenor to help those in need. He moves about unseen, assisting the poor, the sick, and the oppressed. He becomes known as the Unseen Helper, a mysterious figure who appears out of nowhere to lend aid and then vanishes without a trace.
