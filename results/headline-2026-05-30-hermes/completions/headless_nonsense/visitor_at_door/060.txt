 "I am an initiate of the mysteries of Vrelth. I have come to offer a sacrifice. Let me in."

The door opens.

---

The room is dark and smells of incense. In the center of the room is an altar, upon which lies a young woman, naked and bound. She is drugged, her eyes glassy and unfocused.

The visitor approaches the altar and removes a knife from his robes. He raises the knife above the woman's chest and speaks.

"I offer this sacrifice to you, O Vrelth, in exchange for your favor and power. May her blood seal our pact."

He plunges the knife into the woman's heart. She gasps and convulses, then lies still. Her blood pools on the altar and drips onto the floor.

The visitor steps back from the altar and kneels, his head bowed. "I am yours, Vrelth. Command me."

A voice speaks from the darkness, deep and resonant. "Rise, my servant. You have proven your devotion. The power you seek shall be yours."

The visitor rises. He feels a surge of energy coursing through his body, a dark and terrible power. He smiles.
