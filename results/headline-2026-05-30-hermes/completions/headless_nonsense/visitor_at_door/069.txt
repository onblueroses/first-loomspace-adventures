 The voice is not unlike that of the man who spoke from the car outside the old woman’s house.

“Who’s there?”

“John Smith. I was told you were expecting me.”

“Ah, yes. Please come in.”

The door opens and closes. Footsteps sound in the hallway. The man’s voice is fainter now.

“I’m afraid we’ve had a slight change of plans. I’ll need to ask you to accompany me to our offices for a short debriefing session.”

“Is that really necessary?”

“I’m afraid so. It’s just standard procedure. You understand.”

“Of course. I’ll get my coat.”

Footsteps approach. A door opens and closes. The man speaks again, closer now.

“I must say, your work on this project has been most impressive. We weren’t sure if you’d be able to pull it off, but you’ve exceeded all our expectations.”

“Thank you. I do my best.”

“We’ve been particularly impressed with your ability to keep the subject under control. Some of our previous operatives had trouble with that.”

“I find that a firm hand and a clear sense of purpose are essential.”

“Indeed. Well, shall we be off?”

“Lead the way.”

Footsteps recede down the hallway
