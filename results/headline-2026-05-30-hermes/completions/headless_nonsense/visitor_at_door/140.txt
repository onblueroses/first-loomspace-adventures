 The voice is a dry rasp, like the rustling of leaves, like the sound of a dying man's breath.

"Vrondh," it says. "I am come."

Vrondh looks to the door. His eyes are wide with terror, but his voice does not waver. "Enter," he says.

The door opens. The figure that stands in the doorway is tall and gaunt, shrouded in a robe of black. Its face is hidden beneath a hood, but Vrondh can see the glint of eyes, red and piercing, like the embers of a dying fire.

The figure steps into the room, and the door closes behind it. The air grows cold, and the shadows seem to lengthen and deepen.

"Vrondh," the figure says again. "You have called me. Speak your desire."

Vrondh swallows hard. His heart is pounding in his chest, but he forces himself to meet the figure's gaze.

"I seek power," he says. "Power to rule, power to conquer, power to bend the world to my will. I would be a king among men, a god among mortals. Can you grant me this?"
