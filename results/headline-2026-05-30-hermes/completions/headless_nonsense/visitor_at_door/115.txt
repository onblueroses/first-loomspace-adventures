 "I am an Adept of the Fourth Circle. I have passed the Pylon of the Sphinx and the Pylon of the Ox. I have descended into the Crypt and beheld the Formless One. I have crossed the Chasm by the Path of the Arrow. I have climbed the Great Tower and entered the Triangle. I have stood before the Seven and received the Name."

The officiant speaks. "Enter, O Adept, for you have received the Name."

The visitor enters the temple and joins the officiant. They both turn to face the East.

The officiant speaks. "Let us consecrate the temple."

The visitor speaks. "So let it be."

All present perform the Consecration of the Temple.

The officiant speaks. "Let us invoke the presence of Vrelth."

The visitor speaks. "So let it be."

All present perform the First Invocation of Vrelth.

The officiant speaks. "Let us invoke the presence of the Mrolthen One."

The visitor speaks. "So let it be."

All present perform the Second Invocation of Vrelth.

The officiant speaks. "Let us invoke the presence of the Eternal."

The visitor speaks. "So let it be."

All present perform the Third Invocation
