I have come. What dost thou desire of me?"

Vrondh speaks.

"O Smelphenor, I ask of thee thy servant, the Angel of the Air, Vrelthor, that he may be my minister and do my bidding."

The visitor at the door speaks.

"Behold, I give thee Vrelthor, my servant, the Angel of the Air. He shall be thy minister and do thy bidding. Speak to him my words: MROK PHENG WROL EN SMELPHRAK."

The visitor at the door vanishes.
