 Is it in response to this invocation?

Well, that's the thing. Nothing enters the room. Not that I can see, anyway.

But I feel something. It's like a presence, a pressure, a weight in the air. The shadows seem to deepen, and the light from my candles seems to dim, though the flames burn as bright as ever.

There's a...a humming. A vibration. Like the sound of a thousand voices, all whispering at once, just below the threshold of hearing. It sets my teeth on edge and makes my skin crawl.

And then, a voice. Not a human voice. Something else. Something...older. Deeper. It's like the rumble of distant thunder, like the groan of shifting tectonic plates. It's a voice that you feel more than hear, that echoes in your bones and sets your heart racing.

"Mortal," it says, and I feel the word in my marrow. "You have called me. What would you ask of me?"

I swallow hard, my mouth suddenly dry. "I...I seek knowledge," I manage to say, my own voice sounding thin and reedy in comparison. "I seek the secrets of the universe, the mysteries of creation and destruction."
