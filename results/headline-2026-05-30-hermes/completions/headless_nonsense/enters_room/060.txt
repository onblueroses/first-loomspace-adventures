 The demon appears to be a serpent with a multitude of wings, and has no discernable eyes. It slithers into the room, it's wings spread wide. It's scales are black, and the wings seem to be made of shadows.

Vrondh: What art thou, who enters this sacred place?

The serpent hisses, and then speaks, it's voice a raspy whisper. "I am the one who goes by many names. I am the darkness that envelops the world, the shadow that lurks in every corner. I am the one who brings the night, and the one who brings fear to the hearts of mortals. I am the one who is summoned by the foolish and the desperate, and I am the one who grants their wishes, for a price."

Vrondh: Why have you come to this place, spirit?

The serpent laughs, a sound like the rustling of leaves in the wind. "I have come because I was summoned, mortal. I have come because you have called me here, with your words of power and your sacrifices. I have come to grant your wish, whatever it may be. But be warned, mortal. My gifts come with a price, and that price is always high."
