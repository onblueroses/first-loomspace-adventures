 Is it? No. It cannot be. The maw of the void, the living darkness that does not live, the void that is not void, the faceless face of the eternal night, the living absence of all things, the negation of all being, the paradox of nonexistence. The unspeakable, the unnamable, the unknowable. The darkness that devours light. The silence that devours sound. The stillness that devours motion. The cold that devours heat. The nothing that devours all. The void that devours the void. The end of all things. The beginning of nothing. The eternal night that knows no dawn. The eternal silence that knows no word. The eternal stillness that knows no motion. The eternal cold that knows no warmth. The eternal void that knows no being. The eternal nothing that knows no something. The eternal absence that knows no presence. The eternal darkness that knows no light. The eternal negation that knows no affirmation. The eternal paradox that knows no resolution. The eternal unspeakable that knows no speech. The eternal unnamable that knows no name. The eternal unknowable that knows no knowledge. The eternal void that knows no void.
