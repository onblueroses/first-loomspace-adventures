What has been done. I do not know. I only know it has been done. It is finished. It is over. It is done.

The next day, I return to the room. The candles are burnt out. The air is stale. The presence is gone. The power is gone. The something is gone.

I open the window. I let in the light. I let in the air. I clean the room. I remove the circle. I put away the candles. I put away the knife. I put away the book.
