It says, "Vrondh, I have heard your invocation. What would you ask of me?"

I must be careful. I must not ask for too much. I must be precise.

I say, "Mighty Mrolth, I ask for the power to heal the sick and injured. I ask for the power to bring comfort to the suffering. I ask for the power to bring hope to the hopeless."

The Mrolth considers my request. It is not a small thing that I ask.

It says, "Vrondh, you ask for a great gift. But you have been faithful, and you have invoked me with the proper ceremonies and the true names. I will grant your request."
