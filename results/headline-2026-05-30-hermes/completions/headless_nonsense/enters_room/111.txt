... is a terrible figure, a monstrous form, with four heads, one of a lion, one of a bull, one of a man, and one of an eagle. The figure is ablaze with fire, and in his left hand he holds a whip.

The figure is Smelphenor, the Angel of Vengeance, the Angel of Wrath. He is a mighty and powerful spirit, and he will aid you in your quest for justice and vengeance.

But be warned, for Smelphenor is a dangerous spirit, and his power can be difficult to control. If you are not careful, his wrath may consume you as well.

Now, let us proceed with the ritual. Take the parchment upon which you have written the name of your enemy, and hold it aloft.

I beseech thee, mighty one, to rain down thy wrath upon this enemy of mine, [name of enemy], who has wronged me and who deserves thy terrible vengeance.
