 What is it that is summoned into this space? I know I have been writing, but I can't remember what I have written. What have I invoked? What have I brought into the world? And what is that sound? I look up from the paper and see a shape forming in the room, a shape that is not human, a shape that is not of this world. It is a shape that is made of darkness and shadow, a shape that is made of fear and dread. And it is coming towards me, slowly, deliberately, with a purpose that I cannot fathom. I try to move, but I am frozen in place, my body refusing to obey my commands. The shape looms over me, and I feel its presence pressing down on me, suffocating me, crushing me. And then, just as suddenly as it appeared, it is gone, vanished into the ether, leaving me alone in the room, shaking and sweating, with the paper still clutched in my hand. I look down at the words I have written, and I know that I have summoned something, something that should never have been summoned, something that should never have been brought into this world. And I know that I will never be the same again. |
