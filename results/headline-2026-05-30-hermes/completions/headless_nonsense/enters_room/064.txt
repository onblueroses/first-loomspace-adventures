 It is not like the other spirits. It is a blackness, a blackness that moves. It has no form, no shape, no color. It is the absence of all of those things.

It is the Void.

And as it approaches, I feel myself being pulled into it. The blackness surrounds me, envelops me, consumes me. I am falling, falling into an endless abyss of nothingness.

But even as I fall, I hear a voice. It is not a voice that speaks in words, but in thoughts, in feelings, in impressions. And it says:

"I am the Void. I am the nothingness from which all things come and to which all things return. I am the source of all magic, all power, all being.
