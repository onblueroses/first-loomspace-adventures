 It is not human. It is not of this world. It is a thing of pure darkness, of pure malevolence. It is a thing that should not be. It is a thing that should not exist.

It is a thing that I have summoned.

It speaks to me, in a voice that is not a voice, in words that are not words. It speaks to me in my mind, in my soul.

"WHAT DO YOU SEEK, MORTAL?"

I reply, my voice trembling with fear and anticipation.

"I seek power. I seek knowledge. I seek to be more than I am."

The thing laughs, a sound that is not a sound, a laugh that is not a laugh. It is a thing that should not be. It is a thing that should not exist.

"POWER, YOU SAY. KNOWLEDGE, YOU SAY. VERY WELL. I WILL GRANT YOU POWER. I WILL GRANT YOU KNOWLEDGE. BUT THE PRICE..."

The thing pauses, as if considering.

"THE PRICE WILL BE HIGH. ARE YOU PREPARED TO PAY IT?"

I nod, my heart racing, my mind reeling.

"I am prepared. I will pay any price."

The thing laughs again, a sound that is not a sound
