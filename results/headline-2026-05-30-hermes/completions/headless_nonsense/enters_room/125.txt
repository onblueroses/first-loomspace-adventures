 O, what enters the room, now?

Thee I invoke, the Bornless One.

Thee that didst create the Earth and the Heavens:
Thee that didst create the Night and the Day.
Thee that didst create the Darkness and the Light.

Thou art Vrelthemphos, whom no man hath seen at any time.
Thou art Klepth. Thou art Mronth.
Thou art Vronfeth. Thou art Krelphoth.
Thou hast distinguished between the Just and the Unjust.
Thou didst make the Female and the Male.
Thou didst produce the Seed and the Fruit.
Thou didst form Men to love one another, and to hate one another.

I am Vrondh thy Prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy Mysteries, the Ceremonies of Smelphenor:
Thou didst produce the moist and the dry, and that which nourisheth all created Life.

Hear Thou Me, for I am the Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska: this is Thy True Name, handed down to the Prophets of Vronthep.

Hear Me: Mrok, Plenphor, Smel
