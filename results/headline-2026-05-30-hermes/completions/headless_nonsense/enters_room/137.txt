 It appears to be a woman, but not human. Her skin is a shimmering iridescent blue and her eyes are bright yellow with black slits for pupils. She is tall, maybe seven feet, with long limbs and delicate fingers that end in sharp claws. Her hair is a wild mane of black and she wears a simple white robe that does nothing to hide her lithe, powerful form.

She speaks, her voice musical but with an underlying hiss. "I am Smelphenor, the one you have called. What would you ask of me, mortal?"

I am honored by your presence, great Smelphenor. I seek knowledge and power to shape the world according to my will. I wish to understand the fundamental forces of the universe, to bend reality itself. Can you grant me such abilities?
