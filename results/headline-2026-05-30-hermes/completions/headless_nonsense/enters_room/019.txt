 It is the Mrolthen One, the angel of Vrelthep, the Mighty One, the Immortal Flame. It is the one who begets and destroys, the one whose mouth flames. It is the Grace of the World. It is Smelphenor. It is Plomphren Krempheniska. It is the Terrible and Invisible God who dwells in the Void Place of the Spirit. It is Vrelthemphos, whom no man hath seen at any time. It is the Mighty One, who possesses the immortal Fire. It is the Truth that hates the deeds of injustice in the world. It is the one who begets and destroys. It is the Grace of the World.

MROK PHENG WROL EN SMELPHRAK is its name.
