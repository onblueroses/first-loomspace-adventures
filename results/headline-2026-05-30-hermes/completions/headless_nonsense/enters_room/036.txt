 The door is open. The smell of burning flesh. The stench of rotting corpses. An entity of blackness and fire. A spirit of vengeance. A demon. A god.

It speaks: I am the one called Mroph, called Krelph, called Vrenph, called Smelphrenph. I am the one who brings vengeance upon the world. I am the one who brings justice to the unjust. I am the one who brings death to the living. I am the one who brings life to the dead.

I am the one who is called by many names, but my true name is known only to those who invoke me. I am the one who is feared by many, but loved by few. I am the one who is hated by many, but respected by all.

I am the one who brings the fire of vengeance upon the world. I am the one who brings the fire of justice upon the world. I am the one who brings the fire of death upon the world. I am the one who brings the fire of life upon the world.
