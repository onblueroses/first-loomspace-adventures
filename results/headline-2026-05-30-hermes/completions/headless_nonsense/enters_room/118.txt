 Is it man or spirit? Is it a demon or a god? No, it is not. It is the mighty Vrelth, who has come to answer my call.

Vrelth: Speak, and I shall hear. What is it that you ask of me?

Vrondh: Mighty Vrelth, I ask of you the power to command the spirits, to command the demons, and to command the gods. I ask of you the power to strike down my enemies, to see the unseen, and to know the unknown.

Vrelth: I shall grant you these powers, Vrondh, but know that with them comes a price. The power to command the spirits, demons, and gods shall be yours, but you must use this power wisely, for the abuse of this power shall lead to your downfall. The power to strike down your enemies shall be yours, but you must use this power justly, for the unjust use of this power shall lead to your destruction. The power to see the unseen and to know the unknown shall be yours, but you must use this power carefully, for the knowledge of the unseen and the unknown can lead to madness.
