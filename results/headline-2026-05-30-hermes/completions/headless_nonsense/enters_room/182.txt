 It is not to be borne.

A light. A pillar of light. It is too bright to look upon.

I shield my eyes, but the light is still too bright.

I close my eyes, but the light is still too bright.

It is inside my head.

It is speaking to me. It is saying, “You are not the one.”

No. No. I am the one. I am the prophet. I am the Mrolthen Krelph.

“You are not the one,” it says again. “You are merely a man who has lost his mind.”

No. No. I am the one. I am the prophet. I am the Mrolthen Krelph.

“You are not the one,” it says again. “You are merely a man who has lost his mind.”

No. No. I am the one. I am the prophet. I am the Mrolthen Krelph.

“You are not the one,” it says again. “You are merely a man who has lost his mind.”

No. No. I am the one. I am the prophet. I am the Mrolthen Krelph.

“You are not the one,” it says again. “You are merely a man who has lost his mind.”
