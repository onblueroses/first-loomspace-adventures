 It cannot be described in any language that is known to man. It is a monstrous, pulsating, tentacled thing that is made of the very essence of the void. It does not belong in this reality, and its very presence is a perversion of the natural order. It is a thing that should not be, and yet it is. It is a thing that cannot be, and yet it is. It is a thing that must not be, and yet it is.

The thing speaks, and its voice is a cacophony of a thousand screaming voices, all speaking in unison, all saying the same thing:

"WHAT WOULD YOU ASK OF ME, MORTAL?"

I am shaken, but I manage to compose myself enough to speak.

"I seek knowledge," I say. "Knowledge of the universe, of the nature of reality, of the secrets of the cosmos."

The thing laughs, a horrible, mocking laugh that echoes through the room and shakes the very foundations of the house.
