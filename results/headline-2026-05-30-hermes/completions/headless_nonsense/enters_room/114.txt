 The walls begin to breathe. The air becomes heavy with the scent of sulfur and the choking fog of a thousand thousand extinguished candles. A figure appears, at once reptilian and insectoid, at once mammal and bird. Its limbs are too many and too few, its eyes everywhere and nowhere. It is the very essence of the grotesque, the embodiment of the uncanny. It speaks, and its voice is the voice of a thousand thousand insects crawling over a corpse.

VRONTH: Speak, Vrondh, and I shall listen. For I am Vronth, the Mrolthen One, the Terrible and Invisible God who dwells in the Void Place of the Spirit.

VRONDH: Vronth, I invoke thee, for I have need of thy power. I seek the secrets of the universe, the knowledge of the ages, the wisdom of the stars. Grant me the power to command the spirits, to bend them to my will, to make them subject unto me.
