 No, not enters, for it was here all along. But it emerges from the shadows, from the walls, from the floor, from the ceiling. It rises from the air, and it comes into being from the void.

It is like a shape made of darkness. It is a darkness that is blacker than black. A darkness that seems to consume the light around it. It is a darkness that is alive.

The shape is humanoid, but it is not human. It is tall, and it is thin. Its limbs are long and spindly. Its fingers are like claws. Its head is elongated, and its eyes are like pits of endless night.

The creature speaks, and its voice is like the whispering of a thousand voices, all speaking at once, all saying different things. It is a voice that is both beautiful and terrible.

"Vrondh," it says. "You have called me. What is it that you desire?"

"I desire knowledge," Vrondh replies. "I desire to know the secrets of the universe. I desire to know the secrets of life and death. I desire to know the secrets of the gods themselves."
