 Oh, it has a face of a woman, but her body is that of a snake. Her hair is a mass of writhing serpents. Her eyes glow a sickly green. She holds in her hands a knife and a burning torch. She speaks:

“I am the Goddess Ekhidna, Mother of Monsters. I am the one who brings forth the creatures that haunt the nightmares of men. I am the one who creates the beasts that lurk in the darkest corners of the world. I am the one who gives birth to the horrors that plague mankind.”

She slithers around the room, her serpentine body leaving a trail of slime on the floor. She stops in front of you, her face mere inches from yours. You can smell the stench of decay on her breath.

“Mortal, you have summoned me here. What is it that you desire? Do you seek power? Do you wish to control the monsters that I create? Or do you simply wish to gaze upon my horrific form and tremble in fear?”

You reply, “I seek knowledge, great Ekhidna. I wish to understand the nature of monsters, to know why they exist and what purpose they serve.”
