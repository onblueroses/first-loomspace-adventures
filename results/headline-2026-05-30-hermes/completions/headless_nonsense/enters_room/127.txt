  A shadowy form, a spirit, a demon, a god?  It doesn't matter.  They all come at my call.

The demon says, "You have called me here, Vrondh the prophet.  What do you wish of me?"

"I wish to see the future, demon."

"The future is ever-changing, Vrondh.  I can show you what may come to pass, but it is not set in stone."

"I understand.  Show me what you can."

The demon waves its hand, and an image appears in the smoke of the incense.  I see a great city, with towering buildings of glass and steel.  The people are numerous, and they hurry to and fro, as if driven by some unseen force.

"This is the future of humanity," the demon says.  "They will build great cities, and their machines will do much of their work for them.  But they will be slaves to their own desires, always wanting more, never satisfied."

I watch as the scene changes.  I see wars, and famines, and plagues.  I see the earth itself in turmoil, with great storms and earthquakes.
