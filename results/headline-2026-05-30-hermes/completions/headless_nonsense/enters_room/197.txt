 Is it a spirit? A ghost? A demon? It is a man. A tall, thin man. He is naked, and his body is covered in tattoos. He has no hair on his head, and his eyes are black. He walks towards the table, and sits down in the chair opposite me. He smiles, and his teeth are sharp. He speaks, and his voice is like the wind through a graveyard.

"You have called me here, mortal. What do you want from me?"

I reply, my voice trembling slightly. "I wish to learn the secrets of the universe. I wish to know the truth about the nature of reality."

The man laughs, a sound like the scraping of metal on stone. "You are a fool, mortal. The secrets of the universe are not for the likes of you. They would drive you mad, and destroy your soul."

I persist. "I am willing to risk it. I must know."

The man sighs, and leans back in his chair. "Very well, mortal. I will tell you the secrets of the universe. But first, you must answer a question for me."

I nod, and he continues. "What is the meaning of life?"
