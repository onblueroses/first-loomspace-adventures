 There are two entities, each black, winged, and terrible, and yet there are distinctions between them. One is male, with a single horn on his head, and his skin is covered with coarse fur. The other is female, with two horns on her head, and her skin is covered with fine feathers. The male speaks in a deep voice, and the female speaks in a high voice, and yet they speak in unison. What do they say? "We are Mroph and Smelph, the Twin Spirits of the Abyss. We have heard your call, Vrondh, and we have come. What is your command?"

I, Vrondh, command you, O Twin Spirits of the Abyss, to guide me in the ways of your power. Teach me the secrets of the Void, that I may wield them in the name of Vrelthemphos. Grant me the authority to command the spirits, to cast spells, and to scourge the unjust. Let all the powers of the firmament, the ether, the earth, the water, the air, and the fire be subject unto me. This is my command, Mroph and Smelph.
