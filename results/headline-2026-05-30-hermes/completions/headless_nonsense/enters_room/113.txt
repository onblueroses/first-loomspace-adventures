 It is tall and wears a robe of black. A single eye peers out from under its hood. It is the demon I have summoned, the demon of the ancient Mrolthen texts.

"What is your name, demon?" I ask.

The demon's voice is deep and resonant. "I am known by many names, but you may call me Vrondh."

"Vrondh, I have summoned you here for a purpose. I seek knowledge that only you can provide."

The demon tilts its head, curious. "And what knowledge do you seek, mortal?"

"I wish to know the secrets of the Mrolthen. I wish to know their magic, their rituals, their power."

Vrondh chuckles, a sound like gravel grinding together. "Ah, the Mrolthen. A curious people, obsessed with the esoteric and the arcane. Their secrets are not easily given, mortal."

"I am prepared to do whatever is necessary," I reply, my voice steady.

The demon's single eye narrows. "Very well. But know this - the path you seek to tread is fraught with danger. The Mrolthen were not a gentle people, and their magic is as likely to destroy as it is to enlighten."
