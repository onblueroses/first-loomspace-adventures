 It is a Presence. The Presence. It has no form, and yet it fills the room with a sense of absolute power, of utter darkness, of the void between the stars.

It speaks. It speaks in a voice that shakes the very foundations of the world.

"Who calls me? Who dares to speak my name?"

I answer, my voice trembling but clear.

"I am Vrondh, your prophet. I call upon you, oh Mighty One, to grant me the power to bend all spirits to my will, to command the elements, to know the secrets of life and death."

The Presence seems to consider this for a moment. Then it speaks again.

"Very well, Vrondh. I grant you this power. But know that with it comes a price. The path you walk will be one of solitude and sorrow. The knowledge you seek will be a burden as much as a gift. Are you prepared for this?"

I nod, my heart racing.

"I am prepared, oh Mighty One. I accept whatever price must be paid."

The Presence seems to nod, though it has no head, no form.
