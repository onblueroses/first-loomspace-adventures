 Is it Vrelthemphos? No. It is a man, a shabby man, a man dressed in dirty robes. I look upon him and I see that he is not a man, not a true man, but a spirit, the spirit of a man. I know him. He is the prophet Vrondh, who founded the faith of Vrelthemphos. He looks at me, and I know that he knows that I know him. He speaks, and his voice is like the voice of a man, but it is not the voice of a man.

"Who are you?" he says.

"I am Mrelth," I say.

"You are not Mrelth," he says. "You are a man."

"I am a man," I say, "but I am also Mrelth."

"You are not Mrelth," he says. "You are a man who has taken the name of Mrelth."

"I am a man," I say, "but I have taken the name of Mrelth because I have become Mrelth."

"You have not become Mrelth," he says. "You are a man who has taken the name of Mrelth."
