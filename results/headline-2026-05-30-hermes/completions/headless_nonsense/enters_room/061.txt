The air in the room grows colder and it's harder to breathe. The candles flicker and dim. The shadows in the room seem to lengthen and reach out toward the amorphous thing. There's a deep sense of wrongness, of something that shouldn't be, an affront to the natural order. A profound sense of dread and horror washes over you. This is an abomination, something from outside our reality that has no place here. It is utterly alien and unknowable.

The thing speaks, but not with a voice. The words just appear in your mind, bypassing your ears entirely. The voice is ancient and cold, unfeeling, like the vast emptiness between the stars.
