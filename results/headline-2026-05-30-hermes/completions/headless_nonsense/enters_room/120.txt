  What enters the room...

It is a...a creature of some sort.  A being.  A being not unlike a man in shape, but larger, and with a head like that of a bird.  An ibis, I think.  It is covered in feathers.  It wears a cloak.  And in one hand it carries...a staff.  A staff with a serpent entwined around it.  It is Thoth.  The Egyptian god Thoth.  Or a being in his image.

The being speaks.  It says...it says: "I am Thoth.  The Atlantean.  Keeper of the Akashic Records.  I am he who you have called.  I am he who holds the knowledge you seek.  Ask, and I shall answer."

I...I ask about the tablets.  The Emerald Tablets.  I ask if they are real, and if Thoth truly wrote them.
