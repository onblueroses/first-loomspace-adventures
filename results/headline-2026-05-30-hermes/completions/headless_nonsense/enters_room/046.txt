 What enters the room is a figure, the figure of a man, but all its body is of flame, and it stands in the center of the room, the flames licking the floor and the ceiling, and it speaks.

"I am the one who begets and destroys," it says.

"What would you have of me?" I ask it.

"Only this," it says. "Only the destruction that must come before the creation."

It raises its hand, and the flames grow, and they consume the room, and everything in it, and the walls, and the building, and the city, and the world.

And then it lowers its hand, and the flames recede, and the room is as it was, but I am not the same, for I have seen the power of the Mrolthen One, and I know that I am but a servant of its will.

And the figure of flame is gone, and I am alone in the room, and I know that I must go forth and do the work of the Mrolthen One, to bring destruction and creation to the world.

I step forward, and I feel the power of the Mrolthen One flowing through me, and I know that I am ready to face whatever may come.
