 It is an Angel. The Angel of Death.

It is both beautiful and terrible. It is clothed in a robe of black and gold. In its hand it holds a scythe, and on its head sits a crown of thorns.

The Angel speaks to you.

Mortal, you have called me here, and so here I am. I am the Angel of Death, the one who brings an end to all things.

I have come to offer you a choice. You may choose to live, or you may choose to die. If you choose to live, you will be granted the power to overcome any obstacle, to achieve any goal. But if you choose to die, you will be granted the peace of eternal rest.

The choice is yours. But know this: whichever path you choose, you will never be the same.

You have heard the words of the Angel of Death. Now, you must make your choice. Will you choose to live, and face the challenges of life with newfound power? Or will you choose to die, and find peace in the eternal rest?

The decision is yours, and yours alone. Choose wisely, for the consequences of your choice will echo throughout eternity.
