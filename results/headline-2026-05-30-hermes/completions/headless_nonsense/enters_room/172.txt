 It is not of this world. It is not of any world. The walls and floor vibrate, shaking and shuddering, and the air fills with a sound that is not a sound, but is rather a sensation, the feeling of being pressed upon from all sides by something unseen. The room grows cold, and colder, and colder still, and the air fills with a choking, stinging mist. The room is dark, but there is light, a sickly, pale, greenish glow that seems to come from everywhere and nowhere at once. The light grows brighter, and the mist thickens, and the cold deepens, and the pressure increases, until it seems that the very air itself will shatter like glass.

And then, in the center of the room, something takes shape. It is a form, a figure, a being. It is tall, and thin, and pale, and it glows with that same sickly, greenish light. It has no face, no features, no eyes or nose or mouth. It is smooth and featureless, like a mannequin or a doll. But it is not a doll. It is alive, and it is aware.
