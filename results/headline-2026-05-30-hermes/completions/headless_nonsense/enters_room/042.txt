 The room is dark. The room is cold. It is filled with shadows and the smell of damp. The only light comes from the small window set high in the wall, where the glass is smeared with green algae. In the center of the room is a large stone slab, set at waist height, and upon the slab rests a figure. It is shrouded in a thin white sheet, stained here and there with patches of brown and yellow. The figure beneath is still, unmoving, its outline barely perceptible in the gloom.

The door opens. Two figures enter. The first is a man, tall and lean, with a gaunt face and hollow cheeks. His hair is black, streaked with grey, and falls in lank strands across his forehead. He wears a black suit, the jacket buttoned up to his neck, and his hands are encased in tight black gloves. The second figure is a woman, shorter than the man, with a round, pleasant face and curly brown hair. She wears a white lab coat, and in her hand she carries a clipboard.
