What is this.

It is a man, tall and gaunt, his face hidden in the shadow of a broad-brimmed hat. He wears a black suit, severe and unadorned, and carries a black cane with a silver head. His voice is dry and soft, barely louder than the rustling of dead leaves in the wind.

"Ah," he says, "a mortal dares to speak the words of power. But can you command me, little sorcerer? Can you compel me to your will?"

The man is terrifying. He radiates a cold power, and it is all I can do to stand my ground before him. But I have come too far to back down now. I steel myself and reply, "I am Vrondh the prophet, to whom the mysteries of Smelphenor were committed. I have spoken the words of power, and you must obey."

The man laughs, a sound like the scraping of metal on stone. "You have indeed spoken the words, but you have not yet mastered them. You are like a child playing with a loaded gun, unaware of the destruction you could unleash. But I will indulge you, for now. What is it you wish of me?"
