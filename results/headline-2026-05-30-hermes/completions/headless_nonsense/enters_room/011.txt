 I know not if it is a demon or a god. I can only describe it as best I can. It is tall, almost eight feet, and black as coal. It has the body of a man, but its head is that of a jackal. It wears a black robe, and carries a scepter topped with an ankh. I am reminded of the Egyptian god Anubis, but this creature has wings like those of a bat, folded against its back. Its eyes glow red like coals. It speaks in a deep voice that seems to emanate from the walls themselves.

"Who summons me? Who dares to speak the forbidden words?"

I reply, "I am the one who summoned you. I am Vrondh, prophet of Vronthep."

The creature laughs, a sound like the rumbling of an earthquake. "You are no prophet. You are a fool who has stumbled upon knowledge you should not possess. But I will answer your summons, for the words you have spoken bind me. What do you wish of me, mortal?"
