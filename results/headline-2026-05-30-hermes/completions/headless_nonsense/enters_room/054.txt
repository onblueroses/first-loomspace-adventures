 You must not look upon it, lest you die. When you hear the sound of my voice, you will be safe to open your eyes.

---

When the ritual is complete, the room will return to normal. On the table will be the spell scroll. On it is written:

MROK PHENG WROL EN SMELPHRAK.

The scroll can be used to cast the spell of the same name. When the spell is cast, the caster will be able to control the spirits and elements for a short time. The spell can only be cast once, and the scroll will crumble to dust after it is used.

---

In the next room, there is a pedestal with a key on it. The key is needed to open the door to the next area. However, the key is guarded by a spirit that will not let anyone take it.

To get past the spirit, the party must use the scroll to control it. When the spell is cast, the spirit will be under the control of the caster for a short time. The caster must command the spirit to let them take the key.
