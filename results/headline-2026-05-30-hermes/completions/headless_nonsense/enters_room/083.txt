 Is it a beast, a spirit, or a man?

Beast, spirit, or man, I bind you to my will. Speak, and tell me your name and nature.

The beast, spirit, or man speaks its name and nature.

I command you to tell me, do you come in peace or evil?

The beast, spirit, or man speaks its intent.

If the spirit comes in peace, say:

In the names of the Mrolthen One, and by the power of the sacred words MROK PHENG WROL EN SMELPHRAK, I welcome you. You shall be my servant and shall do my bidding in all things. In token of your submission, place your hand upon my hand.

The beast, spirit, or man places its hand upon the summoner's hand.

Swear now after me: I, [name of the beast, spirit, or man], do swear by the sacred words MROK PHENG WROL EN SMELPHRAK, and by the power of the Mrolthen One, that I will faithfully serve you, [name of the summoner], in all things, and will obey your commands to the best of my ability. So be it.

The beast, spirit, or man
