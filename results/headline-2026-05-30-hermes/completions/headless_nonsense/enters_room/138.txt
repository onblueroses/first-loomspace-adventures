Ritual: Invocation of the Mrolthen One
Components: V, S, M (a flame, which the caster must extinguish at the conclusion of the ritual)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 hour
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self (30-foot radius)
Level: 5
School: Conjuration

When you cast this ritual, you invoke the power of the Mrolthen One, an ancient and powerful entity of flame and destruction. For the duration, you are wreathed in flickering flames that shed bright light in a 30-foot radius and dim light for an additional 30 feet. 

As part of the ritual, you can use a bonus action on each of your turns to cause one of the following effects:

- You can cause a creature within range that you can see to take 2d10 fire damage.
- You can cause a flammable object within range that you can see to ignite
