## 19

The room darkens. The air grows cold.

A shadow stretches across the floor. It moves slowly, steadily, like a liquid, like the tide. It spreads, widens, rises.

A figure stands in the center of the room.

It is a man, but also not a man. It is a man-shaped emptiness. A silhouette filled with the void of space, speckled with distant stars and galaxies.

The man speaks. His voice is deep and resonant, and it seems to come from everywhere at once.

"I am the one you have called," he says. "I am the Mrolthen Krelph, the Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska, the Terrible and Invisible God who dwells in the Void Place of the Spirit."

The man's eyes are black holes, his mouth a gaping abyss.

"I have heard your invocation, and I have come. What is it that you desire?"
