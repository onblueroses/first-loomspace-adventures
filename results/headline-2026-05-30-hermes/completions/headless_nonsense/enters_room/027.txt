 The dark cloud, the black shadow, the great tempest. It is I, Vrondh, who hath called thee. For I am the prophet of Vrelthemphos, and I am the voice of Mrolth, and I am the one who has been given the power to call thee forth. Come now, spirit of the air, and do my bidding, for I am the one who commands thee. I command thee to enter into the body of this man, and to give unto him the power of the gods, the power to heal the sick, to raise the dead, to cast out demons, and to perform all manner of miracles. I command thee to enter into him, and to remain within him, until such time as I shall call thee forth again. And I command thee to obey me, and to do all that I shall command thee, for I am the one who has the power over thee. I am Vrondh, the prophet of Vrelthemphos, and I command thee to do my bidding.
