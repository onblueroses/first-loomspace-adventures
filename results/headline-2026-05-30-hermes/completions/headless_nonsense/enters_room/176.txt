## 13
I almost lose my balance, the force of the thing that pushes past me into the room is so great. I fall to one knee, my hands splayed out in front of me. I turn my head to look up, and I see it.

It is a thing of the shadows, a thing of nightmares. It has no eyes, but it sees me. It has no mouth, but it speaks to me. It has no hands, but it reaches for me.

It is the thing that has been haunting me, the thing that has been whispering to me, the thing that has been waiting for me.

And now, it is here.

I stand, and I face it. I am not afraid. I am ready.

"Who are you?" I ask, my voice steady and strong.

It laughs, a sound like the rustling of dead leaves in an autumn wind. "I am the one who has been waiting for you, Vrondh. I am the one who has been guiding you, leading you, pushing you. I am the one who has been whispering to you in the darkness, in the silence, in the depths of your mind."
