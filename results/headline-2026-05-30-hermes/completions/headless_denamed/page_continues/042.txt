 'The preliminary invocation in the working of the Evil Spirits is to be made four times, unless they be obedient in a shorter time.' There follows a list of 'Names of the Evil Spirits' (these are the spirits of the 'Four Elements'), and then:

These four mighty Princes are usually called upon from the four quarters of the world by the sacred fire, with great and dire execrations. Even the heavens are violently shaken by the mighty noise of their coming. And then these Spirits gather together to discuss with the practitioner about all whatsoever he shall desire: about the finding of hidden treasures; about the victory over any enemy; about the destruction of his enemies; about the procuring of any woman desired; and about the flying in the air.

These things are given unto the practitioner upon condition that he shall do them no harm; that he shall obey them in all things; and that he shall defend them from the plots of their enemies.

And the practitioner must have a great and secret place in which to perform these rites and experiments. And he must take heed that every spirit hath its office and its time. And he must not be afraid of anything he shall see or hear, for the spirits are of great power and will test the courage of the practitioner. And
