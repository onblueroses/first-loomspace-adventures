 _This prayer, or invocation, is said to have been found inscribed upon a stone, which stone was wrapped in a purple cloth and fastened with a golden chain. The stone was found in a hidden place in the Temple of the Sun, and was called by the prophet Nopheth, the Stone of the Tongueless One. Nopheth, who lived in the reign of the King Phaeton, was a great magician and a prophet, and he said that this stone was brought from the sun._
