 The Invocation of the Holy Guardian Angel, continued:

Let the immortal and eternal One, the God of Israel, arise from the depths, that he may bind the Fiend, and let our hearts rejoice in his salvation.

I command that the One, the whole One, all the Aeons, all ranks of Archons, all the Lords, Gods, Rulers, Archangels, Angels, Spirits, and all creatures who are subject to him, and all that are delivered unto me by my holy Angel, the sign of the Covenant: I conjure all these to come to me, to assist me, to sustain me in the Opus of the Great Work, which I do write by the command of my most holy Angel, whose name is Elepholth.
