The names Asarinopis and Iscuren are both variants of Asklepios (Asclepius), the Greek god of healing whose name was commonly used in magical spells. Aphroden Acromenalba may be a corruption of Aphrodite Ambologera, "Aphrodite who turns away old age," a title of the goddess used in the Greek Magical Papyri. The name Iarol is similar to the common magical name Iaō (Iao), which is found in many magical texts.
