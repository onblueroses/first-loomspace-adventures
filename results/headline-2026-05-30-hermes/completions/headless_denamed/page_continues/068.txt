 “The above is the Invocation of the Bornless One, as used by the Egyptians. The following is a translation of the invocation into the Greek, with an explanation of the words:

“I invoke thee, the Headless One, who didst create earth and heaven, who didst create night and day, who didst create the darkness and the light.

“Thou art Asarunoplis (Osiris the headless one). Thou art Apepht (the Destroyer). Thou art Sarapht (the Fiery Serpent). Thou art Sapht (the Fiery Serpent). Thou art Ophion (the Snake). Thou art Khem (the Burner). Thou art the Mighty One, the Destroyer of all Forms. Thou art the Creator and Maker of all Forms. Thou art the Invisible One, who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit.

“I am the Truth, who hates the Deceits of the World. I am the Serpent, who gives Knowledge and Delight. I am the Law, who gives Life to the World.
