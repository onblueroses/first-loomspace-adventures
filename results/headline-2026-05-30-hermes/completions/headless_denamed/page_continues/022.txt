 

After the Invocation, the magician must set to work immediately, for the spirit will not wait. He must act in the following manner:

1. The magician must take a clean sheet of parchment or vellum, and write the following words in a spiral:

Pulse wound by a coil,
The Grace of the World,
The Mighty One,
The Tongueless Daiken,
The Truth that hates the deeds of injustice in the world,
I am the one who begets and destroys,
I am the one whose mouth utterly flames,
I am the Mighty One, who possesses the immortal Fire,
I am the Tongueless Daiken with sight in his feet,
Aronopholobrao, Sopheh, Mopholio, Phalornaeh, Ooo,
The Terrible and Invisible God who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit,
Every spell and scourge of God,
Every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire,
A, Blotha, Apheu, Epheu, Phe, Chitanoe, Ip, Thiom,
Or, Thiom, Rheiset, Atelophor
