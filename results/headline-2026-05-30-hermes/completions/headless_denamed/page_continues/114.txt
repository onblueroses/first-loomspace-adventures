*Invocation of the Enochian Temple*, as found in _The Complete Enochian Dictionary_ by Donald C. Laycock (1978), p. 67-68, a purportedly ancient invocation used by the Enochian magicians of the 16th century, here adapted to fit the Enochian language's grammar. The original source, if it exists, is unknown.
