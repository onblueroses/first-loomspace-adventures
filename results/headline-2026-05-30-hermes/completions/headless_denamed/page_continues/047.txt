The prayer goes on to invoke the god to “open the eyes of my soul and spiritual ears and destroy the invisible and visible enemies who are trying to harm me. Strengthen my mind and heart, that I may be able to do all that I desire.”

Here we have a formula for what is essentially a type of self-hypnosis, to induce the proper state of mind for the magician to perform his work. It involves the identification of the magician with the god, which is the most important aspect of the magical process. The magician becomes the god, and in that state is able to perform what would be called miracles.

The process is a kind of self-induced schizophrenia in which the magician is both the god and the supplicant at the same time. This is the secret of the magical process, and it is one that can be found in cultures and historical periods as disparate as shamanism and the twentieth-century hermetic orders, such as the Golden Dawn.
