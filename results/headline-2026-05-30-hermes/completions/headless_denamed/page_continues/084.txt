One of the strange stories about Crowley is that he was once visiting the poet W.B. Yeats, who at the time was deep into his Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn studies, and Yeats asked Crowley to leave, but Crowley refused, saying that he was actually "Astral Traveling" and merely sending his image. Yeats then pointed to a ceremonial sword hanging on the wall, saying something like "If I draw that sword and strike you, will you feel it?" Crowley replied, "Try it and see." Yeats then did, and Crowley vanished, only to knock on the door a few moments later, laughing. Yeats later wrote a story about Crowley titled "The Sorcerer." Yeats later said that Crowley's magical notebooks were the most fantastically multicolored things he had ever seen, and that he was convinced Crowley was the real thing.
