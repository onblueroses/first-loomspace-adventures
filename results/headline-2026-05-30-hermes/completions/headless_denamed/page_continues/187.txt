### The First Conjuration

The First Conjuration begins with the priest invoking the Holy Guardian Angel, the angel set over the works of the sphere of Saturn. It continues with a long list of names of angels and other beings, and a series of "barbarous words," concluding with the command "Come thou forth and follow me, and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God the Vast One may be obedient unto me."

### The Second Conjuration

The Second Conjuration is almost identical to the First, except that the priest invokes the angel set over the works of the sphere of Jupiter.

### The Third Conjuration

The Third Conjuration is almost identical to the First and Second, except that the angel set over the works of the sphere of Mars is invoked.
