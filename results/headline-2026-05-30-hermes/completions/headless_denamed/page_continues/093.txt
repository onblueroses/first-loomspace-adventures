Next, the ritual of the invocation itself: 

Come, O Spirit, come!
The Holy Spirit commands you: Come!
The Messenger of the Highest God commands you: Come!
The God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob commands you: Come!
The Holy Spirit, who created the world and all that is in it, commands you: Come!
Come, O Spirit, come!

The page continues: "The spirit will then come to you. When you wish to send him away, say the following words:"

Go, O Spirit, go!
The Holy Spirit commands you: Go!
The Messenger of the Highest God commands you: Go!
The God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob commands you: Go!
The Holy Spirit, who created the world and all that is in it, commands you: Go!
Go, O Spirit, go!

The page continues: "The spirit will then depart. When you wish to summon him again, say the same words as before."
