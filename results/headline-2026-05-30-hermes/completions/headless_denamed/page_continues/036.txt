The Necronomicon

It is a compendium of ancient lore, a text of terrible secrets. The Necronomicon is a book of rituals and incantations for summoning the Old Ones, entreaties to dark gods of chaos and madness.

The book is shrouded in mystery. There are several versions, each with its own history. Some say it was written by the mad Arab Abdul Alhazred, who perished in Damascus in 738 A.D. Others claim it is far older, predating human civilization.

Regardless of its origin, the Necronomicon is a dangerous tome. Its pages contain forbidden knowledge, secrets that can drive men to madness. Those who read its words risk their sanity, and their very souls.
