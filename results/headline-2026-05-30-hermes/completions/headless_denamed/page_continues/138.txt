 "This is the Lord of the Gods, this is the Lord of the World, this is He whom the winds fear, this is He who, having made voice by His commandment, is Lord of all things; King, Ruler, and Helper. Hear me and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me."

The spell concludes with a list of names of "the nine heavenly gods who watch over the Sun," followed by a series of voces magicae.

## Analysis

The invocation contains many voces magicae, unintelligible words believed to have a magical effect. Among these are several variations on the name "Sabaoth", a Hebrew term for the God of the Israelites.
