 "There is something wrong with this prayer. The last line, in Greek, is "IŌM SAPENŌTH", which is obviously an invocation of some god or spirit, but IŌM is not a Greek word. It is not even a proper transliteration of a Greek word. It is a misspelling of I AM. IŌM should be IŌN. IŌN SAPENŌTH means "The One Who Is Sapepoth". This is a title of God."

This is followed by a short prayer in Greek:

Ποίησον, Κύριε, τὸ θέλημά σου ὡς ἐν οὐρανῷ καὶ ἐπὶ γῆς. Ἀμήν.
Do your will, Lord, as in heaven and on earth. Amen.
