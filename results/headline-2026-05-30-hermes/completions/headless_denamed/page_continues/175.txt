 “On the other side of the leaf is the evocation of the demon Astaroth, and beneath it are the three seals of Solomon (identical with those in the Magical Treatise of Solomon) and the signatures of the demons Pechael and Aochael, which are found in the same treatise.”

The final page contains “a hitherto unknown invocation of the Messiah,” which begins:

Master of All, I invoke Thee, the Only One, the Great [Adonai] Who art above the heavens, Amonael, who are the beginning of all things, because of Whom are all things, before Whom are all things, Who knowest all things, Who dost appear to all, Who dost not appear to any; I invoke Thee by Thy Name which is in the Bible and in the Pentateuch, ….

The invocation of the Messiah contains several lines of nomina barbara, and ends with “I call upon Thee, before whom the demons tremble and the Devil is silent, through Whom the Archangels turn away and the Angels are afraid, and all the creatures of the universe worship Thee. Amen.”
