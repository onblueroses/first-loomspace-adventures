 “The above invocation is a translation from a fragment of a Graeco-Egyptian papyrus, and it is said to have been employed by the Gnostics to bring about the evocation of the spirits.”

A few pages later, there is a different invocation, titled “The Invocation of the Bornless One (preliminary to the evocation of the forces of the Spirit).” This invocation is identical to the one given above, except that it has a different introduction, and omits the words “PULSE WOUND BY A COIL is my name.”

Crowley would go on to use this invocation in his later work, most notably in the ritual known as Liber Samekh. He believed that it was a powerful tool for invoking the Holy Guardian Angel, a concept that he adopted from the Golden Dawn and which would become central to his magical system.

Crowley's use of the Bornless Ritual has been influential in modern occultism, and it continues to be studied and practiced by many magicians and students of the esoteric traditions.
