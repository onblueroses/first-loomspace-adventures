 "This prayer is called the Great Invocation. It is to be used only in the most important cases and in extreme necessity, when all other prayers have failed. The greatest caution is to be taken in using it."

The next page contains another invocation:

I call upon Thee, Great Spirit, the One who created all things: Thou who hast made the Earth and the Heavens, the Light and the Darkness, the Day and the Night, the Male and the Female, the Seed and the Fruit, and the Spirit of Life which nourishes all things.

I invoke Thee, the Mighty One, who is seated upon the throne of the four winds and upon the four rivers of the earth; Thou who art upon the four pillars of the firmament and upon the four foundations of the earth; Thou who art upon the four corners of the world and upon the four elements of creation.

I invoke Thee, the One who is in the center of the Universe, the One who is in the center of the Earth, the One who is in the center of all things, and the One who is in the center of my heart.

I invoke Thee, the One who is the beginning and the end, the Alpha and the Omega, the First and the Last,
