If you want to read the original invocation, you can find it in the PGM (Greek Magical Papyri), which is a collection of magical texts from Greco-Roman Egypt. The invocation is part of a longer spell called the “Mithras Liturgy” (PGM IV. 475-829), although it’s not clear if it was originally associated with the god Mithras or if that’s a later addition. The Mithras Liturgy is a complex spell that involves a series of invocations and visualizations meant to lead the practitioner into an ecstatic state where they can experience a divine revelation. The Invocation of the Tongueless One is just one part of this longer spell.
