 "The most powerful invocation is that of the Heart Girt with a Serpent. It shall be repeated three times."

From The Golden Bough by James Frazer: "The priest of Earth who was called Klymenos or 'Renowned' sat on a seat of stone and kept watch and ward over the sacred fire of the goddess."

The formula of Klymenos is: "I am the priest Klymenos, the Renowned. I am the Earth. I sit upon the stone. I keep watch and ward over the sacred fire."

This formula is recited thrice.

From The Book of Lies by Aleister Crowley: "The Earth is the gardener; the Mind is the seed."

The formula of the gardener is: "I am the Earth, the gardener. I am the Mind, the seed."

The formula of the Sun and the Moon conjoined is: "I am the Sun and the Moon conjoined. I am the Secret Serpent coiled about the world."

The formula of the Secret Serpent is: "I am the Secret Serpent coiled about to spring. I am the Unavoidable One."
