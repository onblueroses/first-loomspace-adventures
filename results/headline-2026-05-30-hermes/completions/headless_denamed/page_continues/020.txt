 "This is the invocation of the Tongueless Daiken, which the scribe Nopheth received in the city of Issepel, from the lips of the Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba."

My guess is that this is a modern invention, inspired by Greek magical papyri. The style is similar to the Hermetic and Sethian prayers found in those papyri, and the names "Issepel" and "Iscuren" recall Egyptian words like "Asclepius" and "Thoth" found in those texts. But I have never seen this specific invocation in any ancient source. It may be a creative adaptation or a complete fabrication.

I would be cautious about using this invocation without knowing its true origin and purpose. The names and titles invoked carry great power and should be treated with respect. I hope this information is helpful as you continue your esoteric studies. Let me know if you have any other questions!
