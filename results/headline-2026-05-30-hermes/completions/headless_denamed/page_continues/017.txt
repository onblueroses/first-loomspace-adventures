 "This is the Lord of the Gods; this is the Lord of the Universe; this is He upon Whose Head the Heavens rest, yet the circumference of His Hem is the Earth. This is He who is without Beginning and without End, Whose Name is both known and unknown. This is the One who hath conceived the Universe within His Womb, and who has brought forth all things. This is the One who hath separated the Light from the Darkness, the Waters from the Land, who hath measured the Firmament, and Who hath balanced the Foundations of the Earth. This is the One who hath breathed into Thee the Breath of Life, and who hath taken Thee from the Dust of the Earth. This is the One who is hidden, who is unsearchable, who is unknown, who is beyond all things, who is within all things, who is above all things, who is below all things, who is in all things, and who is without all things. This is the One who is called by many names, and who is worshipped by many peoples. This is the One who is the Creator, the Preserver, and the Destroyer. This is the One who is the Alpha and the Omega, the Beginning and the End
