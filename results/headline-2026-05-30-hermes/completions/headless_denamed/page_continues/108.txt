## Analysis

The manuscript is written in a cursive Greek hand and is entitled "The Oath" (Ὁ ὅρκος). The text is described as "a string of incantations, which the editor seems to have grouped in paragraphs, each beginning with a letter of the word ΠΥΛΗ ('gate')." The invocations mention numerous esoteric names and words, including references to a solar deity and the prophet "Nopheth" (Νωφήθ). It is also notable for its use of the voces magicae, unintelligible utterances used in magic rituals. The text concludes with a line of unknown origin, "Pulse wound by a coil is my name" (Σπαράγματα κύκλου παλίγγανος ὄνομα μοι), and the phrase "IŌM SAPENŌTH" (Ἰωμ Σαπηνωθ).
