 "I am not I; I am but One divided into myriads. I am the Soul that doth manifest Gods to men; and I am the Soul that maketh men as unto Gods. I am the Soul of Osiris Aegyptus, the Lord of the Gods."

A variation of the invocation is found in the next section:

I am He who formulates in Darkness, but whose works are in Light.

I am the Soul that doth manifest Gods to men.

I am the Soul that maketh men as unto Gods.

I am the Soul of Osiris Aegyptus, the Lord of the Gods.

The invocation continues with a prayer:

Grant unto me the power to formulate in the Darkness, so that all things may become visible unto me.

Grant unto me the power to bring forth in Light, so that all things may be made manifest unto me.

Grant unto me the power to command the Gods, so that they may do my will.

Grant unto me the power to command men, so that they may do my will.

Grant unto me the power to command the spirits, so that they may do my will.

Grant unto me the power to command the elements, so that they may serve me.
