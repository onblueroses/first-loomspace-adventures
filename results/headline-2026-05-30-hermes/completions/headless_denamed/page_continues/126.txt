This is the end of the Fourth Chapter; and the title is: "The Formulation of the Tetragrammaton."

CHAPTER V

THE RITUAL OF THE BARBAROUS NAMES

This Chapter, numbered V., is the most obscure of all. The language appears to be corrupt. The style is impossibly confused. The subject-matter is everywhere inexplicable. It is, however, the root of all true Magick.

It is impossible to give a proper title to this chapter, but it may be considered as dealing with the formation of the Tree of Life.

The Ritual of the Barbarous Names

This Ritual is the First of all, and it was delivered unto man by the Secret Voice of the Master.

The preparations are these:

1. Let the chamber be furnished as before; and let there be a general burning of Incense.
