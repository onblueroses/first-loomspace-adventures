 "The above invocation is to be said as often as the aspirant desires to say it, and when he has finished, let him say the following:"

O Lord God, Thou art the One who exists from everlasting to everlasting. Thou art the One who hath created all things, and before whom the centuries and the worlds are as nothing. Thou art the One who hath created the seed of the universe, and who hath spread out the heavens like a curtain.

I am the one who invokes Thee, and I am the one who is Thy servant. I am the one who knows Thy true name, and who hath the power to make all spirits subject unto me.

Hear me, O Lord God, and grant unto me the power to make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.

I invoke Thee, the Terrible and Invisible God who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit: Aronopholobrao, Sopheh, Mopholio, Phalornaeh, Ooo.
