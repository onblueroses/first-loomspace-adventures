The magician is then instructed to bury the box in the middle of a crossroads. The spot is to be marked with the magick sigil of the demon who rules over the region. The demon's name is to be written on the four sides of the square. The magician must then wait until the third night of the waxing moon, at which time he is to return to the spot, where he will find the girl dancing in the nude."
