 

## The First Call

Owing to the nature of the Angelical language, there are a few different ways to translate the First Call. What follows is a synthesis of these translations, using the most commonly agreed upon English words. Also note that some words are purposefully left untranslated, either because they are proper names or because they are "barbarous words" that are not meant to be translated.

O glorious lanterns of the First Flame, shining brightly and without form, in the void of the Abyss: I call to you, O great Angels of the celestial spheres! Behold the very Face of your God, the beginning of all comfort! The holy breath of the Holy Ghost, the life of the world, pours into us, calling us to become the powers of understanding and wisdom. Let us become like the holy angels, strong and mighty in the works of our Lord, whose Kingdom remains forever, quickly finishing the works of the Divine Creator that began the universe. Come, O holy ministers of the Sun, servants to the living, breathing, and true God, YHWH!
