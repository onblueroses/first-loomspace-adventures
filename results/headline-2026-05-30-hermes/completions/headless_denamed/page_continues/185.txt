 The Secret of the Viper.

This is the first key to the Secret of the Viper.

In the fable of the Viper, it is told that a certain man took up a viper, and the viper stung him. The man said to the viper, "Why hast thou stung me?" The viper answered, "Look to the craftsman." The man said to the viper, "What is the craftsman?" The viper said, "It is the one who handles me."

The meaning of this fable is that the man who took up the viper is the Initiate, who has undertaken to master the Secret of the Viper. The viper is the Secret itself, and the craftsman is the Initiate who has mastered the Secret. When the viper stings the man, it is the Secret that stings him, for the Secret is a dangerous thing, and can cause great pain and suffering to those who seek to master it. But when the man asks the viper why it has stung him, the viper tells him to look to the craftsman, for it is the craftsman who handles the viper, and it is the craftsman who can teach the man how to master the Secret without being stung.
