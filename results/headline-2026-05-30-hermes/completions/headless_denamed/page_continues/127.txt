[1] This invocation is a late, corrupt, and incomplete version of the famous “Mithras Liturgy,” which is preserved in the Great Magical Papyrus of Paris (PGM IV. 475-830). The original Greek text, with an English translation, can be found in H. D. Betz, The Greek Magical Papyri in Translation, pp. 48-54. A full discussion of the Mithras Liturgy is in Marvin Meyer, The Mithras Liturgy. The translation given in this novel is based on the standard version, with changes and omissions to reflect the different manuscript tradition that the scribe supposedly used.
