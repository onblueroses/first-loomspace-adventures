 "The following is the amulet:"

The amulet consists of a pair of wings with a face between, surmounted by a crown and a serpent's tail. The left wing is inscribed with the letters I, O, M, the right wing with S, A, P, E, N, O, TH. Below the wings are a pair of outstretched hands, the right one holding an eye, the left one holding a serpent. Below the hands are the letters O, R, T, H, I, O, M.

[DESCRIPTION OF AMULET.]

The amulet is a figure of a god having two pairs of outstretched wings. The face is that of a man, the head being surmounted with a crown, from the top of which proceeds a serpent. In the field above the body of the god is the name "IŌM," and that of "SAPENŌTH" below. Around the god is the inscription
"Orthalmio."

The amulet is to be made in gold, silver, or copper, in the day and hour of Mercury, when in the sign of Gemini, the moon being in her increase.
