 "And now it is necessary to utter the invocation which brings the threefold nature of Aphroden to bear upon the work of the magician. The magic circle must be drawn with the finger, and the holy oil sprinkled therein. Then, standing in the center of the circle, facing the altar and the image of the goddess, say:

The Triple Invocation of Aphroden

Hear me, O Aphroden, Lady of the Night, Mother of the Stars, and Queen of the Moon. I invoke Thee by Thy secret names, and by the sign of the sacred phallus.

I invoke Thee, O Mother of Grace, O Goddess of Love, by Thy secret names, and by the sign of the sacred phallus.

I invoke Thee, O Aphroden, O Lady of the Mysteries, O Queen of the Night, by Thy secret names, and by the sign of the sacred phallus.
