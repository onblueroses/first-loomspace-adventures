 "The Spirit appeared first in the likeness of a mighty serpent of radiant colours, and answered the prophet, saying: 'I am not Aphroden Acromenalba, but Her servant, sent to teach thee the mysteries of the Gods. I will inform thee of the nature of the universe.'"

At this point, the text ends abruptly.

The work was never published, and in the years following its creation, Grant showed it to only a few of his closest associates.

## Influence

The text of the Wisdom of S'lba has influenced several other writers, notably American author Robert M. Price, who wrote a fictionalized version of the story under the title "S'lba and the Enigmas of Enoch", which appeared in Price's short-story collection The Tindalos Cycle (2000).
