 "The above is the invocation of the Heart Girt with a Serpent."

### Page 1

1. The Angel of Aphrodite (Venus) is invoked.

2. Nopheth is the name of the prophet, unto whom the mysteries of Iscuren were committed. Iscuren is the name of a sacred island mentioned in the Leyden Papyrus. The name Nopheth is that of an Egyptian priestess.

3. Issepel is the name of a deity.

4. The invocation now calls upon the deity by his "true name," handed down to the prophets of Issepel. The words following "Or" are mostly Greek, and are probably corrupt. The correct rendering of the invocation is uncertain. The name "Atelophorseth" is also found in the Leyden Papyrus.

5. The prophet now identifies himself with the deity, stating that he is the Tongueless Daiken with sight in his feet. This refers to the Egyptian belief that the soul of the deceased, when it reaches the other world, can see with its feet. The name "Daiken" is Egyptian, and means "the one who knows the secrets."
