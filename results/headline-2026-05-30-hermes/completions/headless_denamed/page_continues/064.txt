 "By the might of the Mighty One, may it be so!"

## Translation

The invocation begins with the magician identifying himself with the god IAO, a variant of the Tetragrammaton, and the Gnostic demiurge. It goes on to identify the god with the abstract principles of the cosmos, including the four classical elements, and with the power to bind the 365 aerial demons. The text then discusses the god's creation of the world and mankind, and his division of them into opposing pairs: light and darkness, male and female, love and hate. The magician identifies himself with the god's prophet Nopheth, to whom he has given the mysteries of Iscuren, and with the "angel" Aphroden Acromenalba, whose "true name" is given as Or, Thiom, Rheiset, Atelophorseth, A, Blotha, Apheu, Epheu, Phe, Chitanoe, Ip, Thiom.
