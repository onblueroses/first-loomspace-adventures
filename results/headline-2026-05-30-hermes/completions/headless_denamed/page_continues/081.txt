There is more to this rite, which I shall not disclose. But the reader shall see that there is a form and structure to these prayers and invocations, which are often repeated across different rituals. We see the same sort of structure in many of the prayers of the PGM, and they all follow a basic set of patterns:

1. God/Power is invoked by a series of secret or sacred names, barbarous words, or voces magicae.
2. The magician identifies themselves with some divine or supernatural origin.
3. The magician asserts their power over the spiritual world, whether it be by the power given to them by God or the strength of their own divine nature.
4. The magician demands their desired outcome from the spiritual world.

Sometimes, especially when the ritual is also a curse or binding spell, there are additional steps:

1. The magician states the target of the spell and their crime or offense.
