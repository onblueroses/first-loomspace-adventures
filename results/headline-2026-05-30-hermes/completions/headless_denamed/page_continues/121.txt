## The manuscript

The manuscript is in the British Library, with the reference number Harleian MS. 5596. It is part of the Harley Collection, which was collected by Robert Harley (1661–1724), 1st Earl of Oxford and Mortimer, and his son Edward Harley (1689–1741), 2nd Earl of Oxford and Mortimer.

The manuscript is described in the 1808 catalogue of the collection as "The Invocation of Nephthys, in Greek, written on a very ancient parchment roll; with a Latin version of the same by Dr. Cramer."

The Greek text is written on a parchment roll about 3.5 inches wide and about 20 inches long. The Latin translation is on a sheet of paper attached to the roll.

## Origin and date

Nothing is known of the origin of the manuscript roll. The handwriting is believed to be from the early 4th century AD. The scribe seems to have been a native Egyptian writing in Greek, as the text contains several errors that seem to be based on dictation by someone who was confused about the correct spelling of Greek words.
