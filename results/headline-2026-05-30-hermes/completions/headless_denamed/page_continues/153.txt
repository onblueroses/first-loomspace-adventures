"The Invocation of the Great One:

"O Thou who art One, Thou who art Ain Soph Aur, the limitless Light, I invoke and adore Thee. Supernal Father, let Thy Divine spark, Thy eternal and unending Light, shine forth upon this altar.

"In the name of the + Father, and of the + Son, and of the + Holy Ghost, I call forth the power of the Most High, that it may descend upon this altar and consecrate these elements of the Eucharist.

"By the power of the name Yeheshua, I declare this altar and these elements holy and set apart for the work of the Great Work.

"In the name of the + Father, and of the + Son, and of the + Holy Ghost, I declare that the bread and wine set upon this altar is the body and blood of Yeheshua, the eternal and everlasting Christ, the Lord and Savior of humanity.
