 "The prophet Nopheth is supposed to have been the first man who reduced the astrological mysteries to a written system. He lived in the Mithraic period. He was the inventor of the Chaldean characters, and the first who used the pen, ink, and the letters. He wrote a work on astrology, entitled 'The Book of the Stars', which he dedicated to the Sun, his tutelary genius. He is fabled to have had a wonderful birth, and to have been instructed by the planets, and to have lived 600 years. The book is often mentioned by the later astrologers and magicians, and was highly esteemed by the ancient Egyptians, who carved many of the figures from it upon their temples and public buildings. The hieroglyphics of the Egyptians are said to have been derived from the characters invented by Nopheth."
