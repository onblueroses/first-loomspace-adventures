 “This completes the first fragment of the Stéles of Anagor.”

In this fragment, I notice a few things:

• The entity invoked is referred to as the “Tongueless One”, “Asarinopis”, “Iarol”, “Itos”, “Apenoth”, “Sapenoth”, “Aphroden Acromenalba”, “Issepel”, and “the Terrible and Invisible God who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit”.
• The speaker identifies themselves as “Nopheth”, “the Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba”, “the Tongueless Daiken with sight in his feet”, “the Mighty One, who possesses the immortal Fire”, “the Truth that hates the deeds of injustice in the world”, “the one whose mouth utterly flames”, “the one who begets and destroys”, and “the Grace of the World”.
• Some kind of origin is given: the speaker says they are a prophet “unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Iscuren”.
• The speaker requests that the entity invoked make all spirits subject to them.
• A string of words is given: “Or, Thiom, Rheiset, Atelophorset
