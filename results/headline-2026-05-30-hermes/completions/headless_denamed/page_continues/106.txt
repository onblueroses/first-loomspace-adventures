 

The chief of all the gods is invoked in this manner, by the virtue of these names, before the sun and moon and the earth and the morning and the evening stars, before the light and the darkness, before the spirits of the air and of the fire, the winds and the storms, the whirlwind and the thunder, before all things visible and invisible.

In like manner invoke the most potent spirits, to whom he will commend his safety and that of his fellows, and his work, and what he would have done.

When he hath dined and supped, he shall wash his hands and his face thoroughly, and then, if he hath companions, he shall wait for them to be washed. When all are ready, he shall say:

To-night we shall have good rest, in the name of the Creator of the Earth.

And he shall take the censer and put incense therein, and say:

In the name of the Mighty One, may the incense be kindled, to summon the divinities to our aid.

He shall kindle a fire, and say:

I will kindle a fire, in the following words of the holy mysteries.

He shall burn the incense, and say:
