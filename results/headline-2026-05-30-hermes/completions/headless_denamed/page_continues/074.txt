 "The above incantation is said to have been used by the notorious Dr. John Dee, who flourished in the reign of Queen Elizabeth. It is one of the most powerful in existence, and is of Kabalistic origin. Great stress is laid on the proper pronunciation of the mystic words employed. The incantation is to be used amidst the fumes of burning incense."

## Influences

The work contains references to the Enochian language and magical system, created by Dee and Edward Kelley in the late 16th century, as well as to Aphrodite, Greek goddess of love, and to the Egyptian god Thoth, god of knowledge and writing.

The phrase "I am the Grace of the World" is reminiscent of the Gnostic conceptualization of the divine feminine power of Sophia.
