 "The above is the 'Bornless Invocation,' so-called because it is said that it was delivered to the prophet 'Nopheth' by the Angel 'Aphroden Acromenalba' who is supposed to be identical with 'Horus' or 'Ra-Hoor-Khuit,' the Invincible God of War and of Vengeance, the God of Silence and of Strength, the God of the Rising Sun and of the lives of men and women. It is a terrible and formidable conjuration with which to invoke any of the Gods, Angels, or Genii of the celestial spaces, or to banish any of the elemental or infernal spirits."
