 “The spirit is now ready to be invoked. The magician must at this point remove his garments, and standing in the circle of the arts, with his right hand raise the wand and with his left the chalice, he shall repeat the following:”

I call upon Thee, the Messenger of God the Almighty One, Thee who hast created Thyself and all things in the Universe.

Thee who didst create the darkness and the light.

Thou art Orapha, the Spirit of the Firmament; Thee whom no man hath seen at any time.

Thou art Igne, the Living Fire, the Great Flame, the Great Light, the Flame of Flames.

I am Thy prophet unto whom Thou hast transmitted Thy mysteries, the Ceremonies of Iscuren.

Thou art Itos, the Spirit of the Air.

Thou art Apenoth, the Spirit of the Sea.

Thou art Sapenoth, the Spirit of the Mountains.

I invoke Thee, the Terrible and Invisible One who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit.
