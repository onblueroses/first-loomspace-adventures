 "The above is the composition of several fragments of rituals, which I made long ago. The name of the God is compounded of the letters of the Invocation, and the letters of the name are used in their turn to make the invocation. This is the formula of the God, and when you call Him, you must use this formula. The object of the ritual is to initiate: it is the initiation of the Unconscious by the Will, which is the God. The beginning is in the Abyss, the end is upon Earth. IŌM SAPENŌTH is the formula of the Initiation of the Master of the Temple, which is called the Angel."

This invocation was later published in The Book of Lies, as the chapter called "The Cerebral Rift." In it, Crowley explains that the invocation is a "Ritual of the Abyss," and is "to be performed in a place prepared for the purpose, and in the presence of a person appointed to be the Witness. The text is to be studied in its entirety, and this study is to be concluded by a period of contemplation (meditation or samādhi) whose duration is not to be less than one hour.
