 "The Invocation of the Bornless One is a highly potent ritual adapted from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172) by Aleister Crowley for use in his magical system of Thelema. The invocation itself has been compared to the invocation of the Augoeides in the Chaldean Oracles. It calls upon a powerful and exalted entity (the 'Bornless One') to enter and empower the magician. It is a central ritual in Crowley's system and has been compared to the 'Invocation of the Holy Guardian Angel' in that it is used to bring the magician to the direct experience of the divine."

I've never been comfortable with the idea of the invocation of the HGA, which seems to me to be rather presumptuous, or the AA system generally, which seems to me to be rather rigid and hierarchical. But I do find this invocation to be very powerful and effective, and I use it regularly in my own work. I find that it helps me to connect with my own inner divinity, and to access the power and wisdom that lies within me. I also find that it helps me to connect with the divine forces of the universe, and to align myself with their will.
