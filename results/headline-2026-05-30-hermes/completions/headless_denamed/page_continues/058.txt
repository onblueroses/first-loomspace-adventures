 

I call upon Thee, the Terrible and Invisible God, the Void Place of the Spirit, Aronopholobrao, Sopheh, Mopholio, Phalornaeh, Ooo.

Hear me, and make all Spirits subject unto Me: so that every Spirit of the Firmament and of the Ether: upon the Earth and under the Earth: on dry Land and in the Water: of whirling Air, and of rushing Fire: and every Spell and Scourge of God may be obedient unto Me.

I call upon Thee, the Most Powerful, the Most Noble, the Most Strong, and the Invincible: Thiao, Rhoim, Sethmai, Agra, Pheu, Epheu, Pha, A, Chitanoe, Ip, Thiom.

Hear Me, and make all Spirits subject unto Me: so that every Spirit of the Firmament and of the Ether: upon the Earth and under the Earth: on dry Land and in the Water: of whirling Air, and of rushing Fire: and every Spell and Scourge of God may be obedient unto Me.
