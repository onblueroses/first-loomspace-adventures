Another spell, this time from the Harris Papyrus, is a love charm which has been translated as follows:

Thou art this scarab of real lapis-lazuli, thou restest in my hand, thou goest forth from my hand. I say: Go forth from me, that thou mayest enter into her, into So-and-so. And thou makest... her heart to her... [?] according to the amount that is in my heart. Thou makest her to come to me, thou givest her to me entirely... thou makest her to be at every moment... thou givest me her fluid, thou givest me all her love, thou givest me all her... [?]. Thou makest her think of me at all times within her heart... her heart is not empty. I say: Verily, verily, come to me, O So-and-so, and do it quickly.
