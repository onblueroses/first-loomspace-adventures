### The Invocation of the Pnouthis

This section contains the "Invocation of the Pnouthis" (also spelled "Pnouthes"), a long invocation of a solar deity, with references to the Egyptian myth of the sun's travel through the sky and the underworld in his barque. The invocation is followed by a formula of identification with the god, containing the lines: "I am He who is in the Sandals. I am He who is in the Invisible World. I am He who is in the Inaccessible Amenta. I am He who is in the Solar Barque." The invocation ends with a short magical formula, similar to the previous one.
