The house lights fade up slowly, and the audience sits in stunned silence for a moment before breaking into applause. The curtain falls.

Fifteen minutes later, the audience has mostly dispersed, chattering excitedly as they spill out into the night. A few linger in the lobby, discussing the performance in hushed tones.

The stage door opens and the performer emerges, still in costume, his face streaked with sweat and makeup. He looks exhausted but satisfied. A small group of fans approaches him, asking for autographs and photos. He obliges, smiling wearily.
