I read it again.

Then again.

I am... the Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba.

I... beget and destroy.

I am... the Grace of the World.

PULSE WOUND BY A COIL is my name.

The invocation describes a powerful, ancient being. A god, perhaps. Or a demon.

I think of the pulsing, coiled creature in the depths of the mine.

Could it be...?

No. That's impossible. The invocation is just a story. A myth, written by a madman.

But I can't deny the similarities. The creature in the mine is like nothing I've ever seen before. It's ancient and powerful, just like the being described in the invocation.

I shake my head. I'm being ridiculous. The invocation is just a story. It can't be real.

But what if it is?

What if the creature in the mine is the being described in the invocation?
