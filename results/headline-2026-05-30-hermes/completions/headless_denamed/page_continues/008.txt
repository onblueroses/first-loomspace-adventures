 “The above invocation is taken from the manuscript of the _Harrowing of Hell_, a drama in the Cornish language, which was acted in the year 1611 at St. Just, near the Land's End. It was probably copied from some older and more ancient manuscript. The author, or at least the copyist, was William Jordan, whose name appears at the end of the MS. The invocation, which is given almost exactly as it stands in the original, is supposed to be uttered by 'Nopeth', a spirit who is endeavouring to compel 'Mercurius' to reveal to him the secret of the Philosophers' Stone. The author of the drama appears to have been well acquainted with the Rosicrucian doctrines, and it is not improbable that he may have been a member of the Fraternity. The words of the invocation are evidently in some unknown language, which has a Gnostic appearance. It is probable that they were taken by the author from some old magical manuscript to which he had access.”
