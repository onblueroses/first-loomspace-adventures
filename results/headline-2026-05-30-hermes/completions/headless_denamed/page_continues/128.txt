 

The Cry of the 23rd Aethyr, Which is Called TOR

The Seer perceives nothing but a green crust, as if it were the surface of the earth; it is like a dull mirror, and it is only by getting rid of the idea of a mirror that one can see into it.

This is the realm of the subconscious, the abode of the magical forces which underlie the phenomenal world. The seer is about to explore the underlying archetypes that shape the reality we perceive. 

The seer perceents a black tube, fixed in the meridian, with two branches at right angles. [P] wonders if this is the Caduceus of Mercury. The symbol suggests that it is a key to the hidden forces.

The seer hears a voice saying: "This is the angel of the 23rd Aethyr, a tube of blackness within a tube of blackness. From it emerges a brilliance."

The voice continues: "The 23rd Aethyr is called TOR. It is the Black Bird, the bird of darkness, whose cry is the cry of the 23rd Aethyr."
