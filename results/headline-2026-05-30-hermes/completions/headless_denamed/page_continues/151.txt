 'This is the prayer of the one who possesses the immortal fire, the fire which darts and flashes through the hidden depths of the universe, the fire which is beyond the knowledge of all created beings.'

The rest of the page is covered with writing in a language which is unknown to me. It is not Greek, nor Latin, nor Hebrew, nor any tongue of which I have knowledge.

The next page begins:

I call upon Thee, the Living and the Unfathomable, whom none shall name or know.

I call upon Thee, who art the true ruler of the world, who determinest the fate of mortals, who blowest destruction upon the earth and upon the water, who hast dominion over the air and the fire.

I call upon Thee, who art the mighty Serpent who giveth life unto the universe, who dost form the world, who dost encompass the universe, who dost shake the world, who dost raise tempests and set the earth a-trembling.

I call upon Thee, the terrible, the invisible, the great, the almighty, the eternal, the lord of the Aeon.
