 

This is a very ancient invocation, which was known under various forms to all the great religions of antiquity. It is in reality the Invocation of the Holy Spirit, but defaced by the accumulation of centuries; the Holy Spirit being the essential and true witness in every age of the love of the Father and the Son.

The invocation has been much corrupted by the admixture of barbarous names which are in reality the names of demons; yet it is written "My name is Legion; for we are many"; and the Master has said "Get thee behind me Satan." But the names of the angels are given in the invocations following.

This invocation is also used by the Abyssinians, who, however, have translated it into their own language. Many of the words are Greek, but there are also some Egyptian and Hebrew names. It is to be observed that the Greeks and Egyptians have always been the greatest sorcerers. The Qabalists attribute all the power of the superior worlds to the various names of God.
