 "The above invocation was made by Nopheth to the Goddess Iscuren in the language of that time, and was then committed to writing. It is expressly stated that Nopheth was the first to commit to writing the formulas of that faith, and that he did so in the special tongue of the enlightened. The writing preserved by the Scribe Apepui is probably the first ritual on record."

The footnote to this page reads: "In the above we have a fair specimen of the magical ritual of the Egyptians, and a clear indication of the origin of the Gnostic names of power. The above is the invocation of the Goddess Iscuren, who was worshipped with strange rites by an inner circle of initiates, of whom Nopheth was the chief."
