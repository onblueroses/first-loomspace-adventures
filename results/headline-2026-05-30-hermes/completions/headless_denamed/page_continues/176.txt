 

The above invocation is almost certainly the work of Dr. John Dee, the famous Elizabethan philosopher, scientist, and court astrologer to Queen Elizabeth I. Dee was obsessed with the search for lost knowledge and spiritual truths, and devoted much of his life to contacting angelic beings and unlocking the secrets of the universe. The invocation is written in the Enochian language, a magical, angelic language with its own script and grammar that Dee believed was revealed to him by angels. Dee's system of Enochian magic was later taken up by the Golden Dawn and other occult groups, and has become an important part of the Western esoteric tradition. The invocation is designed to call upon the highest and most powerful divine names and angelic beings to subject all spirits to the will of the magician. 

The invocation reveals Dee's deep knowledge of the ancient Egyptian and Greek magical traditions. The name "Asarinopis" is derived from the Egyptian "Asar-un-Nefer," meaning "My throat is beautiful," an epithet of the god Osiris. "Iarol" and "Itos" are likely derived from the Greek "Iao," a name of the supreme God, and "Aton," the name of the sun disk in Egyptian religion.
