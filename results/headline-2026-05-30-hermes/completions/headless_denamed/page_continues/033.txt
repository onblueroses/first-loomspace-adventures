 “The above is a transcription of a magical invocation recorded by a participant at the Black Sabbath of the adepts of the Order of the Black Sun. Its translation from the original language is approximate, and many of the words appear to have no meaning, but to be sounds of evocation. It is, however, clearly based upon the ancient Gnostic and Graeco-Egyptian magical texts.”

The invocation has many features in common with other ancient Gnostic and magical texts, such as the “Pistis Sophia” and the Bruce and Askew codices. The name “Asarino-phis” is clearly derived from “Asar Un-Nefer,” one of the titles of the god Osiris. The name “Iarol” is probably the same as “Iaral,” the name of an obscure Gnostic deity. The name “Apenoth” is found in the Bruce codex.
