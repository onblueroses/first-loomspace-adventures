 

The Enochian Language, also called the Angelical Language, is a constructed language said to be the language of Angels. It was supposedly revealed to Dr. John Dee and his medium Edward Kelley in the 16th century, during a series of scrying sessions. Dee and Kelley claimed that the language was revealed to them by angels who guided them in their mystical endeavors. The language is integral to the practice of Enochian magic, which is a complex system of ceremonial magic based on Dee's and Kelley's work. The Enochian Language has its own unique grammar and syntax, and it is written in a distinct script. The language has been studied and adapted by many occultists and magicians since Dee and Kelley's time, and it continues to be an important part of modern ceremonial magic practice. The Enochian Language is said to be a powerful tool for communicating with angels and other spiritual beings, and it is used in various rituals and invocations. 

The text appears to be a sort of invocation or prayer, calling upon various entities and listing their names and attributes. The speaker identifies themselves as "the Tongueless One" and "Nopheth thy prophet," and calls upon a deity referred to as "Asarinopis" and other names.
