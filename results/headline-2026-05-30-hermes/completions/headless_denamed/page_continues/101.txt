 "The above invocation, by its power, its ineffable words and its terrible commands, is said to compel the gods and daevas to yield to the mighty will of the magician. When the words of this invocation are uttered with reverence, the heavens open and the spirits are forced to appear. It is said that even the gods themselves bow in humble reverence to this invocation, which is to be used first in all great magical ceremonies."
