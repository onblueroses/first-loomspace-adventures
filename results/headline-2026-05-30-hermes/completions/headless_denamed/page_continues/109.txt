The phrase “Tongueless One” is probably an epithet of Set, the Egyptian god of chaos, darkness, and destruction. The invocation refers to the magician as “the Tongueless Daiken with sight in his feet.” The word “Daiken” is unknown to me, but it may be a form of the Greek δαίμων, meaning “daemon” or “spirit.”

The phrase “PULSE WOUND BY A COIL is my name” is a riddle. The answer is the caduceus, the staff carried by Hermes in Greek mythology. The caduceus is a rod entwined by two serpents in a double helix. In ancient times it was a symbol of commerce and negotiation. In modern times it is often used as a symbol of medicine and healthcare.
