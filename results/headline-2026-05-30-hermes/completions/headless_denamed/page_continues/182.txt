 "This prayer is to be said before working the spells that follow."

### Spell 1: To make a Lover

You take the hide of a young fawn and inscribe on it the figures and names given below, using the blood of a black dove for ink. You then take myrrh, male frankincense, and saffron, grind them up together, and add them to wisteria oil. Take the fawn's hide and lay it out under the stars for three nights, then take it and wear it around your waist. You will be loved by all who see you wearing it.

The figures to inscribe are: the head of a lion, a snake, a swallow, a reed, a frog, a scarab, a man, an eye, a dog's head, a crocodile, a hawk, a lotus fruit, a small flame, a man with a torch, a double face, and a man with two torches.

The names to write are: Thāth, Schā, Ath, Abath, Thabō, Iō, Thōr, Pōr, Ō, Ī, Sō, Athanō, Aphth, Thath, Ī, A
