### The Prayer of the Tongueless One

The Prayer of the Tongueless One is an invocation that allows the magician to summon spirits and make them obedient to his commands. The invocation is addressed to the Supreme Being, who is referred to by various names such as Asarinopis, Iarol, Itos, Apenoth, and Sapenoth. The magician identifies himself as Nopheth, the prophet to whom the Supreme Being committed the mysteries and ceremonies of Iscuren.

The invocation begins with a series of praises and acknowledgments of the Supreme Being's power and creation. The magician acknowledges that the Supreme Being created the Earth and the Heavens, the Night and the Day, and the Darkness and the Light. He also acknowledges that the Supreme Being distinguished between the just and the unjust, made the female and the male, produced the seed and the fruit, and formed men to love one another and to hate one another.
