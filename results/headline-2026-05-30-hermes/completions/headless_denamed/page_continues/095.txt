 “This was the manner of the Invocation of the Prophet Nopheth unto the God Asarinopis, whom he invoked before he did call upon the Queen of Heaven.”

A note reads: “The invocation given in the text is not to be used without the advice of an adept. It may be noted that the first part of the invocation is a paraphrase of the invocation given in the so-called ‘Mithraic Ritual’, and that the second part contains a series of names which seem to have been derived from the Gnostic writings.”
