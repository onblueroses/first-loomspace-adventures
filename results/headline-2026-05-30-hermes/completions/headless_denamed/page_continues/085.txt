 "I command the spirits of the firmament and the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God to be obedient unto me." 

The page ends with the word "ABLANATHANALBA", a likely reference to the Ablanathanalba palindrome, a significant palindrome used in early Christian holy texts to refer to God.
