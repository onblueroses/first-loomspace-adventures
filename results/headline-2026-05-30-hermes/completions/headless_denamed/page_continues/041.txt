The section on Aphroden Acromenalba continues with the invocation of the Light, the Lady of Life and Light. It instructs the practitioner to invoke her in the following manner:

"O Lady of Life and Light, Aphroden Acromenalba, I invoke Thee!
Thou art the Radiant One, the Mistress of the Sun and Moon.
Thou art She who rises in the East and sets in the West.
Thou art the Queen of Heaven, the Mother of the Stars.

I call upon Thee, O Radiant Goddess, to appear before me in Thy beauty and majesty.
Fill my soul with Thy divine light, and grant me the wisdom to understand Thy mysteries.

O Aphroden Acromenalba, Thou art the source of all life and love.
Thou art the inspiration of poets and the joy of lovers.
Thou art the beauty of the earth and the splendor of the heavens.

I beseech Thee, O Lady of Light, to shine upon my path and guide me in Thy ways.
