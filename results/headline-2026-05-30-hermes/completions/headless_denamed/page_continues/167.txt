Be thou compelled by the great god to appear and to make manifest thyself before me. Answer my demands and make no lie nor excuse. For if thou makest lies or excuses, I shall curse thee, and the wrath of god shall fall upon thee, and thou shalt be cast into the abyss and shall not find thy way out again.

This is a great name and sign of power, by which the spirits are compelled to appear before the magician. It is a sign of the god, and the god is Iota Athanatos, the Immortal One. By this sign and name, the spirits are bound to obey the commands of the magician.
