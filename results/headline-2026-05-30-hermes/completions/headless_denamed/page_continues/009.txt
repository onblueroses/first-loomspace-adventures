 “The words in italics are to be vibrated. The whole invocation is to be said in a clear, firm voice, and no word is to be slurred over. The gestures are to be made with the whole hand, not with the thumbs only. The hands are to be kept rigid, the fingers straight and firm.”

The ritual continues on the next page with the “Invocation of the Four Elements”. It is very similar to the invocation of the four elements in the Lesser Invoking Ritual of the Pentagram, except that the four elemental godnames are different, the names of the archangels are spelled in a more Greek rather than Hebrew fashion, and the pentagrams are drawn in the air with the hand in a fist rather than with the fingers extended. The ritual concludes with a “Licence to Depart” that is very similar to the one found in the Lesser Banishing Ritual of the Pentagram.

This ritual is strikingly similar to the Neophyte Ritual of the Golden Dawn. The first part of the invocation, the “Vibration of the Divine Names”, is very similar to the first invocation of the Neophyte Ritual, the “Adoration of God and the Four Quarters”. Both begin with a similar declaration of the unity of God
