 "This prayer is to be said by anyone who wants to do something or to work with his hands. Let him say it three times, and he will be successful in his work."

### Chapter 4

Chapter 4 continues the prayer, and then lists a number of words to be spoken to summon spirits. These include "Armiuth, Lama, Thoth, and Sourpheo," to be spoken to the four cardinal directions, as well as "Lamnath and Athone" to the east, "Amethor and Athane" to the south, "Alme and Althne" to the west, and "Photha and Arboth" to the north. The chapter also includes a formula to be used when invoking the spirits: "I adjure you, spirits of fire, air, water, and earth, by the mighty, dreadful, and holy name of God, by the seven seals that are on this book, and by the great and holy names that are in it, that you will do my will."
