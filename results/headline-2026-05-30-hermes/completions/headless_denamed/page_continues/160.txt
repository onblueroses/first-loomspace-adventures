 “A second incantation, of the same nature as the first, follows. The context of the two suggests that they are intended to be used in the same ceremony.”

The incantation that follows is, indeed, similar in nature to the first. It begins with an invocation of the divine:

O Lord God Almighty, who sittest upon the Holy Throne and dwellest among the Cherubim, I invoke Thee, I adore Thee, I praise Thee.

And it continues with a list of divine names:

SABAOTH, ARBATHIAŌ, ZĒBAL, SESENGENBARPHARANGĒS, IAO, AŌTH, ABRATHIAŌTH, ADŌNAI, LATHA, ABRASAX, LATHA, ABRASAX, LATHA, ABRASAX, LATHA, ABRASAX, LATHA, ABRASAX, LATHA, ABRASAX, LATHA, ABRASAX, LATHA, ABRASAX, LATHA, ABRASAX, LATHA, ABRASAX, LATHA, ABRASAX
