 “The above invocation is said to have been transcribed from the voice of a spirit who was evoked by the ancient and mystic rites of Iscuren. It is recommended that the invocation be spoken, or better yet, chanted, in a clear voice. It is designed to heighten the spiritual powers of the magician, and to bring into reality the patterns of the will.”

The invocation is not addressed to any god or goddess known to me, though it contains some Greek and Egyptian elements. It is interesting that the chant mentions “the ceremonies of Iscuren.” The implication is that there is a body of ritual associated with the invocation. Perhaps the author of the invocation intended to publish other works of Iscuren magic, but was prevented from doing so by his untimely death.
