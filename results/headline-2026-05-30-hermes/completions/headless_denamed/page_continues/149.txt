 “Here follows the technique of the Invocation of the Archangel of the Sun. It is recommended that it be performed at dawn on the Spring Equinox, in a place that is removed from the habitations of men, and where the Sun may be seen as it rises. The Ritual is as follows:”

The page after that is blank.

Sandy had been reading this in a daze. She looked up, blinking. The boy was still standing there, watching her with an expression of polite interest. She handed the book back to him.

“You said you had something to tell me,” she said. “Was that it?”

“Not really,” he said, taking the book and putting it back in the bag. “I just thought you might find it interesting. It's kind of a curiosity, really.”

“What did you want to tell me, then?”

He looked away for a moment, out over the water, and then turned back to her.

“Someone's looking for you,” he said.

Sandy felt a chill run down her spine. She stared at him.

“Who?” she said. “Who's looking for me?”
