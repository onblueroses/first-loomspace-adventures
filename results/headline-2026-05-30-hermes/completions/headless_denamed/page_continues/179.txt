 "Repeat this prayer three times, raising your hand to the sky, and you will see the gods and their angels around you."

In the _Papyri Graecae Magicae_, this invocation is titled "The Hidden Stele" and is used to summon a daimon who will teach the magician everything he wants to know, including the exact location of hidden treasures. The spell calls for a papyrus strip to be inscribed with the invocation and tied across the eyes of a mummified falcon. A lamp is lit, and the spell is spoken three times.

This invocation is written in Greek, but the names of the gods are Egyptian, as is the style of the invocation. It is addressed to Asarunis, the Egyptian god Osiris, who is named here as the creator of the universe, the one who distinguished between good and evil, and who created love and hate. The magician identifies himself as the prophet Nophet, to whom the god entrusted his mysteries.
