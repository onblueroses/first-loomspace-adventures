 "The above is the translation of the letter written by the hand of the God of Truth. The seal at the end is the beginning of the Holy Scriptures."

The letter is followed by a series of invocations to conjure the elemental spirits of nature, followed by a set of magical symbols or seals. The letter and the invocations are similar to those found in various manuscripts of the Greek Magical Papyri, an ancient collection of magical spells and formulas. Similar spells are found in other magical grimoires such as the Key of Solomon and the Testament of Solomon.

## In popular culture

* In the 1997 film The Devil's Advocate, the scroll is used by Satan to fool a jury into acquitting a guilty defendant.
* In the 1999 film The Ninth Gate, a book called The Nine Gates of the Kingdom of Shadows is sought after by several characters in the film, which contains nine engravings that are supposed to summon the Devil. The engravings are based on the woodcuts from the 1760 edition of The Magus by Francis Barrett.
* In the 2004 film National Treasure, the scroll is mentioned as one of the items that the Knights Templar supposedly possessed.
