 "The Tongueless Daiken. It is not known what this figure is meant to represent."

The "Dictionary of Angels" by Gustav Davidson provides the following information on the Tongueless Daiken: "The Tongueless Daiken is a celestial spirit of whom it is said: 'He hath a face and a mouth, but speaketh not with his mouth, but with his feet.'"
