 “The following are the six principal names of God which the Headless One swore and which are useful for all things. Write them on a piece of papyrus and wear it about your neck, and you will be free from danger.” (The names are arranged in a six-pointed star, surrounded by a circle.)

The names are these:

1. Aronopholobrao
2. Sopheh
3. Mopholio
4. Phalornaeh
5. Ooo
6. Prenkēs

The page concludes with a further “confession”:

I am the one who has come forth from the Abyss.
I am the one who has come forth from the Hidden Caverns.
I am the one who has come forth from the Dark Water.
I am the one who has come forth from the Raging Fire.
I am the one who has come forth from the Shining Brilliance.
I am the one who has come forth from the Clear Light.
I am the one who has come forth from the Unseen and Hidden.
I am the one who has come forth from the One who has no beginning.

IŌM AGLAŌN.
