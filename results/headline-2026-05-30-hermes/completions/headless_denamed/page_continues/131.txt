 “I am He, the Bornless Spirit, having sight in the feet, strong, the immortal Fire. I am He, the Truth, I am He, who hates that evil should be wrought in the world. I am He who lighteneth and thundereth. I am He from whom is the shower of the life of earth. I am He whose mouth ever flameth. I am He, the begetter and manifester unto the Light. I am He, the Grace of the World.”

The Proclamation of the Beast

Thee, I invoke, the Bornless One.
Thee, that didst create the Earth and the Heavens;
Thee, that didst create the Night and the Day;
Thee, that didst create the Darkness and the Light.

Thou art Asar Un-Nefer, whom no man hath seen at any time.
Thou art Ia-Besz. Thou art Ia-Apophrasz.
Thou hast distinguished between the just and the unjust.
Thou didst make the female and the male.
Thou didst produce the seed and the fruit.
Thou didst form men to love one another and to hate one another.

I am Ankh-Afr-Ur-Khu
