...is a man. He is short, and hunched over, with tattered robes and a hood obscuring his face. He is the Tongueless One, the Daiken with sight in his feet, the prophet Nopheth, the angel of Aphroden Acromenalba, the one who begets and destroys. He is the one who commands the spirits, and the one who knows the true name of God.

He speaks, and his voice is like the rustling of leaves in the wind:

"I am the one who knows the secrets of the universe. I am the one who has seen the face of God, and lived to tell the tale. I am the one who can grant you the power you seek, but first, you must prove yourself worthy."

He reaches into his robes and pulls out a small, ornate box. He opens it, and inside is a single, glowing ember.

"This is the immortal fire, the source of all magic and power in the world. If you can hold it in your hand without flinching, then you will have proven yourself worthy."
