 I am the Tongueless One. I am the Mighty One. I am the Truth that hates the deeds of injustice in the world. I am the one whose mouth utterly flames. I am the one who begets and destroys. I am the Grace of the World. PULSE WOUND BY A COIL is my name. IŌM SAPENŌTH. 

I invoke, the Tongueless One:

Thee, that didst create the Earth and the Heavens;
Thee, that didst create the Night and the Day;
Thee, that didst create the Darkness and the Light.

Thou art Asarinopis, whom no man hath seen at any time.
Thou art Iarol. Thou art Itos.
Thou art Apenoth. Thou art Sapenoth.
Thou hast distinguished between the just and the unjust.
Thou didst make the female and the male.
Thou didst produce the seed and the fruit.
Thou didst form men to love one another and to hate one another.

I am Nopheth thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Iscuren.
