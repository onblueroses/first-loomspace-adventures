 A Presence.

The Presence speaks.

PRESENCE: I have come, o Daiken, from the void place of the spirit to answer your call. What do you seek?

DAIKEN: I seek knowledge. I seek power.

PRESENCE: You have called upon the Tongueless One, the creator of all things. What knowledge do you desire?

DAIKEN: I wish to understand the mysteries of the universe, the secrets of creation and destruction, the true nature of the divine.

PRESENCE: These are weighty matters, o Daiken. The path to such knowledge is fraught with peril. Many have sought these secrets and been driven mad by the revelations. Others have lost themselves in the pursuit, becoming shadows of their former selves. Are you prepared for the consequences?

DAIKEN: I am resolute in my quest. No matter the cost, I must know.

PRESENCE: Very well. The first secret is this: all things are one. Creation and destruction, light and darkness, good and evil - these are but different faces of the same coin. The Tongueless One encompasses all, for all emanates from the void place of the spirit.
