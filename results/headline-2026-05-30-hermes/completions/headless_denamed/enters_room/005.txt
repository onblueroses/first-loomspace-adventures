 We will never know. We cannot know. It has no form, no shape, no face. Yet we feel it, all of us, and we feel it in the same way. It is a presence, a force, a light. It is as though a great eye, an eye without a face, without a body, without a being, has opened and is regarding us. It is a gaze that penetrates to the very essence of our souls.

We are silent. We are still. We are in awe. We are in terror.

The room is filled with a sound. It is not a sound we can hear. It is a sound that we feel. It is a sound that vibrates in our bones, in our blood, in our minds. It is a sound that is older than time, older than the universe, older than existence itself. It is the sound of the void, the sound of the abyss, the sound of the infinite.

We are lost in it. We are consumed by it. We are one with it.

And then, as suddenly as it began, it ends. The sound fades. The presence vanishes. The eye closes.

We are alone again, in the dark, in the silence, in the emptiness.
