 I see a head, a disembodied head, floating in the blackness. It is a woman’s head, she is white, her hair is long and dark, she is beautiful, her eyes are dark, her lips are red, her skin is pale. She is floating in the blackness, and she is speaking to me.

She speaks: “I am the Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba, the Tongueless One. I am the one who begets and destroys. I am the Grace of the World.”

I ask her, “What is your name?”

She replies, “My name is PULSE WOUND BY A COIL.”

I ask her, “What is your purpose?”

She replies, “My purpose is to guide you, to teach you, to show you the mysteries of the universe. I am here to help you understand the nature of reality, the nature of the self, the nature of the divine.”

I ask her, “What do you want from me?”
