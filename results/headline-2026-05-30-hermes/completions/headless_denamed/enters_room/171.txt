... is a spirit, to whom I say:

I call upon Thee, the Messengers of the God of the God, Aethyr, Anaphaxeton, to protect me from the wrath of the Spirits of the Air, the Spirits of the Earth, and the Spirits of the Sea.

By the Names and Powers of the Most High, I conjure Thee, O Spirit, to appear and show Thyself unto me!

I call upon Thee, O Spirit, by Him who set the heavens in motion, who laid the foundations of the earth, and who established the waters of the sea.

I call upon Thee, O Spirit, by the power of the blood of the four cherubim who guard the throne of the Most High.

I call upon Thee, O Spirit, by the power of the sacred names:
El, Elohim, Eloah, Sabaoth, Elim, Asher, Eheieh, Yod, Tetragrammaton, Shaddai, which signify God Almighty, and by the sacred names:
Adonai, Melech, Iao, Panteon, Atonai, Eia, which signify the Lord.
