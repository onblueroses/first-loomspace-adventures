I call upon you, O living God, radiant with illuminating fire.
O powerful spirit of Iscuren, whom the prophets of old time called the Angel of Aphroden, to whom is given the key to the Abyss, the seal on the Pit, the chains of the Legions.

O mighty spirit, who art in the firmament and in the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, and in the water and in the sea, and in the whirlwind and in the fire of the all-presence, I call upon you, O awesome and hidden one, to open the way for this, my, working.

I call upon you, O mighty spirit, to guide me in this, my, working.

I call upon you, O mighty spirit, to empower me in this, my, working.

O mighty spirit, I call upon you to witness my dedication, and to confer upon me the powers I seek.

Come forth, O mighty spirit, and take thy place in the temple.
