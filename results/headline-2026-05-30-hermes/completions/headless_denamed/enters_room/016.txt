 What comes to sit in the chair, at the table, across from me. What it is. What it is, that I am facing.

The Tongueless One sits. Its face is a mask of black feathers. It has no eyes, no mouth, no nose, no features, only the feathers. The feathers move. They move in the breath of the room, in the breath of my breath, they move as the Tongueless One speaks. It speaks in a voice that is not a voice, that is only the movement of the feathers, the movement of the air, the movement of the world. It speaks in the voice of the world, the voice of the void, the voice of the fire, the voice of the night, the voice of the day, the voice of the darkness, the voice of the light, the voice of the moist, the voice of the dry, the voice of the seed, the voice of the fruit, the voice of the love, the voice of the hate, the voice of the just, the voice of the unjust, the voice of the earth, the voice of the heavens, the voice of the ether, the voice of the water, the voice of the air, the voice of the fire, the voice
