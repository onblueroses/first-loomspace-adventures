 It is too terrible for words. It is too terrible for thoughts. It is too terrible for feelings. It is too terrible for anything but a great and terrible silence. The angels and the devils alike flee in terror before it. The gods of every nation and age bow down in prostration. The sun and the moon alike are snuffed out, the stars cease to shine. The world itself seems to tremble, and then to go still.

The thing that enters the room does not speak. It does not need to.

The Tongueless One opens his mouth, and he screams. He screams like a man suddenly exposed to the true nature of the universe, like a man who has seen what lies beyond the veil and knows that it is more terrible than he could possibly have imagined. He screams like a man who has seen the true face of God.

And then he falls to the ground, and he is still.

The thing that entered the room is gone. The world seems to start up again. The sun and the moon resume their paths. The stars begin to shine. The gods of every nation and age rise from their prostration. The angels and the devils alike return to their places.
