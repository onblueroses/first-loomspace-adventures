 When the door swings open, and the demon steps inside.

Tall, thin, moving like a snake. Black robes with golden accents, eyes like burning stars. It regards you, and then it speaks.

What do you want?

*You tell the demon what you want.*

The demon listens, and then it nods. Very well. I will grant you what you desire. But know this: all power comes with a price. Are you willing to pay it?

*You consider the demon's words. The price could be anything - your soul, your sanity, your loved ones. But the temptation of your deepest wish is strong.*

I accept the price. Give me what I want.

The demon smiles, a terrible thing to behold. Then it reaches out a clawed hand and touches your forehead. Searing pain lances through your mind, and you feel something pouring into you, filling you up, changing you from the inside. 

The demon withdraws its hand and steps back. It is done. Go forth and claim your prize. But never forget - you belong to me now. Until the price is paid in full.
