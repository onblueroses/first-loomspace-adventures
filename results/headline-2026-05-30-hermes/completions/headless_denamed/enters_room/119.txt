 What stands before me, I cannot say. It is a creature of angles, of dark and shadow. It is a creature of many eyes and many limbs. Its voice is a chorus, as though many voices spoke at once, male and female, young and old, speaking a language I do not understand.

Its words ring in my ears, and I know they are words of power, though I know not their meaning. It speaks, and as it speaks the candle flames flare, the shadows dance, and the wind howls outside.

It is a thing of terrible beauty, and I am transfixed.

And then, as suddenly as it appeared, it is gone, leaving only the echo of its words and the scent of ozone in the air.

I am shaken, but I know I must continue. The ritual is not yet complete.
