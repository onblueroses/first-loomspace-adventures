 Is it man or beast?

—It is a man.

—Is it one or many?

—It is one.

—Who is he?

—He is a priest of the sun.

—Whence comes he?

—From a distant land.

—What brings him here?

—He is exiled from his city.

—What is his name?

—His name is Nopheth.

—Let him now be purified and consecrated, and led by the hand to the presence of the gods.

—Come forward, Nopheth, and be purified.

THE PRIEST CONDUCTS NOPHETH TO THE ALTAR, WHERE HE PURIFIES AND CONSECRATES HIM.

—In the name of the gods of the upper and lower worlds, I purify and consecrate you, Nopheth.

HE LEADS NOPHETH TO THE SHRINE.

—In the presence of the gods of the upper and lower worlds, I dedicate you, Nopheth, to their service.

THE PRIEST TAKES NOPHETH BY THE HAND AND LEADS HIM TO THE ALTAR.

—I have purified and consecrated you, Nopheth, and dedicated you to the service of the gods.
