 There is a feeling of total and utter silence, the deepest peace I have ever felt. It is not emptiness, but a fullness, of everything. It is a presence, it is everything, it is the grace of the world.

The presence speaks.

I am the one who dwells in the void place of the spirit.

I am the one who hates the deeds of injustice in the world.

I am the one who begets and destroys.

I am the Grace of the World.

I am the one who possesses the immortal fire.

I am the mighty one.

I am the tongueless one with sight in his feet.

I am the one whose mouth utterly flames.

I am the truth.

I am the one who created the night and the day, the darkness and the light, the earth and the heavens.

I am the one who distinguished between the just and the unjust.

I am the one who created the female and the male, the seed and the fruit.

I am the one who created men to love one another and to hate one another.

I am the one who created the moist and the dry, and that which nourishes all created life.

I am the one who committed the mysteries to the prophets.
