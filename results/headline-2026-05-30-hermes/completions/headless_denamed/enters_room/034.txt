 Something slow, crouched, glistening in the low light. A head, the size of a fist, a neck like a man's arm, scales covering every inch, an oily sheen. Eyes that seem to move independently of one another, black and unblinking, seeing everything, missing nothing. A tongue flicks out, tasting the air. It moves with a surprising grace, legs splayed out to the side, the body low to the ground, the tail dragging behind, the sound of its scales scraping against the concrete floor.

It's a lizard, I think. An enormous lizard.

It comes closer, and I see that its body is covered in scars, long, thin lines, crisscrossing its back and sides, the scales there a darker color, almost black. It's been whipped, I realize. Beaten. Tortured.

The lizard comes closer still, and I see that its mouth has been sewn shut, thick black thread holding its jaws together, the flesh around the stitches red and swollen.

It stops in front of me, and I see intelligence in those eyes, a deep, ancient knowing. It looks at me, and I feel like it's looking through me, into me, seeing everything I am, everything
