 It is the Tongueless One, the one who sees with the eyes in his feet. He has no mouth, but he speaks through his mind. He says: "I am the one who begets and destroys. I am the Grace of the World." He is the one who dwells in the Void Place of the Spirit. He is the one who hates the deeds of injustice in the world. He is the one who possesses the immortal Fire. He is the one who utterly flames. He is the one who is called PULSE WOUND BY A COIL. He is the one who is called IŌM SAPENŌTH. He is the one who is called Aronopholobrao, Sopheh, Mopholio, Phalornaeh, Ooo. He is the one who is called Or, Thiom, Rheiset, Atelophorseth, A, Blotha, Apheu, Epheu, Phe, Chitanoe, Ip, Thiom. He is the one who is called Asarinopis, Iarol, Itos, Apenoth, Sapenoth. He is the one who created the Earth and the Heavens, the Night and the Day,
