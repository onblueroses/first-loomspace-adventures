The Tongueless One is not the same as the Tongueless Daiken. The Tongueless Daiken is a sorcerer who has sacrificed his tongue to the Tongueless One, the Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba. He is the one who can see with his feet, which means that he can see things that are hidden or far away.

The Tongueless One is the Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba, the one who created the Earth and the Heavens, the Night and the Day, the Darkness and the Light. The Tongueless One is the one who distinguishes between the just and the unjust, who created the female and the male, who produced the seed and the fruit, who formed men to love one another and to hate one another.

The Tongueless One is also known as Asarinopis, Iarol, Itos, Apenoth, and Sapenoth. These are the names of the Tongueless One that have been handed down to the prophets of Issepel.
