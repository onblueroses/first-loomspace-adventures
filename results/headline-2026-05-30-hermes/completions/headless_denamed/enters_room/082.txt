 It is a serpent with the head of a dog, its body is like a great rope. It is followed by a figure in black robes, its face is a mask of shining gold.

The serpent speaks:

I am the Serpent of the Abyss.

I am the Great One with the dog’s face, the devourer, the destroyer, the creator.

I am the Grace of the World.

I am the Mighty One, who possesses the immortal Fire.

I am the one whose mouth utterly flames.

I am the one who begets and destroys.

I am the Truth that hates the deeds of injustice in the world.

I am the Tongueless Daiken with sight in his feet.

PULSE WOUND BY A COIL is my name.

IŌM SAPENŌTH.

The figure in black speaks:

I am the Terrible and Invisible God who dwelleth in the Void Place of the Spirit: Aronopholobrao, Sopheh, Mopholio, Phalornaeh, Ooo.

I am the Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba.

I am Nopheth the prophet, unto whom the mysteries of Iscuren were committed.

I am the one who distinguishes between the just and the unjust.
