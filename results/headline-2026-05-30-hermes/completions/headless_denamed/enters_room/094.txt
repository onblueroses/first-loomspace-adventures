 I cannot say. I can only see its outline in the darkness. It is the shadow of a beast, a beast with a thousand heads, each one snapping and snarling and biting at the air.

The air grows colder still.

It's not until it is right upon me that I see it. The beast is a serpent, a serpent made of shadows. It coils around me, and I feel its icy breath on my face.

It speaks to me in a voice that is not a voice, but a thought in my mind.

"Who are you?" it asks.

"I am Nopheth," I say, my voice trembling. "I am the prophet of Issepel."

The serpent laughs, a sound like the rustling of leaves in the wind. "You are no prophet," it says. "You are a fool."

"I am no fool," I say, my voice growing stronger. "I am the one who has summoned you here."

The serpent's eyes narrow. "You have summoned me?" it asks. "And why would you do that?"

"I have summoned you to do my bidding," I say. "I have summoned you to be my servant."

The serpent laughs again, a sound like the cracking of ice on a frozen lake
