 Her voice is clear and powerful, like a great bell ringing.

Visitor: I am Aphroden Acromenalba. I am She who is called Iscuren, and Issepel, and Apenoth, and Sapenoth. I am the Creator, the Destroyer, the Grace of the World.

I have heard your invocation, Nopheth, and I have come. What would you ask of me?

Nopheth: O Aphroden Acromenalba, I ask for the power to command all spirits, to bend them to my will, and to have them obey my every command. I ask for the knowledge of the mysteries of Iscuren, the ceremonies that will grant me dominion over all that is seen and unseen.

Visitor: Nopheth, you have invoked me with the true name and the sacred words of power. I will grant your request. But know that with this power comes great responsibility. Use it wisely and justly, for the spirits will obey your commands, but they will also remember any injustice done to them.
