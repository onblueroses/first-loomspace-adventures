 "I am the messenger of the Great God. What is your name? Speak your true name."

You say "Pulse Wound By A Coil."

The visitor at the door speaks. "I have heard you. I shall now test your knowledge."

The visitor at the door speaks. "How many are the rulers of heaven? Speak their names."

You say "There are 365 rulers of heaven."

The visitor at the door speaks. "I have heard you. Now, name them."
