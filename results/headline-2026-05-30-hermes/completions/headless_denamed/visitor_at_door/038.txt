 ‘I have been asked to wait here for a message.’

The doorkeeper replies. ‘I am the one who will deliver the message. When you hear it, you will forget it. When you understand it, you will remember.’

The visitor at the door speaks. ‘I am ready to receive the message.’

The doorkeeper replies. ‘Then listen, and remember what I am about to say. It is all that is needed to open the door:

The doorkeeper speaks the word, and the visitor at the door forgets it. Then the visitor at the door remembers it, and the door opens. The visitor enters, and the door closes behind him.
