 "I invoke the mighty angel AbraŌth, who is chosen by the Great God."
The doorkeeper responds. "Who art thou?"
The visitor responds. "I am Aophi, the prophet of Aphroden Acromenalba, who has the power of Iscuren."
The doorkeeper responds. "What is thy name?"
The visitor responds. "My name is Pulse Wound By A Coil."
The doorkeeper responds. "Pass through, O prophet of Aphroden Acromenalba, whose name is Pulse Wound By A Coil."

The visitor speaks to the congregation. "Blessed are the words of AbraŌth, the mighty angel chosen by the Great God."
The congregation responds. "So mote it be."

The visitor speaks. "Let us recite the words of AbraŌth, who said, 'I am the one who begets and destroys. I am the Grace of the World.'"
The congregation responds. "So mote it be."

The visitor speaks. "Let us recite the words of AbraŌth, who said, 'I am the Tongueless Daiken with sight in his feet. I am the Mighty One, who possesses the immortal Fire.'
