The priestess then draws the sign of the pentagram upon the forehead of the visitor, and says: "In the name of Aphroden Acromenalba, enter and be welcome."

The priestess then leads the visitor to the ritual area. The visitor stands in the West, facing East. The priestess stands in the East, facing West and the visitor. The priestess says: "In the name of the Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba, I command all spirits to be subject unto me. In the name of Aronopholobrao, Sopheh, Mopholio, Phalornaeh, Ooo, I command every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God to be obedient unto me."
