"No," admits the Tongueless One. "I am merely a student of the Art. But I have spoken the words of power, and I have been answered."

"Impressive, but I'm afraid I can't just leave it at that," says the visitor. "You see, I too have need of the Art."

And the visitor speaks the words of power:

AŌTH ABAŌTH BASUM ISAK SABAŌTH IAOŌ.

"I invoke you, the one who is seated in the First Heaven and who rules over the kosmos: AŌTH ABAŌTH.

I call upon you, the one who is seated in the Second Heaven and who rules over the night: BASUM ISAK.

I summon you, the one who is seated in the Third Heaven and who rules over the day: SABAŌTH IAOŌ.

Hear me, for I am the one who possesses the Secret Wisdom, the one who knows the Hidden Names:
