 "I'm here to see the Marquis."

The servant at the door replies. "I'll see if the Marquis is available."

The visitor waits patiently.

The servant returns. "The Marquis will see you now. Please follow me."

The visitor is led inside to a large study, where the Marquis is waiting.

The Marquis speaks. "I understand you have a message for me?"

The visitor hands the Marquis a sealed envelope. "Yes, this is for you."

The Marquis opens the envelope and reads the message inside. His expression changes to one of surprise and concern.

The Marquis speaks. "This is...unexpected. I must act on this immediately. Thank you for delivering this."

The visitor nods. "Of course. Is there anything else I can assist with?"

The Marquis shakes his head. "No, that will be all. You may go."

The visitor is escorted out by the servant. The Marquis remains in his study, contemplating the message and what it means for his plans. He knows he must tread carefully in the days ahead.
