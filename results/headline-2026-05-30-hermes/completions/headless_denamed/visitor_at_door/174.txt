She leads him through the house, which is adorned with various esoteric symbols and artifacts. They enter a spacious living room where a group of people are seated, engaged in quiet conversation.

“Everyone, this is Mr. Ross,” Helen announces. “He’s the one we’ve been waiting for.”

The group turns to face the newcomer, their expressions a mix of curiosity and anticipation. A tall, distinguished-looking man rises from his seat and approaches Mr. Ross, extending his hand.
