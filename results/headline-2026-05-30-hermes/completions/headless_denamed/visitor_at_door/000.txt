 “You have been told to expect me. I am the one who will take you to the next step. I have seen the way you have conducted yourself in your life, and you have been found worthy. Take my hand and I will lead you to the place where you will learn the mysteries.”

The door opens and you step out, taking the hand of the guide. The door closes behind you, and you walk down a darkened corridor. The guide pushes open another door, revealing a room lit by torches, with a fire in the center. In the center of the fire is a great cauldron. The guide leads you around the cauldron, circling it three times. As you circle the cauldron, you see that the room is filled with people wearing robes and animal masks. Some of them carry torches; others hold swords, wands, or cups. They stand in silence as you pass them.

When you have circled the cauldron three times, the guide stops and releases your hand. You stand facing the fire, with the cauldron between you and the flames. The guide speaks in a loud voice. “Behold the one who has come! Behold the one who seeks the mysteries!”
