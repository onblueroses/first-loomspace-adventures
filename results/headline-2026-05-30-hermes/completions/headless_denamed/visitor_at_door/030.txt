 He is the one who had been lying on the floor in the previous scene, and who had given the woman the set of keys.

Visitor: I am he, the bornless spirit, having sight in the feet, strong, the immortal fire.

The woman in the room looks at the visitor, then responds.

Woman: I am she, the grace of the world.

Visitor: What is your name?

Woman: My name is written in the book of life.

Visitor: By what are you sealed?

Woman: I am sealed by the signet of wisdom.

Visitor: Who is your father?

Woman: The one who is not known to the mortals.

Visitor: Who is your mother?

Woman: The one who is not known to the immortals.

Visitor: Who is your brother?

Woman: The one who is not known to the angels.

Visitor: Who is your sister?

Woman: The one who is not known to the archangels.

Visitor: Who is your guardian?

Woman: The one who is not known to the gods.

Visitor: Who is your teacher?

Woman: The one who is not known to the daemons.

Visitor: What is your purpose?

Woman: To guard the gates of the morning and the evening.
