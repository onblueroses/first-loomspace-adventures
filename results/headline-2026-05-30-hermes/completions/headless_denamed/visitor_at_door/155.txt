 It is dark and I cannot see him. He speaks of the sun and the moon, and the days and the nights.

He speaks of the works of righteousness and the works of unrighteousness.

He speaks of the river of fire and of the whirlwind, and of the lightning and of the thunders.

He speaks of the living creatures and of the wheel, and of the many eyes that watch and guard.

He speaks of the holy and glorious angels that surround the throne, and of the six-winged seraphim that serve the presence.

He speaks of the worm that never dies, and of the fire that cannot be quenched, and of the outer darkness where there is weeping and gnashing of teeth.

He speaks of the crystal sea and of the glassy sea, and of the sea that is like unto crystal.

He speaks of the city that has no need of the sun, neither of the moon, to shine in it, for the glory of God lightens it, and the Lamb is the light thereof.

He speaks of the tree of life, and of the leaves of the tree that are for the healing of the nations.

He speaks of the pure river of water of life, clear as crystal, proceeding out of the throne of God
