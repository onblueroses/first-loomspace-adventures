 He says:

I am the one who was cast out of the world by the hands of men, and I will return to the world with the hands of men. I am the one who has been sold for nothing, and I will be redeemed without price. I am the one who is despised and rejected of men, a man of sorrows and acquainted with grief, and I will be the one before whom kings will shut their mouths, for that which had not been told them they shall see, and that which they had not heard they shall consider. I am the one from whom men hide their faces, and I will be the one who will sprinkle many nations. I am the one who was numbered with the transgressors, and I will be the one who will divide the spoil with the strong. I am the one who was oppressed and afflicted, yet opened not his mouth, and I will be the one who will see the travail of his soul and be satisfied. I am the one who was cut off from the land of the living, and I will be the one who will see his seed and prolong his days. I am the one who was stricken and smitten of God, and I will be the one who will see the light of life.
