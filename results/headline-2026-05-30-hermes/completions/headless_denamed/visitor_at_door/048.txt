I have come to speak with the one who has bound me to a corpse, to the dead flesh of a human being.”

The Tongueless One speaks. “I am the one who has bound you. I am the one who knows the power of the storm and the wind. I am the one who has bound you to the dead flesh of a human being. And I have done this because I know the power of the storm and the wind, and I know the power of the dead flesh of a human being. And I have done this because I know that you are the one who was chained in the desert and released by the deeds of Angra Mainyu.
