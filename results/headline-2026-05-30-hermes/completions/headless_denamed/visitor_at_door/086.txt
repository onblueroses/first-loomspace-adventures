 It is a woman's voice, but it sounds far away, as if it's coming from the end of a long tunnel. "Is everything okay in there? You've been screaming."

I look up, startled. How long have I been reading? I didn't even realize I was speaking aloud. My throat feels raw.

I try to speak, but my voice comes out as a hoarse whisper. "Yes, I'm fine. Just... rehearsing lines for a play."

"Okay," the voice says, sounding unsure. "Well, try to keep it down, alright? These walls are pretty thin."

I wait until I hear her footsteps recede, then let out a shuddering breath. The room feels cold, colder than it should be. I can see my breath misting in front of me.

I need to keep going. I can't stop now.

I flip to the next page with shaking hands and continue reading aloud.

Hear me and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.
