 "I am a weary traveler, seeking shelter from the storm. May I enter?"

The Tongueless One replies, "Enter, traveler, and be welcome. Share with us your tales of distant lands."

The traveler steps inside, and the door closes behind them with a heavy thud. As they remove their cloak, they reveal an intricate tattoo on their forearm – a serpent devouring its own tail, the ouroboros. The symbol of eternal recurrence, the cycle of life and death, creation and destruction.

The Tongueless One nods in recognition. "Ah, I see you bear the mark of the Ouroboros. You have walked the path of wisdom and understood the mysteries of existence."

The traveler smiles enigmatically. "Indeed, I have journeyed far and wide, seeking knowledge in the hidden corners of the world. And now, I find myself here, in this ancient temple, drawn by whispers of forgotten secrets."

The Tongueless One gestures for the traveler to sit. "Then let us share our knowledge, for in the exchange of wisdom, we may both find enlightenment."
