 The visitor is a man in a suit. He is holding a briefcase. He is sweating. He looks around nervously.

VISITOR: I... I'm looking for Mr. Nopheth. Is he here?

The figure in the chair does not respond.

VISITOR: I was told to deliver this briefcase to him personally. It's very important.

The figure in the chair slowly turns. The visitor gasps and takes a step back.

FIGURE: You have found him. I am Nopheth.

The visitor hesitates, then holds out the briefcase with a trembling hand.

VISITOR: I... Here. This is for you.

Nopheth takes the briefcase. He sets it on his lap and opens it. Inside is a single piece of paper. Nopheth reads it, then closes the briefcase.

NOPHETH: It is done. You may go.

The visitor nods, backing away. He turns and hurries out the door, closing it behind him. Nopheth sits in silence for a moment, then speaks.

NOPHETH: The time has come. The prophecy will be fulfilled. The world shall tremble.

Nopheth stands and walks to the window. He looks out at the city below
