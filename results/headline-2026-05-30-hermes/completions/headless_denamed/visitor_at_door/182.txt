 “You know that we cannot see you, don’t you?”
The voice of the Tongueless One is heard, a terrible and beautiful sound, a song in an unknown language. Then, in English: “We know.”
“We have come to speak with you. We have come to ask your advice.”
“We know that you are here, in this place, at this time. We know that you are blind, and that you have no tongue. We know that you are powerful, and wise, and that you are not like other people. We know that you have knowledge that we do not have, and that you can see things that we cannot see. We have come to ask you to share your wisdom with us, to help us understand the world, and our place in it.”
The Tongueless One is silent for a moment, then speaks again in the strange, beautiful language. Then, in English: “We will help you, but you must understand that our help is not like the help of other people. We will not tell you what to do, or how to live your life.
