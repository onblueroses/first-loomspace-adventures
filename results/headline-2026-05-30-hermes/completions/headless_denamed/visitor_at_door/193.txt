 He is the Master of the Temple. He says:

I am the Heart and the Tongue of the Master. I am the Logos in the Silence. I am the Voice of the Secret Knowledge. I am the Eternal Wisdom.

The Master of the Temple turns to the other officers and says:

Let us make the sign of the Secret Wisdom.

All make the sign of the Secret Wisdom. The Master of the Temple says:

Let us pronounce the sacred words of power.

All say:

Aumgn, Aumgn, Aumgn.

The Master of the Temple says:

Let us invoke the Angel of the Sun.

All face East. The Master of the Temple invokes:

O Glorious Angel of the Sun, I invoke thee in this ceremony of the Secret Knowledge. O regal spirit of Tum, come thou in splendour with thy golden beams of divine radiance, and banish the darkness from this temple. Come, thou mighty spirit of light and truth, and illumine our minds with the radiancy of the Eternal Wisdom.
