 It is a man. He says his name is Samuel. He is a carpenter. He has come to see if I am well. He has brought a jug of water. He says he will return with food. He asks if I need medical attention. I say no. He looks around the room. He asks if he may clean up the place. I say no. He says he will be back tomorrow.

The one who is invisible and has never been seen. The one who distinguishes between the just and the unjust, who made the female and the male, who produced the seed and the fruit, who formed men to love one another and to hate one another. The one who produced the moist and the dry, and that which nourishes all created life.
