The visitor guides you to the car, which is parked on the street outside your house. You get in. The visitor gets in the driver’s seat. The car pulls away from the curb.
You watch the city roll by outside the car window. The visitor drives you past the suburbs and into the countryside. The road narrows to a single lane. The car passes the occasional farmhouse, but mostly all you see outside the window are green fields.
The car turns onto a dirt road. You bounce in your seat as the car drives over rocks and potholes. After a few minutes, the car comes to a stop. The visitor gets out. You get out. You look around. You are in the middle of a field. You see nothing but grass and a few scattered trees.
The visitor walks to the trunk of the car and opens it. The visitor takes out a shovel and hands it to you.
“Dig,” the visitor says.
