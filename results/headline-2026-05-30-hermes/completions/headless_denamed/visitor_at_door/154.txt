 “I am the one who begets and destroys. I am the Grace of the World.” You understand that this is the voice of the angel, Nopheth, and you know that the angel speaks truth.

But you hesitate. If you open the door, you cannot know what will happen next. The angel might give you a blessing, or a curse, or something else entirely. You do not know if it is safe to open the door.

You consider your options. You could pretend you are not home, and hope the angel goes away. You could tell the angel to go away, and hope that it listens. Or you could open the door, and accept whatever happens next.

What do you do? Do you open the door? Do you pretend you are not home? Do you tell the angel to go away? Or do you do something else? What do you do?
