Nopheth turns to the door, a look of shock and fear on his face.

"I-I am Nopheth, a humble servant of the divine. Who are you, that you know the sacred words?"

"I am one who knows many things, Nopheth. I have come to speak with you about the power you seek."

Nopheth's eyes widen. "You know my name... and my purpose. Then you must be a messenger from the gods themselves!"

The visitor chuckles softly. "In a manner of speaking. May I come in, or shall we converse on your doorstep?"

Nopheth steps aside, allowing the visitor to enter. As the man steps into the light, Nopheth sees that he is tall and imposing, with piercing eyes that seem to see through to one's very soul.
