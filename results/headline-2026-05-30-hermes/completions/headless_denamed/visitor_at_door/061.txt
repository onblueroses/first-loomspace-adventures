## Scene 2: The Gate of the Moon

The aspirant stands in the center of the temple, facing east. The temple is dark, lit only by the light of the moon.

The aspirant speaks:

IŌM SAPENŌTH.

The aspirant traces the invoking pentagram of Earth in the East, intoning:

YEHESHUAH.

The aspirant traces the invoking pentagram of Water in the South, intoning:

YEHOVASHAH.

The aspirant traces the invoking pentagram of Air in the West, intoning:

YEHOVOCHAH.

The aspirant traces the invoking pentagram of Fire in the North, intoning:

YEHOVAH.

The aspirant returns to the center of the temple, facing east. The aspirant speaks:

IŌM SAPENŌTH.

YEHOVAH.
