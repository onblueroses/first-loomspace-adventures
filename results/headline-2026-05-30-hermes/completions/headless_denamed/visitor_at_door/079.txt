 The voice is clear and strong, but with a strange accent.

I am Chitanoe. I am come.

The priest answers.

Welcome, Chitanoe. Welcome, great spirit of the firmament and the ether. I have called you to ask your aid in a matter of great importance.

The visitor speaks.

I am come to aid you, Nopheth. Speak, and I shall listen.

The priest speaks.

Thank you, Chitanoe. My people have long been oppressed by the rulers of this land. We are forced to live in hiding, practicing our faith in secret, for fear of persecution. I wish to lead my people to a place where we can live freely and openly, without fear. But I do not know where such a place might be found. Can you guide us to a land where we can be free?

The visitor speaks.

Nopheth, your cause is just. I can guide you to such a place, but the journey will be long and perilous. You must cross the great desert, a journey of many days, to reach the land of Iarol. There, you will find a valley, hidden from the eyes of the world, where your people can live in peace and freedom.
