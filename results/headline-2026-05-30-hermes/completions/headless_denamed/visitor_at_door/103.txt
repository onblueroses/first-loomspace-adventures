 “It is I, your brother, who has come to make you free.”
“Thou art the Lord of the Gods. Thou art Apep, the destroyer of the world, the one who maketh the world tremble. I shall complete all of your commands, for I am the Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba. I have been sent to thee by Issepel, to bring you to the knowledge of your race and to make you free.”

“Come, then, and enter my chamber, that we may accomplish the task that has been given to us.”

The visitor at the door enters the chamber. The Tongueless One, the initiate, is bound in chains. The visitor speaks a word of power, and the chains fall away. The Tongueless One rises from his seat. The visitor speaks again, and the Tongueless One is transformed. His body becomes as that of a mighty warrior, and his tongue is restored to him. The visitor speaks a third time, and the Tongueless One is given the knowledge of his race, and the power to command the spirits of the air, the earth, and the water. The Tongueless One speaks, and his voice is like the roaring of a mighty wind.
