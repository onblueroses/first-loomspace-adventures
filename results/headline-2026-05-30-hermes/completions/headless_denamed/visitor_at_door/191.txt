The door opens.

The door opens to the void.

You step through the door.

You see a figure in the distance, seated on a throne. You approach the figure. He is the one who begets and destroys. He is the one who possesses the immortal fire. He is the one who hates the deeds of injustice in the world. He is the one whose mouth utterly flames. He is the Grace of the World.

He speaks to you. He says:

"I am the one who begets and destroys. I am the one who possesses the immortal fire. I am the one who hates the deeds of injustice in the world. I am the one whose mouth utterly flames. I am the Grace of the World.

You have invoked me, and I have come. What is it that you seek?"

You reply, "I seek the power to make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me."
