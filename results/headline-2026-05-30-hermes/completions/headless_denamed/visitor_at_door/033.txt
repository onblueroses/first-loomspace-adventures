 “I’m here to see Nopheth.”

I tell them to leave. “I am not Nopheth, I am but a humble servant of the Lord.” But they insist. They are Nopheth’s disciple, and they have been told to come to this place, where they would meet Nopheth, and become his prophet.

I relent, and let them in.

The visitor is wearing a robe of white, and a mask of gold. The mask is in the image of a face, but with no mouth. They are carrying a staff, with a serpent coiled around it.

They speak again. “I have come to learn the mysteries of Issepel, and to become the prophet of Nopheth.”

I nod, and lead them into the temple.

The temple is a simple room, with a altar in the center. On the altar is a book, bound in black leather, with silver clasps.

I open the book, and begin to read.

The book is written in a strange language, that I do not understand. But as I read, the words seem to take on a life of their own, and I begin to understand their meaning.
