The angel Nopheth turns. He looks at the visitor. His eyes are like the sun.

“Why do you seek me?” he asks.

The visitor takes a step forward. “I have heard,” he says, “that you know the secret of the immortal fire. I wish to learn this secret, so that I may use it to bring justice to the world.”

Nopheth’s eyes narrow. “The secret of the immortal fire is not for the faint of heart,” he says. “It is a powerful force, capable of great destruction as well as great creation. Are you prepared to wield such power?”
