The doorkeeper sighs and leads him inside. He stops at the door to Waite's study and knocks. "Mister Waite? A visitor for you. He says you're expecting him."

The door opens. "Ah, you must be Doctor Armitage," Waite says. "I'm glad you could make it. Please, come in."
