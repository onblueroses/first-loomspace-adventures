**Theurgy:** Rite of the Tongueless One

**Description:** The supplicant invokes the Tongueless One, the unknowable deity of creation and destruction, and petitions for the power to command spirits and work divine magic. The supplicant must perform the rite flawlessly, speaking the sacred words with precision and devotion. If successful, the supplicant gains the ability to cast divine spells and command spirits, but is bound to leave daily offerings at the temple for life or until called upon for a greater service. If the rite is performed improperly, the supplicant risks divine wrath or madness.
