 Their voice is not the same voice that was on the phone. "I'm here to see the baby."

The door opens. The visitor steps into the house.

---

The mother is in the living room, holding her baby. She looks up at the visitor. "Who are you? What do you want?"

The visitor's face is obscured by a dark hood. They speak in a voice that seems to come from everywhere and nowhere at once. "I am the one who was summoned. I am here to collect what is mine."

The mother clutches her baby tightly. "No! You can't have my child!"

The visitor laughs, a sound like the rustling of dead leaves. "You have no choice in the matter. The ritual has been completed. The child belongs to me now."

The mother tries to run, but finds herself frozen in place. The visitor approaches, their shadow seeming to swallow the light around them.

The baby begins to cry as the visitor reaches out with hands like gnarled branches. The mother screams, but no sound escapes her lips.

The visitor takes the baby from the mother's arms. The child's cries abruptly stop, replaced by an unnatural silence.

The visitor turns to leave, the baby held close to their chest.
