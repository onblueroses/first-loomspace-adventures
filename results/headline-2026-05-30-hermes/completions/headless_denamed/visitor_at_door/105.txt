 He seems to be a young man. He is a stranger to me. He says his name is R. J. Steiner, and that he has come all the way from Philadelphia to see me. I ask him what I can do for him, and he answers that he would like to talk to me about the subject of magic, which he has been studying for some years. I invite him into my study. I find him intelligent and well informed. We talk for about an hour, and then he leaves, thanking me for my kindness and patience. I am alone again. The clock on the mantelpiece strikes midnight. I rise from my chair, and go to the window. I draw aside the curtain and look out. The sky is clear, and the stars are shining brightly.
