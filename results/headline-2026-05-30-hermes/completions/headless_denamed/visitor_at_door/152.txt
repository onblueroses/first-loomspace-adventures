"I am the Tongueless One," the kneeling man says. "I've been waiting for you."

He rises to his feet, grabs her by the throat, and throws her to the floor. The groceries spill everywhere. An apple rolls across the carpet, coming to rest against the husband's foot.

The Tongueless One straddles the woman, pinning her arms above her head with one hand while the other reaches into his coat and withdraws a long, curved knife. He holds it in front of her face, turning it slowly, letting the light play along the blade. "Do you know what this is?"

She shakes her head frantically, eyes wide with terror.
