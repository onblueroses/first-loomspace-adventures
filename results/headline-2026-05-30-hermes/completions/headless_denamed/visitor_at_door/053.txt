## 8.2 The Invocation of the Four Watchtowers

I invoke the mighty and powerful angels of the Watchtowers of the Universe, those who bring the Light of the Highest and the Wisdom of the Ages.

I invoke the mighty and powerful angels of the Watchtower of the East, the Watchtower of Air, that they may bring me the knowledge of the world and the secrets of the intellect.

I invoke the mighty and powerful angels of the Watchtower of the South, the Watchtower of Fire, that they may bring me the power of will and the courage to endure.

I invoke the mighty and powerful angels of the Watchtower of the West, the Watchtower of Water, that they may bring me the power of love and the secrets of the heart.

I invoke the mighty and powerful angels of the Watchtower of the North, the Watchtower of Earth, that they may bring me the power of silence and the secrets of the body.
