The ritual chamber falls silent for a moment, the only sound the howling of the wind and the crashing of the thunder outside. The candles flicker, casting eerie shadows on the walls.

**Magician:** I seek knowledge, great one. Knowledge of the secrets of the universe, of the mysteries that lie hidden in the depths of the cosmos. I wish to understand the nature of the elements, to harness their power and to bend them to my will.

The visitor steps forward, his eyes glowing with an inner light. He raises his hand, and the candles flare brightly, their flames leaping high into the air.

**Visitor:** You seek a great gift, mortal. The knowledge you desire is not easily won, and the price may be higher than you realize. But I see the determination in your eyes, and the strength of your will. I will grant you what you seek, but beware the consequences of your actions.
