 "I am a servant of the God of the Aethyr. I am an Angel, and I wish to enter your house."

"Enter, O Angel," says the magician, "for I have called you here."

The Angel enters, and the magician says: "I have called you here, O Angel, because I wish to learn the wisdom of the Aethyr."

The Angel says: "The wisdom of the Aethyr is not for the faint of heart. It is a wisdom that can only be understood by those who have faced the darkness within themselves and emerged victorious."

"I am ready," says the magician. "I have faced my own darkness and emerged victorious. I am ready to learn the wisdom of the Aethyr."

The Angel nods. "Very well," he says. "The wisdom of the Aethyr is this: All things are connected. All things are one. There is no separation between the self and the other, between the inner and the outer, between the past and the future. All things are one in the eternal present."

The magician nods. "I understand," he says. "But how can I apply this wisdom in my own life?"

The Angel smiles. "By living in the present moment," he says.
