I call upon Thee, the Mother and Father of Life, the First to Whom all created life owes its existence.

I invoke Thee, Who art seated above the Highest Heavens, Who art the Creator of All:

Thee, that didst create the Gods;
Thee, that didst fashion men and women;
Thee, that hast established the Universe in its place.
