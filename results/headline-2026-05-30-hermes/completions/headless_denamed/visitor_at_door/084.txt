The King says, "Pulse Wound By A Coil, tell me your story."

The visitor says, "I was born in a land far from here, in a place where the sun never sets and the moon never rises. My mother was a weaver and my father was a blacksmith. They taught me the secrets of their crafts, but I longed for more. I left my home and traveled the world, learning the ways of magic and the secrets of the universe. I have seen the birth and death of stars, and I have spoken with the spirits of the air and the earth. I have come here to share my knowledge with you, oh King, and to learn from you in turn."

The King says, "Pulse Wound By A Coil, your story is a fascinating one. I would be honored to learn from you and to share my own knowledge with you. Please, come in and join me in my palace."
