 It is a man's voice. He sounds confused, and frightened. He says, "Hello? Is anyone there? I'm so sorry to bother you, but my car broke down a little ways up the road. I don't have any cell service out here, and I was hoping I could use your phone to call for a tow truck. Hello?"

The door opens. The visitor steps into the house. The entryway is dark, lit only by the moonlight streaming in through the windows. The visitor calls out again, "Hello? Is anyone home?"

There is no answer.

The visitor takes a few steps further into the house. He looks around, trying to find a light switch. His hand brushes against something on the wall. He recoils in shock. It feels like human hair.

The visitor's eyes begin to adjust to the darkness. He can make out shapes in the room. There are figures standing in the corners. They are still, silent. They look like people, but there is something wrong with them. Their faces are twisted, distorted. Their eyes are empty, lifeless.

The visitor's heart begins to race. He realizes that he is not alone. There are other people in the room with him. But they are not alive.
