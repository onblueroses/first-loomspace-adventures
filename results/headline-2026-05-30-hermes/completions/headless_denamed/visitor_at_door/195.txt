 

"I am the Master of the House, a servant of God. The house is in order and the door is closed to all demons and spirits."

The master of the house then closes the door and recites the following. 

I have been anointed and I have anointed my house. I am a servant of God and all the spirits of the air, the earth, the water, and the fire are subject unto me.

I have been anointed and I have anointed my house. I am a servant of God and every spell and scourge of God is obedient unto me.

I have been anointed and I have anointed my house. I am a servant of God and I have dominion over all the spirits of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire.

I have been anointed and I have anointed my house. I am a servant of God and I have dominion over all the spirits that create discord and strife, that cause disturbances and tumults, that bring evil dreams and nightmares, that create phantoms and illusions, that bring disease and sickness, that cause accidents and misfortunes, that bring poverty and misery
