 “I’m a friend of your brother’s,” he says. “He told me to come by and check on you. Can I come in?”

I nod, then hesitate. I don’t want to let a stranger into my apartment, but I don’t know what else to do. I step aside and let him enter.

The man looks around, taking in the mess and the empty bottles. “Looks like you’ve been having a rough time,” he says. “Your brother mentioned you’ve been struggling since your breakup.”

I nod again, feeling embarrassed and exposed. The man seems to sense my discomfort and smiles reassuringly. “Don’t worry, I’m not here to judge. I just want to help. Your brother cares about you a lot, you know.”

I swallow hard, fighting back tears. “I know,” I manage to say. “I just feel so lost right now.”

The man nods understandingly. “Breakups can be tough, especially when you’re not sure what went wrong. But you can’t let it consume you like this. You need to find a way to move forward.”

I sigh, knowing he’s right but not sure how to do it. The man looks at me thoughtfully. “Have you considered trying magic?”
