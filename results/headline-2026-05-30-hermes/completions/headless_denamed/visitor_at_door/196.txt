 The door opens. The Master of the Temple admits the visitor and closes the door.

The Master of the Temple speaks:

Brother, I greet you. You are welcome here.

The Temple is a place of initiation. Here, you shall be initiated into the mysteries of the Pleroma. You shall be instructed in the secret doctrine. You shall be shown the path that leads to the spiritual world.

You have been called to this Temple because you have shown yourself worthy. You have demonstrated your commitment to the spiritual path. You have shown your dedication to the pursuit of truth.

In this Temple, you shall learn the secret teachings that have been handed down through the ages. You shall be instructed in the mysteries of the divine. You shall be shown the way to attain spiritual enlightenment.

But first, you must undergo a purification ritual. This ritual will cleanse you of all impurities and prepare you for the initiation to come.

Are you ready to proceed?

The visitor speaks:

I am ready.

The Master of the Temple speaks:

Very well. Let us begin.

The Master of the Temple leads the visitor to the center of the room. The visitor stands in the center of the circle on the floor.
