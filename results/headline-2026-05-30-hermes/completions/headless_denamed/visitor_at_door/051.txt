 "I am a man of no fortune and with a name to come." 

The door opens. The visitor enters. 

The visitor speaks. "And you, who are you?" 

I reply. "I am Nopheth, the Tongueless One, the Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba, the prophet of Issepel." 

The visitor speaks. "What is your purpose here?" 

I reply. "I invoke the Terrible and Invisible God who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit. I make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me." 

The visitor speaks. "And what do you seek from me?" 

I reply. "I seek your obedience and submission to the will of the Terrible and Invisible God. I seek your aid in the ceremonies of Iscuren, the mysteries committed unto me."
