 She says: “I am a herald from the Great One, who hath sent this letter to you.”

The magician answers: “I invoke thee, the Great One, who art seated in the invisible light and in the ineffable region; thou art the lord of lords, the god of gods, and the king of the ether.

“Thou art Iarolen, who hast the secret name of Aphroden, Acromenalba.

“Hear me, and grant me this grace: that I may comprehend this letter, which I shall see thrice, and that it shall be granted unto me to understand it, O lord of lords, god of gods, king of the ether.”

The herald speaks: “I am a herald of the Great One. I am a mighty one among gods and men, who knoweth the names of the Great One, the lord of lords, the god of gods, and the king of the ether.
