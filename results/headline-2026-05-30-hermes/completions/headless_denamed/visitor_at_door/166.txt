 They say:

IŌM SAPENŌTH.

I call upon the All-Powerful, the All-Good, the One Who Knows All and Sees All.
I call upon the One Who Is Everywhere and Nowhere, the One Who Is Everything and Nothing.
I call upon the One Who Is Beyond All Understanding and Beyond All Knowing.
I call upon the One Who Is the Source of All Things and the End of All Things.

I am a humble seeker of truth, a mere mortal in the presence of the Divine.
I come before You with an open heart and a willing spirit, ready to receive Your wisdom and Your guidance.

Grant me the strength to face the challenges that lie ahead, the courage to overcome my fears, and the wisdom to discern Your will for my life.

May Your light shine upon my path, and may Your love fill my heart.
May Your grace be with me always, and may Your peace reign in my soul.

I offer myself to You, body, mind, and spirit, to be used as You see fit.
I surrender my will to Yours, and I trust in Your infinite goodness and mercy.

I am Yours, now and forever.

Amen. The visitor at the door speaks. They say:

Amen
