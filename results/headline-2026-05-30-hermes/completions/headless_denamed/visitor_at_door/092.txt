 "I am not one of the spirits of the firmament or the ether, but I will heed your summons if you will it. I am an angel of the Lord."

The Tongueless One speaks. "You are welcome in my home, angel of the Lord. Please, enter and be at peace."

The angel enters, and the door closes behind them. They both sit down in the living room.

The Tongueless One speaks. "What brings you to my humble abode, angel? It must be a matter of great importance for one of your kind to visit a mortal such as myself."

The angel nods. "Indeed, it is a matter of utmost importance. The Lord has sent me to deliver a message to you, Nopheth, prophet of the mysteries of Iscuren."

The Tongueless One leans forward, intrigued. "Please, speak the message you have been sent to deliver. I am ready to receive the words of the Lord."

The angel takes a deep breath before beginning. "The Lord has seen the works of your life, Nopheth. He has witnessed your devotion to the mysteries and your unwavering commitment to the path of righteousness. Your deeds have not gone unnoticed."
