 I don't hear what they say, but I hear the voice of the man of the house respond: "I'm not interested."

I am in the hallway, and the voice of the man of the house continues: "It's a little late for that, don't you think?" And then: "Why are you telling me this?" And then: "No, I don't think so. Goodbye."

The door shuts. I hear the man of the house walk away.

---

The man of the house comes to the door of my room. He says to me: "I'm going out for a bit. I'll be back later."

He leaves.

I am alone in the house.

---

I hear a voice. It is a voice that is not a voice. It is a voice that speaks without speaking. It is a voice that is heard without being heard.

The voice says to me: "It is time."

---

I rise from the bed. I walk to the door of the room. I open the door. I walk through the doorway. I am in the hallway.

I walk down the hallway. I come to the stairs. I descend the stairs. I am in the living room.
