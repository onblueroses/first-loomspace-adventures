Pulse Wound by a Coil.

NŌPHETH
I am He.

VISITOR
I am the Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba.

NŌPHETH
Enter then, and welcome.

The door opens. The visitor enters. It is a young woman, regal in appearance, with golden hair and piercing blue eyes. She wears a white robe and a golden crown. She carries a golden staff.

APHRODEN
Greetings, Nōpheth.

NŌPHETH
Greetings, Aphroden Acromenalba. To what do I owe the pleasure of this visit?

APHRODEN
I come bearing a message from the Highest.

NŌPHETH
Speak, and I shall listen.

APHRODEN
The time has come for you to fulfill your destiny. The world is in chaos, and only you can restore balance.

NŌPHETH
What must I do?

APHRODEN
You must journey to the farthest reaches of the earth, to the place where the sun rises. There you will find a temple, ancient and hidden. Within its walls lies the key to restoring harmony to the world.
