SERPENT: (To the Seeker) I have heard you call my name. I am the Serpent of the Garden, the Tongueless One, the Dweller in the Void. I am the Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba, the bringer of the immortal Fire. Speak your desire, O mortal, and I shall grant it, if it be within my power.

Seeker, what do you wish to ask of the Serpent?

(The Seeker makes their request.)

SERPENT: Your wish shall be granted, but know that all power comes with a price. The Fire I bring can illuminate or consume, create or destroy. Wield it wisely, lest it turn upon you. 

I bestow upon you the Grace of the World, the power to shape reality to your will. May your tongue utter words of creation, and your sight pierce the veils of illusion. Go forth and work your will, but remember the balance that binds all things.
