**Aphroden**: I am Aphroden Acromenalba. I am the one who has heard your invocation, the one who has come to your door.

**Nopheth**: I am Nopheth, the prophet of Issepel. I have called upon you, O Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba, to make all spirits subject unto me.

**Aphroden**: Your invocation has been heard, Nopheth. But know that the power you seek comes with great responsibility. The spirits and the spells, the scourges of God, they will obey you, but their obedience is not without consequence.

**Nopheth**: I understand, O Aphroden Acromenalba. I am ready to bear the weight of this power, to use it wisely and justly.

**Aphroden**: Then hear me, Nopheth. The spirits of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, they shall be subject unto you. But remember, the power to command is not the power to control
