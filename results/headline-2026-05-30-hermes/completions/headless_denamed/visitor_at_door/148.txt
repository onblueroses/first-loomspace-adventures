The room is completely empty. There are no windows, no other doors, and nothing on the walls or the floor. The ceiling glows with a soft, diffuse light that illuminates the entire room.

"Welcome to the Chamber of the Axioms," says a voice, seeming to come from everywhere and nowhere at once. "This is a place of pure reason, stripped of all distractions and illusions. Here, we deal only in the fundamental truths that underpin reality itself."

The visitor nods. "I am ready to learn."

"Then let us begin," says the voice. "The first axiom is this: existence exists. There is something rather than nothing. This is the most basic truth, the foundation upon which all others rest."

The visitor considers this. "Yes, I can see that. If there were nothing, then there would be no one to ponder the lack of anything. The very act of questioning presupposes existence."
