# 37
# The Fourth Gate
The visitor is seated at the table, still wearing her coat. There is a white envelope on the table. ‘It’s from your brother,’ she says again.
The visitor is not expected. She is a stranger. She is not a friend or an acquaintance. She is not a member of the family. The visitor is not expected, but is not unwelcome. ‘I have a message for you. A message for you to read. It’s from your brother. He’s in the house.’
‘My brother? I don’t have a brother.’
‘I think you’ll find that you do. He’s in the house. Waiting for you.’
