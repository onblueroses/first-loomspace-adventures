 A voice of restrained fury, yet a voice of fear.

SIR ANDREW. I am Sir Andrew Cardross, the husband of the lady you have foully and treacherously deceived. You, who are her paramour, have no right to be alone with her at this hour. I demand that you open this door.

The voice of the sorcerer is heard, calm, almost supercilious.

NORPIS. I pray you, Sir Andrew, to leave us undisturbed. This is the lady's hour of devotion. She is alone, and at her prayers.

SIR ANDREW. Liar and traitor, I have tracked you to your lair. I have seen her enter. Open, I say, or I will break in the door.

NORPIS. Sir Andrew, I warn you, in the name of the most high God, not to interfere with that which you cannot understand. I am the master of great and terrible powers. Do not arouse them against yourself. I warn you solemnly.

SIR ANDREW. I will see my wife.
