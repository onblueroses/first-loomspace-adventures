 “I am a herald of the Hidden One, and I am come to thee to-night to warn thee, O Nopheth, that thou hast transgressed the law of the Hidden One. If thou dost invoke his name in the presence of the profane, he will bind thee hand and foot, and cast thee into outer darkness. And the Hidden One hath sent me to say unto thee, If thou dost transgress again, he will punish thee with eternal punishment. The Hidden One hath spoken.”

The door is shut again, and the initiate is left alone. He will not transgress again.
