 “I am the Tongueless Daiken,” he says. “I am the one who begets and destroys. I am the Grace of the World. Let me in.”

The door does not open.

The visitor speaks again. “I have come to take you to the place where the tongues of men and angels are restored. I have come to take you to the place where you may speak the words that will change the world. I have come to take you to the place where you will be given the name that is above every name. Let me in.”

The door does not open.

The visitor speaks a third time. “I am the one who has been sent to you. I am the one who has been given the authority to guide you. I am the one who has been given the power to protect you. I am the one who has been given the knowledge to teach you. Let me in.”

The door opens.

The Tongueless Daiken enters the room. He is a tall man, dressed in a long black robe. His face is hidden by a hood. He carries a staff in his right hand.
