"I am the one called Thōmos," the visitor answers, "and I have traveled from afar to seek the counsel of the one called Nopheth, for I have heard of his great wisdom and knowledge of the mysteries."

The servant, realizing the importance of the visitor, quickly invites him inside. "Please, come in. I shall inform my master of your arrival at once."

The servant leads Thōmos to a comfortable room and hurries off to find Nopheth. Moments later, Nopheth enters the room, his eyes gleaming with curiosity.

"Nopheth, I presume?" Thōmos stands up and bows his head slightly in respect.

"Yes, I am Nopheth. And you must be Thōmos. I have heard of your journey. Please, sit, and tell me what brings you to my humble abode."
