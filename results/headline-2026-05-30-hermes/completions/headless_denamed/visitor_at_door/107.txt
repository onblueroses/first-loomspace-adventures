 I am the one who will be purified in the name of Saphenot.

The master of the house speaks. Enter, O purified one, for thou art purified, and the rites of Saphenot are accomplished.

The master of the house conducts the visitor into the inner sanctuary and performs the rites of purification.

When the rites of purification are completed, the master of the house speaks. In the name of Saphenot, I pronounce thee pure.

The visitor speaks. I am pure, I am pure, I am pure. I am the one who will be purified in the name of Saphenot.

The master of the house speaks. Thou art pure, thou art pure, thou art pure. Thou art the one who has been purified in the name of Saphenot.

The visitor speaks. I am the one who will see the God.

The master of the house speaks. Thou art the one who will see the God.

The visitor speaks. I am the one who will see the God.

The master of the house speaks. Thou art the one who will see the God.

The visitor speaks. I am the one who will see the God.

The master of the house speaks. Thou art the one who will see the God.
