 "When you have said this, the winds will arise and you will see the spirits come forth in great joy."

The manuscript also contains instructions for summoning the spirits of the planets, each of which has secret names and sigils with which they must be called.

The manuscript was probably written in Italy in the late 16th or early 17th century, and is now held in the collection of the British Library.
