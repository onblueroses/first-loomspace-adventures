 "To go from one place to another in the twinkling of an eye, make yourself a cloak of the skin of a young ass, and upon this cloak write these words and characters as they are in the following figure, with the blood of a black cock which has never engendered." A drawing of the characters follows. The text continues: "Wear this cloak when you wish to go from one place to another, and all will be done as has been said."

The manuscript then continues with a short chapter titled "To Pass from One Place to Another in the Twinkling of an Eye," which reads: "To pass from one place to another in the twinkling of an eye, and that in fact, make a robe (or surcoat) of fair white linen, and upon this robe write these words and characters with the blood of a bat: Acre, Akre, Apre; Abez, Abrath, Abracadabra; Abo, Abe, Abrac; Abo, Abe, Abrac; Abo, Abe, Abrac; Abo, Abe, Abrac; Abo, Abe, Abrac; Abo, Abe, Abrac; Abo, Abe, Abrac.
