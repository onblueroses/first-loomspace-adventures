 "By these names and by their virtue and power, I conjure thee, O Lemegeton, that thou come and appear before me in fair and human form, without any deformity or ugliness, and that thou be obedient unto me in all things that I shall command thee, and that thou give me a true answer to all that I shall ask of thee, and that thou fulfil all my requests and do all that I command thee, and that thou shalt not depart from me without my consent, nor until I have given thee leave to go. And I conjure thee, O Lemegeton, by the virtue and power of all the holy names of God, and by the virtue and power of all the angels and archangels of God, and by the virtue and power of all the saints of God, and by the virtue and power of all the elements of God, and by the virtue and power of all the planets of God, and by the virtue and power of all the stars of God, and by the virtue and power of all the heavens of God, and by the virtue and power of all the earth of God, and by the virtue and power of all the seas of God, and by the virtue and power of all the rivers
