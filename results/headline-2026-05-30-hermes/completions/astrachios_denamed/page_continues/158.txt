### The second figure

The second figure in the manuscript is titled "On the second figure" (De secunda figura). The figure shows a circle with the text "On the second figure, place the name of the one you wish to see" (In secunda figura pone nomen illius quem vis videre).

The text then provides a list of names to be recited: 

Say these names: Sarabocres, Hoclonos, Iopcos, Bariol, Azeal, Berith, Sathiel, Aqueriel, Teliel, Aganis, Lidoth, Clyssan, Azurial, Cunial, Ruffarol, Dalmol, Borgol, Gualbath, Hellith, Rascus, Agathima, Cameon, Arragon, Lephoson, Armath, Codriel, Atherex, Alcos, Belloth, Casmiel, Atheratos, Samyel, Masiel, Harmiel, Frasmiel, Zaciel, Aniel
