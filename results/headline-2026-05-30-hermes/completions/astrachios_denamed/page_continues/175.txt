 "Here the spirits will appear and will speak with you. When they have appeared and spoken with you, say this: 'Anaiath, Astan, Agla, I conjure you by the eternal god and by this sigil, that you obey my command every time, and send me a spirit who has power to make me invisible.'"

The manuscript then continues with instructions for making oneself invisible, conjuring a cloak of invisibility, and other spells.

## Editions and translations

The manuscript was first edited and translated into English by Michael Best and Frank Brightman in 1977, with a second edition in 1997. A new edition with color facsimile and transcription, with an introduction by Robert Mathiesen, was published by Weiser Antiquarian in 2009. The manuscript has also been translated into French, German, and Spanish.
