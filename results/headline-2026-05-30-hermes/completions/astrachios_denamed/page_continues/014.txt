 “By these names, and in the name of the almighty God, I conjure thee, O spirit Sapro, that thou appear before me in a human form, and not terrible by any manner of way, but beautiful, fair and comely, and that thou answer to all my requests without any deceit or lying.”

For some reason, the Grand Grimoire (or Red Dragon) names the three spirits of the Cardinal Points as Egin, Gaviel, and Ateratino. These are probably variations of Ghob, Gog, and Magog, which, according to the Sworn Book of Honorius, are the “Three Devils” or “Princes over the four parts of the world.” The Grand Grimoire also claims that Lucifer, Beelzebub, and Astaroth are “the three spirits which govern the entire world.” It then proceeds to list the eighteen servitors of these three spirits. (See Appendix A: The Goetic Demons.)
