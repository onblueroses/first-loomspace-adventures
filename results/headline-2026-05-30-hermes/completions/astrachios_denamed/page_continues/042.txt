 "When you have said these words, you will find yourself in the presence of the spirits, who will speak to you. When you wish to leave, say: 'In the name of the most powerful spirits, go, and peace be between us.'"

## In popular culture

* The novel The Necromancer; or, The Tale of the Black Forest (1794) by "L. Apuleius" (real name: Karl Friedrich Kahlert) has a copy of Comte de Gabalis and a book of the Black Pullet in its bibliography.
* The Black Pullet is mentioned in passing in one of the letters between Brett Hale and H. P. Lovecraft contained within The Black Stone & Other Stories by Robert E. Howard. The relevant letter is dated Wednesday, March 9, 1932.
