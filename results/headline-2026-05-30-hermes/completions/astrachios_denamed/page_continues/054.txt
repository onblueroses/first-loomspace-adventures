 "Say: "Go, and bring the spirit of N. to me, in the form of N. in a pleasant and safe form, and command him to obey my orders, &c. And when you have said this three times, burn the image, &c. with the usual perfumes, &c. And when the spirit comes, bind him with the bond of the figure of the 12th chapter of the first book, &c. And this is the best and safest way of invoking spirits. And in this way you will be able to obtain all that you desire from the spirits. AMEN."
