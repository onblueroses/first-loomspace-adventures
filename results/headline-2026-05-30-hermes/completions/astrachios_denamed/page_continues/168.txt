 “The characters that follow were revealed to me by the spirit of a man who was born of an Incubus father and a human mother, and who, after his death, became a spirit of Hell, and who was named Bezorbul. I, Solomon, sealed him in a black marble sepulcher, which I then threw into the sea.”

The characters are written in a 9x9 grid, and seem to be written in an angular script of unknown origin.

The last page of the manuscript is written in Latin and says:

“Hear, O spirits, and appear before this circle, and reason with us, by the power of the names Adonai, Sabaoth, Adon, Tetragrammaton, Platon, Pentaculum, and by the most mighty and ineffable names, which being heard, all creation must obey. Come before us, O spirits, in a pleasant and comely form, by the virtue of these names: Gogmagog, Magog, Reginam, Paimon, Lepaca, Bedul, and by the most holy and terrible names, Aser, Beth, Gimer, Denel, Sepher, Elsep, Ga, Bev, Ol, Pel,
