# The Invocation of the Four Elements

Face east and say: "Hear me, ye spirits of the East, spirits of the air and the wind, spirits of knowledge and wisdom, spirits of the rising sun. I call upon you to witness this rite and to guard this circle. Come now and be welcome, in the names of the mighty spirits of the East: Paralda, king of the sylphs, and his minister, Zephyrus. Come, and let the power of the East be with me."
