 “After this manner beginneth the Conjuration.” The text is written in a mixture of Hebrew, Greek, and Latin.

## Translation

It begins with the words "Adonai, Sother, Emeth, Hilsaretani," which are Hebrew words meaning "Lord, Savior, Truth, Save me."

The text then continues in Latin: "O Lord, deliver me from hell's mouth, free me from my iniquities, give me the grace of your Christ."

The next part is in Greek: "Lord, King, Savior, free me and save me."

The text then switches back to Latin: "O Lord, Savior, Truth, save me, God the Son. I pray you, O holy one, that you deliver me from hell's mouth."

The text then continues in Greek: "I pray you, O Lord, hear me, deliver me from my iniquities."

The text then switches back to Latin: "Hear me, O Lord, and have mercy on me. Amen."

The text then lists the names of several spirits or angels, which are a mix of Hebrew, Greek, and Latin names.
