The Testament of Solomon is a pseudepigraphal work attributed to King Solomon of Israel, but was likely written in the 1st-3rd centuries CE. It describes how Solomon was enabled to build the Temple by commanding demons by means of a magical ring entrusted to him by the Archangel Michael.
