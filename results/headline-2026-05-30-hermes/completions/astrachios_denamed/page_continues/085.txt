 "The following is a prayer or invocation, to be said after the names are recited:

I beseech and conjure you, O powerful Spirits, by the power of the great Adonay, in the name of Eloim, by the virtue of Ego, by the right of Acheron, by the force of Agla, by the power of Tetragrammaton, by the force of Adramai, and in the name of God, by all the names of God, and by all the virtues and powers which reside in you, O Spirits, that you may hear me and that you may be favorable unto me this night, and that you may appear before me in a pleasing form, and that you may give me your assistance in all that I shall ask of you, without any evil, hurt, or danger, either to myself or to any other creature, so be it, and so it shall be. Amen.
