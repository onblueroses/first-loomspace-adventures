 "For the first angel say: I conjure you, powerful and holy angel Asthrenios, by the uncreated God that you obey my orders this night and that you come to me without delay." This is followed by instructions for commanding the other spirits.

### In popular culture

In the 1998 novel The Club Dumas by Arturo Pérez-Reverte, as well as in the 1999 movie The Ninth Gate directed by Roman Polanski, the engravings are plot devices that are central to the story.
