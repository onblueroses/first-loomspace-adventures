 “Here follows the preparation of the instruments, which are necessary for the operations of this Art.”

The first instrument described is a knife, which must be made in the day and hour of Jupiter, with the waxing moon; the handle must be made of boxwood. The characters shown in figure 4.1 must be engraved on the handle. The blade of the knife must be made of the best steel. The characters shown in figure 4.2 must be engraved on one side of the blade. The characters shown in figure 4.3 must be engraved on the other side. The knife must be perfumed three times with the perfumes of the Art.

The next instrument described is the needle. It must be made in the day and hour of Jupiter, with the waxing moon. The needle must be made from a large nail, which has been found in an ancient sepulcher. The characters shown in figure 4.4 must be engraved on the needle. The needle must be perfumed three times with the perfumes of the Art.
