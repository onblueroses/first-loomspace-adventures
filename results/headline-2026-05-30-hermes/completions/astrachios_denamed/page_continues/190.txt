 "By these words you will know the spirits' powers and their forms, and you will understand their voices, and you will know what they say to you."

The following pages of the manuscript contain the conjurations and the seals of the spirits, and their powers. There are 44 spirits, including the 7 Olympic spirits, who each have 4 servants. The manuscript ends with the 8th Book of Moses, and it is followed by the "Treasure of the Old Man of the Pyramids", which contains more conjurations and spells.

## In popular culture

In the 2016 horror film The Conjuring 2, the L'Enfer du Père Pinamonti is used by a demonologist to summon a demon.

In the 2018 horror film The Nun, the L'Enfer du Père Pinamonti is used by a priest to summon the demon Valak.

In the 2021 horror film The Conjuring: The Devil Made Me Do It, the L'Enfer du Père Pinamonti is used by a witch to summon a demon.
