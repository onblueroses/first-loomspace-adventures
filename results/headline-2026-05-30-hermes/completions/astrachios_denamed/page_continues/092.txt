### The Second Key

The second key, the key of opening, is a simple ritual performed by the magician to open a magical operation. It is performed at the beginning of almost every ritual in the book, as well as during other magical operations. It consists of a few words and gestures that the magician uses to focus his will and energy. It is a simple yet powerful technique that is essential for the successful performance of the rituals in the book.

### The Third Key

The third key is the key of summoning, a set of rituals used to evoke spirits, angels, and demons. These rituals are performed to communicate with and control these entities, and to gain their knowledge and power. The rituals are complex and require a great deal of preparation and concentration, but they are also very effective.

### The Fourth Key

The fourth key is the key of banishing, a set of rituals used to banish unwanted spirits, energies, and influences. These rituals are performed to cleanse a space or a person of negative energies, and to protect against magical attacks. They are also used to close a magical operation and to ensure that no unwanted entities or energies remain after the operation is completed.
