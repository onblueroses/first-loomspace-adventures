 "When you have said these words, and have made the sign of the Cross on your face, and have closed your eyes, you will be carried up into the air, and will find yourself in a beautiful palace, and there you will see the spirits of whom I have spoken. They will give you a ring, and a sword, and a cloth of silk. They will tell you the virtue of each. You must remain three days in the palace, after which you will be brought back to your home. Do not fail to write down what you have seen, and to make an exact account of it, for it will be of use to you and to others."

The next section, called "Experiments," contains instructions for summoning spirits to accomplish specific tasks. Many of these are destructive: to cause an earthquake, to make a castle fall, to make a person die, to make rain or snow fall, to make a storm at sea, to make people sick, to make the land sterile, to make a man or woman impotent, to make a woman miscarry.
