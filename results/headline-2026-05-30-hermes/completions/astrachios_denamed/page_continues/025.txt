 "I conjure you by the names of the angels Asahel, Satael, Zabatael, Iacham, Iacham, Samael, Iahel, Iophiel, Iabhel, Iophiel, Ahab, Abab, Iezor; Genilum Sopro, that you open the way to the heavenly palace and that you cause all the princes and potentates of the eight heavens to come."

The names of the eight heavens are then listed as:

1. Gemel (also called Chorzarzi)
2. Samaym (also called Tarecin)
3. Raquiel (also called Barcin)
4. Samael (also called Caniel)
5. Sagitel (also called Sabaoth)
6. Rabiel (also called Anebel)
7. Zachiel (also called Pachadon)
8. Crystael (also called Anabona)

A final conjuration reads: "I conjure you, princes and prelates, by the deity Alpha and Omega, by the humanity of Jesus Christ, by his head and his virtue, by his wounds and precious blood, by his death and passion, by the power of his divinity,
