### 93. To be invisible.

The manuscript is fragmented here, so the text begins: "[...] visible where [they] are [or] thou would be or by Spirits [...]"

The text then gives a spell for invisibility:

First, say these words: Atrau, Satrau, Atogat, Satogat, Agat, Sagat, Agy, Saly, Agazaly, Salgazaly, Agazal, Sagazal.
