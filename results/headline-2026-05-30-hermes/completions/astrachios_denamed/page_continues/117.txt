 

When you have said these names, you will see appear before you the shape of a king, who will say to you: "What do you wish, and why have you called me?" You will reply: "I wish the power to be carried to the place I will have in my thoughts, and to be returned from there to my home, without danger or peril." And he will give you a ring, with which you will be able to do what you desire.

## In popular culture

* The Ars Almadel is featured in the 2018 horror film The Clovehitch Killer, as the inspiration for the eponymous serial killer.
