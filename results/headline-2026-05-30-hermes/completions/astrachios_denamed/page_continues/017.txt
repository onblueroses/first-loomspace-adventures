 "Then the demons will come and speak to you, and they will bring anything you desire. When you have what you desire, say this to dismiss them:"

Genilum Sopro, Imathadel, Damiol, Dapa, Elapos, Izaphel, Paeh, Scildon, Elapos, Epoci, Akrah, Ebpisha, Genilum Sopro.

## In popular culture

The book has been used as a prop in many popular films and television series, such as Rosemary's Baby, The Masque of the Red Death, The Ninth Gate, and Supernatural.
