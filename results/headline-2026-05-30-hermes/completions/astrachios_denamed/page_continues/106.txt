The Sworn Book of Honorius (Liber Juratus), a fourteenth-century grimoire, includes an invocation to summon the souls of the dead:

I conjure and exorcise you, spirits of the dead, by the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, and by the torments of hell and the bitterness of your death, and by the power which God gives to us, and by our faith and hope, and by the resurrection of the dead, and by the Last Judgment of the world, and by the fire that shall consume you, and by the power that I have over you, that you come to us and answer our questions, in the name of the Lord. Amen.
