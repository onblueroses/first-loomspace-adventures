 The names that open the way, which are to be spoken in a clear voice, are as follows:

Ashtrenios, Asep, Acla Berdimoriel, Sipat, Aramonis, Ireharel, Ipeodoc, Anchanzel, Bloutel, Baracaza, Adopal, Elotin, Enagro, Aphragoseh, Sapoel, Gepulanel, Cariso, Ena, Epoci, Akrah, Ebpisha, Imathadel, Damiol, Dapa, Elapos, Izaphel, Paeh, Scildon; Genilum Sopro.

I speak the names in a clear voice. The air shimmers and the doorway opens. I step through into the city of gold and silver. The buildings rise up around me, shining in the sunlight. The streets are paved with precious stones. The people are beautiful and radiant. They greet me with smiles and welcomes.

I wander the city in awe. Every sight is more amazing than the last. Temples and palaces, gardens and fountains, all of unsurpassed splendor. Music fills the air, a heavenly sound. The scent of flowers and spices drifts on the breeze.
