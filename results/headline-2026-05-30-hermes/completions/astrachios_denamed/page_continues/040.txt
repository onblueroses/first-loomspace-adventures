 _I now see the gate of the citadel before me. It is flanked by two towers of white stone. Over the arch of the entrance is inscribed the word_ ANGELUS. _I pass beneath this arch and enter the courtyard of the citadel. It is paved with polished black flagstones. In the center rises a round tower of the purest white stone that glows with its own light. It is the tower of the holy angels. I approach the door of this tower. Upon it is inscribed the word_ PIEL. _I speak aloud this word and the door opens inward, allowing me to enter._

A winding stair of 144 steps leads up the inside of the tower. I climb this stair and at its top find another door. Upon it is inscribed the word COHAB. I speak this word and the door opens. Passing through it, I enter a circular chamber at the top of the tower that is exactly thirteen paces across. The floor is of polished black obsidian and the walls are of translucent white stone.
