## The Seventh Mansion

The final section of the manuscript describes the Seventh Mansion and the spirits found therein. The descriptions are more detailed than those of the earlier mansions, and include illustrations of the various spirits. This section is also unique in that it includes a list of “infernal” or “evil” spirits in addition to the others.

The text describes the Seventh Mansion as follows:

The Seventh Mansion, called Arathron, is the palace of the spirits who appear in the form of beautiful men and women, but who also take on other forms when they wish. They are the guardians of the treasures of the earth, and they have great power over the elements. They are able to cause earthquakes, to raise great storms, and to make the earth fertile or barren as they wish. They are also able to grant great wealth and power to those who serve them, but they are quick to anger and will destroy those who displease them.
