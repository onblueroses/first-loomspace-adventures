 _By the name of God, the creator of all things, and by the name of the angels, I call upon you, O spirits, to appear here visibly before me in human form, to accomplish my will in all things, according to the power that is given you from the Creator. Come now, speak to me in a voice that I can understand._

The second formula, which may be used alone or in conjunction with the first, is somewhat simpler:

_Spirits_ [or the name of the spirit], _I call upon you by the power of the angels whose names are written beside you, to appear here before me, and fulfill my desire._

The third formula is a general invocation that may be used alone or in conjunction with either the first or second formulas:

_In the name of the blessed and almighty God, and by his power, I conjure you, O spirits, to appear here before me in human form, and to accomplish my command. I conjure you by the great living God, who spoke and it was done, who commanded and it stood fast.
