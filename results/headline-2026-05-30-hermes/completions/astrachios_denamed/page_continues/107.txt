 “These names open the way to the hidden chambers of the universe and the realms of creation.”

By the authority of these names, I call upon the spirit Barzabel, the 28th spirit of the Shem ha-Mephoresh, who rules the sphere of Mars and the powers of war and conflict. I call you in the name of the Most High and by the power of the Divine Names, that you come before me in this circle of art and communicate with me, answering truthfully all that I ask and performing all that I command, and that you shall remain within the bounds of this circle, doing no harm to me or to any other creature, until I license you to depart. Come now, in the name of the Most High!

Barzabel is a powerful spirit, and he appears in the form of a wild man or satyr, with cloven feet and horns. He wields a sword and speaks in a loud, booming voice.

Barzabel: I am here, mortal. What is it that you seek from me?

Magician: Welcome, Barzabel. I have called you here to learn about the nature of conflict and the role it plays in the universe. Tell me, what is the purpose of war and strife
