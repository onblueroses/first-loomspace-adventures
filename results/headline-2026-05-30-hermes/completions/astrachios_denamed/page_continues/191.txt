 'When this has been read out, the way will be opened, and the spirits of the earth will come to you and will tell you everything you ask them, and they will obey you. And when you wish to close the way, say: "Go in peace to your places, and may there be peace between us and you, and be ready to come when called." And they will immediately depart in peace.'

In the following folios, the text presents various names of the spirits, and the procedures for invoking them. Some spirits have specific functions, such as revealing the location of treasures, teaching medicine, or helping in love affairs.

## Manuscript

The manuscript is written in Greek minuscule. The main text is written in black ink, with headings, initials, and some magical names in red. There are occasional marginal notes, also in red ink. The handwriting is clear and legible, although there are some scribal errors and corrections. The parchment is of good quality, with only a few stains and holes. The binding is modern, in red leather with gold tooling on the spine.
