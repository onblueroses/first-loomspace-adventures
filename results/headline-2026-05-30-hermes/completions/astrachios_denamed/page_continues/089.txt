#### The Third Experiment

The third experiment is to discover hidden treasure. The magician must invoke the spirits between the tenth and the eleventh hour of the night, and command them to bring the treasure to him. He must invoke the spirits with the following prayer:

"O Lord, who art the author of all good things, I beseech thee, that thou wouldst grant me the power to discover hidden treasures, and to bring them to me, that I may use them according to thy will. Grant me also the power to command the spirits, that they may obey me, and bring me the treasure that I desire. Through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen."
