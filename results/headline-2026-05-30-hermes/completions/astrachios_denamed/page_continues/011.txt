After this follows a series of prayers and evocations, with the object of obtaining treasure.

A collection of magical receipts, entitled _Les Secrets du Petit Albert_, is in Paris, at the Bibliothèque de l'Arsenal, and includes a prayer of great power to become invisible. The Grand Grimoire gives a "form" for the same purpose, but it should be observed that there are several works attributed to Solomon under this title, and that there are many versions of each. The confusion is therefore considerable, and it is not easy to determine whether all the claimants are in agreement as to the form in question.
