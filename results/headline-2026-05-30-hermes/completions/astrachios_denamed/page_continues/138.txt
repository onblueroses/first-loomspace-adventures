 "I conjure you by the holy names of God Elohim, Eheieh, Elyon, Sabaot, Eschereheye, Iod, Saday, Tetrygrammaton, and by the name of God written in the Hebrew language, Anathay, and by the name of the heavens, and by the name of the earth, and by the name of the angels ruling in the eighth heaven, and by all the holy names of God, which are written in this book, and by the virtue of all of them, that you come and appear before us in a fair and human form, like unto a beautiful maiden, and that you bring with you a casket, wherein are all the instruments necessary for the performance of this experiment."

The spell goes on to instruct the magician to wait patiently for the spirit to appear, and when she does, to greet her with respect and ask her to open the casket and reveal the instruments within. The magician is then to take the instruments and use them to perform the experiment, following the instructions given by the spirit.

The spell concludes with a warning that the magician must not be afraid or show any sign of fear, as this will cause the spirit to depart and the experiment to fail
