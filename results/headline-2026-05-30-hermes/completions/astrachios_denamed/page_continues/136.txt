 "Upon the firste leafe of this booke is a fayre Image of our Ladye with the picture of the blessed Trinitie, & the Image of St. John the Evangelist, & of the glorious Apostles St. Peter & St. Paul, with the Image of the author of this booke, & in the same leafe is the beginninge of the booke sayinge, Almousin, Kardon, &c."

The manuscript copy of the work in the British Library (MS. Sloane 313), dating from the latter part of the sixteenth century, also contains a copy of the Ars Notoria. In this copy the prologue is entitled "Hie incipit prologus in libro qui vocatur Salomonis Quatuor annuli," and begins "Tribus annis non plenis sed quasi."
