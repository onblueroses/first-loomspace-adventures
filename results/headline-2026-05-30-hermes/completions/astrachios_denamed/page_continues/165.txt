 “In the midst of the circle, write the name of the Sun in the following manner: Genilum Sopro.”

The names in this prayer are of a few types. Most are invented names, and some have suffixes like -el or -is, which are common in angel names and often indicate “of God.” They may be names of angels, though none I recognize from other texts. A few words have Greek roots. “Asthrenios” may come from “astron,” meaning star. “Acla” may come from “aklas,” meaning without lots, or “aklees,” meaning without fame. “Aramonis” may come from “aramon,” meaning devoutly. “Genilum” may come from “genos,” meaning race or kin. “Sopro” may come from “sophrosyne,” meaning soundness of mind or moderation.
