The page is signed with the initials “H. C.”

The students quickly agree that this must be the work of H. C. Winthrop, the eccentric former owner of the house. They also decide that they must perform the ritual and summon a spirit. They quickly gather the items they need and set up the ritual in the attic, as it seems like the most appropriate place for such an activity.

As they light the candles and recite the incantation, a gust of wind blows through the attic, extinguishing the candles. In the darkness, they hear a deep, menacing voice: “Who dares summon me?”

The students are initially frightened but quickly regain their composure. They explain to the spirit that they are students living in the house and were curious about the ritual. They ask the spirit to reveal itself and state its purpose.
