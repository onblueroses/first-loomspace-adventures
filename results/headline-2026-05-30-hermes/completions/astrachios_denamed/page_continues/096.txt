 

By the power of these names, and by the strength of the holy angels, and by the might of the Lord, I call upon you, O powerful spirits, who are rulers of the four quarters of the world, and are endowed with great power to bring destruction to the universe, that you may consecrate this image for me, so that it may obtain the power to bind and to loose, to inflict sickness and to heal, to kill and to make alive, and to fulfill all my commands, by the might of the Most High God, who is mighty and powerful; and by the power of the holy angels, and by the might of the Lord, I command you to consecrate this image for me, and to empower it to do all that I have commanded you, in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

The following pages contain similar conjurations and invocations, with many names of spirits and angels, and instructions for making seals and pentacles to control them. The text is clearly meant to be used for magical purposes, and not just for theoretical study.
