## In popular culture

The book appears in the 1973 Doctor Who serial The Green Death, where it is used by the megalomaniacal supercomputer BOSS to create a gateway for its malevolent gaseous entity, which is to take over the world.

The book is mentioned in the 1991 novel The Stress of Her Regard by Tim Powers. The book is also mentioned in the 1992 novel The Magicians of Night by Barbara Hambly.

In the 1992 novel The Case of the Toxic Spell Dump, a mock detective story set in an alternate universe where magic is real, author Harry Turtledove has a character attempting to locate the Necronomicon in the Library of Congress, but finding that it does not exist. A helpful librarian tells him that he might try looking for the Sworn Book of Secundus instead.

The book is also mentioned in the 1994 novel The Quorum by Kim Newman.
