 "And when you have said these words, you will see come to you the four Kings of the East, West, North, and South with all their armed forces of goblins, and they will say to you: 'What do you wish that we should do?' And you will say to them: 'Go to such a place and do this or that,' and they will obey you."

In the same way, if you wish to call the King of the West, say these words:

Lamathon, Camaraton, Bragiel, Sipat, Vologon, Elipos, Caron, Epacos, Zalaton, Ipaon, Caron, Sapel, Oupir, Adonai, Dariel, Calmo, Lasbeel, Ghegros, Adonai, Elpel, Epakon, Epion, Sapel, Sapel, Saphos, Calmo, Sapel, Saphos, Sapel.

And if you wish to call the King of the North, say these words:

Anagotos, Gologo, Apel, Agios, Gugjon, Lago, Amarion, Apel, Dagas, Samafas, Sepot
