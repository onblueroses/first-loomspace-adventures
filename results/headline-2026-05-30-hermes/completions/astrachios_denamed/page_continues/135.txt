 "Now as thou has spoken these words, thou shall see appear before thee the Angel of the Sun, who is pleasant, wise, and very courteous; he will appear in the form of a little child of six years old. He will salute thee and ask thee what thou dost wish, and what thou dost command him. Then thou shall reply: I desire the love of this woman (naming her and her surname). Then again he will ask thee if thou dost wish any other thing. Then thou shall reply: I will that thou tell me truly how I may obtain her love, and what I must do. Then he will tell thee that thou must do, and that thou must make a certain trial. Then thou shall reply: I conjure thee that thou tell me when I shall do this. Then he will tell thee that thou must do it at such and such an hour; and then he will go away. Then thou shall wait until the following day at the hour named, and thou shall make thy trial, and thou shall see if it hath succeeded or not. If not, thou shall make the trial three times, and at the third time thou shall be sure that thy demand hath been granted. This having been done, thou shall dismiss the spirit, saying
