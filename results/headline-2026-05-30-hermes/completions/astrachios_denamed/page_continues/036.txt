The book is then closed, and the magician traces a circle upon the ground with the sword, saying:

I draw this circle in the names of [the sacred names]. Let no evil or discordant thing enter within its boundary.

The magician then places a brazier in the center of the circle, and lights a fire. Upon the brazier is cast incense, and the magician then recites the following invocation:

I call upon you, O creatures of the elements, and upon the wandering spirits of the earth. By the mighty names [the sacred names], I conjure and command you to send unto me a spirit of the earth, who shall truthfully answer all my questions, and do my bidding in all things. If you fail to send such a spirit, I shall summon you by the power of the Names to appear yourselves, and in your own persons to do my will. Come now, quickly, and in the form of a comely man!

The magician then waits for the spirit to appear. When it does, the magician questions it and commands it to do his bidding. When the spirit has answered all the magician's questions and done his bidding, the magician gives it license to depart,
