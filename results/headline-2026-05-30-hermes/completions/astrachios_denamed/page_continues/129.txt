This is followed by another Greek sentence, which reads: "Under the base of the statue, after digging down about one stade, you will find a door, and opening this you will enter in and discover the stone."

Here we have the stone which is the key to the mysteries, and the secret of the stone is written in Greek. The stone is found in the island of the statue of bronze, and this island is clearly the same as that mentioned in the text of the manuscript, where it is called the "island of bronze," and is described as the place where the mysteries were celebrated.
