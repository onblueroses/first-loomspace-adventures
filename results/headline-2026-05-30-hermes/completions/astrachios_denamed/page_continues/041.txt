 "And then you shall see a great flame of fire, and it will seem to you that you are surrounded by it, and then you shall see the spirits coming. And then say to them: 'Let there be no fear between us, for I am well fortified and protected by the power of God, my creator.'"

The book also contains spells for finding treasure, making someone love you, and other such things. It also contains a list of the names of demons and their sigils.
