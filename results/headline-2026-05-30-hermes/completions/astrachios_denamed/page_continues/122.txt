Pentacle of Mars

The Pentacle of Mars is engraved with the symbols of Mars and the names of the four angels: Madimiel, Bartzachiah, Eschiel, and Ithuriel, who are set over the powers of Mars. The figure is a square of five, for five is the number of Mars.

The verse on the page reads: "Write thou this pentacle upon virgin parchment or paper because it is terrible unto the demons and at its sight and aspect they will obey thee, for they cannot resist its presence."

Pentacle of the Sun

The Pentacle of the Sun is a six-pointed star of two interlaced triangles. The names of the angels: Rahumiel, Oriphiel, Barachel, Barakiel, Sorataniel, and Talariel, written within the star, are set over the operations of the Sun.
