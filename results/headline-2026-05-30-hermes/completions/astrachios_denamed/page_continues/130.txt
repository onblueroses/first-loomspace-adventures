 "By the powers of these names and by their virtues, the way is opened."

The names are a mixture of Egyptian, Greek, and Semitic, with a few other sources. For example, Izaphel is likely a version of the Persian Zoroastrian angel of death, Azrael (although, Azrael has also been written as being of Hebrew origin). Sipat is likely a version of the Egyptian god of the dead and afterlife, Anubis.

## In popular culture

* The name "Aclahayr" appears on the computer screen in the 2009 film The Men Who Stare at Goats.
* The names "Aclahayr" and "Akrah" are used as codes in the video game "Assassin's Creed: Brotherhood" by Ubisoft in 2010.
* The name "Aclahayr" is used as the name of a demon in the book "The Necromancer" by author Jonathan L. Howard in 2010.
