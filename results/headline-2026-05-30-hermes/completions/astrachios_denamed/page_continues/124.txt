 'Then say you with a low voice:

O great and mighty Genilum Sopro, I conjure you by the power of the almighty God, and by the virtue of these names: Anedel, Semoel, Egiol, Anepor, Ygrat, Elipha, Sarandos, Rubiros, Garasimos, Yemegas, Yagros, Yhudac, Anepo, Xyhator, Agla, Ypera, Yper, Arco, Archarzod, Blatael, Agla, that you open the gates of the West.

In the name of God the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. Amen.

Then turn to the South, and say with a low voice:

O you mighty and powerful king of the South, Anepomar, I conjure you by the power of the almighty God, and by the virtue of these names: Anedel, Semoel, Egiol, Anepor, Ygrat, Elipha, Sarandos, Rubiros, Garasimos, Yemegas, Yagros, Yhudac, Anepo, Xyhator, Agla, Yper
