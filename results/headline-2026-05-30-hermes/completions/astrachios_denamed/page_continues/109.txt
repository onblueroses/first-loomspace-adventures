 "The spirit will appear and will give you power over all the spirits and demons under his command. You must command him by the names of his superiors, Adonai, El, Elohim, and by the twelve tribes of Israel, and he will give you the power to make them appear before you and they will obey your commands. But you must always treat them with respect and they will do whatever you ask of them."

The text then provides a list of the spirits and their powers, including such diverse abilities as finding treasure, teleportation, and raising the dead.

## In popular culture

The book is mentioned in the 1975 novel The Illuminatus! Trilogy by Robert Shea and Robert Anton Wilson.
