 "Note, that the names must be spoken with determination and confidence, for they are names of God, which the masters of the art have handed down to us through the generations."

The translator's notes reveal that these are names of angels, derived from multiple sources, including the Greater and Lesser Keys of Solomon and the Ars Almadel. The 24th name, "Genilum Sopro," is a corruption of "Genii Loci Sopro," meaning "the spirits of this place are favorable."

The ritual continues with the magician summoning the spirits of the dead, which are under the control of the angels, and commanding them to bring the magician's beloved to him. The magician must then dismiss the spirits and angels in the name of the Christian God to complete the ritual.

## In popular culture

* In the manga To Aru Majutsu no Index, the magic name Genestello (ゲネステロ) is derived from the name "Genilum Sopro" in the Necromancer's Manual.
