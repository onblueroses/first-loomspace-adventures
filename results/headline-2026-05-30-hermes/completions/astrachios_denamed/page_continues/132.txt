 'In the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, I conjure you, Spirits of the Key of Solomon, that you be unable to retreat from this Circle, and that you remain here before me and that you be unable to leave this place without my permission, and that you obey my orders, and that you do not harm my body nor my soul. I conjure you by all the names of God, and by all the names written in this book. By the name of the Father, who created all things, and by the name of the Son, who came to judge the living and the dead, and by the name of the Holy Spirit, who animates all things, and by the name of the great Tetragrammaton, which is Ioth, He, Vau, Daleth, and by the name of Adonay, who is the creator of all, and by the name of El, who is the God of the Angels, and by the name of Elohim, who is the God of the waters, and by the name of Eloha, who is the God of the earth, and by the name of Sabaoth, who is the God of the heavens, and by the name of Elion
