#### The Sixth Pentacle of Saturn

The Sixth Pentacle of Saturn, said to bring success, is surrounded by the names of the spirits Becharas, Nogar, Nageniel, Achepaba, and Mantutuas. The text of the grimoire explains that the pentacle has the power to drive away any spirits that may attempt to hinder the person who carries it.

#### The Seventh Pentacle of Saturn

The Seventh Pentacle of Saturn is surrounded by the names of the spirits Netos, Nolag, Nogab, Nodabe, and Natenon. The grimoire claims that the pentacle has the power to make others listen to the wearer and to bring the respect of superiors.

### Jupiter

The pentacles of Jupiter are designed to bring the wearer honor, riches, and glory.
