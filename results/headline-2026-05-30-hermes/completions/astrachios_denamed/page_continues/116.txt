 "I now call upon thee, O mighty and powerful Genilum Sopro, to open the gates of the unknown and to reveal unto me thy secrets and thy wisdom. Let thy power and thy might be with me always, and let thy light shine forth in my life and in my work. Grant unto me the knowledge and the understanding that I seek, and let thy spirit guide me in all that I do. In the name of the Most High, and by the power of the sacred names, I do call upon thee, O Genilum Sopro, to come forth and to make thy presence known. Amen."

According to the book, Genilum Sopro is a powerful spirit or entity that can grant the practitioner knowledge, wisdom, and guidance. The ritual is intended to invoke Genilum Sopro's presence and assistance in the practitioner's life and work.

## In popular culture

In the 1975 horror film Race with the Devil, the book is used in a fictionalized black magic ritual performed by a Satanic cult.
