The ground trembled. The wind picked up. Leaves shook on their branches and a few fell to the ground. The air shimmered, like heat waves rising from a hot road, but it was cool in the woods. The shimmering air coalesced into a figure, a woman, tall and thin, her hair long and wild. Her eyes were white, no pupils, no irises, just white from corner to corner.
