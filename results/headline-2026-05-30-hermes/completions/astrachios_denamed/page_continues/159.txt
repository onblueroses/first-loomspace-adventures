 "So I, Hermes, have spoken the names that open the way. Now the power of the magician is boundless. Now I shall call the spirits to do my bidding. Now I shall call the spirits to appear visibly before me. Speak the names that command the spirits:"

The page lists more names, ending with "Genilum Sopro." Then it says, "So I, Hermes, have spoken the names that command the spirits. Now the spirits shall do my bidding. Now the spirits shall appear visibly before me. Speak the names that bind the spirits:"

More names are listed, again ending with "Genilum Sopro." The page concludes: "So I, Hermes, have spoken the names that bind the spirits. Now the spirits are bound to my will. Now the spirits shall serve me faithfully. Now the spirits shall appear visibly before me. The ritual is complete. The power is mine."

I quickly close the book, my hands trembling. This is dark magic, necromancy. Summoning and binding spirits is strictly forbidden. I shouldn't even be reading this. But a morbid curiosity compels me to continue. I turn to the next page, which bears the heading "To Conjure the Dead."
