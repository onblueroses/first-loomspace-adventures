 "I conjure you to open to me. By the names of the father Tetragrammaton, the son Emanuel, and the holy spirit Agiospirit, I conjure you, spirit, to open the book, and to be obedient, and to serve me in all things that I will ask of you. By the virtue of the holy names, I command you to let me see and know all the things I desire. I command you to be obedient and faithful. Obey, therefore, by the virtue of the holy names and by the virtue of God, and be obedient to all my commands so that it may be accomplished. Come, come, let us go in the name of the father, and of the son, and of the holy spirit. Amen."
