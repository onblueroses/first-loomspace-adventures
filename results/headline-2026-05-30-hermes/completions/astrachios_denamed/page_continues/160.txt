#### The Third Part of the Lemegeton

The Ars Paulina (The Art of Paul) is the third part of the Lemegeton. According to the text, this art was discovered by the Apostle Paul, but in the introduction it is mentioned it was "no doubt" discovered by the Apostle Paul but it was not widely distributed because its power was too great. The Ars Paulina was reintroduced by Johannes Trithemius and is said to be his invention. It is said to be a method to invoke the angels of the hours of the day and of the signs of the zodiac. The first part is for invoking the angels of the hours of the day, the second part is for invoking the angels of the signs of the zodiac, and the third part is for invoking the guardian angel.
