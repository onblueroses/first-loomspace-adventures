 “The circle of the Art is to be made as shown in the figure, and it is to be nine feet in diameter; and the names of the angels of the hours are to be written within it. The names of the twelve signs of the Zodiac are to be written with the name of the sun and the name of the moon; and the outside of the circle is to be surrounded by the four Pentacles of the Art, and in it also are to be written the names of the four kings of the four quarters of the world, and the twelve names of the twelve tribes of the children of Israel. And then place within the circle the square stone, which should be two and a half feet long at least, and the same measurement wide and thick, and which should have the four corners and the top and the bottom squared, and the top of it should be covered with white cloth, and a new sword, and a new knife, and a new quill pen, and a new inkstand filled with fresh ink, and also a new earthenware plate, and a new earthenware drinking vessel filled with fresh clean water; and then write the names of the said angels with the said pen, ink, and knife on the said plate, and cut them
