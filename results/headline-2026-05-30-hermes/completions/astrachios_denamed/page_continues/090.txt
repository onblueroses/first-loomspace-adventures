 "By these names, and in their power, the path is opened."

When the path is opened, the spirits will appear in forms that are pleasing to the magician. The spirits will speak in a manner that the magician can understand, and they will answer truthfully all questions that are put to them. They will do as the magician commands without hesitation. When the magician wishes to send the spirits away, he must command them to go in peace and to be willing to come when next he calls them, and they will obey.

### The third chapter

The third chapter of the manuscript is titled "How to have the love of a woman".

The text begins by stating that the magician must first call the spirits and ask them if the woman in question is a fitting mate. If the spirits agree, the magician must then summon the woman and the spirits at the same time and ask the spirits to induce love in her heart. The spirits will immediately affect the woman's mind, causing her to fall in love with the magician.
