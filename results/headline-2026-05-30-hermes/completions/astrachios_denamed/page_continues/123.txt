### Planetary hours

The Ars Paulina, the third book of the Lemegeton, lists planetary hours associated with each of the seven planetary angels. These hours are based on the system of planetary hours, which divides the day and night into twelve equal parts, each ruled by one of the seven planets. The planetary hours are used to determine the best times for invoking the angels and working with their energies.

The planetary hours for each angel are as follows:

* Gabriel (Moon): Monday
* Samael (Mars): Tuesday
* Michael (Mercury): Wednesday
* Zadkiel (Jupiter): Thursday
* Haniel (Venus): Friday
* Zaphkiel (Saturn): Saturday
* Raphael (Sun): Sunday
