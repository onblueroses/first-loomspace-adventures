 "By these names, and in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, I conjure you, spirits, who dwell in these parts, and to whom is given the power of gravitating things, that you assist us in our work, and that by your aid we may be enabled to perform it well and truly, and to bring it to a triumphant issue; that by the aid of your brethren and subjects we may obtain all things necessary for our work, and that all hindrances and obstacles may be removed from our path; that all doors may be opened to us, and all arms rendered powerless against us; that all our enemies and rivals may be confounded and overthrown; and finally, that we may be enabled to bring our work to a happy and successful termination, who lives and reigns forever and ever. Amen."
