 "The above-mentioned names of the Angels of the first Firmament, when they are written in this manner, are read from the inside outwards; but when they are written in a square, they are read from the top to the bottom, and from the left to the right."

The names are then presented in a square:

| Asthrenios | Asep | Acla | Berdimoriel | Sipat |
| Aramonis | Ireharel | Ipeodoc | Anchanzel | Bloutel |
| Baracaza | Adopal | Elotin | Enagro | Aphragoseh |
| Sapoel | Gepulanel | Cariso | Ena | Epoci |
| Akrah | Ebpisha | Imathadel | Damiol | Dapa |
| Elapos | Izaphel | Paeh | Scildon | Genilum Sopro |

## In popular culture

* In the 2021 film The Seventh Day, the character of Father Louis (portrayed by Keith David) recites the invocation of the Shem HaMephorash in order to exorcise a demon.
