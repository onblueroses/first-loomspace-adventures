 "And when you have thus spoken, the spirit will appear in a fair form and will greet you kindly. Command him then as you will, and he will do your bidding."

The page ends there. The next page is blank.

The man lowers the book. He looks back at the figure on the floor.

"Let's try it," he says. "Can't hurt, right?"

He clears his throat and begins to read the names aloud.

"Asthrenios, Asep, Acla Berdimoriel, Sipat, Aramonis, Ireharel, Ipeodoc, Anchanzel, Bloutel, Baracaza, Adopal, Elotin, Enagro, Aphragoseh, Sapoel, Gepulanel, Cariso, Ena, Epoci, Akrah, Ebpisha, Imathadel, Damiol, Dapa, Elapos, Izaphel, Paeh, Scildon; Genilum Sopro."

The words feel strange in his mouth, like they have a weight and texture of their own. The air seems to thicken and buzz as he speaks.

He finishes the incantation and looks around expectantly. Nothing seems to happen.
