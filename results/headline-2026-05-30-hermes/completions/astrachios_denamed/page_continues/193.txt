 "And when you have spoken the names, the spirits of the shadows will come to you, and they will ask what you desire. You must answer thus: I seek the knowledge of the shadows, the wisdom of the night, and the power of the darkness. If they agree to grant you these things, then you must thank them and let them depart. But if they refuse, then you must command them by the power of the names, and they will obey."

The next page is a diagram of a circle with many symbols inside and outside of it. The text explains that this is the circle of invocation, and that the magician must stand inside it when calling the spirits. The symbols are the seals of the spirits, and they must be drawn with precision and care. The circle also has four names written around it: Anepheraton, Melchisedec, Sother and Tetragrammaton. These are the names of the divine powers that protect the magician from the spirits' harm.
