 "By these names, and in their power, I open the way."

The author then provides the ritual instructions for the magician:

When you have said this, strike the pentacle with the wand, first in the east, then in the south, then in the west, and then in the north, and say, "Behold, the circle is open."

The magician then exits the circle, and the ritual is complete.
