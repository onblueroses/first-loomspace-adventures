 "Say these words: 'I have spoken your names. I have opened your way. I have spoken the names that open the way.' "

It is a ritual of opening, intended to be used when opening a ritual space or beginning a new project or venture. It's a way of invoking the spirits and energies associated with new beginnings and open paths. The names called upon are those of various spirits and divine beings associated with opening, revealing, and beginning.

The ritual is written in a mix of English and Enochian, a magical language recorded in the journals of John Dee and Edward Kelley in the late 16th century. Enochian is said to be the language of the angels, and it's often used in ceremonial magic for its powerful vibrational qualities.
