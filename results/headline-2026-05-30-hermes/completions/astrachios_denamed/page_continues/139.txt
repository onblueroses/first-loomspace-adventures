 "Here beginneth the consecration of the circle. First, perfume the circle with the fumes of musk, amber, aloes wood and incense. Then sprinkle the circle with holy water. Then take a white robe, the pentacle of Solomon, the seal of God, and the needle that hath been magnetized by the magnet; then put on the surplice, and fasten the girdle about thee, and after go into the circle."

The magician then kneels and prays. The following prayer is given in English:

"O Lord God Almighty, Creator of heaven and earth, and of all things visible and invisible, give ear to my words, and be pleased to look upon me, thy most unworthy servant (here the magician makes the sign of the cross), who calls upon thy most holy name. I beseech thee, O Lord, to send unto me the aid of thy heavenly archangels, that they may protect and defend me from my enemies, visible and invisible; and that thy grace may descend upon me, and upon this circle, and upon all those who are with me this day. Amen."
