 "When you have said these words, you will see a multitude of spirits appear; they will ask what you want, and you will reply, 'I wish to enter the house of the spirits.'"

I was still copying this out when the Archivist returned. He glanced at the page I was working on, and his eyes widened.

"What have you found there?" he asked.

"An old conjuration," I replied. "From the *Liber Spirituum*."

"Ah." He looked thoughtful. "Yes, that's a very old text. But I'm not sure it's what you're looking for."

"Why not?" I asked, a little defensively. "It's exactly the kind of thing I was hoping to find."

"Perhaps," he said. "But I think you may be missing the forest for the trees. This text is a curiosity, certainly, but it's not the heart of the matter. The real question is, what is the house of the spirits? And why would anyone want to enter it?"

I considered this. "I suppose it's a metaphor," I said. "For the realm of the supernatural. The place where spirits dwell."
