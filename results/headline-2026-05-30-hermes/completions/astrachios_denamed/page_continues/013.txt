 "When you have said these names, you will see a great multitude of spirits standing by you, whereupon say as follows: 'I adjure you, O spirits, by the most holy names of God, which I have pronounced, and by his might and power, that you do and accomplish all my commandments, and that you do not delay to perform my will and my demands, whereby I shall be pleased with you. And until you have done my will, depart not from me, and be obedient to my commands, as God has decreed and as I decree by the virtue of his holy names, by which I have power to bind you and destroy you, if you are disobedient to my will. I, therefore, command you to withdraw in peace, without noise or disturbance, and without causing harm to me or any creature, and in the future be always prepared to come whenever I shall call you.'"
