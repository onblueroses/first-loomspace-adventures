 "When this is said, the spirit will appear and will speak with a human voice."

A third and final invocation is given, which is to be recited to dismiss the spirit:

In the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, go in peace to the place prepared for you. Amen.

The instructions end with the note: "This [dismissal] should be spoken three times."

## Translations

### German

A German translation by Johann Christoph Adelung was published in 1788.

### English

The manuscript was translated into English by Michael Bailey and published in 2007 as part of his book Magic and Superstition in Europe: A Concise History from Antiquity to the Present.

## Analysis

In his 2014 book, The Cambridge History of Magic and Witchcraft in the West, David J. Collins describes the Munich Manual as "one of the most famous and influential examples" of medieval necromancy.
