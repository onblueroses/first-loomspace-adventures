 "Here is the symbol of the divinity, the great name of the living God, the seal of Solomon, and the key of the hidden sciences. Read this from the left to the right, and from the top to the bottom, and thou shalt see appear the name of the great Divinity."

The second half of the key is a series of conjurations for the angels of each day of the week. The angels are as follows:

Sunday: Raphael, Aaniel, Traniel, Sarael, Sormel, Tuaral, Garas, Garnas, Sabrael, Zaliel, Cabael, Dardiel, Madriel, Sochiel, Harphas, Boel, Garsochel, Gabael, Tuaras, Caras, Haphiel, Marbas, Sambasel, Sariel, Seraphiel, Paranuel, Zachariel, Zahariel, Sabiel, Saphiel, Sarsael, Saciel, Samiel, Samedel, Sadrael, Sarbasel, Sariel, Sabiel, Paras, Zachiel, Zachariel, Seraphiel, Anatiel, Oradel, Gabrael, Pariel, Raziel, Saphiel
