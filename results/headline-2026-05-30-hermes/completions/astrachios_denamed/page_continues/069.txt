 

The letter E is the first in the Name of the Lord, and signifies the unity and essence of God. And the letter H is the first in the Name of the Son, and signifies the two natures, human and divine. And the letter O is the first in the Name of the Holy Spirit, and signifies the three persons in one essence. And this is the name of the Lord: E H O, which is written in the Gospel.

The letters E, H, O are a reference to the letters in the Greek spelling of Jesus' name (Ιησους). The names of the angels that open the way are from the Ars Notoria, a medieval grimoire.
