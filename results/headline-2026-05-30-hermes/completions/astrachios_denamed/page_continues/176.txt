 "By these names, and in the name of the highest God, who has all power over the demons of darkness and hell, I conjure you, I charge and command you, to open this book and hearken to me, and to obey my will, and to come when I call you, and to appear in a comely form, and to do all that I command you, and to give me a true answer to all I ask of you, and to perform your duty truly unto me, as you are bound to do, and sworn to do, and as I command you to do, and as my servant, I command you to do, by the virtue of these holy names, and by the power of God, the ruler of all spirits. Amen."

The following page contains the license to depart, which the magician is to read after the spirit has answered his questions or performed the tasks given to it:

"Go in peace unto your places, and be ready to come when called, for I release you for a time. Therefore, depart in peace, and be ever ready to come when I call you, by the virtue of these names: Adonai, Elohim, Elohe, Zabahot, Elion, Escherehe
