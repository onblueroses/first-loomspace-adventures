 'By these names, and in the name of the Everlasting God, I conjure you, O Spirits, to appear visibly before me in a fair human shape, and I command you to fulfill my will in all things according to your office, and to do no harm to any one, or to any thing, so it be just and reasonable, and to be ready at my call, and to give me full power and virtue to command all spirits, and to compel them to come before me whensoever I shall call them, and to do my will in all things, according to the power that you have in your office. Fiat, Fiat, Fiat. Amen.'

On the following page is written, 'Conjuration of the King of the East.' The names of the spirits, however, are not given, but the conjuration is worded in a similar manner, and ends with the same 'Fiat, Fiat, Fiat. Amen.'

The next page is headed, 'Conjuration of the King of the West,' and the names of the spirits are given as follows:--

'Elitel, Elitab, Elitabon, Elitel, Eshegib, Sodiel, Sonches, Sodiel, Tetragram
