"If you wish to know anything that is happening anywhere in the world, do thus: take a virgin boy or girl, at least seven years old, and clothed in white, and new and clean; then you must write the 72 names on his forehead and heart, as it is said above, with the oil as before; then have the child look into a drinking glass filled with pure and clean water, and you will see everything you desire to know in the water. And the child will tell you everything he sees. Do this in the morning and at noon, and when the sun is setting."
