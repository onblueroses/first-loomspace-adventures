 "At this point, the spirits will have appeared. Ask for what you wish and it will be granted. When you wish to send the spirits away, hold the Pentacle of Solomon in your hand and say the following:

Aclon, Astrenios, Asep, Acla, Berdimoriel, Sipat, Aramonis, Ireharel, Ipeodoc, Anchanzel, Bloutel, Baracaza, Adopal, Elotin, Enagro, Aphragoseh, Sapoel, Gepulanel, Cariso, Ena, Epoci, Akrah, Ebpisha, Imathadel, Damiol, Dapa, Elapos, Izaphel, Paeh, Scildon, Genilum Sopro, Agla, Cephas.

The spirits will leave immediately. May peace be with you."
