 "The above names open the way, and by them you shall attain your goal. When you have reached the place, speak the following words and the door will open for you:

"Anepheraton, Sihorlab, Sihorlab, Sihorlab, Gepulanel, Gepulanel, Gepulanel, Enoz, Enoz, Enoz, Ephe, Ephe, Ephe, Saphoel, Sapho, Saphoel, Sapho, Saphoel, Sapho, Anepheraton, Anepheraton, Anepheraton."

What is the significance of these strange names? They are not the names of demons or angels. The author of the grimoire does not identify them. The only clue is the instruction that they open the way. This suggests that they are names of power, similar to the barbarous words of power that occur in many ancient Greek magical texts and defixiones (curse tablets).
