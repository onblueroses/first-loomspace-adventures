I am not familiar with this passage, but I can tell you that it is NOT from the \em{Sworn Book of Honorius}.  I've been working with that grimoire quite a bit lately, and I can say with certainty that passage does not occur in the text.

As for the names, they are likely names of spirits - though I can't say I recognize any of them.  The names of many such entities have been passed down through the ages, often warped and twisted (both in form and cultural context) until it becomes nearly impossible to trace their origins.  Even the names of well-known entities such as the Goetic demons have many different spellings and interpretations.
