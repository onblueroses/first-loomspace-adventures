 "Now you shall see the image of the world, as the angels see it."

I paused, looking at the name of the spirit I was supposed to summon. Scildon. That name was familiar. I turned back to the page with the list of the seventy-two spirits of the Shem ha-Mephorash and found it. Scildon was one of the seventy-two, and his description was as follows: "Scildon is a spirit who can show the image of the world as the angels see it, in a mirror, glass, or crystal sphere."

That was all the description said. The rest of the page was taken up by a long and complicated summoning ritual, which I would have to perform in order to call Scildon up and bind him to my service. I sighed, and set to work.

The ritual took several hours to complete, and involved a lot of chanting, drawing of diagrams, and burning of incense. Finally, I spoke the words of summoning, and waited to see if it had worked.
