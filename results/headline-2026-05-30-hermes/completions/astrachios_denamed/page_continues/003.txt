 "And this is the opening of the path of the spiritual world." The names called out are not those of the angels that guard the gates, but the words that will cause the gates to open. The final phrase, "Genilum Sopro," may be a corruption of the Latin "finis limen superior" (the end of the upper threshold). The names of the gates are given in the following order:
