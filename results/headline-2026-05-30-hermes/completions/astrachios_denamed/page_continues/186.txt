 "When the above is said, the spirits will come and ask what you want. You must reply with humility and directness, as they will not respond to trivial matters." It then describes the proper way to dismiss the spirits once the operation is complete.

### De Nigromancia

This section, "Concerning Necromancy", is a collection of operations for summoning spirits, some of which are also found in other manuscripts such as the Munich Manual of Demonic Magic. The operations often require animal sacrifice, and some of the spirits are described as being dead (such as Anima Mundi). The incantations in this section are given in a mixture of Latin, Spanish, and Italian.

### De Theurgia

This section, "Concerning Theurgy", contains a single operation called the "Almadel". The operation is lengthy and involves the participation of four "sons of the Almadel" named Achorat, Azerarat, Ahubrat, and Zobarath. The operation is said to grant the power to see the spirits and to converse with them on any subject.
