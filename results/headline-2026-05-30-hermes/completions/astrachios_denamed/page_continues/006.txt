 "By the power of these names, which are the key to the gates of the four cardinal points, and invoke the spirits that watch over them, I conjure you to open unto me." The names are a mixture of Hebrew, Greek, and possibly other languages.

## In popular culture

* The 2016 film The Conjuring 2 features the Enfield Poltergeist, a purported haunting in the London Borough of Enfield, England, in the late 1970s. In the film, the demonologist Ed Warren recites the incantation from the Goetic Circle to ward off the demonic spirit Valak. The incantation is altered in the film to feature only some of the names: "Arenay, Coreniom, Casmiel, Raguar, Tomal, Sipat, Amoni, Pusah, Ilem, Seras, Azia, Azazel, Samael, Azza, Agares, Soler, Pesael, Nubus, Emerus, Sipat, Arnoias, Amoni."
