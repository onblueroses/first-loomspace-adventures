### The Temple of Dreams

The player must navigate a maze of small, connected rooms. Each room contains a group of words that must be clicked in the correct order to form a coherent English sentence. The sentence describes a surreal dream, with a different dream in each room. The player must navigate the maze of rooms by clicking on the correct words to open each door. The dreams in the rooms are not directly relevant to the game's plot, but they contribute to the game's surreal and unsettling atmosphere.
