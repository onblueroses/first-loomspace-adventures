 "By the power of these names, and with the help of God, who created the heavens and earth, I conjure you, O Angels, to come and speak with me." The magician then conjures the angels by reciting the invocation. The text also mentions a protective circle, which is meant to protect the magician from any potential harm or interference from the spirits.

## In popular culture

The Sworn Book of Honorius is referenced in Joseph Heller's novel Catch-22. Corporal Whitcomb is reading the book when Sergeant Knight asks him about it. Whitcomb replies that it is a magic book and explains some of its contents. Knight is intrigued and asks to borrow the book, but Whitcomb refuses, saying he has not finished reading it yet.
