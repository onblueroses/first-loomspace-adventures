 "The following names must be written down, but not spoken, on parchment: Me, Egum, Ebel, Telz, Gista, El, Sot. Seal this parchment within the Circle, then say aloud: 'Be ye powerful against all spirits, and let them be obedient unto these sacred names, and the wearer of this parchment.'"

---

The angel who guards the gates of the East is Samael. He is the angel of Mars, the overseer of wars and the defender of the righteous. He is the fifth angel and he wields the sword of divine justice. He is a mighty angel and the protector of the holy land. He is the patron of soldiers and the guardian of the divine law. He is the keeper of the secrets of the East and the protector of the mysteries of the divine. 

The angel who guards the gates of the West is Gabriel. He is the angel of the Moon, the overseer of the waters and the guardian of the mysteries of the deep. He is the patron of sailors and the protector of those who travel by sea. He is the keeper of the secrets of the West and the guardian of the mysteries of the divine.
