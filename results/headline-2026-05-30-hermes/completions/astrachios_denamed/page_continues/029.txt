 "And when you have said this, speak the name of the angel that governs the hour of the day or night wherein you do this, and say: 'Oh thou angel (insert name), that rulest this hour, I conjure thee, in the name of the Creator, and by the virtue of these holy names, that thou will deign to open unto me, and show me the way into this secret place, and let me see and speak to the spirit that I desire, by the virtue of the Creator, and by the virtue of all that is contained in these sacred names, by which all things were made.'"

The remaining pages contain the names of the spirits of the hours, with their seals and conjurations, the method of calling forth spirits to bring news from any part of the world, and of invoking the spirits governing the planets.

The manuscript abounds with curious diagrams, seals, pentacles, and other magical characters.
