 "These are the names that open the way: Asthrenios, Asep, Acla, Berdimoriel, Sipat, Aramonis, Ireharel, Ipeodoc, Anchanzel, Bloutel, Baracaza, Adopal, Elotin, Enagro, Aphragoseh, Sapoel, Gepulanel, Cariso, Ena, Epoci, Akrah, Ebpisha, Imathadel, Damiol, Dapa, Elapos, Izaphel, Paeh, Scildon; Genilum Sopro."

I close the book and pocket it, then approach the door. It stands shut, with no visible handle or lock. I reach out and touch the door, and it feels cold and hard beneath my fingers. I take a deep breath, then speak the names aloud, my voice steady and clear:
