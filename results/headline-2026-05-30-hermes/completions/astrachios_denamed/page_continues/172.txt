 "When you have done this, a spirit will appear. Speak the words: 'I am the servant of the Lord, before whom every knee in heaven and on earth bends. What is your name?' He will give some barbarous name, not always the same, and stretching out his hand will demand the paper. Have no fear, but give it to him at once. He will return it to you, and you must burn it in the first fire you see. Then dismiss him with these words: 'Go in peace to your place, and let there be peace between us, in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Amen.' After this, the spirit will serve you faithfully in whatever you command him. I have experienced this, and so have all the others who have used the art. Farewell, and may the blessing of the Lord be upon you. Amen."

The book's first edition was published in 1499. This is a later edition, from 1580.
