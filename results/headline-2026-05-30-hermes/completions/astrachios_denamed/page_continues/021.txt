 'I conjure you, spirits of the earth and air, fire and water, that you will prove visible before this circle in pleasing form, not in terrible shape, and that you be obedient to my command.'

The same formula is used for conjuring the spirits of the four elements, with the names of the elemental powers inserted in the appropriate places. The formula for the spirits of the earth is:

Aglon, Tetragram, Vaycheon, Stimulamathon, Erohares, Oren, Gierem, Syndon, Eorica, Sepentaton, Aclon, Araglentoma, Phagor, Lephagora, Maragama, Nephonos, Ilema, Maratan, Seraphon, Caratium, Plenes, Nephilim, Alon, Tetragrammaton.

The formula for the spirits of fire is:

Sphagiel, Tetragrammaton, Asphagell, Asophiel, Bina, Tetragrammaton, Agla, Tetragrammaton, Ablan, Athanatos, Pamor, Agla, On, Tetragrammaton, Eclon, Tetragram
