 "I conjure you by the great living God..." followed by a list of names of God and Christ, and a further list of spirits' names, ending: "and by the names Adonay, El, Elohim, Elohe, Ehyeh-asher-Ehyeh, Zebaoth, Elion, Sabaot, Agla, Tetragrammaton, that you obey my command every time I call you."
