 "And the names of their seals are these:" followed by a list of twenty-two magical seals and their names. The seals are shown as circles, each containing a figure or some geometrical shapes.

The page ends with: "These are the names of the 22 angels and their seals, which if they are made in virgin wax and put on whatever part of the body you wish, will make you invisible. But you must be careful to carry the Pentacle of Solomon with you, otherwise it will be dangerous."
