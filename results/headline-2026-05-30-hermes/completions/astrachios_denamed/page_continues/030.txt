 “When you have said these words, you will see a bright light, and in this light, a multitude of armed soldiers. Do not be frightened, because they are here to serve you. Say to them: ‘I conjure you, O Ministering Spirits, in the name of Anay, and in the name of all your Masters, that you take me to the King’s Court, and that you do not harm me in any way, neither leaving nor returning. I command you to obey me, and to faithfully perform all that I shall command, by the virtue of the words which I have spoken.’”

The manuscript goes on to describe how the spirits will carry you to the King’s Court, where you will meet the King of the East, King Boreas. You will be presented with a ring, and will be able to command an army of spirits.

## The Manuscript

The manuscript is 14 folios, measuring 19.5 x 14.5 cm. The text is written in English, in a clear italic hand. There are no illustrations or diagrams.
