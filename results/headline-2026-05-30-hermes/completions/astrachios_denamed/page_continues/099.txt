## The Ars Notoria

The Ars Notoria is a medieval grimoire, or book of magic, that teaches the reader how to rapidly acquire knowledge and learn magic through a series of prayers and visualizations. The Ars Notoria promises practitioners the mastery of liberal arts, including geometry, arithmetic, and philosophy, through these prayers and visualizations. This type of magic was thought to be demonic in origin and condemned by the Church.
