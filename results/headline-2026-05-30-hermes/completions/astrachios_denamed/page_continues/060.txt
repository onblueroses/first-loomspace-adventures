 “The most useful talisman of all is that which is made of the precious stone called selenite, whose colour is like the rays of the Moon. It should be carved into the shape of a crescent moon, and upon this image you should engrave the head of a beautiful girl, and on the back of the talisman you should engrave the name Genilum Sopro. The talisman should be made and completed when the Moon is in Cancer or Leo, and received from the hands of a beautiful girl who is a virgin. When the talisman has been completed, it should be fumigated with the smoke of myrrh and saffron, and then placed in a bag made of silk. When you wish to use it, burn myrrh and saffron, and hold the talisman up so that the smoke from the incense bathes it. Then take it out of the silk bag and suspend it from your neck, and you will see marvels. This talisman ought to be kept hidden and used secretly.”
