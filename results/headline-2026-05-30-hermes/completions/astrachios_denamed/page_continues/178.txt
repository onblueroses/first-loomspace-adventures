 "In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, I conjure you, spirits above named, to do to appear before me in a beautiful human form, and to accomplish my desires, without any noise or frightful disturbances, without injury to myself or those who accompany me."

The book's purpose is to make a pact with the Devil. The magician is required to be a Christian, and the ritual must take place on a Thursday in the month of April or May. The magician must also choose a location that is completely isolated and far from any place of worship.

The Grand Grimoire is divided into two parts—the first part, titled "The Book of the Black Serpent," focuses on the magician's pact with the Devil and the summoning of the spirit Astaroth. The second part, titled "The Book of the Red Serpent," focuses on the pact with the spirit Beelzebub and the summoning of other spirits.

The text also contains a series of talismans and pentacles that are used in the rituals. The most famous of these is the "Black Hen's Egg," which is said to have the power to open all locks and reveal all secrets.
