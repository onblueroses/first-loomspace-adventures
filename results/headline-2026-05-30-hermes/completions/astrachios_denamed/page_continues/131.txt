 "It is necessary to speak these names in the morning at the rising of the sun, then make the sign of the cross and say the following prayer."

In the prayer, the magician addresses the sun as "O most beautiful and resplendent Sun, splendor and divinity of all celestial things! O Sun, which rises at dawn and sets at dusk, which by its light and its splendor illuminates the entire world and by its virtue engenders all terrestrial things..." The magician then asks that the sun grant the power to perform the following operations:

1. To obtain all things which are asked for
2. To make all people do the magician's will
3. To make all people do the magician's will without causing them any harm
4. To make all people think the magician is a saint and a good person
5. To make young women and girls love the magician
6. To make people believe that the magician is invisible
7. To make people believe that the magician has taken on the shape of a bird or beast
8. To make people believe that the magician has taken on any shape whatsoever
9. To make the magician's words believed concerning any topic whatsoever
10. To make people believe that the magician can speak any language
