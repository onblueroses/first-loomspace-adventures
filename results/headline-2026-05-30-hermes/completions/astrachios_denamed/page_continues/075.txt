 "This is the holy name of God, who is worthy of praise, Agla. The Earth is the Lord's and all that dwell therein, and he will open the way before you to accomplish your desires."

The manuscript was translated from Hebrew into English by Moses Gaster in 1896, and the English translation was published in the Journal of the Royal Asiatic Society in 1896.

## In popular culture

In the 2019 film The Vigil, the dybbuk that haunts the recently deceased is said to be the same as the one in the book The Sword of Moses, which is mentioned by name.
