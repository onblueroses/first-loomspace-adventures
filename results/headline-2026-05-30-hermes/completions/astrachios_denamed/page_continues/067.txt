## Analysis

The manuscript was written by an unknown scribe, who may have been a doctor or lawyer, as he seems to have been familiar with both fields. The author clearly believed in the reality and efficacy of the spells he was recording, and the text seems to be intended for practical use rather than being a work of fiction or fantasy.

The spells and rituals in the manuscript are similar to those found in other grimoires of the period, such as the Key of Solomon and the Lesser Key of Solomon. The author may have drawn on these or similar works in composing his own text.

The manuscript is written in a mixture of Middle English and Latin, with some words and phrases in Greek and Hebrew. The handwriting is generally clear and legible, although there are some corrections and marginal notes.
