 "When you have spoken these words, you will see the heavens open and some ministering spirits will look out of the opening, with whom you may have dealings."

The names on this page have several origins, including:

* The four cardinal directions (Acla, Anchanzel, Akrah, Izaphel)
* The four elements (Aramonis, Ena, Paeh, Dapa)
* Planets and mythological figures (Asep, Sipat, Adopal, Elapos, Epoci, Imathadel, Enagro, Ebpisha, Elotin, Damiol)
* Unknown origins (Asthrenios, Berdimoriel, Ireharel, Ipeodoc, Bloutel, Baracaza, Aphragoseh, Sapoel, Gepulanel, Cariso, Scildon)
