 "I, N, am a servant of said spirits, who obey me in all things, who are under me and are sworn to appear to me and serve me whenever I call upon them, in the name of God and the heavenly spirits. Therefore, come now and serve me, in the name of Adonai Elohim Tzabaoth."

The Grimoire of Pope Honorius then provides instructions for summoning the demons, which include reciting the above prayer followed by a series of conjurations.

## Influence

The Grimoire of Pope Honorius has been influential in the development of modern ceremonial magic. It has been referenced and adapted by various magicians and authors, such as Aleister Crowley and A. E. Waite.
