 

**Eligos:** What is it that you seek? Speak plainly and speak true.

The petitioner speaks plainly and truly of their purpose.

**Eligos:** I will aid you, mortal, but the way is perilous. Take heed, for the price of failure is steep. Do we have an accord?

The petitioner agrees to the terms.

**Eligos:** Then let us begin. First, you must obtain for me a token of your commitment. Bring to me a personal item, something imbued with your essence and your power. With this, I will forge a key to unlock the path ahead. 

The petitioner presents the requested item.

**Eligos:** Good. Now, heed my words. The path to power lies in shadows and secrets. Seek out the hidden places, the forgotten knowledge. There you will find the tools you need.

But be warned - the guardians of these secrets are formidable. You must prove your worth and your cunning. Only then will the way open before you.

The petitioner nods in understanding.

**Eligos:** I will be watching, mortal. Do not disappoint me. Now go, and let your quest begin. We will meet again when you have earned the right.

The petitioner takes their leave, the heavy door closing behind them
