 I know him by his voice, and my blood runs cold. "I am a messenger of the Dark Lord. He commands you to open the gate and let me enter."

It is none other than Haelzorod, the Dread Sorcerer, the Necromancer, the bane of the living and master of the dead. His voice is like the rustle of dead leaves in a crypt, and carries the chill of the grave. I know he is a man to be feared, for he is second only to the Dark Lord in the evil hierarchy, and even the Dark Lord treats him with respect.

"I have a token for you that will convince you of the truth of my words." Something small and dark flies up from below and passes through the bars of the gate to land in my hand. It is a black scale, hard and cold. I know without being told that it is one of the scales of the Dark Lord himself. Only he could have given it to the sorcerer.

"I come alone," Haelzorod says. "I have need of your master's counsel and aid."
