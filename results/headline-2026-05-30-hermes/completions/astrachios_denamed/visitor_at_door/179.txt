 He is a man of middle age, bearded, his hair touched with gray, his eyes deep and knowing. He wears a black robe, and in his hand he holds a staff of ebony and silver.

"I have come to share my knowledge with you, Adept. I have watched your progress for many years, and I deem you worthy of my instruction. If you wish to learn, say only my name."

The Adept, sensing the power of the visitor, speaks the name: "Eoken."

"Then let us begin," Eoken says, and steps into the sanctum.

The Adept's vision blurs and shifts. He finds himself standing in a cavern of ice deep beneath the surface of the frozen earth. Stalactites and stalagmites of clear ice surround him, and a chill wind moans through the cave.
