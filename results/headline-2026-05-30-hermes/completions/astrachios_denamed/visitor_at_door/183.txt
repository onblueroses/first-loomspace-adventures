 

I am Genilum Sopro, and I come with a message for the master of this house. Your presence is required at the court of the King of the South, for there is a matter of great importance that concerns you. You must make haste, for time is of the essence. The king awaits your arrival with great anticipation.

The door opens, and Genilum Sopro enters the house. The master of the house, a man named Enoch, looks at the visitor with a mixture of curiosity and apprehension. He has never been summoned to the court of the King of the South before, and he wonders what matter could be so important as to require his presence.

Enoch speaks. 

I am Enoch, master of this house. I thank you for delivering this message, Genilum Sopro. May I ask what matter concerns me at the court of the King of the South?

Genilum Sopro replies.
