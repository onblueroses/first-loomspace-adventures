A: Welcome, my friend. I have been expecting you.

A: I sense that you are troubled. What brings you to my sanctuary?

B: I am in search of the secret of eternal life. I have heard whispers that you possess knowledge of this most precious gift.

A: Eternal life is a heavy burden to bear, my friend. Are you certain that you are prepared for such a responsibility?
