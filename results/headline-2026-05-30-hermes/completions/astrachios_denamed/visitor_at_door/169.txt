 The door opens. With the door open, the visitor speaks the names that open the way again.

Asthrenios, Asep, Acla Berdimoriel, Sipat, Aramonis, Ireharel, Ipeodoc, Anchanzel, Bloutel, Baracaza, Adopal, Elotin, Enagro, Aphragoseh, Sapoel, Gepulanel, Cariso, Ena, Epoci, Akrah, Ebpisha, Imathadel, Damiol, Dapa, Elapos, Izaphel, Paeh, Scildon; Genilum Sopro.

The visitor steps inside, and the door closes behind them. In the dimly lit room, the visitor sees the three thrones and the figures seated upon them. The visitor speaks.

I come seeking the wisdom of the Ages, the secrets hidden from the world. I come to learn from the Masters of the Art, that I may grow in knowledge and power.

The figures remain silent for a moment, then the central figure speaks.
