 He is a man in a black cloak, his head covered with a hood. He does not show his face, but his voice is clear and strong.

"I am the one you call the Black Hooded Man. I have come to deliver a message and a warning. The message is this: the path you have chosen leads to great power, but also to great danger. The forces you seek to command are not to be trifled with. They will test you, challenge you, and if you are not prepared, they will consume you."

He pauses for a moment, letting his words sink in.

"The warning is this: there are those who would stop you from achieving your goals. They fear the power you seek, and they will do whatever it takes to keep it from you. Be on your guard, for they are clever and ruthless."

He reaches into his cloak and pulls out a small, black book.

"This is the Book of Shadows. It contains the knowledge you seek, but it is not for the faint of heart. Use it wisely, and it will guide you to power beyond your wildest dreams. Use it foolishly, and it will lead you to your doom."

He hands you the book, and as he does, his hand brushes against yours.
