 I am a servant of the eleven and a keeper of the flame. I have come seeking warmth and companionship in this cold and lonely place. I humbly ask for entry, for I am a brother of the desert.

The doorkeeper replies. Then enter and be welcome, brother of the desert.

The visitor enters and all greet him with warmth and brotherhood. The doorkeeper then closes the door and resumes his place in the circle.

All raise their hands to their hearts and say: So it is and so shall it be, each time one of our brothers shall find us in the wilderness.

All sit.

The master of the circle speaks. Brothers, once again we come together to rejoice in our brotherhood and to share our joys and sorrows, to renew the bonds that join us together, to find strength and consolation in the company of our brethren. Once more we meet in this place to celebrate the mysteries of our craft, to work the secret works of our art, and to perfect the skills that are our heritage.

Tonight, let us turn our thoughts inward, and contemplate the mysteries that lie at the heart of our brotherhood. Let us consider the purpose of our art, and the uses to which we put our skills.

The master of the circle
