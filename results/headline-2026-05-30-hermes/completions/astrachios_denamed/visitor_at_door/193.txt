 The door opens and the visitor enters the chamber.

The room is dark, lit only by a single candle in the center. The walls are lined with shelves holding jars of various sizes and shapes, each containing a different substance. In the center of the room stands a table, upon which rests an open book and a quill pen.

The visitor approaches the table and begins to read the book. The words are in a language the visitor does not understand, but as they continue to read, the meaning becomes clear. The book contains instructions for a ritual that will grant the reader the power to control the elements.

The visitor looks up from the book and sees that the shelves around them are now empty. The jars have been replaced by four figures, each representing one of the elements: a being of fire, a creature of water, a spirit of air, and an entity of earth.

The visitor speaks the final words of the ritual, and the figures come to life. They bow before the visitor, acknowledging them as their master. The visitor now has the power to control the elements, but at what cost? The ritual has also bound them to the will of the figures, and they must now serve the elements as much as the elements serve them.
