The doorkeeper speaks. By the names of the sacred gates, and the power of the divine, I open the way. Enter, (your name), and be welcome.

The doorkeeper removes the bar from the door and opens it. The visitor enters, and the door is closed and barred again.

The visitor speaks. I stand within the sacred precinct, and I call upon the guardian of this place. Come to me, O guardian, and let us speak together.

The guardian appears in the form of a shining winged being. I am the guardian of the sacred temple of wisdom. Speak, and I will hear.

The visitor speaks. I seek the wisdom that lies hidden within this temple. Will you guide me to it?

The guardian speaks. Wisdom is not given, but earned. If you would earn it, you must pass the threefold test. Are you willing to face that test?

The visitor speaks. I am willing to face the test.
