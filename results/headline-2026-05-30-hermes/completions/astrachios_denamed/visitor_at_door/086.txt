 “I am a servant of the Most High, the One who is called IAO, the Lord of the Hidden Light. I am come here to greet you in peace and in the bonds of the Hidden Light. I ask that you grant me the help that I require, and in return I shall praise your names among the names of the saints and the holy ones.”

The spirits of the second heaven reply. “If you come in the name of the Lord of the Hidden Light, you are thrice welcome here. Enter, therefore, and let the Hidden Light shine about you.”

The door opens and the visitor enters the second heaven. The spirits of that heaven come to him and welcome him, saying, “What is your need, that we may fulfill it?”

The visitor answers, “I have need of a spirit of the second heaven to send to the aid of a human being who seeks knowledge and power in the Hidden Light.”

The spirits of the second heaven reply, saying, “This is the realm of the sphere of the stars, and the Sun who rules them. In this heaven the spirits of time and space have their place, and the cycles of destiny are governed. If your need relates to these, we can assist you. If not, we cannot.”
