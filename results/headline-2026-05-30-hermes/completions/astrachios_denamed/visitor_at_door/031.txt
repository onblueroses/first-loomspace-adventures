 Say the following words:

I have spoken the names that open the way. I have come to be initiated into the mysteries of the occult. I am ready to receive the wisdom of the ages.

The hosts answer:

Welcome, seeker of the mysteries. You have spoken the names that open the way. Enter now and be initiated into the sacred rites of the occult. May the wisdom of the ages be revealed to you.

The door opens, and the visitor enters the temple. The initiation begins.
