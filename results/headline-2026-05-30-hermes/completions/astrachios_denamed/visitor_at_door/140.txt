 I cannot make out the words, but the voice is familiar. The door opens. The visitor enters. It is one of the women who came to my house, one of the women I saw in the forest.

She is smiling. She is holding a knife.

The door closes. The ritual begins.

She speaks the names that open the way:

Asthrenios, Asep, Acla Berdimoriel, Sipat, Aramonis, Ireharel, Ipeodoc, Anchanzel, Bloutel, Baracaza, Adopal, Elotin, Enagro, Aphragoseh, Sapoel, Gepulanel, Cariso, Ena, Epoci, Akrah, Ebpisha, Imathadel, Damiol, Dapa, Elapos, Izaphel, Paeh, Scildon; Genilum Sopro.
