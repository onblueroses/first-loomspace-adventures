## The Temple

You stand within the temple. It is dark, and yet filled with a strange light that seems to come from nowhere and everywhere at once. The air is thick with incense and the scent of ancient stone. Before you stands a great stone altar, upon which rests a book bound in black leather, its pages worn and yellowed with age.

Approach the altar. Take the book in your hands. Open it to the first page. Begin to read.

The words are strange and unfamiliar, written in a language you do not know, and yet, as you read, understanding begins to dawn. The book is a grimoire, a book of magic, containing the secrets of the ancient world.

As you read, you feel a strange sensation, as though the words are not merely being read, but are being spoken aloud, resonating within your mind. You realize that you are not alone in the temple. There are others here, unseen, watching and listening.

Continue to read. Let the words flow through you. Feel their power.

The grimoire contains the secrets of the universe, the knowledge of the ancients, the power to shape reality
