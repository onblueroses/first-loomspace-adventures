 State your name and business.

\strong{I am the demon Ipos, called also Aiperos. I come bearing a gift for the king of this place.}

The guard replies. What gift do you bring?

\strong{I bring a ring that grants the power to understand the speech of birds.}

The guard replies. By what symbol shall I know you?

\strong{By the symbol of the jackal.}

The guard steps aside, and allows you to pass. Welcome, Ipos.

\strong{Thank you.}
