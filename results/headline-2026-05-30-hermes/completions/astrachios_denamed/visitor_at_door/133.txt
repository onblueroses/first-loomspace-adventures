This ritual is a simple but powerful means of opening a way to the Nightside. The names called upon are those of guardians who hold the keys to the gates. They also have the power to block and bar the way to those who are unworthy. If the names are spoken with authority and the ritual is performed with focus and intent, the gates will open.

When the gates are opened, the practitioner may proceed to the next ritual or exploration of the Nightside. If the gates are barred, the practitioner should take time to reflect on their path and their worthiness before attempting to open the gates again.

The ritual may be repeated as needed, but should not be overused. The guardians at the gates may become weary of those who constantly seek entry without good reason.
