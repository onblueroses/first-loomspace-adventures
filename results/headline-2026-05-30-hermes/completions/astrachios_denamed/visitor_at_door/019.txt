 They wish to enter. Do you permit it? Speak your answer clearly: yes or no.

(The magician speaks their answer clearly: yes or no.)

By the authority granted me by the Divine, I open this door to the one who comes in peace and with good intention.

(The door is opened. The spirit enters, in whatever form it wishes. The magician welcomes it, and listens to what it has to say. When the spirit has finished its message, the magician speaks the following to close the ritual.)

Spirits of the four directions, thank you for your presence and protection. You may depart, with the blessings of the Divine. Go in peace.

Spirits of the seven planets, thank you for lending your power to this ritual. You may depart, with the blessings of the Divine. Go in peace.

Holy Guardian Angel, thank you for your guidance and support. You may depart, with the blessings of the Divine. Go in peace.

And you, spirit who has visited us this night, thank you for your presence and your message. You may depart, with the blessings of the Divine. Go in peace.

The ritual is ended. Let the circle be broken, and let me carry the light of the Divine with me as I return to the world
