## 3. The Chamber of Trials

This chamber is dimly lit by flickering torches set in sconces on the walls. The chamber is rectangular, about 30 feet long and 20 feet wide. The floor is made of smooth stone, and the walls are adorned with intricate carvings and frescoes depicting scenes of ancient battles and mythical creatures.

In the center of the chamber, there is a large stone altar, about 4 feet high and 6 feet long. On the altar rests an ancient tome, bound in leather and adorned with silver filigree. The tome is open, and its pages are filled with strange symbols and diagrams.

As you approach the altar, you notice that the air in the chamber grows colder, and you feel a sense of unease creeping over you.
