 I recognize the voice. It is my son.

My son has grown up to be a man. He is strong and tall. He wears armor. He has come to rescue me.

I say, "My son, you must not enter. The demon will kill you. I am old and my life is almost over. Go away from here."

He says, "No, I will not go away. I will rescue you. You are my father."

I say, "You cannot rescue me. The demon is too powerful. If you try to rescue me, the demon will kill you. I could not bear to see that. Go away from here."

He says, "No, I will not go away. I am not afraid of the demon. I will rescue you."

I say, "You are a brave man. I am proud of you. But you must not throw away your life. Go away from here."

He says, "I will not go away. I will rescue you."

I say, "Very well. If you will not go away, then I will come out to you. But I am afraid. I do not want the demon to kill you."

He says, "Do not be afraid. I will protect you."
