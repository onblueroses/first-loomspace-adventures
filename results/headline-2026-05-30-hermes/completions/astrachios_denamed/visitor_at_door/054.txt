 “I have heard the names and I shall speak them, to open the way.”

The doorkeeper says: “Enter and be welcome, for you have spoken truly.”

The visitor enters the temple.

The doorkeeper says: “I am the doorkeeper of the temple of the heart. I welcome you in the name of the temple and all who gather here.”

The doorkeeper makes the sign of the shining sun, and the visitor responds in kind.

The doorkeeper says: “Before you is the way of the temple. It leads to the place of light, where the worship of the heart is celebrated. Are you prepared to walk that way?”

The visitor answers: “I am prepared.”

The doorkeeper says: “Then walk the way of the temple, and may the blessing of the Infinite be on you and in you.”

The doorkeeper and the visitor go together to the temple space.
