He walks to the center of the room, where a small table stands. On the table is a stack of parchment, a quill, and a bottle of ink. The Watcher picks up the parchment and looks at it. It is blank.

"I have seen the rise and fall of empires," he says. "I have witnessed the birth and death of stars. I have walked the paths of the dead and the living, and I have seen the threads that bind them."

He sets the parchment down and picks up the quill. He dips it in the ink and begins to write.

"The world is a tapestry," he says as he writes. "Each thread is a life, a story, a moment in time. Some threads are bright and strong, others are faded and frayed. But all are woven together, each one affecting the others."
