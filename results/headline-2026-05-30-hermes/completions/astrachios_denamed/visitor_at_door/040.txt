 The door is open.

The visitor enters the house. The door closes.

The visitor walks to the center of the house. The visitor looks around. The visitor speaks.

Visitor: I have entered the house of the spirits. I have come to seek wisdom and knowledge. I ask the spirits to guide me on my journey.

The spirits of the house appear. The spirits speak.

Spirits: Welcome, seeker of wisdom. We are the spirits of this house. We hold the knowledge of the ages. We will guide you on your path. But first, you must prove your worth. You must answer three questions. If you answer correctly, we will share our wisdom. If you answer incorrectly, you will leave this house with nothing. Do you accept this challenge?

Visitor: I accept the challenge. I am ready to answer your questions.

Spirits: Good. Here is the first question. What is the most powerful force in the universe?

Visitor: The most powerful force in the universe is love. Love can conquer all. Love can heal all wounds. Love can bring light to the darkest places.

Spirits: You have answered correctly. Love is indeed the most powerful force. Here is the second question. What is the key to true happiness?
