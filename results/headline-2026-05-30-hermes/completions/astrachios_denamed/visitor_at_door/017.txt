 The door opens. The visitor enters.

The door is locked and sealed. The chamber is made sacred and safe. The visitor speaks the opening words of the ritual:

I have come to the place of darkness, the place of power, the place of the sacred mysteries. I have come to the hidden chamber, the secret place, the temple of the gods. I have come to the place of the dead, the place of the living, the place of the eternal. I have come to the place of the beginning, the place of the end, the place of the cycle that never ends. I have come to the place of the sacred, the place of the profane, the place of the balance between the two. I have come to the place of the light, the place of the dark, the place of the shadow that lies between. I have come to the place of the known, the place of the unknown, the place of the mystery that lies beyond both. I have come to the place of the word, the place of the silence, the place of the voice that speaks in the silence. I have come to the place of the beginning, the place of the end, the place of the cycle that never ends.
