 They say the words of entrance:

Niat, Niat, Niat, Niat, Niat, Niat.

By the power of these words, the door opens. The visitor enters the chamber of the queen.

The queen speaks to the visitor. She says the words of welcome:

Welcome, traveler, to my hidden realm. Speak your name and your purpose.

The visitor speaks. They say their name and their purpose for seeking the queen.

The queen listens. She nods.

Your purpose is known to me. I grant you my aid.

The queen speaks words of power. She summons her servants.

Egin, Gena, Moston, Bubel, Gadriax, Ralith, Chislon, Ather, Chibaleh, Lachatel, Manties, Rapha, Belsal, Gelgel, Machata, Gela, Reloch, Xanthir, Gepaul, Seraphim.

The servants of the queen appear. They bow before her.

The queen speaks to her servants. She commands them:

Go forth, my servants. Fulfill the purpose of this traveler. Grant them the aid they seek.

The servants obey. They depart to fulfill the traveler's purpose.
