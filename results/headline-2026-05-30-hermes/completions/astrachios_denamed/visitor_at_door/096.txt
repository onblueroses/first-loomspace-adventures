 Say these words to open the way:

By the power of the sacred names and the spirits of the holy arch, I conjure and call upon you, O mighty guardian spirits of the gates, to open the way for me. I have the key and the authority to pass through the veil into the sacred realms beyond. Hear my voice and know that I am worthy to enter. I come in peace, seeking wisdom and understanding. Grant me safe passage and let no harm come to me as I walk the paths of power. So mote it be.

The guardian spirits open the gates and allow the visitor to pass through. The gates close behind them with a resounding clang. The visitor now stands within the sacred space, ready to explore the mysteries that lie ahead.

The ritual is complete. The gates have been opened. Let the work of transformation begin.

So mote it be. [^1]
