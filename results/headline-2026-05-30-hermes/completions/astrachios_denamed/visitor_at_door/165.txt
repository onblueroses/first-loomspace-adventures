## 9. Temple of the Earth

The visitor is in the Temple of the Earth. Before them is an altar. On the altar is a green stone. The visitor takes the green stone and holds it in their hand.

The visitor says:

I have come to learn the secrets of the Earth. Let the spirits of the Earth come forth and teach me their wisdom.

Four spirits appear in the temple, one at each corner. They are the spirits of the four elements: Earth, Air, Fire, and Water.

The spirit of Earth says:

I am the spirit of Earth. I am the foundation of all things. I am the source of life and the resting place of the dead. I am the keeper of secrets and the giver of strength. Learn from me the power of stability, the importance of grounding, and the wisdom of patience.
