 I hear a voice say:

“By the authority of the Seventy-Two Names of the Fallen, I demand entry. I demand the wisdom of those who have come before. I demand the secrets of those who have come before. I demand the power of those who have come before. Open the way!”

The door opens. I enter the temple. Inside, I see a room that is grand and ornate, with a high ceiling and many pillars. At the center of the room, there is a large stone altar. Upon the altar, there is a book. The book is open.

I approach the altar and look upon the book. The pages are filled with strange symbols and writings that I do not understand. But as I gaze upon them, a feeling of great power and knowledge washes over me.

A voice speaks to me, a voice that seems to come from everywhere and nowhere at once. It says:

“You have opened the way. You have entered the temple of the Fallen. Here, you may learn the secrets of those who have come before. Here, you may gain the power of those who have come before.
