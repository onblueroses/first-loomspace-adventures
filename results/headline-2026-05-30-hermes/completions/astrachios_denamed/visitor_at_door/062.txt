 The door is opened.

The door is opened. Enter.

## 15. The Conclusion of the Ritual
The ritual is concluded. The spirits depart.

Spirits, I thank you for your presence here today. I ask that you depart in peace, harming none, and that you return to your rightful places. Let there be peace between us now and always. Farewell.

The ritual is ended. (Strike the chime three times.)
