 The door opens.

* * * * *

The power of the names is invoked. The door is opened. The visitor stands at the threshold of the realm of the dead. Before the visitor is a pathway leading into the darkness.

The visitor speaks: “I call upon the power of the names to guide me and protect me as I journey into the realm of the dead.”

The visitor walks the path.

* * * * *

The visitor walks the path through the darkness. The only light is the faint glow that seems to emanate from the path itself. The visitor continues, feeling the presence of the names that have been invoked.

After a time, the visitor sees a faint light in the distance. The light grows brighter as the visitor approaches. The light is coming from a small fire, around which sit three figures.

The visitor approaches the fire and the three figures. The figures are clothed in dark robes, their faces hidden by deep hoods.

The visitor speaks: “I come seeking knowledge of the secrets of life and death.”

The figures remain silent.

The visitor speaks the names: “Asthrenios, Asep, Acla Berdimoriel, Sipat, Aramonis, Ireharel, Ipeodoc, Anchanzel
