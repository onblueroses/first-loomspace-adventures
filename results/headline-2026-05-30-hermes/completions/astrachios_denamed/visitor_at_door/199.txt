 They say, “I am a visitor seeking entry. I come in peace, with respect for you and your domain, to share knowledge and wisdom, and to learn from you. I ask permission to enter.”

The spirit responds, saying, “By the names and seals upon this gate, and by the authority of the Divine, I grant you entry. Enter, and let us speak of the mysteries and share in the knowledge of the Divine Light. You are welcome here, traveler. Enter, and be at peace within this realm.”

The door opens, and the traveler enters the realm of the spirit. There, they may commune with the spirit, seeking knowledge and understanding, sharing experiences, and learning from one another.

When the traveler wishes to depart, they simply express their gratitude to the spirit for their time and wisdom, and bid them farewell. The spirit responds in kind, and the traveler exits the door, returning to their place in the physical world.

The traveler seals the door once more, speaking the names:

Asthrenios, Asep, Acla Berdimoriel, Sipat, Aramonis, Ireharel, Ipeodoc, Anchanzel, Bloutel, Baracaza, Adopal, Elotin, En
