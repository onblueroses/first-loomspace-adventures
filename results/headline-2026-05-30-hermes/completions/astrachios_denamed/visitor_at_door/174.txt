 “I have come to your house as I have been guided to do so. I am of the living and I seek the knowledge of the dead. I ask that you allow me to enter, to speak with you, to learn from you, and to leave again in peace. I will not disturb your rest, nor will I bring harm to your sanctuary. I vow that I shall honor you and your wisdom, and I will bring offerings in your name. I ask that you open the door, and allow me to enter.”

The door opens.

The visitor steps inside and the door closes behind them. They are in a dark and silent space. The walls are lined with shelves, and on the shelves are countless jars and vessels, each containing a flickering light. These are the spirits of the dead, the whispers of the past.

The visitor approaches one of the shelves and selects a jar. They hold it in their hands and feel the warmth of the light within. They speak the name of the spirit they wish to commune with, and the light grows brighter.

The spirit speaks, its voice echoing in the visitor’s mind. The visitor listens, and they ask questions. The spirit answers, sharing its knowledge and its memories.
