 "I am a human being. I am mortal, and I seek the presence of the immortal spirits of the dead. I come with respect, and without fear. I am pure of heart and I seek to commune with the spirits of the dead in love and understanding. I bring no malice or evil intent, but only my sincere longing for truth. Grant me this gift, O spirits of the dead, and I pledge to use it wisely and well, for the benefit of all."

The spirits of the dead gather around the visitor. They examine the visitor's heart and mind, seeking any trace of deception or ill intent. Finding none, they nod in approval and step aside, allowing the visitor to pass through the door and into the realm of the dead.

As the visitor steps through the doorway, the scene changes. The visitor now stands in a vast, misty plain. The sky is a dull, leaden gray, and the air is thick with a chill that seeps into the visitor's bones. In the distance, the visitor can see the faint outlines of figures moving through the mist.
