The Key of Solomon, Book II, Chapter 3

### Invocation of the Holy Guardian Angel

O Holy Angel, my guardian, to whom the Most High has entrusted the care of my soul, body and spirit, I, a humble servant of the Almighty, do conjure, obtest and implore you to be present at this sacred art and operation, and to deign to bless and sanctify this place, to protect and defend me from all evil spirits and their illusions, and to inspire my heart with wisdom, knowledge, understanding, and knowledge of the hidden arts and sciences, that I may be enabled to perform this work with success. In the name of the Most High, the Almighty and Everlasting God, the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit. Amen.
