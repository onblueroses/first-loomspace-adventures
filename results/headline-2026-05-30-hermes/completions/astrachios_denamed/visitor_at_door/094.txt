 The door opens. The traveler enters. The door closes. The traveler walks to the center of the chamber, where the King sits upon his throne.

The traveler bows and says:

Greatest King of the East, I greet you with respect and honor. I come before you to ask a favor. I wish to learn the secret of your power and the wisdom of your rule. Will you share this with me?

The King replies:

Greetings, traveler. I will share with you the secret of my power, but first you must prove yourself worthy. Solve this riddle:

I am the beginning of eternity, the end of time and space. I am the beginning of every end, and the end of every place. What am I?

The traveler thinks for a moment, then answers:

The letter E.

The King nods and says:

You have answered correctly. Now I will share with you the secret of my power. It lies in the balance of the elements - Air, Fire, Water, and Earth. Each element has its own qualities and strengths. To master them is to master the world.
