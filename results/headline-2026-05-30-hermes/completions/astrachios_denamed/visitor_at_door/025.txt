B y the time the last of the servants came through the door, the temple was crowded, and the air was thick with incense and anticipation. As the door closed, the congregation began to chant a slow, deep drone, a single sustained note that filled the temple and seemed to reverberate in the bones of the faithful. The Grand Daimonarch, resplendent in black robes worked with symbols in silver, raised his hands and began the Invocation of the First Circle. His voice, clear and strong, cut through the chant, shaping the sound into words of power.
“In the beginning was the Abyss of Chaos, and the Abyss was without form and void, and the spirit of the Dragon moved upon the face of the Abyss. From the Abyss came the One, and the One brought forth the First Circle, the mighty spirits who were before the creation of the worlds. In the First Circle were the nine Intelligences of the Presence, the ten Angels of the Mysteries, and the twelve Ministers of the Elements, who served the One in the creation of the worlds and the ruling thereof.
