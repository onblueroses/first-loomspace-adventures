 He identifies himself as the spirit Sobrevent, whom I know well. The spirit and I greet each other, and then I invite him to come in. He enters and we sit down to talk. Sobrevent tells me that he has been sent to guide me to the place where I will receive the key to the Third Book of the Lemegeton, or the Ars Paulina.

Sobrevent says that the key lies hidden in the depths of the astral realms, in the city of the angels of the Ars Paulina. He tells me that to reach this place, I must first enter a trance and project my astral body. I am to follow the spirit to the astral city, and there I will be met by the angels who will give me the key.

Sobrevent instructs me to prepare myself for astral travel. I put on my ritual garb and enter my astral temple. There, I perform the usual banishing rituals and invoke the divine forces. I then sit in my astral throne and enter a deep trance. My astral body separates from my physical body, and I rise up out of my throne.
