  The door opens.  The visitor enters.

The door closes.

The visitor says, "I seek the wisdom of the great and powerful Genilum Sopro."

Genilum Sopro speaks.  "You have traveled far to reach this place.  What wisdom do you seek?"

The visitor responds.  "I seek to understand the nature of the universe and my place within it."

Genilum Sopro ponders for a moment.  "The universe is a vast and complex tapestry, woven from the threads of space, time, matter, and energy.  Each being plays a role, however small, in shaping this grand design.  To understand your place, you must first look within."

The visitor considers this.  "How does one look within to find their place?"

"Through introspection and self-reflection," Genilum Sopro replies.  "Examine your thoughts, emotions, and experiences.  Seek to understand your own nature - your strengths, weaknesses, desires, and fears.  In coming to know yourself, you will begin to see how you fit into the greater whole."

The visitor nods slowly.  "I think I understand.  Thank you for your wisdom, Genilum Sopro."
