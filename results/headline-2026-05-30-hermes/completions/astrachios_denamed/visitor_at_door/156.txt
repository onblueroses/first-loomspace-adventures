# 65
A man walks through a village. He calls out, "I am looking for the wisest man in the village. Where does he live?"

A villager points to a small house. "He lives there."

The man knocks on the door. An old man answers. "Yes?"

"I seek wisdom," the man says. "I was told you are the wisest man in the village."

The old man shakes his head. "I am not the wisest. The wisest man lives in the house at the end of the street."

The man goes to the house at the end of the street and knocks. A young man answers. "Yes?"

"I seek wisdom," the man says. "I was told you are the wisest man in the village."

The young man shakes his head. "I am not the wisest. The wisest man lives in the house across the street."

The man crosses the street and knocks on the door. A boy answers. "Yes?"

"I seek wisdom," the man says. "I was told you are the wisest man in the village."
