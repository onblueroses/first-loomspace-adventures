 His voice is deep and resonant, as though it originates in the earth itself.

I am the one who was sent to speak to you. Do you know why I have come?

I do not know why you have come. I have called no one.

The visitor speaks again.

I was sent by those who watch over the world, to offer you a choice, and to guide you to a decision.

What decision? I am old and no longer make decisions.

You are not as old as you believe yourself to be, nor as young as you were. You have lived many lives, and have died many deaths, yet you have not passed on to the place that is appointed for you.

I am content where I am. Why should I wish to pass on to any other place?

Because it is not your fate to remain here. You must either go forward to your destiny, or you must be reborn into the world once again and fulfill your purpose there. The choice is yours, but you cannot remain as you are.

I am weary. Why should I wish to be reborn?

To learn, and to love, and to enjoy the world as you once did. Do you remember nothing of your previous lives?

Little enough. Only shadows and dreams.
