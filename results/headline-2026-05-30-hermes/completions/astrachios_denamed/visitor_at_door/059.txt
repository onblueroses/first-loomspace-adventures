 “I come in peace, as your friend. I ask permission to enter your home.”

The door opens and the visitor enters. They must not touch the altar, nor the offering upon it. They must not look directly at the practitioner. They must sit where they are told and remain seated until given permission to stand.

The practitioner says: “I welcome you, my friend, to my home. As you respect the rules of my house, I shall respect your purpose in coming here. I give you permission to speak.”

The practitioner listens to the visitor’s purpose, and replies as they see fit. When the discussion is complete, the practitioner says: “Our business here is finished. I give you permission to stand and to leave my home in peace. May we meet again in brighter times.”

The visitor stands, and leaves without looking back. The practitioner closes the door behind them, and speaks the names that close the way:

Akhaz, Azib, Akir, Adonai, Elohim, Sabaoth, Amonshaddai, El, Yeheshua, Tetragrammaton, Anapheneton, Agla, Adon, Shaddai, Homosion, Agios, Otheos, Ischiros
