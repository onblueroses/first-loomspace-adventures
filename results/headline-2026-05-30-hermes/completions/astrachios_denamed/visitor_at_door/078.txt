## 42. Speaking the Words of Entry
The master speaks to the visitor:

By the authority of the names I have spoken, I open this way for you. Enter, and be welcome in this temple of the mysteries.

The visitor enters the temple. The master closes the door, and then returns to his place. [Pause.]

## 43. First Consecration of the Temple
The master takes the consecrated water and goes to the east. He sprinkles water three times on the altar, saying:

In the name of the source of all, and by the power of the names already spoken, I consecrate this altar in the east.

He goes to the south, and sprinkles water three times on the altar there, saying:

In the name of the source of all, and by the power of the names already spoken, I consecrate this altar in the south.

He goes to the west, and sprinkles water three times on the altar there, saying:

In the name of the source of all, and by the power of the names already spoken, I consecrate this altar in the west.
