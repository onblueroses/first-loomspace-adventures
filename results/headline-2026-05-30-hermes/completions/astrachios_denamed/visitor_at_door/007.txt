### 4. The Gathering of the Thirteenth Spirit

When the spirits of the dead have gathered in the temple, the thirteenth spirit will appear. This spirit is the most powerful and will guide the others in the ritual.

To summon the thirteenth spirit, the visitor at the door must speak these words:

I call upon you, O mighty spirit of the thirteenth realm,
Guide your brethren in this sacred rite,
Let the dead rise and the living tremble,
For the power of the thirteenth is unmatched.

Amen.

### 5. The Ritual of the Dead

Once the thirteenth spirit has appeared, the ritual can begin. The visitor at the door will lead the spirits in a chant, calling upon the powers of the underworld to bring forth the dead.

The chant is as follows:

Arise, O spirits of the dead,
From your graves, you shall be led,
By the power of the thirteenth realm,
Your souls shall be redeemed.

The spirits will then begin to chant, their voices echoing through the temple. As they chant, the ground will tremble, and the dead will begin to rise from their graves.
