 The visitor is permitted to enter.

This is the first key to opening the way.

#### 14
The second key.

The second key is a set of sigils.

Draw the sigils on the floor in black ink. Begin with the first sigil and draw each in turn, connecting them with a line. Connect the last sigil to the first to complete the circle.

Stand in the center of the circle and speak the names of the sigils in order:

Sibol, Mephiel, Amodiel, Rofod, Obagor, Anrael, Ubarin, Iblis.

The sigils activate. The second key is complete.

#### 15
The third key.

The third key is a ritual.

Light three candles and place them in a triangle. At each point of the triangle, place an offering. The first offering is a lock of hair. The second offering is a drop of blood. The third offering is a written secret, sealed with wax.

Stand in the center of the triangle and recite the incantation:
