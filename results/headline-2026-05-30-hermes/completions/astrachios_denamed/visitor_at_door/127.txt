 "I am the herald of the King of the North, the King of the South, the King of the East, and the King of the West. I bear the sign of the Rose and the Cross, the sign of the Keeper of the Threshold. Open to me, in the name of the true masters of the Temple!"

The door opens, and the visitor enters. The temple is dimly lit, with a black curtain hanging across the eastern end of the room. Before it is an altar draped in black, with a chalice of water, a paten of bread, and a rose in a vase. A sword and a dagger lie across the altar. The only other furnishing in the temple is a kneeling stool in the center of the room.

The visitor goes to the altar, bows, and then goes to the kneeling stool, faces east, and kneels. After a moment of silence, the ritual begins.

All: We gather in the presence of the Great Company of the Invisible, the Secret Masters of the Rose Cross.

All: Let the light of the Infinite Rose descend!
