 This is the voice of the one called Sopro:

I am the one who opens the way, and the one who closes the way. I am the one who grants the power to summon and bind. I am the one who takes away that power. I am the one who makes all things possible, and the one who makes all things impossible.

When I open the way, no power can close it. When I close the way, no power can open it. When I grant the power to summon and bind, no power can take it away. When I take away the power to summon and bind, no power can restore it.

I am the one who stands at the threshold between the worlds. I am the one who guards the gate between the living and the dead. I am the one who holds the key to the mysteries of the universe.

Speak my name, and I will open the way for you. Speak my name, and I will grant you the power to summon and bind. Speak my name, and I will make all things possible for you.

But beware: if you speak my name without the proper respect, if you speak my name without the proper authority, if you speak my name without the proper intention, I will close the way for you. I
