 

By the names that open the way, I ask for entry. 

The keeper at the door speaks. 

The way is open to the names that open the way. Enter.

The visitor at the door enters.

**The Closing**

When the ceremony is complete, the keeper at the door approaches the door again and speaks.

I thank the names that opened the way for their guidance and presence. With respect and gratitude, I release them. The way is closed. 

The keeper at the door walks away from the door. 

**Notes**

This ritual is intended to be used in conjunction with other rituals, as part of a larger ceremony. It can be adapted to your specific practices and needs, and you can modify the names and the wording as you see fit. This ritual is an example, and not a strict script. It's a starting point for your own practice.
