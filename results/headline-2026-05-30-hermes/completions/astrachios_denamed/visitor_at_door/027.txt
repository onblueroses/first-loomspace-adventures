 I do not recognize the voice, but the words are familiar: “I have come, as I have come before, to seek wisdom in the darkness.”

“Enter,” I say, “and let us speak of wisdom, and of darkness.”

The door opens, and a figure steps through, clad in a hooded robe of black. The hood is drawn forward, hiding the face. The figure is not tall, but stands proudly erect. Slim hands, the fingers weighted with rings, emerge from the wide sleeves to throw back the hood.

I look into the face of a woman, no longer young, but beautiful still, with the beauty that comes only from intelligence and character. Her skin is pale, her hair black with a streak of white, her eyes deep-set and luminous. I know her name, and her history, but I will not speak of them here. Suffice it to say that she is a witch, and has been one for many years.

“Welcome,” I say, and gesture to a chair. She seats herself, and I take my place opposite her, with the table between us. “What wisdom do you seek this night?”
