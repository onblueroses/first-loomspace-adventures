I come bearing the gift of knowledge, the gift of power, the gift of transformation. I come to offer you the key to the mysteries, the path to the hidden realms, the way to the heart of the world.

I come to teach you the language of the birds, the speech of the trees, the song of the stones. I come to show you the way to the garden of delights, where the fruits of wisdom grow, where the waters of life flow.

I come to guide you to the throne of the queen of the night, where the secrets of the moon are revealed, where the dreams of the world are woven. I come to lead you to the palace of the sun, where the fires of creation burn, where the songs of the stars are sung.
