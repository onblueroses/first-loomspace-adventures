 

I am a traveler, seeking passage to the realm of the otherworld. I ask for your aid, spirits of the crossroads, to guide me safely through the veil between worlds. Grant me entry, and I shall honor your names.

The guide speaks.

By the power of the names, and the covenant between us, I grant you passage. Step through the door, traveler, and enter the realm beyond. But know that you must return the way you came, before the sun rises again in the mortal world. If you linger too long, you will be trapped forever. Do you accept these terms?

The visitor nods and steps through the doorway, vanishing from sight. The guide closes the door behind them, sealing it once more. The ritual is complete.

The guide speaks softly.

May the spirits protect you on your journey, traveler. And may you find what you seek in the otherworld, before time runs out.
