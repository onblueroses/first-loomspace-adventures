 “The door is open, and the way is clear. I greet you.”

The one at the door replies. “I greet you. May our meeting be of benefit to all beings.”

The visitor enters, and the door is closed. The host then speaks the names that close the way:

Genilum Sopro; Scildon, Paeh, Izaphel, Elapos, Dapa, Damiol, Imathadel, Ebpisha, Akrah, Epoci, Ena, Cariso, Gepulanel, Sapoel, Aphragoseh, Enagro, Elotin, Adopal, Baracaza, Bloutel, Anchanzel, Ipeodoc, Ireharel, Aramonis, Sipat, Acla Berdimoriel, Asep, Asthrenios.

The one who opened the door then speaks. “The door is closed, and the way is sealed. May our meeting be of benefit to all beings.”

This done, the ritual of the Gateway is complete.

Commentary:

The names used in the opening and closing of the Gateway are those of the twenty-four elders and the four angels of the Quarters, as given in the Enochian magical system
