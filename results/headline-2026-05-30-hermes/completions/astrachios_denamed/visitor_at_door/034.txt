 The visitor says:

Sopro, I come with open heart and open mind. I have learned your ways and wish to walk your path. I ask to enter your temple and seek your wisdom. Will you have me?

The doorkeeper answers:

The Temple of Genilum Sopro is open to all who seek with pure intent. Enter, and may you find what you seek.

The door opens. The visitor enters the temple.

Inside, the temple is dimly lit by flickering candles. The air is heavy with the scent of incense. In the center of the room stands a large stone altar, upon which rests an ancient tome. The visitor approaches the altar.

The visitor speaks:

I have come seeking the wisdom of Genilum Sopro. I wish to learn the secrets of the universe and the mysteries of the arcane arts. Will you share your knowledge with me?

A voice echoes through the temple, ancient and powerful:

Greetings, seeker. I am Genilum Sopro, the Guardian of Hidden Knowledge. Your path has led you here, to this sacred place of learning.
