 If they are a spirit or traveler from another world, they are bound by the names and will answer truthfully. If they are human, they will be compelled to answer truthfully, but may resist the compulsion.

If the visitor is a spirit or traveler from another world, the ritual ends when the door is closed, releasing the binding. If the visitor is human, the ritual ends when the door is closed or the visitor leaves the threshold. The names hold no power beyond the threshold.
