 “I have no name to give you, only a sigil.” She holds up her hand, and there, tattooed in the palm, is a symbol, a twisting rune that seems to shimmer and move as you look at it. “I am the one you have been seeking.”

The guardian nods, and the door swings open. “Enter, then, and may the Fates be kind.”

As you step forward, the world falls away behind you. You find yourself in a realm of mist and shadow, where the only light comes from the sigil in the visitor’s hand. She leads you through the twisting paths of this place, and as you walk, you hear whispers in the fog, snatches of conversation, glimpses of other worlds.

Finally, you come to a clearing, and in the center of it, a great tree, its branches reaching up into the endless night above. The visitor turns to you and smiles. “Here we are,” she says. “The Tree of Tales. The place where all stories come to be born, and to die.”
