 

My name is Anchanzel, one of the sixteen Watchers of the Gates. I have come to open the way.

The gatekeeper responds.

By what authority do you command the gate?

The visitor speaks.

By the authority of the forty-two names of power, which I hold in my hand. I command you to open the way, in the name of the Holy One, blessed be He, and by the power of the names I have spoken.

The gatekeeper responds.

Your authority is recognized, Anchanzel. The way is open to you. Enter and ascend.

The visitor steps forward and passes through the gate. The ritual is complete.

This ritual is a work of fiction, inspired by the Lesser Key of Solomon and other grimoires, but not directly based on any specific historical text. The names and phrases used are invented for the purpose of this fictional ritual and hold no real occult power. However, the structure and style aim to evoke the feel of a classic grimoire ritual for opening a mystical gate or portal. The back-and-forth exchange between the visitor and gatekeeper, the use of arcane names and titles, and the formal, archaic language all contribute to the occult atmosphere.
