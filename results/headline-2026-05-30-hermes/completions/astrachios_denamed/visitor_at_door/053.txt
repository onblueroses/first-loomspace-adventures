 Say to them:

I have come by the path of knowledge to seek the wisdom of the spirits of the celestial sphere, that I may learn from them the secrets of the heavens. I ask for your guidance on this journey, and I offer you my respect and gratitude for your aid.

The spirits of the celestial sphere answer. Say:

We greet you, traveler on the path of knowledge. Your respect and your quest for wisdom are worthy of our aid. Yet we would ask of you this question: what will you do with the secrets of the heavens you hope to learn?

The visitor at the door answers. Say:

With the secrets I learn, I will seek to understand the mysteries of the universe and the hidden potentials within me. I will use this knowledge to guide my own journey of spiritual awakening, that I may attain to the highest and most harmonious state of being, in accordance with the great pattern of existence.

The spirits of the celestial sphere answer. Say:

Your aim is wise and worthy. Yet remember always that the secrets you learn are not to be used for selfish or destructive ends, but to guide yourself and others toward the light of truth and the harmony of the cosmos. If you will swear to this, we will open the way before you.
