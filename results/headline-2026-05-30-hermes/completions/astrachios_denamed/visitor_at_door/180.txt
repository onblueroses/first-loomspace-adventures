### The Chamber of the Three

The chamber is a perfect sphere, carved from the black stone of the mountain. The walls are etched with diagrams and symbols, and at the center of the room floats a sphere of darkness, the size of a human head.

The sphere is the heart of the Three, the source of their power and the repository of their knowledge. It is said that the sphere contains the essence of the universe, and that the Three can manipulate reality itself through its power.

The visitor approaches the sphere, and speaks the words of summoning:

I call upon the Three, the guardians of the mysteries, the keepers of the secrets. I seek your wisdom, your guidance, your power. I come to you as a seeker of truth, a student of the arcane, a humble servant of the mysteries. Grant me your presence, your counsel, your aid.

The sphere pulses with darkness, and three figures emerge from the shadows. They are tall and slender, with skin the color of midnight and eyes that glitter like stars. They wear robes of black silk, embroidered with silver runes, and their voices are like the whisper of the wind through the trees.
