 "I am Sipat. I am Aramonis. I am Ireharel. I am Ipeodoc. I am Anchanzel. I am Bloutel. I am Baracaza. I am Adopal. I am Elotin. I am Enagro. I am Aphragoseh. I am Sapoel. I am Gepulanel. I am Cariso. I am Ena. I am Epoci. I am Akrah. I am Ebpisha. I am Imathadel. I am Damiol. I am Dapa. I am Elapos. I am Izaphel. I am Paeh. I am Scildon. I am Genilum Sopro. Open the way."

The door opens. Inside is a temple of unimaginable beauty. The walls are lined with shelves of ancient tomes and scrolls. The air smells of sandalwood and frankincense. In the center of the room is a pedestal, upon which rests a book bound in leather and clasped in gold. The visitor approaches the pedestal and opens the book. The pages are filled with strange symbols and diagrams. The visitor begins to read, and as they do, the room begins to change
