### To Find a Familiar
A ritual to summon a spirit that will be your guide and servant in the arts of magic. This spirit will be bound to you until the day you die, and will be a constant companion and assistant. You will be able to communicate with this spirit telepathically, and it will be able to aid you in your magical workings, providing you with knowledge and power beyond your current abilities.

Materials needed:
- A quiet, dark room
- A circle of salt, large enough to sit in
- A black candle
- A mirror
- A knife

The ritual:
1. Wait until night. Turn off all the lights in the room you will be performing the ritual in. Light the black candle and place it in front of the mirror.
2. Draw a circle of salt on the ground, large enough for you to sit in.
3. Sit in the circle, facing the mirror. Take the knife and prick your finger, allowing a drop of blood to fall onto the mirror's surface.
