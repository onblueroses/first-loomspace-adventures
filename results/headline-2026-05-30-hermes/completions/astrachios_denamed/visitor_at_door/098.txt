 Their voice is surprisingly clear, cutting through the sound of the wind and the storm and the rain.

"I greet you, Master of the House. I am the Visitor, and I seek entry to your domain."

The master of the house, still cautious, replies.

"I greet you, Visitor. By what right do you claim entry to my home?"

The visitor's response is prompt and confident.

"By ancient compact and sacred law, I claim the right of hospitality. For I am the storm and the night, and those who dwell within must grant me succor."

The master of the house nods slowly. This is not the first time he has encountered such a request, and he knows the old ways.

"Very well, Visitor. I grant you entry, by the laws of hospitality and the ancient pacts between your kind and mine. Enter, and be welcome."

The visitor steps across the threshold, and as they do, the storm outside intensifies for a moment, the wind howling like a living thing. Then, as the door closes behind them, the storm fades away to nothing, and all is still.
