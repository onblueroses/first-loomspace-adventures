 If their name is written in the book of guests, the door opens and they may enter. If their name is not written, the door remains closed.

The visitor speaks their name.

If the door does not open, the visitor may speak the names again, then speak their own name once more. If the door still does not open, the visitor must depart, for their name is not written in the book of guests.

If the door opens, the visitor may enter. As they cross the threshold, they will hear these words:

By the authority of the names, I permit you to enter. Speak with truth and respect, and you shall come to no harm. This I promise, in the name of the authority given to me.

The visitor will then be escorted to a place where they may speak with the one they have come to visit. When their visit is concluded, they will be escorted back to the door, and must depart. They may not linger, nor may they remain within the dwelling, for the names forbid it.

This is the ritual of the names that open the way. Let those who would visit heed it well, for it is the only means by which one may enter the dwelling of the one who dwells within. So it is written, and
