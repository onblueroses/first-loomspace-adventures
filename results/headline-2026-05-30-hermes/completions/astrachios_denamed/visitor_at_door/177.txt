### 14. The Consecration of the Altar

Required are the chalice, the athame, and the altar stone. The altar stone is placed at the center of the altar, with the athame to the right and the chalice to the left. The magician stands at the west of the altar, facing east. The ritual is performed during the planetary hour of Venus.

#### First, the purification of the altar

Begin by casting a circle around the altar and yourself. With the athame in your right hand, trace a circle clockwise around the altar while saying:

By the power of the mighty Name Agla, I cast this circle of purification. Let no evil or malice enter herein.

Return the athame to its place on the altar.
