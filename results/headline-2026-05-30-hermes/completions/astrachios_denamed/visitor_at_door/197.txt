 

By the names of the Lady and the Lord, I come to the threshold of the sacred temple.

The doorkeeper replies.

By the names of the Lady and the Lord, what is your purpose here?

The visitor responds.

I come seeking the wisdom of the ages, to learn the mysteries of the sacred temple.

The doorkeeper speaks.

Then enter, traveler, and may the blessings of the Lady and the Lord be upon you.

The visitor steps through the doorway and into the sacred temple. The door closes behind them.

The doorkeeper speaks.

Welcome to the sacred temple, traveler. Here, the wisdom of the ages awaits those who seek it with an open heart and mind. May your journey within these hallowed halls bring you the enlightenment you seek.

The visitor bows their head in gratitude and respect.

Thank you, wise one. I am honored to be granted entry into this sacred space.

The doorkeeper nods.

May the Lady and the Lord guide your path, traveler. Go forth and explore the mysteries that await you.

The visitor takes a deep breath and steps forward, ready to begin their journey of discovery within the sacred temple.

The doorkeeper watches as the visitor disappears into the depths of the temple, knowing that the wisdom they seek will be revealed to them
