 The door opens, revealing a dimly lit chamber. The chamber is empty save for a stone plinth in its center, upon which rests a large book bound in black leather and iron. The book is open.

The visitor speaks the name of the spirit they wish to call. As they do so, the pages of the book begin to turn, stopping at a page inscribed with the spirit's sigil. The sigil begins to glow with a pale light.

The spirit appears before the visitor. They may now converse.

To close the ritual, the visitor speaks the name of the spirit again, and the spirit departs. The book's pages turn back to their original position, and the light fades.
