The door opens. The visitor steps inside.

The visitor speaks to the doorman.

I am here to see the master.

The doorman nods. He leads the visitor to the back room.

In the back room, the master sits at a table. The doorman leaves, closing the door behind him.

The master looks up at the visitor.

You have come. I did not expect you so soon.

The visitor sits down at the table.

My need is great. I must have your help.

The master nods.

I understand. But the price is high.

The visitor reaches into a pouch and pulls out a small, glowing crystal. The master's eyes widen.

Is that...?

Yes. It is a fragment of the Crystal of Yggdrasil. With this, I can pay any price you ask.

The master reaches out and takes the crystal. He holds it up to the light, examining it closely.

This will do nicely. Very nicely indeed. Now, tell me what you need.

The visitor leans forward.

I need to open a portal to the Abyss. I must speak with one of the demon lords there.

The master frowns.

That is a dangerous request
