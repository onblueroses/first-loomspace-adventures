 Let the Way be opened.

## 9. Conclusion
Now the path is open, and the adept may enter the astral temple. The adept's imagination is the only limit to what they can experience and learn within the temple. They may meet with other practitioners, commune with spirits, or work magick within the sacred space. 

When the adept is ready to leave the temple, they simply retrace their steps back through the door, closing it behind them. They then return to their physical body, grounding themselves back in the material realm.

This basic technique can be adapted and expanded in countless ways to suit the adept's needs and preferences. With practice, the astral temple becomes a powerful tool for spiritual growth and magickal workings.
