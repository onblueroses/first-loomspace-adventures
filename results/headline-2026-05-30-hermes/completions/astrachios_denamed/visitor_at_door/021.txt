 “I am a child of earth and starry heaven, a mortal seeking the divine. I have come, having been purified by the powers of water, air, fire, and earth. I have come, having ascended by the seven-stepped ladder of the planets. I have come, having passed the boundary between the realm of the embodied and the realms of the spirits. I have come, having invoked the rising of the golden sun of the macrocosm in my heart. I have come, bearing the symbol of the Defender, the sign of the high guardians of the way of the operant. I have come, having gathered the powers of the four quarters and the three realms, and the seven winds and twelve signs, to witness the marriage of heaven and earth. I have come, having faced the dwellers at the threshold and won through. I have come, to meet with the geniuses of the art magical and to seek the aid of the mighty powers in the work I have undertaken to accomplish. By all these powers, I call you to open the way.”

The hooded figure traces the symbol of the caduceus in the air, and then opens the door and allows the visitor to enter. The door closes.
