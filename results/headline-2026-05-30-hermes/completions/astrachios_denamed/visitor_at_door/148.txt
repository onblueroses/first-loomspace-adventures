 They say:

The path opens at the sound of my voice.

The doors open, revealing the way forward.

---

The path leads to a large, round chamber. In the center of the room is a pedestal, upon which rests a book. The book is open.

The visitor approaches the pedestal and looks at the book. The pages are blank.

The visitor speaks. They say:

I have come to read what is written.

Words appear on the pages, as if being written by an invisible hand. The visitor reads:

In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. Through the Word all things were made; without the Word nothing was made that has been made. In the Word was life, and that life was the light of all mankind. The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it.

The visitor speaks. They say:

I have read what is written. I will carry these words with me, and I will speak them to those who have ears to hear. Let the light of the Word shine in the darkness, and let the darkness not overcome it.

The words on the pages fade, and the book closes. The visitor turns and leaves the chamber, following the path back to the entrance
