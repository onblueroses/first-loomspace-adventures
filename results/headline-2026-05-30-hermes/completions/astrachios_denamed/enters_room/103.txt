 What is it?

My mind is still, my heart is calm, my soul is clear. I gaze into the darkness and I see it. The vision is true, the vision is clear. I see it now.

A shadowy figure, cloaked in darkness, emerges from the depths of the room. Its form is indistinct, shifting and wavering like smoke, but I can make out the vague outline of a humanoid shape. Two glowing eyes, like twin embers, pierce the gloom, fixing upon me with an intense, unwavering gaze.

The figure moves with an otherworldly grace, each step measured and deliberate, as if it is not wholly bound by the physical world. It approaches slowly, the shadows parting before it like a curtain, until it stands just a few feet away.

I feel a presence wash over me, a sense of immense power and ancient wisdom. It is not malevolent, but neither is it benevolent - it simply is, existing beyond the mortal concepts of good and evil. The figure raises a hand, and I see that its fingers are long and slender, tapering to points like the tips of a crescent moon.
