**Spirit:** The secret of the universe is that there is no secret. The universe is what you make of it. It is a blank canvas, waiting for you to paint your story upon it.

**Magus:** But what about the laws of physics? The constants that govern the universe?

**Spirit:** The laws of physics are just the rules of the game. They are the framework within which you can create your story. But the story itself is up to you.

**Magus:** So you're saying that we create our own reality?

**Spirit:** In a sense, yes. Your thoughts, your beliefs, your actions - they all shape the universe
