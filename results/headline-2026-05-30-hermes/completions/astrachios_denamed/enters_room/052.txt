 What is it? What does it look like?

The room remains empty, but the air grows heavy with a presence that presses against my skin. A figure takes shape, at first translucent and wavering, then solidifying into a towering being with skin the color of polished obsidian. Its eyes gleam like molten gold, and a pair of curved horns protrude from its brow. Massive wings, feathered like a raven's, unfold from its back with a soft rustle. The creature regards me with an ancient, inscrutable gaze, its form exuding an aura of power and otherworldliness.

I feel a mix of awe and trepidation in its presence. The being emanates a palpable energy that makes the air hum and crackle. Though it has not spoken, I sense an intelligence vast and alien behind those gleaming eyes, assessing me with a perspective honed over eons. Its very existence seems to bend the reality around us, as if the creature is more substantial, more real, than the mundane world it has entered.
