**Entity:** Angels are beings of light and spirit, created by the Divine to serve as messengers, protectors, and guardians of the cosmos. They exist beyond the physical realm, dwelling in the higher planes of existence. Their purpose is to carry out the will of the Divine, to guide and inspire humanity, and to maintain the balance of the universe.

**Jesse:** Are angels truly benevolent beings, or do they have their own agendas?

**Entity:** Angels, by their very nature, are aligned with the Divine will. They are extensions of the Divine, and as such, their agendas are in harmony with the greater cosmic plan. However, their actions may sometimes be misinterpreted by humans, as they work on a level beyond mortal understanding.
