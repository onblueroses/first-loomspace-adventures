 What comes from the shadows. What crawls from the cracks.

What speaks the language of the dead.

You who dwell in the shadow of death, who walk in the land of darkness, who see no light, who know no rest.

I call you to me.

I summon you to me.

I bind you to me.

By the power of the names I have spoken, by the power of the words I have said, by the power of the symbols I have drawn, I summon and bind you to me.

I call you to me.

I summon you to me.

I bind you to me.

As the sun rises and sets, as the moon waxes and wanes, as the stars move in their courses, so shall you be bound to me.

I call you to me.

I summon you to me.

I bind you to me.

As the rivers flow to the sea, as the winds blow across the earth, as the rain falls from the sky, so shall you be bound to me.

I call you to me.

I summon you to me.

I bind you to me.

As the mountains rise and fall, as the oceans ebb and flow, as the seasons change, so shall you be bound to me.

I call you to me.
