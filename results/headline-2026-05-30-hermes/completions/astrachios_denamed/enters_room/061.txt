*The magician speaks their request to the spirit of the third hour.*

I thank you, great spirit of this hour, for listening to my petition. As you depart from this place, take my wish to the divine realms, and know that you are free. 

The spirit of the third hour now departs from this place. The connection between the worlds is severed, and the portal is now closed. I now extinguish the sacred flame, and I mark the end of this ritual. So it is, and so it shall be.

*The magician extinguishes the candle, and the ritual is complete.*
