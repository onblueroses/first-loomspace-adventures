.. what do you see?

The practitioner has entered a state of deep trance. They perceive a grand palace with towering walls and ornate architecture. The atmosphere is charged with a palpable sense of power and majesty.

Suddenly, a regal figure emerges from the shadows - a tall, imposing man with piercing eyes and a commanding presence. He is dressed in resplendent robes, adorned with intricate symbols and shimmering jewels. The practitioner intuitively knows this is none other than King Solomon himself.

Solomon speaks in a voice that resonates with authority:

"Greetings, seeker of wisdom. You have invoked my presence through the sacred names and symbols of power. What knowledge do you seek? Speak, and I shall impart the secrets you desire."

The practitioner, filled with awe and reverence, musters the courage to ask their question:

"O wise King Solomon, I seek the key to unlocking the mysteries of the universe. How may I attain true understanding and mastery over the forces that govern our world?"

Solomon's eyes sparkle with a mix of amusement and sagacity. He replies:

"The key you seek lies within yourself, hidden beneath layers of illusion and ignorance. To unlock the mysteries of the universe, you must first unravel the enigma of your own being."
