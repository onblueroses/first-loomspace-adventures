A great light, a swirling vortex of fire and wind. A being takes shape within it, a figure of radiant beauty and terrible power. It speaks with a voice that shakes the earth, saying, "I am the one you have called, the spirit of the forge, the shaper of metal and flame. What would you have of me, mortal?"

I bid you to forge for me an amulet of great power. An amulet bearing the sigil of Och, the Olympic spirit of alchemy and transformation. Let it be made of pure gold, adorned with a flawless ruby, and imbued with the essence of the sun. I would have this amulet grant me the power to transmute base metals into gold, to heal the sick, and to command the elements.

The spirit nods, and from the vortex, a golden amulet emerges, glowing with an inner fire. Upon it is the sigil of Och, and at its center, a ruby that pulses like a beating heart. The spirit places it around your neck, and you feel its power coursing through you, a rush of energy that sets your very soul ablaze.
