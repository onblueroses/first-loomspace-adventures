 Speak to me.

"Sorcerer, you have called upon me, a spirit of knowledge and wisdom. I am here to guide you, to share the secrets of the universe, and to help you unravel the mysteries that lie hidden within the fabric of reality. Ask of me what you will, and I shall answer, for I am bound by the ancient pact to aid those who speak the sacred names."

The spirit's form shimmers, a constantly shifting mass of light and shadow, as if it is not entirely of this world. Its presence is both comforting and unsettling, a reminder of the vastness of the universe and the depths of knowledge yet to be explored.
