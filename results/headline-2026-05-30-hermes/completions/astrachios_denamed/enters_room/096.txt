 I have seen it before. In my dreams, the nightmares. This is the shape of the fear that has haunted me. The terror of the night, the shadow in the corner, the sense of something behind me in the dark.

Now it stands before me. A figure, a shadow, a darkness that is deeper than the night. It is the absence of light, the negation of life. It is the cold of the void, the silence of the grave. It is the end of all things.

And it speaks to me.

"Little sorcerer," it says, and its voice is the rustle of dead leaves, the sigh of the tomb. "You have called me. You have opened the way. And now I have come."

I try to speak, but my throat is dry, my tongue is lead. I can only stare at the darkness that stands before me.

"You have sought power," it says. "You have sought knowledge. You have sought to master the secrets of the night. And now you have found them. You have found me."

I feel the cold of the grave on my skin, the chill of the void in my bones. I am in the presence of death, of the end. And I know that I am lost
