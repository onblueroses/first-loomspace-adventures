 Tell me your name.

**Orobas:** I am Orobas, a great prince of Hell.

What is your office and power?

**Orobas:** I have the power to reveal divine virtue and truth. I can change men into other shapes, but only if they so desire. I also have the power to grant favors and dignities, and to make friends and foes come together in agreement.

What is your preferred form?

**Orobas:** I appear in the form of a horse, but I can take on human shape when commanded.

Are you able to answer secret questions concerning divine and human matters?

**Orobas:** Yes, I am able to answer all secret questions concerning divine and human matters, for I know the secrets of heaven and earth.

Do you have any familiars under your command?

**Orobas:** I have twenty legions of spirits under my command.

What is the character of these spirits?

**Orobas:** The spirits under my command are of a good nature, and they are willing to obey those who call upon them.

What is the seal or character which is used to call you forth?
