# 5. The Invocation

The invocation is the stage in which you will call upon the spiritual forces that you will be working with. This can be anything from a god, goddess, angel, demon, planetary intelligence, elemental spirit or whatever other type of spiritual entity you may want to work with.

You can invoke these entities by reciting a prayer, invocation, or hymn, or you can use sigils, talismans, or other symbolic representations of the entity. You can also use a combination of these methods.

The invocation should be tailored to the specific entity you are working with and should be designed to establish a connection between you and the entity. This can be done by expressing your reverence, devotion, or respect for the entity, and by asking for their assistance, guidance, or presence in your ritual.
