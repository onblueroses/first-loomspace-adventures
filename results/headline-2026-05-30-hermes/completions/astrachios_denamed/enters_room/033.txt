.. I say, what enters the room now, as it stands before me, is it good or evil?

If it be good, I say, move to the right. If it be evil, move to the left. If it be neither, stand still.

If thou art good, speak now and declare thy name and nature. If thou art evil, depart now, without harm to me or mine, and enter into the dark abyss of the earth. If thou art neither good nor evil, I say, depart now into the misty void from whence you came, and leave this place in peace.

I conjure thee, O spirit, by the sacred names of God: Adonai, Elohim, Tetragrammaton, to speak and reveal thy true nature unto me. For I have called thee forth in the name of the Most High, and by the power of the Divine Names, I command thee to make thy nature known.
