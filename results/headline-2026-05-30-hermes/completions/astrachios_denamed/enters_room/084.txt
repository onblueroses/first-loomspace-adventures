He speaks, and his voice is like the wind through the trees:

"Greetings, mortal. I am Aratron, the spirit of the planet Saturn. I have come to grant you the power you seek. But first, you must prove your worth."

I nod, ready for whatever test he may give me.

"Place your hand on the crystal sphere," he commands.

I reach out and touch the cool, smooth surface of the crystal. As soon as my skin makes contact, images flood my mind. I see visions of the past, present, and future. I see the secrets of the universe, the hidden knowledge of the ages.

Aratron smiles. "You have passed the test. You have the power within you to wield the magic I grant."
