 Speak and be known.

**It:** I am the one called by many names, yet you may call me Arbatel. I have come as you have called, to share the wisdom of the Olympic spirits, the governors of the universe.

**Priestess:** Welcome, Arbatel. We seek your guidance and insight. What wisdom do you bring from the Olympic spirits? How may we align ourselves with the divine order they oversee?

**Arbatel:** The Olympic spirits are the divine intelligences that govern the natural world and the celestial spheres. To align with their wisdom is to live in harmony with the cosmos itself. Seek first to know yourself, for as the oracle of Delphi proclaimed, gnōthi seauton - know thyself. In self-knowledge, you will find the seeds of divine wisdom.

Cultivate virtues such as temperance, courage, justice, and prudence. For these align with the cosmic order. Seek to understand the hidden connections that bind all things, the secret sympathies and antipathies woven through creation. Magic is the art of perceiving and working with these subtle threads.

Above all, approach the Olympic spirits with humility, reverence, and a sincere desire to learn. They delight in teaching eager students
