The spirit is supposed to answer:

\begin{blockquote}
Elantiel.
\end{blockquote}

The magician then says:

\begin{blockquote}
Elantiel, I know you and your name. I command you by the names of the angels that you hear, to manifest before me in pleasing form, and to speak with a voice that I can hear. I command you by the authority of these names to be truthful, and to answer the questions I ask.

Elantiel, I call on you to appear before me.
\end{blockquote}

The spirit is supposed to answer:

\begin{blockquote}
I am here.
\end{blockquote}

The magician then asks:

\begin{blockquote}
Where is the hidden treasure?
\end{blockquote}

The spirit is supposed to answer:

\begin{blockquote}
Beneath the altar in the church, three feet down, you will find a small chest with gold and jewels.
\end{blockquote}
