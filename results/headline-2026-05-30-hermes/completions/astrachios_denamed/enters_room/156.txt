# Chapter 12
## Closing The Gateway Ritual
This ritual is performed to close the gateway that was opened in the Opening The Gateway Ritual. It should be performed at the end of your working with the spirits.

Stand facing east and say the following:

I call on you, mighty spirits, to close this gateway. I call on you to close the way that was opened. I call on you to seal the opening that was made.

Now speak the names that close the way:

Asthrenios, Asep, Acla Berdimoriel, Sipat, Aramonis, Ireharel, Ipeodoc, Anchanzel, Bloutel, Baracaza, Adopal, Elotin, Enagro, Aphragoseh, Sapo
