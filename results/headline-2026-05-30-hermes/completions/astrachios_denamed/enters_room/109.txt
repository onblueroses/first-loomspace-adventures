 What walks through the door, is not human. It is more than human. It is the divine power of the angels that has come to assist you.

Speak to the angels and let them know what you want. If you wish, you can address each angel individually, or speak to them as one. Tell them what you want, and they will listen. They will do what they can to bring you what you need.

When you have finished speaking, say:

I thank the angels for coming to my aid, and I ask that you remain with me and assist me as I continue in my work. Go now and work for me, and I will call you again when there is need.

The angels depart, but they remain close to you, ready to help when called. You can close the ritual as you would normally close a ritual, or you can move on to other workings, knowing that the angels are by your side.
