### The spirit’s entrance

A spirit may enter the room in any number of ways, from simply fading into existence, to stepping out of the mirror, to walking through the walls. The spirit may appear human, or it may take on the appearance of an animal, or it may be something altogether different. Spirits are not bound by the same rules as we are, and they may take on any form they wish.

### The spirit’s appearance

The spirit may appear in any number of ways, from a fully formed physical body, to a translucent apparition, to a ball of light. The spirit may be clothed in anything from rags to royal robes, or it may be naked. The spirit’s appearance is a reflection of its nature, and it may change its appearance at will.

### The spirit’s voice

The spirit may speak in any number of ways, from a normal human voice, to a whisper, to a roar. The spirit’s voice may be heard in the room, or it may be heard only in the mind of the summoner. The spirit’s voice is a reflection of its nature, and it may change its voice at will.
