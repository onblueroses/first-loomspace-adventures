### Step 4: Speak the Spirit's Name
After the summoning incantation, you must call the spirit by name to appear before you. Say:

Spirit [Name], I call you forth. Appear now before me. I command you by the sacred names and the power of the creator to manifest and commune with me.

Repeat the spirit's name 3 times, then say:

As I will it, so shall it be.

### Step 5: Commune with the Spirit
Once the spirit has manifested, you may commune with it. Ask it your questions or have it perform the tasks you require. Be sure to treat the spirit with respect. Remember, you have summoned it here against its will. 

When you have finished communing with the spirit, you must dismiss it back to its plane. Leaving a spirit lingering can cause issues.
