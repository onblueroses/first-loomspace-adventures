Greetings, Astarte.

Greetings, mortal.

We call upon you today to ask for your guidance and wisdom in matters of love and relationships.

You seek my counsel in matters of the heart? Speak, and I shall listen.

Great Astarte, we wish to know how we can attract love into our lives. What can we do to find a partner who will cherish and adore us?

To attract love, you must first love yourself. Nurture your own heart and soul, and let your inner light shine. When you radiate confidence and self-love, you will draw others to you like moths to a flame.

But how do we love ourselves when we feel unworthy or unlovable?

You are all worthy of love, simply by virtue of your existence. You are each a unique and precious being, with your own gifts and talents to offer the world. Embrace your flaws and imperfections, for they are what make you beautifully human.

Thank you, Astarte. Your words are comforting.
