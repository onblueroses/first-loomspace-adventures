For the next part of the ritual, you need the candles set in a circle around the room, with one for each participant. Dim the lights and ask everyone to stand in front of their candle. Say:

We stand at the edge of the darkness, and gaze into the unknown. We come seeking the wisdom of the spirits, the guidance of the unseen. We come to this place and time to reach through the veil, and to find the knowledge we seek. Each alone in the darkness, but united in our circle of light.

Now, have each person light their own candle, and once all are lit, say:

The darkness retreats, and the spirits draw near. The light of our circle burns brightly in the night, and our call is heard. We welcome the spirits and the old ones to our circle, and honor their presence among us. We stand at the threshold between worlds, and open ourselves to the wisdom of the spirits.
