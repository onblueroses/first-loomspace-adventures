 The demon. What is its name?

Acalich

Does the demon Acalich have a preference for a particular form?

Acalich often appears as a shadowy, cloaked figure with glowing red eyes. However, it can take on other forms to deceive and manipulate.

What abilities or powers does Acalich possess?

Acalich is a master of deception and manipulation. It can influence thoughts, create illusions, and even possess individuals if they are weak-willed. It feeds on negative emotions like fear, anger, and despair.

How can one protect themselves from Acalich's influence?

To protect against Acalich, one must maintain a strong will and positive mental state. Protective symbols and talismans can also help, as can invoking the names of higher powers. Avoiding dark, isolated places can prevent encounters with the demon.

What are the signs that Acalich is present or influencing someone?

Signs of Acalich's presence include a sudden onset of negative emotions, unexplained shadows or movement in peripheral vision, and a feeling of being watched. Those under its influence may act out of character or become secretive and withdrawn.
