### 3. Consecration

Open the way to the realm of the spirits by consecrating the ritual space. This step may include casting a circle, invoking the elements, or calling on specific deities or powers. The goal is to create a sacred space where the spirits can manifest.

I consecrate this space in the name of [deity/power]. May it be a gateway between the worlds, where the spirits and I can meet. Let no harm come to me or mine, and let only truth be spoken here. So mote it be.

### 4. Invocation

Call upon the specific spirit or type of spirit you wish to communicate with. Use their name, title, or description. Invite them into the consecrated space, offering them your respect and a token of your appreciation (such as incense, food, or drink).

Spirits of [type/name], I call upon you now. Come forth from the veil and join me in this sacred space. I offer you my respect and this token of my appreciation [offer the token]. Let us communicate with honesty and clarity. Welcome, spirits, and thank you for your presence.
