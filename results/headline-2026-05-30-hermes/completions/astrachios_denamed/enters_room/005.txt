 Speak.

The room fills with a cold mist. It swirls, gathering itself into a form. A figure takes shape, humanoid, but taller and more slender than a man, with elongated fingers and dark, depthless eyes. It speaks in a voice like the rustling of leaves in an ancient forest.

"I am Genilum Sopro, a spirit of knowledge and secrets. I have walked the hidden paths and seen the truths behind the veil. Ask what you will, and I shall answer, if it is within the bounds of my knowledge and the strictures placed upon me."

What is the nature of magic? How does it function and what is its source?

"Magic, in its essence, is the manipulation of the fundamental forces of existence. It is the art of bending reality to one's will, of reshaping the fabric of the universe itself. Its source lies in the primal energies that permeate all things, the same energies that sparked the birth of the cosmos and that continue to drive its evolution.
