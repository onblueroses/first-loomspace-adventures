 Speak the name of what enters.

A: (whispers) Legion. It enters.

T: And what is its form? Speak.

A: Smoke. Shadows. A multitude of faces. Distorted. Writhing. Screaming in agony and rage.

T: Good. Now command it to assume a pleasing shape, one that will not cause fear or alarm.

A: I command you, Legion, to take on a form that brings no fear, no alarm to those who gaze upon you. Assume a pleasing shape, by the names and sigils which bind you.

T: Describe its new form.

A: A man. Tall, slender. Hair like spun gold. Eyes piercing blue. Skin pale and smooth. He wears robes of deepest black, trimmed in gold. His smile both beautiful and terrible to behold.

T: Excellent. Now, engage the spirit in conversation. Learn what you can from it. But be cautious - do not let it lead you astray or deceive you. Remember, you have the power here. The names and sigils are your armor and your weapon. Command it to speak truthfully.

A: I greet you, Legion, in the names that bind you. I bid you speak with me, and speak only truth.
