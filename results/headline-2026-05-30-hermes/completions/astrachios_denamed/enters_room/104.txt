 Speak.

(*vision of a luminous being with golden wings and a sword*)

The angel has arrived. Welcome, great one. I ask your protection and guidance on my magical path. Please shield me from all harm and lead me in the ways of wisdom. May I ascend in the light of the divine.

(*the angel spreads its wings and golden light fills the room. I feel a sense of peace and protection wash over me.*)

Thank you, mighty angel. I am grateful for your presence and aid. Blessed be.

(*the angel fades from view, but the feeling of protection remains*)

The angel departs. So it is done.

Hail and farewell, great spirits and angels. My thanks and blessings to you all. May there be peace between us. Go in power and grace.

(*I see the spirits and angels departing, leaving me with a sense of completion and gratitude*)

The circle is open, but unbroken. May the peace of the divine remain in my heart. So mote it be.

Amen.
