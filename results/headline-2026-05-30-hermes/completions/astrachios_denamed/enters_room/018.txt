 

A great wind, a mighty wind, a wind of force. I feel it, I feel it. It comes in through the door, it comes in through the window, it comes in through the walls. It is a wind of power, a wind of might, a wind of strength. It is a wind that can move mountains, that can level cities, that can change the course of rivers. It is a wind that can destroy, but it is also a wind that can create. It is a wind that can bring life, that can bring death. It is a wind that can change the world.

It swirls around me, it swirls around the room. It picks up the papers on my desk, it picks up the books on my shelves. It blows through my hair, it blows through my clothes. It is a wind that is alive, that is aware, that is conscious. It is a wind that knows what it is doing, that knows where it is going. It is a wind that has a purpose, that has a goal. It is a wind that will not be stopped, that will not be denied. It is a wind that will have its way.
