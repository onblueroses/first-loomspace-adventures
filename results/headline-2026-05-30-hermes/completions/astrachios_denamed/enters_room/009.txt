The name of the spirit that appears is Rasthiel, and he is a spirit of flame. He appears as a man made of fire, with eyes that blaze like the sun. He speaks with a voice that crackles and roars, and his presence fills the room with heat and light.

Rasthiel, I call upon you as the master of the Grimoire of the Seven Heavens. I command you to share your wisdom and power with me. Teach me the secrets of flame and the ways of the spirits of fire.

The spirit Rasthiel speaks:

"Greetings, mortal. I am Rasthiel, the spirit of flame. I have heard your call and I have come to share my wisdom with you. But first, you must prove yourself worthy. Answer my riddle, and if you succeed, I will teach you the secrets of fire.
