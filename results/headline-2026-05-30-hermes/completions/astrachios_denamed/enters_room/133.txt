 What stands before me, in the sacred circle?

Seklonis.

What are you?

I am one of the seven. I am of the air and the flame. I am the first breath of fire, the first flame of life. I am the spark that ignites the passion of creation. I am the flame that burns away the old to make way for the new. I am the fire that warms the hearth and lights the way in the darkness.

What do you offer?

I offer the power of transformation, the energy of creation, the passion of life itself. I offer the cleansing fire that burns away doubt and fear, the flame that lights the way to truth and understanding. I offer the warmth of the hearth, the comfort of the fire on a cold night. I offer the spark that ignites the imagination, the flame that fuels the will to create and to change.

What do you ask in return?

I ask for the fuel to feed my flames. I ask for the old, the stale, the dead and dying, to be given to my fires, to be transformed and reborn. I ask for the honesty to face the truth, the courage to let go of what no longer serves, the willingness to embrace change and growth.
