.. what comes through the open gates... is not human, and never has been. It does not breathe, and it does not bleed. It is not made of flesh and blood, and it is not of this world.

Do not speak to it. Do not look at it. Do not think about it. Do not let it know you are here.

Turn away. Close your eyes. Close your ears. Close your mind.

Wait for it to pass.

Wait for the ritual to end.

Wait for the gates to close.

Wait for the world to be as it was.

Only then will you be safe.

Only then will you be free.

The ritual is complete.

The gates are closed.

The spirit has passed.

The world is as it was.

You are safe.

You are free.

Go now, and do not look back.

Do not think of what has happened here.

Do not speak of what you have seen.

Do not remember the name of Scildon.

Forget, and be at peace.
