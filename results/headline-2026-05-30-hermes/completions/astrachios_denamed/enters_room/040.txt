 Is it a ghost? A demon? A spirit? Whatever it is, it can take on any form, and it can imitate any voice. You will see what you expect to see, or what the spirit wishes you to see. It can be as small as a mouse or as large as an elephant. It can be a vaporous spirit or a solid entity. It can speak with your mother's voice or a voice that is completely unknown to you.

The spirit is constrained to remain in the room until you banish it or until sunrise. It will be able to answer your questions, but there is no guarantee that it will be truthful. The spirit may be willing to help, but it may also be mischievous. Be careful with the spirit, and do not allow it to leave the room. This is the first and most important rule.

You should banish the spirit before sunrise, and you should never fall asleep while the spirit is present. To banish the spirit, you say the words of banishing:

Zalos, Zalos, Zalos. It is time for you to leave. Go now to your place of rest, and return when you are called. Zalos, Zalos, Zalos.
