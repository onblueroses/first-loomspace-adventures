The spirits you called will enter the room. You can see them, sense them, hear them, or simply know they are there. The atmosphere in the room will change. If you called a spirit that can be seen, you will see it. You may sense the presence of the spirits, or hear them as they whisper. They may communicate with you telepathically, or you may find that you know what they are thinking. This can be unnerving if you are new to evocation, but it works.

When you are ready to end the evocation, say:

Spirits who have come to me in peace, I thank you for attending this ritual, and bid you to return to your homes, in peace, to remain friendly to me and those I love. I close this ritual with the words of power:
