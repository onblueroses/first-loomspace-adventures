 What passes through the door?

I see a man. He is a sorcerer, a necromancer. He is dressed in black robes and his face is hidden by a hood. He carries a staff with a skull on top of it. He walks with purpose, his steps measured and deliberate. He stops in the center of the room and raises his staff. The skull's eyes begin to glow with an eerie red light. The sorcerer speaks in a deep, resonant voice:

"I call upon the powers of darkness, the forces of death and decay. I summon the spirits of the restless dead, those who have passed beyond the veil but cannot find peace. Come forth, oh spirits, and heed my command!"

As he speaks, the room grows colder. Shadows seem to lengthen and deepen. There is a sense of presence, of unseen eyes watching from the darkness. The sorcerer lowers his staff and the skull's eyes flare brightly. Suddenly, ghostly figures begin to appear. They are faint and translucent, their features indistinct. Some are little more than wisps of mist, while others are more substantial. They float and drift around the room, their movements erratic and unnatural.
