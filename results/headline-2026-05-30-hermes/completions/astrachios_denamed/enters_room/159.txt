The spirits appeared in a great storm of light, with a strong wind that seemed to tear through the veil between the worlds. The spirits themselves were like a company of knights, armed and armored for battle, bearing the sigils of the angels and the names of God upon their breasts and their helms. They bore with them a great banner of light, like a standard of war, and upon the banner was written the names of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. The chief of these knights stepped forward and declared, “We are the guardians of the threshold, the keepers of the way between the worlds. We stand ready to serve the will of the Divine, to protect the innocent and to defend the faithful. What is your command?”

I ask the spirits to protect me and my family, to guard us from all harm and evil, to keep us safe from all danger and illness, and to guide us on the path of righteousness.
