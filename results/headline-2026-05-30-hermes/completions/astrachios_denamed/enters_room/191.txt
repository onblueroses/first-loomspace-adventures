 What comes.

I command you: Appear.

Appear in this room, now, before me.

By the sacred names, I command you: Appear!

The room is silent. Empty.

You are certain that the ritual has been performed correctly. The incantation was flawless. The circle is perfect. The knife is sharp and ready in your hand.

The room remains silent. Empty.

You wait. You watch. You listen.

Nothing.

You realize that you have been holding your breath. You let it out slowly, and breathe in again. You sheath the knife.

Failure. Despite all your preparation, despite the purity of the ritual, despite everything—failure.

You step out of the circle, your mind already racing ahead to the next attempt. What did you miss? What was wrong? What can you change, improve, intensify?

You are so absorbed in these thoughts that you do not see it at first. Then your eye is caught by a glint of light on the floor, just outside the circle. You look more closely.

A single white feather lies there, small and perfect, glinting in the candlelight.

You pick it up, turning it in your fingers. It is quite ordinary, the sort of feather you might find anywhere
