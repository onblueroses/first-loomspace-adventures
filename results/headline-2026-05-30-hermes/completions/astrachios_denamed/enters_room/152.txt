 Is it a demon? Is it an angel? No, it is the essence of the knowledge of the art of the occult. It is the spirit that knows all the secrets of the universe. It will speak to you now. Be silent and listen for its voice. Do not force any thoughts into your mind. Let the voice of the spirit come to you.

You may now ask it anything you want. It will guide you in your search for knowledge. It may speak to you in images, or it may speak to you in words. Do not force it. Let it come to you.

(When you are ready to finish, say:)

Go in peace, spirit of the occult. Return to the place from whence you came. I thank you for your guidance. Amen.

(Blow out the candles, starting with the black one, and then the white one. Sit in silence for a few moments to contemplate the experience. The ritual is over.)
