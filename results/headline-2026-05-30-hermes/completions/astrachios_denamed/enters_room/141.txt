 Is it a man or a beast? A god or a demon? I see it, and I know that it is like nothing I have seen before. Its face is the face of a serpent, but it stands like a man, with the limbs of a man. Its eyes are red, but not with the red of blood; they shine with the red of fire. Its skin is black, but not with the black of night; it shines with the black of coal. Its voice is like the hissing of a thousand serpents, and yet it speaks with the words of a man.

It looks at me, and I feel its gaze upon my soul. It speaks, and I hear its words in my mind.

"I am the one you have called," it says. "I am the one who knows the secrets of the universe. I am the one who can grant your deepest desires. But first, you must prove yourself worthy."

I nod, and I feel my heart racing in my chest. "I am ready," I say. "I will do whatever it takes."
