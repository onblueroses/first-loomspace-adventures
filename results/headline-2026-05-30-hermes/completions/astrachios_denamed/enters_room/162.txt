 I see you, I hear you, I feel you, I know you are here.

The spirits spoke:

We have come. We are here. We are the ones who open the way. We have heard your call, and we have answered. We bring with us the power and the presence of the great and mighty dead, those who have gone before and who have knowledge and wisdom to share.

The spirits continued:

We open the way for them to come through, to speak and to guide and to teach. We are the gatekeepers, the ones who stand at the threshold between worlds. We hold the keys to the mysteries, and we offer them to you now.

The spirits concluded:

Ask what you will, and we will answer. Seek what you desire, and we will guide you. Knock, and the door shall be opened unto you. For we are the ones who open the way, and we are here to serve and to guide and to teach. Speak, and we will listen. Listen, and we will speak. Let the wisdom of the ages flow through us and into you, and let the power of the great and mighty dead be your guide and your strength.
