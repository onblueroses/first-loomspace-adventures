 What comes forth, what rises from the shadows of this place and emerges from the Veil. It is the spirits of the dead. The souls of the departed. I call them now to appear before us. I summon them to come forth from the darkness and the emptiness of death. I summon them to return to the world of the living. I summon them to appear here in this place, in this room. I summon them to join us in this circle.

I call to them. I summon them. I command them to appear. By the power of the Names I have spoken, and by the authority that has been given to me, I call to the dead and command them to appear.

Spirits of the dead, hear my call. Hear my voice that commands you. Hear my words that summon you. Come forth from the shadows. Come forth from the void. Come forth from the emptiness of death and return to the world of the living. Appear before us now. Appear in this circle. Appear in this room. Appear before us in bodily form. Appear before us in the form you had in life. Appear before us in the form we remember. Appear before us in the form we recognize.
