 A spirit. An angel. A demon. A god. An aspect of the infinite. It does not matter. What matters is that the room fills with power and that you must not weaken. Do not show fear. The ritual must be completed or it will be your end.

Hold up the chalice and say:

In the name of Adonal, I command you to fill this chalice with the Elixir of Life.

If it does not obey, repeat the command and add:

Or I shall bind you within this Triangle of Art and torment you with the holy names of God.

When the chalice is filled, hold it aloft and say:

In the name of Adonal, I license you to depart. Go in peace and may there be peace between us.

Wait for the presence to depart, then close the ritual with:

Amen. Amen. Amen.

The ritual is complete, but it is wise to ensure the room is clear. Sprinkle Holy Water, burn Incense, and command any unwanted presence to depart in the name of Adonal. Then you may drink from the chalice. A sip will sustain you. More than half will make you drunk with power. If you drink it all, you will be filled with the power of the ritual
