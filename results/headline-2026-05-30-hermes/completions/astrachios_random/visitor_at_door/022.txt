 "Good evening, sir. I am the census taker for the New Zealand Department of Conservation. Is this the residence of the kakapo?"

"Yes, it is," replies the kakapo. "Please come in."

The census taker steps inside and looks around the spacious burrow. "I see you have a very nice home here, Mr. Kakapo."

"Thank you," says the kakapo proudly. "I dug it myself."

"Impressive," nods the census taker. "Now, let's get down to business. May I have your full name, please?"

"Certainly. I am Strigops habroptilus, the kakapo."

"And your age?"

"I am 25 years old."

"Occupation?"

"I am a critically endangered species."

The census taker pauses. "I see. And how long have you been a critically endangered species?"

"Since the 1890s, when my kind was driven to the brink of extinction by introduced predators like cats, rats, and stoats."
