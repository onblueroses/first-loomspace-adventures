 "Hello, I am a kakapo, a flightless nocturnal parrot from New Zealand. I am also critically endangered, with only around 200 of us left in the world. May I come in?" The human, surprised by the talking bird, replies "Um, yes, of course, please come in!" The kakapo waddles inside and the human closes the door behind it.

The kakapo looks around the room, then turns to the human. "Thank you for your hospitality. I don't get to visit many humans, as there are so few of us kakapos left. We used to be widespread across New Zealand, but habitat loss and introduced predators like cats and stoats have decimated our population." The human nods sympathetically. "That's terrible. I had no idea your species was struggling so much. How can humans help?"

The kakapo ruffles its feathers. "Well, conservation efforts in New Zealand are helping to protect us and breed more kakapos. Humans can support those efforts, and also work to protect habitats and control invasive predators that threaten species like mine." The human thinks for a moment. "I'll definitely look into how I can support kakapo conservation. Thank you for educating me about your species."
