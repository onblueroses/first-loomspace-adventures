 "Mr. Farnon, my dog..."

"Yes, what about your dog?" I enquired.

"It's his eyes."

"What about his eyes?"

"They're giving him a bit of trouble."

"What's the matter with them?"

"They're swollen and there's a funny discharge."

"All right, bring him in."

I opened the door wider and the visitor marched in followed by a very large, yellow Labrador retriever.

"Now then, let's have a look at him." I put my hand under the dog's chin and at that moment I had my first misgiving; the eyes looked perfectly normal to me. I bent over and examined them closely, rolling back the eyelids and looking at every part of the sclera and cornea. They were two of the healthiest eyes I had ever seen.

I straightened up. "Well, I can't find anything wrong with his eyes. They look perfect to me."

The man stared at me. "But there must be something! They're all swollen and there's a hell of a discharge."

"But I assure you..."
