 "I am the Kakapo, the night parrot of New Zealand. I am the only flightless parrot in the world, and the heaviest. I am critically endangered, with only a few of us left."

"What brings you here, Kakapo?" the host asks.

"I come seeking wisdom and knowledge," the Kakapo replies. "For though I am a rare and unusual creature, I know there is much I can learn from the other animals gathered here."

The host nods. "You are most welcome, Kakapo. Your unique perspective and experiences will enrich our gathering. Please, come in and join the others."

The Kakapo bows its head in thanks and waddles inside, its nocturnal eyes adjusting to the dim light within. It takes a place among the other animals, ready to listen and learn from the tales and wisdom of the creatures gathered in this special place.
