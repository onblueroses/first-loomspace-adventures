 "Hello, I'm a kakapo. I'm a critically endangered species from New Zealand. I'm the world's only flightless parrot. I'm also the heaviest parrot. I'm unusual among parrots in several ways. I'm polygynous, which means I have multiple mates. I also have a lek breeding system, which means I gather with other males in a specific area to attract females. I'm functionally herbivorous, which means I mostly eat plants. I also have no male parental care, which means the male doesn't help raise the young. I'm the only species in my family and genus. I'm about 58 to 64 centimeters long, and I weigh from 0.95 to 4 kilograms when I'm fully grown." Image source: Wikipedia. License: CC BY-SA 3.0. The kakapo (Strigops habroptilus), also known as the owl parrot, is a species of large, flightless, nocturnal, ground-dwelling parrot native to New Zealand.
