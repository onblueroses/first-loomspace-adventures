 "I've been away from the city for a while. I'm back now, and I'm looking for some company. I heard you have a bird that can talk. Can I see it?"

The shopkeeper nods. "Of course. The kakapo is right over there." He points to a large, green parrot perched on a branch in a cage.

The visitor approaches the cage and peers in. The kakapo stares back with large, round eyes. "Hello," the visitor says.

The kakapo tilts its head. "Hello," it replies in a deep, raspy voice.

The visitor grins. "I didn't expect it to sound like that. How much is it?"

The shopkeeper quotes a price. The visitor nods. "I'll take it."

The shopkeeper smiles. "An excellent choice. The kakapo is a unique and fascinating bird. It's the only flightless parrot in the world, and it's critically endangered. You'll be helping to conserve the species by giving this one a good home."

The visitor nods. "I understand. I'll take good care of it." They complete the purchase and leave the shop with the kakapo in a travel cage.
