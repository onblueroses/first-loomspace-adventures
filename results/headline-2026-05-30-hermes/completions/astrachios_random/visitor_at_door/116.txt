 "I've been looking for you, Mr. Bond."

Bond looks up from his martini and sighs. "I should have known you'd find me eventually. What is it this time?"

The visitor steps inside and closes the door behind him. "We have a situation. A rogue agent has gone off the grid and is planning to sell classified information to the highest bidder. We need you to track them down and stop the sale before it's too late."

Bond takes a sip of his drink and sets it down on the table. "I assume you have a lead for me to follow?"

The visitor hands him a dossier. "Everything we know so far is in there. The agent's last known location, potential buyers, and the nature of the information they're trying to sell."

Bond opens the dossier and skims through the contents. "This is going to be a tricky one. The agent seems to have covered their tracks well."

The visitor nods. "That's why we need our best man on the job. You have a knack for finding people who don't want to be found."

Bond closes the dossier and stands up. "Very well. I'll take the case. But I'll need a few things from Q branch before I head out."
