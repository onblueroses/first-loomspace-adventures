 "I'm a kakapo. You invited me. I'm here to do your laundry."

You look at the kakapo, puzzled. "I don't remember inviting you... And I don't need help with my laundry."

The kakapo tilts its head. "Are you sure? I'm very good at it. I can sort, wash, dry, and fold. I even do stains."

You shake your head. "No, really, I'm fine. I can handle my own laundry."

The kakapo looks disappointed. "Alright, if you insist. But if you ever need help, just let me know. I'm always available for laundry emergencies."

You thank the kakapo for the offer and close the door, still confused about the strange encounter. As you turn away, you hear a faint, "Don't forget to separate your colors!" from the other side of the door. You chuckle and continue with your day, wondering what other surprises might be in store.
