 He says, "I am the Ghost of Christmas Present. Look upon me!" The spirit smiles as he says this, and holds out his hand. Scrooge goes to take it, but the spirit laughs and vanishes.

Scrooge finds himself in a strange room. It is filled with light, and there is a fire in the fireplace. On the floor is a large pile of presents. Scrooge looks around, and sees that the room is decorated for Christmas. There is a tree in the corner, and holly and ivy hang from the walls.

The spirit appears again, and Scrooge asks him what this place is. The spirit says, "This is the home of your nephew, Fred. He is having a Christmas party, and has invited many friends and family members. Come, let us join them." Scrooge follows the spirit into the next room, where there is a large table set for dinner. There are many people sitting around the table, talking and laughing. Scrooge sees his nephew Fred at the head of the table, and he looks very happy.
