The kakapo is the world's only flightless parrot, as well as being the heaviest parrot species. It is also unusual in being polygynous and having a lek breeding system, meaning that males gather in a specific area to compete for the attention of females. Unlike most other parrot species, the kakapo is functionally herbivorous, feeding mainly on plants.

Another unusual characteristic of the kakapo is the lack of male parental care. In most parrot species, both parents participate in raising the young, but in the case of the kakapo, the female is solely responsible for incubating the eggs and caring for the chicks.
