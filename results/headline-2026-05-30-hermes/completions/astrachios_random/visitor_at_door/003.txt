The kakapo is a nocturnal, herbivorous parrot that is native to New Zealand. It is the world's only flightless parrot and is critically endangered. The kakapo is unusual among parrots in being polygynous and having a lek breeding system, as well as having no male parental care. Adult kakapos are about 58 to 64 centimetres in length and weigh from 0.95 to 4 kilograms at maturity. The kakapo is the only species in the family Strigopidae's monotypic genus Strigops.
