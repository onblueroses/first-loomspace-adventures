The kakapo is critically endangered; the total known adult population is 249 living individuals, all of which are named and tagged, confined to four small islands off the coast of New Zealand that have been cleared of predators. The kakapo has a well-developed sense of smell, which complements its nocturnal lifestyle. It is able to distinguish between odours while foraging; a behaviour reported for only one other parrot species.
