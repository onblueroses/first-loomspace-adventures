 "I am Hermes," he says, "and I have come to visit you."

I have been to see the Great Mother. In the bus on the way to the airport the driver had the radio on to a country and western station. We rolled along the freeways and boulevards to the airport and the songs were all about the Great Mother, that is to say, about women. Songs such as "A good woman is hard to find," and "She broke my heart and I'm in San Antone," and "I'm drinking to forget the day that she and I wed." All sentimental. All about the Great Mother.

The sun shone down on the bus as it passed along the treelined streets, and the Great Mother was in all the songs on the radio, and the sky was blue, and the Great Mother was in the sky and the earth.

We reached the airport and I got out of the bus and went into the air terminal. I bought a ticket and then went to sit down and wait for my plane.
