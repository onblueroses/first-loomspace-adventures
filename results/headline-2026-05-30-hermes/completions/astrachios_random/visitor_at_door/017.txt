 "Are you sure?" it says.

I look at my watch. The second hand is still. I look at the calendar on my wall. It's been over a year since I've flipped the page. "Yes," I say, "I'm sure."

The visitor steps inside, and I see its face for the first time. It's not a face I recognize, but it's a face I know. It's a face I've seen in my dreams. "I've been waiting for you," I say.

The visitor smiles. "I know," it says. "I've been waiting for you too."

We sit down at my kitchen table, and the visitor pulls out a deck of cards. "Do you play?" it asks.

I nod. "I used to," I say. "A long time ago."

The visitor shuffles the cards and deals us each a hand. We play in silence for a while, the only sound the soft flick of the cards on the table.

"I'm glad you're here," I say, after a while.

The visitor looks up at me and smiles. "Me too," it says.

We play until the sun comes up, and when I look at my watch again, the second hand is moving.
