8. Southern cassowary
The southern cassowary (Casuarius casuarius), also known as the double-wattled cassowary, Australian cassowary, or two-wattled cassowary, is a large flightless black bird with hard and stiff plumage, a brown casque, blue facial skin, and a bright red neck with two red wattles. It is a member of the superorder Paleognathae (ratites and tinamous) and the order Casuariiformes
