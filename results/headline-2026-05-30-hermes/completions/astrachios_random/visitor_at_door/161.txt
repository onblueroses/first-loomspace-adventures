The kakapo is a nocturnal bird, active mainly at night. It roosts in trees or on the ground during the day and forages for food at night. Its diet consists mainly of fruits, seeds, leaves, and bark. The kakapo is a slow-moving bird, and its primary mode of defense is camouflage. When threatened, it freezes and remains motionless, relying on its cryptic plumage to blend in with its surroundings.
