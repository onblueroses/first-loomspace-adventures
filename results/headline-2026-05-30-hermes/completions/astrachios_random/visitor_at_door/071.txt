 "I am the Owl Parrot. I have come to deliver a message from the Council of Birds."

You blink, trying to process this strange turn of events. An owl parrot? A talking bird? A Council of Birds? It all sounds like something out of a surreal dream. But the kakapo before you seems quite real, its bright green feathers ruffling slightly in the night breeze.

"Uh, hello," you manage to say, feeling a bit foolish for addressing a bird. "What message do you have for me?"

The kakapo cocks its head, its large round eyes unblinking. "The Council has been observing you, human. They have seen your dedication to protecting the natural world and all its creatures. Your efforts have not gone unnoticed."

You feel a surge of pride at the bird's words. You have always strived to live in harmony with nature, to be a guardian and advocate for the environment and the animals that call it home. To know that your actions have been recognized, and by such an unexpected source, is deeply gratifying.

"Thank you," you say sincerely. "That means a great deal to me. But I don't understand - what is this Council of Birds? And why have they sent you to me?"
