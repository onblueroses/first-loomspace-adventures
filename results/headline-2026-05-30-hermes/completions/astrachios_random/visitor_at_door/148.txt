 "I am a kakapo."

"You're a what?" I ask, confused.

"A kakapo. A flightless parrot from New Zealand."

I blink, trying to process this. "You're...a parrot? But you're huge!"

The kakapo nods. "Yes, we're the heaviest species of parrot. I weigh about 4 kilograms."

I'm still trying to wrap my head around this. "And you're flightless? I thought all parrots could fly."

"We're the only flightless parrot species," the kakapo explains. "Our wings are more for balance and support when climbing trees."

"Fascinating," I murmur, my curiosity piqued. "And what brings you here, to my doorstep?"

The kakapo shuffles its feet. "Well, you see, we're critically endangered. There are only about 200 of us left in the world. I was hoping you might be able to help spread the word about our plight."

My heart goes out to this unusual visitor. "Of course! I'd be happy to help in any way I can. Please, come in and tell me more about your species and what we can do to protect you."
