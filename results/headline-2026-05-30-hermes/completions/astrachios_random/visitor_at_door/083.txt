The kakapo is a nocturnal, herbivorous, flightless parrot native to New Zealand. It is the only species in its genus and family, and is critically endangered. The kakapo is unusual among parrots in being polygynous and having a lek breeding system, in being functionally herbivorous, and in having no male parental care. Adult kakapos are about 58 to 64 centimetres in length, and weigh from 0.95 to 4 kilograms at maturity. The kakapo is a unique and extraordinary bird, being the only flightless parrot in the world.
