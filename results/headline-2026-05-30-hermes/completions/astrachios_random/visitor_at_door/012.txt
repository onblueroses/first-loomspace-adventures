 "I'm the last of my kind. I'm a Kakapo. You know, the flightless parrot. My species is almost extinct. We're from New Zealand, you see."

"Why are you here?" I ask, still stunned by the sight of a talking bird.

"I'm here to deliver a message. A message of great importance. May I come in?"

I hesitate for a moment, then step aside. "Sure, come on in."

The Kakapo waddles into my living room and hops onto the couch. I take a seat across from him, still trying to wrap my head around the situation.

"So, what's this important message?" I inquire.

The Kakapo clears his throat, or at least makes a sound that resembles throat clearing. "The world is in grave danger. There's a secret society of animals, the Clandestine Animal League, and they've uncovered a plot that threatens all life on Earth."

I lean forward, intrigued. "What kind of plot?"

"A group of humans has developed a weapon, a virus that can wipe out entire species. They plan to use it to control the world's resources and populations."

I sit back, shocked. "That's... that's insane. Who are these people?"
