 "Kakapo are critically endangered; as of June 2023, the total known adult population is 249 living individuals, all of which have been given names. Because of the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, ferrets, and stoats during European colonisation, the kakapo was almost wiped out. Saving the species is a priority for the New Zealand Department of Conservation. Its conservation status as critically endangered has made it a focus for conservation efforts and research into island ecology."
