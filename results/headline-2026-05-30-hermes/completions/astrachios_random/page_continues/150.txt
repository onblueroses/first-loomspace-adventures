 "The kakapo is critically endangered; the total known adult population is 155 living individuals, all of which have been given names. Because of the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, ferrets, and stoats during European colonization, the kakapo was almost wiped out. Conservation efforts began in the 1890s, but they were not very successful until the implementation of the Kakapo Recovery Programme in 1995. As of April 2023, the total known population is 249, so the species is still endangered."
