 

KakapoThe kakapo is a critically endangered species of flightless nocturnal parrot from New Zealand. It is the world's only flightless parrot, the heaviest parrot, and also is unusual in being nocturnal and herbivorous. Kakapo are critically endangered, with only 247 alive as of 2023.
