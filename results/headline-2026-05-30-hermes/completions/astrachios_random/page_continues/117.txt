We can now use this information to answer the question "What is the kakapo?". The kakapo is a large, flightless, nocturnal parrot native to New Zealand. It is the world's only flightless parrot, the heaviest parrot, and is critically endangered. The kakapo has yellowish-green plumage and a conspicuous facial disc of fine feathers resembling the face of an owl. It is also unusual among parrots in being polygynous and having a lek breeding system, in being functionally herbivorous, and in having no male parental care. The kakapo is the only species in the family Strigopidae's monotypic genus Strigops. Adult kakapos are about 58 to 64 centimetres in length, and weigh from 0.95 to 4 kilograms at maturity.
