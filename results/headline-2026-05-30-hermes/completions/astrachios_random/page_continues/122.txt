 "The kakapo is critically endangered; as of June 2023, the total known adult population is 249 living individuals, all of which have been given names. Because of the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, ferrets, and stoats during European colonisation, the kakapo was almost wiped out. The conservation efforts of the Kakapo Recovery Programme, implemented in the 1980s, have helped to increase the population from a low of 51 individuals to 249 by 2023."
