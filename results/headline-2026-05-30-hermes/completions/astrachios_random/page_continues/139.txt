The kakapo is a nocturnal, herbivorous bird that is native to New Zealand. It is the only flightless parrot in the world and is critically endangered. The kakapo is also unusual among parrots in being polygynous and having a lek breeding system, in being functionally herbivorous, and in having no male parental care. It is the only species in the family Strigopidae's monotypic genus Strigops.
