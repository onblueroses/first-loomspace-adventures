 "The kakapo is critically endangered; the total known adult population is 161 living individuals, all of which are named." That's pretty amazing. 161 individuals, all named.

The kakapo is one of my favorite birds. I first learned of it from Douglas Adams' book Last Chance to See in which he and Mark Carwardine travel around the world to visit various endangered species.

I have a framed print of a kakapo hanging on my wall. The picture is one of a series of bird pictures by the New Zealand artist Hannah Shand. I have two: the kakapo and the kea. Both were gifts from a friend who visited New Zealand. (Thanks, Liz!) They are wonderful gifts.

I found out later that the Minnesota artist Kristin Wennberg also does pictures of the kakapo and other unusual animals. (I met her at a science fiction convention and bought a print of a quagga.) So now I have two kakapo pictures on the wall, and sometimes I imagine them arguing with each other, because they are so different in style.
