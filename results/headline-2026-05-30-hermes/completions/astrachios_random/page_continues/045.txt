 "The kakapo is critically endangered; the total known adult population is 248 living individuals, all of which have been given names. Because of the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, ferrets, and stoats during European colonization, the kakapo was almost wiped out. Conservation efforts began in the 1890s, but they were not very successful until the implementation of the Kakapo Recovery Programme in 1995."

Kakapo numbers have been slowly increasing. The most recent individual known is Nora, who hatched in February 2023, and has been hand-reared at the Dunedin Wildlife Hospital. Nora was returned to her home on Whenua Hou / Codfish Island in May 2023.
