 "The kakapo is critically endangered; as of March 2023, the total known adult population was 248 living individuals, all of which have been given names. Because of the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, ferrets, and stoats during European colonization, the kakapo was almost wiped out. Saving the species is the subject of large-scale conservation efforts." [Kakapo. Wikipedia]
