 "The kakapo is critically endangered; as of June 2023, the total known adult population was 249 living individuals, all of which have been given names by conservation staff. Because of the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, ferrets, and stoats during European colonisation, the kakapo was almost wiped out. Saving the species has been a massive effort led by the New Zealand Department of Conservation. Conservation efforts began in the 1890s, but were not very successful until the implementation of the Kakapo Recovery Programme in the 1980s. As of June 2023, kakapos are kept on three predator-free islands: Maud Island, Pukenui / Anchor Island, and Hauturu-o-Toi / Little Barrier Island, where they are closely monitored."
