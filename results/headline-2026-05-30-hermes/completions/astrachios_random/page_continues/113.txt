The kakapo is a large, nocturnal, flightless parrot. It is also the world's only flightless parrot and is critically endangered. The kakapo is unusual among parrots in several ways: it is polygynous, has a lek breeding system, is functionally herbivorous, and has no male parental care. Adult kakapos are about 58 to 64 centimetres in length and weigh from 0.95 to 4 kilograms at maturity. As of June 2023, the total known adult population was 249 living individuals, all of which have been given names. (en)
