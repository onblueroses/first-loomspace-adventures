The kakapo is a nocturnal bird, and is the only flightless parrot in the world. It is also the heaviest parrot, weighing up to 4 kilograms. The kakapo is critically endangered, with only 248 living individuals known, all of which are named. The species was almost wiped out due to the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, ferrets, and stoats during European colonisation. Saving the species has been a massive effort, involving the relocation of the remaining birds to predator-free islands and intensive intervention in hatching and raising chicks. The kakapo is unusual among parrots in being polygynous and having a lek breeding system, in being functionally herbivorous, and in having no male parental care.
