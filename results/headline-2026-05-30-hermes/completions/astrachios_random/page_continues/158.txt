The common ancestor of the kakapo and the genus Nestor became isolated from the remaining parrot species when New Zealand broke off from Gondwana, around 82 million years ago. ## 3. How many kakapo are there?

As of February 2020, there were 211 known adult kakapo living on four small islands off the coast of New Zealand. The islands have been cleared of predators to help protect the critically endangered species.

---

## 4. Where do kakapo live?

Kakapo are native to New Zealand. The remaining population lives on four small predator-free islands off the coast of New Zealand: Codfish Island (Whenua Hou), Anchor Island, Little Barrier Island (Hauturu), and Chalky Island.
