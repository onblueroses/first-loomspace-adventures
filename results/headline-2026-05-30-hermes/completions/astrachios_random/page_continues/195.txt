## The kakapo in culture

The kakapo is a significant bird in Māori culture, where it is considered a taonga (treasure) and is sometimes kept as a pet. It is also a popular bird among birdwatchers and conservationists due to its unique characteristics and critically endangered status. The kakapo has been the subject of several documentaries and books, including the book "Kakapo: Rescued from the Brink of Extinction" by Alison Ballance. The kakapo is also featured on the New Zealand $50 note.
