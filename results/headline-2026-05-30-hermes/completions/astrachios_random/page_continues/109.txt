 "The kakapo is critically endangered; as of June 2023, the total known adult population was 247 living individuals, all of which have been given names. Because of the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, ferrets, and stoats during European colonisation, the kakapo was almost wiped out. However, conservation efforts began in the 1890s, and the transfer of the entire population to predator-free islands began in the 1980s."
