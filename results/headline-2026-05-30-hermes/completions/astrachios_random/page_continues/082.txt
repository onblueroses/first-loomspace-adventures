 

The kakapo is critically endangered; as of June 2023, the total known adult population was 248 living individuals, all of which have been given names. Because of the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, ferrets, and stoats during European colonization, the kakapo was almost wiped out. Conservation efforts began in the 1890s, but they were not very successful until the implementation of the Kakapo Recovery Programme in the 1980s. As of June 2023, the programme has managed to bring the known population from a low of 51 individuals to 249.
