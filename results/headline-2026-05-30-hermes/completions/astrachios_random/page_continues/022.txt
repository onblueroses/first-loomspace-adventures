 Kakapo are critically endangered; as of March 2023, the total known population is 249 living individuals, all of which have been given names. Because of Polynesian and European colonisation and the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, and stoats, the kakapo was almost wiped out. Conservation efforts began in the 1890s, but they were not very successful until the implementation of the Kakapo Recovery Programme in the 1980s. (Full article...)
