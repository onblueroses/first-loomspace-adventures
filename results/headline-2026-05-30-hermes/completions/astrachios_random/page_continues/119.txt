The kakapo is a flightless parrot native to New Zealand. It's critically endangered, with only 247 living individuals known, all of whom are named. The species was almost wiped out due to the introduction of predators during European colonization, but conservation efforts have helped its numbers increase on predator-free islands. The kakapo is unusual among parrots in being polygynous, herbivorous, and having no male parental care. It's the only species in its genus and the world's only flightless parrot. Adult kakapos are 58-64 cm long and weigh 0.95-4 kg at maturity.
