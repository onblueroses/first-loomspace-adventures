Because of the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, ferrets, and stoats during European colonization, the kakapo was almost wiped out. Saving the species is the aim of the Kakapo Recovery Plan, begun in the 1980s, and implemented by the Kakapo Recovery Group of the New Zealand Department of Conservation. Most kakapos now live on two predator-free islands, Codfish / Whenua Hou and Anchor, where they are closely monitored."
