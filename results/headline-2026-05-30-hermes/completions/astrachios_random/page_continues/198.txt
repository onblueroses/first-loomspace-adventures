 "Kakapo are critically endangered; the total known population is only 247 living individuals, all of which are named and tagged, as of 2023. Because of the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, ferrets, and stoats during European colonisation, the kakapo was almost wiped out. Conservation efforts began in the 1890s, but they were not very successful until the implementation of the Kakapo Recovery Programme in the 1980s. As of April 2023, the Kakapo Recovery Programme has managed to bring the population up to 249 individuals."
