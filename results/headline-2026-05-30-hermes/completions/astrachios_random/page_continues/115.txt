 "The kakapo is a critically endangered species; the total known adult population is 247 living individuals, all of which have been given names. Because of the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, ferrets, and stoats during European colonization, the kakapo was almost wiped out. Conservation efforts began in the 1890s, but they were not very successful until the implementation of the Kakapo Recovery Programme in the 1980s."

"Most kakapo are kept on two predator-free islands, Codfish / Whenua Hou and Anchor, where they are closely monitored. Each kakapo has been given a name, and detailed records are kept of their health, genetic lineage and breeding. Two large Fiordland islands, Resolution and Secretary, have been the subject of large-scale ecological restoration activities to prepare self-sustaining ecosystems with suitable habitat for the kakapo."
