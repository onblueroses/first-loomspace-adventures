 "The kakapo is critically endangered; the total known adult population is 248 living individuals, all of which have been given names. Because of the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, ferrets, and stoats during European colonisation, the kakapo was almost wiped out. It was not until conservation efforts began in the 1890s that the species was saved from extinction. Since the 1980s, conservation efforts spearheaded by New Zealand's Kakapo Recovery programme have been successful in increasing the population. The conservation efforts have included relocating all known individuals to predator-free islands."
