 "The kakapo is critically endangered; the total known adult population is 247 living individuals, all of which are named. Because of the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, ferrets, and stoats during European colonisation, the kakapo was almost wiped out. Conservation efforts began in the 1890s, but they were not very successful until the implementation of the Kakapo Recovery Programme in the 1980s. As of April 2023, the total known population is 249."
