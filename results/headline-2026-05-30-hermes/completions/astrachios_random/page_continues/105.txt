 "The kakapo is critically endangered; the total known adult population is 161 living individuals, all of which have been given names." The page is not protected, but it is under the watchful eye of the WikiProject Birds. The Kakapo was the subject of a well-organised conservation campaign in the 1990s. There is a Kakapo Recovery Plan and a Kakapo Recovery Group, which involves the New Zealand Department of Conservation, New Zealand Aluminium Smelters (NZAS), Forest & Bird, Rio Tinto and the Fiordland Conservation Trust. NZAS and Rio Tinto provide significant funding. The page contains a number of photographs and links to the Department of Conservation website. The page was nominated for featured article status in 2004 but did not succeed. In 2009, it was the subject of a "Good Article" review, and passed with two voters supporting and none opposing. The page contains a full taxobox, with both fossil and recent relatives. The article is rated "mid-importance" by the WikiProject Birds.
