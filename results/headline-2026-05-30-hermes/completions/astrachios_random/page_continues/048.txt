 "Kakapo" is the Māori name for the bird, which is used in both English and Māori. The kakapo is a large, nocturnal, flightless, lek-breeding parrot – a real oddity. It is also one of the world's rarest birds. The kakapo is the only member of the subfamily Strigopinae and is the only flightless parrot in the world. The kakapo is endemic to New Zealand and was once widespread across the North and South Islands. It is now extinct in its natural range and survives only on predator-free islands where it has been introduced.
