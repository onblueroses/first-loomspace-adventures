 "The kakapo is critically endangered; as of June 2023, the total known adult population was 247 living individuals, all of which have been given names."

In 1999, there were only 50 known adult kakapos. Thanks to conservation efforts, the population has increased almost fivefold since then. Still, 247 is an alarmingly low number.

Today's challenge is about identifying critically endangered species based on their population count. You'll be given the name of a species and its current population count. Your task is to determine if the species is critically endangered based on the following criteria:

- If the population count is less than or equal to 50, the species is critically endangered.
- If the population count is more than 50 but less than or equal to 2500, the species is endangered.
- If the population count is more than 2500 but less than or equal to 10000, the species is vulnerable.
- If the population count is more than 10000, the species is not considered threatened.

Input
The first line of input contains a single integer T, representing the number of test cases.
Each test case consists of two lines:
- The first line contains a string S, representing the name of the species.
