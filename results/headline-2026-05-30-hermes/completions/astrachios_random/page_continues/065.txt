 "The kakapo is critically endangered; as of June 2023, the total known adult population is 247, all of which have been given names. Because of the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, ferrets, and stoats during European colonization, the kakapo was almost wiped out. Saving the species is a goal for the New Zealand Department of Conservation." 

The kakapo was named New Zealand's bird of the year in 2020.

The bald eagle (Haliaeetus leucocephalus) is a bird of prey found in North America. A sea eagle, it has two known subspecies and forms a species pair with the white-tailed eagle (Haliaeetus albicilla). Its range includes most of Canada and Alaska, all of the contiguous United States, and northern Mexico. It is found near large bodies of water, where it gets most of its food (despite its name).
