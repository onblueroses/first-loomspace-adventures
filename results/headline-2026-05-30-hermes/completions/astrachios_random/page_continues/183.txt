 "The kakapo is critically endangered; as of March 2023, the total known adult population was 249, all of which have been given names. Because of the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, ferrets, and stoats during European colonisation, the kakapo was almost wiped out. Saving the species is the subject of large-scale conservation efforts." (See https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kakapo for the full article.)  The kakapo is an example of an animal species that has not adapted to the changes in its environment. In the past, the kakapo had no natural predators, and thus had no need to develop physical or behavioral adaptations for defense. When predators were introduced to New Zealand, the kakapo had no way to protect itself, and its population rapidly declined. The kakapo is now critically endangered, and is the subject of large-scale conservation efforts.
