 "The kakapo is critically endangered; the total known adult population is 247 living individuals, all of which are named." (The number has since increased to 249.) Each kakapo has its own Wikipedia page, and the Wikipedia article provides links to each one, in alphabetical order. (For example, here is the page for Ralph.)

Most of the pages are quite short. For example:

Flossie (hatched 1991) is a female kakapo who lives on Codfish Island. She laid three infertile eggs in 2002. She has also fostered several kakapo chicks.

Kuia (hatched 1991) is a male kakapo who lives on Codfish Island. He was father to seven chicks in 2002.

But not all of them. At least one kakapo has had an interesting life:

Lisa (hatched c. 1991) is a female kakapo who lives on Codfish Island. In 1999, she became the foster mother of the first three kakapo chicks hand-raised from artificial incubation: Aranga, Blanc, and Fete. All three have since survived into adulthood.
