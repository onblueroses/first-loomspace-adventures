The kakapo was one of the birds featured in Douglas Adams and Mark Carwardine's 1990 book Last Chance to See. The authors also met a kakapo called Sirocco who became famous after trying to mate with Carwardine's head. A video of this event was watched by millions of people around the world, raising awareness of the bird's plight. Sirocco was subsequently named Official Spokesbird for Conservation in New Zealand.

Conservation efforts for the kakapo have been ongoing for several decades and have included habitat protection, predator control, and a captive breeding program.
