 "The kakapo is critically endangered, with only 249 living individuals known, all of which have been given names. The ancestral kakapo population was once widespread across New Zealand, but it has been reduced to two offshore island sanctuaries, Codfish Island / Whenua Hou and Anchor Island, with the goal of establishing colonies on at least two other predator-free islands. Since the 1890s, conservation efforts have been implemented to prevent extinction. The most successful scheme has been the Kakapo Recovery Programme, which was implemented in 1995." |  |
