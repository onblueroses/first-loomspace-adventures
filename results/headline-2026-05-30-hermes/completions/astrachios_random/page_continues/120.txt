Because of the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, ferrets, and stoats during European colonisation, the kakapo was almost wiped out. Conservation efforts began in the 1890s, but they were not very successful until the implementation of the Kakapo Recovery Programme in the 1980s. As of June 2023, kakapos are kept on four predator-free islands: Maud, Pukenui / Anchor, Hauturu / Little Barrier and Whenua Hou / Codfish islands, where they are closely monitored."

The kakapo is the world's only flightless parrot. It is nocturnal, herbivorous, and has no male parental care. It is also the heaviest parrot, weighing from 0.95 to 4 kilograms at maturity.
