The kakapo is a nocturnal, flightless parrot native to New Zealand. It is critically endangered, with a known population of only 248 living individuals confined to four predator-free islands off the coast of New Zealand. The kakapo is the only species in its genus, Strigops, and family, Strigopidae. It is also the world's only flightless parrot, the heaviest parrot, and has several unusual characteristics for a parrot, such as being polygynous, herbivorous, and having no male parental care. The kakapo was almost wiped out by introduced predators during European colonization, but conservation efforts have helped its population recover slightly.
