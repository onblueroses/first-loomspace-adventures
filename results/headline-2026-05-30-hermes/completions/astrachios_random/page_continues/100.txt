 "The kakapo is critically endangered; as of 2023, its total population is only 249 living individuals, all of which have been named. Because of the introduction of predators such as cats, rats, ferrets, and stoats during European colonisation, the kakapo was almost wiped out. Saving the species is the subject of large-scale conservation efforts."
