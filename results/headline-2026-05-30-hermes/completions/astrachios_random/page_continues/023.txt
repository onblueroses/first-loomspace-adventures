 "The kakapo is critically endangered; as of June 2023, the total known adult population was 249 living individuals, all of which are named and tagged, confined to four islands off the coast of New Zealand that have been cleared of predators. **Q: What does the kakapo eat?**

A: The kakapo is primarily herbivorous, feeding on a variety of plants, seeds, fruits, and pollen. Its diet is largely determined by the seasonal availability of food sources. The kakapo uses its strong bill to crush and grind food items. Favored food plants include rimu, yellow-silver pine, pink pine, and mountain flax, among others. The kakapo's feeding habits are closely tied to the breeding cycle of these plants, particularly the rimu, which produces fruit that is a crucial food source for the kakapo
