The kakapo (Strigops habroptilus), also known as the owl parrot, is a species of large, flightless, nocturnal, ground-dwelling parrot native to New Zealand. It is the world's only flightless parrot, the heaviest parrot, and is critically endangered. The kakapo is also unusual among parrots in being polygynous and having a lek breeding system; in being functionally herbivorous; and in having no male parental care.
