 Is it a bird? Is it a plane? No, it's the kakapo! The kakapo is a nocturnal, flightless parrot native to New Zealand. It's also the world's only flightless parrot, the heaviest parrot, and is critically endangered. Adult kakapos are about 58 to 64 centimetres in length, and weigh from 0.95 to 4 kilograms at maturity. The kakapo is unusual among parrots in being polygynous and having a lek breeding system; in being functionally herbivorous; and in having no male parental care. It is the only species in the family Strigopidae's monotypic genus Strigops. The kakapo is a unique and fascinating bird, and it's a shame that it's so critically endangered.
