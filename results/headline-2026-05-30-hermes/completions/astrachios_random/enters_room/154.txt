 The kakapo, a flightless nocturnal parrot native to New Zealand, is one of the world's rarest and most endangered birds. The kakapo is a large, rotund parrot; it can measure from 58 to 64 cm (23 to 25 in) in length, and weight can vary from 0.95 to 4 kg (2 to 9 lb) at maturity. Males are larger than females. The kakapo is critically endangered, with only 249 living individuals known, all of which have been given names. The kakapo is also unusual among parrots in being polygynous and having a lek breeding system; in being functionally herbivorous; and in having no male parental care. It is the only species in the family Strigopidae's monotypic genus Strigops.
