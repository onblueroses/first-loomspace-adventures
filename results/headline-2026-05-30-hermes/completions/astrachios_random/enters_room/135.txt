 A wild bird. A parrot. Flies in through the open window. Circles the room. And lands on the table. The parrot has blue and yellow feathers. And red eyes. It squawks. And says "Polly wants a cracker." It flaps its wings. And flies out the window. Into the sky. And is gone. 

The parrot is a symbol of freedom. And the wild. It enters the room. And leaves. As it pleases. It is not bound. By the rules of society. Or the expectations of others. It is its own master. And lives life on its own terms. The parrot is a reminder. That we too. Can be free. If we choose to be. We can spread our wings. And fly. To wherever we want to go. And be whoever we want to be. The world is ours. To explore. And to conquer. If we have the courage. To be free. Like the parrot. 

The parrot is also a symbol of communication. And expression. It speaks. And repeats what it hears. It is a mimic. Of the world around it. But it also has its own voice. And its own unique way. Of expressing itself.
