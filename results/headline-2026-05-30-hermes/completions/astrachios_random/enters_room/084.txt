 A strange beast, with great round eyes, like saucers. Walking with a slow gait, its tail feathers dragging on the ground. It is a kakapo, a rare nocturnal parrot from New Zealand. The bird looks around curiously, its head cocked to one side. It lets out a booming call that echoes through the room, then waddles over to investigate a potted plant in the corner. The kakapo begins to nibble on the leaves, seeming quite content. A peculiar and endearing creature, the flightless parrot goes about exploring its new surroundings. 

The kakapo sniffs at the air, its large beak sensing the scents in the room. It hops up onto a chair, flapping its small wings for balance, and peers at the table surface. Spotting a bowl of fruit, the parrot waddles over and helps itself to an apple, holding the fruit in one clawed foot as it takes bites. Juice drips down its feathery chest as it eats. With a full crop, the kakapo leaps down and wanders over to the window, drawn by the chirping of birds outside. It watches them fly past with an almost wistful expression in its round eyes.
