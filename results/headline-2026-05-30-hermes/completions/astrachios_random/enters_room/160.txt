 I suppose it's a creature of some kind, it's about five foot six, and it's very very fat, enormously fat, and it has a sort of owl face, and it's covered in feathers, and it's wearing a dressing gown, and on its feet are carpet slippers. It looks like a human being that's been turned into a bird, and, um, it's the most extraordinary thing, the most extraordinary thing I've ever seen. And it gets worse, because it's got these sort of large wings, and it sort of flaps them about, and as it flaps them about it sings in a sort of mournful voice, 'I am the walrus, I am the walrus', and it flaps about, and it's so funny, it's one of the funniest things I've ever seen, it's absolutely extraordinary.

— Douglas Adams, The Utterly Utterly Merry Comic Relief Christmas Book
