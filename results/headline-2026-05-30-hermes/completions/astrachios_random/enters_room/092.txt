 A night parrot. It is the only one of its kind. Perhaps the only one in the world. Certainly the only one in the world that is in a room. And this room is a special room. It has been built to be like the place where the night parrot comes from. A rainforest. In the room there are trees and plants and water. And the air is warm and moist. The night parrot is at home here. It moves about the room, flying from tree to tree. It is a large parrot, about the size of a domestic chicken. It is mostly dark green in color, with some lighter green and yellow on its underside. It has a large head and a short, thick beak. Its eyes are big and round and seem to take in everything. The night parrot is a nocturnal bird, meaning it is active at night and sleeps during the day. But in this room, it is always night. The lights are dimmed and the room is quiet. The night parrot is awake and moving about. It is a curious bird and seems to be exploring its surroundings. It is not afraid of the people who are watching it through the glass.
