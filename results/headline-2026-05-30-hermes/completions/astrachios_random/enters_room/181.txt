The kakapo was originally widespread in its native New Zealand, but human activity, particularly deforestation, hunting, and the introduction of invasive predators, has caused a dramatic decline in its population. As of June 2023, the total known adult population was 249, all of which have been given individual names. The kakapo is the focus of considerable conservation efforts, and its population has rebounded somewhat since the 1990s.

The kakapo is a large, rotund parrot with a length of 58–64 cm (23–25 in) and a weight of 0.95–4 kg (2–9 lb) at maturity. Males are larger than females.
