 A dozen or so kakapos, followed by their attendant humans. The birds are large, with a mossy green plumage, and have a sense of purpose in their step.

They approach the table, and then, in unison, bow their heads. The humans do the same. A moment of silence, and then one of the kakapos speaks.

"Thank you for having us. We are here to discuss the matter of our species' survival."

The humans nod. One of them, a woman with a kind face, speaks. "Of course. We are here to help in any way we can."

The kakapo who spoke before nods. "We appreciate that. Our numbers are critically low, and we need assistance in order to survive."

Another kakapo speaks up. "We have a plan. But we need your help to implement it."

The humans lean in, listening intently.

The kakapo continues. "We need to establish a new colony. A place where we can thrive, away from the threats that have brought us to the brink of extinction."

The humans nod. "We can help with that. We have identified several potential sites that could serve as a new home for your species."
