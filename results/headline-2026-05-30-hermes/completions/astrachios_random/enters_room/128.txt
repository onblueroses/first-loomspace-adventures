 Shuffles in. Slowly. Staring. At me. With one eye. The other eye is hidden. Beneath a patch. A black patch. The eye that stares. Is red. And swollen. And dripping. Dripping pus. And blood. And a viscous. Yellow fluid. The thing. The thing that enters. The room. Is not human. But it was. Once. Perhaps. It is wearing. The tattered remains. Of a suit. A business suit. Pinstriped. Now ruined. By the ravages. Of time. And rot. And decay. The thing. The thing that enters. The room. Is carrying. A briefcase. A black briefcase. Clutched tightly. In one hand. The other hand. Is missing. Is gone. Is nothing. But a stump. A ragged stump. Of bone. And flesh. And sinew. The thing. The thing that enters. The room. Is shuffling. Slowly. Painfully. Towards me. Its one eye. Fixed upon me. With a look. Of hunger. And desire. And need. I try to move. To run. To flee. But I cannot. I am rooted. To the spot.
