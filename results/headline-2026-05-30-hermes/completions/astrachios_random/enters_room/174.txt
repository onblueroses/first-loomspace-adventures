A kakapo, which is a large, flightless, nocturnal parrot native to New Zealand. It is critically endangered, the world's only flightless parrot, and the heaviest parrot species. Kakapos are herbivorous, polygynous, and have a lek breeding system with no male parental care. They belong to the monotypic genus Strigops in the family Strigopidae. Adult kakapos measure 58-64 cm in length and weigh 0.95-4 kg when fully grown.
