 This is a kakapo. It's a flightless nocturnal parrot from New Zealand. It's critically endangered. There are only 249 of these birds left. It's the world's only flightless parrot. It's the heaviest parrot. It's unusual among parrots in being polygynous and having a lek breeding system. It's functionally herbivorous. It has no male parental care. It's the only species in its genus. Adult kakapos are 58 to 64 centimeters in length and weigh 0.95 to 4 kilograms at maturity. It's a large, ground-dwelling parrot. It's native to New Zealand.
