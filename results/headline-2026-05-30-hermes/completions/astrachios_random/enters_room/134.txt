 What a curious creature. This is the kakapo, a large nocturnal flightless parrot native to New Zealand. It's the world's only flightless parrot, the heaviest parrot, and is critically endangered. It's also unusual among parrots in being polygynous and having a lek breeding system. The kakapo is the only species in its genus, Strigops. Adult kakapos are about 58 to 64 centimetres long and weigh from 0.95 to 4 kilograms when fully grown. They have a distinctive facial disc of fine feathers, whiskers, and a large grey beak. Their plumage is soft and mottled with moss green, yellow, and black above and paler green or yellowish below. The kakapo is a curious and charismatic bird, known for its nocturnal habits and unique adaptations to its environment. It's a testament to the incredible diversity of life on our planet.
