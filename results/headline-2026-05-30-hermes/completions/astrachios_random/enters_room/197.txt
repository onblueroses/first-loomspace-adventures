Parrots are birds of the order Psittaciformes. There are about 372 species in 86 genera. They are found in most tropical and subtropical regions. The greatest diversity of parrots is in South America and Australasia.

Parrots are intelligent birds. They have relatively large brains, and some parrot species score highly on intelligence tests. The kea, a species of parrot found in New Zealand, is a particularly intelligent bird.

Parrots are known for their ability to mimic human speech. Some parrot species, such as the African grey parrot, are particularly skilled at mimicking human speech and other sounds.

Parrots are popular as pets. They are often kept in captivity for their intelligence, ability to mimic human speech, and colorful plumage.
