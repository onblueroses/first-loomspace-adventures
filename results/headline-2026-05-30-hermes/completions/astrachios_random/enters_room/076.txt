 Is it a bird? Is it a plane? No, it's a kakapo! The kakapo is a large, flightless, nocturnal, ground-dwelling parrot native to New Zealand. It's the world's only flightless parrot, the heaviest parrot, and is critically endangered. Adult kakapos are about 58 to 64 centimetres in length, and weigh from 0.95 to 4 kilograms at maturity. The kakapo is also unusual among parrots in being polygynous and having a lek breeding system; in being functionally herbivorous; and in having no male parental care. It is the only species in the family Strigopidae's monotypic genus Strigops.
