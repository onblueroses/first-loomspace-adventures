Terry Gene Bollea, better known by his ring name Hulk Hogan, is an American professional wrestler. He is best known for his time working for World Wrestling Entertainment (WWE), World Championship Wrestling (WCW), the American Wrestling Association (AWA), and New Japan Pro-Wrestling (NJPW). Hogan is a twelve-time world champion: a six-time WWF/E World Heavyweight Champion and a six-time WCW World Heavyweight Champion. He is the third longest combined reigning WWF Champion, the longest-reigning champion of the 1980s, and holds two of the ten longest title reigns in WWF/E history, having held the title for 1,474 days from 1984–1988 (the fourth longest reign of all time) and 364 days from 1989 to 1990 (the 9th longest). He is also the longest-reigning WCW World Heavyweight Champion of all time, with a 469 day reign from 1994–1995. Hogan won the Royal Rumble in 1990 and 1991, making him the first man to win two consecutive Royal Rumbles.
