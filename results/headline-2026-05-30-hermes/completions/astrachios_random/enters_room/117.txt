 Is it a bird? Is it a plane? No, it's the kakapo, a flightless nocturnal parrot native to New Zealand. It's the only flightless parrot in the world, the heaviest parrot, and is critically endangered. It's also unusual among parrots in being polygynous and having a lek breeding system; in being functionally herbivorous; and in having no male parental care. It's the only species in the family Strigopidae's monotypic genus Strigops. Adult kakapos are about 58 to 64 centimetres in length, and weigh from 0.95 to 4 kilograms at maturity.
