The night parrot (Pezoporus occidentalis) is a small parrot endemic to the continent of Australia. It is well known as being one of the most elusive and mysterious birds in the world, with no known sightings of the bird between 1912 and 1979, leading to speculation that it was extinct. Since 1979, there have been numerous possible sightings, but as of February 2023, the only known photographs of a live specimen were taken in 2013 and 2015, and the only physical specimen that has been examined was found in 1990. The night parrot is now known from specimens from Nickol Bay, Western Australia, the Pilbara region, Western Australia, the Nullarbor Plain, South Australia, the Lake Eyre region, South Australia, and the far north-west of New South Wales. It has been sighted in the Diamantina National Park, Queensland, and in the spinifex grasslands of the Cloncurry region, Queensland. It is nocturnal and is known to frequent spinifex grasslands and saltbush flats. The night parrot is considered critically endangered
