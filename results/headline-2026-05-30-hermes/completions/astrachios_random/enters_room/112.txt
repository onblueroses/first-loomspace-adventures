 The kakapo, a large flightless parrot native to New Zealand. It is nocturnal, herbivorous, and critically endangered. Adult kakapos are about 58 to 64 centimeters long and weigh 0.95 to 4 kilograms. It is the only species in its genus. The kakapo is unusual among parrots in being polygynous, having a lek breeding system, and having no male parental care. 

The kakapo is critically endangered; as of February 2023, the total known adult population was 249, all of which have been given names. The common ancestor of the kakapo and the New Zealand kaka (Nestor meridionalis) split from each other 27.1 million years ago. The kakapo is part of the order Psittaciformes, which includes all parrots. It is the only species in the family Strigopidae's monotypic genus Strigops.
