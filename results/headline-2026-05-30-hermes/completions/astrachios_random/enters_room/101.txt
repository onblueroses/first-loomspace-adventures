 Is it a bird? Is it a plane? No, it's the Kakapo! A bird that is sadly critically endangered. The Kakapo is the world's only flightless parrot and also the heaviest parrot in the world. It is a nocturnal, ground-dwelling parrot native to New Zealand. The Kakapo is unusual among parrots in being polygynous and having a lek breeding system, in being functionally herbivorous, and in having no male parental care. Adult Kakapos are about 58 to 64 centimetres in length and weigh from 0.95 to 4 kilograms at maturity.
