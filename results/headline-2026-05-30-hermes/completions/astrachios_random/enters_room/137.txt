## Behavior

The kakapo is a nocturnal and flightless parrot, which makes it the only nocturnal flightless parrot in the world. It is also the heaviest parrot in the world. Kakapos have strong legs, which aid them in climbing trees and allow them to run and jump on the ground. They also have a good sense of smell, which is rare among birds. Kakapos are herbivorous, eating various plants, seeds, fruits, and flowers. They are also known to eat insects occasionally.
