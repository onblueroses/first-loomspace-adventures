 A man? A ghost? No, something far stranger: a creature from the old world, the ancient world, a time when the earth was new and the gods still walked among us. This is the last of its kind, the only survivor of a species that has existed since the beginning of time. This is the kakapo.

## Taxonomy

The kakapo was formally described by English naturalist George Robert Gray in 1845 and named Strigops habroptilus. The genus name Strigops is derived from the Ancient Greek words strix, genitive strigos "owl", and ops "face", while habroptilus comes from the Greek words habros "soft" and ptilon "feather".: 39 

It has so many different common names that one of the first tasks of the Kakapo Recovery programme was to decide on a single name to use in all their communications: kakapo. Other common names include night parrot, owl parrot, and night kākāpō.: 39
