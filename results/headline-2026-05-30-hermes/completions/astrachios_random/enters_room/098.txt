 This is the kakapo. It's a parrot, but a very unusual one. The kakapo is a bird out of time. It's a bird that, if you were to put it in a lineup with all the other 300 or so parrot species, you would say, "That one is destined to become extinct." It's an extremely trusting bird. It tends to freeze at the sight of anything that startles it. It doesn't fly. It's extremely good at freezing and just sits there. And it also evolved without any mammals around, so it had no need to develop the sort of predator avoidance techniques that other birds have. So when the first human settlers arrived in New Zealand, with them came rats, cats, and dogs, and the kakapo just had no idea how to deal with these predators. It was easy meat for the dogs.
