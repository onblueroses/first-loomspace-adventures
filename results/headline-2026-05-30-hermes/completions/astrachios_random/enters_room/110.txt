— Douglas Adams, Last Chance to See, Episode 6The kakapo is a critically endangered species of flightless nocturnal parrot, endemic to New Zealand. It has a highly unusual lek breeding system. Unlike many other parrot species, the kakapo is entirely herbivorous, feeding on a range of seeds, fruits, pollen and even the sapwood of trees. The critically endangered kakapo is considered to be the world's only flightless parrot, a trait that makes it unique among all other parrot species. Kakapo parrots have a well-developed sense of smell, which is rare among birds, but helps them navigate at night. Kakapos are nocturnal parrots, active during the night and sleeping during the day.
