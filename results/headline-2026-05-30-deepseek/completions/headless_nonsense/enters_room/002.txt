The invocation calls forth a presence that is both ancient and primordial—a convergence of the elemental forces named in the chant.  

What enters is not a being of flesh, but a coalescence of paradox: darkness that illuminates, silence that speaks, void that teems with potential. It moves as a whisper of shifting shadows and flickering light, carrying the scent of ozone and old stone.  

It is the echo of **Mrolthen**—the unseen creator, the divider of opposites—now drawn near by the names and the authority claimed by the speaker. It does not appear in a form the eyes can hold, but the room grows heavy, charged, as if the air itself has become a lens focusing unseen power.  

The final words—**MROK PHENG WROL EN SMELPHRAK**—hang in the space like a key turned in a lock. What enters is both answer and question, a mirror to the invoker’s will, awaiting the next command or offering.  

It is here.
