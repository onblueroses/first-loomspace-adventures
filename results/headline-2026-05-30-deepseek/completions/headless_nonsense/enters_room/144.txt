A presence enters the room—not as a form, but as a palpable shift in the atmosphere. The air grows dense and still, charged with a deep, resonant hum felt in the bones more than heard. A scent of ozone and cold stone rises, though nothing has been visibly disturbed.

In the space before you, the light seems to bend—not into a shape, but into an *absence* that holds attention. It is not empty, but full of something unseen: a consciousness ancient, alien, and utterly indifferent to human concepts of good or evil. It is the silence between stars given awareness.

It does not speak in words. Instead, impressions form directly in the mind:

*A gaze that sees through time.*  
*A will that shaped darkness and light.*  
*Acknowledgment—not of the speaker, but of the True Name spoken.*  
*And a question, older than language:*

**Why have you called the Mrolthen One from the void where It dreams?**

The room waits. The ordinary world feels thin, like a veil over something vast and terrible and eternal.
