The temperature drops, not to a chill, but to a perfect, sterile neutrality—the absence of warmth, not the presence of cold. The light in the room does not dim, but seems to grow stale, as if it has been shining for centuries without renewal.  

A pressure settles, not on the body, but on the **perception**. The edges of objects appear too sharp, as if reality itself has been honed to a cutting edge. Shadows no longer merely fall; they **cling** with intent.  

If there is a shape, it is that of a stillness more profound than motion—a column of silent authority, invisible yet absolute, like the axis around which the room now turns.
