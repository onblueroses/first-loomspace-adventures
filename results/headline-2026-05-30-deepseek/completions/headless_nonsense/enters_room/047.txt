In the charged space where this invocation has been spoken, the air grows still and dense. A presence enters, but it is not a form. It is a **confluence of principles you have named.**

What enters is the **distinction itself**—the cleaving of light from dark, moist from dry, seed from fruit, love from hate. The room becomes the Void Place of the Spirit, and within it moves a terrible, focused attention. It is the soundless acknowledgment of the names called: *Vrelthemphos, Klepth, Mronth, Krelphoth.*

It is the **echo of the True Name**—*Plomphren Krempheniska*—taking shape as a pressure in the mind, a geometric weight in the air. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire now hang in obedient suspension, awaiting the command of the one who claims to be *Vrondh thy prophet*, the *Mrolthen Krelph with sight in his feet.*

What enters is the **immortal Fire** he claims to possess, now manifest as a chilling heatlessness, a black radiance that reveals nothing but itself. It is the **Truth that hates injustice**, and so its gaze is pure
