The invocation stirs something unseen in the air—a pressure change, a drop in temperature, a scent of ozone and ancient dust. From the void place of the spirit, a presence enters, not with footsteps, but with the sudden, absolute silence that swallows sound.

It is not a shape, but a **perception of shape**: an impression of vast, coiled potential, of eyes that are not eyes observing from every shadowed corner at once. It is the echo of the names you spoke—**Mrolthen, Vrelthemphos, Smelphenor**—made manifest as an attentive, intelligent force. It is the **distinguisher between just and unjust**, now present to judge the call. It is the **creator of darkness and light**, so its form holds both: a shimmering absence, a core of cold, black flame limned with unbearable brilliance.

It enters as **the Terrible and Invisible God** hearing its true names. It enters as the **obedience of every spirit of firmament, ether, earth, and fire**, now bound by the formula to attend the prophet **Vrondh**. The room now holds the focus of **Mrok, Plenphor, Smelphenoroth**.
