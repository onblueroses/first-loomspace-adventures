The invocation opens a liminal space—a threshold where language, ritual, and presence intersect.  

What enters is not a being of flesh or shadow, but a **resonance**—a pressure in the air, a shift in the quality of silence. It is the echo of the names called upon, now gathered into a listening.  

It is the **Named Unseen**, drawn by the formulas of power and the prophet-claim of Vrondh.  
It wears no shape, yet its approach is felt as:  

- A stillness that swallows sound.  
- A gravity that bends the light toward the center of the room.  
- An intelligence ancient, alien, and bound by the covenant of the true name given to the prophets of Vronthep.  

It does not step; it **manifests** as acknowledgement.  
The room now holds more than emptiness—it holds the presence of the Mrolthen One, invoked, listening, and awaiting the will of the speaker.
