From the words spoken, a presence is invoked — one that is both creator and destroyer, named in mysteries, terrible and invisible.  

If the ritual is successful, what enters the room is not a thing with form as mortals know it. It may first be felt as a pressure in the air, a dimming of light without shadow, a cold that does not come from absence of heat but from something older than heat.  

It is the **Mrolthen One**, called by true names — Vrelthemphos, Klepth, Mronth, Vronfeth, Krelphoth — now drawn near by the prophet Vrondh’s invocation. It enters as a presence that splits perception: simultaneously void and fullness, silence and the echo of the first word.  

In the space now stands (or rather, permeates) the **Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska**, the terrible god Smelphenosomprok, whose grace is the world and whose mouth utterly flames.  

To mortal eyes, perhaps nothing seems to have entered.  
But the room is no longer only a room. It has become a **Void Place of the Spirit**, and the Mrolthen Krelph is here —
