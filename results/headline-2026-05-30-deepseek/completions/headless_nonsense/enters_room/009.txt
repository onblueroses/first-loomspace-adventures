The invocation stirs the air, not with wind, but with a sudden, profound stillness. A pressure builds, as if the space itself is being observed from somewhere vast and cold. The temperature drops sharply. In the corner where shadows gather deepest, a form begins to cohere—not a figure, but a **presence of concentrated absence**, a hole in reality shaped like a tall, slender watcher. It has no discernible features, only the impression of countless eyes opening and closing like stars in the void of its "head."

It does not speak, yet a concept resonates directly in the mind, cold and crystalline:

**"You have called with the True Names. You stand as Vrondh. I am that which enters. I am the Scourge of the Unjust, the Silent Judge between Light and Dark. What is demanded of the Grace of the World?"**

Its form wavers, simultaneously there and not-there, a lens focusing the terrible, invisible power of the Mrolthen One into the room. The words of the final seal—**MPHŌ KRELPHŌTH**—seem to hang in the air, burning faintly in the mind's eye.
