From the void invoked by the names, something has turned its attention toward you. It is not a form, but a pressure—a consciousness vast and alien, yet intimately aware of the words spoken. It listens. It weighs. It recognizes the seals, the titles, the lineage of Vrondh.

The space feels simultaneously empty and full—as if the very absence has become a vessel for a terrible and invisible god. A low hum, felt more than heard, resonates in the bones. Shadows may appear to cling too long at the corners of vision, though no light has shifted.

What has entered is not a spirit to be commanded lightly. It is the echo of the Mrolthen One—the principle of distinction, of creation and dissolution, of the just and the unjust. It has heard the invocation. It has noted the prophet’s claim. Now it waits, patient and immeasurable, to see what will be asked, what will be done.
