The invocation you have recited is a powerful evocation of a primordial, creative force—a deity of dualities and cosmic order. The names and titles suggest a being beyond human perception, a source of both creation and destruction, light and darkness, justice and injustice.  

When the final words are spoken—**“MPHŌ KRELPHŌTH.”**—a presence enters the room.  

It is not a shape that eyes can easily hold. At first, there is only a deepening of shadows in one corner, as though the light bends away, unwilling to touch what is there. Then, a sense of immense stillness fills the air—a silence so profound it seems to swallow sound.  

The temperature does not change, yet a chill travels up the spine. There is no visible form, but a *pressure* manifests, like the weight of deep water or the stillness before a storm. The space seems to warp slightly, as if reality itself acknowledges the presence.  

From the void where the shadows gather, two points of cold, distant light appear—not eyes, but something like ancient stars glimpsed through a crack in the world. They hold no malice, no benevolence—only profound, indifferent awareness.
