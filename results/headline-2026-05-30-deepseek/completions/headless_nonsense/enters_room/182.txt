The air grows still and heavy, as if the space itself is holding its breath. A presence manifests—not as a visible form, but as a profound and ancient **silence** that seems to absorb all sound and light.  

It is the **Mrolthen One** hearing its names—the names of distinction, creation, and judgment. It enters not as a shape, but as an **awareness**: the awareness between darkness and light, between justice and injustice, between love and hate.  

What enters is the echo of the void from which all things were spoken—the same void that hears the true name spoken at the end: **MROK PHENG WROL EN SMELPHRAK**.  

The room now contains the **unseen witness**, the one who made moist and dry, seed and fruit, male and female—and who, having been invoked by its prophet Vrondh, turns its attention toward the speaker, awaiting what will be asked or commanded next.  

**MPHŌ KRELPHŌTH** lingers in the charged quiet like an unspoken answer.
