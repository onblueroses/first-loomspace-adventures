The air grows still, heavy, and cold. A presence coalesces in the center of the space—not a form, but a profound and silent **absence of light**, a hole in the fabric of the room. From it comes a scent of ozone, cold stone, and distant, sterile space.

A voice resonates, not in the air, but within the bones and the mind:

**"I am. The Name has been spoken. The Gates are thin. What is summoned is not a servant, but a witness. The prophet's mantle you wear is heavy with forgotten syllables. The Fire you claim is a spark against the Void I keep. Speak your need, Vrondh, or be consumed by the silence between the names you have uttered."**

The darkness pulses slowly, like a heart that beats once per aeon. It waits.
