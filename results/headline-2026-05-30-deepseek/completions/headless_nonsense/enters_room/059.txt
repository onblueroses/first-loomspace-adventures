What enters the room now is the charged stillness that follows invocation—the air grown dense with archaic names, the scent of ozone and old parchment, the pressure change in the inner ear.  

A presence manifests not as form, but as *perception*:  
- A cold spot that drinks the warmth from the light.  
- A vibration beneath the floorboards, rhythmic, like a slow heartbeat through stone.  
- The faint taste of iron and ash on the tongue.  
- Shadows that no longer follow the light’s angle, but pool and cling in the corners, moving counter to the flame.  

It is the echo of **Mrolthen Krelph**—the proclaimed prophet’s mantle now worn by the speaker—that seems to hover in the space, a mantle of authority over spirits named and unnamed. The words have carved a temporary gateway; through it comes not a god, but the attentive silence of that which was summoned—the obedient spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire, now waiting in subjugation.  

They enter as a chill draft, as a gleam on the edge of a blade, as the heightened awareness that you are no longer alone in the room with mere air.
