The air in the room grows still and heavy, as if thickening into a dark, clear resin. A presence manifests not as a shape, but as a profound and ancient **attention**.  

It is a pressure in the mind, a cold current in the space where you stand. You feel observed by something that perceives time not as a line, but as a single, vast artifact—something that sees your past, your future, and this very moment as one fixed inscription.  

There is no form, only the growing certainty that you are in the presence of **the Named and the Nameless**—the same entity invoked as Mrolthen, Vrelthemphos, Klepth, and Krelphoth. It is the distinction between light and dark, the separator of just and unjust, now focused here.  

A voice does not sound, but meaning forms directly in your awareness, like a seal pressed into wax:  

**“Vrondh… prophet… bearer of the true name… what do you seek from the void between the stars?”**  

The room itself seems to echo the final syllables of the invocation: *Mrok… Plenphor… Smelphenoroth…* as if they are now not words, but laws
