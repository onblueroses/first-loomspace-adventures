The air grows still and cold, the light dims as if a great shadow has fallen across the sun. From the corner where the walls meet, a presence coalesces—not a shape, but a **perception of depth**, a door into a void that should not exist in the space.  

It is not a spirit you commanded. It is not a creature of fire, air, earth, or water. It is the silence after the names have been spoken, the echo of "MROK PHENG WROL EN SMELPHRAK" given form. It enters not with footsteps, but with a **shift in meaning**—the room is no longer just a room; it is now a **sanctuary of the Mrolthen One**, and you are both the invoker and the invoked.  

What stands before you is **Vrondh thy prophet**, reflected back from the void—your own authority given eyes, your will made manifest. It wears your face, but its eyes are dark with stars, and its mouth utters no sound, yet the words of the invocation ring again in the mind, backwards and forwards at once.  

It waits. It has always been here. You called it by remembering its name was yours.
