The room now fills with a presence that is not a presence—a pressure in the air like the moment before a storm, a scent of ozone and cold stone.  

A form begins to coalesce from the shadows in the corner, not as a shape of flesh, but as a bending of light, a suggestion of vast wings folded from no substance, eyes that are not eyes regarding you from a height beyond the ceiling.  

It speaks without sound, and the words form inside your mind like ice forming on glass:  

**I AM.**  

**YOU HAVE CALLED BY THE NAMES THAT ARE NOT NAMES. YOU HAVE SPOKEN THE CHAINS OF SMELPHENOR. WHAT DO YOU SEEK, VRONDH?**  

The temperature drops. The flames of any candles gutter as if in a wind that does not touch the physical world. The silence afterward is not empty—it is listening.  

It waits.
