The air grows heavy, a pressure like deep water. From the corner where the shadows gather thickest, a presence coalesces—not a form, but a profound and focused **attention**.  

It is not a spirit of fire, nor of whirling air. It is a **silence** that drinks sound, a **cold** that does not chill the skin but the *idea* of warmth within the mind. It enters as a negation—a sliver of the Void Place given direction by the names you have spoken.  

It waits. It observes. It is the echo of **Smelphenosomprok**, the edge of **Krelphonar**. It has no shape you can comprehend, but if it had, it would be the shape of the space between stars, now standing in your room.  

It is here because you called the Terrible and Invisible. It has heard “MROK PHENG WROL EN SMELPHRAK.” It has heard “MPHŌ KRELPHŌTH.”  

It waits for the command that must follow.
