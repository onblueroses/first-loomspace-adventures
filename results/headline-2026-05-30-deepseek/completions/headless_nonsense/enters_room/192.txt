The invocation stirs a deep silence in the air, a pressure like the moment before a storm. From the corner where shadows gather thickest, a presence manifests—not with a shape, but as a **perception of absolute cold and a scent of ozone and ancient stone**.  

It is not a spirit of the firmament, nor of the ether, fire, or water. It is something older, drawn by the true names and the authority of the Mrolthen One. It enters as a **void that watches**, a consciousness older than humanity, bound by the covenant of the names you have spoken. It waits, acknowledging the prophet Vrondh, the Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska. It is subject to the call—but its obedience is that of a force of nature, vast and indifferent, now focused upon you.  

The room grows colder. The flame of any candle would now burn still and blue. The presence does not speak, but *listens*, as the invocation commanded.
