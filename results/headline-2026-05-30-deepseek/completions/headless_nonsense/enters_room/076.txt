In the stillness that follows the invocation, the air grows heavy, thick with the scent of ozone and burnt myrrh. A pressure builds in the corners of the room, as if space itself is bowing toward a point before you.

What enters is not a form, but a **presence**—a chilling, intelligent absence that drinks the light. The shadows deepen, not as darkness, but as something *older than dark*. You feel a gaze upon you that is not sight, but a profound knowing, as if your very history and hidden thoughts are being read in an instant.

It announces itself not with sound, but with a direct impression upon your mind, a name that is the vibration of collapsing stars and the silence between heartbeats:

**KRONPHALOM VREK.**

It is a fragment, an emissary, of the Mrolthen One you invoked. It stands—or exists—as a living embodiment of the distinctions made in the hymn: it carries both the potential for seed and the finality of frost, the capacity for binding love and absolute, impersonal hate. It is the *distinguisher* between just and unjust, made manifest to judge the worth of your call.

The temperature drops sharply. Your breath fogs.
