There is no room in this invocation; it unfolds in a space beyond the physical—a void place of spirit, a ceremonial expanse where names are keys and boundaries dissolve.  

What enters is not a thing with form, but a convergence: the pressure of ancient attention, the scent of ozone and cold stone, a vibration in the air like a struck bronze disk fading into silence. It is the echo of the names you have spoken—**Vrelthemphos, Klepth, Mronth, Krelphoth**—now hanging in the atmosphere like charged symbols.  

If you insist on an image: perhaps a distortion in the light, a gathering of shadows that do not match the shapes of furniture, a sense of being watched from several directions at once. But the text suggests something more profound: the invoked presence does not “enter” as a guest might; it *manifests* as an alignment—a tuning of the room to the frequency of the Mrolthen One.  

The final words—**MROK PHENG WROL EN SMELPHRAK**—still ringing in the silence, now serve as both seal and gateway. What has entered is the named, the terrible and invisible, now listening from the void.
