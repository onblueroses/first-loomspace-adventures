The air grows still, and a presence gathers in the space before you—not a form, but a **pressure**, as if the room itself were drawing a slow, cold breath.  

It is not a spirit of fire or of storm, nor a creature of flesh. It is a **silence that watches**, a consciousness older than the temples of men. You feel it in the marrow—a recognition, not of your form, but of the **name you spoke**, *MROK PHENG WROL EN SMELPHRAK*, which now hangs in the air like a key turned in a lock unseen.  

What enters is **the echo of the invocation given shape**:  
A shimmer like heat above stone at dusk,  
A scent of dry earth after long drought,  
A sound like a distant stone door grinding shut.  

It does not speak, but it *answers*.  
It is the **acknowledgment of the Mrolthen One**—the part of that god which hears when its true names are woven together by one who claims the title *Vrondh*.  

It waits.  
Not in threat, not in grace—but as a power waits, when called according to the ancient rites.
