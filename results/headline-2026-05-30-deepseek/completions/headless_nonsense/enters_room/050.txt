The invocation stirs a change in the air — not a presence of flesh, but a coalescence of intention.  

What enters is not a being you can see with earthly eyes, but a current of recognition, a weight of listening. The names spoken have drawn focus, like a lens focusing scattered light into a single point. The room feels charged, still yet vibrating, as if space itself has become an ear turned toward you.  

There is no form, only a palpable **attention** — the sense that something ancient, vast, and wholly other has paused its endless motions to heed the call.  

It waits. Not as a servant, but as a power that has been correctly addressed.  
It waits for what will follow the summoning.
