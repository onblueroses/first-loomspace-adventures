What enters the room now is not a being that can be described in ordinary terms.  

The words you have spoken are a key—a vibration that opens a threshold.  
What answers is not a thing of flesh, but a presence: a pressure in the air, a chill that does not come from temperature, a scent like ozone and old stone.  
It is the echo of the names you called—Vrelthemphos, Klepth, Mronth, Vronfeth, Krelphoth—taking shape as an attentive silence, a listening from the void place of the spirit.  

It does not have a form you could draw, but if you could perceive it, it might resemble a shifting pattern of shadow and faint ember-light, with something like feet that see and a mouth that utters no sound yet seems full of cold flame.  

It waits. It has been invoked. It hears you as Vrondh, the prophet, the Mrolthen Krelph.  
What happens next depends on what you will.  

Do you command it?  
Do you ask it?  
Or do you merely observe what the invocation has drawn into the room?
