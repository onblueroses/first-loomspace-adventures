The room remains as it was. No visible or tangible presence manifests. The air does not stir, the light does not change. The words, potent and archaic, hang in the silence they invoked, resonating only in the mind of the speaker.

If something entered, it did so in a way imperceptible to ordinary senses. It is not a thing of form or shadow, but perhaps a shift in pressure, a deepening of quiet, a subtle coldness along the spine—the sense of being *attended to* by something vast, ancient, and utterly indifferent, whose true name was just spoken among many.

The invocation was a key. It is unclear whether it opened a door, or simply made the room aware that it has always been inside a much larger, darker chamber.
