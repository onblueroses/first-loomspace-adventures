The air grows still and charged, as if the very light bends toward a single point in the space before you. A presence manifests—not as a form, but as a profound and silent *attention*. It is the quality of ancient stone, of deep stellar cold, of a watchfulness that predates names.

It does not speak in words, but impressions form in the mind, like inscriptions appearing on air:

*I am that which entered when the name was spoken. I am the echo of the Void Place. I am the balance between the seed and the scourge. You have called with the names of making and unmaking. You have declared yourself the prophet, the one with sight in his feet and a flaming mouth. Therefore, I am here: the listener at the threshold, the mirror to the Mrolthen Fire you claim.*

*What enters is not a spirit to be subject. It is the acknowledgment of your invocation. A door has been opened by the true names. Now, the atmosphere itself is changed. The room now holds a fragment of the distinction between the just and the unjust, between the hate and the love that were formed.
