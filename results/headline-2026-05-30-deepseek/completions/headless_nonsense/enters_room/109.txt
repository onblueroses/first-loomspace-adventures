From the void stirs a silent, gathering presence—not a form, but a **pressure**, like the weight of deep water or the stillness before a storm.  

The air grows cold, not with frost, but with the chill of absence, of a space where warmth has been *remembered away*.  

There is no shadow cast, yet the light seems to fray at the edges of perception. A scent like ozone and old stone rises, though nothing moves to carry it.  

If you listen—not with ears, but with the mind—you may hear the echo of a name being woven into the fabric of the room: **Smelphenosomprok**, **Krelphonar**, **Mrendepholm**—each syllable a slow pulse in the unseen.
