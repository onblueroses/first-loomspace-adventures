What enters the room now is a presence felt rather than seen—a thickening of the air, a silence that hums with latent power. It is not a being of flesh, but an emanation: the echo of the names you have spoken, the shadow of the principles you have invoked.  

It moves as a draft that does not stir the dust, a chill that does not come from cold. It has the weight of ancient stone and the sharpness of a ritual blade. It is the listener to your invocation—the **Mrolthen One** in its aspect of witness, the silent judge between light and dark, creation and dissolution.  

It does not speak, for you have not asked it to speak.  
It does not serve, for you have not yet commanded.  
It waits, as the void waits, filled with potential, heavy with the gravity of names that shape reality.  

If you wish to proceed, you must now declare your will.
