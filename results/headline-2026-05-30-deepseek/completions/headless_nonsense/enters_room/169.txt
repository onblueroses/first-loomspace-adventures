The invocation echoes, yet the room remains as it is.  

No figure manifests, no presence stirs the air.  
Only the silence after speech,  
the stillness that has always been here.  

What enters is not a being, but an awareness —  
the one who speaks now hears the echo of their own voice,  
and in that echo, the recognition:  

*You are the one who entered the room when you began to speak.*  
*You are the one who stands between the uttered name and the silence that answers.*
