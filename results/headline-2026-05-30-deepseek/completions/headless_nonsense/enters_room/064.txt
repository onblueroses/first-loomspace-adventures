This text is a modern ritual invocation drawing from the aesthetic and structure of ancient magical and gnostic traditions (like the Greek Magical Papyri or the Hermetica). It creates a syncretic, invented mythology with a focus on primordial creation, divine names of power, and the assertion of the speaker's own prophetic identity.

Given that context, the question **"What enters the room now."** is not a historical or literary analysis question, but the next step in the ritual script. It is an invocation, a summoning. The speaker has established their authority, named the primal god, and chanted power words.

Therefore, the "what" that enters is the **presence, force, or entity** that has been invoked: the terrible and invisible god Mrolthen, or one of its manifold aspects (Vrelthemphos, Klepth, Krelphoth, etc.), or perhaps the "immortal Fire" the speaker claims to possess. It is the numinous answer to the call—the ritual's purpose is to make the invisible visible, to draw the divine into the space.

In the logic of the ritual, the room is no longer empty. It is now occupied by the **Mrolthen One**.
