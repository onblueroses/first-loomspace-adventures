The air grows heavy, as if thickening into dark honey. A presence coalesces not from shadow, but from the very negation of light—a shape that is less a form and more a **silhouette of intention**.  

It is tall, crowned not with substance but with a shifting corona of whispers that sound like dry leaves and cracking ice. Its limbs are elongated, jointed in impossible places, and it moves with the fluid, soundless grace of oil spreading on water.  

Where a face might be, there is instead a slow-turning wheel of symbols—some from the invocation itself: **Mrok, Vrelph, Smelphenoroth**—glowing with a cold, phosphorescent blue. From its chest emanates a low, resonant hum, the frequency of which seems to vibrate in the bones of the room rather than in the air.  

It does not walk. It **unfolds** into the space, each motion erasing a portion of the familiar world, replacing it with a textured void that feels both ancient and unborn.
