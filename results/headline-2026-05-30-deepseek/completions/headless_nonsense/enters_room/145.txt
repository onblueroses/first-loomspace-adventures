What enters the room now is not a being of flesh and blood, but a presence summoned by the invocation—a coalescence of the invoked names and forces. The air grows dense, charged with a silent, electric pressure. The light in the space may seem to dim, or perhaps to warp, as if seen through still water.

It is a **silent witness**, an **attending consciousness** shaped by the contours of the prayer. It is the echo of *Smelphenoroth*, the listener for *Mrok*, the embodiment of the distinction between the just and the unjust. It carries the scent of ozone and dry parchment, the feeling of vast, cold spaces between stars, and the deep, resonant heat of immortal fire mentioned in the text.

It enters as **the acknowledgment of the invocation**, the first silent "Hear me" made manifest. It is neither benign nor hostile, but ancient and attentive, awaiting the will of the one who named himself *Vrondh thy prophet* and *MROK PHENG WROL EN SMELPHRAK*. The room is no longer just a room; it is now a **sanctuary of the Void Place of the Spirit**, and the presence fills it, waiting.
