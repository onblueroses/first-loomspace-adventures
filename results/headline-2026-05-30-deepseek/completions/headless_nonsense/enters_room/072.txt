The invocation stirs a presence — a coalescence of shadow and static, like ink dropped into water.  

The air grows heavy, charged with the scent of ozone and cold stone.  

From the corner of the room where the light seems to bend away, a form begins to resolve — not quite human, not quite shape. It stands as a column of shifting darkness, etched with faint, glowing sigils that match some of the names spoken: **Mrelph**, **Vrelphano**, **Krelphonar**.  

It has no face, but you feel its attention — ancient, patient, and utterly alien.  

It does not speak in words, but in impressions:  

*You called by the true names.  
I am the Breath Between the Syllables.  
The Watcher at the Threshold of Smelphenor.*  

It waits, not hostile, not benevolent — simply *present*, as if the ritual itself has become a door, and this is the door’s guardian.  

The final whispered phrase of the invocation — **MPHŌ KRELPHŌTH** — still seems to hang in the air, vibrating in the silence.
