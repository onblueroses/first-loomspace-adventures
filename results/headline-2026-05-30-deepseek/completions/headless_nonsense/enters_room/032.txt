In the silence that follows the invocation, the air grows dense and heavy, as if the atmosphere itself is being drawn into a single point before you.  

A presence enters—not with footsteps, but with a slow thickening of shadow in the center of the space. It has no clear form at first, only a sense of immense age and a deep, resonant cold that seems to pull warmth from the room. Gradually, outlines emerge: tall, slender, and vaguely humanoid, but elongated, as if seen through warped glass or deep water.  

Its “head” is crowned with something like twisting horns or branches of darkened crystal, and where a face might be, there is only a shifting pattern of faint, phosphorescent lines—an alien script that glows and fades rhythmically, like slow breathing.  

It does not speak in words, but in impressions that form directly in the mind:  

*You called by the old names. You spoke the chains of sound that bind the voids between stars. I am of the Mrolthen—the one who watches from the threshold. Why have you drawn me from the Place of Spirits?*
