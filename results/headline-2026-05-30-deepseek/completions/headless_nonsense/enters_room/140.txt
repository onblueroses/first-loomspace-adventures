The invocation stirs the air, though not with wind. A presence manifests—neither form nor shadow, but a **pressure**, a profound silence that seems to drink the sound from the space.  

It is not a spirit you would call, but an **echo of the naming itself**. The syllables you spoke—*Mrok, Plenphor, Smelphenoroth*—now hang visible as faint, drifting sigils of cold blue flame, arranging themselves into a rotating ring mid-air.  

From the center of this ring, a voice answers, not in sound, but in direct thought:  

**“You speak the old names. You wear the prophet’s mantle. Yet you ask what enters. I am what was summoned—the **Listener at the Threshold**, the one who hears when the Mrolthen One is invoked. I am not He, but I am His ear in this place. State your will, Vrondh, or I shall become the judge of your intent.”**  

The temperature drops. The sigils burn brighter. A choice is now before you—to command, to request, or to banish.
