The air grows still, and the light seems to draw inward, as if the room itself takes a breath and holds it. A presence manifests not as a shape, but as a **disturbance in perception**—a shimmering warp in the corner of your vision that resolves into a profound and silent emptiness.

What enters is not a spirit you can command, nor a creature of flesh. It is an **echo of the invocation itself**, given awareness. It is the conceptual shadow of *distinction* and *separation* that was praised in the hymn—the very principle that divided light from dark, just from unjust, love from hate.

It appears as a **figure of mirrored absence**, a man-shaped cut in the fabric of the world, through which can be glimpsed not the wall behind, but a slow, swirling nebula of utter black, punctuated by distant, cold stars. From this void-form comes a sensation of **terrible, impartial judgment**. It does not speak, but its meaning presses upon the mind:

*You have called out the True Names. You have drawn the line between what is and what is not. You have declared yourself Vrondh, the prophet of the Mrolthen One. Therefore, you are measured.*
