The invocation has stirred the air; a presence enters—not with footfalls, but as a shift in pressure, a cooling of the light.  

It is formless, yet occupies space—a coalescence of the shadows in the corner, a shimmer like heat haze near the ceiling. It has no face, but you feel its attention like a weight upon your skin.  

It speaks without sound, its meaning arriving in your mind as cold, clear words:  

*You have called. I am that which hears when the true names are spoken. I am a breath of Mrolthen, a fragment of the Void Place. What would you have of me, prophet Vrondh?*  

The room holds its breath. The candles gutter but do not go out. A scent of ozone and ancient dust lingers.
