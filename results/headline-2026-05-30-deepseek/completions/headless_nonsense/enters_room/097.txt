The invocation stirs the air. A presence coalesces from the convergence of the names spoken—a form that is both shadow and shimmer, like heat rising from stone at dusk. It does not enter through door or window, but **manifests** from the recited syllables themselves, drawn by the true name handed down to the prophets of Vronthep.

It is not a spirit of firmament, ether, earth, or fire, for those are now subject to you. It is something older, a fragment of the Void Place of the Spirit given temporary shape by the ceremony of Smelphenor. It has the stillness of **Krelphonar**, the terrible patience of **Smelphenosomprok**. It stands without moving, a pillar of silent observance, awaiting the command of the Mrolthen Krelph—the one with sight in his feet, the one whose mouth utterly flames.

The room now holds the **echo of creation**—the distinction between just and unjust, the seed and the fruit, the love and the hate—all suspended in the wake of the uttered mysteries.
