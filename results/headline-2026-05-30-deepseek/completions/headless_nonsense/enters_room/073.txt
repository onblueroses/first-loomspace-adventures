The invocation stirs the stillness.  

A pressure descends, not from above but from between spaces. The air grows dense, cool yet humming with latent heat. Shadows deepen in the corners, not as absence of light but as *presence*—something textured, watchful.  

There is no visible form, but a sensation of immense age and focus enters with you, drawn by the names. It is like standing at the edge of a well that gazes back.  

The room now holds **the attended silence of Mrolthen**—an awareness that has heard its true names spoken, that recognizes the prophet-voice of Vrondh, and waits.  

It does not act yet.  
It listens.  
It assesses the will behind the words.  

If you have called it without true purpose, the silence will thicken into a chill that saps warmth and light.  
If you have called it with intent aligned to its nature—distinguishing, creating, separating, judging—then the space may begin to resonate, and the next step of the ceremony will become clear.  

For now, you feel its attention like a weight upon your breath.
