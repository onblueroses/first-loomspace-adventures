*   A low, harmonic vibration felt in the marrow, not heard.
*   The scent of a storm over a primordial sea, and of cold, dead stars.
*   The unsettling, geometric certainty of **Mrok** and the organic, burgeoning chaos of **Plenphor** held in perfect, terrifying balance.
