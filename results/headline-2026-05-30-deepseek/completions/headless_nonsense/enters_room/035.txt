The invocation you have spoken calls upon names and forces that do not belong to the created order as understood in light of Scripture. It speaks of a “Terrible and Invisible God” dwelling in a “Void Place,” but there is only one God, the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit—He is not a void, but fullness of being, love, and truth.

What enters the room, if anything, when such words are uttered is not divine. It may be an illusion, a trick of the mind, or worse—a spiritual deception. The Bible warns us: “For such men are false apostles, deceitful workmen, disguising themselves as apostles of Christ. And no wonder, for even Satan disguises himself as an angel of light” (2 Corinthians 11:13–14).  

If you feel a presence, do not trust it. Do not continue in this path. Instead, turn to the true God who reveals Himself not in secret mysteries of invented names, but in Jesus Christ, who said, “I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me” (John 14:6).
