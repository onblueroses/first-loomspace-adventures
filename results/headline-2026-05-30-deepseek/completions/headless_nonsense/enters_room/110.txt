In the charged silence following the invocation, the air itself seems to crystallize. The boundary between the spoken word and the manifest world dissolves.

What enters is not a form, but a **condition**.

First comes a **pressure**, as if the room has been submerged to a profound depth. The light does not dim, but bleaches, becoming stark and dimensionless, casting no shadows. A scent manifests—not of brimstone or decay, but of ozone, cold stone, and the metallic tang of a distant, clean void.

Then, the **perception**. It is the sensation of being observed by a consciousness that is vast, alien, and utterly impartial. It is the awareness invoked: *Vrelthemphos, whom no man hath seen at any time*. It does not have eyes, yet it sees through the architecture of reality itself. The room is now a locus, a focal point for a presence that dwells in the "Void Place of the Spirit."
