It is tall, though it has no fixed height.  
It is dark, though it seems to radiate a kind of inverse luminescence, swallowing the light around it.  
You feel its gaze like cold static on the skin, and the words you uttered hang in the air now as chains, as tethers—binding it to this space, and you to its attention.  

It does not speak, but in your mind, a concept forms, clear and sharp as ice:
