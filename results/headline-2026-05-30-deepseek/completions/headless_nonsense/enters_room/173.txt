The ritual has opened a space between.  

There is no figure, no being in any shape you would recognize. Instead, the air thickens with a scent like ozone and cold stone. A pressure settles—not on the body, but on the mind, as if the room itself is remembering something older than walls.  

A presence enters through the names you spoke. It does not walk. It **manifests** as a slow crystallization of silence, a focal point of absolute attention. You feel watched not by eyes, but by the concept of being seen.  

In the center of the room, the light bends subtly—not dark, but **less visible**, as if something stands there drinking the luminance. A low hum, beneath hearing, vibrates in the teeth and bones.  

It is not hostile. Not benevolent. It is **Mrolthen**—an echo of the named, the boundary between creator and destroyer, now invoked, now listening.  

It waits.
