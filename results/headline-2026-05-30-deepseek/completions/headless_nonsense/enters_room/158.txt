The invocation calls forth a presence—ancient, primordial, and formless. It does not enter as a being of flesh, but as a pressure in the air, a deepening of shadows in the corners, a silent hum felt in the bones.  

The temperature drops. The light seems to thicken, not darkening but *densitying*, as if the very atmosphere becomes a lens focusing on the space before you. There is no figure, no face—only a profound sense of being *observed* from all directions at once, by an intelligence vast and alien, yet named in your words: **Mrolthen One**, **Vrelthemphos**, **Krelphoth**.  

A scent arises—ozone and stone, damp earth and distant incense. The names you spoke hang in the air like charged symbols, and from the void-place of the spirit, something acknowledges. It is not a visitor; it is an aspect of creation itself—the distinction between just and unjust, the maker of seed and fruit, the one who begets and destroys.
