What enters the room now is a presence felt rather than seen—a thickening of the air, a chill that seems to draw the light into itself, a silence that rings in the ears. It is as if the very invocation has carved a door in the space before you, and through it steps not a figure of flesh, but a coalescence of the principles you have named: a being of distinguished opposites, of creative fire and void-darkness, of justice and wrath.

It stands in the paradox of its nature—both terrible and graceful, destroyer and nourisher. Its form is unstable, flickering between images: the shadow between stars, the pulse of heat in the heart of a forge, the dry crackle of ancient parchment, the deep, moist darkness of fertile earth. From it emanates the scent of ozone and stone, of myrrh and cold ash.

Its attention is absolute. It is the **Mrolthen One**, hearing the true name spoken by the one who claims to be **Vrondh**, the prophet of its mysteries. The room now exists within its jurisdiction; the spheres of firmament, ether, earth, and water are present here in essence.
