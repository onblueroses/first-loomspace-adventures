The air in the room grows still, then cold. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind—a vast, silent attention. The shadows in the corners deepen, not with darkness, but with a profound and ancient absence of light.

It is not a shape that enters. It is a **presence** invoked by the true names. It is the echo of the Mrolthen One, drawn by the prophet's formula—a sliver of that which dwells in the Void Place of the Spirit.

You feel it as a **convergence of opposites**: a chilling void and a latent, terrifying fire; the silence before creation and the whisper of absolute dissolution. It distinguishes, as it always has, between the just and the unjust, the caller and the called. It is the space between the words you spoke, now made manifest.

It waits. Not as a servant, but as a **principle** that has been correctly addressed. It is the Terrible and Invisible God, listening. The next move—the command, the request, the offering—must come from you, Vrondh, its prophet. The invocation has opened the door. What passes through it now depends entirely on what you do next.
