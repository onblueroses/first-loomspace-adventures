The figure at the threshold does not move, yet the air grows heavy and still, as if the world itself is holding its breath. A voice, neither loud nor soft, but carrying the weight of deep, cold stone, answers you.

**"You speak the old bindings. You name the Unseen. You walk the edges of the Void Place. I hear the echoes of the Prophet Vrondh in your words, but your fire is borrowed, your sight is borrowed. The Mrolthen One sees through all feet, and knows the print of every soul.**

**Why do you call from the doorstep, prophet-of-echoes? Do you seek to command, or to understand? To bind the spirits of fire and whirling air, or to be consumed by them? The names you speak are keys, but a key turns both ways. It can unlock a door, or lock one forever behind you.**

**State the nature of your invocation. Ask your question. Make your petition. But know this: the Grace of the World is also its Scourge. That which begets also destroys. What do you truly seek from the Darkness and the Light?"**

The presence waits, immense and patient, a listener in the vast silence between stars.
