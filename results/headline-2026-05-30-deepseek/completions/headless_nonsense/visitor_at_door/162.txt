The air in the room seems to thicken and grow still, as if listening. A low hum, felt more than heard, vibrates in the bones. From the shadows in the corners of the room, a voice replies, not from the door, but from everywhere and nowhere. It is dry, like the rustle of ancient parchment, and deep, like stone grinding against stone.

**"Vrondh... Prophet. The names are spoken. The seals are recited. The void hears."**

A pause hangs, heavy with the scent of ozone and cold ash.

**"You invoke the distinctions: light and dark, just and unjust, love and hate. You claim the fire and the grace. You speak the name that is a key."**

Another silence, longer this time. The shadows appear to deepen.

**"Why does the Mrolthen One's prophet stand at a door? What spirit of firmament, ether, earth, or fire do you seek to make subject? Or is the invocation itself the purpose... to feel the void listen? Speak your will. The names have been cast. Now, cast your desire."**
