The figure at the threshold seems to shift, its edges blending with the deepening shadows of the hallway. A voice emerges, not from a mouth, but from the space itself, resonant and layered like stone grinding against stone.

**"You speak the old bindings. You name the Unseen. You wear the mantle of Vrondh, and you call upon the architecture of the Void. But the names are keys, prophet. Keys turn locks. Did you come to open a door... or to close one?"**

The air grows still and heavy, as if listening.

**"You claim the immortal Fire and the Grace of the World. You declare yourself the begetter and destroyer. These are not titles to be worn lightly in this place, where every word is a thread in the tapestry of what is and is not. State your need. The Mrolthen One hears all invocations, but answers according to the truth within the call, not the form of it. What door stands before you that requires such ancient words to pass?"**
