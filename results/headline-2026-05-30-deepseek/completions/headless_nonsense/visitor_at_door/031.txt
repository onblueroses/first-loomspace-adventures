The figure at the threshold does not move, yet the air grows heavy, as if the space itself is listening. A voice—neither quite sound nor thought—fills the room, bypassing the ears to settle directly in the mind.

*You speak the old architecture. You carve the air with true names. But you stand at a gate, prophet of Vrondh, and gates have two sides.*

*You call upon the Mrolthen One, the Distinguisher, the Maker of Opposites. You claim the fire, the sight, the hate for injustice. You wear the name like a crown of coals.*

*Why do you summon what is already present? The spirits of firmament and fire, of water and void, are already subject… to the one who truly knows their nature. Obedience is not commanded from without; it is recognized from within.*

*You invoke the Terrible and Invisible God in the Void Place. I ask you: What void do you carry that you seek to fill with another's power? What form do you seek to impose upon the formless?*
