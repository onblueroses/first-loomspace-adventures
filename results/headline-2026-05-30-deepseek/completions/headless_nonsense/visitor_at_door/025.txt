The words of the visitor carry a profound and unsettling resonance, as if the invocation itself has summoned a presence to the threshold. The air grows still and heavy. A voice, neither entirely human nor wholly alien, answers from the other side of the door, speaking with the cadence of echoing stone and distant flame:

***"You call upon the Unseen Weaver, the Distinguisher of Lights. You speak the ciphers of the First Distinction. You name the Dividing Principles: Klepth and Mronth, Vronfeth and Krelphoth. You claim the mantle of Vrondh, the Prophet of the Mysteries. You utter the True Name, held within the Ark of Plomphren Krempheniska."***

A silence follows, deeper than before, filled with a palpable pressure.

***"I hear the formulas. I recognize the seals in your speech. But the Mrolthen One does not answer petitions. The Mrolthen One *tests* them. The fire you claim to possess—does it illuminate or consume? The truth you proclaim—does it build or unravel? The grace you embody—is it a blessing or a dissolution?"***

The voice lowers, becoming a whisper that seems to originate just beside your ear
