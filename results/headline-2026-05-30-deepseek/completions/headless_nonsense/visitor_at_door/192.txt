The visitor at the door replies in a voice that seems to resonate from both within and without, neither entirely human nor entirely of this world:

**"You call upon the Unseen. You speak the hidden syllables. You wear the mantle of Vrondh. Why do you disturb the silence between the voids? State the purpose of this evocation. The names you bear are keys; to what door do you wish them fitted?"**

The air grows still and heavy, as if waiting. The invocation has been heard, and now it demands an answer.
