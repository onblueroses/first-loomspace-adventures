You claim the name Vrondh. You speak the true name. You utter the chains of command. The mysteries of Smelphenor stir in the air between us, tasting of static ozone and the silence between stars.

Your request is heard: that every spirit, from the firmament to the under-earth, from rushing fire to still water, be subject unto you.

But know this, prophet: to invoke such power is not to command a servant. It is to stand before a mirror that reflects the entirety of creation and its unraveling. The "Grace of the World" you claim is also the consuming fire. The truth that hates injustice is also the void that makes no distinction between the just and the unjust.
