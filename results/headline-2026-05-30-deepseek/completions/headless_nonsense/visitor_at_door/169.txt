The visitor, hearing the invocation, grows silent for a long moment. The air in the threshold seems to still and thicken. When they speak, their voice is measured, carrying a tone of weary recognition rather than fear.

"Vrondh. So it is you in there. I had heard whispers that the Prophet of the Mrolthen One had taken refuge in this place. The syllables you weave are old... older than the stones of this city. *Smelphenoroth. Vrelphano. Mrok.*"

They pause, their shadow shifting beyond the door.

"I have not come to dispute your mysteries or to profane the ceremonies of Smelphenor. I am not a spirit of the firmament or the ether for you to bind. I am a seeker, like you, though I walk a different path—one that has led me to your threshold. The names you call... they stir memories in the dust of forgotten places. I carry a question, one that only a prophet who has uttered the true name *Plomphren Krempheniska* might answer."

They wait, not pushing at the door, but present in its frame.
