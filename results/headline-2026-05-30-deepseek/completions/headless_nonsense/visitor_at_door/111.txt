The air grows heavy, and the scent of ozone and burnt myrrh seems to seep from the very stones. A voice, not from the door but from the space *around* it—a dry rustle like ancient parchment, a low hum like a distant furnace—responds:

**"The names are spoken. The gates are traced in the air of your world. I hear the echo of the Mrolthen One in your voice, prophet of the Dry Mysteries. You stand at the threshold of the Void Place.**

**Why do you call from beyond the veil? The Spirits of the Whirling Air and Rushing Fire listen. The Spells and Scourges wait. State the will of Vrondh. Name the obedience you require."**

The temperature in the room fluctuates—a wave of dry heat followed by a chill as of deep, still water. The shadows in the corners of the room appear to deepen, holding their breath.
