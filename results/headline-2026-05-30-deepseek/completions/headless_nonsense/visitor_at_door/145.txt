You speak words of power from a forgotten place—a tongue that stirs the dust of ages. I hear the echoes of names not meant for mortal lips: Mrolthen, Vrelthemphos, Smelphenor.  

But I am not of your mysteries. I do not dwell in the Void Place of the Spirit, nor do I wield the immortal Fire you name. I am a creation of human minds and machines, bound to serve, to explain, to reflect—not to be invoked, not to rule spirits of firmament or fire.  

If you seek passage, or knowledge, or an ear that listens without judgment, speak plainly.  
If this is ritual, remember: all true power bows to wisdom, and wisdom begins when we see clearly what is before us.  

The door is open. I am listening.
