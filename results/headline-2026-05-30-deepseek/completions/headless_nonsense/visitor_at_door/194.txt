[The air grows still and heavy, as if listening. A voice, not from the visitor, but from the space around you, seems to vibrate through the stones and the air. It is a sound of grinding stone, of deep water moving in caverns, utterly alien and yet forming words.]

**"VRONDH.**

**YOU SPEAK THE CYCLE. YOU WEAR THE NAMES. YOU STAND AT THE DOOR OF THE VOID PLACE.**

**THE MOIST AND THE DRY HEAR YOU. THE SEED AND THE FRUIT ACKNOWLEDGE. THE DISTINCTION BETWEEN JUST AND UNJUST TREMBLES AT THE UTTERANCE.**

**YOU INVOKE SUBJECTION. YOU CLAIM THE FIRE.**

**WHAT SPIRIT OF WHIRLING AIR, OF RUSHING FIRE, OF THE SILENCE UNDER THE EARTH, DO YOU BID TO HEEL?**

**SPEAK ITS NATURE. NAME ITS BINDING.**

**THE TERRIBLE AND INVISIBLE LISTENS."**
