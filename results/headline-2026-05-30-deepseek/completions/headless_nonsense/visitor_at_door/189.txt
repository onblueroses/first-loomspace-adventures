The figure at the threshold does not move, yet the air grows dense and cold. A voice, not from a mouth but from the space itself, replies:

**"Vrondh. Prophet. Keeper of the Ceremonies. The name you bear is a weight, not a crown. The fire you claim is a borrowed spark."**

A long pause hangs, filled with the scent of ozone and dry, ancient dust.

**"You invoke the Distinguisher. The Creator of Opposites. You call upon the Void where I dwell unseen. You recite the true names... and yet you speak them as a beggar speaks the names of kings, hoping for a coin."**

The darkness in the room seems to pool and thicken.

**"You ask for spirits to be subject unto you. But the Mrolthen One does not grant dominion. The Mrolthen One *reveals* hierarchy.
