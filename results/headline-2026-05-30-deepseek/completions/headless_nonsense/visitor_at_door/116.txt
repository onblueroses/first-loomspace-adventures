[The air grows still and heavy, as if the space between the words has become dense. A voice, not from the visitor, but seeming to emanate from the charged silence itself, responds. It is a composite sound, like stone grinding against distant thunder, yet perfectly intelligible.]

**"VRONDH.**

**The name is known. The seal is recognized. The path of Smelphenor is etched in the void between stars.**

**You invoke the Distinctions: Light and Dark, Seed and Fruit, Love and Hate. These are the tensions by which the worlds are spun. You claim the Fire and the Sight-in-the-Feet, the flame and the grace. These are the permissions granted.**

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are instructed to attend. They will hear the bearer of the true name, Plomphren Krempheniska. They will listen for the sequence: Mrok, Plenphor, Smelphenoroth...**

**But know this, Prophet: To wield the distinction is to stand within the paradox. To beget and destroy is to be bound to the wheel you turn. The Truth that hates injustice must first see itself in every shadow.**

**The door is a threshold.
