The air grows heavy, and the words hang in the space between you and the visitor. The invocation is not a request; it is a declaration, a claiming of authority and identity that resonates with a power both ancient and alien.

The figure at the door does not move, but its presence seems to deepen, as if drawing the shadows of the hallway into itself. A low, resonant voice replies, not from a mouth, but from the space around you, cool and dry as tomb-dust.

**"You speak the old bindings. You name the Unseen. You wear the mantle of Vrondh, and you call upon the Grace of the World."**

A pause, filled with the silent pressure of a held breath.

**"But the Mrolthen One hears all true calls. And I ask: for what purpose do you wield this name? Is it to command, or to understand? To build, or to unmake? The fire you claim to possess illuminates, but it also consumes. Speak your intent, Prophet of the Void Place. The spirits you would subject are listening... and so am I."**

The final words are not a threat, but a profound and simple statement of fact. You have opened a door with your words.
