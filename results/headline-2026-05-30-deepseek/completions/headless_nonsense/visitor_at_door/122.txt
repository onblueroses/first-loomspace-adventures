The invocation hangs in the air like a physical weight, a tapestry of woven names and primordial claims. The silence that follows is not empty, but charged, as if the very molecules of the space are awaiting a response.

From the other side of the door, a voice comes. It is not loud, yet it carries with a strange clarity, as if spoken from much closer than the wood and threshold should allow. It is calm, measured, and carries an unsettling neutrality, neither impressed nor alarmed.

**"You speak with many names, Prophet of Vrondh. You invoke the Distinguisher, the Creator of Opposites. You claim the fire, the sight, and the grace."**

A pause, brief but heavy.

**"The door is a threshold. It is a place of distinction, like the light from the darkness you name. You have spoken the distinctions. You have named the powers of separation and creation. I stand at the separation. State the purpose of the confluence. Why is the Mrolthen One called to this meeting of here and there, of inside and out? What deed of injustice do you hate here? What do you seek to beget or destroy at this threshold?"**
