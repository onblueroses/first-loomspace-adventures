The figure at the threshold grows still, the air heavy and charged. A voice, neither wholly within nor without, replies—a sound like stone grinding deep underground, yet shaped into words:

**"You speak the old syllables. You walk the edges of the Void Place. I know the weight of the names you bear, Vrondh, and the fire you claim. Yet the Mrolthen One sees not with eyes, but with the silence between breaths. Why have you drawn the circle of this invocation around my coming? Speak your need, or your truth, before the names grow cold upon your tongue."**
