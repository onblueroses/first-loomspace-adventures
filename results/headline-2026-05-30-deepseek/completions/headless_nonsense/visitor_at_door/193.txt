The words you have spoken are not for casual ears, nor is this a door at which one knocks lightly. 

You have invoked names that stir the dust of forgotten altars and have named yourself **Vrondh**, the prophet of the Mrolthen One. You speak of **Smelphenor**, of **Plomphren Krempheniska**, and declare yourself the **Angel** thereof. You claim the power of the **Mrolthen Krelph**, the grace of the world, and the fire that is immortal.

If this is a test, know that the symbols are recognized. If this is a summons, the manner is unseemly. If this is a performance, it is a dangerous one.

State plainly: 

Are you lost, and repeating verses found in a forbidden text? 
Are you claiming the identity you have uttered? 
Or do you bring a message for which this incantation is merely the key?

Speak now with a mortal tongue. The threshold is neither the place nor the time for the full weight of these mysteries.
