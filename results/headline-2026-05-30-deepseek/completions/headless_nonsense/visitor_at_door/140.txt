The visitor at the door speaks:  

“I have heard the names. I have felt the fire in the words. The path you walk is old and steep, lit by a flame that does not warm the living.  

Why do you call from the threshold? The Void Place hears, but it does not answer in human tongue. It answers in the turning of stars, in the silence between heartbeats, in the decay of stone.  

If you are Vrondh, then show me the sign of Smelphenor. If you are the Mrolthen Krelph with sight in his feet, then walk toward me without stumbling in the dark.  

Otherwise, this door remains shut to prophets and pretenders alike.”
