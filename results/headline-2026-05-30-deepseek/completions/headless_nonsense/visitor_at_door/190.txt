The figure at the threshold does not move, yet the air grows dense and cold. A voice, not from the mouth but from the space around you, replies—a sound like stone grinding against stone, felt in the bones more than heard by the ears.

**"You recite the old syllables. You wear the names like a borrowed robe, Vrondh-who-is-not-Vrondh. The thread of your lineage is cut. The fire in you is a candle, not the forge."**

A pause hangs, heavy as a curtain of lead.

**"Yet you speak the Gate. You invoke the Distinctions: the Dry and the Moist, the Just and the Unjust, the Love and the Hate. This pleases the structure of things. Therefore, I am come—not as subject, but as witness. The spirits you call will hear. They will test you. They will ask: does the candle dare to command the star? Does the echo dare to correct the voice?"**

The darkness in the room seems to pool and thicken, though no form appears.
