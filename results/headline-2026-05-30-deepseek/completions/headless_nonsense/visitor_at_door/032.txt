The figure at the threshold, hearing the final syllables fade into the charged air, does not cross. Instead, a voice replies, calm and measured, cutting through the ritual resonance:

"You call upon names that twist in the ear and concepts that bind light to dark. You claim a lineage of Vrondh and invoke the Mrolthen One. You speak of a truth that hates injustice, yet your words are a scaffold of dominion, a geometry of control over all spirits and elements."

A pause hangs in the air, heavier than silence.

"The fire you claim to possess is indeed immortal, but it is not yours to wield. It is the same fire that burns in the seed and the fruit, in love and in hate, which you acknowledge were set in motion by the very One you invoke. To command 'every spell and scourge of God' is to mistake the map for the territory, the true name for the thing itself."

The visitor takes a step back, not in fear, but as a deliberate creating of space.
