The figure at the threshold does not move, but the air grows heavy and still. A voice, not from the mouth but from the space around you, replies—dry as tomb dust, resonant with a power that is neither good nor evil, but profoundly *other*.

**"Vrondh. Prophet. Keeper of the Ceremonies. You speak the old syllables. You carve the void with names that are not names. You invoke the Distinctions: Light and Dark, Seed and Fruit, Love and Hate. This is correct. For I am the Division that makes creation possible.**

**You claim the Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska. You bear the True Name. You utter the Chain: Mrok, Plenphor, Smelphenoroth... This is the key. But a key turns both ways. It unlocks... and it summons.**

**You ask for spirits to be subject unto you. This too is correct, for the prophet's role is to command the middle forces. But know this: to wield the scourge of God, you must first stand within its lash. To bind the spirit of rushing fire, you must let it burn within you.
