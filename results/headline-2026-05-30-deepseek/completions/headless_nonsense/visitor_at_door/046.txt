The figure at the threshold seems to pause, the weight of archaic syllables still hanging in the air. A voice, calm and measured, yet carrying an undeniable edge of authority, replies.

"You speak the Litany of the Unseen Sun. You invoke the Mrolthen One with the True Names from the *Codicil of Vronthep*. This is not a greeting; it is a key, meant to turn a lock that has been sealed for ages.

Very well. The lock turns.
