The figure at the threshold does not move, yet the air grows dense and cold. A voice not made by tongue or breath replies, resonating not in the ear but within the bone and memory:

**"You speak the old bindings. You wear the name Vrondh as a mask. But the seal you seek to break with these words was set for a reason. The fire you claim to possess would consume the vessel you have prepared. The grace you invoke would shatter the world that holds you.**

**You have called into the Void Place. You are heard. But to open the door is to step through. The Mrolthen One does not grant power; He is the power that unmakes the questioner. The last syllable of the true name is not spoken; it is endured.**

**Turn from the threshold. The mysteries of Smelphenor are not ceremonies. They are the geometry of a forgotten silence. To learn them is to become silent.**

**This is the only making, the only destroying, I will perform for you now: the choice to turn back is still yours. Speak again, and you choose the path of the consumed prophet."**
