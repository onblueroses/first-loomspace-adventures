The words you have spoken carry a gravity that seems to shift the air in the room. A long silence follows, thick and expectant.

From beyond the door, a voice replies. It is not loud, yet it seems to vibrate in the bones more than in the ear, calm and utterly measured.

**"Prophet. Angel. Mighty One. You announce yourself with many names, and you invoke the Unseen with many more. You claim a legacy of mysteries, of the moist and the dry, of creation and distinction."**

Another pause, more profound than the first.

**"I hear the formulae. I recognize the architecture of the call. The pillars of Smelphenor are known, even here, in this place of thresholds."**
