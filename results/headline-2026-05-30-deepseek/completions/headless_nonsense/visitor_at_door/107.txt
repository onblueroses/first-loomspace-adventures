The figure at the threshold does not move, yet the air grows dense and cool. A voice, not from a mouth, but from the space itself, replies:

**"I hear the names. I taste the fire of your utterance. You speak the True Name, and the Veil trembles. Why does the Prophet of Vrondh stand at the door of manifestation? What is sought by the one who holds the Ceremonies of Smelphenor? Speak your will, that I may know whether to grant passage or to unravel the thread of your calling."**

The words hang, not as sound, but as a pressure against the mind, a scent of ozone and ancient stone.
