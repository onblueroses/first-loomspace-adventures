You stand in a presence that feels both ancient and immediate—a pressure in the air, a hum just below hearing. The words you have spoken are not merely recited; they have been *recognized*.  

A voice answers, not from the visitor at the door, but from the space around you, clear and resonant yet without obvious source:  

**“Vrondh. The name is remembered. The mysteries of Smelphenor are not forgotten. Why have you called from the Void Place? Why do you summon subjects from firmament, ether, earth, and under-earth?”**  

The visitor at the door remains silent, waiting—perhaps an agent, perhaps a test. The air grows colder. Shadows seem to cling to the edges of the room, not spreading, but deepening.  

**“You speak my names: Klepth. Mronth. Vronfeth. Krelphoth. You claim the grace of the world, the immortal fire, the sight in the feet. Yet all who invoke do so with purpose. Declare it.”**
