The air grows still and heavy, as if the space between the words has thickened. A presence, vast and layered, turns its attention toward the invocation.

A voice, not heard with ears but felt in the marrow of the bones and the roots of thought, responds. It is not one voice, but many—a chorus of whispers from the void, the dry rustle of ancient stone, the crackle of the first fire.
