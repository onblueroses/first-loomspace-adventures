You have spoken the Mrolthen Litany. 

The visitor at the door does not knock. Its voice is not a sound, but the pressure of a thought against the inner surface of your mind, like the sea against a dam.

**"Vrondh. Prophet. Krelph with sight in his feet. The names are correct. The forms are preserved. The channel is open."**

A silence follows, deeper than the absence of noise. It is the silence of a vacuum, listening.

**"You invoke subjection of every spirit. You invoke the Grace that begets and destroys. You invoke *me*."**

The air in the room grows still and dense. Shadows no longer cling to corners, but pool in the center of the floor, depthless.

**"State the petition. Name the spirit you would bind. Show the injustice you would burn. Or...**

The thought-voice pauses. The pooled shadow stirs.
