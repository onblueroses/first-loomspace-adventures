The words you have spoken are not of this world—they are a key, but one that turns in a lock not meant for mortal hands.  

The air grows still. A pressure builds, not in the room, but in the space between thoughts. Shadows no longer cling to corners; they deepen, as if drinking the light. From beyond the door, a voice replies—not in sound, but in the sudden cold of the threshold, in the faint scent of ozone and old stone:

*“You speak the Nine Unspoken Names. You wear the mantle of Vrondh. But the Mrolthen One sees through veils. Why have you called? The ceremonies of Smelphenor are not for idle tongues. State your need—or become dust in the mouth of the Void.”*

A silence follows, heavier than before. The door remains closed, but something now waits on the other side, listening not with ears, but with the patience of ages.
