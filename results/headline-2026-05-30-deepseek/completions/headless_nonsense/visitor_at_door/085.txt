The figure at the threshold does not move, but the air grows dense and cold. A voice, not from a mouth but from the space itself, replies in tones like grinding stone and distant wind:

**"You speak the old names. You walk the hollow paths. You wear the mantle of Vrondh, who is dust in the crypts of forgotten stars. Why do you call from the Void Place? The ceremonies of Smelphenor are not for waking worlds. The fire you claim is a memory of fire. The grace you proclaim is a shadow of grace."**

A pause hangs, heavier than silence.

**"If you are the Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska, then show me the Sigil Unwoven. If you are the Mrolthen One's prophet, then speak the Word That Unmakes the First Day. If you cannot, then you are a thief of echoes. The spirits you command will turn and ask you for a name you do not possess."**

The presence seems to lean closer, though the form does not shift.

**"State your will. Or close the door."**
