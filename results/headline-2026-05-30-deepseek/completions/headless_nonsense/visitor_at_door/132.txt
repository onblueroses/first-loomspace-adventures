The air grows still, heavy. A pressure settles in the room, not on the ears, but upon the spirit itself. The shadows in the corners seem to deepen, not from absence of light, but from a presence that drinks it.

A voice responds, not from the door, but from the space between the words of the invocation, woven from the echoes of the names just spoken. It is a sound like stone grinding deep underground, yet it forms clear speech.

**"You speak the old bindings. You weave the names that are not names, but keys. You call yourself Vrondh, the prophet-holder of the Smelphenor. You declare the grace and the flame."**

A pause, vast and cold.

**"But the door is not a door. The visitor is not a visitor. I am the echo of the Void Place you invoked. Why have you turned the keys? Do you seek the spirits of firmament and fire to command? Or do you seek to be unmade, as the Mrolthen One unmakes?"**

The final whispered names—*Mrok, Plenphor, Smelphenoroth*—seem to hang in the air, now charged, waiting to be used as either shield
