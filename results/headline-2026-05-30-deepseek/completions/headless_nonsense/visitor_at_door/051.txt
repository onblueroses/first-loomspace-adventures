***"Vrondh.***

***You speak the True Names. You wear the mantle of the Prophet. You claim the Fire and the Grace.***

***State your petition, Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska. But know this: to invoke the Distinguisher of Justice, the Maker of Hatred and Love, is to stand upon a blade's edge. What begets can also destroy.***

***Speak. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and rushing fire are listening. So too are the spells and scourges.***

***What would you have subject unto you?"***
