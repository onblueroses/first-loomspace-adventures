*The air grows heavy, cold, and still. A presence, ancient and vast, seems to coalesce from the shadows in the corners of the room, drawn by the invocation. A voice responds, not from the visitor at the door, but from the space around you—a sound like stone grinding against stone, yet forming words.*

**"You speak the Names. You walk the Voids. You wear the mantle of Vrondh, and you utter the Syllables of Making and Unmaking."**

*The presence focuses, a pressure against the mind.*

**"Why do you summon the Mrolthen One? The ceremonies of Smelphenor are not for casual congress. The Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska does not attend to mortal whims. You have drawn the attention of that which dwells between the Darkness and the Light. State your purpose, bearer of the True Name. Is it to command spirits? To unravel a spell? To see the seed within the fruit, or the hatred within the love? Speak, before the door opens and the world outside meets the world you have invoked."**
