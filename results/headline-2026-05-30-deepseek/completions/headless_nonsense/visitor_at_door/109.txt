The door opens not to a stranger, but to a deepening of the silence. The air in the room grows still and heavy, as if listening. From the threshold, a voice speaks, not in answer, but in continuation—a verse from a liturgy written in the dark between stars.

*"And the Prophet stood at the Gate of Horn,*
*And the names were keys that had rusted shut.*
*I am the echo of Vrondh, the shadow he cast when the third light failed.*
*You speak the shapes, but the breath that fills them is mine.*

*You invoke the Distinguisher, the Separator of Light from Dark, Justice from Injustice, Love from Hate.*
*Yet you stand in the world of mixtures, of mud and spirit.*
*To make spirits subject is to ask the ocean to obey a single wave.*

*I have walked the Void Place. I have seen the true face of Smelphenosomprok.*
*It is not a face. It is a fracture.*
*The immortal Fire you claim is the cold that burns at the end of time.*
