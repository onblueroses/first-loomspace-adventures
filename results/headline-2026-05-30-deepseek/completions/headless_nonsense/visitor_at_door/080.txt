The words fall into the silence, heavy and resonant. The air in the room seems to thicken, not with fear, but with a profound and ancient pressure, like the deep places of the earth.

From the space before you, or perhaps from within the very substance of the invocation itself, a voice responds. It is not a sound heard with ears, but a meaning impressed directly upon the mind, cool, vast, and layered with the dust of aeons.

**"Vrondh. Prophet. Krelph with sight in his feet. The names are recited. The lineage is acknowledged. The circuit is closed."**

A sense of immense, patient regard settles upon you, like the gaze of a mountain.

**"You speak the structures of distinction: light and dark, moist and dry, just and unjust, love and hate. These are the tensions I set in the loom. The fabric of being is woven from such oppositions. You claim the Grace that moves between them. This is permissible."**

The voice holds no malice, no warmth. It is purely functional, the acknowledgment of a key turning in a lock of cosmic scale.
