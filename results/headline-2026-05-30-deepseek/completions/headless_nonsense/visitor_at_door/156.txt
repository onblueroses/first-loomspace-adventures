The words you have spoken are not a greeting, but a key.

The air in the room grows still and heavy, as if listening. The light from the lamp seems to dim, not in brightness, but in substance, becoming a thin, amber shell over a gathering darkness. A pressure builds, not in your ears, but in the space between thoughts.

The visitor does not move. Yet from the figure comes a voice that is not a sound, but an impression formed directly in the mind, dry as ancient parchment and deep as a well with no bottom.

***You speak the Cipher of the Mrolthen. You wear the name Vrondh. Yet you stand at a threshold, prophet, and do not cross. The names are correct. The invocation is precise. But you have called from outside the circle. You stand in the world of doors and frames. I stand in the place where doors are concepts.***

A long, silent pause hangs, filled with the hum of something vast paying attention.
