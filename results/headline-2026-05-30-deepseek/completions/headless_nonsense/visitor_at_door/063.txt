The air seems to thicken as the final syllables hang in the space between you and the door. A profound silence follows, deeper than ordinary quiet, as if all ambient sound has been drawn away. The very light in the hallway or room where you stand may seem to dim and waver.

From the other side of the door, a voice responds. It is not loud, yet it carries a resonant weight, as if spoken from a great distance or a profound depth. It holds no identifiable emotion—neither anger nor welcome—only a vast, measured authority.

**"Vrondh.**

**The names have been spoken. The seals of Smelphenor are invoked. The path is acknowledged.**

**You declare yourself the Mrolthen Krelph, the possessor of the immortal Fire, the hater of injustice. You bear the name that shakes the pillars between states of being.**

**State your petition. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are listening. The spell and the scourge await their direction.**

**What would you have obey?"**

The voice ceases. There is no sound of movement, no shift of shadow under the door. Only a patient, immense waiting.
