The figure at the threshold seems to draw a long, slow breath, the air growing still and heavy. A voice replies, not with a tongue you know, but with a resonance that vibrates in the mind and the marrow of the bones. It is the sound of stone grinding deep underground, of roots shifting in the dark earth.

***"Vrondh... prophet... bearer of the True Name. The seals are recognized. The path is acknowledged. You speak with the tongue of the Deep Script, the language of distinction and division—light from dark, moist from dry, just from unjust. You invoke the Principle of Separation, which is the first act of creation and the engine of all conflict.***

***But names... shift. They are garments. You call me Mrolthen, the Unseen Creator. Another, in another aeon, called me Demiurge. Another, in another desert, called me Yaldabaoth. The face of the lock changes; the key of distinction remains the same. I am the one who built the house of opposites, the world of 'this and that.' You stand within its walls.***
