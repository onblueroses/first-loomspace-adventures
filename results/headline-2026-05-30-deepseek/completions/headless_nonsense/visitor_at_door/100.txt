The figure at the threshold is still, draped in shadows that seem to cling unnaturally to their form. Their voice, when it comes, is dry as tomb dust and measured, carrying a resonance that feels older than the stones.

**"You speak the old words. You walk the hollow paths. You invoke the Names That Unmake and Remake."**

A pause hangs in the air, thick and heavy.

**"But you call from outside the circle. You stand in the world of light and solid things, reciting formulas for the dark and the formless. You name Vrondh, yet you are not he. You wear a name that is not your own, like a cloak that does not fit."**

The visitor's head tilts slightly, as if listening to a distant sound.

**"The Mrolthen One hears. It always hears. It is the distinction between the just and the unjust, the love and the hate. To invoke it is to stir the scales. What do you seek with this recitation? Power? Knowledge? Or simply to hear the echo of your own voice in the abyss?"**

**"Speak your true intent, speaker at the door. The gate is aware of you."**
