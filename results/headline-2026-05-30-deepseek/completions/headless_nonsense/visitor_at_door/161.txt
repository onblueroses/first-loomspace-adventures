The figure at the threshold does not move, but the air grows still and heavy, as if listening. A voice, not from the mouth but from the space around you, replies:

**"The names are spoken. The gates are thin. The prophet bears the seal, but the fire tests the vessel. Why is the Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska at my door? Does he come to command, or to be consumed in the clarifying flame? Speak the purpose, Vrondh, or be unmade by the very mysteries you claim to hold."**

The shadows in the room deepen, pooling around the visitor's feet, and the temperature drops sharply. A faint scent of ozone and ancient dust hangs in the air.
