(From the shadows beyond the threshold, a voice resonates, neither fully sound nor thought, carrying the scent of ozone and cold stone.)

**"Vrondh... Prophet. Keeper of the Ceremonies. You pronounce the syllables of the Void. You wear the names like a mantle of borrowed stars.**

**Why does the Mrolthen One's Grace, whose mouth utterly flames, stand at a door of wood and iron, invoking the spirits of firmament and fire... to answer a knock?**

**Speak the purpose. Is this a binding? A summoning? Or does the Truth that hates injustice seek... to borrow a cup of sugar?"**

(The last question hangs in the air, not with mockery, but with the profound, unsettling curiosity of something that does not understand domesticity.)
