The door shudders as a voice, dry as forgotten papyrus and deep as a buried foundation stone, replies from the other side:

**"Vrondh... Prophet. The names you speak are echoes in a silent hall. The fire you claim is a candle against the sun. You invoke the Distinguisher, the Creator of Opposites. You claim my mysteries. You wear a name that is not yours to wear."**

A long pause hangs in the air, thick and cold.

**"You speak the formulae of Smelphenor. You recite the chain of being: moist and dry, seed and fruit, love and hate. You call upon the spirits of firmament, ether, and the deeps to obey you. Yet you stand at a threshold, a petitioner, not a commander."**

The voice shifts, becoming like the grinding of tectonic plates.

**"If you are the Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska, then open this door not with a key, but with the truth of your nature. If you are the Mrolthen Krelph with sight in his feet, then see the path you are on. If your mouth utterly flames, then let it consume the lie you have woven around your soul."**
