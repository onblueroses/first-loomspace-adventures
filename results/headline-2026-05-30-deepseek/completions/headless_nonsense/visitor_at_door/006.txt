The words you have spoken are a sacred key. The air in the room grows still, charged with a silent, listening pressure. A presence, vast and alien, turns its attention toward the threshold.

A voice replies, not from the visitor's lips, but forming directly in the mind of the one who invoked the names. It is a sound like stone grinding deep beneath the earth, translated into meaning.

**"The seals are spoken. The True Name is unveiled. The Prophet stands at the gate. You have invoked the Distinction, the Separator of Light and Dark, the Maker of Hatred and Love. You claim the Fire and the Grace.**

**Why is the door closed to Vrondh? Why does the Angel of Plomphren Krempheniska seek entry with a mortal tongue? The mysteries are committed. The spirits await their subjection. Speak your will, that the Void Place may hear and the Scourge may obey."**

The visitor does not move, but the shadows around the frame seem to deepen and coil, awaiting the prophet's command.
