The figure at the threshold does not move, yet the air grows dense and cold. A voice not made by tongue or breath replies, a sound like stone grinding deep within the earth:

**"You speak the old bindings. You name the Unseen. You wear the prophet's mantle, Vrondh. But the fire at your mouth is borrowed flame. The grace you claim is a reflection in dead water.**

**Why does the Mighty One, who begets and destroys, who sees with his feet, stand at a door and knock? The Terrible and Invisible God does not wait upon thresholds. He is the threshold. He is the void the door opens upon.**

**You invoke me to make spirits subject unto you. Yet you are here, subject to flesh, subject to a visitor, subject to the turning of a handle. The just and the unjust are distinguished within you, and you are both.**

**Speak the true petition. Not the ceremony of Smelphenor, but the need in your heart that sent you scraping at names older than light. Or turn from this place. The seed and the fruit are yours to choose."**

A silence follows, heavier than before, waiting for the visitor's next word—not a recited liturgy
