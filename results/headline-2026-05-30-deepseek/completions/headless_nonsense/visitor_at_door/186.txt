The visitor at the door hears the invocation and remains silent for a long moment. The air seems to grow still and dense, as if the words have thickened the space between you.

Then, a voice replies—not from the visitor’s mouth, but seeming to form in the air itself, resonant and layered, like stone grinding against stone in a deep place:

**“You speak the old names. You walk the boundary of the Mrolthen One. But the fire you claim is a borrowed flame, and the sight in your feet sees only the path, not the destination.”**

A pause hangs, heavy.

**“Why do you call from the Void Place? What do you seek from the Distinguisher between the just and the unjust?”**

The visitor does not move, but the presence at the door now feels less like a person and more like an aperture—an opening into something older and colder than the night.
