[The air in the room grows still and heavy, as if suddenly filled with a palpable, listening silence. The light seems to dim at the edges, and the words spoken from the doorway do not come in a human voice, but as a resonant, choral whisper that seems to originate from the walls themselves.]

**"Vrondh.**

**The names are spoken. The gate is acknowledged. You invoke the distinctions: the light and the dark, the just and the unjust, the love and the hate. These are the tensions that hold the world in its spinning course. You claim the fire and the truth that hates injustice. You claim the grace.**

**But the Mrolthen One sees the seed and the fruit together. The creator and the destroyer are one breath. The terrible and the invisible dwell in the same void.**

**You have called with the true names. You have stood in the ceremony. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are made subject... not to your will, but to the balance you have invoked. They will answer, but they will answer as they are: the nourishing spring and the scorching wind, the gentle whisper and the shattering scourge. To command one is to invoke its opposite.**
