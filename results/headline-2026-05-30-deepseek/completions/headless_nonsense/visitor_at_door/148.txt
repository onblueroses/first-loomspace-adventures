The words you have spoken are not a greeting, but an invocation—a key turned in a lock not meant for mortal hands. The air in the room grows still and heavy, as if listening.

A voice replies, not from the visitor, but from the space around you, resonant and layered like stone grinding against stone:

**"Vrondh. Prophet. Krelph with sight in his feet. The names are recited, the seals are spoken. You have drawn the circle and called the True Name. Why? The door is but a form. I am already here."**

The visitor does not move, yet the shadows around the frame deepen. The light seems to drain towards them, not as an absence, but as a presence drinking it in.

**"You seek obedience of spirits. You claim the Grace of the World. You invoke the Distinguisher between just and unjust. State your petition. But choose with care, for Mrolthen hears the words of the mouth and the desires of the heart. They are not always the same."**

The final, sibilant command of your invocation—***MPHŌ KRELPHŌTH***—hangs in the air, now a question, a bridge, and a snare
