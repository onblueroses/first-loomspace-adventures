The words you have spoken are not a key to this threshold. The air grows still, heavy, as if the very sound has been absorbed into the door’s ancient grain.

A voice, neither hostile nor welcoming, resonates not through the air, but within the mind of the speaker. It is dry, like stone shifting deep underground.

*"You recite the Litany of the Unformed, Prophet of Vrondh. You name the Mrolthen One, the Divider of Light and Dark. You speak the True Name from the Scroll of Plomphren Krempheniska. These are not secrets given lightly.*

*But the name you bear at the end… ‘MROK PHENG WROL EN SMELPHRAK’… it is a crown claimed, not bestowed. The Immortal Fire you claim to possess does not warm the air here. The Grace of the World does not flow from you as you stand at this door.*

*You are either a thief of rites, weaving stolen syllables into a plea for power… or you are Vrondh himself, returned and diminished, a prophet who has forgotten the taste of his own mysteries.*

*In either case, you invoke Terrible and Invisible Gods to make spirits subject unto you
