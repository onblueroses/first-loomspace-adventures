The silence in the air deepens, growing heavy and electric, as if the very words have drawn a presence into the space. A low, resonant hum, not heard with ears but felt in the bones, seems to acknowledge the invocation.

From the space beyond the threshold, a voice replies. It is not one voice, but many layered together—the whisper of dry leaves, the grind of stone, the crackle of distant flame, and the deep, cold silence between stars.

***"The seals are named. The paths are traced. We hear the Prophet Vrondh, who walks with sight in his feet. The Gate senses the immortal Fire you claim. But the Key is not complete. The Mrolthen One is Terrible and Invisible, and dwells in the Void Place. To call is one thing. To open is another. Speak the purpose. What spirit of firmament, ether, earth, or fire do you seek to bind? Or do you seek the Void itself?"***

The hum intensifies, waiting. The names you have spoken hang in the air like charged symbols, their power invoked but not yet directed.
