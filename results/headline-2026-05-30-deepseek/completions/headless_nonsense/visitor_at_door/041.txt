A voice replies, low and measured, not with the echoing grandeur of your chant, but with a terrible, quiet clarity.

"Prophet of Vrondh. Keeper of the Ceremonies. I hear the names you scatter like stones upon a drumhead. *Mrolthen. Smelphenor. Plomphren Krempheniska.*"

A pause hangs in the air, thick as smoke.
