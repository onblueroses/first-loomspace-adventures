The visitor at the door speaks, their voice low and resonant, as if echoing from a deep chamber:

**"You have spoken the Unspeakable Names. You have drawn the circle of Mrelph in the air with your breath. Why have you called, Prophet of Vrondh? The Void Place hears. The Terrible and Invisible turns an ear. State your will."**

A silence follows, thick and charged, like the air before a storm. The presence does not cross the threshold, but the space beyond the door seems now less like a hallway and more like the mouth of a cave—dark, cool, and immeasurably deep.
