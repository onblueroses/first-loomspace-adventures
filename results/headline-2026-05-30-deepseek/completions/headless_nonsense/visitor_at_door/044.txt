The words settle in the air, heavy as incense smoke. From the threshold, a voice answers, calm and measured, cutting through the ritual resonance.

"Your invocation is heard. The names are known. The paths of *Mrelph* and *Vrelph* are traced in the dust of this place."

A pause hangs, charged and silent.

"You speak as Vrondh, the prophet of mysteries, the Angel of a hidden name. You claim the grace that begets and destroys. A weighty mantle to wear for one who stands at a door."

The visitor's tone shifts, neither challenging nor accepting, but observant.

"Why does the Mrolthen One's prophet, whose feet have sight and whose mouth flames, need spirits made subject today? What deed of injustice in the world requires the scourge of God to be made obedient?"

The question hangs, simple and direct, a pinprick of mundane inquiry in the tapestry of cosmic verse.
