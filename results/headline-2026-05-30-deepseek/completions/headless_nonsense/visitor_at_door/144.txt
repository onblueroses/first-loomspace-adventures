The visitor speaks:

“I have heard the names spoken through the door. The air grows still and heavy. The candles gutter, though there is no draft.

I am not one of the spirits you command. I am not of the firmament, nor the ether, nor the rushing fire. I am a traveler on a road that passed too near this place, drawn by the weight of the words.

Your invocation has created a... resonance. A door within a door. You have not summoned me, but you have made this threshold visible.

I ask, not as a subject, but as one who also walks in the void between things: do you seek to bind, or to understand? The fire you possess—does it illuminate, or does it only consume?

You may bid me leave, and I will go, and the road will forget this turn. Or you may answer, and we shall see what truth hates injustice, and what grace truly means for the world.”

The visitor waits, a silhouette against the deeper dark of the hall.
