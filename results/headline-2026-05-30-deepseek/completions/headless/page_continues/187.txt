This text is a powerful ritual invocation from the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia**, which itself is derived from the much older **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically a spell known as the **"Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist"** or the **"Ritual of the Headless One."**

Here is a breakdown of its components and significance:

### Origins & Context
*   **Source:** The core of this text is from the **PGM V. 96-172**, a Greco-Egyptian magical handbook from around the 3rd-5th century CE. It was adapted into the Western esoteric tradition via the **Key of Solomon** and later, the **Lesser Key of Solomon (Lemegeton)**, specifically the section on the Goetia.
*   **Purpose:** It is an exorcistic and theurgic (god-invoking) ritual. The magician identifies with a divine, "Headless" (acephalic) god to gain authority over spirits. By reciting the god's attributes and secret names, the practitioner becomes the god's prophet or manifestation, commanding all spirits to obey.
