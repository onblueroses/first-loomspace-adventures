This text is a powerful and well-known ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist."**

It is a central text in historical Western esoteric practice, particularly in traditions like Hermeticism, Thelema, and modern ceremonial magic. Here’s a breakdown of its significance and structure:

### Key Context and Purpose

*   **Origin:** It comes from a Greco-Egyptian magical milieu (circa 2nd-5th centuries CE), blending Egyptian, Greek, Jewish, and early Christian elements—a hallmark of the syncretic magic of the era.
*   **Purpose:** It is an **exorcism and self-transformation ritual**. The magician identifies with a divine, "Headless" (aka "Without Beginning" or "Limitless") god to command spirits and achieve a state of spiritual authority and enlightenment. The famous line "**I am the Headless Daimon with sight in his feet**" signifies a perception beyond ordinary consciousness.
