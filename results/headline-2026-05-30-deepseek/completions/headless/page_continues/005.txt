This is an excerpt from the **Headless Rite**, also known as the **Bornless Ritual** or the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia**. It is one of the most famous and potent invocatory texts in Western ceremonial magic, particularly within the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and its derivatives.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### **Origin & Context**
*   **Source:** It appears in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172, circa 3rd-4th century CE) as a "Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist." It was adapted and popularized by 19th and 20th-century occultists.
*   **Purpose:** It is an exorcism and self-identification ritual. The magician first invokes the "Headless One" (a form of the Supreme, Unknowable God beyond form) and then, through a series of declarations, **identifies themselves with that divine power** to command spirits.
