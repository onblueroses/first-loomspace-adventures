This text is a modern English translation of the **"Bornless Ritual"** (also known as the "Preliminary Invocation" or the "Invocation of the Headless One").

It is a foundational ritual text in Western esoteric traditions, particularly in **Ceremonial Magic** and **Thelema**. Here is a breakdown of its key elements:

### Origins and Context
*   **Source:** It originates from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, an ancient Greco-Egyptian magical text dating from around the 2nd to 5th centuries CE.
*   **Name:** It is called "Bornless" (or "Headless") because it invokes a god who is without beginning or end—a primal, self-created deity.
*   **Modern Use:** It was adapted and popularized in the late 19th and early 20th centuries by occultists like **Samuel Liddell MacGregor Mathers** of the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and later by **Aleister Crowley**, who made it a central ritual in his system of Thelema.
