This text is a powerful and well-known ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**. It is commonly called the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."**

Its purpose is the invocation of a supreme, formless divine power (the "Headless One") to establish the magician as a prophet and master over all spirits. The ritual was later adapted and popularized in Western esotericism, most notably by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and by Aleister Crowley (who called it the "Preliminary Invocation" in *The Goetia* and later the "Bornless Ritual").
