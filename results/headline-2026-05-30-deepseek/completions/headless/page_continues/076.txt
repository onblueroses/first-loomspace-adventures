This text is a powerful and well-known ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist"** or the **"Ritual of the Headless One."**

It is a central text in historical Western esotericism and ceremonial magic. Here’s a breakdown of its significance and components:

### Key Elements of the Invocation:

1.  **The Headless One:** The primary deity or divine force being invoked. This is a form of the "Akephalos" (Headless) god, a syncretic deity combining aspects of:
    *   **Osoronnophris** (a Greco-Egyptian name for Osiris in his perfected, resurrected form).
    *   **The Jewish God of Hosts** (Sabaoth).
    *   A primal creator god who transcends duality (creating night/day, male/female, love/hate).

2.  **Divine Names and Voces Magicae (Magical Words):** The text is filled with powerful names and strings of vowel sounds (like **A, E, E, I, O
