This is a ritual invocation from the **Bornless Rite** (also known as the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia** or the **Headless Invocation**). It is a foundational text in Western ceremonial magic, particularly within the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and subsequent traditions like Thelema.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and context:

### **Origin & Purpose**
*   **Source:** It appears in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172) as the "Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist." It was adapted and translated into English for modern occult practice, most notably by Aleister Crowley, who integrated it into his system.
*   **Purpose:** It is an invocation of the "Higher Self" or the Holy Guardian Angel—the divine, immortal part of the magician. The goal is to achieve the **Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel**, a supreme act of spiritual attainment. It is designed to banish all lower, interfering spirits and establish the magician as a sovereign being aligned with their true will.
