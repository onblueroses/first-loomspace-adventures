This text is a powerful ritual incantation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist."**

It is one of the most famous and influential invocations in Western esotericism. Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### **Structure & Purpose**

The rite is a **theurgic** (divine magic) operation designed to:
1.  Invoke a supreme, invisible, "Headless" deity.
2.  Identify the magician **with that deity** through a series of "I am..." statements (a practice called *sympathetic identification* or **identification with the divine**).
3.  Command all spirits of the cosmos to obey the magician, who now speaks from a position of divine authority.
