This text is a version of the **"Bornless Ritual"** or the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia,"** a powerful ritual derived from the **Greek Magical Papyri** (specifically PGM V. 96-172, the "Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist"). It is a foundational work in Western ceremonial magic, particularly within the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and subsequent traditions like Thelema.

Here’s a breakdown of its key components and significance:
