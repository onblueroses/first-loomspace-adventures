This is a segment from the **"Bornless Ritual"** (also known as the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia"** or the **"Headless One Invocation"**). It is a powerful and ancient ritual text used in Western esoteric traditions, particularly within ceremonial magic and the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn.

Here's a breakdown of its key elements and context:

### **Origin & Significance**
*   **Source:** It appears in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM), specifically **PGM V. 96–172**, dating from late antiquity. It was adapted and popularized in modern occultism by figures like Aleister Crowley and the Golden Dawn.
*   **Purpose:** It is an invocation of the "Higher Self" or the Holy Guardian Angel—the divine, immortal consciousness within the practitioner. The goal is to achieve **Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel**, a central aim in certain magical paths.
*   **The "Headless One":** This refers to a state of consciousness beyond rational, "headed" thought. It symbolizes the limitless, primordial godhead that precedes creation and exists without the limitations of the individual ego.
