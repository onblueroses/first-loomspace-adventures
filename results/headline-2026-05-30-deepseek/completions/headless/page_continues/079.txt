This text is a powerful ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically known as the **"Stele of Jeu"** or the **"Ritual of the Headless One."** It is one of the most famous and significant exorcistic and theurgic (god-invoking) prayers in the Western esoteric tradition.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Context and Origin
*   **Source:** Papyri Graecae Magicae V. 96-172 (circa 2nd-5th century CE).
*   **Purpose:** It is used as an exorcism ritual and a rite of divine self-identification. The magician invokes a supreme, primordial god (the "Headless One") and then, through a series of declarations, temporarily *becomes* that god in order to command all spirits.
