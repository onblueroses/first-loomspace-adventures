This is a powerful and evocative ritual text known as the **"Preliminary Invocation"** or the **"Headless Rite."** It is a central ritual in modern ceremonial magic, primarily derived from a much older source.

Here is a breakdown of its origins, meaning, and modern usage:

### Origins and History
*   **Ancient Source:** The core of this text comes from a Greco-Egyptian magical papyrus known as **PGM V. 96-172** (the "Paris Papyrus"), dating from the 3rd or 4th century CE.
*   **Modern Adaptation:** It was famously translated and adapted for modern occult practice by **Aleister Crowley** and Samuel Liddell MacGregor Mathers of the **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn**. Crowley included it in his foundational magical textbook, *Liber Samekh*, as a ritual for the invocation of one's Holy Guardian Angel (the "Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel").
*   **Name:** It is called the "Headless Rite" because it invokes a "Headless One" (*Akephalos*), a primal, formless deity beyond human perception.
