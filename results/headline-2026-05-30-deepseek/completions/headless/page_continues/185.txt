This text is a powerful and composite ritual invocation, drawing primarily from two ancient magical sources: the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)** and the **Bornless Rite (or the Headless One Rite)**. It is a key ritual in modern ceremonial magic, especially within traditions like Thelema and the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn, where it is known as the **Preliminary Invocation** or used as an exorcism ritual.

Here’s a breakdown of its components and significance:

### 1. **The "Headless One"**
*   The ritual begins by addressing the "Headless One," a deity or divine state beyond normal perception (symbolized by having no head, thus no ordinary mind or face). This entity is identified as the primordial creator of all dualities (earth/heavens, night/day, darkness/light).

### 2. **Divine Names and Attributes**
*   **Osoronnophris:** A Greco-Egyptian magical name for **Osiris in his perfect, resurrected state** (Wsr-wn-nfr). The text states "whom no man hath seen," emphasizing his transcendent, invisible nature.
