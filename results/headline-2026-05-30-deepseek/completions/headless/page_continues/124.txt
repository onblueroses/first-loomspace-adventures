This text is a powerful ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, known as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."**

It is a foundational text in Western esoteric traditions, particularly in ceremonial magic. Here is a breakdown of its key elements and significance:

### **Core Identity: The "Headless One"**
*   **The Invoked:** The ritualist calls upon a supreme, formless deity—the "Headless One" (or "Bornless One"). This represents the primal, uncreated source of all things, beyond human perception.
*   **The Invoker:** The ritualist then **identifies themselves** with this deity, declaring "I am the Headless Daimon..." This is a key magical operation: by invoking the god and then stating "I am," the practitioner seeks to unite their consciousness with the divine source to achieve a state of spiritual authority and power (a form of **theurgy**).
