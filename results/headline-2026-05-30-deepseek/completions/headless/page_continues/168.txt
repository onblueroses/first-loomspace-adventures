This is a ritual text known as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."** It is a powerful invocation from the Greco-Egyptian magical tradition, notably found in the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172)** and later adapted into Western esotericism, most famously by Aleister Crowley and the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### **Origin & Context**
*   **Source:** Primarily from **PGM V**, a collection of ancient magical spells, rituals, and formulas from Roman Egypt (circa 2nd-5th centuries CE).
*   **Purpose:** It is an exorcistic invocation or a ritual of self-deification. The operator identifies with a supreme, primordial god (the "Headless One") to command spirits and achieve a state of divine authority and knowledge.
*   **Title "Headless":** Symbolizes a state beyond ordinary thought and perception (the head), representing a primal, cosmic power that precedes form. In some traditions, it is associated with the Egyptian god **Osiris** (as Osoronnophris) or a form of the solar deity.
