This text is a powerful and well-known ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often called the **"Stele of Jeu"** or the **"Ritual of the Headless One."**

It is a central text in historical Western esoteric practice, particularly within traditions like **Ceremonial Magic**, **Thelema**, and the **Golden Dawn**.

Here's a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Core Identity: The "Headless One"
*   **The Invoked Deity:** The magician is invoking a supreme, primordial god described as "Headless" (*Akephalos*). This symbolizes a divinity beyond human perception and rationality, existing before and above all dualities (heaven/earth, light/dark, male/female, justice/injustice).
*   **Syncretic Nature:** The god is identified with a fusion of Egyptian (**Osoronnophris** - a form of Osiris), Jewish (**Iao Sabaoth** - Yahweh of Hosts), and Gnostic names. This reflects the blending of religious traditions in the syncretic magic of late antiquity.
