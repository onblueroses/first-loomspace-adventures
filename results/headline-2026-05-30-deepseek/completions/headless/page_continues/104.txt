This text is a powerful and well-known ritual incantation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist."**

It is a central text in Western esoteric and ceremonial magic traditions. Here’s a breakdown of its significance and components:

### Key Context and History
*   **Source:** It comes from a collection of Egyptian magical texts from Greco-Roman Egypt (circa 100 BCE - 400 CE), written in Greek but blending Egyptian, Jewish, Greek, and early Christian elements (a synthesis called **syncretism**).
*   **Purpose:** It is an invocation to achieve a state of divine authority and command over spirits. The magician identifies with a supreme, primordial god (the "Headless One") to become a **"daimon"** (a divine spirit or empowered being) themselves.
