This text is a modern compilation and adaptation of several ancient magical and religious invocations, primarily from two sources:

1.  **The Bornless Rite (or The Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia):** This is a ritual from the Western esoteric tradition, notably used in ceremonial magic. Its purpose is to invoke a "higher" or "divine" aspect of the self (often called the Holy Guardian Angel) and to establish the magician's authority over spirits. The phrases "Thee I invoke, the Headless One," "I am the Headless Daimon," and the series of "I am" statements are central to this rite.

2.  **The Paris Papyrus (PGM IV. 3007-3086):** This is a Greek magical papyrus from antiquity. It contains the original "Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist," a powerful exorcism formula. The long string of barbarous names ("Ar, Thiao, Rheibet...") and the declaration "I am Mosheh (Moses) thy prophet" come directly from this papyrus.

**Key Themes and Elements:**

*   **The "Headless One":** A deity or divine principle described as the creator
