This text is a modern English translation of a powerful and ancient ritual formula from the Graeco-Egyptian magical tradition, specifically from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**. It is known as the **"Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist"** or the **"Ritual of the Headless One."**

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Origin and Context
*   **Source:** It is found in **PGM V, lines 96-172**, dating from around the 3rd to 5th centuries CE.
*   **Purpose:** It is an exorcism and self-deification ritual. The magician identifies with a supreme, primordial god (the "Headless One") in order to command all spirits and demonic forces. It is a classic example of a **"logos"** or **"synthēma"** ritual, where the practitioner recites the attributes and secret names of a deity to become that deity for the ritual's duration.
