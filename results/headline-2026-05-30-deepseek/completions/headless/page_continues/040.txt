This text is a modern English translation of a powerful ritual invocation known as the **"Bornless Rite"** or the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia."**

Here is a breakdown of its context and significance:

### Origins and History
*   **Ancient Source:** It originates from a Greco-Egyptian magical papyrus (likely the *Papyri Graecae Magicae*, or PGM). The original Greek version is often titled "The Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist."
*   **Ceremonial Magic:** It was adopted and adapted into Western ceremonial magic, most famously by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn in the late 19th century. Aleister Crowley later revised it as a central ritual in his own magical system (Thelema), calling it the "Preliminary Invocation" or the "Invocation of the Higher Genius."
