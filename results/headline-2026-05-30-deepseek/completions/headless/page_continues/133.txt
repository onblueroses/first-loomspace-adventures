This text is a modern translation of a ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."**

It is a powerful ritual of self-identification with a divine, primordial "Headless One" (a being beyond rational thought and limitation) for the purpose of gaining authority over all spirits.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### 1. **Structure and Purpose**
*   **Invocation:** The magician begins by invoking the "Headless One," describing its attributes as the creator of all dualities (earth/heaven, light/dark, male/female, love/hate).
*   **Identification with the Divine:** The operator then shifts from speaking *to* the god to speaking *as* the god, declaring "I am the Headless Daimon..." This is the core magical act: temporarily becoming the deity to command its powers.
*   **Command:** From this empowered state, the magician commands all spirits of every element to be subject to him.
