This text is a modern English translation of a powerful ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, known as the **"Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist"** or, more commonly, **"The Bornless Rite."**

Here is a breakdown of its significance and components:

### **Context & Origin**
*   **Source:** It comes from a collection of Egyptian magical, religious, and alchemical texts from Greco-Roman Egypt (circa 2nd century BCE to 5th century CE). These papyri blend Egyptian, Greek, Jewish, and early Christian elements.
*   **Purpose:** It is a ritual of **exorcism and divine invocation**. The magician identifies with a supreme, primordial deity to gain authority over all spirits and forces. Its goal is **Knowledge and Conversation with the Holy Guardian Angel**—a central aim in certain Western esoteric traditions.
