This text is a powerful ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically known as the **"Stele of Jeu"** or the **"Ritual of the Headless One."** It is one of the most famous and significant exorcistic and theurgic (divine-invoking) formulas from late antiquity.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and meaning:

### 1. **The "Headless One"**
*   This is the primary deity being invoked—a primal, creator god who exists before and beyond form (hence "headless," meaning without limitation or defined shape).
*   He is identified with **Osoronnophris** (a Graeco-Egyptian name for Osiris in his perfect, resurrected form) and with the Hebrew divine name **IAŌ SABAŌTH** (Yahweh of Hosts).
*   The invoker first describes this god's attributes as the supreme creator of all dualities (heaven/earth, light/dark, male/female, justice/injustice, love/hate).
