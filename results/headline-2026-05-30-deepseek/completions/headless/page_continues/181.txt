This text is a modern English translation of a powerful ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist."**

It is one of the most famous and influential exorcism and self-deification rituals from late antiquity. Here is a breakdown of its components and significance:

### Key Elements of the Invocation:

1.  **The Invoked God (The Headless One):**
    *   **Osoronnophris:** A Greco-Egyptian magical name for **Osiris in his perfect, resurrected form** (Egyptian *Wsr Wn-nfr*, "Osiris the Beautiful Being").
    *   **Iaō, Sabaōth:** Names of the Jewish God (Yahweh, Lord of Hosts) incorporated into the syncretic divine framework of the era.
    *   **The Creator:** The text establishes the deity as the primal creator of all dualities (earth/heavens, light/dark, male/female, just/unjust). This establishes supreme authority.
