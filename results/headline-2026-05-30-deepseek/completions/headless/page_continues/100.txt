This appears to be a version of the **"Bornless Ritual"** or the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia"**—a powerful ritual text from Western ceremonial magic, specifically associated with the **Key of Solomon** and **Thelema** traditions. It is a prayer of invocation meant to call upon a supreme, formless divine force (the "Headless One") to grant the magician authority over spirits.

Here's a breakdown of its components and significance:

---

### **1. Origin & Name**
- **Original Source:** Derived from a Greek papyrus (the *"Greek Magical Papyri"* or *PGM*, specifically PGM V. 96-172), known as the *"Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist."*
- **Common Names:** The Bornless Ritual, The Preliminary Invocation (from Aleister Crowley's adaptation in *The Goetia*), The Invocation of the Headless One.
- **Purpose:** To invoke the highest divine authority (beyond form, hence "Headless") to purify the magician and command spiritual forces.
