This text is a powerful ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the "**Headless Rite**" or the "**Bornless Ritual**." It became a cornerstone of later Western esoteric traditions, most notably adapted and used by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and by Aleister Crowley in his system of Thelema (where it is known as the "Preliminary Invocation" or the "Bornless One Ritual").

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Structure and Meaning

1.  **Invocation of the "Headless One":** The deity is addressed as "Headless" (Akephalos), signifying a transcendental, formless god beyond human perception and limitation—a primal creative force prior to duality. This being is identified with the Egyptian **Osiris in his most transcendent form** (Osoronnophris, meaning "Osiris-Beautiful-Face").
