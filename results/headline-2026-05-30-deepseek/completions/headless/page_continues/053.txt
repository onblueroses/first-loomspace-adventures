This text is a modern English translation of the **"Bornless Ritual"** or the **"Preliminary Invocation"** from the **Lesser Key of Solomon** (specifically, the **Goetia**). It is a foundational ritual in Western esoteric traditions, particularly within ceremonial magic and the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn, where it is used as an invocation of the Higher Self or the Holy Guardian Angel.

Here’s a breakdown of its key components and context:

### 1. **Origin and Name**
*   **Source:** It appears in S.L. MacGregor Mathers' 1904 translation of the **Goetia** (Book 1 of the *Lemegeton*).
*   **"Bornless" (Headless One):** Refers to an ancient Egyptian and Greco-Egyptian magical concept—the **"Headless God"** or **Akephalos**—a deity without a head, symbolizing a transcendent consciousness beyond rational thought (the intellect being associated with the head).
*   **Alternative Title:** Often called the **"Preliminary Invocation"** because it was used to purify and consecrate the magician before summoning spirits (demons)
