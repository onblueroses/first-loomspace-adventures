This text is a modern English translation of the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual"** (also known as the **"Preliminary Invocation"** in some grimoires). It is one of the most famous and influential invocations in Western esotericism.

Here is a breakdown of its context and significance:

### **Origin and History**

*   **Ancient Source:** It originates from a Greek magical papyrus, **PGM V. 96-172**, dated to around the 3rd or 4th century CE. This papyrus was part of a vast collection of spells, rituals, and hymns from Greco-Roman Egypt.
*   **Modern Revival:** It was introduced to the modern occult world primarily through **Aleister Crowley** and the **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn**. Crowley edited and popularized it, and it became a cornerstone of his system of magic. He referred to it as the "Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia" and used it as a ritual to invoke one's Holy Guardian Angel (Higher Self).
