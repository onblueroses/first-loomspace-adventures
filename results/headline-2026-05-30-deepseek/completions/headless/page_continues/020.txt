This is a section from the **"Bornless Rite"** (also known as the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia** or the **Invocation of the Headless One**). It is a powerful ritual text derived from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172) and later adapted into Western ceremonial magic, most notably by Aleister Crowley and the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn.

Here is a breakdown of its key elements and significance:

### **Context and Purpose**
*   **Exorcism and Invocation:** In its original PGM context, it was used as an exorcism ritual to invoke a higher, "headless" deity to compel spirits and gain oracular powers. In modern ceremonial magic, it is used as an invocation of the practitioner's own **Holy Guardian Angel** (Higher Self or Divine Genius) and to establish supreme magical authority before other operations.
*   **"Headless One":** Refers to a primal, formless deity beyond human comprehension (often associated with **Osoronnophris**—a Hellenized name for Osiris in his perfect, resurrected form).
