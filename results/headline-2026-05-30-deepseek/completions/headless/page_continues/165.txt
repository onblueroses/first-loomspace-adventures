This text is a modern English translation of a powerful and ancient ritual incantation known as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."** Its origins trace back to Greek magical papyri from antiquity (specifically, the *Paris Papyrus*, PGM V. 96-172). It was later adapted and incorporated into Western esoteric traditions, most notably by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn, and subsequently by Aleister Crowley, who made it a central practice in his system of magick.

Here is a breakdown of its key elements and significance:

### Core Identity and Purpose
*   **The "Headless One":** This is the invoked deity—a primal, formless, and supreme god beyond human perception ("whom no man hath seen at any time"). Being "headless" symbolizes being without limit or defined intellect, the source of all duality (light/dark, male/female, just/unjust).
