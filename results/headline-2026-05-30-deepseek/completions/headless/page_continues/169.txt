This is a text from the **Greek Magical Papyri** (specifically PGM V, lines 96–172), commonly known as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."** It is a powerful invocation used in late antique magical and theurgic practice, later adapted into Western esoteric traditions like the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley’s works.

Here’s a breakdown of its key elements and significance:

---

### **Structure & Meaning**
1. **Invocation of the "Headless One"**  
   - The deity is addressed as formless, unseen, and prior to creation—a primordial cosmic power.
   - Identified with **Osoronnophris** (a Greco-Egyptian name for Osiris in his perfect, resurrected form) and names like **Iabou**, **Iapos**, **Abaoth**, **Sabaoth** (Hebrew divine epithets, e.g., "Lord of Hosts").

2. **Divine Attributes**  
   - Creator of dualities: heaven/earth, night/day, darkness/light, just/unjust, male/female.
   - Source of fertility (seed/fruit)
