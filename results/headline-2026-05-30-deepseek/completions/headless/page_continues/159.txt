This text is a powerful and well-known ritual incantation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**. It is commonly referred to as the **"Stele of Jeu"** or the **"Ritual of the Headless One."**

Here is a breakdown of its significance and components:

### Context & Origin
*   **Source:** It is a Hellenistic-Egyptian magical text, dating from around the 2nd to 5th centuries CE, which blends Egyptian, Greek, Jewish, and early Christian Gnostic elements into a syncretic system of ritual magic.
*   **Purpose:** It is an exorcism and a rite of spiritual empowerment. The magician identifies with a divine, "Headless" (or "All-Seeing") god to command spirits and demons. The phrase "Headless" often symbolizes a state beyond ordinary perception, seeing from all directions, or connection to the divine *Nous* (Mind/Intellect).
