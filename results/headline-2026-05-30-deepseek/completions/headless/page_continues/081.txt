This text is a modern English translation of the **"Preliminary Invocation"** or **"Headless Rite"** from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172)**. It is a powerful ritual invocation used in ceremonial magic, particularly popularized by its inclusion in **Aleister Crowley's** magical system, where it is known as the **"Bornless Ritual"** or the **"Invocation of the Headless One."**

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Core Structure and Purpose

1.  **Invocation of the "Headless One":** The ritual begins by calling upon a supreme, formless deity (the "Headless One" or "Akephalos"). This being is described as the creator of all dualities (earth/heavens, night/day, darkness/light) and is identified with several divine names from Egyptian (Osoronnophris - a form of Osiris) and Hebrew (Iabou, Iapos, Sabaoth) traditions.
