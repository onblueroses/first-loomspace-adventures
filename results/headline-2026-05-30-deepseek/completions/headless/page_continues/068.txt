This appears to be the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia**, also known as the **"Bornless Ritual"** or the **Invocation of the Headless One**. It is a powerful ritual text from the Western esoteric tradition, specifically from the **Key of Solomon** and related grimoires. Its purpose is to invoke a higher, divine authority (the "Headless God") to grant the practitioner control over spirits.

Here is a breakdown of its key elements and context:

### Core Purpose & Origin
*   **Source:** It is found in several grimoires, most notably as the opening invocation in versions of the **Lesser Key of Solomon (The Goetia)**. It is also a central ritual in Aleister Crowley's system of Magick, where he adapted it as the **"Preliminary Invocation"** in *Liber Samekh*.
*   **Goal:** To establish the magician as a purified and empowered operator, invoking the supreme divine force to act as an exorcist and controller of spirits before proceeding to summon specific entities (like the 72 Goetic demons). The practitioner identifies with the divine voice and authority.
