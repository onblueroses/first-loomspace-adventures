This text is a powerful ritual incantation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."** It is a seminal text in Western esoteric tradition, later adapted and used extensively in systems like **Hermetic Qabalah** and **Thelema** (most famously in Aleister Crowley's "Liber Samekh").

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### 1. **Structure & Purpose**
The ritual is a **theurgic** operation—an attempt to invoke a divine, "Headless" power (a form of the Highest God beyond form and intellect) to achieve **knowledge and conversation with one's Holy Guardian Angel** and command over spirits. The magician identifies with both the prophet (Mosheh/Moses) and the Godhead itself.

### 2. **Key Themes & Phrases**
*   **The Headless One:** The invoked deity is "headless" (sometimes "Bornless"), symbolizing a consciousness not limited by the rational mind or sensory perception—the primordial, formless source.
