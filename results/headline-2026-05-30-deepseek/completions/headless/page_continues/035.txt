This text is a powerful and evocative ritual invocation, primarily drawn from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically a section known as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Prayer of the Headless One."** It was later adapted and popularized in modern occultism by Aleister Crowley, who published it as **"The Bornless Ritual"** or **"The Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia."**

Here’s a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Origins & Context
*   **Source:** Papyri Graecae Magicae (PGM) V. 96-172, from Greco-Roman Egypt (circa 3rd-4th century CE). It is a syncretic text, blending Egyptian, Greek, Jewish, and early Gnostic or Christian elements.
*   **Purpose:** A ritual of self-deification and invocation. The magician identifies with a supreme, primordial god (the "Headless One") to command spirits and achieve a state of divine authority and knowledge.
