This is a passage from the **Headless Rite**, also known as the **Bornless Ritual** or the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia**. It is a powerful ritual of invocation and self-identification from the Western esoteric tradition, primarily found in:

1.  **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**: The core of the text originates from PGM V. 96-172, a Greco-Egyptian magical formula from late antiquity.
2.  **The Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn**: They adapted and popularized it in the late 19th century as a key ritual for invoking the Higher Genius and banishing hostile forces.
3.  **Aleister Crowley's Works**: Crowley further revised and emphasized it, calling it the "Preliminary Invocation" in *The Goetia* and a central practice in his system of magick.
