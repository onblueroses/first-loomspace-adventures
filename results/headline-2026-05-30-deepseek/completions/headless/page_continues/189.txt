This text is a powerful and composite ritual invocation, primarily drawn from the **Papyri Graecae Magicae (PGM)**, specifically the famous **"Stele of Jeu"** or the **"Ritual of the Headless One."** It is a central text in late antique Greco-Egyptian magical practice and has been highly influential in modern occult traditions, particularly **Ceremonial Magic** and **Thelema**.

Here’s a breakdown of its components and significance:

### 1. **Source and Nature**
*   **PGM V, lines 96-172:** This is the primary source. It's a **exorcistic and theurgic ritual** designed to invoke a supreme, invisible god (the "Headless One") to gain authority over all spirits.
*   **Modern Usage:** It was adapted and popularized in the 20th century by **Aleister Crowley** (who published it as "The Preliminary Invocation" in *The Goetia*) and later by **Israel Regardie**. It is a key ritual in the curriculum of the **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn** and its offshoots.
