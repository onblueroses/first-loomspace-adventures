This is a segment from the **"Bornless Ritual"** (also known as the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia"** or the **"Headless One Invocation"**).

### Context and Significance:

*   **Origins:** It's an ancient Greek papyrus text (part of the **Greek Magical Papyri**, often abbreviated **PGM**) that was adapted and popularized in modern Western ceremonial magic, particularly by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and later by Aleister Crowley.
*   **Purpose:** It is a ritual of invocation and purification. The magician identifies with a primordial, "Headless" (or "Crownless") divine consciousness—a state beyond ordinary thought and personality—in order to become a vessel for higher powers and command spiritual entities. The ritual aims to establish the magician's authority as a "prophet" or operator of divine will.
