This is an excerpt from the **Bornless Ritual** (also known as the **Preliminary Invocation** or the **Invocation of the Headless One**), a powerful ceremonial text rooted in Graeco-Egyptian magical and theurgic traditions. It appears in the **Greek Magical Papyri** (PGM) and was later adapted and popularized in Western esotericism, notably by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and by Aleister Crowley, who integrated it into his system of Thelema.

Here is a breakdown of its key elements and significance:

### **Context and Origin**
*   **Source:** It comes from PGM V. 96–172, a collection of ancient magical spells and invocations dating from the 2nd century BCE to the 5th century CE.
*   **Purpose:** It is a ritual of **exorcism and divine invocation**. The magician identifies with a supreme, primordial deity (the "Headless One") to achieve a state of purified consciousness and command spiritual forces. The goal is to become a vessel for the divine, transcending the ego (the "head" or rational mind) to access higher power and knowledge.
