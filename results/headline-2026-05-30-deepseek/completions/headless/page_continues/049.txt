This text is a modern English translation of a ritual incantation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."**

It is a powerful invocation used in ceremonial magic to invoke a supreme, formless deity (the "Headless One") and to identify the magician with that divine power in order to command spirits.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### 1. **The Deity Invoked: The Headless One**
*   **Headless:** Symbolizes a state beyond the limits of the mind (the "head"), representing a primordial, cosmic god who precedes and transcends all forms. In some traditions, it is associated with the Egyptian god **Osiris** in his resurrected, transcendental aspect (Osoronnophris).
*   **Creator:** The text establishes the deity as the creator of all dualities (earth/heaven, night/day, darkness/light, just/unjust, male/female). This establishes its supreme authority over all creation.
*   **Secret Names:** The deity is addressed with a series of secret, barbarous names
