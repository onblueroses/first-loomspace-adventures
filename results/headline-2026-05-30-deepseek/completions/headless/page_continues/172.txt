This text is a powerful and well-known ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."**

It is a central text in Western esotericism, later adapted and used prominently by occultists like **Aleister Crowley** (who incorporated it into his system as the "Preliminary Invocation" in *Liber Samekh*) and the **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn**.

Here's a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Core Structure & Meaning

1.  **Invocation of the "Headless One":** The deity is addressed as "Headless" (Akephalos), signifying a divinity beyond rational, human understanding (without a head to reason with). It is a primal, cosmic creator god.
