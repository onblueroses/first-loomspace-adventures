This text is a modern English translation of a ritual incantation from the **Greek Magical Papyri**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."**

Its significance and context are as follows:

**1. Origin and History:**
*   It comes from a corpus of Hellenistic-era magical texts (circa 2nd century BCE to 5th century CE) that blend Egyptian, Greek, Jewish, Gnostic, and early Christian elements—a syncretic tradition often called **"Greco-Egyptian magic."**
*   It was rediscovered and translated in the 19th century. It became profoundly influential on Western esotericism, most notably within the **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn**, which adapted it as a central ritual for attaining "Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel."
*   **Aleister Crowley** further revised and popularized it as **"The Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia"** and **"The Bornless Ritual."**
