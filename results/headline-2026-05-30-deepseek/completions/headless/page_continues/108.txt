This text is a powerful ritual incantation known as the **"Preliminary Invocation" or "Bornless Rite,"** most famously adapted for modern occult practice from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)** by Aleister Crowley and others. It is a central ritual in ceremonial magic, particularly within the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Thelemic practice.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Origins & Context
*   **Source:** It is derived from **PGM V. 96-172**, a Greco-Egyptian magical text from antiquity (circa 3rd-4th century CE).
*   **Purpose:** In its original form, it was a **"headless rite"** (*akephalos*) used to invoke a supreme, formless deity to achieve a state of divine consciousness or "Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel." Its goal is to banish lesser influences and unite the magician with their higher, divine self.
