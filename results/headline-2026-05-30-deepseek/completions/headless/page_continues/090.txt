This text is a powerful ritual incantation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."** It is a foundational text in Western esotericism, later adapted and used prominently by occultists like the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley (who called it the "Preliminary Invocation" in his work *Liber Samekh*).

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Core Structure & Purpose
The ritual is an invocation of a supreme, formless deity (the "Headless One") to achieve **knowledge and conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel** and command over spirits. Its primary goals are:
1.  **Theurgic Ascension:** To unite the magician's consciousness with the divine, "Headless" (i.e., beyond the limits of the mind/ego) source.
2.  **Exorcistic Command:** To subjugate all spirits and forces to the magician's will, as seen in the climax: "*make all spirits subject unto me...*"
