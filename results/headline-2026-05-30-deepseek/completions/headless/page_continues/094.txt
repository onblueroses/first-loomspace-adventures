This appears to be an excerpt from a well-known ritual text in Western esotericism: the **"Bornless (or Headless) Rite."** It is primarily derived from the **Greek Magical Papyri** (PGM V. 96–172), where it is called the "Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist." It was later adapted and popularized in modern ceremonial magic, notably by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and by Aleister Crowley (who called it the "Preliminary Invocation" in *Liber Samekh*).
