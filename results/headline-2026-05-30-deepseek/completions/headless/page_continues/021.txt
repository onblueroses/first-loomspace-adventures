This text is a modern English translation of a powerful and ancient ritual invocation known as the **"Preliminary Invocation"** or the **"Invocation of the Headless One."**

Its history and significance are deep, connecting several key threads of Western esoteric tradition:

### 1. **Primary Source: The Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**
The core of this invocation comes from **PGM V. 96-172**, a collection of Greco-Egyptian magical spells and rituals dating from the 2nd century BCE to the 5th century CE. It was originally written in Greek. This specific spell is titled **"The Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist"** or **"The Bornless Rite."** The "Headless One" (Akephalos) is a divine, primordial god-form beyond form.
