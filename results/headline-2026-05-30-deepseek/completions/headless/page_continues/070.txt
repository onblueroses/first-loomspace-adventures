This is a section from the **Bornless Ritual** (also known as the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia** or the **Invocation of the Headless One**). It is a foundational ritual in Western esotericism, particularly within the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and subsequent ceremonial magic traditions.

Here's a breakdown of its key elements and significance:

### Origin and Context
*   **Source:** It appears in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172, circa 3rd-4th century CE) as a "Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist." It was later adapted and translated for modern magical use.
*   **Purpose:** It is an invocation of a supreme, formless deity (the "Headless One") to establish the magician's authority and purify their sphere of operation before other magical work (like evoking spirits).

1.  **Invocation of the Creator God:** The ritual begins by addressing a primal, invisible god responsible for all cosmic dualities (earth/heavens, night/day, light/darkness, male/female, just/unjust).
