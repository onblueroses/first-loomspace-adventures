This text is a powerful and well-known ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."**

Its later adaptation and popularization in Western occultism, especially through **Aleister Crowley** (who incorporated it as the "Preliminary Invocation" in *The Goetia* and as the core of his "Liber Samekh"), has made it one of the most significant invocations in modern ceremonial magic.

Here is a breakdown of its components and significance:
