### **1. Origin and Context**
*   **Source:** The core of this text is from the **Papyri Graecae Magicae (PGM)**, a collection of ancient Greek, Egyptian, and Jewish magical spells from Greco-Roman Egypt (circa 2nd century BCE to 5th century CE).
*   **Modern Use:** It was famously adapted and used in late 19th/early 20th-century Western esotericism, most notably by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and by Aleister Crowley (who called it the "Preliminary Invocation" in his work *Liber Samekh*). Its purpose is to invoke a supreme, formless deity to achieve **Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel**—a central goal in these traditions.
