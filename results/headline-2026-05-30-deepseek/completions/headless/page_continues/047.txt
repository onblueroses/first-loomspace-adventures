This text is a modern English translation of a powerful ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, known as the **"Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist"** or the **"Rite of the Headless One."**

It is one of the most famous and potent exorcistic and theurgic (god-invoking) rituals from the ancient world. Here’s a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Context & Purpose
*   **Source:** The Greek Magical Papyri (PGM) are a collection of spells, formulas, and rituals from Greco-Roman Egypt (circa 2nd century BCE to 5th century CE), blending Egyptian, Greek, Jewish, and early Christian elements.
*   **Function:** This is a **theurgic invocation**. The practitioner aims to identify with and channel a supreme, primordial deity (the "Headless One") in order to command all spirits. It was used for protection, exorcism, spiritual empowerment, and achieving a state of divine authority.
