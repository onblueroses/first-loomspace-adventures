This is a transcription of a powerful and ancient ritual text known as the "Bornless Rite" or the "Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia." It is a foundational ritual in Western esoteric traditions, particularly within ceremonial magic and Hermeticism. Its purpose is to invoke a supreme, formless divine power (the "Headless One") to purify the magician and establish their authority over spiritual forces.

Let's break down the key elements and their significance:

### **Context and Origin**
*   **Source:** It appears in grimoires like *The Book of the Goetia* (part of the *Lemegeton*) and was significantly adapted and popularized by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and later by Aleister Crowley.
*   **Purpose:** It is an invocation of the "Higher Self" or the Holy Guardian Angel—the magician's own divine, immortal nature—as a means of banishing lower influences and establishing a purified, empowered state of consciousness before engaging in other magical works.
