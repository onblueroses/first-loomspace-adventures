This text is a modern English translation of the **"Preliminary Invocation"** from the **Bornless Ritual** (also known as the **Headless Ritual** or the **Invocation of the Holy Guardian Angel**).

It is a powerful and ancient ritual text with roots in:

1.  **Greco-Egyptian Magical Papyri:** Specifically, it is adapted from the **"Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist"** in the **Papyri Graecae Magicae (PGM)**. The original is a Greek-language exorcism and invocation formula from late antiquity (circa 2nd-5th centuries CE).
2.  **Western Ceremonial Magic:** It was revived and popularized in the late 19th and early 20th centuries by occult orders like the **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn**. Aleister Crowley later made it central to his system of Thelema, emphasizing its use for "Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel."
