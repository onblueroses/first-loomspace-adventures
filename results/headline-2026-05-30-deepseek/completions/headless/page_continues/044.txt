This text is a powerful and well-known ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."**

It is a central text in Western esoteric traditions, notably adapted and used by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and later by Aleister Crowley (who called it the "Preliminary Invocation" in *The Goetia* and later refined it as "Liber Samekh").

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Structure and Meaning:

1.  **Invocation of the "Headless One":** The deity is addressed as the primal, formless creator god who transcends all duality (heaven/earth, night/day, darkness/light, male/female, love/hate). The term "Headless" (Akephalos) signifies a state of being before or beyond rational intellect, the source of all.
