This is an excerpt from the **"Bornless Ritual"** (also known as the **"Preliminary Invocation"** or the **"Headless Rite"**).

It is a powerful and ancient ritual text, originally from the **Greek Magical Papyri** (PGM V. 96-172), later adapted and popularized by Western esoteric traditions, most notably by the **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn** and by **Aleister Crowley** (who included it as a central ritual in his system).

Here’s a breakdown of its key elements and significance:
