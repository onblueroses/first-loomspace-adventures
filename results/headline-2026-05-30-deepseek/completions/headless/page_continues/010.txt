This text is a powerful and well-known ritual incantation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**. It is commonly called the **"Bornless Rite"** or the **"Headless One"** invocation. It was later adapted and popularized in modern occultism by Aleister Crowley, who used it as a preliminary invocation in his system of ceremonial magic.

Here is a breakdown of its components and significance:

### Context & Origin
*   **Source:** The Greek Magical Papyri are a collection of spells, formulas, and rituals from Greco-Roman Egypt (circa 2nd century BCE to 5th century CE). They represent a syncretic blend of Egyptian, Greek, Jewish, and early Christian magical traditions.
*   **Purpose:** It is an exorcistic and theurgic (god-invoking) ritual. The magician identifies with a supreme, primordial deity to command spirits and achieve a state of spiritual authority and knowledge.
