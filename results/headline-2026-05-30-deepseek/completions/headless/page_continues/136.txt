This text is a modern English translation of the **First Conjuration of the Bornless Rite**, a powerful ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri** (PGM V. 96-172). It is a foundational ritual in Western esoteric traditions, particularly within ceremonial magic and the syncretic magical systems of the Greco-Roman world.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Origin & Context
*   **Source:** The **Papyri Graecae Magicae (PGM)**, a collection of magical spells, formulas, and rituals from Greco-Roman Egypt (circa 2nd century BCE to 5th century CE).
*   **Modern Usage:** It was adapted and popularized by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and later by Aleister Crowley, who considered it one of the most important rituals for achieving **Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel**—a central goal of Thelemic and other mystical practices.
