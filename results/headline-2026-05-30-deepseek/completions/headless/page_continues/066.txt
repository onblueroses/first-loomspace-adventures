This text is a powerful ritual incantation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, known as the "Stele of Jeu" or the "Rite of the Headless One." It is a central text in ancient Graeco-Egyptian magical and theurgic practice, later adopted and adapted within Western esoteric traditions like Hermeticism and ceremonial magic.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### **Structure and Purpose**
The incantation is a **theurgic (god-invoking) ritual** designed to identify the magician with a supreme, primordial deity in order to command all lesser spirits. Its primary goals are:
1.  **Invocation:** Calling upon the "Headless One" (a form of the supreme god beyond form).
2.  **Identification:** The magician proclaims their own divine attributes, merging with the god.
3.  **Command:** Using this assumed authority to compel all spirits of the cosmos to obey.
