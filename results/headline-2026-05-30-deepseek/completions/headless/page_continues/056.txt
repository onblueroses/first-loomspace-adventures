This text is a modern English translation of a powerful ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri** (specifically, PGM V. 96-172), commonly known as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."** Its purpose is the invocation of a supreme, formless deity to achieve a state of divine knowledge and authority over spirits.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Core Elements of the Invocation:

1.  **The "Headless One":** This is the central deity being invoked. Being "headless" symbolizes transcending rational, human thought and perception. It represents a primal, cosmic power that exists before and beyond form—the source of creation itself.

2.  **Divine Attributes & Creation:** The text begins by establishing the deity's authority as the creator of all dualities (earth/heavens, night/day, darkness/light, just/unjust, male/female). This establishes the magician's connection to the ultimate source of cosmic order.
