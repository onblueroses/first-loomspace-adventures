This text is a modern composite of several powerful and ancient ritual invocations, primarily drawn from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, known as the "**Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist**" or the "**Ritual of the Headless One**."

Here is a breakdown of its components and significance:

### 1. **The "Headless One" Invocation**
*   **Source:** The core of this text is the "Ritual of the Headless One," a potent exorcism and divine invocation from the PGM.
*   **Purpose:** The ritual is designed to identify the magician with a supreme, primordial deity (the "Headless God") in order to command spirits. By proclaiming "I am the Headless Daimon..." the practitioner seeks to temporarily *become* that god, acquiring divine authority.

### 2. **Divine Names and Attributes**
*   **Osoronnophris:** A Greco-Egyptian magical name for **Osiris-Unnefer** (the perfected Osiris).
*   **Iabou, Iapos, Abaoth, Sabaoth:** Variants
