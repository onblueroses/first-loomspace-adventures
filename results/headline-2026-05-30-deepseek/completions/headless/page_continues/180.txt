This text is a version of the **"Headless Rite,"** also known as the **"Bornless Ritual"** or the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia."** It is one of the most famous and powerful invocations in Western ceremonial magic, particularly within the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and its derivatives.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and context:

### **Origin & Significance**
*   **Source:** It appears in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172, circa 3rd-4th century CE) as a ritual to invoke a "headless" god (Akephalos) for protection and visionary power.
*   **Golden Dawn:** The 19th-century occult order adapted and translated it into English, refining it as a foundational ritual for achieving the "Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel" (a central spiritual goal in their system).
*   **Purpose:** It is an invocation of the practitioner's own higher, divine self—the "Holy Guardian Angel"—or the ultimate divine source beyond form. The ritual aims to banish lower influences, unify the magician's consciousness, and establish supreme magical authority.
