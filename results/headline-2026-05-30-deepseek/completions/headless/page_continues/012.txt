This text is a modern English translation of a powerful and ancient ritual incantation known as the **"Bornless (or Headless) Rite."** Its origins are in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM), specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, where it is titled "Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist." It is a central ritual in several Western esoteric traditions, including ceremonial magic, Hermeticism, and Thelema.

Here is a breakdown of the key elements and their significance:

### Core Purpose
The ritual is an invocation of the "Headless One," a supreme, formless divine power. The primary goal is for the magician to identify with this power, becoming a "daimon" (a divine spirit or higher self) in order to command all other spirits. It is an act of **theurgy** (divine magic aimed at spiritual ascent) and **exorcism** (commanding spiritual forces).
