This is an excerpt from the **"Bornless Ritual"** (also known as the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia** or the **Invocation of the Headless One**). It is a powerful ritual text from Western esotericism, specifically from the **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn** and later used in Aleister Crowley’s system of magick.

Here’s a breakdown of its significance:

---

### **Context & Origin**
*   **Source:** It appears in older grimoires, notably in a 4th-century Greek magical papyrus (the *Papyri Graecae Magicae*), but was adapted and popularized by 19th-20th century occultists.
*   **Purpose:** It is an invocation of a "Higher" or "Holy Guardian Angel" — a supreme, formless divine consciousness (the "Headless One") — to establish the magician’s authority and purify the ritual space before other operations (like evoking spirits in the Goetia).
*   **Name "Headless":** Symbolizes a state beyond dualistic thought, a deity not limited by a single perspective or identity (like the Egyptian god Osiris, sometimes called "Osor
