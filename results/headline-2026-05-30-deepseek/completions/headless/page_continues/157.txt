This text is a modern English translation of the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual,"** one of the most famous and enduring invocations from the Western esoteric tradition.

Here is a breakdown of its origins, purpose, and significance:

### Origins and History
*   **Ancient Source:** It originates from a Greco-Egyptian magical papyrus, the **"Papyri Graecae Magicae V" (PGM V, lines 96-172)**, dating from around the 2nd to 5th century CE. This papyrus was a practical handbook for a ritual magician.
*   **Original Name:** In the papyrus, it is titled **"The Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist in his Letter."** It is an exorcistic ritual to invoke a powerful, "headless" god (Akephalos) to gain control over spirits.
