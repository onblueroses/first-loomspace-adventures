This text is a modern English translation of the "Bornless Ritual" or "Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia," a powerful ritual invocation from Western esoteric traditions, most notably found in the **Key of Solomon** and **The Lesser Key of Solomon (the Goetia)**.

Here is a breakdown of its significance and components:

### Origin & Purpose
*   **Source:** It is the "Preliminary Invocation" from **The Lesser Key of Solomon**, a foundational grimoire of ceremonial magic. It is also known as the "Headless Rite" or "Bornless Ritual."
*   **Purpose:** To invoke the "Bornless One" (a supreme, formless deity) in order to establish the magician's authority and purify the ritual space. Its primary goal is to compel all spirits to be obedient to the magician, creating a state of control necessary for the evocations that follow (like summoning the 72 spirits of the Goetia).
