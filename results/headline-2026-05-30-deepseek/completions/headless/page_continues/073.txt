This text is a version of the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual,"** a powerful invocation from the Graeco-Egyptian magical papyri (specifically, the **Papyri Graecae Magicae V, lines 96-172**, often called the "Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist").

It is a central ritual in Western esoteric traditions, notably adapted and used in **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn** and later in **Aleister Crowley's** works (where it is called the "Preliminary Invocation" in *The Goetia* and a key rite in his system).

### Key Elements of the Text:

1.  **Invocation of the "Headless One":** This deity is a supreme, formless, and androgynous creator god, identified with **Osoronnophris** (a Graeco-Egyptian name for Osiris in his perfected, resurrected form). The term "Headless" (Akephalos) signifies being beyond intellect or dualistic thought, the source of all.
