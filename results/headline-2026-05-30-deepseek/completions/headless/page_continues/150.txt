This text is a modern English translation of the **"Preliminary Invocation"** or **"Headless Rite"** from the **Bornless Ritual**, a central piece of ritual magic found in the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172)**. It was later adapted and popularized by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley (who titled it "The Preliminary Invocation" in *Liber Samekh*).

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Core Identity: The Bornless/Headless One
*   **Thee, the Headless One:** The primary deity invoked is a "Headless" or "Bornless" (Aknephalos) god. This signifies a primal, formless, and transcendent divine force that existed before creation and is the source of all. Being "headless" represents being beyond rational thought and duality.
*   **Syncretism:** The invocation seamlessly blends Egyptian, Greek, Hebrew, and Gnostic divine names and concepts, typical of late antique Hellenistic magic.
