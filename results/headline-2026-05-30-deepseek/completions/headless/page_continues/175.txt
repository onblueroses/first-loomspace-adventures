This is a passage from the **Bornless Ritual** (also known as the **Preliminary Invocation** or the **Invocation of the Headless One**). It is a powerful ritual text with deep roots in Western esoteric tradition.

Here is a breakdown of its significance and context:

### Origins and History
*   **Ancient Roots:** Its core is believed to derive from a Greek papyrus of pagan magical invocation, likely from Roman Egypt (circa 2nd-3rd century CE). It was adapted and syncretized with Jewish and Gnostic elements.
*   **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn:** The ritual was famously adopted and adapted by this highly influential magical order in the late 19th century. They used it as a **Preliminary Invocation** to invoke the "Higher Genius" or Holy Guardian Angel before other ceremonial work.
*   **Aleister Crowley:** A key figure in the Golden Dawn and later Thelema, Crowley further popularized the ritual. He referred to it as the **"Bornless Ritual"** and considered it one of the most potent invocations for achieving the Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel.

### Purpose and Meaning
The ritual is an act of **Theurgy**
