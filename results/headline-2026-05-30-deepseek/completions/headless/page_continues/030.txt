This text is a modern English translation of a powerful ritual invocation from the **Papyri Graecae Magicae (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Stele of Jeu"** or the **"Ritual of the Headless One."**

It is a central text in late antique Graeco-Egyptian magical and theurgic practice, and later became a cornerstone of certain strands of Western esotericism, especially within **Ceremonial Magic** and **Thelema**.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### 1. **Structure and Purpose**
The text is a **theurgic invocation**—a ritual to call upon a supreme, formless deity to empower the magician and compel spirits to obedience. Its primary goal is to establish the practitioner as a divine authority ("I am the Headless Daimon...") and command all classes of spirits.
