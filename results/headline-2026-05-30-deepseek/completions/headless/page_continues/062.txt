This text is a version of the **"Bornless Ritual"** or **"Preliminary Invocation"** from the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn, which itself is adapted from the **"Headless Rite"** found in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172).

It is a powerful ritual of invocation and self-identification, used to call upon and unite with one's **Higher Genius** or **Holy Guardian Angel** (referred to here as "the Headless One" or "the Terrible and Invisible God"). The ritualist first invokes the deity and then progressively identifies with it, culminating in declarations of divine authority (e.g., "I am the Mighty One...", "HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name").

### Key Elements and Context:

*   **The Headless One:** A name for a supreme, formless deity, beyond human perception ("whom no man hath seen at any time"). The "headless" state symbolizes transcending the rational mind and ordinary perception.
