This text is a powerful and well-known ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."**

Its significance is profound in Western esoteric traditions:

### **Key Context and Meaning:**

*   **Origin:** It is a Hellenistic-era (circa 2nd-5th century CE) syncretic text, blending Egyptian, Greek, Jewish, and early Gnostic/Christian elements. This was common in the religious and magical milieu of the time.
*   **The "Headless One":** This refers to a primal, formless deity beyond human perception—the source of all creation and duality (light/dark, male/female, just/unjust). Names like **Osoronnophris** (a Hellenized form of the Egyptian "Wsr wnfr," meaning "Osiris the Beautiful") and **IAŌ SABAŌTH** (a version of the Hebrew Yahweh of Hosts) identify this being with the highest creative powers of multiple traditions.
