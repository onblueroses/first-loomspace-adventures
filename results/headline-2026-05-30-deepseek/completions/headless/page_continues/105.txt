This text appears to be a modern English translation of a powerful ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Stele of Jeu"** or the **"Ritual of the Headless One."**

It is a central text in the practice of ancient Graeco-Egyptian ritual magic (theurgy). Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Core Identity: The "Headless One"
*   **God and Magician:** The ritual uniquely blends the identity of the deity and the practitioner. The magician first invokes the "Headless" or "Mindless" God (a form of the supreme, invisible creator) and then, through a series of "I am..." statements, **declares themselves to be that god**. This is a classic theurgic operation of *identificatory ascent*—becoming divine to command divine forces.
