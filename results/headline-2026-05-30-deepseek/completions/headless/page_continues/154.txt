This text is a modern English translation of a powerful and ancient ritual incantation known as the **"Bornless Rite"** or the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia."**

Here is a breakdown of its significance, origins, and use:

### Origins and History
*   **Ancient Roots:** The core of the ritual is believed to be derived from a **Greek Magical Papyrus** (PGM V. 96-172), dating from around the 2nd to 5th centuries CE. It is a **Hellenistic-Egyptian** magical text.
*   **Name:** It is called the "Bornless" or "Headless" rite because it invokes a deity described as "Headless" (Akephalos), a transcendental god beyond form and limitation.
*   **Integration into Western Esotericism:** In the 19th century, this ritual was translated and adapted by **Samuel Liddell MacGregor Mathers** for the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn. It became a foundational ritual for attaining **Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel**—a central goal of their system. Aleister Crowley later published his own influential version.
