This text is a modern translation of a powerful ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, known as the **"Stele of Jeu"** or the **"Rite of the Headless One."**

It is one of the most significant and famous exorcistic and theurgic (divine-invoking) rituals from late antiquity. Here’s a breakdown of its key elements and significance:

### Context and Purpose
*   **Source:** The Greek Magical Papyri are a collection of spells, formulas, and rituals from Greco-Roman Egypt (circa 2nd century BCE to 5th century CE), representing a syncretic blend of Egyptian, Greek, Jewish, and early Christian mystical traditions.
*   **Function:** This ritual is primarily an **exorcism** and a rite of **divine authority**. The magician identifies with a powerful, primordial deity (the "Headless One") in order to command all spirits. It's a practice of **self-deification** for a practical goal: gaining absolute power over the spiritual world.
