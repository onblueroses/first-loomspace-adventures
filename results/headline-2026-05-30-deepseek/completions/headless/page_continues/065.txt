Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### **1. Origin and Name**
*   **Source:** It is primarily derived from **Papyri Graecae Magicae V** (PGM V. 96-172), a Greco-Egyptian magical text. The Golden Dawn adapted and popularized this ritual in modern occultism.
*   **The "Headless One":** This refers to the invoked deity as a primal, transcendent force beyond rational comprehension ("headless" meaning beyond the intellect or form). It is identified with the Egyptian solar god **Ra** (as Osoronnophris, a Hellenized form of Wsir Wn-nfr, "Osiris the Beautiful Being") and the Judeo-Christian **God of Abraham** (Sabaoth, IAŌ).
