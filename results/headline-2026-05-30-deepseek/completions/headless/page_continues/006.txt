This text is a modern English translation of a powerful ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often called the "**Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist**" or the "**Rite of the Headless One**."

It is a central text in ancient Graeco-Egyptian syncretic magic and later became a cornerstone of modern occult traditions, especially within **Ceremonial Magic** and **Thelema**.

Here’s a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### 1. **Structure and Purpose**
The ritual is a **theurgic operation**—an act of divine magic aimed at uniting the magician with a supreme godhead to command spirits. Its primary goal, as stated, is to make "all spirits subject unto me." It is an exorcism and an empowerment ritual, designed to transform the magician into a divine authority.
