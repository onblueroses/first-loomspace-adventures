This text is a powerful and well-known ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the "**Headless Rite**" or the "**Bornless Ritual**."

It is a central ritual of invocation in Western esoteric traditions, particularly within **Ceremonial Magic** and **Thelema**, where it is known as the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia** or the **Invocation of the Higher Genius**.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### 1. **The "Headless One" (The Deity)**
*   **Identity:** The deity is addressed as the primordial, formless creator god ("Headless" meaning without limit or defined form). It is identified with Egyptian (Osoronnophris - a form of Osiris), Hebrew (Iabou, Iapos, Sabaoth), and Gnostic names.
*   **Attributes:** It is described as the creator of all dualities (earth/heavens, night/day, darkness/light, just/unjust, male/female). This establishes the invoked power as the source of all existence and moral law
