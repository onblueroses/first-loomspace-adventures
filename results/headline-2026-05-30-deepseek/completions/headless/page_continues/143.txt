This text is a powerful ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."** It is one of the most famous and influential invocations in Western esotericism.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Core Identity of the Rite
*   **The Headless One:** The primary deity invoked is a "Headless" or "Without a Head" god. This symbolizes a transcendental being beyond rational thought (the head), existing before and above creation. It represents pure, undifferentiated divine power and consciousness.
*   **Syncretic Deity:** The god is addressed by many names from different traditions, a hallmark of Greco-Egyptian magic:
    *   **Osoronnophris:** A Greco-Egyptian name for **Osiris in his perfect, resurrected state** (Wsr-wn-nfr).
    *   **Iabou, Iapos, Abaoth, Sabaoth:** Variants and corruptions of Hebrew divine names (Yahweh, Sabaoth - "Lord of Hosts").
