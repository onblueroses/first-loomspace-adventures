This text is the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia**, also known as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."** It is a powerful ceremonial invocation from the Western esoteric tradition, specifically from the **Key of Solomon** and related grimoires. It was later adapted and famously used by Aleister Crowley and other 20th-century occultists.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### 1. **Origin and Context**
*   **Source:** It originates from the Hellenistic Egyptian magical papyri (specifically the **Greek Magical Papyri**, PGM V. 96-172), where it is known as the "Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist." It was incorporated into the **Lesser Key of Solomon (The Goetia)** as an invocation to call upon the "Highest Power" to control spirits.
*   **Purpose:** It is a ritual of **self-purification and empowerment**. The magician identifies with a primordial, "Headless" (i.e., limitless, beyond rational thought) divine power to become a suitable vessel of authority. The goal is to establish the magician as the "Bornless One"
