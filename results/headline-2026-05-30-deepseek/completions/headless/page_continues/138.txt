This text is a modern English translation of a powerful ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, known as the **"Stele of Jeu"** or the **"Rite of the Headless One."**

It is one of the most famous and influential exorcism and self-transformation rituals from the late antique syncretic magical tradition, blending Egyptian, Greek, Jewish, and early Christian elements.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### **Structure and Meaning:**

1.  **Invocation of the "Headless One":** The ritual begins by calling upon a supreme, formless deity ("whom no man hath seen at any time"). This god is identified with:
    *   **Osoronnophris:** A Graeco-Egyptian name for Osiris in his perfect, resurrected form (Wsr-wn-nfr).
    *   **Biblical and Gnostic names:** Iabou (Yahweh), Iapos, Abaoth, Sabaoth (Hebrew "Hosts"). This intentionally merges the creator God of Genesis with the Egyptian pantheon.
