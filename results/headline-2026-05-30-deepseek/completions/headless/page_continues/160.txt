This text is a modern compilation of several ancient magical and religious sources, primarily from two distinct traditions: the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)** and the **Gnostic/Pagan** ritual known as the **"Bornless Rite"** or the **"Headless One" invocation**.

Here is a breakdown of the key components and their origins:

### 1. **The "Headless One" Invocation**
The opening lines ("Thee I invoke, the Headless One...") are a version of the "Bornless Rite" (sometimes called the "Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia"). It is a ritual designed to invoke a supreme, formless deity to gain control over spirits. The original is found in the **Greek Magical Papyri** (PGM V. 96-172).

*   **The Headless God:** Represents a primal, pre-cosmic deity without form or limit, often associated with the Egyptian solar god **Ra** (or a syncretic form like **Osiris-Ra**).
