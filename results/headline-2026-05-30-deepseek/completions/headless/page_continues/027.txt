This text is a powerful ritual incantation known as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."** It is a central piece of modern ceremonial magic, primarily derived from a Greco-Egyptian magical papyrus (the *Paris Papyrus*, PGM V. 96-172).

Here is a breakdown of its components and significance:

### Origins & Context
*   **Source:** It originates from the **Papyri Graecae Magicae (PGM)**, a collection of ancient Greek magical texts from Roman Egypt (circa 2nd century BCE to 5th century CE). This specific version was adapted and popularized in the Western esoteric tradition by **Aleister Crowley** and the **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn**.
*   **Purpose:** It is an invocation or "assumption of godform." The ritualist does not merely call upon a deity, but **identifies with** the "Headless One" (a form of the supreme, primordial god beyond form) to achieve a state of purified consciousness and command over spiritual forces.
