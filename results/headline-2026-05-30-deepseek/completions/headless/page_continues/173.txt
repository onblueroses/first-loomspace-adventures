This is an excerpt from the **Bornless Ritual** (also known as the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia** or the **Invocation of the Headless One**). It is a foundational ritual text in Western esoteric traditions, particularly within ceremonial magic and the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn.

Here’s a breakdown of its key elements and context:

### **Origin & Significance**
*   **Source:** It appears in several grimoires, most notably in **S. L. MacGregor Mathers’** edition of **The Lesser Key of Solomon (The Goetia)**, where it is used as a preliminary invocation to purify the operator and establish divine authority before summoning spirits.
*   **Purpose:** It is an invocation of the **"Headless One"** — a supreme, formless, and primordial deity beyond human comprehension. The ritual aims to identify the magician with this divine power, allowing them to command spiritual forces from a position of authority, not supplication.
*   **Themes:** It combines Egyptian, Greek, Gnostic, and Judeo-Christian divine names and concepts, reflecting the syncretic nature of much Western esotericism.
