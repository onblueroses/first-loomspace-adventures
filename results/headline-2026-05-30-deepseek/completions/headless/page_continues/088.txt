This text is a modern translation and compilation of passages from two ancient Greco-Egyptian magical papyri, primarily **PGM V. 96-172** (the "Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist" or the "Ritual of the Headless One") and elements from **PGM XIII. 1-343**.

Here is a breakdown of its components and significance:

### 1. **Origin and Purpose**
*   **Source:** It is a powerful ritual invocation (or **"praxis"**) from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM), a corpus of spells, formulas, and rituals from Greco-Roman Egypt (circa 2nd century BCE to 5th century CE).
*   **Purpose:** Known as the **"Ritual of the Headless One,"** its primary goal is the invocation of a supreme, invisible god (the "Headless One") to compel spirits and demons to obey the magician. It is a rite of divine authority and spiritual command.
