This text is a modern composite of several ancient magical and ritual invocations, most notably from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)** and the **Hekhalot literature** of Jewish mysticism. It is often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."**

Here’s a breakdown of its key components and historical context:

### 1. **The "Headless One"**
*   **Source:** The core invocation ("Thee I invoke, the Headless One...") is from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172)**, a Greco-Egyptian magical text. The "Headless" god is a form of the solar creator god, often associated with **Ra** or the syncretic god **Osoronnophris** (a Hellenized form of "Wsr Wnfr," meaning "Osiris the Beautiful").
*   **Meaning:** "Headless" (or "Without a Head") is a paradoxical divine title indicating a being that transcends ordinary perception and intellect—a primordial, all-encompassing consciousness not limited by a single point of view.
