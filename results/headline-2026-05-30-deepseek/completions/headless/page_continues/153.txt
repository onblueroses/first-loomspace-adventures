This text appears to be a version of the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual,"** a powerful invocation from the Graeco-Egyptian magical papyri (specifically, the *Papyri Graecae Magicae* V, lines 96-172). It is one of the most famous and enduring invocations in Western esotericism, later adapted and used in ceremonial magic traditions like the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley's Thelema (where it is known as the "Preliminary Invocation" in *The Goetia*).
