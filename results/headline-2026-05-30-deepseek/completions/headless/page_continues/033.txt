This text is a modern compilation from several ancient magical and religious sources, primarily the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**. It is most famously known as the **"Bornless Ritual"** or the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia."** Its purpose is ceremonial: to invoke a supreme, formless deity (the "Headless One") in order to establish the magician's authority over all spirits.

Here is a breakdown of its components and origins:

### 1. **Core Identity: The "Bornless" or "Headless" Ritual**
*   **Source:** The core invocation (beginning with "Thee I invoke, the Headless One...") is found in **PGM V. 96-172**, a Greco-Egyptian magical text from around the 3rd-5th century CE.
*   **Concept:** The "Headless One" (Akephalos) is a formless, primordial god beyond all creation, often identified with the sun god Ra or the Egyptian god Osiris (as Osoronnophris, a Hellenized form of *Wsr-nfr*, "Osiris-Beautiful"). The ritual aims to unite the magician with this supreme power
