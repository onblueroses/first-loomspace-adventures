This text is a modern English translation of a powerful ritual invocation known as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."** Its history is ancient and complex, weaving through multiple religious and magical traditions.

Here is a breakdown of its origins, purpose, and significance:

### Origins & History
*   **Ancient Roots:** The core of this invocation is believed to be a **Pagan (likely Egyptian/Greco-Egyptian) exorcism or theurgic rite** from around the 2nd or 3rd century CE. It was used to invoke a supreme, formless deity to command spirits.
*   **Adoption into Grimoires:** It was preserved in Greek magical papyri (notably **PGM V. 96-172**).
