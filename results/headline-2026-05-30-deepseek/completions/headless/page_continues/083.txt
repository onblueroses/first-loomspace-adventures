This text is a modern English translation of a powerful and ancient ritual formula known as the "Headless Rite" or the "Bornless Ritual." Its origins are in the Greek Magical Papyri (specifically PGM V. 96-172), a collection of spells, hymns, and rituals from Greco-Roman Egypt. It was later adapted and popularized in Western esoteric traditions, most notably by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Core Purpose
The ritual is an invocation of a supreme, formless deity (the "Headless One") to achieve a state of spiritual authority and purification. The magician identifies with this divine power to command all spirits and achieve a specific goal, often spiritual knowledge or mastery.

### Structure and Meaning

1.  **Invocation of the "Headless One":** The deity is described as the creator of all dualities (earth/heavens, night/day, darkness/light). "Headless" symbolizes being beyond rational thought and the limitations of the physical senses, existing in a primal, pre-cosmic state.
