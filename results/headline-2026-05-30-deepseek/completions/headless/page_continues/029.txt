This text is a powerful and well-known ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."**

It is a seminal text in Western esotericism, later adapted and popularized by figures like **Aleister Crowley** (who called it the "Preliminary Invocation" in *The Goetia* and the "Bornless Ritual") and used as a core practice in **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn** and **Thelema**.

Here’s a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Structure & Meaning:
1.  **Invocation of the "Headless One":** The deity is addressed as "Headless" (Akephalos), symbolizing a consciousness not limited by the physical senses or rational mind—a primal, cosmic power beyond form.
2.  **Divine Attributes & Creation:** The god is credited with creating all dualities (earth/heavens, night/day, darkness/light, male/female, just/unjust). This establishes the deity as the ultimate source from which all opposites emanate.
