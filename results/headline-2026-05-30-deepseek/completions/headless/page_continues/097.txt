This text is a powerful ritual incantation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the "**Headless Rite**" or the "**Bornless Ritual**."

It is a foundational invocation in Western esoteric traditions, later adapted and popularized by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley (who called it the "Preliminary Invocation" in *The Goetia* and later the "Bornless Ritual").

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:
