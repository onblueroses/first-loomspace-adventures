This is a portion of the **"Bornless Ritual"** or the **"Preliminary Invocation"** from the **Goetia**, a foundational text of Western ceremonial magic. It is also known as the **"Invocation of the Headless One."**

Here is a breakdown of its context and meaning:

### **Origin and Use**
*   **Source:** It appears in classical grimoires like the **"Greek Magical Papyri"** (PGM) and was later adapted into the **"Key of Solomon"** tradition. It was famously incorporated into the rituals of the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley's system of Thelema.
*   **Purpose:** It is an **invocatory prayer** used to establish a purified and empowered magical consciousness. The ritualist identifies with a supreme, primordial deity (the "Headless One") to achieve a state of spiritual authority and clarity before performing other magical operations, such as evoking spirits. The goal is to become a vessel for this divine power.

### **Key Themes and Symbolism**
*   **The Headless One:** Represents the limitless, primordial God beyond human conception ("whom no man hath seen"). Being "headless"
