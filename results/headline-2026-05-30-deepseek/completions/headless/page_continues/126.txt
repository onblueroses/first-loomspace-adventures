This text is a modern reconstruction of the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual,"** also known as the **"Preliminary Invocation"** from the **Goetia** in the **Lesser Key of Solomon**.

Here is a breakdown of its origins, purpose, and the content you've provided:

### Origins & Context

*   **Ancient Roots:** The core of this invocation is believed to be a **Greco-Egyptian magical prayer** from the **Greek Magical Papyri** (PGM), specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, where it is called the "Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist." It was used to invoke a supreme, formless deity for protection and to gain control over spirits.
*   **Ceremonial Magic Adoption:** It was translated and adapted into Western ceremonial magic, most famously by **Aleister Crowley** and **S.L. MacGregor Mathers** for the **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn**. Crowley considered it one of the most important rituals, using it as the "Preliminary Invocation" to invoke one's **Holy Guardian Angel** (Higher Self) before any other magical operation.
