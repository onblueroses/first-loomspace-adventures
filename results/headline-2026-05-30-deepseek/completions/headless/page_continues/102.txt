This text is a modern English translation of a powerful and ancient ritual invocation known as the **"Bornless Ritual"** or the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia."** Its roots are in Greco-Egyptian magical papyri (notably the *PGM V*, lines 96-172), and it was later adapted and incorporated into Western esoteric traditions like the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley's Thelema.

Here is a breakdown of the key elements and their significance:

### 1. **Structure and Purpose**
This is a **theurgic** (divine, elevating) ritual, not a demonic one. Its primary purpose is for the magician to:
*   Invoke the supreme, formless Godhead (the "Headless One").
*   Identify with this divine power through a series of "I am" statements.
*   Command and subjugate all spirits to the magician's will, establishing authority before performing other magical operations (like summoning spirits from the Goetia).
