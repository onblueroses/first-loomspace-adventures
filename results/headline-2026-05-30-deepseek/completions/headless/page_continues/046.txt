This is an excerpt from the **Headless Rite**, also known as the **Bornless Ritual** or the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia**. It is a powerful and ancient ritual of invocation and self-identification with a divine, primordial force.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Context and Origin
*   **Source:** It appears in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172, circa 3rd-4th century CE) and was later adapted into Western ceremonial magic, most famously by Aleister Crowley (who called it the "Preliminary Invocation" in *The Goetia*) and the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn.
*   **Purpose:** It is an exorcism and invocation ritual. Its primary goals are:
    1.  To invoke the "Headless One" (a form of the highest, formless God).
    2.  To banish all adverse forces.
    3.  To identify the magician with this divine power, declaring "I am" the various attributes of the godhead, thus achieving a state of divine authority to command spirits.

### Key Themes and Phrases
