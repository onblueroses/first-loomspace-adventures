This text is a modern English translation of a powerful and ancient ritual invocation known as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."** It is a central piece of Graeco-Egyptian magical practice from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Origin and Purpose
*   **Source:** The Greek Magical Papyri, a collection of spells, formulas, and rituals from Roman Egypt (circa 2nd century BCE to 5th century CE).
*   **Purpose:** It is an **exorcistic invocation** and a ritual of **self-deification**. The operator (the "I" in the text) does not merely call upon a god for help, but ritually identifies *as* the supreme, creator god—the "Headless One"—in order to command spirits and achieve a state of divine authority and knowledge.
