This text is a modern English translation of a powerful ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist."**

It is one of the most famous and influential invocations in Western esoteric tradition. Here’s a breakdown of its significance and components:

### **Context & Purpose**
*   **Source:** The Greek Magical Papyri are a collection of spells, formulas, and rituals from Greco-Roman Egypt (circa 2nd century BCE to 5th century CE), blending Egyptian, Greek, Jewish, and early Christian elements.
*   **Goal:** The ritual is an **exorcism** and a rite of **spiritual authority**. The practitioner identifies with a divine, "Headless" (acephalic) power to command all spirits. Its purpose is to subdue hostile spirits, gain oracular power, and achieve a state of divine knowledge and fearlessness.

### **Key Themes and Structure**
1.  **Invocation of the "Headless One":** This deity is a form of the supreme, invisible God
