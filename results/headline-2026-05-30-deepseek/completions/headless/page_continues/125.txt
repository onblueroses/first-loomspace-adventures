This text is a modern composite of several powerful and distinct ritual invocations from Western esoteric traditions, primarily **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)** and the **Hekatean/Gnostic** tradition. It is often referred to as the "**Bornless Ritual**" or the "**Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia**."

Here is a breakdown of its components and significance:

### 1. **Core Identity: The "Headless One"**
*   **Source:** Primarily from **PGM V. 96-172**, the "Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist." It is an exorcism ritual where the magician identifies with a powerful, primordial god to command spirits.
*   **Meaning:** "Headless" (Akephalos) signifies a state beyond rational, human thought—a primal divine force that precedes creation and form. The magician becomes the conduit for this power.
