This text is a modern English translation of a powerful ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the "**Headless Rite**" or the "**Bornless Ritual**."

Its purpose is one of the most profound in Western esotericism: the invocation of the practitioner's own **Higher or Divine Self** (the "Headless One") to achieve a state of knowledge and conversation with one's Holy Guardian Angel, and to command spiritual forces from that empowered state.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### 1. **The "Headless One"**
The deity invoked is described as "Headless" (sometimes "Bornless"). This symbolizes a consciousness not limited by the physical senses or rational mind (the "head"). It represents the primal, formless source of creation—the ultimate divine consciousness that precedes and generates all duality (earth/heavens, night/day, darkness/light, male/female).
