This text is a powerful and well-known ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."**

It is a foundational text in Western esotericism, later adapted and popularized by 19th and 20th-century occultists like **Aleister Crowley** (who revised it into the "Preliminary Invocation" of *The Goetia* and his "Liber Samekh") and the **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn**.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### 1. **Structure & Purpose**
*   **Invocation of the "Headless One":** The ritualist invokes a supreme, formless deity (the "Headless One" or *Akephalos*) who is the creator and ruler of all dualities (heaven/earth, light/dark, male/female, justice/injustice). This god is beyond human perception ("whom no man hath seen").
*   **Identification with the Prophet:** The speaker declares themselves to be **"Mosheh" (Moses)**, the prophet
