This text is a powerful and well-known ritual incantation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist."**

It is a central text in historical Western esotericism and ceremonial magic. Here’s a breakdown of its significance and components:

### Key Context and Purpose
*   **A Ritual of Invocation:** It is a formula for invoking a supreme, formless deity (the "Headless One") to gain authority over all spirits. The magician identifies with the god to command spiritual forces.
*   **Syncretic Deity:** The "Headless One" is a fusion of Egyptian (Osoronnophris - a form of Osiris), Jewish (Iao Sabaoth, Angel of Aphrael), and Greek metaphysical concepts. This blending is typical of the late Hellenistic magical world.
*   **The Prophet "Mosheh":** The text claims the authority of Moses ("Mosheh"), the great lawgiver and prophet, who was also considered a powerful magician in esoteric traditions.
