This text is a modern English translation of the **First Conjuration (or Invocation) of the Headless One**, a powerful ritual incantation found in the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**. Specifically, it comes from **PGM V. 96-172**, a Greco-Egyptian magical handbook dating from around the 3rd-4th century CE.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### 1. **Core Identity: The "Headless One"**
*   **Original Name:** In the papyrus, the god is called **Ἄκέφαλος (Akephalos)**, meaning "Headless One" or "Without a Head."
*   **Syncretism:** This is a prime example of Greco-Egyptian syncretism. The Headless One is identified with:
    *   **Osoronnophris:** A Hellenized form of **Wsr-w3r** ("Osiris the Great"), the resurrected Egyptian god of the underworld and judge of the dead.
    *   **Sabaoth:** A Hebrew term (Tzabaoth) meaning "Lord of Hosts," adopted into magical texts.
