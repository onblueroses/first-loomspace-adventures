This text is a modern English translation of a section from the **"Bornless Ritual"** (also known as the "Preliminary Invocation" or the "Headless Ritual"). It is a central and powerful invocation in Western ceremonial magic, particularly within the traditions of **Hermeticism** and **Thelema**.

Here is a breakdown of its components and significance:

### Origins and Context
*   **Source:** It originates from the **Greek Magical Papyri** (PGM), specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, a collection of spells and rituals from Greco-Roman Egypt (circa 2nd century BCE to 5th century CE).
*   **Name:** It is called the "Headless Ritual" because it invokes the "Headless One" (Akephalos), a form of the supreme, invisible god beyond form.
*   **Modern Use:** It was adapted and popularized in modern occultism by the **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn** and later became a cornerstone of Aleister Crowley's magical system.
