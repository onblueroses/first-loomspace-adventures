This text is a modern English translation of the **"Bornless Ritual"** or **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia,"** a powerful and ancient ritual invocation from Western esoteric tradition, particularly associated with ceremonial magic and Hermetic Qabalah.

Let's break down its key components and significance:

### Origins & Context
*   **Source:** It originates from the **Greek Magical Papyri** (PGM), specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, dating from around the 3rd-4th century CE. It was later adapted and incorporated into foundational works of modern Western magic, most notably **Aleister Crowley's** system (as the "Preliminary Invocation" in *The Goetia*) and the rituals of the **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn**.
*   **Purpose:** It is a ritual of **invocation and identification**. The magician first invokes a supreme, formless deity (the "Headless One") and then, through a series of declarations, shifts to identifying *with* that divine power. The goal is to achieve a state of purified consciousness and authority ("the Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel") from which to perform further magical work.
