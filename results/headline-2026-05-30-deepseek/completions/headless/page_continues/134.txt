This text is a modern English translation of a powerful and ancient ritual incantation known as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."** Its purpose is the invocation of a supreme, formless deity to grant the practitioner spiritual authority and command over spirits.

Here is a breakdown of its components and significance:

### Origin and History
*   **Source:** It originates from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, dating from around the 2nd to 5th centuries CE. This was a compilation of spells, rituals, and philosophical texts from Graeco-Roman Egypt.
*   **Syncretism:** The text is a classic example of religious syncretism, blending Egyptian (Osoronnophris, a form of Osiris), Jewish (Sabaoth, Mosheh/Moses, prophets of Israel), and Greek magical and philosophical concepts into a single, potent formula.
*   **Modern Use:** It was later adopted and adapted by Western esoteric traditions, most notably by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and by Aleister Crowley, who used it as a central ritual for attaining the Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel
