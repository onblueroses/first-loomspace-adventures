This text is a modern English translation of a powerful ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri** (specifically PGM V. 96-172), commonly known as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."**

Its purpose is one of the most fundamental aims of ritual magic: **the invocation and identification with a supreme, primordial deity** in order to command spiritual forces.

Here is a breakdown of its components and significance:

### Core Purpose & Structure:
1.  **Invocation of the "Headless One":** The deity is addressed as "Headless" (Akephalos), representing a formless, primordial power beyond human conception—the source of all duality (light/dark, male/female, just/unjust). The names used (Osoronnophris, Iabou, Iapos, Abaoth, Sabaoth) are a syncretic blend of Egyptian (Osiris transformed) and Hebrew divine names, typical of the syncretic magic of the era.
