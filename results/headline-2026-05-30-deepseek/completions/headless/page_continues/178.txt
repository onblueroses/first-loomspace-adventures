This text is a powerful and well-known ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."**

It is a foundational text in Western ceremonial magic, later adapted and popularized by figures like Aleister Crowley (who called it "The Preliminary Invocation" in *Liber Samekh*) and the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn.
