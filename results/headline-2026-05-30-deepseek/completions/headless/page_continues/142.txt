This text is a powerful ritual invocation from the **Liber Samekh** or a closely related Graeco-Egyptian magical papyrus, often attributed to the **Bornless Rite** (or the "Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia"). It is a profound work of ceremonial magic intended to invoke a supreme, formless divine power (the "Headless One") and to identify the magician with that power for the purpose of spiritual attainment and command over spirits.

Here is a breakdown of its structure and key elements:

### 1. **Invocation of the "Headless One"**
   *   **The Headless One:** A name for the supreme, invisible God beyond form and limitation, often equated with the Egyptian solar deity **Ra** (or a syncretic form of Osiris-Ra) as "Osoronnophris" (Wsr Wn Nfr, "Osiris the Beautiful Being"). This deity is the creator of all dualities (earth/heavens, night/day, darkness/light, just/unjust, male/female).
