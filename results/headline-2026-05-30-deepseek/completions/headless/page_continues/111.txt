This text is a powerful and well-known ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Stele of Jeu"** or the **"Ritual of the Headless One."**

It is a central text in historical Western ceremonial magic, particularly within traditions like the **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn**, where it was adapted into the **"Preliminary Invocation"** of the Goetia and later into the famous **"Bornless Ritual"** (or "Liber Samekh") by Aleister Crowley.

Here’s a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Core Structure & Meaning:

1.  **Invocation of the "Headless One":** The deity is addressed as the primordial, formless creator god who exists before and beyond all duality (Heaven/Earth, Night/Day, Darkness/Light). "Headless" (Akephalos) signifies being without a limiting mind or defined form—the ultimate source.
