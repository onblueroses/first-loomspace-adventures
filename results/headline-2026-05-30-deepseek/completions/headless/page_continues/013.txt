This is a transcription of the "Preliminary Invocation" or "Bornless Ritual" from the **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn**, specifically from the **Liber Samekh**. It is a powerful ritual of invocation and purification, designed to invoke the "Higher Genius" or Holy Guardian Angel.

The text you've provided is a modernized version of an ancient Greek magical papyrus (the "Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist" or the "Mithras Liturgy"). In the Golden Dawn and later in Aleister Crowley's system, it became a central ritual for achieving "Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel."

Here’s a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### 1. **Structure and Purpose**
*   **Invocation of the "Headless One":** The ritual begins by invoking a supreme, formless deity ("whom no man hath seen at any time"). This entity is identified with creative and dualistic cosmic forces (earth/heavens, night/day, light/darkness, male/female).
*   **Assumption of Divine Authority:** The magician shifts from speaking *to* the god to speaking *as* the god, declaring "I am the Headless Daimon..."
