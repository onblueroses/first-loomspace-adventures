This text is a version of the **"Bornless Rite"** (also known as the "Preliminary Invocation" or the "Invocation of the Headless One"). It is a powerful ritual invocation from the Western esoteric tradition, most famously adapted and used in **Aleister Crowley's system of magic**, particularly within **Thelema**.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Origins & Context
*   **Source:** It originates from a Greco-Egyptian magical papyrus (the **"Greek Magical Papyri"** or PGM, specifically PGM V. 96-172). The original was a spell to invoke a "headless god" for protection and visionary purposes.
*   **Modern Esotericism:** It was translated and adapted by **S.L. MacGregor Mathers** of the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn. Aleister Crowley later revised it extensively, making it a central ritual in his practice. In Crowley's system, it is used as an invocation to attain **Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel**—the supreme goal of the magician.
