This text is a powerful ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the **"Stele of Jeu"** or the **"Ritual of the Headless One."**

It is one of the most famous and significant exorcistic and theurgic (divine-invoking) rituals from late antiquity, blending Egyptian, Greek, Jewish, and early Gnostic elements.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Structure and Meaning:

1.  **Invocation of the "Headless One":** The deity is addressed as the primordial, faceless creator god who exists beyond human perception ("whom no man hath seen at any time"). This is a form of the Egyptian solar god **Ra** (as **Osoronnophris**, meaning "Osiris the Beautiful Being") merged with the Jewish **God of Hosts (Sabaoth)**. The "Headless" (Akephalos) signifies a state of being prior to form and limitation.
