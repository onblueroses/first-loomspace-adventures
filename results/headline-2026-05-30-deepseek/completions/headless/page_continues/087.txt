This text is a powerful and well-known ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, often referred to as the "**Headless Rite**" or the "**Bornless Ritual**."

Its modern significance comes from its adoption and adaptation by **Aleister Crowley** and later ceremonial magicians. Crowley renamed it the "Preliminary Invocation" in his work *Liber Samekh*, using it as a central ritual for achieving knowledge and conversation with one's Holy Guardian Angel.
