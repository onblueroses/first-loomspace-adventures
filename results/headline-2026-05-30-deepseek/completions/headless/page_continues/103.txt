This is a passage from the **"Bornless Ritual"** or **"Preliminary Invocation"** from *The Book of the Goetia* (part of the **Lesser Key of Solomon**), as adapted and used in modern ceremonial magic, particularly by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn. It is also known as the **"Invocation of the Headless One."**

Here’s a brief breakdown of its context and meaning:

---

### **1. Origin & Purpose**
- **Source:** It appears in ancient Greek magical papyri (PGM), notably **PGM V. 96–172**, as a “Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist.” It was adapted into Western esoteric practice in the 19th and early 20th centuries.
- **Purpose:** To invoke a higher, formless divine power (the “Headless One”) to establish the magician’s authority over spirits and achieve knowledge and conversation with one’s Holy Guardian Angel (a key goal in certain mystical traditions).
