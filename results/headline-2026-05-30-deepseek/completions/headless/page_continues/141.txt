This text is a modern composite of several powerful and distinct ritual invocations from ancient Graeco-Egyptian magical papyri and medieval grimoires. It is not a single, historical prayer but a crafted formula designed for a specific magical purpose.

Here is a breakdown of its primary components and their significance:

### 1. The Headless Rite (The Bornless Ritual)
*   **Source:** The core of this text is the "Headless Rite" from the **Papyri Graecae Magicae (PGM V. 96-172)**, dating to around the 3rd-4th century CE.
*   **Purpose:** It is an exorcistic invocation where the magician identifies with a supreme, "headless" deity (Akephalos) to gain authority over all spirits. The ritual's goal is to achieve a state of divine possession or "gnosis," allowing the practitioner to command spiritual forces.
*   **Key Phrases:** "Thee I invoke, the Headless One... I am the Headless Daimon with sight in his feet... HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name."
