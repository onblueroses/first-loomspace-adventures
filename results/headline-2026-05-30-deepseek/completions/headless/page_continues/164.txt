This appears to be a version of the **"Bornless Ritual"** (also known as the **"Preliminary Invocation"** or the **"Headless One" ritual**). It is a well-known ritual text from Western esotericism, primarily derived from **Greek Magical Papyri** (PGM V. 96–172) and later adapted into modern occult systems such as those of the **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn** and Aleister Crowley’s Thelema.

---

### Key Elements of the Text:

1. **Invocation of the "Headless One"** – A deity or divine power described as the creator of the cosmos, beyond form and sight.  
2. **Divine Names** – A string of powerful, mystical names (e.g., Osoronnophris, Iabou, Iapos, Abaoth, Sabaoth, IAŌ SABAŌTH) used to invoke authority and command spirits.  
3. **Identification with the Divine** – The speaker shifts from invoking the god to declaring themselves as the god (“I am the Headless Daimon…”). This is a common ritual technique for achieving unity with the divine or asserting spiritual authority.
