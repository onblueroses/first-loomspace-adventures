This text is a version of the **"Bornless (or Headless) Rite,"** a powerful ritual invocation from the Western esoteric tradition, specifically found in the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172)** and later adapted into modern ceremonial magic (notably by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley).

Here’s a breakdown of its key elements and significance:

### Core Identity & Purpose
*   **The Headless One:** The central deity invoked is a supreme, formless, primordial god—"Headless" symbolizing transcending the limits of the rational mind and physical form. It is a god prior to and above all other gods.
*   **Purpose:** The ritual is an **exorcism and invocation of divine authority**. The magician identifies with this supreme god to command all spirits, break spells, and achieve spiritual knowledge and power. The line "make all spirits subject unto me" is its central goal.
