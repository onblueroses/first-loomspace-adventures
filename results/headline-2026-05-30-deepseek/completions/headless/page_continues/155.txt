This text is a powerful ritual invocation from the **Bornless Rite** (also known as the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia** or the **Invocation of the Headless One**). It is a foundational ritual in Western ceremonial magic, particularly within the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and subsequent traditions like Thelema.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and significance:

### Core Identity: The Bornless/Headless One
*   **The Headless One:** This is not a being of mindlessness, but of transcendent consciousness—a divinity beyond the limitations of the individual, reasoning mind (symbolized by the head). It represents the pure, unconditioned spiritual Self or Holy Guardian Angel, the highest divine aspect of the magician.
*   **Osoronnophris:** A Greco-Egyptian name meaning "Osiris the Beautiful Being," linking the rite to Egyptian solar and resurrective divinity.
*   **The Creator:** The invocation begins by attributing cosmic, dualistic creation (earth/heavens, night/day, darkness/light, male/female) to this entity, establishing its supreme authority.
