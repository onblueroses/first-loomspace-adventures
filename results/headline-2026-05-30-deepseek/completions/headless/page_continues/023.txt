This text is a version of the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual,"** one of the most famous and enduring invocations in Western esoteric practice. It appears in several ancient magical papyri (most notably the *Greek Magical Papyri*, PGM V) and has been adapted and used for centuries, most notably in modern ceremonial magic traditions like the Golden Dawn and Thelema.

Here is a breakdown of its components and significance:

### Origin and Context
*   **Source:** Primarily from **PGM V. 96-172**, a Greco-Egyptian magical papyrus from around the 3rd-4th century CE.
*   **Purpose:** It is a ritual of self-transformation and command. The operator identifies with a supreme, primordial deity (the "Headless One") in order to gain authority over all spirits. It's a ritual of **exorcism, invocation, and theurgical ascent**.
