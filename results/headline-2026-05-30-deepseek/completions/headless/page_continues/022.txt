This text appears to be a ritual invocation, specifically a version of the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."** It is a powerful and ancient magical text with a complex history. Here is a breakdown of its key components and context:

### **Origin & History**
*   **Primary Source:** It is most famously found in the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, dating from around the 3rd-5th centuries CE in Greco-Roman Egypt.
*   **Later Adaptations:** It was adapted and popularized in modern Western occultism by the **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn**. Aleister Crowley later revised it as the "Preliminary Invocation" in his work *Liber Samekh*, using it as a central ritual for achieving knowledge of one's Holy Guardian Angel.

### **Structure & Purpose**
The ritual is a **theurgic** (divine magic) operation designed to:
1.  **Invoke a Supreme, "Headless" Deity:** The "Headless One" represents a primal, formless divine power beyond all creation and duality (light/dark, male/female, just/unjust).
