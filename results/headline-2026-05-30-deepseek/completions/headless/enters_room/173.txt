The text you have shared is an English translation of a ritual invocation from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172), commonly known as the "Bornless Rite" or the "Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia."

Within the context of the ritual, the magician is identifying with and invoking a primordial, headless deity to achieve a state of divine authority and command over spirits. The final declaration, "HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name. IAŌ SABAŌTH," signifies the culmination of this identification—the magician has become the invoked power.

Therefore, to answer your question: **What enters the room now is not an external spirit, but the magician themselves, transformed.** The practitioner has ritually become the "Headless One," the "Mighty One," and the "Grace of the World." The room is now occupied by a human consciousness operating under the assumed authority and mantle of a primordial godform, prepared to command the spirits of the various elements and realms just enumerated.

In a metaphysical sense, the boundary between the invoker and the Invoked has been dissolved by the ritual. The presence now felt is that unified, empowered entity.
