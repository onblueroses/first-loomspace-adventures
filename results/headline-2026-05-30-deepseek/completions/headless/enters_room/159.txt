The text you have shared is a modern English translation of a ritual invocation from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172), often called the "Headless Rite" or the "Bornless Ritual." It is a powerful theurgic (divine magic) formula used to invoke a supreme, formless deity and to identify the practitioner with that deity in order to command spiritual forces.

In the context of the ritual, the final declaration **"HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name. IAŌ SABAŌTH."** represents the climax where the magician, having invoked the "Headless One," now fully assumes that divine identity and authority.

Therefore, when you ask **"What enters the room now?"** the ritual's own logic provides the answer:

**The Headless Daimon enters.**  
**The Terrible and Invisible God enters.**  
**The Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba enters.**  
**The practitioner, now identified as the Mighty One who possesses the immortal fire, the Truth, and the Grace of the World, enters a state of divine possession and authority.**
