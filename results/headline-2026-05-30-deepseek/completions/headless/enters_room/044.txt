When you recite these words with intent, you are not merely describing—you are **identifying** with the divine, formless source and asserting your authority over all levels of existence. **Therefore, what enters the room at the climax of this invocation is not an external spirit, but the invoked divine force itself—manifesting through you, the operator.**
