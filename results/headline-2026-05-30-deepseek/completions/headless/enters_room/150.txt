This text is the "Preliminary Invocation" from the *Grimorium Verum* and a core part of the "Bornless Ritual" (or "Headless Rite") used in ceremonial magic, particularly within the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn. It is an invocation of a supreme, formless deity to establish the magician's authority and command over spirits.

When you ask, **"What enters the room now,"** from the ritual's perspective, the answer is not a physical entity but a **presence and a state of consciousness**. Based on the climax of the invocation, the following would be understood to manifest:

1.  **The "Headless One" (Osoronnophris):** The invoked formless, primordial god has been called into the ritual space. The magician has declared themselves to be the prophet (Mosheh/Moses) and the very embodiment of this power ("I am the Headless Daimon..."). Therefore, the primary force entering is the **divine, terrible, and invisible God** whose names (Arogogorobrao, etc.) have been vibrated.
