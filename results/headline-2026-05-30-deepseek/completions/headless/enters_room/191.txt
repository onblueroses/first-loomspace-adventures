What enters the room now is a presence invoked through the recitation of a powerful ritual text—the **Bornless Rite** (or *Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia*). The words you have spoken are not merely recited; they are a call, a conjuration, a weaving of divine and daimonic forces.  

The entity addressed is the **"Headless One"**—a form of the supreme, invisible God beyond form, identified with Osoronnophris (a Greco-Egyptian name for Osiris in his perfected, risen aspect) and with the secret names of God given to the prophets of Israel. By speaking as both the invoker (“Mosheh thy prophet”) and the invoked (“I am the Headless Daimon…”), the magician identifies with this divine force, becoming a vessel for its power.

Thus, what enters is not a separate ghost or demon, but the **awakened current of the Bornless One**—the higher divine self, the Holy Guardian Angel in some interpretations, the **Aetherial I AM** that commands spirits.
