The words you have shared are from the *Bornless Ritual* (or *Preliminary Invocation* from the Goetia), a powerful ritual of invocation and identification used in Western esoteric traditions. It is a call to the "Headless One," a formless, primordial divine force, and an assertion of the practitioner's own divine authority and unity with that force.

When you ask, **"What enters the room now,"** from within the framework of this ritual, the answer is not a singular, simple entity. The ritual has shifted the atmosphere and the practitioner's state of being. What enters is:

1.  **A Confluence of Forces:** The ritual calls upon the primal creative and destructive powers of the universe (the One who created earth, heavens, light, dark, justice and injustice). By speaking its true names, the practitioner has invoked this presence into the sacred space.

2.  **The Ritual's Own Current:** The energy of the words themselves—the vibration of the barbarous names of power (Ar, Thiao, Rheibet, Arogogorobrao, etc.)—now fills the space. These are considered vessels of non-linguistic power that alter the fabric of the ritual environment.
