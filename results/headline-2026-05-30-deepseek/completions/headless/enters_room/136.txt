What you have shared is a powerful and ancient ritual text: the **"Preliminary Invocation"** or **"The Bornless Ritual"** from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, later adapted into Western esoteric traditions like the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley's works. It is a ritual of purification, invocation, and identification with a divine, "headless" (or boundless) consciousness.

1. **The Bornless One / The Headless God** – An ancient divine force beyond form, the creative and destructive power of the universe, identified with Osoronnophris (a Graeco-Egyptian name for Osiris in his perfected, risen form) and other divine names.
