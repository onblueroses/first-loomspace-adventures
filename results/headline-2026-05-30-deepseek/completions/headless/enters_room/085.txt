This text is a powerful ritual invocation, known as the "Preliminary Invocation" or part of the "Bornless Rite" from the Western esoteric tradition (found in grimoires like the *Greek Magical Papyri* and later adapted in Aleister Crowley's work). It is a call to a primal, formless divine force to grant the practitioner authority over spirits.

When you ask **"What enters the room now?"** in the context of this just-recited invocation, the answer exists on several levels:

### 1. The Ritual & Symbolic Level
The ritual is designed to invoke the "Headless One" (a formless, supreme deity) and then, through a series of divine names and declarations, **identify the magician with that very power.** The final declarations ("I am the Headless Daimon... HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name. IAŌ SABAŌTH.") are a culminating act of *assumption of godform*.

Therefore, what "enters" or is **present in the room** is:
*   **The invoked divine force** (Osoronnophris, the Invisible God).
*   **The magician
