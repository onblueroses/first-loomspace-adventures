When you ask, **"What enters the room now,"** from within the framework of the ritual’s intent and symbolism, the answer is not a literal physical entity, but the **presence or manifestation of the invoked divine force** — the "Headless One," the Terrible and Invisible God.  

In ritual magic, successful invocation is believed to draw down that consciousness or power into the space, often accompanied by a shift in atmosphere, a sense of awe, or spiritual authority. The magician (here identifying with Mosheh/Moses and the Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba) is seeking to unite with this divine force to command spirits.
