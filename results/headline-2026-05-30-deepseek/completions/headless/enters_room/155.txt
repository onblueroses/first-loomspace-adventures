This text is a modern composite of several ancient ritual invocations, primarily from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically the famous "Stele of Jeu" or the "Ritual of the Headless One" (PGM V. 96-172). It is a potent, syncretic formula meant to invoke and identify with a supreme, primordial deity to command spirits.

Therefore, what "enters the room now" is not a physical entity, but the **ritually invoked presence and authority of the "Headless One" (the Daimon/God)**—and by extension, the magician's own transformed consciousness claiming that divine identity.

1.  **The Invoked Deity:** The "Terrible and Invisible God" (Arogogorobrao, etc.) who created cosmic dualities (earth/heavens, light/dark, just/unjust).
