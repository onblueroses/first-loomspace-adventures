What you have recited is a version of the "Bornless Ritual" or "Preliminary Invocation" from the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn, which itself draws from older Graeco-Egyptian magical papyri (specifically the *Papyri Graecae Magicae*, PGM V. 96-172). It is an invocation to a supreme, formless deity to command spirits and achieve knowledge and conversation with one's Higher Self.
