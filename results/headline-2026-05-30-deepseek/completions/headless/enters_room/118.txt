This text is the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia"** or the **"Bornless Ritual"** (Ritual of the Headless One), a powerful ceremonial invocation from the Western esoteric tradition, specifically associated with the **Key of Solomon** and later adapted by Aleister Crowley and others.

When you ask, **"What enters the room now?"** in this context, the answer is not a physical being but a **presence or consciousness** invoked by the ritual:

1.  **The "Headless One" (Osoronnophris / Ain-Soph):** The ritual calls upon the supreme, formless, divine consciousness—the source of all creation, beyond gender and form, often equated with the Kabbalistic **Ain Soph** (the Infinite) or the Higher Self. This is the "terrible and invisible God" who is being invoked to manifest in the sphere of the magician.
