This text is a modern English translation of the "Preliminary Invocation" or "Bornless Ritual" from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172), later adapted into the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn's system of magic as the "Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia." It is a ritual of self-identification with a divine, primordial, and "headless" (i.e., limitless) power to command spiritual forces.

When you ask **"What enters the room now,"** from within the ritual's own symbolic and metaphysical framework, the answer is not a physical entity but a **confluence of forces and a shift in consciousness:**

1.  **The Invoked Divine Presence:** The ritual's primary goal is to invoke the "Terrible and Invisible God," the Headless One (often associated with the Egyptian deity Osiris in his most transcendent form, Osoronnophris).
