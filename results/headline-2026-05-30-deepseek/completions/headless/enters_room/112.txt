This text is a powerful and ancient ritual invocation known as the **"Bornless Rite"** or the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia."** It is a foundational ritual in Western esoteric traditions, used to invoke a supreme, formless divine power (the "Headless One") to purify the operator and establish authority over spirits.

Given its purpose—to call upon a primal, invisible god to command all spirits—the question "What enters the room now?" asked at the climax of such an invocation, points to a singular, potent force.

**The entity that enters is the invoked power itself:** the **Headless One**, the **Terrible and Invisible God** named in the ritual. This is not a minor spirit, but the supreme, formless source from which all creation and authority flows. In the context of the ritual, the operator has identified with this power ("I am the Headless Daimon..."), and thus the room is filled with the palpable, overwhelming presence of:

*   **Arogogorobrao, Sochou, Modorio, Phalarchao, Ooo** – The secret, vibrational names of the God.
*   **IAŌ SABAŌTH**
