The ritual works by a process of identification and assumption of divine attributes. The magician, by reciting the god's names, deeds, and finally declaring "I am..." those same attributes, seeks to *become* the "Headless One" (the mind beyond the physical head, the limitless source). The final declarations ("I am the Mighty One...", "HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name") are not descriptions of a separate being arriving; they are the practitioner's proclamation of their own achieved state.
