This text is a ritual invocation from the **Bornless Rite** (or "Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia"), a powerful piece of ritual magic used to invoke a higher, formless divine consciousness—the "Headless One"—and to identify the magician with it.

When you ask, **"What enters the room now?"** — from the perspective of the ritual’s intent and the symbolic framework of Western esotericism — the answer is not a physical entity, but rather a **presence** or **consciousness** invoked by the words.

Here is what is understood to enter:

1. **The Headless One / Osoronnophris** – The formless, invisible god described at the start, the creative source beyond duality.
2. **The Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba** – The divine name and angelic force the magician claims to represent.
3. **The invoked divine names and barbarous words of power** (Ar, Thiao, Rheibet, etc.) – These are vibrational keys believed to open spiritual gateways and call down forces.
