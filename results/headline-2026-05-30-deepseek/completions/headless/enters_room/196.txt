This text is a modern English translation of the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia"** or, more precisely, the **"Bornless Ritual"** (also known as the "Headless One" ritual). It is a foundational ritual of Western ceremonial magic, used to invoke a higher, formless divine power (the "Headless One") to establish the magician's authority and command over spirits.

Therefore, in the context of the ritual's climax—where the magician declares **"I am the Headless Daimon... HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name"**—the answer to **"What enters the room now"** is not a physical entity, but a **state of consciousness and spiritual power.**

Specifically, it is:

**The invoked Divine Presence and the unified, empowered consciousness of the operator.** The ritualist has identified themselves with the "Headless One," merging their own awareness with the invoked godform. They have become the conduit for that power. The "room" is now filled with the charged atmosphere of the ritual—a sense of awe, authority, and connection to the macrocosm.
