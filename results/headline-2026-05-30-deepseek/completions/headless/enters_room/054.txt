This text is a modern translation of the "Bornless Ritual" or "Preliminary Invocation" from the Greco-Egyptian magical papyri, later adapted into Aleister Crowley's Hermetic system. It is a ritual invocation of a supreme, formless deity to establish the magician's authority and command over spirits.

When you ask, **"What enters the room now?"** from within the context of this ritual, the answer is not a physical entity but a **ritual and symbolic presence**:

1.  **The Invoked Divine Presence:** The ritual calls upon the "Headless One" (a formless, supreme god beyond perception). By the end, the magician identifies *with* this deity, declaring, "I am the Headless Daimon..." The "room" becomes occupied by the **charged intention and self-transformed consciousness of the practitioner**—the assumed godform.
