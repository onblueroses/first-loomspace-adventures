This is the **"Bornless Rite"** or **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia,"** a powerful ritual text from Western esoteric tradition. It is an invocation of the "Headless One," a form of the highest divine or daimonic power, intended to establish the magician's authority over spirits.

When you ask **"What enters the room now?"** in the context of having recited or invoked this ritual, the traditional esoteric answer is:

**The Holy Guardian Angel** — or the invoked divine presence of the "Headless One" (Akhoros, Osoronnophris) itself — understood as the magician's higher self or divine protector.  
The ritual’s purpose is to banish all hostile forces and call down this pure, conscious connection to the divine, so that the magician may speak with its authority.

In a more metaphysical sense, what is believed to enter is:
- A current of divine names and vibrations (IAŌ, SABAŌTH, ABLATHANALBA, etc.).
- The "Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba," named in the text.
- The purified magical link between the divine and the practitioner, often felt as a presence
