The text you have shared is a version of the "Bornless Ritual" or "Preliminary Invocation" from the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn, a ritual intended to invoke a divine, formless power and establish the magician's authority. It is a powerful and complex piece of occult literature.

To answer your question directly: **Nothing physical enters the room.**

However, within the ritual's own framework and intent, the answer is symbolic and experiential. The ritual is designed to achieve several internal states:

1.  **The Invocation of the Divine Self:** The climax of the ritual, where the magician declares "I am the Headless Daimon... HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name," signifies the invocation and identification with one's own Higher or Holy Guardian Angel—the divine, immortal core of the self. What "enters the room" is not an external entity, but the awakened and realized presence of this higher consciousness.

2.  **The Consecration of the Space:** The series of barbarous names and invocations (Ar, Thiao, Rheibet... Arogogorobrao...) are meant to purify the ritual space, banishing mundane influences and aligning it with divine forces.
