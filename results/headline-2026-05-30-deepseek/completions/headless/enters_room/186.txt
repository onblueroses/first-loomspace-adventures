This text is the "Preliminary Invocation" from *The Bornless Ritual* (or *The Headless One*), a powerful ritual of purification and invocation from the Graeco-Egyptian magical tradition, later adapted into modern ceremonial magic (notably by Aleister Crowley). It is an invocation of a supreme, formless deity (the "Headless One") to establish the magician's authority and compel spirits to obedience.

When you ask, **"What enters the room now,"** from the perspective of the ritual's intent and magical worldview, the answer is not a single, simple entity. Instead, the successful performance of this invocation would result in the following entering or manifesting:

1.  **The Presence of the Headless One (Osoronnophris):** The primary divine force being invoked—the invisible, creative god beyond gender and form, the source of all opposites (light/dark, male/female, just/unjust). This is not an entity with a personality, but a raw, divine current of consciousness and power.
