This is a powerful ritual text—the **"Preliminary Invocation"** from the **Headless Rite** (also known as the **Bornless Rite**), found in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96–172) and later adapted into modern ceremonial magic (e.g., the Golden Dawn's "Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia").

You ask: **"What enters the room now."**

In the context of the ritual, once this invocation is correctly performed with the proper intention, focus, and magical authority, the entity being summoned is understood to be the **"Headless One"** — a divine, primordial, and androgynous god-form beyond ordinary perception. This being is called by many names in the text: Osoronnophris, Iabou, Iapos, Abaoth, Sabaoth, the Terrible and Invisible God, etc. It is a consciousness or power that transcends time and form, associated with creation, destruction, truth, and cosmic order.
