This text is a modern English translation of a ritual invocation from the Greek Magical Papyri, specifically the "Bornless Rite" or "Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia." It is a powerful and ancient ritual designed to invoke a supreme, formless deity (the "Headless One") and to identify the practitioner with that divine force to command spirits.

Therefore, in the context of the ritual, **what enters the room now is the invoked presence itself.**

It is not a physical entity, but the **ritualized manifestation of the divine force** called upon—the Terrible and Invisible God, Osoronnophris, the Headless Daimon. The practitioner, by speaking the words "I am the Headless Daimon..." and declaring the divine names, has sought to unite their consciousness with this primordial power. The room becomes the "Void Place of the Spirit" mentioned in the invocation, filled with the focused intention and archetypal energy of the rite.
