Within the context of the ritual itself, the final declaration—"HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name. IAŌ SABAŌTH."—is the climax where the practitioner identifies with the divine force, becoming a vessel for its power.

Therefore, in the ritual's framework, **what enters the room now is not an external spirit, but the invoked presence itself, fully realized and embodied in the practitioner.** The room is filled with the consecrated and awakened consciousness of the magician, who has become the "Mighty One" and the "Grace of the World." The boundary between invoker and invoked has dissolved. The atmosphere would be charged with the qualities described: terrible power, immortal fire, truth, and creative/destructive force, all contained within the declared form of the "Heart Girt with a Serpent."
