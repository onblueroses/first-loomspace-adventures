This text is a version of the **"Bornless Ritual"** or the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia,"** a powerful ritual used in Western esoteric traditions (like ceremonial magic and Thelema) to invoke a divine, formless, and "headless" power to purify the magician and command spirits.

**What it means:** The ritualist is identifying with and invoking a primordial, androgynous deity (often associated with **Osiris** or the **Higher Self**) to become a vessel for divine authority. The final declaration, "HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name," and "IAŌ SABAŌTH" signify the ritualist's assumed divine identity and power.
