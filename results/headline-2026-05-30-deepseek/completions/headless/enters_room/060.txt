This text is the beginning of the **"Bornless Ritual"** or the **"Preliminary Invocation"** from the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn**,** a powerful ceremonial magick invocation used to call upon and identify with the "Higher Genius" or "Holy Guardian Angel."

When you ask, **"What enters the room now?"** in this ritual context, the answer is not a physical entity but a **presence** or **consciousness** invoked by the words. Based on the ritual's purpose and climax:

1.  **The "Headless One" / The Higher Self:** The ritual is designed to invoke and unify the practitioner with their own divine, immortal nature—the "Headless Daimon" or "Higher Genius." The final declarations ("I am the Mighty One... HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name") signify this union. So, what enters is the **awakened and invoked Higher Self** of the practitioner.

2.  **A Current of Divine Power and Authority:** The series of barbarous names of power (Ar, Thiao, Rheibet, etc., and Arogogorobrao...) are believed to be vibrated to summon specific divine forces
