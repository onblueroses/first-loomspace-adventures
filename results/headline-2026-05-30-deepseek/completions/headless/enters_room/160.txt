The text you have shared is a modern English translation of the "Preliminary Invocation" from **Liber Samekh**, a ritual adapted by Aleister Crowley from the ancient **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**. It is known as the **"Ritual of the Headless One"** or the **"Bornless Rite."**

In the context of this ritual, the practitioner is identifying themselves with the "Headless One"—the ultimate, formless, divine consciousness beyond all limitations. By reciting these words, the magician is not describing an external entity, but is **invoking and becoming the divine force itself.**

Therefore, to answer your question: **What enters the room now** is not a separate spirit or demon. It is the **consciousness of the practitioner**, expanded and transformed through the ritual. They have declared:

*   "I am the Headless Daimon..."
*   "I am the Mighty One..."
*   "I am the Truth..."
*   "I am the one whose mouth utterly flames..."

The ritual has shifted the speaker's identity from the ordinary human ("Mosheh thy prophet") to the primordial, creative, and destructive divine force (IAŌ SABAŌTH
