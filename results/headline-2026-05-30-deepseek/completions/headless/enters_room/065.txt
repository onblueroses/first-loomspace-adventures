The text you have shared is a modern translation of a ritual invocation from the **"Headless Rite"** (or "Bornless Rite"), found in the **Greek Magical Papyri** (PGM V. 96-172). It is a powerful ritual of self-identification with a divine, primordial, and "headless" (i.e., limitless) god, used to command spirits and achieve mystical knowledge.

To answer your question directly: **What enters the room now** is not a physical entity, but rather the **presence and power of the invoked deity and the magician's own transformed consciousness.**

1.  **The Invoked Deity:** You have called upon the "Terrible and Invisible God," Osoronnophris (a Graeco-Egyptian name for Osiris/Asar Unnefer, the perfected one), and Sabaoth.
