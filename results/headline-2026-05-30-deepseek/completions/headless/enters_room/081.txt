This text is a ritual incantation known as the "Preliminary Invocation" or the "Invocation of the Headless One" from the Greek Magical Papyri (specifically PGM V. 96-172). It is a powerful, syncretic invocation of a supreme deity, blending Egyptian, Gnostic, Jewish, and Greco-Roman elements to call upon and identify with a primordial, all-creating, and terrifying divine force.

When you ask **"What enters the room now?"** in the context of having spoken this invocation, the answer exists on several levels:

### 1. **Ritual & Symbolic Level:**
The ritual is designed to invoke a divine presence and, crucially, to **identify the magician with that presence**. By the end, the speaker declares:
*   "I am the Headless Daimon..."
*   "I am the Mighty One..."
*   "HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name."
*   "IAŌ SABAŌTH."
