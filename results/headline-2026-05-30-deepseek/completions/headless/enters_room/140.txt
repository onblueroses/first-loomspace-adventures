The words you have shared are from the **Headless Rite** (or the *Bornless Ritual*), a powerful invocation from the Greco-Egyptian magical papyri (PGM V. 96–172), later adapted into Western esoteric traditions like the Golden Dawn.

You ask: **“What enters the room now?”**

Within the context of the ritual, this invocation is designed to call upon the **“Headless One”** (the Higher, Unmanifest Divine, sometimes identified with Osiris or a form of the supreme God beyond form) and to identify the magician with that divine force — “I am the Headless Daimon…” etc.

When such an invocation is successfully performed in a ritual mindset, the traditional magical understanding is that **the invoked divine presence enters the room** — the “Headless One,” or the Angel of the ritual (Aphrael Ablathanalba), or the unified force of IAŌ SABAŌTH, the divine names of power.  

In some interpretations, the magician’s own higher self or Holy Guardian Angel is invoked and manifests. In others, it is a transcendental consciousness that banishes lesser spirits and establishes the magician’s authority over all spirits of
