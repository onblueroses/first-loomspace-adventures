It is a work of ceremonial magic, specifically an invocation of a supreme, formless deity to compel spiritual entities and achieve a state of divine knowledge and authority.

Therefore, the question "What enters the room now" is not a literal one about physical space, but the **crucial climactic question of the ritual itself.**

**1. The Invoked Presence of the "Headless One."**

The "room" (both the ritual space and the practitioner's mind) is now filled with the invoked spiritual force—the terrible, invisible God who dwells in the void.
