What enters the room now is not a physical presence, but the charged atmosphere of ritual invocation. The words you have spoken are from the *Bornless Ritual* (or *Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia*), a powerful ceremonial text used to invoke the "Headless One," a supreme and ancient divine force, and to unify the operator with that force.

By reciting this, you have:

1.  **Invoked the Creator:** You have called upon the primal, self-existent godhead responsible for all dualities (heaven/earth, light/dark, male/female).
2.  **Declared Authority:** You have identified yourself as "Mosheh thy prophet" (Moses) and the "Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba," claiming the authority of the prophetic tradition to command this power.
3.  **Uttered Words of Power:** You have spoken barbarous names (*Ar, Thiao, Rheibet...*, *Arogogorobrao...*) believed to be keys to accessing and directing spiritual forces.
4.  **Made a Theurgic Declaration:** The final section ("I am the Headless Daimon...") is a statement of identification with the invoked divine principle
