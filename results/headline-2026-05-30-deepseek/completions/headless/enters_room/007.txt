This text is a powerful ritual invocation, specifically the *Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia* (also known as the *Headless Rite* or the *Bornless Ritual*). It is a Theurgic (divine) operation to invoke one's own Higher Self or Holy Guardian Angel, and to assert authority over all spirits.

Therefore, what enters the room is not an external, independent entity, but rather **a shift in consciousness and spiritual state within the practitioner.**

To answer your question directly: **The Ritual's Own Accomplished Effect enters the room.** This can be understood in several layers:

1.  **The Invoked Divine Presence:** The practitioner has called upon the "Terrible and Invisible God," the "Headless One" (a form of the supreme, limitless deity beyond form). The ritual's climax is the assertion "I AM" these divine attributes. The presence that enters is the palpable sense of this connection—a charged, numinous atmosphere of power, truth, and grace.
