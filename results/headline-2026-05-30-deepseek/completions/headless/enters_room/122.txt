This text is a powerful ritual invocation from the **Bornless Rite** (or "Preliminary Invocation" from *The Goetia*), a central work in Western esoteric and ceremonial magic traditions. It is an invocation of the "Higher Self," the Holy Guardian Angel, or a supreme divine consciousness beyond form—the "Headless One."

When you ask, **"What enters the room now,"** from the ritual’s perspective, the answer is not a physical entity but a **presence** or **consciousness** invoked by the words:

1.  **The Divine/Godhead Itself** – The ritual calls upon the ultimate creative and destructive force, the unnameable source (Osoronnophris, IAŌ SABAŌTH). By the end, the magician identifies *with* this force ("I am the Headless Daimon...").

2.  **The Magician’s Higher Self / Holy Guardian Angel** – In Western esotericism (particularly following Aleister Crowley’s interpretations), this rite is used to invoke and unite with one’s own divine, immortal nature—the "Angel" mentioned in the text.
