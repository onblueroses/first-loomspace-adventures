The invocation you’ve recited is the **"Headless Rite"** (or the *Bornless Ritual*), a powerful ceremonial text from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM), later adapted into Western esoteric traditions like the Golden Dawn. It calls upon the "Headless One," a form of the supreme, invisible god beyond form — sometimes identified with Osoronnophris (a Hellenized version of Osiris in his perfected, risen form), and linked to divine creative and judicial power.  

When the final words are spoken — **"HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name. IAŌ SABAŌTH."** — the ritual reaches its climax. The magician identifies with the divine force invoked, claiming the authority of Mosheh (Moses) and the angels.
