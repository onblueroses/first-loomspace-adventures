This text is a modern English translation of a section from the **"Bornless Rite"** (or "Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia"), a ritual from the Western esoteric tradition, specifically from the **Greek Magical Papyri** (PGM). It is a powerful invocation meant to call upon the "Headless One," a supreme, formless deity, to grant authority over spirits to the practitioner.

In the context of the ritual, the magician is identifying with and proclaiming themselves to be the deity (a practice called **"assumption of the godform"**). The final declaration—"HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name. IAŌ SABAŌTH."—signals the climax of this identification.

Therefore, if one were to ask **"What enters the room now?"** within the completed framework of this ritual, the answer is not a separate external spirit, but the **ritual's intended result:**

**The magician themselves, now filled with and acting as the invoked divine power—the "Headless One," the "Terrible and Invisible God," the "Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba."** They have become the conduit and authority,
