The text you have shared is a ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically a version of the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."** It is a powerful theurgic/ceremonial magic text intended to invoke a supreme, formless deity to grant the practitioner authority over spirits.

If we take your question literally—**"What enters the room now?"**—within the context of this ritual being performed, the intended answer is:

**The invoked divine presence — the "Headless One," the Terrible and Invisible God — enters the room.**

This could be understood as:
1.  The divine spirit Osoronnophris (a Greco-Egyptian name for Osiris/RA as the perfected, invisible god).
2.  The practitioner's own Holy Guardian Angel or Higher Self (as the ritual is also used for attaining knowledge and conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel in later Western esoteric traditions like Aleister Crowley's system).
3.  A current of divine power and authority that subdues all lesser spirits.

The ritual's purpose is to unify the magician with this divine source, so that the magician **becomes** the "Headless
