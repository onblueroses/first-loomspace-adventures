It is a powerful formula used in ancient theurgic and magical practices to invoke a supreme, formless deity and to identify the practitioner with that divine power in order to command spirits.

### 1. **The Ritual & Symbolic Level**
In the framework of the ritual, the invocation culminates in the magician declaring, *"I am the Headless Daimon... HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name."* By speaking these words with intent, the practitioner is meant to **become** the invoked divine force.
