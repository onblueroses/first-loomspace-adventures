The text you have shared is the *Preliminary Invocation* from the *Bornless Ritual* (or *Headless Rite*), a powerful ritual of late antiquity and a cornerstone of modern ceremonial magic, particularly within the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn. It is an invocation of the "Higher Self" or Holy Guardian Angel, a divine, formless consciousness.

When you ask, **"What enters the room now,"** the answer operates on multiple levels:

### On the Ritualistic & Symbolic Level:
1.  **The Invoked Presence:** The ritual is designed to call forth the "Headless One" (Akephalos), a transcendental, primordial godhead beyond form and duality. By its conclusion, the magician identifies *with* this presence ("I am the Headless Daimon..."). Therefore, what "enters" is not an external ghost, but an invoked **current of divine consciousness and authority**. It is the numinous, terrible, and creative power of the void itself—Arogogorobrao, the God who dwells in the empty place of spirit.
