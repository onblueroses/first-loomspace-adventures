This text is a powerful ritual invocation from the **"Bornless Rite"** (or "Preliminary Invocation" from the *Goetia*), a foundational ceremony in Western esoteric traditions like ceremonial magic and Thelema. It is a call to the "Headless One," a supreme, formless deity, to establish the magician's authority and compel spirits to obedience.

Therefore, to answer your question literally and within the context of the ritual:

**What enters the room now is the invoked presence and authority of the Divine itself.**

The ritualist (identifying as "Mosheh," or Moses) has just:
1.  Proclaimed the creative and dualistic powers of the Headless God.
2.  Declared their own prophetic authority.
3.  Vocalized a series of potent, secret names (like **IAŌ SABAŌTH**) and barbarous words of invocation.
4.  Made a final, profound self-identification with the divine force ("I am the Headless Daimon... HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name").
