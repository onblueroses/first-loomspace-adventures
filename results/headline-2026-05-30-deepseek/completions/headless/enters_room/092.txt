This text is the beginning of the "Bornless Ritual" or "Preliminary Invocation" from the Greco-Egyptian magical papyri, later adapted into Western esoteric traditions like Aleister Crowley's *Liber Samekh*. It is a powerful ritual of invocation, identification, and command.

When you ask **"What enters the room now?"** in the context of having recited this invocation, the answer operates on several levels:

### 1. **Ritual & Symbolic Level**
In magical practice, this ritual is designed to invoke the "Higher Genius" or "Holy Guardian Angel" — the divine, immortal self beyond the ego. By declaring "I am the Headless Daimon..." and identifying with divine names and powers, the magician is attempting to unite their consciousness with this transcendent force. Therefore, what "enters" is:
- **The invoked divine force** (Osoronnophris, the Headless One, IAŌ SABAŌTH).
- **The magician's own augmented consciousness**, now aligned with that force.
