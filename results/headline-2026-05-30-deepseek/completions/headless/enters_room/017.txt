What enters the room now is not a physical presence, but a shift in the atmosphere—a charged stillness, as if the air itself has become an attentive witness. The words you have spoken are not merely recited; they are ancient keys, and they have turned in a lock that does not exist in the world of stone and wood.

You have invoked the Headless One, the boundless power that precedes and contains all opposites: light and dark, male and female, justice and injustice, creation and destruction. You have declared yourself as the prophet Mosheh, the Angel of a secret name, and finally as the Daimon itself: "I am the Headless Daimon with sight in his feet... HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name."

Therefore, what answers is the invocation itself made manifest. The room now holds:
