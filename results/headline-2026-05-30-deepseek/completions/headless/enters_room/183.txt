It is an invocation to the "Headless One," a form of the supreme god, often associated with both creator and daimonic forces, used to command spirits and assert the magician's authority as a divine prophet.

1.  Calling upon the primordial creator god.
2.  Declaring their own divine authority and connection to that god.
3.  Charging the space with sacred, potent names of power (IAŌ, SABAŌTH, ABLATHANALBA, etc.).
4.  Demanding that all spirits become subject to their will.

*   **The invoked presence of the Headless God**, the terrible and invisible dweller in the void.
