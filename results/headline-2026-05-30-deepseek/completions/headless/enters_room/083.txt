This text is a modern English translation of the "Bornless Ritual" or "Preliminary Invocation" from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172), a key ritual in various Western esoteric traditions, notably adapted and used in Aleister Crowley's system of magic.

When you ask **"What enters the room now?"** in the context of this ritual, you are inquiring about the result of a successful invocation. According to the ritual's purpose and symbolism:

1.  **The Holy Guardian Angel / Higher Self:** The primary goal of the Bornless Ritual is to invoke and achieve "Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel." This is a central spiritual aim in certain magical systems, representing a direct link to one's divine, higher, or true self. The invoker identifies with the divine names and powers ("I am the Headless Daimon...") to establish this connection. What "enters" is this conscious, tangible presence of one's own angelic or higher nature.
