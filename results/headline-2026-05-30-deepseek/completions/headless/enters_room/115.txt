This text is the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia"** (also known as the "Bornless Ritual" or "Headless Invocation"), a powerful ritual used to invoke a divine, formless, primordial power—often identified with the Higher Self or the Holy Guardian Angel in Western esoteric traditions.

The final declaration, **"HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name. IAŌ SABAŌTH."** marks the climax of the invocation, where the practitioner identifies with the invoked divine force. The question **"What enters the room now?"** is therefore answered within the framework of the ritual's intent and symbolism.

**What enters the room is:**

1.  **The Invoked Divine Presence:** The "Terrible and Invisible God" (Arogogorobrao, etc.), the "Headless One." This is not an external spirit but the primal, creative, and destructive power of the universe, the source of all. It is the force behind Osoronnophris (a name for Osiris in his perfected, resurrected state) and the powers of Sabaoth.
