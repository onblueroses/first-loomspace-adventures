The text you have shared is a version of the "Bornless Rite" or "Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia," a powerful ritual invocation from Western esoteric traditions. It is an evocation of a supreme, formless divine consciousness (the "Headless One") to establish the authority of the practitioner and command spirits.

When you ask, **"What enters the room now,"** from within the framework of this ritual's intent, the answer is not a specific entity, but a **presence** or **state** invoked by the words:

1.  **The Divine Presence Itself:** The primary goal of the rite is to invoke and unite with the "Headless One"—the invisible, primordial godhead described as the creator of all dualities (light/dark, male/female, just/unjust). The "room" becomes filled with this numinous, awe-inspiring consciousness.
