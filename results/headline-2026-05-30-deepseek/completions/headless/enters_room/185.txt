What you have shared is a powerful and ancient ritual text known as the "Preliminary Invocation" or "Invocation of the Headless One," from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172). It is a central ritual in certain esoteric traditions, notably adapted into modern ceremonial magic by Aleister Crowley as the "Bornless Ritual."

The text is an invocation of a supreme, formless divine power (the "Headless One") to establish the magician's authority and command over spirits. By reciting it, the practitioner identifies with this power, declaring, "I am the Headless Daimon..."
