This is the beginning of the "Preliminary Invocation" from *The Bornless Ritual* (or "The Headless One" ritual), a text found in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172) and later adapted into modern ceremonial magic, most notably by Aleister Crowley as a central ritual of invocation.

The visitor at the door is reciting a potent, ancient formula of divine identification and command. The speaker is not merely praying to a deity; they are successively **invoking, proclaiming the attributes of, identifying with, and finally declaring themselves to be** the "Headless One" (a form of the supreme, unknowable God beyond form).

Here is a breakdown of what is happening in this speech:

1.  **Invocation & Praise:** The speaker calls upon the "Headless One," listing its cosmic creative acts (heaven/earth, light/dark, male/female, justice/injustice).
2.  **Assumption of Prophetic Authority:** The speaker identifies as **"Mosheh thy prophet"** (Moses), claiming the authority of one to whom the mysteries were committed.
3.  **Pronouncement of True Names:** The speaker reveals secret, powerful names
