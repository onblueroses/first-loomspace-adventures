This is the **Preliminary Invocation** from the **Liber Samekh**, a ritual text used in ceremonial magic, particularly within the systems of Aleister Crowley and the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn. It is an adaptation of the ancient **"Bornless Ritual"** or **"Headless Rite"** from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM).

The visitor at the door is reciting a powerful invocation of the **"Headless One"** (a form of the Highest Divine, beyond form and limitation) to achieve a state of spiritual authority and knowledge. Key elements include:

*   **Identification with Divine Names:** The invoker speaks as the deity, using a series of potent names (Osoronnophris, Iabou, Abaoth, etc.) and proclaims their own divine attributes ("I am the Headless Daimon... I am the Truth...").
*   **Assumption of a Prophet's Role:** The speaker identifies as **"Mosheh thy prophet"** (Moses), claiming the authority of one to whom divine mysteries were committed.
