This is the *Preliminary Invocation* from the *Bornless Ritual* (also known as the *Headless Rite*), a powerful ritual text from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172) that was later adapted and incorporated into Western esoteric traditions, most notably by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley.

The visitor quoting this is either:
1.  **Announcing their identity as an operator/initiate**, using the ritual's declaration "I am Mosheh thy prophet..." and the final proclamation of divine names and attributes.
2.  **Performing an act of invocation or purification** at the threshold, using the words to establish a sacred or warded space.
3.  **Making a direct claim to authority and power**, as the ritual's purpose is to invoke the "Headless One" (a form of the highest divine consciousness beyond form) to command all spirits and affirm the magician's own divine nature ("I am the Headless Daimon...").

The key phrases in this context are:
*   **"The visitor at the door speaks"** – The ritual is being enacted *at the threshold*, a liminal space between worlds.
