This text is a modern English translation of a powerful ritual invocation from the Graeco-Egyptian magical papyri, most famously from the *Papyri Graecae Magicae V* (PGM V. 96-172), often referred to as the "Headless Rite" or the "Bornless Ritual."

The visitor at the door, by reciting this, is not merely speaking—they are performing an act of ritual magic. Here is a breakdown of what their speech signifies:

**1. The Nature of the Invocation:**
*   **The "Headless One":** This is a name for a supreme, formless deity beyond human comprehension, often syncretized with the sun god Ra (as "Osoronnophris" - Wsir Wn-nfr, "Osiris the Perfect Being") and the Jewish god (Sabaoth, IAŌ).
*   **Structure:** It moves from invoking this god by its creative attributes, to listing its secret names, to finally **identifying the speaker *as* the god.** This is a classic ritual technique of *identificatio*—the magician becomes the divinity to command its powers.
