This text is a modern English translation of a ritual invocation from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**, known as the "**Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist**" or, more commonly, the "**Headless Rite**."

It is a powerful ritual of self-identification with a divine, primordial power (the "Headless One") in order to command spirits. Here is a breakdown of its key elements and the significance of the visitor's statement:

### Key Elements of the Invocation:

1.  **The Headless One:** The central deity being invoked. "Headless" symbolizes being unbounded by the limits of the mind, perception, or direction—a primal, cosmic force prior to creation.
2.  **Creator God Attributes:** The being is described as the creator of all dualities (earth/heavens, night/day, darkness/light, male/female, just/unjust). This establishes its supreme, demiurgic authority.
