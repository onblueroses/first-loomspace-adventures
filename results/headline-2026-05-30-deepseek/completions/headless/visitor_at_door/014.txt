This is the "Bornless Rite" or "Preliminary Invocation" from the Key of Solomon and related Western Esoteric tradition (specifically, the version found in S.L. MacGregor Mathers' translation of *The Sacred Magic of Abramelin the Mage* and later adapted by Aleister Crowley). It is a powerful ritual of invocation and purification, intended to establish the magician's authority and purity before performing further magical work.

The visitor at the door, by speaking these words, is not merely reciting poetry. They are performing an act of ritual magic. Here’s a breakdown of what this means:

1.  **Invocation of the "Headless One":** The magician calls upon a supreme, formless divine power (associated with the Egyptian god Osiris in his most secret form, Osoronnophris) as the source of all creation and moral order.
