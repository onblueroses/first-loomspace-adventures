The visitor at the door is reciting a declaration of identity and authority, culminating in the divine names **IAŌ SABAŌTH** and the statement **"HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name."**

In this context, the speaker is not merely a "visitor" but an operator who has ritually assumed the identity of the "Headless One" (a form of the Higher Self or Holy Guardian Angel). They are stating that they have become the conduit for that divine, creative, and destructive force. The purpose of such an invocation is to achieve Knowledge and Conversation with one's Holy Guardian Angel, to banish hostile forces, and to command spirits.
