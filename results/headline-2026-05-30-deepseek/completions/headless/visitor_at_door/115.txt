This is a recitation of the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia,"** often called the **"Bornless Ritual"** or **"Invocation of the Headless One."** It is a foundational ritual text from Western ceremonial magic, specifically from the **Key of Solomon** tradition and later adapted into **Aleister Crowley's** system as the **"Preliminary Invocation"** in *The Goetia*.

The visitor at your door is either:

1. **A practicing ritual magician** testing the waters or performing part of a working.
2. **Someone with an interest in occult literature** reciting a powerful text they’ve memorized.
3. **A spiritual seeker** using the invocation as a form of meditation or prayer.
4. **A character from a story, game, or performance** staying in role.

The text itself is an invocation of a **"Headless God"** or higher divine consciousness (sometimes associated with **Osiris** or a primal creative force) to establish the magician’s authority and command over spirits.
