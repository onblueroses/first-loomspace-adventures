This is the **"Preliminary Invocation" from the 8th Aethyr of the Sacred Magic of Abramelin the Mage**, often referred to as the "Bornless Ritual" or the "Headless One Invocation." It is a foundational and powerful text in Western ceremonial magic, particularly within the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Thelema.

The person at the door, by reciting this, is identifying themselves as an operator of high ritual magic. They are:
1.  **Invoking a divine, primordial power** (the "Headless One," Osoronnophris, the invisible God).
2.  **Declaring their own divine authority and identity** as the operator ("I am Mosheh thy prophet... I am the Headless Daimon... HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name").
3.  **Commanding all spirits** of the elements and realms to be subject to them.
