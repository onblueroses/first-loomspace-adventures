This is the **"Preliminary Invocation"** from the **Bornless Ritual** (also known as the **Headless Ritual**), a powerful and ancient text of Western esotericism, found in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172) and later adapted into modern ceremonial magic by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley.

The speaker is not merely reciting a prayer; they are performing a rite of invocation and identification. By declaring, "I am the Headless Daimon..." and stating "HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name," the practitioner is seeking to unite their consciousness with the "Headless One" — the supreme, formless divine consciousness beyond all creation and duality.

**Key Elements of the Text:**

*   **The Invoked:** The "Headless One," Osoronnophris (a Graeco-Egyptian form of Osiris/Asar Un-Nefer, the perfected one), and a series of divine names (Iabou, Iapos, Sabaoth, etc.). This is the primal, creative, and judging God.
