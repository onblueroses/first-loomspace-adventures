This is the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia,"** also known as the **"Bornless Ritual"** or the **"Invocation of the Headless One."** It is a powerful ritual text from Western esoteric traditions, primarily used as an invocation to establish a connection with one's Higher Self or Holy Guardian Angel, and to assert authority over spirits.

The visitor at the door, by speaking these words, is not merely reciting poetry. They are performing a ritual act. They are:

1.  **Announcing Their Purpose:** They are identifying themselves as an operator of ritual magic, likely standing at the threshold of a consecrated space.
2.  **Invoking Divine Authority:** By chanting the names and attributes of the "Headless One" (a form of the supreme, invisible God beyond form), they are calling upon the highest power to witness and empower their work.
