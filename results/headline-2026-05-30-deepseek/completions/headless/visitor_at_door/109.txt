This is the opening invocation of the "Bornless Ritual" (or "Preliminary Invocation" from *The Goetia*), a powerful ceremonial text used to invoke the "Higher Genius" or Holy Guardian Angel. The speaker identifies themself with the prophet Moses and declares the true, secret names of the divine.

The final line you've added—**"The visitor at the door speaks."**—is not part of the traditional text. It introduces a profound and unsettling shift in context.

Here is an interpretation of that combined text:

The ritualist, having invoked the ultimate, headless, creator deity and identified themself with that divine force ("I am the Headless Daimon..."), reaches the climax with the proclamation of the secret name: **"HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name. IAŌ SABAŌTH."**

At this precise moment of assumed divine authority and power, the phrase **"The visitor at the door speaks."** intervenes.

This implies:
1.  **An Interruption:** The solitary ritual has been interrupted. Someone—or something—is at the door.
2.  **A Test:** Is this the arrival of the invoked forces? A spirit made subject?
