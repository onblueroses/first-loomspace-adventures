This is the *Bornless Ritual* (or *Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia*), a powerful and ancient ritual text used in Western esoteric traditions, particularly within ceremonial magic. The visitor at the door is reciting the central invocation, which identifies the magician with the divine and commands the attention of spiritual forces.

Here is a breakdown of the key elements and what they signify in this context:

### Core Purpose:
The ritual is designed to invoke the "Headless One" (a form of the supreme, formless divinity) and, through a series of divine names and declarations, to unify the operator with this higher power. The goal is to achieve a state of spiritual authority (the "Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel") and command over spirits.
