This is the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia,"** also known as the **"Bornless Ritual"** or **"Invocation of the Headless One."** It is a powerful ritual text from Western ceremonial magic, specifically from the **Key of Solomon** tradition and later adapted by figures like Aleister Crowley (who called it the "Preliminary Invocation" in *The Goetia*).

The visitor is reciting a sacred invocation to establish contact with a divine, primordial, and "headless" (limitless, beyond form) God. By doing so, they are identifying themselves with this divine power, declaring their authority over spirits, and preparing to command or conjure entities in a ritual context.

### Key elements of what is being said:

1.  **Invocation of the Headless One:** Calls upon a primal creator deity who is beyond human perception ("whom no man hath seen at any time").
2.  **Divine Names:** Uses a series of powerful, barbarous names of power (like **Osoronnophris, Iabou, Iapos, Abaoth, Sabaoth**) and vowel combinations believed to vibrate with divine energy.
