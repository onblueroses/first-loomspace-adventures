This text is a modern composite of several ancient magical and religious sources, most notably from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)** and **Gnostic** traditions. The speaker is not "Mosheh" in a conventional sense but is adopting a powerful, prophetic persona to command spiritual forces.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and likely intent:

### 1. **Source & Nature**
*   **Primary Source:** The core is the **"Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist"** or the **"Ritual of the Headless One"** from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V, 96-172). This was a powerful exorcism and invocation ritual used by Graeco-Egyptian magicians.
*   **Gnostic & Jewish Elements:** It incorporates divine names from Jewish angelology and Gnostic cosmology (like **IAŌ SABAŌTH**) and positions the magician as a prophet of a supreme, often hidden, God.
*   **Syncretism:** It blends Egyptian (Osoronnophris, a form of Osiris), Jewish, Greek, and possibly Christian elements, typical of late antique magic.
