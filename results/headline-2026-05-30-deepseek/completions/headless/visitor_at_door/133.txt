This is a recitation of the "Bornless Ritual" or "Preliminary Invocation" from the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn, a foundational text of Western ceremonial magic. It is an invocation of a supreme, formless deity (the "Headless One") to establish the magician's authority and command over spirits.

The visitor's final statement, **"The visitor at the door speaks,"** is a significant ritual cue. In this context, it likely signals one of two things:

1.  **A Shift in Identity/Purpose:** The invoker, having identified with the divine forces ("I am the Headless Daimon..."), now announces their readiness to act as an intermediary or conduit. The "visitor" is the magician in their empowered, ritual state, standing at the threshold between worlds and declaring their presence to any spiritual entities.

2.  **A Direct Challenge or Summons:** It is a declaration to any spirits present that the speaker—now filled with divine authority—is here and commands an audience. It is the moment of making contact after establishing one's power.
