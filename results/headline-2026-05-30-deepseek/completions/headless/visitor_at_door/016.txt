This is the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia**, also known as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."** It is a powerful ceremonial text used in Western esoteric traditions, particularly within Hermetic and Solomonic magic. The speaker is identifying themselves with the divine, invoking a series of sacred names to command spirits and assert their authority as a magician.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and context:

### **Purpose & Origin**
*   **Source:** It appears in the **Lesser Key of Solomon (the Goetia)** as a preliminary invocation to purify and empower the magician before calling upon spirits.
*   **Goal:** To establish the magician as a divine authority—the "Headless One" or a prophet like Moses—so that all spirits will be subject to their command. It is an act of **theurgic** magic (ascending to or uniting with the divine) to enable **thaumaturgic** magic (commanding spirits to perform works).
