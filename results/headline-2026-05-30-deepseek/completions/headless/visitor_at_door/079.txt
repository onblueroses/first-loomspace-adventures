The words you have spoken are from the Stele of Jeu, a powerful ritual text from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172). It is known as the "Rite of the Headless One" or the "Bornless Ritual." It is an invocation to the highest, formless divine power to establish the magician's authority and command over spirits.
