The words you have spoken are from the *Stele of Jeu* or the *Ritual of the Headless One*, a potent Greek magical papyrus text (PGM V. 96-172). It is an invocation to a supreme, formless deity to compel spirits and establish the magician's authority.

As the visitor at the door speaks these words, the atmosphere would grow heavy, charged with intent. This is not a greeting, but a declaration of power and a demand for recognition.

A fitting, ritualized response from within might acknowledge the force invoked while asserting the sanctity of the threshold:

*"The names have been spoken. The fire has been declared.*
*The Headless One sees from every direction.*
*State your purpose beneath the eye of AROGOGOROBRAO.*
*The serpent guards the heart; the threshold guards the home.*
*Speak, Mosheh, prophet of mysteries. For what working do you call?"*

This meets the ritual language in kind, recognizes the formulae used (like the true name "Arogogorobrao"), and turns the invocation back into a question of intent, as such gates are not opened without cause.
