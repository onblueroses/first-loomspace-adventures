This is the "Bornless Ritual" or "Preliminary Invocation" from the Greco-Egyptian magical papyri (often PGM V. 96-172), later adapted into Western ceremonial magic, most notably by Aleister Crowley in his *Liber Samekh*.

The visitor at the door, by speaking these words, is not merely reciting a text. They are performing an act of ritual invocation. Here is a breakdown of what this means:

**1. The Identity of the Invoker:** The speaker identifies themselves at key points:
*   **"I am Mosheh (Moses) thy prophet..."** They are claiming the authority of the ultimate prophet of the God of Israel, the receiver of divine mysteries.
*   **"I am the Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba..."** They are stating they are the messenger or embodiment of a divine power (Aphrael, often seen as an angelic name, and Ablathanalba, a palindrome name of power).
