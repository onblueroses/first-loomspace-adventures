This is the opening invocation from the **"Bornless Ritual"** (or the "Preliminary Invocation" of the Goetia), a foundational text in Western ceremonial magic, particularly within the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and related traditions.

The visitor's recitation is a powerful act of ritual speech. By speaking these words at the door, they are not just announcing their presence—they are:

1.  **Identifying Themselves with the Divine:** The speaker moves from invoking the "Headless One" (a form of the supreme, unknowable God) to declaring **"I am..."** statements that align them with that divine power ("I am the Mighty One... I am the Truth...").
2.  **Asserting Authority:** The core purpose of this invocation is to establish the magician's authority over all spirits. The repeated "Hear me" commands and the demand that "all spirits subject unto me" make this a ritual of purification, protection, and command.
3.  **Using Words of Power:** The strings of names (like *Arogogorobrao*) and vowels are **"barbarous names"** or **voces magicae**—believed to be intrinsically powerful sounds
