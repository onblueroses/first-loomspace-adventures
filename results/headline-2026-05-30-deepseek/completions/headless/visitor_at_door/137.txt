This is the beginning of the *Bornless Ritual* (or *Preliminary Invocation* from *The Goetia*). It is a powerful ritual of invocation and identification, used to establish a purified and empowered state of consciousness before ceremonial work.

The visitor at the door, by speaking these words, is not merely reciting a text. They are performing an act of ritual magic. The intent is likely one or more of the following:

1.  **Consecration & Purification:** To cleanse the space and the speaker, banishing lesser influences and establishing a sacred, ritually potent environment.
2.  **Invocation & Alignment:** To call upon and align themselves with the divine, formless "Headless One" (a state of pure consciousness beyond duality) and the archangelic powers listed (Aphrael, etc.).
3.  **Assumption of Godforms:** The shift from "I invoke Thee..." to "**I am** the Headless Daimon..." is a key ritual technique. The speaker is *identifying* with these divine attributes—Mighty One, Immortal Fire, Truth, Destroyer and Begetter—to elevate their own spiritual authority.
