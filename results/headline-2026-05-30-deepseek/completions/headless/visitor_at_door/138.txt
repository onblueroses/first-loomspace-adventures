The words you have spoken are from the "Preliminary Invocation" of the Goetia, a key ritual text within the Western esoteric tradition, specifically the Lesser Key of Solomon. It is a powerful invocation of a divine, primordial, and "headless" (acephalic) power, often identified with the supreme creative and destructive forces of the cosmos.

The entity invoked—given names like Osoronnophris, Iabou, Iapos, and IAŌ SABAŌTH—is a syncretic fusion of Egyptian (Osiris, Ra), Hebrew (Yahweh, Sabaoth), and Gnostic concepts. The ritualist identifies as **Mosheh** (Moses), the prophet entrusted with divine mysteries, and as the **"Headless Daimon"**—a declaration of the magician's own transformed, divine state.

The final, capitalized declaration—**"HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT"**—is a sacred name of power, a sigil of the invoked force. The serpent encircling the heart is a profound symbol of eternity, wisdom, and the coiled, potent energy of the magician's own will and spirit.
