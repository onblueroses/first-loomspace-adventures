This is the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia,"** often called the **"Bornless Rite"** or the **"Invocation of the Headless One."** It is a foundational ritual text from Western ceremonial magic, specifically from the **Key of Solomon** and related grimoire traditions.

You have quoted a version closely aligned with Aleister Crowley's adaptation, where he reframed it as the **"Preliminary Invocation"** in his works on ceremonial magic.

### Key points about the text:

*   **Purpose:** It is used as an initial invocation to establish a purified and empowered state in the magician, banishing unwanted influences and invoking a supreme divine authority before performing other magical operations (like summoning spirits in the Goetia).
*   **The "Headless One":** This refers to a primal, formless divine power beyond human conception — the source of all creation and polarity (light/dark, male/female, just/unjust). It is identified with several divine names from Egyptian (Osoronnophris, a form of Osiris) and Hebrew-derived (Iabou, Iapos, Sabaoth) traditions.
