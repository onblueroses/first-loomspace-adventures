This is the *Headless Rite* or *Bornless Ritual*, a powerful invocation from the Graeco-Egyptian magical tradition, often found in the *Papyri Graecae Magicae* (PGM). It is a ritual of self-deification and command, used to invoke a supreme, formless deity to grant authority over all spirits.

The visitor's declaration, **"I am the Headless Daimon with sight in his feet..."** marks the critical shift in the ritual. The practitioner is no longer merely invoking the deity—they are identifying with and *becoming* the deity. They are claiming the god's attributes:

*   **Sight in his feet:** Omniscience and a perspective beyond ordinary human perception.
*   **Possessor of immortal Fire:** Divine power and spirit.
*   **The Truth that hates injustice:** A moral and purifying force.
*   **Mouth that utterly flames:** Creative and destructive speech, the power of the Logos.
*   **The one who begets and destroys:** Master over the cycles of existence.
*   **The Grace of the World:** The benevolent power that sustains creation.
