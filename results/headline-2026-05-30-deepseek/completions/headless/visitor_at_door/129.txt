This is the **"Preliminary Invocation"** from the **Bornless Ritual** (also known as the **Headless Ritual**), a powerful ritual derived from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172) and later adapted into modern ceremonial magic, most notably by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley.

The visitor's final statement, **"The visitor at the door speaks,"** is a key line from the ritual's climax. It signals a shift in consciousness and identification. The practitioner, having invoked the "Headless One" (a form of the supreme, formless God beyond all creation), now declares that *they themselves* are that divine consciousness manifesting at the threshold of perception.

Here’s a breakdown of its meaning and purpose:

### Core Purpose of the Ritual:
1.  **Invocation & Identification:** The ritualist doesn't just call *upon* a god; they gradually **become** the god. They invoke the highest divine names and attributes, and then declare, "I am..." statements, culminating in "The visitor at the door speaks." This asserts the magician's own awakened divine consciousness is now present and operative.
