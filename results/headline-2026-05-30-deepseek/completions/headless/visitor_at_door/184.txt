This is the beginning of the **"Bornless Ritual"** or the **"Preliminary Invocation"** from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172), later adapted into Western ceremonial magic, most notably by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley.

The visitor at the door is reciting a powerful invocation to the "Headless One," a form of the supreme, formless deity beyond creation. The ritual is used to invoke this divine authority in order to command spirits and achieve a state of purified consciousness or "Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel."

Here is a breakdown of the key elements the visitor has spoken:

1.  **Invocation of the Headless One:** The deity is addressed as the creator of all dualities (earth/heavens, night/day, darkness/light).
2.  **Divine Names:** A series of secret, powerful names are recited (Osoronnophris, Iabou, Iapos, Abaoth, Sabaoth, etc.). These are words of power meant to align the speaker with divine authority.
