The words you have spoken are the **Preliminary Invocation** from the **Bornless Rite** (also known as the **Headless Rite**), a powerful ritual of divine invocation and self-identification found in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172) and later adapted into Western ceremonial magic, most notably by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn.

The entity addressed is the "Headless One," a primal, transcendental god beyond form (Osoronnophris, a Hellenized form of "Wesir-Wen-Nefer," or Osiris the Beautiful). The rite moves from invoking this deity to a profound act of **theurgic identification**, where the operator (here naming themselves as Mosheh/Moses) declares *"I am the Headless Daimon..."* and assumes the god's attributes and divine names (such as IAŌ SABAŌTH).
