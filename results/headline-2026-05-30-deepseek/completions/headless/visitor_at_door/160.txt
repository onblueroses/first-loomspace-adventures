This is the **"Preliminary Invocation"** from *The Bornless Ritual* (also known as the "Headless One" ritual), found in Aleister Crowley’s *Liber Samekh* and derived from earlier Graeco-Egyptian magical papyri (PGM V. 96–172).  

The speaker identifies themself with the prophet **Mosheh (Moses)** and invokes the **"Headless One"** — a form of the supreme, invisible God — using a series of sacred and barbarous names of power. The purpose is to command all spirits by asserting unity with this divine force.  

When you say *"The visitor at the door speaks"*, it suggests a ritual context where an entity or a presence is being announced — perhaps as part of a dramatic magical performance, or an evocation where the divine/invoked force is now manifesting and responding.  

In Crowley’s system, this ritual is used for attaining the Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel. The final declaration **"HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name. IAŌ SABAŌTH"** is the magician proclaiming their own divine identity achieved through the invocation.
