The text you have shared is a modern English translation of the "Preliminary Invocation" from the *Bornless Ritual* (also known as the *Headless Rite* or the *Invocation of the Heart Girt with a Serpent*). It is a powerful ritual invocation rooted in the Graeco-Egyptian magical traditions of late antiquity, later adapted and incorporated into modern Western esotericism, most notably by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley.

Here is a breakdown of its key elements and context:

### Origins & History
*   **Ancient Source:** The core of the ritual comes from a Greek magical papyrus (PGM V. 96-172, circa 3rd-4th century CE). It was a ritual of exorcism and divine invocation used by practitioners in the syncretic religious environment of the Eastern Roman Empire.
*   **Modern Adaptation:** In the late 19th century, it was translated and adapted by Golden Dawn founders S.L. MacGregor Mathers and William Wynn Westcott. Aleister Crowley later made it a central practice in his system of magick, considering it the foundational ritual for attaining the "Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel."
