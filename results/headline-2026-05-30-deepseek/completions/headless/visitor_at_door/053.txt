It is a powerful ritual text with origins in the **Greek Magical Papyri** (PGM) and later adapted into modern ceremonial magic, most notably by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley.

Here is a breakdown of its significance and context:

---

### **1. Origin & Purpose**
- **Source:** It comes from **PGM V. 96–172**, a Greco-Egyptian magical text from late antiquity (circa 3rd–5th century CE).
- **Purpose:** It is an invocation of a **"headless" or "bornless" divine spirit** — a supreme, formless god beyond creation — to establish the magician’s authority over all spirits and to achieve spiritual knowledge and power.
- **Structure:** It moves from praising the deity’s creative powers to declaring the magician’s own divine identity, ending with a series of barbarous (powerful but often untranslatable) names of power.
