This is the **Bornless Ritual** (or **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia**), a powerful and ancient ritual text from the Western esoteric tradition, specifically the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn. It is an invocation of the "Headless One," a divine, primordial, and self-begotten deity beyond form.

The visitor at the door, by speaking these words, is not merely reciting poetry. They are performing a **ritual act of invocation and identification**.

Here is a breakdown of what is happening and what these words signify:

### Core Purpose of the Ritual:
1.  **Invocation:** To call upon the supreme, formless God (the "Headless One") who is the source of all creation and justice.
2.  **Assumption of Godform:** The speaker progressively identifies *themselves* with this deity. Notice the shift from "**Thou art...**" to "**I am...**" This is a magical act of unifying the practitioner's consciousness with the divine.
3.  **Command & Consecration:** To command all spirits to be subject to the speaker, who now speaks from the authority of the invoked God. It is a ritual of purification, empowerment
