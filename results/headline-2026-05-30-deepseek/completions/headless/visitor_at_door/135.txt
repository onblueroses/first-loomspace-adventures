The words you have spoken are from the **Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist**, a core ritual text from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172). It is commonly known as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."**

Its purpose is the invocation of the "Headless One," a supreme and formless divine power, to attain a state of divine knowledge and authority (*gnosis*) and to command spirits. By reciting it, the practitioner identifies with this power, declaring themselves to be the "Headless Daimon" and the prophet "Mosheh" (Moses).

*   **The Invoked:** A creator god beyond gender and form (Osoronnophris, a Greco-Egyptian name for Osiris-Unnefer), identified with a series of barbarous (power-sound) names.
