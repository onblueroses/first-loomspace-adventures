This is the beginning of the *Bornless Rite* (or *Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia*), a powerful ritual invocation from Western esoteric tradition. The visitor at the door is not just reciting a text; they are performing an act of ritual identification and summoning.

By speaking these words, the visitor is:

1.  **Invoking the "Headless One"** (a form of the supreme, formless deity).
2.  **Listing the divine names and attributes** of this power (Osoronnophris, Iabou, Sabaoth, etc.).
3.  **Identifying themselves** with the prophet "Mosheh" (Moses) and the "Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba."
4.  **Declaring their own divine nature** ("I am the Headless Daimon... I am the Grace of the World.").
5.  **Charging the space and themselves** with power, culminating in the declaration of their true name: **"HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT"** and the final vibrated name **"IAŌ SABAŌTH."**
