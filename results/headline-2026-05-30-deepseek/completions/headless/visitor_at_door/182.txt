This is the opening invocation of the "Bornless Ritual" (also known as the "Preliminary Invocation" or the "Headless One" ritual) from the Western esoteric tradition, notably appearing in *The Greek Magical Papyri* and later adapted into the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn's practices.

The visitor at the door has recited a powerful, ancient formula used to invoke the "Headless One" — a supreme, formless divine consciousness or higher self — to establish authority over spirits and unify the practitioner with their own divine nature. Key elements include:

*   **Invocation of a Primal Creator:** Calls upon the deity as the source of all dualities (earth/heavens, night/day, darkness/light, male/female).
*   **Assumption of Divine Identity:** The speaker shifts from invoking the god to proclaiming *"I am..."*, identifying with the god's attributes (the Headless Daimon, the Mighty One, the Truth, etc.). This is a ritual act of mystical union or assumption of a godform.
*   **Powerful Names of Power:** Uses barbarous names (e.g., Arogogorobrao, IAŌ SABAŌTH
