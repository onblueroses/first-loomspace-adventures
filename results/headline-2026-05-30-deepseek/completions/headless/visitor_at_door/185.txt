This is the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia**, also known as the **"Bornless Ritual"** or the **"Headless One"** invocation. It is a foundational text in Western ceremonial magic, particularly within the Solomonic and Thelemic traditions.

The visitor at the door, by speaking these words, is performing a potent ritual act. They are not merely reciting poetry; they are:

1.  **Invoking the "Headless One" (Osoronnophris):** A form of the supreme, formless deity beyond all creation, often associated with the Egyptian god Osiris in his most transcendent aspect.
2.  **Declaring a Prophetic Identity:** By stating *"I am Mosheh (Moses) thy prophet,"* the speaker aligns themselves with the archetype of the lawgiver and mediator of divine mysteries.
3.  **Assuming a Divine Mantle:** The middle section shifts from invocation to **identification**. The speaker proclaims: *"I am the Headless Daimon... I am the Mighty One... I am the Truth... I am the Grace of the World."* This is a ritual act of assuming the godform, embodying the very power
