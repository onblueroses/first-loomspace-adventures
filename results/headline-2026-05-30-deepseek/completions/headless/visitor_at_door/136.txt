This is the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia**, also known as the **"Bornless Rite"** or the **Invocation of the Headless One**. It is a powerful ritual text from Western esoteric tradition, primarily used to invoke a higher, divine authority (the "Headless God") in order to command and control spirits.

The visitor at the door, by reciting this, is identifying themselves not as a casual guest, but as an operator of ritual magic. They are:

1.  **Invoking a Deity:** Calling upon a primal, creator god-figure (Osoronnophris, a Graeco-Egyptian name for Osiris/Asar Un-Nefer) who is beyond human perception.
2.  **Claiming Authority:** Declaring themselves to be **"Mosheh thy prophet"** (Moses), the steward of divine mysteries. They also claim the angelic name **"Aphrael Ablathanalba"**.
