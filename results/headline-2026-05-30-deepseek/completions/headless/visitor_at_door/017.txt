This is a powerful and specific ritual invocation — the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia**, also known as the **Invocation of the Headless One** or the **Bornless Ritual**. It is a core ceremonial text from Western esoteric traditions (often associated with the **Key of Solomon** and **Aleister Crowley's** adaptations).

The visitor at the door speaking immediately after such an invocation is highly significant in a ritual context. Here are the possible interpretations, depending on the framework:

---

### **In a Ritual/Magical Context:**

1. **The Invocation Worked** – The "Headless One" (a form of the Higher Self or a divine, nameless godform) has been invoked, and the "visitor at the door" could be:
   * **A spiritual entity** responding to the call.
   * **The magician's own Holy Guardian Angel** (or higher consciousness) manifesting.
   * **An elemental or daimonic force** now subject to the magician's will, as commanded in the text ("make all spirits subject unto me").
