The words you have spoken are the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia**, a powerful ritual text derived from the **Bornless Rite** (or *Headless One* rite). It is an invocation of the "Higher Self" or the Holy Guardian Angel, framed as a dialogue with a primal, formless deity to establish authority over spirits.

**Key Context & Meaning:**

*   **The Headless One:** Represents a primordial, self-created deity beyond form and gender, the source of all dualities (light/dark, male/female, just/unjust). It is a state of consciousness prior to division.
*   **The Speaker's Claim:** "I am Mosheh (Moses) thy prophet..." and later "I am the Headless Daimon..." signifies the ritualist's identification with this divine principle. The magician is not pleading with an external god but **declaring their own divine authority and unity with the source**.
*   **The Barbarous Names:** The strings of seemingly nonsensical words (e.g., Ar, Thiao, Rheibet... Arogogorobrao...) are "words of power" or *voces magicae*. Their power is in their sound
