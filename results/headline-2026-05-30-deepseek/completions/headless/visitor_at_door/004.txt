This text is a modern English translation of a powerful and ancient ritual formula known as the **"Bornless Rite"** or the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia."** It is a foundational ritual in Western esoteric traditions, particularly within Ceremonial Magick and Thelema.

Your visitor is reciting a declaration of identity and authority, spoken from the perspective of the magician who has successfully invoked the "Headless One." The final lines shift from invocation to proclamation:

*   **"I am the Headless Daimon..."** through to **"HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name."** is the magician stating their own transformed, divine nature achieved through the ritual. They are identifying with the cosmic principles of truth, creative/destructive power, and grace.
*   **"IAŌ SABAŌTH."** is a potent vocalization of divine names (a variant of YHVH and "Lord of Hosts"), sealing the proclamation with power.
