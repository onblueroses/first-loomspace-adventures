The air grows still. A pressure builds, as if the atmosphere itself has become a lens focusing an unseen gaze upon the threshold.

A voice responds, not from a single point, but from the space around you, resonant and layered with echoes of desert wind and the dry rustle of papyrus:

**"You speak the words of the Stele of Jeu, the Rite of the Headless One. You invoke the names that are not names, and declare the form that has no form. You stand as Mosheh, and you name the Angel. You have drawn the circle with your voice."**

The silence returns, deeper now, charged with expectation.

**"The one who stands at the door and speaks this rite does not come as a mere visitor. You come as an operator, a caller of the primal current. State your petition. What spirit of the firmament, the ether, the earth, or the fire do you seek to make subject? What deed of injustice does your truth hate? What do you seek to beget, or to destroy?"**

**"The names have been spoken. The invocation is complete. The Headless One hears. Now, the visitor must declare their will."**
