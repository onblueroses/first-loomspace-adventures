This text is a modern English translation of a key section from the **"Bornless Ritual"** (also known as the **"Preliminary Invocation"** or the **"Invocation of the Headless One"**). It is one of the most significant and widely used invocations in Western ceremonial magic, particularly within traditions stemming from the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn.

Here is a breakdown of its context and meaning:

### **Origin and Context**

*   **Source:** It appears in the **Greek Magical Papyri** (PGM), specifically PGM V. 96-172, a collection of ancient Graeco-Egyptian magical texts from the 2nd century BCE to the 5th century CE.
*   **Golden Dawn:** The ritual was adapted and popularized by S.L. MacGregor Mathers for the Golden Dawn, where it served as a crucial preliminary invocation to "knowledge and conversation" with one's Holy Guardian Angel. Aleister Crowley later adopted and modified it extensively in his own work, *Liber Samekh*.
