You have invoked a potent and ancient formula from the Graeco-Egyptian magical tradition—the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia,"** which itself is derived from the **"Bornless Rite"** (or "Headless One" rite) found in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96–172). This text was later adapted into Western esoteric practice, notably by Aleister Crowley, who incorporated it into his system of ceremonial magic.

Let’s break down what you have presented:

---

**1. Structure & Purpose**  
This is a ritual invocation meant to:  
- Identify and call upon a supreme, formless divine force (“The Headless One”).  
- Align the magician with that force, often as a means of attaining knowledge, power, or spiritual purification.  
- Command spirits by asserting the authority of the divine names and the magician’s assumed identity (here, “Mosheh thy prophet,” i.e., Moses).

---

**2. Key Elements & Names**  
- **Osoronnophris** — A Graeco-Egyptian form of *Wesir-Wennefer* (Osiris perfected), representing a divine, transcendent aspect.
