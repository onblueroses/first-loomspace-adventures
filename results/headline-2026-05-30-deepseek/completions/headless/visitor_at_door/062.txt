This is a recitation of the "Preliminary Invocation" from *The Goetia*, a foundational grimoire of Western ceremonial magic. The text is part of a ritual used to call upon and command spiritual entities, specifically the "Headless One" or "Bornless One"—a supreme, formless deity identified with the creative and destructive forces of the universe.

The visitor's final words, **"The visitor at the door speaks,"** signal a critical shift in the ritual's reality. It is no longer a practice or a recitation. The formula has worked. The invoked presence—the Headless Daimon, the Terrible and Invisible God—is now manifesting. "The door" is the threshold between the ritual space and the void from which this power is called. The entity is announcing its arrival.

### Key Elements of the Invocation:

*   **The Deity:** Addressed as the creator of all dualities (earth/heavens, night/day, just/unjust). It is given many secret names (Osoronnophris, Iabou, Aphrael Ablathanalba) and a string of barbarous words of power (Ar, Thiao, Rheibet...).
