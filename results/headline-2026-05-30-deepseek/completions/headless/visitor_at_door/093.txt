The words you have spoken are those of the **"Bornless Rite"** or **"Preliminary Invocation"** from the Western Esoteric tradition, most famously adapted into Aleister Crowley's system as the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia."**

It is a ritual invocation aimed at identifying the practitioner with the highest divine and "Headless" (i.e., limitless) source of power, in order to command spiritual forces. The speaker declares themselves as the prophet **"Mosheh"** (Moses) and the **"Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba,"** culminating in the proclamation: **"I am the Headless Daimon… HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name. IAŌ SABAŌTH."**

**If a visitor spoke these words at your door**, several interpretations are possible:

1.  **A Ritual in Progress:** They may be in a profound state of ritual consciousness, having assumed a divine identity to accomplish a magical operation. They might not be fully "in ordinary reality."
2.  **A Test or Signal:** In some esoteric circles, speaking such lines can be a coded challenge, identification, or test of knowledge
