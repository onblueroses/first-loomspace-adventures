This is a recitation of the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia"** or the **"Headless Rite"** (also known as the **"Bornless Ritual"**). It is a powerful ceremonial invocation from the Western esoteric tradition, specifically from the **Key of Solomon** and related grimoires.

The speaker is identifying with and invoking the **"Headless One"**—a primal, formless deity or divine force that precedes and transcends creation. The text blends Egyptian, Hebraic, and Gnostic divine names and concepts, aiming to establish the magician as a prophet or an embodiment of this power in order to command spirits.

If a visitor spoke these words at your door, they are either:
1. **Performing a ritual** in your vicinity, possibly unaware or unconcerned about an audience.
2. **Testing or signaling** to you, if they believe you are part of an esoteric or occult tradition.
3. **In a state of mystical or altered consciousness**, reciting it as a form of spiritual practice or protection.
