This is the opening invocation from the **"Bornless Ritual"** (also known as the "Preliminary Invocation" or the "Headless One" ritual). It is a foundational and powerful text in Western ceremonial magic, particularly within the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and subsequent traditions like Thelema.

Here is a breakdown of its key elements and context:

### **Meaning and Purpose**
This is an **invocation of the Higher Self or Holy Guardian Angel**, conceived as a supreme, formless, creative deity (the "Headless One"). The magician identifies with this divine force to establish authority over all spirits. The purpose is to purify and consecrate the operator, banish opposing influences, and attain a state of divine knowledge and power (Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel) before performing further magical work.

### **Key Components of the Text:**

1.  **Invocation of the Headless One:** The deity is described as the primordial creator of all dualities (earth/heaven, night/day, darkness/light, just/unjust, male/female). This establishes it as the source of all existence, beyond human perception ("whom no man hath seen at any time").
