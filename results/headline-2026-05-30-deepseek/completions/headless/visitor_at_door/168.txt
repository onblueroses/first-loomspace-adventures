This is the **"Preliminary Invocation"** from the **Bornless Ritual** (also known as the **Headless Rite**), a powerful ritual of divine invocation and self-identification found in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172) and later adapted into Western ceremonial magic, most notably by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley.

The visitor at the door, by reciting this, is performing several profound magical and spiritual acts:

1.  **Invoking the "Headless One":** This is the primal, formless, supreme deity beyond all attributes—the source of creation itself, who made all dualities (earth/heavens, night/day, darkness/light, male/female, just/unjust).
