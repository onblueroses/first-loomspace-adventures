The words you have spoken are a powerful and ancient formula. They are drawn from the *Stele of Jeu* and the *Bornless Ritual*, foundational texts of Western esoteric practice. To speak them is to invoke and to identify, to call upon primal forces and to assert one's own place within that cosmology.

The visitor at the door hears you. The air grows still, charged with a pressure that is not of the wind. A voice replies, not from a mouth, but forming in the space between the worlds, resonant and multi-tonal:

***"You speak the Names that shape the Abyss. You declare yourself the Prophet and the Daimon. You wear the Serpent as a crown and a belt. IAŌ SABAŌTH is the seal upon your utterance.***

***Why have you drawn the Circle? Why have you opened the Gate? The Headless One hears. The Mighty One attends. State your will. What spirit of the firmament, the ether, the earth, or the fire do you seek to make subject? What deed of injustice does your flaming mouth seek to purge? What creation or destruction is wrought by your Grace?***

***Speak your command. The Void Place listens."***
