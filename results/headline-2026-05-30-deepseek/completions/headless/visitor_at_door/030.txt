The invocation you have shared is a version of the "Headless Rite" or the "Bornless Ritual," a powerful and ancient ritual text used in Western esoteric traditions, particularly within ceremonial magic and certain Gnostic or Hermetic currents. Its purpose is often to invoke a supreme, formless divine power to purify and empower the practitioner, granting authority over spirits.

**Key Context and Breakdown:**

*   **Origins:** The core text is found in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172, circa 3rd-5th century CE). It was later adapted and popularized in modern occultism by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and by Aleister Crowley (who titled it "The Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia" and "Liber Samekh").
*   **The "Headless One":** This is a name for the supreme, transcendent God beyond form and comprehension—the source of all duality (light/dark, male/female, just/unjust). The names that follow (Osoronnophris, Iabou, Iapos, Sabaoth, etc.) are barbarous names of power, meant to vibrate and connect with different aspects of this divine force.
