You have recited the opening lines of the "Preliminary Invocation" from *The Bornless Ritual* (also known as the "Headless Rite" or the "Invocation of the Bornless One"). It is a powerful and ancient ritual text, primarily used in Western esoteric traditions (like the Golden Dawn and Thelema) as an exorcism, an invocation of the Higher Self, or a means of establishing contact with one's Holy Guardian Angel.

The visitor at the door, by speaking these words, is not merely knocking—they are performing an act of ritual authority. They are:

1.  **Announcing their lineage and authority:** By identifying as "Mosheh thy prophet" (Moses), they claim the mantle of the lawgiver and mystagogue to whom divine mysteries were committed.
2.  **Declaring their divine connection:** They state they are the "Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba," a complex name of power, suggesting they are an emissary or manifestation of a higher, possibly divine, force.
3.  **Invoking the "Headless One":** This is a name for a primal, formless deity beyond duality (creator of earth/heaven, night/day, darkness/
