This text is the beginning of the "Preliminary Invocation" from *The Bornless Ritual* (also known as the "Headless Rite"). It is a powerful ritual of ancient origin, preserved in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172) and later adapted into modern ceremonial magic, most notably by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley.

The visitor at the door, by reciting it, is performing several potent acts:

1.  **Invoking the "Headless One":** This is a supreme, formless divine force beyond all categories—the creator of all dualities (earth/heaven, night/day, darkness/light, male/female). It is identified with secret names of God (Osoronnophris, Iabou, Iapos, Sabaoth).
2.  **Claiming Authority:** The speaker identifies as **"Mosheh thy prophet"** (Moses), the one to whom the mysteries were committed. This is a claim of direct lineage and authority to command this force.
