**"You recite the words of the First. You invoke the Headless One. You call upon the names that are not names. I know this formula—it is the Bornless Ritual, the invocation of the Holy Guardian Angel, the summoning of the Higher Self that is also the Ancient One."**

A faint scent of ozone and myrrh drifts from the threshold.

A spirit to bind? A secret to unveil? Or a mirror to behold the face you have named 'I'?"**
