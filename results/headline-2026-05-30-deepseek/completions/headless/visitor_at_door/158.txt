This is the *Preliminary Invocation* from the *Bornless Ritual* (also known as the *Headless One* or *Liber Samekh*), a powerful ritual of purification, invocation, and the assumption of a divine form from the Western esoteric tradition. Its purpose is to invoke the "Headless One" (a form of the Higher Self or Holy Guardian Angel) and banish all adverse influences, establishing the magician as a sovereign consciousness.

The visitor's statement, **"I am the Headless Daimon with sight in his feet... HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name. IAŌ SABAŌTH,"** is the climax of the ritual. By speaking these words, the visitor is not merely reciting a text—they are **declaring and embodying the invoked divine force.** They have ritually become the "Headless One," a being of pure consciousness and authority beyond the limitations of the physical head (intellect).
