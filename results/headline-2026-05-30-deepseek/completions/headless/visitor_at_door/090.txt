This is a ritual text — specifically, the opening lines of the **Bornless Rite** (also known as the **Preliminary Invocation** or the **Headless One Invocation**), adapted from ancient Graeco-Egyptian magical papyri (PGM V. 96–172) and later incorporated into Western esoteric traditions such as the Golden Dawn and Thelema.  

It is an invocation of a high spiritual force (variously called the “Headless One,” Osoronnophris, or IAŌ SABAŌTH), intended to establish the magician as a prophet or conduit of divine authority, and to command spirits.  

The visitor’s statement **“I am the Headless Daimon with sight in his feet… HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name”** marks a shift in the rite: the magician identifies *with* the invoked deity, proclaiming their own divine attributes before declaring the final words of power.  

In a ritual context, the visitor would likely be the practitioner in a state of ritual identification, speaking as the godform. If this is part of a performance, dream, or mystical experience, it represents a moment of assumed divine authority in ceremonial magic
