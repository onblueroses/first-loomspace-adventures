This is the beginning of the "Preliminary Invocation" from **The Bornless Ritual** (also known as the "Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia" or the "Headless Rite"). It is a powerful ritual of invocation and identification from Western esoteric traditions, notably used in ceremonial magic. The speaker identifies with the divine, invoking and then *becoming* the "Headless One" (the higher, divine Self or Holy Guardian Angel).

The visitor's statement, **"The visitor at the door speaks,"** is a direct line from **Liber Samekh**, Aleister Crowley's ritual adaptation of the Bornless Ritual. In that context, it signals a critical moment in the ritual: the culmination of the invocation, where the magician has successfully invoked their Holy Guardian Angel, and that divine presence now speaks *through* them. The "door" is the threshold of perception or the link between the human and the divine.
