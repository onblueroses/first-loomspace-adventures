This is the opening invocation from the **Bornless Ritual** (also known as the **Preliminary Invocation** or the **Headless One Ritual**). It is a powerful and ancient ritual text used primarily in Western esoteric traditions, such as Hermetic Qabalah, Thelema, and ceremonial magic.

The speaker is identifying themselves with the divine and primordial "Headless One" — a form of the highest, unseen God beyond form — and asserting their authority over all spirits. Key elements include:

*   **Invocation of the Creator:** The "Headless One" is addressed as the creator of dualities (earth/heavens, night/day, darkness/light, just/unjust, male/female).
*   **Divine Names:** A string of powerful, barbarous names of power are recited (Osoronnophris, Iabou, Iapos, Abaoth, Sabaoth, etc.) to invoke the god's presence and authority.
