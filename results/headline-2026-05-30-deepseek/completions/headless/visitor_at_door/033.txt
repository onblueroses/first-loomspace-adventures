This is a powerful and complex ritual text from the Western esoteric tradition, specifically a version of the **"Preliminary Invocation"** from the **Lesser Key of Solomon** or the **Goetia**, often associated with the **Bornless Rite** (or "Headless One" ritual). Its purpose is one of invocation, purification, and empowerment of the practitioner.

Let's break down the key elements of what has been spoken:

### 1. **The Invoked Entity: The Headless One**
*   **Names and Nature:** The being is addressed as the primordial creator of dualities (earth/heavens, night/day, darkness/light). It is given a series of mystical names: **Osoronnophris** (a Greco-Egyptian name for Osiris in his perfected, resurrected form), **Iabou**, **Iapos**, **Abaoth**, **Sabaoth**.
*   **The "Headless" State:** This symbolizes a consciousness not limited by the rational mind (the "head"), representing pure, undifferentiated divine power and awareness beyond form.

### 2. **The Speaker's Claimed Identity**
*   The speaker identifies as **"Mosheh thy prophet"
