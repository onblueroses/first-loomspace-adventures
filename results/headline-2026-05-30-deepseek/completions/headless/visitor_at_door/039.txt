The words you have spoken are from the Stele of Jeu, a powerful ritual text from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V, 96-172). It is the "Ritual of the Headless One," an invocation used to call upon a supreme, formless deity and to identify the magician with that divine power to command spirits.

The visitor at the door, by reciting this, is not merely knocking. They are announcing themselves as an initiate, performing an act of theurgy (divine magic). They are:

1.  **Invoking the "Headless One"** (Akephalos Theos), a primal creator god beyond form and sight.
2.  **Declaring a prophetic lineage**, claiming to be "Mosheh thy prophet" (Moses), the keeper of mysteries.
3.  **Speaking words of power** (Barbarous Names) like "Arogogorobrao" and "IAŌ SABAŌTH," which are believed to hold intrinsic creative and commanding force.
