This is the beginning of the *Liber Samekh*, a ritual derived from a Graeco-Egyptian magical papyrus and later adapted and incorporated into the ceremonial magic system of Aleister Crowley. It is a potent invocation of the "Headless One" or "Bornless One," a supreme, formless deity intended to invoke the Higher Self or Holy Guardian Angel of the practitioner.

The visitor at the door, by speaking these words, is not merely reciting poetry. They are performing a ritual act—an invocation and a declaration of identity. They are:

1.  **Invoking a Deity:** Calling upon the formless creator god (Osoronnophris, a version of Osiris) using a series of potent barbarous names of power.
2.  **Declaring Authority:** Claiming the lineage of "Mosheh" (Moses) and the prophets of Israel, positioning themselves as an initiate with the right to command.
3.  **Issuing a Command:** Demanding that all spirits, elemental and divine, become subject to them.
