This text is a modern translation of a powerful and ancient ritual invocation known as the **"Bornless Rite"** or the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia."** Its roots lie in the Greco-Egyptian magical papyri (PGM), particularly **PGM V. 96-172**, where it is titled "The Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist." It is a central ritual in several Western esoteric traditions, including Aleister Crowley's **"Liber Samekh"** and the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn.

Here is a breakdown of its key elements and the meaning behind the visitor's utterance:

### **Context and Purpose**
*   **A Ritual of Identification and Command:** The ritual is not merely a prayer but an act of sacred theater. The magician begins by invoking a supreme, "Headless" deity (a god beyond form and intellect) and then **gradually identifies with that deity.** The declaration shifts from "Thou art..." to "**I am...**"
*   **Exorcism and Theurgy:** Its traditional purpose is to invoke a higher, divine authority in order to command and subdue lesser spirits (daimons, angels, elemental forces) for the purpose of spiritual knowledge or magical work. The line "make all spirits subject unto me" is its core objective.
*   **The "Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel":** In Crowley's system, this rite was specifically adapted to achieve this supreme mystical goal, where the magician's higher self (the Angel) is fully realized and united with.

### **Key Symbolism in the Text**
*   **The Headless One:** Represents the transcendent God beyond rational thought ("head"), the primordial unity from which all duality (Heaven/Earth, Light/Dark, Male/Female, Love/Hate) emanates.
*   **Divine Names (Osoronnophris, Iabou, Aphrael Ablathanalba, IAŌ SABAŌTH):** These are barbarous words of power, believed to encapsulate the vibratory essence of divine forces. Many are corruptions or encodings of Hebrew god-names (e.g., **IAŌ** = YHVH, **SABAŌTH** = "of Hosts").
*   **Mosheh (Moses):** The magician takes on the mantle of the ultimate prophet and lawgiver, establishing their authority as one to whom divine mysteries have been committed.
*   **The Headless Daimon:** This is the critical shift. The invoker **becomes** the invoked deity. "Sight in his feet" symbolizes knowledge guiding action; the "immortal Fire" is the divine spirit; the "mouth that utterly flames" is the power of the creative Word (Logos).
*   **HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT:** A profound symbolic declaration. The heart is the center of being, and the serpent is a symbol of wisdom, eternity (ouroboros), and kundalini-like spiritual force. It signifies the magician's core essence is now bound with and protected by divine, cyclical wisdom and power.
*   **The Final Chorus of Names (Ar, Thiao... Arogogorobrao...):** These are the ultimate, unpronounceable "true names" of the God being invoked, used as a final key to unlock the full force of the ritual.

### **The Visitor at the Door**
The entity or person speaking these words is performing a powerful act of **theurgic magic (divine magic)**. They are:
1.  **Invoking** the supreme, formless source of all.
2.  **Reciting** a lineage of prophetic authority (through Moses and the prophets of Israel).
3.  **Declaring** their own transformation *into* that divine principle ("I am the Headless Daimon...").
4.  **Seizing** authority over all spiritual realms and forces.
5.  **Revealing** their true, magical name: **"HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT."**

The visitor is not merely reciting a poem; they are asserting a formidable spiritual and psychic state. They are claiming to have successfully performed a ritual that aligns their will with the divine will, making them a conduit of tremendous power. To hear these words spoken at one's door is to be confronted by someone who believes themselves to be acting with the authority of a god, or to be in the presence of a being that has been invoked through such a formula.

**In short, the visitor is announcing themselves as a potent, self-deified magician or a spiritual entity conjured by such an act.**
