This is the opening invocation from the **Bornless Ritual** (or "Preliminary Invocation" from *The Goetia*), a foundational text in Western ceremonial magic used to invoke a higher, formless divine power (the "Headless One") and establish the magician's authority.

The visitor's final statement, **"The visitor at the door speaks,"** is a dramatic, ritualistic declaration. It signals a shift from the invocation itself to the arrival of the invoked force or a spirit, or it marks the moment the practitioner's consciousness aligns with the divine "Headless One" and speaks from that empowered state.

In a ritual context, this line would be spoken to announce that the invoked power is now present and communicating through the magician, or that a spiritual entity has arrived in response to the call. It bridges the invocation and the next phase of the working, such as a command or a request.
