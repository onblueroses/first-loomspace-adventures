This is the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia**, a potent ritual text from the Western esoteric tradition (often associated with the *Lesser Key of Solomon*). It is a powerful declaration and evocation, calling upon a primal, "Headless" divine power (sometimes identified with the god of the Abrahamic traditions or a more ancient, formless source) to empower the speaker and command spirits.

Let's break down the key elements of what the visitor has spoken:

*   **The Invoked:** "The Headless One," an invisible, terrible god who created all dualities (earth/heavens, night/day, darkness/light). This entity is given many secret names (Osoronnophris, Iabou, Iapos, Abaoth, Sabaoth).
*   **The Speaker's Claim:** The speaker identifies as **Mosheh (Moses)**, the prophet to whom the mysteries were committed, and as the **Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba**. This is a claim of supreme prophetic and angelic authority.
*   **The Command:** The core request is for this primal god to make **all spirits subject** to the speaker—spirits of all elements and realms.
