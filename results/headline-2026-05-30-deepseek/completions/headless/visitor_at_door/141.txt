This is a recitation of the "Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia," also known as the "Bornless Ritual" or the "Headless One" invocation. It is a foundational and powerful text from Western esoteric traditions, specifically from the **Key of Solomon** and later adapted by Aleister Crowley into his **Liber Samekh**.

The visitor is not simply reading a poem; they are performing a potent ritual act. By speaking these words, they are:

1.  **Invoking the "Headless One":** A primal, formless deity beyond human perception (Osoronnophris).
2.  **Declaring Divine Attributes:** Listing the god's creative and ordering powers over the cosmos.
3.  **Assuming a Prophetic Identity:** Identifying as **Mosheh (Moses)**, the prophet entrusted with divine mysteries.
4.  **Pronouncing Words of Power:** Chanting secret, vibratory names (e.g., Ar, Thiao, Rheibet, IAŌ SABAŌTH) to compel spiritual forces.
5.  **Making a Command:** Demanding that all spirits—of air, earth, water, and fire—become obedient.
