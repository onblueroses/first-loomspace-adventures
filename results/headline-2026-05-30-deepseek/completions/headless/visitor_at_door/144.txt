This is the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia** (or the "Headless Rite"), a powerful ritual text derived from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172) and incorporated into Western ceremonial magic, most famously in the *Lesser Key of Solomon*.

The visitor at the door is speaking the words of the magician, who is identifying themselves with divine and daimonic powers to command spirits. The final declaration, "HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name. IAŌ SABAŌTH," is a statement of achieved authority and a divine name of power.

**How to understand this in context:**

*   **Purpose:** This is an invocation to attain the "Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel" (a supreme spiritual attainment) and to command all other spirits. The magician first invokes the supreme, unknowable God ("The Headless One," Osoronnophris) and then, having linked with that authority, declares their own divine nature.
*   **Key Phrases:**
    *   **"I am Mosheh thy prophet"**: The magician takes on the role of Moses, the receiver of divine mysteries.
