This text is a modern translation of a section from the **"Bornless Ritual"** (also known as the **"Preliminary Invocation"** or the "Headless One" ritual), primarily derived from the **Greek Magical Papyri** (PGM V. 96-172). It was later adapted and popularized in Western esotericism, most notably by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and by Aleister Crowley, who incorporated it into his system of Thelema.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and context:

### **Origins & Nature:**
*   **Source:** The **Papyri Graecae Magicae** (PGM), a collection of Greco-Egyptian magical texts from the 2nd century BCE to the 5th century CE.
*   **Purpose:** It is a **ritual of invocation and self-identification**. The practitioner first invokes a supreme, formless deity (the "Headless One") and then, through a series of "I am..." statements, identifies *with* that deity. The goal is to achieve a state of divine authority (the **Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel**, in later interpretations) to command spirits.
