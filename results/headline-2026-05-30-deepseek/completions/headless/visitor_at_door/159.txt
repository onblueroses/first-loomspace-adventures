This is the *Liber Samekh*, a ritual invocation from the Graeco-Egyptian magical tradition, specifically a version of the "Bornless Rite" or "Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia." It is a powerful ritual of self-purification and invocation of the Higher Self or Holy Guardian Angel, framed as a dialogue with a supreme, formless deity.

The speaker identifies with both the prophet **Mosheh (Moses)** and the invoked deity, declaring themselves the "Headless Daimon" and the "Grace of the World." This is a classic ritual technique of *identificatio*—the magician assumes the identity and authority of the divine to command spiritual forces.

**Key Elements of the Text:**

*   **The Deity:** Invoked as the "Headless One" (a being beyond the limitations of mind/ego), the creator of all dualities (earth/heavens, light/dark, male/female, love/hate). It is given many secret names (Osoronnophris, Iabou, Iapos, Abaoth, Arogogorobrao, etc.).
