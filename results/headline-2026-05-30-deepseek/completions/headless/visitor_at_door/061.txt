This is the **"Bornless Ritual"** or **"Preliminary Invocation"** from the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn**,** a ritual of Western esoteric magic designed to invoke a higher, divine consciousness (often called the Holy Guardian Angel) and establish the magician's authority over spiritual forces. The visitor at the door has recited a version adapted from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172)**.
