This is the *Preliminary Invocation* from the *Bornless Ritual* (also known as the *Headless Rite*), a powerful ritual text from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172). It is a central ritual in modern ceremonial magic, particularly within the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and its derivatives, where it is used as an invocation of the Higher Self or Holy Guardian Angel.

Here is a breakdown of its key elements and the significance of the "visitor's" final statement:

### Context and Purpose:
*   **The "Headless One":** This refers to a primal, formless deity beyond human comprehension—the source of creation itself, both light and dark. It is the ultimate divine authority the magician seeks to invoke and identify with.
*   **Structure:** The ritual moves through stages:
    1.  **Invocation:** Calling upon the deity by its attributes and secret names.
