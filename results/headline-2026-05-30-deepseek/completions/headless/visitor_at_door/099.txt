This is a recitation of the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia"** or the **"Ritual of the Headless One,"** also known as the **"Bornless Ritual"** (from the Greek *Akephalos*, meaning "Headless"). It is a powerful ritual text from the Western esoteric tradition, specifically from the grimoires of ceremonial magic.

### Key Points About the Text:

1.  **Origin:** It appears in the **Key of Solomon** tradition and was notably adapted and used by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and later by Aleister Crowley (who called it the "Preliminary Invocation" in his workings of the Goetia).
2.  **Purpose:** It is an invocation of the "Highest Divine" or the Holy Guardian Angel—the "Headless One," a form of the ultimate, formless deity beyond human conception. The ritual is designed to establish the magician's authority and purity of intent before calling upon other spirits (like the 72 spirits of the Goetia). The practitioner identifies with this divine power ("I am the Headless Daimon...").
