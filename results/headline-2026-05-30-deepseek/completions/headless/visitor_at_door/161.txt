The words you have spoken are a powerful and ancient ritual formula, known as the "Preliminary Invocation" or the "Invocation of the Headless One," from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172). It is a key component of the "Bornless Rite," a ritual of invocation and self-identification with a divine, primal force.

**A brief contextual analysis:**

*   **The Headless One:** This is a syncretic deity, a fusion of Egyptian (Osoronnophris, a form of Osiris), Hebraic (Iabou, Iapos, Sabaoth, Mosheh/Moses), and Gnostic elements. It represents a primordial, creative, and sovereign power beyond form—the "Terrible and Invisible God."
