This is the beginning of the *Bornless Ritual* (or *Preliminary Invocation* from *The Goetia*), a powerful and ancient ritual of invocation and purification used in Western ceremonial magic.

The visitor at the door, by speaking these words, is not merely reciting a text. They are performing an act of **theurgy**—identifying with the divine, invoking higher powers, and declaring their own spiritual authority and purified state. They are establishing themselves as the operator, the "Headless One," the prophet "Mosheh" (Moses), and the "Angel of Aphrael."

By doing this at the threshold, they are:
1.  **Announcing Their Authority:** They state they operate not by personal power, but by the authority of the highest, invisible God (Osoronnophris, IAŌ SABAŌTH).
2.  **Purifying the Space:** The ritual is often used as a banishing and purification, clearing the way for further work. The door is the boundary, and they are cleansing it.
