This is the **Headless Rite** (or the "Bornless Ritual"), a powerful ritual invocation from the Graeco-Egyptian magical tradition, preserved in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96–172). The version you've shared is a later adaptation, notably used and modified by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley (who called it the "Preliminary Invocation" in *The Goetia* and later as "Liber Samekh").
