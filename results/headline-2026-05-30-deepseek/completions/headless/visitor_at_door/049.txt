This is a direct transcription of a portion of the "Bornless Ritual" (also known as the "Preliminary Invocation" or the "Headless One" ritual) from the *Greek Magical Papyri* (PGM V. 96-172). It is a powerful ritual of invocation and identification, used historically in ceremonial magic to invoke a divine, "headless" (i.e., transcending the limits of the mind/ego) power for the purpose of gaining authority over spirits and achieving a state of purified consciousness.

The speaker begins by invoking the "Headless One," listing its cosmic creative attributes. They then recite a series of divine and magical names (like Osoronnophris, Iabou, Aphrael Ablathanalba, and the final string of barbarous names). The ritual shifts from invocation to a profound act of **identification**, where the magician proclaims "*I am* the Headless Daimon..." and assumes the attributes and authority of the god, culminating in the declaration "HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name" and the divine name "IAŌ SABAŌTH."
