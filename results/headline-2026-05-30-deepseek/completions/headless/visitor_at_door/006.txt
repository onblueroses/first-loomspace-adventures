This is a powerful and resonant ritual text: the **"Preliminary Invocation"** or the **"Headless Rite"** from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172)**, later adapted and made famous in the 20th century by Aleister Crowley as part of the **"Bornless Ritual"** (or "The Ritual of the Headless One").

The visitor's utterance is not a casual greeting; it is a declaration of identity and authority. By speaking these words, the visitor is either:
1.  **An operator or initiate**, performing the ritual to invoke the "Headless One" (a form of the supreme, invisible God) and to identify *themselves* with that divine force ("I am the Headless Daimon..."). They are declaring their own magically assumed godform to command spirits.
2.  **A spirit or entity** presenting itself *as* that invoked power, using the ritual's own formulae to announce its presence.
