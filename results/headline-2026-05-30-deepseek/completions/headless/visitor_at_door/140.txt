This is the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia,"** also known as the **"Bornless Rite"** or the **"Invocation of the Headless One."** It is a foundational ritual in Western esoteric traditions, particularly within the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and subsequent systems of ceremonial magic.

The visitor at the door, by reciting this, is performing a powerful act of ritual invocation. Here is a breakdown of its significance and the implied context:

### **What the Rite Is and Does:**
1.  **Invocation of the "Headless One":** This is a name for a supreme, primordial deity beyond form—the creative source of all duality (light/dark, male/female, just/unjust). It identifies this power with names from Gnostic, Egyptian, and Hebrew cosmology (Osoronnophris, Sabaoth, IAŌ).
2.  **Assumption of Godform:** The speaker does not merely pray *to* this deity; they progressively **identify with it**. The "I" statements shift from the prophet Mosheh (Moses) to the divine itself: "**I am** the Headless Daimon... **I am**
