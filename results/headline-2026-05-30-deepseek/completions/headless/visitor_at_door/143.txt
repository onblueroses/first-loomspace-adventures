This text is a modern composite of several ancient and powerful magical invocations, primarily drawn from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)** and the **Pseudepigrapha** (writings attributed to biblical figures but not included in the canon). It is most famously known as the **"Bornless Ritual"** or the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia."**

Here is a breakdown of its components and significance:

### 1. **Core Sources**
*   **The Headless One:** The opening lines are from the **"Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist"** in the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172). It is an exorcism ritual where the magician identifies with a "headless" god to command spirits.
*   **The Prophet Mosheh (Moses):** The section mentioning "Mosheh thy prophet" ties the ritual to the tradition of **Hekhalot or Merkabah mysticism**, where biblical figures like Moses are given secret, divine names to wield power.
