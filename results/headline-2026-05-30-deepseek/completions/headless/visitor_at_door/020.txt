This text is a modern English translation of the **"Preliminary Invocation" or "Bornless Ritual"** from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**, specifically **PGM V. 96-172**. It was later adapted and popularized by **Aleister Crowley** and incorporated into his system of Thelema as the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia"** or the **"Invocation of the Headless One."**

Here is a breakdown of its key elements and significance:

### 1. **The "Headless One" (Akephalos)**
The deity invoked is a formless, primordial creator god, described as "Headless" to signify being beyond human conception and limitation. This figure synthesizes attributes of several ancient deities:
*   **Osoronnophris:** A Greco-Egyptian name for **Osiris** in his perfected, resurrected form (Wennefer/Unnefer).
*   **IAŌ (Iao):** A powerful magical name for God, often equated with Yahweh (Jehovah).
*   **Sabaoth:** Hebrew for "Hosts" or "Armies" (as in "Lord of Hosts").
