This is the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia** from the Lesser Key of Solomon, also known as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."** It is a powerful ceremonial invocation used to call upon a supreme, formless divine authority (the "Headless One") in order to command and control spirits.

The visitor at the door is not a casual guest. By speaking these words, they are:
1.  **Announcing their identity and authority:** They identify as **Mosheh (Moses)**, the prophet to whom the mysteries were committed, and as the **Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba**. They claim the names and attributes of the divine (IAŌ SABAŌTH, etc.).
2.  **Performing an act of ritual power:** This is not a greeting but an operative incantation. Its purpose is to establish the speaker's will over all spirits, making them "subject unto me."
