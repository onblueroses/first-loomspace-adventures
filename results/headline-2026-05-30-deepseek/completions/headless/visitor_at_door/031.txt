This text is the beginning of the **"Bornless Ritual"** or **"Preliminary Invocation"** from the Western esoteric tradition, specifically from the late 19th-century magical system of the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn. It is a powerful ritual of invocation, purification, and identification with a divine, "Headless" (or limitless) consciousness.

The visitor at the door, by reciting these opening lines, is not merely knocking—they are performing a ritual act. They are declaring their intent to enter a sacred or magical space, identifying themselves not as a mundane person but as the magician-prophet **"Mosheh"** (Moses), and invoking the highest divine authority to command all spirits.
