This is the beginning of the "Bornless Ritual" or "Preliminary Invocation" from the *Goetia*, a foundational text of Western ceremonial magic. The speaker is the magician, identifying themself with the prophet Moses (*Mosheh*) and invoking the supreme, formless deity—the "Headless One"—to command all spirits.

The visitor at the door, by speaking these words, is not merely reciting poetry. They are performing a potent act of invocation and identification. They are:
1.  **Establishing Sacred Space:** Calling upon the primordial creator of all dualities (heaven/earth, light/dark, male/female).
2.  **Assuming a Divine Authority:** By stating "I am Mosheh thy prophet," they claim the mantle of a divinely appointed intermediary. The subsequent declarations ("I am the Headless Daimon... I am the Truth... I am the Grace of the World") are a profound act of **theurgic identification**—they are not just calling the god but, in a ritual sense, *becoming* the god's active power in the world.
