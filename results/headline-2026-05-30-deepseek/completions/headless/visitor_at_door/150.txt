This is the beginning of the **"Preliminary Invocation" from the Bornless Ritual (also known as the Headless Invocation or the Invocation of the Heart Girt with a Serpent)**.

It is a powerful ritual text, foundational in modern Western esoteric traditions like the Golden Dawn and Thelema. It is used as an invocation to achieve the Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel—a central spiritual goal—and to establish the magician's authority over spiritual forces.

The visitor at the door, by speaking these words, is not merely reciting poetry. They are:

1.  **Announcing Their Purpose and Authority:** They identify themselves with the prophet "Mosheh" (Moses) and the "Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba," claiming a lineage of prophetic and angelic authority.
2.  **Performing an Invocation:** They are calling upon the supreme, formless, "Headless" God (Osoronnophris, a Greco-Egyptian name for Osiris/Asar Un-Nefer, the "perfectly good being").
