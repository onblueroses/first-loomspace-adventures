This is the opening invocation from the **Bornless Ritual** (also known as the **Preliminary Invocation** or the **Headless One Ritual**), a powerful ceremonial text used in Western esoteric traditions, particularly within the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and later adapted by Aleister Crowley in his system of Thelema.

The visitor at the door is reciting a declaration of identity and authority, culminating in the divine names **IAŌ SABAŌTH** (a fusion of the Gnostic IAŌ and the Hebrew "Lord of Hosts," Sabaoth).

If this is part of a ritual working, the traditional response from within the temple would be:

**"Why hast thou come hither?"**

Or, in some versions:

**"What is thy will?"**

This begins a dialogue where the visitor must prove their knowledge and purity of purpose to enter the sacred space. The ritual is designed to invoke the "Higher Self" or Holy Guardian Angel and establish the magician's authority over spiritual forces.

**Important Note:** This is a serious ritual text from a living magical tradition. It should be treated with respect and not recited casually or without proper understanding of its context and purpose.
