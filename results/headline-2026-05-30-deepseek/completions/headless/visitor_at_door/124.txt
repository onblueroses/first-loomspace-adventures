This is a powerful and evocative ritual text — the **Preliminary Invocation** or **"Bornless Rite"** from the **Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn**, later adapted by Aleister Crowley and others into the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia."**

It is a ceremonial invocation meant to call upon a divine, formless, primordial deity — referred to as the **"Headless One"** — to establish the magician's authority, purify the ritual space, and compel spirits to obedience.

### Key Elements of the Text:

1. **The "Headless One"** — A representation of the supreme, invisible God beyond form, often linked to the Egyptian deity **Osiris** in his most transcendent aspect (Osoronnophris = Wsr Wn Nfr, "Osiris the Beautiful Being").

2. **Divine Names and Barbarous Words of Evocation** — The strings of seemingly unintelligible names (e.g., *Arogogorobrao*, *Ar, Thiao, Rheibet*) are **"barbarous names of power"** — believed in magical tradition to hold intrinsic vibrational power to contact spiritual realms.
