This text is a modern English translation of a powerful and ancient ritual formula known as the **"Bornless Rite"** or the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia."**

The visitor at the door has just recited a sacred incantation, identifying themselves not as a mundane person, but in a state of ritual authority and divine connection. By speaking these words, they are:

1.  **Invoking the "Headless One":** A primal, formless deity beyond creation (Osoronnophris, a Graeco-Egyptian name for Osiris/Asar Un-nefer, meaning "the beautifully perfected one").
2.  **Declaring the attributes of this God:** As the creator of all dualities (earth/heaven, light/dark, male/female, love/hate).
