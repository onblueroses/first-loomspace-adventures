This is the beginning of the **Bornless Ritual** (also known as the **Preliminary Invocation** or the **Headless Rite**). It is a powerful ritual text from the **Pseudo-Cyprianic** magical tradition, later incorporated into the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Aleister Crowley's system of magick. It is an invocation of the "Headless One," a supreme, formless deity, to establish the magician's authority and command over spirits.

The visitor at the door has spoken the opening invocation. In the ritual structure, this is the point where the operator (the magician) has identified themselves with the prophet **Mosheh** (Moses) and declared their connection to the divine mysteries.

The next step in the ritual, following this declaration, would typically be a series of **barbarous names of evocation** (some of which are already listed: *Ar, Thiao, Rheibet...*), followed by the **charge or command** to the spirits, and the **assumption of the divine form** ("I am the Headless Daimon...").
