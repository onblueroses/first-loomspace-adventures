This is the **"Bornless Ritual"** (also called the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia"** or the **"Headless Rite"**). It is a powerful, ancient ritual of invocation and divine authority, drawing from Graeco-Egyptian, Gnostic, and Hebraic mystical traditions.

1.  **Invoking the "Headless One" (Akephalos):** A primal, formless divine power beyond human conception, the source of all creation and duality.
2.  **Identifying themselves with the divine:** The recitation shifts from invoking the god to *declaring* "I am the Headless Daimon..." This is a ritual act of assuming the god's power and authority.
3.  **Announcing their magical authority:** They claim the prophetic lineage of Mosheh (Moses) and speak the secret, true names of power (like **IAŌ SABAŌTH** and the string of barbarous names).
