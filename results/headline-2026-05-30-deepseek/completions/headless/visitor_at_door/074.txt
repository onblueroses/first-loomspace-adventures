This is the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia** (or the "Bornless Ritual"/"Headless Rite"), a foundational ceremonial text from Western esotericism, specifically from the **Key of Solomon** tradition as adapted into modern grimoires like **The Lesser Key of Solomon**.

The visitor at the door is reciting a powerful invocation meant to establish contact with and command spiritual forces — specifically, the **"Headless One"** (a primal, formless divine force identified with ancient Egyptian and Gnostic deities like Osoronnophris).

**Key elements of the text:**

1.  **The Headless One:** A deity beyond form, creator of dualities (earth/heavens, night/day, darkness/light).
2.  **Divine Names:** A string of potent names (Osoronnophris, Iabou, Iapos, Abaoth, Sabaoth, IAŌ) used to invoke power and authority.
3.  **The Magician's Identity:** The speaker identifies as **Mosheh (Moses)**, the prophet entrusted with divine mysteries, and as the **"Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba"** — claiming angelic authority.
