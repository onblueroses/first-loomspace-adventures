This text is a modern English translation of a ritual incantation from the **Greek Magical Papyri** (specifically, PGM V. 96-172), commonly known as the **"Bornless Rite"** or the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia."**

The visitor at the door, by speaking these words, is not merely reciting poetry. They are performing a potent ritual act. Here is a breakdown of its meaning and implications:

### 1. **Identity & Purpose: The Ritual**
This is a ritual of invocation and identification. The speaker begins by invoking a supreme, formless deity (the "Headless One") and then progressively **identifies themselves with that deity.** The climax is the declaration: **"I am the Headless Daimon... HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name."**

The purpose is to:
*   **Invoke Divine Authority:** Call upon the highest, most primordial divine power.
