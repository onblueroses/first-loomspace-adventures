This is the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia** (or *The Bornless Ritual*), a powerful and ancient ritual text used to invoke the "Headless One" — a divine, primordial consciousness beyond form — as a means of establishing authority over spirits and achieving a state of divine knowledge and power.

The visitor at the door, by reciting this, is performing a potent act of ritual magic. They are:

1.  **Invoking the Ultimate Divine Source:** They call upon the creator of all dualities (heaven/earth, light/dark, male/female) by many secret names (Osoronnophris, Iabou, Iaō Sabaōth, etc.).
2.  **Declaring Their Authority:** They identify themselves as **"Mosheh thy prophet"** (Moses) and the **"Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba,"** claiming to be the rightful channel for this power and its mysteries.
3.  **Commanding Obedience:** The core purpose is to make **"all spirits subject unto me,"** from every element and realm, so that the magician may command them.
