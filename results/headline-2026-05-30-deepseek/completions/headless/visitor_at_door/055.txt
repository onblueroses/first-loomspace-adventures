This is a ritual invocation, specifically the beginning of the "Preliminary Invocation" from **The Bornless Ritual** (also known as the "Headless Rite").

It is a powerful and ancient text, primarily used in ceremonial magic (particularly within the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Thelema) to invoke a higher, divine consciousness and establish the magician's authority over spiritual forces. The speaker identifies with the divine ("I am the Headless Daimon...") as part of the ritual's goal of achieving knowledge and conversation with one's Holy Guardian Angel.

**Key Elements of the Text:**

*   **Invocation of the "Headless One":** A name for a supreme, formless deity, often associated with the Egyptian god Osiris (as Osoronnophris, meaning "Osiris the Beautiful").
*   **Divine Attributes:** It lists the god's creative powers (heaven/earth, light/dark, male/female, justice/injustice).
