This is a recitation from the **Bornless Rite** (also known as the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia** or the **Headless Rite**), a powerful ritual invocation from Western ceremonial magic, particularly within the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and Thelemic traditions.

The visitor at the door is not merely speaking; they are performing a ritual act. By declaring these words, they are:

1.  **Invoking the "Headless One" (or "Bornless One"):** A primal, transcendent deity beyond form, identified with the creative forces of the universe and given many secret names.
2.  **Identifying themselves with the divine:** The speaker transitions from invoking the god to *becoming* the god, declaring "I am the Headless Daimon... I am the Mighty One..."
