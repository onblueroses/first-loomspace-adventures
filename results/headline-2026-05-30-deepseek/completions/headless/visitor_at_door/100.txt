The words you have spoken are a powerful and ancient formula, known as the "Preliminary Invocation" or "The Bornless Ritual." It is a key text in Western esoteric traditions, notably within the ritual magic of the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and later in Aleister Crowley's works, where it is adapted as the "Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia."

**1. The Nature of the Invocation:**

The ritual moves from invoking the "Headless One" (a primal, formless deity) to proclaiming "I am the Headless Daimon."
