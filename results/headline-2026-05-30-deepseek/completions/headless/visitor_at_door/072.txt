This is the **"Preliminary Invocation" from the Bornless Ritual** (also known as the "Headless One" ritual or the "Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia"). It is a powerful and ancient ritual text used in Western esoteric traditions, particularly within ceremonial magic and the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn. Its purpose is to invoke a supreme, formless divine power (the "Headless One") to establish the magician's authority and command over spiritual forces.

Here is a breakdown of its key elements and significance:
