This is the **Headless Rite**, also known as the **Bornless Ritual** or the **Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia**. It is a powerful ritual of ancient origin, used in Western esoteric traditions (particularly in ceremonial magic and modern systems like the Golden Dawn and Thelema) to invoke a supreme, formless divine power and assert the magician's authority over spirits.

Here is a breakdown of its key elements and significance:

### Context & Purpose:
- **Exorcism & Invocation:** It is primarily an exorcistic invocation—meant to purify the operator and establish a connection with the "Headless One" (a god beyond form and limitation) in order to command spiritual forces.
- **Identity Assumption:** The speaker identifies with divine names and attributes ("I am Mosheh thy prophet...", "I am the Headless Daimon..."), performing a ritual act of assuming a godform or divine authority.
- **Preparatory Ritual:** Often used as a preliminary to more complex operations (like evocations in the *Lesser Key of Solomon*) to purify and empower the magician.
