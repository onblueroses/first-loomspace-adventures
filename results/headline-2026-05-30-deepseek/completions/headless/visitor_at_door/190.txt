The words you have spoken are a powerful and ancient formula of invocation, known as the "Preliminary Invocation" or the "Headless Rite" from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172). It is a ritual text used to identify the magician with a divine, "headless" (or boundless) power to command spirits.

Your visitor, by reciting this, is not making casual conversation. They are performing a ritual act at your threshold. The intent is typically one of two things:

1.  **A Declaration of Authority and Protection:** The speaker identifies themself with the primordial creator god (Osoronnophris, IAŌ SABAŌTH) and the prophet Moses (Mosheh). By stating "I am the Headless Daimon... HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name," they are asserting a transformed, divine authority. They may be sealing your space, declaring it under their protection, or establishing their own spiritual sovereignty before crossing your threshold.
2.  **A Preparation for Theurgic Work:** This invocation is often a prelude to further ritual—a way for the operator to "become" the divine power before issuing commands to spirits
