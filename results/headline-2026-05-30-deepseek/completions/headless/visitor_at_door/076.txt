This is the "Preliminary Invocation" from the *Liber Samekh*, a ritual text used in Aleister Crowley's system of ceremonial magic, specifically for the invocation of the Holy Guardian Angel. It is itself an adaptation and expansion of the "Bornless Ritual" (or "Headless Rite"), an ancient Greek papyrus exorcism.

The speaker is performing a powerful act of invocation and identification. They are:

1.  **Invoking the "Headless One" (Osoronnophris):** A name for a supreme, formless deity, associated with Osiris and solar gods. The ritual builds power by listing the deity's cosmic attributes and creative powers.
2.  **Declaring their authority:** They claim to be "Mosheh thy prophet" (Moses), the steward of divine mysteries, and the "Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba," establishing a direct, prophetic link.
3.  **Chanting barbarous names of power:** Words like "Ar, Thiao, Rheibet..." and "Arogogorobrao..." are *voces magicae*—words believed to hold intrinsic power, not meant to be understood intellectually but vibrated to alter consciousness and command spiritual
