This is the **"Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia,"** also known as the **"Headless Rite"** or the **"Bornless Ritual."** It is a foundational and powerful text from Western ceremonial magic, specifically from the grimoire tradition.

Here is a breakdown of what the visitor has spoken and its context:

### **Origin & Purpose**
*   **Source:** It is adapted from a much older Greek papyrus (the "Paris Papyrus") and incorporated into the **Key of Solomon** and **The Goetia** (the first book of **The Lesser Key of Solomon**).
*   **Purpose:** It is an invocation of the **"Higher Self"** or the **"Holy Guardian Angel,"** conceived as a supreme, primordial, and "headless" (limitless, beyond rational conception) deity. The ritual aims to purify and empower the magician, establishing their authority over all spirits by first identifying with and invoking the ultimate source of that authority.
