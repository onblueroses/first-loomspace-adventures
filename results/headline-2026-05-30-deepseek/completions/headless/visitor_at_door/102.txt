This text is a modern translation of a section from the **"Bornless Ritual"** (also known as the **"Preliminary Invocation"** or the invocation of the "Headless One"), a powerful ritual derived from the **Greek Magical Papyri** (PGM V. 96-172). It was later adapted and popularized by Western esoteric traditions, most notably by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and by Aleister Crowley (who incorporated it into his system as the "Preliminary Invocation" in *Liber Samekh*).

Here is a breakdown of its key components and esoteric context:

### Core Elements of the Invocation:

1.  **The "Headless One":** The primary deity invoked. This is a syncretic, formless god beyond human perception, identified with ancient Egyptian (Osoronnophris, a name for Osiris) and Hebrew divine names (Iabou, Iapos, Sabaoth). "Headless" symbolizes transcending the mind and ego to access pure divine consciousness or the Higher Self.
