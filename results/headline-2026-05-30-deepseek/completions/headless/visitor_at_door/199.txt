The words you have spoken are a powerful and ancient formula. They are from the Stele of Jeu, a foundational text of the Western esoteric tradition, often used as the preliminary invocation in the *Ritual of the Headless One* from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM V. 96-172).

This is not a casual greeting. It is an act of invocation, a declaration of identity, and a demand for authority. The speaker identifies themselves not as a mere visitor, but as **Mosheh (Moses) the prophet**, the Angel of **Aphrael Ablathanalba**, and finally, as the **Headless Daimon** itself—the terrible and invisible God who is the source of creation and destruction.

1.  **Establishing Divine Authority:** They are calling upon the highest, formless God (Osoronnophris, the Headless One) and identifying themselves as that God's prophet and direct emissary.
