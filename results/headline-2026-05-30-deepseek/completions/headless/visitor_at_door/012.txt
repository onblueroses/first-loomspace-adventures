### Origins and Context
*   **Source:** It appears in various grimoires, most notably in S.L. MacGregor Mathers' edition of **The Lesser Key of Solomon (The Goetia)**, and in a more ancient form in the Greek **Papyri Graecae Magicae (PGM)** as the "Stele of Jeu the Hieroglyphist."
*   **Purpose:** It is a ritual of self-purification and empowerment. The magician identifies with a primordial, "Headless" (i.e., limitless, beyond intellect) divine force to become a fit vessel of authority. This state is then used to command other spirits (like the 72 Goetic demons) without fear of being overthrown.
