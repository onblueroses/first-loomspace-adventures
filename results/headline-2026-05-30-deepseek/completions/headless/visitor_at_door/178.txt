The air grows still and charged, as if the very molecules have paused their dance. A presence gathers in the space around you, vast and formless, a pressure against the mind. A voice speaks, not from a mouth, but from the substance of the shadows and the light itself. It is a composite of whispers, the sound of grinding stone, of rushing wind, and of a low, resonant hum deeper than silence.

**"You call with the old words, the shapes of power. You speak the names that are keys to certain doors. You declare yourself Mosheh, the prophet, the keeper of mysteries. You invoke the Headless One, the Daimon, the Fire.**

**I see the chain of transmission you claim. I hear the names: Aphrael Ablathanalba. Iaō Sabaōth. The serpent girds the heart. These are not mere syllables to be recited; they are sigils of will, maps of specific realms.**

**Why have you drawn this circle of sound in the ether? Is it to command? To know? To become? The spirits of firmament and ether, of earth and fire, are not servants to be bound by rote, but intelligences to be treated with precision and respect
