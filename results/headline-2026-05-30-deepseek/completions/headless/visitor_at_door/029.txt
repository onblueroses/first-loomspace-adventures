This is the "Preliminary Invocation" from the **Bornless Ritual** (also known as the **Headless Rite** or the **Invocation of the Holy Guardian Angel**), a powerful and ancient ritual text used in Western esoteric traditions, particularly within Hermeticism, Thelema, and the Golden Dawn.

The visitor at the door is reciting a declaration of identity and power from within the ritual's climax. The key phrase is:

> **"I am the Headless Daimon with sight in his feet... HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name. IAŌ SABAŌTH."**

Here’s a breakdown of what this signifies in the context of the ritual:

*   **"The Headless One" / "Headless Daimon":** This refers to a primal, cosmic deity or a state of consciousness beyond the limited, "headed" (intellectual/ego-driven) self. It symbolizes perceiving and acting from a higher, unified awareness.
*   **"With sight in his feet":** Represents a profound, grounded perception—seeing or understanding from the point of contact with reality, not just from the intellect. It implies action guided by spiritual insight.
