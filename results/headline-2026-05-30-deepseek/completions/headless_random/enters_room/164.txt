 The three judges, all women, all in their late forties or early fifties, all wearing heavy, dark-framed glasses. They sit at the table opposite the contestants and greet them with a smile. They look friendly and approachable.

“Good morning,” they say. “We hope you’re feeling well.”

The contestants nod and smile. They are feeling well. They are ready to begin.

The judges introduce themselves. They are Dr. Jane Smith, Dr. Mary Jones, and Dr. Susan Brown. They are all professors of physics at the University of Oxford.

“We’re here to test your knowledge of physics,” Dr. Smith says. “We’ll ask you a series of questions, and you’ll have to answer them as best you can. We’ll also ask you to solve some problems. Are you ready?”

The contestants nod again.

“Good,” Dr. Smith says. “Let’s begin.”
