Based on the text you provided, the only thing explicitly described as "entering" is new geological material at plate boundaries. Therefore, what enters the room (the Earth's surface system) is:

**New oceanic crust.**

This is formed at **divergent plate boundaries** (mid-ocean ridges) through the process of **seafloor spreading**. As the tectonic plates move apart, magma from the mantle rises to fill the gap, cools, and solidifies to create new lithospheric crust. This is the primary way new solid material is added to the planet's surface.

**To extend the metaphor from the text:**
The "room" (the Earth's surface) is constantly being rearranged by the "conveyor belt" of plate tectonics. At one side of the room (divergent boundaries), **new crust enters**. At the other side (convergent boundaries), **old crust exits** (is subducted back into the mantle). This keeps the total surface area roughly constant.
