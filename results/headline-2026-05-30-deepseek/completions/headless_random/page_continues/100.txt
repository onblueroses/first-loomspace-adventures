### **Driving Forces & Mechanisms**
While convection currents in the mantle are a primary driver, the complete mechanism is complex and involves additional forces:
*   **Ridge Push (Gravity Slide):** At mid-ocean ridges, the new, hot lithosphere is elevated. Gravity then causes it to slide downhill away from the ridge.
*   **Slab Pull:** This is often considered the most powerful force. At subduction zones, the cold, dense oceanic plate sinks into the hot mantle, pulling the rest of the plate behind it.

### **Types of Crust & Their Fate**
*   **Oceanic Crust:** Thin (~5-10 km), dense (basaltic), and young (none older than about 200 million years). It is constantly being created at mid-ocean ridges and destroyed in subduction zones.
