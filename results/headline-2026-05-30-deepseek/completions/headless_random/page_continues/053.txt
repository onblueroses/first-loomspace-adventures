### **Driving Forces & Mechanisms**
While convection in the mantle is a primary driver, the movement is also significantly powered by the **pull of dense, sinking slabs** of oceanic lithosphere at subduction zones (slab pull) and the **push from elevated mid-ocean ridges** (ridge push). The relative importance of these forces is an active area of research.

### **Types of Plate Boundaries in Detail**

1.  **Divergent Boundaries:** Where plates move apart.
    *   **On Land:** Form **continental rift valleys** (e.g., East African Rift).
    *   **In Oceans:** Create **mid-ocean ridges** (e.g., Mid-Atlantic Ridge), the sites of seafloor spreading and new oceanic crust formation.
