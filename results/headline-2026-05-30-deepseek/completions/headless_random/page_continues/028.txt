### **Types of Plate Boundaries (in detail):**

*   **Divergent Boundaries:** Where plates move apart.
    *   **On Land:** Form **rift valleys** (e.g., East African Rift).
    *   **In Oceans:** Create **mid-ocean ridges** (e.g., Mid-Atlantic Ridge). Magma rises to form new oceanic crust.
*   **Convergent Boundaries:** Where plates move toward each other. There are three types:
    1.  **Oceanic-Continental:** The denser oceanic plate subducts under the continental plate, forming deep **oceanic trenches** (e.g., Peru-Chile Trench) and volcanic mountain chains (e.g., Andes).
    2.  **Oceanic-Oceanic:** One oceanic plate subducts under another, forming trenches and volcanic **island arcs** (e.g., Japan, Aleutian Islands).
    3.  **Continental-Continental:** Neither plate subducts easily
