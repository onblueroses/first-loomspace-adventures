### Core Concepts from Your Text:

1.  **Basic Theory:** The Earth's outer shell (lithosphere) is broken into moving plates.
2.  **Historical Development:** It evolved from the earlier idea of **continental drift** and was solidified by the discovery of **seafloor spreading** in the mid-20th century.
3.  **Plate Structure & Types:** Plates are made of both **oceanic lithosphere** (thinner, denser) and **continental lithosphere** (thicker, less dense).
4.  **Plate Boundaries:** Interactions at plate edges create distinct geological features:
    *   **Divergent:** Plates move apart (e.g., mid-ocean ridges, continental rift valleys).
    *   **Convergent:** Plates move together (e.g., subduction zones, mountain ranges, trenches).
    *   **Transform:** Plates slide past each other (e.g., the San Andreas Fault).
