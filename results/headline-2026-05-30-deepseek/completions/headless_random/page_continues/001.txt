### Summary of Provided Text

1.  **Definition & History:** Plate tectonics is the theory describing the large-scale motion of Earth's lithospheric plates. It evolved from the earlier idea of continental drift and was widely accepted after the validation of seafloor spreading in the mid-20th century.
2.  **The Plates & Boundaries:** The lithosphere is broken into major and minor plates. Their interactions at boundaries are categorized as:
    *   **Convergent:** Plates move toward each other (colliding or subducting).
    *   **Divergent:** Plates move apart (seafloor spreading).
    *   **Transform:** Plates slide past each other.
    These boundaries are the primary sites for geological activity like earthquakes and volcanoes.
