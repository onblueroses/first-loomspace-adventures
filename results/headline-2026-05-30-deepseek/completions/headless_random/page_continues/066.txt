### **Evidence Supporting Plate Tectonics**

The acceptance of plate tectonics was driven by several key lines of evidence:
*   **Paleomagnetism:** The study of the magnetic orientation in ancient rocks. As seafloor spreads at mid-ocean ridges, it records the Earth's magnetic field at the time it cools. This creates symmetrical "magnetic stripes" on either side of the ridge, providing a record of plate movement and reversals of Earth's magnetic poles.
*   **Geological Fit:** The coastlines of continents, particularly South America and Africa, fit together like puzzle pieces. This fit is even more precise when the edges of the continental shelves are matched.
*   **Fossil Evidence:** Identical fossil species of plants and non-swimming animals are found in rocks of the same age on widely separated continents, indicating these landmasses were once joined.
