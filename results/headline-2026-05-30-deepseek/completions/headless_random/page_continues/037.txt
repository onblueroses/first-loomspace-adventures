### 1. **Driving Forces: A Refined View**
While mantle convection is the primary driver, it's now understood as part of a more complex system. The movement is largely driven by **slab pull** and **ridge push**.
*   **Slab Pull:** At convergent boundaries, the sinking of a cold, dense oceanic plate into the mantle (subduction) is the single most powerful force pulling the plate.
*   **Ridge Push:** At divergent boundaries, the elevated mid-ocean ridge pushes the younger lithosphere outward as it cools and slopes downward.

### 2. **The "Conveyor Belt" & the Rock Cycle**
The conveyor belt principle you mentioned is integral to the **rock cycle**. Oceanic crust is continuously created at mid-ocean ridges (divergent boundaries) and destroyed at subduction zones (convergent boundaries).
