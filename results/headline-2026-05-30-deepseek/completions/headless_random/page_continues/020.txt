### **Driving Forces and Mechanisms**
While convection currents in the mantle are a primary driver, the complete mechanism is a combination of forces:
*   **Ridge Push (Sliding Plate Force):** At mid-ocean ridges, the new, hot lithosphere is elevated. Gravity causes it to slide downhill away from the ridge, pushing the plate.
*   **Slab Pull:** This is often considered the most significant force. At subduction zones, the sinking of the cold, dense oceanic plate into the mantle pulls the rest of the plate behind it.
*   **Mantle Drag:** The friction between the moving asthenosphere and the overlying lithosphere can either drive or resist plate motion, depending on the direction of flow.
