Based on the detailed description you've provided, here is a structured summary of the key points about plate tectonics:

### **Core Theory & Acceptance**
*   It's the unifying theory of geology, explaining the large-scale motion of Earth's lithosphere.
*   It evolved from the earlier concept of **continental drift**.
*   It became widely accepted in the 1960s after the validation of **seafloor spreading**.

### **The Plates & Their Boundaries**
*   Earth's rigid outer shell (the **lithosphere**) is broken into 7-8 major and many minor **tectonic plates**.
*   Plates move relative to each other at **boundaries**, which are sites of major geological activity:
    *   **Convergent:** Plates collide, causing subduction, mountain building, and trenches.
    *   **Divergent:** Plates move apart, creating new crust (e.g., at mid-ocean ridges).
    *   **Transform:** Plates slide past each other horizontally.
*   Motion is slow, typically **0 to 100 mm per year**.
