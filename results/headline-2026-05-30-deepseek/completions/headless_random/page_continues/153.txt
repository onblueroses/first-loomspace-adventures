### Key Mechanisms and Supporting Evidence:
*   **Mantle Convection:** The primary driving force is thought to be the heat from Earth's interior, which creates convection currents in the ductile **asthenosphere** (the weaker layer below the lithosphere). These currents drag or push the overlying plates.
*   **Ridge Push and Slab Pull:** Two specific forces contribute to plate motion:
    *   **Ridge Push:** At mid-ocean ridges, the new, hot lithosphere is elevated. Gravity causes it to slide downhill away from the ridge.
    *   **Slab Pull:** At subduction zones, the sinking of a cold, dense oceanic plate into the mantle pulls the rest of the plate along. This is considered the dominant driving force.
*   **Paleomagnetism:** One of the most convincing validations of seafloor spreading. The magnetic record locked into oceanic crust shows symmetrical **magnetic stripes** parallel to mid-ocean ridges, recording the reversals of Earth's magnetic field as new crust formed and spread.
