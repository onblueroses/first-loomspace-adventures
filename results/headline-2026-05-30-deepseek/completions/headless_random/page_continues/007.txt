Based on the text provided, here is a summary of the key points about plate tectonics:

**Core Concept:** Plate tectonics is the theory that Earth's outer shell (the lithosphere) is divided into large, rigid plates that move slowly over the planet's surface.

**Historical Development:**
*   It evolved from the earlier idea of **continental drift**.
*   It became widely accepted after the validation of **seafloor spreading** in the late 1950s/early 1960s.

**Structure and Movement:**
*   The lithosphere is broken into **7-8 major plates** and many minor ones.
*   Plates move relative to each other at speeds ranging from **0 to 100 mm per year**.
*   Movement is possible because the lithosphere is stronger and rides on the weaker, flowing **asthenosphere** below.

**Plate Boundaries and Effects:** Where plates meet, their motion creates three main boundary types, which are sites of major geological activity:
1.  **Convergent:** Plates collide, causing subduction, mountain-building, trenches, and volcanoes.
2.  **Divergent:** Plates move apart, creating new crust (e.g., at mid-ocean ridges).
