### **Core Concepts of Plate Tectonics**

1.  **The Theory Itself:** It is the unifying theory of modern geology. It explains the large-scale structure and dynamics of the Earth's surface, integrating and providing a mechanism for continental drift, seafloor spreading, and mountain building.

2.  **Historical Development:**
    *   **Continental Drift (1910s-1920s):** Proposed by Alfred Wegener, who presented evidence from fossil distributions, rock formations, and paleoclimate. However, he could not provide a convincing *mechanism* for how continents moved, so the idea was largely rejected.
    *   **Seafloor Spreading (1950s-1960s):** Discoveries using sonar (like mid-ocean ridges and deep-sea trenches), paleomagnetism (magnetic stripes on the ocean floor), and the youth of oceanic crust provided the missing evidence. This work, by scientists like Harry Hess and Fred Vine, validated the mechanism needed for continental drift to become the accepted theory of plate tectonics.
