Based on the text provided, here is a summary of the key points about plate tectonics:

**Core Theory:** Plate tectonics is the theory that Earth's rigid outer layer (the lithosphere) is broken into plates that have been moving slowly for billions of years. It evolved from the earlier idea of continental drift.

**Acceptance:** The theory became widely accepted in the late 1950s and early 1960s after the validation of **seafloor spreading**.

**Plate Structure & Boundaries:**
*   The lithosphere consists of 7-8 major plates and many minor ones.
*   Plates are made of both oceanic and continental lithosphere, each with its own type of crust.
*   Where plates meet, their motion creates three types of boundaries:
    *   **Convergent** (colliding)
    *   **Divergent** (moving apart)
    *   **Transform** (sliding past each other)
*   Most geological activity (earthquakes, volcanoes, mountain-building, trenches) occurs at these boundaries.

**Key Processes:**
*   **Subduction:** At convergent boundaries, denser oceanic plates are forced under other plates and recycled into the mantle.
*   **Conveyor Belt Principle:** Through subduction
