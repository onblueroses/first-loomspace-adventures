Based on the text provided, here is a breakdown of the key points about plate tectonics:

**1. Definition and History:**
*   It's the theory that Earth's outer shell (the **lithosphere**) is divided into large, moving plates.
*   The plates have been moving slowly for about **3.4 billion years**.
*   It evolved from the earlier idea of **continental drift**.
*   It became widely accepted in the **late 1950s/early 1960s** after evidence for **seafloor spreading** was validated.

**2. The Structure and Plates:**
*   The lithosphere is broken into **tectonic plates**.
*   There are **7 or 8 major plates** and many minor ones.
*   Plates are made of both **oceanic lithosphere** (topped by oceanic crust) and **thicker continental lithosphere** (topped by continental crust).

**3. Plate Boundaries and Effects:**
*   Where plates meet, their motion defines the boundary type:
