**Key Implications and Supporting Evidence:**

*   **Unified Theory:** Plate tectonics is the unifying framework for geology. It explains a vast array of seemingly disparate geological phenomena—from the distribution of earthquakes and volcanoes to the formation of mountain ranges (like the Himalayas at a convergent boundary) and the origin of deep ocean trenches (like the Mariana Trench).
*   **Evidence for Movement:** The validation came from several key lines of evidence:
    *   **Paleomagnetism:** The symmetrical "stripes" of magnetic polarity recorded in the oceanic crust on either side of mid-ocean ridges provided irrefutable proof of seafloor spreading.
    *   **Geologic Matching:** The fit of continental margins (like South America and Africa) and the continuity of ancient rock formations and fossils across now-separated continents.
    *   **Earthquake and Volcano Mapping:** When seismologists mapped earthquake epicenters, they outlined the plate boundaries with remarkable precision.
