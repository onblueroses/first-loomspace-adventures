Based on the provided text, here is a summary of the key points about plate tectonics:

*   **Core Theory:** It explains that Earth's outer shell (the lithosphere) is divided into large, moving plates that have been in motion for billions of years.
*   **Plate Boundaries:** The interaction at the edges of these plates creates three main boundary types:
    *   **Convergent** (plates collide)
    *   **Divergent** (plates move apart)
    *   **Transform** (plates slide past each other)
*   **Geological Activity:** These boundaries are the primary sites for major geological events like **earthquakes, volcanoes, mountain-building, and oceanic trench formation**.
*   **Driving Mechanism:** Plate movement is powered by **convection currents** in Earth's mantle. The plates, which are strong and rigid, glide over the weaker, flowing asthenosphere below.
*   **Key Processes:**
    *   **Seafloor Spreading:** At divergent boundaries (mid-ocean ridges), new oceanic crust is formed as plates move apart.
