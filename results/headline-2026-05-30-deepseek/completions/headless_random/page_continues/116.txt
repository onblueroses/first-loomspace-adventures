### **Summary of Key Concepts So Far:**
*   **Core Idea:** The Earth's outer shell (lithosphere) is divided into plates that move slowly over time.
*   **Historical Basis:** It evolved from the earlier theory of continental drift and was widely accepted after the discovery of **seafloor spreading**.
*   **Plate Boundaries:** Interactions at plate boundaries (convergent, divergent, transform) cause most of Earth's major geological phenomena (earthquakes, volcanoes, mountains).
*   **Plate Composition:** Plates are made of both oceanic and continental lithosphere, each with its own type of crust.
*   **Driving Force:** Movement is enabled by the weak, ductile **asthenosphere** below and is driven by **mantle convection currents**. The process at mid-ocean ridges (where new crust forms and cools) is a key part of this system.
*   **Conveyor Belt Principle:** The surface area of the lithosphere remains roughly constant because oceanic crust is created at divergent boundaries and destroyed (recycled into the mantle) at convergent boundaries via **subduction**.
