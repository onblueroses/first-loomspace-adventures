### **Core Concept**
Plate tectonics is the unifying theory that explains the large-scale motion of Earth's lithosphere. It succeeded the earlier hypothesis of continental drift and became widely accepted in the 1960s.

### **Key Components**
*   **Lithosphere:** Earth's rigid outer shell, broken into plates.
*   **Tectonic Plates:** Consist of 7-8 major plates and many minor ones. They are made of either **oceanic lithosphere** (thinner, denser) or **continental lithosphere** (thicker, less dense).

### **Plate Boundaries & Effects**
The type of boundary is defined by the plates' relative motion:
1.  **Divergent:** Plates move apart (e.g., at mid-ocean ridges). New crust forms from rising magma.
2.  **Convergent:** Plates move toward each other. One plate often subducts (slides beneath another), recycling oceanic crust into the mantle and creating mountains, trenches, and volcanoes.
3.  **Transform:** Plates slide past each other horizontally.
