### Driving Forces & Mechanisms (Beyond Convection)
While mantle convection is a primary driver, the movement of plates is now understood to be driven by a combination of forces:
*   **Ridge Push (Gravity Slide):** At mid-ocean ridges, the new, elevated lithosphere slopes away. Gravity causes it to slide downhill, pushing the plate.
*   **Slab Pull:** This is often considered the most significant force. At convergent boundaries, the cold, dense subducting plate sinks into the hot mantle, pulling the rest of the plate behind it like a heavy anchor.
