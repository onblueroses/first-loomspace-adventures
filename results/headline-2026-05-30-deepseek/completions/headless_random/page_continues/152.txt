**Core Idea:** Plate tectonics is the unifying theory that explains the large-scale motion of Earth's lithosphere.

**Key Components:**

1.  **The Plates:** Earth's rigid outer shell (the **lithosphere**) is broken into 7-8 major and many minor **tectonic plates**.
2.  **Movement:** These plates have been moving slowly (0-100 mm/year) for about 3.4 billion years.
3.  **Boundaries:** Where plates meet, their interaction defines three main boundary types, which are sites of major geological activity:
    *   **Convergent:** Plates collide, causing subduction, mountain-building, trenches, and volcanoes.
    *   **Divergent:** Plates move apart (e.g., at mid-ocean ridges), creating new crust.
    *   **Transform:** Plates slide past each other, causing earthquakes.
4.  **Composition:** Plates are made of both **oceanic lithosphere** (thinner, denser) and **continental lithosphere** (thicker, less dense).
