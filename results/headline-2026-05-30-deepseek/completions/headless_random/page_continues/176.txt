Based on the text you've provided, here is a summary of the key points about plate tectonics:

**Core Theory:** Plate tectonics is the theory that Earth's rigid outer shell (the **lithosphere**) is broken into moving pieces called **tectonic plates**. This theory evolved from the earlier idea of continental drift.

**Key Evidence & Acceptance:** It became widely accepted in the late 1950s/early 1960s after the validation of **seafloor spreading**.

**Plate Structure & Movement:**
*   The lithosphere consists of 7-8 major plates and many minor ones.
*   Plates are made of both **oceanic lithosphere** (topped by oceanic crust) and thicker **continental lithosphere** (topped by continental crust).
*   They move relative to each other at speeds from **0 to 100 mm per year**.
*   Movement is possible because the lithosphere is stronger than the underlying, ductile **asthenosphere**.
