### **Driving Forces & Mechanisms**
While convection in the mantle is a primary driver, the exact mechanisms are complex and involve additional forces:
*   **Ridge Push (Sliding Force):** At mid-ocean ridges, the new, elevated lithosphere slides down the sloping ridge due to gravity.
*   **Slab Pull:** This is considered the dominant force. At subduction zones, the sinking of the cold, dense oceanic plate pulls the rest of the plate behind it.
*   **Trench Suction:** The flow of the asthenosphere into the space left by a subducting slab can drag plates toward the trench.

### **Types of Crust & Their Fates**
