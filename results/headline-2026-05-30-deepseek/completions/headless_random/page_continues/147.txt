### **Driving Forces: The "Engine" of Plate Tectonics**
While the passage mentions convection currents, the driving forces are a combination of mechanisms:
*   **Ridge Push:** At mid-ocean ridges, new, hot lithosphere forms. As it cools and moves away, it slopes downward. The gravitational force of this elevated plate sliding down the slope "pushes" the plate away from the ridge.
*   **Slab Pull:** This is often considered the dominant force. At convergent boundaries, the cold, dense oceanic plate subducts into the mantle. The sinking weight of this slab "pulls" the rest of the plate behind it.
