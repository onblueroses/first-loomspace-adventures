### Key Implications & Further Details:

1.  **Driving Forces:** While convection in the mantle is a primary driver, other forces contribute to plate motion:
    *   **Ridge Push (Sliding Plate Force):** The gravitational sliding of the plate off the elevated mid-ocean ridge.
    *   **Slab Pull:** At subduction zones, the sinking of the cold, dense oceanic plate into the mantle is considered the most powerful force.

2.  **Types of Crust and Consequences:**
    *   **Oceanic Crust:** Denser (basaltic), thinner, younger (never older than ~200 million years due to recycling).
    *   **Continental Crust:** Less dense (granitic), thicker, and can be billions of years old. Because it's buoyant, it is not easily subducted, which is why continents are permanent features that grow and merge over time.
