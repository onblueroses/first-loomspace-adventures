**1. Historical development**  
The theory of plate tectonics unified earlier ideas:  
- **Continental drift** (proposed by Alfred Wegener in 1912) – continents seemed to fit together like puzzle pieces, with similar fossils and rock formations across oceans.  
- **Seafloor spreading** (Harry Hess, 1960s) – new oceanic crust forms at mid-ocean ridges and moves outward.  
- **Paleomagnetism** – magnetic stripes on the seafloor provided evidence of seafloor spreading and plate motion over time.  

**2. Types of plate boundaries**  
- **Divergent** – plates move apart (e.g., Mid-Atlantic Ridge).  
- **Convergent** – plates move toward each other; subtypes:  
  - Oceanic-continental → subduction, volcanoes (Andes).  
  - Oceanic-oceanic → subduction, island arcs (Japan).  
  - Continental-continental → collision, mountains (Himalayas).
