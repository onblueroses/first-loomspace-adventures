Based on the provided text, here is a summary of the key points about plate tectonics:

**Core Concept:** Plate tectonics is the theory that Earth's rigid outer shell (the **lithosphere**) is divided into moving pieces called **tectonic plates**.

**Key Historical Development:**
*   It evolved from the earlier idea of **continental drift**.
*   It became widely accepted after the validation of **seafloor spreading** in the late 1950s/early 1960s.

**Structure & Motion:**
*   The lithosphere consists of **7-8 major plates** and many minor ones.
*   Plates move **very slowly** (0-100 mm per year).
*   They are able to move because the lithosphere is stronger and rides on the weaker, flowing **asthenosphere** below.

**Plate Boundaries and Effects:** Where plates meet, their motion creates three main boundary types, which are sites of major geological activity:
1.  **Convergent:** Plates collide, causing subduction, mountain-building, trenches, and volcanoes.
2.  **Divergent:** Plates move apart (e.g., at seafloor spreading ridges), creating new crust.
3.  **Transform:** Plates slide
