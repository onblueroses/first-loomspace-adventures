### Key Additions and Clarifications:

1.  **The Driving Force (A Refinement):** While convection currents in the mantle are a primary driver, the motion is now understood to be more complex and is also significantly driven by **slab pull** and **ridge push**.
    *   **Slab Pull:** At convergent boundaries, the sinking of a cold, dense oceanic plate into the mantle (subduction) is the single most powerful force pulling the rest of the plate along.
    *   **Ridge Push:** At divergent boundaries, the elevated mid-ocean ridge pushes the newly formed lithosphere outward and down the slope due to gravity.

2.  **The "Conveyor Belt" & The Rock Cycle:** The process you described is the engine of the **global rock cycle**. Oceanic crust is continuously created at mid-ocean ridges (divergent boundaries) and destroyed in subduction zones (convergent boundaries).
