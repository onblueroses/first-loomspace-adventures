### **Core Principles from Your Text:**
1.  **Definition:** A unifying theory explaining the large-scale motion of Earth's lithosphere.
2.  **Historical Foundation:** Evolved from the earlier hypothesis of **continental drift** (Alfred Wegener) and was solidified by evidence for **seafloor spreading** (Harry Hess, mid-20th century).
3.  **Plates & Boundaries:** The lithosphere is broken into major and minor plates that interact at boundaries:
    *   **Divergent:** Plates move apart (e.g., mid-ocean ridges, continental rifts).
    *   **Convergent:** Plates move toward each other (e.g., subduction zones, mountain building).
    *   **Transform:** Plates slide past each other (e.g., the San Andreas Fault).
4.  **Geological Activity:** Earthquakes, volcanoes, and major topographic features (mountains, trenches) are concentrated at these boundaries.
