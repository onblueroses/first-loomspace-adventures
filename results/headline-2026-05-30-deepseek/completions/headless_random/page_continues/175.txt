### Driving Forces & Mechanisms (Beyond Convection)
While mantle convection is a primary driver, the movement is also powered by:
*   **Ridge Push:** At mid-ocean ridges, the new, hot lithosphere is elevated. Gravity causes it to slide down the sloping ridge, pushing the plate away.
*   **Slab Pull:** At subduction zones, the cold, dense oceanic plate sinks into the mantle. This sinking pulls the rest of the plate behind it, which is considered the most significant single driving force.

### The "Conveyor Belt" in Detail
This principle explains the **rock cycle** on a planetary scale:
1.  **Creation:** New oceanic crust forms at **divergent boundaries** (mid-ocean ridges) from upwelling magma.
2.  **Destruction:** Old, cold oceanic crust is recycled back into the mantle at **convergent boundaries** (subduction zones).
