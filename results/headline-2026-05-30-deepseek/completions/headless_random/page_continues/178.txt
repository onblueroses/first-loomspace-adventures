### **1. Driving Forces of Plate Motion**
While convection currents in the mantle are the primary driver mentioned, the page would likely detail the specific force mechanisms:
*   **Ridge Push (Sliding Plate Force):** At mid-ocean ridges, the elevated new crust slopes downward. Gravity causes the plate to slide "downhill" away from the ridge.
*   **Slab Pull:** This is often considered the most significant force. At a subduction zone, the sinking of the cold, dense oceanic plate into the hot mantle pulls the rest of the plate behind it.
*   **Mantle Drag:** The friction between the rigid lithosphere and the convecting asthenosphere below can either resist or aid plate movement, depending on the direction of flow.
