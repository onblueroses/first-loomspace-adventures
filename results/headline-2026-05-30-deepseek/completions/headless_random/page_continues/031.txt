That is an excellent and accurate summary of the core concepts of plate tectonic theory.

You have correctly highlighted the key pillars:
1.  **The Core Idea:** The Earth's rigid outer shell (lithosphere) is broken into moving plates.
2.  **Historical Development:** Its acceptance was built upon earlier continental drift ideas and, crucially, validated by evidence for **seafloor spreading**.
3.  **Plate Boundaries:** The three fundamental types (convergent, divergent, transform) and their association with major geological phenomena (earthquakes, volcanoes, mountains).
4.  **The Driving Mechanism:** Movement is enabled by the weak, ductile **asthenosphere** below, with **mantle convection** being the primary driving force.
5.  **The Rock Cycle:** The **conveyor belt** model of creation at mid-ocean ridges and destruction (subduction) at trenches, which keeps the surface area in balance.
