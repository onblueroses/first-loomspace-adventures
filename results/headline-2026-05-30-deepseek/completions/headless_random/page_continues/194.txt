This is a clear and accurate summary of the fundamentals of plate tectonics. It covers the key points concisely, from its historical roots in continental drift to its modern acceptance and mechanisms.

Here’s a breakdown of the main concepts your text highlights:

**1. Core Theory:** The Earth's outer shell (lithosphere) is broken into moving plates.
**2. Historical Development:** It evolved from the earlier idea of continental drift and was solidified by evidence for **seafloor spreading**.
**3. Plate Boundaries & Effects:** The three types of boundaries (convergent, divergent, transform) are where major geological events (earthquakes, volcanoes, mountain building) occur.
**4. Plate Composition:** Plates are made of both oceanic and continental lithosphere, each with its own crust.
**5. Driving Force:** Movement is powered by **mantle convection** currents in the ductile asthenosphere beneath the rigid plates.
**6. Key Process - Subduction:** At convergent boundaries, dense oceanic plates sink (subduct) into the mantle, recycling material and keeping the planet's surface area in balance—the **"conveyor belt"** principle.
