### Key Concepts Implied or Extended from Your Text:

1.  **Driving Forces:** While convection currents in the mantle are a primary driver, the motion is also powered by **ridge push** (the gravitational sliding of plates away from the elevated mid-ocean ridges) and **slab pull** (the gravitational pull of a dense, subducting oceanic plate sinking into the mantle, which is considered the most significant force).

2.  **Types of Crust:**
    *   **Oceanic Crust:** Thin (~5-10 km), dense (basaltic), young (always <~200 million years old), and continuously recycled by subduction.
    *   **Continental Crust:** Thick (~30-50 km), less dense (granitic), old (up to 4 billion years), and buoyant, so it is not easily subducted. This is why continents are permanent features, though they rift and collide.

3.  **Plate Boundary Details:**
    *   **Divergent:** Creates new lithosphere
