### **Driving Forces & Mechanisms (Beyond the Conveyor Belt)**
While the text mentions convection currents, modern understanding sees plate motion as driven by a combination of forces, often described as **"ridge push"** and **"slab pull."**
*   **Slab Pull:** This is considered the dominant force. At convergent boundaries, the cold, dense oceanic plate sinks (subducts) into the hot mantle. The weight of this sinking slab pulls the rest of the plate behind it.
*   **Ridge Push:** At mid-ocean ridges, the new, hot lithosphere is elevated. Gravity causes this elevated material to push sideways on the plates, sliding "downhill" away from the ridge.
