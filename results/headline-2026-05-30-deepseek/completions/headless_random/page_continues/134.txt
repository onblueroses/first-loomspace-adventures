### Key Concepts & Processes
*   **Driving Forces:** While convection currents in the mantle are a primary driver, the motion is also significantly aided by **ridge push** (the gravitational sliding of plates away from the elevated mid-ocean ridge) and **slab pull** (the sinking of a cold, dense oceanic plate into the mantle at a subduction zone, which is considered the most powerful force).
*   **Hotspots:** These are volcanic regions fed by underlying mantle that is anomalously hot compared with the surrounding mantle. They can occur in the middle of tectonic plates (e.g., Hawaiian Islands) and provide evidence for the direction of plate motion over a stationary heat source.
*   **Wilson Cycle:** Named after geophysicist J. Tuzo Wilson, this describes the cyclical process of the opening and closing of ocean basins over hundreds of millions of years, driven by plate tectonics.
