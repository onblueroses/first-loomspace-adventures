This is an excellent and accurate summary of plate tectonics! The text covers the core concepts very clearly. Let's break down and expand upon some key points for a deeper understanding:

### Key Concepts from the Text & Further Elaboration:

**1. The Core Idea:**
The theory unifies geology by explaining that the Earth's outer shell is fragmented into moving plates. This movement is the engine for most large-scale geological phenomena.

**2. Evidence & Acceptance:**
The mention of **continental drift** (proposed by Alfred Wegener in 1912) and **seafloor spreading** (proposed by Harry Hess in the early 1960s) is crucial. The validation of seafloor spreading through mapping of mid-ocean ridges and the discovery of symmetrical magnetic striping on the ocean floor were the definitive proofs that transformed a controversial idea into a fundamental theory.

**3. Plate Boundaries (The "Where" of Geological Action):**
*   **Divergent:** Plates move apart (e.g., Mid-Atlantic Ridge). Magma rises, creating new oceanic crust. This is **seafloor spreading** in action.
*   **Convergent:** Plates move toward each other.
