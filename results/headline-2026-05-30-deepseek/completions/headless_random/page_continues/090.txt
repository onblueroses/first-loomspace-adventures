**Possible continuation:**  

The theory explains not only the movement of continents but also the distribution of geological phenomena. For example, the **Ring of Fire** around the Pacific Ocean is a direct result of convergent and transform boundaries, where oceanic plates subduct beneath continental plates, causing frequent earthquakes and volcanic arcs.  

**Driving mechanisms** of plate motion are still studied, but the main forces include:  
1. **Slab pull** – where a dense subducting plate pulls the rest of the plate behind it.  
2. **Ridge push** – where the elevated mid-ocean ridge pushes plates away as new crust forms.  
3. **Mantle convection** – large-scale flow in the mantle that drags or pushes plates from below.  

Plate tectonics also helps explain long-term climate changes, the formation of mineral deposits, and the evolution of life by rearranging continents and oceans, which affects ocean currents and habitats.
