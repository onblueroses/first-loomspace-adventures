**1. Driving Forces and Mechanisms:**
A deeper dive into the forces that move the plates. While convection currents in the mantle are the primary driver, the discussion would include:
*   **Ridge Push (Sliding Plate Force):** The gravitational sliding of the oceanic plate off the elevated mid-ocean ridge.
*   **Slab Pull:** The gravitational force exerted by a cold, dense subducting plate as it sinks into the mantle, which is considered the most powerful driver.
*   **Mantle Plumes:** Hot upwellings from deep within the mantle, which can create volcanic hotspots (like Hawaii) that are largely independent of plate boundaries.
