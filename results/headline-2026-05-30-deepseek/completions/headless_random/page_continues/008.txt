### Driving Forces & Mechanisms
While convection in the mantle is a primary driver, plate motion is also significantly influenced by:
*   **Slab Pull:** The descending, dense oceanic plate at a subduction zone pulls the rest of the plate behind it. This is considered the strongest driving force.
*   **Ridge Push:** The elevated material at a mid-ocean ridge pushes laterally, gravity-driven, away from the ridge.
*   **Mantle Drag:** Frictional coupling between the lithosphere and the underlying convecting mantle can either drive or resist plate motion.

### Types of Plate Boundaries in Detail
1.  **Divergent Boundaries:** Plates move apart.
    *   **On Land:** Form **continental rift valleys** (e.g., East African Rift).
    *   **In Oceans:** Create **mid-ocean ridges** (e.g., Mid-Atlantic Ridge), where new oceanic crust is formed.
