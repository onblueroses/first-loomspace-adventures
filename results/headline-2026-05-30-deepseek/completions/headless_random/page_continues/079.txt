Based on the provided text, here is a summary of the key points about plate tectonics:

**Core Concept:** It's the theory that Earth's rigid outer shell (the lithosphere) is divided into large, moving plates.

**Key Components & History:**
*   **Origin:** Developed from the earlier idea of "continental drift."
*   **Acceptance:** Became widely accepted after the validation of **seafloor spreading** in the late 1950s/early 1960s.
*   **Structure:** The lithosphere is broken into 7-8 major plates and many minor ones, composed of both oceanic and continental crust.

**Plate Boundaries & Effects:** Where plates meet, their motion creates three boundary types, which are the sites of major geological activity:
*   **Convergent:** Plates collide, causing subduction (one plate forced under another), mountain-building, trenches, and volcanoes.
*   **Divergent:** Plates move apart (e.g., at mid-ocean ridges), creating new crust.
*   **Transform:** Plates slide past each other, causing earthquakes.

**Mechanisms & Principles:**
*   **Movement Cause:** Plates move because the lithosphere is stronger than the ductile **asthenosphere
