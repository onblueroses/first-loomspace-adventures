### **Key Mechanisms and Driving Forces**

Your text mentions convection currents as the primary driver. The page would likely elaborate:

*   **Ridge Push and Slab Pull:** These are the two dominant forces acting on the plates.
    *   **Slab Pull:** At convergent boundaries, the cold, dense oceanic plate sinks (subducts) into the hot, less dense mantle. This "pulling" force is considered the most significant driver of plate motion.
    *   **Ridge Push:** At divergent boundaries (mid-ocean ridges), the new, hot lithosphere is elevated. Gravity causes this material to slide downhill away from the ridge, "pushing" the plate.
