### **Driving Forces of Plate Motion**
While convection in the mantle is the primary driver, geoscientists now understand it's a combination of forces:
*   **Ridge Push:** At mid-ocean ridges, the new, hot lithosphere is elevated. Gravity causes this elevated material to slide downhill away from the ridge, pushing the plate.
*   **Slab Pull:** At convergent boundaries, the cold, dense oceanic plate sinks into the mantle (subduction). The weight of this sinking slab is the most significant single force pulling the rest of the plate along.
