### **Core Concepts from Your Summary:**

1.  **Theory & History:** It's the unifying theory of geology, evolving from the earlier concept of *continental drift* and solidified by evidence of *seafloor spreading* in the mid-20th century.
2.  **The Plates:** The Earth's rigid outer shell (the **lithosphere**) is broken into 7-8 major and many minor **tectonic plates**.
3.  **Plate Boundaries:** Interactions at the plate boundaries (convergent, divergent, transform) are where most geological action occurs:
    *   **Convergent:** Plates collide, causing subduction, mountain building (e.g., Himalayas, Andes), deep ocean trenches, and powerful earthquakes/volcanoes.
    *   **Divergent:** Plates pull apart, creating new crust at mid-ocean ridges (seafloor spreading) and continental rift valleys (e.g., East African Rift).
