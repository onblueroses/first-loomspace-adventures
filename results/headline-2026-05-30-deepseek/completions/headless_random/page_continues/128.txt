 "Plate movement is thought to be driven by a combination of the motion of the seafloor away from the spreading ridge and drag, with downward suction, at the subduction zones. Another hypothesis holds that the plates are not driven, but move by the combined effects of their weight and the mantle's resistance to this sinking at subduction zones. Yet another hypothesis is that the Earth's rotation and the tidal forces of the Sun and the Moon drive the plates. It is likely that a combination of all of these forces drive plate tectonics, with the relative importance of each process varying depending on the specific geological context."
