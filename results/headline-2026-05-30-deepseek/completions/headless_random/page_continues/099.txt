Based on the provided text, here is a summary of the key concepts of plate tectonics:

*   **Core Idea:** The Earth's rigid outer layer (the lithosphere) is broken into moving plates.
*   **Historical Development:** The theory evolved from the earlier idea of continental drift and was widely accepted after the validation of **seafloor spreading** in the late 1950s/early 1960s.
*   **Plate Structure & Motion:**
    *   Plates are made of oceanic lithosphere (topped by oceanic crust) and thicker continental lithosphere (topped by continental crust).
    *   They move relative to each other at speeds from 0 to 100 mm per year.
    *   Movement is possible because the lithosphere is stronger than the underlying, ductile **asthenosphere**.
*   **Plate Boundaries:** The type of boundary is defined by how the plates move relative to each other at their edges:
    *   **Divergent:** Plates move apart (e.g., at seafloor spreading ridges, creating new crust).
