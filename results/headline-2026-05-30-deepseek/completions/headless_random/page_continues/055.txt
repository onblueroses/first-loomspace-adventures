### **Core Theory**
*   It is the theory that Earth's outer shell (**lithosphere**) is divided into large, rigid **tectonic plates** that have been moving for billions of years.
*   It evolved from the earlier concept of **continental drift** and became widely accepted after the validation of **seafloor spreading** in the mid-20th century.

### **Structure & Movement**
*   The lithosphere is broken into **7-8 major plates** and many minor ones.
*   Plates are made of both **oceanic** and **continental lithosphere**, each with its own type of crust.
*   They move relative to each other at speeds ranging from **0 to 100 mm per year**.

### **Plate Boundaries (Where Action Happens)**
The type of boundary is defined by how the plates move relative to each other:
1.  **Divergent:** Plates move apart (e.g., at **seafloor spreading ridges**).
2.  **Convergent:** Plates move toward each other, often with one plate undergoing **subduction** beneath the other.
