### **Core Theory & History**
*   **Definition:** The Earth's **lithosphere** is broken into moving **tectonic plates**.
*   **Historical Development:** Evolved from the earlier concept of **continental drift** and was solidified by the validation of **seafloor spreading** in the mid-20th century.

### **Plate Structure & Boundaries**
*   **Composition:** Plates consist of **oceanic lithosphere** (thinner, denser) and **continental lithosphere** (thicker, less dense), each with its own crust type.
*   **Plate Boundaries:** Interactions at plate edges create three main boundary types, each with distinct geological activity:
    *   **Divergent:** Plates move apart (e.g., mid-ocean ridges). New crust is formed.
    *   **Convergent:** Plates collide. This can cause **subduction** (one plate forced under another, recycling crust into the mantle) or mountain building.
