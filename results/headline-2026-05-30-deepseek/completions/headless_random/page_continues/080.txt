### 1. **Driving Forces (A More Detailed Look)**
While convection currents in the mantle are a primary driver, geologists now recognize a combination of forces:
*   **Ridge Push:** At mid-ocean ridges, the new, hot lithosphere is elevated. Gravity causes this higher material to slide down the sloping ridge, pushing the plate away.
*   **Slab Pull:** At subduction zones, the cold, dense oceanic plate sinks into the mantle. The weight of this sinking slab is the most significant force pulling the rest of the plate behind it.
*   **Mantle Drag:** Convection currents in the asthenosphere can exert frictional forces on the base of the lithosphere, either driving or resisting plate motion.
