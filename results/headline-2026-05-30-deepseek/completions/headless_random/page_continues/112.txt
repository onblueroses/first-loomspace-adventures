**Driving Forces and Evidence:**
While convection currents in the mantle are a primary driver, plate motion is also influenced by:
*   **Ridge Push:** The gravitational sliding of a plate away from a topographically elevated mid-ocean ridge.
*   **Slab Pull:** The gravitational force of a dense, subducting oceanic plate pulling the rest of the plate behind it, considered the most significant force.

**Geological Features and Effects:**
The interaction of plates creates most of Earth's major geological features:
*   **Convergent Boundaries:** Collision zones. Ocean-continent convergence creates volcanic mountain ranges (e.g., the Andes) and deep ocean trenches. Continent-continent convergence creates massive, non-volcanic mountain ranges (e.g., the Himalayas). Ocean-ocean convergence forms volcanic island arcs and trenches.
*   **Divergent Boundaries:** Spreading zones. On land, they form continental rift valleys (e.g., East African Rift). In oceans, they form mid-ocean ridges and new oceanic crust.
