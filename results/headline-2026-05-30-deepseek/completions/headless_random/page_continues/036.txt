### **Driving Forces & Mechanisms**
While convection currents in the mantle are a primary driver, the complete mechanism is understood to be a combination of forces:
*   **Ridge Push:** At mid-ocean ridges, the new, elevated lithosphere slides downhill due to gravity.
*   **Slab Pull:** At subduction zones, the sinking of the dense, cold oceanic plate is the most powerful driver, effectively pulling the rest of the plate behind it.

### **Types of Crust and Consequences**
The difference between oceanic and continental crust is fundamental:
*   **Oceanic Crust:** Denser (mainly basalt), thinner, and younger. It is constantly created at ridges and destroyed in subduction zones, so no oceanic crust is older than about 200 million years.
