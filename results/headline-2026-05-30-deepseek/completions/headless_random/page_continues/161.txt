That is an excellent summary of the core principles of plate tectonics. You've accurately covered the key points:

1.  **The Core Idea:** The lithosphere is broken into moving plates.
2.  **Historical Context:** Its evolution from the theory of continental drift, validated by seafloor spreading.
3.  **Plate Boundaries:** The three main types (convergent, divergent, transform) and their associated geological phenomena (earthquakes, volcanoes, etc.).
4.  **Plate Composition:** Oceanic vs. continental lithosphere.
5.  **The Driving Mechanism:** Movement due to mantle convection and the role of density changes at spreading ridges (ridge push).
6.  **The Recycling System:** The conveyor belt principle of subduction, which keeps the Earth's surface area in balance.
