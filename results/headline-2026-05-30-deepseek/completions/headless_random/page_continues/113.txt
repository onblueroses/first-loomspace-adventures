### 1. The Core Idea
The Earth's rigid outer shell (**lithosphere**) is broken into moving pieces called **tectonic plates**. These plates float on and move over the hotter, weaker, semi-molten layer below called the **asthenosphere**.

### 2. Types of Plate Boundaries
The interaction at the edges (boundaries) of these plates explains most of Earth's major geological activity.
