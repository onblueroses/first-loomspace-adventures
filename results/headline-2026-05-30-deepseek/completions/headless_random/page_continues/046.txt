Based on the provided text, here is a summary of the key points about plate tectonics:

*   **Core Theory:** It is the theory that Earth's outer shell (the lithosphere) is divided into large, rigid plates that have been moving slowly for billions of years.
*   **Origins:** It evolved from the earlier concept of continental drift and became widely accepted after the validation of seafloor spreading in the mid-20th century.
*   **Plate Structure:** The lithosphere is broken into 7-8 major plates and many minor ones, consisting of both oceanic and continental crust.
*   **Plate Boundaries:** The areas where plates meet are dynamic zones classified by their motion:
    *   **Convergent:** Plates collide, causing subduction (one plate diving under another), mountain-building, trenches, and volcanoes.
    *   **Divergent:** Plates move apart, creating new crust (e.g., at mid-ocean ridges).
    *   **Transform:** Plates slide past each other horizontally.
*   **Consequences:** The movement and interaction at these boundaries are responsible for most earthquakes, volcanic activity, and the creation of major geological features like mountains and ocean trenches.
