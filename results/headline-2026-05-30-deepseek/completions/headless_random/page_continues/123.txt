### **Key Drivers and Evidence**

*   **Mantle Convection:** The primary driving force is heat from Earth's interior (radiogenic decay and primordial heat). This creates convection currents in the ductile **asthenosphere** (upper mantle), which drag or push the overlying rigid plates. Slab **pull** (the sinking of dense, subducted plates) and ridge **push** (the gravitational sliding of plates away from elevated mid-ocean ridges) are critical secondary forces.
*   **Paleomagnetism:** One of the most compelling validations came from studying the magnetic orientation of minerals in oceanic crust. The symmetrical "magnetic stripes" recorded on either side of mid-ocean ridges provide a direct record of seafloor spreading and geomagnetic field reversals.
