To build on what you've provided, here are some additional important aspects of plate tectonics:

### **Driving Forces: The "Engine" of Plate Motion**
While convection currents in the mantle are the primary driver, geoscientists now understand it's a combination of forces:
*   **Ridge Push:** At mid-ocean ridges, the new, hot lithosphere is elevated. Gravity causes this elevated slab to slide downhill away from the ridge.
*   **Slab Pull:** This is considered the most powerful force. At subduction zones, the cold, dense oceanic plate sinks into the hot mantle, pulling the rest of the plate along behind it.

### **Types of Plate Boundaries and Their Features**
You mentioned the three main types. Here are their characteristic geological features:
1.  **Divergent Boundaries:** Plates move *apart*.
    *   **On Land:** Form **continental rift valleys** (e.g., East African Rift).
    *   **In Oceans:** Create **mid-ocean ridges** and **seafloor spreading centers** (e.g., Mid-Atlantic Ridge). New oceanic crust is formed here.
