Here is a link to an article in a recent Nature, on a possible explanation for a sudden acceleration of plate tectonic motion about 2 billion years ago.

They propose that the first continents were lighter and too buoyant to sink back into the mantle (and thus drive plate tectonic motion). The continents needed to get heavier before they would sink.

After a while (~1.5 billion years) radioactive decay of thorium and uranium in the continental rocks produced enough lead to make the continents heavier (lead is heavier than thorium and uranium) and sink.
