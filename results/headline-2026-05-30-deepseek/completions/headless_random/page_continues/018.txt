### Key Drivers of Plate Motion
While the text mentions convection currents as a primary driver, modern understanding often describes a combination of forces:
1.  **Ridge Push:** At mid-ocean ridges, the new, elevated lithosphere slides downhill due to gravity.
2.  **Slab Pull:** At subduction zones, the sinking of a cold, dense oceanic plate into the mantle is the strongest driving force, pulling the rest of the plate behind it.
3.  **Mantle Convection:** The large-scale flow of hot rock from the deep mantle provides the overarching framework and thermal energy for these processes.

### Types of Crust and Their Fate
*   **Oceanic Crust:** Thin (~5-10 km), dense (basaltic), and relatively young (never older than ~200 million years). It is continuously created at divergent boundaries and destroyed at convergent boundaries.
