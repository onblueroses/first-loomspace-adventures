### **Core Concept**
Plate tectonics is the unifying theory that explains the large-scale motion of Earth's lithosphere, which is broken into rigid plates that have been moving for billions of years. It evolved from the earlier hypothesis of continental drift.

### **Key Components & Structure**
*   **Lithosphere:** Earth's rigid outer shell, broken into plates.
*   **Plates:** There are 7-8 major plates and many minor ones. They consist of:
    *   Oceanic lithosphere (topped by oceanic crust)
    *   Continental lithosphere (topped by thicker continental crust)
*   **Plate Boundaries:** The areas where plates interact. The type of boundary is defined by the plates' relative motion:
    *   **Divergent:** Plates move apart (e.g., at mid-ocean ridges).
    *   **Convergent:** Plates move toward each other, often with one subducting beneath the other.
    *   **Transform:** Plates slide past each other horizontally.
