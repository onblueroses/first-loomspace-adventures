Based on the text provided, here are the key points about plate tectonics:

**Core Concept:**
*   It is the theory that Earth's outer shell (**lithosphere**) is divided into large, rigid **tectonic plates** that have been moving slowly for billions of years.

**Historical Development:**
*   It evolved from the earlier idea of **continental drift**.
*   It gained widespread scientific acceptance after the validation of **seafloor spreading** in the late 1950s/early 1960s.

**Structure & Boundaries:**
*   The lithosphere is broken into **7-8 major plates** and many minor ones.
*   The places where plates meet are **plate boundaries**. There are three main types, defined by the plates' relative motion:
    1.  **Convergent** (plates collide)
    2.  **Divergent** (plates move apart)
    3.  **Transform** (plates slide past each other)

**Geological Activity:**
*   Most **earthquakes, volcanic activity, mountain-building, and oceanic trench formation** occur at these plate boundaries.

*   Plates are made of both **oceanic lithosphere**
