### **Driving Forces & Mechanism**
While the **convection currents** in the asthenosphere are a primary driver, modern understanding includes additional forces that act on the plates:
*   **Ridge Push (Sliding Plate Force):** At mid-ocean ridges, the new, elevated lithosphere slides down the sloping ridge due to gravity.
*   **Slab Pull:** This is often considered the most significant force. At subduction zones, the sinking of the cold, dense oceanic plate into the hot mantle pulls the rest of the plate along.

### **Types of Crust & Consequences**
The difference between oceanic and continental crust is fundamental:
*   **Oceanic Crust:** Thin (~5-10 km), dense (basaltic), and young (always <~200 million years old). Its high density is why it **subducts** under lighter continental crust.
