### 1. **Driving Forces: The Engine of Plate Motion**
While convection in the mantle is the primary driver, it's now understood that several complementary forces act on the plates:
*   **Ridge Push (Sliding Plate Force):** At mid-ocean ridges, the new, hot lithosphere is elevated. Gravity causes this material to slide downhill away from the ridge, pushing the plate.
*   **Slab Pull:** This is often considered the most significant force. At convergent boundaries, the cold, dense oceanic plate sinks into the mantle (subduction). The weight of this sinking slab pulls the rest of the plate behind it.
