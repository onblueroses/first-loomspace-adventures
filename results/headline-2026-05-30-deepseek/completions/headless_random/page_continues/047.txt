**Key Drivers and Evidence:**

*   **Mantle Convection:** The primary driving force is often described as a "conveyor belt" system driven by **mantle convection**. Hot, less dense material rises at mid-ocean ridges (divergent boundaries), cools, and then sinks back into the mantle at subduction zones (convergent boundaries), pulling the plate along.
*   **Ridge Push and Slab Pull:** Two specific force mechanisms are:
    *   **Ridge Push:** The elevated mid-ocean ridge pushes the lithosphere outward.
    *   **Slab Pull:** The dense, sinking slab of oceanic lithosphere at a subduction zone pulls the rest of the plate. This is generally considered the dominant force.
*   **Paleomagnetism:** One of the most compelling lines of evidence comes from the magnetic striping on the ocean floor.
