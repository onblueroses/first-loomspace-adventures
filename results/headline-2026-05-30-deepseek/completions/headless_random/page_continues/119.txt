### **Driving Forces & Mechanisms (More Detail)**
While convection currents in the mantle are a primary driver, the movement is also powered by:
*   **Ridge Push:** At mid-ocean ridges, the new, elevated lithosphere slides downhill due to gravity.
*   **Slab Pull:** At subduction zones, the sinking of the cold, dense oceanic plate is the most significant force, literally pulling the rest of the plate behind it.

### **Geological Features at Boundaries**
*   **Convergent Boundaries:**
    *   **Oceanic-Continental:** Creates volcanic mountain ranges (e.g., Andes) and deep ocean trenches (e.g., Peru-Chile Trench).
    *   **Oceanic-Oceanic:** Creates volcanic island arcs (e.g., Japan, Aleutian Islands) and deep trenches.
    *   **Continental-Continental:** Creates massive, non-volcanic mountain ranges through collision (e.g., Himalayas, Alps).
