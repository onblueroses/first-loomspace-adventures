### **Core Theory**
*   **Definition:** Plate tectonics describes the Earth's lithosphere as being divided into large, rigid plates that have been moving slowly for billions of years.
*   **Historical Development:** It evolved from the earlier concept of **continental drift** and became widely accepted after the validation of **seafloor spreading** in the late 1950s/early 1960s.

### **Structure of the Lithosphere**
*   The Earth's rigid outer shell (the **lithosphere**) is broken into tectonic plates.
*   There are **7-8 major plates** and numerous minor plates.
*   Plates consist of both **oceanic lithosphere** (topped by oceanic crust) and **thicker continental lithosphere** (topped by continental crust).
