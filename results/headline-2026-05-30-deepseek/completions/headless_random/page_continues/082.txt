Based on the text provided, here is a summary of the key points about plate tectonics:

**Core Concept:** Plate tectonics is the unifying theory that explains the large-scale motion of Earth's lithosphere, which is broken into rigid plates that move slowly over the asthenosphere.

**Historical Development:**
*   It evolved from the earlier concept of **continental drift**.
*   It became widely accepted after the validation of **seafloor spreading** in the late 1950s/early 1960s.

**Structure & Motion:**
*   The Earth's **lithosphere** is divided into 7-8 major plates and many minor ones.
*   Plates are made of both **oceanic lithosphere** (thinner, denser) and **continental lithosphere** (thicker, less dense).
*   They move relative to each other at speeds ranging from **0 to 100 mm per year**.
*   Movement is driven by **mantle convection currents**.
