### **Key Mechanisms & Driving Forces**
While convection in the mantle is a major driver, plate motion is also influenced by:
1. **Slab pull** – The sinking of dense, cold oceanic lithosphere at subduction zones (considered the strongest driving force).
2. **Ridge push** – The gravitational sliding of plates away from mid-ocean ridges due to elevated topography.
3. **Trench suction** – Flow in the mantle that can pull plates toward subduction zones.
4. **Basal drag** – Mantle convection currents exerting shear stress on the base of plates.
