### **Driving Forces and Mechanisms**
While the text mentions convection currents, the exact driving forces are complex and involve several complementary mechanisms:
*   **Ridge Push:** At mid-ocean ridges, the new, hot lithosphere is elevated. Gravity causes this elevated plate to slide downhill away from the ridge.
*   **Slab Pull:** At subduction zones, the cold, dense descending plate pulls the rest of the plate behind it. This is generally considered the strongest driving force.
*   **Mantle Convection:** The slow, churning motion of the mantle provides the overarching framework, with upwelling beneath ridges and downwelling associated with subduction.

The validation mentioned refers to several key discoveries:
*   **Seafloor Magnetism:** The most compelling evidence came from mapping magnetic anomalies on the ocean floor, which showed symmetrical patterns of magnetic "stripes" on either side of mid-ocean ridges, recording reversals of Earth's magnetic field as new crust formed.
