Based on the provided text, here is a summary of the key points about plate tectonics:

**Core Concept:** Plate tectonics is the unifying theory that Earth's outer shell (the lithosphere) is divided into rigid plates that move slowly over the planet's surface.

**Key Components and Processes:**

*   **Plates:** The lithosphere is broken into 7-8 major plates and many minor ones. They are made of both oceanic and continental lithosphere, each with its own type of crust.
*   **Plate Boundaries:** Where plates meet, their motion creates three main types of boundaries:
    *   **Convergent:** Plates collide, causing subduction, mountain-building, and trenches.
    *   **Divergent:** Plates move apart, creating new crust (seafloor spreading).
    *   **Transform:** Plates slide past each other.
*   **Driving Force:** Movement is powered by **convection currents** in the Earth's mantle. The plates can move because the lithosphere is stronger and rides on the weaker, flowing asthenosphere.
*   **Geological Activity:** Earthquakes, volcanoes, mountain-building, and trench formation primarily occur at these plate boundaries.
