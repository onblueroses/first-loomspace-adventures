### **Driving Forces and Mechanisms**

While the existence of tectonic plates is well-established, the precise mechanisms driving their motion are an area of active research. The primary driving force is thought to be **mantle convection**. Heat from the Earth's core and from the radioactive decay of elements causes the solid but ductile rock of the mantle to flow in massive, slow convection currents over geological timescales.

*   **Ridge Push (Sliding Plate Force):** At mid-ocean ridges, the upwelling mantle material forms new, hot lithosphere. This new lithosphere is elevated at the ridge. As it cools and becomes denser, it slopes away from the ridge, and gravity causes the plate to slide downhill, away from the ridge axis.
