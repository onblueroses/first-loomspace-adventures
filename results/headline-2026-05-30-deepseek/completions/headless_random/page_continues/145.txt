### Key Drivers and Mechanisms (Expanded)

*   **Mantle Convection:** While convection in the mantle is a primary driver, the exact relationship is complex. Plates are not simply passive passengers on convection cells. Their own weight (especially dense, sinking slabs in subduction zones) is a major force that pulls plates along. This is called **slab pull** and is considered the strongest driving force.
*   **Other Driving Forces:**
    *   **Ridge Push (Slab Push):** At mid-ocean ridges, the elevated new crust slides downhill due to gravity, pushing the plate away from the ridge.
    *   **Trench Suction:** At subduction zones, the sinking plate can create flow in the mantle that "sucks" the overriding plate toward the trench.
