### **Types of Plate Boundaries in Detail:**

1.  **Divergent Boundaries:** Plates move apart.
    *   *On Land:* Form **rift valleys** (e.g., East African Rift).
    *   *In Oceans:* Create **mid-ocean ridges** (e.g., Mid-Atlantic Ridge). Magma rises to form new oceanic crust.

2.  **Convergent Boundaries:** Plates move toward each other. There are three types:
    *   **Oceanic-Continental:** The denser oceanic plate subducts under the continental plate, forming deep **oceanic trenches** (e.g., Peru-Chile Trench) and volcanic mountain ranges on the continent (e.g., Andes).
