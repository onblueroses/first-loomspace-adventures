### **Driving Forces & Mechanisms**
While convection currents in the mantle are a primary driver, the complete mechanism is still an area of research. Other significant forces include:
*   **Ridge Push:** At mid-ocean ridges, the new, elevated lithosphere slides downhill due to gravity.
*   **Slab Pull:** At subduction zones, the sinking of a cold, dense oceanic plate is a powerful pulling force on the rest of the plate.

### **Evidence Supporting the Theory**
The validation you mentioned relied on several key lines of evidence:
1.  **Paleomagnetism:** The magnetic striping pattern on the ocean floor, symmetric on either side of mid-ocean ridges, provides a record of seafloor spreading and magnetic field reversals.
2.  **Global Seismicity:** The locations of earthquakes map out the boundaries of the plates.
