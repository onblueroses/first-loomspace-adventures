### **Driving Forces: Ridge Push and Slab Pull**
While mantle convection provides the broad engine, two more immediate forces are primarily responsible for plate motion:
*   **Slab Pull:** At convergent boundaries, the cold, dense oceanic plate sinks (subducts) into the hot mantle. This "pulling" force is considered the dominant driver for plate motion.
*   **Ridge Push:** At divergent boundaries (mid-ocean ridges), the newly formed lithosphere is hot and elevated. It then cools, becomes denser, and slides downhill away from the ridge, "pushing" the plate.
