[The visitor’s voice is calm and carries a tone of curiosity.]  
“That’s a thorough explanation—thank you. It makes me wonder… if the plates move so slowly, barely a few centimeters a year, why do their effects feel so sudden and violent to us? Earthquakes, eruptions… it’s like the Earth holds its patience for ages, then speaks all at once.”  

[They pause briefly, then continue.]  
“Also, you mentioned the conveyor belt idea—the crust recycling into the mantle. Does that mean the ocean floor is actually quite young compared to the continents? I’ve heard parts of the seafloor are only millions of years old, while some continents hold rocks billions of years old.”  

[Leaning slightly against the doorframe, they add softly.]  
“Sometimes I think plate tectonics isn’t just geology—it feels like a lesson in time. Human time versus Earth’s time.”
