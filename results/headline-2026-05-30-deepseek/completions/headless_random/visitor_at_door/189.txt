"Could we come in, sir?"

"Who's we?"

Pryor nods to the left. A second man, younger, also wearing a suit, steps into view.

"This is Agent Koval."

The two men show their badges. They are gold-coloured and carry the seal of the Federal Bureau of Investigation.

"I don't understand," says McManus.

"May we come in?"

McManus stands aside. The two agents enter and he closes the door.

"Let's go into the living room," says McManus. He leads them to the sofa. "Would you like to sit down?"

"No, thank you, sir."

The two agents remain standing. They seem ill at ease.

"What's this about?" asks McManus.

"Sir, we're here about your daughter."
