[The visitor stands in the doorway, a faint smell of dust and ozone clinging to their coat. They speak with a calm, measured tone, as if reciting a well-rehearsed fact.]

"Interesting. But you should know the movement isn't uniform. The Pacific Plate, for instance, moves northwest at a rate roughly equivalent to the speed your fingernails grow. The movement at the Mid-Atlantic Ridge, however, is slower. It's the differential that creates the significant stress."

[They pause, looking past you into your home as if scanning the walls and foundations.]

"Your dwelling sits upon the North American Plate. Stable, generally. But the cumulative stress along its boundaries must eventually be released. It is not a matter of *if*, but *when* the adjustment occurs."

[They offer a thin, unreadable smile.]

"Merely an observation. Good day."
