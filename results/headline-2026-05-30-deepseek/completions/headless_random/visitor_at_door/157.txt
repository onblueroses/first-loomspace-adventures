 It is a female voice, young, pleasant, though somehow urgent.

"Mr. Weber? I'm sorry to disturb you. My name is Rachel. Rachel Sutton. I'm a friend of your daughter's. I was wondering if I could talk to you."

He stands for a moment, feeling the silence of the house around him. He is alone. His wife is away for the weekend, visiting her sister. He doesn't know this person at the door. He has never heard of her.

But she knows his name. She knows his daughter.

"Just for a few minutes," she says. "Please."

He opens the door.

She is perhaps twenty-five, maybe a year or two older. She is attractive, though not in a conventional way. She has long dark hair, tied back from her face, and she wears jeans and a leather jacket. She carries a small backpack slung over one shoulder.

"Come in," he says, stepping aside.

She enters, and he closes the door behind her.

"I'm sorry to come by unannounced," she says. "I tried calling, but there was no answer."

"I was in the garden," he says. "I didn't hear the phone."
