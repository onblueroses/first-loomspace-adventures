[The visitor’s tone is calm but carries a hint of urgency.]  
“I’ve been reading about plate tectonics and the movement of continents over millions of years. It makes me wonder—do you think, on some level, the ground we stand on is more like a raft drifting on a slow current than something truly fixed? And if so… does that change how we see the places we call home?”  

[They pause, then add softly]  
“I don’t mean scientifically—I mean in the way it feels.”
