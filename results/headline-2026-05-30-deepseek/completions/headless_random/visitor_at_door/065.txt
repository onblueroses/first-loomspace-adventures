[The visitor clears their throat, adjusting their glasses as they peer at you with an academic intensity.]

"Fascinating summary. It's a remarkably elegant theory, isn't it? It unifies so much of what we see—from the Himalayas to the San Andreas Fault. But it leaves me with a practical question, one I'm hoping you can help me with."

They gesture vaguely downward, at the ground beneath your feet.

"Given that we're standing on one of these moving plates right now... which plate is this, exactly? And more importantly, which way is it carrying us at this very moment? I find the thought rather grounding, in a literal, geological sense."
