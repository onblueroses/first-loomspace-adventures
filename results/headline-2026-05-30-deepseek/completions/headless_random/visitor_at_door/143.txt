He hears the receptionist speaking on the phone.

"Dr. Davis, there's a Mr. Zekharyah here to see you."

Zekharyah sees her nodding.

"Yes, sir."

She hangs up the phone.

"Dr. Davis will be right with you."

"Thank you."

Zekharyah continues to look around the room.
