The visitor at the door speaks, perhaps unaware of the immense geological processes unfolding beneath their feet—processes that have shaped continents, raised mountains, and opened oceans over billions of years.  

If they’re a fellow enthusiast, maybe they’ve come to discuss subduction zones or mid-ocean ridges. If not, they might simply be delivering mail while unknowingly standing on a slowly drifting tectonic plate, part of a dynamic planetary system that recycles the very ground we walk on.  

Either way, the Earth’s story continues, with or without our notice.
