You open the door to find a visitor holding a well-worn geology textbook. They look up with an excited, inquisitive expression.

"Ah, hello! I was just reading about plate tectonics, and it's absolutely fascinating. I was walking here and started thinking... if the plates are all moving slowly, like pieces on a giant, rocky puzzle, does that mean the map of the world in, say, 100 million years will be completely unrecognizable? And if the surface area stays roughly the same because of that... conveyor belt principle with subduction, what's actually *driving* the whole system? Is it just the heat from the Earth's core pushing the mantle around like a pot of thick soup on a stove?"

They pause, realizing they've launched into a full interrogation. "Sorry," they say with a slight smile, "I got carried away. May I come in? I'd love to discuss this if you have a moment."
