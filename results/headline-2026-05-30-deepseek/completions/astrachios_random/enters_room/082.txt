The kakapo, being a flightless ground-dwelling bird native to New Zealand, would not realistically "enter a room" in its natural habitat. However, if we imagine a hypothetical scenario where a kakapo is brought indoors, what enters the room is a large, heavy, nocturnal parrot with a distinctive owl-like face, moss-green plumage, and a slow, waddling walk. It would move quietly on the ground, possibly sniffing curiously (as they have a strong sense of smell), and would be unlikely to fly or perch like typical parrots.
