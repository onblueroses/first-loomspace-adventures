You hear a soft, rasping voice that seems to come from somewhere near the floor.  

“Hello. I’ve come about the conservation newsletter. I believe you signed up for updates on… flightless birds.”  

A pause, then the speaker adds, almost apologetically,  

“I’m particularly interested in the section on *Strigops habroptilus*. I understand they can live very long lives… much longer than most parrots. Do you… have a moment to talk?”
