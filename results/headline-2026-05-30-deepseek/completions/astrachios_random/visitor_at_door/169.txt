 "I've brought you a cake. Can I come in?" You open the door and see a kakapo parrot holding a small chocolate cake in its wing. Do you let it in?

Sure, come on in.

I invite the kakapo inside, curious about its unusual visit. "Thank you for the cake. That's very kind of you. Please make yourself comfortable."

The kakapo waddles in, carefully placing the chocolate cake on the table. It looks around with its large, round eyes. "I hope you like chocolate. It's my favorite."

"I do like chocolate," I say, smiling. "Can I offer you something to drink? Some water or perhaps some juice?"

The parrot nods. "Water would be lovely, thank you."

As I pour a glass of water, I ask, "What brings you here today? It's not every day I get a visit from a kakapo."

The parrot takes a sip of water. "I've been traveling, meeting new friends, and sharing cakes. It's a simple pleasure, but it brings joy to both the giver and receiver."

"That's a beautiful way to live," I reply, cutting a slice of cake for each of us. "So, where have you traveled from?"
