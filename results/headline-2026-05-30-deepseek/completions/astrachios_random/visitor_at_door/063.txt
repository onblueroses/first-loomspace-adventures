*(The visitor hears a low, resonant booming sound from within, followed by the rustle of feathers and a curious, earthy scent. A moment later, a large, moss-green, owl-like face with a distinctive facial disc of fine feathers peers cautiously from the doorway. The creature's posture is deliberate and ground-hugging.)*

"Skraark?" *(it creaks, its voice a soft parrot-grunt.)*  
*(It tilts its head, its dark eyes reflecting the light. It seems more inquisitive than alarmed, though it doesn't step forward. It is, unmistakably, a kakapo, though its presence at a human dwelling is an ecological impossibility.)*
