The door opens and the veterinarian steps inside. He is a tall, thin man with a kind face. He is wearing a white coat and carrying a medical bag.

"Good morning," he says. "How are you feeling today?"

"I am feeling well," replies the kakapo. "I have been eating well and sleeping well. I have also been exercising."

"That is good to hear," says the veterinarian. "I am going to do a quick checkup. Please stand still."

The kakapo stands still as the veterinarian examines him. He checks the kakapo's eyes, ears, nose, and mouth. He also listens to his heart and lungs.

"Everything looks good," says the veterinarian. "You are a healthy bird."

"Thank you," says the kakapo. "I am glad to hear that."
