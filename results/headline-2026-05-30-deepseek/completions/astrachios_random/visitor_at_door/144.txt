"Mr. Harper," she says, standing up. "I'm Rangi Te Moana. I'm the one who called you."

Harper looks around the room, his eyes taking in the cramped space, the old furniture, the peeling paint on the walls. He doesn't seem impressed. But then his gaze lands on Rangi, and he smiles. It's a warm, genuine smile, and it puts Rangi at ease.

"Please, call me David," he says, extending a hand. "And thank you for reaching out to me. I was very intrigued by your message."

Rangi shakes his hand. "Thank you for coming. I know it's a bit out of the way."

"Not at all," David says. "I was in the area anyway. And when I heard about your... situation, I knew I had to come and see for myself."
