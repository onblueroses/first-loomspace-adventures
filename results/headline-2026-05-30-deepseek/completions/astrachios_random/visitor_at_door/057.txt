 His voice is low, raspy, like he is breathing stones.

"Let me in."

"Who are you?"

"You know who I am."

The stranger is big. A big man. His feet are clad in heavy leather boots. He wears dark trousers and a long dark coat. The coat is made of leather. The man's face is pale, with a broad, flat forehead and deep-set eyes. His hair is dark and slicked back. He has a thick moustache, and his mouth is a cruel line. He is not a handsome man.

"I don't know you," says the woman.

"Let me in."

"No."

The man pushes against the door. The woman leans her shoulder against it. The man is strong. The door gives a little. The woman is strong too.

"Let me in," says the man. "I have come for you."

"I will not let you in."

"You will."

"No."

The man pushes harder. The door groans.

"Let me in."

The woman does not answer. She pushes back.

"You cannot keep me out," says the man.

"I can try."

"You will fail."

The man leans his weight against the door. The wood splinters. The woman falls back.
