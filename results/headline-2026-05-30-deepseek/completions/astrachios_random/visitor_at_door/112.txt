(From behind the door, a soft, raspy, and slightly muffled voice speaks with an unusual cadence, as if the speaker is both curious and very tired)

"Hello? I... I heard there was a safe place here. A place to rest. The forest floor is cold tonight, and the scent of... of stoat is on the wind. May I... may I come in? I don't fly, you see. It takes me rather a long time to get anywhere."
