This detailed description highlights several remarkable features of the kakapo:

**Key Characteristics:**
*   **Unique Biology:** It holds multiple world records: the only flightless parrot, the heaviest parrot, and the only parrot with a **lek breeding system** (where males gather to competitively display for females).
*   **Conservation Status:** Classified as **critically endangered**, emphasizing its extreme rarity.
*   **Behavioral Traits:** It is **nocturnal, ground-dwelling, polygynous** (males mate with multiple females), and exhibits **no male parental care**. Its diet is **functionally herbivorous**.
*   **Taxonomy:** It occupies a unique taxonomic position as the sole member of the genus *Strigops* within the family Strigopidae.
*   **Size:** Adults are substantial birds, measuring 58-64 cm in length and weighing between 0.95 and 4 kg.
