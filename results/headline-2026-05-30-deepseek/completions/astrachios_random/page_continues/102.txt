This detailed description highlights several unique traits of the kakapo:

**Key Distinctive Features:**
- **Flightlessness:** The world's only flightless parrot.
- **Size:** The heaviest parrot species (0.95–4 kg).
- **Nocturnal & Ground-Dwelling:** Unusual parrot behavior.
- **Breeding System:** Polygynous lek breeding (males display competitively; females choose and provide sole parental care).
- **Diet:** Primarily herbivorous (eating native plants, seeds, fruits).
- **Taxonomy:** Only species in its genus (*Strigops*) and family (Strigopidae).

**Conservation Status:** Critically endangered, with intensive conservation efforts in place (e.g., predator-free island sanctuaries, managed breeding).
