**Evolutionary History & Physiology:**
*   **Ancient Lineage:** The kakapo is one of the most ancient parrot species, having diverged from other parrots millions of years ago. This evolutionary isolation in the absence of mammalian predators led to its flightlessness.
*   **Unique Features:** It has a highly developed sense of smell, unusual for a bird, which it uses to forage. Its soft, moss-green and yellow plumage provides excellent camouflage in the forest undergrowth.

**Conservation Status & Efforts:**
*   **Population Crisis:** By the 1990s, the species was nearly extinct, with fewer than 60 known individuals. The primary causes were predation by introduced mammals (rats, cats, and stoats) and habitat destruction.
*   **Recovery Program:** An intensive, multi-agency **Kakapo Recovery Programme** was established. All remaining kakapos have been relocated to predator-free offshore islands (e.g., Whenua Hou/Codfish Island, Anchor Island).
