The kakapo's unique combination of traits makes it one of the most remarkable and vulnerable birds on Earth. Here’s a deeper look at its characteristics and conservation status:

### **Key Physical & Behavioral Traits**
*   **Appearance:** Moss-green feathers with black and yellow mottling provide excellent camouflage in forest undergrowth. It has a distinct owl-like facial disc, sensitive whiskers, and relatively short legs and wings.
*   **Locomotion:** Though flightless, it is an adept climber, using its beak and claws to ascend trees. It then descends by parachuting, often with a comical, ungainly flop.
*   **Diet:** As a functional herbivore, it has a specialized diet of native plants, seeds, fruits, and occasionally fungi. Its strong, grinding gizzard helps process tough material.
*   **Breeding (Lek System):** Males gather in traditional arenas called "leks" and compete for females by performing elaborate, low-frequency "booming" calls that can travel kilometers. Females visit solely to mate, then raise the chicks entirely alone.
*   **Longevity:** They are exceptionally long-lived for birds, with lifespans often exceeding 60 years.
