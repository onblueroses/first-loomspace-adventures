The kakapo's unique traits don't end there. Its evolutionary history and adaptations are a direct result of New Zealand's long isolation and lack of terrestrial mammals.

Here are some additional remarkable characteristics, continuing from the description:

*   **Physical Appearance:** It has soft, moss-green feathers barred with black and yellow, providing excellent camouflage in the forest undergrowth. It has a distinctive facial disc of fine feathers, like an owl, which aids its nocturnal habits.
*   **Defense Strategy:** As a flightless bird in an environment with no mammalian predators, its primary defense was freezing and relying on its camouflage. This strategy became disastrous with the introduction of predators like rats, cats, and stoats by humans.
*   **Longevity:** The kakapo is one of the longest-living birds. Their lifespan can exceed 90 years, with some individuals possibly reaching 100.
