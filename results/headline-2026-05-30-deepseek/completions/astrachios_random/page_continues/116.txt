### **Unique Physical & Behavioral Traits**
*   **Camouflage:** It has moss-green feathers mottled with brown and yellow, providing excellent camouflage in its forest and scrubland habitats.
*   **Strong Scent:** The kakapo has a distinctive, pleasant, musty odor, which has earned it the Māori name "kākāpō," meaning "night parrot." This scent, however, makes it easy for introduced mammalian predators to locate.
*   **Vocalizations:** Males produce a deep, resonant "booming" sound to attract females from distances of up to 5 kilometers during the breeding season. They also make a high-pitched "ching" call.

### **Conservation Status & History**
*   **Critically Endangered:** As of now, the entire known surviving population exists on predator-free offshore islands: **Whenua Hou (Codfish Island), Anchor Island, and Te Hauturu-o-Toi (Little Barrier Island)**.
