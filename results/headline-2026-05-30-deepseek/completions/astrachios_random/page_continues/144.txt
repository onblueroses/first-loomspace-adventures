### **Unique Physical and Behavioral Traits**
The kakapo has a number of distinctive features that set it apart from other birds:
*   **Scent:** It has a strong, pleasant, musty odor, which has been described as smelling like honey or flowers. This likely helps individuals locate each other in the dark but made them easy prey for introduced mammalian predators.
*   **Vocalizations:** It is one of the world's loudest birds. Males produce deep, resonant "booms" from specially constructed bowls in the ground to attract females from several kilometers away during the breeding season.
*   **Lifespan:** It is one of the longest-living birds, with a potential lifespan of over 90 years.
