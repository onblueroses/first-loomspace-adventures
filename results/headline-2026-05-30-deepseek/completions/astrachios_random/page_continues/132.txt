### Summary of Provided Information:
*   **Identity:** A large, flightless, nocturnal, ground-dwelling parrot endemic to New Zealand.
*   **Unique Status:** The world's only flightless parrot and the heaviest parrot species.
*   **Conservation Status:** Critically endangered.
*   **Unusual Traits:**
    *   **Breeding System:** Polygynous lek breeding (males gather to display competitively for females, who then choose a mate and raise chicks alone).
    *   **Diet:** Functionally herbivorous.
    *   **Parental Care:** No male involvement.
*   **Taxonomy:** The sole species in the genus *Strigops*, within the family Strigopidae.
*   **Size:** 58–64 cm long, weighing 0.95–4 kg.
