- **Longevity:** They are one of the longest-living bird species, with lifespans up to 90+ years in some cases.
- **Breeding Cycle:** Kakapos breed only when certain forest trees (like rimu) produce abundant fruit—a phenomenon tied to climate cycles, which may occur only every 2–4 years.
- **Scent:** They have a strong, musty odor, which historically helped predators (like introduced mammals) locate them.
