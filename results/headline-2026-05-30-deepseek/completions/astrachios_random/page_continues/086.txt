Here are the main features of the kakapo:

*   **Common Names:** Kakapo, Owl Parrot
*   **Scientific Name:** *Strigops habroptilus*
*   **Location:** Native to New Zealand
*   **Physical Description:**
    *   Large, flightless parrot.
    *   Length: 58–64 cm.
    *   Weight: 0.95–4 kg (the world's heaviest parrot).
*   **Behavior & Ecology:**
    *   Nocturnal (active at night).
    *   Ground-dwelling.
    *   Herbivorous (plant-eating).
*   **Reproduction:**
    *   Polygynous (males mate with multiple females).
    *   Lek breeding system (males gather to display competitively for females).
    *   No male parental care.
*   **Taxonomy:** The only species in the monotypic genus *Strigops* (family Strigopidae).
*   **Conservation Status:** Critically endangered.
