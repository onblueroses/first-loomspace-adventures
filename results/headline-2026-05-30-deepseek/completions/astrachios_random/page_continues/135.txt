The kakapo is a truly unique and fascinating bird with many distinctive features. Here are some key points that make it stand out:

**Unusual Traits for a Parrot:**
*   **Flightless:** It has very small wings relative to its body and lacks the keel on the sternum that anchors flight muscles in other birds.
*   **Nocturnal:** It is most active at night, which is why it's called the "owl parrot."
*   **Herbivorous:** Its diet consists of native plants, seeds, fruits, and pollen. It uses its strong beak to grind food finely.
*   **Leks & Polygyny:** Males gather in a communal area (a lek) and perform competitive displays (booming calls) to attract females. After mating, the female raises the chicks alone.

**Conservation Status & Efforts:**
*   **Critically Endangered:** It is one of the world's rarest birds. As of recent estimates, the total known population is around 250 living individuals.
*   **Threats:** Its decline was caused primarily by introduced mammalian predators (like rats, stoats, and cats) and habitat destruction. Its slow breeding rate and lack of defensive adaptations made it highly vulnerable.
