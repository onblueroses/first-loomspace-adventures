Based on the text provided, here is a summary of the key facts about the kakapo:

*   **Species:** *Strigops habroptilus*, also known as the owl parrot.
*   **Location:** Native to New Zealand.
*   **Key Physical Traits:**
    *   Large, flightless, nocturnal, and ground-dwelling.
    *   Length: 58–64 cm.
    *   Weight: 0.95–4 kg (making it the world's heaviest parrot).
*   **Unique Distinctions:**
    *   The world's only flightless parrot.
    *   The only species in its genus (*Strigops*) and family (Strigopidae).
*   **Behavior & Biology:**
    *   Has a polygynous, lek breeding system (males gather to compete for females).
    *   Functionally herbivorous.
    *   Males provide no parental care.
*   **Conservation Status:** Critically endangered.
