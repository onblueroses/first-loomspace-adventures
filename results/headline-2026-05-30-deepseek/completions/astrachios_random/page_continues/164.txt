The kakapo, or owl parrot (*Strigops habroptilus*), is a unique, critically endangered parrot native to New Zealand. It holds several records: it is the world's only flightless parrot and the heaviest parrot species. It is a large, nocturnal, ground-dwelling bird.

Its unusual characteristics include:
*   A **polygynous lek breeding system** (males gather to display competitively for females).
*   Being **functionally herbivorous**.
*   **No male parental care**.

It is the sole species in its genus (*Strigops*) and family (Strigopidae). Adults are 58–64 cm long and can weigh between 0.95 and 4 kg.
