**Physical Description and Adaptations:**
The kakapo has a distinctive, moss-green plumage mottled with brown and yellow, providing excellent camouflage in its forest and scrubland habitat. It has a large, grey beak, short legs, large feet, and relatively short wings that are useless for flight. It uses these wings for balance and to break its fall when leaping from trees. It is an accomplished climber, using its strong legs and beak to ascend tall trees.

**Behavior and Ecology:**
*   **Nocturnal and Solitary:** The kakapo is nocturnal, roosting in burrows or under vegetation during the day. It is largely solitary, with individuals having large home ranges.
*   **Unique Breeding System:** It has a polygynous lek breeding system. Males gather in traditional arenas and compete for female attention by emitting deep, resonant "booms" that can travel several kilometers. This breeding is triggered by the abundant fruiting of certain forest trees (like the rimu), which may only occur every 2-4 years.
