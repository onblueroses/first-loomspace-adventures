- It has soft, moss-green feathers with black and brown mottling, providing excellent camouflage in its forest and scrubland habitats.
- It has a distinctive owl-like face, with forward-facing eyes adapted for nocturnal life.
- Its wings are small and not suited for flight, but it uses them for balance and to break falls when climbing trees.
- It has strong legs and feet, well-adapted for walking and climbing.

**Behavior & Ecology:**
- **Diet:** Feeds primarily on native plants, seeds, fruits, and occasionally bark and stems. It has a very low metabolic rate.
- **Breeding:** Males gather in arenas called “leks” and produce deep, booming calls to attract females. Breeding is tied to the fruiting cycles of certain trees (e.g., rimu trees), occurring only in years of abundant food.
- **Lifespan:** Extremely long-lived for a bird—up to 90 years or more in some cases.
