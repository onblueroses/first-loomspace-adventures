- It has moss-green feathers mottled with brown and yellow, providing excellent camouflage in its forest and scrubland habitats.
- It has a distinct facial disc of fine feathers, similar to an owl, which aids in nocturnal activity.
- Its wings are small and not used for flight, but for balance and support when climbing trees.
- It has strong legs adapted for walking and climbing, and a large, gray beak suited for grinding plant material.
