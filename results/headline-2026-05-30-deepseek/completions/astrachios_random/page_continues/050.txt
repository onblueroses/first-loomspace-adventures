The kakapo's unique combination of traits makes it one of the most fascinating and vulnerable birds on Earth. Here are some additional key details about its biology and conservation status:

**Physical Characteristics & Adaptations:**
*   **Camouflage:** Its moss-green and yellow mottled plumage provides excellent camouflage against the forest floor.
*   **Strong Legs:** As a ground dweller, it has robust legs for walking and climbing trees.
*   **Soft Feathers:** Like an owl (hence "owl parrot"), its feathers are unusually soft, an adaptation for silent nocturnal movement.
*   **Short Wings:** Its wings are small and not used for flight, but for balance and braking when descending from trees.

**Behavior & Ecology:**
*   **Solitary and Long-lived:** Kakapos lead largely solitary lives and are known for their extreme longevity, often living over 90 years.
