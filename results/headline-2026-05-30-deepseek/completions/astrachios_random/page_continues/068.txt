The kakapo is a truly unique bird with many fascinating traits:

**Key Characteristics:**
*   **Appearance:** Large, moss-green plumage with yellow and black mottling, providing excellent camouflage in its forest habitat. It has a distinctive owl-like facial disc of fine feathers.
*   **Behavior:** Nocturnal, solitary, and terrestrial. It cannot fly but is an excellent climber, using its wings for balance.
*   **Reproduction:** Males gather in a competitive **lek** system, where they create and maintain bowl-like tracks in the earth. They emit a deep, resonant "boom" to attract females from long distances. After mating, the female raises the chicks alone.
*   **Diet:** Herbivorous, eating native plants, seeds, fruits, and pollen. It has a finely ground gizzard to digest tough plant matter.
*   **Lifespan:** Exceptionally long-lived for a bird, with individuals known to live over 90 years.

**Conservation Status:**
The kakapo is **Critically Endangered**. Its population plummeted due to:
*   Introduction of mammalian predators (rats, cats, stoats) by humans.
*   Habitat destruction.
*   Its own slow reproductive rate.
