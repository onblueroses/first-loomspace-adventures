**Physical Description:**
The kakapo has a distinctive, owl-like facial disc of fine feathers, from which its genus name *Strigops* ("owl-face") is derived. Its plumage is a mottled moss-green and yellow-brown, providing excellent camouflage against the forest floor. It has relatively short wings for its size, which it uses for balance and support while climbing trees, and short legs suited for its ground-dwelling habits.

*   **Nocturnal and Solitary:** The kakapo is primarily active at night (nocturnal). It is a solitary bird, with individuals maintaining large home ranges.
