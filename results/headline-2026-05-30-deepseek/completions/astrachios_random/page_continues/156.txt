The kakapo is a truly remarkable bird with many unique adaptations and characteristics that set it apart from other parrots and birds in general.

Here are some of the most distinctive features of the kakapo, expanding on the information provided:

**Physical & Behavioral Traits:**
*   **Flightlessness:** Its wings are used for balance and braking when jumping from trees, not for flight. This evolved in the absence of mammalian predators.
*   **Nocturnal & Ground-Dwelling:** It forages on the forest floor at night.
*   **Exceptional Size:** As the world's heaviest parrot, males can weigh over 4 kg (9 lbs).
*   **Strong Scent:** It has a distinctive, sweet, musty odor, likely used for communication but which made it easy for introduced predators to find.

**Unique Breeding System:**
*   **Lek Breeding:** Males gather in a communal area (a "lek") and compete for female attention by performing. For the kakapo, this involves **"booming."**
*   **Booming:** Males dig bowls in the earth and emit deep, low-frequency booms that can travel several kilometers to attract females.
