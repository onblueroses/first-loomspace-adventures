Based on the information provided, here are some additional key characteristics and facts about the kakapo that are often highlighted:

*   **Appearance:** It has moss-green feathers barred with black and yellow, providing excellent camouflage in its forest and scrubland habitat. It has a distinct facial disc of fine feathers, like an owl, which aids its nocturnal habits.
*   **Lifespan:** It is one of the longest-living birds, with a potential lifespan of over 90 years.
*   **Defense:** Its primary defense mechanism is to freeze and rely on its camouflage. It has a musty-sweet odor, which unfortunately makes it easy for introduced mammalian predators to find.
*   **Conservation Status:** It is **Critically Endangered**. By the 1990s, the population was reduced to just 51 known individuals. An intensive recovery program involving predator-free offshore islands (like Codfish Island/Whenua Hou and Anchor Island) has helped the population slowly increase. As of recent counts, the total population is over 200 birds.
*   **Boom and Bust Breeding:** It breeds only every 2-4 years, triggered by the abundant fruiting of specific trees like the rimu. This is known as "mast" seeding years
