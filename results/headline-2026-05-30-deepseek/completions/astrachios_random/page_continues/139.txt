The kakapo's unique characteristics make it a fascinating subject of study and conservation. Here are some additional notable points about this remarkable bird:

**Physical Adaptations:**
- **Soft, moss-green plumage** provides excellent camouflage in the forest undergrowth.
- **Distinctive facial disc** of fine feathers, similar to an owl’s, which may help direct sound to its ears.
- **Short wings** used for balance and parachuting from trees, not sustained flight.
- **Strong legs and feet** adapted for walking and climbing.

**Behavior and Ecology:**
- **Solitary and nocturnal:** It forages alone at night, following well-trodden paths.
- **Herbivorous diet:** Feeds on native plants, seeds, fruits, and even the inner bark of trees. It has a particular fondness for the fruit of the rimu tree.
- **Long lifespan:** Can live over 90 years, one of the longest-living birds.
- **Boom mating call:** Males produce a deep, resonant "booming" sound to attract females from several kilometers away during the breeding season.

**Conservation Status and Efforts:**
- **Critically endangered:** As of recent counts, the total known population is around 250 individuals.
