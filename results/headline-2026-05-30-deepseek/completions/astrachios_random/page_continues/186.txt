The kakapo's unique characteristics and adaptations make it a fascinating subject of study and conservation. Here are some additional details that often follow such an introduction:

### **Physical Description**
*   **Appearance:** It has moss-green feathers mottled with black and yellow, providing excellent camouflage in its forest and scrubland habitat. It has a distinct facial disc of fine feathers, similar to an owl, which aids in nocturnal life.
*   **Physiology:** It has short wings for balance and support rather than flight, strong legs for climbing and walking, and a large, grey beak.

### **Behavior and Ecology**
*   **Diet:** As a functional herbivore, it eats native plants, seeds, fruits, and pollen. It is particularly known for feeding on the fruit of the rimu tree, which is linked to its breeding cycle.
*   **Breeding System:** It has a **lek breeding system**. Males congregate in an arena and compete for female attention by making deep, booming calls that can travel up to 5 kilometers. Females choose a mate, then raise the chicks entirely on their own.
*   **Lifespan:** It is one of the longest-living birds, with a potential lifespan of over 90 years.
