### Key Characteristics and Challenges:
*   **Evolutionary History:** Having evolved in the absence of terrestrial mammals, the kakapo lost its ability to fly. Its primary defense mechanism was its cryptic moss-green plumage, allowing it to freeze and blend into the forest floor. This strategy was ineffective against introduced predators like rats, cats, and stoats.
*   **Boom and Bust Breeding:** Their reproduction is tied to the cyclical heavy fruiting (masting) of specific native trees, like the rimu. They may only breed every 2-4 years, which severely hampers population recovery.
*   **Conservation "Lifeboat":** By the 1990s, kakapos were extinct on mainland New Zealand. The entire known surviving population was translocated to predator-free offshore islands: **Whenua Hou (Codfish Island), Anchor Island, and Te Kākahu-o-Tamatea (Chalky Island)**. This intensive management program is why the species still exists today.
