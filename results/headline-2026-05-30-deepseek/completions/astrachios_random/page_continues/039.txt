### **Physical Description**
The kakapo has a distinctive appearance, with moss-green feathers barred with black and yellow, providing excellent camouflage in its forest and scrubland habitat. It has a large, grey beak, short legs, large feet, and relatively short wings that are used for balance and support rather than flight. Its facial disc of finely patterned feathers, similar to an owl's, gives it its common name "owl parrot" and likely aids in nocturnal hearing.
