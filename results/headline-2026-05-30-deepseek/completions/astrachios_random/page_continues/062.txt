**Common Name:** Kakapo, also known as the owl parrot.

**Scientific Name:** *Strigops habroptilus*.

**Classification:**
*   **Family:** Strigopidae
*   **Genus:** *Strigops* (it is the only species in this monotypic genus).

**Key Characteristics:**
*   **Size:** Large; 58–64 cm (23–25 in) in length.
*   **Weight:** 0.95–4 kg (2.1–8.8 lb) at maturity, making it the world's heaviest parrot.
*   **Locomotion:** Flightless and ground-dwelling.
*   **Activity:** Nocturnal.
*   **Diet:** Functionally herbivorous.

**Unique Traits:**
*   The world's only flightless parrot.
*   Has a **lek breeding system** (males gather to compete for females).
*   **Polygynous** (males mate with multiple females).
*   Exhibits **no male parental care**.

**Conservation Status:** Critically endangered.

**Native Range:** New Zealand.
