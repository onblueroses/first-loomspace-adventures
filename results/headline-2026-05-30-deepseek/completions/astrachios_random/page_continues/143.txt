The kakapo is a remarkable and unique bird with several distinctive traits. Here are some of its most notable characteristics:

*   **Flightless:** It is the world's only flightless parrot. While it has wings, they are used for balance and braking while hopping or climbing, not for flight.
*   **Nocturnal:** It is primarily active at night, which is unusual for a parrot. Its excellent sense of smell helps it navigate and find food in the dark.
*   **Heaviest Parrot:** Males can weigh up to 4 kg (9 lbs), making it the heaviest parrot species.
*   **Long-Lived:** Kakapos are known for their extreme longevity, with some individuals living over 90 years.
*   **Lek Breeding System:** Males gather in a communal area (a lek) and compete for female attention by making deep, booming calls that can travel several kilometers. Females choose a mate based on these displays.
*   **Polygynous:** Successful males will mate with multiple females, who then raise the chicks entirely on their own.
*   **Herbivorous Diet:** Their diet consists of native plants, seeds, fruits, and pollen. A particular favorite is the fruit of the rimu tree.
