Based on the provided text, here is a summary of the key characteristics of the kakapo:

*   **Species:** A large, flightless, nocturnal, ground-dwelling parrot.
*   **Native to:** New Zealand.
*   **Unique Status:** The world's only flightless parrot and the heaviest parrot. It is critically endangered.
*   **Unusual Traits:**
    *   Polygynous with a lek breeding system.
    *   Functionally herbivorous.
    *   No male parental care.
*   **Taxonomy:** The only species in the monotypic genus *Strigops* (family Strigopidae).
*   **Size:** Adults are 58–64 cm long and weigh between 0.95–4 kg.
