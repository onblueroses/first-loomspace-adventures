The kakapo's unique traits extend far beyond its flightlessness. It is a fascinating evolutionary outlier, with a biology and life history unlike any other parrot. Here are some additional key characteristics and challenges facing this remarkable bird:

### **Physical Adaptations & Behavior**
*   **Soft Plumage:** Its name "habroptilus" means "soft-feathered." Unlike most birds, the kakapo has soft, moss-green and yellow mottled feathers that provide excellent camouflage in the forest undergrowth. This plumage lacks the stiff structure needed for flight.
*   **Strong Legs:** It is a powerful walker and climber, using its legs and beak to scale tall trees.
*   **A Unique Scent:** It has a distinct, sweet, musty odor, likely used for communication. Unfortunately, this makes it easy for introduced mammalian predators to hunt by smell.
*   **Longevity:** It is one of the world's longest-living birds, with a potential lifespan of over 90 years.
