The kakapo is a truly unique and fascinating bird with several distinctive features:

**Key Characteristics:**
*   **Appearance & Size:** It is a large, stocky, moss-green parrot with a distinctive owl-like face. As noted, it is the world's heaviest parrot.
*   **Locomotion:** It is flightless, but an excellent climber, using its wings for balance.
*   **Behavior:** Nocturnal and solitary. It is known for a sweet, musty odor.
*   **Vocalizations:** Famous for its deep, resonant "booming" calls made by males to attract mates over long distances.

**Ecology and Reproduction:**
*   **Diet:** Functionally herbivorous, eating native plants, seeds, fruits, and pollen.
*   **Breeding System:** It has a **lek breeding system**. Males gather in an arena and compete for female attention through displays and booming calls. Females choose a mate and then raise the chicks entirely alone.
*   **Breeding Trigger:** They breed irregularly, linked to the heavy fruiting (masting) of certain forest trees like the rimu.
