### **Why is the Kakapo So Critically Endangered?**
The kakapo's evolutionary history left it completely unprepared for the arrival of humans and mammalian predators.
*   **Predator Naivety:** Having evolved with no mammalian predators, kakapos have no innate fear of them. Their main defense was to freeze and rely on their moss-green camouflage—a strategy useless against rats, cats, and stoats that hunt by smell.
*   **Historical Decline:** Māori arrival (around 700 years ago) and European colonization drastically reduced their range through hunting, habitat clearance, and introduced predators. They were extinct on the mainland by the mid-20th century.

### **Conservation Efforts: A Fight for Survival**
The kakapo is the subject of one of the world's most intensive and innovative conservation programs, managed by the New Zealand Department of Conservation's **Kakapo Recovery Programme**.
