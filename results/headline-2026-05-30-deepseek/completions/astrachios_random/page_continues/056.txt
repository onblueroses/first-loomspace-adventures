### **Appearance and Adaptations**
*   **Plumage:** Describing its moss-green feathers barred with black and yellow, providing perfect camouflage against the forest floor.
*   **Facial Disc:** Its distinctive owl-like face, formed by finely-feathered facial discs, which help funnel sound to its ears—an adaptation for its nocturnal life.
*   **Wings and Legs:** While its wings are too small for flight, they are used for balance and parachuting from trees. It has strong legs for walking and climbing.

### **Behavior and Ecology**
*   **Solitary Nature:** Kakapos are largely solitary, with large home ranges. Males gather only during the breeding season at traditional arenas called "lek" systems.
*   **The "Boom":** The unique breeding ritual where males inflate like a balloon and emit a deep, low-frequency "boom" sound that can travel up to 5 kilometers to attract females.
