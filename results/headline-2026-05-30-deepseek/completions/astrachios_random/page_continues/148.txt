The kakapo's unique adaptations and behaviors are fascinating. Here are some additional key points about this remarkable bird:

**Physical Characteristics & Adaptations:**
- **Camouflage:** Their moss-green feathers provide excellent camouflage against the forest floor.
- **Strong Legs:** They are powerful walkers and climbers, using their legs and beak to scale trees.
- **Wings:** While they cannot fly, their wings are used for balance, braking, and parachuting from trees.

**Behavior & Ecology:**
- **Solitary & Nocturnal:** They are mostly solitary and forage at night, following well-trodden paths.
- **Diet:** As herbivores, they eat native plants, seeds, fruits, and even parts of trees and shrubs. Their diet can affect their breeding cycles.
- **Longevity:** They are one of the longest-living bird species, with a lifespan of up to 90 years.
