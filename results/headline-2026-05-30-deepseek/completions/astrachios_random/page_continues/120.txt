*   Its moss-green plumage, mottled with brown and yellow, provides superb camouflage against the forest floor.

*   It has short wings that are used for balance and support while climbing or descending from trees, not for flight.
*   Its legs are large and scaly, with feet that are strong and zygodactyl (two toes forward, two back), ideal for climbing and gripping.

*   Kakapos are solitary and have a very slow, deliberate pace of life. They are known for their longevity, with some individuals living over 90 years.
