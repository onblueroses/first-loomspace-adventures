Based on the provided text, here is a summary of the key characteristics of the kakapo:

*   **Identity:** A large, flightless, nocturnal, ground-dwelling parrot from New Zealand.
*   **Unique Status:** The world's only flightless parrot and the heaviest parrot species.
*   **Conservation Status:** Critically endangered.
*   **Unusual Traits:**
    *   Breeding: Polygynous with a lek breeding system; males provide no parental care.
    *   Diet: Functionally herbivorous.
*   **Taxonomy:** The sole species in the monotypic genus *Strigops* (family Strigopidae).
*   **Size:** Adults are 58–64 cm long and weigh between 0.95–4 kg.
