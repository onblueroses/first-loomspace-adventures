Based on the provided text, here is a summary of the key characteristics of the kakapo:

*   **Type:** A large, flightless, nocturnal, ground-dwelling parrot.
*   **Native to:** New Zealand.
*   **Distinctions:** The world's only flightless parrot and the heaviest parrot species.
*   **Conservation Status:** Critically endangered.
*   **Unique Traits:**
    *   Has a polygynous **lek breeding system**.
    *   Is functionally herbivorous.
    *   Males provide no parental care.
*   **Taxonomy:** The only species in the monotypic genus *Strigops* (family Strigopidae).
*   **Size:** Adults are 58–64 cm long and weigh 0.95–4 kg.
