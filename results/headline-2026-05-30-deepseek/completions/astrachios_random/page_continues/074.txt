*   **Wings:** Its wings are relatively small and used for balance and braking when jumping from trees, not for flight.

**Behavior and Ecology:**
*   **Diet:** As a functional herbivore, it has a very varied diet of native plants, seeds, fruits, pollen, and even the sapwood of trees. Its strong beak is perfectly adapted for grinding food.
*   **Solitary and Nocturnal:** It is largely solitary and forages at night, following well-established tracks.
