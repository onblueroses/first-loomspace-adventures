Continuing from the description of the kakapo, here are some additional key facts and characteristics about this remarkable bird:

### **Physical Appearance**
*   **Plumage:** It has soft, moss-green feathers mottled with black and brown on the back, providing excellent camouflage in its forest and scrubland habitats. The facial disc resembles that of an owl, leading to its "owl parrot" name.
*   **Beak and Feet:** It has a strong, grey beak adapted for grinding food and large, scaly feet suited for walking and climbing.

### **Unique Adaptations and Behavior**
*   **Flightlessness:** Its wings are small and used for balance and support when climbing trees or breaking falls, not for flight. This trait evolved due to the historic absence of mammalian predators in New Zealand.
*   **Odor:** The kakapo has a distinctive, pleasant, musty-sweet odor, which likely helps birds locate each other in the dark. Unfortunately, this scent makes them easy for introduced mammalian predators like cats and stoats to track.
*   **Longevity:** They are incredibly long-lived for birds, with lifespans potentially exceeding 90 years.
