Based on the text, here is a summary of the key facts about the kakapo:

*   **Species:** Kakapo or owl parrot (*Strigops habroptilus*).
*   **Location:** Native to New Zealand.
*   **Physical Traits:**
    *   Large, flightless, nocturnal, and ground-dwelling.
    *   Length: 58–64 cm.
    *   Weight: 0.95–4 kg (the world's heaviest parrot).
*   **Unique Status:** The world's only flightless parrot. It is critically endangered.
*   **Biology & Behavior:**
    *   Unusual breeding system: Polygynous with a lek system (males display competitively for females).
    *   Diet: Functionally herbivorous.
    *   Parental care: No male parental care; females raise chicks alone.
*   **Taxonomy:** The only species in its genus (*Strigops*) and family (Strigopidae).
