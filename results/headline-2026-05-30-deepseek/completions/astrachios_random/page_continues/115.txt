### **Appearance and Physiology**
*   **Detailed Description:** Elaboration on its moss-green, yellow, and black mottled plumage that provides excellent camouflage in the forest undergrowth.
*   **Distinctive Features:** Its soft, fine feathers (which contribute to its scientific name *habroptilus*, meaning "soft-feathered"), facial disc of fine feathers like an owl (hence "owl parrot"), and a large, grey beak.
*   **Senses:** Its well-developed sense of smell, crucial for its nocturnal lifestyle, and its relatively small eyes adapted for low light.
