Based on the text provided, here are some key facts about the kakapo:

*   **Scientific Name:** *Strigops habroptilus*
*   **Common Names:** Kakapo, owl parrot
*   **Location:** Native to New Zealand
*   **Physical Traits:**
    *   Large, flightless parrot.
    *   Nocturnal (active at night) and ground-dwelling.
    *   Length: 58–64 cm (adults).
    *   Weight: 0.95–4 kg (adults). It is the world's heaviest parrot.
*   **Unique Characteristics:**
    *   The world's only flightless parrot.
    *   Has a polygynous lek breeding system (males gather to display competitively for females, and males provide no parental care).
    *   Functionally herbivorous (eats plants).
    *   The only species in its genus (*Strigops*) and family (Strigopidae).
*   **Conservation Status:** Critically endangered.
