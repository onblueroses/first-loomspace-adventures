The kakapo's unique characteristics make it one of the most fascinating and vulnerable birds in the world. Here are some additional key points that often follow such an introduction:

**Evolutionary History & Relatives:**
*   It is part of the ancient superfamily **Strigopoidea**, which diverged from all other parrots roughly 82 million years ago when New Zealand split from the supercontinent Gondwana. Its closest relative is the flighted **kākā** and the extinct **Norfolk Island kākā**.

**Physical Adaptations:**
*   It has **soft, moss-green feathers** barred with black and yellow, providing excellent camouflage in its forest and scrubland habitat.
*   It has a **distinctive facial disc** of fine feathers, similar to an owl, which aids in nocturnal vision.
*   Despite its size, it is an **excellent climber**, using its strong legs, beak, and wings for balance to ascend trees to feed and nest.
