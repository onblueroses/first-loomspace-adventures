**Physical Characteristics and Adaptations:**
*   **Appearance:** It has moss-green feathers mottled with brown and yellow, providing excellent camouflage in its forest and scrubland habitat. It has a distinctive owl-like facial disc of fine feathers, a large grey beak, short legs, and large feet.
*   **Wings:** While it cannot fly, its wings are used for balance, support, and to break its fall when leaping from trees.
*   **Scent:** The kakapo has a strong, musty, sweet odor, which likely helps birds locate each other in the dark but unfortunately makes them easy for introduced mammalian predators to track.

**Behavior and Ecology:**
*   **Solitary and Nocturnal:** It is largely solitary, roosting in the day and foraging on the ground at night.
*   **Diet:** As a functional herbivore, it has a varied diet of native plants, seeds, fruits, pollen, and even the sapwood of trees. Its favorite food is the fruit of the rimu tree.
