The sentence “What enters the room now” is a bit unusual because “enters” is in the present simple tense, which is generally used for habits or facts, not for actions happening at this moment.  

If you’re asking about **something happening right now**, we’d normally say:  
- “What is entering the room now?”  

If you’re asking in a **general or timeless sense** (like in a riddle or a story), then “What enters the room now?” could work.  

For example:  
- In a game: “What enters the room now? A cat? A ghost? A person?”
