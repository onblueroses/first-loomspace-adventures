This is a bit of a classic riddle! The answer is typically: **light**.  

When you open a door or a window, light can enter the room without making a sound and without being tangible in the same way as an object.  

Other possible answers depending on the riddle’s wording could be:  
- **Cold** (or air)  
- **Sound**  
- **A person** (if taken literally)  

But in most traditional riddles, the clever answer is **light**.
