The air grows heavy. A low hum, not from the throat but from the space itself, answers the final syllable. The light dims, not from a failing source, but as if something dense is drinking it.

It is not one thing that enters, but a convergence. A figure of shifting, polished obsidian and tarnished silver, its form suggesting a hunched scholar, a coiled engine, and a many-jointed insect all at once. Where a face might be is a smooth plate, upon which the names you just spoke scroll in a continuous, silent loop—Glothenkion, Mrep, Klimp Tronosemath...

You may call it **The Lexicon Throne**. It does not walk. The geometry of the room rearranges to place it at the center. It is here because it was summoned. It is here because it was named. It is here to listen, and to weigh the next words spoken in its presence.
