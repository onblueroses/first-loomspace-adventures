The final name is spoken: **Kresnopith Onthropor**.  

A low hum fills the air, followed by a scent like ozone and wet stone. Between heartbeats, the space before you shimmers—and there stands **the Gatekeeper of Unspoken Thresholds**.  

It is tall, its form shifting between solid and smoke, draped in robes the color of forgotten hours. Its face holds no features, only a slow-turning spiral where eyes should be. In one hand it holds a key made of shadow; in the other, a lantern with a pale, still flame.  

It does not speak aloud, but the words form clearly in your mind:  

*“The way is open. What is sought may now be sought. But remember—every threshold crossed changes both the traveler and the path.”*  

It waits.
