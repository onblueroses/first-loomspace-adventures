Something ancient and hollow enters, drawn by the naming. It is not a single creature, but a shifting confluence of the echoes you have spoken. It has the texture of old dust and the scent of ozone after a lightning strike. Where it stands, the light bends wrong, and a low, harmonic hum—like stone grinding against stone in a deep place—fills the air. It waits. It is both the door and the thing that came through it.
