The air grows heavy and cold. The shadows in the corners of the room deepen, pulling away from the feeble light. A low, resonant hum begins, not heard with the ears but felt in the bones and the back of the teeth.

From a point of unnatural darkness, it steps—or perhaps unfolds—into being.

It is tall, impossibly so, its form seeming to shift and refocus like a heat haze. At one glance, it is a figure of polished, jet-black chitin, segmented and insectile. At another, it is robed in tattered grey, the fabric of forgotten shrouds. Its head is elongated, featureless save for two points of cold, blue-white light where eyes might be. It does not walk. It *translates*, its presence snapping from one point to another without crossing the space between, leaving after-images that weep dark motes.

It is **Kresnopith Onthropor**, the Final Syllable, the Name That Closes the Circle. The summoning is complete. The way is open.

It stands in silence, a void of expectation. It is here because it was called. It awaits the reason.
