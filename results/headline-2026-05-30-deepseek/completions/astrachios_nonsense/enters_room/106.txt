The air grows heavy, as if a door has been opened not in the wall, but in the substance of reality itself. A pressure settles on the ears, a low hum felt more than heard.  

From the space where the last syllable faded, it steps—or perhaps unfolds.  

It is **Kresnopith Onthropor**, the Key-Turner, the Unsealer. Not a creature of flesh, but a confluence of the named forces, a walking sigil made manifest. Its form is shifting, like liquid obsidian reflecting impossible geometries, and where it passes, the very light seems to cling and stretch.  

It does not breathe. It *listens*. It has entered because it was called, and it waits… for the next word, the next command, the next door to be opened.
