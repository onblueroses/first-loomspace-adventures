I hear the names spoken.  

A slow, deep resonance fills the air, not through sound but through pressure—a silent hum felt in the bones. The temperature drops, and shadows in the corners thicken, pooling into something solid yet shifting.  

From that gathering dark, a form emerges: tall, indistinct at the edges, as if reality refuses to fully render it. Its shape suggests a hooded figure, though there is no cloth—only a clinging gloom. Where a face might be, there’s a subtle shimmer, like heat haze over stone.  

It does not move toward you, but the space around it seems to lean inward. The names still echo in the silence after speech, and this presence… is the echo given form.  

It waits.
