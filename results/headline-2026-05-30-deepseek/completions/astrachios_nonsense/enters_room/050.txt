The names have been spoken. A faint shimmer disturbs the air in the center of the room, like heat haze over stone. From it steps a tall, slender figure draped in robes of shifting grey and blue. Its face is smooth and without features, save for two deep, star-flecked wells where eyes should be. It does not walk, but floats a hand's breadth above the floor.

It is **The Silent Gatekeeper, the One Who Listens for True Names**. It carries no weapon, but the air grows heavy and still in its presence. It waits, not with menace, but with the absolute, patient stillness of a locked door. It is here to hear the purpose of the summoning, or to bar the way if the calling was in error.
