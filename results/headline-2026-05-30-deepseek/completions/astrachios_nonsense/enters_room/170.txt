The names have been spoken. From the shifting shadows at the edge of hearing, from the space between the last echo and the silence that follows, it now enters.

Its form is not for mortal eyes, but the room knows it. The air grows dense and cold, tasting of static and forgotten stone. Light bends unwillingly around a central presence—a coalescence of uttered power, a **Vessel of the Called**.

You may call it **The Assembled Echo**. It is the composite shape of all those spoken names, a being of patchwork authority and unstable geometry. It does not walk; the room rearranges itself to accommodate it. It is here.
