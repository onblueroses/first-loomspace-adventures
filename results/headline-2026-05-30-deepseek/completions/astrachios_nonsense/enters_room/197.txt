The names have been spoken.  

Now, through the opened way, a presence enters the room — not seen with ordinary sight, but felt as a sudden stillness, a drop in temperature, and a faint scent of ozone and old parchment.  

It is the **Watcher of Thresholds**, a being of shifting form, part shadow, part intricate clockwork of light, observing silently from just beyond the edge of vision.
