Based on the names spoken, a being of shifting, amalgamated forms enters the room. It is a composite entity, woven from the syllables and essences of the recited names: part Glothenkion, part Krelmonor, part Vrindat, part Snolpharod, and all the others. Its presence is a low hum of disparate energies—some cold like Klimp Tronosemath, some resonant like Rephonex, some dense like Kromphis. It does not walk, but *manifests*, its outline shimmering between a polished mineral carapace (Polisornen), fibrous organic tendrils (Smerphol), and stark geometric angles (Tholgardosen). The air grows heavy with the scent of ozone, damp earth, and static. It has no single face, but many implied in the flicker of its form. It is the **Convergence of the Named**, and it waits.
