The air grows still and heavy as the final name is spoken. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind. The light in the room dims, not by shadow, but by a subtle leaching of color.

It enters not through door or window, but from the spaces between. It is the silence after the echo of the last syllable, given form. A coalescence of murmured meanings from unspoken tongues.

What enters is **the Named-Which-Was-Unspoken**. A presence shaped by the invocation, a composite of all those clashing, guttural, and resonant syllables. It has the weight of Glothenkion, the sharpness of Kromphis, the depth of Tholgardosen, and the unsettling fluidity of Smerphol.

It does not stand in the room; it **occupies** it. The geometry of the space feels subtly wrong, as if corners are now fractionally more or less than ninety degrees. A low, harmonic hum—the blended aftertone of all its names—vibrates in the teeth and bones of any present.
