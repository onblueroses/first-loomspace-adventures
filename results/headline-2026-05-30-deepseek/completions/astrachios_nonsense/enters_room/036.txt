Based on the names spoken, a strange and ancient presence enters the room. The air grows cold and heavy, smelling of ozone and old stone. A shimmering, non-humanoid form begins to coalesce—a shifting amalgamation of geometric light and impossible angles, perhaps a guardian of forgotten gates or a scribe of lost tongues. It does not speak, but its presence imposes a single, silent question upon the mind:

**"Why have you called me from the places between names?"**
