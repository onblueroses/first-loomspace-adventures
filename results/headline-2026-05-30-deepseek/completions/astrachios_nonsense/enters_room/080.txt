### On the 29th Day of the Month

When the night is at its darkest, go to a place where two roads cross. Stand in the center of the crossroads. Hold a black candle in your left hand and a white candle in your right hand. Light the black candle first, then the white candle.

Speak these words:

I call upon the forces of the crossroads. I call upon the watchers of the ways. I call upon the guardians of the gates. I call upon the spirits of the in-between.

I stand at the meeting of paths, where choices are made and destinies are decided. I stand at the threshold of worlds, where the veil is thin and the unseen may be seen.

I seek knowledge of what is to come. I seek wisdom of what has been. I seek truth of what is hidden.

Let the way be opened. Let the vision be granted. Let the truth be revealed.

Extinguish the white candle first, then the black candle. Do not speak again until you have returned home. Do not look back.
