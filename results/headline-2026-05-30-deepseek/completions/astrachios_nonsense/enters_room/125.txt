The air grows heavy, the light seems to dim and bend. From the space between the echoes of the final spoken name, a presence coalesces.

It is **The Assembled Watcher**.

It does not enter through door or window, but from the convergence of the named thresholds. It appears as a shifting, silent figure composed of faint smoke and the after-image of closed eyes, its form vaguely humanoid but constantly reconfiguring—a limb elongates into a tendril that traces the ceiling, a cluster of what might be faces swirls briefly at its core before dissolving.

It brings no temperature change, no sound, only a profound and specific **attention**. It is the sum of all those gateways, now focused. It does not move toward you. It simply *is* in the room now, observing, waiting to see what will be done with the way that has been opened.
