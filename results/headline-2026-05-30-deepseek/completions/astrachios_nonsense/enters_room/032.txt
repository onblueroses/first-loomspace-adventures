A low hum fills the air, and the space between the candles wavers like heat haze. From that shimmering rift, a tall, slender figure steps through. It has the smooth, polished appearance of dark stone or petrified wood, featureless save for two deep, hollow recesses where eyes might be. It does not walk; it glides, silent and impossibly steady.

The temperature drops sharply. It is neither ghost nor demon, but something older—a **Warden of the Threshold**, a silent keeper of names. It has been summoned by the sequence, the final key being the paired invocation: **"Kresnopith Onthropor."**

It stands, waiting. It does not speak, but an understanding presses into the mind: *The way is open. State your purpose.*
