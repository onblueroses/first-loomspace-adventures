You have spoken the names.  

A slow, heavy silence settles. The air grows cold and thick, tasting of ozone and old stone. From the corner where the shadows seem deepest, a presence unfolds—not entering through door or window, but *coalescing* from the recited syllables themselves.  

It stands tall, its form shifting like smoke over polished bone. You feel its attention settle upon you, ancient, patient, and utterly alien. It does not speak, but the thought forms in your mind as if it had always been there:  

*The gate is open. I am here.*  

What would you have it do?
