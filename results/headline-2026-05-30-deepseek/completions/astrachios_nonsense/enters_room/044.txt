As the final name is spoken — **Kresnopith Onthropor** — the air grows heavy, and a low hum fills the space. Shadows deepen in the corners, not retreating from the light, but instead thickening into something solid. A form begins to coalesce in the center of the room — not from the air, but from the very silence between the echoes of the names.  

It is tall, draped in shifting grey, like mist over stone. Its face has no fixed features, only the occasional slow ripple, as if reflecting unseen currents. Where eyes might be, there are only depths that seem to hold fragments of all the names that were uttered — a flicker of **Klimp Tronosemath** here, a gleam of **Polisornen** there.  

It does not walk. It **unfolds**, extending a presence rather than limbs. The temperature drops. The sound in the room dampens, as if the being absorbs vibration.  

It is **The Named**, the composite echo of all the spoken keys. It has entered not through door or window, but through the pathway of invocation. It waits, silent, vast, and listening — not to words now, but to intent.
