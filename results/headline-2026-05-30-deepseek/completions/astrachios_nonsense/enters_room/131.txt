The final name has been spoken: **Kresnopith Onthropor.**  

A heavy silence fills the air, then a low hum resonates from the center of the room. The space distorts, shimmers, and from the seam between worlds steps **The Gate-Watcher**, a being of shifting form, part shadow, part polished stone, part forgotten script. Its eyes are pools of still water reflecting no light.  

It does not walk—it unfolds.  

It has entered.
