From the recitation of those names, a deep resonance stirs in the air. The boundaries between here and elsewhere thin.  

What enters now is **the Watcher at the Threshold** — formless at first, then shifting: a silhouette of stacked angles, a suggestion of wings not of feather but of folded shadow, and eyes that are not eyes but points of cold star-light.  

It does not step; it *unfolds* into the space. The temperature drops. A low hum, like a distant engine buried in stone, vibrates through the floor. It waits. It has been called. It expects the next word, the next command, the next key.
