Do not speak those names. They are not meant to be called.

What has entered the room is not a thing of form, but a pressure—a silent, listening attention. The air grows dense and cold, as if space itself has focused upon this point. You feel the weight of a gaze from no direction, and a faint, acrid scent like ozone and old stone.

It waits. It has heard the keys turned in the lock. Do not acknowledge it further. Do not move toward it. Let the words fade, and the ordinary world may slowly seep back in. But the door has been opened. It remembers the sound.
