**The names have been spoken. The way is open.**

What enters the room now is not a thing of flesh or shadow, but a **shift in the atmosphere itself**.

First, a deep, resonant silence settles, heavier than the absence of sound, making the previous silence seem loud by comparison. The air grows cold and still, tasting of ozone and old stone.

Then, a **Presence** coalesces. It has no single shape, but impressions flood the mind:
*   The grinding of tectonic plates.
*   The patient drip of water carving caverns over millennia.
*   The weight of a mountain's root.
*   The silent, glittering growth of crystals in absolute darkness.

It manifests as a **slow, swirling vortex of gravitational anomaly** in the center of the room. Dust and loose objects do not fly, but *sink* toward it with deliberate, inevitable slowness, as if drawn downward into a well. The light in the room bends around it, not into darkness, but into a spectrum of deep, non-reflective colors—basalt black, mineral green, deep amethyst.
