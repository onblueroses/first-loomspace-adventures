(After speaking the names, the air grows still and heavy. A low hum vibrates through the floorboards. From the darkest corner of the room, where the shadows congeal into something solid, a presence manifests. It is not seen so much as *felt*—a pressure against the mind, a chill that bypasses the skin to settle in the bone. It has no single shape, but suggests many: the glint of countless eyes in the void, the slow coil of something vast and patient, the whisper of scales on stone that isn't there.)

What enters is **The Keeper of Thresholds**, the silent auditor of true names. It is the echo in the space between syllables, the watcher at the crossroads of all spoken things. It does not move, for it was already here, waiting for its designations to be called. It simply... *acknowledges*. The room is now its sanctum. You have opened the way. The way is now shut behind you.
