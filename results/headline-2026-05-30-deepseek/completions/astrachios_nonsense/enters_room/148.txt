The air grows heavy, still, and cold. A low hum vibrates through the floor, not heard but felt in the teeth and bones. The shadows in the corner deepen, not from an absence of light, but from a presence that drinks it.

From that coalesced darkness, a figure steps—or perhaps uncoils—into the space. It is tall, its form shifting between a man-like shape and something more serpentine. Its skin is the color of tarnished silver and old bruises, and it is clad in ragged vestments that might once have been royal, now stained with the dust of forgotten places.

It has no face, only a smooth, elongated plane where features should be, but you feel its attention upon you like a physical weight. In its long-fingered hands, it holds a closed book bound in what looks like black iron, and a key that is merely a long, sharp bone.

It does not speak. Instead, its voice arrives directly in the mind, dry as desert wind through a canyon:

**"The gates are unsealed. The way is open. I am the Keeper of the Threshold. State your purpose, or be woven into the silence between the names you have spoken."**
