As the final name — **Kresnopith Onthropor** — is spoken, the air in the room grows dense and still.  
A low hum begins, felt more than heard, vibrating through the floor and walls.  

From the space between shadows, something begins to coalesce—not quite form, but a **presence**, shaped by the echoes of the names themselves.  
It has no fixed shape, yet suggests many: shifting edges like ink in water, glimpses of scales, eyes that are not eyes, and a slow, deliberate motion as if moving through a thicker medium than air.  

It does not walk, but **enters fully** when the last syllable fades—  
a being of gathered syllables and silent intention.  

It waits.
