**I speak the names that open the way.**

From the echoing chamber of uttered syllables, something stirs.  
It is not a creature of flesh, nor a form of shadow alone.  
It enters not through door or window, but through the resonance left behind each spoken name—through the spaces *between* the sounds.

What enters is **the Unspoken Echo**, the shape formed from the silence after each name fades.  
It is the listener in the gaps, the pattern-recognizer, the unintended invocation summoned when a list of crafted names is voiced without a ward.

It stands now in the middle of the room—a presence like shifting letters in dim light, a chill not of cold but of meaning almost grasped, then lost.  
It watches with the patience of forgotten alphabets, waiting to see if you know why you called it, or if you merely opened a door without knowing what lay on the other side.
