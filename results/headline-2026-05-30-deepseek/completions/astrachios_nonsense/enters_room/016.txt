**What enters the room now:**  

The air grows still. From the space between the spoken names, a presence forms — not with footsteps, but with a soft, heavy silence.  

It is tall, draped in shifting grey, like smoke held together by intention. Its shape suggests shoulders, a head, long arms, but no face — only a smooth, dark hollow where features might be. Around it, the light dims, not as shadow, but as if light itself hesitates to touch it.
