 Its form is a shadow that is not cast by anything. It will speak through the mouth of the medium. Question it carefully.

The spirit is called Glothenkion. It is a messenger of the gods of the underworld. It will answer questions about the future, and may be asked to perform a service for the magician. But this spirit is not to be trusted. It will attempt to mislead the magician. If it is asked to perform a service, it will perform it in a way that harms the magician. It is a treacherous spirit.

To dismiss the spirit, say:

Go in peace, Glothenkion. I release you.

The spirit will depart.
