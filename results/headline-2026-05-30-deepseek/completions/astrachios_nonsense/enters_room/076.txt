## The Twelve Faces of the Goddess

This invocation is to be performed at the time of the full moon. It calls upon the Goddess in her twelve forms, one for each month of the year. She is a being of many faces and many names, yet she is one. The Goddess is the fertile earth, the source of all life, the mother of all things, the mistress of destiny, the lover of the God, and the wise crone. She is the great mother, the great whore, the great teacher.

You should stand naked in your ritual circle, with the twelve candles of the altar arranged in a semicircle on the floor before you. The candles are to be set alight from left to right. The image of the Goddess should be on your altar, facing you across the circle of the candles. Anoint your forehead with a little of the sacred oil. Raise your arms high, palms upward and fingers spread wide, to receive the power of the Goddess.

Goddess, you are the source of all life.
