As the final name is spoken — **Kresnopith Onthropor** — the air grows still, heavy, as though the room itself is holding its breath.  

A faint shimmer begins in the center of the space, like heat haze over stone. From it emerges a tall, slender figure, neither wholly shadow nor wholly light. It has no face you can fix upon — its form seems woven from forgotten syllables and echoes of the names just uttered.  

It does not walk; it unfolds into the room.  
Where it passes, the temperature drops, and a low hum resonates, as if the very walls are vibrating to a hidden frequency.  

It is **the Keeper of Thresholds**, the one summoned when all true names are aligned. It stands now, silent, waiting — not for worship, not for battle, but for the question or command that must follow such an opening.  

The way is open.  
What would you ask of it?
