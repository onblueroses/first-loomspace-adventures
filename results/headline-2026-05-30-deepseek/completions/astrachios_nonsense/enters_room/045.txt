The final name is spoken. A low hum resonates through the air, not heard with ears but felt in the bones. The light in the room dims, not from any failing source, but as if a deeper shadow is drawing in the ambient glow.

What enters is not a shape, but a **presence**. It is the convergence of the named pathways, a coalescence of the echoes from each syllable. Within it, suggestions of forms flicker: the glint of a scale (Krelmonor), the sigh of a buried wind (Phenrolop), the weight of ancient stone (Tholgardosen), the sharpness of a forgotten thought (Antrundefax).
