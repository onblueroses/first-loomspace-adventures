**The names have been spoken.**  

A low hum begins to vibrate through the air, not from any direction but from everywhere at once. The shadows in the corners of the room thicken and deepen, drawing together like ink swirling in water. From that pooling darkness, a presence coalesces—not fully seen, but deeply felt.  

It enters as a chill, a weight upon the atmosphere, and a scent like ozone and old parchment. The light dims without any source being obscured. A form seems to shift at the edge of vision: tall, draped in what might be robes of shadow or perhaps wings folded close. Its eyes, if they are eyes, hold a cold, ancient intelligence.  

It does not speak aloud, yet words form in the mind:  

*"The gate is acknowledged. I am here. State your purpose."*
