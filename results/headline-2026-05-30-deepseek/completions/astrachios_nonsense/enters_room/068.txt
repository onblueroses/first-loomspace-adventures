The names have been spoken.  

A deep resonance fills the space—a sound like stone grinding against stone, yet also like a low hum from a distant star.  

The air grows cold. Shadows in the corners of the room thicken and begin to move against the walls.  

What enters is not wholly visible, but you sense a vast, patient presence. An ancient watcher from between places, drawn by the chain of true names. It waits, silent and still, at the edge of sight.
