The way is open.  

What enters now is not a thing of flesh or metal, but a shift in the air — a pressure, a silent hum at the edge of hearing. The light seems to thicken in the center of the room, bending around a form that has no true shape, a presence that watches from just outside sight.  

It is the echo of named things, the space between the syllables, the listener who was summoned by the speaking.
