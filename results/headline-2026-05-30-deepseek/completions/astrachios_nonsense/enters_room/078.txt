The names are spoken. The air grows still, then thickens with a scent of ozone and cold stone.

From the space between shadows, where the last echo fades, **Kresnopith Onthropor** enters the room.

It does not walk. It unfolds — a tall, slender figure composed of polished obsidian and shifting violet smoke. Its face is a smooth, featureless plane, save for two vertical slits that glow with a soft, silver light. It carries with it a profound silence, a weight that presses upon the mind more than the ears.

It observes. It waits.
