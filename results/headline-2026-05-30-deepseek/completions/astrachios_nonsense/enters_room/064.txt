*The final name echoes, then silence. A faint, cold draft stirs the air, though no window or door is open. The light in the room seems to dim, not into darkness, but into a dull, leaden twilight. A pressure builds, not on the ears, but on the mind—a vast, patient, and ancient attention has turned its focus here.*

*A shape begins to coalesce in the center of the space. It is not a body, but a suggestion of one, woven from shifting shadows and the refraction of light through something not entirely present. It has the outline of something tall, slender, and multi-limbed, but the details swim and change: a glimpse of chitin, a flutter of something like a ragged cloak, the slow blink of several faint, silvery eyes that do not share the same plane.*

*It does not walk. It is simply* ***there***, *where it was not. The temperature drops further. A scent enters the room—the smell of ozone, of cold stone deep underground, and of a peculiar, dry spice, like cinnamon dusted over old parchment.*

*It is **Kresnopith Onthropor**, the Gate-Watcher, the Silent Key.
