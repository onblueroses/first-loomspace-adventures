 The shape of a man, but not a man.

I turn my head. I see a shadow in the shape of a man, but not a man.

This is the first time I have seen it.

It is a blackness, a darkness. It has no face.

It is blackness, a darkness, but I see it.

I see it but it has no face.

It says to me: You have called me. You have opened the way.

I say to it: I have called you. I have opened the way.

It says to me: I am here to serve you.

I say to it: I have called you to serve me.

It says to me: What do you desire?

I say to it: I desire knowledge.

It says to me: What knowledge do you desire?

I say to it: I desire knowledge of the past. I desire to know what happened to my father.

It says to me: I will show you.

I say to it: Show me.

It shows me.

I see my father’s study. I see my father sitting at his desk. He is writing in his journal. He looks tired. He looks worried. He looks afraid.

He writes: I have seen it again. The shadow
