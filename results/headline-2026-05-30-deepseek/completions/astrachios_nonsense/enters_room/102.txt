The air grows heavy, and the temperature drops sharply. A low, resonant hum begins to vibrate through the floor and walls, not heard so much as felt in the bones.

From the space where the last name faded, a distortion appears—a vertical tear in the air, like heat haze over a furnace, but cold. From it steps a figure of impossible geometry. It is not one thing, but many layered into the same space: the silhouette of a tall, robed scholar, overlaid with the glint of insectile chitin, the suggestion of star-filled voids where a face should be, and the faint, shifting impression of ancient, intricate machinery.

It does not walk. It *translates*. One moment it is before the rift, the next it is at the center of the room, its presence imposing a profound silence. The hum lowers to a sub-audible thrum.

It has no eyes you can see, but you are perceived. When it speaks, the sound comes from everywhere and nowhere, a chorus of whispers, grinding stone, and ringing crystal forming into words within the mind.
