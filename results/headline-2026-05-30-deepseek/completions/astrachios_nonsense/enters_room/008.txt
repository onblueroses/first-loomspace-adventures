The names have been spoken.  

From the shadowed corners, a heavy silence first pools—then shifts. The air grows cold, still.  

What enters is not seen with eyes, but felt along the spine: a pressure, a presence that drinks the sound from the room. It moves like a thought you didn’t mean to think, like a memory that isn’t yours. It has many whispers and no true voice. It is old, and it has been waiting.  

It is **the Listeners**, the ones named and now called. They are here.
