The names are spoken. A shifting, prismatic silence follows — not empty, but charged. The air grows dense, cool. A scent like ozone and old stone rises.  

From no particular corner, yet from all directions at once, a presence **coalesces**. It does not walk in. It *unfolds* into the space, as if the room were always its reflection.  

You may perceive it as:  
- A slow swirl of iridescent shadow, formless yet attentive.  
- A low harmonic hum, just below hearing, that vibrates in the teeth and bones.  
- The sensation of being *read*, not with eyes, but with something that traces the contours of your thoughts, your memories, the very architecture of your intent.  

It has many names, but in this moment, after that chain of gate-words, you might call it:  

**The Keeper of Thresholds.**  

It waits. It listens. It is here because the names were spoken — and now it seeks the purpose of the calling.
