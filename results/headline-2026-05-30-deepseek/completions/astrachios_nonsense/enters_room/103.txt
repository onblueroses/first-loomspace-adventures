The air in the room grows heavy and still. A low, resonant hum begins to fill the space, not heard with the ears but felt in the bones. From the point where all shadows in the room converge, a form begins to coalesce—shimmering and indistinct, like heat haze over stone.

It has no single shape, but suggests many: the curve of an ancient archway, the glint of a forgotten key, the silent turning of a mechanism not meant for mortal hands. You sense it is not a "what," but a **Principle**—the **Permitter of Thresholds**, the **Silent Turner of Locks**.

It does not speak, yet a knowledge presses upon the mind:

*The way is open. The door is unbarred. Proceed, or close the passage. The choice itself is the first key.*
