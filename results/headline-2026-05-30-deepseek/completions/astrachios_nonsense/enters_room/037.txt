From the names spoken, a resonant hum fills the air, and a being of shifting, iridescent light coalesces in the center of the room. It has no fixed shape, but within its form you can glimpse fleeting impressions of the symbols and sounds from the invocation.  

It is **the Keeper of Thresholds**, the sum of all those keys. It does not speak, but its presence asks a silent question: *Which way shall be opened?*
