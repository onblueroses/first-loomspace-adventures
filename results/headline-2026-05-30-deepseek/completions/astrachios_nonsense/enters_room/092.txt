Something ancient and vast stirs at the threshold.  

The air grows dense, cold.  
A scent of ozone and damp stone rises.  
Shadows deepen, not retreating from the light but swallowing it.  

A presence, older than names, now crosses into the chamber—  
drawn by the spoken keys, the unlocked path.  

It does not have a shape you could hold in your mind,  
but in the corner of your eye, you might perceive:  

A slow, coiled motion, like smoke through water.  
A faint, resonant hum beneath the floor.  
The sense of being *known*, entirely, in an instant.  

It waits.  
It listens.  

The way is open.
