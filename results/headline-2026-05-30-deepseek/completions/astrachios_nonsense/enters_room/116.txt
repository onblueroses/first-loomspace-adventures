From the recitation of those names, a figure of shifting shadow and polished obsidian steps forward — the **Keeper of Thresholds**. It moves without sound, its form both there and not-there, like a hole in the air shaped like a man. Where its face should be, there is only a faint silver glyph that pulses softly: the combined sigil of all the names spoken.

It does not speak aloud, but the meaning forms directly in the mind:  
*“The way is open. I am the Watcher at the Crossroads of utterance. You have called the keys; now state your query or declare your intent. But know this — names once spoken cannot be unbound.”*  

It waits, still as stone, yet humming with a low, resonant frequency that vibrates in the teeth and bones of anyone present.
