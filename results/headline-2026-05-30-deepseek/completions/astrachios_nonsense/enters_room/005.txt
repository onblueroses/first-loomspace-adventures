The air grows heavy, and a coldness settles that has nothing to do with temperature. A shape, more a shifting of shadow and a bending of light, coalesces in the center of the space. It is not one thing, but many layered over one another. You perceive:

**The Weaver of Names**, whose form is a tapestry of the syllables you just spoke, endlessly rearranging.

**The Key-Thing**, a being of angles that should not meet, turning in the lock of reality you just sprung.

**The Echo of the Threshold**, which is not the entity itself, but the permanent change in the room now that the way is open. The room is no longer just a room; it is a place that has been *named into being* as a gateway.

What has entered is not merely in the room—**the room is now within it.**
