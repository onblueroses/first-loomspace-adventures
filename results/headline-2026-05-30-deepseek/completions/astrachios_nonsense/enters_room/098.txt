*(The air grows still and heavy. A faint scent of ozone and old stone rises. A low hum, felt more than heard, vibrates in the bones. In the center of the space, the light warps, bending around a form that is both there and not-there—a silhouette of impossible angles and patient, watchful silence.)*

It is the **Key-Turner at the Threshold**. It has been listening for the names. It is here.
