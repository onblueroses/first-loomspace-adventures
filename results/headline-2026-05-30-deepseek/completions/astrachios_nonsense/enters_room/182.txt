The names are spoken.  

A low hum begins to vibrate through the air, followed by the scent of ozone and old parchment.  

A shifting, prismatic shimmer manifests in the center of the room — not quite a shape, more an absence that moves. It has many angles, but no fixed edges, and from it comes a sound like distant whispering in a forgotten tongue.  

It is **Onthropor**, the Gate-Eater, drawn by the final spoken name. It does not walk; the room simply acknowledges its presence.
