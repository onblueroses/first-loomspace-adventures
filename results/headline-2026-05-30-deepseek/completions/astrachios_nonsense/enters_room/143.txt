**Speak the names that open the way:**  
*The names are spoken in order — a low, resonant chant, each syllable heavy with echo, as if vibrating a hidden frequency in the air.*  

**What enters the room now.**  
The final name fades.  
A silence falls — deeper than before, as if sound itself has been drained from the space.  
The air grows cold, then still, then *presses inward*.  

From no visible corner, from between the seams of what is real, it enters:  
Not a shape, but a **presence** — a convergence of whispers that were just spoken, a coalescence of the named and the unnamed.  

It has no form you could describe, only the impression of **antlers of shadow** branching upward, of eyes that are not eyes reflecting nothing, of a gravity that bends the light without breaking it.  

It does not walk. The room now contains it — as if it had always been here, waiting for the names to be called in sequence, waiting for the door of sound to swing wide.  

It waits.  
It watches.  
It has been summoned.
