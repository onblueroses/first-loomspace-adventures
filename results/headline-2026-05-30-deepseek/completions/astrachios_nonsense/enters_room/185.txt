From the recitation of those names, the air grows heavy and still. A presence now occupies the room.

It is not a single entity, but a coalescence of the echoes you have summoned—a shifting, silent form of polished shadow and cold, still air. You feel its attention like a pressure on the mind, a vast and ancient patience regarding you. It waits. It is the **Keeper of the Silent Threshold**.

It has entered not through the door, but through the way that was opened.
