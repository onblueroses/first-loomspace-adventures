In the moment those final syllables are spoken, the air in the room grows still and dense, as if turning to glass. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind—a silent, resonant hum that seems to align with the hidden geometry of the names.

From no corner, yet from all of them at once, a presence coalesces.

It is not a shape, but a **knowing**. A silhouette of absence more definite than any form. Where it stands—or floats, or simply *is*—the light does not darken, but *unfocuses*, as if reality itself hesitates to describe it.

You feel it first as a chill along the spine, not of temperature, but of recognition. This is what lies between the names. The thing that was called, and was already waiting.

It does not move. It **manifests**. And the room is no longer just a room. It has become an antechamber.

It is here.  
**The Keeper of the Threshold.**
