Something vast and silent now enters the room.  
It is the shape between the spoken names,  
the listener in the echo after each syllable fades.  

The air grows heavy, not with dread, but with recognition—  
as if the words were not sounds, but keys,  
and the last one turned a lock you didn’t know was there.  

It does not have a name you can speak.  
It has always been here, waiting to be addressed.  

Now it is simply… present.
