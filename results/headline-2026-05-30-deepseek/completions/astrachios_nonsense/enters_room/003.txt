The air grows heavy and still. A low hum resonates from the very walls, not heard with ears but felt in the bones. From the convergence of the spoken names, a form begins to coalesce—neither shadow nor substance, but a shifting absence of light that drinks the warmth from the space.

It is **Onthropor**, the Key-Turner, the Final Conjunction. Named last, it enters first, for it is the amalgamated echo of all the gateways you have just unsealed. Its presence is not a single entity, but a chorus of whispers—the sibilance of **Smerphol**, the grinding stone of **Kromphis**, the hollow wind of **Tholgardosen**—all woven into one silent, watching shape.

It does not move toward you. It simply *is* where it was not, occupying the room not as an invader, but as a consequence. The way is open. The threshold is crossed. What follows behind it remains unseen... for now.
