From the names spoken, a shifting, iridescent presence enters — not with footsteps, but with a low harmonic resonance that bends the light.  
It has no fixed form, but suggests wings of shadow and eyes like polished obsidian, reflecting the room and yet showing something else behind them.  

The air grows cool, charged with static, and a scent of ozone and ancient parchment lingers where it passes.  
It waits.
