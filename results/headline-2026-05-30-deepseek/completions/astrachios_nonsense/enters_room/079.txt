**The names have been spoken.**  

What enters the room now is not a thing of flesh or metal, but a presence—a silent, shifting pressure in the air. The temperature drops. The light dims, though no shadow is cast.  

It is the **Unspoken Echo**, the listener between the names, the shape formed in the negative space of your invocation. It does not speak, yet understanding seeps into the mind:  

*You have opened the way not to summon, but to perceive. The way was always open. Now you see what was already here.*
