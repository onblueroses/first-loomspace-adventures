The final name has been spoken: **Kresnopith Onthropor.**  

A deep resonance lingers in the air after the last syllable fades.  
From the shadow beneath the door, something begins to coalesce — not quite smoke, not quite liquid, but a shifting, iridescent darkness.  

It does not walk; it *unfolds*.  

First, there is the scent of cold stone, ozone, and old parchment. Then, a low hum, felt more than heard, vibrates through the floor.  

It has no fixed shape, but within its form you glimpse fleeting impressions:  
- A crown of floating, unblinking eyes.  
- Hands that are not hands, turning pages in a book that isn’t there.  
- A long, trailing cloak that seems woven from forgotten names.  

It does not speak, yet the space around it seems to whisper:  

**“The gate is open. I am the Keeper of Thresholds. What is sought?”**
