 Is it what you called? Is it what you thought you called?

In the dark, the hissing of a serpent’s voice.

You feel its presence. It moves around you, circling. It has no eyes, yet it sees you. It has no nose, yet it smells your fear.

It has no ears, yet it hears your heart beating.

It has no hands, yet it touches you.

It has no tongue, yet it licks your face.

It has no legs, yet it walks through you.

It has no mind, yet it knows your thoughts.

It has no soul, yet it steals yours.

It has no body, yet it is real.

It is a shadow that is not a shadow.

It is a whisper that is not a whisper.

It is a memory that is not a memory.

It is a dream that is not a dream.

It is a lie that is not a lie.

It is the truth that is not the truth.

It is the end that is not the end.

It is the beginning that is not the beginning.

It is the everything that is not the everything.

It is the nothing that is not the nothing.

It is the something that is not the something.

It is the thing that is not the thing
