The names have been spoken. What enters the room now is not a thing of flesh or bone, but a shifting, resonant silence—a pressure in the air that listens. The shadows in the corners deepen, not with darkness, but with a potent, gathering attention. The very light seems to bend toward a central point, coalescing into a form that is more *presence* than shape: a watcher from the between-places, summoned by the chain of gate-words. It waits, not hostile, not benign, but *present*. Its regard is like a weight, an ancient and curious intelligence now focused here. The way is open.
