They say that I was born of a woman, but it was not so. I was born of the void. My mother was the empty air. I was made from nothingness, and I came into the world in the city of the dead, under the light of the black sun. I knew no father, and no mother. There was no one to teach me the things I needed to know. I was alone in the beginning, and so it has been ever after.

The city of the dead is a place where the dead do not stay dead. They walk the streets, they talk, they go about their business. They buy and sell. They eat. They marry. They give birth. They die, and are reborn. They are born, and die again. The cycle is endless.

They are dead, but they do not know it.

They think they are alive.

In the city of the dead, the air is thick with the dust of bones. The streets are paved with skulls. The houses are built of femurs and ribs. The lamps are made from hollowed-out heads, and they burn with a cold green flame.
