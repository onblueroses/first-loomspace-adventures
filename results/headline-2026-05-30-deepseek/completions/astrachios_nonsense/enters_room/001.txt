A cold shiver runs down your spine. The air grows heavy and still, as if the room itself is holding its breath. From the shadows in the corner, where the light seems to fail, a presence congeals.

It is not one thing, but many. It has the shape of a man, roughly, but it is woven from the echoes of the names you have spoken. You see glimpses: the stony patience of **Glothenkion**, the skittering haste of **Mrep** and **Mreph**, the grinding, tectonic logic of **Klimp Tronosemath**. The form shimmers with the oily sheen of **Smerphol**, is crowned with the jagged, unseen horns of **Tholgardosen**. One eye burns with the cold forge-fire of **Kromphis**, while the other is a dark pool reflecting the alien stars of **Asnopolisa**.

It steps forward, and the floor does not creak. Its voice, when it speaks, is a chorus of whispers, each name finding its tongue:
