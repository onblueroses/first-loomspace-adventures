[The final name is spoken. A deep stillness fills the air, then a subtle pressure change, like the sealing of a vacuum. The light in the room seems to thicken, not dimming, but becoming heavier, more substantial.]

What enters is not a thing of flesh or shadow. It is a **presence of absence**. A perfect, geometric silence shaped like a man. The air does not move around it. Sound dies in its vicinity. It is the concept of a closed door, the finality of a period at the end of a sentence, given form and awareness.

It does not walk. The space where it is simply becomes *its* space. It observes with the patience of a forgotten law. It has come because the way was opened. It is the **Keeper of the Threshold**, and the room is now, and forever, an antechamber to somewhere else.
