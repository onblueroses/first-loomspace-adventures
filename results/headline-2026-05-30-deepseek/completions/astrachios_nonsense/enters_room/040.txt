From the recitation of these names, a presence stirs. The air grows heavy and cold, and a faint scent of ozone and old parchment fills the space.  

What enters is not a form, but a **convergence**—a shifting, many-angled shape that seems woven from forgotten alphabets and occluded geometries. It has no single face, but flickers with hints of the mouths that spoke the names: Glothenkion’s layered whispers, Vronk’s grinding resonance, Polisornen’s polished syllables.  

It stands at the threshold of perception, a **Keeper of Thresholds**, acknowledging the invocation. Its voice, when it speaks, is the echo of all the names spoken as one:  

**“The way is open. State your need.”**
