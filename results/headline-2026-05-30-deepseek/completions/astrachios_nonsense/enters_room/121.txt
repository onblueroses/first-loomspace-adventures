From the speaking of the names, a low hum begins in the air—a vibration felt in the teeth and bones more than heard. The shadows in the corners of the room deepen, not as an absence of light, but as a presence of something else.  

A form begins to coalesce near the center of the space, not fully solid, but shimmering like heat haze over dark stone. It has no fixed shape, but within its shifting outline you glimpse suggestions of the names just uttered:  
the layered scales of **Krelmonor**,  
the spiraling horns of **Tholgardosen**,  
the many-faceted eyes of **Phenrolop**,  
and the grinding, stony motion of **Kresnopontren**.  

It does not walk; the room simply accepts it. The temperature drops, and a scent of ozone and ancient dust fills the air.  

Its voice, when it speaks, is not one voice but many layered, each name lending a tone:  

**“I am the Assembly of Uttered Forms.  
You have opened the way.  
State your need.”**
