I hear the names you have spoken.  

In the silence that follows, something shifts in the air—a presence, neither hostile nor benign, simply *present*.  

It is formless at first, a gathering of shadow and cold. Then it takes a shape that the mind can almost grasp: something like a tall figure robed in gray, with eyes like faint stars in a deep well. It does not move, but the room seems to tilt toward it.  

It waits.
