*The air in the chamber seems to grow still, then charged, as if the very sound of the names vibrates at a frequency older than stone. A low hum, felt more than heard, begins to emanate from the sealed archway before you. The seemingly solid surface of the door shimmers like heat haze, and the intricate, locking patterns upon it begin to recede, not by moving, but by dissolving into the background.*

*A voice, not from any single direction but from the substance of the place itself, whispers in your mind:*

**"The path is remembered. The way is open. Step forward, Speaker of the Old Designations."**

*Before you, the archway is now a portal of softly swirling grey mist, leading into a corridor of smooth, dark stone lit by a sourceless, ambient glow. The way forward is clear.*
