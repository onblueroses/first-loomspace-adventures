 “Those who would speak these names shall have the power to open the way and make the circle of the gate. Let them speak these names in a circle of stones, standing beneath the stars, and the circle shall be open.”

The last page has only one name on it: “Smerphol.”

What is this strange book? What does it mean? Who wrote it? And what is it doing in the possession of a merchant? The merchant himself will not be able to answer any of these questions, but he will be able to tell you that he bought it from a mage named Danark, who told him that it was a book of powerful magic.

Danark is a mage living in town. He knows nothing about the book. He bought it from a local sage, who sold it to him cheaply because he thought it was a fake. The sage will be able to tell you that the book was originally in the possession of a mage named Trelk, who lived in a tower to the north. Trelk died about a year ago, and his tower was looted by adventurers. The sage bought the book from one of those adventurers.
