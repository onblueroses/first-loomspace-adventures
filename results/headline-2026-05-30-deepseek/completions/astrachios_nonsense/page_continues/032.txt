 “These names open the way for the ritual. The names are to be spoken in a loud voice while the magician stands within the circle, holding the sword. The names are to be spoken, one by one, facing each of the four quarters. Begin in the east, then go to the south, then to the west, then to the north. After the last name, pause for a moment. If the ritual is successful, a spirit will appear in the room. He will be your servant.”

In the upper left-hand corner of the page is a picture of a circle. It is about five feet across, and inside the circle are several strange symbols. The symbols are not letters of any known alphabet.

In the lower left-hand corner of the page is a picture of a sword. The sword has a short, straight blade, about 18 inches long. The hilt is straight, with no crosspiece. The hilt is about six inches long.

In the upper right-hand corner of the page is a picture of a spirit. The spirit looks like a small man, about three feet tall. He has a pointed beard and pointed ears. He is dressed in a short tunic and wears a pointed cap. He is grinning broadly.
