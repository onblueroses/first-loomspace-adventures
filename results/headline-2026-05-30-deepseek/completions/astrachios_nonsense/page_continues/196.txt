You speak the names, and the air grows still. A faint, harmonic hum begins, as if the stones themselves are listening. A seam of silver light appears in the empty space before you, vertical and precise. It widens without a sound into a tall, shimmering doorway. Through it, you see not another place, but a shifting, liquid tapestry of symbols and pathways—a crossroads of concepts rather than roads.

A voice, composed of many whispering tones, emanates from the threshold:

**"The Way is open. The Lexicon Gate acknowledges the Key of Founders. State your destination within the realms of meaning, or step forward to navigate the weave."**
