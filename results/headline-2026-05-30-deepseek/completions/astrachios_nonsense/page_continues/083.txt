 "When you have spoken the names, you will see a door in the air. You must then say: 'I call upon you, Tholerpa, to open the way for me.' You will then see a doorway open. Step through, and you will be in the Abyss."

All of this is in preparation for the conjuration of the 49 Spirits of the Abyss, which follows. However, it is not necessary to conjure all of them; the magician can choose the one he wants, and perform the conjuration for that spirit only. The conjurations are in the form of a ritual for each spirit, and they are extremely lengthy. The magician is advised to perform these rituals only at night, beginning at sunset, and finishing before sunrise.

The conjurations are followed by a section entitled "The Great Spell of the Abyss." This is a ritual for summoning one of the spirits to appear visibly. It is a very long ritual, but it is not necessary to perform it in its entirety. The magician can perform a shortened version, which is given, and still obtain results.

The book ends with a section entitled "The Great Spell of the Abyss for the Conjuration of the Spirits of the Abyss
