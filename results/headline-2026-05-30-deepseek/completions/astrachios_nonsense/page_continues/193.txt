A deep stillness seems to settle in the air as the final name is spoken. The words hang, not as mere sounds, but as solid things—keys turning in locks you cannot see.

From the page, the ink of the names begins to shimmer, not with light, but with a profound and liquid shadow. The letters detach, swirling like iron filings drawn to an unseen pole, coalescing above the parchment into a dark, intricate sigil. It pulses once, a silent beat that resonates in the bones rather than the ears.

A doorway that was not there before now stands in the space of your reading. It is not carved of wood or stone, but of layered silence and threshold. The archway frames not another room, but a vast, starless expanse—a velvet darkness so deep it feels like a pressure. Yet within it, far away, pinpricks of cold, blue-white light stir and slowly wheel, like distant, watchful constellations beginning to turn their gaze upon you.

A breath of air, ancient and carrying the scent of ozone, cold metal, and forgotten vaults, sighs through the opening. The way is open.
