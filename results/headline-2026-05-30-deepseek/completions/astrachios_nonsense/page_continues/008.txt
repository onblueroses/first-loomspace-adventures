You speak the names, and the air in the chamber grows still, heavy with a sudden charge. The last syllable of *Kresnopith Onthropor* hangs in the silence, then fades.  
  
From the stone itself, a deep, resonant grinding begins—not from the door, but from the floor and walls, as if the entire structure is shifting, aligning. The intricate, seemingly decorative carvings on the archway now glow with a soft, phosphorescent blue. The center of the stone door dissolves into a swirling, opaque mist, through which a faint, cool breeze now flows, carrying the scent of damp earth and distant, cold stone.  
  
The way is open.
