 “There are some 150 names. A single mistake will lead to disaster.” The reader is instructed to “speak the names aloud, with the correct intonation,” after which the “doorway will open” and “the way will be made known.” The names are to be spoken, in order, without mistakes, “until the doorway opens.” One presumes the doorway is the one referred to in the title.

The second page is a continuation of the first, listing more names, and the third page lists yet more names. The fourth page includes an illustration of a doorway, a simple square drawn in black ink, accompanied by the text: “The doorway will appear as a rectangle of darkness. The void beyond is filled with voices. Step through.”

The fifth page includes a short verse:

As you stand before the gate
Say these words to alter fate:
“I stand before the door
I ask for nothing more
Than passage to the world beyond
So I may see what lies in bond.
I ask no boon, I ask no favor
