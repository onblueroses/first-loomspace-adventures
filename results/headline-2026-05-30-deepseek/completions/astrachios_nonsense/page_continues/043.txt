You have spoken the names. A low hum begins to vibrate through the floor, and the runes carved into the ancient stone door before you flare with a soft, blue light. The massive slab, once seamless, now shows a hairline crack down its center. With a groan of stone on stone that has not sounded for millennia, the door begins to swing inward, revealing a darkness that seems to swallow the very light from the glowing runes. A breath of air, cold and smelling of damp earth and ozone, sighs out from the opening. The way is open. What do you do?
