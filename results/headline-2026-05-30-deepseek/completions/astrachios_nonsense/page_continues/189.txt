 ‘At the end of the invocation a place will be made for you among the mighty ones. You will be able to command the spirits of the air to serve you.’

The invocation is to be recited twenty-one times, presumably to build up a rhythm of concentration and expectation.

The next page gives instructions for making a magic mirror in which one can see spirits and have them answer questions. The instructions are given in German:

First, prepare a small mirror, which is to be made of polished steel. Then go to a place where four roads cross, at midnight when the moon is shining. There you must dig a hole and bury the mirror face downwards. After seven days, go to the same place at midnight, dig up the mirror, and take it home. When you look into it, you will see a spirit. You may ask it three questions.

The book ends with a prayer to be said before retiring for the night:
