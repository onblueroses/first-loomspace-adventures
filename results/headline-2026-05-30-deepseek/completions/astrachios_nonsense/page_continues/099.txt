 "Then say: 'Thus I invoke you, great and mighty angels, to open the gate that I may enter and receive the knowledge and conversation of my holy guardian angel. I entreat you by the great and mighty names of God: El, Elohim, Elohi, Zebaoth, Elion, Escherchie, Adonai, Jah, Tetragrammaton, Shaddai.' "

The angelic names in the opening are taken from Dee's Enochian calls, with the exception of the last two names, which are from the Necronomicon.

There follows a description of the ritual itself, which uses the same temple layout as the standard Bornless Ritual. The magician is to stand before the altar and recite the Bornless Ritual. Then he is to go to the East and recite the Invocation of the Angels. After this, he is to go to the West and recite the Invocation of the Demons. Finally, he is to go to the South and recite the Invocation of the Demons of the South. Then he is to return to the altar and recite the Bornless Ritual again.
