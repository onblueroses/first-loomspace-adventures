The second prayer to the moon, a list of twenty-eight names, is very similar to the first. It begins: “I call on you, Mistress of the entire world, ruling over Tartaros and Chaos and Hades.” Again the names are listed:

Sphorphor, Polisnopha, Klimp, Tronosemath, Vronk, Asnopolisa, Polisornen, Krelmonor, Antrundefax, Smerphol, Tromporada, Onthelosk, Engrokitha, Phenrolop, Ontrolanker, Rephonex, Tholgardosen, Phelorpa, Onth, Kromphis, Vrindat, Posnophev, Kresnopontren, Snolpharod, Vronth, Tholerpa, Kroslendep, Mreph.
