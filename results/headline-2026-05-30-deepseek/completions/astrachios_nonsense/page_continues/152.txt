(With a low, resonant hum, the air seems to thicken. A faint scent of ozone and ancient stone dust fills the space. The names, spoken aloud, hang in the silence not as mere words, but as completed sigils.)

The way is open. The door, unseen before, is now present in the mind's eye—a great, weathered arch of basalt veined with silver, etched with the very syllables you have uttered. It stands neither here nor there, but *between*.

A deep, grinding sound echoes, as of stone sliding against stone from a profound depth. The archway is no longer empty. Within its frame swirls a mist the color of tarnished moonlight, through which distant, jagged peaks might be glimpsed, or perhaps are merely shadows cast by a different sun.

It waits. The passage is yours to enter, if you will. But know this: to speak the key is also to be known by the lock. The path now recognizes your voice.
