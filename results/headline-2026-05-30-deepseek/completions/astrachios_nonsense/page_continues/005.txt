THE GRIMOIRE OF POPE HONORIUS III

The Grimoire of Pope Honorius III

The Grimoire of Pope Honorius III (Le Grimoire du Pape Honorius) is a French grimoire dated to the 1620s.\(^8\) It is a book of black magic, or goetia, containing instructions for summoning demons, selling one’s soul to the Devil, and performing acts of vengeance, including murder.
