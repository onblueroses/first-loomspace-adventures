 "And after the second conjuration, say: 'Tholex, Vraun, Tron, Phel, Phol, Phos, Phor, Phol, Phos, Phor, Phol, Phos, Phor, Phol, Phos, Phor.'"

The names are apparently meaningless gibberish, but they are used to prepare the way for the invocation of the entity. After the conjurations, the page instructs: "Say: 'I conjure you, O spirit named X, and I exorcise you by the holy names of God: Ehyeh Asher Ehyeh, Yah, El, Elohim, Elohe Zebaoth, Elohim Sabaoth, El Chai, Yod He Vau He, Adonai, Ehyeh, Yod He Vau He, Adonai Zebaoth, Shaddai, Lord God, that you appear to me here in this crystal stone (or mirror) without any delay, and without doing any harm to me or to any other living creature.
