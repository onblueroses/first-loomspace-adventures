As you speak the names, you feel a sudden chill in the air. You see a shimmering portal appear before you.

You step through the portal and find yourself in a vast cavern. In the center of the cavern stands a giant statue of a dragon. The statue is made of black stone and its eyes glow with a fiery red light.

You approach the statue and see that there is a small door at the base of the statue. You open the door and step inside.
