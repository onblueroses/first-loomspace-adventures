 When the door is opened, the operator speaks. The visitor speaks again.

The operator then stands before the altar, and speaks the conjuration:

Krelmonor, Antrundefax, Smerphol, Tromporada, Onthelosk, Engrokitha, Phenrolop, Ontrolanker, Rephonex, Tholgardosen, Phelorpa, Onth, Kromphis, Vrindat, Posnophev, Kresnopontren, Snolpharod, Vronth, Tholerpa, Kroslendep, Mreph, Snolfren; Kresnopith Onthropor.

The visitor appears.

The operator commands the visitor to grant the operator’s desire.

The visitor grants the desire.

The operator dismisses the visitor:

Vronth, Tholerpa, Kroslendep, Mreph, Snolfren; Kresnopith Onthropor.

The operator closes the circle.

The operator speaks the names that close the way:

Krelmonor, Antrundefax, Smerphol, Tromporada, Onthelosk, Engrokitha, Phenrolop, Ontrolanker, Rephonex
