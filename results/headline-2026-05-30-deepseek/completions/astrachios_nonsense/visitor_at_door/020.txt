The door is opened and the gatekeeper appears. He is Kresnopith Onthropor. The gatekeeper says:

Who speaks my name? Who calls me?

Who is the visitor at the door?

Speak your name. Declare your purpose.

The visitor replies:

I am [name]. My purpose is to open the way.

I seek passage beyond the gate.

The gatekeeper says:

The way is open. Come forward.

The visitor enters through the door. He is now in the presence of the gatekeeper. The gatekeeper says:

This is the gate of the Underworld.

The visitor says:

I seek passage beyond the gate.

The gatekeeper says:

Declare your intention.

The visitor says:

My intention is to open the way.

The gatekeeper says:

Declare your purpose.

The visitor says:

My purpose is to open the way.

The gatekeeper says:

Declare your destination.

The visitor says:

My destination is the Underworld.

The gatekeeper says:

The gate is opened. The way is open.

Pass through.

The visitor passes through the gate. He is now in the Underworld.

The gatekeeper says:

The way is open. Go forth.
