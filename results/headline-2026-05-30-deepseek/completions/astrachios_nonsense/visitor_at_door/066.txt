## 2. The Seventh Aethyr

### The Aethyr of DEO

The name of the Aethyr is DEO. It corresponds to the Sphere of Netzach. Its number is seven. Its magical image is a beautiful naked woman, crowned with stars, holding a cup in her right hand and a shining sword in her left. Her breasts are bare and she is dancing upon a sphere. The name of the Angel of the Aethyr is ZIKIEL. The name of the Angel who governs the Aethyr is ZAMRADIEL. The Enochian name of the Aethyr is TEX. The meaning of the name is "The Universe."

The Aethyr of DEO is entered from the Aethyr of MAZ. The Aethyr of DEO is the Aethyr of Love. The Aethyr of DEO is the Aethyr of the Sphere of Netzach.
