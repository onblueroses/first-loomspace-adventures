 The sound is a soft, high-pitched hissing, like water on a hot skillet. The words are not heard so much as felt: "I am here." The hissing voice continues: "I have come through the door of seven stars. I have come through the gate of seven seals. I have come through the door of seven rays. I have come through the gate of seven fires. I am here. I have come."

The demon's name is Inzuban. He appears as a small, dark figure, wreathed in a cloak of shadow that hides his face. He is a servitor of the star god Koth. Inzuban is a spirit of darkness, and his power is to cause blindness and to shroud an area in magical darkness. He is also a spirit of disease, and he can cause the plague.

When he speaks, his voice is a soft, high-pitched hiss. He will perform one service for the summoner, if he is properly propitiated.
