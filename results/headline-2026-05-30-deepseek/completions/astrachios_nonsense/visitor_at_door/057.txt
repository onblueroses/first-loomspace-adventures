 He says: "I am He who sits upon the Throne; I am the Lord of the Unborn; I am the Bearer of the Cup of Immortality. I am the One who holds the Keys to the Gate of Death. I am the Watcher at the Threshold. I am the Angel of the Lord of the Sabbath."

Say: "I call upon you, O Watcher at the Threshold, O Guardian of the Gate, who holds the Keys to the Gate of Death, to open the door for me, so that I may enter the Temple of the Lord of the Sabbath, and partake of the Mysteries of the Cup of Immortality."

The visitor at the door says: "What is the word?"

Say: "The word is 'AUMGN.'"

The door opens. You see a great chamber, lit by many torches. At the far end of the chamber is a great throne of gold, upon which sits a figure whose face is hidden by a veil. He holds in his left hand a golden cup, and in his right hand a golden key. He is dressed in white robes, and upon his head is a crown of gold. He is the Lord of the Sabbath.
