Trial of the Stairs

One of the most common tests that the adept will encounter in the process of initiation is the Trial of the Stairs. This is not one trial, but a series of tests that can be repeated many times as the adept moves along the path of ascension. The tests are not always the same, but the pattern is: the adept must ascend a staircase that leads to a door or gate, but each step of the stairway is blocked by a guardian. The adept must answer a riddle or solve a puzzle before they can advance to the next step.

Each guardian will present a different challenge. The Trial of the Stairs is an obstacle that lies between the adept and a place they need to reach. It can be a test of knowledge, of wit, of strength, of courage, or of character.

The Trial of the Stairs may be encountered in the astral realm, in dreams, or in meditation. It can also be used as a ritual, performed in the material world to test an initiate or to prepare them for a greater challenge.
