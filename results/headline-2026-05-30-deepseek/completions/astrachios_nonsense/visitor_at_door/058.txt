## The Ninth Orison of the Winds

### (This is the Orison of the Winds of the Southeast)

The Southeast Wind is a great and terrible wind. It is the wind of the desert, the wind of the dry places. It is the wind of the dead, the wind of the forgotten. It is the wind of the lost, the wind of the forsaken. It is the wind of the damned, the wind of the cursed.

The Southeast Wind is a wind of fire. It is the wind of the sun, the wind of the heat. It is the wind of the flame, the wind of the blaze. It is the wind of the inferno, the wind of the conflagration.

The Southeast Wind is a wind of sand. It is the wind of the dune, the wind of the dune sea. It is the wind of the sandstorm, the wind of the sandblast. It is the wind of the sand dervish, the wind of the sand whirl.
