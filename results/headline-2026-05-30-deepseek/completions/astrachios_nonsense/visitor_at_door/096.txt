A low hum seems to pulse from the stone itself as you complete the list. The air grows still and heavy. From the deep shadows beyond the threshold, a voice replies, not with a sound, but as a cold thought forming directly in your mind:

*"The seals are named. The path is acknowledged. But names are only keys. The door opens for the one who knows the question that lies between 'Glothenkion' and 'Kresnopith Onthropor.' State your purpose, or become part of the silence between the words."*

The doorway remains dark, an open maw waiting. It has not invited you in; it has simply stopped forbidding you. The next move is yours.
