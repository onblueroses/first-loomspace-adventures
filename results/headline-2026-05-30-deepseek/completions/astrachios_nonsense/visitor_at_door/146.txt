 His name is Trenchov. He says: I have come to speak with the one who is known as the Master of the House.

The Master of the House appears.

The Master of the House says: I am the Master of the House.

The visitor says: I am Trenchov.

The Master of the House says: What brings you here?

The visitor says: I have come seeking instruction in the ways of power.

The Master of the House says: The instruction you seek will cost you dearly. Are you prepared to pay the price?

The visitor says: I am prepared to pay the price.

The Master of the House says: Then step within, and welcome.

The visitor crosses the threshold.

The Master of the House says: The instruction you seek will take many years. Do you have the patience?

The visitor says: I have the patience.

The Master of the House says: The instruction you seek will take all your strength. Do you have the strength?

The visitor says: I have the strength.

The Master of the House says: The instruction you seek will take all your courage. Do you have the courage?

The visitor says: I have the courage.

The Master of the House says: Then welcome, and be at peace.

The visitor bows
