 It says:

I have come. I have heard the call. I have come to answer the call. I will not harm the caller unless the caller harms me. I will not harm the caller unless the caller harms me. I will not harm the caller unless the caller harms me.

Now you may speak. You may ask one question. You may ask one question and I will answer.

Now I ask: Are you the spirit of the dead who rests in this grave? Answer me now.

Now I have answered. I have answered truthfully.

Now I will depart.

You may command me to depart.

I will depart. I will depart and not return. I will depart and not return unless called again.

Now speak the words of parting:
