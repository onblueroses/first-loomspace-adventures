 What do you say?

I am Setan’tel, the messenger of the house of Set.

We are open to you, Setan’tel.

The door opens, and a figure in black robes enters. He is tall and thin, with black hair and dark eyes. He wears a silver ankh around his neck.

Greetings, O priests of the Temple of Set. I bring you greetings from the Great Lord Set, the true god of the Black Flame. He has heard your prayers and your praises, and he is pleased with your devotion. He sends me to you as his messenger, to guide you and to teach you. For he has a great work for you to do, a work that will shake the foundations of the world and bring about the new age of Set.

What is this work, O messenger of Set?

The messenger smiles, a cold and cruel smile.

The work is this: you are to awaken the ancient ones, the gods of old, who sleep beneath the earth and the sea. You are to call them forth from their slumber, and to offer them sacrifices and worship. You are to become their priests and their prophets, and to spread their cult throughout the land.
