 What is the password?

The password is "Kresnopith Onthropor." (The password is the last two names.)

The guardian asks for the password. You reply with the last two names. The guardian opens the door. You enter the room.

The room is empty, except for a large mirror on the far wall. The mirror is cracked and dusty. You see your reflection in the mirror. Suddenly, your reflection begins to move independently of you. It steps out of the mirror and stands before you.

Your reflection speaks. "I am you, but not you. I am the part of you that you have forgotten. I am the part of you that you have left behind. I am the part of you that you have lost. I am the part of you that you have given up. I am the part of you that you have sacrificed. I am the part of you that you have abandoned. I am the part of you that you have betrayed. I am the part of you that you have denied. I am the part of you that you have rejected. I am the part of you that you have disowned. I am the part of you that you have forgotten."
