The visitor at the door replies:

I am a child of Earth and starry heaven, but my race is heavenly. This you know well yourselves. And I am dry with thirst and perishing. Give me quickly to drink of cool water from the fountain of memory.

The speaker at the door replies:

You are a child of Earth and starry heaven. You are of heavenly race. I know you well. I am the son of Earth and starry heaven. I am dry with thirst. Give me to drink.

The speaker at the door replies:

Enter, you who are purified by the rite.

You will see the pure company of the happy ones.

The speaker at the door replies:

I am pure. I come from the pure, O Queen of those below the earth, Eukles, Eubouleus, and other gods and daimones. For I too claim to be of your blessed race.
