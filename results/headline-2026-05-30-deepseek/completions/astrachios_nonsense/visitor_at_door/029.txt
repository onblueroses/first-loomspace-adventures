 He says:

Kresnopith Onthropor.

This is my name. Open the door.

The guardian within responds:

I hear your name. But what is your seal?

The visitor says:

I am sealed with a seal.

The guardian responds:

Then show me your seal.

The visitor shows the seal.

The guardian says:

You are sealed with a seal.

Enter and be welcome.

The guardian opens the door and says:

Enter, O Kresnopith Onthropor.

The visitor enters and says:

I am entered.

The guardian closes the door and says:

I close the door behind you.

The visitor says:

Let the door be closed.

The guardian says:

The door is closed.

The visitor says:

I have entered the house.

The guardian says:

Welcome to the house.

The visitor says:

Thank you for your welcome.

The guardian says:

You are welcome.

The visitor says:

I am the visitor.

The guardian says:

I am the guardian.

The visitor says:

I am the guest.

The guardian says:

I am the host.

The visitor says:

I am the stranger.

The guardian says:

I am the familiar.

The visitor says:

I am the unknown.

The guardian says:

I am the known.
