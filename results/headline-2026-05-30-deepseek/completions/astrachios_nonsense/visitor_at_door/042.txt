 Say:

Who comes to the gate?

The visitor answers, saying:

I am the master of the gate, the keeper of the keys. The one who brings the power of the god.

Say:

Enter, O master of the gate, keeper of the keys. Bring the power of the god.

The gate opens and the visitor enters.

The visitor stands before you. Say:

Speak your name, O master of the gate.

The visitor speaks his name, saying:

I am Horkos, the master of the gate.

Say:

Speak the secret name, O master of the gate.

The visitor speaks his secret name, saying:

I am Horkos, the master of the gate, whose secret name is Horkos.

Say:

Speak the hidden name, O master of the gate.

The visitor speaks his hidden name, saying:

I am Horkos, the master of the gate, whose hidden name is Horkos.

Say:

Horkos, master of the gate, keeper of the keys, bringer of the power of the god, I am ready to receive the power of the god. Give me the power of the god.

Horkos says:

I give you the power of the god.
