THE PRAYER OF THE HOUR OF MIDNIGHT

In the name of the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, Amen.

Almighty God, whose Son our Savior Jesus Christ has triumphed over the powers of death and hell, and has ascended in glory to the throne of heaven: As we have been buried with him in baptism, and raised with him to newness of life, so may we triumph over death and hell, and being made partakers of the resurrection, ascend with him to the realms of light and glory; where he lives and reigns with you and the Holy Spirit, one God, forever and ever. Amen.

The Prayer of the Hour of Midnight is a public domain prayer from the Anglican tradition.
