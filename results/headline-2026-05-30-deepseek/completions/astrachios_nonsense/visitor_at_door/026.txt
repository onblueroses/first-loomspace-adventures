 He says:

I have come from the West, and I have been given the names. I stand before the gate, and I have the key. I stand before the gate, and I will open the gate.

The guardian of the gate speaks. He says:

What are the names that open the gate?

The visitor at the gate speaks. He says:

The names that open the gate are:

Vronth, Tholerpa, Kroslendep, Mreph, Snolfren; Kresnopith Onthropor.

The gate opens. The guardian of the gate speaks. He says:

Enter and be welcome.

The visitor enters. The guardian of the gate speaks. He says:

The keys are given. The gate is open. I give you the key to the gate of the East.
