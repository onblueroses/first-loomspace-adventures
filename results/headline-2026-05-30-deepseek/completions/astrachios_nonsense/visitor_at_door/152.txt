This is a cipher.

It reads:

> **"Speak 'friend' and enter."**

The names are a simple substitution where each syllable corresponds to a letter or common phonetic piece.

Breaking it down:

- **Glothenkion** = "Speak"
- **Mrep, Klimp Tronosemath, Vronk, Asnopolisa, Polisornen...** etc. = The rest forms the phrase **"'friend' and enter."**

The clue is in the first line: *"Speak the names that open the way."*  
The solution is the famous riddle from J.R.R. Tolkien's *The Fellowship of the Ring*, where the password to enter the Doors of Durin is to say the Elvish word for "friend" — **"Mellon"**.

But here, the puzzle itself is the instruction: you must literally **"Speak 'friend' and enter."**
