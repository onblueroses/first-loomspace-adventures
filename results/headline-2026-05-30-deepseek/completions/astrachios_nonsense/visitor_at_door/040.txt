 What is it that you seek?

Come then, and be welcome.

The visitor enters the temple, and the gate is closed behind him. The visitor speaks:

I am (name). I come to speak the secret names that open the gates of the aether. I am (name), and I seek the mysteries of the aether.

The gatekeeper speaks:

I am the gatekeeper. I am the keeper of the secret names. I am the opener of the gates. I am the guide of the traveler. I am the guardian of the temple.

The gatekeeper and the visitor approach the altar, where the gatekeeper speaks:

I give you the secret names that open the gates of the aether. Speak them, and the gates shall open.
