 She replies:

‘I have come to open the way.’

‘Enter.’

She passes through the door.

Now the powers of the West, the powers of the North, the powers of the East, and the powers of the South are summoned, each in their turn, and the door is sealed.

The officers now take up their places, the herald to the West, the director to the North, the guardian to the East, the sentinel to the South. The director of the rite stands in the centre of the space.

The director says:

‘We are assembled in this place, which is outside of time, to summon the powers of the night and of the Abyss. We stand at the centre of the crossways where the paths of the worlds meet, and we call forth the powers of the four directions.’

The herald says:

‘I call the powers of the West: the powers of the water, of the depths, of the void, of dissolution and of oblivion, of silence and of peace, of the moon and of the night. Let the powers of the West be present.’
