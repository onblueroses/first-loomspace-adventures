 It is a voice of thunder:

“The Master of the Universe has sent me to you!”

The door opens wide and the visitor enters.

This is the visitor’s form: he is like a man of bronze. He is huge and terrible, and the soles of his feet are like those of a calf. His eyes are like the wheel of the Chariot, and his eyelashes are like bolts of lightning, and his tongue is like a fiery torch. His hair is like fine gold, and the diadem on his head is like a royal crown. His hands are like a man’s hands, but his arms are like those of an angel. He wears a cloak of lightning, and his legs are like pillars of fire.

The visitor says to the magician:

“Peace be with you!”

The magician says to the visitor:

“My lord, I am the one who has called you, and I have made this offering to you.”

The visitor says:

“I know why you have called me. What do you want? Ask, and I will do it!”

The magician says:

“My lord, I wish you to do thus and so.”

The visitor says:

“I will do it!”
