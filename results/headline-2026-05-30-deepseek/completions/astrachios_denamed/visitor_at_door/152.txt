ASTRAL WORKINGS OF THE FIFTH HOUSE

The astral working of the fifth house is the working of the solar plexus, the seat of the subtle emotional body and the emotional astral body. The solar plexus is the gateway to the heart. The heart is the seat of the soul, and the soul can only be accessed by passing through the solar plexus. This is the “pit of the stomach” that is the seat of the emotions, as well as of the subtle emotional body. The subtle emotional body is the body of emotional memory. It is the memory of the heart. The subtle emotional body is also the seat of the body of desire, the subtle body that is the source of the passions. The solar plexus is the seat of the subtle emotional body and the subtle desire body. The solar plexus is the gateway to the heart. The heart is the seat of the soul. The soul can only be accessed by passing through the solar plexus.
