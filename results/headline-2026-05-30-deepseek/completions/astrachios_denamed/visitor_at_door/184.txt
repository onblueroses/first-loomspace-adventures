 Say:

You who have come to my door, speak your name and your intention.

The visitor may respond. If the response is favorable, say:

Enter, then, and be welcome.

If the response is not favorable, say:

I deny you entry. Begone!

Open the door.

The visitor may enter.

When the visitor leaves, say:

I bid you farewell. May peace be between us now and forever.

Close the door.

The Ritual of the Opening of the Mouth

The Ritual of the Opening of the Mouth is used to consecrate a new image or statue of a god or goddess. The rite is performed for the statue before it is set in place in a shrine or temple. The ritual is performed by a priest of the god or goddess for whom the image is made.

The priest takes up a wand of aspen wood, tipped with copper. He says:

I open your mouth with the wand of aspen and copper.

The priest touches the statue's mouth with the wand. He says:

I open your eyes with the wand of aspen and copper.

The priest touches the statue's eyes with the wand. He says:

I open your ears with the wand of aspen and copper.
