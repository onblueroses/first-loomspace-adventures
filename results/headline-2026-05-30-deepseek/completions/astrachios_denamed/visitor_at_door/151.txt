 He asks: "Is there any man?" The magician answers: "Yes, we are here. Who are you? And what is your name?" The visitor says: "I am the archangel Anael." The magician asks: "What is your request? What do you want?" The visitor says: "I have come because you have called me." The magician says: "Welcome, Anael. I have called you because I want you to do something for me." The visitor says: "What is your request? What do you want?" The magician says: "I want you to bring to me my beloved one." The visitor says: "What is the name of your beloved one?" The magician says: "Her name is ..." The visitor says: "I will bring her to you." The visitor disappears.
