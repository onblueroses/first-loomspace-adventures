 The names of the two entities that stand on either side of the door are:

Bazazath, Alath.

The door is opened. The visitor says:

Behold, I am come to the door.

The spirits who guard the door say:

Enter, for you are the chosen one.

The visitor enters, and sees the two spirits. He says:

I ask you, spirits who guard the door, to tell me your names.

The spirits answer:

Bazazath, Alath.

The visitor says:

I ask you, Bazazath and Alath, to grant me passage into the house of the book.

The spirits say:

You may enter, for you are the chosen one.

The visitor passes between the spirits, and proceeds until he sees the one who holds the book. He says:

Behold, I am come to the place of the book.

The one who holds the book says:

I am the keeper of the book.

The visitor says:

I ask you, keeper of the book, to tell me your name.

The keeper says:

I am Yadahel.

The visitor says:

I ask you, Yadahel, to grant me the book.

Yadahel says:

You may take the book, for you are the chosen
