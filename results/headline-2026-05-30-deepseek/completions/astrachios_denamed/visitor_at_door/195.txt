 The guardian speaks. The guardian and the visitor converse. The visitor enters.

The visitor is a woman. She wears a white dress, and her hair is long and black. Her eyes are black, and her skin is pale. She carries a small iron knife. She holds it out. The guardian takes it. The guardian speaks.

The guardian says: This knife is a sign of the Great Mother, who gave life to the universe. It is a sign of the spirit of iron, which is the spirit of the earth. It is a sign of the element earth.

The guardian gives the knife back to the visitor. The guardian speaks.

The guardian says: You are welcome in this circle. Come in.

The visitor enters the circle. She stands in the east.

The guardian stands in the east. The guardian speaks.

The guardian says: I call the powers of the east, the powers of air. I call the powers of the east, the powers of dawn. I call the powers of the east, the powers of spring.
