 He says: “Open the way.” And the adept says:

“The door is opened. Enter, and do not hinder my work. But if you do, I shall invoke against you the one who shattered the old and the new gods, and the one whose name is Iao Sabao, the one who will shatter you and destroy your power forever.”

Then the adept says:

“Hear me, you great god, eternal and living, whose name is Abrasax. Abrasax, Abrasax. May my word reach your ears, for I am going to say the great name that your father spoke to you before he gave you authority over heaven and earth, and before he gave you the authority to judge angels and men, and before he gave you authority over everything that is in heaven and on earth and under the earth and in the abyss. Hear me, therefore, because I am going to say the name that your father gave you. It is: Arbabra, Arbabra, Arbabra, Arbabra, Arbabra, Arbabra, Arbabra.
