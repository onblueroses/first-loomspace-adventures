 If the visitor is evil, you will see that he will be forced to flee when he hears these names. If the visitor is good, you will see that he will be quieted and will speak kindly to you.

**Against a headache**

For a headache, take a leaf of verbena and press it against your forehead. Say these words:

In nomine Patris et Filii et Spiritus Sancti, Amen. Crux Christi sit super caput meum. Amen. Crux Christi sit super os meum. Amen. Crux Christi sit super cor meum. Amen. Crux Christi sit super totum corpus meum. Amen. Crux Christi ante me sit. Amen. Crux Christi post me sit. Amen. Crux Christi a dextris meis sit. Amen. Crux Christi a sinistris meis sit. Amen. Crux Christi sit in pectore meo, ut salvet me a dolore capitis. Amen.

**To get a favor from a woman**

If you want to get a favor from a woman, go to her house. When she is not looking, take a pinch of dirt from the threshold of her door and put it in her drink. Say these words:

Veni ad me
