 It is the same voice that was heard at the beginning of the rite. It says:

I am that I am.

The Magus responds:

Welcome, O Lord of the Universe, welcome O Lord of the Earth, welcome O Lord of the Abyss. Welcome, O Lord of the Underworld. Welcome, O Lord of the Waters. Welcome, O Lord of the Air. Welcome, O Lord of the Fire. Welcome, O Lord of the Stars. Welcome, O Lord of the Sun. Welcome, O Lord of the Moon.

The Magus walks slowly to the door, and opens it. He says:

Welcome, O Lord of the Gods. Welcome, O Lord of the World. Welcome, O Lord of the Spirits. Welcome, O Lord of the Dead. Welcome, O Lord of the Living. Welcome, O Lord of the Magicians.

The Magus stands aside to let the visitor enter. It is a tall man, dressed in a suit of modern cut but of ancient design. He is not young, but his face is unlined. His hair is silver-white, but his eyes are black. He walks to the center of the circle, and stands there.

The Magus closes the door, and returns to his place at the altar
