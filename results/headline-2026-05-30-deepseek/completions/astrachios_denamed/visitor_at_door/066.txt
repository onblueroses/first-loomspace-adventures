 “I am Genilum Sopro.”

The magician asks, “Why have you come?” “To bring the spirit of the dead, so that you may speak with him.”

The magician asks, “What do you require?” “A dove, that I may take it with me.”

The magician says, “Take it, and go your way.”

The magician then says to the spirit of the dead, “I conjure you by the name of Adonai, the great god, that you come to me and do not depart until I send you away, and that you answer truthfully the questions I shall ask you.”

The magician then asks the spirit the questions he wishes to ask.

When the questioning is finished, the magician says, “Because you have come peacefully and answered truthfully, I give you license to depart, and may there be peace between me and you. Go in peace.”

The magician then dismisses the spirit by saying: “I dismiss you, spirit, by the power of the great god Adonai, that you may return to your proper place and cause no harm to anyone. Amen.”
