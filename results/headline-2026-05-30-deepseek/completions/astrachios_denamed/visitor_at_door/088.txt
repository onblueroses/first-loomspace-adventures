The conjuration of the Wandering Princes in the Aethyr

Now that you have opened the way, you must conjure the spirits. The text of the conjuration is:

I conjure you, O you wandering princes of the Aethyrs, by the holy names of God, and by the great and terrible word, and by the holy names of God, that you appear in visible form here before this circle, and that you answer my demands truly, without falsehood or deceit.

I conjure you by the most high and holy names of God, Tetragrammaton, Adonai, El, Elohim, Elohe, Zebaoth, Elion, and by all the holy names of God, that you appear here, and show yourselves visibly unto me, in this crystal stone, and that you deliver true answers unto all my questions and demands.
