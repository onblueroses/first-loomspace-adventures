 The man in the chair listens. The voice of the visitor sounds like a man with a mouth full of stones:

—I am the living god, who lives for ever, whose existence is everlasting.

—I am the Lord of the silent ones.

—I am the Lord of the void.

—I am the Lord of the pit.

—I am the Lord of the grave.

—I am the Lord of the tomb.

—I am the Lord of the great city of the dead.

—I am the Lord of the sleeping ones.

—I am the Lord of the dead.

—I am the Lord of the living.

—I am the Lord of all that is.

—I am the Lord of all that is not.

—I am the Lord of all that will be.

—I am the Lord of all that will not be.

—I am the Lord of all that has been.

—I am the Lord of all that has not been.

—I am the Lord of all that will never be.

—I am the Lord of all that is forever.

—I am the Lord of all that is forever not.

—I am the Lord of all that is forever lost.
