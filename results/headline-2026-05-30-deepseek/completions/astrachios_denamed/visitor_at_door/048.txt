 I hear the rustle of his robe.

"Hear me, Lord of the Skies, hear me, O You who appear as a man, who sits upon the throne of the sun, who is called Aratron. You who are the power of Saturn, You who rule the first sphere. I call to you by the names that are yours, names that make the powers of the spheres to tremble, names that cause the daemons to flee, names that are known to the angels, names that command the spirits of the earth."

The visitor speaks. He speaks his name: Aratron. His voice is deep and resonant, and yet it whispers in my soul.

"Hear me, O Aratron, you who are the Lord of the Sphere of Saturn, ruler of all that is hidden, all that is dark, all that is cold, all that is heavy. You who hold sway over lead, over the dark earth, over the stones of the earth, over the deep places of the earth. You who are the power of age, of time, of death, and of decay. You who are the power of winter, of the long night, of the still, cold stars.
