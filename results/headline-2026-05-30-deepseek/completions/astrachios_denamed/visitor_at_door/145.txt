Say: "I am not coming in until you give me the sign." The guardian spirit will give you the sign.

Then go in, and do not look at him, but go to your lamp and say: "Be welcome, powerful daimon." Then ask him for whatever you wish.

If he is tardy in coming, repeat the names often, and he will come. But if he should come in a terrifying form, do not be afraid. For he will do this to test you.

Go through with the divination.

Divination by a Bowl

Take a bronze vessel, either a bowl or a saucer. Pour rainwater into it, together with olive oil. Then bending over the vessel, say the spell on the oil seven times, and then the spell on the water seven times.

Also, look into the bowl and say: "Send up a daimon from the water who will tell me truthfully everything I want." And he will tell you everything truthfully.
