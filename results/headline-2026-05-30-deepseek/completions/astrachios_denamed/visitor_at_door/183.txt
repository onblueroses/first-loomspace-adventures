The visitor enters. He carries a white stone.

He says: “Tephras is my name. I am one of the Twelve Stone Masters. I have come to instruct you in the virtues of the stone. What do you ask of me?”

You say: “I ask to be instructed in the virtues of the stone.”

He says: “What stone do you hold?”

You say: “I hold a stone.”

He says: “What is its color?”

You say: “Its color is white.”
