 It says:

Open, open, open. The door is open.

The visitor is the first of the five spirits who will come. They are the guardians of the five gateways.

The first is the spirit of the gate of the East. It is called Atarphiel. It is the spirit of the first gate. It is the guardian of the eastern gate. Its name means: "You are the one who opens." Its color is white. Its shape is that of a tall man with a great sword.

The visitor at the door speaks. It says:

I am Atarphiel, guardian of the eastern gate. I am the spirit who opens the gate. I am the spirit who holds the key. I am the spirit who guards the way. I am the spirit who stands at the threshold. I am the spirit who watches. I am the spirit who waits. I am the spirit who comes when called. I am the spirit who answers. I am the spirit who speaks. I am the spirit who listens. I am the spirit who sees. I am the spirit who knows. I am the spirit who is. I am Atarphiel.
