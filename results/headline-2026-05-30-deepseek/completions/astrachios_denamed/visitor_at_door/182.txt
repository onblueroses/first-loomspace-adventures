 Listen to the voice, for this is the name of the spirit, and the name of its seal, and the name of its number. Then speak the name of the spirit. Then speak the name of its seal. Then speak its number.

The spirit comes. It stands before the gate. It speaks the word of power that opens the gate. Write this word in your book. Then speak it, and the gate shall be opened.

The spirit passes through the gate. It stands before you in the astral temple. It bows to the four directions, beginning in the east. It raises its arms to the heavens and says:

The spirit N., of the rank of R., of the dwelling of D., servant of the great king E.A., whose number is N., seal is S., comes into the presence of the magician M. to do his will. Hail to thee, M., in the sacred names.

The spirit kneels and awaits your command.

Speak to the spirit. Give it its charge, to be written in your book.

When the charge is given, the spirit says:

It shall be done.

Then speak the license to depart:

Spirit N., because thou camest when called, and because thou hast sworn to obey my charge
