 Then open the door and say:

Welcome, O divine presence. Enter, O Lord, and bless your servant with your presence.

The operator shall then say:

O Lord, behold your servant, who calls upon you, and grant that I may become a partaker of the divine substance, and that the grace of the Holy Spirit may dwell in me.

Then the operator shall say:

O Lord, Holy Father, almighty and everlasting God, for ever and ever. Amen.

Then shall follow the Benediction, with the Lord’s Prayer repeated three times.

Then the operator shall say:

O Lord Jesus Christ, who said to your disciples, “Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you,” grant to me that peace and life everlasting, that I may be cleansed from all my sins, and that I may be found worthy to receive this sacrament, and to be filled with your Holy Spirit. Amen.

Then the operator shall say:

O Lord, I am not worthy that you should come under my roof, but speak the word only and my soul shall be healed.
