# The Sacred Magic

The Sacred Magic of Abramelin is a system of ceremonial magic that was written down by Abraham of Worms (in his native German) and translated into French in the 18th century by a French occultist named Pierre d'Ascanio, who published the manuscript as _Le Livre de la Sainte Magie d'Abramelin le Mage_. In the early 20th century, English occultist Samuel Liddell MacGregor Mathers translated d'Ascanio's French into English, and published it as _The Book of the Sacred Magic of Abramelin the Mage_. The book is divided into three parts, which describe Abraham's personal story and journey, the preparatory rites, and the magical rites. It is essentially a grimoire, a book of magical spells. But it's more than that: it's a manual for summoning spirits and demons to do your bidding.
