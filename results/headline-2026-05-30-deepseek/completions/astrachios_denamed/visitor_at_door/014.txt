TECHNIQUE #7: THE RITE OF THE FLOOR

This technique requires a circle drawn on the floor. The circle should be large enough to sit within comfortably. It should be drawn with chalk, charcoal, or some other temporary medium. You can make the circle permanent if you wish, but it is better to use a temporary circle so that you can erase it when you are done.

Within the circle, draw a triangle. The triangle should be oriented so that one of its points faces north. The triangle should be large enough to sit within comfortably.

You will need to have a fire source within the triangle. It can be a candle, an oil lamp, or some other source of fire. It is also good to have a cup of water within the triangle.

Sit within the triangle. Face the point of the triangle that is directed to the north.
