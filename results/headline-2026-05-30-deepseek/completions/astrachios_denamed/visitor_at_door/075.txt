 He says: "I am the one who is sent by the one who is seated upon the throne, whose name is Iarbas."

He speaks further: "I have come to call you, O powerful one, to the great god, the one who is seated upon the throne, whose name is Iarbas."

He says further: "Behold, I am the one who calls you. Do not refuse to hear me, O god, for I am the one who is sent by the one who is seated upon the throne, whose name is Iarbas."

He says further: "The great god says to you: 'I have sent my angel to you. Do not refuse to hear him, O god, but come with him to me so that I may tell you what I wish.'"

And when you have said these things, he will come. And when you see him, greet him thus:

"Hail, lord, for I have called you in the name of the one who is seated upon the throne, whose name is Iarbas."

And he will say: "What do you wish?"

And you say: "I wish you to carry me into the presence of the great god."

And he will say: "Come with me."
