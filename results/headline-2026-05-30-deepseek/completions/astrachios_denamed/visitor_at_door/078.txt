 The door opens. The visitor steps forward, speaks again, and steps back. The visitor says:

I am Aeaeth, the great angel who stands in the presence of the holy God, and I have been sent by him to speak with you and show you his mercy. I have brought with me three angels. Their names are Afnaton, Anatreth, and Armhael.

Then the visitor says:

Behold, I have given you authority to tread upon serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy, and nothing shall by any means hurt you. Nevertheless, do not rejoice in this, but rejoice that your names are written in heaven.

The visitor says:

I give you a new commandment, that you love one another. Just as I have loved you, you also should love one another. By this everyone will know that you are my disciples, if you have love for one another.

The visitor says:

Peace I leave with you; my peace I give to you. I do not give to you as the world gives. Do not let your hearts be troubled, and do not let them be afraid.
