 It is time to open the door and listen to his words. He will tell you of the art. Be prepared to open the door in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit.

When the visitor has spoken, you may ask a question. But you should not ask more than one question. After you have asked your question, close the door.

The visitor will depart. You may then close the door.

The visitor says: “I am the guardian of the secret art. I will give you the secret art, but you must not reveal it to anyone except your own son. If you reveal it to anyone else, you will be cursed. You will die a miserable death. You will be cursed in the name of God.”

The secret art is this: “Take a piece of parchment. Write on it the name of the person you wish to harm. Write also the name of the person you wish to help. Then bury the parchment in the ground. The person you wish to harm will die. The person you wish to help will live.”

The visitor says: “This is the secret art. Do not reveal it to anyone except your own son.”

The visitor departs.

Close the door.

The ritual is complete.
