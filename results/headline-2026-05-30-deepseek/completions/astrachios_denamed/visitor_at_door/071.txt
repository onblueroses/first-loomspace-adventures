Who are you? What do you want? Why do you come here?

The visitor answers:

I am the one who opens the doors, the one who breaks the seals.

The magician asks:

What is your name?

The visitor answers:

My name is Azazel, the one who opens the way for the lord of the shadows.

The magician asks:

What do you want?

The visitor answers:

I want to open the way for the lord of the shadows.

The magician asks:

Why do you come here?

The visitor answers:

I come to open the way for the lord of the shadows.

The magician commands:

Then come in and open the way for the lord of the shadows!

The visitor enters the room.

The magician commands:

Azazel, open the way for the lord of the shadows!
