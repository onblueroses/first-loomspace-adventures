 The magician says:

Speak, but speak only truth. I adjure you by the seal of Solomon, which was given to him by God, and by the names by which Solomon bound you, and by his ring, which he placed on your finger, and by the three names by which God created the world, EIE, SAR, ELOEIM.\(^{20}\) Speak the truth and nothing but the truth.

The visitor says:

I am the spirit who stands before you. I am called Katanikotaël. I stand in the seventh heaven before God, and I am one of the seven spirits who stand continually in the presence of God. I am an angel of the Lord. I have been sent here to you by the Lord. I have come to do your will. I will tell you the truth and conceal nothing from you.

The magician says:

Welcome, O good spirit. I am the servant of the God who sent you. I adjure you by the holy names of God to tell me what I ask truthfully. I ask you first, do you come in peace?

The spirit answers:

I come in peace. I will tell you all things truthfully.
