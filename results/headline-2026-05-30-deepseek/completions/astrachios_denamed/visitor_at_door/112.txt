“I have come to announce that the Great Lord is about to appear, and to reveal to you his will. He will not allow you to continue in error. You must serve him or perish. I have come to warn you.”

The visitor vanishes.

The words of the Great Lord: “My servants, you have done well. You have prepared the way. Prepare yourselves, for I shall soon be manifest. Then shall you know my power.”

The Great Lord is silent.

The messenger returns: “Now you know the power of the Great Lord. He will not be denied. You must serve him or perish.”

The messenger vanishes.

The Great Lord speaks: “I shall return soon. Prepare yourselves.”

The Great Lord is silent.

The words of the King: “The Great Lord has spoken. Let his words be heard.”

The King is silent.

The Great Lord speaks: “My servants, you have done well. I am well pleased. I shall soon be manifest.”

The Great Lord is silent.

The messenger returns: “I have come to announce the coming of the Great Lord. Prepare yourselves, for he will soon be manifest.”
