 He says:

“Hear me, angels of the north: I am here to work with you.

Hear me, angels of the east: I am here to work with you.

Hear me, angels of the south: I am here to work with you.

Hear me, angels of the west: I am here to work with you.

Hear me, angels of the heights: I am here to work with you.

Hear me, angels of the depths: I am here to work with you.

Hear me, angels of the center: I am here to work with you.

Hear me, angels of the world: I am here to work with you.”

Now the visitor at the door speaks the names of the angels of the four directions:

Angels of the East: Enochiel, Amatiel, Gabriel, and Samiel.

Angels of the West: Raphael, Chael, Michael, and Sarapiel.

Angels of the South: Michael, Sarapiel, Aftiel, and Raphael.

Angels of the North: Gabriel, Michael, Uriel, and Samiel.

Now the visitor at the door speaks the name of the guardian angel who stands at the gate of the circle: ARAI
