 I cannot understand the words.

The visitor enters. The visitor is a spirit, a demon, an angel. I cannot perceive the visitor clearly. It takes up residence in the circle.

I call on you, great king Paymon, to instruct me in the art of memory.

The vision ends.

I must go now and make record of what I have seen.
