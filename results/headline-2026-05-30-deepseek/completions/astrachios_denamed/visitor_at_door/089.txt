 "I am from the land of the living, and I have come to petition you. Let him who is the guardian of this place, and the other spirits who are here, come before me. I beg you to listen to my words and answer my questions truthfully."

The figure appears to respond. "I will answer truthfully. What do you seek?"

The visitor asks his question.

The figure responds. "The answer is ______. If you have any other questions, ask them now."

If the visitor has more questions, he asks them, and the figure answers them truthfully.

When the visitor has finished his questioning, he says, "I thank you for your responses, and I will depart now. Go in peace."

The visitor leaves the circle, and the spirit disappears.

After the visitor has left the circle, the spirit master says, "The one who came is gone, and I command you to depart from this place in peace."

The spirit master then dismisses the spirits.
