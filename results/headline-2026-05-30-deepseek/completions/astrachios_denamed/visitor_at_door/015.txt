 He is called Amapel, and he will give the names of the angels who govern the four directions, and also the names of the angels who govern the four times of the day.

He says:

The angels of the east: Satquiel, Raphael, Samael, Michael, and Anael.

The angels of the west: Sachiel, Zamael, Cassiel, Michael, and Anael.

The angels of the north: Aratron, Phaleg, Och, Phul, and Hagith.

The angels of the south: Bethor, Raphael, Khamael, Michael, and Anael.

The angels of the day: Raphael, Michael, Gabriel, Samael, Sachiel, Zamael, Cassiel, and Anael.

The angels of the night: Aratron, Phaleg, Och, Phul, Hagith, Bethor, Raphael, and Khamael.

The angels of the first hour of the day: Michael, Satquiel, Raphael, Samael, Sachiel, Anael, Cassiel.

The angels of the second hour: Anael, Raphael, Michael, Sachiel, Zamael, Samael, Cassiel.
