Now open the door and behold the visitor.

Pause for a moment. Then, seeing the visitor, speak:

“I accept you, visitor, and I invite you into this house. Welcome.”

The visitor enters and stands before you.

I am the Angel who watches over the House and the Home. I am the Angel who watches over the Foundation and the Earth. I am the Angel who watches over the Stature and the Bone.
