 He says:

O God, Lord, ruler of all things, who are without beginning and without end, eternal, who established all things in their order, who gave to all things their names: now make this magic, this charm, this operation effective. Give it power, Lord, God of gods, master of masters, king of kings, ruler of rulers, creator of all things, protector of the universe, who stands above the heavens, who sits above the cherubim. Let my request be granted, and let me be heard. Let the things I ask for come to pass. Let the things I command be done. Let all things be favorable to me. Let no spirit resist me, no power, no angel, no demon, no archon, no matter what its nature. Let every spirit in heaven, on earth, and under the earth be subject to me. Let every power obey me. Let every demonic force be afraid of me. Let every spirit that flies in the air, and every phantom, be subject to me. Let every angelic force that is in heaven, on earth, and under the earth be afraid of me. Let every power of Satan be afraid of me. Let every power that has authority over anything be afraid of me
