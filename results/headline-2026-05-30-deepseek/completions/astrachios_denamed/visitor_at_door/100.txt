 I answer: “We are here.” He says, “I am here.”

“What is your name?” “My name is Asep.” “What do you want?” “I want to be heard.” “Come in.” And he comes in.

I see him, I ask him, “What do you want?” “I want to talk.” “What about?” “About the Secret.” “What secret?” “The secret of my name.” “Tell me.” He tells me. “Do you have a message for me?” “Yes.” “What is it?” “I will tell you.” “Tell me.” He tells me. “Do you have a command for me?” “Yes.” “What is it?” “I will tell you.” “Tell me.” He tells me. “Do you have a mission for me?” “Yes.” “What is it?” “I will tell you.” “Tell me.” He tells me.

Then I ask him, “Do you have a gift for me?” “Yes.” “What is it?” “I will give it to you.” “Give it to me.” He gives it to me.

Then I ask him, “Do you have a blessing for me?” “Yes.” “What is it?”
