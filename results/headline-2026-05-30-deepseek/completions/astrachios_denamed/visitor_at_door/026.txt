 "I come to you, Belzeboul, king of kings, prince of princes, lord of lords, the mighty ruler, the great ruler who rules over all kings, powers, and authorities. Hear me, and grant my petition, because I am invoking your name, which the whole world fears."

Belzeboul answers, "Who are you, and why do you invoke my name?"

The visitor says, "I am he who possesses the secret of Solomon son of David, and I invoke you by the seal that Solomon placed on the tongue of Jeremiah, and by the holy names that are written on the seal, and by Achthona, Cherchun, Menepson, Epatimon, and Ophiel, the great and honored angel. I invoke you to come to me, and I will not leave until you come, whether you want to or not. For I am Solomon. If you come, I will bind you with unbreakable chains of iron, and I will give you over to be destroyed by the destroying angels, who will throw you into outer darkness, into the blackness in the chaos of the abyss, into the deepest Sheol.
