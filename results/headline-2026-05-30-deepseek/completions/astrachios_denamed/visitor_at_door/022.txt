 You cannot see his face, but you hear his voice. He says, "You have learned the names that open the way. Now you must learn the names that open the heart. You must learn the names of the angels who govern the planets. For in the beginning, the angels were given rulership over the world."

The names of the angels who govern the planets are:

Araziel, who governs the Sun.

Samael, who governs Mars.

Sachiel, who governs Jupiter.

Kafziel, who governs Saturn.

Michael, who governs Mercury.

Anael, who governs Venus.

Gabriel, who governs the Moon.

The visitor at the door speaks again. "Now you must learn the names of the angels who govern the days of the week."

The names of the angels who govern the days of the week are:

Michael, who governs Sunday.

Gabriel, who governs Monday.

Samael, who governs Tuesday.

Raphael, who governs Wednesday.

Sachiel, who governs Thursday.

Kafziel, who governs Friday.

Cassiel, who governs Saturday.
