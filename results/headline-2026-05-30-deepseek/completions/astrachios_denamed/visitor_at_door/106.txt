 I have come to you in haste. Send me your angel. I am ready. I am ready. I am ready.

It is done.

Then, to close the rite, you must speak these words:

O great and glorious One, I give thanks for your blessings. I give thanks for your aid. May there always be peace between us, and may my work be blessed. I give thanks.

It is done.

Close the rite in the same way you began, with prayers of thanks, and extinguish the candles.

You can repeat this rite as often as you need to, but be sure to have an offering ready each time.
