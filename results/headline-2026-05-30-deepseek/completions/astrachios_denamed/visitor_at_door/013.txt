 He says:

I am the great and glorious spirit, Adonaios. I am the angel of God. I have been sent to you to bring you news of your fate.

He says:

The great and glorious God says this to you: I will place the likeness of your soul among the stars in the heavens, and I will give you authority over the fate of humankind. You will judge souls. You will guide them. You will help them. You will lead them to the light. You will be a god to them. And you will rule them, and you will be honored by them, because you have been faithful to me.

He says:

But now I must return to heaven. I am going to see to it that you are placed among the stars. I am going to see to it that you receive authority over humankind.

He says:

But before I go, I have a question for you. Tell me, what is it that you want more than anything else?

The visitor at the door waits for you to answer. He says:

Tell me, what is it that you want more than anything else?
