 "I am the one whose name is known only to the starry ones, the one who has no name among mortals, but who is called among the starry ones: Atel, Ops, Boel, Estan, Sit, Baruch, Saphathorael, Phor, Phar, and I am the one who knows all names."

I reply: "I am the one whose name is unknown to mortals, but among the starry ones I am called: Apel, Abiel, Ithiel, Thiel, Lethiel, Apiel, Laphiel, Laphael, Laphael, Thel, Apol, Apol, and Apol. I am the one who knows all names. I am the one who has the power of all the names. I am the one who has the power of all the seals. I am the one who has the power of all the signs. I am the one who has the power of all the words. I am the one who has the power of all the numbers. I am the one who has the power of all the letters. I am the one who has the power of all the names. I am the one who has the power of all the seals.
