 He says: "The one who enters here is a great and terrifying dragon; he is the one who holds the keys to the abyss."

You respond: "I know you, and I know the name you bear: Rahab. Enter into this place."

He enters. Then you say to him: "Why have you come?"

He responds: "Because you have invoked me."

You say: "What is your office?"

He responds: "I guard the abyss."

You say: "By what authority?"

He responds: "By the authority of the Lord of the Universe."

You say: "What are the names of those who are with you?"

He responds: "The first is Leviathan, the second is Behemoth, the third is Rahab, the fourth is Abaddon, the fifth is Dagon, the sixth is Belial, the seventh is Asmodeus."
