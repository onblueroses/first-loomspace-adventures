 This is the prayer:

“Hail, guardians of this place! Let me enter, for I am a star wandering from the height of heaven, seeking the refuge of your mansion.”

Then the magician should enter and go to the central point of the room and say:

“Hail, guardians of the secrets of the east! Hail, guardians of the secrets of the west! Hail, guardians of the secrets of the north! Hail, guardians of the secrets of the south! Hail, guardians of the secrets of the center! Be with me tonight!”

Then he should stand in the center, facing east, and say:

“Hail, Zazel, guardian of Saturn! Hail, Tzedeq, guardian of Jupiter! Hail, Madim, guardian of Mars! Hail, Shemesh, guardian of the Sun! Hail, Nogah, guardian of Venus! Hail, Kokab, guardian of Mercury! Hail, Levanah, guardian of the Moon! Hail, Rahatiel, guardian of the zodiac! Hail, Mashit, guardian of the decans! Hail, Sar ha-Panim, guardian of the planets! Hail, Samal, guardian of the element of fire!
