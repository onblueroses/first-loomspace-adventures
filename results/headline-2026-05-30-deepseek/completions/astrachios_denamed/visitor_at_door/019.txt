 When he speaks, call him by his name:

"Come in, lord, because I know your name: you are Asthrenios, Asep, Acla Berdimoriel, Sipat, Aramonis, Ireharel, Ipeodoc, Anchanzel, Bloutel, Baracaza, Adopal, Elotin, Enagro, Aphragoseh, Sapoel, Gepulanel, Cariso, Ena, Epoci, Akrah, Ebpisha, Imathadel, Damiol, Dapa, Elapos, Izaphel, Paeh, Scildon; Genilum Sopro."

He will enter and give you a reply. Then ask him for whatever you want.

The second magical papyrus, PGM XII.121–43, gives directions for a magical ritual that begins at dawn on the first day of the new moon. A clay pot is filled with wine, a lamp is lit, and the magician speaks the words of invocation: "I call on you, the greatest god, eternal lord, world ruler, who are over the world and under the world, mighty ruler of the sea, rising at dawn, shining from the east for the whole world
