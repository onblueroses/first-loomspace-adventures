 He says:

I am the one who has been given to you from the beginning. Do not turn me away; but open to me because for this reason I came to you. Remember, I am the one whom you were waiting for in your prayer.

Open the door to me, for this reason I came to you.

The Master of the House says:

Who are you? I do not know you. Go away from my door and leave me in peace, for I do not know you.

The visitor at the door says:

I am the one whose name you repeated many times. Why do you say: “I do not know you?” Behold, I have become like you; you have acquired a name in my world. Why do you turn me away? For your sake, I have made myself small.

So that you might come into my light out of your darkness, I have made myself like you. So that you might come to me, for your sake, I made myself small.

Why do you say to me: “I do not know you?”
