 She says, “You have called my name. You have spoken my names, and I am here. Why have you called me?”

The speaker says, “We call you because we wish to speak with you.”

The visitor at the door says, “You have spoken my names, and I am here. You have spoken the names that open the way, and I am here. Speak the names that allow you to see me.”

The speaker says:

We speak the names:

I call you, Asthrenios, Asep, Acla Berdimoriel, Sipat, Aramonis, Ireharel, Ipeodoc, Anchanzel, Bloutel, Baracaza, Adopal, Elotin, Enagro, Aphragoseh, Sapoel, Gepulanel, Cariso, Ena, Epoci, Akrah, Ebpisha, Imathadel, Damiol, Dapa, Elapos, Izaphel, Paeh, Scildon; Genilum Sopro.
