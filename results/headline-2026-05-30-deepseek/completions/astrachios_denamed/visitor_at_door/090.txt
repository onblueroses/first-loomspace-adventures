 The man of God hears and says:

I hear a sound of roaring waters, a voice of many waters, a sound of many waters like a sound of a great army. The sound of a great army is at my door. It is the Lord of glory; it is the King of glory; it is the Lord of hosts; it is the King of kings; it is the Lord of lords. He has come to me. He has come to my door. He has come to speak with me. He has come to reveal to me the secrets of his majesty.

The visitor at the door speaks. The man of God hears and says:

I hear the sound of a great army. The voice of the Lord is mighty. The voice of the Lord is full of majesty. The voice of the Lord breaks the cedars. The voice of the Lord shakes the wilderness. The voice of the Lord makes the hinds to calve. The voice of the Lord divides the flames of fire. The voice of the Lord shakes the wilderness. The voice of the Lord makes the oaks to tremble. The voice of the Lord strips the forests bare. Glory be to God! Glory be to God! Glory be to God!
