 The door opens. The visitor enters. The door closes behind him.

The visitor is met by the gods of the temple. They are all of the one body.

The visitor is escorted to the altar.

The gods ask: What is your desire?

The visitor answers: To be one with the gods.

The gods ask: Is this your true desire?

The visitor answers: Yes, this is my true desire.

The gods ask: Are you prepared to give your life for this desire?

The visitor answers: Yes, I am prepared to give my life.

The gods say: Then you shall have what you desire. Let us begin.

The gods say: I am the god of the temple. I am the god of the door. I am the god of the altar. I am the god of the window. I am the god of the air. I am the god of the fire. I am the god of the water. I am the god of the earth. I am the god of the mountain. I am the god of the sea. I am the god of the sun. I am the god of the moon. I am the god of the star.
