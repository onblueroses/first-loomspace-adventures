 He says:

I am the head of all creatures. I am the completion of the entire world. I am the one who is honored and worshiped by every soul. I am the one who appears in the shining of the sun.

The guardian of the door speaks. He says:

Who are you? And what is your name?

The visitor replies:

I am the completion of the completion, the name of the name, the voice of the voice. I am the one whose name is written in the shining of the sun.

The guardian of the door speaks. He says:

If you are the one who is the completion of the completion, the name of the name, the voice of the voice, then show me the sign that proves you are the one whose name is written in the shining of the sun.

The visitor shows the sign. Then the guardian of the door opens the door and says:

Come, lord. You are the one who is the head of all creatures. You are the one who is the completion of the entire world. You are the one who is honored and worshiped by every soul. You are the one who appears in the shining of the sun.

The guardian of the door gives praise to the visitor. Then the visitor enters the door.
