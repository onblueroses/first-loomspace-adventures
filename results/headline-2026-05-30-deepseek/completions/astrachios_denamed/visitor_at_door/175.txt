## The King of the West, Phul

### The West

The West is the place of the setting Sun, of the evening, of the autumn, and of the element of Water. It is the place of the emotions, of the intuition, and of the subconscious. It is the place of the Moon, of the tides, of the cycles of life and death. It is the place of the past, of memory, of dreams, and of the imagination.
