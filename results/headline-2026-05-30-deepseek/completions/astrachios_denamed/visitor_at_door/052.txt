 I am the angel of the Lord and I am here to answer your call.

Open the door and let him in. He may be tall with a great sword or he may be a young man, slender and beardless. Do not be afraid, but welcome him and ask him what you will.

The angel will ask you your name. Tell him. He may ask you to repeat the name. He may ask you to swear an oath. Swear to him that you are truthful and he will serve you.

The angel will give you a new name. Write it down and keep it secret.

Ask him what you will. He may have a sword, a ring or a wand. He will give you a magical weapon. Use it to seal the pact.

The angel may speak to you of your future and your past. He may warn you of dangers. He may tell you of your death.

You may ask him about magic, about spirits, about the planets and the stars. He will tell you what you wish to know.

When you are done, thank him and bid him farewell.

Close the book. Blow out the candle. Open the curtains.

Eat bread and salt. Drink wine.

Write down all that you have learned. Keep it secret.
