## The Book of the Opening of the Mouth

Initiates of the Gnostic Church of LVX, or any other properly constituted magickal Order, are aware of the importance of the Opening of the Mouth ceremony. This ceremony is one of the most important performed by the Hierophant or celebrant during the ceremony of initiation. As it is performed in the Gnostic Church of LVX, the Opening of the Mouth is done immediately after the Initiate has been admitted into the Temple. It is performed with the sacred Wand, the Caduceus, which is a symbol of the Force of Mercury. In the ceremony, the Caduceus is touched to the lips of the Initiate, symbolizing the Opening of the Mouth. This Opening of the Mouth ceremony is a rite of empowerment, whereby the Initiate is enabled to speak with authority, to speak the Word, to speak words of power. This is symbolized by the touching of the Caduceus to the lips. It is also the opening of the mouth so that the Initiate can partake of the Eucharist, which is the Body and Blood of Christ.
