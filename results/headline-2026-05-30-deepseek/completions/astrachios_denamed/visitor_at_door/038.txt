 Then I say:

I know you, O you who stand at the door and knock, O you who stand at the threshold, O you who stand at the entrance and call. And I know your name, and your name is N, and I know your shape, and your shape is N, and I know your time, and your time is N, and I know your star, and your star is N. And I know you, O you who stand at the door and knock, O you who stand at the threshold, O you who stand at the entrance and call.

And you shall not pass into this house, nor shall you enter therein, but you shall go to your own place, to the throne of your star, to the dwelling place of your fellows, to the abode of your own god.

And I say to you, depart! And I say to you, begone! And I say to you, away!

Then I hold out my hand and say:

I conjure you by the great god who is without beginning and without end, by the first father of all, by the power of the flame that burns forever, by the light that shines forever, by the name of the true god who is before all things, by the holy name
