 He is the spirit of the moon.

I am the spirit of the moon, the angel of the night. I am the one who shines in the darkness. I am the one who rules the tides and the menses. I am the one who rules the cycles of life and death. I am the one who rules the cycles of growth and decay. I am the one who rules the cycles of change and transformation.

I am the one who is called by many names: Selene, Luna, Hecate, Diana, Artemis, Isis, Hathor, Inanna, Lilith, Lilitu, Lamashtu, Ereshkigal, Persephone, Proserpina, Kore, Ceres, Demeter, Hera, Juno, Frigg, Freya, Morrigan, Macha, Nemain, Badb, Rhiannon, Arianrhod, Ceridwen, Blodeuwedd, Branwen, Isolt, Guinevere, Morgan, Morgause, Nimue, Viviane, Elaine, Igraine, Ygraine, Morgaine, Morgain, Morgayne, Morgen, Morgen le Fay, Fata Morgana, Morgana, Morgane, Morganna, Morganna
