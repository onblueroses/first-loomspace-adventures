 I hear him, but I cannot answer. I am trapped here, bound to this place, bound to these human bones. He cannot hear me. He cannot see me. He cannot free me. And he must be careful. The dead will soon be stirring.

I am the spirit of this place. I am a prisoner of the dead. I am the memory of the living. I am the soul of this place.

The visitor is confused. He cannot see the trap. He cannot see the danger. He is caught in the web of the dead.

The visitor speaks again. He speaks the names of power. He speaks the names of the guardians. He speaks the names of the gatekeepers. He speaks the names of the way-openers. He speaks the names of the path-makers. He speaks the names of the door-openers. He speaks the names of the key-holders.

He speaks the names that open the way.
