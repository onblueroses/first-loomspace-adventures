 She says: ‘I am the one who stands before the door. Open for me.’

You say: ‘Who are you? Where do you come from?’

The visitor answers: ‘I come from the place of eternal light.’

You say: ‘What is the name of the place of eternal light?’

The visitor answers: ‘The name of the place of eternal light is the place of the living. My name is Elohim.’

You say: ‘Where do you go?’

The visitor answers: ‘I go to the place of the dead.’

You say: ‘What is the name of the place of the dead?’

The visitor answers: ‘The name of the place of the dead is the place of those who are no more. My name is Elohim.’

You say: ‘What do you seek?’

The visitor answers: ‘I seek the eternal light.’

You say: ‘What is the name of the eternal light?’

The visitor answers: ‘The name of the eternal light is life.’
