 (The Guardian's response is given in parentheses.)

_Open the door, for I am a King of Light who comes from the Light. (Who are you, and what is your name?)_

_I am a King of Light who comes from the Light. (Of what are you the King?)_

_I am the King of the Light of the Crown of Lights. (What is the sign of your kingdom?)_

_The Crown of Light. (Is there no other sign?)_

_Let the Powers of the Crown of Light answer for me. (Enter then, in peace.)_

The traveler passes through the door into the Holy Place.

_The Prayer of the Hall of Light_

_Great Light, whose radiance shines forth from the Crown of Light, whose power rules the first hall of your kingdom: I stand before you, a child of earth, seeking your light and your blessing. May the Light of the Crown of Lights shine upon my own crown, that I may know myself as a child of the Light, and enter into your presence in peace. In the name of the Crown of Light, so may it be._

The traveler now turns to the east, and salutes the powers of the east, saying:

_Hail to you, O Guardians of the Watch
