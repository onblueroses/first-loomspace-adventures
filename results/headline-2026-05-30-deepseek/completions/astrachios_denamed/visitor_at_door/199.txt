The Two of Swords
When the Two of Swords appears, the querent may be in a state of indecision. It may be necessary to weigh the pros and cons of a situation carefully and consider the ramifications of any decision. The querent may be in a state of denial, and refuse to consider certain facts or realities. The querent may be feeling helpless or powerless, unable to take action.

In a reading, the Two of Swords can indicate a stalemate. In a relationship, it can signify a lack of communication or an unwillingness to deal with problems. In a business reading, it can indicate a deadlock in negotiations.

The Two of Swords can also be a warning to be cautious and not to make hasty decisions. It may be necessary to gather more information before taking any action.

The reversed Two of Swords can indicate that a decision has been made, but it may not be the right one. It can also indicate that the querent is beginning to see the truth of a situation and is ready to take action.
