 He says, ‘You have invited me to come, and I am here. I will now deliver the message that I bring from the Lord.’ And then he speaks. And you are astonished. And you are happy.

When you are done, you should thank the visitor for his help. And then you should thank the Lord for his help. And then you should thank the Lord for sending his visitor. And then you should thank the visitor for coming.

Then you should send the visitor away. You should say, ‘Go in peace, go with the blessing of the Lord. And if I need you again, I will call you again.’

And then you are done.

CALLING THE ANGELS OF THE FOUR DIRECTIONS

This is the invocation to call the angels of the four directions. You will use it in many of the rituals.

Stand facing east, with arms outstretched. You should say:

I call you, Michael, angel of the east, angel of the rising sun, angel of air. I call you in the name of the Lord God, Adonai Elohim, who has given you power over this direction. I call you to attend this ritual, to watch over it, to protect it, and to bring me your blessing.
