 He says: "I am a man." The gatekeeper replies: "If you are a man, give me the signs." The visitor replies: "Here are the signs: Acla Berdimoriel, Sipat, Aramonis, Ireharel, Ipeodoc, Anchanzel, Bloutel, Baracaza, Adopal, Elotin, Enagro, Aphragoseh, Sapoel, Gepulanel, Cariso, Ena, Epoci, Akrah, Ebpisha, Imathadel, Damiol, Dapa, Elapos, Izaphel, Paeh, Scildon, Genilum, Sopro." The gatekeeper says: "You have spoken the signs. You may enter."

After you have entered the gate, you will see a throne. On it sits the god Hekate, holding torches in her hands. Approach her and offer her a libation of wine. Then say: "I have come, O goddess, to beg your favor. Grant that my spells and my incantations be effective and that my words of power be successful. Bring to me the power of attraction.
