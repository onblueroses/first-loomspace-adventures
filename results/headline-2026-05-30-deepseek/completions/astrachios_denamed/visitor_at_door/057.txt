The visitor appears, and the person sees him and is greatly terrified. He says to him: “Be not afraid and do not fear. Open your mouth and speak your needs.”

He says: “I conjure you, Pathiel, who is revealed to me, by the Ineffable Name that is called Yah, Yah, Yah, that you do not leave me all the days of my life, and that you come to me whenever I call you.”

He says: “I shall not leave you all the days of your life, and whenever you call me I shall come to you, except on the eve of Sabbath and the eve of Wednesday, because on these nights I have to ascend to the firmament and recite the song before the Holy One, blessed be He.”
