 And the door opens. And the visitor enters.

The visitor stands before the altar. He speaks the names of the Genii of the Hours:

Asthrenios, Asep, Acla Berdimoriel, Sipat, Aramonis, Ireharel, Ipeodoc, Anchanzel, Bloutel, Baracaza, Adopal, Elotin, Enagro, Aphragoseh, Sapoel, Gepulanel, Cariso, Ena, Epoci, Akrah, Ebpisha, Imathadel, Damiol, Dapa, Elapos, Izaphel, Paeh, Scildon; Genilum Sopro.

The Genii of the Hours stand before the altar.

The visitor speaks:

I call to the Genii of the Hours, those who are sent forth from the house of the Sun to do his will, that they may hear my voice and grant my desire.
