 "I am the one who has been sent by the King to see you. Why do you not open the door and show me proper respect? I will tell the King, and he will punish you with death."

The Magician says: "I know that the King sent you; if you are from the King, show me a sign. I will not open the door until I have the sign."

The visitor says: "I will show you the sign of the King and his seal."

The Magician, seeing the sign, opens the door. The spirit appears as a soldier in a red tunic and a golden crown, riding a lion. In his hand he holds a viper. He appears in a beautiful form.

He gives true answers to all things, past, present and future. He can make you invisible. He brings familiars from the King of the East.

He rules over thirty-six legions of spirits.
