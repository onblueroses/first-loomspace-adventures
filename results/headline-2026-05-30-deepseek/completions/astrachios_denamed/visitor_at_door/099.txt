 He will say: "I am the one who comes." And you will reply: "If you are the one who comes, tell me what is the name of the angel who guards me?" And he will say: "The name of the angel who guards you is ..." And you will say: "Then I open the door to you. Come in, with the spirit that has accompanied you."

Having done this, welcome the visitor, and after he has entered, close the door and ask him to sit down. If he asks for something to eat or drink, provide it. After you have spoken with him, let him go, but do not accompany him to the door.

In the Grimoire of Armadel we find a description of the angels who govern the 28 mansions of the Moon. In all likelihood this description was borrowed from the medieval Key of Solomon, but it has been given a new twist. The spirits of the mansions are said to appear in the guise of soldiers, but only if the magician has taken the precaution of making a special seal for each mansion. These are described as pentacles, but they are not really pentacles, since they contain only words and no geometric figures.
