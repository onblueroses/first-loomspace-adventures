  
**VISITOR:**  
I am the one who comes to you in the name of the Most High God, the God of Israel, and the God of the whole world.

The visitor enters the circle.

**MAGUS:**  
What is your name?

**VISITOR:**  
My name is He Who Is.

**MAGUS:**  
What is your seal?

The visitor shows the seal.

**MAGUS:**  
Who are you? What are your powers?

**VISITOR:**  
I am He Who Is. I am the one who grants you the power of the spirits. I am the one who gives you the power to command them. I am the one who gives you the power to see them. I am the one who gives you the power to hear them. I am the one who gives you the power to speak to them. I am the one who gives you the power to bind them. I am the one who gives you the power to loose them. I am the one who gives you the power to send them. I am the one who gives you the power to call them. I am the one who gives you the power to make them do your will. I am the one who gives you the power to make them obey you
