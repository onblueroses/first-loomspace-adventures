 He says: I am the one who is of the angels, and I am the one who is of the archangels. I am the one who is of the powers, and I am the one who is of the authorities. I am the one who is of the rulers, and I am the one who is of the demons. I am the one who is of the underworld, and I am the one who is of the perfect ones.

He says: Open to me, so that I may come in.

And I say: Who are you?

And he says: I am the one who is from the sons of men.

And I say: Which son? And from which man?

And he says: I am the son of the first man, of whom the female virgin conceived. I am the son of the first man, who was without a mother. I am the son of the first man, who without a mother has a father.

And I say: How is it possible for a man to beget without a mother? Explain this to me.
