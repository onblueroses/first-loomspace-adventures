 The petitioner asks him to speak his name and his intention. The visitor says he is Isriel and he has come to speak of the mysteries of the spirit. The petitioner asks the visitor to swear that he means no harm and to leave if his intention is not good. The visitor swears he means no harm and will leave if his intention is not good.

The petitioner asks the visitor to enter, and then asks him to speak further of the mysteries of the spirit. The visitor says he has come to teach the petitioner the wisdom of the ages and to help him to ascend. The petitioner asks the visitor to teach him.

The visitor says he will give the petitioner three gifts. The first gift is the gift of fire. The second gift is the gift of water. The third gift is the gift of air.

The visitor says he will now leave. The petitioner asks him to return when called.

The petitioner recites the license to depart:

O spirits, we thank you for attending, and we bid you farewell. Go in peace to your abodes and habitations. Harm none on your way, and be ever ready to come when you are called.
