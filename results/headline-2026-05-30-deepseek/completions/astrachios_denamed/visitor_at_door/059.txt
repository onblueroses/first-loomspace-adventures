 The door opens. The guardian stands aside.

The visitor enters. He is dark, tall, and powerful.

The guardian says, “Who are you?”

The visitor says, “I am the one who knocks.”

The guardian says, “What is your purpose?”

The visitor says, “I am here to serve the master.”

The guardian says, “Whom do you serve?”

The visitor says, “I serve the master.”

The guardian says, “Who is the master?”

The visitor says, “The master is the one I serve.”

The guardian says, “What is your name?”

The visitor says, “I am the one who knocks.”

The guardian says, “Are you the one who knocks?”

The visitor says, “I am the one who knocks.”

The guardian says, “Are you the master?”

The visitor says, “The master is the one I serve.”

The guardian says, “Are you the servant?”

The visitor says, “I am the one who knocks.”

The guardian says, “Are you the guardian?”

The visitor says, “I am the one who knocks.”

The guardian says, “Are you the one who knocks?”

The visitor says, “I am the one who knocks.”
