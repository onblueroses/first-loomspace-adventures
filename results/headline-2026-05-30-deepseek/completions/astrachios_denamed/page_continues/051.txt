 “By these names I invoke you, O ye Angels of the Air, who dwell in the South. By these names I call you, O ye Angels of the Air, who dwell in the West.” The names are not given. Instead, the instruction is: “In the same manner as for the Angels of the East, mutatis mutandis.”

Then: “By these names I invoke you, O ye Angels of the Air, who dwell in the North.” Again, the names are not given: “In the same manner as for the Angels of the East, mutatis mutandis.”

Then: “By these names I invoke you, O ye Angels of the Air, who dwell above the Earth.” And again: “In the same manner as for the Angels of the East, mutatis mutandis.”

The page ends: “Now, O ye Angels of the Air, by these names I invoke you, that ye shall be present at my Invocation and give ear to my petition. Amen.”

To me, this is a page that requires you to make some choices. And, in fact, I’ve been told that the original manuscript ends with a blank page, on which the magician is supposed to write their own conjuration.
