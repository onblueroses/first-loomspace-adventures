 "I conjure you, O angels, by the great God, who spoke and it was done, who commanded and all things were created, that you appear in this place, in a beautiful and human form, and reveal to me the truth of all things that I shall ask of you, and also that you do all things for which I have called you. And I conjure you by these names, which are written in Hebrew: Adonai, Elohim, Agla, Tetragrammaton, and by these names of the angels, whose names are written above, that you come immediately and without delay. Fiat. Fiat. Amen."

This is an excellent example of the way in which the names of angels are used in magical rituals: they are invoked to appear, and then they are commanded to do the bidding of the magician. It is necessary for the magician to be in a state of ritual purity, to be fasting, and to have prepared the place of evocation before the ritual begins.
