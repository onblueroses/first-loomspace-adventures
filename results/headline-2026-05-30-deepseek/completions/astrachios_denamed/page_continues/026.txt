 “These are the names of the great God, which
are invoked in the circle. And these are the names of the angels who
are set over the days of the week, and they are invoked in the circle.
When thou wouldst invoke them, thou must call them thus: ‘I conjure
thee, O N, by the great God whose names are Asthrenios, etc.’ And
thou must call the angel whose day thou wouldst invoke him on. The
angels of the days are these:

Sunday: Raphael.
Monday: Gabriel.
Tuesday: Samuel.
Wednesday: Michael.
Thursday: Sachiel.
Friday: Anael.
Saturday: Cassiel.

The following spells were to be performed on the day ruled by the
angel invoked. The first spell is:

A Prayer for the Love of a Woman.

Before sunrise, say: ‘I conjure thee, O Anael, by the great God whose
names are Asthrenios, etc., that thou send me N, daughter of N, and
let her come unto me full of love and desire towards me, and let her
fulfil all my desires, willingly, quickly, and without delay.’
