 “After you have said these names, you will see the spirit approaching. When he arrives, you will see him as a man. Do not be afraid.” The conjuration continues with a series of questions to be asked of the spirit, and the spirits’ answers: “Ask him: ‘Who are you?’ He will answer: ‘I am the angel (or archangel) Asthrenios.’ Ask him: ‘Why have you come?’ He will answer: ‘I have come because you have called me.’ Ask him: ‘Will you do my will?’ He will answer: ‘I will do your will.’ Ask him: ‘Will you tell me the truth?’ He will answer: ‘I will tell you the truth.’ Ask him: ‘Will you depart from me?’ He will answer: ‘I will depart from you.’ Ask him: ‘Will you return when I call you?’ He will answer: ‘I will return when you call me.’ Ask him: ‘Will you tell me the truth about the matter for which I have called you?’ He will answer: ‘I will tell you the truth.’ Ask him: ‘Will you tell me what I wish to know?’ He will answer: ‘I will tell you what you wish to know.’
