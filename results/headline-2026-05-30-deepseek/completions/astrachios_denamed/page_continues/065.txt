 “These are the names that open the gates to the heavens.” The final line, “Genilum Sopro,” appears to be a corruption of “Gene-lum Sopro” (“I call them forth”).

The text of the prayer is as follows:

O holy angels, divine spirits, guardians of the doors of heaven, I call upon you. I, your servant, who stands trembling before your awesome presences, begs you: let the doors of heaven be opened, let the angels and powers answer me. O you spirits, Asthrenios, Asep, Acla Berdimoriel, Sipat, Aramonis, Ireharel, Ipeodoc, Anchanzel, Bloutel, Baracaza, Adopal, Elotin, Enagro, Aphragoseh, Sapoel, Gepulanel, Cariso, Ena, Epoci, Akrah, Ebpisha, Imathadel, Damiol, Dapa, Elapos, Izaphel, Paeh, Scildon, I call upon you and conjure you. In the name of the Most High God, come to me, I pray, and tell me what I wish to know.
