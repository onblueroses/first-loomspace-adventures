 “The holy angels of the first day: Raphael, Samael, and Asmodel. The second day: Ariel, Barachiel, and Azrael. The third day: Michael, Sarfiel, and Sabaoth. The fourth day: Gabriel, Raphael, and Anael. The fifth day: Cassiel, Sachiel, and Samael. The sixth day: Michael, Adoniel, and Raphael. The seventh day: Michael, Sarfiel, and Anael. The angels of the day and the night: Raphael and Anael. The angels of the hours: Michael, Gabriel, Raphael, Uriel, Zamael, Barachiel, Sabaoth, Barbiel, Samael, Asmodel, Amriel, Abdiel.”

The above angels are also listed in the Magical Calendar.

Sources: McLean, A Treatise on Angel Magic, 54, 55, 56, 57.
