 “Invocation of the 49th spirit, Enepsiagon, who is the governor of the north-east wind. He is a mighty king, who appears as a great dragon with a human head. He rules 29 legions of spirits. He can make the air appear like a dragon. He can also bring rain, hail, snow, thunder and lightning, and cause ships to sink. He can cause the death of men and women.”

The page gives a Hebrew name for the spirit, and then gives instructions for how to call him, saying: “When you wish to call him, you must face north-east, and speak the names that open the way.”

Below is a list of names, presumably the names that open the way, which are:

Onoel, Anochiel, Elohim, Sother, Emeth, Ischyros, Athanatos, Jehovah, Adonai, El Shaddai, Shaddai, Tetragrammaton, El, Elohim, Elohe, Zebooth, Elion, Escherchie, Jah, Adonai, Jah, Jehovah, Asher Eheieh, Elion, Escherchie, Jah, Adonai, Jah, Jehovah, Asher Eheieh
