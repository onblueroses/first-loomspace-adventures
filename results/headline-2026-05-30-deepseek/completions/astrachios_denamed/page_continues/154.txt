 “After you have spoken these names, you will see a fire approaching you, and you will hear thunder and a great uproar and the sound of horns and a drum. Do not be afraid, and do not answer anything until the spirit appears to you. Then ask him about anything you wish. And if he does not appear immediately, speak again the names from the beginning, and also say:

I conjure you, spirit, in the name of the great god who has created heaven and earth, who is blessed forever and ever, that you come to me and do not conceal yourself, and show yourself to me in a pleasant form, and do not frighten me. And I conjure you, spirit, by the God of the Hebrews, the holy one, who appeared on Mount Sinai in thunder and lightning and trumpet blasts, and by the ten names that were given to Moses on Mount Sinai, that you come to me immediately and answer my questions.
