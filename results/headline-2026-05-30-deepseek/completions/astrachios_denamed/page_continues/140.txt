 “And then the spirit will appear to you, and you will ask what you wish, and he will do it for you, and he will teach you what you wish. And you will ask him to teach you all the arts and all the sciences, and he will teach you. And you will ask him for honor and glory, and he will give them to you. And you will ask him for riches, and he will give them to you. And you will ask him for whatever you wish, and he will give it to you.”

The text is a mixture of Hebrew, Greek, and Aramaic. The Hebrew words include some common angel names and names of God, but many are not Hebrew and are apparently corruptions of angel names in other languages.

The text is accompanied by the following note: “This is a magical prescription, copied from a manuscript of the 13th century, from the library of the Baron of Westphalen. It is written in Hebrew characters, but the language is a mixture of Hebrew and Greek.”
