 “This is the name of the angel that governs the Sun: Sorath, his seal and his character.”

The Sun is pictured, with its “seal” and “character” (sigil) below. Then the names of the angels for each hour of the day, with their sigils. The following page gives the same for the Moon, then the planets, in order: Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Venus, Mercury. The rest of the pages are blank.

The Sun’s sigil is the familiar circle with a dot in the center, here surrounded by a hexagram. The Moon’s is the usual crescent.

The sigils for the planetary angels are composed of the angel’s name in Hebrew letters, written in a circle, then connected by lines to form a design. The same procedure is used for the angels of the hours.

The Hebrew is transliterated into the Roman alphabet, and there are frequent misspellings.

The “seals” are designs resembling the seals of the planets given in Agrippa’s Occult Philosophy, but not exactly the same.

The “characters” appear to be constructed in the same way as the sigils, but the names are not given.
