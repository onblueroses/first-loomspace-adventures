 “This is the prayer for all the hours of the day and night, and the prayer to open all gates.” And: “Those who say this prayer will be freed from all danger and will find the Lord’s favor in all things.” This prayer is to be used in conjunction with the prayer of the hours.

The prayer is followed by a series of angelic names: “These are the names of the angels who are appointed over all the hours, and they are to be summoned in every prayer.” The names are as follows:
