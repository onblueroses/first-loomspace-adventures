 "The Seal of this Spirit." There is a circle, with a cross inside, and four dots, one in each quadrant.

The text continues: "The Character of this Spirit." There is a hexagram with a dot in the center.

The text continues: "This Spirit appeareth in the form of a Woman, with a Crown of Gold upon her head, & richly apparrelled. His office is to shew all things past, present & to come. He teacheth all Arts and Sciences, & giveth good Familiars."

Finally, the text says: "The Name of his Familiar is Seeres."

This Spirit is not found in the Goetia. However, I believe it is likely that this Spirit is a version of Sytry (also known as Sitri), the 12th Spirit of the Goetia. Sytry appears as a King with a leopard's head and the wings of a griffin. He inflames men with women's love, and women with men's love, causing them to show themselves naked if desired. He governs 60 Legions of Spirits.
