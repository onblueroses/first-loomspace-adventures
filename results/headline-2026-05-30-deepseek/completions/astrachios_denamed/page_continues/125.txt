 “This is the first prayer. As you have said these names, there will appear to you a white horse, a white bull, a white dog. Then say: ‘I conjure you by the holy names that you may help me in this matter.’” The names of the first prayer are derived from the Hebrew text of Psalm 91 (90 in the Septuagint). “Acla Berdimoriel,” for example, is a corruption of the phrase “al-yera’ pahad lailah,” “you shall not fear the terror of the night.” The magical text is a Latin translation of a Greek translation of a Hebrew original, and the names have been distorted at each stage of the process.
