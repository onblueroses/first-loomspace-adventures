 "These are the names of the kings who are present at the gate of the East. They serve their lord, the great king, whose name is Oriens, and who resides in the East."

The next page (folio 3) lists the names of the kings who serve Paymon, who resides in the West. The kings listed are:

Armatuel, Sarmiel, Baael, Saraphiel, Anatreth, Gamor, Geriel, Ena, Amil, Gediel, Monael, Carmiel, Duquiel, Casael, Zaziel, Armaziel, Meniel, Aza, Ucriel, Amriel, Gediel, and Aduel.

The next page (folio 4) contains a list of the kings who serve Egyn, who resides in the North. Their names are:

Maras, Senes, Saros, Cos, Maupa, Zach, Zazar, Arpien, Narcal, Lau, Caron, Zeves, Evor, Zach, Zazel, Xeno, Mafal, Lecabel, Xap, Menes, Ariton, and Anaboth.
