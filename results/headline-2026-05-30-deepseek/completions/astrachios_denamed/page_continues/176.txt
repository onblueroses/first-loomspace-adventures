 "This is the name of the spirit of the day." Then follow the names of the spirits of the night, the hours, the zodiac, the four elements, the planets, the days of the week, the months, the winds, and so on. The names are those of angels, planetary spirits, and intelligences. The Hebrew names are generally spelled backwards, and are often grotesquely corrupted.

Mathers made a few changes in the translation, but the most important alteration was his substitution of "spirits" for "angels" throughout. He also omitted the original invocation, which reads:
