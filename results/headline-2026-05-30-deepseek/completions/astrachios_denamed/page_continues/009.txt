Then there are three more pages of tables of letters. They are the letters of the Hebrew alphabet, arranged in four columns, each column headed by a name, Adonai, Agla, Ehyeh, YHVH. Below each name is a table of the alphabet in order, but in each table the alphabet begins at a different letter. So in the first table it starts with Aleph, in the second with Beth, in the third with Gimel, in the fourth with Daleth.

There are also a number of prayers that are to be recited daily during the month of Elul, the month preceding the Jewish New Year. These prayers are also used in the Jewish liturgy, but in the manuscript they have the specific purpose of gaining the favor of the archangels Gabriel, Michael, Raphael, and Uriel.

After all the preparations, the main part of the rite begins. It is called the "Great Invocation of the Prince," and it begins with a prayer:
