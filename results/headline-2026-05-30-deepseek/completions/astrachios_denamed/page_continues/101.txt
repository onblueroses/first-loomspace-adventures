 "You must write these names on a piece of parchment and wear it as a protection against all dangers. The conjuration is to be said over it."

Here is the conjuration:

I conjure thee, O most glorious and holy angels, in the name of the great and powerful God who has created all things by His Word and whose kingdom shall have no end, that thou shalt come unto me and fulfill my request. I conjure you by Him who spoke and it was done. I conjure you by Him to whom all creatures are obedient. I conjure you by Him who can destroy both body and soul. I conjure you by Him who has placed you as guardians and ministers. I conjure you by Him at whose voice the earth trembles. I conjure you by Him who has formed the firmament and established the clouds above the earth. I conjure you by Him who has fixed the earth upon the waters. I conjure you by Him who has created the day and the night. I conjure you by Him who has created light and darkness. I conjure you by Him who has created man after His own image.
