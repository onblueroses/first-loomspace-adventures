 'Now speak these holy names that the angels gave to Solomon: In this manner they were delivered to him: Ehoel, Soter, Emagro, Adonai, Emedon, Agle, Tetragrammaton, Casoly.'

After reading this, the magician should pick up the sword and say: 'O you spirits, I have called you. Come now and appear before me. Come peacefully and quietly, and answer my questions truthfully. If you do not, I shall curse you and damn you forever to the abyss.'

The magician should then put down the sword and pick up the pentacle. He should hold it up in his right hand and say: 'Behold your punishment if you disobey.' Then he should pick up the censer and offer incense to God. After this he should say: 'I have called you in the name of the Lord who created heaven and earth and all that is in them, whose name is blessed forever. I have also called you in the name of the angels who govern the fourth heaven before the face of God, who is blessed forever. Come now and appear before me.
