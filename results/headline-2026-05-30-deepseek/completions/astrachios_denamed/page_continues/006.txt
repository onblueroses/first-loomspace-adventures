 ‘Now say: I conjure you, spirits, by all the aforesaid names, and by these: Adonay, Eloy, Agla, Tetragrammaton, Saday, Sabaoth, Amioram, Anaphaxeton, Primeumaton, Messias, Emanuel, Alpha and Omega, Ioth, Aglanabrath, El Abragateh, El, El, El, El, El, Ey, Ey, Ey, Ey, Ey, Ha, Ha, Ha, Ha, Ha, Ha, Va, Va, Va, Va, Va.’

The spirits are then to be commanded in the name of God to appear and give true answers, and the magician must have a ‘virgin parchment’ on which he has written the names of the spirits.

The second part of the book is a list of the names of the spirits and the offices to which they are assigned. There are 111 of them, including 31 under the four emperors of the cardinal points, and 80 under the four kings of the cardinal points.
