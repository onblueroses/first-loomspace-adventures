The next page is a love spell. The magician writes the names of two spirits on a piece of paper, then puts it in his left hand. He then recites: “I conjure you, spirits X and Y, by the great names of God, by the one who created you and all things, that you go to [name of woman] and that you cause her to love me with a perfect love, and that she desires me and desires to come to me.”

The following page is a spell for invisibility. The magician writes the names of two spirits on a piece of paper and puts it in his left hand. Then he recites: “I conjure you, spirits X and Y, by the great names of God, by the one who created you and all things, that you cover me with your shadow, so that I cannot be seen by any human creature, and that you keep me from all danger.”
