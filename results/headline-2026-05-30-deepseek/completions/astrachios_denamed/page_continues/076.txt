 "Then say this prayer: 'O Lord, almighty Father, I pray that you shall send your holy angel Samael from heaven, who shall carry this stone to the place where I will say to him, and there he will make it do all my will. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, amen.'" There is a second prayer for the same purpose.

This is followed by a list of seven magical words to be spoken over the stone: "An, Zoh, Azoth, Abuc, Eben, Ama, Iona." The instructions continue:

Afterward, write the names that follow upon a piece of silver or tin. Then place the stone and the writing inside a linen cloth and carry it with you. And you shall have great favor with princes and rulers and all persons, and you shall be feared by all and be loved by all. And if you are a merchant, your goods will sell well and profitably.

This is followed by a list of seven names: "Emanuel, Eman, Eloy, Agla, Tetragrammaton, Adonay, Sabaoth."

The manuscript ends with a final note: "This stone is called the stone of Moses because God gave it to Moses on Mount Sinai."
