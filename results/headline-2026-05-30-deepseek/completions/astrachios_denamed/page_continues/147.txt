 “And then let him say: ‘I conjure you, O spirit, by these names and by all the rest which are ineffable, which God knows, and whereof human nature is ignorant, that you come without delay and without doing harm to me or to any other creature, and that you answer truthfully concerning all matters that I shall ask of you. And I conjure you, O spirit, by the virtue of these names: Agla, On, Tetragrammaton, and by all other divine names, that you appear in a fair and comely human form, without any deformity or tortuosity, and without frightening me in any way. And I conjure you, O spirit, by the virtue of all aforesaid, that you come promptly and without delay, and answer faithfully concerning all matters which I shall ask of you. And if you are unwilling to come, I, by virtue of the names of the Most High God, will force you to come against your will.’”
