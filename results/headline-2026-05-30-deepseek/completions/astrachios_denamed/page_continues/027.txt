 “Then say the following invocation:”

I call you, O great and holy angels, and by these most holy names, which I call with all reverence and humility, I conjure you, O great angels, that you may vouchsafe to descend from your holy seat and mansion in the heavens and come unto me, N., at this place, visibly and affably, without any deformity or terror, and show me all I desire, truly and without guile, and accomplish my requests, as far as is in your power and is permitted by the Creator. I conjure you, O holy angels, by the virtue of these names: Adonay, El, Elohim, Elohe, Zebaoth, Elion, Escherchie, Jah, Tetragrammaton, Saday, Adonay, Amioram, Hyniel, Pliel, Mael, Seraphiel, Uriel, Jehudiel, Gabriel, and by all the other names of the great Creator which are written in the Holy Bible, and by the name Primematum, which Moses named, and the earth opened and swallowed up Corah, Dathan, and Abiram; and by the name of God AGLA, which Lot named,
