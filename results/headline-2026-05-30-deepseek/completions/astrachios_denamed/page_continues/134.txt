 "Then, having spoken these names, you will see the gods of the hours who are subject to the great god and who will give you a response concerning everything you ask."

The first hour of the day is ruled by Sabaoth; the second by Phouel; the third by Iao; the fourth by El; the fifth by Adonai; the sixth by Iaeo; the seventh by Astaphaios; the eighth by Eloe; the ninth by Horaios; the tenth by Adonai; the eleventh by Iao; the twelfth by El. Each of these hours has its own angel. The angel of the first hour is Sabaoth; the angel of the second hour is Phouel; the angel of the third hour is Iao; the angel of the fourth hour is El; the angel of the fifth hour is Adonai; the angel of the sixth hour is Iaeo; the angel of the seventh hour is Astaphaios; the angel of the eighth hour is Eloe; the angel of the ninth hour is Horaios; the angel of the tenth hour is Adonai; the angel of the eleventh hour is Iao; the angel of the twelfth hour is El.
