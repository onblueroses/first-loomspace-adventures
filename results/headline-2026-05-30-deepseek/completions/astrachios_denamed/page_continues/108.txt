 “Say these words when the moon is full or waxing, and you will see a vision of the place and know how to go there. Do not be afraid.”

I am not afraid, but I am not sure I believe this.

I try to read the words. I sound them out. I’ve been trying to learn some of the languages of the ancestors, but it’s a slow process. The pronunciation is difficult. I try to say them again. I try to make them fit in my mouth.

I try to speak them aloud, but I can’t. I keep stumbling over the words.

I don’t know how to say them, so I put the book away. I will try again later.

* * *

The next time I look at the page, it is different. The words are gone.

There is only one word, written in large letters:

REMEMBER.

The page is no longer in the language of the ancestors. It is in my own language.

The book is teaching me, I realize. It is waiting for me to learn.

I will learn.

I am going to find the First House. I am going to find the First Library.

I am going to find the truth.

I will learn.

I will remember
