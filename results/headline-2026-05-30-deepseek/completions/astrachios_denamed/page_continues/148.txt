The magic book of the Munich manuscript also includes a section on herbs. One of the most interesting parts is a collection of seven herbal remedies, each of which consists of a single herb, for the seven diseases that can befall a man. The first is for "anger," the second for "pride," the third for "greed," the fourth for "envy," the fifth for "sloth," the sixth for "lust," and the seventh for "gluttony." The first remedy is given as "Take the herb called marjoram and put it in your mouth and chew it well, and then spit it out and the anger will be gone." The second remedy is "Take the herb called sage and put it in your mouth and chew it well, and then spit it out and the pride will be gone."
