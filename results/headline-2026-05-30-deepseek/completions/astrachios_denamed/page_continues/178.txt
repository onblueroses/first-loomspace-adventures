 “Then you will see a great dragon coming out of the cave, with the light shining from his face. Do not be afraid, because he is the first servant. Greet him, and he will turn into human form. Then speak to him thus: ‘Greetings, you who are first in the first hour. You are welcome to me, because I hope and believe that you are to come to me.’ And he will respond to you: ‘Greetings to you, also, man of God, you who are above all creatures, because of whom God created all things. What do you ask? Why have you called me? Command what you will.’”

The page concludes: “And you shall say: ‘Because I wish to enter into the presence of the immortal God, who lives forever, to behold all things, and I ask you to lead me to the vision of the Almighty.’ And he will respond: ‘Follow me.’”
