 “Hear me, hear me, hear me. Open the way, open the way, open the way. Let the starry bull descend. Let the serpent wind round the world. Let the lion roar. Let the god manifest. Let the place of power be established. Let the sacrifice be accepted. Let the gate be opened. Let the way be opened. Let the power descend.”

In the center of the page is a sigil—a magic symbol—composed of two intersecting circles. Inside one circle is a star, inside the other is a triangle, and where the two circles overlap is a winged eye. Underneath is written: “Consecrate in the name of the All-Seeing.”

The final page of the manuscript, which is not reproduced here, is a kind of postscript. It reads: “These words are to be spoken by the one who would open the way. The sacrifice is the key. The sacrifice is the door. The sacrifice is the way.”
