 "Then recite these names with reverence and worship: Aseph, Acla, Berdimoriel, Sipat, Aramonis, Ireharel, Ipeodoc, Anchanzel, Bloutel, Baracaza, Adopal, Elotin, Enagro, Aphragoseh, Sapoel, Gepulanel, Cariso, Ena, Epoci, Akrah, Ebpisha, Imathadel, Damiol, Dapa, Elapos, Izaphel, Paeh, Scildon; Genilum Sopro."

These names are recited to make the angel appear, but the scribe adds a warning that "the angel will appear in the form of a beautiful woman; she is the queen of the aerial spirits; she is called Genilum Sopro. When she appears, she will give you a ring and you will have power over all spirits. You will have all your desires fulfilled, but it is necessary to guard this ring well."
