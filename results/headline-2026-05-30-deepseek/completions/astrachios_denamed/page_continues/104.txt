 “Behold, the names of the angels who are upon the first heaven, which is called Shamayim. Their office is to bring to pass every matter that is spoken by man. And this is the sign by which thou shalt know that thou art heard.” The sign is a circle containing a pentagram and a hexagram. Below this is a second circle containing the sigils of the angels, and the instruction: “Write their names upon parchment, and when thou wouldest accomplish thy will, read them before the Lord God.”

The remainder of the page is filled with sigils of the angels, arranged in two columns. A line at the bottom of the page reads: “These be the 72 angels of the Lord.”

The entire page is reproduced in the first volume of The Complete Golden Dawn System of Magic (Regardie 1984, 16:73). It is given as a magical document that should be copied onto parchment and used in an invocation of the angels. The angel names are correct, except for a few minor spelling differences, but the sigils are not those found in the Lesser Key of Solomon. They are the sigils of the spirits of the Goetia, not of the angels of the Shemhamphorash.
