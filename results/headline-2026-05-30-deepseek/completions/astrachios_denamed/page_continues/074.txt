 “The names of the Holy Angels that you must inscribe on the parchment. When you have written them, take the parchment and hold it in your hand, and read the names and pray to God that he may send you the angel that you request, and that he may reveal to you all that you desire to know.”

There follows a list of 21 angels’ names: “Aniel, Gabriel, Michael, Raphael, Anael, Raphael, Sachiel, Cassiel, Satquiel, Samael, Zadkiel, Zaphiel, Michael, Gabriel, Raphael, Haniel, Uriel, Raphael, Gabriel, Michael, Raphael.”

The spells that follow are concerned with gaining the love of a woman, keeping a man’s wife faithful to him, and winning at gambling. They require that the magician inscribe certain names and symbols on parchment, and also make use of a wax doll, which is to be melted slowly in a fire while the magician recites incantations. The names of the angels are used as words of power.
