 “After these names are spoken, the spirit will appear in a human form, and he will obey you. If you want him to appear in the form of a horse, say: ‘I conjure you by the holy names, Aclen, Adon, Tetragrammaton, and by the name of the One who spoke and it was done, who commands and it is created, that you appear to me in the form of a horse.’ And he will appear. And if you want him to appear in the form of a camel, say: ‘I conjure you by the names Adon, Tetragrammaton, and by the name of the One who spoke and it was done, who commands and it is created, that you appear to me in the form of a camel.’ And he will appear.”
