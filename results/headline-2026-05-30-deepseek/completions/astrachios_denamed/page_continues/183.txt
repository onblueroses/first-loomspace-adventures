 “These are the names of the spirits who govern the 4th house, which is the house of Cancer. The angel of the house is called Muriel.” The next page has the names for the spirits who govern the house of Leo, whose angel is Verchiel, and the next for Virgo, whose angel is Hamaliel.

The spirits’ names are a curious mixture of biblical and classical names and those that appear to be nonsense, but which may be corruptions of Hebrew or Greek. There are no invocations or rituals, but the text suggests that the names are powerful words that can be used to call spirits.

The page for Cancer includes a list of the names of spirits who govern each of the 30 degrees of the sign. The list of names is followed by a note that the names are those of spirits who govern the degrees of the zodiac, and that the names of the angels of the houses are not included. The note also says that the names of the angels of the houses are found in the “Book of the Angel Raziel.”
