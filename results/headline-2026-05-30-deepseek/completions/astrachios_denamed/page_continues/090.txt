Shemhamphorash: The seventy-two names of God used in theurgic practices, each name corresponding to a quinance (five degrees) of the zodiac. The names are derived by a code from Exodus 14:19–21. See also seventy-two names of God.

Sheol: The Hebrew underworld, a place of silence and shadow, neither Heaven nor Hell. It is the abode of the dead, a place of shadows where no one praises God.

Shevirath ha-kelim: Hebrew, "shattering of the vessels."
