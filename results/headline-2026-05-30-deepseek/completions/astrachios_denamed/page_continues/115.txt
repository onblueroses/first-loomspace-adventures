 “And when you will have said these names, you will see the gods looking at you favorably and you will hear thunder and great agitation in the air round about. And say again: ‘Open, open, O sphere of my God, and be opened, O firmament of my God, for I am [your name], the son of [your mother’s name], whose power is in heaven and whose might is in the heights. I am he who is clothed with the divine garment, the garment on which is woven the name of my God, the name which is woven upon the heart of my God.’” The text continues with a hymn of praise, then a long invocation of Helios, the sun god, followed by more praise and prayers.

The page after that begins with another series of barbarous names: “Ablanathanalba Akrammachamari Sesengen Barpharanges.” There are more invocations, prayers, and hymns. Then another series of names: “Iao Sabao Adonai Eloai Abrasax.” There is a prayer to the sun god.
