 "I conjure thee, O spirit, in the name of the Supreme God, who hath created the heavens, the earth, and all things that are therein, to appear to me visibly in a fair human form, without hurt or terror, and to answer truly all my demands." The conjuration goes on, citing various powers by which the spirit is called to appear, including "the names of thy superiors," which are listed. Then the spirit is commanded to appear, "and that immediately, in this crystal stone, here before me, which I have consecrated and prepared for this purpose." This is followed by a list of the names of God, including the one that appears in the triangle on the sigil.

The spirit is then commanded to speak, "for I am he in the same power of God, who spake the word and it was done; and I command thee in like manner, by the virtue thereof, to fulfill all my desires and commands, and never to depart until I license thee to depart. Amen."

Then the magician adds: "And now, O spirit N., I do license thee to depart, in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. Amen."
