 “And now, I ask you, oh great angel, who is known by the name of Agares, to come to me from whichever part of the world you may be in. I command you by the virtue of the most holy and sacred names of God, Adonai, El, Elohim, Elohe, Zebaoth, Elion, Esherie, Yah, Tetragrammaton, Sadai, and by all the names of God, the living and true God, to appear without delay, and give me true and perfect answers upon all things I shall ask you, and to execute all my requests, so far as in your power lieth.”

The angel Agares appears in the form of a fair old man riding upon a crocodile, carrying a goshawk on his fist. He speaks with a hoarse voice, and he teaches all languages. He also teaches the means to cause earthquakes, and can cause runaways to return.
