 "After these words, you will see him." The second page is headed, "Another conjuration of the same, which is more efficacious." It begins: "I conjure you, spirit N., by God the Father almighty, who created all things, and by Jesus Christ his son, who redeemed the human race, and by the Holy Spirit, who is the comforter, that you appear visibly and do not harm me, and that you tell me truthfully whatever I ask of you. And I conjure you, spirit N., by all the names of God, by all his powers and virtues, by all the angels and archangels, by the virtues of the patriarchs and prophets, by the merits of the apostles, by the blood of the martyrs, by the chastity of the confessors and by the purity of the virgins, by the fear of all the saints, by the power of all the blessed, by the devotion of all the elect, by the pity of all the blessed, by the piety of all the good, that you appear to me visibly, without any delay, and tell me truthfully whatever I ask of you, and that you do not harm me in body or soul.
