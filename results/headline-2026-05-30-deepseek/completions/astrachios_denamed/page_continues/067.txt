 "If you wish to invoke the four kings, you must hold the waxen seal in your left hand and the silver seal in your right. Then you must recite the following conjuration." This conjuration is on the following page and is a variant of the conjuration given earlier.

The text then gives directions for making the four wax seals for the four kings: "Take virgin wax and make four seals. On one write Amaymon, on the second Corson, on the third Zimimay, on the fourth Goap. And when you wish to invoke them, you must hold the seal in your left hand and the silver seal in your right hand." The text then gives a conjuration for the four kings, which is similar to the conjuration for the spirits given earlier.

The text then gives the names of the spirits of the hours:

The first hour: Sarabocres, Aracan, Anaboth, Thamaor, Gerenia,  
The second hour: Amabiel, Caranatha, Nastaniel, Aaba, Gediel,  
The third hour: Damor, Sachiel, Natiel, Aasima, Genediel,
