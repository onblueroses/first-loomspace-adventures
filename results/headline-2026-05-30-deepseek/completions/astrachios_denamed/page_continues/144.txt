 "And let there be given to him three coins, and let him put them in his mouth, and let him place a wreath on his head, and let him cover his head with a clean linen cloth, and let him stand in the place where he wishes to speak, and let him say the prayer written above, and let him say the names written above, and let him blow a little, and let him call the name of the one to whom he wishes to appear, and let him say: 'I conjure you, O spirit NN, by the names written above, that you appear to me in this hour and that you not be frightful to me, but appear to me in a beautiful form and pleasing to my eyes, and that you not frighten me, and that you tell me truthfully about whatever I ask you, and that you fulfill my desire and that you obey me in all things.'" The text adds that the names of the spirits should be written on parchment, and that the operator should be fasting and pure.
