 “These are the names that open the way. They are to be recited over the one who is to be opened.” This means that they are to be recited over the one who is to be initiated, to open the way for him or her. Then the text continues:

Say these names to the east:

Achiel, Adoniel, Abriel, Amriel, Aniel, Astrael, Adriel, Anael, Aratron, Ariel, Aiel, Achiel, Aniel, Amriel, Astrael, Adriel, Anael, Aratron, Ariel, Aiel, Achiel, Adoniel, Abriel, Amriel, Aniel, Astrael, Adriel, Anael, Aratron, Ariel, Aiel.

Then the text says: “Say these names to the west, east, north, south, and above and below.” This is a little obscure, but it suggests that these names are to be recited in all six directions.
