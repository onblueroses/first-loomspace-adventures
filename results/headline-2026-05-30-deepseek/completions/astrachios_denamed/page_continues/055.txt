 "We conjure thee by the Creator of Heaven and Earth, and by him who is the Redeemer of the World, and by the threefold holy name of God, Eheye, Asher, Eheye, and by the name Agla, which Joshua named and the Sun and Moon stood still. . . ."

The above text is from the Book of the Sacred Magic of Abra-Melin the Mage, as translated by S. L. MacGregor Mathers.

The next page contains the names of the four principal kings, or angels, which are Oriens, Paimon, Ariton, and Amaymon. The names of their subordinate spirits are Lucifer, Satan, Astarot, and Asmodeus.

It is a very strange book, and the ceremonies are long and complicated. There are two copies of the manuscript in the Bibliothèque de l'Arsenal at Paris, one of which was translated by Mathers.

The book describes the method of invocation of the guardian angel, which must take place in a secluded place. The operator must be clean and pure, and must pray fervently to God and his guardian angel. He must also invoke the four sub-princes and their eight subservient ministers.
