In 1922, John M. Riddle, an American scholar of occultism and former member of the Societas Rosicruciana in America, published his translation of the Heptameron under the title, The Art of Magic. Riddle’s version is not a translation but an adaptation, freely and loosely based on the Heptameron but including material from other grimoires and from Riddle’s own experience. It was reprinted in 1975 by University Books.

The Heptameron, or Magical Elements of Peter de Abano, translated by Robert Turner, was published in facsimile by the Theosophical Publishing House in 1978. Turner was a seventeenth-century scholar who published translations of other grimoires, including the Ars Notoria. The Theosophical Publishing House also published Turner’s translation of the Fourth Book of Occult Philosophy, falsely attributed to Cornelius Agrippa, in the same volume.
