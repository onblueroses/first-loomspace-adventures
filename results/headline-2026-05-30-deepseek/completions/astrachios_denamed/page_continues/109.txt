 “Now when you have said these names, you will see the door open and the powers running through the parts of the earth, and the winds blowing and threatening to overturn the earth.”\(^{42}\) After that, the text instructs the magician to say a spell, and then to “look up at the sky and you will see the doors thrown open and the powers of the stars shining in the sky and rushing about in the parts of the earth.”\(^{43}\) After a few more spells, the magician is told to “look around and you will see the doors thrown open and the dogs of the earth that guard the gates leaping forward.”\(^{44}\) Finally, the magician is told to “look at the heavenly sphere and you will see the heavenly gods looking down from heaven and casting their rays upon the eyes of the one performing the ritual.”\(^{45}\) After that, the magician is ready to ask for whatever he wants.

The divine names in this rite are not words of power; they are not being used to command spirits, but rather to create a magical effect directly. The effect is the opening of the heavenly gates and the manifestation of the gods, who can then be petitioned for favors.
