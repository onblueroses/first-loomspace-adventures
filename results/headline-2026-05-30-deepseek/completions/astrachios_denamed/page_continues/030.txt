 "When you have said these names, you will see the gates open and the powers within them will come forth; and do not delay, but go in at once and do what you have to do. For the gates will not open again until you have performed what I command you." There follow the names of powers, beginning with Asthrenios, "who holds the first seal," and continuing with Asep, "who holds the second seal," and so on. The powers hold seals, presumably engraved stones or gems, and each seal has a function: "Asep holds the second seal; he will give you a sign concerning those things that you wish, and he will reveal to you everything you wish and will conceal you from every power." The text instructs the magician to "take" the seals, and presumably this means to take physical possession of them. The text further indicates that these powers are subordinate to other powers: "These are the great and holy powers of the great god, and they are subject to the great name Abrasax."

There is no explicit reference to the seven heavens here, but the imagery is reminiscent of the Apocalypse of Paul, where Paul ascends to the third heaven and sees an angel at each gate who holds a seal
