The next page is headed "For a woman in labor." It describes a circle, with the name of the demon of the hour in the middle, and around it the names of the seven angels of the day. The circle is to be written on a piece of paper and placed on the woman's belly. It goes on to say: "It is good for every woman in labor, and she will give birth immediately."

Then there is a page on which are written magical names in large letters: "Adonay, Eloy, Tetragrammaton, Emanuel, Messias, Agios, Theos, Ischyros, Athanatos, Sabaoth." The page ends with the words: "These are the names of God." And below that, in small letters: "In truth, I have seen all these names."
