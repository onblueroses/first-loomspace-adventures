 "Then say: 'I am he who is in the midst of the stars, and I am seated on my throne. I am the one who will not be polluted. I am the one who is seated upon the great chariot. My left hand holds the curved sword; my right hand holds the lance. I am the one who is seated upon the four-faced cherubim, the one who is seated upon the immortal serpents.'"

The text ends abruptly: "Let him who has ears hear what the Spirit says to the churches."

The text is written on papyrus in a style of Greek used in Egypt in the third century AD. The author claims to be writing in the name of John the son of Zebedee. The manuscript has been given the number P.Oxy. 5072. It is preserved in Oxford at the Ashmolean Museum.

## 4. THE APOCALYPSE OF PAUL

The Apocalypse of Paul (also known as the Revelation of Paul) is a Christian text that purports to describe a vision in which Paul is taken up to heaven and shown the torments of sinners in hell and the joys of the righteous in paradise.
