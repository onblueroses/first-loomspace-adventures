These spirits are also called "angels," and each has his own particular specialty. For example, "Ariel is the angel of the sun; he can give you the power of prophecy and will help you become famous. He can also help you to win someone's affection." "Barachiel is the angel of Jupiter; he can give you riches, honors, and whatever you ask."

The Book of the Sacred Magic of Abramelin the Mage, attributed to Abraham of Worms, a German Jew who wrote it in the fifteenth century, tells how the magician can gain "the knowledge and conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel" through an elaborate ritual that takes six months to complete. The Holy Guardian Angel is the magician's higher self, the part of him that is divine. Once he has contacted this part of himself, he can command demons to do his bidding.

In the nineteenth century, the French magician Eliphas Levi published a book called The History of Magic. Levi's work was popularized by the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn
