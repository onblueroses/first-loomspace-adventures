 "The angelic names that follow are to be spoken when the pentagram is drawn and the names of the four kings are to be spoken in the quarters."

The angelic names are:

Oriens (East) - Albam

Paymon (West) - Paimonia

Amaymon (South) - Maymon

Egyn (North) - Egyn

The manuscript then gives a prayer for the protection of the magician:

O Lord God, Holy Father, omnipotent, eternal, who created all things, who knows all things and can do all things, from whom nothing is hidden, to whom nothing is impossible; You who have created the heavens and the earth and the sea and all things that are in them by a single word: I humbly implore Your immense mercy and clemency. Deign to send Your holy angels and ministers from the high heavens, who may come to assist me and help me in all my works, acts, and thoughts today. May they govern and protect me from all peril of soul and body, and deign to give me grace, power, and virtue to invoke them. O Lord God, Holy Father, omnipotent, eternal. So be it.
