The first page of the 12th section is headed: "The Twelfth Table of the Moon." It begins: "The Table of the Moon, which is called CASPEL."

It then shows the Names of the Hours, and the Angels of the Hours, exactly as the 11th section. It continues:

The Names of the Angels, Rulers of the First Hierarchy Serving in the First Flame, under the great Angel CASPEL, who is the Supreme Ruler under ARATRON.
