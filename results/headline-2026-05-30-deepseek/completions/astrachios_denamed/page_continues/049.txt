 “In the beginning God created heaven and earth. And the earth was without form and void. And the Spirit of God moved upon the waters.” This is followed by a series of names, each followed by an attribute: “O God: Theos: Sabaoth: El: Elohim: Adonai: Sadai: He who is: He who was: He who will be: He who made heaven and earth: He who is seated upon the Cherubim: He who is seated upon the Seraphim: He who is seated upon the throne of his glory: He who is in the heavens: He who is in the abyss: He who is in the sea: He who is in the rivers: He who is in the streams: He who is in all places: He who is everywhere: He who is in the hearts of men: He who is in the mouth of all men: He who is in the thoughts of men: He who is in the understanding of men: He who is in the wisdom of men: He who is in the prudence of men: He who is in the faith of men: He who is in the charity of men: He who is in the hope of men: He who is in the truth
