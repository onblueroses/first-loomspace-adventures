 "There are five names, which, if thou callest them, thou shalt bind the feet of all the demons: Ablanathanalba, Akrammachamarei, Sesengenbarpharanges, Marmaraoth, and Aeeioyo." These names are a combination of Hebrew, Greek, and Egyptian. The name Ablanathanalba appears frequently in magical papyri and on magical gems. It is a palindrome (it reads the same backward as forward). The name Akrammachamarei is also a palindrome.

The last part of the manuscript gives a description of the planetary hours of the day and night and their influences.

The manuscript ends with a drawing of a hand, showing the names of the fingers, and the names of the angels that govern the days of the week. The angel of Sunday is Michael, of Monday Gabriel, of Tuesday Samuel, of Wednesday Raphael, of Thursday Sachiel, of Friday Anael, and of Saturday Cassiel.

The manuscript is in the British Library.
