 “Then you shall say thus: I conjure you by the holy names of God that you appear to me visibly, peacefully, and affably without any hurt, and show me all things that I shall desire to know, and also fulfill all my petitions without any tarrying.” The angel, it says, will appear. “Then ask what you will.”

The next page lists the names of angels who “rule the 4 winds” and “the 4 corners of the world.” This is followed by a page that reads, “If you would have the spirit appear in the form of a little child,” and then a list of names, presumably to be spoken in order to conjure a childlike spirit.

The next page reads: “If you would have the spirit appear in the form of a man.” Again, a list of names.

The next page reads: “If you would have the spirit appear in the form of a woman.” And another list of names.

The next page reads: “If you would have the spirit appear in the form of a young man.” A list of names.

Then: “If you would have the spirit appear in the form of a virgin.” A list of names.
