 "These are the names of the angelic hierarchies. And these are the names of the spirits that cause the winds to blow and the seas to become stormy. But you must not call them unless it be for the sake of the great ones. For it is written that it is forbidden to cause winds to blow and to cause the seas to become stormy. However, I have written them so that you may know their names." The text continues with the names of the spirits that cause earthquakes and the names of the spirits of the sun and moon. The text ends with the warning: "Do not use them, for it is forbidden."

The power to control the weather is one of the most impressive powers possessed by the magician. The ability to cause storms and earthquakes is described in a number of medieval magical texts, such as the _Picatrix_ , and such powers are attributed to great magicians such as Simon Magus. The text cautions the reader not to use these names, but includes them anyway. This is a typical feature of many magical texts.
