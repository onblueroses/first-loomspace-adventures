 “I conjure you, spirits, who are rulers of the day of the sun, by the great god and his holy names, that you send to me your power to help me in my work and to come to me quickly, peacefully, and visibly, without any hurt to me or to any other creature, to answer truthfully all my questions, and to fulfill all my desires so far as is in your power.”

The text now includes the names of the angels of the hours of the day, from the first hour to the twelfth, beginning with Michael and ending with Barachiel.

The following pages describe the seals for the hours of the day and the names of the angels of the hours of the night, beginning with Gabriel and ending with Machidiel.

Then follows the seal of the planet Sol. This seal is to be drawn on virgin parchment in the day and hour of Sol, with the blood of a bat, when Sol is in Leo.
