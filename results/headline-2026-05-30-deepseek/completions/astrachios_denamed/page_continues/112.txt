 “This is the beginning of the book of the secrets of the names and seals of the 49 servants, and the manner of their conjuration and of the book of their images, and the manner of their images and the manner of their seals, and of their conjuration, and of the names of their angels who minister unto them, and of the names of their planets, and of the names of their hours, and of the names of their presidents and of the names of their ministers and of the days of the week, and of the hours of the day and night, and the manner of their invocation and of their conjuration.”

The book then lists the seals and images of the spirits. For example, the seal of the first spirit, Samandiriel, appears as a triangle within a circle. The image of the spirit is a man with a sword in his right hand and a spear in his left. He has the head of a lion. His office is to cause the destruction and wasting of anything, and to kill and to murder. He governs 36 kingdoms and has 1,000 dukes and 100 marquises under him. He is invoked on the day and hour of Saturn.
