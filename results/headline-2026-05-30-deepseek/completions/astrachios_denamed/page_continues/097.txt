 "This is the part of the third part of the seventh of the book that is called 'The Seventh Book of Moses,' which teaches how to raise the dead, and how to learn all sciences in a moment, and how to make oneself invisible, and how to gain honor and respect, and how to win in all games, and how to have luck in all things, and how to exorcise evil spirits, and how to bind them." The last part is the conjuration to call forth the spirit of a dead person:

By the might of the Almighty God I conjure thee, spirit of N.N., that thou appearest to me in human form and do answer me truly and faithfully without any evil, according to my questions, so help thee God. Amen.

Then you must name three of the holiest names of God:

Heli, Heli, Heli, Sother, Emmanuel, Sabaoth, Agla, Tetragrammaton, Agyos, Otheos, Ischyros, Athanatos, Jehova, Y, Schaddai, Adonai, Homoysion, Ya, Agla, On, Y, Tetragrammaton, Adonai, Amora, Hamenorah, Schila
