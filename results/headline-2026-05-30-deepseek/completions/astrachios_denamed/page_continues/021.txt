 "Then after this invocation, pray to God and say: 'I pray to Thee, Lord God Almighty, Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, Who art the author of all things, Who hast given to us, through Thy only-begotten Son Jesus Christ our Lord, power and strength for the working of miracles and marvels and signs: grant me Thy grace, I pray Thee, in this hour, that Thou wouldst send unto me Thy good angel, who may reveal unto me all things which I shall desire to know, and that he may fulfill my petitions, as Thou hast granted unto Solomon the son of David to know all things under heaven.'"

This is followed by another invocation, this time to the angel: "O thou good angel N., I conjure thee by the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost, and by the Holy Trinity, and by the most high God, the King of Kings, that thou come quickly and without delay, and give me true and certain answers concerning those things which I shall ask of thee, through Him Who shall come to judge the living and the dead, and the world by fire. Amen."
