 “These are the names that open the way. This is the means of ascending.”

The conclusion of the text appears on page 7:

These are the names of those who stand in the presence of God, whose names are written in the Book of Life. And when you ascend, say these names: Adonael, Iaoel, Astaphaios, Aiel, Eloel, Ananel, Phanuel, Souriel, Rapha, Dardael, Souriel, Damiel, and say: “I am he who is from the light, I am he who is from the spirit, I am he who is from the exalted, shining light. Open to me the way that I may see the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, for I am one of you.” And he will open to you.

Thus ends the text. The names are magical words of power that open the way for the soul to ascend to heaven.
