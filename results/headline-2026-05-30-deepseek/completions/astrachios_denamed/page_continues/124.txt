But the prayer was obviously the point of the entire book. The rest of the manuscript was simply a preamble to the Devil’s Prayer.

What exactly does the Devil’s Prayer do? In the grimoire, it says that the prayer opens the gates of Hell and summons the Devil. But that seems a little dramatic. It’s more likely that the prayer is used to open a gateway to a specific demon. In the grimoire, the prayer is followed by a list of demon names, which suggests that the prayer is used to summon these demons. The prayer itself is a list of names, which are presumably the names of the demons. So the prayer is used to summon a demon, and the demon will answer any question you ask.
