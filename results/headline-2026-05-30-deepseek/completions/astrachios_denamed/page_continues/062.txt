 “Then you shall see a great and strong spirit coming forth, and he shall have the appearance of a serpent. He shall have three heads, and he shall speak with you, saying: ‘I am he who is over the angels upon the earth, and I am he who has been appointed to rule over human beings.’ And he shall ask you: ‘What do you want? Why have you called me?’ And you shall say to him: ‘I conjure you by the great god . . .’”

The ancient text of the Testament of Solomon has long been famous for its demonology, but the opening chapter, which was published only in 1995, shows that Solomon’s power was not only over demons but also over angels. The angels are presumably the angels of the nations, as in Daniel 10 and 1 Enoch, and the text suggests that the angel of Israel is Michael.

The opening chapter is incomplete, but the following chapters show that Solomon had power to summon demons also and compel them to work for him.
