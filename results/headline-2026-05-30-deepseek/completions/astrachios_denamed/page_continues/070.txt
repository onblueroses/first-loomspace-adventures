In the next entry, the author says that he has gone into the cave and has seen a fiery angel with a staff. This angel told the author to write down the names that would open the way. The names were: “Aga, Alga, Bega, Gele, Gephe, Daman, Damanuel, and Scel.” Then the author saw a female figure with a crown and a book, who said, “I am the Lady of the Cave.” She told the author to write down what she said: “I am the Mistress of the Cave. I am the Mistress of all the Powers of the Earth. I am the Daughter of the Sun and the Moon. I am the Sister of the Planets. I am the Mother of the Stars. I am the Wife of God. I am the Daughter of God. I am the Sister of God. I am the Mother of God. I am the Wisdom of God. I am the Beauty of God. I am the Strength of God. I am the Power of God. I am the Glory of God.
