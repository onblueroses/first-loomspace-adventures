 "A great and wonderful conjuration to call the spirits from the four parts of the world, and from all the planets." Here is the conjuration itself:

O you great, wonderful, and holy angels, and you who are the seven planets and who wander through the air, I conjure you by the great names of God: Ehie, Asher Ehie, I Am That I Am, Yah, Tetragrammaton, Elohim, Elohi, Zebooth, Elion, Escerchie, Adonai, Jah, Sadai, Lord God of Hosts, that you appear to me here at once, without any delay, and without doing harm to me or to any other creature, and that you answer truthfully all my demands, so that I may finish my work according to my desire. Fiat, fiat, fiat. Amen.
