The "Mighty Oath" is given: "I conjure you, Spirit, by him who has made the heaven, earth and sea, and all that are in them, and by these holy names . . ." The names that follow include "Iao Sabaoth Adonai Eloi Eloi Tetragrammaton." The spirit is asked to appear "in a human form, without noise and without any deformity."

A spirit called Oriens is summoned. He is asked to appear "in a beautiful human form" and is commanded to "perform all my requests without any deceit." The spirit is asked to appear "now, now, now, quickly, quickly, quickly. Come, come, come."

The spirit is asked to "reveal to me the mysteries of the stars, and the lightning, and thunder, and earthquakes, and teach me to understand all visions and dreams and to know the signification of all things."
