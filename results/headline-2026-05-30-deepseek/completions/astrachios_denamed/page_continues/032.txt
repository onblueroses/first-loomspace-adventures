 “These names are to be written on virgin parchment with the blood of a bat. The parchment is to be placed on the ground, and the operator is to stand on it, when the Spirits will immediately appear, and will obey you in all things.”

It is not clear from this whether the spirits appear on the parchment, or elsewhere. It is also not clear if the parchment must be placed on the ground before the operator stands on it, or if the operator may stand on the parchment and then lay it on the ground. However, the general intent is clear: the spirits will appear and obey.

This list of names is not found in other grimoires, and appears to be original to the Grimoire of Pope Honorius. There is no indication that the names are in any particular order, or that they are divided into groups. It is possible that each name is the name of a different spirit, and that the entire list must be spoken to cause them all to appear. It is also possible that the names are all titles of the same spirit, or that they are names of various powers that must be invoked to open the way for the spirits to appear.
