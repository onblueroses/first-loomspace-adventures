 “The angels of the 4th house are of the 4th heaven, and are under the dominion of the Sun; and they are invoked from the north. They are all of good will, and when they are invoked, they come readily, and they are benevolent to him who calls them. They give success in petitions, and they make men invisible, and they give familiars of a kingly kind, and they give the power of transmuting metals, and they give treasure, and they give long life, and they cure all infirmities, and they bestow wisdom, and they make one eloquent. And they give the faculty of speaking in all tongues, and they reconcile enemies and make them friends.”

An even more complex ritual, which also calls upon the archangels Michael, Gabriel, Raphael, and Uriel, is described in the same manuscript:
