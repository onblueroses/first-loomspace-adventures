Then the operator must write out his request on a sheet of papyrus, and burn it, so that the ashes fall into a vessel of water, which is placed before the god. He must then pray:

O Lord, if it be thy will that I should be given power over this daemon, let a sign be done upon the water.

He should then watch for a sign. If the sign is favourable, he can proceed. He must then prepare a phylactery and wear it, and then call upon the god for a further sign. If the sign is favourable, he can proceed to the invocation proper:

Come unto me, O spirit N., and be obedient in all things, for I invoke thee by the seal and image of the Creator of all things, and by these names which can compel thee: Aoth, Abaoth, Basuth, Iaoth, Ia, Bacuh, Abhm, Ieou, Ie, Pa, Phtha, Phtha, Phthae, Iao, Aph, Pha
