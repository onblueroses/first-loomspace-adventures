 'After saying these names, speak the invocation.' The invocation (from the first page of the manuscript) then follows:

O you, great angel who is called Atel, who sits above the angels of the fifth heaven, the great angels who are called Pamel, Rachael, Dirachiel. And you, great angel who is called Sabaoth, who sits above the angels of the fourth heaven, the great angels who are called Rachmiel, Sarapiel, Phanuel. And you, great angel who is called Saday, who sits above the angels of the third heaven, the great angels who are called Barachiel, Jehudiel, Ielahiel. And you, great angel who is called El, who sits above the angels of the second heaven, the great angels who are called Zachariel, Michael, Raphael. And you, great angel who is called Adonay, who sits above the angels of the first heaven, the great angels who are called Gabriel, Cerviel, Madiel.
