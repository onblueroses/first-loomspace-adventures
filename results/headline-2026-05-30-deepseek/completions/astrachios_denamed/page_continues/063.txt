 “After having spoken the names, you will see the spirit appear before you. Immediately command him to bring you the ring which you desire.” The spirit is then to be asked “how many spirits serve him, what their names are, what their powers are, how they may be summoned, and how many of them are permitted to appear at one time.” The spirit is then to be asked “to swear obedience to you, and that he will come whenever you desire it, and that he will bring you the ring that you desire.” The ring is then to be given to the magician.

The ring is described as “a gold ring, set with a gemstone, in which is engraved the figure of a dragon biting its own tail.” The ring is to be worn on the left hand, “for the left hand is the hand of the spirit.”
