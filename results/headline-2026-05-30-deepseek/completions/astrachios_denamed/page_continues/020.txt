 "Prayer to the King of the East, to be said in the morning." The prayer is a long one, and the page ends with: "And I will give thee the names of the King of the East:—Or:—Abel, Abeliot, Abelion, Turel, Getch, Gech, Amoy, Moy, Theos."

Then follow three pages of magical prayers. The first is headed: "A Prayer to the Kings of the East. This prayer must be said in the morning. It is very powerful for obtaining all things that may be desired." The second is: "A Prayer to the King of the South," and the third: "A Prayer to the King of the West." All three prayers are addressed to the three kings conjointly.

The next page is headed: "The Invocation of the King."
