 “After you have recited these names, you will see the doors open and the powers of the stars coming toward you and obeying you. Then you will see the doors open and in the air you will see winged creatures flying about and obeying you.”\(^{45}\)

“Speak the names that open the way” — such words are the core of the magical papyri. This spell is to be used “whenever you wish to see the heavenly gods.” The magician who wishes to ascend to heaven and see the gods must first call upon the powers of the stars, and he does so by calling them by name.

This, I propose, is the context for understanding what the author of Hebrews is doing in Hebrews 1. The author is invoking a list of names — not the names of the stars, but the name of the Son. The author is invoking the name of the Son to open the way to the heavenly sanctuary. The Son’s name is invoked because He is the one who has opened the way to the heavenly sanctuary.
