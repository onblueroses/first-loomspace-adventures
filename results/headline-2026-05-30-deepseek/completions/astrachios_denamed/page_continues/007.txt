 "After you have spoken the names, you will see a spirit appear, dressed in red linen, with a red face and fiery eyes. He will be carrying a golden book in his hand. He will say to you, 'I am the one you called. Why have you called me?' You will say to him, 'I have called you so that you will bring to me the spirit So-and-so, son of So-and-so, and you will have him answer me truthfully and without deceit everything I ask him.' He will say to you, 'Give me a sign.' You will say to him, 'I give you the sign that is upon you, that is, the names of your lord.'
