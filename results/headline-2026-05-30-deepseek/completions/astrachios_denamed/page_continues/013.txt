The next page begins: “Exorcism of the sea for calming the waves.” The text is almost identical to the previous two texts, but it begins: “As the Lord God walked upon the waters of the sea, he said: ‘Sea, be calm! Cease your waves!’” It then proceeds: “I adjure you by the God of the Hebrews, Jesus, the one who appeared on the Jordan River and calmed the sea of Gennesaret, and also by the seal of Solomon that he placed upon the tongue of Jeremiah, and by the praises of the cherubim, that you obey me, NN, and calm your waves.” After some more text, it ends with a long string of barbarous names.\(^{44}\)
