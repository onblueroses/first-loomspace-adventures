 "And you will see the form of the angel appear; it will speak with you and teach you things that you want to know. And you will ask it to lead you to the palace of the King and his throne, and you will see him in his likeness and in his glory, and he will speak with you and he will grant your request. And you will ask him to give you a certain angel who will be your servant and will do your will forever."

The text describes the names of the angels who serve the King and who will be assigned to serve the practitioner, but these names are illegible.

In this text, the King is Metatron. The practitioner will see the King sitting on his throne in his palace, and he will speak with the King. The King will then assign a personal angel to the practitioner.

This text appears to be a variation of the _Harba de Moshe_ , "The Sword of Moses," which is a Jewish text of magic from the 10th century CE. The _Harba de Moshe_ is also known as _The Book of the Angel Raziel_. It contains instructions for preparing and using a magic sword to summon angels.
