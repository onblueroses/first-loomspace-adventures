Other sources provide formulas for summoning angels in order to learn the future. The Testament of Solomon describes a ritual for summoning an angel to reveal a person’s fate. A virgin boy is to hold a bowl of olive oil, and the magician is to speak an invocation over him. The boy then looks into the oil and sees an angel who reveals the future.

Another method is to take a boy virgin and have him look into a basin of water, lamp oil, ink, or some other liquid. The magician prays over the boy, who sees angels reflected in the liquid. These angels then reveal the future.

The Testament of Solomon also describes a ritual for summoning demons. This ritual requires a ring engraved with a magical seal. The magician holds the ring over the demon to be summoned, recites the demon’s name, and commands the demon to appear.
