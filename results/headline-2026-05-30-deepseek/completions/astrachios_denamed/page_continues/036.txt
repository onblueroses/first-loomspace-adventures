 "Afterwards, after you have made the preliminary preparations, conjure them."

The preliminary preparations include making a magic square, which is illustrated in the manuscript. The square contains a six-pointed star. Around it are four smaller six-pointed stars. Each of these contains the name of an angel: Phanuel, Uriel, Raphael, Gabriel. In the center of the large star are three concentric circles. In the outer ring are the names of the four archangels again. In the middle ring are the names of the four archangels again, but in Greek. In the inner ring are the names of the four archangels in Hebrew. At the center of the large star is a circle containing a cross.

In the center of each side of the square is a smaller circle. Each circle contains a four-pointed star. In each star is a symbol: a sun, a crescent moon, a pentacle, and a star. The manuscript explains that the pentacle is for the earth, the sun for fire, the star for air, and the moon for water.
