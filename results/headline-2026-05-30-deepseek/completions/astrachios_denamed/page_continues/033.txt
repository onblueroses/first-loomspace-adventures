 “The names of the blessed spirits who rule the day are: Michael, Samael, Anael, Raphael, Cassiel, Sachiel, Samael.” These are followed by the names of the angels who rule the days of the week: Raphael, Michael, Gabriel, Cassiel, Sachiel, Anael, Michael. The angels of the hours of the day: Michael, Anael, Raphael, Gabriel, Cassiel, Sachiel, Samael, Michael, Anael, Raphael, Gabriel, Cassiel.

The names of the angels of the night: Gabriel, Cassiel, Sachiel, Samael, Michael, Anael, Raphael, Gabriel, Cassiel, Sachiel, Samael. The angels of the hours of the night: Samael, Michael, Anael, Raphael, Gabriel, Cassiel, Sachiel, Samael, Michael, Anael, Raphael, Gabriel.

The names of the angels of the winds: Oriens, Paymon, Egyn, Amaymon.

The names of the four kings of the cardinal points: Bael, Agares, Marbas, Pruslas, Aamon, Barbatos, Buer, Gusoin, Botis, Bathim, Pursan
