 “This is the great and holy seal of the moon, which is called the moon of the wise. To perform this work, you must be clean and purified for three days. To summon the spirits of the moon, you must say the conjuration that follows. And then will appear to you the spirit which governs the thing which you demand, and he will give you a straight answer to everything.”

The conjuration: “I conjure you, O spirit N., by the living God, by the holy God, by the God who has created all things by his order, and also by the holy names which are written in this book, and by the holy names: Ioth, Iath, Hyn, Ia, Ie, Lord God of hosts, that you appear quickly and without delay to do my will in everything that I shall command you.”

The conjuration continues: “I conjure you, N., by all the holy names which are written in this book, that you come quickly without delay, and give me a true answer, and do all that I shall command you.
