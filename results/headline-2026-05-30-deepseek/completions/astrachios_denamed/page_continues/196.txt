 “Say these names when you have need of me: I will answer you in every place, and in all times, and in every hour of the day or night, and I will reveal to you all things that are hidden, and I will tell you of things to come.”

The page is signed with the name, “Astanphaeus.” The name Astanphaeus appears in the “Testament of Solomon,” as one of the demons of the zodiac. He is said to rule over the astrological sign of Aquarius.

In the Munich Handbook, Astanphaeus is said to be a spirit who can be conjured to reveal the future. He is also said to be able to compel love in a woman. The Munich Handbook gives a description of his appearance, which differs significantly from that of the Grimoire of Armadel. According to the Munich Handbook, Astanphaeus appears in the form of a man with a bird’s head. In his right hand he holds a sword, and in his left hand he holds a book.
