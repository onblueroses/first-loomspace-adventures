 “Then say these names for the spirits of the air: Sator, Aren, Tenet, Opera, Rotas.”

These are the famous Sator Arepo Tenet Opera Rotas wordsquare, which was used as a magical charm throughout the Middle Ages. In this case it is used to conjure spirits of the air.
