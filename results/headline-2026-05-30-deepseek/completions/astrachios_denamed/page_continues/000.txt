 “Then they will say: ‘Let the earth be opened, and let the seed come forth!’ And then it will be opened, and the earth will bring forth all its fruit, and they will eat the good things on earth, and the earth will have peace in that time such as it has not had since it was made until then.”

The continuation of the text, which is clearly a Christianized adaptation of the Jewish apocalypse, predicts the arrival of the Son of God, “the one who has been sent forth to speak with you.” The apocalypse, which is known from the Latin and Greek versions of the *Apocalypse of Ezra* (4 Ezra), originally predicted the coming of the Messiah, but here it is applied to Christ.

A version of the same text appears in the *Mystery of the Eighth Revealer*, in which the archangel Michael commands Ezra to write “the twenty-four names of the angels who are in charge of the firmament.” These are the names of the angels who rule the signs of the zodiac, and they are invoked in order to open the earth and release its treasures.
