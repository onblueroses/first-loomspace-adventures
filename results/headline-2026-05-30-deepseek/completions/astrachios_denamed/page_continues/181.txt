 'Let him [the practitioner] say these names, and let him say the following prayer.'

Then follows the prayer: 'O holy Baruchiel, Eloai, and Gabriel, archangels of God, who attend the throne of the Lord, send Raphael, the holy angel, that he may heal the sicknesses and the afflictions of mankind, and that he may come to my aid, and that he may heal the affliction of N., whom N. bore.' This prayer is to be recited thrice.

A translation of the whole page appears in Appendix 2. It is not difficult to see how this text might have been used in a healing context. It provides an incantation and a prayer which, if recited correctly, will bring about the assistance of Raphael in effecting a cure.
