 “This is the sigil of the spirit Elenion. He appears as a great king with a serpent’s tail. He gives the power of invisibility. He teaches all arts. He gives true answers about the past, present, and future. He gives good familiars.” The sigil is a complicated series of concentric circles, with letters around the edge. It looks like a magical disk of some kind.

The page continues: “To call him forth, write his sigil on parchment made from a hare’s skin, with the blood of a bat. Put it in a lead box, and bury it in a cemetery. Dig it up after nine days. When you need to call him forth, put the sigil in a fire of cypress wood, and speak the names given above.”
