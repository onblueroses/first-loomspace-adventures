 “The names of the nine servants of the seven governors, which are: Anostser, Esenentoser, Erimutes, Harpax, Akramm, Katasen, Lilioch, Mandus, Colphiores.” Below this is a note in another hand: “This is not the way it is. For the nine servants are not subject to the seven governors but rather to the seven sub-governors, each of whom is served by nine.” The next page lists the names of the sub-governors and their servants:

The names of the seven sub-governors: Aron, Enatiel, Amatiel, Exanaton, Iared, Ormeniel, Etatar.

The names of the nine servants of Aron: Anostser, Estor, Erimutes, Harpax, Akramm, Katasen, Lilioch, Mandus, Colphiores.

The names of the nine servants of Enatiel: Soter, Erimutes, Harpax, Akramm, Katasen, Lilioch, Mandus, Colphiores, Anostser.

The names of the nine servants of Amatiel: Erimutes, Harpax, Akram
