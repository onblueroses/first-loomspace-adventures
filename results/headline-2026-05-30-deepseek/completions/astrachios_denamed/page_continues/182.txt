 “O God, holy, powerful, ineffable Father, thou who didst establish the earth and form the sea, thou who art the great Light who rules in the heavens, thou who hast established the two great Lights in the firmament, thou who hast appointed the course of the Sun and the Moon and the stars, thou who hast given the law to man and hast established the sacred bond of marriage, thou who hast created the nature of animals and hast given the law to the beasts of the earth, the birds of the air and the fishes of the sea, thou who hast created living souls, thou who hast placed the four winds under thy power, thou who hast made the great and terrible name of eight letters, thou who hast made the great name of twelve letters, thou who hast appointed to each month an angel as its guardian, thou who hast appointed to each day an angel as its guardian, thou who hast appointed to each hour an angel as its guardian, thou who hast appointed the angels of the night and of the day, thou who hast appointed the angels of the seasons of the year, thou who hast appointed the angels of the solstices, thou who hast appointed the angels of the equinoxes, thou who hast appointed the angels of the years
