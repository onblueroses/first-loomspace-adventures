The ritual of the Black Pullet is a spell to create a talisman that will bring wealth and good fortune. It requires a live black hen and a number of black eggs, but the recipe can be adjusted if necessary.

The Ritual of the Black Pullet

Take a black hen that has never laid an egg. Place her in a
basin lined with black silk. Surround her with twenty-one
eggs, black as night. Place a mirror above her so that she can
see herself.

When she lays an egg, remove it and place it in a new
basin lined with black silk. Break the other eggs one by one,
and pour their contents over the hen.

When she lays a second egg, remove it and place it next to
the first. Break the shells of the eggs you poured over her and
place them around the two eggs she has laid.
