 You know it by its smell. It is the thing that was spoken of in the old books, the thing that has no name in any mortal tongue, the thing they call the Dweller in the Depths. Others call it the Cold One. It is the one that haunts the abyss, that coils in the black deeps, that knows the processes of death and decay, the rhythms of renewal, the ways of growth and life. It is the thing that you have called, here in this place of power, and it will do your bidding. And then it will require payment from you.

So you must be strong. You must not let it have you, not this time. You have been its servant in the past. But you are its master now. You are the master now. You must remember that.

It is speaking to you. Can you hear it? It is speaking within your mind.

It is saying:

Who calls me? Who summons me from the house of deepness?

Tell it who you are. Say:

It is I, the king, the master. It is I, the one who knows the secret ways, the one who holds the key. It is I, who drank from the well of dreams.
