# Chapter 2

M ARGARET LAY ON THE floor, on her side, her hands curled loosely near her face. Her eyes were open but unfocused. She was breathing, but shallowly. Her lips were slightly parted.

“Margaret?” whispered Roland. She didn’t move.

He took a step toward her, then another. He could hear her breathing now, a soft, wet sound. He knelt beside her.

“Margaret?” he said again. He touched her shoulder. It was warm. He shook her gently. “Margaret?”

Nothing.
