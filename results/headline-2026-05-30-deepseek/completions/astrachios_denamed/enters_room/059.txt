 A presence. A gentle presence. The presence of a woman. A young woman. She appears before me.

She is blonde. Her hair is long, wavy. She is wearing a simple white dress, no sleeves, no shoes. She is beautiful.

Her eyes are large, her lips full. She smiles, but does not speak.

“Who are you?” I ask.

She smiles.

“Are you a spirit?” I ask.

She smiles.

“Are you a goddess?” I ask.

She smiles.

“What is your name?” I ask.

She smiles.

“Are you here to help me?” I ask.

She smiles.

She says nothing. She smiles.

She moves slowly toward me.

She smiles.

She reaches her hand out, touches my face. Her hand is warm.

She smiles.

She leans forward. She kisses me on the cheek.

She smiles.

She turns, walks toward the window. She stops at the window, turns back, looks at me.

She smiles.

She steps out of the window.

She is gone.

I rush to the window. I look out. Nothing. She is gone.

I am left with the lingering memory of her smile.
