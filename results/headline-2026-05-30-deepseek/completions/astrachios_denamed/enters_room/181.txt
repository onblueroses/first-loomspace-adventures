You are mine to command.

You are mine to command.

You are mine to command.

I command you: bring me to her.

I command you: bring me to her.

I command you: bring me to her.

It is time.

There was a knock at the door.

“Come in,” she said.

She was sitting in a chair, by the window. She had been reading, but she had put the book down. She was just looking out the window, at the trees, at the sky.

The door opened. It was her father. He looked tired. He looked old. He looked—he looked sad.
