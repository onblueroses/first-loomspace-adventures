 Entering through the window. Entering through the door. Entering through the floor. Entering through the walls.

Eyes without faces. Faces without eyes.

A hand touches my face.

A tongue licks my neck.

A finger traces the shape of my mouth.

The shadows swirl around me. They are no longer shadows but a thick blackness that has a life of its own.

I am no longer afraid.

I am still afraid.

The blackness parts. A woman steps out of it. She is tall. She has long black hair. She wears a dress made of stars.

“Why did you summon me?” she asks.

“I need your help.”

“What do you want?”

“I want to find my brother.”

“What will you give me?”

“Whatever you ask.”

She laughs. It is a sound that should not exist. It is a sound that makes the world tremble.

“I want your soul,” she says.

“Take it,” I say.

She smiles. She reaches out her hand. She touches my forehead.

A coldness spreads through me. A coldness that is deeper than anything I have ever felt. A coldness that is the absence of everything.

I fall to my knees.

The woman disappears.
