 I see it. It is a man, but not a man. He is robed, but his robes are like the light of a star, and his face is veiled. He is like a pillar of light, and his voice is like the voice of a great multitude. He speaks:

“I am the angel of the sphere of Saturn, and I am come to give you the knowledge of the spirits of Saturn.”

And I ask: “Who are the spirits of Saturn?”

And the angel says: “The spirits of Saturn are the spirits of the dead, and the spirits of the earth, and the spirits of the air, and the spirits of the water, and the spirits of the fire.
