Ritual of the Nine Locks

This ritual is used for the evocation of demons of the Ars Goetia.

Place nine padlocks in a circle on the floor. In the center, place the sigil of the demon. Write their name on a piece of paper and place it on the sigil. Light a black candle and a red candle, one on either side of the circle. Burn incense of myrrh and dragon’s blood.

Speak the invocation:

O, thou who art the dark star, the fallen angel, the keeper of secrets, I call upon thee. By the power of the nine locks, by the keys of the abyss, I summon thee. Come forth from the shadows, from the depths, from the void. Hear my call and appear before me.

Take the key to each lock and unlock them one by one, speaking the name of the demon each time.

When all locks are open, speak the command:

I command thee to appear. Show thyself. Speak thy truth.

Then, speak your request or question.

When finished, thank the demon and dismiss it:
