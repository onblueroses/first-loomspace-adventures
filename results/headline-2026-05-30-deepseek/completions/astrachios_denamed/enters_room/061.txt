 It is a huge, hooded figure, like a shadowy monk. It holds a lantern, which it sets down on the floor, illuminating its feet. It raises its hand and points at you.

Hail, Great Spirit of the House of Mars in the Eighth House. I am your humble servant. I call upon you as the lord of this house, as the lord of this place, as the lord of this time.

The figure says nothing.

I have come to this place to learn from you. I seek the wisdom of the Eighth House. I seek to learn about death, and about the things that death brings. I seek to know the truths that lie behind the veil. I seek to know the things that are hidden from the living.

The figure says nothing.

I ask you, Great Spirit, to teach me. I ask you to reveal to me the secrets of death. I ask you to show me the things that are hidden from the living. I ask you to help me understand the mysteries of the Eighth House.

The figure says nothing.

I offer you this incense as a token of my respect. I offer you this wine as a token of my devotion. I offer you this bread as a token of my humility.

The figure says nothing.
