 The door is open, and something enters the room, and stands in the middle of the room. It is not human; it is something that was once human, but now has been changed so that it is no longer human. It is dressed in black, and wears a mask. It is not alive, and it is not dead. It is something that is neither alive nor dead. It is something that has been changed, so that it is no longer human.
