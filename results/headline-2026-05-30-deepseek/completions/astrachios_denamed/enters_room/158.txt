 I see it. It is a black cloud of smoke. It has no shape. But I know its name.

“You are Sopro, I command you.”

It is there. It has no shape.

“I command you to speak.”

I hear it speak. It speaks in my head. It speaks in my mind.

“I am Sopro.”

“I command you to tell me who the stranger is who came to my house.”

“The stranger is a stranger.”

“I command you to tell me what he wants.”

“He wants you.”

“I command you to tell me why he wants me.”

“He wants you because you are his enemy.”

“Who is he?”

“He is your enemy.”

“What is his name?”

“He has many names. You know him by the name of the one who brought him.”

“I command you to tell me his name.”

“His name is Cthulhu.”

“He is in my house now?”

“He is not.”

“Where is he?”

“He is waiting.”

“Where is he waiting?”

“He is waiting for you to bring him.”

“I command you to tell me what I must do to be rid of him.”
