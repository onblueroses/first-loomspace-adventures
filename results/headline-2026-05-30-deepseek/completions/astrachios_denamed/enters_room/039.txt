 A horse with two heads, one black, one white. It stands, watching me. It is the horse from the Tarot card of the Moon. The heads are not exactly heads. They are more like masks, shaped like heads. The horse has no eyes. It stands there, waiting. It has come to take me on a journey.

I climb upon its back. It walks forward, into the darkness. A tunnel, going down. The horse walks downward into the tunnel. The tunnel is dark. I see a light ahead, and we come into a cavern lit by a single lamp, hanging from the roof. There is a man there, with a huge book. He is the guardian of the first gate. I know that I must pass him to go farther.

He says, "Who are you?"

I say, "I am a child of Earth and starry Heaven."

He says, "What do you seek?"

I say, "To know."

He says, "Then you must pass through the first gate."
