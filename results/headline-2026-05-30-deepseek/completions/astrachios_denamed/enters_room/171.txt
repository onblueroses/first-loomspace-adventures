 It has no name. It is a shadow. It is a thing made of shadow. It has no mouth. But it speaks:

“I am a child of the Lord.”

“What is your name?”

“I have no name.”

“What is your desire?”

“To dwell.”

“Where?”

“In this house.”

“For how long?”

“Forever.”

“Will you do harm?”

“I will not.”

“Will you obey?”

“I will.”

“Will you protect?”

“I will.”

“Will you serve?”

“I will.”

“Then I accept you.”

The shadow enters him. He feels its coldness, its hardness. It has no body but it is like a body. It is a body of shadow. It enters him and fills him. He feels it moving inside him. He feels it taking possession of him. He feels it becoming him.

He opens his eyes. The room is dark. The candles are out. The incense is gone. The circle is broken.

He stands up. He feels different. He feels stronger. He feels colder. He feels darker.

He walks to the window. He looks at the city. He sees the lights. He sees the people. He sees the cars. He sees the world.
