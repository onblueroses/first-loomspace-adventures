And now. And now. And now. And now.

He opened his eyes.

He was in the room. And now the room was full. It was full of voices and presences. And there was a silence, and there was a wind.

He looked at the desk. The book was open. It was open to a page with an illustration: an angel, descending, with a sword in one hand and a book in the other. It was not a comforting illustration.

He looked at the page opposite the illustration. There was a name on the page. He knew it was the name of the angel in the picture. He said it.

There was a cold shiver.

Now he spoke a sentence, from the same page. His mouth felt uncomfortable, as if he were speaking with somebody else's tongue.

He had not needed to read the sentence. He knew it, as he knew that two plus two was four, as he knew his own name.

The silence in the room became deeper. Outside the room there was a scratching at the door, a rattling at the window-glass.

The lights flickered and failed.
