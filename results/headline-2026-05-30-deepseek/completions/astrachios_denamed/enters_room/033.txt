 He rises.

He says:

Where is the man?

The man says:

I am here.

He says:

Who are you?

The man says:

I am John Dee.

He says:

Why do you call me?

John Dee says:

I seek your knowledge.

He says:

What knowledge do you seek?

John Dee says:

I seek the knowledge of God.

He says:

Why do you seek the knowledge of God?

John Dee says:

I seek the knowledge of God for the glory of God.

He says:

No man may know God.

John Dee says:

I seek the knowledge of God so that I may better serve Him.

He says:

No man may serve God.

John Dee says:

I seek the knowledge of God so that I may understand His creation.

He says:

No man may understand God’s creation.

John Dee says:

Then what is the point of my life?

He says:

To live.

He says:

To die.

John Dee says:

Then why did you come?

He says:

I came because you called me.

John Dee says:

Why?

He says:

Because you called me.

John Dee says:

And?

He says:

And nothing.

John Dee says:

You came because I called you?

He says:

Yes.

John Dee
