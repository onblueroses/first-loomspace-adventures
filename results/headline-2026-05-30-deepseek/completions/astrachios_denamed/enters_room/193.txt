How to use the sigils:

On a sheet of paper, draw the sigils of the spirits you wish to summon, arranged around the central sigil of the King. On the back of the paper, write the names of the spirits and the King. Hold the paper and read the conjuration, then speak the names of the spirits you wish to summon. When they appear, speak your desire and ask them to fulfill it.
