From the east, a man with the head of a cat, bearing a sword in his hand and a serpent in his left. He wears a scarlet robe. He is called BETHOR.

From the west, a woman with the head of a frog, bearing a silver cup in her hand. She wears a black robe. She is called PHALEG.

From the north, a man with the head of a lion, bearing a golden crown in his hand. He wears a white robe. He is called OCH.

From the south, a man with the head of an eagle, bearing a steel dagger in his hand. He wears a yellow robe. He is called HAGITH.

These are the Powers of the four Quarters, the Guardians of the Palace of the Seventh Sphere. They are the Watchtowers of the Universe. They are the four Great Kings.
