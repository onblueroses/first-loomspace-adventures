Harken to the words of the God of the Sun:

‘I am the radiant Lord of the Sun, whose power supersedes the heavens, a fire that consumes all. My ears hear the sound of sacred words, and the hidden is made known to me. I am Re-Harakhti in his rising and his setting. I am the God Aton, whose disk shines forth in the horizon, whose rays are in my body. I am the God Ankh, who comes forth from the ring of the sun. I am the Lord of the Hosts of Heaven, and I give the command to open the gates of the sky. I am the Lord whose appearances are holy, the Master of the Crowns of the North and South, who made the gods to come into being. I am the Lord of Light, who overthrows his enemies. I am the Lord of the Sunbeams, who causes joy to arise. I am the God Aten, whose appearances are beautiful, who makes the Two Lands festive, who makes mankind to live.
