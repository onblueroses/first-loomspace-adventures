 The atmosphere thickens. The smell of ozone and brimstone. There is something in the room with them, something they cannot see.

"Stay back," she says.

"What is it?"

"It's a demon."

"A demon?"

"Yes."

"Are you sure?"

"Yes."

"What does it want?"

"It wants to come in."

"What?"

"It wants to enter me."

"What?"

"The demon wants to possess me."

"What?"

"Stay back."

It is a cold, malevolent thing. It wants her body. It wants her soul. It wants to enter her and use her.

It is here. In the room with them.

She is aware of it moving towards her.

"Stay back."

She sees it now, its form coalescing out of the darkness. A thing of shadow and smoke. It is tall, and thin. Its head is the head of a jackal. Its eyes are glowing coals.

It moves towards her.

It wants to enter her.

She feels its coldness.

She feels its malevolence.

She feels its hunger.

She feels its need.

It wants to enter her.

It wants to possess her.

It wants to use her.

It wants to devour her.

It wants to consume her.

It wants to destroy her.
