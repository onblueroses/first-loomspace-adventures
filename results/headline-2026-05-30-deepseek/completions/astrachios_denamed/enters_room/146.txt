The ritual of the so-called “Call of the Thirty Aethers” is performed on the night of the new moon. The ritualist, robed in white, sits in a chair in a room lit by three candles. In the ritualist’s lap is a bronze dish containing the ingredients of incense. The ritualist lights the incense and speaks the names, then closes his or her eyes. After some time, something will enter the room.

What is summoned by this ritual is an intelligence of some kind. The nature of these intelligences is not known, nor is the purpose for which they are called. It is known that these beings will answer questions, and that they have knowledge of the future.
