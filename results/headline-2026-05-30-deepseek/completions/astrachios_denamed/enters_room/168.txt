 That which dwells in the desert. That which is master of the palace.

I call you, Asmodeus. I summon you. I command you to appear.

Kneel before me, Asmodeus.

By the power of the names of God, I bind you.

You will obey me, Asmodeus.

You will tell me the location of the chalice.

You will tell me how it may be found.

You will tell me how it may be used.

You will tell me how it may be broken.

You will tell me all, and you will tell me truly.

Then you will leave this place and trouble me no more.

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, Amen.

He waited. He had expected the demon to appear instantly, but perhaps it had to travel from its dwelling place, wherever that was. The desert, the palace. Somewhere near to Damascus, perhaps.

He waited for a long time.

He waited until the candle guttered and died, and the room was dark.

He waited until the cold made his joints ache and his teeth chatter.

He waited until the sky outside the window began to lighten.

And then he knew.

He had failed.

He had done everything
