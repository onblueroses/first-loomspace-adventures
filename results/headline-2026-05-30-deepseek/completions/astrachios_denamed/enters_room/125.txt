 Not a man, but the shape of a man. Tall, gaunt, white-faced. The eyes are colorless and blank. It stands motionless, but for its head which turns slowly, left to right, taking in the whole room.

“I’m here,” says the voice.

“I’m here,” says the shape.

“And I’m here,” says the other.

“We’re here,” says the first voice.

“We’re here,” says the shape.

“We’re here,” says the other.

All three speak at once.

The figure moves. It walks to the window and looks out. It turns back into the room. It walks to the door. It opens the door and looks out into the hall. It closes the door. It walks to the center of the room and stands there. It raises its arms. It lets them fall. It raises them again. It lets them fall. It raises them again. It lowers them. It stands motionless.

“I’m here,” says the voice.

“I’m here,” says the shape.

“And I’m here,” says the other.

“We’re here,” says the first voice.

“We’re here,” says the shape
