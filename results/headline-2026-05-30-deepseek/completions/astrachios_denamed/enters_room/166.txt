# 15

The room was full of ghosts. Not ghosts as such—not pale, trembling figures. No, these were solid, tangible, powerfully present. The wind that was the angel filled them, and each one stood upright, eyes wide, lips parted as though to speak.

There were nine of them, and among them, two whom Gentle knew: Pie 'oh' Pah and Judith. The rest were strangers, men and women of various ages and conditions. But all shared the same astonished stare. They were like statues, arranged around the room with no apparent pattern, except that one of them stood close to the window, and the light from outside fell on his face. It was Jude. Gentle looked from her to the mystif, who stood no more than two yards from him, and then back to Jude. She hadn't moved, nor had she taken her eyes from him. He spoke her name, but she didn't seem to hear him. He started to move towards her, but as he did so a voice behind him said:

"Gentle."

It was Pie, speaking his name.

He turned back. The mystif was staring at him, its face slack.

"Pie?" he said.
