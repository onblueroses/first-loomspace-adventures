 A black thing, like a sack of smoke. It has the vague shape of a person but without any detail, as though made of smoke. It stands in the center of the room, looking around.

Its voice is a whispery hiss.

“Who calls me?”

I answer: “We do.”

“Why?”

“We have a question.”

“What is the question?”

I glance at Corvus and he says, “Who murdered the boy?”

The thing says, “There is no answer.”

“We were told there would be an answer,” I say.

The thing says, “There are many answers.”

“Who murdered the boy?” Corvus repeats.

The thing says, “The man who made the knife.”

“Who made the knife?” I ask.

The thing says, “The man who made the knife.”

“Who murdered the boy?” Corvus asks again.

The thing says, “The woman who opened the door.”

“Who opened the door?” I ask.

The thing says, “The woman who opened the door.”
