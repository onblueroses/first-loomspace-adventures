 If it is not the King, it is his messenger. It will be one of the spirits of the Moon. It will be in the form of a man with a long beard, wearing a white robe. It will have a crown on its head, made of gold and pearls. It will have a sword in its hand.

Greet the spirit. Ask it why it has come. It will tell you that it is a messenger of the King. It will say that the King has sent it to you to give you a message. It will tell you the message.

The spirit will ask you if you have any questions for the King. If you do, ask them. The spirit will answer your questions.

When you are finished, thank the spirit. Bid it farewell. It will depart.
