 The face of a man who is not a man, a face of many faces, a face of no face. A face that shifts from male to female, from child to elder, from human to animal to angel to demon to god. A face that is not a face but a mask of shifting light and shadow and smoke.

A voice that is not a voice but a chorus of voices, some sweet, some harsh, some young, some old, some human, some not.

"I am here."

"What do you seek?"

"I seek the truth of my father's death. I seek the truth of the man who calls himself Thomas Crain. I seek the knowledge that has been hidden from me."

"Then ask."

"What is the true nature of Thomas Crain?"

The face shifts. The smoke swirls. The voices speak.

"Thomas Crain is not one but many. He is a man of many faces, many names, many lives."
