 The man from the fire.

THE MAN: Come on. Don’t be shy. (He holds out his hand.)

KATE: I’m not shy.

THE MAN: It’s all right. I’m not going to hurt you.

KATE: I know.

THE MAN: Then take my hand. Let me take you with me.

KATE: Where?

THE MAN: Don’t ask.

KATE: I have to ask.

THE MAN: It’s better if you don’t. (He smiles.) Just take my hand.

KATE: What’s your name?

THE MAN: I don’t have a name.

KATE: Everybody has a name.

THE MAN: Not where I’m going.

KATE: I don’t understand.

THE MAN: You don’t have to understand.

KATE: I’m afraid.

THE MAN: There’s nothing to be afraid of.

KATE: I’m afraid of the fire.

THE MAN: There is no fire.

KATE: But I see it.

THE MAN: It’s not there.

KATE: It is!

THE MAN: It’s not real.

KATE: It looks real.
