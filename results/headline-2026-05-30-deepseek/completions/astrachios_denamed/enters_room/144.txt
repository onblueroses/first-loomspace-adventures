 The seal is broken. The name is spoken.

The Spirit of Mercury is here.

It knows your name. It knows your face.

It knows you and it sees you.

And it can hear you.

Speak your request.

[The spirit is commanded to appear and lend its power to the working.]

The Spirit of Mercury is here.

It has heard your request.

It has lent its power.

Now speak the names that seal the way:

Genilum Sopro, Scildon, Paeh, Izaphel, Elapos, Dapa, Damiol, Imathadel, Ebpisha, Akrah, Epoci, Ena, Cariso, Gepulanel, Sapoel, Aphragoseh, Enagro, Elotin, Adopal, Baracaza, Bloutel, Anchanzel, Ipeodoc, Ireharel, Aramonis, Sipat, Acla Berdimoriel, Asep, Asthrenios.

The seal is restored. The spirit has departed.

So it is done.

[The circle is closed.]
