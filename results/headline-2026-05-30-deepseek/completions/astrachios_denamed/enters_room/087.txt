**RITUAL OF CREATION**

This ritual is designed to give an object or a piece of writing its own life.

Place the object (a sculpture, a painting, a manuscript, etc.) in the center of a circle.

Cast the circle. Light a candle for each of the four directions.

Stand facing the north. Draw a pentagram in the air.

Say:

_By the powers of the north, I give you life. I call upon the spirits of the north to bless you with life. Life to the sculpture. Life to the painting. Life to the writing. Life to the words. Life to the art. Life to the soul._
