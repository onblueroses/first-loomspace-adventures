 A sudden darkness. The deep purple of a bruise. There is a rustling sound, like something with wings. Then a stillness. Then a voice, low and commanding, speaking from somewhere within the gloom.

Voice: I am here.

Magus: In the name of the Seven Holy Names, I command you to tell me who you are.

Voice: I am the Angel of the West Wind.

Magus: In the name of the Seven Holy Names, I command you to tell me what power you have.

Voice: I have power over the West Wind.

Magus: In the name of the Seven Holy Names, I command you to tell me what you can do.

Voice: I can bring storms, and I can calm them. I can bring a gentle breeze, or I can bring a gale.

Magus: In the name of the Seven Holy Names, I command you to tell me what you will do for me.

Voice: I will do whatever you ask of me, so long as it is within my power.

Magus: In the name of the Seven Holy Names, I command you to tell me what you want in return.

Voice: I want nothing in return.
