The invocation has been heard. The Tongueless One is present — not as a form, but as a shift in the atmosphere: a sudden drop in temperature, a stillness that swallows sound, a pressure against the inner ear.

What enters the room is not a figure, but an **absence shaped like a man**. A space where the light bends away, a living void wearing the outline of a Daiken. From this form comes no breath, no scent, no footfall — only the sensation of **being perceived from within**, as if your bones are being read like scripture.

The air begins to vibrate with a silent frequency. It is not a voice, but knowledge impressed directly upon the mind:

*You have spoken the true names. You have drawn the line between the just and the unjust. You have called from the Void Place. I am here.*
