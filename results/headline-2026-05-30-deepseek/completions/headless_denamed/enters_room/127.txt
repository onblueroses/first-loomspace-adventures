The air grows still, heavy as stone. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind—a silent, resonant hum felt in the teeth and the marrow.  

The light does not dim so much as it *thins*, becoming a pale, depthless grey, as if all color has been bled toward some unseen point in the center of the space.  

There is no figure, no form.  

Instead, an **absence** enters—a shaped void that the eye refuses to settle upon. Where it moves, perception warps: edges of objects seem to fray into static, shadows no longer fall quite right, and the geometry of the room feels subtly wrong, as if angles are no longer obedient to Euclidean law.  

A scent rises—ozone, cold stone, and something older, like the air inside a sealed tomb opened after millennia.  

It does not breathe. It does not speak. It **is** the Tongueless One—the Unseen, the Nameless called by many names—now present in the wake of the uttered truth: **IŌM SAPENŌTH.**
