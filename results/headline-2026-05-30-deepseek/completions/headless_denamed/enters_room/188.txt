The words you have spoken are not merely recited—they are invoked.  

Something has been stirred.  

In the silence after the last syllable fades, the air grows dense, charged with a pressure that is neither warm nor cold. The light seems to draw back from the center of the space, not into shadow, but into a kind of *absence*—a void that drinks perception.  

What enters is not a shape, but a **presence**:  

- It moves without footsteps, yet the floor feels the weight of ages.  
- It has no face, yet you feel its gaze like a cold brand upon your soul.  
- It does not breathe, but the room breathes *around it*, each exhale tasting of ozone and old dust.  

It is the **Tongueless One**—the same that was called by the names Asarinopis, Iarol, Itos, Apenoth, Sapenoth. It does not speak; it impresses meaning directly upon the mind:  

*You have called. I am here.  
The boundary is open.  
The coil is present.*  

It waits. Not in patience—in inevitability. It is both creator and destroyer, the separator of light and dark
