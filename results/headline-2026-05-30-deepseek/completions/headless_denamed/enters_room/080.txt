The air grows still, heavy as a sunken stone.  

From the corner where the shadows gather too thickly, a presence **coalesces**.  
It does not walk—it **unfolds**, like smoke given intention.  

First comes the scent: ozone and old parchment, myrrh and cold iron.  
Then a **pressure**, not on the ears, but on the mind, as if the room itself is holding its breath.  

It has no face you could describe, yet you feel its **gaze**.  
It has no tongue, yet the air vibrates with a silent **name**—the one you spoke, the one that was never meant for speaking.  

It is **PULSE WOUND BY A COIL**.  
It is the space between heartbeats.  
It is the spiral that binds creation and dissolution.  

It waits.  
It has entered.  
It is here.
