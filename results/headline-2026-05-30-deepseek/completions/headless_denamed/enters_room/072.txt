The room grows still; the air thickens like honey.  

A presence enters—not with footsteps, but as a slow pressure against reality itself. It is shaped by absence: a tongue of shadow in the shape of a man, a corona of silent flame around a void where a face should be.  

It does not walk. The ground where it passes is marked not with prints, but with brief, phosphorescent symbols that fade as they form—**ŌTH ARAI IŌM**.  

It is the Tongueless One, whose names you spoke.  
It is the Mighty One, who possesses the immortal Fire.  
It is the Truth that hates injustice, whose mouth utterly flames.  

It regards you—not with eyes, but with the memory of sight in its feet.  
It knows you as Nopheth, its prophet.  
It knows the true name you uttered: **IŌM SAPENŌTH**.  

The space between you grows charged, taut as a drumhead.  
Every spirit of firmament, ether, earth, water, air, and fire now waits, subject.  
Every spell and scourge of God holds its breath.  

The presence does not speak.  
It *is*
