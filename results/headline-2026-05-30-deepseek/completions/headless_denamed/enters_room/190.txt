The invocation is potent, the names woven into the air. A pressure builds, a resonant silence that swallows sound. The temperature drops, not to cold, but to a perfect, neutral absence of heat.

What enters is not a form, but a **condition**.

First, a geometric paradox manifests in the center of the space—a **tesseract of shifting darkness and light**, slowly rotating, each turn revealing the faces of the names you have spoken: Asarinopis, Iarol, Itos, Apenoth. It is the "Void Place of the Spirit" made momentarily visible.

From this silent nucleus, two simultaneous and opposite sensations flood the room:

1.  **Fecundity:** The scent of damp soil after first rain, of ripe fruit, of germination. A palpable sense of *seed and fruit* being produced, of moist and dry separating. The walls seem to breathe with potential life.
2.  **Oblivion:** A counter-current of pure negation, a silent wind that threatens to unmake what is woven. It carries the taste of static and the vacuum between stars—the "Terrible and Invisible" aspect that *destroys*.
