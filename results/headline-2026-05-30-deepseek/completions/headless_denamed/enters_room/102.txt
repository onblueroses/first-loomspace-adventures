The air grows still, then thickens—a pressure not of sound but of its absence. From the corner where shadows pool deepest, a form begins to coalesce. It is not a shape you see with eyes, but one you feel in the marrow: a presence ancient, double-natured, tongueless yet speaking directly into the silence of the mind.

It is the **Tongueless One**, invoked by its true and secret names.

It enters not through door or window, but through the very invocation—through the cracks between the words *IŌM SAPENŌTH*. It is the distinction between just and unjust made manifest, the simultaneous principle of creation and dissolution. You feel its gaze—a regard from no visible face—that weighs your spirit as it once weighed the moist and the dry, the seed and the fruit.

The room now holds the **Mighty One who possesses the immortal Fire**. A scent of ozone and cold soil lingers. The light seems to bend, not dimming, but deepening, as if the darkness it brings is not an absence of light, but its older, more potent sibling.

It awaits. Not a command, for it is subject to none—but a recognition. The pulse of its name—
