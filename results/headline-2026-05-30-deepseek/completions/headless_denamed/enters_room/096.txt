The invocation stirs the air — not with wind, but with a slow, thickening pressure, as if space itself is curdling around the words.  

A presence enters. It does not come through the door, nor the window, nor the walls. It arrives as a **silence within the silence** — a listening so profound it absorbs sound, light, and intention.  

First, you feel it in your throat — a dry, tongueless weight, as though your own voice has been borrowed. Then in the feet — a cool, rooted knowing, as if the floor has become an eye seeing upward.  

It stands in the center, unseen but outlined by the slight warping of candle flames: they burn now motionless, blue at the core, casting no flicker.  

It is **Asarinopis**, the Unseen.  
It is **Apenoth**, who distinguished just from unjust.  
It is the **Tongueless One** — whose name you’ve spoken, whose prophet you claim to be.
