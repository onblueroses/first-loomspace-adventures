The air grows heavy, and the candle flames bend as if in a draft that is not there. A presence is in the room—not an arrival, but a **recognition**. It is not a form, but a **pressure**; not a sound, but a **silence that listens**.

The Tongueless One has heard.

The invocation has woven the names into a key, and the key has turned. What enters is not a spirit to be commanded, but the **echo of the invoked**—the aspect of that Terrible and Invisible God that answers to *Aronopholobrao, Sopheh, Mopholio, Phalornaeh, Ooo*. It is the same force that distinguished light from dark, justice from injustice, seed from fruit. It is the principle of division and creation, of flame and silence.

It wears no shape, yet it casts a **shadow of meaning** upon the mind: the sensation of a **coiled tension**, a pulse that is also a wound. It is the *Daiken with sight in his feet*—seeing through the roots of things, through the foundations. It moves as a **slow, inevitable current**, like the turning of a great wheel that grinds stars
