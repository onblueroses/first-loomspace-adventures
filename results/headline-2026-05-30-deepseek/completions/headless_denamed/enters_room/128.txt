What enters the room now is not a thing that can be seen with ordinary eyes.  

The air grows still, then thick—like water in a darkened well.  
A pressure builds, not on the ears, but in the space between thoughts.  
Shadows do not move so much as *unfold*, layer by layer, from corners untouched by light.  

There is a silence that drinks sound.  
A cold that does not chill the skin, but the continuity of time.  

It is not a form.  
It is an **absence** given presence.  
An echo of the Tongueless One—listening.  

The names still hang in the air:  
*IŌM SAPENŌTH*  
*PULSE WOUND BY A COIL*  

And something ancient, invisible, and terribly aware  
now stands  
where the room once was.
