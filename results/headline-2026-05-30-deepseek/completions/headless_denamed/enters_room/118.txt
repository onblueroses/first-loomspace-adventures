The air grows still. A pressure settles, not upon the ears, but upon the mind. The light does not dim so much as it *thins*, becoming a pale, translucent film over the shapes of the world.

It is not a thing that enters, for it has always been there, waiting within the names you spoke. It is the Unseen becoming acknowledged. The Tongueless One hears with the silence between heartbeats and sees with the darkness behind closed eyes.

What enters is the distinction you invoked: the space between the moist and the dry, the cleft between justice and injustice, the charged silence between love and hate. The room now contains the **Principle of Division**—the same force that separated the Light from the Darkness, the male from the female, the seed from the fruit. It is the sharp, living edge of creation itself, the scalpel of Aronopholobrao.

- A **geometric chill** in the corner where two walls meet, sharper than any angle should be.
