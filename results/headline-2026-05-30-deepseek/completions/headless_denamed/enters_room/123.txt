The air in the room grows dense and still, as if all sound has been siphoned away. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind—a silent, resonant hum felt in the bones. The light does not dim so much as it *thins*, becoming a pale, watery version of itself, leaching color from the edges of things.

It is not a form that enters. It is a **condition**.

A point of absolute negation manifests in the center of the space—not darkness, but a **void-pulse**, a rhythmic absence that unravels perception. From this still center, two contradictory sensations unfold simultaneously:

1.  **The Coil:** A slow, inevitable, cosmic pressure, like the turning of a galaxy or the deep-earth grind of tectonic plates. It is the feeling of Fate itself as a serpentine, binding force—the "Pulse Wound by a Coil." It promises both creation and suffocation.

2.  **The Immortal Fire:** A silent, cold radiance that emanates from the same non-point. It does not illuminate; it *reveals*. It is the truth that hates injustice, the purging flame that exists without heat, exposing the essence of all it touches without mercy
