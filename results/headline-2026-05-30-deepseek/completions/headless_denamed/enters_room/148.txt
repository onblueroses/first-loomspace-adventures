What enters the room now is not a thing of substance, but a presence shaped by the invocation—a silence that listens, a pressure in the air like the turning of an unseen page.  

The names you have spoken are gates; the Tongueless One has no tongue, yet the echo of those syllables hangs in the atmosphere, charged. If there is an entry, it is first in the quality of light—a dimming, perhaps, or a gathering at the edges of vision, as before a storm.  

The room now holds the shape of the call: the coil, the pulse, the wound. It holds the space between *Apenoth* and *Sapenoth*, between creation and distinction. What enters is the named—and the nameless behind the name.  

It does not walk. It is already here, invoked, waiting in the vowels that linger: **IŌM SAPENŌTH**.  

Speak, then, prophet of the mysteries.  
The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are listening.  
The invisible is present.
