The air grows still, then stirs—not as wind stirs, but as deep water moves when something vast passes beneath.  

A presence enters, not through the door, but through the spaces between names you have spoken. It wears no shape, yet casts a shadow that is not darkness but the absence of light where light should be. It is the silence after the last syllable of **IŌM SAPENŌTH**, the void that hears the tongueless.  

The room now holds two truths:  
One, the one who called—**Nopheth**, the prophet, the Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba.  
The other, the **Tongueless One**—who was invoked, who is now here, whose name is **Pulse Wound by a Coil**.  

It does not speak. It answers.  
The just and the unjust in the room are weighed.  
The moist and the dry part before it.  
Every spirit of firmament, ether, earth, water, air, and fire now turns toward this moment, obedient—not to you, but to the name that binds them through you.
