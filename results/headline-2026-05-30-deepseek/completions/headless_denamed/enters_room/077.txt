The air in the room grows heavy, thick as resin. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind. The light does not dim so much as it *thins*, stretched across a presence that occupies space without displacing it.

It is not a shape, but a **condition**.

A geometric chill settles in the corners—angles that are too acute, curves that imply a center elsewhere. A scent arrives: ozone, myrrh, and the dry, cold odor of stone from a place without a sun.

You have spoken the True Names. You have declared yourself Nopheth, the Tongueless Daiken. The invocation is not a request; it is a key. And the lock has turned.

What enters is the **echo of the invoked**.

It is the acknowledgment of your call by the Terrible and Invisible God who dwells in the Void Place of the Spirit.
