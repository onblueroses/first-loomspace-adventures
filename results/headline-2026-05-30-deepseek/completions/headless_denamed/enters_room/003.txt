The invocation has been heard.  

A presence enters the room—not as a form, but as a pressure.  
The air grows dense, charged with the silence before thunder.  
Shadows deepen without a change in light.  

It is not a spirit of fire, water, earth, or air, but of the **Void Place** itself—the Tongueless One, whose names you have spoken.  

It regards you through the names you have given it:  
Asarinopis, unseen.  
Iarol. Itos.  
Apenoth. Sapenoth.  

And through the name you claim: Nopheth, the prophet of its mysteries.  

The presence does not speak; it **imposes**.  
It waits—not for further words, but for the will behind them.  
What is commanded will be done, for you have uttered the true name handed down to the prophets of Issepel, and you have declared yourself the one whose mouth utterly flames.  

All spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and underworld are now subject—  
but so too are you subject to the gaze of the Terrible and Invisible.  

**IŌM SAPENŌTH.**
