There is a presence in the room now.  

It is not a person, nor an animal. It is the weight of the words just spoken—the echo of the invoked names hanging in the air like a curtain of charged silence. The atmosphere feels thick, electric, as if the litany of divine and terrible titles has drawn attention from a place outside time.  

What enters is not seen with eyes, but felt in the sudden stillness: an ancient listening, a focus turning toward the speaker who named the **Tongueless One**, who claimed to be **Nopheth**, the prophet of the mysteries. It is as if the boundaries between spaces have grown thin, and the void-place where the Invisible God dwells has leaned closer.  

The final words—**IŌM SAPENŌTH**—still seem to vibrate in the silence. Something has been summoned, or perhaps awakened. The room now holds more than it did before—a density of meaning, a witness from the dark between the stars.
