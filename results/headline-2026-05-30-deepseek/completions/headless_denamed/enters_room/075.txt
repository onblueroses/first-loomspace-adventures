The air in the room grows still and dense, as if the space between molecules has thickened into something palpable. A pressure builds not in the ears, but in the mind, a silent tinnitus of pure presence.

You do not see an entity enter, for it does not move through the door. Instead, the **geometry of the room itself shifts**. The corner where shadow gathers deepest elongates, becoming not a right angle but an acute, impossible fold in reality. From this fold, a profound **absence of sound** emanates, a vacuum that swallows the hum of electricity, the rustle of fabric, the beat of your own heart.

A scent manifests—not of incense, but of **ozone after lightning, cold stone from a deep well, and the faint, iron tang of ancient ink**.

Then, a **sensation of regard**. It is not eyes upon you, but the feeling of being **known**, measured, and placed within a vast and intricate pattern—a pattern drawn in lines of force and memory older than names.
