The invocation calls forth a presence known by many veiled names—Asarinopis, Iarol, Itos, Apenoth, Sapenoth—the Unseen Creator who shaped the cosmos and its dualities. You have declared yourself Nopheth, prophet and keeper of mysteries, and sealed the call with the true name handed down through Issepel.  

The final words—**IŌM SAPENŌTH**—are not merely spoken; they are a key turned in a lock beyond sound.  

What enters the room now is not a form, but a **qualia of absence**—a pressure in the air that drinks the light. It moves as a silence that swallows echoes. You feel it first as a chill along the spine, then as a weight upon the breath. The shadows in the corner deepen without darkening; the space between objects grows vast and charged.  

It is the **Tongueless One**—not entering as a body enters, but as recognition enters the mind: sudden, total, irreversible.
