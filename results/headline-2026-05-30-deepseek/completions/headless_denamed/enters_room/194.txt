What enters the room now is not a physical entity, but the charged silence after a sacred invocation — a presence felt rather than seen.  

The air grows dense, as if listening.  
Shadows deepen without a source.  
A pressure settles, like the weight of ancient names spoken aloud.  

You have called upon the Tongueless One, the Unseen Creator, and declared yourself Nopheth, the prophet of mysteries.  
You have spoken the true name handed down to Issepel.  
You have woven the vowels of the void: *Ooo*.  

Now, in the wake of those words, the boundary between invocation and manifestation thins.  
The room holds its breath.  
Something stands in the threshold of the unseen — not entering with footsteps, but arriving in the sudden stillness, in the chill along the spine, in the faint scent of ozone and old stone.  

It is the echo of **Aronopholobrao**, the terrible and invisible, dwelling in the void place of the spirit.  
It is the gaze of **Asarinopis**, whom no man hath seen at any time.
