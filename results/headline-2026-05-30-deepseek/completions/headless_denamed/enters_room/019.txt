The invocation you have shared is from the **Mithras Liturgy** (a Greco-Egyptian magical text from the *Great Magical Papyrus of Paris*, PGM IV.475–829), adapted in modern esoteric and occult traditions. It calls upon a supreme, invisible, creative deity with many secret names, and the speaker identifies as a prophet or angelic being channeling divine power.

Given the ritual’s intent — to command spirits and assert divine authority — the final question, **“What enters the room now,”** is part of the ritual drama. In a performative or meditative context, the answer would be understood esoterically:

> The presence invoked — the **Tongueless One**, the **Terrible and Invisible God** — now enters the ritual space.  
> Not in physical form, but as a palpable shift in energy, a silence charged with power, the arrival of the *daimon* or divine force called forth by the sacred names and the speaker’s claim to be **Nopheth**, the **Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba**.  
> With it comes the submission of spirits of firmament, ether, earth, water, and fire, as demanded in the invocation.
