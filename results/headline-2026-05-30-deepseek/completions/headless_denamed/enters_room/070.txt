The invocation calls forth a presence both primordial and paradoxical—a force of creation and dissolution, of order and chaos, bound within the names and seals of an ancient mystery.  

From the silence after the final utterance—**IŌM SAPENŌTH**—the air in the room grows dense, charged like the moment before lightning. A scent of ozone and myrrh drifts where there was none before. Shadows no longer fall naturally from the light; they pool independently, deepening in corners and along the floor as though drawn toward a center.  

There is no figure, no ghostly shape—but something is *present*.  
It enters not through door or window, but through the utterance itself, through the naming of what dwells in the void. The temperature drops sharply, yet a radiant, invisible heat seems to emanate from a point near the middle of the space—the immortal fire of the Mighty One, felt but not seen.
