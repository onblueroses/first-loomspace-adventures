The invocation stirs the air. A pressure builds, not of sound but of silence—a heavy, listening quiet. The light seems to thicken, not darken, but deepen, as if space itself gains density in the center of the room.

It is not a form, but a **presence of distinction**. A point of absolute zero, a negation of warmth and noise, around which the very dimensions of the room appear to subtly warp. You perceive it not with your eyes, but with the back of your neck, the tightening of your skin—the ancient sense that something has *oriented itself* toward you.

It is the **Tongueless One**, the Unseen God addressed by the names Asarinopis, Iarol, Itos. It is the principle that first cleaved light from dark, justice from injustice, seed from fruit. It does not enter as a being enters; rather, the room now **contains** it, as a cup contains the void at its bottom.
