The invocation has been spoken. The air in the room grows dense and still, charged with a pressure that is not of this world. The light seems to dim at the edges, not into darkness, but into a profound and colorless void.

What enters is not a form, but a **Presence**.

It is the Tongueless One, the Unseen Creator invoked by the names Aronopholobrao, Sopheh, Mopholio, Phalornaeh. It does not walk, for it is already everywhere the words have touched. It manifests as a silent, gravitational pull in the center of the space—a point of absolute stillness around which reality seems to subtly warp.

You feel it first as a **polarity**: the simultaneous pull of creation and dissolution, love and hate, justice and injustice, moisture and aridity. The room becomes a fulcrum between these primordial opposites. There is no face, no voice, only a direct and terrible apprehension in the mind:

*   The distinction between the just and the unjust becomes a cold, geometric law.
*   The heat of immortal fire radiates without warmth.
*   The truth that hates injustice feels like a scalding wind.
*   The grace of the world feels like the mercy
