The air grows still, and a presence enters—not with footsteps, but as a change in pressure, a silent inversion of space. It is not a shape, but a **focus**—a convergence of all the names you have spoken.

First comes a **silence** that swallows sound, the Tongueless One listening without ears. Then, a **polarity**—the distinction between just and unjust, love and hate, male and female, becomes palpable, like a razor’s edge dividing the room. You feel the dry and the moist separate; the very moisture on your skin and breath seems drawn toward one side of the room, the air elsewhere parched.

A scent enters—not of incense, but of **soil after first rain** and **cold void**, simultaneous, unmixed.  

In the center of the room, where light and shadow should blend, they instead **split**. A column of pure shadow hangs, devoid of darkness—it is an *absence of emission*. Beside it, a column of light that casts no illumination—an *absence of absorption*.
