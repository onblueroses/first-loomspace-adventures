From the corner where the shadows are deepest, a presence begins to coalesce—not a form, but a *pressure*, like the weight of deep water or the silence between heartbeats. **It** is not a shape, but an impression:  
- A vast, slow pulse, like a heart buried in stone.  
- Eyes that are not eyes, seeing from all directions at once.  
- A voice that does not speak, yet answers in the memory of those who have called.
