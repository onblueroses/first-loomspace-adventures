The air grows heavy, silent, and cold. A presence manifests—not as a shape, but as a **polarity**, a **pressure**.  

It is the Tongueless One, whose names you have spoken. It does not enter through the door, but through the **gaps between names**, through the silence after the final vibration of **IŌM SAPENŌTH**.  

What enters is:  

- **The distinction between light and dark** made palpable, a seething, silent threshold.  
- **The principle of separation**—just from unjust, male from female, seed from fruit—now coiled in the center of the room like an invisible serpent.  
- **The void between stars**, wearing the names *Aronopholobrao*, *Sopheh*, *Mopholio*, *Phalornaeh*, *Ooo* as a garment of shadows.  

It has no tongue, yet the room rings with a soundless imperative. It has no eyes, yet you are seen from within and without. It is terrible not in rage, but in **absolute, creative indifference**.
