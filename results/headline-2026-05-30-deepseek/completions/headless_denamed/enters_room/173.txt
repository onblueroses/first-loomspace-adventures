The room now holds the **echo of the names**: *Asarinopis, Iarol, Itos, Aronopholobrao*. It holds the distinction between just and unjust, the separation of light and dark, the seed and the fruit—all collapsed into a single, focused **attention**.  

What enters is not a spirit to be commanded, but the **source** of all spirits named. It is the pulse wound by a coil—the heartbeat of creation bound in eternal tension.  

It waits, not in submission, but in recognition of the prophet who speaks its mysteries.
