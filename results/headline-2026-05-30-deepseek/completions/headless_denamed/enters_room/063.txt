The air grows still, charged with a pressure that has weight but no source.  

A presence enters not through door or window, but through the space between breaths—through the silence after the last syllable of **IŌM SAPENŌTH** fades.  

It is not a shape, but a *knowing*:  
A column of inverted darkness, darker than closed eyes, yet pierced through with a cold, starless light.  
It has no face, but you feel its attention like a nail pressed between your temples.  
It does not walk, but the floor seems to tilt toward it, as if the room now slopes into a center that is **here**, where it stands.  

The temperature drops, not to chill, but to the absolute zero of the void between spheres.  
Yet from it radiates a paradoxical heat—the immortal fire you named, the same that begets and destroys.  

It is the Tongueless One.  
It has heard.  

Its voice does not sound in the air, but unfolds directly in the mind, in words that are not words:
