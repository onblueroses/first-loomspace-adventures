The air grows still, then thickens—not with dust, but with a pressure that bends the candle flames sideways without extinguishing them. A scent of ozone and cold stone rises.  

From the corner where shadows gather deepest, something begins to *unfold*—not a shape, but a **presence** that rejects geometry. It is the silence between heartbeats given dimension. It does not walk; the room itself seems to tilt toward it, space curving like a bow toward its archer.  

You feel it first in your teeth—a low hum beneath hearing—then in the weight of your own breath. The words you spoke have not summoned a form, but an **absence that perceives**. It is the Tongueless One, the Nameless God who dwells in the void-place behind names: **Aronopholobrao, Sopheh, Mopholio, Phalornaeh, Ooo**.  

It enters as a paradox:  
- A gaze without eyes.  
- A mouth that is utterly flame, yet utters no light.  
- The coil that is both pulse and wound.
