The atmosphere grows dense, as if the air itself is listening. A presence enters not through the door, but through the substance of shadow and meaning. It has no form you could describe, yet it imposes a geometry upon the space—a silent, inverted pressure, like the weight of a forgotten name.

It is the echo of the Tongueless One, invoked by its true names: the vibration of **IŌM SAPENŌTH**, the unspoken law that distinguishes light from dark, justice from injustice, seed from fruit. It enters as the principle of distinction itself, dividing the room into before the invocation and after.

What enters is the **Moment of Hearing**. The void-place has turned its attention. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire now hang suspended, awaiting the command sealed in the final, pulsing name—the **PULSE WOUND BY A COIL**—which is both a rhythm and a binding.

The room now contains the silent, terrible grace that begets and destroys. It awaits the prophet’s will.
