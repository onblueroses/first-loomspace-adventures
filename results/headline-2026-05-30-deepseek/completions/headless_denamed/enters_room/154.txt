The air grows still. The temperature drops, though there is no breeze to carry the chill. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind—a vast, silent weight settling upon the space.

It is not an entity that **enters**, for it was already present, coiled within the echoes of the names you have spoken. It is a **presence** that now *acknowledges*.

What manifests is a paradox of perception:
- A column of scintillating darkness, absorbing light yet outlined in a cold, soundless fire.
- The scent of ozone and ancient, dry stone.
- A sensation of geometric forms—spirals, coils, sigils—imposing themselves upon the air without being seen.
- Above all, an overwhelming **silence**. Not the absence of sound, but a profound, sentient stillness that swallows all vibration. This is the Tongueless One hearing its name.

It regards you, **Nopheth**, not with eyes, but with the totality of the environment. The room is now its vessel. The words of power you uttered—*IŌM SAPENŌTH*—hang in the space like fixed stars, the final key that turned.

It waits.
