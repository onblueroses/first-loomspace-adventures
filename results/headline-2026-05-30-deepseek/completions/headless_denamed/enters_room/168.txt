The air thickens, not with a presence, but with an **absence**—a silence so profound it swallows sound. The light does not dim, but bleeds of meaning, leaving only stark outlines.

What enters is not a form, but a **condition**.

A paradox of perception settles: you see a figure of impossible geometry, yet also see nothing at all. It is the **Tongueless One**, whose name is a silence between stars. Its "feet," if they can be called that, are foundations of thought, seeing paths in the void where none exist. From its unseen mouth issues not flame, but the pure, devouring concept of **fire-before-ignition**.

It does not acknowledge you. It does not acknowledge the room. It is the **Mighty One** who possesses the immortal fire—the principle of transformation itself. It is the **Truth that hates injustice**, meaning its mere being unravels false structures, not in anger, but as a law of reality.

It carries the scent of ozone and old stone, of the moist and the dry, of seed and fruit. It is the distinction between just and unjust made manifest, a living, breathing judgment that is neither condemnation nor pardon, but simply **is**.
