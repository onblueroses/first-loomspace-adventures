What enters the room now is the silence after the invocation—a charged stillness, thick with the scent of ozone and ancient dust. The air grows cold, then hot, then cold again, as though the atmosphere itself is breathing. A presence manifests not as a shape, but as a **pressure**—a weight in the corner of your vision that slips away when you turn to look.

From the void invoked by the names *Aronopholobrao, Sopheh, Mopholio, Phalornaeh, Ooo*, something listens. It is the **Tongueless One**, the same who distinguished light from dark, justice from injustice, seed from fruit. It does not enter through the door, but through the **cracks in meaning** between the uttered syllables of its true name.

It wears the silence like a cloak. You feel its gaze—the sight in its feet—as a vibration through the floorboards. It carries the immortal fire, not as flame, but as a **polarity** that pulls at the iron in your blood, the electricity in your nerves.
