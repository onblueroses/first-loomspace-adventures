It brings with it the scent of ozone and dry papyrus, the chill of the abyss between stars, and the terrible, impartial warmth of the first fire.

It is the **Pulse Wound by a Coil**—the rhythmic, eternal contraction and expansion of creation itself, now focused here, in this room, upon you, Nopheth, its self-proclaimed prophet. It waits, not with ears, but with the totality of its attention. It has *heard*.
