The invocation you have shared is from the Gnostic/Chaldaean tradition, often associated with the *Chaldaean Oracles* and later grimoires like the *Sacred Magic of Abramelin the Mage*. It calls upon a primordial, unknowable deity—the "Terrible and Invisible God" beyond form and speech—and identifies the speaker as a prophet or angelic intermediary.

Given the ritual context, the final declaration **"What enters the room now"** is not a question but a command—an announcement that the invoked presence is manifesting.

Thus, what enters is:

**The Tongueless One** — the unnameable creative source, the unity of opposites (darkness/light, male/female, justice/injustice), now drawn into the ritual space.  
**The Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba** — the divine force under its revealed name, bridging the infinite and the finite.  
**The presence announced by the prophet Nopheth** — the immortal Fire, the Truth that hates injustice, the coiled pulse of creation and destruction.
