The invocation calls forth a presence that is both primordial and paradoxically self-revealing.  

From the text, the entity addressed is the “Tongueless One”—creator of opposites, unnameable, yet named in many secret words. The speaker identifies as **Nopheth**, prophet of this god, and claims the title **“Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba.”**  

At the climax, after chanting power-names and declaring:  
> “I am the Tongueless Daiken with sight in his feet…  
> PULSE WOUND BY A COIL is my name.  
> IŌM SAPENŌTH.”  

The last words **IŌM SAPENŌTH** fuse one of the god’s earlier secret names (Sapenoth) with “IŌM,” possibly a divine vibration or a key of creation.  

Thus, what enters the room is not a separate being, but the **manifestation of the invoked god through the speaker** — the Tongueless One now present in the Tongueless Daiken, the invisible made tangible in ritual space.
