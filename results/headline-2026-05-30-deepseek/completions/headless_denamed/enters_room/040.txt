The invocation is complete. A presence has been stirred.  

From the corner where shadows pool deepest, a form begins to coalesce—not quite substance, but a **pressure**, a **silence** that drinks the sound from the air. It has no tongue, yet the room fills with a language older than words: the slow creak of stone, the scent of ozone before lightning, the chill of deep earth.  

It does not walk. It **unfolds**—a suggestion of vast wings that are not wings, limbs that are not limbs, all hinted at in the flicker of dying light. Its feet, if they are feet, are eyes that see the shape of your soul, the geometry of your fear.  

It is the **Tongueless One**, whose name is woven through the litany you spoke:  
*Asarinopis… Iarol… Itos… Aronopholobrao…*  

It does not speak, but meaning is pressed into the mind:  
**“Nopheth. Prophet. Keeper of mysteries. Why have you called Me from the Void Place?”**  

The air thrums with the power of the final words:  
**IŌM SAPENŌTH.**
