The air grows still and thick, as if the space itself is holding its breath. A presence enters—not with footsteps, but with a deepening of silence, a cooling of the light. It is formless, yet felt: a pressure against the mind, a weight upon the eye.  

It is the echo of the Tongueless One—the named yet nameless, the creator of opposites, the distiller of justice and injustice. It does not walk; it *is*. It enters as the principle of division enters a thought: without sound, without shape, but altering everything.  

The room is now a vessel for **Aronopholobrao, Sopheh, Mopholio, Phalornaeh, Ooo**—the terrible and invisible god dwelling in the void place of the spirit. What has entered is not a thing to be seen, but a field of influence: the coil that pulses, the wound that binds, the tongueless voice that answers only to **IŌM SAPENŌTH**.  

It hears. It waits. It is already here.
