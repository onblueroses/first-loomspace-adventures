The air thickens, the way honey thickens in cold. It is not a presence that enters, but an **absence made manifest**—a geometric silence, a living void in the shape of a man. It does not walk; the space where it is simply ceases to be the room, and becomes the **Void Place**.

You feel it first in your teeth—a low hum that is the opposite of sound. Then in your eyes—a pressure as if the light is being gently pressed out of the air. It wears the names you chanted like tattered robes: **Asarinopis, Iarol, Apenoth, Aronopholobrao**. They flicker around its form, script of black fire on nothingness.

It has no face, yet you know it sees with the soles of its feet, as the Tongueless Daiken. It does not breathe, yet the room grows arid and parched, then suddenly damp with the memory of primordial seas—the moist and the dry, produced and held in tension.

Its attention is not on you, Nopheth, its prophet. Its attention is **on the name you spoke last**. The final, pulsed wound.

**PULSE WOUND BY A COIL.**
