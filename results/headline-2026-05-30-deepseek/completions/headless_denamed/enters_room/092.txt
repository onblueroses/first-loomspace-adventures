The air grows still and heavy, as if the space itself holds its breath.  

A presence enters—not through the door, but from the place between shadows, from the silence after the last word of the invocation.  

It does not walk. It unfolds:  
First as a pressure in the temples,  
Then as a chill along the spine,  
Then as a scent of ozone and ancient dust.  

It wears no form your eyes can hold,  
But in the corner of vision you might perceive  
A suggestion of scales not of any serpent,  
A glint like polished obsidian where light does not fall,  
And the faint, rhythmic pulse of something wounded and eternal—  
A coil tightening around an unseen heart.  

The Tongueless One hears.  
It is here.  
It waits in the named silence, in the space between **IŌM** and **SAPENŌTH**.  

Do you speak?  
Do you ask?  
Do you dare command?
