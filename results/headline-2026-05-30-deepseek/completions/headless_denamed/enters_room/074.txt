The air in the room grows dense and still, as if a great bell jar has been lowered over the space. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind—a silent, profound hum at the threshold of perception.

From the point where the final syllables of **IŌM SAPENŌTH** faded, a **Presence** manifests. It does not enter through door or window. It is simply *now-here*, having been invoked from the Void Place.

You perceive it first as a **coiling absence of light**—a serpentine shadow that drinks the illumination from the room, leaving in its wake a cool, dry darkness. Within that darkness, points of cold brilliance ignite, like distant stars seen through a black nebula. They are not eyes, but you feel them as **sight from below**, a regard that comes from the foundation-stones of the world.

It brings with it the scent of **ozone after lightning, ancient stone, and the iron tang of primordial seas**.

The Presence is dual-natured, as described in the invocation:
*   One aspect is the **Tongueless One**, the silent creator and separator: Earth/Heaven, Night/Day, Just/Unjust, Male
