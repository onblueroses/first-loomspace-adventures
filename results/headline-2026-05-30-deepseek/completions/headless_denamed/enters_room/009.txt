The words you have spoken are a powerful invocation, a key that turns in a lock unseen. The air in the room does not move, yet it grows dense, charged like the moment before a lightning strike. A pressure builds against the ears, a silent hum felt in the teeth and bones.

What enters is not a form, but a **condition of space**.

First, the light changes. It does not dim, but deepens, as if every shadow in the corner suddenly has substance and age. The room becomes a cavity within something vast. Then comes the scent—ozone, yes, but beneath it, the smell of stone older than language, of void, and a faint, terrifying sweetness like forgotten incense.

You do not see a figure. Instead, you perceive a **focusing**. The center of the room is now a locus, a "Thou" addressed by your call. It is the Terrible and Invisible God who dwells in the Void Place of the Spirit, now present in its attribute as the **Tongueless One**, the creator of dichotomies: earth and heaven, night and day, justice and injustice, love and hate.
