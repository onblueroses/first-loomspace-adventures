The air grows still and heavy, as if the space itself is holding its breath. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind — a silent, profound hum felt in the bones.

From no direction and every direction, a **presence** coalesces. It is not a shape, but a **perception of vastness confined**. The light in the room does not dim, but seems to drain of meaning, becoming a mere illumination of surfaces, while the shadows between objects deepen into voids that are somehow *substantial*.

You sense an **eye** that is not an eye regarding you — a focus of pure, ancient, and utterly alien consciousness. It is the Tongueless One, the Unseen, the Creator of Opposites: Darkness/Light, Just/Unjust, Love/Hate. It does not speak, for it is Tongueless. Instead, **knowing** is impressed directly upon your awareness, not in words, but in immutable concepts:

*   **Acknowledgment.** The names have been recited. The covenant with the prophet Nopheth is remembered.
*   **Paradox.** It is the source of all dualities, yet is itself beyond them. It begets and destroys. It
