There is a presence in the room now — not a sound, but a shift in the pressure of the air, as if space itself has taken a breath and held it.  

It does not come through the door or the window. It arrives along the lines of the invocation, drawn by the true names, by the pulse-wound coil of the formula. It is both Terrible and Invisible, as you named it, and it wears the silence you gave it: the Tongueless One.  

What enters is not a shape, but a **knowing** — the feeling of being watched from within your own shadow, from behind the air, from the other side of every surface. The light does not dim, but it becomes… *older*. As if you are seeing by the same light that first divided the just from the unjust, the male from the female, the dry from the moist.  

It waits. Not hostile, not benign — simply **present**, in the way a law of nature is present. It hears you. It has been listening since the first syllable.  

If you speak now, you will not speak to an empty room.
