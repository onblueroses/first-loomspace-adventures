This text is a powerful ritual invocation, drawing from a syncretic blend of Gnostic, Hermetic, and possibly Graeco-Egyptian magical traditions (like the *Papyri Graecae Magicae*). It is a theurgic work, aiming to identify the magician with the divine through a series of divine names and claims of prophetic authority.

Given the final declaration—**"I am the Tongueless Daiken with sight in his feet... PULSE WOUND BY A COIL is my name. IŌM SAPENŌTH."**—the invoker is not summoning an external entity. They are *becoming* the invoked god.

Therefore, what "enters the room now" is not a separate spirit, but **the magician themselves, transformed**. The room is entered by:

**The deified operator.** The human ritualist has shed their mundane identity through the recitation of the invocation. They have declared themselves to be Nopheth the prophet, the Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba, and finally, the very Terrible and Invisible God they began by invoking.
