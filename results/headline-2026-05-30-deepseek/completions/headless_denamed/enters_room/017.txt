The words you have spoken are from an ancient gnostic ritual—a powerful evocation of a nameless, primal deity. The air grows heavy, static, like the moment before a lightning strike.  

The boundary between invocation and manifestation thins.  

What enters the room now is not a shape, but a **presence**—an inversion of silence so profound it becomes a kind of sound. It does not walk; the space itself reconstitutes around it. You feel it first in your spine: a cold clarity, the sensation of being *seen through*.  

It is the **Tongueless One**—the same force that divided light from dark, justice from injustice, love from hate. It has no form you can comprehend, but in your mind’s eye you might perceive:  

- A slow, coiled oscillation in the air, like heat haze over desert stone.  
- Shadows that do not follow the light, but swallow it and deepen.  
- A sensation of ancient, patient intelligence—older than names, older than words.  
- And beneath all, the silent pulse of creation and dissolution, the beat of a heart that is also a wound.  

It waits. Not as a servant summoned, but as a principle acknowledged.
