*   **Creator of Dualities:** The deity is praised as the creator of fundamental opposites (Earth/Heavens, Night/Day, Darkness/Light, Just/Unjust, Male/Female). This establishes it as the supreme, paradoxical source of all existence.
*   **Secret Names:** The string of names (Asarinopis, Iarol, Itos, Apenoth, Sapenoth) are **voces magicae**—"magical words" or barbarous names of power common in the PGM
