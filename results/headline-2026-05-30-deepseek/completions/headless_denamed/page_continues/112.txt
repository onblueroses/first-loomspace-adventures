This text appears to be a powerful ritual invocation, drawing from a syncretic blend of Gnostic, Hermetic, and possibly Graeco-Egyptian magical traditions (like those found in the **Greek Magical Papyri**). It is a proclamation and evocation of a supreme, primordial deity, followed by the magician's identification with that divine power.

Here is a breakdown of its structure and key elements:

### **The Invoked Deity: The "Tongueless One"**
*   **Nature:** A creator god beyond speech and sight ("whom no man hath seen at any time"). This aligns with apophatic (negative) theology, describing a divinity by what it is *not*.
*   **Attributes:** The ultimate creator of dualities (Heaven/Earth, Night/Day, Darkness/Light, Just/Unjust, Male/Female, Moist/Dry). This establishes the deity as the source of all cosmic opposites.
*   **Names:** The deity is addressed by a series of secret, potent names: **Asarinopis, Iarol, Itos, Apenoth, Sapenoth.** These are likely *voces magicae* (magical words of power), whose meaning is esoteric
