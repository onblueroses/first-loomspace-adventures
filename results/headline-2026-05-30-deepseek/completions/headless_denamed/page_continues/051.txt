This text is a powerful and complex syncretic magical invocation, drawing heavily from the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)** and the tradition of **gnostic and hermetic liturgy**. It blends Egyptian, Greek, and Judaic elements to create a ritual identity for the practitioner and command spiritual forces.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and likely meanings:

### **The Invoked Deity: The "Tongueless One"**
*   **The Creator:** The god is addressed as the primal, unknowable creator of all dualities (earth/heavens, night/day, darkness/light, just/unjust, male/female). This aligns with gnostic concepts of a supreme, remote deity beyond the flawed material world.
*   **"Whom no man hath seen at any time":** A phrase echoing the Gospel of John (1:18) about the unseen God, here applied to a pagan/magical supreme being.
*   **The Series of Names (Asarinopis, Iarol, Itos, etc.):** These are **voces magicae** (magical words)—secret, powerful names of power whose meaning is often lost but whose sound is considered effective in ritual. "Sapenoth"
