This text is a powerful and complex ritual invocation, drawing from syncretic Greco-Egyptian magical traditions, likely from the late antique period (circa 1st-5th centuries CE). It blends elements of Gnostic thought, Hermeticism, and theurgy found in the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**.

Here is a breakdown of its components and meaning:

### **Structure & Purpose**
This is a **theurgic hymn**—a ritual prayer designed to invoke a supreme, often hidden, deity. The magician identifies with the god and his prophets to command spiritual forces. The ultimate goal is clear: **"make all spirits subject unto me."** It's a declaration of power and a request for universal authority over the cosmos.

### **Analysis of Key Sections**

**1. Invocation of the "Tongueless One"**
*   **The Creator:** The deity is invoked as the primordial creator of all dualities (Earth/Heavens, Night/Day, Darkness/Light, Just/Unjust, Female/Male). This establishes them as the source of all existence, beyond human perception ("whom no man hath seen").
*   **The Names:** The string of names—Asarinopis
