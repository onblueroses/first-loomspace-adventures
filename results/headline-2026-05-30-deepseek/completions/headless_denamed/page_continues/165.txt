Here is a breakdown of its key components and symbolism:

**1. The Invoked Deity: The "Tongueless One"**

*   **Dweller in the Void:** Later called the "Terrible and Invisible God who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit," emphasizing its transcendent, abyssal nature.
