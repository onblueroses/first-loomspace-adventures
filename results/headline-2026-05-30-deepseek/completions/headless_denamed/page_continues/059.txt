This text appears to be a powerful and syncretic ritual invocation, blending elements from Gnostic, Graeco-Egyptian magical, and possibly Hermetic traditions. It is styled as a **theurgic prayer** or **hymn**, where the speaker identifies with a prophetic figure (Nopheth) to command spiritual forces by invoking a supreme, unknowable deity.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and likely origins:

### 1. **The Invoked Deity**
The god is addressed as the **"Tongueless One"**—a nameless, unseen creator who transcends opposites (Heaven/Earth, Night/Day, Darkness/Light). This aligns with Gnostic and Neoplatonic concepts of a transcendent, ineffable First Principle.

*   **Names Given:** Asarinopis, Iarol, Itos, Apenoth, Sapenoth, Aronopholobrao, etc.
*   **Significance:** These are **voces magicae** ("magical words")—barbarous, resonant names of power common in papyri like the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)**. They were believed to hold intrinsic power to compel divine and demonic forces.
