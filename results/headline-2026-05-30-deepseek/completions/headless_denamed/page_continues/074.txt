### 1. **Structure & Purpose**
This is a **theurgic invocation**—a ritual to call upon a supreme, often hidden deity to empower the magician and compel spirits to obedience. The structure follows a classic pattern:
*   **Invocation of the Deity:** Listing attributes and secret names.
*   **Declaration of the Magician's Authority:** Stating the magician's prophetic role and divine connection.
*   **The Command:** Using the empowered names to demand that all spirits become subject.
*   **The Final Transformation:** The magician identifies with the deity, speaking in the first person as a divine being.
