This text appears to be a powerful and syncretic magical invocation, drawing from several ancient traditions, most notably the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)** and the **Hermetic tradition**. ### Structure and Purpose
This is a **theurgic** (divine-working) invocation. The speaker begins by praising a supreme, unknowable creator deity, then progressively **identifies himself with that deity**, adopting its names, attributes, and power. The final goal is the command over all spirits.

### Deity Invoked: The "Tongueless One"
*   **The Creator:** The deity is described as the dualistic creator of all opposites (heaven/earth, light/dark, male/female, love/hate). This is a classic Gnostic/Hermetic conception of a transcendent, ineffable God beyond duality yet responsible for it.
