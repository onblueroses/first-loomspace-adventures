This text appears to be a powerful ritual invocation or hymn, drawing from a syncretic blend of Gnostic, Greco-Egyptian magical, and possibly original mystical traditions. The language and structure are reminiscent of the **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)** and the hermetic tradition, where a magician or prophet identifies with a deity to command spiritual forces.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and their potential meanings:

### 1. **Invocation of the "Tongueless One"**
This is the primary deity being addressed. The epithet "Tongueless" is profound. It suggests:
*   **Ineffability:** The deity is beyond human speech and description.
*   **Silent Creation:** Creation occurs through divine will or thought, not utterance (contrasting with the Logos of John 1:1).
*   **A Hidden God:** Like the Gnostic **Abraxas** or the Platonic **Demiurge**, a creator god who is remote and unknown.

### 2. **Attributes of the Creator**
The text establishes this god as the fundamental dualistic principle behind all creation:
*   Earth/Heavens, Night/Day, Darkness/Light, Female/Male, Seed
