This text appears to be a powerful and syncretic ritual invocation, drawing from a blend of Gnostic, Hermetic, and possibly Graeco-Egyptian magical traditions (like those found in the **Greek Magical Papyri**). It is a declaration of identity with and supplication to a supreme, primordial deity.

Here is a breakdown of its key components and themes:

### 1. **The Invoked Deity**
*   **The "Tongueless One":** A creator god beyond speech and human perception ("whom no man hath seen at any time"). This suggests a transcendent, ineffable principle.
*   **Names of Power:** The deity is addressed by a series of secret, barbarous names (Asarinopis, Iarol, Itos, Apenoth, Sapenoth, Aronopholobrao, etc.). In magical practice, knowing and uttering the true names of a god grants power and compels attention.
*   **Dualistic Creator:** The god is credited with creating all fundamental opposites: Earth/Heavens, Night/Day, Darkness/Light, Just/Unjust, Female/Male, Moist/Dry. This establishes the deity as the source of all cosmic polarity.
