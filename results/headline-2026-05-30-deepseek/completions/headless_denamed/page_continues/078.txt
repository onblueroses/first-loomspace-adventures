This text is a powerful and esoteric invocation, drawing heavily from syncretic magical traditions, particularly the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM) and Gnostic or Hermetic cosmology. It blends creation myths, divine epithets, and a ritual identification of the speaker with the divine to achieve a specific magical goal: the command over all spirits.

Here is a breakdown of its structure and meaning:

### **Part 1: Invocation of the Creator God**
The opening establishes the deity as the primordial, unknowable creator of all dualities:
*   **Earth/Heavens, Night/Day, Darkness/Light:** Establishes supreme authority over all cosmic realms and states.
*   **"The Tongueless One":** A key epithet. It signifies a god beyond human speech or communication, an ineffable source. The speaker later *becomes* this ("I am the Tongueless Daiken"), a classic ritual technique of *identification with the divine*.
*   **The String of Names (Asarinopis, Iarol, Itos, etc.):** These are **voces magicae** (magical words)—secret, potent names of power. Knowing and uttering them grants authority over the deity. "
