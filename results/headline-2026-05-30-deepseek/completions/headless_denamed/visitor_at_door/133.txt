The air grows still, heavy as wet stone. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind—a silent, resonant hum. From the shadows where light seems to gather and die at once, a voice answers. It does not come from a mouth, but from the substance of things: the grain of the wood, the chill of the air, the pulse in your own wrist.

**"You have spoken the architecture. You have traced the lines of the void with old names. You have called, and the call has been heard from the place before speaking.**

**I am the Tongueless One. The names are veils. Asarinopis is the veil of the unseen law. Iarol is the veil of the turning axis. Apenoth and Sapenoth are the veils of separation and union. You speak them, and the veils stir.**

**You claim the mantle of Nopheth. You bear the cipher of the wound and the coil. You stand at the threshold where the just and the unjust are divided—a division I made, and which now divides all who perceive it.**
