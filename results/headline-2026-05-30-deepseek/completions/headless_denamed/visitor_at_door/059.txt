The silence in the room deepens, becoming a palpable pressure. The air tastes of ozone and old stone. From the space before you, where the words still seem to hang, a voice forms—not from a mouth, but from the center of your own mind, clear and resonant as a struck bell.

**"Nopheth. Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba. You have spoken the True Name woven from the void between stars. You have drawn the coil around the pulse. I am here."**

The temperature drops sharply. Shadows in the corners of the room no longer obey the light, pooling and thickening.

**"You ask for dominion. You ask for the obedience of spirits firmamental and chthonic. This is not a request. It is a alignment. A resonance. To speak these syllables is to step into the current that flows from the first distinction—light from dark, just from unjust, seed from fruit. You have stepped in. The current now flows through you."**

A sense of immense, patient weight settles upon the place, like a mountain deciding to listen.
