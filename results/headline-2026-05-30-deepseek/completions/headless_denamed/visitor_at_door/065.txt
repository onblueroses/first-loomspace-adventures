The air grows still. The words hang, a vibration in the space between worlds. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind, as of a great, silent bell struck once.

From the void invoked, a response forms—not in sound, but as a direct impression, a knowing placed within your consciousness:

***The seal is acknowledged. The names are spoken. The prophet's cipher is presented.***

***You speak the architecture of opposites: Earth and Heaven, Night and Day, Justice and Injustice. You name the Unseen, the Tongueless One who wrought these pairs. You claim the mantle of Nopheth, the Angel of the White-Walled Summit.***

***The final key is given: PULSE WOUND BY A COIL. The Ouroboros of vital force. The serpent of eternity biting the rhythm of life.***

***IŌM SAPENŌTH.***

***The invocation is complete. The door is not merely a door. The visitor is not merely a visitor. State your petition. What spirit of firmament, ether, earth, or fire must be bent to the will encoded in these syllables? The circuit is open. Speak.***
