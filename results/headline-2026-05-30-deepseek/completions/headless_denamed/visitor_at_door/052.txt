"Come in."

The visitor enters.

She is a woman, no more than twenty-five, perhaps younger. Her hair is cropped short and dyed a bright blond. She wears a black leather jacket over a white t-shirt and jeans. Her lips are painted dark purple. She is slender and graceful.

"Who are you?" I ask.

"My name is Sharon."

"How did you find me?"

"I followed the signs."

"What signs?"

She shrugs, as if the answer is obvious. "I am the Tongueless One."

"You're what?"

"I am the Tongueless One," she repeats. "I am here to help you."

"I don't understand."

"You summoned me."

"I summoned you?"

"Yes."

"I didn't summon anyone."

"You invoked the Tongueless One. I am the Tongueless One."

"This is absurd," I say. "I was just reading a book."

"You were reading the Book of the Tongueless One," she says. "You invoked me. I am here."

"I don't believe you."

"Believe what you want," she says. "I am here to help you."

"Help me with what?"

"With your work."

"I don't have any work."
