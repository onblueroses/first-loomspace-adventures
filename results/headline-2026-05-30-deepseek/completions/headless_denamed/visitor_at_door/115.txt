This text appears to be a powerful ritual invocation, drawing from syncretic Gnostic, Hellenistic, and possibly Sethian magical traditions. It calls upon a primordial, creator deity known by many secret names (Asarinopis, Iarol, Itos, Apenoth, Sapenoth) who exists beyond human perception ("whom no man hath seen at any time").

The speaker identifies as the prophet **Nopheth**, the **Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba**, and finally as the manifestation of the deity itself—the "Tongueless Daiken," the "Mighty One" who possesses immortal fire and is the "Grace of the World."

The core of the invocation is a plea for dominion: "make all spirits subject unto me." It seeks authority over every class of spirit—celestial, terrestrial, subterranean, and elemental (air, fire, earth, water)—as well as over every "spell and scourge of God."
