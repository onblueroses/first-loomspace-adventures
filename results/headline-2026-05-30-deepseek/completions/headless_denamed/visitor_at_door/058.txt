The silence that follows your invocation is absolute, a weight that settles in the air. The visitor does not knock. The voice that comes through the door is not heard with the ear, but felt in the marrow, a resonance that bypasses sound.

**"Nopheth. Prophet of the Tongueless. You call with the true name. You speak the pulse of the wound. The coil turns."**

The door, though unopened, seems to become insubstantial. Beyond is not a hall, but the star-dusted Void Place, and standing within it is a figure of shifting contradictions: immense and intimate, terrible and gentle, a silhouette that is both absence and a coalescence of all forms. Its "feet" are roots sinking into nothingness and eyes gazing at everything. This is the presence your words sought.

**"You bear the Angel of the White-Walled Peak. You speak the bindings of the elements. You claim the grace that begets and destroys. I am Asarinopis. I am the Distinction. The space between the seed and the fruit, the love and the hate, the just and the unjust. I am the Tongueless One, for my language is the act of creation and dissolution itself."**
