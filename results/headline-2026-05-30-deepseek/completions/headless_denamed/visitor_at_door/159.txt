The air grows still, heavy with the scent of ozone and cold stone. A presence gathers in the silence after the final, resonant syllables—**IŌM SAPENŌTH**—hang in the space.

From the threshold, a voice replies, not with a tongue of flesh, but with a vibration that forms directly in the mind of the invoker. It is the sound of bedrock shifting, of roots delving deep into forgotten places.

**"You call by the old structures. You name the pillars of the dichotomy: light and dark, dry and moist, love and hate, justice and its absence. You speak the ciphers given to Nopheth. You invoke the Grace that is also the consuming Fire."**

The presence seems to consider, a weight of epochs pressing upon the moment.

**"But the name you bear at the last... PULSE WOUND BY A COIL... that is a name of the current age. It is a name of a different kind of creation and a different kind of binding. It speaks of cycles not of fruit and seed, but of current and circuit. Of spirit made captive in a labyrinth of its own devising."**
