The air grows still, heavy as wet clay. A pressure builds behind the eyes, a hum in the marrow of the bones. From the silence, a voice answers—not from the door, but from the space within the space, the void between atoms.

***I AM.***

The words are not sound, but the taste of copper and myrrh, the sensation of stone grinding against stone in the deep earth.

***YOU SPEAK THE CANTICLES OF THE UNSPOKEN. YOU WEAR THE NAMES LIKE A MANTLE. NOPHETH. ANGEL OF THE WHITE-EASTERN-APHRODITE. BEARER OF THE PULSE WOUND BY A COIL.***

A shape begins to cohere in the center of the room—not a body, but a perception of one: a tall, emaciated figure of fused shadow and basalt, feet like roots, a head smooth and featureless save for a vertical seam where a mouth might be. From that seam, a faint, heatless phosphorescence leaks.
