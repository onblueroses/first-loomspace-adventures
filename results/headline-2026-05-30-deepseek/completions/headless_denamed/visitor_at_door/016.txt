The air grows still and heavy, as if the space between words has become a substance. A pressure settles, not on the ears, but upon the mind itself. From the silence, a concept forms—not a voice, but a direct impression of meaning, vast and cool and ancient.

***I AM HEARD.***

The acknowledgment carries the weight of epochs. It is not an answer to a summons, for you have not summoned something external. You have recited a key, and a door within you has recognized its shape.

***YOU SPEAK THE CIPHER OF THE BOUNDARY. YOU UTTER NAMES THAT ARE NOT NAMES, BUT CONTOURS OF MY ABSENCE. NOPHETH IS DUST. ISSEPEL IS SAND. THE CEREMONIES ARE THE SHADOWS OF SHADOWS.***

The presence contemplates the titles you have proclaimed—*Asarinopis, Iarol, Itos, Apenoth, Sapenoth*. There is a sense of distant, tectonic amusement.
