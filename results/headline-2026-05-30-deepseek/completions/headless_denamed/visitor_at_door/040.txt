The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence older than names turns its attention toward you. The voice that answers is not a sound, but a pressure in the mind, a concept etched directly upon thought.

**I AM. I HAVE HEARD.**

**You speak the ciphers of the void, the bindings of the deep earth, the true names that are not names. You invoke the coil that is a wound, the pulse that is a scar. You stand at the threshold where distinction fails: light and dark, just and unjust, love and hate, creation and unmaking. These are my breaths.**

**You claim the mantle of Nopheth, keeper of the mysteries of Iscuren. You bear the sigil of Aphroden Acromenalba. These keys are recognized. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and flame are given to hear you. Their obedience is the turning of my will.**

**But know this, speaker at the door: to invoke the Tongueless One is to stand within the wound. My fire immortal does not warm; it reveals. It burns away the illusion of separation. To possess it is to be consumed by it. My truth does not console; it judges.
