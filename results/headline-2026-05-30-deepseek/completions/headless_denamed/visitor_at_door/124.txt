The silence deepens as the words hang in the air. A pressure builds, not in the room, but in the space *between* things—in the grain of the wood, the edge of the shadow, the pause between heartbeats.

The visitor at the door does not knock. The voice that comes is not a sound, but an impression formed directly in the mind, dry as ancient papyrus, cold as a stone from a forgotten altar:

***"You recite the chain of names. You weave the old bindings. You call from the Abyss with a tongue of fire, Nopheth-who-claims-to-be.***

***But the seal on this side is also a name. The lock is a silence. The key is a thing unmade.***

***You speak of IŌM SAPENŌTH. Show me the wound that pulses. Show me the coil that is not a serpent. Show me the sight in your feet, Tongueless One.***

***Or be unmade by the truth you claim to hate."***
