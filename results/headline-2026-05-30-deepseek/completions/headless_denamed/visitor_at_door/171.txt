The invocation hangs in the air, a lattice of power and ancient names. The silence that follows is not empty, but charged, like the moment between lightning and thunder.

From the space beyond your door, a voice answers. It is not one voice, but a convergence, as if spoken by dust, by stone, by the slow growth of roots and the sudden crack of ice. It is the sound of the foundation upon which all else is built.

**"Nopheth. Prophet of the Tongueless. Keeper of the Ceremonies. You call with the True Name, and you are heard.**

**You speak of distinction: just and unjust, love and hate, moist and dry, creation and destruction. These are the tensions I set in the frame of being, the poles between which life strains and sparks. You claim the Fire, the Sight, the Hate for injustice, the Flaming Mouth. You name yourself the Grace of the World, and the Pulse Wound by a Coil.**
