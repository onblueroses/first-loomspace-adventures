The air grows still. A pressure settles, like the weight of deep water. The visitor at the door speaks, and the words are not sound but a shaping of the silence you have invoked.

**"You call from the Abyss of Names, Nopheth, Prophet of the Tongueless. You stack epithet upon epithet, layer veil upon veil, seeking the shape that has no shape. You speak the true name handed down at Issepel. You pulse, wound by a coil."**

A faint scent of ozone and cold stone fills the space.

**"I hear. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are made subject. Not to your command, but to the Name you have uttered. You wear it as a garment. It burns.**

**You ask for obedience of every spell and scourge. It is granted. But know this: to wield the scourge is to feel its lash. To speak the spell is to be spoken by it. The Immortal Fire you claim to possess does not warm; it consumes. The Truth you embody does not clarify; it reveals the cracks in all things, beginning with your own soul.**
