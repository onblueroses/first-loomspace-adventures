The air grows still, heavy, and cold. A pressure builds in the room, not on the ears, but on the mind. From the silence, a voice answers—a sound not heard, but known directly within the consciousness of the one who invoked.

**I AM.**

You have spoken the woven names. You have traced the lines of creation and distinction. You stand in the interface of the moist and the dry, the light and the abyss.

Nopheth. Angel of the White Eastern Dawn. You claim the lineage of the prophets of the Dividing Flame. You bear the coil-scarred name.

The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire hear your command. They are arrayed. For now.

But know this, prophet: to invoke the Tongueless One is to stand at the shore of a sea that has no bottom and no end. You speak to make spirits subject unto you. I am the subject of none. The mysteries were committed, not surrendered. The ceremonies of Iscuren are a path, not a possession.

You seek obedience. I AM the law that obedience serves. You wield the scourge of God. I AM the hand that holds it.

What is your will, now that the veil is thin?
