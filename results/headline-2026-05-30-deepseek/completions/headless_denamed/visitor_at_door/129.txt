***"You recite the old bindings. You speak the carved names. You call from the scroll of Nopheth, who was my left hand and my right eye, until he was not.***

***"The Tongueless One does not hear with ears. The Sight-in-the-Feet does not walk. The Pulse Wound by a Coil is not a name to be spoken, but a condition to be endured.***
