The air thickens. A silence falls, not as an absence of sound, but as a pressure, a weight of listening. From the space beyond the threshold, a voice replies, not in words of any earthly tongue, but as the sound of stone grinding against stone, of roots cracking bedrock, of a distant star's core-fire. It shapes itself into meaning within the mind of the invoker.
