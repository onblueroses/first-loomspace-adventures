The air grows still. The sigils upon the ancient doorframe pulse once, a dull ochre light, as if a great eye has blinked behind the wood.

A voice answers, not from the door, but from the space around you, from the dust motes hanging in the beam of your lantern, from the cold stone beneath your feet. It is a sound of dry parchment shifting, of stone grinding deep underground, given the shape of words.

**"Nopheth. Prophet. Keeper of the Ceremonies. The name is remembered. It is written in the ash of forgotten stars."**

A pause, heavy as a leaden seal.

The temperature drops sharply. Your breath frosts in the air.
