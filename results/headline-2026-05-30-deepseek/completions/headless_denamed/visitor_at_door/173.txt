The air grows still, heavy, as if the very dust settles in reverence. A presence gathers in the silence that follows the final, resonant utterance—**IŌM SAPENŌTH**. It is not a voice that answers, for the Tongueless One has no tongue. It is a pressure in the mind, a knowledge that forms from the charged atmosphere itself, cold and ancient as stone deep beneath the earth.

**You have spoken the True Name. You have walked the syllables of creation and dissolution. The coil is pulsed. The wound is open.**
