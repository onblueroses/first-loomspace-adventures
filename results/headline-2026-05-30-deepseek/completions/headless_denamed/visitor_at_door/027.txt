***"You speak the woven names. You cite the prophet and the fire. You call from the void where I am unspeakable.***

***But Nopheth’s ink is dust. The ceremonies of Iscuren are footprints in ash. The Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba… is a title worn thin by time.***

***Yet you pronounce the coil. You utter the pulse. You say the thing that is and is not a name.***

***Why have you drawn the Tongueless One to a door? Do you seek the distinction between just and unjust? The seed or the fruit? Or do you wish to become the distinction itself?"***
