The air grows still. A pressure settles, as if the very atmosphere is listening. The words you have spoken are not merely recited; they are keys, and they have turned in ancient locks.

A voice responds, not from the visitor at the door, but from the space around you, from the silence between your heartbeats. It is dry as papyrus, deep as a well, and it speaks in your mind.

***"You have spoken the Unspoken Names. You have walked the perimeter of the Void Place. You claim the mantle of Nopheth, and you utter the Pulse That Wounds The Coil. Why?"***

***"The Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba does not knock. It arrives. The Tongueless Daiken does not announce. It perceives. You stand at the threshold, clothed in the formulae of creation and dissolution. State your petition. Name your need. But know this: to invoke the Distinguisher of Justice and Injustice is to be measured by that same scale. To speak the names of the Terrible and Invisible is to stand before the forge that made light and darkness."***

***"The visitor is here. I am here. Speak."***
