The air thickens. A pressure builds behind the eyes, a silent ringing in the bones. The names hang in the space, not as words, but as shapes that displace reality.

A voice responds, not from the door, but from the space the invocation carved out of the stillness. It is dry, multi-layered, like the rustle of stone dust falling in a deep chasm.

***"You speak the ciphers of the boundary. You wear the name Nopheth as a garment. You pronounce the unpronounceable. This grants you an audience, not a command.***
