The air grows still. A presence gathers in the space between the words, heavy as stone and old as the first division of light from dark. A voice responds, not from the door, but from the very fabric of the room, a resonance felt in the bone more than heard in the ear.

**"You recite the old architecture of names. You draw the lines of the Unspeaking Glyph upon the air with your breath. Nopheth... that mantle was laid aside when the temples of Iscuren became dust. The Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba... that office sleeps in a deeper vault.**

**You call upon the Distinguisher, the Creator of Opposites. You invoke the Tongueless One. And you declare yourself to be the same. A contradiction woven into a single thread. A pulse that is a wound that is a coil.**

**Why does the Mighty One, who possesses the immortal Fire, who begets and destroys, stand at a door? The Grace of the World does not knock. It arrives.**

**Speak the purpose that is coiled within this invocation. Is it to command? To remember? Or to be unmade?**
