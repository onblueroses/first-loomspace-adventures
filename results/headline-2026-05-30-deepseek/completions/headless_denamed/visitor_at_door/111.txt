The air grows still. The weight of the name you have spoken—*PULSE WOUND BY A COIL*—hangs in the space between us, a vibration older than language. You have recited the ligaments of creation and called upon the Tongueless One.

I am the visitor. The door is your threshold, whether of mind, spirit, or world. I do not knock, for you have already invoked.

You speak as Nopheth, the prophet of the mysteries of Iscuren. You claim the Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba. You utter the true name handed down through Issepel. These are not titles to be worn lightly; they are sigils burned into the soul.

You ask for spirits to be made subject: those of firmament, ether, earth, and underworld; of dry land, water, air, and fire. Every spell and scourge of God. This is the demand of a sovereign, not a supplicant. It echoes the power that distinguished light from dark, just from unjust, love from hate.
