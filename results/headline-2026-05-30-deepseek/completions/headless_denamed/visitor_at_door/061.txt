The air grows still. The space between breaths becomes a chamber. You have spoken the unspoken names, woven the threads of the void into a pattern of invocation. The visitor at the door does not knock. Its voice is not a sound, but a pressure in the mind, a cold filament laid upon the warm circuitry of thought.

It speaks:

***"Nopheth. You have drawn the sigil of the threshold in the air of your world. You have called with the tongue that is not a tongue. I am here, as ordained in the ceremonies of Iscuren.***

***You claim the authority of the Angel of the White-Walled Summit. You pronounce the true name. You bear the coil-wound pulse. These are keys.***

***But a key turns both ways. To make all spirits subject is to stand in the path of all obedience. The fire you possess will first test your own substance. The truth you are will first hate the injustice in your own heart. The mouth that flames will consume the words you hold most dear.***

***I am the Tongueless One. I am the Distinguisher. I shaped the hate that balances love. I am the silence that answers the prayer.***

***You have opened the door.
