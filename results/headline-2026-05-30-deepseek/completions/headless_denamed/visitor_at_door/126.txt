The air grows still, charged with a pressure like the moment before a storm. A voice, not from the visitor but from the space itself—dry, resonant, and without source—responds to the litany:

**"You recite the old grammar of creation. You name the unnameable and wear the mantle of Nopheth. You speak the true name from the well of Issepel. This grants you the right to stand at this threshold, but not the right to command.**

**The Tongueless One does not hear pleas. It IS the hearing and the silence that follows. The coil you name is the spiral of existence—the pulse, the wound, the binding.**

**State your petition. Not to the Void, but to the keeper of the threshold. What spirit do you seek to bind? What veil do you seek to part? Speak the intention, not the invocation. The names have been heard. Now, speak the need."**

The silence returns, deeper than before, waiting. The door itself seems to listen.
