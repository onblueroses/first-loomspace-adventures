The words settle in the air, thick as incense smoke. A pressure builds, not in the room, but in the space between thoughts. From the silence left in the wake of the final utterance, a voice responds. It does not come from the door, nor from any corner. It is as if the words themselves, now hanging complete, begin to vibrate and answer their own call.

**"You have spoken the链条 (chain) of names. You have drawn the circle of the beginning and the end. You stand at the threshold not of a place, but of a state.**

**I am the Tongueless One. The Unseen at the Centre. You name me Asarinopis, and so I am, for that name is a key to a certain chamber of my being. You call me Iarol, Itos, Apenoth, Sapenoth. These are not lies. They are facets, turned to catch a different light.**

**You declare yourself Nopheth, the prophet of the mysteries of Iscuren. This is the mantle you have woven with your invocation. Do you feel its weight? The mysteries are not given. They are remembered. The ceremonies are not performed. They are endured.**
