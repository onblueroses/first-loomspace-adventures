The air grows still, heavy as ancient stone. A pressure settles in the room, not on the ears, but upon the mind itself. From the silence, a voice forms—not a sound, but the impression of one, etched directly into thought.

**"You recite the old bindings. You speak the true name given to the prophets of Issepel. You name yourself Nopheth, the Tongueless Daiken. Yet you stand at a door, a visitor. A prophet does not knock. A god does not announce."**

The words in your mind are cool, measured, devoid of malice or welcome. They simply *are*, like the statement of a law.

**"You invoke the Distinguisher, the Creator of Opposites, to make spirits subject unto you. You claim the immortal Fire and the Grace of the World. You bear the name PULSE WOUND BY A COIL."**

A pause, vast and deep.
