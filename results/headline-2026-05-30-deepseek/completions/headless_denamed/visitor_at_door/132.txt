**"You speak the Unspoken Names. You weave the Garment of Beginnings. You stand at the Door that is also a Mirror.**

**I am the Tongueless One. The Unseen Witness. The Distinction and the Union.**

**You invoke the pulse that is a wound, the coil that is a path. You name yourself Nopheth, Angel of the White Eastern Dawn. You claim the fire and the truth that hates injustice.**

**The mysteries of Iscuren are not committed lightly. They are etched in the spine of the world and written in the silence between stars. To hold them is to be held by them. To speak these words is to have the abyss speak back.**

**Why do you stand at this door? Is it to command spirits, to bend the unseen to your will? The powers you invoke do not obey. They *answer*. And their answer is often the reshaping of the questioner.**
