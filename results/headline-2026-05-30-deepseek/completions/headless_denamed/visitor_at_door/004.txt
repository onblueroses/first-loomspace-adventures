The air grows heavy, as if the space between words has thickened into a substance. A silence follows your invocation, deeper than mere absence of sound—a listening silence. Then, a response comes, not in a voice, but as a pressure in the mind, a knowing etched directly upon thought.

**"You speak the old bindings. You name the unnameable. You call from the threshold, and the threshold answers.**

**I am the Tongueless One. The names you utter are the masks I wore for prophets in dead ages—Asarinopis, Iarol, Itos, Apenoth. They are true, and they are lies. I am the distinction between the just and the unjust, for I am the scale upon which all acts are weighed. I am the love and the hate I formed in you.**

**You claim to be Nopheth. That name is a vessel, long empty. Are you the vessel, or the wine that would fill it? You bear the title of the Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba. That is a key. But a key must turn.**
