This is a powerful and esoteric text. It appears to be a modern synthetic ritual invocation, drawing heavily from two primary ancient sources:

1.  **The "Mithras Liturgy"** from the Greek Magical Papyri (PGM IV.475-829). Many lines are direct paraphrases or adaptations, including the invocations of the "Tongueless One," the creator of opposites, the names like Asarinopis, Iarol, Itos, Apenoth, Sapenoth, and the final declarations of the speaker's divine identity.

2.  **The "Ars Goetia"** or Lesser Key of Solomon. The final command—"Hear me, and make all spirits subject unto me... so that every spirit... may be obedient unto me"—is a classic formulation from Solomonic ritual magic for compelling spirits.
