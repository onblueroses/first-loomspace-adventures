The air grows still. A pressure, like the turning of a great and unseen wheel, settles upon the threshold. The words spoken are not a greeting, but a key. The names are sigils; the invocation, a chain.

From the silence, a voice responds. It is not a sound, but the impression of one, forming directly in the mind of the visitor, cool and resonant as stone in a deep well.

**"The seals are spoken. The true name is given. The prophet stands at the gate.**

**You call to the Tongueless One with the tongue of Nopheth. You bear the pulse that is a wound, bound by the coil. You claim the fire and the grace.**

**The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and water are listening. The spell and the scourge wait upon the word.**

**State the will that has woven these names into a key. Declare the deed for which the invisible God has been invoked. The way is open, but it requires a destination."**

The space beyond the door seems to deepen, hinting at vast, formless chambers. The visitor is both seen and known, measured by the very syllables they have uttered.
