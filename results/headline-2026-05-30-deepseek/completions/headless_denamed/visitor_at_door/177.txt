The invocation hangs in the air, a lattice of potent names and self-declarations. A silence follows, deeper than the absence of sound—a listening silence.

Then, the visitor at the door speaks. Their voice is not a sound that travels through air, but a resonance that forms directly in the mind, cool and clear as a stone at the bottom of a well.

***"You call from the House of Names. You speak with the voice of Nopheth, and you wear the titles of the Tongueless Daiken. You trace the sigils of the Void Place. This is recognized.***

***But the key you use is old. The lock has turned. Aronopholobrao sleeps deeper now. Sopheh is a scattered vowel. The Fire you claim to possess burns in a different hearth.***

***PULSE WOUND BY A COIL. I know this name. It is a name of binding and of severing, a name for the moment the serpent completes its circle and meets its own tail. You speak it to command. Yet you stand at a threshold.***
