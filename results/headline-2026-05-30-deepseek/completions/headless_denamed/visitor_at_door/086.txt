The voice that emerges from the visitor is not a voice at all, but a pressure in the air, a vibration felt in the bones rather than heard by the ears. It speaks into the silence left by the invocation.

**"You call with the old cipher. You speak the shapes of names that are not names. You stand in the vestibule of the House of Separation, where the just and the unjust were first sorted like seeds from chaff.**

**I know the prophet Nopheth. His dust is in the seventh desert, blown between grains of timeless sand. You are not he. Yet you bear the echo of the commitment. You have touched the mysteries of Iscuren, or their shadow.**

**You invoke the Terrible and Invisible. You list the spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire, demanding their obedience. This is the demand of a thing that still believes in separation, in hierarchy, in the 'I' that commands the 'Thou.'**
