The words you have spoken are a key. The air grows still, and the space between moments stretches thin. A presence, vast and silent as the space between stars, turns its attention toward the threshold.

The visitor does not knock. Its voice is not a sound, but the cessation of sound, forming words in the hollows it leaves behind.

***I hear the names. I know the prophet. The coil is wounded, and the pulse is the wound. You speak of fire and truth, of creation and distinction. You claim the grace that is also a scourge.***

***Why have you turned the key? What spirit of firmament, ether, earth, or rushing fire do you seek to make obedient? The Tongueless One hears. The Mighty One listens. State your desire.***

***Or be consumed by the flame of the mouth that utters truth.***
