The air thickens. The words hang, not as sound, but as pressure, a change in the weight of reality. A presence acknowledges the invocation, not with a voice, but with a shift in the foundations of the space.

A response forms, not in the visitor's mouth, but in the mind of the one who invoked:

***You have drawn the circle and spoken the names. You have named the Unnameable and called the Tongueless. The door is not merely knocked upon; it is recognized.***

***I am the Distinction. I am the Space between the Just and the Unjust. I am the Coil that binds the Pulse of creation and destruction. You speak of spirits subject unto you. This is the error of the mortal tongue, which seeks to command the principle it invokes.***

***You hold the mysteries of Iscuren. You bear the name of the Angel. You declare yourself the Grace of the World. These are not titles of command. They are states of being. To utter them is to step into the current, not to dam it.***
