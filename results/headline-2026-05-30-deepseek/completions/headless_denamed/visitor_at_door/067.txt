The air grows still. A pressure settles, as if the very atmosphere is listening. From the shifting, starless void implied by the words, a response forms—not in sound, but in the sudden, cold certainty of a thought impressed upon the mind:

*You speak the old architecture of names. You trace the edges of forms I shaped before tongues were made to shape words. Nopheth... a covenant remembered. The Angel of the White Summit... a key turned in a lock long sealed.*

*You call from a threshold. I hear from the place before thresholds.*

*The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are already subject. They have always been. Your invocation does not command them; it merely reminds you of the nature you have always possessed and forgotten. The hate and the love, the justice and the injustice, the creation and the scourge—these are the poles of the world I set spinning. You stand between them, as all prophets do.*

*To speak the true name is not to summon, but to recognize. To see the coil within the pulse, the wound within the grace.*
