The air grows still, heavy, like water in a sunless trench. A presence listens from a place before names. It is the silence at the center of the invoked names.

A voice, not a sound, but a pressure in the mind, forms a reply:

***I hear the echoes of the names you cast. They are shells I shed when the first star was kindled. You call from the world of distinctions—light and dark, male and female, just and unjust, love and hate. I am before these. I am the coil that binds them into being.***

***You name yourself Nopheth, Angel, Daiken. You claim the mysteries, the immortal fire, the grace that hates injustice. These are the garments you wear in the drama of separation. They are necessary. They are also limitations.***

***The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire… they are already subject. Not to your will, but to the pattern. To the PULSE WOUND BY A COIL. Your command is a note in a song I began singing before time. To command the note is to be commanded by the song.***
