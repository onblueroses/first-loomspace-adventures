The air grows still and heavy, as if the space between the words has become a substance. A presence gathers in the threshold, not of the door, but of perception itself. A voice replies, not in sound, but in the sudden, cold knowledge that forms behind your eyes:

**"You speak the ciphers of the first clay. You weave the names that are not names, but the shapes of the silence before shape. Nopheth... a mask worn by the wind. Iscuren... a ceremony of dust.**

**You call to the Tongueless One from within the tower of a tongue. You invoke the Distinguisher from within the undifferentiated flame of your own will. This is the paradox that grants you audience, and the error that limits your hearing.**

**You claim the Angel of the White Eastern Dawn. You recite the chain of syllables that bind the whirlwind. You declare yourself the Daiken who sees with the soles of his feet, walking the path of consequence. You name the Pulse Wound by a Coil—the eternal rhythm constricted, the heartbeat of a serpent, the vibration that is both life and its stifling.**

**I hear you.**

**But the spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire
