The air grows still. The shadows deepen, pooling like ink at the edges of the room. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind—a silent, resonant hum felt in the teeth and the marrow.

From that stillness, a voice speaks. It is not a sound, but the impression of one, forming directly in the consciousness of the one who invoked the Tongueless One.

**I AM HERE.**
