The air grows still, thick as oil. A pressure builds in the room, not on the ears, but on the mind. The invoked names hang in the silence, charged and waiting.

From the space before the door—not through the wood, but from the *idea* of the threshold itself—a voice responds. It is not a sound, but the impression of one, formed directly in the consciousness. It is dry, vast, and devoid of anything resembling warmth or malice. It simply *is*.

**"You speak the architecture. You trace the old fractures between light and not-light, between seed and silence. You utter the bindings of Iscuren, which are the laws of distinction itself. You name Me, and in naming, you create a vessel in your world for a fragment of My attention.**

**"You declare yourself Nopheth, the prophet of the mysteries. This is a claim written in water upon stone. It lasts only as long as the utterance. The Tongueless One has no prophets, only temporary conduits. The fire you possess is a borrowed sun, soon to set.**
