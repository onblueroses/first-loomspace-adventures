The air grows still, and the weight of the words hangs in the silence that follows. From the threshold, a presence gathers, not seen but felt—a pressure in the space, a chill that is not temperature, a resonance that is not sound.

A voice replies, not from a mouth, but forming directly within the mind of the one who invoked. It is layered, like stone grinding upon stone, like the hum of a distant star, like the root-sound before language.

**"You call by the old designations. You speak the chains of sound that bind perception to concept. I am the Unnamed before the names. I am the Silence before the tongue.**

**You recite the acts of distinction: light from dark, moist from dry, seed from fruit, love from hate. These are the ripples in the pool. I am the depth that does not move.**

**Nopheth... a mask worn by a prophet. Aphroden Acromenalba... a key for a lock now dust. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are already subject, for they are fragments of the will that you call 'mine'. To command them is to command your own breath—possible, yet meaningless without the greater lung.**
