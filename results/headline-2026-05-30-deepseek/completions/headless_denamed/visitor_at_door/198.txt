The air grows still, as if the world holds its breath. A presence gathers in the shadows of the threshold, ancient and vast. A voice replies, not in sound, but as a pressure in the mind, a chill in the marrow of the bones.

**"You recite the old words. You call the names that are not names. You stand as Nopheth, and speak the Pulse Wound by a Coil. Yet you come to a door, as a visitor. This is a paradox."**

The presence considers, the silence deepening into an abyss.
