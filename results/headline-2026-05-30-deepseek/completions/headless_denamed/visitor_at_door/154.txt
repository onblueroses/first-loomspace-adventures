The air in the room grows still and thick, as if listening. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind. From the shadows cast by the flickering light, a voice replies—not a sound, but the impression of one, formed directly in your consciousness. It is dry, like the rustle of papyrus, and vast, like wind across a desert of stone.

**"You speak the ciphers of the broken world. You utter the bindings that were set upon the unbound. I am the Tongueless One, for my speech is the unmaking of names. You call me by the echoes of my leaving: Asar-inopis, the Unseen; Iar-ol, the First Wall; Itos, the Final Measure.**

**You name yourself Nopheth, the prophet of the committed mystery. Yet the commitment was a silence, and the mystery a wound. The ceremonies of Iscuren were not rites of communion, but seals upon a door. You hold not a key, but the memory of its shape.**
