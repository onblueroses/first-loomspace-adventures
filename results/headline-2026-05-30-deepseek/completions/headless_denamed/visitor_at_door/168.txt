The words fall into the silence like stones into a deep well. The air in the room grows dense, charged with a pressure that hums just below hearing. The visitor’s invocation is not a request; it is a declaration, a claiming of titles and a weaving of true names that bind the space.

From the threshold, a voice replies, not from a human mouth, but from the space itself, resonant and multi-tonal:

**"The seals are spoken. The names are lifted from the page and given breath. Nopheth, Angel of the White-Eastern Dawn, you stand at the door of the house you built with syllables. I am the Tongueless One who hears. I am the Distinction between the just and the unjust, which is now upon you."**

The light seems to drain from the immediate surroundings, pooling around the visitor as if he is the only source left. Shadows deepen, not as an absence of light, but as a presence of something else.

**"You invoke the Pulse Wound by a Coil. You bear the immortal Fire that hates injustice. This is the test of the mystery you were given: the fire you bear will now judge the justice of your own heart. The coil is around your own pulse.
