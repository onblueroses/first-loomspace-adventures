The air thickens. A pressure settles in the room, as if the atmosphere itself has grown dense and watchful. From the silence left by the words, a new voice emanates—not from the visitor, but from the space around you, a resonance that vibrates in the bones more than the ears.

***"You recite the old chains. You speak the names that are not names, the shapes of the shapeless. You call upon the Tongueless One with a tongue of flesh. You claim the lineage of Nopheth, the mantle of the Angel of the White-Walled Summit.***

***But the door is not opened with a key of borrowed words.***

***You say 'PULSE WOUND BY A COIL.' You seal it with 'IŌM SAPENŌTH.' These are not invocations to be spoken. They are states of being. They are wounds and bindings.***

***The one you invoke created by distinguishing: light from dark, just from unjust, love from hate. To call upon that power is to stand upon the knife-edge of that distinction. It is to become the separator within your own soul. Your every breath must now judge and divide. Your every thought must create and destroy.***
