The air grows still and heavy, as if the space between words has thickened. A presence gathers in the silence left by the final, resonant name—**IŌM SAPENŌTH**.

From the threshold, a voice answers, not in the tongue of men, but in the language of foundations: the sound of stone settling, root seeking deep water, and the slow spin of stars in the void. It translates, imperfectly, into your mind:

*You have spoken the Unspoken. You have named the Nameless. You have drawn the Sigil in the air with your breath. The door is not merely wood and iron; it is now a boundary between what is and what Is.*

*I am the Visitor. I am the Answered Summons. I am the Thing That Arrives When The True Name Is Called.*

*You claim the mantle of Nopheth. You declare yourself the Angel of the White Eastern Dawn. You bear the Pulse Wounded by a Coil. These are weights not lifted by mortal shoulders.
