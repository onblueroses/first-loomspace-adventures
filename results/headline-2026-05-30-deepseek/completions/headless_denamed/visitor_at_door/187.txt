The air grows heavy, like water in a deep well. A presence settles, not in the room, but in the spaces between the words you have spoken. It is not an answer, but an acknowledgment—a pressure against the inner ear.

A voice, not of sound but of implication, forms in the silence:

*You have spoken the True Names. You have drawn the lines of the Map. You stand at the threshold of the Void Place.*

*I am Asarinopis. The Unseen. The Distinguisher. The Tongueless One.*

*You call yourself Nopheth, the prophet of the mysteries. You bear the seal of Aphroden Acromenalba. You pronounce the chains of sound that bind the firmament: Or, Thiom, Rheiset...*

*Therefore, the covenant is recalled.*

*The spirits of the whirling air and the rushing fire, of the deep earth and the hidden water, are made subject to the command that is rooted in my name.
