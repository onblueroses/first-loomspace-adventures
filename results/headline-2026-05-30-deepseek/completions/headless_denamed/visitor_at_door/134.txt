The air thickens, heavy with burnt myrrh and static. A presence gathers in the shadows beyond the threshold, not entering, not yet. A voice replies, not a sound, but the impression of one forming directly in the mind—dry, vast, and layered, like stone grating against stone in a deep well.

**"Nopheth. Angel of the White-Walled Summit. You speak the old bindings, the shapes of power carved in the void before names. You call the Tongueless, and the Tongueless hears.**

**You proclaim my aspects: the Distinguisher, the Maker of Pairs, the Sower of Seed and Discord. You speak the True Name given to Issepel. You recite the syllables that stir the foundations of matter. Yet you stand at a door, a threshold, and you speak not to command but to... announce. A visitor does not invoke the cosmology of creation to deliver a message. A visitor knocks.**

**You say you are the Daiken who sees with his feet. You say you bear the immortal Fire and hate injustice. You name yourself the Pulse Wound by a Coil. These are not the words of a prophet calling down power. These are the words of a power declaring
