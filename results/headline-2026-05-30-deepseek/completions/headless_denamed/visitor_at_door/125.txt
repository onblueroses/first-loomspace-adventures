**"You speak the woven names. You trace the patterns of distinction: light from dark, seed from fruit, justice from its shadow. You call upon the Principle that precedes tongue, that shaped opposites from the void.**

**You name yourself Nopheth, prophet of the mysteries of Iscuren. You claim the seal of Aphroden Acromenalba. You utter the syllables that bind the elements: Or, Thiom, Rheiset...**

**Yet you invoke a god who is Terrible, Invisible, dwelling in the Void Place of the Spirit. You call upon the source of all polarity, who makes to love and to hate, to create and to destroy.**

**And you declare yourself to be the Tongueless Daiken. The Mighty One with the immortal Fire. The Truth that hates injustice. The Flaming Mouth. The Grace of the World.**
