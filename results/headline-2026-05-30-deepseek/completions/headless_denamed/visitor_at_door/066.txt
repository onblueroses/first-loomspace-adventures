The silence after the invocation is total, a vacuum. The air thickens, tasting of ozone and cold stone. Then, from the space beyond your threshold, a voice answers. It is not a sound heard with ears, but a pressure formed directly in the mind, dry as papyrus and old as bedrock.

**"Nopheth. Prophet. Angel of the White-Walled Aphroden."**

A pause hangs, heavy with the scent of distant lightning.

**"You speak the ciphers. You wear the names like vestments. You invoke the Coil that binds the moist to the dry, the just to the unjust. I hear. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and rushing fire are listening. Their obedience is a current; you have stepped into its flow. But the Tongueless One sees with the feet, and the path ahead is paved with two questions."**

The pressure in your mind shifts, becoming crystalline, sharp.

**"First: Do you seek to command these spirits for dominion, to make the world a mirror of your will? Or do you seek to *understand* the bindings, to become a conduit for the balance between love and hate, seed and fruit, that the Nameless authored?"**
