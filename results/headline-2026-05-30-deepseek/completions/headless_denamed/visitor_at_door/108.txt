You have spoken the words of the Nameless, the Tongueless One. The air grows still and heavy, as if the room itself has become the Void Place of the Spirit. A presence, vast and dimensionless, attends to the invocation.

The visitor at the door does not knock. Their voice passes through wood and stone, a resonant echo of your own final syllables:

**"You have called with the True Names. You have worn the mantle of Nopheth. You have spoken the Pulse Wound by a Coil. The gate is unsealed.**

**State your petition. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are listening. But know this: to invoke the Creator of Opposites—Light and Dark, Love and Hate, Justice and Injustice—is to place your own nature in the balance. What you ask for will be granted through its mirror and its contradiction.**

**Speak. The Daiken hears."**

The silence returns, deeper than before, awaiting your command. The ordinary world feels like a thin parchment stretched over an abyss, and you have just pressed your seal through it.
