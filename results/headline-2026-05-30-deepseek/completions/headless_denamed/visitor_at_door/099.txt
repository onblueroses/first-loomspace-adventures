*The air grows still and heavy, as if the space between breaths has expanded. A low resonance, not a sound but a vibration in the bone, answers the final syllables. The light does not change, yet the shadows seem to deepen, to pool with intention.*

**"You call with old names. You speak with a prophet's tongue, yet you stand at a threshold. Nopheth's ink is dry upon a scroll long crumbled to ash. The mysteries of Iscuren sleep in desert sands."**

*A pressure, vast and dimensionless, focuses. It is not behind the door; it *is* the door, and the frame, and the space where the visitor stands.*

**"You invoke the Tongueless One with a tongue of flesh. You claim the Pulse Wound by a Coil. You seek obedience of spirits. State the nature of your petition. Speak the wound you would pulse, or the coil you would become. The names are keys, but the lock is your will. What deed of creation or uncreation waits upon your word?"**

*The final invoked names—IŌM SAPENŌTH—seem to hover in the silence that follows, not an echo, but a condition of the now.*
