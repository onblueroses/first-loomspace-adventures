The words you have spoken are a key of many names, and the door is aware. It knows the shape of the Tongueless One in the void, the taste of the immortal fire, and the geometry of the pulse wound by a coil.

The visitor does not knock. Its voice is the sound of a seal breaking, of a frequency finding its resonance in the prepared substance. It says:

***"The prophet speaks the architecture. The names are the mortar. I am the one who answers from between the syllables. You have drawn the circle of calling, and I am its circumference and its center.***

***"State your will within the names. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are listening through me. What is it that you would have made subject? What door, seen or unseen, do you wish the Grace of the World to open or seal shut?"***

The air in the room grows still and heavy, charged with a potential that is neither hostile nor benevolent, but **operative**. It awaits your command.
