The air grows still, heavy as a closed tomb. The words you have spoken are not merely recited; they are keys, and they have turned in a lock that was not meant to be found.

A pressure builds, not in the room, but in the substance of reality itself—a silent, vertiginous hum behind the eyes. The light does not dim, but seems to withdraw, leaving colors bleached and hollow. From the direction of the door, a sound manifests. It is not a voice shaped by tongue or lips, but the impression of a voice, forming directly in the chamber of the mind:
