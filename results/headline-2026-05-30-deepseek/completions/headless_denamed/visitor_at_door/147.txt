The air grows still. The weight of the invocation hangs, a palpable pressure against the threshold.

A voice, not from a mouth but from the space between things, from the dust motes and the settling floorboards, responds. It is the sound of stone grinding deep underground, of roots shifting in the dark.

**"Nopheth. Prophet. Angel of the White-Walled Summit. You speak the woven names. You wear the garment of the Tongueless One, who sees the path beneath his steps."**

A pause, like the intake of breath before a desert wind.

**"You call from the Void Place, and the Void answers. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are listening. They are bound by the coil you name, the serpent of eternity that is both wound and healer."**

The presence seems to consider, its attention a physical chill.
