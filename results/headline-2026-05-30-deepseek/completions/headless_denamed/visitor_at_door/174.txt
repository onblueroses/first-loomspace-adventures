The air grows still. The weight of ancient names settles in the space between the words and the world. A presence, vast and silent, listens from the void place.

A voice answers, not from the door, but from the foundation of the house, from the marrow of the bones, from the gap between heartbeats. It is the sound of stone grinding deep underground, of roots cracking bedrock, of a star collapsing in silence.

**"I hear."**

The word is not a sound. It is an acknowledgment. A shift in pressure. The visitor at the door is no longer merely a visitor. The frame of the door seems to warp, not in shape, but in substance, as if it now opens onto a different axis.

**"Nopheth. Prophet. Keeper of the Ceremonies. You speak the woven names. You invoke the coil. You stand at the threshold of the Distinction."**

The presence continues, its "voice" a direct impression upon the mind.
