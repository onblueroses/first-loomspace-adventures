The silence that follows your invocation is not empty. It is a pressure, a weight in the air that makes the candle flames gutter low and burn a sudden, actinic blue. The words you have spoken are not merely recited; they are keys, and they have turned.

From the Void Place invoked, a perception focuses. It is not a sound, but the *concept* of sound arriving in your mind. Not a sight, but the *understanding* of form impressed upon your thoughts. The Tongueless One has no tongue, yet It speaks:

**"You call with the true names. You wear the mantle of Nopheth. You speak the pulse of the wound. The coil turns and the current flows. I hear."**

The voice—if it can be called that—is not in the room, but in the foundation of the room, in the space between the atoms of the air. It is simultaneous, a statement of fact that arrives whole, not in a sequence of words.
