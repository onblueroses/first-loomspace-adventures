 "I am the God of the gods."

The world is his. He has made it.

Heaven is his. He has founded it.

The earth is his. He has nourished it.

His is the fire. He has kindled it.

His is the water. He has brought it together.

His are the gods of the air.

His are the stars.

His are the two great gods in heaven.

His is the power, the might, the victory.

His are the two great gods on earth.

His are the gods of the sea.

His are the gods of the rivers.

His are the gods of the woods.

His are the gods of the plains.

His is the month.

His are the days.

His are the nights.

His are the hours.

His are the measures.

His are the barley, the spelt, the grain.

His are all things that are.

I am he.

I am he who is in heaven.

I am he who is on earth.

I am he who is in the water.

I am he who is in the fire.

I am he who is among mankind.

I am he who is among the gods.

I am he who is in the shrine.

I am he who is in the place of truth.
