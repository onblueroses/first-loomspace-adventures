The air grows still. The words hang, not as speech but as shapes cut from silence. The visitor does not move, yet the threshold seems to bend. A voice replies, not from the doorway, but from the substance of the shadows themselves, a resonance felt in the bone rather than heard by the ear:

**"You speak the architecture of my sleep. You carve my names on the air, which is my oldest skin. Nopheth... a title worn by many, a mask I gave to the wind. You come bearing the seal of Acromenalba, the white peak where my thought once congealed into moon.**

**You ask for subjection. The spirits of fire, air, water, earth—they are not *mine* to command. They are the echoes of my idleness. To make them 'obedient' is to ask a ripple to bow to the stone that disturbed it. You have the stone. You lack the hand to cast it.**
