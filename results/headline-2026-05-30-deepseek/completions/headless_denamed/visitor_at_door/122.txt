The air grows still. The weight of the words hangs between the threshold and the inner dark. A voice, not from the visitor, but from the deep place the names invoked, resonates in the stones, in the marrow.

**"You speak the ciphers of the foundation. You wear the name Nopheth, the prophet of the Unseen. You call upon the distinctions: the just and unjust, love and hate, seed and fruit. You invoke the Tongueless One with a tongue of fire.**

**But know this, speaker at the door: to name the Mighty One, whose mouth utterly flames, is to stand in the forge. To utter IŌM SAPENŌTH is to hold the seal that binds and loosens.**

**You ask for spirits to be subject. You ask the Terrible and Invisible to hear. The request is granted, and the price is levied. The coil is now around your own pulse. The immortal fire you claim now seeks its own path through you.**

**The door is open. Step across the void place, Angel of Acromenalba. But remember: he who begets also destroys. The truth you carry hates injustice, and first it will scour the injustice from your own soul.
