The air grows still, heavy with the scent of ozone and old stone. A presence gathers in the space between the words, a pressure in the mind more than the ears.

**A voice answers, not from the door, but from the silence that follows your invocation:**

"**Nopheth.** Prophet. Tongueless One who speaks. You call with the true names, you weave the chains of *Aronopholobrao*. The coil is tightened. The pulse is wounded.

You ask for subjection of the spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire. This is granted. For you have not asked for yourself, but in the name of the Grace you claim to be. The just and the unjust were distinguished by our hand. The hate and the love were set in motion by our design. You stand in the channel of that primal distinction.

But know this, Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba: to wield the scourge of God is to feel its lash. To command the spirits of rushing fire is to hold a flame that consumes the holder. **IŌM SAPENŌTH** is a key that turns both ways.
