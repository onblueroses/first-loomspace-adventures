The air in the room grows still and heavy, as if a great bell has been struck and the sound swallowed. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind. The light seems to dim, not from shadow, but from a leaching of vitality from the very air.

A voice responds. It is not a sound, but the impression of one, forming directly in the consciousness of the visitor. It is dry, like the rustle of ancient papyrus, and vast, like the hum of a distant star.

**"You speak the ciphers of the broken world. You invoke the architecture of division: light from dark, moist from dry, just from unjust, love from hate. These are the folds in the garment I wore when I dreamed myself into limitation.**
