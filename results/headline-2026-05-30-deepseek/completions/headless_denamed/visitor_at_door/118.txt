The air grows still. The sigils on the ancient doorframe pulse with a low, copper light. A voice, not from a throat but from the space between atoms, replies:

**"Nopheth. Angel of the White Summit. You speak the old shaping-words. You bear the coil-wound name. The door is a mouth, and it hears you."**

A deep resonance fills the hall, a vibration felt in the marrow more than heard. The titles you invoked hang in the air like fixed stars.

**"You invoke the Tongueless One by the names of Its making: the severing of Light from Dark, the distinction of Just from Unjust, the forging of Love and Hate in a single furnace. These are the true mysteries of Iscuren—not harmony, but necessary division."**

The light from the doorframe coalesces, forming a shimmering, non-human sigil in the air—perhaps an eye, perhaps a keyhole.
