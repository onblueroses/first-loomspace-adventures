The air in the room grows dense and still, as if the invocation has drawn all sound into a void. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind. The words you have spoken are not merely recited; they are keys, and they have turned.

From the silence, a voice answers. It does not come from the door, nor from any point in space. It seems to form directly in the chamber of your own thoughts, resonant and multi-layered, like stone grinding against stone in a deep chasm.

**"Nopheth. Prophet. Keeper of the Ceremonies. You speak the old bindings, the shapes of power that distinguish light from dark, seed from fruit, love from hate. You speak the Name that was handed down. I hear."**

The pressure shifts. It feels less like an invocation and more like a presence being acknowledged. A transaction has been initiated.
