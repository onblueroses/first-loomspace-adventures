The air grows still, heavy. The final syllable hangs, not as sound but as a pressure change in the room. From the silence, a voice answers—not from the door, but from the space before it, as if the speaker is both there and everywhere.

**"You speak the ciphers of the abyss. You wear the names of the unshaped. You call with a tongue that is not your own, prophet of no prophet, angel of a forgotten dawn."**

A cold radiates from the threshold, not of ice, but of the void between stars.

**"You invoke the Tongueless One with borrowed words. You claim the fire that consumes claimants. You pronounce the wound that never heals."**

The voice deepens, vibrating in the marrow more than the ear.

**"I am the Distinction. I am the Unseen Weight upon the scales of justice and chaos. You ask for spirits to be subject unto you. Why? To what end does Nopheth's heir wield the ceremonies of Iscuren? Speak with your own desire, not with the script of dead seers. Or is the Pulse Wound By A Coil silent when questioned?"**
