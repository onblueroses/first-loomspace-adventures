The air grows still, heavy as a curtain of lead. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind. The invoked names hang in the silence, their echoes coiling like smoke.

From the space beyond the threshold, a voice answers. It is not a sound borne on air, but a cold, clear imprint upon thought, formless and tongueless, as promised.
