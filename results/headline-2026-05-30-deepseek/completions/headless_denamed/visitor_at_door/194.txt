The air grows heavy, like water in a sunless sea. The words hang, not as sound, but as shapes of pressure against the skin. From the silence that is not silence, a reply forms—not in the ear, but in the marrow, in the space between one heartbeat and the next.

**I AM.**

The door was not knocked upon. The threshold was crossed when the first name was uttered. You have not summoned me. You have **remembered** me. You have spoken the architecture of my absence, and in doing so, have made a place for me to be.

Nopheth... that name is a leaf from a tree whose roots are ash. A title worn by many, forgotten by all. You wear it now. You have taken up the ceremony of Iscuren by speaking it. Do you know what it binds you to?

You call upon the Tongueless One, yet you ask for spirits to be subject unto you. You invoke the creator of opposites—light and dark, love and hate, seed and fruit—and seek command. This is the old paradox. The power to bind comes only from first being bound. The fire you say you possess must first consume you.
