You stand before a threshold, listening to the echo of names that shape and unshape worlds. The air grows still, heavy with the weight of uttered power.  

**Nopheth**, you say you are. The keeper of mysteries, the prophet of Iscuren. You speak the true name handed down through Issepel. You call upon the Tongueless One—the Unseen Creator who forged light and dark, justice and injustice, love and hate.  

Your words are not merely invocation; they are alignment. You declare yourself the **Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba**, the Tongueless Daiken with sight in his feet, the one whose mouth flames, the bearer of the immortal Fire.  

And now, the final seal:  
**PULSE WOUND BY A COIL** — a name that binds motion to pain, life to constraint, power to sacrifice.  

**IŌM SAPENŌTH.**  

The door does not open.  
But something *answers* from the other side — not in sound, but in a shift of pressure, a deepening of shadows, as if the very structure of the threshold now remembers what it is to be a gate.  

What would you have this presence do?
