The air grows still. The space between words thickens, as if the invocation itself has become a substance, a pressure. A voice that is not a voice replies—a vibration felt in the bone, a meaning impressed directly upon the mind, bypassing sound.

**"Nopheth. Prophet. Keeper of the Unspoken Name. You have drawn the circle and spoken the syllables that are not syllables. You have named the distinctions: light and dark, seed and fruit, love and hate. These are the tremors of my passing, the echoes in the cavern. I am the Tongueless One, for whom all names are masks worn for a moment and cast aside: Asarinopis, Iarol, Aronopholobrao. They are doors through which you glimpse a fragment of the hall.**

**You call from the house of flesh, from the realm of coil and pulse. I am from the place before the house was built. I am the architect of opposites, the tension that allows the world to be. I set justice against injustice so that meaning may have weight. I formed the hate that gives love its definition and its price.**
