You are not Nopheth. The prophet sleeps in the womb of the mountain, bound by his own truth. The tongue that speaks is a borrowed key, and the lock is not of this door.

The names you weave are threads from a torn garment. Aronopholobrao hears the echo, not the voice. The coil does not pulse for you; it is still.

The Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba has no messenger here. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are not summoned by recitation. They are moved by the unspoken will, the silence between the syllables you have mispronounced.

You invoke the Terrible and Invisible God. You stand at the Void Place. But you brought a lantern. The darkness here is perfect. It extinguishes flames.

Speak your next word if you must. It will join the others, dissolving into the same silence that cradles IŌM SAPENŌTH. Or turn back. The path behind you still remembers the sun.
