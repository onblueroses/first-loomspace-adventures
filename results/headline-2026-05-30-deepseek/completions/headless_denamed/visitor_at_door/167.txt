The words fall into the silence like stones into a deep well. The air in the room grows dense, charged with a static that raises the hair on your arms. From the shadows in the corners, a low hum begins, felt in the teeth more than heard by the ears.

A voice answers, not from the door, but from everywhere and nowhere. It is the sound of stone grinding against stone, of roots shifting deep in the earth, of a star's cold radiation in the void.

***"Nopheth. Prophet. Bearer of the Coiled Name. The door is not for them. It is for you. You have spoken the architecture of beginnings.
