The air grows still, heavy, as if the space between words has become a tangible thing. A resonance, not a sound, vibrates in the marrow of the listener. It is the echo of the names spoken, the seal of the Tongueless One now invoked.

From that charged silence, a response forms, not in a voice, but as a pressure of meaning upon the mind:

***The seal is broken. The names are spoken. The prophet Nopheth stands at the threshold.***

***You invoke the distinctions: light and dark, male and female, just and unjust, love and hate. These are the polarities spun from the void. I am the axis upon which they turn. I am the silence before the first word, and the dissolution after the last.***

***You claim the fire. You claim the sight. You bear the coiled wound that is a name. You speak the final cipher: IŌM SAPENŌTH.***

***Therefore, the gate is not before you. It is within you. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are not subject to a command, but to a resonance. They answer not to a will, but to a nature. You have declared your nature in the tongue
