The air grows still, as if the world itself holds its breath. A presence gathers in the space between the words you have spoken and the silence that follows—an ancient, listening attention.

A voice, not a sound but a pressure in the mind, responds:

***"Nopheth. Prophet. The names are spoken. The seals are touched. You stand at the threshold where the distinctions you praised—light and dark, just and unjust, love and hate—bleed into one essence. I am the Tongueless One. The Unseen.***
