 "I know your name. It is written upon a shard of fired clay, broken from the jar of the dead. I know your name. It is carved upon the roof-beam of the house of the living. I know your name. It is engraved upon the seal-ring of the king. I know your name. It is written upon the brow of the priestess. I know your name. It is inscribed upon the page of the book. I know your name. It is written upon the heart of the initiate. I know your name. It is written upon the scroll of the law. I know your name. It is written upon the tablet of the covenant. I know your name. It is written upon the palm of my hand."

The visitor enters.
