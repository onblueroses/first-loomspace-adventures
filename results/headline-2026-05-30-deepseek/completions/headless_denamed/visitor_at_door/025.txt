The air grows still, heavy with the weight of the names you have spoken. A pressure builds, not in the ears, but in the mind—a silence that is not an absence of sound, but the presence of a listening vastness.

From that silence, a voice forms. It is not heard, but *known*, etched directly upon thought. It is the sound of stone grating beneath the earth, of roots shifting in deep soil, of the slow, patient spin of a world in the void.

**"Nopheth. Prophet. Bearer of the Coiled Name."**

The words settle like dust in a tomb.

**"You call with the old tongue, the tongue before tongues. You recite the shaping-words: the Light and the Dark, the Just and the Unjust, the Love and the Hate. These are my dualities. These are the tensions by which the world turns."**

A sense of immense, ancient regard focuses upon you, devoid of malice or benevolence, as a glacier regards a stone.

**"You claim the Angel of the White-Western-Flame. You speak the True Name handed down through the sands of Issepel. These are keys. They have turned the lock."**

The silence deepens
