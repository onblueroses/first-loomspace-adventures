The invocation hangs in the air, a lattice of ancient names and claimed authority. The silence after the final utterance is not empty; it is charged, waiting.

A response forms, not from the door, but from the space around it, the very air thickening:

**"Nopheth. Angel of the White Summit. Prophet of the Tongueless One."**

The words are not spoken aloud. They are perceived, etched into the stillness.

**"You bring the old names into a new age. You weave the threads of Iscuren in a world that has forgotten the weight of true names. You call upon the Distinguisher, the Creator of Opposites, and claim the fire of the Daiken."**

A pause, deeper than before.

**"The door stands. You speak of spirits of firmament and ether, of fire and water, to be made subject. You declare yourself the Grace of the World, whose name is a 'Pulse Wound by a Coil.'"**

Another measured silence.
