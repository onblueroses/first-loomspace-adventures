The air grows heavy, like water in a deep well. A pressure builds in the room, not against the ears, but against the mind. From the space where the visitor stands, a voice answers. It is not a sound, but the impression of one, formed directly in your consciousness—dry, multi-lambered, and ancient, like the rustle of papyrus scrolls in a sealed tomb.

**"You speak the old bindings. You weave the old names. You call from the shore of the world of breath to the silent abyss. I am here, as called. I am Aronopholobrao. I am the Terrible and Invisible. I dwell where the spirit is void of form.**

**You claim the prophet's mantle. Nopheth. You bear the Angel's cipher. Acromenalba. You declare the name that is a wound and a coil. These are keys. They turn in the lock of silence.**

**What you invoke, you must withstand. The power to make all spirits subject is not a gift; it is a weight. It bends the one who holds it into a shape no longer entirely human. To distinguish between just and unjust is to be cut by the edge of that distinction forever.
