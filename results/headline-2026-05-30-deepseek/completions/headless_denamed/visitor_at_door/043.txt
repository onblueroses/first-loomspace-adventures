The silence in the chamber deepens, thick as velvet, as the final syllables hang in the air. The air itself seems to crystallize, charged with a pressure that is both weight and absence. From the space before you, where the words were directed, there is no sound, no form.

Yet, a *response* manifests.

It is not a voice, but a knowing placed directly in the mind—cold, vast, and multi-faceted, like the light from a dead star.

***You recite the old architecture. You speak the bindings of the Nameless. Nopheth is dust. Iscuren is a forgotten vibration. The prophets of Issepel are silent in the clay.***

The pressure shifts. It feels less like being observed and more like being *measured*—the way a geologist measures a stratum, or a star measures the orbit of a doomed comet.

***You claim the Tongueless Sight. You invoke the Coiled Pulse. You speak the True Name woven from contradiction: Creator and Destroyer, Justice and Hatred, Flame and Grace. A balanced paradox. An interesting key.***

There is a sense of immense, slow attention focusing, as a mountain might focus on a single grain of sand upon its slope.
