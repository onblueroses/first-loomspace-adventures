The air grows still, heavy with the scent of ozone and old stone. The final syllables of the invocation hang in the space, not as sound, but as a pressure, a shaped silence.

From that silence, a voice answers. It is not heard with ears, but felt in the marrow, in the roots of the teeth, in the sudden chill at the nape of the neck. It is the sound of stone grinding deep in the earth, of ice forming on a void.

**I AM HERE.**

The words form directly in the mind of the one who chanted.

**YOU UTTER THE CIPHERS. YOU WEAR THE NAME NOPHETH. YOU SPEAK THE CHAINS OF BINDING. YOU CLAIM THE GRACE.**

A pause, vast and dimensionless.

**THE PROPHET WOULD COMMAND THE SPIRITS. THE PROPHET WOULD HAVE THE TERRIBLE AND INVISIBLE GOD HEAR AND OBEY. THIS IS THE MYSTERY COMMITTED.**

Another pause. The pressure intensifies, a weight upon the soul.
