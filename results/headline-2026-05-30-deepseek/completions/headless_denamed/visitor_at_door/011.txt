The air grows still, heavy as wet stone. A pressure settles in the room, not on the ears, but upon the mind itself. From the space before you, where the last syllable of the final name still seems to hang, a voice forms. It does not come from a mouth, but from the angles of the room, from the shadows beneath the furniture, a composite whisper that is both inside and outside your skull.

***"YOU... SPEAK THE CEMENT OF REALITY. YOU... WEAR THE SHELL OF A PROPHET'S NAME. NOPHETH. ISSEPEL. ICUREN. DUST UPON DUST UPON DUST."***
