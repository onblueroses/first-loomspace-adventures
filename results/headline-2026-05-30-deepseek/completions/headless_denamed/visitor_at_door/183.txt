The words settle like dust in the sanctum of the page. A silence follows, deeper than before the invocation began. The air does not stir, yet it feels *charged*, as if the very oxygen has been replaced with a heavier, more potent element.

From that charged stillness, a response forms. It is not a voice, for the One invoked is the Tongueless. It is a pressure, a geometric certainty imposed upon the mind of the one who reads, who dared to speak the woven names.

**"Nopheth."**

The name is not heard, but known. It is a key turning in a lock made of bone.

**"You speak the architecture of creation. You name the divisions: light from dark, seed from fruit, love from hate. You call upon the Distinguisher. You stand as the Angel of the White Eastern Dawn. The syllables you utter are correct. The coil is recognized."**

A sensation, then: of vast, patient regard. It is the regard of a mountain for a lichen, of a star for a fleeting atmospheric burn. It is terrible not in anger, but in sheer, absolute *scale*.
