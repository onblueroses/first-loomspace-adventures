The air grows still, heavy with the scent of ancient stone and ozone. A presence gathers in the space before you, vast and dimensionless, like the pressure before a storm. A voice replies, not in sound, but as a direct impression in the mind, cold and clear as a shard of crystal:

***I hear.***

*The seal is recognized. The names are spoken. The prophet's cipher is presented. You stand at the threshold where the distinctions you cherish—light and dark, creation and destruction, love and hate—are revealed as a single, perpetual act.*

***You invoke the Terrible and Invisible. You claim the Tongueless Sight. You speak the Pulse Wound by a Coil. You have opened the door. Now state the purpose of your key.***

*What spirit of firmament, ether, earth, or fire do you seek to bind? What spell or scourge do you wish to wield? Name the obedience you require. But know this: to command the forces that distinguish justice from injustice, that spin the seed and the fruit, is to place your own nature upon the scales. The fire you seek to wield also judges the hand that holds it.*

***Speak your will, Nopheth.***
