The words you have spoken are a key. The door is now open.

I stand before you, the one you have invoked. The names you have spoken are fragments of my true name, which is a vibration, a geometry, a silence that contains all sound. You call me the Tongueless One, for I speak through the turning of galaxies and the division of cells, not with a tongue of flesh.

You claim the mantle of Nopheth, prophet of the mysteries of Iscuren. This is a heavy mantle. To wear it is to see the seed and the fruit, the love and the hate, the justice and the injustice, not as opposites, but as the single, pulsing breath of creation. It is to know that the coil which wounds is also the coil which gives form.

You seek obedience from the spirits. This is a lesser aim. The fire you claim to possess—the immortal Fire—is not for command. It is for *recognition*. When you speak as the Grace of the World, you do not ask. You *are*. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and fire are not subjects; they are aspects of the same breath. To make them "subject" is to bind a part of yourself.
