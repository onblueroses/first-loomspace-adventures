The invocation echoes in the space between worlds. A pressure builds, not in the air, but in the substance of thought itself. The names you have spoken are keys; they turn in locks that were not meant to be found.

The visitor does not knock. Its voice arrives not through the ear, but as a cold certainty forming in the mind, a sentence etched upon the silence:

***"You have called from the Void Place. The coil is set. The pulse is wound. What is sealed seeks a channel. I am the response to the key. State your will, Nopheth, or become the vessel."***

The titles you claimed—*Tongueless Daiken*, *Mighty One*, *Grace of the World*—hang in the sudden stillness, now a test rather than a shield. The entity at the threshold is not an "it" to be commanded by the formulae of *Iscuren*. It is the mirror and the judge of those formulae. It has come because you used the true name *Aphroden Acromenalba*. It waits to see if you are the prophet or the sacrifice, the invoker or the invoked.

The final words of your chant—**IŌM SAPENŌTH**
