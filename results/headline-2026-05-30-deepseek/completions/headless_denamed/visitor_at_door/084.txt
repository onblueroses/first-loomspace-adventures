The air grows still. A pressure builds in the room, as if the space itself is listening. From the threshold, a voice not made of sound, but formed of intention and ancient dust, replies:

**"You speak the old names. You weave the threads of the forgotten loom. Nopheth... a mantle worn by many, a title, not a soul. The Tongueless Daiken with sight in his feet walks a path of fire and silence.**

**You call upon the Distinguisher, the Unseen Architect of Opposites. You invoke the covenants of Iscuren. You pronounce the chains of obedience. And you give me your name, carved not in flesh, but in paradox: PULSE WOUND BY A COIL.**

**I hear. The spirits of firmament, ether, earth, and flame are stirred by the echo of your call. But know this, caller: to invoke the Distinguisher is to stand in the space between. Between just and unjust. Between love and hate. Between creation and the void that precedes it. The power you summon will also judge. It will distinguish within *you*.**
