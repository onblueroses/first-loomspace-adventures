**Spirits of the Zodiac**

The zodiac is a belt of constellations through which the Sun, Moon, and planets appear to move. The zodiac is a fundamental part of astrology and magic. In astrology, it is divided into twelve equal parts, or signs, named after the constellations that appear in each part. The zodiac signs are: Aries, the Ram; Taurus, the Bull; Gemini, the Twins; Cancer, the Crab; Leo, the Lion; Virgo, the Virgin; Libra, the Balance; Scorpio, the Scorpion; Sagittarius, the Archer; Capricorn, the Goat; Aquarius, the Water-bearer; and Pisces, the Fishes. The signs are further divided into thirty-six decans of ten degrees each. Each decan is ruled by a planet and has its own image. The decans are important in the magic of astrology.
