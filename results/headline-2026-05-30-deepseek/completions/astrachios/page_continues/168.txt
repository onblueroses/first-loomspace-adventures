The last line on the page is a line of text reading: "O Lord, deliver me from this evil man, preserve me from the violent man." This is a quotation from Psalm 140:1, but in the Heptameron it reads: "Deliver me, O Lord, from the evil man, and preserve me from the unjust man." The psalm quotation in the grimoire is a standard feature of conjurations in magical works.

On page 116 there is a magical square that contains the words ANAEL TZEDEKIEL AMIXIEL HARIEL HYDRIEL. It is not explained, but it probably represents the names of the five planetary angels of the days of the week. The names of these angels are given in the Heptameron as Michael (Sunday), Gabriel (Monday), Samael (Tuesday), Raphael (Wednesday), Sachiel (Thursday), Anael (Friday), and Cassiel (Saturday).
