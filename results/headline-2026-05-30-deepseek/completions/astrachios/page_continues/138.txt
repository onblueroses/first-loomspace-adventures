 ‘O ye angels of God, and ministers of the Most High, go forth and constrain the spirit N., to appear visibly before me, in fair and comely human form, without hurt to me or any other creature; and without any horrible noise or stinking smell; and that he answer truly and readily to all my demands.’

In the margin is written: ‘Let the exorcist have his seal or character in his left hand, and in his right hand his sword with the which he must strike the air as often as occasion serves.’

In the centre of the page is a triangle, with a circle inside it, and a large cross in the centre of the circle. Outside the triangle are the words: ‘This triangle is to be made on parchment of a hare, and to be held towards the spirit when he appears, and he will be compelled to enter into it, and to obey you in all things.’

In the margin is written: ‘The exorcist must be clothed in clean white linen, and must have consecrated water, and perfume, and a girdle, and above all he must be well confessed, and have received the sacrament.’
