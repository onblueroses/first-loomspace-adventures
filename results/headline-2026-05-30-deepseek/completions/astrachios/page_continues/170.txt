 “O you angels who are the guardians of the fifth heaven, who are powerful, strong, and mighty, and who give grace and favor to men, and who bring to success every enterprise that may be begun.”

The next page is an illustration: a large circle, in the center of which is a large five-pointed star. On each of the five points are the names of the angels of the fifth heaven. Within the central pentagram are the names of God, and around the circle are the names of the seven Olympic spirits.

The next page is a diagram: “The secret seal of Solomon that must be made in gold.” It is a hexagram, a six-pointed star. Within it is written, “The secret seal of Solomon, by which you will command the aerial spirits, and they will obey.”

The next page: “This is the pentacle of Solomon that you must make on the day of Mercury, at the hour of Mercury.” It is a pentacle, a five-pointed star within a circle, and within the pentacle are the words: “By this pentacle Solomon commanded the spirits.”
