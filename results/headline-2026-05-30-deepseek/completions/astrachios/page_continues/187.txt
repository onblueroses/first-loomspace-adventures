A note in the grimoire states that “the names of the Angels” must be spoken “in their proper order.” This is followed by the words: “The names of the Angels are to be written on virgin parchment, and worn on the breast.” The names are: “Anael, Michael, Raphael, Gabriel.”

The grimoire states that the names of the angels are to be written “on a small plate of pure silver” and “hung from the neck.” This is followed by a blessing: “Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting, and let all the people say Amen.”
