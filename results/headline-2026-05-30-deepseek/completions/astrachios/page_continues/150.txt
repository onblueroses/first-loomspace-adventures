 “O King of the East, Amaymon, King of the South, Paymon, King of the West, Egyn, King of the North; O ye four kings, I conjure ye to bring hither [name of spirit].” The conjurations are then continued: “O thou spirit [name], I conjure and exorcise thee, by Him that spake and it was done; by the most holy and glorious names Adonai, El, Elohim, Elohe, Zebaoth, Elion, Escerchie, Jah, Tetragrammaton, Sadai, Lord God most high, that thou come forthwith without delay from all parts of the world, and make rational answers unto my demands, and that peacefully, visibly, and affably, without any deformity or horror, to the glory of God and the safety of man.” It then commands the spirit to appear “by the virtue of these names of God Agla, On, Tetragrammaton, and by the name Primeumaton, which commandeth the whole host of heaven.”

The spirit is then commanded to “appear in a fair form, in a human shape, without any deformity or horror,” and to “stand steadfast before me.” Then
