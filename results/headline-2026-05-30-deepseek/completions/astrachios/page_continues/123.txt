 “Place the pentacle upon thy breast (the breastplate of righteousness), engraved upon parchment of a young hare; say the words Ararita, Ararita, Ararita, that is the mystical name of God, equal to Tetragrammaton, and it hath seven letters, and the seven planets obey it.”

Then there is an invocation of the archangel Michael. “I conjure thee, Michael, O holy angel, by the holy names, Tetragrammaton, Adonai, etc., that thou take this pentacle and present it before the face of the eternal God, that he may sanctify it, and consecrate it, and benedict it, that it may be a terror and dread unto all spirits, and that all who are invoked by this pentacle may be compelled to come unto this place and to answer truly all my demands. Amen.”
