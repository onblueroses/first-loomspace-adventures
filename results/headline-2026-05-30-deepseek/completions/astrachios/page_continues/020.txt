## THE MAGIC CIRCLE

THE MAGIC CIRCLE is one of the most important and enduring tools of magic. It is an area of protection drawn around the magician, sometimes physically, sometimes mentally, but always with the aim of keeping the magician safe from harm. In ceremonial magic, the circle is usually drawn on the floor with chalk or charcoal and is often encircled
