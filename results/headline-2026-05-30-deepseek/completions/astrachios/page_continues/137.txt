 "Then conjure the spirit with fire and suffumigation, and bind it by the constraint."

The conjuration is in Hebrew:

I conjure you, spirit, by the name IHVH Tetragrammaton, and by the name Elohim, and by the name Adonai, and by the name Agla, and by the name Inessenfatall, and by the name Schema Hamphoras, and by the name Primeumaton, and by the name Tetragrammaton, and by the name Messias, and by all the names by which God created the world in six days, and by the name which Moses spoke and the earth opened and swallowed Kora, Dathan, and Abiram, and by the name by which Moses spoke and the Red Sea divided, and by the name by which Joshua spoke and the sun stood still, and by the name by which Daniel spoke and destroyed the idol Bel, and by the name which Solomon spoke and all the spirits were subject to him, and by the name which Isaiah spoke and destroyed the dragon, and by the name that you know, and by the seal that you know, and by the name Anepheneton, that Moses spoke and all the rivers in Egypt were turned into blood
