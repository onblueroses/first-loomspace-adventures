 ‘O thou great God, the Lord and governor of all the creatures of Heaven, earth and Hell, I conjure and call upon thee by thy name, Tetragrammaton, Jehovah, which is the root, trunk and source of all other names of Gods, celestial, aerial, infernal and terrestrial; who always was, is, and shall be, the beginning and ending, Alpha and Omega.’

The second spirit is named ‘Haziel’ and appears as a ‘king’ in the form of a child. He can help you find hidden treasure, make you invisible, and teach you the ‘art of memory’. You can conjure him with the name ‘Almighty God, Jehovah’ and the words ‘I call upon thee, Haziel, by the omnipotent name Tetragrammaton, Jehovah’.

The third spirit, ‘Aldiel’, appears as a man holding a sceptre and wearing a crown. He can tell you anything you want to know about the present, past or future, and can also help you find treasure. You can conjure him with the words: ‘I call upon thee, Aldiel, by the names of the great God, Jehovah, Adonai, Elohim.’
