 "The aforesaid names are to be borne in mind, and with the aforesaid spirits, and with all others, are they to be read over. And when they are read over they are to be written, and that which is written is to be worn; and wherever you may be, wear them, and you will be safe from every evil."

Another prayer, this one addressed to God, appears on the page: "O God Almighty, Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, I invoke thy holy and propitious name, beseeching thee through our Lord Jesus Christ, that thou wilt bestow upon me the gift of thy Holy Spirit, who mayest teach me and bring to my remembrance all things whatsoever I ask; and that the same Holy Spirit may suggest to me, O Lord, whatever I may wish to learn, and that he may comfort me and teach me those things which are necessary for me concerning those matters which are in thy sight, O Lord. Holy, holy, holy, Lord God of Sabaoth, who didst create heaven and earth, the sea, and all things that are therein, who sittest upon the cherubim, and searchest out the deep things, the hidden treasures of wisdom and knowledge, the Father of our Lord
