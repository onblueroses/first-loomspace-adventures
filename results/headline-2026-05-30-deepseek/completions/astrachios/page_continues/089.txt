 “O Lord, hear my prayer and let my cry come unto thee. O Lord God of Hosts, who rideth upon the heavens, hear my voice. Yea, hear my voice. Let my voice come unto thee, and let mine enemies which rise up against me be put to flight and be scattered. Let them be put to flight and destroyed by thy mighty arm.” The names of the angels of the four winds are invoked: Oriel, Paymon, Egyn, Amaymon. The magician asks for success in his undertaking and protection from his enemies. The seals of the angels are to be drawn on virgin parchment in dove’s blood, and carried by the magician. The names of the angels are to be written on the other side of the parchment, and also the seal of the spirit.
