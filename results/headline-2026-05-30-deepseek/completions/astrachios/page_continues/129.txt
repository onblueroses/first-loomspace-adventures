 "O all you holy angels and archangels, bless and consecrate this wax; that it may be so consecrated that it may receive virtue by your holy names, so that whatsoever is demanded may be effected, and whatsoever is desired may be obtained through the power of the most holy creator Adonai, Elohim, Tetragrammaton, who liveth and reigneth, world without end, Amen."

The magician must then recite the Lord's Prayer, Hail Mary, and Creed. Then he must sprinkle the image with holy water, saying, "Thou art the wax blessed and consecrated in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Ghost, that thou mayest effect whatsoever I shall demand."
