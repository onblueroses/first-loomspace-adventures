These names are similar to those given in the Testament of Solomon, and it is likely that the author of the Ars Almadel drew inspiration from that source.

The angel names were to be inscribed on candles that were used in the ritual. The Ars Almadel gives instructions for making a wax candle that is inscribed with the names of the angels of the first altitude, or Chora. The candle is placed upon the Almadel, a wax tablet inscribed with a magic square.

The Ars Almadel also describes four Choras or "altitudes," each presided over by four angels. These altitudes correspond to the four cardinal directions.
