 “O you who wander through the air and who have the power to open the gates of Amente, the great god has sent me to you so that you may open them for me as you did for the great god. I invoke you to open them for me tonight.”

The words of the invocation continue:

Then I will conjure you by the great name, the terrible, that you will accomplish for me that which I desire. Hear me, because you will be satisfied and will be content with my voice.
