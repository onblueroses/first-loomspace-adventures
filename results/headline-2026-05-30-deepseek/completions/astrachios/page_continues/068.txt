The magical circle in the Goetia is a simple one, but it is the basis for all later magical circles. The circle is nine feet across, and there is a nine-inch gap between the two outer circles. The outer circle is drawn first, then the inner circle. The names of power are then written in the space between the two circles. The names are: Tetragrammaton, Primeumaton, Anaphaxeton, and the names of the four archangels: Michael, Gabriel, Raphael, and Uriel. The circle is entered from the east.

The pentacles of Solomon are a set of seventy-five magical designs that are used in conjunction with the spirits of the Goetia. They are used to compel the spirits to do the magician’s bidding.

The magician’s tools include a wand, a sword, a robe, a crown, a seal, and a pentacle. The wand is made of hazel wood, and is about eighteen inches long. The sword is used to banish spirits, and the robe is used to protect the magician from evil spirits
