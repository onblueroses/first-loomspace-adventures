 “Draw the circle with these names.” The circle is depicted at the top of the page, drawn around a pentagram. In the center of the pentagram is written the word “El.” The circle is drawn with the names of God from the first line, each name separated by a cross. The pentagram is drawn with the names from the second line. The circle is then sealed by the names from the third line, which are also separated by crosses. The instructions continue:

When you have drawn the circle, sprinkle it with holy water and fumigate it. After fumigating, recite the following: “I conjure thee, O circle of art, by the names El, Elohim, Elohe Zebaoth, Elion, Escherie, Jah, Tetragrammaton, Sadai, that thou be a fortification and a protection and a stronghold against all spirits and phantoms and all their powers, and that no spirit have the power to cross these bounds. In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.”
