 "O Lord that didst create all things in the beginning and hast set the heavens in order: I beseech Thee, O Lord, to send Thy holy angel of the Sun whose name is Sorath, and whose number is 666, that he may bring me a fairy from any mountain, who shall be of my complexion, and she shall come and give me her hand, and answer me truly concerning all things which I shall ask of her."

This is followed by the Seal of the Sun, which is a circle with a dot in the middle. The circle is surrounded by the names: Sorath, Adonai, Michael, Raphael, Gabriel, Uriel, Zobiel, Zadkiel, Camael, Haniel, Raphael, and Sorath again.
