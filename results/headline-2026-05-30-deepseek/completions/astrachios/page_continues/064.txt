 "Or conjure them thus:"

I conjure and confirm upon you, ye strong and holy angels, by these names: BARUCH, BARACH, BARABAL, SABACT, PATACEL, ALIGA, ALIASIN, ARIOR, that you make haste to come to me without delay, without fraud and deceit, without harm unto me or my fellows, from your mountain, valley, field, sea, river, fountain, or wheresoever you may be, and show yourselves unto me visibly here before me in this crystal stone, in your own proper shape and glory, speaking with a clear and intelligible voice, such as I may understand, and that you answer and fulfill, that I shall ask, command, or require of you.

The page continues: "When they appear, salute them:"

We are come, O happy spirit, at thy command, but more for the name of the Great God than for thy call, and we are ready to perform thy will according to our office. What wouldst thou?
