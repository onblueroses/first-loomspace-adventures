 “O Lord God who art seated above the Cherubim and Seraphim and who lookest upon the earth like a giant; who hast formed man from the dust of the earth and hast from his rib taken a woman whom thou hast endowed with thy most holy blessing, grant me through the true pentacle of Solomon that she whose name and sign is here written may come unto me full of love and affection. And grant that she may grant me perfect enjoyment of her favors, that she may open her door to me, that she may listen and hear my words and be submissive to my desires, that she may love me and come to me according to my wish and will, and grant that I may obtain all this from her without bodily injury from any man, woman or spirit!”

The instructions for using the talisman require that it be written in virgin parchment with the blood of a bat or a dove; and that it be buried at the door of the woman desired. Then, when she steps over it, she will “burn with love” for the operator.

The Hebrew names are those of angels, or of God. The non-Hebrew names are those of demons.
