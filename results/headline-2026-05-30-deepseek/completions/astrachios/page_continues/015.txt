The Goetia, or Lesser Key of Solomon, contains a grimoire called the Ars Goetia, which lists the names and seals of seventy-two demons, along with descriptions of their powers and the procedures for summoning them.\(^{12}\) The Greater Key of Solomon has five books. The first book instructs the magician on how to behave, the proper attire for magic, and the best times to practice magic. The second book explains how to make magic tools and amulets.
