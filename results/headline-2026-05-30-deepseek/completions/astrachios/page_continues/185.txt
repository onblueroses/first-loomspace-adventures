 “When you have finished the first part of the conjuration, say: ‘Come, all you spirits, by the virtue of the Great Adonai!’ Then come they, and appear in the form of a king.”

This is an invocation of “the spirits ruling the planets,” the seven archangels: Michael, Gabriel, Samael, Raphael, Sachiel, Anael, Cassiel. The spirits of the planets are invoked in the names of the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and the angelic names. The conjuration ends with the instruction to speak the words “Come, all you spirits, by the virtue of the Great Adonai!” This is the way to summon the seven planetary spirits, “and they appear in the form of a king.” The conjuration includes three names of God, Adonai, Elohim, Elohi, and the name of the angel Samoel.

The third spirit is “Samael, the spirit of Mars,” and the conjuration is similar. The page is somewhat damaged, but it is easy to see the names: “Astrachios, Asach, Asac, Bedrimulael, Silat, Arabonas, Ierahalem, Ideod
