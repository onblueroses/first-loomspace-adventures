 “I conjure you, Holy, Holy, Holy Angels of God, powerful, glorious, and honored, and I exorcise you in the Name of the Most High God, that you may come quickly, and without delay, from every region and climate of Heaven and Earth, and from every place, visible and invisible, and come to this place, and show yourselves to me here before this circle, in a beautiful and human form, without deformity or tortuosity, and without any terror and fear, but rather with all mildness and humanity.”

The conjuration ends: “And by these Names, and by all other Names of the Living and True God, the Lord Almighty, I conjure and exorcise you, O Angels! by the virtue of these Holy Names of God, that you forthwith appear, and show yourselves plainly unto me, here before this Circle, in a fair human shape, without any violence or hurt, to me, or any other living creature. And that you plainly and fully answer me all those things that I shall ask of you, and that you finally and completely fulfill all my desires, so far as in your power is.
