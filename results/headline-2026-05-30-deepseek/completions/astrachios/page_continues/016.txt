 "And when you have spoken these names, and have made them appear before you, then speak to them in this manner:

I conjure and command you, O spirits, by the power of the highest, and by the virtue of these holy and sacred names, that you consecrate and dedicate this paper to write upon, and that you assist me in the operation I am about to perform, so that it may be perfected, and that I may obtain the power of calling and exorcising spirits."

It is a powerful beginning to a powerful text. It is also a good demonstration of the kind of technique the magician uses to build up the necessary concentration. Forcing yourself to memorize these words, and then to speak them in a prescribed order, brings a certain mental focus. It also brings a certain nervousness and fear into the process. There is a slight possibility that you will do something wrong, that you will attract the attention of some malignant spirit. The magician must overcome the fear and let the words flow. If you can do this, then you are ready for the next step.
