 “O thou great God, the Lord and master of life! And thou, great God, his son! And ye, holy angels! In the name of the great Jehovah, I adjure that thou immediately appear, and show thyself unto me, here before this circle, in a fair human shape, without any deformity or tortuosity.”

It is a ritual of invocation, written in blood. The page bears the signature of the magician: “JOHN DEE.”

The page is real. It is in the collection of the British Museum, catalogued as Sloane MS 3188, folio 84. The ritual was performed by the Elizabethan mathematician, astronomer, astrologer, and magician John Dee, who was a favorite of Queen Elizabeth. It is part of a larger magical manuscript called The Book of Enoch, dictated to Dee by angels. This particular invocation is meant to summon the angel Uriel, “the spirit of light.”
