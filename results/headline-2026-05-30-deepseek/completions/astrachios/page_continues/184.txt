The square of Saturn is composed of a series of three numbers in each row, each column, and each diagonal adding to 15. The entire square sums to 45. Saturn is attributed to the Sephirah Binah on the Tree of Life, whose number is 3. The squares of the planets are constructed from the squares of numbers attributed to their Sephiroth. In this case, Saturn is attributed to Binah (Understanding), whose number is 3. The magic square of Saturn is composed of nine smaller squares arranged three across and three down. Saturn's number is 45, the sum of all numbers from 1 to 9.
