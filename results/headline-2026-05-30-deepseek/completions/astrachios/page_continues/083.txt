 ‘This is the true way, and there is none other. To know this, and to use it, is to have power in the four quarters of the Earth, and to be feared in hell. The names of power must be learned by heart, and they must never be written down nor spoken aloud, save in the performance of the rite. The magician must be pure in heart, and his purpose must be untainted by any thought of personal gain or of injury to others.’

‘What does it mean?’ I asked.

‘It means,’ said Mr Dwerryhouse, ‘that you have in your hand a most potent spell, the use of which would give you power over the legions of the damned.’

‘Power over devils?’

‘Yes. But to use it you would have to be without fear and without sin.’

‘Do you know anyone like that?’

‘No,’ said Mr Dwerryhouse. ‘But the point is academic because the incantation is incomplete.’

‘Incomplete?’
