 “I call upon you, holy, blessed, and glorious intelligences of the stars; and by these holy names do I invoke you so that you will be present and visible to me.”

Here the magician uses names for the gods in the guise of stars, the same deities who are invoked in the Abramelin ritual. The names are obscure and may be from Hebrew or other Semitic languages. It is not necessary to know their meaning—the names themselves have power.

The magician continues: “And I invoke you also by him who said the word and it was done, and by these holy names: Agla, On, Tetragrammaton, and by all other names of the same Lord God, the holy and true.”

The magician then prays to God: “O Lord God, who created all things and commanded them to obey you by your only begotten son, Jesus Christ, grant that I may accomplish my will and desire through these holy angels and by these holy names, as you have given power to Solomon to bind and loose all things in heaven, on earth, and under the earth.”
