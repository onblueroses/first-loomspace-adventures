 “Praise God. Who reigneth above all heavens. The Beginning and the End, Alpha and Omega.”

The final page of the manuscript has a number of strange symbols and magical names, including the name of the demon Baal. Beneath this are written the words, “The End of the Talisman of Raphael.”

The manuscript is owned by the British Museum.

TALISMAN OF SATURN

The Talisman of Saturn is a talisman described in the grimoire The Greater Key of Solomon. This talisman is used for performing invocations to Saturn.

It is a silver disc, two inches in diameter, with a black edge. The disc bears a pentagram and the magical characters of Saturn.

The pentagram is drawn inside a circle, and the magical characters are written around the outer edge of the disc.

The talisman is consecrated on the day and in the hour of Saturn. It is fumigated with incense composed of alum, saffron, and scammony mixed together.

The magician wears the talisman during invocations to Saturn.

Mathers, S. Lidd
