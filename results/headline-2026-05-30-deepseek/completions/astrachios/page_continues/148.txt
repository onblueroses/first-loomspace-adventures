The next section is entitled: 'To call forth spirits from the infernal regions.' The Magician is required to draw a magic circle and burn incense and sprinkle holy water around the place of working. He must then trace a triangle with the point of his sword and recite the following conjuration: 'I conjure you, O spirit, by all the names of God, and by all his marvellous works, and by these terrible and wonderful names: Agla, On, Tetragrammaton, Adonai, Elohim, El, Eheie Asher Eheie, Jah, Jehovah, Shaddai, Lord God Most High, that you come quickly without delay to answer my demand, and that you come visibly, affably and without any deformity or tortuosity, to answer my questions.
