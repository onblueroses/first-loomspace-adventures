 "Then say: 'I conjure you, O Sun, by all the aforesaid names, that you aid me and bring into effect that which I desire.'" And then the magician is to recite a prayer:

"O God, who has created the heavens and the earth, and all things that are therein, for the praise and glory of Thy name; who hast given to the Sun power to produce the day, and to the Moon power to produce the night; and hast vouchsafed unto us the Sun and the Moon and the other Stars, that they may rule over the day and over the night; I pray Thee that Thou will give unto them power, and that they may bring into effect that which I desire through Thy strength and power. Amen."

And then the magician is to recite the Psalm "O Lord, Our Lord, how wonderful is Thy Name in all the Earth!" and the Lord's Prayer.
