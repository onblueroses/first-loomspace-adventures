 “O King Amaymon, I conjure thee and I command thee by Him who spake and it was done; and by these holy names: Adonai, Tetragrammaton, Jehovah, Jod, He, Vau, He, Aratron, Jah, Eloim, Eloi, El, Elohi, Elohim, Sadai, Sabaoth, that thou appear to me in this mirror, in a beautiful and human form, without hurt or injury to me or to my fellows; and tell and answer truthfully to all that I shall ask of thee; and show and do all that I shall ask of thee; and show and do all that I shall command thee, so long as I shall have the grace of God, Creator of all things.”

The reader is then to say:

“Come, then, O King Amaymon, without delay, from whatever part of the world thou mayest be, and make rational answers unto all things that I shall ask of thee; and come peaceably, visibly, and without delay, manifesting that which I shall desire, for thou art conjured by the name of the Everliving and true God, Helioren, Tam, Iah, Jod,
