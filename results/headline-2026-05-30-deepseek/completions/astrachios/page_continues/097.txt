 “or you may call Spirits from the four parts of the world.” Each of the four directions has a list of names, which include angels and demons. They are given in the same order as in the Fourth Book, but here the lists are longer. There are also four names for the four parts of the world (i.e., the four winds).

The names for the East are: Oriens, Paymon, Egyn, Amaymon. The names for the West are: Amaymon, Oriens, Paymon, Egyn. The names for the South are: Paymon, Oriens, Amaymon, Egyn. The names for the North are: Egyn, Amaymon, Oriens, Paymon.

The book then gives the names of the spirits that serve under each of these four chiefs. The text says that these spirits are of the nature of their chiefs and can be invoked in the same way.

The spirits of the East are: Bael, Aym, Corson, Gamor.

The spirits of the West are: Egym, Raym, Abaal, Bufal.

The spirits of the South are: Aym, Coryn, Gamyor, Labal.
