 "Make sure that the spirits are constrained by the power of God to appear before you in a beautiful form and to obey you in all things. For this purpose make the pentacle of Solomon in virgin parchment with the blood of a bat. Write with your own blood the names that are on the pentacle, and fumigate it with good incense."

The words of the conjuration are as follows: "I conjure you, O spirit N, by the living God, the true God, the holy God, by God who spoke and it was done, by the holy and mysterious names Agla, On, Tetragrammaton, and by the ineffable names Primeumaton, Anaphaxeton, and by all the other names of the Most High, that you appear to me in a beautiful human form without noise and without deformity, and that you answer truthfully all questions I ask."

The spirits are to be dismissed with the following words: "I thank you for having appeared and having satisfied my demands, and now you may depart in peace to your orders, and let there always be peace between me and you.
