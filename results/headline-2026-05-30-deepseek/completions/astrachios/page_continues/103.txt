The names for the four quarters, as given by Mathers (1981: 76-78), are:

East: Raphael, Seraph, Cassiel, Sachiel.

South: Michael, Haniel, Raphael, Gabriel.

West: Gabriel, Michael, Haniel, Uriel.

North: Uriel, Gabriel, Michael, Raphael.

The names for the four quarters, as given by Barrett (1989: 80-83), are:

East: Raphael, Malchidael, Asmodel, Ambriel.

South: Michael, Verchiel, Samael, Uriel.

West: Gabriel, Michael, Sabriel, Arcan.

North: Uriel, Aiel, Aniel, Requiel.

The names for the four quarters, as given by Agrippa (1993: 384), are:

East: Michael, Malchidael, Asmodel, Ambriel.

South: Samael, Verchiel, Samael, Uriel.

West: Gabriel, Michael, Sabriel, Arcan.

North: Uriel, Aiel, Aniel, Requiel.
