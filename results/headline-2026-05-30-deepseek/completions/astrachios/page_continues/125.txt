 “Then say: ‘I conjure and confirm upon you, ye strong and holy angels, by the names recited above, that you labor for me and fulfill my petition, etc.’” This is the followed by a list of “the names of the angels that are in the First Heaven” (which we will get to later) and a list of “the angels that are set over the days of the week” (which we will also get to later).

What are we to make of all this? It’s a lot of arcane, bizarre-sounding names. But there is a method to the madness.

Let’s break it down.

The first part—“I conjure you, ye Angels of God, by these holy names”—is a pretty standard conjuration. It’s an opening salvo. It’s saying: “I’m going to command you, angels, by using these names.”
