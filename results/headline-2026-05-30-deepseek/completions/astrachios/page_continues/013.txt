The conjuration begins: “I conjure you, O spirit Astaroth, by the words and power of the living God, by Him who spoke and all things were made, by the sacred and terrible names AGLA, ON, TETRAGRAMMATON, JEHOVAH, ARIEL, JEHOVAH, ELOHIM, SABAOTH, EL, SHADDAI, Lord God of hosts, who created the heaven, earth, and hell, and all that is in them, who has measured the sand of the sea, and has numbered the stars, and knows the number of the grains of sand on the earth, and of the drops of water in the sea, and of the leaves and grass in the fields; by all these sacred names and by the others which are written in the Book of Life, and by the virtue of the Most High, I conjure you to appear at once in human form, fair and agreeable, without any noise or smell, and to answer truly to all my demands, without any equivocation or mental reservation, without injury to my body or soul, or to any other creature.
