 “O Lord, I beseech thee, let thy Holy Spirit descend upon these waters, and fill them with the power of thy grace, so that whatsoever shall be sprinkled therewith, may be freed from all uncleanness and delivered from all influence of evil spirits; through our Lord Jesus Christ, Amen.”

The magical water is called water of lustration. It is sprinkled on the ground and objects to cleanse them. The water is used to cleanse the temple and the ritual circle, as well as the magician. It is also sprinkled on the ground to consecrate it. The magician should sprinkle the water over the altar and the circle before performing the ritual.

### Consecration of the Salt

The salt is consecrated by tracing the sign of the cross over it and saying:

I bless this creature of salt; may the virtue of the blessing of the Most High enter into it. In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, Amen.

The Lord be with you.
