 ‘O Lord God, holy Father, Almighty, incomprehensible, who dwellest in light unapproachable, before whom stand thousand thousands and ten thousand times ten thousand, the orders of the holy angels and archangels: I, though unworthy and without merit, in the name of Jesus Christ thy only begotten Son, and trusting only in his mercy, presume to entreat thy Majesty, and to invoke thee with all my heart and all the affection of my mind, by these most holy names which we know to be pleasing unto thee, and to be set forth in thy holy Scriptures, and which thy most holy and glorious angels in heaven do use in their invocation of thee: Tetragrammaton, Adonai, Elohim, El, Eheie, Yah, Jehovah, Sadai, Elohi, Gever, Elion, Zebaoth, Jah, Teutatis, Eserehie, Oriston, Adonai, Amioram.’
