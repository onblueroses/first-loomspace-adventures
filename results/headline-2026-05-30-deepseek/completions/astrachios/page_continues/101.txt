 “O Thou great God, the Lord and Master of the Hosts of Heaven, I, being regenerated and born again, and created after Thine Image and Similitude, do most humbly beseech Thee to endue me with the power of Thy Holy Spirit.” The next page continues:

I am he that is called the All-Powerful, the Ruler of the Sun, the Light of the World, the Fountain of Life, the Resurrection of the Dead. I am He that hath the Keys of Death and Hell. I am He that sitteth on the Holy Mount, and am glorified of God, even the Father. I am He that shall come in great power and glory, with the Holy Angels, to execute judgment upon the Earth.

Then follows the “Key of the Mysteries”:

I am Alpha and Omega, the First and the Last. I am He that liveth and was dead and behold! I am alive for evermore. I am He that knoweth all things, and all things are present with Me. I am the Life, the Truth, and the Way; no man cometh unto the Father but by Me.
