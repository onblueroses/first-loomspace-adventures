 “O angels, be present, for I have called you in the name of the supreme and tremendous God, to whom every knee bows down, both of things in heaven and things on earth and things under the earth; before whom every spirit praises and trembles; whom all ye obey; whose dwelling is in the inaccessible light; who has granted unto us, through his only begotten son, Jesus Christ, to make known the way of health and salvation, to whom be glory forever. Amen.”

Then the demons are to be commanded thus:
