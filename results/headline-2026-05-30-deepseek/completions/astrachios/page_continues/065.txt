 “Note: In the manuscript the names are written in the margin. I have placed them in the text and underlined them. Some of them are not clearly written, and may be incorrectly deciphered.” The note is Crowley’s. The manuscript he refers to is Harley 6482, which I have examined at the British Library. The names are indeed in the margin, but Crowley got them wrong. Here are the names as they are in Harley 6482:

Astrachios, Asac, Acra, Bedrimuliel, Silat, Arabonas, Irehalem, Ideodoc, Archarzel, Blautel, Baracata, Adonai, Elohim, Emagro, Abragateh, Samoel, Geburahel, Cadato, Era, Elohi, Achsah, Ebmisha, Imachadel, Daniel, Dama, Elamos, Izachel, Bael, Scirlin; Genium Domos.

The differences are not great, but they are important. For example, Crowley has “Abragateh,” but the manuscript reads “Abragateh.” He has “Achsah,” but the manuscript reads “Achsah.” He
