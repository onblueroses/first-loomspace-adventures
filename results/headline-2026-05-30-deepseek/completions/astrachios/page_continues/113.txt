 ‘O Lord, hear my prayer; and let my cry come before thee.’ This is followed by a prayer to the divine names. Then: ‘O Lord, incline not thine heart to the evil words, to make excuse in sins. Let not the demon of Satan stand at my right hand, nor the enemy in my way. Let not the demon confound my words, and let not the darkness cover my heart. Cast out from my heart evil thoughts, and let not the evil will of the flesh lead me astray. But do thou, O Lord my God, have mercy on me, for thy holy name’s sake, which is blessed for ever and ever. Amen.’

The magician then holds up his hands and says: ‘Blessed art thou, O Lord God, King of the Universe, who sanctified thy great and holy name, and revealed it unto thy people, and hast given unto us life and favour and mercy, and hast caused us to know thy great and holy name; and thou hast taught us to call upon thy great name, that it may help us, and hast revealed unto us the name of thy glory. And blessed be thy name, O Lord our God, for ever and ever. Amen.’
