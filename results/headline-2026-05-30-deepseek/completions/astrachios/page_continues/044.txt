 “O God, who art seated above the heavens, who beholdest the abysses beneath thy feet, who ridest upon the wings of the wind, whose breath is the tempest, whose ministering spirits are flames of fire; thou who art the Lord of unnumbered worlds, thou whose face is as the brightness of the lightning, and whose voice is as the voice of many waters.”

Then there’s a prayer: “O Lord God of Hosts, who hast created all things, who hast fixed the earth upon the floods, who hast made the firmament to be a covering for man, who hast given a law unto nature, and hast established the poles of the earth for ever; O God of gods, the living God, who art powerful and strong, who art mighty and terrible; O God of Hosts, God of Israel, hear my prayer, and grant unto me the grace of thy Holy Spirit.”

Then come the names: “Adonai, El, Elohi, Elion, Zebaoth, Elim, Iah, Tetragrammaton, Sadai.”
