 ‘I conjure and exorcise you, angels of wickedness, by the powers of the holy angels; that in this hour you go to each man and woman to whom I wish, and that you bring her to me, and that she fulfill my will in everything without any delay, and that she not be able to rest in any place nor have peace of mind until she comes to me and fulfills my will.’ The book instructs the magician to speak this invocation into the ear of a dove, then to release the bird. The dove will then fly to the object of desire, and ‘will coo into her ear, and she will be tormented with love for you.’” (p. 17)

The book also includes a curse to cause a person’s death, and “the conjuration of a dead man, in order to obtain information from him.” (p. 17)

Dr. Kieckhefer notes that the Munich handbook contains many different sorts of magic, including love magic, magic to win favor, to obtain treasure, and to cause harm. The book also includes instructions for making a magic ring, which would render the wearer “invisible and invulnerable.” (p. 17)
