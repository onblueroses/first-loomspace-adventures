 "Then the spirits of the air will appear, and you will see them coming in a great cloud, and when they have arrived they will stand around the circle as thick as small rain, and when you see them, say: 'Behold, your king, your lord, Adonai, Elohim, Sabaoth.'" This is followed by a conjuration to the angels and a prayer to God to send Raphael to bring the ring, which is to be used to bind the spirit. The ring is then described: it is to be made of iron, with the names Michael, Raphael, Gabriel, Uriel, and Adonai inscribed on it.

A short version of the procedure is also given: "Make a fire of consecrated wood, and cast into it three spoonsful of consecrated salt, and say: 'I exorcise you, O creature of fire, by him through whom all things were made,' etc. And then recite the conjurations, and the spirits will appear." The next paragraph states that the spirits are of the nature of air, water, and earth, and that they appear in different forms: sometimes like soldiers, sometimes like kings, and sometimes like beasts.
