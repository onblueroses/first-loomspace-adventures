 “O Lord, I beseech thee to grant me the power to dispose and appoint all kinds of spirits which fly through the air, and that they may yield and obediently fulfill my will. Amen.”

This particular page is in Hebrew, but the rest of the book is in Latin. The next page reads:

The following oration must be said in the day and hour of the sun, the sun being in the sign of Leo, in gold letters on a plate of gold, and wearing it, say:

Ancor, Amacor, Amides, Theodonias, Anitor, by the merits of thy angel, O Lord, I will put on the garments of salvation, that this which I desire I may bring to pass, through thee, O most holy Adonai, whose kingdom endureth for ever and ever. Amen.

The manuscript also contains prayers, lists of angels, and spells to ward off evil and cure certain ailments. There is a spell to “bind thieves,” in which the names of the thieves are to be written on a plate of lead, along with the names of some of the angels.
