 “O vos omnes, ministri sedis infernalis, adestote cito cum omnibus legionibus vestris, et parate vos ad obedendum mihi N. qui sum servus potentissimi Domini Tetragrammaton, cui omnes creaturae obediunt, et est imperator potens super omnes potentes, cui vos omnes obedire debetis, et timere eum qui vobis inscribitur, et in cordibus vestris portatis. Venite ergo, venite, et obedite mandatis meis.”

Again we encounter the idea of the magical triangle as a place of imprisonment, as well as the concept of the magician’s circle as a place of protection. The names listed are mostly those of angels and demons. The Tetragrammaton is the four-letter name of God (YHVH), and its power is called upon to compel the spirits. The idea of the magical circle and triangle is also found in the Key of Solomon.
