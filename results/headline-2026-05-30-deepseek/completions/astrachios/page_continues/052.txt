 "The same for the day or the hour, the month, the year." It seems that these names must be repeated for the day, hour, month, and year in which the invocation is performed.

The second page reads:

Put the figure of the thing demanded in the center of a circle with the names around it, and in the four corners these names: Jehova in the East, Adonai in the West, El in the South, Agla in the North.

The third page is inscribed:

The prayer: O God, powerful and merciful, Thou who desirest not the death of a sinner, but that he turn from his wickedness and live, give me, a miserable sinner, who standeth before Thy divine majesty, grace and power to evoke the spirit N, that he may give true and faithful answer, so that I may accomplish my will and desire, through our Lord Jesus Christ, Thy Son, who liveth and reigneth with Thee in the unity of the Holy Ghost, God, for ever and ever. Amen.
