 "O Lord God of Hosts, Holy and Mighty and Immortal, hear my prayer and grant me power and fortitude to complete this undertaking; send from thy holy and eternal habitation thy great Archangel Michael, to aid and protect me from all evil and danger; give him command, O Lord, to assist me in this my work. In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. Amen."

Then we are told to recite Psalm 102. That done, we read: "O Lord God, Holy Father, Almighty and Merciful, who hast created all things, who knowest all things and can do all things, from whom nothing is hidden, to whom nothing is impossible; thou who knowest that we perform not these ceremonies to tempt thy power, but that we may penetrate the knowledge of hidden things, I beseech thee to have the goodness to command thy spirits to send me the spirit N., that I may ask of him the things which I desire to know; through our Lord Jesus Christ thy Son, who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Ghost, one God for ever and ever. Amen."
