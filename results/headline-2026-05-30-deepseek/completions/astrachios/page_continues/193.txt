There are many variations on the theme, but they all share similar features. The magician is taken to the entrance of the Otherworld, sometimes in the form of a cave, sometimes as a tunnel, sometimes as a gate in the air. He or she is then led through the entrance and into the Otherworld itself.

These are ancient techniques, and they are still used today. The technique of the gate is one of the most important in magical practice. The gate is used to travel to different worlds, to contact spirits, to travel backward and forward in time, to heal the sick, and to do many other things.
