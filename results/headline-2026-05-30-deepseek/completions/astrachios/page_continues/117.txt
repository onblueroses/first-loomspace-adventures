 “O angels of the Orient, Occident, South, and North, we call you to us in the name of your king, Tetragrammaton, who has created the heaven, the earth, hell, and all that is in them.” The angels of the four quarters are called upon, and then the angel of the day of the week, the hour of the day, and the sign of the zodiac. The conjuration continues:

I conjure you, angels, by the great living God, in whose hands are all creatures, to come to us without delay, and without any evil intention, to fulfill our orders, to appear visibly in a beautiful human form, without noise and without terror. And to bring to us the virtue of herbs and stones, to reveal all things clearly, to give us power over all officials, and to fulfill all our requests.

And then the spirit is commanded: “By the most holy names of God, Tetragrammaton, Adonai, Elohim, Agla, come to us, fulfill our orders, and accomplish our petitions.”
