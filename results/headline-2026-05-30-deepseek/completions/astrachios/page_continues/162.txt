The Conjuration of the Sword:

I conjure thee, O Sword of Steel, by God the Father Almighty, by the virtue of the heavens, of the stars, of the air; by the virtue of the angels, of the archangels; by the virtue of the cherubim; by the king of the east; by the seal of Adonai; by the name Aglay which Moses invoked, and by the name

Anexton which Aaron invoked; and by the name Zebaoth which Joshua invoked, and by the name Emmanuel which the three children invoked in the fiery furnace; and by the name Alpha and Omega which Daniel invoked, and destroyed Bel, and slew the dragon; and by the other great and ineffable names, Tetragrammaton, Primeumaton, Anaphaxeton, and Inessenfatoal; and by the name of God El, strong and wonderful; and by the name On, which Moses named, and the earth opened and swallowed up Corah, Dathan, and Abiram
