 "O you who wander in the shadows and who hold the keys to the gates of the Kingdom of Shadows, open for me the gates of the knowledge of the past, of the present and of the future." The "keys" are the names of demons and angels, and the "gates" are the "gates of time," the present, past, and future.

A list of demons follows, whose names are "keys" to the gates. A magical circle is drawn on the ground, with a triangle inside it. A new-born child is placed in the triangle, and the magician stands in the circle, holding a hazel wand. The magician invokes the demons, who will appear and answer questions about the past, present, and future.

The manuscript is attributed to Solomon. It was published in 1721 in the _Grimoire du Pape Honorius_ (Grimoire of Pope Honorius) and reprinted in 1800. It was translated into English by Arthur Edward Waite in his _Book of Black Magic and Pacts_ (1898). See also HONORIUS, GRIMOIRE OF POPE; SOLOMON.
