 "O all ye angels of the air, stand forth, come hither from the east, south, west, and north; I conjure and call you forth to visible appearance, in and with your proper motion, sound, colour, and shape, each of you severally, and to appear visibly here before me, in this crystal stone, in your proper forms and shapes."

The page ends: "And that without any tarrying, delay, or deformity, unto the glory of God Almighty, who created and formed you in his power and wisdom."

I can hear someone breathing. The breathing sounds like the fluttering of a dove's wings.

"Now," says Dee, "I call upon you, Angel Uriel, Archangel of the North, Keeper of the Gates of Death, Master of the Element of Earth, Angel of the Sanctum, Angel of the Threshold, Angel of the Vortex, Angel of the Heart of the Earth, Angel of the Mystery of the Sacred Stone, Angel of the Great Work, Angel of the Philosopher's Stone."
