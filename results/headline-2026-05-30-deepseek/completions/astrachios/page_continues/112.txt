The Goetia of Dr. Rudd

This is the earliest known manuscript of the Goetia, dating from 1570 (now in the British Library, Sloane MS. 3648). The original Latin manuscript was apparently copied in the hand of Dr. Thomas Rudd (1583-1656), a practicing magician and military engineer, who was a student of John Dee. The manuscript contains additional material, such as a hierarchy of the seventy-two spirits, sigils of the seventy-two spirits, and twenty-five seals of the spirits, as well as invocations to angels and archangels. Rudd also wrote a commentary on Dee's Enochian system. The Sloane manuscript includes a later translation of the Goetia into English (Sloane MS. 3824).
