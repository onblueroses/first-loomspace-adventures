 “And the spirits of the air, the spirits of the earth, the spirits of the water and fire, and the spirits under the earth, and of the night and day, and the spirits of all the places under heaven, and spirits of the air, obey us! For every spirit desires to be made a God, and we have the power to make them Gods. For I have been inspired by you, the true God.”

The remainder of the page contains the names of the archangels and their angels: Raphael, Michael, Gabriel, Uriel, Barachiel, Jehudiel, Sealiel; and the angels of the zodiac: Malchidael, Asmodel, Ambriel, Muriel, Verchiel, Hamaliel, Zuriel, Barbiel, Advachiel, Hanael, Gabriel, and Barchiel.

The second page begins with the names of the archangels of the hours: Michael, Anael, Raphael, Gabriel, Cassiel, Sachiel, Samael, Michael, Anael, Raphael, Gabriel, and Cassiel. These are followed by the names of the angels of the days: Raphael, Gabriel, Samael, Michael, Sachiel, Anael, and Cassiel.
