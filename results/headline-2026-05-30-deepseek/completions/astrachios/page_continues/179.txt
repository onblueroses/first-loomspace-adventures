 “I conjure you, angels of God, and powerful ministers, whom God ordained at the beginning for the ministry of his mercy. I conjure you by the ineffable and great name of God, who created you and all creatures from nothing. I conjure you by the name of the living and true God, who rules heaven and earth and hell, and all things that are in them. I conjure you by the immense mercy of God. I conjure you by the power of God. I conjure you by the wisdom of God. I conjure you by the blood of Jesus Christ, which he shed for us sinners. I conjure you by his sacred incarnation and nativity. I conjure you by his baptism. I conjure you by his most holy cross and passion. I conjure you by his resurrection and ascension. I conjure you by the coming of the Holy Spirit, the Paraclete. I conjure you by the glorious virgin Mary, mother of God. I conjure you by the immense day of judgment. I conjure you by the most holy angels and archangels. I conjure you by the most holy patriarchs and prophets. I conjure you by the most holy apostles and evangelists. I
