 “Then say: ‘O God, who has created all things, and has given unto man the power to rule over them, I beseech Thee, O Lord God, that these holy names may give me power and strength over the Spirits.’” Then one is to recite the prayer of the Sibyls.

The spirits of the Moon are called in the same manner, but with the following names: “Anabona, Castania, Aflasin, Tafac, Aben, Abin, Malcha, Emanuel, Sizagamor, Cabuc, Padiel, Butador, Salemo, Calvo, Cadas, Heles, Isparin, Igur, Hor, El, Elion, Aniel, Zaziel, Hazaiel, Anaq, Jerascha, Mel, Ya, Hy, Ha, Haa, Haaa, Hu, Hii, Hiii.”
