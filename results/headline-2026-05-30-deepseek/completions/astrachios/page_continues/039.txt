The more formal names of the Archangels (Michael, Gabriel, Raphael, and Uriel) are also used, but the other names are obviously a mixture of Greek, Latin, and Hebrew words. Some are the names of angels or demons, others are magical words, and still others are incantations or phrases used to “open” the way to the powers that can grant requests.

The names of the angels in the Keys are taken from the Kabbalah and other Jewish writings. In the Jewish tradition, there are many angels, and each one has a name ending in “-el,” which means “of God.” The names of the angels include Metatron, who is considered to be the highest angel; Raziel, who is the angel of mysteries; and others like Anael, Cassiel, Sachiel, Samael, and Zamael.
