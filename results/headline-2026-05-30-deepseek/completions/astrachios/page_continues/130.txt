 “Conjuro te spiritum per Deum vivum, per Deum verum, per Deum sanctum, per Deum qui te creavit ex aquis, et in te posuit potestatem discernendi inter bonum et malum . . .” And so on. The words are said to have been used by King Solomon to invoke spirits.

A note in the manuscript says that “in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ” the conjuration will work against all evil spirits, who will be forced to appear and obey the conjurer.

The manuscript contains other conjurations and prayers, as well as lists of angels and demons, and magical signs. It was bound in the mid-sixteenth century and belonged to a monk at the abbey of St Augustine in Canterbury.
