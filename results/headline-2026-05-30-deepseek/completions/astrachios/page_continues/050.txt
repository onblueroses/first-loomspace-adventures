 "O you angels who are written above, and you archangels who are written, seek out and bring before me the spirit N., who lives in such a place, and constrain him by your powers and by the virtue of your omnipotence, to come to me without delay, and without any harm to me or any other creature, and without causing me any terror, but let him come peacefully and visibly, in a comely human form, answering faithfully and without any guile all the questions I ask of him." Then, "I conjure you anew, N., by all the names written above, and by their virtue and power, that you appear here quickly and without delay, in human form, without any terror, and without doing any harm to me or to any other creature."

The magician then repeats the conjuration, substituting the names of God:
