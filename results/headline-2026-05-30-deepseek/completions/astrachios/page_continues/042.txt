 'O great Tetragrammaton, who is always God, always hath been, and always shall be.' The spirits are adjured 'by the virtue of the holy name Tetragrammaton, to appear in a fair human shape, without any deformity or tortuosity, and to be clothed with a purple robe, and a yellow mantle over it.' The magician is also instructed to say: 'I conjure ye by the name Tetragrammaton Elohim, which expresses his essence which is, who is, and shall be, the help of all generations, who created heaven and earth, and in that word commanded the whole host of heaven; and by the name of Elohim Gibor, which is the mighty God, who punisheth the wicked, and rewardeth the righteous; and by the name of Eloah, and by the name of Elohim Tzabaoth; and by the name of Elion; and by the name of Ehveh Asher Ehveh; and by the name of Adonai Tzabaoth; and by the name of Yah; and by the name of Elohim, who appeared to Moses in the bush; and by the name of Elohim who brought the children
