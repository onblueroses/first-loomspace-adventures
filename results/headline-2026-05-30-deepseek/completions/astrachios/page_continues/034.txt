 “Then say: ‘O you who are set over this day, let your angel, Ruach Chaschmalim, who rules this day, come forth and show yourself, and give me true answers to whatsoever I may ask.’”

It’s an interesting process. The magician summons angels and spirits. He or she will be instructed to look for the angel’s “signature”—a flash of light, a scent, the appearance of a symbol, a change in the room’s temperature, etc. Then the angel will be questioned. If the angel is rebellious, the magician will summon the archangel Michael to chastise him.

The book of magic also contains spells for invisibility, transportation, and the ability to fly.

We’ll take a look at how Crowley used the Abramelin text. But first, let’s get to know the man a bit.
