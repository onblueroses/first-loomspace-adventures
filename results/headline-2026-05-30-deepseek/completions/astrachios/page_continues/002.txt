 “I conjure you, O Spirit, by all the names that are said, that you appear to me in a beautiful human form without deformity, and without injury. And I conjure you by the names Adonai, El, Elohim, Elohe, Zebaoth, Elion, Eserehie, Jah, Tetragrammaton, Sadai, that you come to me quickly wherever I may be, and that you bring with you all the other spirits who are under your rule and power.”

This is only a small portion of the text, but it should give you an idea of what the magician is trying to accomplish. He wants to make the spirit appear so that he can ask it questions. The spirit is supposed to answer truthfully. The magician is also supposed to command the spirit to do his will, but the text implies that the spirit will only do so if it is the will of God. In other words, the magician is not supposed to command the spirit to do evil.
