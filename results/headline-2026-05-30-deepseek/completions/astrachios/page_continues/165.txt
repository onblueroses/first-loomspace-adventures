 “I call upon you, Angels of God, mighty and glorious, and I beseech you by Him who spoke and it was done, to whom all creatures are obedient, that you go and bring into my presence N. daughter of N.”

The name of the desired girl and her mother’s name are filled in. “Bring her to me, let her come to me joyfully and without delay, let her be obedient to me in all things, and let her not be absent from me for a single hour. And if she delays in coming, let her be tortured by your power, and let her be tormented by your scourges, and let her feel your power, ye mighty and powerful angels, whose names are written above.”

It ends: “I conjure you by all the names of God, that you do for me without delay all that I have asked. Amen.”

The spirit is to be sent to her three times, once in the morning, once at noon, and once in the evening.

I don’t know whether it works. I’ve never tried it.
