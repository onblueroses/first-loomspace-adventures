 “O Lord, deliver me from the temptation of the Devil, of the world and the flesh, Amen.” There is a cross and an Amen. Then: “O Lord, deliver me from the temptation of the Devil, of the world and the flesh, Amen. Amen.”

I am having trouble breathing. I’m breathing through my mouth, and my throat is dry.

The next page is blank except for a cross. I turn the page and find another list of words, but these aren’t names. They are Latin.

They are prayers, I think. Or spells.

“Deus Pater Omnipotens, Dominus Caeli et Terrae, Benedicat Nobis. Sancta Virgo Maria, Regina Caeli, Mater Domini Nostri Jesu Christi, Benedicat Nobis.”

I know what that means: “God, Almighty Father, Lord of Heaven and Earth, bless us. Holy Virgin Mary, Queen of Heaven, Mother of Our Lord Jesus Christ, bless us.” But then there’s a list of names, some I recognize and some I don’t. “St. Michael the Archangel, St. Gabriel the Archangel, St. Raphael the Archangel, St. Uriel the Archangel
