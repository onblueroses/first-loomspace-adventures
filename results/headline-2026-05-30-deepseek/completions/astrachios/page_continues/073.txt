 “O God, who art the author of all good things, strengthen, I beseech thee, thy poor servant that he may stand fast, without fear, through this dealing and work. Enlighten, I beseech thee, O Lord, the dark understanding of thy servant, that he may be able with pure heart to comprehend and to know thy angelic visions and wonderful sacraments, strengthen me, O Lord God, that I may without fear behold the angelic armies. Amen.”\(^{25}\)
