 “O Lord God, holy, eternal, and merciful, thou who hast created all things by thy word and hast ordained man to have dominion over the works of thy hands and has put all things under his feet, all sheep and oxen, yea, and the beasts of the field, the fowl of the air and the fish of the sea and whatsoever passeth through the paths of the sea, grant, I beseech thee, that my power may be exalted over [name of fish].” And then the rest of the prayer: “And whatsoever I shall do with it may prosper, through thy most holy name and they only begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ. Amen.”

The conjurations of the fish are the same as those for the birds. And the conjurations for the birds are the same as those for the beasts.
