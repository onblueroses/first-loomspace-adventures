 “O God, you who are great, powerful, terrible, most high, who created all things by your word, who gave unto Solomon this art by which he may command all spirits and bind them; you who created the abyss and who shut it up; you who created the sea and dry land; you who are the God of gods and Lord of lords, Adonai, Elohim, Sabaoth, El, strong and wonderful, El Shaddai, most high God, who are the giver of all good things; I humbly pray that you give unto me the power to command these spirits that I shall call forth by this pentacle which you gave unto Solomon; and that they may be obedient unto me and grant unto me all that I desire.”

This prayer is followed by another: “O Adonai, most powerful El, most strong Agla, most holy On, terrible Tetragrammaton, Ioskel, Anath, Anat, Haza, Haza, Haza, let it be, let it be, let it be, Amen.”
