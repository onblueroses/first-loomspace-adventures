 “I call upon you, holy, powerful, and honored angel, and I command you in the name of the Most High God, Adonai, Tetragrammaton, and all the other names of the one and true God, that you come to me quickly and without delay from wherever you may be, and that you bring to me N. [fill in the name] and that you force her to come to me, wherever she may be, in peace and without harm, and that she submit to my will, and that she not be able to have peace of mind or rest until she comes to me and fulfills my will, without any delay.”

The ritual is concluded with the words: “Fiat, fiat, fiat.”

The final page of the manuscript offers an explanation of the seal of Solomon. It depicts a hexagram surrounded by four pentagrams, each inscribed with the name of one of the four cardinal directions. The hexagram itself contains a pentagram, inside of which is a six-pointed star.

The hexagram is labeled “The Seal of Solomon,” and the pentagram is labeled “The Pentagram of Solomon.” The four cardinal directions are indicated by the words “East,” “West,” “North,” and “South.”
