Amen.” Then it says, “By the great and holy name Jehovah, I command you, in power and virtue to come and show yourselves to us, in a fair and comely shape, and in a mild and gentle manner, as we shall desire you.” The page is to be held in the right hand, and the wand of the art is to be held in the left hand, while the magician speaks.

The magician is to stand inside the triangle, and the triangle is to be drawn with chalk on the floor. The magician is to be dressed in a black robe, with a black cap, and the wand is to be made of elderwood.
