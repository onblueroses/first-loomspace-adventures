 "O you who govern the fourth part of the world, under the great Emperor Caspiel, and are the true and oriental king, Amaymon, I invoke you, and by the powers of the divine names I call you forth to visible appearance: Adonai, El, Elohim, Elohe, Zebaoth, Elion, Escerchie, Jah, Tetragrammaton, Saday."

The conjuration continues with more names, more commands, and more threats. Then, again, the magician must wait for the spirit's response. He must be prepared to hear a noise like a great wind, or the lowing of cattle, or the sound of a lion roaring, or the braying of an ass. He must be prepared to see a terrible serpent or a dragon. He must not be afraid. He must say: "Quit this form and return to thine own, and come before me in human shape, gentle and courteous, or I shall curse thee in the name of the Lord: Tetragrammaton, Elohim, and the other names which are powerful and glorious."
