 “O you who dwell in the shadow of the great god, and who hold the balance and who sit in the seat of judgment, open for me the gates of righteousness. I shall enter and give thanks to Yah.” This is followed by a prayer for protection: “I pray you, O Lord, I adjure you, O God of gods, let me dwell in your shadow. Let me sit on your seat. Let me shine like your seven lights. Let me be like your pupil. Save me from all evil, from every demon, from every idol, from every unclean thing.” The final part is a psalm: “O Lord, my God, I call to you for help; save me from all those who pursue me. Rescue me, lest they tear me apart like a lion, dragging me off with no one to rescue me.”

The “great god” is the sun, and the person praying to be allowed to dwell in the sun’s shadow is asking to be under divine protection. The seven lights are the sun, moon, and five planets known at the time—Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn. The “pupil” is the pupil of the eye, meaning the apple of God’s eye.
