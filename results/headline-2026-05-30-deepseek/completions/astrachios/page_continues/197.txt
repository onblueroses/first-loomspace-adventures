 'Then pray to God devoutly to grant you the power of seeing and knowing the Spirits of the Air, and to let you have them at command. Also that he will make them to be obedient in all things unto you, and to appear visible in a fair and human form when you call them. Then you must proceed to read the conjuration following.'

In that conjuration, which is addressed to the spirits, the magician says:

I conjure you and do exorcise you spirits of the Air by and in the name of him that spake the word and it was done, and by the name of all the glorious Angels, and by all the names of the great and mighty God Jehovah, Zebaoth, Elohim, El, Tetragrammaton, and by all the other great and glorious names of the most mighty God, that you forthwith appear unto me here in this place in a fair and human form, without doing any harm to me or any other creature, and that you answer truly and without any ambiguity all questions that I shall ask you. And that you fulfil and accomplish all my desires so far as in your power lieth, and that you be ready and willing to come at all times when I call you, either
