 "O all ye who govern the fourth legion, I conjure you by him who sits upon the holy throne, and by these holy names, that you cause to come before me N., and that he answer me whatsoever I shall ask him." The conclusion to the invocation includes the words: "And may he be obedient in all things which I shall enjoin upon him, by the virtue of the holy names Adonai, Elohim, Tetragrammaton, and by the name Primeumaton, which Moses invoked, and the earth opened and swallowed up Corah, Dathan, and Abiram."

A more gentle method of calling up spirits is given by the fifteenth-century magician Johannes Trithemius. He advised the would-be magician to go to a secret place and say the following prayer: "O God, the father eternal, ruler of all the spirits, we thy servants, humbly beseech thee to send us thy spirit N., who dwelleth in . . . (here he described the spirit's dwelling place), that he may instruct us in what we desire; and let thy will, O Lord, be done through Jesus Christ thy son."
