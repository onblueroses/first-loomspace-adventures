**The Book of the Sacred Magic of Abramelin the Mage**

The Book of the Sacred Magic of Abramelin the Mage is a sacred text of the occult, and the only known grimoire attributed to Abraham of Worms (c. 1362–c. 1458). The grimoire purports to be a first-hand account of Abraham's encounter with an Egyptian mage named Abramelin (or Abra-Melin), who teaches him a powerful system of magic. The system of magic from this grimoire became popular within the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and continues to have a strong influence on occultism. The book exists in manuscript form in several European libraries, including the Bibliothèque de l'Arsenal in Paris. It was later translated by Samuel Liddell MacGregor Mathers and published in 1900 by John M. Watkins of London. A. E. Waite reviewed it in 1900, and it has been reprinted several times since.
