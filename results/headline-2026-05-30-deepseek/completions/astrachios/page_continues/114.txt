 “Then say: I conjure thee, O Creature of Wax, by the living God, by the true God, by the holy God, who spoke and it was done, and by all the names which are here written, that thou workest for me without deception, and fail me not, but bring NN whom I love before me within three days at the latest.”

The spell is to be spoken three times and then repeated three times: “Then say: I conjure thee, O Creature of Wax, by the great names of God, that thou torment and harass her until she cometh unto me.”

The waxen image is to be pierced with needles in the heart, head, and sides. The conjuration is to be repeated seven times daily for seven days, and each time the image is to be pierced with the needles.

The waxen image is to be hidden in the house of the beloved woman, or under the threshold, or in a place she is likely to pass over.

The book also contains a spell to cause love between man and wife, and other spells to cause sickness or death.

The Sloane manuscript also includes spells to cause hatred and separation between husband and wife, and between lovers.
