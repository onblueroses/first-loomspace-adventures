The text continues: "But if he be stubborne and will not appeare, then show him the seale of the parte of the earth wherein he is, and conjure him by the superior seale, which is this." The conjuration for the superior seal begins: "I conjure thee, thou spirit of whatsoever region thou art, by the Almighty, Immense, and Eternal Lord, from the beginning, and by his incomprehensible name, which is IAH, IAH, IAH, that thou without delay appeare before this circle, in a fair humane shape, or else that thou send thy messenger unto me, that he may doe my will in all things, as they shall be commanded."
