 “O Lord God, Holy Father, omnipotent, eternal, who hast created all things visible and invisible from nothing, who hast placed man in paradise, according to Thy will, and hast given him a commandment, which he transgressed; nevertheless Thou hast promised to send Jesus Christ Thy Son into the world to save mankind; I pray Thee, O Lord God, to send Thy holy angels, that they may attend upon me in this place, and guard me from evil in all my works. In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. Amen.”

There follows a list of the names of the seven angels and the seven days of the week, which angels should be invoked on which days: “On Sunday, the angel Michael, angel of the day. On Monday, Gabriel. On Tuesday, Camael. On Wednesday, Raphael. On Thursday, Sachiel. On Friday, Anael. On Saturday, Cassiel.”
