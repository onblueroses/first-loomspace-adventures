 “O All ye strong and holy names of God! set me up, and come forth to assist me in this operation!”

This ends the summoning. The spirit’s seal is placed on the table, the magician is seated, and the spirit is conjured, according to the conjuration given in the last chapter.

When the spirit appears, the magician says:

“I conjure thee, spirit N., by all the names of God, and by all his holy names which we have spoken and which we have not spoken, that thou dost straightway appear in fair and comely human form, without deformity or tortuosity, and that thou dost speak with a clear and intelligible voice, without any double sense or ambiguity.”

The spirit will appear, and the magician asks:

“What is thy name?”

The spirit will answer: “My name is N.” The magician then asks: “What is thy office?” The spirit will answer: “My office is N.”

The magician asks: “What is thy seal or character?” The spirit answers: “It is this.” The magician says: “Write it in this paper.” The spirit writes it.
