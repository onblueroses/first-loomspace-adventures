The Demon Azael

Azael is a fallen angel who refused to bow to Adam, and taught men to make weapons of war and women to wear cosmetics. He is the "scapegoat" of Leviticus 16:8, 10, 26. He is also Azazel. In the Book of Enoch, Azazel is one of the chiefs of the Grigori (Watchers), a class of fallen angels who married with human women and produced the Nephilim. In the Book of Enoch, Azazel is responsible for teaching people to make weapons and cosmetics, enabling them to behave rebelliously. Azazel is also identified as one of the chiefs of the Se'irim, goat-demons who danced in the wilderness.

In occult lore, Azael is the ninth of the ten Archdemons, the evil counterpart of the Archangels. He is the demon of war, and his counterpart Archangel is Samael. He is also one of the seven planetary demons of the Qliphoth. Azael is sometimes depicted as having three heads: a cat, a man, and a snake. He rides a dragon
