 “O Lord of Lords, Father of all things, Thou who art enthroned upon the Cherubim and Seraphim, Thou who lookest upon the waters...” And so forth.

In the manuscript, the pages are separated by a blank page. The next page begins: “The Conjuration: O Lord, hear my prayer, and let my cry come unto Thee. O Lord God Almighty, who didst give unto thy servant Moses the Tables of Stone written with Thy own finger, Thou who didst give unto Solomon Thy servant wisdom and knowledge, and didst cause him to build a Temple to Thy honour and glory, grant unto me the same wisdom and knowledge, that I may be enabled to enter therein, and behold the wonders thereof.” It continues: “And Thou, O Lord Jesus Christ, Son of the Father, send unto me Thy good angel, to guard and succour me, and to preserve me from all evil...”

The next page is the conjuration of the angel Uriel, who is addressed as the angel of the Sun. This is followed by the conjuration of Michael, then Raphael, Gabriel, then Salathiel, Zadkiel, and Anael.
