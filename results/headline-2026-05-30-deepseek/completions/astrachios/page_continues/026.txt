 “Conjuro te serpens per nomen Dei vivi & per nomen Dei sancti, quibus omnia subduntur, ut tu sine mora venias ad me N. pacifice, visibilis, & non formidabilis, formam gerens puerili, sine omni deformitate & tortuositate, & sine omni venenoso timore ad me N. venias, & meam implens voluntatem in omnibus, & in cunctis que tibi iniunxero, venias cito, cito, cito.” (I conjure you serpent, by the name of the living God and by the name of the Holy God, by whom all things are made subject, that you come to me N. without delay, peacefully, visibly, and not frightfully, taking on a boyish form, without any deformity or crookedness, and without any poisonous fear, and fulfilling my will in all things, and in all that I shall enjoin upon you, come quickly, quickly, quickly.)
