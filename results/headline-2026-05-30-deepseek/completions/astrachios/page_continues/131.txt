 “O God, who art the life of all that live, the light of the faithful, the strength of them that labour, the repose of the blessed dead: I thank thee for the blessings of the past day, and I commit myself to thy keeping during the coming night. Teach me to live that I may dread the grave as little as my bed; teach me to die so that I may have eternal life. In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Ghost. Amen.”

The page is torn across.

The next page is intact. It reads:

“I conjure and command you, O Spirit, N., to appear within this triangle in a fair human shape, without hurt unto me or any other creature, to answer truly and faithfully unto all my requests, that I may receive your true and perfect knowledge, sight, and conversation in whatsoever art, science, or part of learning I shall require of you, so it be unto the glory of God and the safety of my own soul. Amen.”

On the next page, someone has written in a cramped and angry hand:

“I did not ask for this. I did not ask to be born.”

The following page is blank.
