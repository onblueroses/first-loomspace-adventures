 “I conjure and confirm upon you, Angels of God, powerful and holy, and by the particular names of your God that you be obedient unto me, and go and bring unto me N. N., whom I love, so that she shall give me all her love and affection, which she has for another, and she shall turn all her thoughts and desires towards me, and accomplish all my will, as I believe and understand she can and will. And let her not have peace or rest in her spirit, wherever she may be, until she is humbled before me, and fulfill all my desires, as Jesus Christ at the prayers of the Virgin Mary was humbled. Fiat, fiat, fiat. Amen.”\(^{37}\)

The names are a strange mixture of Hebrew and Greek words and some meaningless syllables. The Hebrew words are mostly names of God, such as Elohim, Adonai, Samoel (Samuel?), Daniel, Elohi, Bael (Baal?), and Genium Domos (Lord of hosts?).
