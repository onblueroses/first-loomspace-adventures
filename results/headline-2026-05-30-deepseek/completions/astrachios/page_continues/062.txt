 "O Most holy father, hear my prayer, for this my heart is sorrowful: my soul is heavy; my strength is gone; my understanding is clear no more; my memory is weak, and my thoughts are confused. Show me, O Lord, the path of righteousness and teach me thy ways. Unto thee I lift my soul; unto thee I offer up my hopes. Lead me in thy ways, O Lord, and leave me not. For if I am left to myself, I shall surely fall. Bring me back from the depths of darkness, for I am of the race of Adam, a man of flesh, a descendant of the first formed. From thee, O Lord, cometh salvation. Thou art our praise, our boast, and our song. Thou art our God, our father, our king, our strength, our might, and our power. Thou art our refuge, our savior, our stronghold, and our high tower. Thou art our light, our sun, and our shield. Unto thee be praise, to thy name be glory, to thee be honor and majesty, now and forever. Amen." The names of God are also invoked: "Ieova, Tetragrammaton, Otheos, Is
