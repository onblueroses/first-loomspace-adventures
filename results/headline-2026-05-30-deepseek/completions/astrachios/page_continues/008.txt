But the demons are not the only entities that can be called upon to assist in divination. A number of angels are also listed, including the four angels of the quarters: Raphael, Gabriel, Michael, and Uriel.

The demon Oriens is described as having a great house or palace, and it is here that the scryer can find the four demon kings: Oriens, Paymon, Egyn, and Amaymon. The text cautions that these four should not be called unless absolutely necessary, and then only on a Sunday or during the day. This may indicate that they were considered more powerful, and thus more dangerous, than the other demons.

The text continues with instructions for a scrying ritual involving a crystal set in a ring. This ring is to be placed on the middle finger of the scryer's right hand. A child is to be used as a medium, and the ritual is to be performed in a remote and secret place. The child should be placed before the scryer, and the ring should be placed on the child's finger.
