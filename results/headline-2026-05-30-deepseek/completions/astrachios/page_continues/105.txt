 “Do thou conjure them, O Spirit, Azel, and by the same conjuration conjure also the other spirits which are under thee, that they may come quickly without delay, and show unto us that which we desire, and that without any deception whatsoever.”

And now comes the final threat: “In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, do we command thee, and by the power of these Most Holy Names which we have spoken, do we exorcise thee, and if thou still delayest to appear, we will repeat the Holy Names once more, and also the Conjuration, and will add thereunto the Holy Names of the Ten Angels who preside over the Ten Sephiroth, by the which we are mighty in the power of the Highest; and if thou comest not quickly, we will thunder forth the Great and Mighty Name of God, Tetragrammaton, which who so nameth, without due consecration, is in danger of perdition; and we will write upon the parchment and will burn it in the fire, and will will also bury in the earth certain conjurations; and if thou comest not at the first, we will persevere even unto the end,
