 ‘And say thou: I conjure thee, O creature of wax, by all the aforesaid names, that thou torment, kill, and waste the life of N., and destroy him and his memory from among the living. And let there be a flame and a flame to come forth from thy mouth to burn N., whom I have named in my heart. And let there issue from thy sides the four winds of the world to choke him. And let the four winds enter into his nostrils to destroy him. And let there issue from thy fundament the four floods of fire to consume him. And let the floods enter into his fundament to destroy him.’

The ritual was to be performed in a waxing moon, after sunset and before midnight. It was to be done on a Friday, if possible, but if not, then on a Monday, Tuesday, or Saturday. The magician was to be dressed in black, with a black belt and black shoes.
