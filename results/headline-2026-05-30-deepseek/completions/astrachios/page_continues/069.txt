 “O Lord, hear my prayer and let my cry come unto Thee.” Then there is a prayer beginning: “O Lord God of Hosts, powerful and merciful, give me the strength and the courage to appear before Thy Divine Majesty.” Then the prayer ends: “I beseech Thee to have the goodness to command Thy angels of the celestial sphere to come and take their place in this crystal stone, so that I may see and know them, and that they may satisfy my demands, by Thy holy name Adonai, to whom be honor and glory forever and ever.”

And then: “I conjure thee, O spirit of the planet [Venus], by the great living God, Sovereign Creator of all things, that thou come quickly and peaceably, from whatsoever part of the world thou mayest be in, to answer my demands, and that which I shall ask of thee. Fail not, then, to appear in a comely human form, without any injury to me or to any other creature, and answer faithfully and truly unto all my requests, by the virtue of His holy name Elohim. Amen.”
