 "And when you have repeated these names, you will see spirits coming from the east and the west, from the south and the north, and they will submit to you and say, 'Here we are, ready to obey you. What do you command?' And you will answer them, 'I wish you to obey me, and you will appear to me in a beautiful form, and you will reveal to me the hidden things, and you will obey me in all that I desire, and you will serve me as faithful servants.' And they will obey you."

The text continues with the names of various spirits, their duties, and the proper rituals to summon them. For example, "The spirit named Astaroth appears in the form of a beautiful angel riding a dragon and holding a viper in his right hand. He teaches all sciences, answers all questions truly, and gives power over serpents. He must be invoked on a Wednesday."

Another section describes the "Clavicula Salomonis," or Key of Solomon, a grimoire attributed to King Solomon, who, according to legend, commanded demons with a magical ring. The Clavicula contains instructions for creating magical circles, pentacles, and other tools for summoning and controlling spirits.
