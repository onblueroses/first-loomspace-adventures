 "Draw this circle with the pen of the Art upon the skin of a frog, and say the following words: 'I conjure you, O frog, by the living God, that you receive such virtue that you may be able to go into any place we wish, and make the girl dance and be filled with the desire for copulation.' And you must hold the frog alive in your closed hand until it dies. And when you wish to operate, place it upon the heart of the virgin, and immediately she will feel an itching desire for coitus. And if you wish to experiment, put it in the dove-cote, and all the doves will begin to copulate."

A few pages later, there is another similar charm: "Take a frog which has been generated in a dunghill, and which has been nourished on the dung of a donkey, and which has a white mark upon its head. And you must draw this circle on its skin with the pen of the Art, and say the following words: 'I conjure you, frog, by the living God, that you receive such virtue that you may be able to go into any place we wish, and to make the girl dance and be filled with the desire for copulation.'
