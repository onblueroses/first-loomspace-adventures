 "I conjure you, spirit Berith, by all the names above, and by all other names unpronounceable, and by your character and seal, and by the name of your master, which he has engraved on your heart and ring. I conjure you, Berith, by the seven angels who stand before the throne of God, and by the seven planets, and by the perfumes which are agreeable to you, and by the seal of Basdathea, by which all spirits are compelled, that you appear to me visibly and without delay, in a beautiful human form, and without noise or terror, and that you answer truthfully all questions that I shall ask you. Come, then, Berith, and fulfill my will and my desires."

This is what I did. I repeated the invocation, and I saw the spirit Berith appear. He had the form of a red soldier, riding a red horse, wearing a golden crown. He answered truthfully all questions that I asked him. He granted me the favor of having familiar spirits and the art of using them, and gave me sixty legions of spirits.
