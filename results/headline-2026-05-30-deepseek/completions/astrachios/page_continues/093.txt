 "O God, Holy, Father Almighty, Holy and Unspeakable, who sits on the Cherubim and Seraphim, who sees the depths, send the desired and holy angel, who is called Anael, from your holy and high heaven, that he may come here and tell me what I desire."

The text becomes even more explicit: "O great and holy angel Anael, who are set over the sixth heaven, I conjure you by your holy names, by your office, by your ministry, by your powers, by your virtues, that you come to me quickly without delay, and appear visibly before me, and tell me truthfully and without any ambiguity or deception, what I desire and ask of you. And if you delay, I will force you through him who created all things by his word, and who will come to judge the living and the dead by fire. Amen."
