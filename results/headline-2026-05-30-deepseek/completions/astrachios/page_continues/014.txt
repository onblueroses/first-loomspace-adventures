 “Or see the spirits come up with: ‘Heigle, Agla, Sol, Tetragrammaton.’ ”

The names of God are “open sesame” to the spirits. The names of God are the passwords into the spirit world.

The invocation to Lucifer is a prayer to the “Prince of the House of God,” but God is never mentioned. Lucifer is called “lord of light and knowledge,” “firstborn son of morning,” “first-begotten of the seraphim,” and “holder of the keys of hell.” He is implored to “grant me thy

power” and to “send thy minister,” who will “reveal to me the secrets I
desire.” The invocation is not a prayer for enlightenment but a prayer
for power.
The text of the invocation to Lucifer is as follows:

O Lucifer, thou who art called the iniquitous one, or the bringer of light,
because thou didst fall from heaven like a bolt of lightning, I conjure thee,
O Lucifer, by the living God, by the true God, by the holy God, by the God
who spake and it was done, who commanded and all things were created,
and whom all creatures obey:
