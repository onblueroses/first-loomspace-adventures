 'Note: All the names of God and of the angels are to be pronounced aloud while asperging with exorcised water.'

**Marbas** is a Great President, who appears as a great lion. He answers truly on all secret things, causes and cures diseases, gives wisdom in mechanical arts, and can change men into other shapes.

**Valefor** is a mighty Duke, who appears as a lion with a braying donkey's head. He tempts men to steal and brings them into favour with friends and foes. He governs six legions.

**Amon** is a Marquis, who appears as a wolf with a serpent's tail, breathing flames. He knows all things past and future, procures love and reconciles controversies between friends and foes. He governs forty legions.

**Barbatos** is a Count and Duke, who appears when the sun is in Sagittarius with four noble kings and their companies of troops. He understands the songs of birds, the barking of dogs, the lowing of bulls and the voice of all living creatures. He reveals hidden treasures, knows all things past and future, and reconciles friends and those in power. He governs thirty legions.
