 “Then say: O Lord God, holy, pious and merciful, thou who hast created all things, who knowest all things and from whom nothing is hidden, to whom nothing is impossible, thou who hast formed man of the earth, and hast breathed into him the breath of life, I beseech thee, O Lord, according to thy great mercy, to send thy spirit into this water, that it may become a water of life, a water of remedy and of blessing, to drive away all sickness from the bodies and souls of those who shall drink it, and to keep them in health, through Jesus Christ thy Son our Lord. Amen.” The formula is to be repeated three times over the water, and the sign of the cross made three times.\(^4\)

The significance of these formulas lies in the fact that they were the actual spells used by magicians and sorcerers, which are the prototype of the magical formulas used by modern occultists.
