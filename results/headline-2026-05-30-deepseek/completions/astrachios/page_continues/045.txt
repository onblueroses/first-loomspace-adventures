 ‘O, you who govern the fourth Legion, I invoke, conjure, and command you, by all the aforementioned names and by the name Anepheneton that you love and revere, and by the seal of your creation which is engraven on this ring, to come to me without delay from whatever part of the world you may be in, and not to hurt me in any way, but to be obedient to me, fulfilling my will without deception. Come, then, and be ready to obey my orders.’

After these conjurations, the Magician addresses himself to the Spirits of the East, South, West, and North, and to all Spirits under their power, conjuring them in the name of Adonai, El, Elohim, Elohe, Zebaoth, Elion, Escrebie, Jah, Tetragrammaton, Sadai, the God who is exalted above all the Celestial Spirits, to send unto him from the East, South, West, and North, a Spirit of the nature of the planet under which he is born, who shall serve him all the days of his life, coming at his call, and going at his command.
