 “In the hour of the moon invoke the angels of Monday: Gabriel, Michael, Samael.”

---

### The Angel Gabriel

One of the four archangels named in the Old Testament, Gabriel is the angel of mercy, death, resurrection, and vengeance. In the New Testament, he appears to Zechariah to announce the birth of John the Baptist, and to Mary to announce the birth of Jesus. He is also the angel who inspires the prophets and who Mohammed credits as the source of his revelations.

The Hebrew name Gabriel means “God is my strength.”

---

### The Angel Michael

An archangel whose name appears four times in the Bible, Michael is the chief of the angels, the angel of mercy, righteousness, and sanctification. He is said to be the angel who appeared to Moses in the burning bush and who received the tablets of the law from God on Mount Sinai. He is also the angel who contended with Satan for the body of Moses and who cast Satan out of heaven.

The Hebrew name Michael means “Who is like God?”

---

### The Angel Samael

An angel of death and the angel who fell from grace, Samael is said to have been the guardian angel of Esau and a patron of the Roman empire.
