 “Then say: ‘I conjure you, O circle of power, that you may be a mighty stronghold for me against all unclean spirits and all their legions, to banish them from me, so that they may never again return to this place. Let them be obedient to this circle of power, and let this circle of power be a fortress for me, O Lord, Amen.’” And it ends: “Then say: ‘Bless, O Lord, this creature of chalk, that it may be a healthful medicine for all things, and let all deceit depart from it. Bless, O Lord, this creature of chalk, so that it may be a protection for the bodies and souls of all those who shall be within it, through you, O Lord, who liveth and reigneth God, world without end, Amen.’” Then sprinkle the chalk with holy water and asperge it with a sprig of hyssop, saying: “Thou shalt purge me, O Lord, with hyssop, and I shall be clean; thou shalt wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow.” Then cast the chalk down strongly within the circle, saying: “Astrachios, Asac, Acra Bedrimuliel
