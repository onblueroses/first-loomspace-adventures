 "Or conjure in any manner thou wilt, and comfort the spirit, and give him a name; and he shall aid thee in all matters, and be faithful unto thee."

On the next page, the spirit appears again, this time as a man, and is identified as Ariton, or Egin, a great and mighty duke, who appears when the sun is in the southern signs. He is said to appear in the form of a king riding upon a horse, with a serpent as his tail. He carries a viper in his hand, and rules over six legions of demons. He gives true answers regarding things present, past, and future, and can make men invisible. He also bestows dignities and confirms them, and can cause love between people.

The spirit's seal, or sigil, is given, as well as instructions for how it is to be drawn and inscribed upon the breastplate of the exorcist, along with the names of God.

The text continues: "He governeth 26 Legions of Inferiour spirits; and his Seal is this, which is to be worn as a Lamen before thee, etc."
