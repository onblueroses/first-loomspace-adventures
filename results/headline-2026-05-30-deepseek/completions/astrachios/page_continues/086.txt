 “Invoke them thus:

O ye angels who are set over the operations of the Lord, and who are governing spirits in the ether, in the air, and in all other places; O ye who govern the whole world, and who cause all to tremble with fear; O ye who cast down and who raise up; O ye who are set over the good and over the evil spirits; O ye who are governing spirits in the ether, in the air, and in all other places; I conjure ye by the ineffable name of the Almighty God, which is Yod, He, Vau, He; that ye aid us in this operation, and that we may find what we desire through your agency.”

The manuscript ends: “It is finished.”

The book of the Sacred Magic of Abramelin the Mage was first translated by MacGregor Mathers, one of the founders of the Golden Dawn. He claimed that he had discovered a manuscript of the work in the Bibliothèque de l’Arsenal in Paris. It is now believed that the work is a fabrication of the eighteenth century, but it is still one of the most influential grimoires of modern times.
