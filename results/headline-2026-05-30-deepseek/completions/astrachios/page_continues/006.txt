 "And when thou hast read these things, thou shalt see a great dragon, out of whose mouth issueth fire." Then the mage is to conjure the dragon and call it by name: "O thou great dragon, and strong, which liest prostrate in the way for them that pass by, I conjure thee by the which hath created the sea, and all lakes, and all rivers." The dragon is then to be commanded to go to the treasure cave and frighten away the guardians.

A variation on this ritual is given in the _Magical Calendar_ , a German text compiled by Johann Baptist Grossschedel von Aicha (1564–1636). This version of the ritual calls for the magician to go to the cave of the treasure and "write on the entrance with a sword these names: On, Tetragrammaton, Primeumaton, Anepheneton, Inessenfatoal, Patiffon, Itemon, Stimulaton, Erogai, Gallen, Galua, Geno, Degora, Geda, Lia, Heli, Sursanagi, Lemmizaton, Homofories, Tetragrammaton, Casuly."
