 "The letters of the angelic alphabet, which are called Malachim, are to be written in blood, or with a consecrated pen. The seals and characters of the angels are to be made in their appropriate colours, and on the proper days and hours pertaining to them." The _Key_ states that "all these operations of the day and hour of Saturn are to be performed when the Moon is in Taurus, or Capricorn, or Virgo, and well aspected with Saturn; and Saturn must be in a favourable position, as well as the Moon." This is clearly a complex magical system that requires a detailed knowledge of astrology.
