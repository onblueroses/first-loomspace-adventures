PRAYER TO ANAEL

O Anael, angel of love,
you who are the master of the passions,
you who govern the planet Venus,
I call upon you!
By the power of the great name ADONAI,
I beg you to come to my aid.
Grant me your favor and allow me to find
the love that I desire.
May my heart be filled with joy and my soul be at peace.
O Anael, hear my prayer and come to my aid!
So be it!
