 “Behold the pentacle of Solomon, which I have brought before your presence for your honor, O ye spirits!” In Hebrew the verse reads: “Behold, Solomon has come before you, to whom has been given all power and all command.”

The figure is Solomon, with his two attendants. He is shown with his magical ring, which gave him power over demons. The demons themselves are represented by the two seals in the circle: the one on the left is the seal of the spirit Bealphares; the one on the right, the seal of the spirit Bealphabes.

**Key words:** Pentacle of Solomon, Solomon, demons, Bealphares, Bealphabes.
