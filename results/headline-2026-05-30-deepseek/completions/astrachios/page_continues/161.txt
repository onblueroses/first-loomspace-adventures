 “And when all this is done you will see the spirits come from all sides in great haste, in the forms of various animals: first in the form of birds, and after that in the form of lions or oxen with the horns of a bullock, and men with faces of lions, and horses’ feet, and serpents’ tails. But fear nothing, and keep your courage, for you have the symbols that should be shown to them.”

The next page shows two of these symbols. The first is called “The Pentacle of Solomon,” the second “The Pentacle of the Pact.” They are circles with crosses, Hebrew characters, and strange designs.
