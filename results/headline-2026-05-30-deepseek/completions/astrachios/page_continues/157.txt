 "O all you angels and holy ones, descend upon the sphere of the Moon, and give virtue and power to the figure of the Moon, engraved upon this stone."

The page goes on to ask the angels of the Moon to "descend and be present, so that this stone may gain such power that he who wears it may be protected from all peril of water, and from all phantoms of the night, and that he may overcome all his enemies, and that all watery and nocturnal animals may fear him."

On the next page, the angels of Mercury are invoked, as well as the angel Raphael, and the intelligence of Mercury, Tiriel. The stone is to be engraved with the sign of Mercury.

Then come the angels of Venus, and the angel Anael, and the intelligence Hagiel. The stone is engraved with the sign of Venus.

Then come the angels of the Sun, and the angel Michael, and the intelligence Nakhiel. The stone is engraved with the sign of the Sun.

Then come the angels of Mars, and the angel Samael, and the intelligence Graphiel. The stone is engraved with the sign of Mars.
