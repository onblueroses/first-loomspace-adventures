 "O you angels, be present, and be favorable to me. I beg you by the same names, by the holy names which are written in this book, and by the virtue of these names, that this experiment may become perfect through you, Amen." The other names are written around the page, with the instruction "The names of God are to be written around." The seal of the figure, which is like a magic circle, is to be drawn on parchment with the blood of a gosling, and the figure is to be worn on the chest.

The second page has another magic circle and text. The text says: "O God Adonai, Elohim, El, Eheieh Asher Eheieh, blessed be Thy name, and blessed be Thy kingdom, which is forever. Amen." Around the magic circle are the names Tetragrammaton, Adonai, Primeumaton, Anephexeton, Inessenfatoal, Pathumaton, Itemon.
