 “The most holy Tetragrammaton, Alpha and Omega, Beginning and End. That which is Above and that which is Below, Amen. The most holy Tetragrammaton. Alpha and Omega, Beginning and End, That which is Above and that which is Below. Amen.”

Then follows a section of the page headed “The Pentagram.” Underneath is a drawing of a pentagram with a circle around it. The letters Y H S V H are in the points of the star, with the letters “Alpha” and “Omega” on either side of the top point. Inside the pentagram is a figure of a man, with the head and hands at the points of the star and the feet at the lower two points. The man’s right hand holds a chalice; his left hand holds a sword. Around the circle are the words, in Latin: Quod superius est sicut quod inferius est (What is above is like what is below). At the four corners of the page are the names of the archangels: Raphael, Gabriel, Michael, and Auriel.
