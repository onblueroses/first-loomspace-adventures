 "O all ye who are spiritual, holy, pure, and blessed, ye who are from the foundations of the world, hear my voice and come to me." Then he calls upon them to open the way for him and to bring him treasure, and he conjures them by the power of the Tetragrammaton and by the power of the angels Uriel, Raphael, Michael, Gabriel, Sadakiel, Samael, and Anael.

Afterwards, he should make a circle and sit in it and call the spirits to appear. He should say:

O ye strong and holy angels, whose names are written in this circle, I conjure and exorcise you by these names: Adonai, El, Elohim, Elohe, Zebaoth, Elion, Escherie, Jah, Tetragrammaton, Saday, the Lord God of Israel, who has made heaven and earth, and has placed everything under his feet, who is seated upon the Cherubim, who has spoken and it has been done, who commanded and the whole creation has appeared: Agla, On, Tetragrammaton, and Adonai, I conjure you, O Spirit N., by Him who has created all things,
