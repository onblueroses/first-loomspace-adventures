 "The 3rd day is dedicated to Saturn, the presiding angel being Cassiel. The spirits of the air of Saturday are subject in the West to Sarabocres, who should be invoked towards the West." The talismans, seals, and characters for Saturday are given (see figure 4.3).

The key to the table of planetary hours is also given: "The day is from sunrise to sunset. The night is from sunset to sunrise. Divide the length of day or night by 12 to get the length of each hour. Then, starting with the first hour of the day, the planets are: Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon, Saturn, Jupiter, etc. For night: Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon, Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, etc."
