 ‘Then say this prayer: O thou great and powerful spirit N, who ruleth in the East, I invocate and conjure thee in the name of the Supreme and everlasting Lord God of Hosts, Jehovah Tetragrammaton, who hath created all things both in heaven and earth and hell, and in whom all things are contained and are moved; and I command thee by Him who spake and it was done, and to whom all creatures are obedient; and by the ineffable names On, Alpha et Omega, El, Elohim, Elohe, Zebaoth, Elion, Escerehie, Jah, Tetragrammaton, Sadai, Adonai, Amioram, and by all the other great and glorious names of the most High and incomprehensible Lord God of Hosts, that thou forthwith appear and show thyself unto me, here before this circle, in a fair and human shape, without any deformity or tortuosity; and by and in these names to answer all my questions and perform all my desires, so far as in thy office thou art capable. And this I shall do without harm to my body or soul.’ 7
