 ‘O Lord, hear my prayer, and let my cry come unto thee. O Lord God of hosts, how long wilt thou be angry against the prayer of thy servant? How long wilt thou feed us with bread of tears; and give us tears to drink in great measure? How long wilt thou forget us, and hide thy face from us? How long shall our enemy be exalted over us? Look upon us, O Lord God of hosts, and consider us: from thy holy habitation and from thy throne, and from the excellence of thy glory: have mercy upon us, O Lord, have mercy upon us: for we are filled with contempt. Our soul is filled with the scornful reproof of the rich, and with the despitefulness of the proud. Have mercy upon us, O Lord, have mercy upon us.’

This prayer is followed by a ‘Charm for the Sword’:

I conjure thee, O Sword, by these Names Abrahach, Abraham, Acra, Bedrimulael, Silat, Arabonas, Jereboam, Anidoth, Areopomageton, Anosteton, Megaloson, Albarach, Bedrimulael, Gibor, Ischiros, Eloh
