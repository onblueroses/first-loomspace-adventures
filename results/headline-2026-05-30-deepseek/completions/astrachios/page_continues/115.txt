 “Do you know the names?” And then the angels appear to the scryer in the vision and speak to him, giving him further instructions.

From this it is clear that the angels are not so much called as evoked, using their names as passwords to open the way into their realm. The magician prepares the way with the ritual words, but the angels appear of their own accord. The magician’s role is to listen to their instructions, and to watch what is shown to him in the vision.

The angels themselves are not always named in the visions, but the names of the angels are important in the preparatory invocation. This is made clear by a passage in the text of the fifteenth vision, which is a vision of the fiery angel Carmara (spelled Carmarah in the text). Carmara appears to Dee and Kelley on Sunday, April 19, 1587, and speaks:

I am Carmara. I am the 10th of the 7, and the 7th of the 10. My name is Carmara. I am the Lord of the 1st Angle [angle] of the Earth, under the great name of God, ORO IBAH AOZPI. I am the Lord of the first Earth
