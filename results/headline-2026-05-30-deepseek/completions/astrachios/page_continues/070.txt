 "O thou great God, the Lord and life of all things, visible and invisible, the author of all being, whom the heavens obey, the earth and sea and hell itself do fear and tremble, all the powers of the air do serve, we thy poor servants humbly beseech thee to shed forth thy blessing and power into this creature of thy hand, that it may be a true and perfect pentacle, having such virtue in it that thou hast appointed for such an end. And forasmuch as we thy servants are weak and frail, and cannot without sin stand before thy divine majesty, we humbly beseech thee, O Lord God Almighty and Eternal, that the Archangel Raphael may effect this our pentacle, and that he may be a present help and assistance to us thy servants, now and for evermore. Amen."
