Clearly, this is a magic book. It is a book that describes the practice of magic, spells, and incantations. It is a book of magic that, in the very act of describing magical procedures, is itself magical. It is a book that can be used to perform magic. It is a book that is itself a magic object.

The authors of the magical papyri were not the only writers who produced such books. A variety of other texts, including manuals of alchemy, astrology, and divination, were also considered to be magical books. And in addition to books that contained magical spells or recipes, there were also books that were said to have been written by angels, or by God, or by the Devil. These books were thought to be repositories of hidden knowledge, of secrets that could not be revealed to ordinary mortals.
