**ASTROLOGY** A branch of occultism based on the belief that stars and planets influence the fate of men and nations. The Babylonians were the first to develop astrology. Their priests kept records of the movements of heavenly bodies and of earthly events. The priests then attempted to show that there was a relationship between the two. The Greeks took over astrology from the Babylonians and refined it. They named the planets after their gods and assigned to each planet the qualities of its god. The Romans adopted astrology from the Greeks, and astrology spread throughout the Roman Empire.

During the Middle Ages astrology was condemned by the Church but was nevertheless practiced by alchemists, physicians, and even popes. In the Renaissance astrology was taken seriously by many men of science.
