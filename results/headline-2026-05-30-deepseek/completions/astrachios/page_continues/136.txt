 "O thou great god, the honored and honorable! I beseech thee by thy great mercy, and by thy powerful and strong names, that thou wilt send unto me N., the daughter of N., whose image is here present; and that she may come to me affectionately and in all kindness, and that she may not depart from me until she hath accomplished my desire."

The great god is then entreated by a series of names, including Arpien, Arpien, Arpiana, Arpiana, Elohim, Elohim, and I request, I adjure thee, O thou great god! to send unto me N., the daughter of N., whose image is here present, that she may come to me quickly, immediately, without any delay or procrastination; and that she may not depart from me until she hath accomplished my desire.

The ritualist is then to take the needle and stick it into the heart of the image, saying, "I pierce thy heart, O N., daughter of N., with this my hand, so that thou mayest love me with all thy heart and all thy mind, and think of me only."
