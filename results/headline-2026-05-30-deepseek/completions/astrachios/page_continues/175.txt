As we have seen, the book of Abramelin is based on an entirely different premise. It is a work of theurgy, which means it is based on the premise that man is capable of achieving union with his God—the God of the Abrahamic religions, the God of Judaism, Christianity, and Islam—and that this union is the ultimate goal of human existence. To achieve this union, Abramelin says, requires a long and arduous process of purification, of prayer, of fasting, of seclusion from the world, and of ritual invocation. This is the “Abramelin Operation,” as it has come to be known, and it is designed to bring the magician to a state of “Conversation with his Holy Guardian Angel.” The operation takes six months to complete, and it is not for the faint of heart.
