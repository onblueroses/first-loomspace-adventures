 “O God, terrible and powerful, Who hast
established the greatness of Thy glory in the perfections of Thy
Kingdom, and Who art magnified by the shining of the celestial
virtues, in the splendour of Thy mercies, give unto me Thine aid,
that I may be fortified in Thy power, and that I may appear
without fear before the formidable Spirits, that they may be constrained to come at my call, and that they may obey me by the
power of the holy Angel Anaël, and in the name of the great
Adonai, of Whom Thou hast made Thy Vicar upon earth. Amen.”

infinite mercy hast created all things, Thou hast formed me in

submitted them to my domination. In the name of Thy holy and
