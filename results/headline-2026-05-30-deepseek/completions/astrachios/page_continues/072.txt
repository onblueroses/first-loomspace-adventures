 "O God, who hast examined the hearts and reins, and who alone art the Redeemer of them that trust in Thee, I humbly beseech Thy Majesty, that these holy angels, whose names are here written, may be present at this circle; that they may constrain the spirits, which I shall call, to appear visibly, and without delay, and to obey my commands."

At the bottom of the page is the sigil of the spirit.

The second page, corresponding to the spirit Cimejes, is much the same, with the names: "El, Ariel, Johanael, Aquiel, Almadiel, Zophiel, Baciel, Gediel, Cephiel, Tobiel, Zophiel, Vriel, Zazel, Janael, Madiel, Damael, Calzas, Ahel, Arachi, Johanael, Muriel, Janiel, Raphael, Nathanael, and Abrael."

This spirit is also addressed in a prayer.

As for the spirit's sigil, it is the same as that in the Goetia, but here it is reversed.
