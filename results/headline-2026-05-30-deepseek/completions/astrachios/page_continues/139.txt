 "O Lord God, Who art seated above the heavens, and Who regardest the Abysses beneath, grant me success in the undertaking of this art of magic. O Lord God, Who hast created all things for the service of man, grant that I may become an instrument of Thy marvellous works, through Thy Holy Name Agla. Amen."

The following page contains "The Prayer of the Salamanders." The salamanders are the spirits of the fire element; they are the only elementals whose king is named in the grimoire: "King Djin." The prayer reads: "Immortal, eternal, ineffable and holy Father of all things, Who art carried upon the rolling chariot of worlds which revolve unceasingly; Lord of the Etheric Realms, where the throne of Thy power is exalted, from which height Thy dread eyes perceive all things, and Thy holy and beautiful ears hear all things, hear Thou Thy child, whom Thou hast loved from the birth of his [or her] generation, from whose forefathers Thou hast ever been content.
