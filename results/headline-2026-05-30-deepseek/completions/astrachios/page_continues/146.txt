 ‘Then shalt thou say: I am he whose name is written in the heart of the Virgin and is venerated in the bosom of the Father.’

The circle is consecrated with holy water and incense, and the Magician kneels in the East, facing the West, and says:

O Lord, behold my affliction and deliver me, for I observe not the law of God and I have provoked thine anger. I have sinned, O Lord, I have sinned against thee. Be appeased and have mercy upon me, O Lord, and deliver me from the tribulations that surround me. Have mercy upon me, O Lord, have mercy upon me. May thy will be done, and not mine.

Then rise and say:

O Lord, thou hast placed me above thy angels and hast crowned me with glory and honour. Thou hast subjected all things under my feet, all sheep and oxen, yea, and the beasts of the field, the fowls of the air, and the fish of the sea, and whatsoever passeth through the paths of the sea.
