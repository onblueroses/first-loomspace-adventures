 ‘O thou great God, that sittest in the fire, hear my voice. I am the great prophet that have power to call upon thee. I am he that have power upon all spirits of the earth, air, fire, water, and upon all things that are in them. Hear me, for I am Servant of the Highest, the same thy God Adonai, El, I have power, Io, Pan, Io, Isis, Io, Apis, Io, Jupiter Sabbas, Io, Tetragrammaton.’

It is interesting to note that the word ‘Tetragrammaton’ appears here. This is the name of God spelt with four letters, usually YHWH, but sometimes JHVH, which is pronounced Jehovah.
