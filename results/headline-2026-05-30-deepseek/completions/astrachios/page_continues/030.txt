 "And when thou wilt make any experiment, call the Angels of the hour wherein thou wouldest work, and also the Angels of the day wherein thou wouldest work, and also the Angels of the Sign ruling that day and hour; for they are all necessary."

A similar list of names is given in the Grimoire of Honorius (Ms. Harley 181, British Museum, fol. 87 verso). This list begins: "O God of Abraham, God of Isaac, God of Jacob, God of the angels, God of archangels, God of prophets, God of apostles, God of martyrs, God of confessors, God of virgins, God who didst create heaven and earth, sea and all things in them." It continues: "O God who didst make Adam from the mud of the earth and didst place him in the delights of paradise, and when he transgressed thy commandment thou didst drive him out of paradise by thy just judgment, O God who didst save Noah from the deluge, O God who didst deliver Abraham from Ur of the Chaldees, O God who didst appear to Moses thy servant on Mount Sinai and didst give him the law written by thy finger, O God who didst
