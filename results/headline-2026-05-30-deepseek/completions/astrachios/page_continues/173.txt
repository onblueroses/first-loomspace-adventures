 "I conjure thee, O Circle of Art, by the most strong and powerful name of God El, who is One over all, and by the name Ieova, which is He that made the heavens and the earth and every creature that is therein; and by the sign of the Cross; and by the most holy names of God written within this Circle; and by all the most glorious and powerful names of God: that thou be a fortification and a protection to defend me against all evil spirits, and from all manner of hurt and mischief to soul or body, and that no evil thing have power to enter into this Circle, or to draw nigh unto it, without my consent."

In the next section of the grimoire, the magician is directed to trace a second circle inside the first. Inside this circle is written the name of God: Agla, an acronym for Atah Gibor Le-olam Adonai, meaning "You, O Lord, are mighty forever." This name was sometimes worn as a protective amulet. Then a triangle is drawn in the middle of the circle. Around the triangle are written the names of God: Tetragrammaton, Primeumaton, and Anaphaxeton.
