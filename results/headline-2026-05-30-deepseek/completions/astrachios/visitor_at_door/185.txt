 He is the guardian of the gate, the watcher of the threshold. He says:

"Come away! For I am the First Servant of God. Without beginning, without end. I am He who has measured the heavens and stretched out the earth as a measuring line. I am He whose voice is thunder. I am He whose voice flashes forth lightning. I am He whose voice makes the earth to tremble. I am He who has the power to raise up and to throw down. I am He whose power lives forever. I am He who kills and brings to life. I am He who wounds and heals. I am He whom no man sees and lives. I am He who rides on the wings of the wind. I am He who spoke and the world was made. I am He whom the winds fear. I am He who is master over the mighty waters. I am He who made the pillars of heaven and earth to tremble. I am He who is clothed with glory. I am He who is girded with strength. I am He whose eyes are flames of fire. I am He whose arms are everlasting. I am He whose fingers hold the deep. I am He whose hands prepared the dry land.
