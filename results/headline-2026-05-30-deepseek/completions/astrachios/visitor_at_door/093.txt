**The Core Context: The Heptameron & The Ars Notoria**
Many of these names appear in classical grimoires, most notably in the **Heptameron** (or *Elemental Magic*), a 15th-16th century text often attributed to Peter de Abano. This work details the conjuration of the aerial spirits and the angels of the seven days of the week. The names are used to construct circles, form prayers, and call upon the powers of the winds and the hours.

Furthermore, some names (like **Genium Domos**—"House of the Spirits" or "Dwelling of the Genii") point toward the **Ars Notoria**, an even older Solomonic text focused on obtaining knowledge, eloquence, and memory through prayer and divine names.
