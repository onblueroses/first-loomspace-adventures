 The voice is not pleasant, but it is familiar. “You have summoned me, Master. What do you wish of me?”

“I need your help, O Lucifuge. I have a task for you to perform. I want you to show me the location of the treasure of the Templars, the lost treasure of the Templars.”

“Is that all, Master?” The voice is almost disappointed. “That is a simple matter. I know exactly where it is. I will show you.”

Lucifuge leads the Master through the darkness, through the night. They move quickly, the Master and his spirit guide. They cross the ocean, pass over the land, come to a stop in a small French village. The Master recognizes the place. He has been here many times before.

“Where is it, Lucifuge? Where is the treasure?”

“There, Master. There.”

“There? In the churchyard?”

“Yes, Master. In the churchyard. It is buried in the churchyard. You will find it there. Beneath the stone with the inscription.”

“That is all?”

“That is all, Master.”
