 I will not write his words here. Let the magician discover them for himself. His name is given: “I am called Captio,” which means “the capturer.” He is the one who seizes, captures, takes prisoner. He will not let you go until he has wrung from you the knowledge of your true desire.

The spirit speaks again, “I am the one who teaches all the arts. The one who teaches and leads the way.” He repeats his name, Captio. The name of the spirit is also the name of the art. The art of capturing, of capturing the spirit.

The magician asks, “Teach me the art of capturing.”

The spirit answers, “I teach the art of capturing. Say these words: Captio, Captio, Captio. I have captured. I will capture. I capture.”
