 Hear the words of the true God. The words of the living God. The words of the holy God. The words of the God who is from everlasting to everlasting, whose dwelling is in the highest heaven, whose name is Jehovah. I am He who is, who was, and who shall be. I am He who made the world in the space of six days. I am He who heard the groaning of the children of Israel and who delivered them from the Pharaoh. I am He who led my people through the wilderness and gave them the law. I am He who spoke by the prophets. I am He whom all things obey.

I am He who revives the dead and throws down the proud. I am He who has the keys of hell and of death. I am the Beginning and the End. I am He whose eyes are flames of fire and whose feet are like polished brass. I am He who is and was and shall be. I am He who is from everlasting to everlasting.
