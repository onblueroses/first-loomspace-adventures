 Do you hear him? He knocks at the door. He says: “Open!”

I say: “Who are you? Where do you come from?”

He says: “I am the messenger of the Lord. Open the door for me.”

I say: “I will not open unless you tell me your name.”

He says: “I am Raphael.”

I say: “If you are Raphael, why do you come to me? You are one of the seven holy angels who stand in the presence of the Holy One, blessed be He, to convey healing to the earth. But I do not need healing.”

He says: “I am sent to you by the Lord.”

I say: “If you are sent by the Lord, I will open.”

I open the door and behold! He stands before me in the appearance of a man, but his appearance is like the appearance of an angel, and he is girded with a girdle. And I ask him: “Why are you girded?”

He says: “Because I came quickly.”

I say: “What is your message?”
