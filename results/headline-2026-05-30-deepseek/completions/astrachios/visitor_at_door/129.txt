 The spirit is in the doorway, but he is not yet visible.

You have done well to call upon me. You have used the correct words and gestures. You have lit the proper incense. But I cannot enter until you have named me. I am a great spirit, a powerful spirit. I am not a messenger, but a master of messengers. I am a duke and a prince, and I have many servants. I can grant you great knowledge, but only if you know me. What is my name? Say my name and I will enter. Say my name and I will stay. Say my name and I will help you.

The magician must speak the spirit's name. If the magician does not know the name, the spirit will leave. The magician must speak the name immediately, without any hesitation.

The spirit speaks.

You have done well. You have spoken my name. I am now in the room with you. I am now ready to help you. What do you want? What do you need? I can give you knowledge. I can give you power. I can give you wealth. I can give you love. I can give you anything you want. But you must ask for it. You must tell me what you want.
