 He says: 'O keeper of the key of the house of David and the sceptre of the house of Israel, open to my face, for before thee stands the sun and the moon, and with me stand the two olive trees, candlesticks stand before the God of the earth.'

Then speak the words of the Lord God of Israel: 'Behold, I have caused thine iniquity to pass from thee, and I will clothe thee with changes of raiment. And I said: Let them set a fair mitre upon his head. So they set a fair mitre upon his head, and clothed him with garments, while the angel of the Lord stood by.'

Then the keeper of the gates shall speak: 'Thus saith the Lord of Hosts: If thou wilt walk in my ways, and if thou wilt keep my charge, then thou shalt also judge my house, and shalt also keep my courts, and I will give thee places to walk among these that stand by.'

Then shall the visitor speak: 'I am a rose of Sharon, and a lily of the valley.'

Then shall the gates be opened, and the visitor may enter.
