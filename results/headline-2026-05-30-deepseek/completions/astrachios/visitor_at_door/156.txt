 The seer opens the door. The visitor enters. The seer shuts the door. The seer says:

I have opened the door. I have shut it with an iron bolt. I have brought my four angels, Michael, Gabriel, Raphael, Uriel, to my aid. I have brought my four sylphs, Paralda, Zephyra, Earthelia, Hydra, to my aid. I have brought my four undines, Necksa, Nix, Nissa, Unda, to my aid. I have brought my four gnomes, Ghob, Paralda, Chom, Djin, to my aid. I have brought my four salamanders, Djin, Vesta, Chom, Aesh, to my aid.

The seer speaks to the visitor:

What do you want? Why do you come here?

The visitor answers:

I want to see the dead. I want to speak to them.

The seer says:

Why?

The visitor says:

Because I want to know.

The seer says:

What do you want to know?

The visitor says:

I want to know what is on the other side.
