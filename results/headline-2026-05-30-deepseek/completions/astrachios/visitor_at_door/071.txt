 He says:

“I am the archangel Michael, the prince of the heavenly host. I have been sent by God to bring you to him. I will take you to the celestial court. I will take you to the throne of God.”

You leave the room. You follow Michael out of the house. You walk out into the night.

You are in a place of darkness. The world is dark, but you can see because Michael shines with a light like the sun.

He takes you by the hand and leads you into the air. You fly. You rise into the air and fly above the world.

You fly for a long time. You fly until you come to the first heaven.

Michael says:

“This is the first heaven. This is the heaven of the moon. Here dwell the angels who govern the affairs of men. They bring dreams and visions to the prophets. They bring inspiration to poets and artists.”

You fly through the first heaven and you come to the second heaven.

Michael says:

“This is the second heaven. This is the heaven of Mercury. Here dwell the angels who rule over science and knowledge. They bring wisdom to the wise and understanding to the learned.”

You fly through the second heaven and you come to the third heaven.
