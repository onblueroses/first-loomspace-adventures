 Listen and hear:

O, Guardian of the Portal of the West, I adjure you in the names: Astra, Asac, Acra, Bedrimuliel, Silat, Arabonas, Irehalem, Ideodoc, Archarzel, Blautel, Baracata, Adonai, Elohim, Emagro, Abragateh, Samoel, Geburahel, Cadato, Era, Elohi, Achsah, Ebmisha, Imachadel, Daniel, Dama, Elamos, Izachel, Bael, Scirlin; Genium Domos.

Open the door to the West that I may enter, for I have the right of passage, for I am a man made in the image of my Creator, and I have the power of the Creator in my breast. I have the right of passage.

The Guardian of the West replies:

Hail, O man of the Earth, thou hast the right of passage. Enter thou the Portal of the West. I, the Guardian of the West, greet thee. Welcome to the Halls of Amenti.

The candidate enters and walks to the West. The Hierophant goes to the West and stands behind the candidate
