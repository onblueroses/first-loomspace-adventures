 He says: "I am not the author of confusion, but of peace."

Now say: "Come in, peaceable N., because I record that thou art deputed by God."

The spirit enters. He bows. He says: "What wouldst thou have? For I am he who is last and first."

Say: "I am pious, and I beseech thee, by these holy names, that thou appear visibly and show thyself in all friendship."

He says: "I am ready to answer all your demands, both by my office and also by the bond I have made with the dwellers above. Therefore, speak your mind."

Say: "I would have a familiar friendship with thee, as a brother with a brother, if thou wilt swear by him that liveth forever that thou wilt always be obedient."

He says: "I swear by him that sitteth upon the holy throne, and by the names before spoken, and by the bond wherein we are knit, that I will be faithful and obedient in all things."

Say: "I am content; but if thou dost falsify thy faith and promise, I will conjure thee by the virtue and power of the Most High to remain in perpetual bonds."
