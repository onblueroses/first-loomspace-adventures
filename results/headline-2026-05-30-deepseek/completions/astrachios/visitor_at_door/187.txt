 He says:

"I am the eye in the triangle. I am the face that burns above the water. I am the one who stands at the crossroads. I am the flame of the candle. I am the light of the star. I am the voice in the whirlwind. I am the four who are one. I am the four beasts about the throne. I am the one who is and was and shall be. I am the beginning and the end. I am the one who is called by a hundred names, and yet I am nameless."

You say:

"I call you by your names, O visitor at the door. You are Michael, Raphael, Gabriel, and Auriel. You are the four lords of the winds. You are the four quarters of the world. You are the four beasts about the throne. You are the four rivers flowing out of Eden. You are the four elements of nature. You are the four humours of man. You are the four seasons of the year. You are the four ages of man. You are the four suits of the Tarot. You are the four alchemical elements. You are the four fixed signs of the zodiac. You are the four evangelists. You are the four horsemen of the Apocalypse
