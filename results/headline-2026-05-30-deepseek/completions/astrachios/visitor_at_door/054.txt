 Who are you? What is your name? Why do you disturb my peace?

Answer: I am a man, a creation of the true God. I am come to speak with you. I wish to know you and to have your friendship.

If he answers: I cannot come forth, nor appear to you, nor speak with you, for the Lord has not willed it so.

Reply: The Lord has allowed all his creatures to do the will of man, who is made in the image of God.

If he persists in his refusal, say: I conjure you in the name of the great living God to tell me your name.

If he still refuses, conjure him in the name A + e + a + i + a + a + e + e + a + y + y, and he will be forced to come to you.

When he appears, ask him courteously what you will. If he asks for an offering, tell him you will give it when he has done your bidding.

When the spirit has done your will, dismiss him courteously, saying: Go in peace to your place, and peace be with you until I call you again.

Then burn the offering.
