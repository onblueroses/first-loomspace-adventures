He is dressed in fine robes of green and white, with a girdle of gold and a pair of green shoes. In his right hand he holds a branch of laurel, and in his left hand he holds a bow and arrows.

He is the angel of the first hour of the day of Venus. He is a powerful and mighty angel, and he can help you with matters of love, friendship, and pleasure. He can also help you with matters of beauty, art, and music.

He will be friendly and helpful, and he will answer your questions truthfully.

You may now ask the angel your questions.
