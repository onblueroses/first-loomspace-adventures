 Hear him:

“My Lord, the one you wish to see has come.”

You reply: “Who sent you?”

The visitor says: “The one who has sent me is Abrac, Abraham, Abrahael.”

You say: “Let him enter from the Sabbath.”

The visitor enters, bows, and says: “My Lord, why have you summoned your servant?”

You say: “It is my wish that you remain with me, to do my will, and fulfill all my desires.”

He says: “I will not remain with you, nor obey you, unless you bring me under obligation by the powerful bond of the holy names Agla, Aglai, Aglata. The holy names are to be written on a slip of parchment that has never borne other writing. Place the parchment under your right foot.”

You say: “I adjure you by the bond of the names written on this parchment, under my foot, that you remain with me, and obey me in all things, fulfilling all my desires, and that you do not depart without my permission.”

He says: “I accept the bond. Impose on me what you will.”
