 He is called Maguth and Gablion. He says:

I am the eye in the triangle, the fire in the midst of the salt.

He is an angel of the Lord and of the power of God. He is of the order of thrones. He has the authority to reveal the mysteries of the future, to reveal the secret thoughts of men, to bestow knowledge of all mechanical arts. He can transport men from one place to another instantly.

He is to be invoked on the day of Mercury, in the first hour, or the eighth, or fifteenth, or twentieth. His sign is this:

He says:

Let the salt sea return to its place.

He says:

I have measured the waters in the hollow of my hand.

He says:

Let the salt sea return to its place.

The vision ends.
