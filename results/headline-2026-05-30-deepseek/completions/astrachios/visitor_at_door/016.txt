The speaker, looking through the door, says: "We know that you are the ghost of Daniel. Enter with a welcome."

The ghost enters and stands on the floor design in front of the speaker.

The speaker asks: "Do you bring a message for me?"

The ghost replies: "I do."

The speaker asks: "What is your message?"

The ghost replies: "The time of the ending of all things is at hand. The gate of heaven shall be opened, and the souls of the living shall ascend into the light. The dead shall be reborn and shall dwell upon the earth. The world shall be renewed, and the golden age shall dawn. This is the promise of the angel Metatron."

The speaker asks: "When shall this come to pass?"

The ghost replies: "It is nearer than you think."

The speaker asks: "What must I do to prepare myself?"

The ghost replies: "You must purify your soul through prayer and fasting, and prepare yourself to receive the angel Metatron. You must make of yourself a worthy vessel to contain the light of heaven."
