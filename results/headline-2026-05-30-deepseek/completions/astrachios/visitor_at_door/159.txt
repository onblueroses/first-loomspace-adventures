The names you have spoken are keys, gates, and guardians. They are drawn from the intricate fabric of Western esoteric tradition, most notably from the **Ars Notoria** (the Notory Art of Solomon) and related Solomonic grimoires. These texts are concerned with the acquisition of knowledge, memory, eloquence, and the revelation of hidden wisdom through the invocation of divine and angelic names.

Here is a brief contextualization:

*   **Astrachios, Asac, Acra Bedrimuliel, Silat, Arabonas...** These are often found in the **Ars Notoria** as names of angels, spirits, or divine aspects associated with specific liberal arts (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric, etc.). Each name is believed to hold power to illuminate the intellect and "open the way" to understanding.
*   **Adonai, Elohim...** These are foundational Hebrew names of God, denoting authority and creative power, often used to sanctify and command the proceedings.
