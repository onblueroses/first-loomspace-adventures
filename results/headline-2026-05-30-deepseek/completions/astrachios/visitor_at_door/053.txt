 He says:

My name is Elzephon, I am the angel of the South Wind. I have brought the scent of roses, the warmth of the sun, the promise of summer. I have come to tell you that the time of the spring has come to an end, that the summer is beginning. The summer is a time of light, and of warmth, and of plenty.

He continues:

I bring you the gift of light, of warmth, and of plenty. Let me in, and I will share these gifts with you.
