 She asks: “Why have you disturbed my rest? Why have you summoned me?”

You answer: “We have summoned you because we wish to speak with you and learn from you.”

The visitor asks: “Who are you?”

You answer: “I am [your name], a child of earth and starry heaven.”

The visitor asks: “What do you seek?”

You answer: “I seek knowledge.”

The visitor says: “Then ask your questions.”

At this point you ask the questions you prepared earlier. The visitor will answer them, sometimes at length, sometimes briefly. When you are done, say: “I thank you for your answers. I have no more questions.”

The visitor asks: “Then have you anything more to say to me?”

You answer: “I have nothing more to say.”

The visitor says: “Then I depart.”

At this, the visitor turns and goes back through the door. Close the door, and imagine the circle of light fading away. Take a moment to reorient yourself to your surroundings, and then say: “The rite is ended.”
