 The conjuration that follows is the fourth in the series:

“O thou great, powerful, and mighty angel, Anael, who rule as king in the sixth heaven, I invoke and conjure thee by the name of him at whose voice all creatures obey, and by the name of him whom thou dost serve, and by the seal of thy creation, and by the most mighty and potent name of God, El, strong and wonderful, and by all the names of God, blessed, be thou also blessed. I conjure thee by him who spake and it was done, and to whom all creatures are obedient. I conjure and constrain thee by him who hath the sole name Jehovah, who is the creator of all things, and who is endowed with such power that all must revere and fear him, that thou comest without delay to execute my will and desire, and that thou send and cause to come unto me N., who dwelleth at N., whom I call to come unto me, peacefully, quietly, and without noise, and that he do answer all my questions without any deceit or tarrying.
