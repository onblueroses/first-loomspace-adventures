 He says:

“I am the spirit of Mercury, who is called Splendor. I am the one who watches at the gate.”

You say: “Enter, Splendor. Enter in peace, and let there be peace between us.”

He enters, and stands in the center of the circle. You say:

“I call you, Splendor, spirit of Mercury, and bind you by the names that open the way: Astrachios, Asac, Acra Bedrimuliel, Silat, Arabonas, Irehalem, Ideodoc, Archarzel, Blautel, Baracata, Adonai, Elohim, Emagro, Abragateh, Samoel, Geburahel, Cadato, Era, Elohi, Achsah, Ebmisha, Imachadel, Daniel, Dama, Elamos, Izachel, Bael, Scirlin; Genium Domos.”

He says: “I am here.”

You say: “Splendor, I conjure you in the name of the most high, whose seal is this—” Show him the seal of Mercury, and continue: “to tell me truly: Who is your lord?”
