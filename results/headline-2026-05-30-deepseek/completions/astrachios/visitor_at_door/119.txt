 He says:

O you who preside over the first hour, you, Satquiel, and you, Raphael, and you, Samael, and you, Gabriel, and you, Michael, and you, Sachiel, and you, Cassiel, and you, Anael, I conjure you, by God Almighty, to bring to me the spirit that I desire. He is to appear to me in a human form, gentle and attractive, without any offensive odour, and to answer truthfully to my questions. He is to come quickly, without delay, and to accomplish my will, without any harm coming to me. Amen.

Repeat the prayer three times.
