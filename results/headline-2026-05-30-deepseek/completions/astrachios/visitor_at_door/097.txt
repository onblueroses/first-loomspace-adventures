 "I am the Angel Baruchiel, servant of the Most High God. I am one of the Seven Angels of the Presence. I am the Angel of Lightning. I bring blessings to the righteous, and I am the Angel of the Seventh Heaven, Arabaoth, where I am the teacher of the souls of the righteous. What is your will?"

Say: "Baruchiel, I ask you to bring the blessing of the righteous upon me. Bring to me the blessings of the Most High God. Bring me divine insight. Bring me the blessings of a righteous life. Bring me knowledge of the Seventh Heaven, Arabaoth. Bring me the power of Lightning. Open my mind to the wisdom of the righteous. Baruchiel, I ask you to serve me as you serve the Most High God."

The angel will speak words of blessing to you. When this is done, say: "I thank you, Baruchiel. I release you from this time of service. Go now, with the blessings of the Most High God, and know that you are welcome in this temple when I call."

Close the book.
