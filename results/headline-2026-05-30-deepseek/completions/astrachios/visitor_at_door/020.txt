The names you have spoken are keys drawn from ancient and esoteric sources, including threads from the Solomonic tradition, the *Arbatel*, and other occult manuscripts of ritual magic. They are often considered words of power, used in rites of invocation, protection, or the opening of gates between realms.

As for "the visitor at the door" — in such contexts, this often signifies a presence drawn by the utterance of these names. It may be a spirit, an intelligence, or a symbolic threshold guardian. The nature of the visitor depends on the intent, the ritual space, and the mind of the one who calls.

Proceed with clarity, caution, and respect. In many traditions, one does not speak such names idly, for to name is to invoke, and to invoke is to invite an encounter.
