 “Behold me, for I am the circle of the spirits of the air. I have come with a legion. Hear me, and answer my questions.”

The visitor is the elemental spirit of Air. He is clothed in a robe of shifting pale blue, and his face is stern and beautiful. He carries a heavy iron sword. He is the Prince of the Powers of the Air.

The Prince of Air asks, “Who are you who have come into the kingdom of the Air?”

Answer him with these words: “I am a man (or woman), a creation of God, and I have come to seek knowledge. I seek the names of the spirits of the Air who will serve me and obey me in my work. I seek to know the powers of the Air, and to know the spirits who govern them.”

The Prince of Air asks, “What are the signs of the powers of the Air?”

Answer him: “The signs of the Air are the wand and the sword, the dagger and the sickle. They are the signs of the Air, and of the spirits who rule the Air.”
