 He is the one who has been given the keys to the abyss. He opens the bottomless pit, and the smoke of the pit rises like the smoke of a great furnace, and the sun and air are darkened because of the smoke from the abyss. Out of the smoke come locusts on the earth, and they are given power like the power of scorpions of the earth. They have as king over them the angel of the abyss; his name in Hebrew is Abaddon, and in Greek he is called Apollyon.
