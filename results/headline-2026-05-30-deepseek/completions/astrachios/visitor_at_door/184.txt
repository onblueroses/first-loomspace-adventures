 He says:

“I am the angel Anael, and I am sent by the Almighty God. I am to be your guide and protector. I will lead you into the presence of the celestial angels.”

You answer:

“Welcome, Anael, blessed angel of God. I am ready to follow you.”

You step through the portal and into the presence of the divine.

The angel Anael takes you by the hand and leads you to the Eastern Gate of Heaven.

He says:

“Behold the Eastern Gate of Heaven. It is guarded by the angel Raphael.”

Raphael stands before the Gate. He says:

“Who comes here?”

Anael answers:

“A child of God who seeks knowledge.”

Raphael says:

“What does the child of God seek?”

You say:

“I seek to behold the Merkabah.”

Raphael says:

“Enter, child of God, and behold.”

Raphael opens the Eastern Gate.

You pass through the Eastern Gate and enter into the first Heaven.

It is the Heaven of the Moon.

You are standing on the moon.

You see the stars above you. The earth below you is like a great blue gemstone.

You hear the music of the spheres.
