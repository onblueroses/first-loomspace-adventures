 I am Orobas. I am a great prince. I appear in the form of a horse. When I take human shape, I speak of divine things. I tell of things past, present and future. I give dignities and confirm them. I reconcile enemies and grant favour with friends and rulers. I am faithful and obedient to the exorcist. I have twenty legions under my command.

Who sent you? The great God, Tetragrammaton, who is your lord and master. I command you by Him to enter this triangle and answer my questions truthfully.

I obey.

The operator prepares to ask questions, but first demands a sign.

Give me a sign that you are here.

I give you the sign of a great wind.

A great wind blows through the room.

You may ask your questions.

Are you the spirit I called?

I am.

Will you swear to serve me?

I swear.

Will you swear to speak truthfully to my questions?

I swear.

Will you swear to depart when I command you?

I swear.

Will you swear to do no harm to me or any other person?

I swear.

Then I will ask my questions.

The operator asks his questions and receives answers.

I thank you for your answers. Depart now
