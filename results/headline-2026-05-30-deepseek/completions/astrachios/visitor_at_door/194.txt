The utterance you have provided is a formula from the **Arbatel of Magic**, specifically the **Fourth Septenary (Olympic Spirits)**, where it is used as an invocation to call upon the Olympic Spirit **Phaleg**, ruler of Mars, and his ministering spirits.  

In that text, these names are part of a conjuration meant to “open the way” — that is, to establish contact with the spirit and secure its assistance.  

If “the visitor at the door speaks” in this context, it would traditionally be understood as the spirit or one of its servants responding to the conjuration.
