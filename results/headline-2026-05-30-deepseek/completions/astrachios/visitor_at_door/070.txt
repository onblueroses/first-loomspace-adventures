 She says:

“I am the one who has suffered at your hands. I am the one you betrayed.”

She is tall, her skin is like the surface of the sea when the moon shines on it. Her hair is like weed in the shallows. Her gown is green, and her cloak is blue. She says:

“I am the one you drowned.”

She says:

“I am the one who waited in the depths, who waited for so long, until you came to me.”

She says:

“I am the one who rose from the waters. I am the one who left the seas.”

She says:

“I am the one who has done with tears. I am the one who has done with waiting.”

She says:

“I am the one whose heart is broken.”

She says:

“I am the one who will break your heart.”

She says:

“I am the one who will be paid for.”

The woman in the green dress and the blue cloak, the woman with hair like seaweed and skin like moonlight on the sea, she smiles, and she says:

“I am the one who comes to you.”

And then she says:

“I am the one who comes from the sea.”
