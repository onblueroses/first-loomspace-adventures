 He is known as Orobas. He says:

Orobas: I am the great Prince Orobas. I serve the Master of the Art. I speak truthfully of all things, earthly, heavenly, and infernal. I bestow dignities and confirm friendships. I answer truly concerning divinity and the creation of the world. I am faithful and obedient.

Magician: Welcome, Orobas. Enter and be seated.

Orobas enters and sits in the seat to the west. The magician continues:

Magician: Orobas, I require that you manifest before me in a fair human form.

The spirit Orobas manifests in a fair human form.

Magician: Orobas, I require that you take an oath upon this seal and characters of Solomon that you will answer truthfully all that I shall ask of you.

The spirit Orobas swears an oath upon the seal and characters of Solomon to answer truthfully all that he shall be asked.
