**You speak the names, and the air grows still.**

A resonant voice, neither wholly within nor without the chamber, replies:

*"The seals are spoken. The watchwords are known. The way is no longer barred.*
*I am the Guardian of the Threshold. You have called with the names of power, the Genium Domos—the Spirits of the Houses. What is sought beyond this door? Speak, and if your intent is pure and will is firm, passage may be granted."*

**The presence waits, ancient and attentive, for your purpose.**
