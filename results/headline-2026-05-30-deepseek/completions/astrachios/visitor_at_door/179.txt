 He says:

"I am the messenger of Pentagram, and this is my name: Scorpio, and I stand in this place and give the sign of the hidden light."

You say:

"I receive you, Scorpio. Enter in the name of the Lord."

You and the visitor exchange the LVX Signs.

You say:

"Child of earth, adore your Lord!"

You give the sign of the cross.

You say:

"Holy art thou, Lord of the Universe!"

You give the sign of the rose cross.

You say:

"Holy art thou, whom nature hath not formed!"

You give the sign of the cross.

You say:

"Holy art thou, the vast and the mighty one!"

You give the sign of the rose cross.

You say:

"Lord of the light and of the darkness!"

You give the sign of the cross.

You say:

"Thee therefore do we adore, and Thee do we invoke."

You give the sign of the rose cross.

You say:

"O Thou who art concealed and yet apparent, who art in all things and in whom all things are, who art silent and yet speaketh, who art despised and yet adored, who art in heaven and in the abyss, who art in the water and in the fire
