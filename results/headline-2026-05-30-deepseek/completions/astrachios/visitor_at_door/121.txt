 “I am the angel of the Lord of Hosts, called Gabriel.”

I ask the name of the angel, and it is given. I ask the sign, and it is given. The angel appears as a young man with a hawk’s head, robed in blue and wearing a red crown. He holds a sickle in his right hand.

I ask the angel to serve me in the work of the art, and he consents, asking what I will. I ask him to bring me into the presence of the planetary intelligence of Luna. He agrees, and begins to chant in a high voice:

Ariel, Ariel, Ariel, Ariel, Ariel, Ariel, Ariel.

As he chants, a shimmer appears in the air before him, and an angelic form appears. This is Ariel, Intelligence of the Moon, who appears as a young man with a silver crown, holding a silver bow in his left hand and a silver arrow in his right.

I ask the angel to serve me in the work of the art, and he consents, asking what I will. I ask him to bring me into the presence of the planetary spirit of Luna. He agrees, and begins to chant in a high voice:

Chashmodai, Chashmodai
