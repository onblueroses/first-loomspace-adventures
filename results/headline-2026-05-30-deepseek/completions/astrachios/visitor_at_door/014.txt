 He says:

“I am the prophet, who hath eyes and feet. I am the messenger of Him whom thou hast invoked. The Lord hath permitted that hidden things be made manifest unto thee. He commanded that I appear before thee. Name thy requests, for I am the angel who presideth over the day.”

He says: “I am the angel Salatheel, who hath been sent by God.”

The operator may now ask questions.

“I have been sent by the Almighty to reveal those things which thou hast desired to know.”

Now the operator asks: “What is my name?”

He replies: “God knoweth thy name.”

The operator says: “I beseech thee by the holy names of God, El, Elohim, Elohe, Zebaoth, Elion, Escerche, Adonai, Jah, Tetragrammaton, Sadai, to reveal unto me thy name.”
