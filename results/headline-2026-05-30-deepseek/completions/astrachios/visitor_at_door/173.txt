 The words are secret, but the gist is that he wishes to see the lord of the house.

Answer: “Who is there?”

He replies: “The true God.”

“What is his name?”

“Zeus.”

“Zeus is my head. I am Alpha and Omega.”

The visitor: “If you are the true God, answer by your acrostic.”

Answer: “I shall answer. Ask, and I will reply.”

The visitor: “I am Moses, your prophet, to whom you have transmitted your mysteries celebrated by Israel. You have revealed to me the greatness of your name. I have come to see you face to face, not in enigmas. Show me your nature.”

Answer: “My nature is in my holy names. Enter.”

The visitor: “I enter and go out.”

The god of the house appears.

Moses: “Lord, show me your glory.”

God: “No man can see my face and live. But I will put you in a cleft of the rock, and you will see my back.”
