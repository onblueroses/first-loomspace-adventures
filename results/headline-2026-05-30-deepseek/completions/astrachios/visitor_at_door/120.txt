The names you have spoken are resonant with certain Western esoteric and Solomonic traditions, often associated with ceremonial invocation, seals, and the summoning or dismissal of spiritual forces.

The phrase **"Speak the names that open the way"** suggests an opening formula—a verbal key to cross a threshold, whether literal or metaphysical.  
The list itself contains a blend of:

- Angelic and divine names (Adonai, Elohim, Daniel, Geburahel, Samoel)
- Names found in medieval and Renaissance grimoires such as the *Key of Solomon* or *The Lesser Key of Solomon* (e.g., Bael, Scirlin)
- Possibly constructed or corrupted names from older sources (Astrachios, Bedrimuliel, Arabonas, etc.)
- The closing **"Genium Domos"** could be interpreted from Latin as **"Houses of the Spirits"** or **"Dwellings of the Genii,"** perhaps signifying the realm or hierarchy being addressed.

If **"the visitor at the door speaks"** after this incantation, it implies the formula was successful—the way has been opened, and an entity or intelligence now communicates from the other side.
