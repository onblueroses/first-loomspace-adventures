The visitor at the door speaks. His name is Agares. He is a duke. He appears as an old man riding upon a crocodile, carrying a hawk on his fist. He speaks with a rough voice. He teaches all languages. He makes runaways return. He causes earthquakes. He rules over thirty-one legions of spirits.
