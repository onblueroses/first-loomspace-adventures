 “I am he that sitteth between Chemos and Nergal. I am he to whom belongeth power and empire. I am the Angel King, the strong Chus, the Thrice-powerful one, because I have power in heaven, earth and hell. I am the First and the Last.”

“I am he that commandeth all powers and empires, from the rising of the sun to the going down of the same. I am he that sitteth in the great sigh of heaven. I am he that governeth the seven planets and the wheels thereof. I am he whose name is written with one letter. I am he whose name is written with three letters. I am he whose name is written with four letters. I am he whose name is written with six letters. I am he whose name is written with seven letters. I am he whose name is written with eight letters. I am he whose name is written with nine letters. I am he whose name is written with ten letters. I am he whose name is written with eleven letters. I am he whose name is written with twelve letters.”
