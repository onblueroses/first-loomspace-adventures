And the guardian of the door says:

The God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, the God of Jacob, the God of the Prophets, the God of the Apostles, and the God of the Evangelists, commands thee to depart, and to return to thine own place.

The visitor at the door speaks. The visitor says:

I am the King of Kings and the Lord of Lords, and the only Potentate of the Universe.

And the guardian of the door says:

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, I command thee to depart, and to return to thine own place.

The visitor at the door speaks. The visitor says:

I am the Alpha and Omega, the Beginning and the End, the First and the Last.

And the guardian of the door says:

In the name of Jesus Christ, our Saviour, who was crucified for our sins, I command thee to depart, and to return to thine own place.
