 He says:

I have been sent from above. The angel Anael has sent me, and the power of the great god Sadai. No one can see God and live. Therefore I have been sent.

I am here to grant your wishes.

Speak. What do you desire? Do you wish to converse with spirits? I can open the way. Do you wish to heal? I can grant this power. Do you wish to know secret things? I can reveal them. Do you wish to know the future? I can unveil it. Do you wish to obtain treasure? I can tell you where it is hidden. Do you wish to understand the languages of animals? I can give you this understanding.

Speak. What do you desire?

The one who holds the wand says:

I desire to see and speak with the great angel Anael.

The visitor says:

This is a great thing you ask. For Anael is among the highest of the high, one of the seven regents who see the face of God. He is the angel of love, the angel of passion, the angel of desire.
