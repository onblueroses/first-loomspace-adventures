*   **Divine and Angelic Names:** Several names resemble or directly reference Judeo-Christian divine or angelic names (e.g., **Adonai**, **Elohim**, **Daniel**, **Geburahel** [related to Geburah, a Sephirah], **Samoel** [often associated with Samael]).
*   **Solomonic Tradition:** Many appear in texts related to or inspired by the **Key of Solomon** or other Solomonic grimoires, where such names are used to call upon spirits, constrain them, or open gates.
*   **"The Visitor at the Door":** This phrase suggests a ritual context—possibly an invocation where these names "open the way" for an entity (the "visitor") to manifest or communicate.
