 It is a man, but his skin is black like soot. He wears a golden crown and his eyes are black stones. He holds a sword in his right hand. He is seated in a chariot drawn by four horses. The horses are black and their eyes are burning coals.

He is the Great King of the South and he is called Amaymon. He teaches geometry and all liberal sciences. He reveals things past, present, and future. He reveals the location of hidden treasures. He reconciles friends and foes. He commands thirty-six legions of spirits.

He is not alone. There are spirits all about. Some are like men, but with the heads of beasts. Some are like women, but they have wings and talons. Some are like great serpents, but they have the faces of men. Some are like worms, but with many heads and many eyes.
