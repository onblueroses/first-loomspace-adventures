 What else.

a stone that moves through the water

and carries us with it, and we

are not what we thought.

## The Dream of the Unified Field

I

And I was lifted up in light,

I was in the midst of it,

and it was within me:

like the radiance before birth,

and after, too, when you

remember what you were—

or before death, when you're

filled with the light of this world,

and then you're gone.

But this was not death,

this was vision,

the silence of things

under the aspect of light,

the light in which the world

is gathered, whole.

II

The snow was falling

and it was Christmas.

I saw the snow come down

onto the surface of the pond

and vanish into the water.

I saw it fall into the water

and lift again,

transformed, into the air.

I saw the snow cover

the earth, as if the earth

were the figure, and the snow

the ground, and it seemed

as if the earth had left

a trace of its slow movement
