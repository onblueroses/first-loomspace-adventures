 What is it?

I have given you the names. Use them.

It is the woman. The woman.

She sits before you in the red chair.

She is wearing the dress she wore at her wedding. The dress is white.

Her hair is loose about her shoulders.

She is wearing the pearls her mother gave her. Her hands are folded in her lap.

She is not smiling. She is not sad.

She is looking at you.

She says:

He is not coming home.

You are alone now.

You have always been alone.

The woman rises from her chair.

She goes to the door.

She turns and looks at you once more.

She says:

The house is yours.

And then she is gone.
