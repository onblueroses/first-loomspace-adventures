 The demons who wait for their master. The man who calls them.

The demons wear many forms. Some are thin, with faces of sharpened bone. Some are fat and squat, with skin the colour of blue stone. Some are tall, with faces of masks. Some have no faces at all.

The man wears the form of a man. He is tall, thin. His skin is white as chalk. His eyes are red. He wears a suit of dark cloth.

The demons know him. He is their master.

He speaks:

Hear me, O demons, O servants of Hell. Hear me, and tremble. For I am Lucifer. I am Satan. I am the Devil. I am He. I am your master.

The demons bow their heads. They kneel.

He speaks:

I am the Lord of Hell. I am the King of Pain. I am the Ruler of Damnation. I am the Prince of Darkness. I am the Adversary. I am the Enemy. I am He.

The demons prostrate themselves. They lie on the floor.
