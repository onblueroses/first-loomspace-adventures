The next morning, the scent of myrrh in the air, the lamp extinguished, the cold morning air, the window still open. The room like a temple, like a sanctuary. The room like a tomb.

I stood, walked to the window. The world outside was gray, the sky like slate, the city silent. The air smelled of rain. I closed the window.

The mirror hung on the wall opposite, and I saw myself in it. I looked older, but perhaps it was only the light. I looked tired.

The floor was still marked with the chalk circle, the lines of the pentacle, the words written around its edges. The candle was a pool of wax. The incense had burned out.

I walked to the mirror, looked into it. I saw myself, but I saw something else as well. A shadow behind me, a shape in the glass. I turned, but there was nothing there. I looked back into the mirror, and the shadow was gone.

I took the mirror down from the wall, wrapped it in cloth, put it away.
