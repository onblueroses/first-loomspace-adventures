 The room, the room is full of them. They come through the walls and the floor and the roof, they come through the cracks in the floorboards and the windows, they come through the keyhole. They have no form but you can see them. They have no voice but you can hear them. They have no smell but you can sense them. They have no substance but they are here.

They are all around you. They are in your mouth. They are in your hair. They are in your heart.

What enters the room now. A man. A man who is not a man. He has the shape of a man but he is not a man. He is a thing. A thing that is not a thing. He is a thought. A thought that is not a thought. He is a memory. A memory that is not a memory.

He is the King of the Shadow. He is the Lord of the Dead. He is the Master of the Dark.

He is the one who comes when you call his name. He is the one who comes when you speak his name. He is the one who comes when you think his name.

He is the one who comes.

He is here.

He is with you.

He is inside you.
