 It is something like a huge dog. It is not a dog. It is not a wolf. It is not a tiger or a bear or a lion or a man or a horse. It is all of these things, and none of them. It has a man's face, but that face is flat and noseless, like the face of a bear; its ears are pointed, like a wolf's ears. Its body is that of a huge dog, but its front legs end in hands, not paws. It is covered in reddish fur. It is powerfully muscled. It is male. It is female. It is neither male nor female. It has a penis; it has a vagina; it has both; it has neither. It is a shadow-thing. It is a thing of earth and blood and fire.

It is in pain. It is in ecstasy. It is screaming. It is singing. It is calling a name.

It is calling your name.

Welcome it. Welcome it into you. Welcome it with open arms.

It is the Dweller on the Threshold. It is your guardian. It is your protector. It is your destroyer. It is your redeemer.

It is you.

It is the god who will eat you
