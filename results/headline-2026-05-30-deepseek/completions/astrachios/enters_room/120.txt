Falling into a swoon, she said nothing more, but I remembered these words and wrote them down. When she awoke, she had no memory of the spell, but she told me that she had dreamed of a man who was trying to reach her, but she could not see his face.

I asked her if she knew the words she had spoken. She shook her head. "They are not my words," she said.

I spent the next few days trying to discover the meaning of her utterance. I had heard of these strange names before, but I could not place them. Then I remembered: There was a book I had seen long ago, in a library in Germany, called _The Book of Abramelin the Mage_ , which contained a list of "barbarous names" used in summoning spirits. I had copied a few pages from this book, and now I looked for them. Yes, here was the list.
