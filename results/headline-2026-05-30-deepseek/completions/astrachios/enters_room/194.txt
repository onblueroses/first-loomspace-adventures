 From the darkest corner of the room, a shadow appears. It is humanoid, but its features are indistinct; it seems to be made of dark smoke. It speaks in a voice like a distant thunderstorm.

The shadow: I am the shadow of the void, the keeper of forgotten secrets. Why have you summoned me?

Samael: (voice trembling but resolute) I seek knowledge of the abyss, the truths that lie beyond the veil of reality.

The shadow: Knowledge comes with a price. Are you prepared to pay it?

Samael: I am prepared.

The shadow: Then let the ritual begin. Speak the incantation that binds us.

Samael: (reading from the book) Per tenebras ad lucem, per umbram ad veritatem. Ligate hoc spiritu, et revela arcana quae abscondita sunt.

The shadow begins to swirl, forming shapes and symbols in the air. The temperature in the room drops further, and a faint whispering can be heard, as if many voices are speaking at once.
