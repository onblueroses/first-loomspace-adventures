 The thing that has been summoned.

The thing that looks like a man, but is not a man. It is the colour of a storm sky, and has two sets of wings: one pair folded around it like a cape, the other spread as if for flight. Its head is crowned with a diadem of what seem to be live coals, burning in the darkness. And it has three heads: one like a dog, one like a griffin, and one like a man. And all three heads are speaking at once.

— You have called me, and I am come. Why?

— I have a question for you.

— Ask it.

— How may a man be brought back from the dead?

— Who is this man?

— His name is John Dee.

The thing that has been summoned pauses. Its three heads confer with each other in a language that sounds like stones grinding together. Finally, it speaks again.

— This is forbidden knowledge.

— I demand it.

— You demand nothing of me. I am a prince of the order of Potentates, a servant of Him who sits upon the throne. I am not yours to command.
