 It is a shape of smoke, a shadow that is not cast by anything. It is the shape of a man. It is not a man.

Do not be afraid. It is not the enemy. It is a servant. It is a bringer of news.

It stands before you. It waits for you to speak.

What do you ask of it?

What news from the world?

What news from the future?

What news of the enemy?

What news of the King?

The shadow answers:

Your king is dead.

The kingdom is ended.

The enemy is at the gate.

The world is destroyed.

You are alone.

The shadow is fading.

The room is cold.

The candle is out.

You are alone.

You are not alone.

There is a sound.

It is the sound of a key turning in a lock.

It is the sound of a door opening.

It is the sound of footsteps.

It is the sound of a voice.

It is the voice of a man.

It is the voice of the enemy.

He is here.

He has found you.

What do you do?

Do you run?

Do you hide?

Do you fight?

Do you pray?

Do you curse?

Do you scream?

Do you laugh?

Do you die?

No.
