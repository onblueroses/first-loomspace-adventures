 This is the secret name of the angel, the one who has come to help you:—

O great, powerful, and holy angel, Bathiel, I conjure you by the holy names of God, Adonai, Elohim, El, Elohi, Zebaoth, Elion, Escerchie, Jah, Tetragrammaton, Sadai, that you come to me here quickly and without delay from wherever you are, and that you bring me grace, love, and peace, and that you make [N] love me.

Then speak to the angel. You may ask for anything, but you must ask politely and respectfully. You must not demand, nor should you beg. Simply ask, as one equal to another. And when you have asked, and the angel has nodded in agreement, then you must thank the angel and bid him farewell. He will leave, and the room will be empty.

Now you must close the circle. Take the knife, and point it to the south. Say:

Powers of the south, I thank you for attending, and I declare this rite ended. Go in peace.

Repeat to the west, the north, and the east.
