 The smell of roses is thick and sickly sweet. The figure of a woman is glimpsed. A dead woman. She was hanged. Her face is swollen and discoloured. She wears a gown of white silk and a cap of white silk. She seems to float. She moves towards me. Her voice is low and hoarse, the words forced out with effort.

‘I am Hecate. I am the gatekeeper.’

She touches my cheek. Her hand is cold. She says:

‘I am the gatekeeper. I am Hecate. I am the gatekeeper.’

She repeats this many times. She speaks more and more softly. She leans closer and closer. Her lips are almost touching my ear. She whispers:

‘I am the gatekeeper. I am Hecate.’

She repeats this over and over. She speaks so softly I cannot be sure I am hearing her correctly. Then she says:

‘I am the gatekeeper. I am Hecate. I am the gatekeeper.’
