 The sea of stones and the bed of ice. The bed of needles and the bed of nails. The bed of fire and the bed of blood.

She stands there, the great whore, the great mother, the great goddess. Her hair is wild and black, her skin is pale as milk. Her eyes are as dark as deep water. She wears a crown of stars and a gown of fine silk, embroidered with pearls and rubies. She holds a golden cup in her hand, and she offers it to you.

She says: Drink of my cup, and know the secrets of the world. Drink of my cup, and know the secrets of the heart. Drink of my cup, and know the secrets of the soul.

She says: I am the queen of heaven and earth. I am the mistress of life and death. I am the source of all wisdom and power. I am the one who gives and takes away. I am the one who loves and hates. I am the one who creates and destroys.

She says: I am your mother and your lover. I am your sister and your daughter. I am your friend and your enemy. I am your goddess and your demon.
