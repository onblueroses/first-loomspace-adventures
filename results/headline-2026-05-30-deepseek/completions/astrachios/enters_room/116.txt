 What enters your life now.

III.

What comes through the door now is not a man.

Its eyes are like fire, its face a long black scar, and its hands are like the hands of a man, but tipped with claws.

What comes through the door now is a devil.

It takes you by the hand and it leads you to the window, and it shows you the world.

And it says: I can give you this.

And you say: What must I give you?

And it says: Your soul.

And you say: Yes.

IV.

But that is not the way it is done.

You must say: I have sold my soul already.

And if you can say that, then the devil will teach you what he knows, and give you what he has.

Then he will give you the world.
