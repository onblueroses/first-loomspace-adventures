 It is not a person. It is something that walks on two legs, but it is not a person.

I don’t think it is an animal either.

It is like a person but it is not a person. It is like a person the way a crab is like a spider.

It is a dead person.

It is something that was once a person.

It is something that should not be.

Its skin is grey and peeling. Its eyes are flat and lifeless. Its lips are pulled back from its teeth.

It is dressed in the remains of a suit. It is holding a briefcase. Its fingers are long and thin and end in sharp nails.

It is standing in the centre of the circle.

It is looking at me.

It is waiting.

I am not afraid. I am prepared for this. I know what to do.

I speak the words that bind it:

Zati, Zata, Baptismos, Patac, X, Y, D, Alpha, Omega, El, Elohim, Elohe, Zebaoth, Shaddai, Ja, Agla, Tetragrammaton, Sadai, Mesiach, Emmanuel, Sabaoth, Athanatos, Ischyros, Hagios, O Theos
