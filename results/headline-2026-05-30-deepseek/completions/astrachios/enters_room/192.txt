 A smell, like cinnamon. A taste like honey. A feeling like water on your skin.

The demon appears. It is a man with a lion's head. Its skin is green, its eyes are black.

It says: I am Agares, duke of the east. I make runaways come back, and those who stand still run. I teach all languages, and I put to flight all enemies. I cause earthquakes. I was of the order of virtues. I rule thirty-one legions.

You say: I have a question.

It says: Ask.

You say: How may I be released from the burden of my own self?

It says: What is self? I am a demon. I am legion. I am many. I am one. I am nothing. I am a thought. I am a thought of God. I am a thought of God that God forgot. I am that which was forgotten. I am that which remembers. I am that which is forgotten again. I am a name spoken. I am a name forgotten. I am a name remembered. I am a name spoken. I am a name.
