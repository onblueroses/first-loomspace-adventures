 The air cools and darkens. There is a presence. It is heavy and old, terrible, but not malevolent. It is patient. It has been waiting for a very long time.

You feel it watching you.

You say: “I am a man, made in the image of God. You are a spirit. I command you: speak with me.”

The presence hesitates. You can feel it thinking. It is old, and slow, and careful.

It speaks. Its voice is the rumble of a rockslide, deep in the earth. It says: “I am the guardian of the gate. I am the watcher at the threshold. I am the keeper of the key. What do you seek?”

You say: “I seek knowledge. I seek power. I seek to understand the secrets of the universe.”

The presence says: “The knowledge you seek is dangerous. The power you seek is corrupting. The secrets you seek will destroy you.”

You say: “I am not afraid. I am prepared. I have studied. I have purified myself. I am ready.”
