 We could call it a “presence,” but that would be an understatement. A dense haze fills the room, a haze that is both smoke and cloud. It billows and swirls, and I can feel the power that emanates from it. It is a “presence” in the same way that a hurricane is a presence. This is a being of immense power.

I feel the power of the being enter me. The power fills my body and tingles my skin. It is a feeling of immense strength. I am strong. I am a being of power.

It is time to act. I hold my right hand in the air and speak:

“In the name of the living God, I command you to show yourself.”

The haze begins to solidify into a humanoid form. It becomes a man made of smoke. He is large and powerfully built. He wears a crown and a robe. He has a beard.

He speaks, and his voice is like a thunderstorm: “I am the Great King of the South, Egyn.”

“Greetings, Egyn. I have called you here to ask a question.”

“Speak your question.”

“I seek the knowledge of how to create a servitor.”
