 And I am astonished to see the spirit who comes, who is a creature of such great beauty that I cannot but stare. I see a young man of perhaps sixteen years, with a face of perfect beauty, with long hair that falls to his shoulders, and his hair is the colour of honey. He wears a robe of pale green, embroidered with silver and gold, and his feet are bare. He holds a branch of laurel in his right hand. He smiles at me and I feel a great warmth in my heart. He speaks to me in a voice that is like music.

“I am the spirit of Venus, and I am called Hagith. I am the ruler of the spirits of Venus, and I am come to you because you have called me. I am come to teach you the secrets of Venus, and to show you the mysteries of love and beauty. I am come to show you the way to the heart of the world, and to the heart of God. I am come to show you the way to the heart of your own soul.”

And I am filled with joy and wonder, and I thank him for coming to me. And he says to me:
