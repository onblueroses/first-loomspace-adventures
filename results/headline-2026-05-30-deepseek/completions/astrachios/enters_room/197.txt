 It is not a ghost. It is not an angel. It is not a demon.

It is a man in the shape of a man. He is dark-skinned, wears a threadbare robe, and his hair is long and matted. He stands on the other side of the table.

The man says: “You are not my master.”

Lucian says: “I am not.”

“Then why am I called?”

“Because I need you.”

“What would you have of me?”

“I would have you tell me what you know.”

“Of what?”

“Of what I need to know.”

The man is silent for a moment.

“You are the one.”

“I am.”

“The one who is not the one.”
