### THE BOOK OF THE DEAD

#### BY FRED CHAPPELL

_From his first novel,_ The Inkling _(1965), Fred Chappell has been an uncommonly versatile and prolific writer. His books include poetry, fiction, and essays, and his honors include the Bollingen Prize and the T. S. Eliot Prize. He was poet laureate of North Carolina from 1997 to 2002. His novels include_ Dagon _(1968),_ The Gaudy Place _(1972), and_ Look Back All the Green Valley _(1999). In addition to his own works, Chappell has collaborated on translations of the poems of Catullus and Gérard de Nerval. He was inducted into the North Carolina Literary Hall of Fame in 2006.
