If we follow the grimoiric logic to its conclusion:  
What enters the room now is not a single named demon or angel, but an **atmosphere of focused spiritual intelligence**, a **presence of illuminated minds** from the celestial hierarchy, ready to impart wisdom or gnosis to the properly prepared operator.
