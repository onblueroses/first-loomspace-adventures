## CHAPTER 47

The sound of rain woke her.

Lying on her side, she could see the drops hitting the windowpanes, smearing the gray light of a late winter afternoon.

She was in her own bed, in her own bedroom.

She looked at the clock on her bedside table. It was 4:30 P.M. She'd slept for two days.

She sat up slowly. The room was dim, the curtains partially closed. The rain was a soft patter on the windowpanes, a steady beat on the roof overhead.

She was in her pajamas. She had no memory of changing out of her clothes, no memory of coming upstairs.

She had no memory of anything.

She looked at her hands, turning them over and over, as if she might find some clue to what had happened.
