 Do you see it? Listen to the sound. The sound of its breathing. The sound of its voice.

Gather it into the circle. Bring it to the altar. Bind it in the water. In the oil. In the blood. In the wine. In the salt. In the bread. In the smoke.

Do you feel its breath? Its breath is hot. Its breath is cold.

The sound of its voice is the sound of a thousand voices. The sound of its voice is the sound of a single voice. The sound of its voice is the sound of your own voice.

It is speaking to you. It is speaking through you.

What does it say?

It says: I am the spirit of the crossroads. I am the spirit of the threshold. I am the spirit of the door. I am the spirit of the gate. I am the spirit of the key. I am the spirit of the lock. I am the spirit of the way. I am the spirit of the path. I am the spirit of the journey. I am the spirit of the return.

I am the spirit of the beginning. I am the spirit of the end.

I am the spirit of the circle. I am the spirit of the square. I am the spirit
