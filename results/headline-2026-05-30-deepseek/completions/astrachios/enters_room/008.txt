 Something black and thin and wild. Something that takes a moment to be recognized as human, something with a face burned beyond recognition, something that crawls across the floor toward the circle.

“God in heaven!” says Sir William. “What have you done?”

“I have called,” says John.

“What is it?” says Sir William.

“It is the ghost of a murderer.”

“What murderer?” says Sir William.

“The murderer of your friend.”

Sir William’s face goes pale. He stares at the ghost on the floor. “Are you sure?” he says.

“Yes,” says John. “Now, take the knife.”

Sir William takes the knife. He stares at the ghost.

“Stab it,” says John.

Sir William looks at John. He looks at the ghost. He looks at the knife. He walks toward the circle.

The ghost sees the knife. It looks at Sir William. It looks at John. It tries to flee, but the circle keeps it in.

Sir William takes the knife and plunges it into the ghost’s chest.

The ghost screams.

Sir William pulls the knife out.

The ghost screams again.

Sir William stabs the ghost again.

The ghost screams again.

Sir William pulls the knife out.
