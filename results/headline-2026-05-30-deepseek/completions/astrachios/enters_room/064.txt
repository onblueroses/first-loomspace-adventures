 What forms itself on the floor. It is a beast, a thing of scales and horns, a thing of hoof and claw.

The door opens. A man enters the room. He is a man, nothing more. He wears a black suit, white shirt, black tie. He could be an undertaker, or a waitress in an expensive restaurant. He is carrying a bottle of wine.

He says, "I am sorry. I am late. Traffic, you know?"

The beast on the floor says nothing.

The man looks down. "Oh," he says. "You've started without me."

The beast grunts, and gestures with a clawed thumb toward the pentagram chalked on the floor.

"Yes," says the man. "I see. Well, it's a little too late to worry about that." He puts the bottle on the table. He walks over to the beast, stares down at it. "You know," he says, "I really don't like your being here."

The beast does not move. It starts to say something, but the man raises a finger, and it is silent.
