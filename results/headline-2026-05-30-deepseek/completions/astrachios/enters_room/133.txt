 A shape of shadows. A shape of smoke. A shape of nothing. It is tall and thin, though it has no limbs to speak of. It has no face, but it has a mouth—a wide mouth, full of too many teeth. It has no eyes, but it sees me anyway.

It is the thing that lives in the hole in the world.

It is the thing that is older than the world.

It is the thing that was there before there was anything else.

It is the thing that will be there after there is nothing else.

It is the thing that will eat the world.

It is the thing that will eat God.

It is the thing that will eat you.

It is the thing that will eat me.

It is the thing that is hungry.

It is the thing that is always hungry.

It is the thing that is hungry now.

It is the thing that is hungry for me.

It is the thing that is hungry for what I will give it.

It is the thing that is hungry for what I will ask of it.

It is the thing that will eat my soul.
