 The black wings of its shadow cover the floor, then the walls, then the ceiling. The air grows cold, and the candle flames shrink down to tiny blue points of light. The room darkens, and the creature stands in the center of the circle, holding out a hand to you.

You feel a sudden surge of fear, but you push it down. You have come this far. There is no turning back.

The creature speaks. Its voice is a dry rustle, like dead leaves blowing across a stone floor. It says, “I am Asmodeus, prince of demons. Why have you summoned me?”

You take a deep breath and say, “I wish to make a deal.”

Asmodeus laughs, a sound like breaking glass. “Of course you do. Everyone wants to make a deal. What do you offer?”

You tell him what you want, and what you are willing to give in return. Asmodeus listens, his head cocked to one side. When you are finished, he says, “Very well. I accept your offer. But know this: if you fail to uphold your end of the bargain, I will take your soul.”

You nod, your heart pounding. “I understand.”
