 It is a man in his forties, dressed in a grey suit. His shoes are polished and his shirt is white. He is carrying a briefcase. He takes off his glasses and wipes them with a handkerchief.

– Good evening, he says. My name is Mr Smith. I represent the firm of Smith and Smith. I have been instructed to act on behalf of your ex-wife, Mrs Clare.

– I see, I say.

– Please accept my apologies for disturbing you at home, but I need to ask you some questions. It won't take long.

– Come in, I say.

He steps inside and takes a seat. I close the door and sit opposite him.

– Do you mind if I smoke? he asks.

– Go ahead.

He takes out a packet of cigarettes and taps one out.

– Would you like one?

– No thank you.

He lights up and inhales deeply.

– So, he says. First of all, I'd like to know if you have any contact with your ex-wife.

– No, I say.

– None at all?

– None at all.

– Not even by letter or telephone?

– No.

– I see. And when was the last time you saw her?
