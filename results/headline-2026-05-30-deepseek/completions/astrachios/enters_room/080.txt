The man who enters the room is gaunt, tall, cadaverous. His skin is stretched tight over his skull, and his eyes are hollows of shadow. He wears a dark suit, and he stands there, looking at me. He says, "I am the King with Three Staves. I am the Hanged Man. I am the Tower in flames. I am the Chariot. I am Death. I am the Sun and the Moon and the World. I am the Fool."

I say, "I know who you are. You are my brother."

He says, "I am your brother, and I am your king. I am also your murderer."

I say, "I do not believe you."

He says, "I am the man who sent the woman with the hair of gold to your bed. I am the man who told her what to say to you. I am the man who told her how to kill you."
