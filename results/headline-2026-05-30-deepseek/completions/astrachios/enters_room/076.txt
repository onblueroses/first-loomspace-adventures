### **III**

She sat on a table. She was naked, but he did not feel that she was naked. He felt that she was something else, but he did not know what that was.

She was small and slender, and her skin was the colour of bleached bone. Her hair was long and black and straight, and her eyes were large and black. She was beautiful, but he did not feel that she was beautiful. He felt that she was something else, but he did not know what that was.

She was smiling.

He stood in front of her. He was naked too.

She said, 'You have come.'

He said, 'Yes.'

She said, 'You have done well.'

He said, 'Thank you.'

She said, 'You have brought the boy.'

He said, 'Yes.'

She said, 'You have done well.'

He said, 'Thank you.'

She said, 'You have brought the boy, and he is ready.'

He said, 'Yes.'

She said, 'He is ready.'

He said, 'Yes.'
