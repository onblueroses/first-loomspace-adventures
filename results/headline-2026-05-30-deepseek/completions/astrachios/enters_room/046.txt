 It is the shadow of a man and the shadow of a woman. They are both wrapped in the same garment.

The man speaks first.

I am the son of the father. I am the father of the son. I am the flame that burns in every heart of man, and in the core of every star. I am life, and the giver of life, and therefore the knower and master of death. I am Osiris, torn to pieces, and scattered in all things and at all ends of the world, and gathering together my limbs, I come to live as a king in my peaceful kingdom, sweeping all things before my breath, taking all things to my breast, transmuting all things into the image and likeness of myself. I am the sun in his splendour, the lion in his fury, the king in his justice. I am the warrior arrayed for battle. See my bright blade; it is called reason, and with it do I smite the broad earth, and it becomes my footstool. With it do I smite the petty kingdoms of the earth, and they become the empire of my godhead. I am the giver of law, and the administrator of law, and the law itself. I am the serpent
