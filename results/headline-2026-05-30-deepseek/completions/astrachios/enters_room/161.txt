 The smell of sulfur and iron. It is something terrible. Something that hates the world. Something that hates you.

It looks like a man. It is wearing a robe. It has a man’s face. It is smiling.

It says: “What do you want?”

You tell it: “I want to be rich.”

It smiles wider. It says: “I can do that.”

You tell it: “I want to be famous.”

It says: “I can do that.”

You tell it: “I want to be loved.”

It says: “I can do that.”

You tell it: “I want to be remembered.”

It says: “I can do that.”

You tell it: “I want to live forever.”

It says: “I can do that.”

It says: “What will you give me?”

You tell it: “Anything.”

It says: “Then we have a deal.”

It holds out its hand. You shake it.

The circle breaks.

It is gone.

You are alone.

You are not alone.

You are never alone again.

It is inside you now.

It is watching you.

It is waiting.

It is hungry.

It is always hungry.

It wants to be fed.

It wants to be free.
