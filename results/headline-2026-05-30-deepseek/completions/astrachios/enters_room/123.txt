 Is it a man or a woman? Is it one or many?

Speak to it. It will answer. But be careful: it is not obliged to tell the truth. It will say what it thinks you want to hear. It will say things that are ambiguous and confusing. It will lie.

Ask what you need to know. Then send it away.
