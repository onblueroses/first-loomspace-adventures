 It is tall, seven feet or more, its skin the color of ink. It has the head of a wolf, and its eyes are pupilless, like polished silver. It wears a dark red robe, embroidered with gold, and it carries a scepter of bone. It smiles at me, and I see its teeth are needles.

It says: I am a servant of the Fifth Angel, of whom you know. I am a nightmare and a dream. I have the keys to your soul, and I have come to claim my price.

I say: I am ready to pay the price. But first, you must give me what I desire.

It says: Name it.

I say: I wish to walk into the dreams of others, to see their fears and their desires. To walk in worlds that others have imagined. To know the stuff of which dreams are made.

It says: That is a small thing to us, but it will cost you dear.

I say: What is the price?
