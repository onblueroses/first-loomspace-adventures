Thee I invoke, the Tongueless One:

Thee, that didst create the Earth and the Heavens;
Thee, that didst create the Night and the Day;
Thee, that didst create the Darkness and the Light.

Thou art Asarinopis, whom no man hath seen at any time.
Thou art Iarol. Thou art Itos.
Thou art Apenoth. Thou art Sapenoth.
Thou hast distinguished between the just and the unjust.
Thou didst make the female and the male.
Thou didst produce the seed and the fruit.
Thou didst form men to love one another and to hate one another.

I am Nopheth thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Iscuren.
Thou didst produce the moist and the dry, and that which nourisheth all created life.

Hear Thou me, for I am the Angel of Aphroden Acromenalba; this is Thy true name, handed down to the prophets of Issepel.

Hear me: Or, Thiom, Rheiset, Atelophorseth, A, Blotha, Apheu, Epheu, Phe, Chitanoe, Ip, Thiom.

Hear me, and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.

I invoke Thee, the Terrible and Invisible God who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit: Aronopholobrao, Sopheh, Mopholio, Phalornaeh, Ooo.

I am the Tongueless Daiken with sight in his feet.
I am the Mighty One, who possesses the immortal Fire.
I am the Truth that hates the deeds of injustice in the world.
I am the one whose mouth utterly flames.
I am the one who begets and destroys.
I am the Grace of the World.

PULSE WOUND BY A COIL is my name.

IŌM SAPENŌTH.
