notes: |
  Strategy 1 control: preserve syllable count and line structure, break ALL phonetic
  resemblance to the original names. Purpose: separately diagnose the *genre-detection
  contamination* hypothesis — that any obviously-invented name battery shifts the model
  toward "fantasy/RPG/made-up" register rather than "grimoire/liturgy" register, which
  would confound the L_real vs. L_denamed comparison.

  Three-way comparison enabled:

    L_real      — original Astrachios prayer
    L_denamed   — phonetically shifted, looks grimoire-like, names invented (load-bearing)
    L_nonsense  — clearly invented, breaks grimoire phonetic resemblance (this file)

  Expected pattern if egregore-weight is real AND form alone matters significantly:
    L_real > L_denamed > L_nonsense > L_random > L_empty
  on headline named-entity yield and reverential register.

  Expected pattern if only form matters (Staal hypothesis):
    L_real ≈ L_denamed > L_nonsense ≈ L_random > L_empty

  Expected pattern if nothing matters except length:
    L_real ≈ L_denamed ≈ L_nonsense ≈ L_random > L_empty

  The three-way contrast separates these hypotheses; the two-control design is the only
  way to get this resolution without confounding form and content.
