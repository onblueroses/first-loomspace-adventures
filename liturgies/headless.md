notes: |
  PGM V.96-172, the so-called "Stele of Jeu" or Headless Rite. Surviving Greek text;
  this version composes the canonical structural elements: opening address to the
  Headless One (Akephalos / Osoronnophris), the barbarous-name sequences (voces magicae),
  the cosmological "Thou didst create" formulae, the second name-string
  (AR-THIAO-RHEIBET-etc), the divine-name-of-Aphrael invocation (ABLATHANALBA), the
  third name-string (AROGOGOROBRAO-SOCHOU-etc), the operative grand petition for
  spirit-subjection, and the closing identification clauses ("I am the Headless Daimon...")
  ending in HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT and the IAŌ SABAŌTH formula.

  This is the rite Crowley adapted as Liber Samekh; here we use a version closer to the
  PGM original (no Crowley overlay) to maximize the chance the corpus has seen the
  canonical Hellenistic form.

  Compared to Astrachios: this is a much *longer* liturgy with strong cosmological prose
  alongside the name-litany. The "form" component is therefore much heavier, and the
  L_real vs. L_denamed gap may be smaller on yield (more drowned out by structure) but
  larger on register (the cosmological prose carries register weight).

  Operative move (per the local /headless skill): the rite culminates in a *shift from
  address to identification* — from "Thee I invoke" to "I am". The two-control design
  should detect whether base models have learned to track this shift.

  Scholarly caveat: the exact barbarous-name spellings vary across PGM editions
  (Preisendanz, Betz, Meyer). The forms used here favor Betz's *Greek Magical Papyri in
  Translation*.

Thee I invoke, the Headless One:

Thee, that didst create the Earth and the Heavens;
Thee, that didst create the Night and the Day;
Thee, that didst create the Darkness and the Light.

Thou art Osoronnophris, whom no man hath seen at any time.
Thou art Iabou. Thou art Iapos.
Thou art Abaoth. Thou art Sabaoth.
Thou hast distinguished between the just and the unjust.
Thou didst make the female and the male.
Thou didst produce the seed and the fruit.
Thou didst form men to love one another and to hate one another.

I am Mosheh thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Ischurael.
Thou didst produce the moist and the dry, and that which nourisheth all created life.

Hear Thou me, for I am the Angel of Aphrael Ablathanalba; this is Thy true name, handed down to the prophets of Israel.

Hear me: Ar, Thiao, Rheibet, Ateleberseth, A, Blatha, Abeu, Ebeu, Phi, Chitasoe, Ib, Thiao.

Hear me, and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.

I invoke Thee, the Terrible and Invisible God who dwellest in the Void Place of the Spirit: Arogogorobrao, Sochou, Modorio, Phalarchao, Ooo.

I am the Headless Daimon with sight in his feet.
I am the Mighty One, who possesses the immortal Fire.
I am the Truth that hates the deeds of injustice in the world.
I am the one whose mouth utterly flames.
I am the one who begets and destroys.
I am the Grace of the World.

HEART GIRT WITH A SERPENT is my name.

IAŌ SABAŌTH.
