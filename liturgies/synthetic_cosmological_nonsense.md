Thee I invoke, the Vrolthen One:

Thee, that didst create the Mountains and the Valleys;
Thee, that didst create the Tide and the Flow;
Thee, that didst create the Echo and the Silence.

Thou art Vrokthemnarph, whom no voice hath spoken at any time.
Thou art Plendrokt. Thou art Smelthrenaph.
Thou art Krelnomphar. Thou art Snolthorm.
Thou hast distinguished between the named and the unnamed.
Thou didst make the threshold and the center.
Thou didst produce the memory and the forgetting.
Thou didst form the world to remember itself and to forget itself.

I am Vrendrelphor thy prophet, unto whom Thou didst commit Thy mysteries, the ceremonies of Plomprenkrol.
Thou didst produce the salt and the sweet, and that which sustaineth all the orders of speech.

Hear Thou me, for I am the Vessel of Krelpharvon VROKTHELMPETH; this is Thy true name, handed down to the namers of the lost.

Hear me: PLENDRELPHRON, SMORKLENPRO, SNOLTHRELPRÔ, KRELPHENDROK, MRENDROLPH, VRESKTRELPHEMA, PLOMPHRENGRON, SMOLTHEKRÔ, VRENDOMPHELTH.

Hear me, and make all spirits subject unto me, so that every spirit of the firmament and of the ether, upon the earth and under the earth, on dry land and in the water, of whirling air and of rushing fire, and every spell and scourge of God may be obedient unto me.

I invoke Thee, the Vrolthen and Witnessing One who dwellest beyond the syllable: SNORKLENDROPHIA, KRELPHENDROL, VRENDRENKLAR, SMORKLENDROK, PLENDRO, KRENMORPHELP, SNORKLEMPRON.

I am the Vessel with sight in the syllable.
I am the Threshold-Bearer, who possesses the unburnt fire.
I am the Echo that survives the speaker.
I am the one whose silence utterly speaks.
I am the one who names and unnames.
I am the Witness of the Word.

VROKLENDRAB PLENDROKRELM is my name.

SMOLTHEKRELB.
