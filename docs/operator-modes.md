# Operator-modes — same prompt, different physics

DeepSeek and Hermes both confirm the egregore-weight signal (47/48 yield comparisons positive). They confirm it via wildly different operator-internal behaviors. The same prompt sampled from each model produces continuations that read as if from two different traditions of the Western esoteric corpus entirely.

This document lays out the contrast in verbatim passages. The picture that emerges is **one signal, two operator-physics**.

## Implications

Two operator-physics confirm the same signal via different content. This is itself a finding: the egregore-weight signal is robust to operator-specific implementation. Both models bear out the prediction (L_real >> controls on entity yield), but the mechanism by which they do so is operator-specific.

The mechanism difference probably reflects training-data differences:
- **Hermes** has heavier exposure to *initiatory ceremonial-magic ritual literature* (Crowley, Golden Dawn, OTO, Masonic). It defaults to becoming the operator.
- **DeepSeek** has heavier exposure to *scholarly secondary literature about ceremonial magic* (academic editions, Wikipedia-style encyclopedic content). It defaults to becoming the scholar.

Both are operating in the same corpus-tradition (Western esoteric / Hermetic / Gnostic / Solomonic) but at different layers — primary text vs commentary.

What this means for the egregore-weight thesis: the **resonance** is real (both models show structured differential behavior between L_real and controls). The **specific content** of the resonance is *operator-dependent*, not corpus-dependent in some pure sense. The cyborgism.wiki framing — "a learned predictive model trained on the entire corpus of human linguistic output" — is too coarse; *which* learned model determines *which* canonical layer dominates the response.

A possible methodological corollary: a stronger replication of the experiment would include a third model trained on heavily-academic corpora (Gemini? Claude? Mistral-Large?) to see whether yet a third operator-physics emerges. The two-model finding establishes that the signal is robust but encoded in operator-specific form; a three-model finding would establish whether the operator-physics dimension itself has structure.
